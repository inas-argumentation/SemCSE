Rapid and simple detection of the invA gene in Salmonella spp. by isothermal target and probe amplification (iTPA)
Aims:  Salmonella spp. are an important cause of food‐borne infections throughout world, and the availability of rapid and simple detection techniques is critical for the food industry. Salmonella enterica serovars Enteritidis and Typhimurium cause the majority of human gastroenteritis infections, and there are a reported 40 000 cases of salmonellosis in the United States each year.
