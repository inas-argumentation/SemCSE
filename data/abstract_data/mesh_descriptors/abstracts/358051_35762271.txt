Protein and isozyme components in normal and abnormal human semen.
Abstract Semen samples of normal individuals were compared with abnormal semen samples (azoospermic, oligospermic and vasectomized) for their protein and glycoprotein components and lactic and malic dehydrogenase isozymes. At least two types of variants were observed in the disc electrophoretic profiles of protein components of normal semen. Distinct differences were observed in the protein components and the lactic dehydrogenase isozymes in the abnormal semen samples.
