Cotton-type collagen insertion at mediastinoscopy for easier node dissection after induction chemoradiotherapy for lung cancer.
Induction chemoradiotherapy followed by anatomical resection is a current therapeutic strategy for non–small cell lung cancer with mediastinal node involvement. However, mediastinal node dissection after chemoradiotherapy is technically difficult because of the severe adhesion and fibrosis caused by the therapy. We describe a novel technique to make the node dissection easier after induction chemoradiotherapy.
