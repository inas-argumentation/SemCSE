[A scirrhous carcinoma of the nipple suspected of being Paget's disease].
Discussed is a 38-year-old woman, whose chief complaints were an eczema-like lesion and itching of the nipple in the left breast. Thus, Paget's disease was suspected, and touch cytology and a cytological biopsy of an aspirated specimen were done. The results indicated a class II and class V status, respectively. A radical mastectomy, therefore, was performed immediately. Macroscopic findings of the resected tissue showed no tumor in the nipple or in the mammary gland. A histological examination led to the determination of a scirrhous carcinoma of the nipple. Seventeen months after the operation, the patient has had no recurrence.
