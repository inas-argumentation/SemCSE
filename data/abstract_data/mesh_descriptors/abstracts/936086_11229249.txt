DECREASED NOREPINEPHRINE CONTENT IN THE MEDULLA OBLONGATA IN SEVERELY HYPERTENSIVE RATS
1. To clarify possible abnormalities in catecholamines in the medulla oblongata in relation to severe hypertension, the authors measured changes in catecholamine levels in the medulla oblongata of malignant stroke‐prone spontaneously hypertensive rats (M‐SHRSP). Effects of the adrenal medullae and peripheral nerves were ruled out by adrenal demedullation and chemical sym‐pathectomy.
