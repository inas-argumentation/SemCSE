SUMMARY OF RESULTS WITH TRIETHYLENE THIOPHOSPHORAMIDE
Triethylene thiophosphoramidet (N,N’,N”-triethylene thiophosphoramide (TSPA, Thio-TEPA) is the sulfur derivative of triethylene phosphoramide. Like triethylene melamine (TEM) and triethylene phosphoramide (TEPA), it is a member of the class of compounds known as the ethylenimines.
