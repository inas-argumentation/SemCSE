The purpose of this article is t o briefly review the relationship between the inhibition of the renal Na+ + K+-ATPase system and the excretion of Na+, and t o present our new work on this subject. The inhibitors considered will be only cardiac glycosides, ethacrynic acid, and mercurials since the renal work with other inhibitors of the enzyme is too fragmentary for coherent analysis. The emphasis, although not t o the exclusion of other approaches, will be on in viuo experiments since most other renal preparations lack the dynamic qualities of filtration, reabsorption, and readjustment t o intervention.
