Hashimoto encephalopathy following iodine 131 (131 I) radiotherapy of Graves disease.
H ashimoto encephalopathy (HE) is defined by spontaneous subacute encephalopathy with raised thyroid autoantibodies and a remarkable responsiveness to steroids. Pathophysiologically, an association with Hashimoto thyroiditis is characterized by observations of elevated thyroid peroxidase antibody (TPO-Ab) levels or thyroglobulin antibody (TG-Ab) levels. We describe a patient who developed HE following iodine 131 (I) radiotherapy of Graves disease.
