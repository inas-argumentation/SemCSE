Mechanical Treatment for Obesity
The management of obesity involves not only the promotion of weight loss but also the treatment of coexistent complications or associated conditions, both medical and psychological. Indeed, in some circumstances, weight reduction itself may be of only secondary importance. Life-long modification of eating habits remains the cornerstone of effective weight reduction. A number of additional therapies, however, have been developed to assist the more morbidly obese with weight loss. These include various mechanical methods of promoting weight loss.
