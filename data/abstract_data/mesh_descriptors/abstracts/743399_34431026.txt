DNA analysis in patients with hereditary fructose intolerance
Restriction fragments of the adlolase B gene were studied in 11 patients with hereditary fructose intolerance and compared with the normal pattern. No major deletion of the gene was observed. One patient was found to be a compound heterozygote since one allele with normal restriction sites was inherited from the mother and the other with an abnormal Barn HI site was inherited from the father. The anomaly of the Barn HI fragment observed in this family was not found in 62 normal controls from the same origin as the patient.
