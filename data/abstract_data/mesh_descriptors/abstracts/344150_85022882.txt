Induction studies of Δ1-tetrahydrocannabinol metabolism by rat and rabbit lungs
1. Δ 1 -THC was metabolized by the isolated, perfused lung of rabbits and lung post-mitochondrial supernatant of rats but not by the lung subcellular fractions of rabbits. 2. Metabolism by rat lung post-mitochondrial supernatant declined in the 3–9 week postpartum period. 3. Pretreatment with 3-methylcholanthrene induced metabolism by the isolated, perfused lung of rabbits and rats, while pretreatment with phenobarbital induced metabolism only by the isolated, perfused rat lung. 4. Δ 1 -THC metabolism by the lung appears to be affected by species, age, lung preparation and chemical pretreatment.
