Electron microscopy of native DNA in sea urchin cells.
A method is described for the extraction of native DNA from cells and its spreading with chymotrypsin. No evidence of discontinuities in the fibrils was recorded either after staining or shadowing. Evidence is presented for the DNA nature of the strands. The DNA from sea urchin sperm and sea urchin embryo nuclei appears as strands longer than 100 μ. The height of the strands is about 21–25 . Proteases do not affect the dimensions of the strands, but DNase and shearing rapidly degrade them.
