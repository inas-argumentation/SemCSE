Type 2 Diabetes in Children and Adolescents: Information from the National Diabetes Education Program
iabetes is a chronic disease in which the body does not mak or properly use insulin, a hormone that is needed to convert sugar, starches, and other food into energy. People with diabetes have increased blood glucose (sugar) levels because of a lack of insulin, insufficient insulin, or resistance to insulin’s effects. High levels of glucose build up in the blood and spill into the urine and out of the body.
