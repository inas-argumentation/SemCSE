HIV-1 prevention in the context of increasing access to treatment
In their Comment Helene Gayle and Joep Lange discuss recommendations for HIV-1 prevention in the context of increasing access to antiretroviral treatment. They state that prevention services should be specifically tailored for people with HIV but do not mention sexual and reproductive health needs. Medecins Sans Frontieres and the Thai Network for People with HIV/AIDS support holistic care including antiretroviral treatment in two district hospitals in rural Thailand. On July 15 2004 205 adults (114 men and 91 women) and 14 children were taking highly active antiretroviral therapy. Of the 91 women five are pregnant. All said their pregnancies were unexpected even though condom use was inconsistent (two cases) or absent (three cases) and no other contraception was used. (excerpt)
