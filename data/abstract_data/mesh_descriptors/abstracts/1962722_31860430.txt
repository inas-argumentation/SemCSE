Morphometry of capillaries in three zones of rabbit lungs fixed by vascular perfusion
In large lungs in the upright position, three functional conditions originated by gravity‐related changes in the arterial (Pa) and venous (Pv) pressures with respect to alveolar air pressure (PA) are defined as follows: Zone 1 (apex), where PA > Pa > Pv, Zone 2 (middle), where Pa > PA > Pv, and Zone 3 (bottom), where Pa > Pv > PA. This article attempts to clarify the anatomic correlates of these “zonal” changes.
