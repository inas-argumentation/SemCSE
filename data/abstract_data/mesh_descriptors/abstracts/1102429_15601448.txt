Regional Distribution of Proteinase K‐Resistant α‐Synuclein Correlates with Lewy Body Disease Stage
Lewy bodies (LBs) containing α-synuclein (αSYN) fibrils are the hallmark lesions of Parkinson disease, dementia with LBs, and related neurodegenerative diseases. Here we have investigated the susceptibility to proteolysis of αSYN from brain samples of patients with different subtypes of LB diseases. While soluble αSYN was completely degradable in all samples, the affected brain regions additionally contained insoluble, proteinase K (PK)-resistant αSYN. In brainstem-predominant subtype LB disease cases, PK-resistant αSYN was found in the medulla and substantia nigra, but not in cerebral cortex. In limbic subtype LB disease cases, PK resistance of αSYN spread to the cingulate and parahippocampal cortex, and further to the frontal cortex in neocortical subtype LB disease cases. Variable amounts of neuritic PK-resistant αSYN were found in the striatum of all cases. Thus, PK resistance of αSYN may be useful for the development of biomarkers of LB diseases.
