Acute effect of L‐796568, a novel β3‐adrenergic receptor agonist, on energy expenditure in obese men
Our objective was to investigate the thermogenic efficacy of single oral doses of the novel β3‐adrenergic receptor agonist L‐796568 [(R)‐N‐[4‐[2‐[[2‐hydroxy‐2‐(3‐pyridinyl)ethyl]amino]ethyl]‐phenyl]‐4‐[4‐[4‐(trifluoromethyl)phenyl]thiazol‐2‐yl]‐benzenesulfonamide, dihydrochloride] inhumans.
