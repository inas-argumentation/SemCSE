[Comparison of donor defects of fibula and iliac crest grafts].
In order to study the donor-site morbidity following osteocutaneous fibula and iliac-crest flaps, 17 patients were asked to answer a questionnaire and to present for a follow-up examination. The donor defect after fibula graft was preferred to the iliac crest donor defect with respect to esthetic appearance and complication rate.
