Paclitaxel embolism in a transplant kidney: a rare complication of drug-coated balloon angioplasty
Drug-coated balloon (DCB) angioplasty with paclitaxel has been shown to improve the outcome of interventions for lower extremity atherosclerotic disease. However, liberalization of paclitaxel into bloodstream causing downstream embolization has been demonstrated in experimental animal studies [1], and in human case reports [2, 3]. We report a case of biopsy-proven foreign body embolization after repeated paclitaxel DCB angioplasties for recurrent transplant renal artery stenosis.
