Carcinoma of the lung presenting with digital ischaemia.
Non-metastatic syndromes associated with lung neoplasms are well recognised.' In a prospective study endocrine or metabolic disorders were found in 12% of 240 patients with lung cancer.2 Venous thromboembolism occurred in 14% of 2360 patients presenting to the Mayo Clinic with primary lung neoplasm,' but digital ischaemia as a presenting feature of carcinoma of the lung has not been previously described.
