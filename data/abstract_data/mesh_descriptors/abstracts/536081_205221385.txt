Complete nucleotide sequence of human glutamate dehydrogenase cDNA
Human glutamate dehydrogenase (GDH) cDNAs were isolated from a fibroblast cDNA pCD library (1,2). Two cDNAs which were identical in sequence were apparently cloned independently, since the vectors had different lengths of poly-G at their 5' ends. This sequence contains an open reading frame beginning with ATG at base 14 encoding a 53 amino acid presequence followed by the complete mature protein sequence of 505 amino acids. The presequence is apparently a mitochondrial matrix targeting signal. There follows a 3' untranslated sequence of 1261 nucleotides. Several potential polyadenylation sites are contained within this sequence. At least two of these were used in the fibroblasts, since two additional cDNAs ended with a poly-A sequence at base 1960. Partial sequences derived from incomplete GDH cDNAs previously isolated from human brain and human liver did not differ from this sequence over their extent (3).
