Quantitative electroencephalography as a marker of cognitive fluctuations in dementia with Lewy bodies and an aid to differential diagnosis
Highlights • EEG slowing was evident in dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB) and Parkinson’s disease dementia (PDD) and less in Alzheimer’s disease (AD) patients compared to controls.• Dominant rhythm variability was larger in AD but only correlated with cognitive fluctuations in DLB.• QEEG variables classified DLB and AD patients with high sensitivity and specificity.
