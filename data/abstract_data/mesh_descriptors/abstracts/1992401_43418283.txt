ASK-753, a new iron-containing antibiotic.
A new iron-containing peptide which inhibits the growth of Gram-positive and some Gram-negative bacteria was isolated from the broth of Streptomyces AS-K-753. The antibiotic was obtained as light buff plates. On acid hydrolysis eight amino acids, three organic acids and one keto acid could be detected.ASK-753 is similar in many properties to the sideromycin but differs from it in certain crucial characteristics.
