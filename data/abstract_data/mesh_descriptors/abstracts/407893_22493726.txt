Predicting hypocalcaemia post‐thyroidectomy: a retrospective audit of results compared to a previously published nomogram in 64 patients treated at a district general hospital
• This is a unique paper to concentrate purely on readmissions for epistaxis in terms of extent and clinical predictors. • Re-admissions for epistaxis appear to be falling year on year, with an average of 9.4%. • Significant predictors of re-admission were the presence of diabetes and hypertension, after other cofactors were adjusted for. • 54.5% of our epistaxis population were considered eligible to use oral tranexamic acid on discharge. • Emerging evidence from other surgical/trauma specialties shows promising results for the use of tranexamic acid in reducing bleeding. Further evidence for both topical and oral use is eagerly sought in uncomplicated epistaxis.
