Is the amino acid but not the nucleotide sequence of the Escherichia coli araC gene conserved?
The nucleotide sequence of the ara C regulatory gene from Escherichia coli K-12 was determined. Comparison with the ara C gene sequence from strain B/r reveals nine base-pair differences, two in the 163-base messenger leader region and seven in the translated region. All seven differences in the translated region are in the third, “wobble”, position of the codon and none results in an altered amino acid. This drift in nucleotide sequence, but conservation in amino acid sequence, suggests that few amino acid substitutions in ara C regulatory protein are tolerated on an evolutionary time-scale.
