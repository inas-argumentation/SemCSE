Angiographic aspects of renal cell carcinoma metastatic to the female genitalia
Selective angiography of the hypogastric artery was performed in two patients presenting vaginal metastases from renal cell carcinoma, in order to better define the extent of the metastases. In another case left renal angiography revealed an unsuspected metastasis to the left ovary, fed by a middle perforating capsular artery.
