Erythrocyte sedimentation rate values in active tuberculosis with and without HIV co-infection.
A retrospective descriptive study was undertaken to examine ESR values within the active period of TB disease. TB patients seen at the hospital during a 12-month period from 1 April 2004 to 31 March 2005 were included. Values were described in terms of two categories namely ESR = 100 mm/h and ESR < 100 mm/h in TB cases with and without concomitant HIV infection. For the purposes of the study active TB disease was defined as recently diagnosed TB that had not been treated for more than 2 weeks. The NHLS in Nelspruit measures the ESR using the Westergren method and gives normal reference ranges of 0 - 10 mm/h for males 0 - 15 mm/h for females and 0 - 31 mm/h for people over 60 years of age. The laboratory uses the enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) method as the confirmatory test for HIV infection. (excerpt)
