Anatomical relationships between type II pneumonocytes and alveolar septal gaps in the human lung
This report describes a relationship between type II pneumonocytes and breaks in continuity in the alveolar septum of the human lung. Breaks in continuity of the septum are defined as gaps in the connective tissue matrix of the alveolar septum, with or without discontinuity of the accompanying alveolar epithelium. Septal connective tissue gaps accompanied by epithelial discontinuity are recognized as interalveolar pores of Kohn. When the discontinuity is confined to the connective tissue matrix, epithelial continuity may be maintained by either a single or a double layer of type I epithelium, by a type II cell, or by both type I and type II epithelial cells.
