The ilvEDA operon of Escherichia coli K12 encodes only one valine-α-ketoglutarate transaminas activity
Transaminase B of E. coli K12 was purified to apparent homogeneity as measured by SDS acrylamide gel electrophoresis, immunoelectrophoresis, and amino terminal sequence analysis. The valine- and isoleucine-α-ketoglutarate dependent transaminase activities of pure enzyme as well as crude extracts were characterized by immunologic and kinetic methods. The data disprove the existence of a separate valine-α-ketoglutarate transaminase within the ilvEDA operon.
