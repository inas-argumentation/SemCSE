Tissue and plasma electrolyte levels in the awake state during the year, in summer cold exposure and during arousal from hibernation in the hedgehog (Erinaceus europaeus).
1. 1. Sodium, potassium, magnesium and calcium plasma and tissue levels were measured in the hedgehog. 2. 2. In winter awake animals the plasma sodium was significantly higher (3·3 per cent) and the plasma potassium lower (23 per cent) than in summer awake animals. 3. 3. During summer cold exposure there was a fall in the plasma magnesium (21 per cent) and calcium (5 per cent). 4. 4. Plasma magnesium reached a peak in arousal at a body temperature of 16–20°C. 5. 5. Most tissue ion levels were unaltered in the winter and in summer cold exposure; changes which did occur in the tissue and plasma ion levels were different in the winter awake state to those of summer cold exposure. 6. 6. Tissue magnesium levels in arousal were similar to those in hibernation but showed signs of returning to awake levels.
