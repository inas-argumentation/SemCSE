Determination of picomole quantities of eukaryotic amino-acyl-tRNA with bacterial elongation factor Tu: the extent of in vivo acylation of tRNA.
The formation of a ternary complex between elongation factor Tu (EF Tu) from Escherichia coli, eukaryotic aminoacyl-tRNA, and [3H]GTP has been investigated by means of a nitrocellulose filter assay. Under optimal assay conditions the quantitative determination of pmole amounts of either labeled or unlabeled aminoacyl-tRNA by the formation of the ternary complex is demonstrated. The EF Tu-binding assay has been used to determine the proportion of avian liver tRNA acylated to amino acids in vivo. The results from 1-day, 10-day, adult and estrogen-stimulated animals are identical; 35 % of the tRNA is acylated in all cases. The effectiveness of several published tRNA deacylation methods has been assessed by the EF Tu-binding assay. For maximum deacylation a two-step procedure using a base-catalyzed step followed by a Cu2+-catalyzed step is described.
