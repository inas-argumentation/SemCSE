Breast cancer risk from diet, tobacco, and alcohol.
To the Editor. —A large prospective study of 89 538 nurses by Willett et al 1 concluded after 4 years of follow-up that moderate alcohol consumption is a risk factor for development of breast cancer. In a recent paper by the same group of investigators, additional information is given on the number of women who developed breast cancer by stratum of alcohol consumption. The purpose of this letter is to draw attention to an apparent diminishing temporal trend in the risk of breast cancer associated with alcohol in the study.
