Smoking-related cancers and potential years of life lost attributable to cigarette smoking, ireland, 1968 to 1978
SummaryA Ttributable risks within maximum and minimum limits were calculated for six smoking-related cancers in Ireland— lung, larynx, oesophagus, bladder, kidney, and pancreas. From these calculations were derived potential years of life lost to Irish men and women aged between 30 and 64 years from each cancer site which could be directly attributed to cigarette smoking. The relationship between the calculated attributable risks and the potential reduction of disease are discussed from a public health point of view.
