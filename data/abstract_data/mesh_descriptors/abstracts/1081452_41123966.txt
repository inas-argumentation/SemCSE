Expression of the glyceraldehyde‐3‐phosphate dehydrogenase gene from the extremely thermophilic archaebacterium Methanothermus fervidus in E. coli
The gene of glyceraldehyde‐3‐phosphate dehydrogenase (GAPDH) from the extremely thermophilic archaebacterium Methanothermus fervidus (growth optimum 82°C) was cloned in vector pJF118EH and expressed in E. coli cells. As shown by molecular mass determination, protein sequencing, heat stability, and substrate saturation kinetics, the enzyme synthesized in E. coli is identical to the original enzyme from M. fervidus. The high thermostability of the E. coli‐produced M. fervidus GAPDH allows rapid purification to homogeneity. From this enzyme protein crystals were grown which proved to be suitable for X‐ray analysis. The crystals are of tetragonal space group P4122 and contain a dimer per asymmetric unit.
