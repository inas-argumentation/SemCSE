Primary peripheral T‐cell lymphoma in subcutaneous tissue: Sonographic findings
We describe the sonographic findings in a case of Lennert's lymphoma, a rare type of peripheral T‐cell lymphoma, involving the subcutaneous tissues of the arm. The sonographic appearance was thought to be more helpful than MRI to establish the diagnosis. © 2004 Wiley Periodicals, Inc. J Clin Ultrasound 32:361–364, 2004
