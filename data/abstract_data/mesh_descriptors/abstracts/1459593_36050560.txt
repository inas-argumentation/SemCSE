Quality-of-life assessment in cancer treatment protocols: research issues in protocol development.
BACKGROUND
Interest in incorporating quality of life as an end point in clinical studies of cancer treatment has intensified in recent years.


PURPOSE
We provide practical suggestions that will assist investigators considering including quality-of-life assessment in phase III therapeutic trials.


METHODS
We discuss issues important in study planning, including quality-of-life definition, priority studies for quality-of-life assessment, eligibility requirements, and design.


CONCLUSIONS
Many of the problems that quality-of-life studies have encountered, from protocol approval to data analysis, could be addressed and alleviated during protocol development. This discussion is intended to assist and stimulate investigators conducting research in this area.
