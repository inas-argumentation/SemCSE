High Expression of the Penicillin Acylase Gene in Genetically Engineered Escherichia colia
Penicillin acylase (penicillin amidase or benzyl penicillin amidohydrolase EC 3.5.1 . I 1 ) is an enzyme used commercially to hydrolyze penicillin G to 6-aminopenicillanic acid (6-APA),’ which is the intermediate for production of various semisynthetic penicillins. Penicillin acylase enzyme has been found in many microorganisms, but the enzyme from E. coli is the most widely studied. In general, production of penicillin acylase (PA) is induced by phenylacetic acid (PAA) and subjected to catabolite repression by glucose.’ The maximum level of penicillin acylase activity has been found in cells grown at low t e m p e r a t ~ r e . ~ To increase enzyme production, we have cloned the penicillin acylase gene4 from the recombinant plasmid pMLV7 and have placed it downstream from a strong promoter “ptac”, which contains the 35 region of the trp promoter fused to the lac uv5-10 region and lac operator sequences. The enzyme production from the penicillin acylase gene of the new clone is compared to that of the original strains. The condition of enzyme induction is also investigated.
