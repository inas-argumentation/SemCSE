PD‐1 and PD‐L1 in neoplastic cells and the tumor microenvironment of Merkel cell carcinoma
Merkel cell carcinoma (MCC) is an aggressive neoplasm, which is often associated with Merkel cell polyomavirus (MCPyV). Programmed death‐1 (PD‐1) and its ligand PD‐L1 are key players of the tumor microenvironment (TME).
