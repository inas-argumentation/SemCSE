In vitro effects of oestrogen on testosterone metabolism by human breast cancers.
Abstract The effect of in vitro addition of oestradiol on the metabolism of 7 α 3 H testosterone by 15 human breast cancers has been investigated. Oestradiol significantly inhibited metabolism and conversion to 5 α dihydrotestosterone, 5 α androstanediol and Δ 4 androstenedione.
