Prokaryotic Cells Growth Stimulation Induced by Electromagnetic Radiation at a Frequency of the Molecular Oxygen Absorption Spectrum
It was demonstrated earlier that electromagnetic radiation (EMR) in the millimeter wavelength range stimulated growth of microorganisms [1–3]. However, these studies were performed using eukaryotic cells of algae or yeast. Although prokaryotic cells were studied in [4–6], the EMR frequencies used in these experiments (42.2 and 53.5 GHz) fell within the frequency range of classical EHF (extremely high frequency) therapy.
