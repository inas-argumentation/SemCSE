A comparison of D-, L-, DL-methionine and methionine hydroxy analogue calcium in chick diets.
Abstract NUMEROUS reports indicate equal utilization of the d - and l -isomers of methionine by chickens (Grau and Almquist, 1943; Fell et al., 1959; Leveille et al., 1960; Bauriedel, 1963; Featherston et al., 1962). On the other hand, Bruggemann et al. (1962) reported the d- isomer to be inferior to the l- isomer in purified diets. Marrett et al. (1964) observed that the d- isomer was inferior to the l- isomer when the diet contained an appreciable amount of d- amino acids, but was nutritionally equivalent to l -methionine in the absence of other d- isomers. Data indicating that d -isomers of some amino acids depress the utilization of d -methionine more than others were reported recently (Marrett and Sunde, 1965). Some short term utilization and excretion studies have also indicated poorer utilization of the d -isomer of methionine.
