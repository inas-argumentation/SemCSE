Ultrastructure of hepatic parenchyma in murine hepatosplenic bilharziasis
SummaryMice infected withSchistosoma mansoni show mild liver cell injury as early as 2 weeks after infection regardless of the infection intensity used in this experiment, and before the development of overt hepatosplenic bilharziasis. The changes involve vesiculation of the rough-surfaced endoplasmic reticulum, propagation of the smooth-surfaced vesicles, and hyperactivation of the Golgi complex. With the onset of overt hepatosplenic bilharziasis an increase in the number of microbodies and the appearance of cytoplasmic fibrils are observed. The liver cells adjacent to the inflammatory granulomas show loss of their microvilli.These results provide evidence for a probable toxic effect executed by the parasite and the local edema set up around the inflammatory granulomas enclosing the embolised eggs of the parasite.
