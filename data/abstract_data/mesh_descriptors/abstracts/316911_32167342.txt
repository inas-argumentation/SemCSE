Spontaneous development of cytosolic phosphoenolpyruvate carboxylase in foetal rat liver cultures.
Abstract Phosphoenolpyruvate carboxylase activity rises in foetal rat liver culture after 45 h of incubation from no detectable or very low values, to high levels, very close to those usually found in vivo 48–72 h after birth. Both hydrocortisone and glucagon enhance in vitro the phosphoenolpyruvate carboxylase activity, whereas cycloheximide completely prevents this spontaneous activation.
