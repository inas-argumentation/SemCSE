Inverse correlation between body mass index and clinical outcomes in men with advanced castration–recurrent prostate cancer
Obesity has a variety of adverse health outcomes, but to the authors' knowledge, the effect of obesity on outcome in patients with advanced prostate cancer is not known. For this reason, the correlation between an elevated body mass index (BMI) and clinical outcomes in patients with metastatic, castration–recurrent prostate cancer (CRPC) was evaluated.
