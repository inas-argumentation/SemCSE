Draft Genome Sequence of a Novel Bacterial Strain, LSJC7, Belonging to the Family Enterobacteriaceae with Dual Resistance to Arsenic and Tetracycline
Strain LSJC7, with dual resistance to arsenic and tetracycline, was isolated from an antimony tailing in China. Its 16S rRNA gene sequence has the highest similarity to that of Enterobacter cloacae subsp. dissolvens LMG 2683T (97.02%). Here we present the approximately 4.6-Mbp draft genome sequence of strain LSJC7.
