Thoughts on ‘Redefining residential rehabilitation in Australia’
Saraf and Newton describe the Community Recovery Program, a novel adaptation of the Community Care Unit (CCU) model, with emphasis on recovery, partnership and community.1 Innovative efforts to further shift existing models of residential rehabilitation closer towards realizing recovery-oriented practice should be encouraged. Similar efforts have occurred across Queensland over recent years. These have included the integrated staffing model at the Metro South Addiction and Mental Health Service (MSAMHS)2 and collaborative partnerships with the nongovernment sector.
