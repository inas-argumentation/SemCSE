When HIV breaks through.

 Even though highly active antiretroviral therapy (HAART) is now the treatment norm, there are still many cases where patients do not benefit from this aggressive therapy. As many as one-third of AIDS patients may experience treatment failure due to resistance, intolerance, or the inability to adhere to the treatment requirements. One problem in addressing the resistance issue is the shortage of drug-resistant, mutant HIV for use in drug experiments, and drug companies are working with the National Institutes of Health (NIH) to develop an AIDS Research Reagent Repository. A protease inhibitor response project is underway to study unusual results to the therapy. San Francisco General Hospital is developing a yet-unnamed Breakthrough Clinic by incorporating specialists from several areas to examine the problem of viral breakthroughs. Understanding the complex nature of HIV drug resistance is one of the most difficult challenges in determining effective HIV treatments.
