Effect of metal ions on the hydroxylation of γ-butyrobetaine to carnitine in rat liver homogenates
The formation of carnitine from γ-butyrobetaine in the 20 000 × g supernatant fraction of rat liver homogenates was inhibited by several metal-ion complexing agents. The inhibition by 1,10-phenanthroline could be reversed completely by Fe 2+ . In the presence of 14 mM ascorbate, low concentrations of Fe 2+ (about 0.04 mM in Tris-HCl buffer) gave a several-fold stimulation of carnitine formation, whereas an inhibition was observed with higher concentrations of Fe 2+ . Several transition-metal ions were inhibitory. The results suggest that Fe 2+ is required for the hydroxylation of γ-butyrobetaine to carnitine in rat liver.
