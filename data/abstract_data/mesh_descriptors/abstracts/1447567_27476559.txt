The effect of acute and chronic injections of d-amphetamine sulfate and substantia nigra lesions on the distribution of amphetamine and para-hydroxyamphetamine in the rat brain.
Amphetamine and para -hydroxyamphetamine have been identified in four rat brain regions after various treatments with d-amphetamine sulfate. The data indicates a rapid and prolonged concentration of para -hydroxyamphetamine into the striatum and olfactory tubercles. Electrolytic lesions placed in the substantia nigra reduced para -hydroxyamphetamine accumulation in the ipsilateral striatum suggesting that this amine was concentrated into neuronal structures originating in the substantia nigra.
