Focal hypoechoic areas in the porta hepatis.
To the Editor:-I read with much interest the article l by Dr. Lincoln L. Berland, entitled "Focal Areas of Decreased Echogenicity in the Liver at the Porta Hepatis," that appeared in the March issue of JUM. We have seen two similar cases and want to comment on them. The first case was a 39-year-old diabetic man referred for gallbladder sonogram because of abdominal pain. A hypoechoic lesion (Fig. 1) in the porta hepatis similar to that described by Dr. Berland was seen. Contrary to Dr. Berland's case, a high-density area (Fig. 2) corresponding to the lesion was seen on CT scan after intravenous contrast administration. No precontrast CT was performed.
