Postnatal development of cholinesterase, acetylcholinesterase, aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase and monoamine oxidase C57B1-6 and DBA-2 mice.
The in vitro activities of acetylcholinesterase (AChE), cholinesterase (ChE), aromatic L -amino acid decarboxylase (AAD), and monoamine oxidase (MAO) were determined in whole brains of C57BL/6 and DBA/2 mice at 0, 7, 14, 21, 28, and 35 days of age. Mean activities of AChE, AAD, and MAO were higher in DBA/2 than in C57BL/6 mice at all ages sampled. Brain weight, on the other hand, was higher in C57BL/6 than in DBA/2 mice at all ages, as was ChE activity from 7 days onward. Differences in AAD and MAO activities were greatest in early postnatal development and tended to dissipate by 35 days of age. AChE activity was significantly different at all ages except 7 and 14 days. ChE activity was significantly different olnly at 21 and 28 days of age in this experiment.
