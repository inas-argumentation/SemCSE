Gangliosides in the myelin fraction of developing rats.
1. 1. Highly pure myelin fractions have been obtained from whole rat brains of various developmental stages. They contained 0.13–0.26% nucleic acids and a total ATPase activity of 0.3–0.8 μmole P per mg per h. 2. 2. These fractions contained a relatively constant amount of total ganglioside N -acetylneuraminic acid at all ages, but the distribution of individual major gangliosides changed drastically as the animal matured. The proportion of the normal major monosialoganglioside in myelin was higher than that in whole brain even at 15 days of age. With increasing age, its proportion increased rapidly reaching 90 mole % of total ganglioside in myelin by the age of 5 months. 3. 3. It is not known whether the gangliosides in the myelin fractions are intrinsic myelin components, or present in contaminating subcellular structures.
