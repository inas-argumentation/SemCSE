The age-related loss of skeletal muscle mass and function: Measurement and physiology of muscle fibre atrophy and muscle fibre loss in humans
Highlights • Loss of muscle mass with age is due to atrophy and loss of individual muscle fibres.• Anabolic resistance is fundamental in age-related fibre atrophy.• Fibre loss is associated with denervation and remodelling of motor units.• The plasticity of both factors should be considered in future research.
