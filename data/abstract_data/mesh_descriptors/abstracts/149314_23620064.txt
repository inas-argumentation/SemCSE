A quantitative histochemical study of 5′nucleotidase activity in rat liver after ischaemia
The lead salt method of Wachstein and Meisel15 has been applied using incubation media containing polyvinyl alcohol for the localization and quantification of 5′‐nucleotidase (E.C. 3.1.3.5) activity in cryostat sections from rat liver after ischaemia in vitro and ischaemia in vivo followed by different periods of re‐perfusion.
