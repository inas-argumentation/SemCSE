Bronchial reactivity to inhaled polymyxin B in subjects with asthma and chronic obstructive bronchitis
Bronchial challenge with polymyxin B was performed in 85 patients with asthma and 23 patients with chronic bronchitis. Eighty-five percent of the asthmatics exhibited a significant decline of FEV1.0 after polymyxin B concentrations of 0.5 – 5 mg/ml. None of the bronchitics responded to such concentrations. Only three bronchitics reacted to 10, 15, and 20 mg/ml polymyxin B. In 21 patients with hay fever, reactivity to polymyxin B paralleled changes in clinical state. The authors discuss the relationship between polymyxin B-induced changes in bronchial reactivity and the interval between provocation and manifestation of allergy.
