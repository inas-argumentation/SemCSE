Suppression of metachromasy by basic proteins.
Abstract A spectrophotometric method of measuring metachromasy (Wiame) in solution is adapted to the study of suppression of heparin metachromasy by basic proteins. It is shown that the absence of metachromasy is not necessarily an indication of the absence of a metachromatic substance. The sensitivity of heparin-protein combinations to heat, x-rays, and trypsin is also indicated. Certain results are related to histological observations.
