Implementation of Acupuncture Therapy in the Care of Patients with Breast Cancer.
Acupuncture therapy is gaining popularity day by day as a complementary and alternative medicine in the field of oncology and palliative medicine, as it has shown promising results in the management of various symptoms, which may be either disease related or secondary to the side effects of therapy, in patients with cancer. Hot flushes and vomiting, as well as preoperative and postoperative pain, are among the common symptoms experienced by such patients. Hot flushes resulting from antiestrogenic drugs, e.g., aromatase inhibitors, tamoxifene, etc., and from chemotherapy and surgery are troublesome for these patients. Especially, frequent nocturnal episodes of hot flushes disrupt their sleep and can promote anxiety, depression, and pain. Reductions in the intensity and frequency of hot flushes, and thus, improved quality of sleep, have been reported in patients with breast cancer who have undergone acupuncture therapy, but the mechanism behind such results is not completely understood [1]. Chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting is common among cancer sufferers, and that suffering is managed using antiemetics. However, when acupuncture therapy is used along with antiemetics, the frequency and severity of nausea and vomiting are reduced more effectively than when antiemetics are used alone. Acupuncture also alleviates the symptoms of depression, insomnia, fatigue, and nervousness in patients with breast cancer [1]. Pain in patients with breast cancer significantly affects their quality of life, and is mostly due to bone, brain, and liver metastasis or postmastectomy pain syndrome.
