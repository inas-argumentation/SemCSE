Complementary and alternative medicine in cancer: a National Cancer Institute perspective
The percentage of cancer patients that report use of at least one complementary and alternative medicine (CAM) modality has ranged across surveys from 9 83% [1]. Most cancer patients use more than one CAM modality and many use CAM in combination with conventional treatment [1]. In one survey nearly all cancer patients questioned about the use of scientifically unproven treatments of cancer or cancer remedies (80%) had also received conventional therapy [2]. Although the conventional treatment usually preceded the unproven method, 20% of the unproven treatments were administered while the patient was undergoing chemotherapy or radiation [2]. An additional study reports similar findings: 29% of cancer patients in that sample reported taking an herbal supplement while there were receiving chemotherapy [3].
