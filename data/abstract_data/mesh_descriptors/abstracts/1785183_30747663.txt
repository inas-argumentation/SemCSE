Armstrong opined that the cancer registry is essential to cancer control, and cancer control is essential (to the survival of) the cancer registry-, Cancer registration was first attempted in 17283 and the first cancer registry in Nigeria commenced operation in Ibadan in 19604. The Ibadan Cancer Registry has in the past contributed to epidemiological studies, collection of treatment results, follow-up activities, etc. 5 The UDUTH Cancer Registry is located in a single room. The staff consists of a coordinator, an abstractor and a part-time assistant. Budgetary constraints have not allowed the hospital administration to provide for computers, index cards and other working materials. Therefore, case finding is still by active collection through periodic visits to assigned places in the hospital to collect information on new cases of cancer. The principal sources of data are the medical records department, histopathology, haematology and paediatrics, but there are plans to expand to cover other areas soon. There are considerable delays in data storage and retrieval because this manual system is quite inefficient. Nevertheless, we have developed a more comprehensive cancer input form that we hope will ease the storage and transcription of informatron, The numbers of cases in the records of the registry are 95 (in 1999), 147 (in 2000), 20I (in 2001) and 279 (in 2002). The most common cancer in men is cancer of the bladder, in women it is cancer of the breast while among children it is Burkitt's lymphoma. The steady increase in absolute number of cancers may reflect accession of more sources of data by the abstractor. Annual reports have been generated each year since 1999. There are plans by the registry to initiate a series of regional seminars on the prevention and control of cancer but this is being frustrated by lack of funds. Perhaps the most pressing problem facing the UDUTH Cancer Registry is acute shortage of working materials. There is urgent need for computerization of the registry, complete with appropriate software for the coding of tumours. We are also aware that cancer registration in our centre is incomplete. This is more so because no formal case notification of cancer is in place in our institution. Another important factor militating against our activities is under-staffing. The services of at least two more data abstractors are required in addition to an epidemiologist and a specialist in information technology management. Since inception we have participated in only two very short training workshops, therefore, staff training and development is a big problem. The hospital community is becoming more aware of the important work we are doing. Our mission is to create a truly population-based cancer registry working under a legislative mandate and able to playa central role in the implementation of cancer control programmes, situation analysis and programme monitoring.
