Lenalidomide proved effective in multisystem Langerhans cell histiocytosis
Langerhans cell histiocytosis (LCH) is a rare idiopathic
disease with diverse clinical manifestations ranging from a
single osteolytic lesion to generalized disease. Various
treatment regimens have been proposed for the multisystem type,
however, with inconsistent outcomes. Herein we are the first to
report on a therapy effect of a lenalidomide-based regimen in a
patient with repeatedly relapsed aggressive form of multisystem
LCH.
