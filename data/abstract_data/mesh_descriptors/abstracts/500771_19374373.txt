Modest association of malignant melanoma with the rs910873 and rs1885120 markers on chromosome 20: a population-based study.
Editor, I read the recent publication by Vittoria et al. [1] with a great interest. Vittoria et al. [1] reached the conclusion that ‘this study shows for the first time the existence of an acidic catalase in association with clinically defined risk characteristics in low phototype skin in vivo, contributing to the knowledge of a new biochemical marker of cutaneous photosusceptibility.’ The finding is indeed very interesting but there are several points to be criticized. First, the number of tested individuals is too few and are Caucasian only, hence the distribution of the phenotype might not represent the actual scenario. Second, the quality control of the test procedure in this work is questionable; many factors can result in the presentation of acidic catalase. The underlying cutaneous condition, as a preanalytical factor, must be investigated thoroughly.
