HOSPITAL MORTALITY OF MYOCARDIAL INFARCTION, BEFORE AND AFTER CORONARY CARE
The effect of a coronary care unit on the mortality rate from acute myocardial infarction in an unselected total hospital series, matched for prognostic factors, is reported. In the five years before the establishment of the unit the rate did not vary significantly from a mean of 36%. Cases observed for 12 months before and for nine months after the unit opened were comparable for most major prognostic factors. The exceptions were those factors which could be expected to worsen, not improve, results. In spite of this, after the introduction of the unit there was a statistically significant fall in the hospital mortality rate to 24%.
