Treatment of recalcitrant squamous carcinoma in situ of penis with tapering imiquimod 5–3.75% cream
BACKGROUND
The efficacy of imiquimod as treatment of genital squamous carcinoma (SCC) is known and described in literature.


OBJECTIVE
Evaluate the efficacy of tapering doses of imiquimod 5%-3,75% cream in the treatment of recalcitrant penis SCC in situ.


METHODS
A patient with a SCC in situ received three cycles of therapy with imiquimod cream. First cycle with imiquimod 5% 4-times a week for 6-weeks, second cycle with imiquimod 5% 3-times a week for other 6-weeks, and the third cycle with imiquimod 3,75% daily for two 2-weeks separated by 2-weeks of no treatment period. Histological evaluations were done at the beginning of first cycle and after the third cycle of therapy.


RESULTS
The patient at the end of the third cycle of therapy showed a complete clinical and histological remission of the SCC in situ.


CONCLUSION
