Fractionation of nuclear proteins from red and white skeletal muscle, heart and liver of rabbit.
1. 1. Nuclei have been isolated from red and white skeletal muscles, livers and hearts of rabbit. 2. 2. Nuclear proteins have been fractionated using three different methods and proteins from various fractions were compared following their electrophoresis on SDS∗-polyacrylamide gels. 3. 3. Proteins co-electrophoresing with actin, myosin and possibly some other contractile proteins were found in the nuclei of all the above tissues even after extensive washing of the nuclei with Triton X-100. 4. 4. Comparison of nuclear proteins from red and white skeletal muscle revealed some quantitative differences. Nuclear proteins from heart were similar to those of the red muscle. Several qualitative differences were found between nuclear proteins of muscle and liver.
