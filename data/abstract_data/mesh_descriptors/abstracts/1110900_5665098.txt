SENSITIVITY OF MONOMERIC AND DIMERIC FORMS OF E. COLI THYMIDINE KINASE TO ULTRAVIOLET RADIATION *
THE EFFECTS of small molecules on the ultraviolet (u.v.) sensitivity of E. coli thymidine kinase has been investigated[ 1 I. ATP and thymidine are substrates for this enzyme and were found to decrease the rate of loss of catalytic activity during irradiation, whereas compounds which do not interact with this enzyme had no effect. Since the compounds used in these studies were not transparent to U.V. light, it was not clear whether the protection resulted from the absorption of energy by the substrate while in complex with the catalytic site or from an alteration in enzyme conformation. We decided, therefore, to investigate the effect of allosteric regulators (dCDP, dTTP) on the u.v.sensitivity of the catalytic activity of thymidine kinase. Since these compounds alter enzyme conformation without interacting with the catalytic site it is possible to study the effects of altered protein conformation without the complication of effects due to complex formation at the catalytic site.
