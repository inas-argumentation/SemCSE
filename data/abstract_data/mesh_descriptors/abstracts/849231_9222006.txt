Twelfth annual Mallinckrodt institute of radiology-radiation oncology center symposium for radiation and biological sciences.
This year's meeting, held from November 8-10, 1996, consisted of two plenary lectures, three symposia and three workshops. The meeting was held in conjunction with the 5th Annual Tolmach Memorial Lecture, which was given by Dr. Helen Evans on "The Characteristics of Radon-Induced TK-/Mutants." Dr. Evans presented data on TK-/mutants of cells of both mouse L5178Y cell lines and the human TK-6 cell line exposed to radon.
