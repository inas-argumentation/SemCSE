Targeted nanoblobs spot treat cancer and heart disease.
Nanoprobes that can be injected into your body to diagnose and treat disease have a distinguished history in science fiction. Raquel Welch glamorized nanomedicine in the 1966 movie Fantastic Voyage, based on the Isaac Asimov book about a miniaturized submarine's journey through a human body. An underrated precursor was George S. Chapell's 1930 travelogue Through the Alimentary Canal with Gun and Camera.
