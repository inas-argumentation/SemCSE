Phosphorylation of a purified (Na + +K + ) adenosine triphosphatase.
Summary The concentration of the sodium dependent phosphorylated intermediate in several preparations of the (Na + +K + ) adenosine triphosphatase from renal medulla at various stages of purification was measured. During purification the apparent turnover number of the enzyme decreases from 9,300 min −1 to 3,000 min −1 . The phosphorylated intermediate is the larger of the two polypeptides present in the final preparation of the enzyme.
