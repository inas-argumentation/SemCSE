Choosing the right bioanalytical assay platform(s) to support the PK assessment of protein biotherapeutic programs.
There are many options when it comes to choosing the right bioanalytical assay platform(s) to support the PK assessment of a protein biotherapeutic project. In order to make the best choice, the bioanalyst must look at some key considerations depending on the complexities of the individual program needs and the stage of development of the biotherapeutic. Additionally, with the advances in the sensitivity of LC–MS assays, the choice of platforms has expanded beyond the immunoassay category and it is important to know when a ligand binding assay (LBA) or LC–MS might better fulfill program needs.
