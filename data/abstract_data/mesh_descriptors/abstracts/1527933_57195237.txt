The value of vasectomy in patients undergoing prostatectomy.
A controlled study of 184 patients some of whom underwent unilateral or bilateral vasectomy either before or after prostate enucleation showed an overall low incidence of postoperative epididymo-orchitis in all groups whether or not the vas was ligated. The incidence increased where the vas was not tied. Although no evidence suggested that vasectomy before enucleation confers any advantage over that performed after enucleation there was little to suggest that preoperative urinary tract infection caused a greater incidence of postoperative epididymo-orchitis.
