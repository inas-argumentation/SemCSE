Biochemical changes in isolated perfused livers from rats intoxicated with CCl4 or fed with steatogenous diet.
Summary The changes of some functional and biochemical parameters have been studied in perfused isolated livers from rats intoxicated with CCl 4 or fed with steatogenous diet (Handler diet). With both types of intoxication, a decreased bile volume flow and a increased perfusion pressure were noted. Among the biochemical parameters examined in the perfusion medium transaminases were increased in CCl 4 -rats and glucose in Handler-rats. In hepatic tissue, glycogen and glucose levels in CCl 4 -rats were extremely low, as indicated by the value obtained at zero time (T 0 ) and at 120 minutes (T 120 ). In Handlerrats, glycogen levels were very high at T 0 and then fell to control levels at T 120 . The total lipids in CCl 4 -rats did not differ from the controls, whereas total lipids increased considerably in Handler-rats. No changes of this parameter were evident after 120 minutes of perfusion.
