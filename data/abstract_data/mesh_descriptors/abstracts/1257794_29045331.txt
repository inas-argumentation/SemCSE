Age-adjusted death rates of malignant neoplasms of various sites for 33 selected countries in the world.
We have computed age-adjusted death rates of malignant neoplasms of various sites for 33 selected countries in the world on the basis of the latest reports of the World Health Organization. Age-adjustment was done by direct method and by using a world population as standard population. Fourteen sites or types of malignant neoplasms were selected. The results of our computation are presented in two tables and twenty-five figures.
