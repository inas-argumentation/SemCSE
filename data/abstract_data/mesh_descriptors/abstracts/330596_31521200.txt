Changes in plasma or serum concentrations of magnesium, iron and thyroxine, in leukocyte triglyceride content, and in peritoneal fluid protein concentration in rabbits injected with adrenocorticotropin or beta-melanocyte-stimulating hormone.
Injection of 40–60 U ACTH increasedplasma FFA from 500 to 3000 μEq/1 for at 2hr; the level remained over 1000 μEq/1 for atleast 8 hr. Plasma magnesium concentration increasedprogressively from 3.3 (at 0 hr) to 5.5mg/100 ml at 6 hr, while plasma calcium fellfrom 13.9 to 10.3 mg/100 ml. Serum iron, initially160 μg/100 ml, declined to 105 μg/100 ml at 5 hr,with an associated reduction in saturation of theiron-binding capacity from 43 to 29%. Serumtotal thyroxine, initially 1.2 μg/100 ml, declinedto 0.9 μg/100 ml at 5 hr, with an associated risein the ratio (free thyroxine/total thyroxine)X100 from 0.065 to 0.237. Seven hr after injectionof 60 U ACTH sudanophilic granules appearedin the circulating polymorphonuclearleukocytes and the peritoneal leukocytes showeda 300% increase in triglyceride content.
