Clinical features of hepatitis C virus‐related hepatocellular carcinoma and their association with α‐fetoprotein and protein induced by vitamin K absence or antagonist‐II
: Purpose: We investigated the differences in clinical features between α‐fetoprotein (AFP)‐predominant hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and protein induced by vitamin K absence or antagonist‐II (PIVKA‐II)‐predominant HCC, especially regarding host factors thought to contribute to hepatocarcinogenesis in chronic hepatitis C virus (HCV) infection.
