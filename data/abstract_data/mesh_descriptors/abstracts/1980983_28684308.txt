HIV/AIDS case histories: acute HIV infection.
This 37-year old African-American woman presented with persistent fever of several weeks duration. Past medical history included depression, schizoaffective disorder, asthma, alcohol abuse, and positive rapid plasma reagin (RPR) test treated with penicillin. She denied IV drug use or unprotected sex, and reported HIV testing from 4 months earlier as negative. Two weeks prior she had a 2-day hospital admission for intermittent fever to 40°C
