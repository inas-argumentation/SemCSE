Source of sexually dimorphic galanin‐like immunoreactive projections in the teleost fish Poecilia latipinna
A galaninlike peptide has a sexually dimorphic distribution in the teleost fish, the sailfin molly. An extensive system of galanin‐like immunoreactive (GAL‐LI) fibers has been described in the brainstem and spinal cord of the male molly, which is absent in the female (Cornbrooks and Parsons, companion paper). As GAL‐LI in the mammalian spinal cord has been localized to neurons of origin in the dorsal root ganglia and dorsal and ventral horns, the present study was undertaken to determine whether the sexually dimorphic GAL‐LI in the male molly may originate in part from corresponding sources in this species.
