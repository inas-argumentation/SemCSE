The relationship between the transferability of enterotoxin plasmids and the simultaneous transfer of antibiotic resistance plasmids.
Summary 
 
Established donors of enterotoxin plasmids were used in a study to determine the relationship between the transferability of Ent plasmids and the simultaneous transfer of antibiotic resistance plasmids. The transfer of Ent plasmids from R plasmid-containing E. coli was not detected when drug-resistant transconjugants were selected. The addition of a sub-inhibitory concentration of an antibiotic to the mating mixture did not result in increased formation of Ent/R plasmid-containing transconjugants.
