[Age-related changes in the dental arches of preschoolers with sagittal bite anomalies].
A total of 160 children (3 to 6 years) with sagittal occlusion anomalies were studied. The features of age-related dentition changes characteristic for each of the anomalies were singled out. The differences in rate and localization of dentition changes were noted in prognathic and progenic occlusion.
