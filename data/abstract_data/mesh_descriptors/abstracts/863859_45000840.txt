Clinical study of hypertensive pontine hemorrhage.
From March 1982 to February 1985,63 cases of Hypertensive pontine hemorrhage were collected, 37 males and 26 females. The incidence was 9.6%of total hypertensive intracerebral hemorrhage. Age ranged from 36 years to 86 years. Survival rate was 28.6%. The highest incidence of attack was encountered in November and the lowest was in June. Patients with the better conscious level, light reflex, or motor response on admission was found to have the better prognosis. On computed tomography scan, 35 cases of massive type, 12 cases of basis tegmentum type and 16 cases of tegmentum type were found, and 34 cases out of the 35 massive type were expired. All of the cases were treated conservatively.
