An unvalidated online risk predictor tool for melanoma.
The recent emergence of an unvalidated online risk predictor tool for melanoma as described in the article by Mar and colleagues raises some public health concerns and may be clinically misleading. The goal of a risk predictor model is to construct an accurate and discriminating model from multiple variables. As the number and accessibility of these models has increased it is becoming increasingly important to ensure they are correctly developed and rigorously evaluated. The fundamental requirements for the development of a risk predictor tool include: (i) the development of a multivariable prognostic model using data which represent the population of interest; (ii) estimating the model’s predictive performance through calibration and discrimination; and (iii) externally validating the model’s outcome probabilities. This study does not meet these basic requirements.
