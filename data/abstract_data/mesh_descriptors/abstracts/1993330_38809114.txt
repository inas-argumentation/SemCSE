THE VALUE OF X-RAY THERAPY IN THE MANAGEMENT OF OSTEOCLASTOMA.
Summary A series of fifty cases of osteoclastoma is presented, all having been treated by x-rays at some time. The cases are divided into those treated by x-rays alone, those having x-ray therapy after some initial surgical procedure, and those requiring surgery after initial x-ray treatment. The results in each group are given. Results are given also for histologically proven osteoclastomata occurring at any age, and in patients over twenty years. The place of x-ray therapy in the management of these tumours is discussed, and the literature briefly reviewed. This review is based on a paper read at the 10th International Congress of Radiology, Montreal, Canada, August 1962.
