Capillary electrophoresis of DNA for molecular diagnostics: an update.
The use of capillary electrophoresis in sieving liquid polymers for DNA analysis in the biomedical field is reviewed. One hundred and six references have been compiled and divided into the following categories: 1) screening for inherited human genetic defects, 2) quantitative gene dosage, 3) microbiology/virology, 4) forensic analysis, and 5) therapeutic DNA (antisense nucleotides).
