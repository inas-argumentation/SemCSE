Minireview: Rare and polymorphic fragile sites and cancer
The rare and polymorphic fragile sites stand in contrast with the common fragile sites. The aim of this minireview is to survey the currently known rare and polymorphic fragile sites, especially in regard to cancer. General background information on fragile sites was provided by Sutherland and Hecht [1]. The March 1988 issue of Cancer Genet ics and Cytogenet ics was devoted to fragile sites and cancer chromosome breakpoints [2].
