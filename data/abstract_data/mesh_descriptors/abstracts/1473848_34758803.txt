Dynamics of the neurosecretory system in the brain of an insect, Rhodnius prolixus, during growth and molting.
The behavior of the neurosecretory system of Rhodnius throughout the final molting cycle is inferred from changes in the numbers of the five cell types. 
 
An interpretive model is constructed from analysis of the interconversions among the cell types. It is supported by changes in the density of stainable material in the axons and estimates of the rate of transport of material. 
 
There are two phases of release of neurosecretion during one molting cycle and one protracted phase of synthesis, which overlaps both phases of release. 
 
The first phase of release begins immediately on feeding and lasts for 6–8 days. The reality of Wigglesworth's “critical period” is confirmed. The second phase of release accompanies ecdysis. Resynthesis is first detected between 2 and 4 days after feeding and continues at a constant rate until ecdysis, when it is briefly intensified.
