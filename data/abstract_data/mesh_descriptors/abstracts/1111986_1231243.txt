The Use of Corticosteroids in the Treatment of Asthma and Related Conditions
The Greek word ' asthma ' means no more than ' a hard-drawn or gasping breath.' It may be applied to the dyspnoea of left ventricular failure, but is more usually taken to indicate bronchial asthma. It is with this sort of asthma that this article is concerned, and for this purpose asthma may be defined as dyspnoea due to generalized bronchiolar obstruction characterized clinically by wheezing respiration. It will be assumed that the' diagnosis has been confirmed by the usual investigations, thereby excluding wheezing due to localized bronchial obstruction.
