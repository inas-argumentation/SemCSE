Chemical character of proteins in rat incisors.
Abstract 1. 1.|With a simple preparative procedure, the proteins of rat incisors were separated into three fractions: enamel proteins; phosphoprotein(s), rich in aspartic acid and serine; and collagen. The three fractions accounted for approx. 4, 11 and 85% of the weight of proteins, respectively. 2. 2.|Analytical data presented indicated that the three fractions have distinctly different amino acid and phosphate compositions. 3. 3.|These findings bear on the proposal 4 that phosphoproteins of teeth are covalently attached to dentin collagen.
