A very rare cause of milk of calcium gallbladder: carcinoma of the ampulla of Vater.
Milk of calcium bile or limy bile is a well known entity which was first reported by Churchman in 1911 and by Balachandra and Simarak in 1968, but its definite aetiology is still uncertain (Besic et at., 1965; Marquis and Densler, 1970). Its common cause is believed to be stone, in the cystic duct with obstruction (Rothman, 1965; Paul and Juhl, 1966; Balachandra and Simarak, 1968; Marquis and Densler, 1970). In 1955 and 1957, Cohen and Simmonds respectively, reported cases of milk of calcium bile in the common bile duct which were produced by common duct stones. To our knowledge, milk .of calcium gallbladder caused by ampullary carcinoma has not yet been reported. The purpose of this paper is to report such a case with a review of the literature.
