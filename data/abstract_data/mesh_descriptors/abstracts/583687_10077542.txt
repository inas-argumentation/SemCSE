Lymphocyte β2‐Receptor Activity, Metoprolol Kinetics, and Response to Metoprolol in Hypertensive Black Men
Study Objectives. To evaluate whether variability in S‐metoprolol kinetics and lymphocyte β2‐receptor‐mediated cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP) accumulation is related to the variability in antihypertensive response to metoprolol of black men.
