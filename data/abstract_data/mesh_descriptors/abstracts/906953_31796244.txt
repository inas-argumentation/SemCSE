Clinical Reasoning: An unusual cause of transverse myelitis?
Bhargava and Elble1 excluded the common differentials of longitudinal transverse myelitis but overlooked paraneoplastic myelopathy (PNM). Cord lesions can still have an etiology despite biopsy-proven metastases in the brain. Having made the final diagnosis of small-cell lung carcinoma, the authors should have considered PNM.
