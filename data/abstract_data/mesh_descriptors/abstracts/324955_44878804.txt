The Microbes Made Me Eat It
Susceptibility to obesity is influenced by the interaction between microbes and immune system function in our digestive system. We share our bodies with a huge array of microorganisms. Many of these live in the intestine and number in the trillions (1). On page 228 in this issue, Vijay-Kumar et al. (2) show that the interaction between our immune system and these gut microbes plays an important role in the metabolic diseases that plague developed countries, with profound implications for the rise in obesity and what can be done about it.
