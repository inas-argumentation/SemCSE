Evidence for the existence of an active form of tyrosine transaminase of very low molecular weight.
An active form of tyrosine transaminase, whose molecular weight is approximately 6000, has been observed in gel-filtration experiments. This form appears only after an enzyme preparation has been allowed to stand for a few weeks. It is stable at 4°C, and requires added pyridoxal phosphate for activity. It is inactivated by CCl3COOH and by heating to 100°C.
