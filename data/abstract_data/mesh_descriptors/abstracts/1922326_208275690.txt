Chronic type A aortic dissection after transcatheter aortic valve replacement.
An 87-year-old man with hostile ascending aorta who underwent transfemoral transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) using 26-mm Sapien 3 (Edwards Lifesciences, Irvine, CA, USA) 2 years ago, presented with chronic type A aortic dissection, which was retrospectively caused by the edge of the pusher of the TAVR implantation system. Redo sternotomy, extraction of Sapien valve followed replacement of ascending aorta and aortic valve was done successfully. Retrospective image analysis showed importance of respecting the anatomy of the ascending aorta before considering transfemoral TAVR.
