Unilateral Nephrotoxicity Associated With Methoxyflurane Anesthesia: A Case Report
A case of unilateral nephrotoxicity following methoxyflurane anesthesia is presented. The patient's nonfunctioning and surgically isolated kidney was protected from toxic concentrations of the nephrotoxic methoxyflurane metabolite, inorganic fluoride, renal tubular pathology being localized to the fully functioning kidney.
