Bedside diagnosis of the acute myocardial infarction and its complications.
Abstract The early detection and successful management of the acute myocardial infarction and its potential complications pose an important and continuing challenge to the bedside diagnostician. As witnessed in this monograph, a multitude of physical findings are the reward of the seasoned clinician who is skilled in the use of his unaided senses, aware of the complications of AMI, and adept at their discovery.
