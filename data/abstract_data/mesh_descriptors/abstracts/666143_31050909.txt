Effect of long-term treatment with norethynodrel on A-J and C3H-HeJ mice.
The effect of long-term treatment with norethynodrel on female mice of the A/J and C3H/HeJ strains was investigated. Fifty animals of each strain were injected twice weekly with 125 μg of norethynodrel/20 g body weight and 50 control animals of each strain were given comparable volumes of vehicle. The A/J mice were followed for periods of time up to 106 weeks, while C3H/HeJ animals were studied for a maximum of 88 weeks. Whole mounts and histologic sections of the mammary glands of both strains revealed mild lobulo-alveolar development and the presence of hyperplastic alveolar nodules following norethynodrel treatment. These changes were absent in controls. In the A/J strain, 8 norethynodrel-treated mice developed type B mammary adenocarcinomas, while none were found in the control group. Twenty-eight mammary adenocarcinomas appeared in the norethynodrel-treated C3H/HeJ mice, while only 12 were found in the controls.
