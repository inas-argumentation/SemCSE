[The proposition of a rule for evaluating the percentage of health impairment due to occupationally related myocardial infarction].
The authors propose the principles how to calculate a proportional health impairment due to myocardial infarction. The proposals are based on own experience, literature and regulations. It is suggested that the following should be taken into account for the calculation purposes: case history, resting electrocardiogram, postexercise electrocardiogram, results of Holter's examination, chest x-ray including evaluation of the heart silhoutte and UCG.
