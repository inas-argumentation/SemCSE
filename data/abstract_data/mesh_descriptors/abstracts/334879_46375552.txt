Mean electromechanical P- t: an indirect index of the peak rate of rise of left ventricular pressure.
Abstract Eighteen patients with acute myocardial infarction were studied by means of pulmonary arterial and left ventricular catheterization. An indirect index of the maximal rate of rise of left ventricular pressure, dP/dt, was derived from measurement of developed isovolumic pressure (arterial diastolic minus left ventricular diastolic) and the preejection period (PEP): mean electromechanical ΔPΔt = developed pressure/PEP. The correlation of this index with left ventricular dP/dt was excellent ( r = 0.96), with a residual variance of 8 percent. In 50 subjects with acute myocardial infarction ΔPΔt also correlated closely with the presence of pulmonary vascular congestion and shock and with ultimate survival. In contrast, mean arterial pressure, left ventricular filling pressure, left ventricular ejection time (LVET), preejection period and LVET/PEP demonstrated marked overlap between groups. Mean electromechanical ΔPΔt was accurate and useful as an indirect index of left ventricular dP/dt and, thus, of the functional state of the left ventricle.
