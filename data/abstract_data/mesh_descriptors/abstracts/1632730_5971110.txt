Views and news from Melbourne, the Sixth World Conference on Lung Cancer. The EORTC Lung Cancer Co-operative Group.
The Sixth World Confer ence on Lung Cancer was held in Melbourne, Australia, 10-14 November 1991, in the well-equipped World Conference Center, by the river Yarra. About 1,000 par ticipants came from all over the world, and almost 700 a bstracts were presented. The scientific and the social programme were both extremely stimulating and perfectly organized, thanks to D. Ball and the organizing committee, from the McCallum Cancer Center in Melbourne.
