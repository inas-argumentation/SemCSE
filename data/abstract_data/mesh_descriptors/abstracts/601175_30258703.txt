Antimicrobial Prophylaxis for Recurrent Otitis Media
A cute otitis media is recognized as one of the most common infectious diseases of childhood. International task forces and review committees have outlined epidemiologic factors, bacteriology, terminology, and treatment options related to this disease, yet controversy continues to surround the issue of best treatment for children with recurrent otitis media. This report will summarize the potential benefits of chemoprophylaxis for the prevention of new episodes of this disorder in children with histories of recurrent disease.
