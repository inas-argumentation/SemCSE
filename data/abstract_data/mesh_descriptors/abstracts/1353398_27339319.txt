Distribution of glycine in human spinal cord and selected regions of brain.
Abstract (1) The concentration and distribution of glycine in the cervical, thoracic and lumbar portions were determined post mortem in 20 anatomically normal human spinal cords and in selected regions of brain. (2) Representative blocks from each portion of the spinal cord at 3 different loci (cervical, thoracic, lumbar) were dissected to provide samples of anterior and posterior grey matter, anterior, posterior and lateral white matter. (3) Statistical evaluation of the results demonstrated the following order of concentration with highest levels in ventral grey followed by lateral column, dorsal grey, ventral white. This order of concentration is in good agreement with studies in other animal tissues. (4) The highest concentration was found in the ventral grey of the lumbar region compared with more proximal regions.
