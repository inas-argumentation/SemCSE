The binding of calcium ions by component I of the blood sera of diethylstilbestrol-treated cockerels.
Abstract Equilibrium dialysis experiments indicate that the high phosphorus component found in the blood sera of DESB-treated cockerels (Component I) has 110 binding sites per molecule. The ΔGinto calculated from the binding data of calcium ions to Component I was −7.82 kcal./mole, and the equilibrium constant K was 1.66 × 106. These values explain the presence of the extra calcium in the above mentioned birds as bound calcium.
