[Emergency psychiatric care in 2014].
Emergency psychiatric care is an essential part of psychiatric practice where every detail must be taken into account. The skill of the psychiatric nurses is a major advantage, but for this skill to be activated, it must be enlightened, supported by a psychiatrist, not just in terms of knowledge, but also for recognition of the value of the "team". Their availability as well as the importance of the environment help to establish the necessary trust to enable the patient to face his or her suffering.
