The application of psychosocial factors to HIV risk among Belizean adolescents.
In the current study we examined the relevance of these psychosocial factors to abstinence and condom use (for sexually active adolescents). Based on the review of the literature we hypothesized that the following variables would be associated with condom use among sexually active adolescents: intentions to use condoms attitudes towards condoms peer norms/pressure parent-child communication self-efficacy for condom use and perceived risk for contracting HIV.
