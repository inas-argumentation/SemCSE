Maple Syrup Urine Disease: A Manifestation of an Unusual Metabolic Error
At birth the baby appears normal and probably is normal. However, by 48 to 72 hours it is usually evident that the baby is not faring well. He feeds poorly and may vomit. Hypertonicity and convulsions may occur. Death may end the story in one to two weeks, the disease being considerably more lethal than phenylketonuria (Table 1). Those who survive longer suffer from severe mental retardation and neurologic damage. An intervening infection is commonly the direct cause of death.
