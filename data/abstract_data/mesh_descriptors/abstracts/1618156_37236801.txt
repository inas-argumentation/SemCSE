We confer with Saavedra and Perman that the relationship between small bowel transit and whole gut or colon transit may not be direct. The point they made about the role of exercise in the management of chronic constipation is well taken. By no means would we wish to give the impression that exercise has a similar effect on the entire gastrointestinal tract to that it has on mouthto-cecum transit. Hence, exercise may well have a solubrious effect on chronic constipation. Needed to establish this, however, are presently lacking data on the influence of exercise on colon transit.
