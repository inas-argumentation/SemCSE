Introduction: Linkage Analysis of Full‐Genome Screens
Asthma and asthma-associated phenotypes are complex traits for which genetic predisposition is important but poorly understood [Sanford et al., 1997). There may be common genetic and environmental pathways contributing to asthma susceptibility and variation in these traits [Postma et al., 1995]. In this summary, we report on the fmdings obtained by groups that have examined evidence for linkage to asthma and serum lgE, an intermediate phenotype of asthma. Using genome screen and chromosome 5q data and multiple analytic approaches, several regions of interest were identified that could be explored in the future.
