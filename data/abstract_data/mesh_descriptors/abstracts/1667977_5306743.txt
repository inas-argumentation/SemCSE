Diagnosis of metastatic liver cancer.
To the Editor.— The Jan 13, 1984, issue ofThe Journalcontains favorable statements concerning the efficacy of radionuclide scans. The article by Dr Silberstein 1 is a little biased in favor of nuclear medicine. What is needed to document the reliability of this diagnostic technique and its efficacy compared with alternative diagnostic methods are prospective comparative studies involving the modalities in question. There are few published prospective comparative studies. Among the best known, in Europe, is the Belgian study by Mulder et al. 2 One hundred patients with carcinoma of the digestive tract had a computed tomographic (CT) scan and a radionuclide liver scan prior to operation. Both were reported to have the same degree of diagnostic reliability (about 80% correct diagnosis).
