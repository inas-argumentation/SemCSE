Retracted: p62 as a therapeutic target for inhibition of autophagy in prostate cancer
Retraction: [Wang, L, Kim, D, Wise, JT, Shi, X, Zhang, Z, DiPaola, RS. p62 as a therapeutic target for inhibition of autophagy in prostate cancer. The Prostate. 2018; 78: 390 to 400. https://doi.org/10.1002/pros.23483] The above article from The Prostate, published online on 26 January 2018 in Wiley Online Library (wileyonlinelibrary.com) and in Volume 78, pp. 390‐400 has been retracted by agreement between the journal Editor in Chief, John Isaacs, and Wiley Periodicals, Inc. The retraction has been agreed due to an investigation by the University of Kentucky which identified data fabrication as part of a larger pattern of research misconduct by co‐authors Kim, Shi, and Zhang.
