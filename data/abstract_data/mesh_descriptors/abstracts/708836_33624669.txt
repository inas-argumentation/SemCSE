Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs and the kidney
Introduction Nonsteroidal anti-in ̄ammatory drugs (NSAIDs) are widely used to alleviate pain in chronic conditions such as osteoarthritis. Because of the strong increase in prevalence of most of these conditions with age and the demographic development in industrialized countries, NSAID use is likely to become even more prevalent in the future. The introduction of a new class of NSAIDs, cyclo-oxygenase-2 inhibitors, with the prospect of fewer gastrointestinal side effects, is likely to increase NSAID use (or dose in users) further.
