Although reported as “not significantly different”, the rate of paracentesis after discharge and the dose of albumin that these patients received after the first week were not described in detail. Indeed, the subgroup of patients needing frequent paracentesis could develop progressive deterioration if the half-dose albumin regimen were maintained over time. This point should be specifically addressed when future, large-scale studies are conducted to confirm/dispute the presented data.
