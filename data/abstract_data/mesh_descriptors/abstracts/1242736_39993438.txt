Hypopituitary dwarfism following radiotherapy for nasopharyngeal carcinoma.
Radiation is a rare cause of hypopituitary dwarfism. The authors present what is probably the first patient with pituitary dwarfism following deep x-ray therapy for carcinoma of the nasopharynx, and discuss the effects of pituitary irradiation during radiotherapy for nasopharyngeal carcinoma. Pituitary dysfunction is practically unknown in such patients, presumably because the pituitary gland is relatively radio-resistant to external irradiation, as shown by animal experiments and human studies. In this patient, there is incomplete pituitary inhibition.
