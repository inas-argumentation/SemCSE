Unusual roentgenographic data in unilateral renal aplasia.
A survey of the literature discloses that aplasia of the kidney is not a very rare disorder and has often been reported. The embryology, types, clinical aspects, and pathological characteristics have been presented in detail in several excellent papers, 1 and these points will be omitted. The following case is reported in view of an erroneous diagnosis made because of an unusual feature found in presacral pneumography, 2 a diagnostic aid for the visualization of the retroperitoneal organs.
