Synthesis and preliminary characterization of a novel substrate for γ-glutamyl transferase
Abstract The synthesis and chemical characterization of the γ-glutamyl adduct of phenylenediamine mustard is reported. The activity of this compound, γ -( N , N -bis(2-chloroethyl)- p -phenylenediamine]-glutamate, as a substrate for γ-glutamyl transferase is demonstrated and compared with the activity of glutathione. The possible use of this material as a directed anti-hepatoma agent is discussed.
