China's HIV/AIDS epidemic is sharply on the rise
China's HIV/AIDS epidemic is threatening to grow out of control unless concerted efforts are made to stop its spread, a report by China and United Nations' agencies warns. “Make no mistake—China's AIDS epidemic is growing,” said Henk Bekedam, the World Health Organization's representative in China.
