Association of serum γ‐glutamyltransferase and alanine aminotransferase activities with risk of type 2 diabetes mellitus independent of fatty liver
Although elevated serum concentrations of γ‐glutamyltrans‐ ferase (GGT) or alanine aminotransferase (ALT) have been associated with type 2 diabetes mellitus, it is unclear whether each is an independent predictor of type 2 diabetes or merely a surrogate marker for fatty liver or hepatic injury.
