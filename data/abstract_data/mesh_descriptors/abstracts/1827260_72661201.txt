The nuclear DNA-content of tumour cells in the oral cavity
Summary From ten cases of squamous cell carcinoma and one case of tuberculosis of the mucosa, smears or swabs were prepared to determine the DNA content of cell nuclei cytophotometrically. The so obtained histograms of squamous cell carcinomas show individually or combined: 1. a strikingly broad distribution of values. 2. secondary maxima which correspond to polyploid cell nuclei, and 3. in individual cases, an aneuploid maximum. The mere examination of the sections of a carcinoma and mucosal tuberculosis lead to no differentiation from normal tissue, since these features could not be observed in them.
