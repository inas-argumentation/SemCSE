Management of breast cancer: limited mastectomy.
MY stand on the treatment of breast cancer has been widely misunderstood. Many surgeons believe that I advocate what the British call "lumpectomy" and irradiation. Nothing could be further from the truth. I do, however, stand firmly against the traditional radical mastectomy. I consider it to be archaic, and I believe that it has no place in the modern treatment of breast cancer. I strongly support modified radical mastectomy. It is primarily indicated for a patient who, at the time of operation, has palpable involvement of a few low or central axillary nodes. If the involvement is extensive, however, and particularly if the nodes at the apex are palpably involved, I agree with Haagenson that the axilla should not be dissected.
