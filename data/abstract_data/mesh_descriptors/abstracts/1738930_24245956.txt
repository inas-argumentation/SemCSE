Distribution of cytochrome oxidase activity in Ehrlich ascites cell particulates obtained from glycerol homogenates.
A method of fractionation of Ehrlich ascites cells has been developed for the separation of particulates derived by the previously developed glycerol technique for homogenization of this type of cell suspension. The success of the fractionation procedure which yields mitochondrial fractions containing much larger total cellular cytochrome oxidase than obtained by other methods was due to the effectiveness of glycerol in the cell disruption process and to the dilution of the glycerol homogenates of the Ehrlich ascites cells with saline solution containing sucrose. Fairly pure mitochondria, microsomes, and nonparticulate supernatants as judged by cytochrome oxidase activities were easily obtained.
