Purification of estradiol receptor from rat uterus and blockade of its estrogen-binding function by specific antibody.
Protein fractions which selectively bind 3H-estradiol-17β have been isolated from rat uterus by gel filtration and repeated ion-exchange chromatography. The purified preparation of major activity migrates essentially as a single component upon disc gel electrophoresis at pH 8.1 and 4.5, or on immunoelectrophoresis. Antireceptor γ-globulin selectively abolishes the estrogen-binding peak which characteristically sediments at “9.5 S ” on sucrose gradient centrifugation.
