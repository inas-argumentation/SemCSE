Nevirapine toxicity--implications for management of South African patients.
Nevirapine was the first non-nucleoside drug (NNRTI) to be approved by the Federal Drug Administration (FDA) for use in combination therapy of HIV-1 infection in 1996. It has been approved for use in children of 2 months or older, and following the publication of the HIVNET 012 study in Uganda has been widely used as single-dose prophylaxis for prevention of mother-to-child HIV transmission (MTCT) in resource-poor settings.
