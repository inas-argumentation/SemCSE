Physical Activity, Glucose Tolerance, and Diabetes Mellitus: the Whitehall Study
In the Whitehall Study, 6 672 men aged 40–64 years completed a questionnaire concerning leisure time physical activity and had a capillary blood glucose measurement on a sample taken 2 h after a 50 g glucose load. An activity score was calculated by grouping subjects into three broad categories: inactive, moderately active, and active. In both univariate and multiple regression analysis no significant association between blood glucose level and activity score could be found.
