The pill and the breast.
There are no research studies to date which document deleterious eff ects on the breast of prolonged estrogen-progestin contraceptive therapy. It is agreed that patients should be taken off pills if they develop adverse breast changes. As a precaution long periods of therapy with unbalanced estrogens are not recommended. Inclusion of a natural progesterone for its estrogen-antagonist effect is recommended. Large detailed retrospective studies will be necessary to document beneficial and/or adverse effects of the pill on breasts.
