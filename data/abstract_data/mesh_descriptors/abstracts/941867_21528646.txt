DNA torsional dynamics by multifrequency phase fluorometry
The time decay of the fluorescence polarization anisotropy of calf thymus DNA‐ethidium complexes is obtained from measurements with sine‐modulated excitation employing the so‐called multifrequency phase fluorometry. A torsional dynamics model developed by J. M. Schurr [(1984) Chemical Physics, Vol. 84, pp. 71–96] and translated into the frequency domain is found here to describe accurately DNA‐ethidium fluorescence data collected under modulated excitation.
