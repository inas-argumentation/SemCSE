USE OF DIFFERENTIATED CHOLINERGIC AND SECOND MESSENGER ENDPOINTS TO EVALUATE CHOLINESTERASE INHIBITORS
1. The effects of organophosphorus compounds on the cholinergic properties of a mouse septal neuronal cell line, SN56, were investigated. Treatment of the cells with 10 μmol/L paraoxon or 10 μmol/L diisopropylphosphorofluoridate (DFP) resulted, respectively, in rapid 27‐ and 11‐fold increases of intracellular acetylcholine content.
