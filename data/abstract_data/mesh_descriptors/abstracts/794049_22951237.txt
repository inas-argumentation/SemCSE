Requesting patterns for serum calcium concentration in patients on long‐term lithium therapy
Aim:  Long‐term lithium therapy is associated with hypercalcaemia in 10–60% of patients, but unlike creatinine and thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH), monitoring by general practitioners of serum calcium for patients on lithium is not a requirement of the Qualities and Outcomes Framework (QOF) of 2004. We aimed to assess requesting patterns for serum calcium in patients on long‐term lithium therapy and subsequent diagnosis of hypercalcaemia.
