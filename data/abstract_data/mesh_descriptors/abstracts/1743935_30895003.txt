Cytophotometric analysis of DNA in enzymatically-dissociated submandibular gland cells of the mouse.
– DNA ia isolated secretory cells of mouse submandibular gland was analyzed by absorption microspectrophotometry and microfluoromotry. Cells were obtained by dissociation of the gland using 0.1 % collagenase and 0.2 % hyaluronidase in calcium- and magnesium-free Hanks Balanced Salt Solution. Both cytophotometric techniques showed approximately 40 % of the cells in glands of 20–25 g prepubertal male mice to be involved in DNA synthesis.
