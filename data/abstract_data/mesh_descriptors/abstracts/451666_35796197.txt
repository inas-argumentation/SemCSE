Re-assessment of normalization between fractionated and continuous radiotherapy for the CRE and TDF equations.
In a recent article Liversage (1979) reported on the use of several time-dose models for comparing conventional intracavitary Ca cervix treatment schedules at low dose rate with the more recently introduced high dose rate fractionated (remote afterloading) regimes. His analysis showed that, compared with typical low dose rate techniques, the equivalent dose at high dose rate was overestimated by 82% using CRE (Kirk et al., 1971, 1975a, 1975b, 1977), 30% with the Liversage method (Liversage, 1969,1971), and 11% with TDF (Orton and Ellis, 1973; Orton, 1974).
