Impact of Behavior Therapy on Weight Loss
Intervention: Behavior Therapy for Treatment of Obesity Outcome: Weight Loss Impact of Intervention on Outcome: Behavior therapy has shown success in helping the obese lose modest amounts of weight? Average length of treatment is 18 weeks; average weight losses are 9.9 kg. Individuals maintain about 66% of their losses at 52-weeks followup, with gradual return to baseline after three to five years.
