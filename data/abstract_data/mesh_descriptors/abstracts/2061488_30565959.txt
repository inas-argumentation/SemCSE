Lack of serologic evidence for Helicobacter pylori infection in head and neck cancer
Several epidemiologic investigations have established a link between Helicobacter pylori infection and gastric malignancies. Because the stomach is in continuity with the oral cavity and the bacterium has been isolated from dental plaque and saliva, we hypothesized that H. pylori infection of the upper aerodigestive tract might result in mucosal disruption, allowing for subsequent transformation by known carcinogens such as tobacco and alcohol.
