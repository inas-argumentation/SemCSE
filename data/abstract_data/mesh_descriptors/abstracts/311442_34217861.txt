The intragastric diet of milk powder and glucose maintained metabolic balance except for a negative chloride balance. The urinary total nitrogen, ammonia, and aminoacids were similar on the two diets, but urea excretion was reduced on the intravenous diet, suggesting a decrease in protein catabolism.
