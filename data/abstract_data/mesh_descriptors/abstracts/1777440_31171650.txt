Anticancer therapy with Hydrea: Comparative study of daily vs, intermittent oral dosage
No significant difference can be demonstrated in this evaluation between daily doses and intermittent doses of Hydrea in either subjective or objective response. Although a slightly better result was obtained by the daily dose in patients with malignant melanoma, colonrectum carcinoma, sarcoma, and ovarian cancer. No significant increase in longevity was noted by the intermittent dose over the daily dose. Because the difference between the two methods of administration is so slight, the intermittent method recommends itself because of the superior hematopoietic picture that can be maintained following its implementation.
