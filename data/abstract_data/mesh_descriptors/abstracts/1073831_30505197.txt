Primary liver cancer (angiography and scintigraphy).
SUMMARY 
 
Angiographic findings in 67 cases of primary hepatic carcinoma seen at the Aichi Cancer Center Hospital were analyzed. Five of these cases with interesting findings were presented. The hepatoscintigram was shown to be of great help not only as a screening test but also in assisting in the interpretation of the angiographic picture. The relationship between α1-fetoprotein measurements and angiography was further discussed and the future direction of research was commented on.
