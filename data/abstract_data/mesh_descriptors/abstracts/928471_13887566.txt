Polarization of actin cytoskeleton is reduced in dendritic protrusions during early spine development in hippocampal neuron
This is the first measurement of F-actin retrograde flow rate in dendritic filopodia (1.20 µm/min), the precursor of dendritic spines, and in newly formed spines (0.03 µm/min). The difference indicates a remodeling of the force-generation mechanism during spinogenesis. It is also found that myosin II plays a role in regulating F-actin flow.
