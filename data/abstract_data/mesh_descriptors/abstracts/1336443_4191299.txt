Inhibition of Oxygen Consumption of Liver Homogenates from Mice injected with Radioprotectors in the Presence of Pyruvate
FOLLOWING the working hypothesis put forward by Bacq and Alexander1 according to which the ‘biochemical shock’ induced by the injection of radioprotectors, at radioprotective dose, must have some importance in the mechanisms of action of these substances, we have measured the oxygen consumption of liver homogenates of mice injected with a dose of radioprotector when pyruvate was offered as substrate.
