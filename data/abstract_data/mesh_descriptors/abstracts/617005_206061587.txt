As such, it cannot be excluded that the nutritional supplements influenced the endotoxin-induced immune response and/or the development of endotoxin tolerance. Second, only area under the curve data are provided. In our opinion, it would be preferable to show cytokine and cortisol concentrations across time because the kinetics might be different between the two endotoxin administrations. Third, there is a trend toward a decreased proinflammatory and an increased anti-inflammatory cytokine profile. This is likely caused by endotoxin tolerance because it is characterized by an attenuated production of proinflammatory and increased production of anti-inflammatory cytokines (5). In conclusion, we feel that this study does not provide definitive evidence that endotoxin tolerance is absent 5 weeks after endotoxin administration, and that results of crossover studies should be interpreted with caution.
