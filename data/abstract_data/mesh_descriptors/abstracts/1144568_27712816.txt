Incidental detection of residual radiation in a transplanted kidney.
A patient with possible brain death had cerebral scintigraphy with ethyl cysteinate dimer (Tc-99m ECD). Brain death was established at 7:00 A.M. One of his donated kidneys was transplanted into a 39-year-old woman at 11:00 A.M. After recovering from surgery, renal scintigraphy was performed on the recipient at 4:00 P.M. to determine the status of the kidney. In positioning the patient, radiation was perceived in the kidney area. Seventy-five percent of the injected dose of Tc-99m ECD was found in the urine within 6 hours; possibly part of this was retained by dysfunction of the transplanted kidney.
