The evolving role of cytopathology in the era of advanced diagnostic and therapeutic bronchoscopy
C. Matthew Kinsey, MD, MPH, is an Assistant Professor at the University of Vermont College of Medicine and is the Director of Interventional Pulmonary at the University of Vermont Medical Center. He received his medical degree from the Albert Einstein College of Medicine and a Master in Public Health from the Harvard School of Public Health. He completed dedicated subspecialty training in Interventional Pulmonary in the combined Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and Massachusetts General Hospital Fellowship. Dr. Kinsey is a physician-investigator who uses computational imaging and modeling to attempt to address the decision of which of the many computed tomography screen-detected lung nodules should be biopsied. He also investigates advanced therapeutic bronchoscopy in the laboratory setting and as the principal investigator of investigator-initiated and industry clinical trials.
