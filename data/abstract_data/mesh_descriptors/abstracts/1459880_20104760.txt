The effect of laser power at 2650 A on deoxyribonucleic acid.
A study was conducted to determine the effect of laser power at a wavelength of 2650 A on template activity of calf thymus DNA. It was observed that template activity decreased exponentially with increasing U.V. laser exposures and that U.V. laser-treated DNA inhibited the activity of non-irradiated DNA. The optical density of the treated DNA showed less than 7% decrease at 2650 A. Although these results were qualitatively similar to those reported for conventional U.V. effects, there was a suggestion of a lack of reciprocity using the high peak powers of the U.V. laser.
