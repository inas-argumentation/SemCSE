Measurement of Percent Body Fat during Weight Loss in Obese Women: Comparison of Four Methods
Dual X-ray absorptiometry (DXA), total body water (TBW), bioelectrical impedance (BIA), and anthropometrics (DUR) are four widely used methods for measuring percent body fat (%BF). The relative validity of these methods in obese people, however, has been debated. These methods are based on different assumptions and either twoor three-compartment modeling. Regardless of their limitations in obese populations,1–4 these methods are used to follow changes in body composition as a result of disease or weight change. In the present study, we compared the %BF measured by these four methods in obese women. This is the first study of voluntary weight loss using the four methods to follow the change in %BF.
