-Aminolevulinic acid dehydratase from shell gland and liver of Japanese quail, Coturnix coturnix Japonica. I. Purification, properties and hormonal induction.
Abstract 1. 1.σ-Aminolevulinic acid dehydratase(5-aminolevulinate hydro-lyase, EC 4.2.1.24) was prepared from shell gland and liver of Japanese quail, Coturnix coturnix japonica . The enzyme had the optimum pH at 6.9–7.1 and the maximal activity of the enzyme was obtained at around 60 °C. No significant differences for the heat denaturation were obtained between the shell gland and liver enzymes. p -Chloromercuri-benzoate, ethylenediaminetetraacetate and iodoacetate acted as potent inhibitors for both enzymes. The K m value for σ-aminolevulinic acid of the liver enzyme was 2.0·10 −4 M at pH 6.9 and 41°C, while that of shell gland enzyme was 6.1·10 −5 M. 2. 2. Change of σ-aminolevulinic acid dehydrates activity was observed in the shell gland during the pigmentation process, but not in liver. The maximal enzyme activity in the shell gland was observed before pigmentation of the egg shell of a developing egg. 3. 3. Induction by 17β-estradiol of σ-aminolevulinic acid dehydratase in shell gland and liver of immature quail was observed. The maximal enzyme activity induced by a single injection of 17β-estradiol was obtained on the third day after injection.
