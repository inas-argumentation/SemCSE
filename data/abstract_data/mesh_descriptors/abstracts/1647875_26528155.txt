Axillary block for vascular insufficiency after repair of radial club hands in an infant.
Vascular insufficiency may occur after a variety of surgical procedures and has been described after repair of congenital radial club hand. Treatment of vascular insufficiency by use of sympathectomy has recently been reported in the pediatric population. We describe successful use of bilateral axillary blocks to relieve acute vascular insufficiency in a 7-month-old infant after surgical repair of radial club hands
