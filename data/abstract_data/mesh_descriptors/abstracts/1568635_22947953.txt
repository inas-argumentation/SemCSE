Quantitative and immunohistochemical evaluation of PCNA, androgen receptors, apoptosis, and Glutathione‐S‐Transferase P1 on preneoplastic changes induced by cadmium and zinc chloride in the rat ventral prostate
This study was directed to evaluate the immunoexpression of markers for cell proliferation, apoptosis, nuclear androgen receptors, and Glutathione‐S‐Transferase P1 (GSTP1), in preneoplastic changes induced by cadmium chloride (Cd) and cadmium plus zinc chloride (Cd + Zn) in rat prostate.
