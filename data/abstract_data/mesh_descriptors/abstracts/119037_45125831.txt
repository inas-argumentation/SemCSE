The thermal response of mouse adenocarcinoma cells at low pH.
The response of MADCAP 37 tumor cells to 42.0°C and 45.5°C was determined at various pH levels. Cell viability, monitored by plating efficiency, did not vary in the pH range of 6.50–8.00 at 37.0°C. However, heating the cells to 45.5°C at pH values below 7.00 reduced survival 10- to 40-fold. Surviving fraction remained unchanged when the cells were heated at pH values greater than 7.00. The Do is approximately halved from pH 7.00 to 6.50. At 42.0°C, the sensitivity of the cells to heat at low pH was even more pronounced. The Do of the heat survival curve decreased 7-fold from pH 7.30 to pH 6.50.
