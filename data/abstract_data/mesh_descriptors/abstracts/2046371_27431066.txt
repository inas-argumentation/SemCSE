Combined screening: blood pressure and cholesterol.
To the Editor.— We believe, because of our favorable experience in rural Maine, that cholesterol screening and counseling can be added successfully to an ongoing hypertension program, and this is relevant to the impending nationwide cholesterol education campaign. 1 The Franklin Blood Pressure Program has conducted hypertension screening, follow-up, referral, and education since 1974. 2 With medical staff endorsement and state subsidy for a pilot project, we added cholesterol in November 1986, performing on-site finger-prick measurements. To ensure continued focus on hypertension, we decided always to check blood pressure whenever we measured cholesterol level. During the five-month period ending March 1987, cholesterol level and blood pressure together were measured for 1388 individuals at 12 community clinics and 24 work sites. Cholesterol/blood pressure screenees were 42% male overall and 50% male in the 20- to 40-year age group.
