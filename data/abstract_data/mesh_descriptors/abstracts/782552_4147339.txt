Homologous Tumour Growth in Methylcholanthrene-induced Sarcoma-bearing Mice
IN 1958, Southam and Moore1 reported that implants of cultivated cancer cells continued to grow in cancer-bearing patients, whereas its regression occurred in healthy volunteer recipients. We, therefore, examined whether growth of a transplanted tumour was better in methyl-cholanthrene-induced sarcoma-bearing mice than in normal hosts or not.
