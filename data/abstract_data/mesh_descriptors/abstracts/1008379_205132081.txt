In our case the reflex could not protect because the high-powered pulse was shorter than the time lag of the aversion reflex (<100 milliseconds).3 This case demonstrates that the high energy level at the wavelength used for laser epilation is capable of causing macular injury with rapid choroidal neovascularization development and severe vision loss.
