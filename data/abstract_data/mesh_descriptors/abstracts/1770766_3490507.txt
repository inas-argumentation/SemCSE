Identification of the l-Aspartate Transporter in Bacillus subtilis
ABSTRACT YveA of Bacillus subtilis, a putative transporter of the amino acid/polyamine/organocation (APC) superfamily, is shown to mediate uptake of both l-aspartate and l-glutamate as well as having sensitivity to l-aspartate hydroxamate. This 14 TMS protein is the primary aspartate uptake system in B. subtilis and serves as the prototype for a new family within the APC superfamily.
