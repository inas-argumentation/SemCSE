[Perianal basal cell carcinoma. Apropos of 2 cases].
INTRODUCTION
Two cases of perianal basal cell carcinoma were reported. Less than 100 cases have been so far described.


OBSERVATIONS
The first case was a pigmented tumour, in the second an erosive erythematous plaque somewhat similar to Paget's disease.


COMMENTS
Basal cell carcinoma of the anal margin is about 15-fold less common than squamous cell carcinoma. The tumour is often ulcerated, sometimes infiltrative but metastasis never occurs. Histopathological findings are often close to what is observed in cloacogenic carcinoma. Surgical resection of the lesion is the best treatment but electrontherapy permitted lesion healing in one of our cases.
