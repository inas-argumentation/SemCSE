DNA base composition of some free-living nematode species.
The mean base compositions (% GC) of DNA samples from five free-living nematodes were determined by CsCl equilibrium buoyant-density centrifugation and thermal denaturation studies. Both methods gave similar results indicating that there is no extensive replacement of the usual bases in nematode DNA.
