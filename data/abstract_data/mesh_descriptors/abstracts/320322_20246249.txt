Effects of Schistosoma mansoni and Schistosoma japonicum infections on induction of hepatic tyrosine aminotransferase in mice.
1. 1. Tyrosine aminotransferase activity could not be detected in either adult Schistosoma mansoni or S. japonicum . 2. 2. Livers from female mice infected with S. mansoni for 10 weeks had reduced tyrosine aminotransferase activity and a reduced ability to respond to enzyme induction by hydrocortisone-21 phosphate. 3. 3. Livers from female mice infected with S. japonicum for 10 weeks showed no reduction in tyrosine aminotransferase activity but exhibited a moderate reduction in ability (84% of normal) to respond to enzyme induction by hydrocortisone-21 phosphate.
