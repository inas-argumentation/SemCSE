Surgical management of a recurrent solitary multilocular nonparasitic cyst of the liver.
This is a case report of the successful management of a recurrent multilocular solitary nonparasitic cyst of the liver. The patient had a subtotal excision twenty-eight years before her symptoms recurred. The tumor was removed by total left lobectomy. She is currently in good condition, one year postresection. Total excision or total lobectomy is recommended as the definitive management of the solitary multilocular nonparasitic cyst of the liver.
