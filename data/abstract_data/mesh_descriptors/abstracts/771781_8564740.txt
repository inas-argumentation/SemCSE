ULTRAVIOLET‐LIGHT‐INDUCED CONFORMATIONAL CHANGES IN DNA
— Irradiation of DNA at 254 nm produces alterations in the secondary structure leading to changes in the circular‐dichroism (CD) spectrum. A new Cotton effect around 320 nm, and a change in the rotatory strength of the CD maximum at 275 nm, was observed. Whereas the new negative maximum at 320 nm generally increases with increasing radiation dose, the maximum at 275 nm is enhanced at lower UV doses but is diminished at higher doses. Thus we suppose that there are two different DNA conformations, the transition between which occurs at 15times104 ergs/mm2. Below this dose, the DNA is in a partially closed and twisted state, but above it the DNA has a C‐like conformation.
