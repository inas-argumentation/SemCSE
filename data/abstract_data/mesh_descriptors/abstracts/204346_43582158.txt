Differential hormonal profiles of adrenomedullin and proadrenomedullin n‐terminal 20 peptide in patients with heart failure and effect of treatment on their plasma levels
Background: Adrenomedullin (AM) is a potent vasodilatory peptide discovered in human pheochromocytoma tissue. Proadrenomedullin N‐terminal 20 peptide (PAMP) processed from an AM precursor is also a novel hypotensive peptide which inhibits catecholamine secretion from sympathetic nerve endings.
