The occurrence of protein-bound mixed disulfides in rat tissues.
The occurrence of glutathione and cysteine bound to protein by mixed disulfide linkages is reported in a range of rat tissues. Mixed disulfides of glutathione and serum albumin were reducible in presence of glutathione reductase, though similar derivatives containing cysteine in place of glutathione could not be reduced by this enzyme. No evidence was found that enzymes other than glutathione reductase were required for the reduction of disulphide bonds linking glutathione to protein in the range of tissues examined.
