Aberrant nuclear Cdx2 expression in morule‐forming tumours in different organs, accompanied by cytoplasmic reactivity
Sir: The Cdx2, an intestine-specific homeobox gene transcription factor, is expressed not only in normal intestinal mucosa but also in colonic adenocarcinoma. Furthermore, Cdx2 expression has been documented in adenocarcinomas at other sites such as the stomach, pancreas, gallbladder, and urinary bladder, and this expression has been interpreted as intestinal differentiation. In the female genital tract, aberrant expression of Cdx2 in morular foci in endometrioid proliferations or carcinomas of the uterus and ovary has recently been described. Furthermore, in our report of nuclear Cdx2 expression in pyloric gland adenoma of the gallbladder, we showed that Cdx2 expression is correlated with morular foci and nuclear accumulation of b-catenin, but not with either intestinal metaplasia or MUC2 expression. However, the Cdx2 status of morular foci in other organs or lesions has not been explored thus far. In addition, a few rare cases of endometrioid carcinoma with only cytoplasmic Cdx2 expression have also been observed.
