Test–retest reliability of the Italian version of the M‐BACK questionnaire to assess the barriers, attitudes, confidence, and knowledge of mental health staff regarding metabolic health of psychiatric patients
The Metabolic‐Barriers, Attitudes, Confidence, and Knowledge Questionnaire (M‐BACK) was developed to determine the barriers, attitudes, confidence, and knowledge of mental health practitioners regarding the metabolic health of patients in order to determine the efficacy of targeted training interventions. This study aimed to validate the Italian version of M‐BACK questionnaire (M‐BACK‐IT) and to determine the test–retest reliability.
