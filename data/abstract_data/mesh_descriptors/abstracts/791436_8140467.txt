Developing a Case Management Strategy in Israel
As elsewhere in the West, Israel has been riding the crest of deinstitutionalization (Aviram 1996). While Israel's population is approaching 6 million, the absolute number of psychiatric hospital beds has been reduced to 5,868, which represents a reduction in the rate per thousand population from 2.4 to less than 1.0 over the past 3 decades. Sixty percent of these beds are in government hospitals; most of the remainder are found in government-financed private hospitals. Chronic beds constitute 70% of the total, or 0.70 beds per 1,000 population. Hospital discharges of chronic patients have been accompanied by the parallel development of community alternatives.
