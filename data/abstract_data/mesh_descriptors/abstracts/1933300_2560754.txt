Regression of estrone-induced mammary tumors in the rat.
Mammary tumors induced in rats by prolonged stimulation with estrone regress if this source of estrogen is removed. Regression of this type of tumor is rapid and complete, and is accompanied by shrinkage of cells, increased nuclear pyknosis and a decrease in the number of dividing cells. The regression is permanent and is maintained as long as the animals are free of the exogenous source of estrogen.
