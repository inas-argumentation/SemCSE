The efficacy of self‐monitoring of blood glucose in the management of patients with type 2 diabetes treated with a gliclazide modified release–based regimen. A multicentre, randomized, parallel‐group, 6‐month evaluation (DINAMIC 1 study)
Aim:  To determine if therapeutic management programmes for type 2 diabetes that include self‐monitoring of blood glucose (SMBG) result in greater reductions in glycated haemoglobin (HbA1c) compared with programmes without SMBG in non‐insulin requiring patients.
