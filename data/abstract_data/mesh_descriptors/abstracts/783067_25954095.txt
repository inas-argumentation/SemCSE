Morphogenesis of the photoreceptive site and development of the electrical responses in the butterfly genital photoreceptors during the pupal period
This paper describes the process of morphogenesis of the photoreceptive site of the butterfly genital photoreceptors. Associated development of the electrical responses is also described. The photoreceptor is a sensory neuron whose cell body is located in the genitalia and has a photoreceptive site of the phaosome‐type. This consists of the distal processes and the tubular membranes, which protrude from the tip of distal processes.
