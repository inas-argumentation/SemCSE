Inhibition of protein synthesis by aminoacyl 3'-(2')-adenosine.
Abstract 1. 1. The synthesis of leucyl 3′-adenosine, and the isolation of a mixture of ribonuclease-free phenylalanyl- and tyrosyl-adenosines from charged sRNA, are described. 2. 2. These aminoacyl-adenosines inhibited Escherichia coli mRNA-mediated protein synthesis from aminoacyl-sRNA in a manner comparable to that of puromycin. 3. 3. Poly U-mediated poly Phe synthesis was inhibited by leucyl-adenosine, thus indicating that its aminoacyl residue does not confer specificity for the inhibition of incorporation of that particular amino acid. 4. 4. Like puromycin, leucyl-adenosine caused the release of alcohol-soluble products in E. coli mRNA-mediated, as well as in poly U-mediated synthesis. In the absence of ribosomes, no deacylation of aminoacyl-sRNA due to leucyl-adenosine was observed. 5. 5. Addition of leucyl-adenosine resulted in release of poly Phe from ribosome-bound poly Phe sRNA.
