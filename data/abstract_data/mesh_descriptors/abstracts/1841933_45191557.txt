Probably it is connected with eosinophil peroxidase inhibition and subsequent blocking of mast cell degranulation, as well as further IL-5-mediated eosinophil activation. Our case confirms excellent response to dapsone, although EAE is usually refractory to most available treatments. After Manriquez et al. 2015, this is the second report of a favourable effect of dapsone in EAE, and we consider this inexpensive and safe option as the drug of first choice in EAE management.
