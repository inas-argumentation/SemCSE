Transient and steady state simulation of the renal countercurrent mechanism.
A mathematical description is given for transport of sodium, urea and water within the tubule system composed of Henle loops, distal tubules, collecting ducts and vasa recta. Transtubular fluxes are assumed passive, except for sodium in the ascending Henle loops, distal tubules and collecting ducts. Calculated steady state values, using parameters based on available data, are given for profiles of water velocity and of sodium and urea concentrations along the various tubules, which are compatible with observations. Starting with antidiuresis values, transient and steady state values have been obtained to simulate a variety of experimental situations, reproducing known features of kidney function.
