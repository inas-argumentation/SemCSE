Neurogenic pulmonary edema in unexpected, unexplained death of epileptic patients
Eight cases of unexpected, unexplained death in young ambulatory epileptics were examined postmortem with special attention to the heart and lungs. Lung weights uniformly exceeded the expected value, with gross evidence of hemorrhagic pulmonary edema. Microscopic examination revealed moderate to severe pulmonary edema with protein‐rich fluid as well as alveolar hemorrhage. There was no evidence of recent or old myocardial disease.
