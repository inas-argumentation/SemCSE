Reports of the european study group on antibiotic breakpoints
For some time, a number of members of the European Society of Clinical Microbiology and Infectious Diseases (ESCMID) have felt that it Would be desirable to have a consensus on antibiotic susceptibility breakpoints. At a preliminary meeting in Nice, France, the breakpoint recommendations of as many European countries as possible were reviewed and the degree of diversity established. The general conclusion was that, guided by Chabbert's characterisation of a breakpoint as a pragmatic consensus, it should be possible to agree on a systematic approach to the methodology of breakpoint setting, and then to apply that approach to individual antibiotics. Baquero (1) has summarised the present position and defined the range of problems to be solved.
