Phase II study of aclarubicin in patients with breast cancer previously untreated with adriamycin
Twenty‐five breast cancer patients previously not exposed to adriamycin, were treated with 30 mg/m2/day aclarubicin for four consecutive days. Out of the 19 evaluable cases 1 CR and 2 PR were observed giving an overall remission rate of 16%. No cardiotoxicity was observed. Myelotoxicity and hair loss were mild (WHO grades 1 and 2). Grade 2 and 3 nausea and vomiting occurred frequently.
