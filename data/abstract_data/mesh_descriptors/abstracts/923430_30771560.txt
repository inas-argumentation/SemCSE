Immunity suppression by bleomycin-methotrexate combined treatment in patients with epidermoid carcinoma of the oral cavity.
Summary Bleomycine and Methotrexate have both proved to be of significant value for treatment of squamous cell carcinomas of the oral cavity. An overall primary remission rate of 70% was achieved in twenty patients not treated before by any method. Because of possible immune-suppression, caused by almost all cytostatics, it must be taken into account a later tumor relapse by break down of immune mechanisms. To estimate this possibility, the most important way of immunological tumor-defence — the specific transformation of lymphocytes — was examined during Bleomycine-Methotrexate application. The observed suppression of transformation only correlated with Methotrexate Schedule and was reestablished with overshooting capacity shortly after treatment ceased. Bleomycine did not seem to bring about any immune-suppression.
