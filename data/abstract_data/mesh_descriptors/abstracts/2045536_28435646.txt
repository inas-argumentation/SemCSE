Apomorphine antagonism of the elevation of homovanillic acid induced by antipsychotic drugs.
Apomorphine was found to be effective in antagonizing the homovanillic acid elevating activity of several antipsychotic agents. Increasing doses of chlorpromazine overcame the effect of apomorphine on homovanillic acid and demonstrated the competitive nature of these drugs for the dopaminergic receptors. Several apomorphine-like compounds, and amantadine, did not exert the antagonistic effect displayed by apomorphine. The relationship of these effects to the production of extrapyramidal side effects and to antipsychotic activity is briefly discussed.
