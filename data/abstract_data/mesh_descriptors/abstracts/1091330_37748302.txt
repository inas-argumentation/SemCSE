Intracardiac blood cyst: rare finding in a complex congenital heart lesion.
Intracardiac blood cysts are rare congenital malformations located in the endocardium of semilunar or atrioventricular valves. Patients are mostly asymptomatic and diagnosed in the paediatric age, particularly in the first months. Appearance in adults is extremely rare.
