Aromatic amine carcinogenesis: the importance of N-hydroxylation.
1. The evidence for the metabolic activation of carcinogenic aromatic amines is reviewed.2. There is a three-step process, N-hydroxylation, esterification and decomposition of the ester to a positively charged electrophile.3. The difficulties in assessing the nature of the esterification step from its reaction products in the tissue are described.
