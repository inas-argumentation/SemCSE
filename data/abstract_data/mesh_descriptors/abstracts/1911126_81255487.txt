On the structure of the Escherichia coli C cell wall lipopolysaccharide core and on its øX174 receptor region
Mutants of Escherichia coli C were selected for resistance towards a set of cell wall LPS core specific bacteriophages, including oX174. Increasingly deficient LPS's from wt and mutant E. coli C were tested for inactivation of oX174, and the core oligosaccharides were subjected to structural analysis by methylation/g.l.c./m.s.
