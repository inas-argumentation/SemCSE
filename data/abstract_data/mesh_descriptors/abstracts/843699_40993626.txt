Hormonal Influence on Rat Submandibular-Gland Histochemistry
Atrophic changes occur in the morphology of the mouse and rat submandibular gland, subsequent to hypophysectomy.1-' Various investigators have shown that the administration of thyroxine and testosterone, particularly in combination, is successful in preventing, or reversing, these changes.5-7 In addition, these hormones appeared to reverse certain hypophysectomy-induced biochemical alterations.8 I The purpose of this study was to investigate the effects of testosterone and thyroxine, individually and in combination, on the histochemistry of normal and hypophysectomized rat submandibular glands.
