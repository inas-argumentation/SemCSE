It's not my fault, blame it on my microenvironment.
For decades, pathologists have recognized that the stroma surrounding tumors is in many cases altered with obvious changes in the cellular composition and the extracellular matrix (ECM). Over the years, descriptive studies have verified that gene expression is altered in carcinoma-associated stroma or ‘‘tumor stroma‘‘, and for some time, investigators have considered and explored the role of tumor stroma in the emergence and progression of carcinomas. A pivotal monograph in this area was the book edited by David Tarin entitled ‘‘Tissue Interactions in Carcinogenesis’’ published in 1972 (Tarin, 1972).
