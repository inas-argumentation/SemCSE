NMDA receptor subunit gating – uncovered
Kinetically distinct contributions of NMDA receptor NR1 and NR2 subunits to channel gating have recently been inferred by Banke and Traynelis from single-channel recordings of recombinant NR1-NR2B receptors. The results suggest a new mechanism for receptor activation that will enhance our insight into excitatory synaptic transmission in the brain. In the post-genomic era, this represents an exciting advance in understanding the relationship between structure and function for this unique class of neurotransmitter receptor.
