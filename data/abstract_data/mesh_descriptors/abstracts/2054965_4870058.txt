Actinic lentigines versus skin cancer risk in albinos in northern Tanzania.
Patients with albinism have an increased susceptibility to skin cancer. lm6 Cutaneous malignancies are particularly prevalent in albinos exposed to equatorial sunlight. ‘, 3* 4 In this regard we have reported our findings in a population of 164 albino patients living in the Kilimanjaro region of Tanzania.7 A report from South Africa indicated that skin cancer is less common in albinos with ephelides than in those without ephelides.2 We have analyzed the data from our patient population to determine whether a relation exists between pigmented skin lesions and the development of skin cancer. The results support the notion that an inverse relation exists.
