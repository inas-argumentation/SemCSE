Kidney cancer: Prognostic and predictive biomarkers for RCC emerge
Although prognostic biomarkers have been identified in renal cell cancer (RCC), none have been routinely applied in the clinic. Furthermore, no predictive biomarkers are used to identify patients who might benefit from a given treatment. New research from a team led by John Heymach and published in The Lancet Oncology has addressed this gap in RCC biomarker research.
