Endovascular AAA repair: Classification of aneurysm sac volumetric change using spiral computed tomo
Purpose:To classify and analyze the volumetric changes seen on spiral computed tomographic angiography (CTA) following endovascular abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA) repair.Methods:Fifty patients (46 men; mean age 71 years, range 51–83) with >1 year of imaging follow-up were retrospectively selected. The volume of the aneurysm sac was calculated on standard CT workstations to obtain plots of volume changes over time. For the purpose of this study, a 10% change in sac volume was considered significant.Results:Over a mean 32-month follow-up, 256 CTA scans were performed; initial mean sac volume was 259 mL and initial mean AAA diameter was 6.5 cm.
