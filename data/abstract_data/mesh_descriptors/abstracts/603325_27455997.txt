Preliminary results of intraperitoneal chemohyperthermia with oxaliplatin in peritoneal carcinomatosis of colorectal origin
Complete resection of macroscopic colorectal peritoneal carcinomatosis, followed by intraoperative intraperitoneal chemohyperthermia (IPCH) to treat the residual microscopic disease, may cure some patients1. Mitomycin C and cisplatin are the chemotherapeutic agents usually used for IPCH. However, oxaliplatin has potential for use in colorectal cancer2. This study reports preliminary results on postoperative survival in a phase II study that used oxaliplatin during IPCH.
