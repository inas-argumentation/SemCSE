Melanoma following treatment with alemtuzumab for multiple sclerosis
Sir, Alemtuzumab (Campath-1H , Genzyme Corp., Cambridge, MA, USA) is one of several monoclonal antibodies currently investigated for treatment of aggressive multiple sclerosis (MS). To date, concerns on its use have centred on the emergence of other autoimmune conditions following treatment, including thyrotoxicosis and idiopathic thrombocytopaenic purpura. Here we describe a case of malignant melanoma following treatment with alemtuzumab, which to our knowledge is the first to occur in MS.
