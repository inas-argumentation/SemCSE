Obtaining therapeutic antibiotic blood levels in children.
Hospitalized children usually require parenteral antibiotics as part of their treatment regimens. Achieving therapeutic antibiotic blood levels poses several problems: the medications must be administered using a standard dilution, over consistent time periods, with levels drawn at the appropriate time. It was found that the use of a special pump provided enough control of these variables to greatly decrease the number of subtherapeutic and uninterpretable blood levels. The number of therapeutic levels increased dramatically. This method was found to be both safe and cost effective.
