Anthropometric Risk Factors for Colorectal Polyps in African‐American Women
Objective: Colorectal adenomas are thought to be precursor lesions to colorectal cancer, a leading cause of cancer incidence and mortality in African‐American women. Studies suggest that obesity is associated with risk of adenomas in white women, but little is known about the relation in African‐American women. We prospectively examined the association between selected anthropometric factors and colorectal polyps in African‐American women.
