Increased 5‐Hydroxytryptamine and Norepinephrine Release from Rat Brain Slices by the Red Sea Flatfish
: The effect of the Red Sea flatfish toxin pardaxin was examined on K+‐evoked and on basal release of either [3H]norepinephrine or [3H]5‐hydroxytryptamine from preloaded rat cortical slices. The K+‐induced release of the neurotransmitters was stimulated in a dose‐related manner at concentrations ranging from 0.5 to 4 μg/ml. Basal release of the two transmitters was elevated to a lesser extent. Although the stimulation of evoked release was approximately equivalent for the two neurotransmitters, the response to 5‐hydroxytryptamine was reversible whereas that of norepinephrine was not washed by 20 min of superfusion. The mechanisms involved in producing these actions of pardaxin are not known; however, they may be mediated by changes in electrolyte fluxes across the neuronal membranes.
