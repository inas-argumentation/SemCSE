Fibroblastic reactions in other areas have been associated with the beta-blocker practolol,4 and psoriatic skin lesions and dry eyes have occurred after treatment with oxprenolol.5 But, so far as we know, retroperitoneal fibrosis has not been reported in association with oxprenolol. Retroperitoneal fibrosis is a serious condition which is easily overlooked. It has been reported in association with another beta-blocker, and it should be considered in any patient taking a beta-blocker who develops chronic impairment of renal function.
