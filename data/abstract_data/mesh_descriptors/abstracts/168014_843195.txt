Focal adenitis developing after immune reconstitution with HAART
Sir: It is now well recognized that successful highly active antiretroviral therapy (HAART) can unmask previously subclinical Mycobacterium avium-intracellulare (MAI) infection1,2. This often manifests as a focal adenitisÐ often cervical gland enlargement, but the case described here also shows mediastinal involvement. A 24-year-old gay man was admitted to the medical unit with weight loss, fatigue and a history of intermittent oropharyngeal candidiasis. Haemoglobin was low at 8.7 g/dl. He was found to be HIV positive with a T4 count of 14 and a viral load of 376,000. Bone marrow aspirate revealed normocellular and normocytic marrow spaces.
