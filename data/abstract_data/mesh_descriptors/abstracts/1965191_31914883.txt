An Audit of Pain/Discomfort Experienced during Image‐Guided Breast Biopsy Procedures
Abstract:  A prospective audit of 221 breast biopsies was carried out to assess the pain/discomfort experienced during image‐guided breast biopsies. The only significant factor in pain scores was the size of the needle used. Fine‐needle aspiration cytology using a 21‐gauge needle was found to cause the most discomfort.
