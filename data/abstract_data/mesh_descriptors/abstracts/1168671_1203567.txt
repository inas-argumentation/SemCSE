The role of urodynamic tests in micturitional pathology in gynecological oncology.
The postoperative urodynamic test results applied in the oncological field, compared case by case with preoperative results, allow us to gain interesting speculative hints at the etiopathogenesis of the micturitional disorders after radical gynaecological surgery, but it does not entitle us, up to date to recognize preliminarily those patients who are more easily predisposed to postoperative uro-gynaecological pathology.
