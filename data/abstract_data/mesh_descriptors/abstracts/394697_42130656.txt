Parenchymal leiomyoma of breast, mimicking cystosarcoma phylloides
Benign smooth muscle tumours are commonly seen in the genitourinary and gastrointestinal tracts. These tumours are frequently found in the nipple and subareolar region and are extremely rare in the breast parenchyma. Since its initial description in 1913, fewer than 22 cases have been reported.1–5 A 35-year-old woman presented with a gradually increasing painless mass in the upper outer quadrant of the left breast since 1 year. On examination, the lump was firm, non-tender, mobile and measured 6 cm · 7 cm. There was no history or family history of breast carcinoma. A clinical diagnosis of cystosarcoma phylloides was made.
