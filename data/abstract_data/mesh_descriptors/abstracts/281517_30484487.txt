Comparative study of the cystine-cysteine content of the mucoprotein of normal, silicotic and cancerous sera.
Abstract The cystine-cysteine contents of mucoprotein of normal, silicotic and cancerous sera were determined. The amount of the amino acid determined in the native protein of silicotic sera was greater than in normal sera, while in cancerous sera the amount is less. The proportion of the total cystine-cysteine content to the quantity of the mucoprotein is greater in silicotic than in normal sera; the values do not differ in normal and cancerous sera. The results indicate a qualitative change in the molecular structure of the mucoprotein both in silicotic and cancerous sera, but the direction of this change is not identical in the two pathological cases.
