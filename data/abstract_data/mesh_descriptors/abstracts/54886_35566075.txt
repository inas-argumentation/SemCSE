Isolation, purification, and assay of an initiation factor from rat liver cytosol that promotes binding of aminoacyl-tRNA to 40 S ribosomal subunits.
Publisher Summary This chapter describes the purification, from rat liver cytosol, of a factor that promotes the binding of Phe-tRNA to 40 S ribosomal subunits. The purified factor also directs the binding of fMet-tRNA and N-acetyl- Phe-tRNA to 40 S subunits and appears to be similar to the reticulocyte initiation factor IF-M 1 and analogous to the bacterial factor IF-2.
