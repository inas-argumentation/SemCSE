Studies on the Permeability of the Oral Mucosa I. Permeability of Inorganic Ions
It has been reported that various chemical substances permeate the oral mucosa by simple diffusion.'-5 However, the basic physiologic mechanism of this phenomenon has not yet been confirmed experimentally. Recently, many basic problems concerning the mechanism of ionic transport through biological membranes have been elucidated experimentally and theoretically.6-10 Most of these studies have been concerned only with monolayer membranes; hence the results may not be applicable to oral mucosa. In this experiment the permeability of K, Na, and Ca ions across the oral mucosa was investigated in order to understand the principal mechanism of ionic transport across the tissue membrane.
