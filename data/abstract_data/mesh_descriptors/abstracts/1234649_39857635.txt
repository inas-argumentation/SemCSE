International Symposium on Control Mechanisms of Carcinogenesis
The International Symposium on Control Mechanisms of Carcinogenesis, organized by the center grant 302 (control mechanisms of carcinogenesis) and center grant 1551 (organand cell-type specificity of carcinogenesis, tumor development, and prevention), was held on 28-30 September in Mainz, Germany. The symposium was attended by more than 200 participants and provided 2 days of expert information on the following topics: Session 1. Metabolic control of carcinogens; chairmen: E Oesch (Mainz) and ER Guengerich (Nashville, Tennessee) Session 2. DNA adducts and repair; chairman: C.R. Wolf (Dundee, Scotland) Session 3. Oncogenes, tumor-suppressor genes, cell proliferation and transcription factors; chairman: O. Hankinson (Los Angeles, California) Session 4. The cell cycle and its dysregulation in carcinogenesis; chairman: T. Friedberg (Dundee, Scotland) Session 5. Cell adhesion and its dysregulation in carcinogenesis; chairman: H. Kunz (Mainz) In each session lectures were held summarizing the whole field of relevant research, complemented by information on recent achievments and frontier contributions. The proceedings of the symposium will be published in the book Control mechanisms of carcinogenesis (Springer Verlag) edited by J.G. Hengstler and E Oesch, which will appear early in 1996.
