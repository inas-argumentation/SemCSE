Matter related to the research article published in Obesity Reviews (2009; 9: 389-399), entitled 'Obesity in Mediterranean region (1997-2007): a systematic review'.
Dear editor, I would like to bring to your attention a matter related to the research article published in Obesity Reviews (2008; 9: 389–399), entitled ‘Obesity in Mediterranean region (1997–2007): a systematic review’. In this article, the authors referred to two studies by Chakar et al. (2006) and Jabre et al. (2005) as the references for data from Lebanon. However, please note that both studies were performed on selected population groups not representative of the Lebanese population. Chakar et al. (2006) reported on adolescents from selected Lebanese private schools and Jabre et al. (2005) on children from Beirut area. I would like also to note that it was surprising that the authors’ search did not select the two nationally representative studies that were published on obesity prevalence in the Lebanese population in different age groups. One is that of Sibai et al. (2003), published in Obesity Research (2003; 11: 1353–1361) and entitled ‘Prevalence and covariates of obesity in Lebanon: findings from the first epidemiological study’, and the other is of Hwalla et al. (2005), published in World Review of Nutrition and Dietetics (2005; 94: 42–50) and entitled ‘Adolescent obesity and physical activity’. Attached please find copies of the two mentioned articles (Mehio-Sibai A, Hwalla (Baba) N, Adra N, Rahal B. Prevalence and Co-variates of Overweight and Obesity in Lebanon: Findings from the First epidemiological Study. Obesity Research 2003; 11(11): 1353–1361. Hwalla N, Mehio Sibai A, Adra N. Adolescent obesity and physical activity. World Review of Nutrition and Dietetics 2005; 94: 42–50). We hope that the above remarks will be taken into consideration and recorded in the journal as erratum.
