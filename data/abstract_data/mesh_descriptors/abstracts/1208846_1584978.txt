Localization and regulation of the structural gene for transcription-termination factor rho of Escherichia coli.
Abstract The structural gene for the transcription-termination factor rho of Escherichia coli has been cloned. The exact location of the rho gene has been identified by comparing the amino acid sequence of the N-terminal part of rho with the DNA sequence. Our results show rho to be transcribed in the clockwise direction on the chromosome map. We have determined the location of four amber mutations, one temperature-sensitive mutation and two Tn5 insertions in rho . rho protein and rho messenger RNA have been measured in strains with different rho alleles. Their levels were found to vary co-ordinately, indicating transcriptional control of rho gene expression. Deletion analysis locates the rho promoter between 308 and 230 base-pairs from the ATG. We have located the transcriptional start site by nuclease S 1 treatment of mRNA:DNA hybrids to 256 bases from the start of the structural gene. The same start site has been found in both rho + and rho − strains. Attenuated RNA molecules, terminating five and seven bases upstream from the rho ATG, have been observed in a strain containing rho cloned into pBR322. This result indicates that rho expression is regulated via an attenuator.
