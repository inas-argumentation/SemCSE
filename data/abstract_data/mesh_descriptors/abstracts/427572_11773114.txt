Formation of acyl phosphatidyl glycerol by Escherichia coli extracts.
Cell-free extracts of Escherichia coli were found to catalyse the conversion of phosphatidyl glycerol to acyl phosphatidyl glycerol (semilysobisphosphatidic acid). The product of this conversion was characterized by detailed chromatographic and chemical analysis. The intact lipid had an ester:glycerol:phosphate ratio of 3:2:1 and an infrared spectrum displaying less absorption at 3200 cm −1 , than phosphatidyl glycerol. Mild alkaline hydrolysis of the lipid yielded glycerophosphoryl glycerol as sole product, acetolysis, diacylmonoacetin and monoacyldiacetin and acetic acid hydrolysis, lysophosphatidic acid and diglyceride in proportions expected for acyl phosphatidyl glycerol.
