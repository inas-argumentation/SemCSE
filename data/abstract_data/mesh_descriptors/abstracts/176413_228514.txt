Identification of the ubiD Gene on theEscherichia coli Chromosome
The open reading frame at 86.7 min on the Escherichia coli chromosome, “yigC,” complemented aubiD mutant strain, AN66, indicating that yigCis the ubiD gene. The gene product, a 497-amino-acid-residue protein, showed extensive homology to the UPF 00096 family of proteins in the Swiss-Prot database.
