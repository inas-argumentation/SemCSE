Metformin and risk of hepatocellular carcinoma: are statins the missing link?
We read with great interest, the recent article titled ‘Metformin decreases hepatocellular carcinoma risk in a dose-dependent manner: population-based and in vitro studies’ by Chen and colleagues published online in July in the Journal .1 In this large, population-based, case-control study using the Taiwanese National Health Insurance Research Database, they reported that in patients with diabetes mellitus (DM), metformin use is associated with a decrease in the risk of hepatocellular cancer (HCC) in a dose- and duration-dependent manner.
