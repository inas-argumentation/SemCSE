[Case 3: Elderly female patient with acute myocardial infarction presenting sudden shock during thrombolytic treatment].
Editor da Seção: Alfredo José Mansur (ajmansur@incor.usp.br) Editores Associados: Desidério Favarato (dclfavarato@incor.usp.br) Vera Demarchi Aiello (anpvera@incor.usp.br) The laboratory assessment disclosed levels of urea: e68 mg/dl, creatinine: 1.2 mg/dl, glycemia: 189 mg/dl and creatine kinase: 42 U/l. A diagnosis of ongoing myocardial infarction was achieved and TNK-tPA (Tecneplase a genetically modified form of t-PA, with increased PAI-1 resistance, increased fibrin specificity and longer half-life) ficiwas administered as bolus of 40 mg with non-fractionated heparin as bolus of 6,060 UI/kg, followed by 1,000 UI/h IV. A residual retrosternal pain persisted.
