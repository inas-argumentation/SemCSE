Plasma catecholamines and their major metabolites in spontaneously hypertensive rats.
Abstract The function of the sympathetic nervous system was evaluated in spontaneously hypertensive rats (SHR) and their Wistar Kyoto (WK) control rats by measuring plasma concentrations of catecholamines and their major metabolic products from both the 0-methylated and the deaminated metabolic pathways. By using specific and sensitive radioenzymatic assays plasma concentrations of norepinephrine (NE), epinephrine (E), normetanephrine (NMN), dihydroxyphenylglycol (DOPEG) and dihydroxymandelic acid (DOMA) were determined in blood specimens obtained by venopuncture of tail vein of 12 adult male SHR and 12 age and sex matched WK rats.
