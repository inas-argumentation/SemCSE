Stimulation of hepatic β-hydroxy-β-methylglutaryl coenzyme a reductase activity in allylamine-fed rats
Abstract Rats fed a diet containing 0.2% allylamine hydrochloride had a 5-fold higher level of hepatic β-hydroxy-β-methylglutaryl coenzyme A reductase activity than corresponding controls. Supplementation of the allylamine-containing diet with 10% egg yolk reduced this elevated level of reductase activity. In contrast to the marked rise in their liver enzyme activity, allylamine-fed rats had no increase in serum cholesterol levels.
