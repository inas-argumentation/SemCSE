Mammographic screening of women aged 40–49 years. Benefit, risk, and cost considerations
A recently published meta‐analysis of seven randomized trials showed a statistically significant 24% reduction in mortality rate with the use of mammographic screening for women age 40–49 years. The benefit would have been even greater if screening had been performed at shorter annual intervals with the most up‐to‐date mammographic equipment. Additionally, these trials underestimated benefit due to inclusion of breast cancer deaths among study group women who refused to undergo screening.
