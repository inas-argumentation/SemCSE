Deep vein thrombosis after total hip replacement: A clinical and phlebographic study
Two hundred and forty‐seven phlebograms (113 bilateral and 21 unilateral) were performed in 134 patients 10–14 days after total hip replacement. Fifty‐eight per cent of the patients were found to have deep vein thrombosis. The patients with DVT were significantly older than patients without DVT, but there was no difference regarding sex, type of hip prosthesis, side of operation or day of mobilization. No difference was found in the duration of operation and anaesthesia, the operative haemorrhage and the amount of bank blood transfused in patients with and without DVT.
