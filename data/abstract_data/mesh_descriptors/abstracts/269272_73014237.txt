Atrial natriuretic peptide in human essential hypertension.
We measured the circulating levels of atrial natriuretic peptide (ANP) in 62 patients with untreated uncomplicated essential hypertension and in 30 normotensive subjects. In the hypertensive patients, mean systolic and diastolic blood pressures were 148 and 101 mm Hg, respectively, and the mean heart rate was 73 beats/min.
