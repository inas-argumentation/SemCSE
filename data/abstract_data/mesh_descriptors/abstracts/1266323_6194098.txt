APPLICATIONS OF DYE‐SENSITIZED PHOTOREACTIONS IN NEUROBIOLOGY
Dye-sensitized photoreactions have been used for many purposes in the biomedical sciences (Spikes, 1985, 1989). During the past few years, a number of exciting applications of these photoreactions to various areas of neurobiology have been developed. This review briefly surveys the literature on dyesensitized photoeffects in neurobiology in order to illustrate the scope of this rapidly developing field.
