Somatostatin inhibition and reversal of parathyroid hormone-, calcium-, and acetylcholine-induced gastrin release in the pig.
The ability of somatostatin (somatotropin release-inhibiting factor or SRIF) to inhibit or reverse an increased release of gastrin induced by infusion of CaCl2, parathyroid hormone (PTH), and acetylcholine (Ach) was examined in 21 young anesthetized pigs, weighing 15–25 kg. Test substances were administered (0.05–0.1 ml/min for 20 min) directly into the antral circulation via a catheter in the right gastroepiploic artery. Samples of venous blood draining the antrum were collected from a catheter in the right gastroepiploic vein, and gastrin was measured by RIA.
