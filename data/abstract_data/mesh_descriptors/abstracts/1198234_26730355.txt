A suggested method for selecting patients for clinical trails of new radiotherapeutic techniques.
While radiobiological observation has been the basis for the clinical trial of certain new radiotherapeutic techniques, knowledge is not available on which to select patients for whom these techniques are most likely to be advantageous. There are no specific diagnostic tests on which to base judgements as to the probability that radiation will fail in local control because of the persistent presence of hypoxic cells, or because of a large wastage per fraction of radiation dose attributable to the sublethal damage that tumour cells will shed between dose fractions.
