Purification and reconstitution of the cytochrome o-type oxidase from Escherichia coli.
Publisher Summary This chapter describes the purification of the cytochrome o -type oxidase from the membrane of E. coli and its functional reconstitution into proteoliposomes.The chapter describes a procedure for the simultaneous reconstitution of the o -type oxidase and lac permease into proteoliposomes. The cytochrome d -deficient mutant of E. coli is useful for the purification of cytochrome o , as membranes from the mutant contain only cytochrome o as a terminal oxidase. As for lac permease, sequential extraction of E. coli membranes with high concentrations of urea and cholate provides significant in situ purification of the o -type oxidase. Proteoliposomes accumulate lactose against a concentration gradient during oxidase turnover, and the phenomenon is abolished by addition of valinomycin and nigericin or by cyanide. Thus, proteoliposomes containing cytochrome o and the lac permease catalyze the active transport of lactose in a fashion that closely resembles intact cells and right-side-out membrane vesicles.
