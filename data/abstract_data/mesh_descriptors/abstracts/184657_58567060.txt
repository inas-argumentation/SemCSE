An unusual finding after surgical treatment of corrosive oesophageal stricture.
QUESTION Two patients, a 28-year-old male and a 40-year-old female, presented with difficulty in swallowing solid food, 6 months following surgery for corrosive injury to oesophagus. Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy findings revealed a narrowing just below the cricopharynx with the inability to negotiate the scope beyond the stricture. Under fluoroscopic guidance, attempted passage of guide wire through the opening resulted in it traversing only up to the mid-oesophagus.
