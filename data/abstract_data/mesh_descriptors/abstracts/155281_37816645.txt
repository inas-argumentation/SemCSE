[Adenoma of the liver and focal nodular hyperplasia. Relationship with oral contraceptives (author's transl)].
The authors analyze recent publications concerning adenoma of the liver and focal nodular hyperplasia. 260 cases of liver adenoma have been reported; this benign tumor is more frequent in women between 26-30 and it is usually revealed by intraperitoneal hemorrhage. Focal nodular hyperplasia 320 cases of which have been reported is another benign tumor usually asymptomatic and with anatomical characteristics similar to those of liver adenoma. Of all the cases reported 147 were related to oral contraception (OC). It seems that the frequency of benign liver tumors augments with the duration of OC treatment. The pathogenesis of such tumors is still unknown.
