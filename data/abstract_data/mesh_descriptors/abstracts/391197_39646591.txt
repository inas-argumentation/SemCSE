Variation of strontium-calcium observed ratio (urine-diet) in man.
The amounts of fallout sOSr and stable Ca in the diets and urine of several volunteers were determined for about 90 days and the strontium-calcium Observed Ratio (urine/diet) was calculated from the data. The OR values obtained are interpreted as a function of the ratio of urinary Ca to dietary Ca. The OR values obtained for the present Japanese volunteers (0.2-0.4) were less than one half of American and European values (0.75-l.G). It is suggested that the level of dietary Ca plays an important role in determining the OR values.
