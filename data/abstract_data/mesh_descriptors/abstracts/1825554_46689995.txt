[The diagnosis and treatment of blunt renal trauma].
225 patient with blunt renal trauma were treated from 1955 to 1994. Coexistent injuries of other organs were noted in 79 patients (35.11%). The extent of renal trauma was not demonstrated by hematuria. B-ultrasonography seemed to be best for the diagnosis of renal trauma. Abdominal incision was helpful to explore injuries of other organs and manage renal trauma. Early treatment of renal trauma was necessary for the treatment of shock and prevention of serious complication.
