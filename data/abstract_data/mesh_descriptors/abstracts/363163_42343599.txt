A case of contrecoup skull fracture caused by mild head injury

Contrecoup kull fractures are not rare in fatal traumatic brain injuries due to alls, and mainly occur in the frontal fossa with occipital impact oint of the head [3]. The orbital roofs and ethmoid areas are ommon sites involved in the fracture inwhich the bone is transluently thin [3].
