Trends in Interpolated Flap Repairs on the Nose, Eyelids, Ears, and Lips After Skin Cancer Excision in the United States From 2007 to 2016.
This work was presented at the 2019 American College of Mohs Surgery Annual Conference in Baltimore, Maryland. Dermatologists perform most skin cancer excisions and reconstructive surgery for the Medicare population. However, interpolated flap closures were not included or were grouped with other local flaps in analyses of Medicare data, and trends outside the Medicare population have not been described. This study sought (1) to characterize what specialties are performing interpolated flaps in cosmetically sensitive areas (nose, eyelids, ears, and lips) after skin cancer resection and (2) to describe the trends over time. A repeated cross-sectional analysis from 2007 to 2016 was performed using Optum Clinformatics DataMart deidentified commercial claims to evaluate specialties providing interpolated flap repairs. The Optum database contains deidentified paid claims data for approximately 12 to 14 million annually insured patients in the United States that are similar to the demographics of the United States population with respect to sex, age, and geographic distribution. Basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, and melanoma skin cancer cases were identified by International Classification of Diseases 9 and 10th editions and linked to Current Procedural Terminology codes for interpolated flap repair (15,731 and 15,576). Claims with unknown provider specialty, unclear individual specialty due to surgical center or hospital facility designation, or specialties that performed less than 1% of the total number of interpolated flap repairs for a given year were excluded from analysis. Descriptive analysis was used to summarize the breakdown of specialties and trends over time. There were 4,368 interpolated flap repairs performed from 2007 to 2016. Utilization of interpolated flaps has increased over time with dermatologists performing 36.3% of all interpolated flaps during the analyzed period.
