CA 72‐4 levels in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus
Objective:  CA 72‐4 is one of the blood group carbohydrate antigens which can be used as a tumour marker in ovarian, pancreatic and gastrointestinal carcinomas. It can also be elevated in various benign conditions including pancreatitis. Diabetes mellitus is a chronic disorder related with the pancreas. In this study, we investigated CA 72‐4 levels in patients with type 2 diabetes and its relation to the metabolic status.
