Neuroprotective role of curcumin from Curcuma longa on ethanol‐induced brain damage
In the present study, curcumin from Curcuma longa was screened for neuroprotective activity using ethanol as a model of brain injury. Oral administration of curcumin to rats caused a significant reversal in lipid peroxidation, brain lipids and produced enhancement of glutathione, a non‐enzymic antioxidant in ethanol intoxicated rats, revealing that the antioxidative and hypolipidaemic action of curcumin isresponsible for its protective role against ethanol induced brain injury.
