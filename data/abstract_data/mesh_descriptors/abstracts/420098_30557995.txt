Rape on trial in South Africa
In the most politically explosive trial since the end of apartheid Jacob Zuma the man once destined to be the next president of South Africa stands trial March 6 on rape charges. Zuma who was dismissed last year as national deputy president under a cloud of corruption but remains deputy president of the ruling African National Congress (ANC) is accused of raping a longstanding family acquaintance and well-known HIV-positive AIDS activist at his home last November. The case has thrown the spotlight on sexual violence in South Africa which has among the highest murder rape and HIV infection rates in the world. In a separate lower profile case a man was jailed for life on Feb 16 for raping an AIDS activist Lorna Mlosana and beating her to death after she told him she was HIV positive. (excerpt)
