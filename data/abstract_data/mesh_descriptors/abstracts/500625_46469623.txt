Progress in the 1980s and New Directions in the 1990s with Hypertension Management
When arterial hypertension was considered as a borderline entity associated with the physiology of aging, and with a pathology with unknown associated risks, treatment of the disease was not a major concern. During the first third of the twentieth century, arterial hypertension did not even appear as a defined disease in the classical books of medicine (Page 1988)
