Nanoplatforms with Remarkably Enhanced Absorption in the Second Biological Window for Effective Tumor Thermo-Radiotherapy.
Thermo-radiotherapy acts as an important antitumor modality because heating can increase the blood flow and improve the oxygen level in tumor, thus remission of hypoxia-associated resistance for radiotherapy (RT). However, most agents for thermo-radiotherapy are performed either in the first near-infrared biological window or low photothermal conversion efficiency. Here, a facile method to prepare the CuxS/Au nanocomposites via reduction methods from CuxS templates in mild synthetic conditions (i.e. aqueous solution and room temperature) is presented. After the growth of Au nanoparticles, the CuxS/Au nanocomposites have greater benefits for photothermal efficiency than that of CuxS nanoparticles due to the enhanced absorbance in the second near-infrared window. Moreover, biocompatibility and stability of these nanocomposites are greatly improved by lipoic acid polyethylene glycol. After tumors irradiated with a 1064 nm laser, oxygenation status of tumor are subsequently improved and the combination of PTT and RT achieves remarkable synergistic therapeutic effects. This work provides a novel idea to design a new generation nanomedicine for the tumor thermo-radiotherapy.
