Expression of monoclonal antibody Q113 immunoreactivity in the rat cerebral cortex: Unique differential sublayering of layer I. Staining of radial glia
Monoclonal antibody mabQ113 recognizes a 120‐kilodalton polypeptide which, in the cerebellar cortex, is confined exclusively to a subset of Purkinje cells which are organized in parasagittal bands (Hawkes et al.: Brain Research 333:359–365, 1985). In all other areas of the adult rat brain examined the localization of the mabQ113 epitope was marked by regional neuronal and glial coexpression (Plioplys and Hawkes: Brain Research 375:1–12, 1986).
