Properties of Rat Liver Alkaline Phosphatase Before and After Bile Duct Ligation
Summary Bile-duct ligation causes a 5-10-fold increase in rat-liver alkaline phosphatase activity. The alkaline phosphatase in bile-duct-ligated rat liver is identical to electrophoretic mobility on polyacrylamide gel, rate of sedimentation in a sucrose gradient, rates of heat and urea denaturation, pH optima, and Michaelis constants. Since the “induced” and base-level enzyme are identical by all criteria studied, it is unlikely that the increase in alkaline phosphatase activity is due to activation of a preformed enzyme. Enzyme induction appears a more likely mechanism.
