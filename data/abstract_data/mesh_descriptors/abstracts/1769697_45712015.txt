Dissociated effects of tamoxifen and oestradiol-17 beta on uterus and liver functions.
The effects of Tamoxifen (100 μ/day) and oestradiol (1 μ/day) administration for 7 days, on the uterine weight, protein, DNA and progesterone receptor content as well as on the plasma renin substrate were studied in prepubertal castrated rats. As expected from previous studies, after 2 days, Tamoxifen induced a partial response in uterine weight, protein and DNA content, significantly lower than the stimulation induced by oestradiol. The progesterone receptor content of the entire uterus increased for 7 days and was higher after oestradiol than after Tamoxifen treatment. However, on a per weight, per mg of protein and DNA basis, the progesterone receptor concentration was increased similarly by Tamoxifen and oestradiol. After 7 days, the plasma renin substrate was significantly increased after Tamoxifen treatment when compared with untreated rats but no significant increase was observed after oestradiol treatment.
