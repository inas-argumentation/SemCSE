Myocardial infarction caused by compression of the left coronary artery by an aortic pseudoaneurysm
Aortic pseudoaneurysm is most commonly seen as a complication of ascending aorta replacement, appearing several years after the operation (1). Pseudoaneurysm after cardiac catheterization, on the other hand, is very unusual. We present a rare case of successful management of a myocardial infarction caused by aortic pseudoaneurysm more than 50 years after surgical closure of a ventricular septal defect.
