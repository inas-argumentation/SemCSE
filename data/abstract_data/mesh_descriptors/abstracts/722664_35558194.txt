RRESULTS:Blood flow could be successfully restored in 616 (72.1%) patients. Recanalization of chronic coronary occlusion failed in 238 (27.9%) patients. CCONCLUSION:Recanalization of chronic coronary occlusions is a highly effective and relatively safe technique. The efficiency of the procedure largely depends on the duration of occlusion, its X-ray morphological characteristics, and the experience of a physician.
