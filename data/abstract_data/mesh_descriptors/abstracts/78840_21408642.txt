Smears reported as code 6 – glandular abnormality – are associated with a high probability of a clinically significant lesion
Dear Editor, Kirwan et al. reported positive predictive values (PPVs) for a significant histological abnormality following a result code 6 smear (glandular abnormality). These were 80.6% following a smear reported as cervical glandular intra-epithelial neoplasia, and 86.1% following a smear suggestive of endometrial adenocarcinoma. This study was carried out retrospectively on cases from 114 women over a 5-year period. Here we report on a similar study carried out at York Hospital that looked retrospectively at cases from 100 women whose smears were reported as code 6 between 1996 and 2004.
