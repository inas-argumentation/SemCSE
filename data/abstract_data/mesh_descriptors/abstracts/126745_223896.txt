Metastatic breast carcinoma presenting as acute liver failure and portal hypertension
Metastatic carcinoma is a rare but important cause of acutehepatic failure. We report the case of a relatively youngpatient who presented with the previously undescribed com-bination of acute hepatic failure and variceal bleeding due tointrasinusoidal hepatic inﬁltration from metastatic breastcarcinoma. As breast carcinoma is a common disease (1),this potential disease manifestation warrants note.
