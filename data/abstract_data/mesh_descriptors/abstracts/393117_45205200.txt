One Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP) is a firm, stony nodule which infiltrates the skin and subcutaneous tissue. It grows locally for many years and very rarely metastasizes, but it has a strong tendency to recur after removal. Histopathologically, it is essentially a welldifferentiated fibrosarcoma. The origin of DFSP is not clear, and some authors regard the tumor cells as fibroblasts (1, 2) or histiocytes (3). We found incidentally that DFSP tissues had a higher lactate dehydrogenase activity than normal skin. To our knowledge, no systemic lists of enzyme activities in DFSP tissue have been published. We therefore measured the activities of several enzymes in the cytosol, mitochondria and peroxisomes of the tumor tissue of DFSP, the normal skin from a DFSP patient, and three normal skin samples obtained from surgical specimens. The enzyme activities of cultured cells originating from DFSP were also compared with those of normal fibroblasts. As shown in Table 1, in the DFSP tissue the activities of the enzymes (per mg protein) involved in glycolysis were 3 to 8 times higher than those of the normal skin, except for glucokinase, glucosephosphate isomerase, and 6phosphofructokinase.
