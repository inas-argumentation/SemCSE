Nodular bleeding lesion on the sole of an elderly patient.
A man, 84 years of age, presented to the dermatology clinic with a progressively growing and occasionally bleeding lesion on the sole of his left foot. Of note, there was no history of trauma and he was otherwise healthy. The patient did not remember when the lesion first appeared and was only aware of the lesion when it began to bleed approximately two months before the consultation. On examination, a purplish nodule with clear-cut borders and central ulceration was seen (Figure 1).
