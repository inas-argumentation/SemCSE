Milk granuloma: an unusual appearance in lactational breast tissue
Biopsy of the breast is infrequently performed during pregnancy or lactation. However, some lesions. including abscess, lactating adenoma, infarction of hyperplastic tissue and galactocoele’ , occur specifically during this period. We describe four lesions developing in women during lactation or immediately thereafter, in which the pathological appearances were unusual, seemingly resulting from leakage of milk into the mammary stroma.
