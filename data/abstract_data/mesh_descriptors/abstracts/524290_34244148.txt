Bone loss after cardiac transplantation.
Although clinicians generally consider osteoporosis to be a disease primarily affecting postmenopausal women, other populations are also at high risk. One such group is recipients of organ transplants, who, as their long-term survival improves, have begun to face other medical problems, including osteoporosis. Transplant recipients have generally had long-standing, chronic disease and must receive high doses of antirejection medications designed to suppress their immune system.
