Bullous pemphigoid induced by pembrolizumab in a patient with advanced melanoma expressing collagen XVII
Figure 1. (a) Primary lesion of black nodule on the left cheek. (b) Recurrent skin metastases appeared in the left cheek. (c) Regressed metastatic lesion with vitiligo due to pembrolizumab. (d) Nests of atypical melanocytes with large nuclei and melanin pigments in the sentinel lymph node (Hematoxylin-eosin, stain, original magnification 9400). (e) Tumor cells in the lymph node were positive for collagen XVII (anti-collagen XVII Ab, stain, 9400). (f) Bullae and erosions on the trunk developed after 17 cycles of pembrolizumab. (g) Subepidermal blister with infiltration of eosinophils (Hematoxylin-eosin, stain, 940).
