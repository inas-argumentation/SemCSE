Isosorbide dinitrate inhibits platelet adhesion and aggregation in nonthrombolyzed patients with acute myocardial infarction
Background: Apart from their vasodilatator properties, nitrates have been shown to inhibit platelet aggregation. The effects of nitrates on platelet adhesion have not been studied. Nonselected patients with acute myocardial infarction (AMI) have been suggested to gain no benefit from administration of nitrates. However, the importance of nitrates may be greater in a subgroup of nonthrombolyzed patients with AMI.
