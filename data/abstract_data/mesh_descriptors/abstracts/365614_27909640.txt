[Radiotherapy for Japanese elderly patients].
The purpose of this manuscript is to examine the survival outcomes of elderly patients with cancer treated with radiotherapy(RT). Ninety-seven cancer patients>/=80-years-old who were treated with RT were evaluated. The 2-year overall survival for 64 patients who received RT with curative or semi-radical intent was 75%. Meanwhile, the 1-year overall survival for 33 patients who received RT with palliative intent was 25%. RT for elderly patients with cancer is generally effective and safe.
