Invasion of Esophageal Leiomyoma by Coexistent Overlying Squamous Cell Carcinoma
IntroductionCoexistence of leiomyoma and squamous cell carcinoma of esophagus has rarely been reported.Case ReportHerein, we report a rare case of multiple esophageal leiomyoma, which has been invaded by overlying squamous cell carcinoma.
