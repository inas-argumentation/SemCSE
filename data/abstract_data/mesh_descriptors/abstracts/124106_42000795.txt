Hysteria and its historiography: the future perspective
Within the history of science and medicine, perhaps no field of enquiry is expanding more energetically today than psychiatric history. More books and articles are appearing on psychiatry’s famous, fascinating past all the time. Judging from academic dissertations either recently completed or in preparation, a generation of young scholars from a range of countries and disciplinary backgrounds is entering the field. Two new collections of reprinted primary texts Tavistock Classics in the History of Psychiatry and Classics in the History of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences promise to bring to prominence a series of important writings of past psychiatric figures.
