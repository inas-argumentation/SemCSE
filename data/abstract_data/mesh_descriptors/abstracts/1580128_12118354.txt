[Behavior of maximal tubular re-resorption for glucose after N1-sulfanilyl-N2-n-butylcarbamide administration in the dog].
The intravenously administration of daily 50 mg BZ 55 through 10 to 45 days produced a marked depression of the glucose TM in normal dogs, compared with the glucose TM of the same animals which have been estimated before the beginning of the BZ 55-administration.
