Urea biosynthesis in the South American lungfish, Lepidosiren paradoxa: relation to its ecology.
Abstract 1. 1. Rates of hepatic urea biosynthesis, as measured by the incorporation of 14 C-bicarbonate into urea, and activities of the separate ornithineurea cycle enzymes in the South American lungfish, Lepidosiren , are intermediate between corresponding values previously determined in the African lungfish, Protopterus , and in the Australian lungfish, Neoceratodus . 2. 2. These findings are consistent with the semi-aquatic estivating condition of the South American form which is intermediate between the dry cocoon-like state of Protopterus and the completely aquatic nature of Neoceratodus .
