Structure‐Activity Relationships for the Ca2+‐releasing Activity of 6‐Hydroxy‐β‐carboline Analogues in Skeletal Muscle Sarcoplasmic Reticulum—The Effects of Halogen Substitution at C‐5 and C‐7
This study of structure‐activity relationships of 6‐hydroxy‐β‐carboline analogues has been performed on the basis of quantitative measurement of Ca2+‐releasing activity in the sarcoplasmic reticulum of skinned fibres of skeletal muscle.
