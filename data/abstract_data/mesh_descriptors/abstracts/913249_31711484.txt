[Ethinyl estradiol and cyclophosphamide in progressive metastasizing breast carcinoma].
A study of the therapeutic effectiveness of estrogens with cyclophosphamide and of estrogens alone in 74 patients with metastasizing breast cancer is reported. Average survival of patients treated with estrogens alone was 12.8 months while that of patients treated with estrogens and cyclophosphamide was 16.5 months. (These figures include patients who died during the study as well as patients still alive at the cut-off date.) Skin lympth node and bone metastases seemed to respond more favorably than liver metastases to treatment. It was concluded that combined estrogen-cyclophosphamide treatment is preferable to treatment with estrogens alone.
