Blood cells of newborn calves were more enriched in 15N and 13C than that of their mothers, indicating the deposition of fetal protein from maternal stores. To quantify pathways of N flow in reindeer, N balance was measured by confining animals to cages for 10 d at 4 wk from parturition. N balance was inversely related to 15N/14N in urea‐N but not related to 15N/14N of blood cells, creatinine, and feces.
