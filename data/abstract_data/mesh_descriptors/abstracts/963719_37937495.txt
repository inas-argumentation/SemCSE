Plasma homocysteine and cardiovascular disease mortality
Homocysteine (Hcy) is a sulphur-containing aminoacid which accumulates in tissues in the inherited genetic disorder homocystinuria as a result of cystathionine beta-synthase (CBS) deficiency. Individuals with this condition are susceptible to early vascular disease and thromboembolism. Plasma Hcy is considered to be an independent risk factor for cardiovascular disease (CVD) . I Differing mean plasma Hcy values for healthy people from various countries have been reported, ranging from 6 pmol/L in Japan to 13 pmol/L in South Africa.' These differences may be due to the use of different analytical techniques or may be real differences between populations. T o investigate differences in plasma total Hcy values between populations and any possible association with CVD mortality we determined the total plasma Hcy concentrations of randomly chosen healthy men from 11 countries in which the age-standardised mortality (ICD 390-458 in 1985; males 30-69 years) varies about three-fold according to WHO data.'
