Studies on the exocrino-enteric circulation of Mus musculus.
1. 1. The rate of hepatic secretion of intestinally absorbed L -methionine-methyl- 14 C-saline is studied in normally functioning and pathologically altered Mus musculus . 2. 2. Time analyses of 14 C radiation in the peripheral blood demonstrate, vivo , the existence of a steady-state system between host and parasite. 3. 3. The effect of parasitism on host physiology is clearly demonstrated by different products of methionine metabolism occurring in the hepatic secretions of parasitized and non-parasitized M. musculus .
