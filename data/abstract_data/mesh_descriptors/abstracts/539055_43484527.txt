Affordable hypertension therapy for diabetic patients.
Type 2 diabetes is one of the most expensive chronic diseases to treat, and in South Africa, with prescribed minimum benefits (PMB) applying to this disease, both private and public-sector funders are on the alert for cost-benefit issues. Perhaps one of the cost-benefit issues funders have missed is the current and future savings that can be made with effective anti-hypertension therapy from the outset at diabetes diagnosis.
