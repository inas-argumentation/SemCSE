Interestingly, a significant increase in the number of mast cells has been found around EPF hair follicles, which suggests an important role of mast cells in the pathogenesis of EPF. We speculate that topical pimecrolimus acts against EPF through not only T-cell blockade, but also the inhibition of mast cells. In terms of the topical treatment of EPF, pimecrolimus may provide an advantage over tacrolimus in that its higher lipophilicity (20 times more lipophilic than tacrolimus) allows pimecrolimus to have a higher affinity for hair follicles.
