Update: Collaborative Efforts between AAOHN and the National Cancer Institute
As a part of the collaborative relationship established between AAOHN and the National Cancer Institute (NCI), an invitational working session was held April 12and 13,1984, at the NCI in Bethesda, Maryland. Veronica L. Conley, PhD, of NCI, was instrumental in initiating and facilitating this activity. The purpose of the day-and-a-half workshop was to identify and discuss issues and current activities related to primary cancer preventionat the worksite.
