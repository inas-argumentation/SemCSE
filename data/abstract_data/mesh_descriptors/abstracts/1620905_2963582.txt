Developmental changes in cyclo‐oxygenase mRNA induction by hypoxia in rat kidney
Background : Prostaglandins, synthesized by cyclo‐oxygenase (COX), regulate renal hemodynamics and also epithelial water and solute transport. Developmental changes occur in COX‐2 mRNA expression and its response to lipopolysaccharide stimulation in rats. We examined age‐related changes in COX mRNA expression induced by hypoxia in the renal cortex and medulla of developing rats.
