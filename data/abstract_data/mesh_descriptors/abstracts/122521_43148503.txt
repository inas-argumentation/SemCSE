Retinoic acid syndrome: a potentially fatal side effect of retinoic acid therapy.
The management of acute promyelocytic leukemia (APL) has changed over the past few years. The discoveiy of specific molecular abnormality (PML/RAR-cz gene product) has raised considerable interest in the management of the disorder with All transretinoic acid (ATRA). The compound is now being increasingly used for the remission-induction in APL. However, the drug is not free of side effects. We report a potentially fatal side effect of the drug in two patients.
