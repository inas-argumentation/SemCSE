Is it safe to prescribe digoxin after acute myocardial infarction? Update on continued controversy.
The findings of recent clinical trials confirm the efficacy of digoxin in the t reatment of patients with heart failure. 1' 2 However, the results of these trials did not resolve the long-standing controversy concerning the safety of this drug in patients who have had an acute myocardial infarction. 3-7 Previous clinical observations have suggested that digoxin might increase mortality among survivors of acute myocardial infarction, whereas other observations revealed no association between digoxin use and increased mortality risk in these patients. Limitations of the analytic methods used in these studies prevent clinicians and investigators from reaching definite conclusions. 3 Thus the problem of digoxin safety after acute myocardial infarction remains unresolved. The purpose of this commentary is to review the available information on the effects of digoxin on the survival of patients after acute myocardial infarction and to discuss problems that arise when considering a digoxin prescription for these patients.
