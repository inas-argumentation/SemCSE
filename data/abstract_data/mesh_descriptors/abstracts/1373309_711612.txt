Esophageal motility disorders occurring after sclerotherapy for varices is well documented though the pathogenesis is poorly understood (2– 4). Low-amplitude contractions, simultaneous contractions, and nonprogressive contractions are the most frequently observed. Literature is unclear whether these abnormalities occur or persist several months after sclerotherapy. Similarly, therapeutic interventions are ill defined.
