Circular Lipectomy with Lateral Thigh-Buttock Lift
Patients with body contour deformities are among the challenges that plastic surgeons most frequently face. These body contour deformities vary from patient to patient in number, intensity, and anatomical location depending on racial and genetic factors, and these are worsened with overweight and even more with obesity. These patients usually have tried different weightreduction methods, including diets, reductive massages, amphetamines, exercise, etc. Nevertheless, the majority do not succeed and they fall into a vicious cycle that only increases the problem.
