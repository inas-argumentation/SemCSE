Isolation of bile ducts from rat liver: technique and preliminary ultrastructural characterization.
Abstract A technique is described for isolation from the liver of a fraction rich in bile ducts. Ultrastructurally the isolated bile ducts showed good overall cellular preservation. The staining of the isolated bile ducts with the ruthenium red procedure clearly demonstrates the surface coat on the luminal surface of biliary epithelial cells and the intracellular microfilaments. The technology presented in this paper should make possible a new approach to the investigation of the hepotobiliary system.
