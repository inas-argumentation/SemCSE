Prevention of various forms of metabolic myocardial necrosis by catatoxic steroids.
In the rat, it has been possible to prevent the production of various forms of nonocclusive experimental cardiac necroses by prophylactic treatment with certain steroid compounds. The models used were Electrolyte Steroid Cardiopathies with Necrosis elicited by fluorocortisol or digitoxin in combination with sodium phosphate, sodium perchlorate, excess fat intake or the stress of restraint. The production of fatal myocardial necrosis by various combinations of these agents could be most effectively inhibited by those steroids which had previously been shown to possess actions which counteracts the toxic effects of various other types of injurious agents.
