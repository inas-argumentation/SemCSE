What's new in drugs.
Revisions in the labeling of products containing estrogen has been ordered by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA). Links have been reported between the prolonged use of estrogen by postmenopausal women and the development of endometrial cancer. The warning on the label of estrogens will advise physicians to limit the doses prescribed to be the smallest effective and for the shortest possible time. Women will be warned to stop taking estrogen products if they become pregnant. The risk of developing nonmalignant but dangerous liver tumors is increased by taking estrogens. Doctors will be warned by the literature that patients taking estrogens run twice as great a risk of developing gallbladder disease as other patients. The FDA advises women over 40 years of age to use a method other than the pill for birth control. Whether or not the benefits of estrogens outweigh their risks must be determined for each patient individually but women should know all the risks.
