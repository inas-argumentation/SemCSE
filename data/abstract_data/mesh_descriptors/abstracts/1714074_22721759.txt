Molecular databases on the internet
With current research in molecular medicine or biology, it is really as simple as this: you turn on your computer, double-click on your favorite browser’s icon, and there it is: a wealth of information at your fingertips, waiting for you to make sense of it in the light of your most recent experimental findings. The central piece in your current puzzle may be only one or two (or, to be honest, several) mouse-clicks away! And that’s where the trouble usually starts... What follows is meant to be an introduction to molecular databases on the internet for those readers who feel their work could benefit from efficient use of the publicly available data on the internet, but who have, for one reason or another, not yet mustered the courage to get entangled in “the web”. In order not to bother you with clumsy internet addresses throughout the text, we have compiled a list of addresses of the sites mentioned below (Table 1). These links can be accessed by pointing your browser to http://genome.dkfz-heidelberg.de/ biounit/ moldb_links.html. This list of course does not claim to be complete. However, we suggest you use it as a starting point for your own explorations. We are convinced that several of the given addresses will be found among your “bookmarks” soon.
