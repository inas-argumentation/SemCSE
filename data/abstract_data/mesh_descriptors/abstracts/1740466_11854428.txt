Reconstruction of a Lower Conchal Bowl Defect.
An 81-year-old man presented for treatment of a basal cell carcinoma of the right lower conchal cavum, located at the base of the external auditory meatus (Figure 1). The lesion had been present for more than 6 months. At presentation, it measured 0.6 · 0.5 cm. Mohs micrographic surgery was performed by the surgeon (S.Q.W.). The tumor was removed after 2 stages of Mohs excision. The resulting defect measured 1.7 · 1.5 cm with exposed cartilage and small areas of perichondrium. How would you reconstruct this defect?
