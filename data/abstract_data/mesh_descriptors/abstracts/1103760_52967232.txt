Building blocks of the human brain
Area-specific excitatory neuron subtypes emerge before sensory experience Classical computer software programs perform tasks in series: One algorithm has to be performed, and the output of that algorithm is used as an input into another. However, the most powerful computers in the world take advantage of the fact that complex computational tasks can be broken down into independent components. Distributing the computation over many parallel processes taking place simultaneously rapidly increases the overall speed. This paradigm is very similar to how the human brain performs its complex functions.
