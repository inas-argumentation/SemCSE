In stress-sensitive August rats, doses of DSP induce local increases in monoamine oxidase (MAO, substrate: serotonin) activity in the basal ganglia (caudate nucleus and nucleus accumbens [7]. The regulatory effect of DSP on CNS functions is associated with its actions on the state of the serotoninergic system. In particular, in conditions of dopaminergic system hyperactivity induced by doses of L-dihydroxyphenylalanine (L-DOPA), the effects of DSP appear to result from its actions on serotonin metabolism [2]. However, it is not known how DSP would affect the brain in August rats, with genetically lower levels of this peptide (as compared with Wistar rats), on a background of administration of L-DOPA to induce a stress state in the animals. In experimentally induced hyperactivity of the dopaminergic system, animals display psychomotor excitation and afferent overload of the basal ganglia [3]. In stress-sensitive August rats, this state correlates with more marked morphochemical rearrangements in the brain than in the more resistant Wistar rats [6]. The aim of the present work was to conduct quantitative cytochemical studies of the effects of DSP on a number of August rat brain structures involved in forming and performing purposive behavior in conditions of L-DOPA administration.
