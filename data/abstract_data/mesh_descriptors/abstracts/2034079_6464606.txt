Macroporous high-performance anion-exchange supports for proteins.
Silicas of 1000 and 4000 A pore diameter coated with heavy layers of polyethylenimines and crosslinked with 1,4-butanediol diglycidyl ether show enhanced anion-exchange capacity and resolution for proteins as well as small molecules. The influence of the polyamine (1) molecular size, (2) concentration in the coating solution, and (3) density on the surface of the silica particles on ion-exchange capacity, resolution, retention, and mass transfer kinetics of proteins and small molecules has been investigated.
