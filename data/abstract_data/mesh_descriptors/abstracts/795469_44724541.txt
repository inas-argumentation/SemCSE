Model for end‐stage liver disease (MELD) exception for uncommon hepatic tumors
Liver transplantation has been performed for a widevariety of rare hepatic tumors. The anecdotal nature ofthis experience makes evidence-based decisions re-garding suitability for transplantation very challenging.The largest reports of transplantation for tumors otherthan hepatocellular carcinoma and cholangiocarci-noma have been for neuroendocrine tumors. Hepaticmetastases are present in approximately 75% of pa-tients at the time a neuroendocrine tumor is diagnosed,and cure by liver transplantation (LT) is rare.
