Correlation of Renal Size, Renal Artery Calibre and Effective Renal Plasma Flow in Man
Stenosis of the renal arteries has been a subject of intense physiologic and clinical investigation. Changes in the renal arterial calibre may however occur without stenosis. Dilatation of these arteries may be observed for in­ stance after contralateral nephrectomy, whereas narrowing is often evident in severe renal parenchymal diseases. An attempt has been made to find out whether a correlation exists between the size of the kidneys, renal arterial calibre and renal blood flow, as well as whether the observation that in pyelonephritis the renal arteries are especially narrow in relation to renal size is correct, at least as long as the kidneys have not yet shrunk markedly.
