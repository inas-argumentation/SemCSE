Breast carcinoma metastatic to the bilateral orbits
An 83-year-old woman with recently diagnosed breast cancer presented with progressive injection and globe dystopia of the left eye for 10 days (Figure 1a). Ocular rotations were diminished bilaterally, left greater than right. CT revealed bilaterally enlarged rectus muscles and a poorly circumscribed left inferior intraconal mass (Figure 1b). PET-CT disclosed hypermetabolic activity corresponding to the orbital pathology (Figure1c). Histopathology from left orbital biopsy showed sheets of poorly differentiated malignant cells (Figure 1d) that stained with CK7 and GATA3 confirming metastatic breast carcinoma to both orbits.
