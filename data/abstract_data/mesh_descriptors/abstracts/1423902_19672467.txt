Differences in effectiveness of combined treatments with ionizing radiation and vinblastine, evaluated for experimental sarcomas and squamous cell carcinomas in rats.
Two types of sarcomas, a rhabdomyosarcoma R-1 and a osteosarcoma ROS-1, and two types of carcinomas, a skin squamous cell carcinoma RSC-1 and a ureter squamous cell carcinoma RUC-1, transplantable In rats, have been compared with respect to the effectiveness of combined treatments for the induction of growth delay. The administration of a dose of 1.5 mg of vinblastine per kg rat body weight before irradiation with a dose of 1000 or 2000 rad of X-rays did not alter the growth delay induced by radiation alone. Combined treatments in which vinblastine was given up to 12 days after irradiation yielded an enhanced effect for the R-1 sarcoma and to a lesser extent for the ROS-1 sarcoma, but not for the RSC-1 and the RUC-1 carcinomas. This difference might be related to the influence of radiation induced changes in cell proliferation kinetics.
