Loss of heterozygosity on chromosome 2 in Japanese patients with prostate cancer
Loss of heterozygosity (LOH) on chromosome 2 is thought to occur only occasionally in prostate cancer (PCa), but allelic losses in this region are frequent in other types of human cancer, such as lung, thyroid, head and neck, and cervix. Here, we show a high‐resolution deletion map of markers on chromosome 2 in Japanese patients with PCa.
