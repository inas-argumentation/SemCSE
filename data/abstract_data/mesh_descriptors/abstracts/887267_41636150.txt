Isolation of E. coli R factor DNA by molecular sieve chromatography in 4 M guanidine hydrochloride
As reported previously [l-4] the R factor DNA from E. coli K12 may be isolated in a single circular form having molecular weight of approx. 55-65 X lo6 daltons. It is however difficult to separate because of its presence in low proportion (about 1%) [5] and its large molecular size which make it sensitive to nicking t61. Previous work from our laboratory has indicated that bacterial particles can be solubilized in 4 M guanidine hydrochloride [7]. In this study 4 M GHCl has been used for lysing the E. coli K12 mutant HK26 Rl and parent R-strains followed by the fractionation of lysates by molecular sieve chromatography into total DNA (chromosomal + R factor DNA), RNA, proteins and other cellular components. The R factor DNA was then separated and characterized.
