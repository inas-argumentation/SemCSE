THE TUMOUR‐NODE‐METASTASIS STAGING OF PROSTATE CANCER IN TRANSURETHRAL RESECTION OF THE PROSTATE CHIPS REQUIRES REVISION
B J U I N T E R N A T I O N A L © 2 0 11 B J U I N T E R N A T I O N A L | 1 0 7 , 3 5 1 – 3 5 4 | doi:10.1111/j.1464-410X.2010.09950,09953.x 3 5 1 Approximately 20% of prostate cancers (PCs) occur in the transition zone [1] and a significant number continue to be diagnosed by TURP, either serendipitously, or where a TRUS biopsy is not available. Treatment decisions for TURP-diagnosed PC remain difficult and tumour extent is used as an important parameter for method of treatment. The 2010 TNM staging of PC on TURP chips stratifies patients into T1a and T1b, based on the percentage area of tumour ( < 5% vs ≥ 5%) [2]. However, this stratification system, unchanged for 30 years, is outdated and may lead to overtreatment in pT1b tumours.
