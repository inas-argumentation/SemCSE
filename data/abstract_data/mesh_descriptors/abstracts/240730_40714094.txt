Living related liver retransplantation in a 6-month-old child after 60 hours of anhepatic phase following hepatectomy of thrombosed primary liver graft.
LIVING RELATED liver transplantation (LRLT) in children is well established with excellent results in elective recipients, but this procedure should not be performed in an emergency situation according to the Council of Europe recommendation no. R (97) 16. We have performed LRLT in a child following hepatectomy of the primary liver graft due to thrombosis of both the hepatic artery and the portal vein.
