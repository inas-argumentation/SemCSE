[Persistent retrograde ejaculation following incision of the bladder neck for functional bladder neck stenosis? Side effects of the Turner-Warwick bladder neck incision].
Twenty patients, between 20 and 51 years of age suffering from bladder neck dysfunction, were treated by bladder neck incision as proposed by Turner-Warwick. Good results and only minor side-effects were noticed. There was no case of permanent retrograde ejaculation.
