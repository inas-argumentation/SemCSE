Renal clearance–lipophilicity relationships of some organic acids in rabbits, rats and mice
Abstract— The effect of lipophilicity on the renal clearance for a group of weak organic acids (benzoic, phenylacetic and hippuric acid derivatives) was studied in rabbits, rats and mice.
