DNA synthesis in the fission yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe.
A method is described for determining the time of DNA synthesis in the cell cycle from information on asynchronous cultures. It is based on measurements of the mean amount of DNA per cell at various stages of the growth of an asynchronous culture. For S. pombe such a method shows that there is no detectable G1 and the S phase is short. The DNA content of stationary phase cells varies with phosphate content of the medium. At low phosphate concentrations, cells stop growing in G1, whereas in high phosphate concentrations growth ceases in G2. Continuous culturing of S. pombe at low phosphate concentrations results in cells with a greatly altered DNA synthesis cycle.
