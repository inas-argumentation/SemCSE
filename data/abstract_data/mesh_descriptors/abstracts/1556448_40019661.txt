[Effect of chronic hypoxia (Fi02 = 0.06 - Fi02 = 0.08) on mouse liver cytochrome P450 and b5 content].
During a eight days period of chronic hypoxia (FiO2 : 0,06) the cytochrome P 450 content of mice'liver decreases the 1st day, increases the 2nd and the 3rd day and decreases again and remains at a low levels. During a 13 days period of chronic hypoxia (FiO2 = 0,08) the P 450 level remains inchanged during 5 days. It decreases after until the 13teen day. G6Pase, an endoplasmic reticulum enzyme marker is quite inchanged in these conditions.
