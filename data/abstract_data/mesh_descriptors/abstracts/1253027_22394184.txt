Late thrombosis of sirolimus‐eluting stents following noncardiac surgery
We describe two patients with in‐stent thrombosis occurring 4 and 21 months after implantation of sirolimus‐eluting stents. Both cases occurred following noncardiac surgery. In both cases, aspirin had been stopped prior to surgery. Both patient sustained a severe myocardial infarction; one died. The occurrence of late thrombosis of sirolimus‐eluting stents is of concern. © 2005 Wiley‐Liss, Inc.
