When and How Should Subcentimetric Lung Nodules Be Referred for Operation?
We read with great interest the article recently published in The Annals of Thoracic Surgery by Taioli and associates [1], and we appreciate the authors’ efforts to conduct a comprehensive systematic review. However, we think two methodological concerns should be emphasized. First, a meta-analysis of invalid studies may produce an inaccurate result. Evaluation of the quality of the included studies is essential [2], so before pooling the individual results, the authors should evaluate the risk of bias using an assessment tool (eg, the Newcastle-Ottawa Scale [3]). Second, because the authors stated that “If such selection bias exists it cannot be accounted for and adjusted in the metaanalysis,” it is necessary to measure and adjust “the confounding by indication [4]” in nonrandomized studies. Why not try to contact authors and researchers to obtain individual patient data including stage or perfomance status. We will then be able to determine a more accurate prognosis after pleurectomy decortication and extrapleural pneumonectomy in mesothelioma.
