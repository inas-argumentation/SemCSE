The incidence and dynamics of the development of alveolitis over the course of a year
: Analysis of the results of 8019 operations for removal of teeth, carried out within 5 years by one physician under the same conditions, has shown that alveolitis developed in 1.87 +/- 0.15% of cases, on an average. The risk of alveolitis was found the highest from December to March and the lowest from June to September, as evidenced by estimation of the index of the seasonal fluctuations in the morbidity (a total of 120 to 140 teeth were removed within a month, on an average).
