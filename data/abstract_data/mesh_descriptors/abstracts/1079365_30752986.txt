GROUPS B, C, AND G STREPTOCOCCAL INFECTIONS IN A CANCER HOSPITAL
Disease due to streptococci other than Lancefield groups A or D has recently been reviewed at various medical centers.1-3 Group G streptococci in one reviewS appeared more commonly in patients with neoplastic disease, and we have noted severe infections due to this group in similar patients? It might be anticipated that in the immunologically altered host these infections would be more frequent and severe. It is the purpose of this paper to report our experience with the isolation and evaluation of organisms belonging to Lancefield's groups B, C and G in a hospital caring for patients with neoplastic disease.
