Glycemic Management of Diabetes in the Perioperative Setting
The patient with diabetes poses unique challenges in the perioperative setting. In addition to the increased risk of poor wound healing, postoperative infection, and vascular events, patients with diabetes are at ongoing risk of metabolic decompensation due to alteration in their antihyperglycemic regimens and to the stress of surgery itself. In the following pages, I review current approaches to glycemic control in patients with diabetes undergoing surgical procedures.
