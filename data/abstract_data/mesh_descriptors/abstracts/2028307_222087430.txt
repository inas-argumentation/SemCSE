PHARMACOLOGIC AND GENETIC DETERMINANTS OF DISORDERED BILIRUBIN TRANSPORT AND METABOLISM IN THE LIVER *
Inheritable disorders of hepatic bilirubin uptake, conjugation and excretion have been described in both man and animals. Specific pharmacologic agents produce effects similar to the defects observed in these disorders. Studies of these inheritable disorders of bilirubin transport and metabolism and of pharmacologic effects on these processes has led to considerable understanding of the mechanisms of bilirubin uptake,. conjugation and excretion by the liver.
