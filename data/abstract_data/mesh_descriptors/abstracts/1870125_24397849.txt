A randomized study investigating the efficacy and safety of amprenavir in combination with low‐dose ritonavir in protease inhibitor‐experienced HIV‐infected adults
To compare the safety and efficacy of amprenavir [APV/j Agenerase™; GlaxoSmithKline, [Ware, UK; 600 mg twice a day (bid)] boosted with low‐dose ritonavir (RTV, 100 mg bid) with those of other protease inhibitors (PIs) in PI‐experienced HIV‐infected patients.
