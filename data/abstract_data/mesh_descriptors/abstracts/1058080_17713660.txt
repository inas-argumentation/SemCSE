Taxonomic classification of the oil destructing bacterium using mass spectrometry methods by the results of analysis of cellular proteins and study of cellular fatty acids
Species classification of a strain of the oil-destructing bacterium Acinetobacter sp. IB DT-5.1/1 is established by means of gas chromatography-mass spectrometry fatty acid methyl esters in the cell wall and matrix-assisted laser desorption/ionization (MALDI) mass spectrometry of proteins of a cell.
