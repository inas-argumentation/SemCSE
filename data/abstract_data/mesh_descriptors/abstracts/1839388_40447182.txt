IL‐1RN gene polymorphisms are associated with breast cancer risk in a Chinese Han population
The interleukins (ILs) are a large family of endogenous cytokines that are crucial in the regulation of inflammation and immunological responses. The IL‐1 receptor antagonist (IL‐1RN) has been found to be associated with risk breast cancer (BC) in Korean and Indian women. However, little information is found about the polymorphisms of IL‐1RN in Chinese Han BC patients.
