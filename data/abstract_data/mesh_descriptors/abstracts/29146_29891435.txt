The Relationship Between Bilirubin Levels and Atherosclerosis
We read the article entitled ‘‘Associations of cardiovascular disease risk factors and calcified atherosclerosis with aortoiliac bifurcation position: the multiethnic study of atherosclerosis’’ by Forbang et al with interest. They found relationships between aortoiliac bifurcation position and age and gender and vascular risk factors. There are reports of a relationship between circulating bilirubin levels and atherosclerosis and a protective effect of bilirubin has also been reported in some studies. This includes links between some aortic characteristics and bilirubin levels. Bilirubin levels were not reported by Forbang et al. Are bilirubin values available in their study?
