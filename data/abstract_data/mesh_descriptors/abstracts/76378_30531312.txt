A case of stroke‐like migraine attacks after radiation therapy
A 40-year-old man presented following a generalized tonic clonic seizure at home and had two further episodes in emergency department requiring intravenous lorazepam. Seven years ago, he was treated for testicular seminoma that had metastasized to the left occipitoparietal region. The management included orchidectomy, resection of the metastatic lesion, and cranial irradiation. His wife reported a 1-week history of unilateral intermittent throbbing headache and blurred vision
