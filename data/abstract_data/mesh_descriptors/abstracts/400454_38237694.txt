Use of long-acting β2 agonists in arginine-16 homozygous patients with asthma
We read with interest the study by Palmer and colleagues.1 Their findings suggest that patients who are Arg/Arg homozygous at position 16 of the β2 adrenoceptor may be at increased risk of asthma exacerbations, particularly when treated with inhaled salmeterol.
