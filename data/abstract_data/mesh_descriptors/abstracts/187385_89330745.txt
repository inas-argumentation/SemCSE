The ultrastructural heterogeneity of the pulmonary phagocytes in acute and chronic inflammation
: Summarized are data on cytologic and ultrastructural characteristics of lung phagocytes obtained from bronchoalveolar lavage in patients with inflammatory lung diseases of different genesis. Autoradiographic studies in vitro, supplementing data of structural analysis, enabled us to get the information on metabolic and proliferative activity of alveolar macrophages.
