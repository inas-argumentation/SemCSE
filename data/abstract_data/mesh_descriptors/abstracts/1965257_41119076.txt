Lung and bronchus cancer in women: a 21st-century epidemic.
On Jan. 11, 1964, 200 reporters gathered in a State Department auditorium in Washington, D.C., as U.S. Surgeon General Dr. Luther Terry unveiled “Smoking and Health,” a long-awaited report by an elite 12-member panel of scientists and physicians. The message of the 357-page document was simple and direct: Smoking causes cancer.
