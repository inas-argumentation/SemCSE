Mucus-producing adenocarcinoma of renal pelvis.
Abstract Three additional cases of mucus-producing adenocarcinoma of the renal pelvis are presented. Two patients had a combination of various histologic types of tumor (squamous cell carcinoma in one and grade 4 transitional-cell epithelioma in the other) demonstrating the multiple potentialities of the urothelium. The apparent common characteristics of this malignant tumor are discussed.
