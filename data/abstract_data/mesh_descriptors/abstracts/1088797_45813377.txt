Changes in peripheral venous compliance after myocardial infarction.
Changes in the compliance of a superficial hand vein have been studied in patients suffering from myocardial infarction. Venoconstriction was a constant finding during the first day after infarction, and the response appeared to be mediated by the sympathetic nervous system. A number of possible initiating factors have been investigated but did not appear to be important in determining the response; the main cause remains to be identified.
