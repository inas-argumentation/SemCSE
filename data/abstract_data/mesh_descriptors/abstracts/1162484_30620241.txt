CHEMOTHERAPY BASED ON DRUG SENSITIVITY TESTS PERFORMED IN GENERAL PRACTICE
A MAJOR PROBLEM of the chemotherapeutic era continues to be the degree of empirical use of antibiotics forced upon or accepted by doctors, particularly those engaged in extrahospital practice. The disadvantages of indiscriminate chemotherapy which have been repeatedly stressed (Garrod, 1957; Gray, 1954) are, briefly, toxic and hypersensitivity reactions, the increasing incidence of bacterial resistance, superinfection and the high cost of many drugs.
