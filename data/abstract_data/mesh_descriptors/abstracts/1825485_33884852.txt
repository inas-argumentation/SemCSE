Naevus spilus‐type congenital melanocytic naevus associated with a novel NRAS codon 61 mutation
DEAR EDITOR, We recently described 17 patients with a new subtype of congenital melanocytic nevi (CMN), designated as ‘naevus spilus-type CMN’. The affected patients present with a large caf e au lait background macule and dozens of superimposed small and medium-sized CMN. Here we report an additional case with a novel NRAS mutation (Q61L). Congenital melanocytic naevi are categorized by their largest diameter (projected adult size, PAS). Small CMN (< 1 5 cm PAS) and medium-sized CMN (1 5–20 cm PAS) represent rather common, solitary, slightly thickened plaques with prominent terminal hairs.
