The clinical evaluation of analogues, IV. Nitrosoureas.
The importance of analogues is a manifestation of success in cancer chemotherapy. The magnitude of the potential for analogue development results from the wide range of chemical structures that have been uncovered which have clinically demonstrated effective tumor cell kill. Since the ‘magic bullet’ has not yet been found, all effective anticancer drugs are limited by toxicity to normal tissues and inability to eradicate all tumor cells with the first dose or before resistance occurs. Analogue development hopes to improve the therapeutic index of an active chemical structure either by increasing the cell kill ability or by diminishing the toxicity so that higher effective doses can be achieved.
