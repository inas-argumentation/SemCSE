The presence of 2-methylthio-N 6 -( 2 -isopentenyl)adenosine in leucine, tryptophan and cysteine tRNA's from Escherichia coli.
The isolation and characterization of 2-methylthio-N6-(Δ2-isopentenyl)adenosine from tRNALeu1- a-2, tRNATrp2 and tRNACys2 of Escherichia coli is described. The presence of this sulfur-containing adenosine derivative was shown in E. coli tRNA's specific to all the six amino acids corresponding to codons starting with U.
