Pravastatin activates platelet nitric oxide synthase (NOS) in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus and NOS activation is accompanied by serine phosphorylation.
Statins cause reductions in myocardial infarction and also morbidity and mortality of type 2 diabetes related to atheromatous disease and its thrombotic complications beyond those predicted by reductions in low density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL).~ 1,2 It has been suggested that the protective mechanism(s) may relate to improved endothelial function and/or antithrombotic effects, but the mechanism is not clear.
