A histochemical study of liver enzymes involved in glycogen metabolism in the X-irradiated rat
SummaryThe liver enzymes responsible for the breakdown and synthesis of glycogen from glucose have been investigated cytochemically in rats exposed to 1200 rads of x-irradiation. It was found that significant changes occur in their activities and that amylophosphorylase and amylo-1,6-glucosidase (debranching enzyme), both of which are responsible for the conversion of glycogen to glucose, are markedly inhibited by radiation. A significant inhibition of the activity of 1,4→1,6 transglucosidase (branching enzyme) was also observed. In contrast, the activity of UDPG-glycogen transglucosylase, which is responsible for the in vivo synthesis of 1,4-polysaccharides, was found to be stimulated.
