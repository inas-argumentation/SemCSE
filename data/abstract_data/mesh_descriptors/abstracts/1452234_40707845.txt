Burton's Anatomy of Melancholy: Philosophically, Medically and Historically: Part 1
These were the worst of times. The world had become a place of violence and pain, of fear and sorrow, of poverty, idleness and superstition. It was a mad world. And in this mad world, peopled by generations of men and women in a permanent state of delirium, inconsistency was the order of the day. This, alas, was Robert Burton’s world.
