Differential hepatic uptake of [35S]-labelled HDL apolipoproteins in a dual rat spleen-liver perfusion system.
In this study the cholesterol and protein moieties of high density lipoprotein (HDL) were differentially labelled in order to compare their uptake by the liver. The inclusion of a perfused spleen increases the uptake of perfusate HDL by the liver [ 1 I. As it can be pre-labelled with [3H]cholesterol in vivo prior to isolation, it causes the HDL during perfusion to be labelled with [3H]cholesterol in a similar manner to that which would be expected to occur during reverse cholesterol transport.
