The role of biological response modifiers in malignant melanoma
This article reviews the existing data in an attempt to address the role of biological response modifiers (IFN-α and IL-2) in the management of melanoma. Eight prospective controlled Phase III trials evaluating IFN-α therapy are discussed. As the results from these trials have been heterogenous and inconsistent, the role of IFN-α in the adjuvant treatment of stage II and III melanoma patients remains to be defined. IL-2 possesses modest antitumour activity in melanoma yielding objective response in ~ 15% of patients. Many toxic effects are induced by high-dose IL-2 therapy. Combinations of chemotherapy and biological response modifiers and their impact on tumour response and survival are discussed.
