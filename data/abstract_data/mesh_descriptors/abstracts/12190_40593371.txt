Simultaneous DNA content and cell-surface immunofluorescence analysis.
Publisher Summary low cytometric measurement of relative DNA content per cell using intercalating dyes has become a well-established method to study proliferation of tissues and cell populations. This chapter describes a single-laser method for bivariate analysis of DNA content and cell-surface immunofluorescence. It uses a standard immunofluorescence technique, which is followed by propidium iodide (PI) staining of double-stranded DNA under hypotonic incubation conditions. S phase DNA content has been extensively used to study cell kinetics in hematological malignancies, because it is simple, rapid, and reproducible. Because of these characteristics, this technique is often preferred over [ 3 ]thymidine incorporation. Simultaneous detection of DNA content and DNA synthesis using halogenated thymidine analogues (BrdUrd, IdUrd) can be used to obtain additional cell kinetic information. However, this method is relatively laborious, necessitates the in vivo administration of IdUrd, and requires an optimal sampling time.
