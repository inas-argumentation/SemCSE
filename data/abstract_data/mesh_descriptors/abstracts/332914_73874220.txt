[Budd-Chiari syndrome liver cell adenoma and polycythemia following contraceptives]
A 35-year old patient developed Budd-chiari syndrome (BCS) liver cell adenoma (LCA) and polycythemia with increased erythropoietin following a 2-year use of hormonal contraceptives in 1967-68 and treatment with ethinyl estradiol for abnormal menstrual bleeding in 1978-79. The patient died of cardiovascular failure in 1980 following kidney failure and hepatic coma. Autopsy revealed thrombotic occlusion of all hepatic venous branches several hepatocellular adenomas and foci of pelosis hepatica with necrosis of the liver parenchyma. An increase in BCS has been observed especially in women taking oral contraceptives (OCs); the same is true for LCA in pill users (3/100000 women under 30 years of age). Incidence increases with length of pill taking and increase in steroid dose. Concurrent occurrence of BSC and LCA is rare. No single pathogenic mechanism is seen in BCS. Besides primary thrombosis or venous obstruction inflammation of the intima with secondary thrombosis is observed. Blood coagulation is altered in contraceptive users as it is seen sometimes in pregnancy. A genetic disorder may lead to the formation of toxic oncogenic metabolites in the metabolism of mestranol or ethinyl estradiol; this may induce LCA. The latter are mostly solitary and must be differentiated from congenital hamartomas. Symptoms of BCS are hepatomegaly ascites and upper abdominal pain. Chronic obstruction is at first free of symptoms; acute obstruction leads to shock and liver failure mostly with fatal outcome. Angiography and liver biopsy are diagnostic methods. LCA manifests itself with recurrent abdominal colic and palpable liver tumor. Rupture of a tumor leads to hemoperitoneum and shock necessitatin g emergency laparotomy. Sonography scintigram and angiography are the best diagnostic methods. Prognosis for BCS is poor (30-40% fatalities). Fibrinolytic treatment or liver transplantation can sometimes effect a cure. LCA can be resected if detected in time.
