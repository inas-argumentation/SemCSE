Geomagnetism, cancer, weather and cosmic radiation.
Both isometric cancer death rates and depth contours of the last ice age glacier in North America were shown to correspond with isometric horizontal geomagnetic intensity lines. When geomagnetic flux was held constant, an altitude effect was demonstrated. Similar associations between cancer incidence and horizontal geomagnetic flux was demonstrated on a world-wide basis. Non-uniform heating of the stratosphere by a combination of cosmic and solar radiation as influenced by geomagnetic flux was postulated as a mechanism for many of the weather conditions on Earth, including location of the last North American ice age glacier.
