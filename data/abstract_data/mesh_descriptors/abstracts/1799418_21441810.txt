Liaison psychiatry in childhood cancer--a systems approach.
The strategies of negotiating the vicissitudes of cancer shift as the disease progresses. These shifts are tied to the milestones on the disease continuum (diagnosis, remission, relapse, death). The authors regard the shifts as quantitatively different stages and have designated them optimism, disappointment, disillusionment, abandonment, rapprochement, and reconciliation. This article will explore the psychological meaning of these stages and address itself to the way the child, his family, and the pediatric oncologist cope with childhood cancer.
