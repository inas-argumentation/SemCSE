Men, testosterone, and arteriosclerosis.
Dr Vicky MacRae speaks to Mark Nicholls about research which could help explain why men are more likely to suffer cardiovascular disease than their similarly aged female counterparts  

![Graphic][1] 

It is well known that men appear to have a greater risk of cardiovascular disease than women of a similar age, and that vascular calcification is a strong predictor of mortality and morbidity from cardiovascular disease.

Exploring a link between gender and calcification has been the focus of work by a team from the University of Edinburgh, which they hope could potentially unlock the pathway to new therapies.

The research team has been looking at whether sex hormones could be the common ground, focusing on testosterone as the primary and most well-recognized androgen in men.
