Differential effects of chlorpromazine and chlorpromazine free radical on calcium transport in sarcoplasmic reticulum vesicles.
Abstract Chlorpromazine activates, and chlorpromazine free radical inhibits, the calcium transport system of fragmented sarcoplasmic reticulum. The associated calcium-dependent ATPase is affected in a similar manner. However, the calcium independent (basal) ATPase is not reduced in activity by the free radical. It is suggested that unmodified chlorpromazine rather than its free radical form is the pharmacologically active moiety.
