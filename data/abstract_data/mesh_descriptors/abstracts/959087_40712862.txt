Haemolymph proteins in Porcellio laevis latreille (Porcellionidae, Isopoda): the molecular weight.
Abstract 1. 1. By means of Sephadex G200-agarose hydrolysed starch gel, the haemolymph proteins of a tenth instar Porcellio laevis were separated into three distinct fractions: fraction A (a glycoprotein), fraction B (an unknown protein) and fraction C (the haemocyanin). 2. 2. The molecular weight of fraction A (glycoprotein), as determined on Sephadex G-150 gel and on Sepharose −4B column amounted to 9500–11,000, while that of fraction C (haemocyanin), as calculated on Sepharose −4B column, was 1,200,000. 3. 3. Fraction B (the unknown protein) gave a molecular weight of 42,000–52,000 on Sephadex G-150, but 70,000–80,000 on Sepharose −4B. This behaviour of this fraction on different media supports the contention that fraction B is a subunit.
