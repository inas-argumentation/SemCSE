It is unclear whether drug reaction data from these medically tenuous populations of patients are applicable to patients who require immunosuppressive medications for inflammatory dermatoses. Future studies might investigate the rate of serious adverse reactions to PCP prophylaxis in a cohort of dermatology patients specifically. Ultimately, we aim to raise awareness amongst dermatologists of the rare, frequently fatal, but readily preventable opportunistic infection PCP, and to help guide informed decisions regarding the provision of PCP prophylaxis in our patients at risk.
