Sturge-Weber syndrome with transient focal hyperperfusion.
The authors present a case of a 72-year-old woman with Sturge-Weber syndrome showing transient postictal hyperperfusion on I-123 IMP SPECT. Brain SPECT obtained 2 days after the seizure demonstrated hyperperfusion in the affected cortex shown by CT and MRI. The follow-up SPECT obtained 26 days later showed hypoperfusion in the corresponding area. This case shows that patients with the Sturge-Weber syndrome may have hyperperfusion in the pathologic cerebral regions In the postictal phase.
