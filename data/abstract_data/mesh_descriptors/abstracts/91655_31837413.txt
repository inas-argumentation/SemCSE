VASCULAR SMOOTH MUSCLE POLYPLOIDY IN THE DEVELOPMENT AND REGRESSION OF HYPERTENSION
1. Two groups of spontaneously hypertensive rats (SHR) were treated with enalapril (25–30 mg/kg per day): Group I received treatment from 4 to 14 weeks of age to inhibit development of hypertension and Group R received the drug from 14 to 20 weeks of age to reverse established hypertension.
