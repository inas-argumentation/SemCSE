From diagnostic tool to disease monitoring: the growing role of prostate biopsies.
The current issue of European Urology contains an outstanding review on the role of prostate biopsy (PBx) in the diagnosis and management of prostate cancer (PCa) entitled, ‘‘Contemporary Role of Systematic Prostate Biopsies: Indications, Techniques, and Implications for Patient Care’’ [1]. The authors concluded that an extended PBx (10–12 cores) remains the standard for the initial diagnostic evaluation of a suspicious prostate. In a repeat PBx after an initial negative biopsy, the authors suggest balancing the risk of overdetection with the risk of missing significant cancers. Several options for a more selective approach, including the use of additional biomarkers, risk stratification tools, and advanced imaging techniques, are discussed.
