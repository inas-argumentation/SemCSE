Midlife Characteristics of Older Men Using Statins
To the Editor: The association between statins and physical performance was investigated in older women, and no consistent benefit was found. Statin treatment is increasingly initiated in old age, although no randomized studies have investigated the initiation of statins at age 80 and older. Statins have side effects such as muscle symptoms and possibly diabetes mellitus that may be especially harmful in older adults, but healthy user bias (users are healthier than nonusers) or reverse causality (users are sicker than nonusers) may limit cross-sectional and even prospective studies—if started in late life. These biases can only be circumvented by analyzing the characteristics of older statin users earlier in life.
