The complications of synchronous combined excision for adenocarcinoma of the rectum at St Mark's hospital
The complications of synchronous combined excision of the rectum for adenocarcinoma in 227 patients were analysed. Urinary retention with infection occurred in 78 patients (34‐4 per cent) and was the commonest complication. There was a significant correlation between tumours involving the anterior quadrants of the rectum and urinary complications.
