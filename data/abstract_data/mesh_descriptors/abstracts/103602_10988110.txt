Anomalous Lymphatic Drainage Patterns in Patients with Cutaneous Melanoma
Preoperative lymphoscintigraphy performed over recent years in large numbers of patients with primary cutaneous melanoma has revealed many lymphatic drainage pathways which had not been previously documented or even suspected 1,2. These "anomalous" drainage pathways cannot be predicted on clinical grounds in individual patients, and their identification has critically important implications for accurate staging by sentinel lymph node biopsy.
