Improvement of QoS Routing Method Using Ant Colony Algorithm and Fuzzy Logic in Ad hoc Networks
The ad hoc network is a system of network elements that combine to form a network requiring little or no planning. This may not be feasible as nodes can enter and leave the network. In such networks, each node can receive the packet (host) and the packet sender (router) to act. The goal of routing is finding paths that meet the needs of the network and effectively use network resources. This paper presents a method for QoS routing in ad hoc networks based on ant colony optimization (ACO) algorithm and fuzzy logic. The advantages of this method flexibility and routing are based on several criteria. The results show that the proposed method in comparison with the algorithm IACA has better performance, higher efficiency and greater throughput. Therefore, the combination of ant algorithm with Fuzzy Logic due to its simplicity fuzzy computing is appropriate for QoS routing.
