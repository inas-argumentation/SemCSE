UFMC system performance analysis for discrete narrow-band private networks
In this paper, the performance of universal-filtered multi-carrier (UFMC) system is analyzed. Specifically, the UFMC signals are generated with Dolph-Chebyshev, Hamming and Hanning filtering. The characteristics of UFMC signals are also compared with OFDM signal, in both software and hardware-in-the-loop simulations. Experimental results show that UFMC signals have better performance than OFDM signal, by means of side-lobe attenuation, bit-error-ratio (BER) and Error Vector Magnitude (EVM). The results indicate that the UFMC signal is more suitable than OFDM signal for discrete narrow-band private networks. The results can provide useful information on the design of multi-carrier radio systems.
