Semantic role labeling for Arabic language using case-based reasoning approach
On the basis of the robust syntactic parsers development, a considerable progress is carried out within the natural language processing (NLP). However, the parsing itself does not allow to pinpointing the phrase entirely meaning. It is essential to know the relations between verb and components in order to answer questions such as who did this, where, when, how, etc. In accordance with, theories, lexical semantic resources, and methods for semantic role labeling are carried out to answer such questions and to provide more complete representation within the sentence meaning (Palmer et al. 2011). For the semantic role labeling (SRL), lexical resources such as FrameNet (Baker et al. 1998), PropBank (Palmer et al. 2005) help to the achievement of the methods which identify the predicate and its arguments and their semantic roles within-side. In the sentence; ‘Hassan watches a movie’, for instance, the predicate is ‘watch’, the first argument is the subject ‘Hassan’, and the second argument is the object ‘movie’(Diab et al. 2008; Zaghouani et al. 2010). Likewise, the semantic role labeling methods development has a major impact on the improvement of the natural language processing applications (NLP) such as machine translation, document search, question answering, and information extraction (Diab et al. 2008). In fact, works on semantic role labeling in languages like English, and Chinese, etc., are of great numbers unless Arabic since the semantic role labeling, the lexical resources, and the construction of annotated corpus are of the lowest limit and do not respond to the research requirements sometimes. In this paper, we present a semantic role labeling method within Arabic sentences. This method is based on the casebased reasoning approach (CBR) that uses annotated arguments of previous sentences in order to annotate new ones Abstract Many natural language processing areas use semantic roles in order to improve the applications of the extracted information, the question answering and the machine translation, etc. In Arabic, the work of constructing the semantic role labeling system or the annotated corpus is extremely limited compared to their speaker’s number and to English language as well. In this paper, we present a supervised method for the semantic role labeling of Arabic sentences. Hence, we use the feedback capacity of the case-based reasoning to annotate new sentences from already annotated ones besides the use of the Arabic PropBank as a reference to the semantic labels. We test our method under a wide range corpus that contains 2332 attributes and 5291 arguments. Accordingly, an Arabic semantic role labeling system is tested, for the first time, in that corpus. As a result, our method shows the ability to annotate new sentences from the labeled sentences or the construction of the annotated corpus.
