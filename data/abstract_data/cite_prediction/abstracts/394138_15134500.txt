LEXICAL PREDICTORS OFPERSONALITY TYPE
We are currently pursuing methods for “author profiling” in w hich various aspects of the author’s identity might be identified from a text, with out necessarily having a corpus of documents from the same individual. A key componen t of such an identity profile is personality; this paper addresses distinguishin g igh from low neuroticism and extraversion in authors of informal text. We consider fo ur different sets of lexical features for this task: a standard function word list, conju nctive phrases, modality indicators, and appraisal adjectives and modifiers. SMO, a suppo rt vector machine learner, was used to learn linear separators for the high and low class es in each of the two tasks. We find that appraisal use is the best predictor for neurotici sm, and that function words work best for extraversion. Further, examination of the spe cifically most important features yields insight into how neuroticism and extraversion differentially affect language use.
