Low cost platform for monitoring honey production and bees health
This paper presents the construction of a low cost platform for monitoring honey production and bees health in a small colony. The proposed system monitors: the weight, the outside and inside temperatures, the humidity and the CO2 concentration of the beehive. Based on the gathered data the system can estimate the honey production, the health of the bees and possible pests like: Aethina tumida beetle, Varroa destructor mite, etc. Unlike other systems on the market that monitor only honey production or bees health the proposed system evaluates both and sends a phone message to the beekeeper if any problem arise.
