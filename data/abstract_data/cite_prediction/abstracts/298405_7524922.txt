Fuzzy Logic-Based Natural Language Processing and Its Application to Speech Recognition
In this paper we describe a fuzzy logic-based language processing method, which is applied to speech recognition. Our purpose is to create a system that can learn from a linguistic corpus the fuzzy semantic relations between the concepts represented by words and use such relations to process the word sequences generated by speech recognition systems. In particular, the system will be able to predict the words failed to be recognized by a speech recognition system. This will help to increase the accuracy of a speech recognition system. This will also serve as the first stage of deep semantic processing of speech recognition results by providing “semantic relatedness” between the recognized words. We report the fuzzy inference rule learning system, which we have developed and also report the experimental results based on the system. Key-words: Fuzzy logic, Natural Language Analysis, Speech Recognition, Corpus Linguistics.
