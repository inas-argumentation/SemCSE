Grouper: A Dynamic Clustering Interface to Web Search Results
There were two ways to cluster: pre-retrieval or post-retrieval clustering. In pre-retrieval clustering, documents are clustered before results are obtained. Here, there are two drawbacks. First, pre-retrieval clusters might be based on features that are infrequent in the retrieved set. Second, many non-retrieved documents can influence the clusters. In postretrieval clustering, documents are clustered after results were obtained. Post-retrieval clustering has better results respect to pre-retrieval clustering so Grouper preferred this way of clustering.
