The Geography of Financial Misconduct ∗
Financial misconduct (FM) varies across cities, and over time. City-level FM is highly correlated with political corruption (enforced non-locally), and with ethically questionable (but not illegal) behavior by local physicians, both inconsistent with regional variation in enforcement. Further, among firms for whom detection is likely very similar (ex-clients of Arthur Andersen forced to switch auditors), a similar ranking of FM rates across cities emerges. FM is uncorrelated with economic and demographic variation, but is consistent with social norms transmitted by peer effects. In particular, FM rates in a city’s “dominant” industry predict FM among local firms outside the dominant sector.
