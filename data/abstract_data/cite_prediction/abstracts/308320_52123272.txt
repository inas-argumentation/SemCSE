A Fast Stereo Visual-Inertial Odometry for MAVs
Motion estimation is one of the key perception problems for Micro Aerial Vehicles (MAVs). Recent developments based on visual-inertial sensors exhibit promising performance. In this paper, we propose a fast stereo-inertial odometry approach leveraging the advantages of both visual feature tracking and visual inertial fusion. Instead of tracking visual feature on every frame, our method estimates short-term motion with inertial-assisted flow tracking. By that, the frame-rate of visual feature detection and tracking is reduced to save computational power. Also, we design an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) to achieve full state estimation, including position, velocity and attitude. Our method is able to estimate camera motion with less computational effort, while maintaining comparative state estimation performance. Experiments on the Euroc dataset which is captured onboard a MAV show the good performance of our method.
