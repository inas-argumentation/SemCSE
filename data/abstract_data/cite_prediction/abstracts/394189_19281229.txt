A wideband circularly polarized microstrip array antenna at Ka-band
A circularly polarized microstrip array antenna is proposed for Ka-band satellite applications. The antenna element consists of an L-shaped patch with parasitic circular-ring radiator. A sequentially rotated 2×2 antenna array exhibits a wideband 3-dB axial ratio bandwidth of 20.6% (25.75 GHz - 31.75 GHz) and 2;1-VSWR bandwidth of 24.0% (25.5 GHz - 32.5 GHz). A boresight gain of 10-11.8 dBic is achieved across a frequency range from 26 GHz to 32 GHz. An 8×8 antenna array exhibits a boresight gain of greater than 24 dBic over 27.25 GHz-31.25 GHz.
