TMCoI-SIOT: A trust management system based on communities of interest for the social Internet of Things
We propose a trust management system (TMS) for the social Internet of things (SIOT) called TMCoI-SIOT. The proposed system integrates numerous factors such as direct and indirect trust; transaction factors and social modeling of trust. The novelty of our approach can be summed up in three aspects. The first aspect concerns the network architecture which is seen as a set of nodes groups called community of interest (CoI). Each community nodes shares the same interests and is headed by an administrator (admin. The second aspect is the trust management system that prevents On-Off attacks. The third aspect concerns the trust prediction method that uses the Kalman filter technique in order to estimate nodes behaviors and prevent possible attacks. We prove the effectiveness of the proposed system by simulation against TMS attacks.
