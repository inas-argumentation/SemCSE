Battery energy storage systems (BESSs), while at the moment still expensive, are from a technical point of view exceptionally well suited to support a distribution system operator (DSO) in the challenges created by increasing distributed, fluctuating and uncertain generation from renewable energy sources (RES), as well as by the unbundling of electricity retailing and grid operation. This paper presents three grid applications and measurement results from the grid-connected Zurich 1 MW BESS.
