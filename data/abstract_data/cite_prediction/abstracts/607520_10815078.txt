Free for All! Assessing User Data Exposure to Advertising Libraries on Android
• We have modeled an opportunistic ad library that aggressively collects targeted data from Android devices. • We demonstrate that the access channels considered are realistic. • We have designed a reliable and extensible framework that can be leveraged to assess user data exposure by an app to a library.
