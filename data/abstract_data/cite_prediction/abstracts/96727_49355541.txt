Detailed 3 D Reconstruction of Static Scenes from a Multi-Camera System
Virtual reality (VR) has recently become a popular subject in which effective 3D scene construction techniques are necessary for a smooth and fine reconstruction scene. Aiming at constructing a dense 3D scene from multipleperspective frames captured by six fisheye cameras, this essay will explore a novel technique which applies superpixel segmentation to frames in order to obtain densified 3D information of a piecewise planar. In combination with a structure-from-motion (SFM) algorithm, we shall demonstrate that this technique is a simple solution for dense 3D reconstruction and that is also efficient and effective. Furthermore, since it requires no further information concerning scale, object segmentation and other factors, it may be applied to a wide range of scenarios.
