Microcontroller based DC-DC Cascode Buck-Boost Converter
In this paper DC-DC Cascode Buck-Boost Converter is proposed by using two buck-boost converter. The structure of the proposed convert is very simple and the switching operation is done by using one active switch IGBT. The Buck-Boost Converters are named as higher semi stage and lower semi stage. The input DC supply to the lower semi stage converter is given by means of the input DC supply and the input to the higher semi stage is given by means of DC supply and the output voltage of the lower semi stage converter. Thus overall output voltage is given by the output sum of individual semi stage converters by cascode. Hence, from this Cascode DC-DC Converter can achieve a very high output voltage gain in a single stage without having an extreme high duty ratio. The input pulse to the active switch IGBT is given by Arduino. The operating principle and steady analysis are discussed in detail in this paper and also the voltage gain is compared with the conventional Boost converter, Buck-Boost converter and Cuk converter. The dynamic analysis of complete circuit is analyzed and MATLAB/Simulink simulation is done and finally to verify the proposed cascode converter laboratory experiment of 50W has been implemented to test both the simulation and experimental result.
