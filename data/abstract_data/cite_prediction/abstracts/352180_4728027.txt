Multimodal learning for topic sentiment analysis in microblogging
Microblogging has become a widely-spread platform of human communication. The massive amount of opinion-rich data in microblogging is helpful to analyze and manage public opinion and social emotion. Different from traditional texts, microblogging data are multimodal, containing multifarious data such as emoticons, image, etc. Most existing sentiment and topic detection approaches treat the unique microblogging data as noise. However, this may lead to unsatisfactoriness in sentiment classification and topic identification.
