SAR implementation using LFM signal
Synthetic Aperture Radar is a coherent airborne or space borne sidelooking radar system which is regarded as one of the most recent advancement in the field of research and defence and used extensively for mapping of a terrain. With the help of this technology we are capable of obtaining 3-D images of objects such as landscapes, terrain images with very high precision and accuracy. From RADAR basics, if the aperture of RADAR is increased then the image resolution of the terrain which is under consideration also increases, this concept is true for all RADAR whether if it is actually having a large physical area or if it is of `synthetic nature' that is here the large physical area is basically simulated artificially even though if it's not available in reality [2]. With the help of this technology a resolution of about 10cm can be achieved and research has also been made wherein the resolution was further improvised to be in the order of few millimetres. In this paper the authors describe the simulation of a SAR system.
