An Optimal Graph Theoretic Approach to Data Clustering: Theory and Its Application to Image Segmentation
(57) Data collected from devices and human condition may be used to forewarn of critical events such as machine/structural failure or events from brain/heart wave data stroke. By moni toring the data, and determining what values are indicative of a failure forewarning, one can provide adequate notice of the impending failure in order to take preventive measures. This disclosure teaches a computer-based method to convert dynamical numeric data representing physical objects (un structured data) into discrete-phase-space states, and hence into a graph (Structured data) for extraction of condition change.
