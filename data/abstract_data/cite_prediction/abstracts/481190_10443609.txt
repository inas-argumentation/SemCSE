Sentiment Analysis of Travel Reviews
Opinion mining or commonly known as sentiment analysis is the process where a sentence or a document is categorized based on its polarity. The polarity here refers to a sentence or an entity as positive, neutral or negative. Recently, sentiment analysis has attained much attention. In this paper, the goal of achieving polarity for hotel reviews is described in detail. The data is collected from Expedia.com. Also, a survey of recent papers is discussed comprehensively to gain insight into the latest update in the field of sentiment analysis. The paper also includes the algorithm adopted to achieve the goal and its future aspects.
