LMVP : Video Predictor with Leaked Motion Information
We propose a Leaked Motion Video Predictor (LMVP) to predict future frames by 1 capturing the spatial and temporal dependencies from given inputs. The motion is 2 modeled by a newly proposed component, motion guider, which plays the role of 3 both learner and teacher. Specifically, it learns the temporal features from real data 4 and guides the generator to predict future frames. The spatial consistency in video 5 is modeled by an adaptive filtering network. To further ensure the spatio-temporal 6 consistency of the prediction, a discriminator is also adopted to distinguish the real 7 and generated frames. Further, the discriminator leaks information to the motion 8 guider and the generator to help the learning of motion. The proposed LMVP can 9 effectively learn the static and temporal features in videos without the need for 10 human labeling.
