Robust Product Design Using SOSM for Control of Shielded Metal Arc-Welding (SMAW) Process
Parametric variations, slow and fast disturbances, and nonlinearity are inherently present in abundance in all types of arc-welding processes, and shielded metal arc-welding (SMAW) process is no exception. PI has remained, for decades, a dominating control idea for control of all arc-welding processes. Its replacement by superior control concept is long overdue particularly for handling parametric variations, nonlinearity in load behavior, and several forms of disturbances present in the system. Though the work-in-progress concepts, such as sliding-mode control (SMC), are equipped to handle many of these issues, their market penetration in shaping up a complete industrial product is below expected line. In this paper, the second-order sliding mode (SOSM) control concept is practically implemented to create a market-ready product for robust control of SMAW process. Here, each aspect of the entire process such as welding at different current using different types of electrodes is detailed. It further proposes, with complete practical validation, a simple and easily implementable control function that emulates robust control features of SOSM.
