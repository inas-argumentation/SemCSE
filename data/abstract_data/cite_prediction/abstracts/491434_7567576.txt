Backlobe and Sidelobe Suppression of a Q-Band Patch Antenna Array by Using Substrate Integrated Coaxial Line Feeding Technique
A low profile aperture-coupled patch antenna array with high front-to-back-ratio (FTBR) is designed at <italic>Q </italic>-band by using substrate integrated coaxial line technique, the intrinsic shielded enclosure of which is capable of constructing an equal-phase and amplitude-tapering feeding network with backlobe suppression. An illustrative example of a 6 <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\times$</tex-math></inline-formula> 6 patch antenna array is designed and fabricated with a 2 <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\times$</tex-math> </inline-formula> 2 prior verification module by using multilayer liquid crystal polymer process. Measured results indicate that the array can achieve a gain of 17.6 dBi at 41.5 GHz and the first sidelobe levels is <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$-$</tex-math></inline-formula>24.1 dB in the <italic>E</italic>-plane and <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$-$</tex-math></inline-formula>20.4 dB in the <italic>H</italic>-plane. The holistic FTBR is better than 27 dB in both planes.
