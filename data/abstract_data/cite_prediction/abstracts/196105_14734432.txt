On Learning High Dimensional Structured Single Index Models
Single Index Models (SIMs) are simple yet flexible semiparametric models for machine learning, where the response variable is modeled as a monotonic function of a linear combination of features. Estimation in this context requires l earning both the feature weights and the nonlinear function that relates features to observations. While methods have been described to learn SIMs in the low dimensional regime, a method that can efficiently learn SIMs in high dimensions, and under general structural assumptions, has not been fort hcoming. In this paper, we propose computationally efficient algorithms for SIM inference in high dimensions with structural constraints. Our general approach specializes to spa rsity, group sparsity, and low-rank assumptions among other s. Experiments show that the proposed method enjoys superior predictive performance when compared to generalized linea r models, and achieves results comparable to or better than si ngle layer feedforward neural networks with significantly le ss computational cost.
