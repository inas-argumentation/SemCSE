RUCIR at the NTCIR-13 STC-2 Task
This paper describes the RUCIR’s systems which participated in the Short Text Conversation (STC-2) task (Chinese) at NTCIR-13[9]. Contrary to the STC task in NTCIR12, generation-based methods are considered and participants are encouraged to explore some effective ways to combine both retrieval-based and generation-based approaches to get a more intelligent chatbot. This paper introduces two systems we proposed: (1) a retrieval-based system (2) a generation-based system combined with retrieval-based methods. Besides, we do some analysis and discussion on the two systems based on the results.
