Learning to Identify Sentence Parallelism in Student Essays
Parallelism is an important rhetorical device. We propose a machine learning approach for automated sentence parallelism identification in student essays. We b uild an essay dataset with sentence level parallelism annotated. We derive features by combining gen eralized word alignment strategies and the alignment measures between word sequences. The experiment al r sults show that sentence parallelism can be effectively identified with a F1 score of 82% at pair-wise level and 72% at parallelism chunk l evel. Based on this approach, we automatically identify sentence parallelism in more than 2000 student essays and study the correlation between the use of sentence parall elism and the types and quality of essays.
