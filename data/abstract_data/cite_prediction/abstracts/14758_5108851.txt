A case of accidental decapitation in a farm worker.
Every year in France, about 75,000 occupational accidents are reported among farm workers, of which 150 are fatal [1, 2]. One third of these deaths involve machines and usually result from crushing injuries. However, decapitations resulting from occupational accidents in the agricultural setting appear to be rare events. We report the unusual case of a farmer who suffered decapitation after his scarf was accidentally caught by the drive shaft of a trailer.
