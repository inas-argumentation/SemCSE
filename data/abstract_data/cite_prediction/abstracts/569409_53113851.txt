Robust Neural Abstractive Summarization Systems and Evaluation against Adversarial Information
Sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) neural models have been actively investigated 1 for abstractive summarization. Nevertheless, existing neural abstractive systems 2 frequently generate factually incorrect summaries and are vulnerable to adversarial 3 information, suggesting a crucial lack of semantic understanding. In this paper, we 4 propose a novel semantic-aware neural abstractive summarization model that learns 5 to generate high quality summaries through semantic interpretation over salient 6 content. A novel evaluation scheme with adversarial samples is introduced to 7 measure how well a model identifies off-topic information, where our model yields 8 significantly better performance than the popular pointer-generator summarizer. 9 Human evaluation also confirms that our system summaries are uniformly more 10 informative and faithful as well as less redundant than the seq2seq model. 11
