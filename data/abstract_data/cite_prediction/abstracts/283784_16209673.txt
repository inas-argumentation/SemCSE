Detecting Phishing Web Pages Based on Image Perceptual Hashing Technology
Phishing is a real threat to Internet communication and web economy. Criminals are trying to convince unsuspecting on-line users to reveal account numbers, passwords or other personal information. The acquired information is subsequently used to steal identities or gain access to money. It is absolutely necessary that developing the efficient techniques to improve the detecting performance.This paper explores the possibility of utilizing the image perceptual hashing technology, named Phash, to detect phishing web pages. The web pages are first converted into the images, and then the primary visual image pixels are extracted to form the perception hash sequence. After that, we make the hash sequence similarity match. This method not only overcomes the problem of phishing’s short survival time, but also makes the image similarity match quickly. The experiments show that the algorithm is effective, which greatly improves the matching speed ensuring the false positive rate and recall.
