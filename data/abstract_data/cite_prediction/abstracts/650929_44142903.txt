Multi-task convolutional neural network for car attribute recognition
This paper explores a multi-task Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) for vehicle attributes recognition. It can not only reduce the consumption of resources, but also reduce the total classification time. Besides, a single-task CNN for vehicle attributes recognition is proposed. We experiment with this different networks and try to understand their performance on my dataset. Vehicle attributes recognition are generally severely affected by environmental or contextual factors, such as occlusions and illuminations. While existing attribute datasets barely care about these factors. To tackle these problems, we build a Car Surveillance Scenarios (CSS) dataset from real multi-camera surveillance scenarios with long term collection. Data samples of CSS are annotated with not only fine-grained vehicle attributes but also environmental and contextual factors. The car's bounding box attaches to the contour of the car. The CSS dataset include 19,039 vehicle samples, each of which is annotated with 2 attributes. Experiments show that the multi-task CNN can be effectively used for vehicle attributes.
