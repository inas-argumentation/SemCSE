The Conversation: Deep Audio-Visual Speech Enhancement
The enhancement model consists of two modules: A phase and a magnitude sub-network: ● Input: Noisy audio & cropped video of lip region; Output: Clean audio ● Visual features are extracted with a spatio-temporal (3D & 2D convolutions) residual network. ● Linear scale spectrograms are extracted through STFT ● The magnitude sub-network predicts a soft-mask that filters the noisy magnitude spectrogram ● The phase sub-network produces a phase residual that corrects the noisy phase Our goal is to isolate individual voices in videos with multiple simultaneous speakers:
