Building a Semantic Ontology for Internet of Things (IoT) Systems
The complexity of Internet of Things (IoT) systems requires designers, operators, and users to understand both the structure and semantics embedded in the IoT architecture. A semantic IoT ontology must support a full understanding of system flows, services, and qualities (FSQ). In our research, we will discuss how sematic ontologies are currently utilized in IoT systems and how they could be extended to include semantic definitions for critical flows, services, and qualities in IoT applications. We suggest that a focus on the semantics of the system and the entities in the system be enriched with a definition and understanding of FSQ semantics. Examples of FSQ semantics in an actual IoT environment are presented and discussed.
