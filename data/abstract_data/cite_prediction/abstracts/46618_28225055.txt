Classification of human activities on UWB radar using a support vector machine
The classification of human activity using radar sensors has potential application in many different fields, including law enforcement, military and security operations. One unique way to classify human motion is the use of Ultra Wide Band (UWB) Radar. Work has been done to detect the presence of humans with UWB radar using the CLEAN algorithm [1]. For a more detailed analysis of human activities, micro-Doppler signatures [3] have been used to characterize human gait and other human activities. However, the potential for classifying different human activities using UWB radar has not been extensively studied yet. If the UWB radar can provide target motion information as well as range information, the effectiveness of the radar would be increased.
