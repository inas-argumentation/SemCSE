A Nonparametric Conjugate Prior Distribution for the Maximizing Argument of a Noisy Function

Previous work has focused on representing possible functions explicitly, whic h leads to a two-step procedure of first, doing inference over the function space and s econd, finding the extrema of these functions. Here we skip the representation s ep and directly model the distribution over extrema. To this end, we devise a non-parametric conjugate prior based on a kernel regressor. The resulting p osterior distribution directly captures the uncertainty over the maximum of the un known function.
