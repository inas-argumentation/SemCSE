A high-speed end-to-end approach for retinal arteriovenous segmentation
Retinal artery/vein segmentation plays a significant role in retinal relevant diseases diagnosis. This paper presents a novel, end-to-end and multi-task convolutional neural network structure for retinal arteriovenous segmentation task. The proposed method can simultaneously output the same size as the original image of the artery map and the vein map, respectively. Then we evaluate the neural network on the AVDRIVE dataset. This method achieves sensitivity values of 0.86, specificity values of 0.82, precision values of 0.87, F1score values of 0.86 and accuracy values of 0.94. In this case the time it cost on segment one image only needed 0.13 seconds, which are 60 times faster than other studies we cite.
