High Resolution CCD Polarization Imaging Sensor
We present a novel polarization imaging sensor by monolithically integrating aluminum nanowire optical filters with an array of CCD imaging elements. The CCD polarization image sensor is composed of 1000 by 1000 imaging elements with 7.4m pixel pitch. The image sensor has a dynamic range of 65dB and signal-to-noise ratio of 45dB. The CCD array is covered with an array of pixel-pitch matched nanowire polarization filters with four different orientations offset by 45. The complete imaging sensor is used for real-time reconstruction of the shape of various objects.
