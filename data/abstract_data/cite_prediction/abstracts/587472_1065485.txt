Detection of plant disease using threshold, k-mean cluster and ann algorithm
Indian economy highly depends on agricultural productivity. An important role is played by the detection of disease to obtain a perfect results in agriculture, and it is natural to have disease in plants. Proper care should be taken in this area for product quality and quantity. To reduce the large amount of monitoring in field automatic detection techniques can be used. This paper discuss different processes for segmentation technique which can be applied for different lesion disease detection. Thresholding and K-means cluster algorithms are done to detect different diseases in plant leaf.
