Detecting associations between parameters of printed Yagi antenna based on MIC
To achieve better antenna performance, the optimization algorithms, changing the parameters of antenna configuration, have been widely applied in antenna design field. However, different physical parameters have significantly different impact on the optimization results and the awareness of their influence will improve the optimization efficiency. In this paper, Maximal Information Coefficient (MIC) is utilized to detect pairwise correlations for configuration and performance parameters of printed Yagi antenna. Finally, the relationships between these parameters have been derived by analyzing the large data sets.
