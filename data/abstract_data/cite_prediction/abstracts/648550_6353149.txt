DTMC: an actionable e-customer lifetime value model based on markov chains and decision trees
We describe a model for estimating the customer lifetime value (CLV) of customers in an e-commerce environment. The model is explained and experiments are performed on real-life data from a large Dutch Internet retailer.
 Our method results in CLV estimates that have similar accuracy to estimates generated by the commonly used model, while keeping the number of customer segments much lower,and thus more 'actionable'.
