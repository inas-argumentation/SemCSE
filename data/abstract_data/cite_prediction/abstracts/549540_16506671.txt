A method of entity relationship modeling of Internet of Things based on supernetwork
Currently, the methods of entity relationship modeling are mainly based on existing historical data or Complex Networks, because one-dimensional elements characterizing clusters relationship can't be a true reflection of the entity relationship and potential structure of IOT(Internet of Things). This paper proposes a method of entity relationship modeling of IOT based on Supernetwork, by considering both dominant relationship and implicit relationship of interest similarity, we build a multidimensional model of entity relationship of IOT, and use the MIT Media Lab's real datasets to verify the feasibility and effectiveness of the model. It lays a solid foundation for further study of the dynamic growth of the entity relationship network.
