Efficient model-based 3D tracking of hand articulations using Kinect
The 3D tracking of the articulation of human hands is a theoretically interesting and challenging problem with numerous and diverse applications [1]. Several attempts have been made to address the problem by considering markerless visual data. Existing approaches can be categorized into modeland appearance-based [1]. In this work, we propose a model-based approach to the problem (Fig. 1). We formulate it as an optimization problem that minimizes the discrepancy between the 3D structure and appearance of hypothesized 3D hand model instances, and actual visual observations. Optimization is performed with a variant of an existing stochastic optimization method (Particle Swarm Optimization PSO) [2]. The most computationally demanding parts of the process have been implemented to run efficiently on a GPU [3]. The resulting system performs hand articulations tracking at a rate of 15Hz.
