Second iteration of slotted fractal log periodic dipole antenna (LPDA)
A compact log periodic antenna (LPDA) is presented in this paper. In this paper, a slotted second iteration of LPDA is demonstrated for size reduction that used for UHF digital TV band application. The proposed antenna operates from 0.5GHz to 1.5GHz for the reflection coefficient ≤ −10dB and maintain the gain of more than 5dBi. The antenna is designed on FR4 Epoxy dielectric substrate with the thickness of 1.6mm and relative permittivity of 4.6. The size of LPDA fractal Koch with slotted second iteration is successfully reduced by 26% as opposed to the size of the antenna in 2th iteration.
