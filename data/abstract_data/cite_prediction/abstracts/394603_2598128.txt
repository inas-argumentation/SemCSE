Thresholding Techniques applied for Segmentation of RGB and multispectral images
Image segmentation is a process of partitioning an image into a set of non-overlapping regions. The goal of segmentation is to simplify and/or change the representation of an image into something that is more meaningful and easier to analyze. In this paper we have segmented a RGB image and a multispectral image. The image has been segmented through three threshold techniques (i. e. Iterative threshold techniques, Otsu&apos;s threshold technique, Local Threshold Technique). Thresholding techniques are computationally simple and never fails to define disjoints regions with closed boundaries. Threshold technique is one of the important techniques in image segmentation.
