From Classical To Hip-Hop : Can Machines Learn Genres ?
The ability to classify the genre of a song is an easy task for the human ear. After listening to a song for just several seconds, it is often not difficult to take note of that song’s characteristics and subsequently identify this genre. However, this task is one that computers have historically not been able to solve well, at least not until the advent of more sophisticated machine learning techniques. Our team explored several of these techniques over the course of this project and successfully built a system that achieves 61.873% accuracy in classifying music genres. This paper discusses the methods we used for exploratory data analysis, feature selection, hyperparameter optimization, and eventual implementation of several algorithms for classification.
