Modified reduced switch symmetrical multilevel inverter
Multilevel inverters (MLI) are widely used at the range of high voltage and high power for industrial applications. It is because of low harmonic distortion, less electromagnetic interference and upper range of dc link voltages. But the following type of inverter has few disadvantages like difficulty in voltage balancing, higher number of components and complexity in pulse width modulation control method. Objective of this paper is to compare the existing topologies by designing a 7-level modified reduced switch symmetrical MLI (MRSMLI) with five switches and same voltage sources in each level. Simulation and comparison of seven-level seven-switch, seven-level six-switch and seven-level five-switch MLI will be done using MATLAB/SIMULINK environment. The results obtained from a five-switch MLI will be verified with FFT Analysis design of seven-switch MLI. Output obtained will be then utilized for control of an induction motor. Simulation results will be verified by designing hardware setup of the proposed MRSMLI.
