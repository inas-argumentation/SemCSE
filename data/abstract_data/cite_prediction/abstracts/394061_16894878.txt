Information Theoretic Learning with Infinitely Divisible Kernels
In this paper, we develop a framework for information theore tic l arning based on infinitely divisible matrices. We formulate an entropy-lik e functional on positive definite matrices based on Renyi’s axiomatic definition of en tropy and examine some key properties of this functional that lead to the conce pt of infinite divisibility. The proposed formulation avoids the plug in estimation of density and brings along the representation power of reproducing kernel Hilbe rt spaces. As an application example, we derive a supervised metric learning algo rithm using a matrix based analogue to conditional entropy achieving results co mparable with the state of the art.
