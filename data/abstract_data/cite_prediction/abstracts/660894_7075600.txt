A NOVEL SOFT-SWITCHING BUCK CONVERTER WITH COUPLED INDUCTOR Josna
A novel topology for a soft-switching buck dc– dc converter with a coupled inductor is proposed. The softswitching buck converter has advantages over the traditional hardswitching converters. The most significant advantage is that it offers a lower switching loss. This converter operates under a zero-current switching condition at turn on and a zerovoltage switching condition at turn off. It presents the circuit configuration with a least components for realizing soft switching. Because of soft switching, the proposed converter can attain a high efficiency under heavy load conditions. Likewise, a high efficiency is also attained under light load conditions, which is significantly different from other soft switching buck converters. Keywords— Buck converter, coupled inductor, soft switching, zero-current switching (ZCS), zero-voltage switching (ZVS).
