Dual band dual polarized conformai microstrip array for base stations
A new design of a 1×8 dual-band dual-polarized microstrip conformal array antenna, for GSM900/DCS1800 MHz Base Stations with the requirement of polarization diversity, is presented. The series feeding technique is used for feeding the array which is composed of aperture coupled microstrip antenna elements. The simulation results of conformal array antenna show a VSWR<; 2 and a gain of about 17dBi at both GSM (890-960 MHz) and DCS (1710-1880 MHz) bands. It also provides a good port decoupling, less than -30dB over its operating bands. One element of planar array is fabricated and measured and a good agreement between simulation and measurement results is achieved.
