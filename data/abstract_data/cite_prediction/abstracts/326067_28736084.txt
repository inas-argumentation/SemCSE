Facial Expression Recognition with Convolutional Neural Networks
Facial expression recognition systems have attracted much research interest within the field of artificial intelligence. Many established facial expression recognition (FER) systems apply standard machine learning to extracted image features, and these methods generalize poorly to previously unseen data. This project builds upon recent research to classify images of human faces into discrete emotion categories using convolutional neural networks (CNNs). We experimented with different architectures and methods such as fractional max-pooling and finetuning, ultimately achieving an accuracy of 0.48 in a sevenclass classification task.
