Continuous Requirements Engineering and Human-Centered Agile Software Development
The idea of Continuous Requirements Engineering in relation to a Human-Centered Agile Development Process is discussed. First, it is argued that Continuous Requirements Engineering has to cover design-time and runtime aspects. In this way maintenance is covered as well. Second, arguments are provided for integrating aspects of usability and user experience into requirements specifications. This has to be done continuously. Therefore, the term Continuous Human-Centered Development is introduced and discussed. Based on a process model for SCRUM some aspects of integrating HCD into the development process are discussed.
