Mutual Information in Learning Feature Transformations
Wepresentfeaturetransformationsusefulfor exploratorydataanalysisor for patternrecognition. Transformationsare learnedfrom exampledata setsby maximizing the mutual information betweentransformeddataandtheirclasslabels.We make useof Renyi’s quadraticentropy, andwe extendthe work of Principeet al. to mutual informationbetweencontinuousmultidimensional variablesanddiscrete-valuedclasslabels.
