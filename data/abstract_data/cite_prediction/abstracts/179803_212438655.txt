Simple Fire-like Animation Using Noise-based Dynamic Texture
In this paper, a new method is presented for animating fire using noise for wind approximation. Flame appearances such as combustion, separation, and flickering were achieved using noise-based texture generation fire primitives. P-type Fourier descriptors were then applied for modeling the motion of fire being blown by wind. The parameters (speed of combustion, scale, fire shape) can be tuned in real-time mode in our fire model due to the simplicity of realization based on a lack of physical modeling. Our simulation method is suitable for largeand small-scale fire behavior animation and is applicable to interactive applications.
