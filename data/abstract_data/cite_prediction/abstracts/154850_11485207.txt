Synthesis of Dance Performance Based on Analyses of Human Motion and Music
Recently, demands for synthesizing realistic human motions are rapidly increasing in computer graphics (CG) and robotics fields. In most cases, human movements are induced by external signals such as visual/audio information. Considering these aspects makes it possible to automatically synthesize more human-like motion, and, despite this possibility, only a few methods considering these aspects have been developed. To achieve this, we are focusing on dance performance as an experimental subject, and propose three methods that aim at realizing dancing-to-music ability for CG characters and humanoid robots.
