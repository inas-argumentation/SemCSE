Dockless Bike-Sharing Reallocation Based on Data Analysis: Solving Complex Problem with Simple Method
Dockless bike-sharing service is currently revolutionizing the traditional bike-sharing market and it saves on strat-up costs because it gives up the fixed docking stations. This innovation also opens up a new window for applying operations research techniques based on data analysis. Given the growing importance of bike-sharing system nowadays, in this paper we propose an integrated method for reallocating dockless bikes after a whole day operation. Initially, we collect and preprocess the GPS data of dockless bikes and cluster these bikes into several groups. Then we formulate the reallocation of bikes to be an integer programming model. We consider two scenarios, a workday and a weekend day, in our numerical test. The results demonstrate that our method can provide tangible policy recommendations for managing the dockless bikes.
