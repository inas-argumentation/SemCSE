European standards for the documentation of historic buildings and their relationship with CIDOC-CRM
Integration of architectural datasets concerning historic buildings depends on their interoperability, which has as first step a mapping to a common schema. The paper investigates current approaches and proposes mapping to a CIDOC-CRM extension as the common glue to overcome the fragmentation of datasets provided by large national institutions such as MIBAC in Italy, EH in the UK, and so on, and by EU projects, each one structured according to a different metadata schema. The paper describes the mapping of the MA-CA MIBAC-ICCD schemas, probably the most comprehensive, to CRM.
