On Developing a Technology Acceptance Model for Pervasive Computing
Since the majority of proposed pervasive computing applications rely heavily on infrastructure that is not yet widely available, understanding longterm usage and acceptance is of utmost importance for determining if a technology/application is worth the required investment. Yet performing such an evaluation is difficult before the necessary infrastructure is available. We propose developing a mathematical model to predict user acceptance over time of pervasive computing applications based on the user’s perception of usefulness, ease-of-use, social influence, trustworthiness and integration. This paper is a first step towards a predictive model of user acceptance in pervasive computing environments. We conclude the paper outlining the next steps to fully instantiate the model. Ultimately, the model can be used to direct research efforts and resources towards those applications most likely to be used by people.
