MOON: A Mixed Objective Optimization Network for the Recognition of Facial Attributes
Overview Facial attributes have implicit latent correlations – which are naturally learnt through a multi-label approach. Due to demographic correlations, it is difficult to obtain a balanced multi-label facial attribute training set; thus a multi-label classifier trained for one demographic might perform poorly on another due to training set biases. CelebA, for example, contains images of celebrities where Young is over-represented and anti-correlated attributes like Bald and Gray Hair are under-represented. Label Bias of the CelebA Dataset
