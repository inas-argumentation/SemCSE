A wideband dual-polarized dipole linear array with resistive loading
A dual-polarized, tightly-coupled dipole linear array, delivering 5.7:1 bandwidth, is presented. Most of previous work on tightly-coupled array is focused on two-dimensional array. This communication presents a dual-polarized, tightly-coupled dipole linear array, which has much more potential in application. The linear array incorporates a resistive loop as the substrate load to broaden the bandwidth of both polarization. For vertical polarized dipole of linear array, lacking tightly-coupled environment, there are sever methods implemented to broaden the bandwidth, such as gradient slot and folded arms. Simulated results show that the linear array can achieve 5.7:1 bandwidth while scan to ±50°, for VSWR<;3(sever frequency <;3.5), and the radiation efficiency maintain above 50%.
