Effective or Efficient ? Effects of Beijing ’ s Vehicle Lottery System on Fleet Composition and Environment *
To control vehicle growth and air pollution, Beijing’s municipal government imposed a vehicle lottery system in 2011, which randomly allocated a quota of licenses to potential buyers. This paper investigates the effect of this policy on fleet composition, fuel consumption, air pollution, and social welfare. Using car registration data, we estimate a random coefficient discrete choice model and conduct counterfactual analysis based on the estimated parameters.
