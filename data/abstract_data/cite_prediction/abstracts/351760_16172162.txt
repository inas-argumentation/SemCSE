Fruit and vegetable consumption and waste in Australia Recommendations towards a food supply system framework
Recommendations towards a food supply system framework that will deliver healthy food in a sustainable way. In 2007, Emily Morgan was one of fifteen Americans to be granted a Fulbright Postgraduate Scholarship to Australia. A Tufts University postgraduate student and former Mount Holyoke College graduate, Emily carried out her Fulbright research on the relationship between food, health and the environment. This project was completed at VicHealth, under the direction of nutrition promotion and food policy expert Dr Tony Worsley and in collaboration with the School of Exercise and Nutrition Sciences at Deakin University.
