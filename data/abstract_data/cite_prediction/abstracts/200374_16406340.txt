Impact of Utilitarian and Hedonic Shopping Values on Individual ’ s Perceived Benefits and Risks in Online Shopping
[Abstract] An individual’s overall perceived shopping value has two dimensions – utilitarian and hedonic. Utilitarian shopping value relates to the functional aspects of the shopping context. Hedonic shopping value is derived from the perceived fun or playfulness of the shopping. An individual also assumes some benefits and risks in any shopping context. The e-shopping tendency is increasing rapidly among buyers across the world, which is the focus of this study. This study investigates how the individual buyer’s perceived benefits and risks in e-shopping are influenced by his or her perceived utilitarian or hedonic shopping values.
