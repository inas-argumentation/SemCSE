Evaluating search features of Google Knowledge Graph and Bing Satori: Entity types, list searches and query interfaces
Purpose – The purpose of this study is to enhance our understanding of three main aspects of semantic search engines of Google Knowledge Graph and Bing Satori. To this end we investigated 1) coverage of entity type, 2) the extent of their support for list search services and 3) the capabilities of their natural language query interfaces.
