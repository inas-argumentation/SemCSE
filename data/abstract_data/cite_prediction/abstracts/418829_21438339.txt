Features-level Sentiment Analysis of Movie Reviews
Most sentiment analysis of reviews focused on extracting overall evaluation for a single product which makes difficult for a customer to know all the features of product and make a decision. Thus, mining this data, identifying the user opinions about different features and classify them is an important task. This paper is devoted to identify opinion object from short comments of movies, and analyze sentiment of movies based on features-level. CRFs model based on word embedding feature is adopted by identifying opinion object. In addition, calculate rules based on syntax parsing are proposed to accomplish featureslevel sentiment analysis. Experimental results show the effectiveness of our approach.
