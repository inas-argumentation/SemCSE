Color based vehicle classification using road surveillance camera images
In this study, we propose novel methods for color based vehicle classification using roadway surveillance camera images. Proposed approach utilizes single shot multibox detector to localize the vehicle and parts of the vehicle within roadway surveillance camera images. Next, color features are extracted around the detected regions to infer the color of the vehicle. Experiments are conducted using 437 images that contain a large variation in terms of reflection, shadow and other illumination effects. We achieved an overall accuracy of % 80 using test dataset.
