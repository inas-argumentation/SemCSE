1 Personalized E-learning System Architecture Using 2 Data Mining Approach 3
Educational data mining is an emerging discipline that focuses on development of 9 self-learning and adaptive methods. It is used for finding hidden patterns or intrinsic structures of 10 educational data. In the field of education, the heterogeneous data is involved and continuously 11 growing in the paradigm of big data. To extract meaningful knowledge adaptively from big 12 educational data, some specific data mining techniques are needed. This paper presents a 13 personalized e-learning system architecture which detects and responds teaching contents 14 according to the students’ learning capabilities. Furthermore, the clustering approach is also 15 presented to partition the students into different groups based on their learning behavior. The 16 primary objective includes the discovery of optimal settings, in which learners can improve their 17 learning capabilities to boost up their outcomes. Moreover, the administration can find essential 18 hidden patterns to bring the effective reforms in the existing system. The various clustering 19 methods K-means, Clustering by Fast Search and Finding of Density Peaks (CFSFDP), and CFSFDP 20 via Heat Diffusion (CFSFDP-HD) are also analyzed using educational data mining. It is observed 21 that more robust results can be achieved by the replacement of K-means with CFSFDP and 22 CFSFDP-HD. The proposed e-learning system using data mining techniques is vigorous compared 23 to typical e-learning systems. The data mining techniques are equally effective to analyze the big 24 data to make education systems robust. 25
