Solid state transformer application to grid connected photovoltaic inverters
In the paper, an architecture, including a solid state transformer (SST) which is different from the conventional style is proposed The photovoltaic system with SST consists of two power stages. A dual half bridge (DHB) converter constitutes the first power stage, which aims to achieve the maximum power point tracking (MPPT) functions. A grid connected inverter is used to stabilize the DC bus voltage and make the output current sinusoidal. The control technique is presented and validated by simulation implemented on a photovoltaic system with SST. The simulation results prove the effectiveness of the proposed photovoltaic system and the proposed control technique.
