Impact of Technology on Teens' Written Language
Information technologies are having a profound effect on all aspects of life, language is no exception. Digital technology has become an increasingly popular means of communication; however, there are worries that this trend is having a detrimental effect on the writing skills of students. In this study, the relationship of new technologies to writing and its implications is reviewed; case studies and existing research on the effects of electronic communication on literacy and language development more specifically on English writing among teens are evaluated. Finally solutions are proposed with regard to future trends of technology-intensive writing.
