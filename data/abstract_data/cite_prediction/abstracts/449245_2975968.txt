1 BAGGING AND BOOSTING CLASSIFICATION TREES TO PREDICT CHURN
In this paper, the bagging and boosting classification techniques are brought to the attention of marketing researchers. Applied to a customer database of an anonymous U.S. wireless telecom company, bagging — as well as boosting — is proven to significantly improve accuracy in predicting churn. This higher predictive performance could ultimately lead to incremental profit for companies that would be willing to use these methods. Furthermore, results illustrate that the use of a balanced sampling scheme is recommended when predicting a rare event from large datasets, but consequently requires an appropriate bias correction.
