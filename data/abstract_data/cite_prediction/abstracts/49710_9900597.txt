The Use of Information Retrieval Techniques for Intrusion Detection
Intrusion detection is a broad problem, and we need a greater range of tools than is currently available. In this article, we report a new approach. We have applied information retrieval techniques to index audit trails. These indexes can be extremely e cient at detecting attacks whose signature is an unusual combination of events, and they may consume only a very small additional amount of storage. This approach allows the intrusion detection community to adopt a wide range of techniques developed in applications ranging from library science to web search engines.
