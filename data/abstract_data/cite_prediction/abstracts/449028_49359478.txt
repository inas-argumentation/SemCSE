Most European cities are characterized by very large areas, often formed by buildings with 12 low quality, from a series of point of view. The possibility of renovating them is strategic to improve 13 both the quality of life and to the possibility of economic recovery for building companies. In the 14 last decades, the attention of the scientific community has been addressed to the energy renovation, 15 thanks to the strong activities of the European Community in this field. However, since a relevant 16 part of the EC territory is at risk of earthquake, the possibility to combine both energy and seismic 17 renovation actions may be strategic for many countries. In particular, Italy and Romania are linked 18 by a common social tradition that springs from the Roman Empire. Nowadays, this link is stronger, 19 thanks to common interests in social, cultural and business fields. Therefore, the investigation of 20 possible synergies for seismic and energy renovation strategies may be really interesting for both 21 countries. This paper represents the first step in this direction. After an overview of regulations and 22 common practices for buildings with reinforced concrete structures, in both states, some key com23 bined renovation interventions will be described and discussed, as well as advantages and perspec24 tives of integrated renovation approaches. 25
