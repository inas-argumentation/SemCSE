Layered architecture for DoS attack detection system by combine approach of Naive bayes and Improved K-means Clustering Algorithm
The aim of a DoS attack is to consume the resources of a victim or the resources on the way to communicate with a victim. By wasting the victim’s resources, the attacker disallows it from serving legitimate customers. A victim can be a host, server, router, or any computing entity connected to the network. DoS attack can cause harm to these computer and network services. Therefore, effective detection of DoS attacks is essential to the protection of network and resources. Detection System is built by using layered frame work approach for an effective attack detection system.
