An overview of legged and wheeled robotic locomotion
Many of the unique abilities that robots possess come from the fact that they are often made mobile. Achieving the mobility required is however rarely done without difficulty. The terrain over which robots must be able to move is often uneven, slippery or muddy, which gives rise to many challenges, particularity stability. Two of the most common methods for robot mobility are wheels and legs. Many experimental robots have been developed using both of these methods. This paper makes an overview of some of the robots from each category, both wheeled robots and legged robots, and accounts for the advantages and disadvantages of each. Tracked robots are also covered.
