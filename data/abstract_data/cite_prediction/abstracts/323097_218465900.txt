11 Abstract. The present studies examine how culturally held stereotypes about gender (that women eat more healthfully than men) implicitly 12 influence food preferences. In Study 1, priming masculinity led both male and female participants to prefer unhealthy foods, while priming 13 femininity led both male and female participants to prefer healthy foods. Study 2 extended these effects to gendered food packaging.
