Partitioned residual echo power estimation for frequency-domain acoustic echo cancellation and postfiltering
Residual echo arises in hands–free telephony equipment due to insufficient echo canceler convergence, but can be suppressed using a postfilter. The residual echo po wer spectral density is the most crucial control parameter for both frequency–domain acoustic echo cancellation and c ombined residual echo and noise postfiltering. In this contribution we present and compare residual echo power spectr al estimation techniques. We introduce a new partitioned block-adaptive estimation technique delivering consider ably improved residual echo estimates in strongly reverber ant and noisy acoustic environments. We show that the adaptation lo p of the frequency–domain adaptive filter (FDAF) can be used simultaneously for residual echo power estimation and tracking of the echo path impulse response. In this way, the FDAF and the postfilter concept supplement each other in a tru e synergy with low complexity. The resulting echo and noise control system proves to be robust in double talk situa tions as well.
