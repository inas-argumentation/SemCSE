Sub-2-Sub: Self-Organizing Content-Based Publish Subscribe for Dynamic Large Scale Collaborative Networks
In this paper, we address the problem of constructing scalable content-based publish/subscribe systems. Publish/subscribe systems are asynchronous event-notification systems in which a published event is forwarded to exactly those nodes that have previously subscribed for that event. Subscriptions can range from a simple specification of merely the type of an event to a specification of the value ranges that an event’s attributes can have. Notably the latter poses potential scalability problems. Structured peer-to-peer systems can provide scalable solutions to publish/subscribe systems with simple subscription patterns. For complex subscription types their applicability is less obvious. In this paper, we present SUB-2-SUB, a collaborative self-organizing publish/subscribe system deploying an unstructured overlay network. SUB-2-SUB relies on an epidemic-based algorithm in which peers continuously exchange subscription information to get clustered to similar peers. In contrast to many existing approaches, SUB-2-SUB supports both value-based and interval-based subscriptions. Simulations ofSUB-2-SUB on synthetic and reusable workloads convey its good properties in terms of routing efficiency, fairness, accuracy
