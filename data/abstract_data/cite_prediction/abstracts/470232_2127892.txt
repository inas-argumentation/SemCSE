An effective, low-cost measure of semantic relatedness obtained from Wikipedia links
relatedness between terms using the links found within their corresponding Wikipedia articles. Unlike other techniques based on Wikipedia, WLM is able to provide accurate measures efficiently, using only the links between articles rather than their textual content. Before describing the details, we first outline the other systems to which it can be compared. This is followed by a description of the algorithm, and its evaluation against manually-defined ground truth. The paper concludes with a discussion of the strengths and weaknesses of the new approach. Abstract
