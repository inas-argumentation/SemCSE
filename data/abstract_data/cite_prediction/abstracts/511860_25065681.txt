Perspectives on usability guidelines for smartphone applications: An empirical investigation and systematic literature review
Context: Several usability guidelines have been proposed to improve the usability of smartphone apps. These guidelines can be classified into three disjoint sets: platform specific guidelines, genre specific guidelines, and generic guidelines. However, smartphone applications are usually developed for multiple platforms and targeted for a variety of users. Hence the usefulness of existing guidelines is severally limited. Objective: This study aims to develop a comprehensive list of usability guidelines suitable for multiple platforms and genres of smartphone applications.
