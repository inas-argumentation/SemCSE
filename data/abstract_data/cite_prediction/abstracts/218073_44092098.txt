The Seven Pillars of Causal Reasoning with Reflections on Machine Learning
Systems that operate in purely statistical mode of inference entails theoretical limits on their power and performance. Such systems cannot reason about interventions and retrospection and, therefore, cannot serve as the basis for strong AI. To achieve human-level intelligence, learning machines need the guidance of a model of external reality, similar to the ones used in causal inference tasks. To demonstrate the essential role of such models, this paper presents a summary of seven tasks which are beyond reach of associational learning systems and which have been accomplished using the tools of causal modeling.
