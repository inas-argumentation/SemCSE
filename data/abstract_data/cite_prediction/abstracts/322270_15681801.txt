A Survey of Clustering with Instance Level Constraints 1 2
Clustering with constraints is an important recent develop ment in the clustering literature. The addition of constraints allows users to incorporate domain expertise into the clustering process by explicitly specifying what are desirable properties in a clustering solution. This is part icularly useful for applications in domains where considerable domain expertise already exists. In this first extens iv survey of the field, we discuss the uses, benefits and importantly the problems associated with using constraint s. We cover approaches that make use of constraints for partitional and hierarchical algorithms to both enforc e the constraints or to learn a distance function from the constraints.
