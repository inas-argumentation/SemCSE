Efficient Combining Keypoints and Correlation Filter for Long-Term Tracking
I72 this paper, we propose a tracker (CKCF) to tackle the challenges of long-term tracking where the target encounter occlusion, deformation. For these problems, we decompose the task of the long-term tracking into translation filter and re-detector, where the re-detector consists of point tracking, a simple method for estimating location and GMS (Grid-based Motion Statistics) filter and it is activated in case of tracking failure, which is detected by computing a score. Finally, the CKCF was evaluated on OTB2015 datasets and the experimental results show that our tracker outperforms favorably many state-of-art trackers in term of speed (∼50 FPS), accuracy and robustness, especially occlusion is encountered.
