A Survey on Parts of Speech Tagging for Indian Languages
Part of speech (POS) tagging is basically the process of automatically assigning its lexical category to each word according to its context and definition. Each word of sentence is marked in croups as corresponding to a particular part of speech like noun, verb, adjective and adverb. POS serves as a first step in natural language process applications like information extraction, parsing, and word sense disambiguation etc. this paper presents a survey on Part of Speech taggers used for Indian languages. The main problem of tagging is to find proper way to tag each word according to particular part of speech.
