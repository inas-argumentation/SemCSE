1 Integration of Data Mining Clustering Approach with 2 the Personalized E-Learning System 3
Educational data-mining is an evolving discipline that focuses on the improvement of 10 self-learning and adaptive methods. It is used for finding hidden patterns or intrinsic structures of 11 educational data. In the arena of education, the heterogeneous data is involved and continuously 12 growing in the paradigm of big-data. To extract meaningful information adaptively from big 13 educational data, some specific data mining techniques are needed. This paper presents a 14 clustering approach to partition students into different groups or clusters based on their learning 15 behavior. Furthermore, personalized e-learning system architecture is also presented which detects 16 and responds teaching contents according to the students’ learning capabilities. The primary 17 objective includes the discovery of optimal settings, in which learners can improve their learning 18 capabilities. Moreover, the administration can find essential hidden patterns to bring the effective 19 reforms in the existing system. The clustering methods K-Means, K-Medoids, Density-based 20 Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise, Agglomerative Hierarchical Cluster Tree and 21 Clustering by Fast Search and Finding of Density Peaks via Heat Diffusion (CFSFDP − HD) are 22 analyzed using educational data mining. It is observed that more robust results can be achieved by 23 the replacement of existing methods with CFSFDP-HD. The data mining techniques are equally 24 effective to analyze the big data to make education systems vigorous. 25
