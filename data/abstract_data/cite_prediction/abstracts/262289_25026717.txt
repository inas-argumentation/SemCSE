ScholarBase: Towards a Cross-Domain Knowledgebase for Linked Scholarly Data
Linked Data has gained a significant momentum over the last years through various initiatives embraced by academia and industry as well. In this paper, we introduce ScholarBase, a research project in progress that is aimed to serve as a Linked Data repository for cross-domain scholarly data. ScholarBase can be conceived as a knowledgebase that weaves links among scholars, institutions, research areas, publications, and geographical locations in a Linked Data fashion. Initially, the primary source of data stems from Google Scholar, specifically the profile pages of scholars. Subsequently, the collected dataset is transformed into a semantic-based format (i.e. RDF). The semantified dataset will enable a machine-readable expression of entity relationships, and can in turn be linked to external knowledge bases (e.g. DBpedia). Through the paper, we discuss the architecture and implementation of the project. ScholarBase can play an important role in the Linked Data web by opening extended opportunities for interesting applications, and data exploration of scholarly data.
