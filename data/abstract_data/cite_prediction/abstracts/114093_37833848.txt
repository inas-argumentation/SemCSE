An Extended Model of IT Governance: A Conceptual Proposal
Although a significant amount of work has be done on the subject of IT governance there still appears to be some disjoint and confusion about what IT governance really is and how it may be realized in practice. This research-in-progress paper draws on existing research related to IT governance to provide a more in-depth understanding of the concept. It describes the current understanding of IT governance and argues for the extension and further development of the various dimensions of governance. An extended model of IT governance is proposed. A research agenda is outlined.
