Low voltage area efficient current-mode CMOS bandgap reference in deep submicron technology
We report on the design of a low voltage area efficient current-mode CMOS bandgap reference implemented in 130 nm technology. The conventional voltage-mode and current-mode bandgap reference architectures with their properties and performance limiting factors are described. Due to the low supply voltage V<sub>dd</sub> requirement the current-mode architecture was chosen. The simulated power dissipation P<sub>diss</sub> = 150 μW at the nominal supply voltage V<sub>DD</sub> = 1.2 V. As a result of Monte-Carlo analysis the average output reference current I<sub>REF</sub> ≈ 1 μA and its standard deviation σ = 11 nA are obtained. The reference current changes ΔI<sub>REF</sub> = 8 nA over the temperature range from 0 <sup>o</sup>C up to 100 <sup>o</sup>C. The reference current changes ΔI<sub>ref</sub> = 4 nA for the supply voltage V<sub>dd</sub> within the range from 1 V up to 1.5 V. The silicon chip area occupation is 0.07 mm<sup>2</sup>.
