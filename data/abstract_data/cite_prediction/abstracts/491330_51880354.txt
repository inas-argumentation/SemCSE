GTR-LSTM: A Triple Encoder for Sentence Generation from RDF Data
“Walk east on Flinders St/State Route 30 towards Market St; Turn right onto St Kilda Rd/Swanston St” vs. “Walk east on Flinders St/State Route 30 towards Market St; Turn right onto St Kilda Rd/Swanston St after Flinders Street Station, a yellow building with a green dome.” T1: <Flinders Street Station, front, Federation Square> T2: <Flinders Street Station, color, yellow> T3: <Flinders Street Station, has, green dome> Sent: Flinders Street Station is a yellow building with a green dome roof located in front of Federation Square
