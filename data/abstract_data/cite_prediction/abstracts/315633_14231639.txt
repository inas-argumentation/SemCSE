Notes on Hierarchical Splines, DCLNs and i-theory
We define an extension of classical additive splines for multivariate function approximation that we call hierarchical splines. We show that the case of hierarchical, additive, piece-wise linear splines includes present-day Deep Convolutional Learning Networks (DCLNs) with linear rectifers and pooling (sum or max). We discuss how these observations together with i-theory may provide a framework for a general theory of deep networks.
