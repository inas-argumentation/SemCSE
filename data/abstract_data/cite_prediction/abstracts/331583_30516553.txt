Investigating the global trend of RF power amplifiers with the arrival of 5G
To satisfy the continuously increasing demand for high data rates and mobility required by new wireless applications, the 5G has gained much attention recently. Radio frequency power amplifiers (RF-PAs), as one of critical components of 5G transmitter system, are becoming a hot issue. In this paper, the statistical analysis on RF PA papers shows the research on RF-PAs in Asia-Pacific and cooperation between different affiliations and countries are gradually becoming more prominent, showing the globalization trend of RF PA research and the 5G technologies. The decreased research cycle of RF PA shows the processes of research on PA and 5G technologies are speeding up. Some promising RF-PA technologies for 5G wireless communication system are also been discussed.
