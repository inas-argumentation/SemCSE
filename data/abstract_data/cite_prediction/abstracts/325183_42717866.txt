The On/Off (LMCA) Dual Arabic Handwriting Database
For   many   years   ago,   handwriting   is   a   challenger interest,   both   online   and   off   line   recognition   of   the handwriting   are   largely   presented   in   literature.   Latin script   studies  were   largely  presented  using  UNIPEN or IRONOFF   databases.   But   Arabic   studies   were   poorly presented   in   literature   because   there   are   no   common Arabic  data bases.   In   this  paper,  we present  an Arabic data base which called LMCA (in Frensh  it   is  "Lettres, Mots et Chiffres Arabe") and composed of letters, words and digits. We present also some related works using this database. Note that LCMA database can be used in online or in off line recognition of the Arabic handwritten script.
