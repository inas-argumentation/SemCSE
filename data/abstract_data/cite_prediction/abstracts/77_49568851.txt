Differential quasi self-complimentary (QSC) ultra-wideband (UWB) MIMO antenna
In this paper, a differentially excited ultra-wideband (UWB) MIMO antenna is designed for pattern diversity applications, utilizing quasi self-complimentary (QSC) antenna elements. Four QSC elements are realized using half- octagon shaped monopoles, and their complementary cuts from the ground plane. Due to the QSC property, wide impedance bandwidth covering the UWB frequency range of 3–11 GHz is achieved within very less antenna footprint. The QSC elements are placed symmetrically about the four edges of a square substrate. Two oppositely positioned elements are fed with 180o phase difference, in order to form a single differential pair. Two such differential pairs are placed perpendicular to each other for realizing pattern diversity performance with high value of differential isolation.
