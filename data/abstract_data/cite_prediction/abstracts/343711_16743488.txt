A COMPARATIVE STUDY OF SENTIMENT ANALYSIS TECHNIQUES 1
The growth of social web contributes vast amount of user generated content such as customer reviews, comments and opinions. This user generated content can be about products, people, events, etc. This information is very useful for businesses, governments and individuals. While this content meant to be helpful analyzing this bulk of user generated content is difficult and time consuming. So there is a need to develop an intelligent system which automatically mine such huge content and classify them into positive, negative and neutral category. Sentiment analysis is the automated mining of attitudes, opinions, and emotions from text, speech, and database sources through Natural Language Processing (NLP). The objective of this paper is to discover the concept of Sentiment Analysis in the field of Natural Language Processing, and presents a comparative study of its techniques in this field. Keywords— Natural Language Processing, Sentiment Analysis, Sentiment Lexicon, Sentiment Score.
