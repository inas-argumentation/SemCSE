Convergence Rates of Inexact Proximal-Gradient Methods for Convex Optimization
Efficient to compute in some notable cases: ✦ L1-regularization ✦ Disjoint Group L1-regularization ✦ Can be approximated in other important scenarios: ✦ Total-variation regularization and graph-guided fused LASSO ✦ Nuclear-norm regularization and other singular value penalties ✦ Different formulations of overlapping group L1-regularization ✦ We show that the convergence rates are preserved if the errors in the proximity and gradient decrease at appropriate rates. ✦Assumptions: ✦ g is convex and has L-Lipschitz continuous gradient:
