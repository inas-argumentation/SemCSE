Chapter 9 Applications 9.1 Overview 9.1.1 Motivation
In this chapter, we apply our model with object typicality to recommendation system and propose a typicality-based recommendation approach named ROT and a typicality-based collaborative filtering approach named TyCo, which are different from previous recommendation methods. To the best of our knowledge, there is no work on applying typicality to recommender systems. In general, current recommendation methods can be classified into content-based, collaborative filtering and hybrid methods. These methods are based on similarity measurements among items or users. In this chapter, we investigate recommendation systems from a new perspective based on object typ-icality and propose two novel typicality-based recommendation approaches. Following the development of the World Wide Web and electronic commerce, more and more information and items exist on the Web. It is difficult for users to find out what they exactly want or their favorite items among such huge amount items, as a result recommender system becomes popular. The task of a recommender system is to estimate preference or ratings for the items that have not been seen by a user, and then decide whether to recommend the item to the user or not. There have been a lot of works done in both industry and academia on the development of recommender system in the past decade.
