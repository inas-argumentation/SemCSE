A broadband dual-polarized magneto-electric dipole antenna
A new type of dual-polarized planar antenna excited by two Gamma-shaped strips is designed and simulated. The antenna has about 66.7% (SWRLt2) impedance bandwidth and the isolation is less than -32.5 dB over the whole impedance bandwidth. The antenna yields the maximum gain of 9.8 dBi. And the radiation pattern across the operating bandwidth is stable.
