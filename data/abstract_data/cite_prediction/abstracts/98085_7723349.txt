Simox: A Robotics Toolbox for Simulation, Motion and Grasp Planning
Simox is a C++ robotics toolbox offering a comprehensive framework for robot simulation and visualization, motion and grasp planning. The expandable open-source project (http://simox.sf.net) hosts a wide range of algorithms that are needed for setting up a virtual representation of a robot and its environment. The framework explicitly supports robot systems with a high number of degrees of freedom (DoF), such as mobile manipulators or humanoid robots. Further, Simox comprises state-of-the-art algorithms for motion and grasp planning that allow to efficiently plan collision-free motions and to automatically compute grasp sets for arbitrary objects and end effectors. The library is successfully employed on the robots iCub [1] and ARMAR-III [2].
