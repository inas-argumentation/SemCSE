Policy Gradient Methods for Reinforcement Learning with Function Approximation and Action-Dependent Baselines
1 Notation and Background We assume that the reader is familiar with the seminal paper by Sutton et al. (2000), which shows how the policy gradient theorem (Baxter and Bartlett, 1999) can be extended to include function approximation and action-independent baselines. Our paper is intended to be read immediately after reviewing Section 3 of the paper by Sutton et al. (2000). Although here we adopt the episodic setting, the extension of this work to the average reward setting is straightforward.
