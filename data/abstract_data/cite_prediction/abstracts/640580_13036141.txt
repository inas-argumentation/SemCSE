Filtering, Negotiating and Shifting in the Understanding of Information System Requirements
Traditional requirement engineering approaches pay little attention to how the requirements are interpreted and shared by different parties in an organization. Our study extends the previous research of social and organizational requirement elicitation by suggesting that requirement shaping during a project can be described as a process where attitudes and expectations are filtered, shifted and negotiated repeatedly.
