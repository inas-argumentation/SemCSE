Using an Intermediate Skeleton and Inverse Kinematics for Motion Retargeting
In this paper , we presenta new methodfor solving the Motion Retargeting Problem,by usingan intermediate skeleton.Thisallowsus to convert movementsbetweenhierarchically andgeometricallydifferentcharacters. An InverseKinematicsengineis thenusedto enforceCartesianconstraintswhile stayingascloseaspossibleto the capturedmotion.
