Assortment Optimization Under General Choice
We consider the static assortment optimization problem – a central decision problem faced by operations managers that has been widely studied in the literature. The decision deals with finding the assortment of products (from a larger universe of products) that maximizes the expected revenue subject to a constraint on the size of the assortment.
