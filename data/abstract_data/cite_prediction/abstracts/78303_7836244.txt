UCB revisited: Improved regret bounds for the stochastic multi-armed bandit problem
In the stochastic multi-armed bandit problem we consider a modification of the UCB algorithm of Auer et al. [4]. For this modified algorithm we give an improved bound on the regret with respect to the optimal reward. While for the original UCB algorithm the regret in Karmed bandits after T trials is bounded by const · K log(T ) ∆ , where ∆ measures the distance between a suboptimal arm and the optimal arm, for the modified UCB algorithm we show an upper bound on the regret of const · K log(T∆ 2) ∆ .
