Shuffled Frog Leaping Algorithm Based Clustering Algorithm for Mobile Ad hoc Networks
Mobile Ad Hoc Networks (MANET) consists of a set of mobile node that these networks have dynamic topology and the nodes connected via wireless links. The nodes are grouped in clusters for handle various operations. In this paper, we propose a Shuffled frog leaping algorithm Based Clustering Algorithm (SBCA) for mobile ad hoc networks. We show how Shuffled Frog Leaping Algorithm (SFLA) can be useful in raising the performance of clustering algorithm. It has the ability to find the optimal or near-optimal clusterheads to efficiently manage the resources of the network. The SFLA is derived from a virtual population of frogs that each frog contains information about clusterheads and ordinary nodes. It is based on the evolution of memes carried by individuals and a global exchange of information among the population. This study also utilized a genetic algorithm operator called the single point crossover operator for generate new offspring in SFLA. The proposed Clustering Algorithm considers the Degree-difference, sum of the distance, average of the speed, total time and battery remaining in order to calculate the node’s weight. The propose algorithm was compared with WCA and L-ID in terms of reaffiliation, dominant set update and load balancing factors. The simulation result show that our algorithm performs better than WCA and L_ID. KeywordsMANET, Shuffled Frog Leaping Algorithm, Clusterhead, crossover operator, evolution
