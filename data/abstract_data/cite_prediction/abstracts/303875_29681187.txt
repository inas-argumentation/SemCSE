Expert opinion: "1 Voice": Learn Urinary Tract Dilation (UTD) classification to assess fetal & newborn urological ultrasound findings.
This CEV-ule is a natural sequel to the JPU publication on UTD classification. It hosts fun inter-actives to learn this method. By using this application, it is reasonable that pediatric urological, pediatric, and Obstetric colleagues will learn the method and show they have learned. Once learned, prospective research may be done to define pediatric outcomes. Data inputs will be credible as assessments will be similar within and across institutions. The authors have clarified the UTD A classification by re-labeling risk for UTD A2-3 now as UTD A3. The concept of “risk” and likelihood is not yet defined in the UTD system. Despite this temporary drawback, prenatal risk of “increased APRPD” may be accessed using the risk reckoner [1].
