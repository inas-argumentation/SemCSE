virtual tryon system in augmented reality using RGB-D cameras or footwear personalization
This paper presents a system for design evaluation of footwear using commercial depth-sensing technologies. In a mixed reality environment, the system allows users to virtually try on 3D shoe models in a live video stream. A two-stage object tracking algorithm was developed to correctly align shoe models to moving feet during the try-on process. Color markers on the user’s foot enabled markerless tracking.
