The 10 kVA SiC-based grid connected AC/DC converter with extended functionalities — Experimental investigation
The paper presents a SiC-based 10 kVA AC/DC converter and its experimental verification during various operation modes when is connected to the 3x 400V grid. Besides a standard feature of the AC/DC converter - bidirectional power flow - new functionalities are enabled: a compensation of highorder harmonics and reactive power, a fast response to step load changes. These features are available due applied control method based on Direct Power Control and the elevated switching frequency (40 kHz). Finally, the conducted measurements show that for the discussed AC/DC converter peak efficiency close to 98.75% is available.
