Re-engineering the ISO 15926 Data Model: A Multi-level Metamodel Perspective
The ISO 15926 standard was developed to facilitate the integration of life-cycle data of process plants. The core of the standard is a highly generic and extensible data model. However the data model is replete with confusing terminology, circular definitions and inconsistencies that make the standard difficult to understand and less likely to be adopted in practice. In this paper we look at re-engineering the ISO 15926 data model as a multilevel metamodel, and aim to (re-)formalise critical aspects of the data model which we believe simplifies the model and eases the adoption process.
