Identifying Raga Similarity in Hindustani Classical Music through Distributed Representation of Raga Names
This paper investigates a method to identify the similarity between ragas in Hindustani classical music using textual discussions on ragas. The proposed approach learn representations in the form of word vectors for raga names, which can be used to extract similarities between ragas. This can be applied for generating word vectors for any specific domain with data scarcity. The method learns word vectors of dataset specific words (absent in the general pool of word vectors) with the help of the specific dataset and pre-trained word vectors trained on general English corpus like Google word vectors. The proposed method yields better results over the baselines signifying its appropriateness to any specific dataset.
