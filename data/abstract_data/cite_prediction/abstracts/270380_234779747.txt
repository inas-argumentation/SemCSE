Literary study-1-Using the ISO 9126 Quality Model in Architectural Assessment methods
In this essay the following question will be investigated: Several software architecture assessment methods, such as ATAM, SAAM and CBAM, are known from literature. This literature study consists of an investigation into the applicability of these methods for the assessment of various quality attributes, e.g. functionality, reliability, usability, efficiency, maintainability, and portability [ISO/IEC 9126]. To answer this question, we will first take a look at the Quality Model of the ISO/IEC 9126 standard. Second we discuss the need for software architecture assessment and the different architecture assessment methods. We will conclude with an analysis of the applicability of the methods in relation to the Quality Model and topics for improvement.
