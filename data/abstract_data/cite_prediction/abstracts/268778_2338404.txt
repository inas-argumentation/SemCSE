The Computational Linguistics Summarization Pilot Task
The Computational Linguistics (CL) Summarization Pilot Task was created to encourage a community effort to address the research problem of summarizing research articles as “faceted summaries” in the domain of computational linguistics. In this pilot stage, a handannotated set of citing papers was provided for ten reference papers to help in automating the citation span and discourse facet identification problems. This paper details the corpus construction efforts by the organizers and the participating teams, who also participated in the task-based evaluation.
