A fingerprint retrieval system based on level-1 and level-2 features
0957-4174/$ see front matter 2012 Elsevier Ltd. A doi:10.1016/j.eswa.2012.02.064 ⇑ Corresponding author. E-mail addresses: raffaele.cappelli@unibo.it (R. C bo.it (M. Ferrara). This paper proposes a novel fingerprint retrieval system that combines level-1 (local orientation and frequencies) and level-2 (minutiae) features. Various scoreand rank-level fusion strategies and a novel hybrid fusion approach are evaluated. Extensive experiments are carried out on six public databases and a systematic comparison is made with eighteen retrieval methods and seventeen exclusive classification techniques published in the literature. The novel approach achieves impressive results: its retrieval accuracy is definitely higher than competing state-of-the-art methods, with error rates that in some cases are even one or two orders of magnitude smaller. 2012 Elsevier Ltd.
