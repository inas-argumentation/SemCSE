Limitations of the approximation capabilities of neural networks with one hidden layer
Let s/> 1 be an integer and W be the class of all functions having integrable partial derivatives on [0, 1] s. We are interested in the minimum number of neurons in a neural network with a single hidden layer required in order to provide a mean approximation order of a preassigned e > 0 to each function in W. We prove that this number cannot be O(e -s log(I/e)) if a spline-like localization is required. This cannot be improved even if one allows different neurons to evaluate different activation functions, even depending upon the target function. Nevertheless, for any 6 > 0, a network with O(c -s-6) neurons can be constructed to provide this order of approximation, with localization. Analogous results are also valid for other L p n o r m s .
