Reinforcement Learning and Markov Decision Processes
Reinforcement learning (RL) has developed into a large research field. The current state-ofthe-art is comprised of several subfields dealing with, for example, hierarchical abstraction and relational representations. This overview is targeted at researchers interested in RL who want to know where to start when studying RL in general, and where to start within the field of RL when faced with specific problem domains. This overview is by no means complete, nor does it describe all relevant texts. In fact, there are many more. The main function of this overview is to provide a reasonable amount of good entry points into the rich field of RL. All texts are widely available and most of them are online. General and Introductory Texts There are many texts that introduce the exciting field of RL and Markov decision processes (see for example the mentioned PhD theses at the end of this overview).
