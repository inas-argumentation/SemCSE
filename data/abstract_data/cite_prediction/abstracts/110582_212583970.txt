HAND GESTURE RECOGNITION SYSTEM FOR DUMB PEOPLE Sagar P . More * 1 ,
Few years ago, there is no way to communicate between the dumb people in a normal speed. And also the normal people never try to communicate between dumb persons. But now a days so many facility are available for understanding to communicate a dumb person using machine translator, hidden grove method etc. But drawback of this method is that is requiring more power supply and this type of machine translator is very costly. To overcome this drawback, In this paper use the static hand gesture recognition system using digital image processing.
