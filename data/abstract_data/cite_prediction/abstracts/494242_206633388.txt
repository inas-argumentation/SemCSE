Open Theoretical Questions in Reinforcement Learning
Reinforcement learning (RL) concerns the problem of a learning agent interacting with its environment to achieve a goal. Instead of being given examples of desired behavior, the learning agent must discover by trial and error how to behave in order to get the most reward.
