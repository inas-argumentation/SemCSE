Learned local descriptors for recognition and matching
In the past local features have become popular for a wide variety of applications such as object recognition, object tracking, structure from motion or stereo vision. Many different interest point detectors as well as local descriptors capturing the information of the local surrounding patches have been proposed. This work investigates the idea of considering keypoint description as a discriminative classification task. We propose to learn descriptors as it enables, in contrast to hand-crafted descriptors, to adjust the amount of invariance and distinctiveness to the task at hand. For this purpose, in a training stage reliable keypoints are extracted from a synthetically generated data set, which contains the amount of invariance needed. We investigate two trainable models for obtaining descriptors. Both are based on convolutional neural networks. We compare the proposed method against the widely used SIFT approach.
