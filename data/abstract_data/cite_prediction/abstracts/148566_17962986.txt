Tail Sensitivity Analysis in Bayesian Networks
The paper presents an efficient method for simulating the tails of a target variable Z = h(X) which depends on a set of basic variables X = (X1, ... , Xn)· To this aim, variables X;; i = 1, ... , n are sequentially simulated in such a manner that Z = h(xt, . . . ,x;_1,X;, ... ,Xn) is guaranteed to be in the tail of Z. When this method is difficult to apply, an alternative method is proposed, which leads to a low rejection proportion of sample values, when compared with the Monte Carlo method. Both meth­ ods are shown to be very useful to per­ form a sensitivity analysis of Bayesian net­ works, when very large confidence intervals for the marginal/ conditional probabilities are required, as in reliability or risk analysis. The methods are shown to behave best when all scores coincide. The required modifications for this to occur are discussed. The methods are illustrated with several examples and one example of application to a real case is used to illustrate the whole process.
