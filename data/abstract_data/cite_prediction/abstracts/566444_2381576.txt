This research aims to naturally evoke human-robot communications in ambient space based on a hierarchical model of gazecommunication. The interactive ambient space is created with our remote gaze-tracking technology based on image analyses and our gaze-reactive robot system. A single remote camera detects the user’s gaze in unrestricted situations by using eye-ball estimation. The robot’s gaze reacts with both 1) “positive evocation” by direct eye contactwith multimodal reactions and 2) “passive evocation” by indirectco-gazing(watching a common object or place) according to the user’s conscious/unconscious gaze. 1 Background Gaze is one of the most important communication channels [Kendon67] . Many gaze-tracking systems and their applications [Santella06], including communicative systems [Kojima02, etc.] , exploit the possibilities of gaze. However, no system capable of ambient gaze detection has been developed, and most of the communicative systems do not consider a hierarchical model of gaze-communication (GC), consequently, the human-robot interactions designed thus far are neither natural nor attractive. Accordingly, we have developed a system not only capable of ambient gaze tracking but also implemented with consideration given to a model of GC. For the appropriate GC, this paper focuses on connecting the user’s conscious/unconscious gaze to its hierarchical model and on the corresponding gaze-reactions of the robot. 2 Hierarchical Model of Gaze-communication Figure 1 shows our approach to evoking communication by using robots. We made a model of hierarchical communications in gaze: The indirect evocation enables the participants to engage in turntaking of the initiative by “co-gazing,” which creates an atmosphere of communication in ambient space, and the direct evocation precedes a natural reaction to the establishment of eye contact. 3 Remote Gaze Tracking in Free Head Motion Differently from previous works[Li06, SMI, etc.], our single-camerabased gaze tracking algorithm, consisting of facial-feature tracking and eye-ball model estimations, offers the advantages of not needing any attachments of any devices/markers not fixating the user’s head-motion, and not requiring the user to look at reference points in the calibration. These features permit gaze tracking in various ambient situations with5◦ (horizontal) and7◦ (vertical) accuracy.
