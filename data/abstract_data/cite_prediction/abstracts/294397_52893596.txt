A Modified MDP Algorithm for Multi-Pedestrians Tracking
The multi-pedestrians tracking(MPT) are crucial for the majority of computer vision applications. We propose a modified MDP framework so that it effectively solves the sharp task of data association in the tracking process. We formulate online crowd tracking as decision making in modified Markov Decision Processes(MDPs)., where we added a new state to address frequent and long-term occlusions based on the original period policy. Taking into account the characteristics of pedestrian tracking, we added color features to the original twelve features. Our modified tracker performs state-of-the-art on the MOT15 benchmark and the result of experiments show it helps to recover heavily occluded persons and to reduce false positives., showing superior performance than the original algorithm.
