Fuzzy Logic Based Intrusion Detection Scheme against DoS Attack in MANET
A mobile ad hoc network (MANET) is made up of various mobile nodes defined as free to move anywhere in the network and they communicate with each other wirelessly. MANET is also known as self organized network, which is a collection of mobile nodes responsible for every operation performed by them and responsible for its maintenance. Trust and cooperation among mobile ad hoc network (MANET) nodes affects the performance and lifetime of the network. Due to the dynamic nature of MANET nodes, they are exposed to various security attacks. The attack of creating and forwarding route request packets can lead to hogging of network resources and cause denial of service to legitimate nodes. This type of DoS attack is difficult to detect because malicious nodes mimic legitimate nodes in all aspects except flooding the network with request packets. A fuzzy based intrusion detection system has been proposed to mitigate such situation and reduce the loss of throughput. The proposed mechanism could prevent this specific kind of DoS attack and does not use any additional network bandwidth.
