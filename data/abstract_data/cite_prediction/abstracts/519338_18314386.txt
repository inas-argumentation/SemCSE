A geometric alternative to Nesterov's accelerated gradient descent
We propose a new method for unconstrained optimization of a s mooth and strongly convex function, which attains the optimal rate of convergence of N esterov’s accelerated gradient descent. The new algorithm has a simple geometric interpret ation, loosely inspired by the ellipsoid method. We provide some numerical evidence that t he new method can be superior to Nesterov’s accelerated gradient descent.
