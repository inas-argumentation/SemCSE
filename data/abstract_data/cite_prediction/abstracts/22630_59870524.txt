Vehicle Traffic Light Control Using SARSA
SARSA (Sutton, 1996) is applied to a simulated, traac-light control problem (Thorpe, 1997) and its performance is compared with several, xed control strategies. The performance of SARSA with four diierent representations of the current state of traac is analyzed using two reinforcement schemes. Training on one intersection is compared to, and is as eeective as training on all intersections in the environment. SARSA is shown to be better than xed-duration light timing and four-way stops for minimizing total traac travel time, individual vehicle travel times, and vehicle wait times. Comparisons of performance using a constant reinforcement function versus a variable reinforcement function dependent on the number of vehicles at an intersection showed that the variable reinforcement resulted in slightly improved performance for some cases.
