NATURE-INSPIRED TECHNIQUES FOR OPTIMIZATION : A BRIEF REVIEW
Nature plays a very important role to solve problems in a very effective and well-organized way. Few researchers are trying to create computational methods that can assist human to solve difficult problems. Nature inspired techniques like swarm intelligence, bio-inspired, physics/chemistry and many more have helped in solving difficult problems and also provide most favourable solution. Nature inspired techniques are wellmatched for soft computing application because parallel, dynamic and self organising behaviour. These algorithms motivated from the working group of social agents like ants, bees and insect. This paper is a complete survey of nature inspired techniques.
