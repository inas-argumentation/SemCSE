A 6–18 GHz 40 W Reactively Matched GaN MMIC Power Amplifier
The design and performance of a two-stage, wideband, reactively matched power amplifier MMIC, fabricated in a 0.18 μm No-Field-Plate Scaled-Channel GaN process, is presented. Measured results for the MMIC covering the 6–18 GHz frequency range demonstrate 21.7 dB average small-signal gain, 30.3 W to 52.8 W output power with a 40 W average, and 13 % to 30 % power-added efficiency.
