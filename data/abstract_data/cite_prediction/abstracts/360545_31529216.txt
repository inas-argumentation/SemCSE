Criteria for Human-Compatible AI in Two-Player Vision-Language Tasks
We propose rule-based search systems that outperform not only the state-of-the-art but the human performance, measured in accuracy, in GuessWhat?!, a vision-language game where either of two players can be a human. Although those systems achieve the high accuracy, they do not meet other requirements to be considered as an AI system that communicates effectively with humans. To clarify what they lack, we suggest the use of three criteria to enable effective communication with humans in vision-language tasks. These criteria also apply to other two-player vision-language tasks that require communication with humans, e.g., ReferIt.
