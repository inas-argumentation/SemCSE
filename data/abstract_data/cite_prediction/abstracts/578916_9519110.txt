Recommending Personalized Tips on New Courses for Guiding Course Selection
Selecting right courses, while crucial for assuring student academic success, can be challenging. Typical resources available to students during the course selection process such as course descriptions, course syllabi, course evaluations, and comments from previous students provide the same information to all students. However, personalized information about new courses will help individual students better find their right courses and eventually achieve their academic goals.
 In this paper, we propose a new application of recommender systems for course selection in the education domain. The proposed course tip recommender is based on the multi-criteria rating collaborative filtering-based recommender system, using students' characterizations of courses with respect to a set of course characterization criteria. It can provide individual students with a variety of personalized tips on new courses and serve as a useful personalization tool to guide students in the course selection process.
