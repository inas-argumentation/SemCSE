Social media and Tourism Destinations : TripAdvisor Case Study
Online social networking site are the most popular sites on the internet. The second generation of web based services is characterized by having a consumer generated content (CGC), which allow people to share information. This paper examines CGC on TripAdvisor, with a case study on the city of Lisbon. Along with a discussion on the radical changes implied by new forms of collaboration and business models, it is explored how the users collaborate to image a destination. It is analyzed a sample of all the hotels In TripSdvisor.Com for the city of Lisbon, as well as forum authors and advisors profile.
