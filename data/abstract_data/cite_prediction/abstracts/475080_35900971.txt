Metric Learning and Manifolds Metric Learning and Manifolds : Preserving the Intrinsic Geometry
A variety of algorithms exist for performing non-linear dimension reduction, but these algorithms do not preserve the original geometry of the data except in special cases. In general, in the low-dimensional representations obtained, distances are distorted, as well as angles, areas, etc. This paper proposes a generic method to estimate the distortion incurred at each point of an embedding, and subsequently to correct distances and other intrinsic geometric quantities back to their original values (up to sampling noise). Our approach is based on augmenting the output of an embedding algorithm with geometric information embodied in the Riemannian metric of the manifold. The Riemannian metric allows one to compute geometric quantities (such as angle, length, or volume) for any coordinate system or embedding of the manifold. In this work, we provide an algorithm for estimating the Riemannian metric from data, consider its consistency, and demonstrate the uses of our approach in a variety of examples.
