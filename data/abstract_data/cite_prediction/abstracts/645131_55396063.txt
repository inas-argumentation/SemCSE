BIASES IN THE DECISION-MAKING PROCESS AND POSSIBILITIES OF OVERCOMING THEM
In the conditions of uncertainty, the promulgation of good decisions becomes the key factor for a company’s success. The decision-making process represents a set of the activities that lead to making the final choice of one out of a set of possible actions by which the desired aim will be achieved (Babić, 1995, 14). Starting from the assumption that decision-makers are completely informed and perfectly rational beings, the development of the normative models of decisionmaking is the answer to the observed need to prescribe the procedures of decision-making that will enable a rational choice.
