The Evolution and Future Scope of Augmented Reality
This paper evaluates current practices and research being done in the field of augmented reality, reviews best practices and suggests new ways of incorporating this technology in our everyday lives. In doing so, it discusses the problems faced while implementing this technology and some ways to overcome them. It describes enabling technologies such as displays and trackers that are essential in building any AR system. This paper describes various fields in which AR is already being used (such as medical, military, entertainment, manufacturing, education etc.) and also presents new areas of applications for it. It can be used as a starting point for anyone who is new to this area of technology.
