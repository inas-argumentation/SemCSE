Simplifying surfaces with color and texture using quadric error metrics
There are a variety of application areas in which there is a ne ed for simplifying complex polygonal surface models. These mo dels often have material properties such as colors, textures, an d surface normals. Our surface simplification algorithm, based on ite rative edge contraction and quadric error metrics, can rapidly pro duce high quality approximations of such models. We present a nat ural extension of our original error metric that can account for a wide range of vertex attributes.
