Making Learning and Web 2.0 Technologies Work for Higher Learning Institutions in Africa
Purpose – This paper seeks to assess the extent to which the learning and web 2.0 technologies are utilized to support learning and teaching in Africa’s higher learning institutions, with a specific focus on Tanzania's public universities. Design/methodology/approach – A combination of "content analysis" and "semistructured interviews" was used to collect data. The study conducted semi-structured interviews with ICT personnel from six out of the eight public universities in Tanzania in 2011. Findings – The study found that the adoption of e-learning and web 2.0 technologies is still in its infancy stages in the Tanzania public universities.
