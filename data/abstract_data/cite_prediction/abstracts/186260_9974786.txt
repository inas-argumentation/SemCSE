Procedural Modelling of Urban Road Networks
We present a model for growing procedural road networks in and clos e t cities. The main idea of our paper is that a city cannot be meaningfully simulated without taking its neighbourhood into ac c unt. A simple traffic simulation that considers this neighbourhood is then used to grow new major roads a n to influence the locations of minor road growth. Waterways are introduced and used to help position the city nu clei on the map. The resulting cities are formed by allowing several smaller settlements to grow together and to for m a rich road structure, much like in real world, and require only minimal per-city input, allowing for batch gen eration.
