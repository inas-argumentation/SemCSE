A genetic algorithm to obtain the optimal recurrent neural network
Selecting the optimal topology of a neural network for a particular application is a dicult task. In the case of recurrent neural networks, most methods only induce topologies in which their neurons are fully connected. In this paper, we present a genetic algorithm capable of obtaining not only the optimal topology of a recurrent neural network but also the least number of connections necessary.
