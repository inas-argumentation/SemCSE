ORGANIZATIONAL CULTURE AND ITS IMPACT ON THE QUALITY OF ACCOUNTING INFORMATION SYSTEMS
This paper examines the impact of organizational culture on the quality of accounting information systems. The conceptual model using a sample of employees of the accounting department at several colleges in Bandung. Overall, the results support the hypothesis that organizational culture affects the quality of accounting information systems. The results showed that the role of organizational culture in improving the quality of accounting information system is an important factor.
