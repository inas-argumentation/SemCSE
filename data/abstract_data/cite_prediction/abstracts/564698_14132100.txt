Online Path Planning in a Continuous Gaussian Process Occupancy Map
This paper demonstrates the application of Gaussian Process (GP) occupancy map to 3D online path planning for a 6DOF Rotary Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (RUAV) operating in a naturally cluttered environment. Traditional grid-based occupancy map suffers from the curse in dimensionality for platforms that operate in higher configuration space. The flexibility of the path planner is also limited by the resolution of the grid size. The GP map however, performs inference directly on the collected sensor datasets which allows it to infer the probability of collision for any query point in continuous 3D space. Dealing with datasets directly removes the need of maintaining a full discrete map; its continuous property maximizes the flexibility of the path planner; the planned path inheritantly embeded with the the probability of collision which is valuable for decision making. We embed the GP map approach into our previous work on smooth path planning and control for an aerial vehicle and present results in a simulated cluttered environment.
