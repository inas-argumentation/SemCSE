Hepta-band CPW bow-tie aperture antenna for WiFi (2.4 GHz and 5.5 GHz) and other bands
A Hepta-band Bowtie Antenna is proposed to function at two Wi-Fi bands and other bands. The following seven frequency bands are operational for the proposed antenna: 2.4, 3.9, 5.5, 6.7, 8.4, 10.2 and 12.8 GHz. The antenna structure consists of six loaded metallic strips in addition to interconnected loaded parasitic rectangular components near to the edge of the left side of the antenna. The loaded metallic strips resulted in five frequency bands and the interconnected parasitic components resulted in the remaining additional two frequency bands. The four metallic strips that are near the center of the antenna are symmetric while the remaining two metallic strips have different heights. All the metallic strips have the same width. The hepta-band antenna is simulated with HFSS to obtain the results of the return loss, radiation patterns and other antenna parameters. It will be shown from the simulation results that the antenna has seven operational frequency bands including the two Wi-Fi bands 2.4 and 5.5 GHz.
