IOT-based drone for improvement of crop quality in agricultural field
Unmanned Aerial Vehicles are becoming more and more popular to meet the demands of increased population and agriculture. Drones equipped with appropriate cameras, sensors and integrating modules will help in achieving easy, efficient, precision agriculture. The proposed solutions related to these drones, if integrated with various Machine Learning and Internet of Things concepts, can help in increasing the scope of further improvement. In this paper, the related work in this field has been highlighted along with proposed solutions that can be integrated into the drone using Raspberry Pi 3 B module.
