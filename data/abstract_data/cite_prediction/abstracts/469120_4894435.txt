Private Nearest Neighbors Classification in Federated Databases
Privacy-preserving data analysis in the context of federated databases distributed across multiple parties has the potential to produce richer and more accurate models than what each party can learn with their own data. Secure Multi-Party Computation (MPC) offers a robust cryptographic approach to this problem, and in fact several protocols have been proposed for various learning tasks on parametric models. In this paper we focus on k-NN, shifting the attention towards nonparametric models. We tackle several challenges arising in privacy-preserving k-NN classification on federated databases, and implement a concrete protocol for document classification. Our solution is faster than the state-of-the-art custom MPC protocol by at least one an order of magnitude.
