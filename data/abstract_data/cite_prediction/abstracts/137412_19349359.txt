Design of miniaturized ultra-wideband Quasi-Yagi antenna
A novel Quasi-Yagi antenna was proposed with ultra-wideband and miniaturization characteristics. The butterfly dipoles and parasitic branches are adopting to improve the bandwidth of the Quasi-Yagi antenna. Affections of antenna structure parameters on antenna was analyzed in this paper. After optimization, the operating frequency ranges from 3.3 to 10.2 GHz. Meanwhile, gain is more than 5.1 dB, and the maximum is 9.8 dB.
