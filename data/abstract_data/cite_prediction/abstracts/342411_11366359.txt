Gap Trap : A Pothole Detection and Reporting System Utilizing Mobile Devices
In this paper we present a new method and implementation for detecting and reporting roadway potholes. Potholes are a major problem that cost cities and motorists around the world billions of dollars per year. Gap Trap addresses the issue by automatically discovering and reporting potholes so that motorists may avoid them, and cities may quickly repair them. This paper compares Gap Trap with similarly designed systems, and goes on to discuss the implemented prototype system that demonstrates the usefulness of this idea.
