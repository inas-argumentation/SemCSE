Broadband Radial Waveguide Power Combiner with Improved Isolation among Adjacent Output Ports
An eight-way waveguide-based power combiner/divider is presented and investigated in the frequency range 7.5–10.5GHz. A simple approach is proposed for design purposes. The measured combiner shows a good agreement between the simulated and measured results. Insertion loss is about −0.3 dB, return loss is less than −15 dB and isolation between adjacent output ports is better than −11 dB at 8.5GHz and reaches about −14 dB at 9.5GHz.
