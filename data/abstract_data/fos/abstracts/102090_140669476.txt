Biological behaviour and accretion rates of Patella coerulea L. as indicated by oxygen isotope measurements
Oxygen isotope measurements have been carried out on shells of P. coerulea L. from the coast of Tuscany (Italy). The results obtained indicate that: the CaCO3 of the shells is precipitated under isotopic equilibrium conditions with the environmental water; the deposition of CaCO3 takes place all year round even though the accretion rate of the shells apparently decreases during summer; the accretion rate is quite fast, shells of about one year showing a major axis of about 30 mm or more; different ecotypes with different thermal optima seem to exist in nearby areas under similar climatic conditions.
