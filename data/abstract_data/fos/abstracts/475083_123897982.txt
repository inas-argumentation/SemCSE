RELABILTY ANALYSIS ON COMPOSITE FOUNATION SETTLEMENT BASED ON BOUSSINESQ'S STRESS AND DISPLACEMENT SOLUTION
The reinforced area and non-reinforced area of a composite foundation are regarded as a whole,the reinforced area is taken as a natural foundation whose compression modulus equals as a composite modulus;the settlement of the composite foundation is computed by means of the layering summation method of a natural foundation.The models for the reliability analysis on the composite foundation settlement based on the Boussinesq's stress and displacement solution under a vertical concentrated load is established.The effect factors on the reliability index of the composite foundation settlement are analyzed through an engineering example.Considered the function's non-linear and included integral term,the reliability index computation is difficulty by the checking point method then by Monte-carlo method.Monte-carlo method does not deal with the computation of partial derivatives,and the limit of the function's non-linear and numerical integral.
