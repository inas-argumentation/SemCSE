Characterization of Epitaxial β-(Al,Ga,In)2O3-Based Films and Applications as UV Photodetectors
Epitaxial films of β-(AlxGa1−x)2O3, β-Ga2O3, and β-(InxGa1−x)2O3 were grown on (001) sapphire substrates via metalorganic chemical vapor deposition (MOCVD). The compositions of the films as determined from energy dispersive x-ray analysis (EDX) and x-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) results were XAl = 0.57 ± 0.05 and 0.76 ± 0.05 and XIn = 0.12 ± 0.05 and 0.21 ± 0.05. The optical bandgap was found to correspondingly vary between 6.0 ± 0.2 and 3.9 ± 0.1 eV, as a function of composition via XPS and UV–visible spectroscopy (UV–Vis).
