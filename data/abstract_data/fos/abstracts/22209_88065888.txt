Analyses of Expression and Distribution of GFAP and NSE at the Terminal Stage in the Brain Tissues of the Hamsters Infected with Scrapie Strain 263K
Prion diseases are a group of neurodegenerative diseases,with the characteristics of vacuoles,gliosis,loss of neuronal cells and abnormal prion protein(PrP~ (Sc)).In order to observe the quantitative and functional changes of astrocytes and neurons in the hamsters infected with scrapie strain 263K,the expression and distribution of glial fibrillary acidic protein(GFAP)and neuron-specific enolase(NSE)were analyzed with Western blot and immunohistochemistry,while comparatively analyzed with the characteristics of PrP~ (Sc) deposits and neuropathological changes.The expression of GFAP was significantly increased in the brain tissues of infected hamsters.Compared with healthy controls,the number of GFAP-stained cells in brain stem,cerebellum and cerebrum were 369, 241 and 1.56 folds increased respectively.The expression of NSE in the infected animals was less than that in normal ones.Quantitative calculation revealed that the NSE-stained cells in cerebellum,CA1 of hippocampi and cerebrum of the sick animals accounted for only 22.5%,54.2% and 53.9% in that of the controls.Moreover,the affected regions with increased GFAP and decreased NSE corresponded well with that of PrP~ (Sc) deposits and spongiform degeneration.These results confirm that the changes of GFAP and NSE in infected brain tissues can reflect the quantitative and functional status of astrocytes and neurons,it can be taken as an important pathological targets during the pathogenesis of prion diseases.
