Compatative Study of China’s Ecomomics Training Programms with Foreign Countries
Although China in firmly heading for market-oriented system, its economics training programms are under sig- nificant challenges both ideologically and technically. This study compares the economics training counrses by the so-called na- tional economies talent training bases (10 Chinese Universities) with those in mature market economies and transition econo- mies. Economics courses at intermediate level and corresponding mathematics courses are suggested to be incorporated widely in the system of the undergraduate economics training courses.
