Measurement of the branching ratio for the dissociative recombination of H/sub 3//sup +/+e
The dissociative recombination of H/sub 3//sup +/ with electrons can have two exit channels, namely e+H/sub 3//sup +/..-->..H+H+H (Channel I), and e+H/sub 3//sup +/..-->..H/sub 2/+H (channel II). A new technique has been developed which has been used to determine the relative contributions of channels I and II to the overall recombination process. Over the energy range from 0.01 to 0.50 eV it has been found that Channel I dominates.
