The change of the level of T_H1 type cytokines and cytotoxicity of iIELs and its subset (Thy1~-) from mice with experimental digestive ulcer
Objective To investigate T_H1 type cytokine secretary and cytotoxic activity of iIELs and its subset (Thy1 -) from mice with experimental digestive ulcer. Methods Using the animal models with digestive ulcer induced by water-immersion restraint stress, ELISA, Northern dot blotting and biological techniques were used to detect the levels of T_H1 type cytokines IFN-γ, IL-2 and TNF-α synthesis, mRNA expression and cytotoxicity in iIELs and the subset (Thy1 -). Results Three to four days after water-immersion restraint, IFN-γ, IL-2 reached peak level in the culture supernatant of iIEL, and maintained a considerable high level until the 6th day. mRNA expression of IFN-γ, IL-2 rose correspondingly with the cytokines detected. IFN-γ and IL-2 in the culture supernatant of Thy1 - subset of iIEL is not significantly different to that of the control group. Cytotoxicity of iIEL reached peak level on the second day after water-immersion restraint and turned back to normal level in one week. Conclusion In certain period after digestive ulcer induced by stress, mRNA expression of T_H1 type cytokines such as IFN-γ, IL-2 and TNF-α rise up, and secretion of IFN-γ and IL-2 increase with enhanced cytotoxicity of iIEL. These changes may protect the function of gastrointestinal tract. Thy1 - seems not be responsible for the increased secretion of T_H1 cytokines.
