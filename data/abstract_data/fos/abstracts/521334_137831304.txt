Study on Potato Modified Starch as Additive of Coating for Lost Foam Casting
Through the single-factor experiment which sets the content of the potato modified starch into five gradients,the effect of potato modified starch as the additive on the process properties of coating for lost-foam casting was studied.Strictly according to the technology of the preparation of the coating,the suspension property,thixotropy and bending strength at room temperature were tested.The experiment data show that potato modified starch is an excellent binder and can promote the suspension property and the thixotropy.
