Study on the extraction technology of polysaccharide from the bark of Eucommia ulmoides Oliver
The content of polysaccharide was determined by 3,5-dinitrosalicylic acid colour reaction and anthrone-sulfuric colorimetric method.Taking extracting yield of polysaccharide as investigation index,the technology of water extraction and ethanol precipitation was optimized by orthogonal experiment.The optimum water extraction conditions were that the material-water ratio was 1:10,the extracting temperature was 100 ℃,the extracting time was 4 h,the extracting number of times was 2 times.The optimum ethanol precipitation process was as follows:the concentrated solution was precipitated with 3 times of 100% ethanol for 4 h.The extracting yield of polysaccharide was 1.58% under the former conditions.The method was simple and suitable to extract polysaccharide from the bark of Eucommia ulmoides Oliver.
