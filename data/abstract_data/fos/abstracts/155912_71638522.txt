Reducing The Risk Of Mistakes In The Operating Theatre: Responsability Of The Surgical Team
PURPOSE Aim of the paper is to analyse the specific responsability of nurses of the operating theatre, as well as that of surgeons and anestesiologists in the surgical team, according to the Recommendations of the Ministry of Public Health released on July 2nd 2006. MATERIAL AND METHODS The recommendation concern specific protocols and procedures to avoid leaving instruments, gauzes or other material in the surgical field. In the Recommendations, it is underlined that free and efficient comunication inside all the components of the surgical team is necessary, as first step to reduce the risk of such events. In many hospitals internal procedures have been developed to ease applying the Recommendations. Protocols have been written to monitor “adverse events” or “avoided adverse events”, in order to promote the report of mistakes. RESULTS The procedures realized with the aim of reducing the risk in the operating theatre are analyzed by means of quantitative FMECA (Failure Mode, Effect rand Criticality Analisys) analysis. Though such recommendations should not be necessary, the mistakes of Ministry Public Health reminds that, in absence of official data, such event occurs in 1 every 1000-3000 surgical procedures. Also, such events are not always reported. CONCLUSIONS It is verified what could happen it the event, that recommended procedures have not been followed. The system analyzes the potential problems and specific preventive or corrective measures to reduce the risk of mistakes.
