The Cypress Hills Formation (Upper Eocene to Miocene): a semi-arid braidplain deposit resulting from intrusive uplift
The Upper Eocene – Miocene Cypress Hills Formation of the Cypress Hills plateau, in southwestern Saskatchewan and southeastern Alberta, is interpreted as a braidplain deposit. The regional paleoslope dipped to the northeast, and the east–west distribution of outcrop exposes facies representing lateral variation across the slope of the braidplain. Overall, the average clast size of the deposits decreases from west to east, with western area sediments dominated by boulder-sized gravels deposited on longitudinal bars.
