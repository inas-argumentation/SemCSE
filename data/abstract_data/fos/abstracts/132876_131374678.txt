The Environmental Protecting Experience of Singapore as a Reference for Nanjing
As one of the Newly Industrial Economics, Singapore's economy has been developing rapidly since the 1970s. It is a small island, small city, small country, and it is a beautiful, livable Garden City suitable for work and play. Its ecological environment is very beautiful, and attracts both high technology industry and financial services settle in Singapore. Good experiences of Singapore in the field of environmental protection were introduced mainly from the aspects of environmental legislation, environmental planning, environmental law enforcement and environmental education. The countermeasures and suggestions of Nanjing environmental protection and ecological construction were put forward.
