Again, the theme of privilege versus the under society emerges. D’Araujo’s investigation of the inclusion of women and homosexuals in the modern military is perhaps the best article in the volume. She explains that despite limitations and opposition, the assimilation of females in the three branches has been carried out with relative effectiveness. Inclusion of homosexuals, however, is far more difficult due to moral and religious objections. Peter Beattie touches upon much of the same in the second half of the nineteenth and the first third of the twentieth century. However, the first five pages of his article are missing, as are the last 13 pages of the piece by Hendrik Kraay, thereby leaving the reader partly in the dark.
