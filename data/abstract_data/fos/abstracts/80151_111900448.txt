Compiling and Studying on the Scheme of dynamic Water Closing of the Gezhouba Power Plant Turbine-Generating Units' Intake Working Gate
This paper focuses on the emergency stations occurred in the Gezhouba power plant that hydro-turbine distributor was out of control and turbine-generating unit could not stop down properly result from the breaking down of a majority of guide-blade shear pins and the failure of governor system. The prevention and management of unexpected situation is to guarantee that the emergency coutermeasure and treatment run effectively and orderly,and finally reduce the damage of the turbine-generating units and the influence of the power system to the maximal degree.
