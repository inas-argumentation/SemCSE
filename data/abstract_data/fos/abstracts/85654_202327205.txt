Dynamic Fuzzy Comprehensive Evaluation of the Economic Growth Quality of the Cities in Anhui Province:Based on Adjusted AHP-FCE Integrated Model
Based on the information provided by evaluating indexes and adjusted AHP-FCE integrated model,this paper takes a dynamic fuzzy comprehensive evaluation on the quality of economic growth of the cities in Anhui Province from 2002to 2011.It chooses eight representative indexes from five-dimension index system describing quality of economic growth,establishes fuzzy relation matrix,and obtains dynamic comprehensive evaluation result from fuzzy compound calculation.The analysis of dynamic comprehensive evaluation value of the quality of economic growth of the cities,maximum rank difference and influence factors shows that there was a dynamically changing trace and regional variance of the economic growth quality of the cities in Anhui Province during the ten years;only one or two cities were in the core area of the quality of economic growth,while all other cities were in the"leaping"areas;and the development level of various dimension of economic growth quality was unbalanced.
