Design of correction of secondary spectrum by hybrid diffractive-refractive
Binary optics can be used to increase optical performances,decrease size and weight,and decrease systems costs in numerous applications.By means of hybrid diffractive-refractive,using two kind of glasses,a method and processes of apochromats have been introduced to realize decreasing secondary spectrum in long focus telescope for secondary planet in detail.The research results show that secondary spectrum doesn't affect the quality of sight field in the corrected telescope.The correction method is simple and satisfiable.
