Indolobenzofurans. 1. Synthesis of isomeric indolobenzo[b]furans
The synthesis of four progenitors of a new tetracyclic condensed system of indolobenzo [b]furans on the basis of the Fischer reaction has been described. It has been shown that the cyclization of 2-dibenzofuranyl- and 3-dibenzofuranylhydrazones of ethyl pyruvate in ethyl polyphosphate results in the simultaneous formation of the corresponding cyclic ethers with both angular and linear structures.
