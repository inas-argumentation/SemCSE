Design and development of a decision making support system of integrated prevention and cure for Dangshansu pear scab
Pear scab(Venturia pirina) is one of the most serious diseases on Dangshansu pear.Every year,the peasants suffer large losses.With the development of agricultual information,there are many reports about expert systems for disease and prevention on fruit trees,but a support system of integreted prevention and cure for Dangshansu pear scab has not been reported.Based on the information of pear scab and the practice experience of the experts on plants diseases,combined with the current developping situation of Dangshansu pear in the area of yellow river,we build up a support system of integreted prevention and cure for Dangshansu pear scab using Visual Basic6.0 and Photoshop softwares.The system was composed of two main modules "knowing pear scab","integreted prevention and cure" and five sub-modules "pathogenic bacteria","regularity of epidemic","symptoms of the disease","methods of prevention and cure","the year calendar of prevention and cure" so on.The system has an advantage of comprehension,simple interface and easily operation.It can provide some helps for many agricultual technicians and farmers in the area of yellow river.
