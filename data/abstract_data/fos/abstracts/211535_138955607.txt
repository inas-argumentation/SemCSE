Study on Solution of Decreasing Micro-arc Oxidation Current Density of High Si Cast Al Alloy
For decreasing the current density and the disturbing of silicon of micro-arc oxidation on cast aluminum alloy,the effects of micro-arc oxidation solution systems on current density for ZL102 cast aluminum alloy were studied. The base solution was consisted of sodium silicon and sodium hydroxide. The addition agents included of NH4 F, C3H8O3 and H3BO3.The current density was tested under the conditions of adding one additive and adding multi-additives at various concentration.The results show that adding one additive in base solution takes an irregular influence on current density. The current density decreases with adding 2 kinds of additives. When the 3 kinds of additions were added synchronously in 10#solution, the current density decreases obviously. The surface morphologies of ceramic coatings processed in 10#solution of ZL102 and LY12 in 0#solution are alike. This indicates that 10#solution can efficiently decrease the disturbing of silicon in cast aluminum alloy. 10#solution is a perfect compounding for cast aluminum alloy micro-arc oxidation.
