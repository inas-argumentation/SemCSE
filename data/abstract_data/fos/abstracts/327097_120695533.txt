Stimulated emission of ultraviolet radiation by benzoxazoles
It is shown that the lasing ability of benzoxazoles depends on the structure of different types of their electronic energy levels. Compounds with Tππ*,S*ππ*,Tnπ*,and S*nπ* lower levels emit radiation. Stimulated emission was obtained from solutions of 2-phenylbenzoxazole derivatives in the shortest wavelength range of 319–360 nm and from solutions of 4-(benzoxazole-2-yl)–4'-phenylstilbene derivatives in the 426–446 nm range. A nonselective resonator pumped by λp=308 nm radiation from a pulse-periodic excimer laser was used. The laser action was achieved in two bands corresponding to the vibrational structure in the gain spectrum.
