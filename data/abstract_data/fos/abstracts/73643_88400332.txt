Identification and Control Effect on Banana Anthracnose of Seven Antagonistic Strains
Seven antagonistic bacteria were selected from a large number of isolates which were isolated from crop rhizosphere soil. The strains were identified according to the culture characteristic, morphology, physiological and biochemical property and 16 s r RNA sequence analysis. The control efficiencies on banana anthracnose were also examined. The results showed that strains Bb7802, Bb7304, Bb7202, Bb7108, Bb700, and Bb5911 were identified as Brevibacillus brevis and the strain Bc6301 was identified as Bacillus cereus. The strain Bc6301 got the highest control efficiency as 45.94% against banana anthracnose. Control efficiencies of the other six strains were ranged from 22.35% to 43.29%.
