Sequence determination of E and NS1 gene for 2 strains of dengue virus type 2 and its phylogenetic tree analysis
Objective To clarify the differences in envelope gene and NS1 gene sequences between dengue virus type 2 NGC strain and B strain which were isolated from a dengue hemorrhagic fever (DHF) patient. Methods The E and NS1 gene of B and NGC strains were amplified by RT-PCR, directly sequenced by the RT-PCR products of two strains. We have predicated their related amino acid sequence with DNAssist software, then compared their homology and constructed the phylogenetic tree with PHYLIP biochemistry software. Results The homology in nucleotide sequences of envelope, NS1 gene between B strain and NGC strain were 99.1% and 98.3% respectively. The phylogenetic tree of the envelope, NS1 gene sequence has been constructed through comparing the sequences between DEN-2 virus B strain and other 11 strains. Phylogenetic tree analysis showed that B strain was mostly close to 44 strain and NGC strain. Conclusion There are big differences in the nucleotide and amino acid sequences of envelope, NS1 gene between B strain and NGC strain. B strain is the closest one to the “DEN-2 44” strain which was isolated in Hainan province in 1989, and NGC strain as well.
