Laser diode end-pumped accousto-optical Q-switched Nd:YVO_4 laser with high power and high slope efficiency
A high average output power,high slope efficiency acousto-optical Q-switched 1 064 nm laser is reported with a laser diode(LD) end-pumped Nd:YVO4 double-bonded crystal scheme.With the proper estimation of thermal lensing effects under intense pumping,the mode matching and thermally stable cavity parameters are discussed and optimized,and a stable high-power high-slope efficiency output is obtained.At the highest repetition rate of 50 kHz and pump power of 46.8 W,a maximum average output power of 17.6 W is achieved with optical conversion efficiency of 37.6%,slope efficiency of 70.1% and pulse width of 51.3 ns.At the lowest repetition rate of 10 kHz,a maximum single pulse energy of 0.91 mJ and peak power of 46.2 kW are measured with pulse width of 19.8 ns.
