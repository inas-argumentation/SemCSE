Patterns and Trends of Suicide Among Females in Peshawar
INTRODUCTION: Every woman undergoes certain hormonal changes as soon she step into her teens. These hormones are related with appearance of secondary sexual characters, such as change in behavior, mood disorders, aggression, and depression. OBJECTIVE: To examine suicide trends and patterns among females of District Peshawar; and to explore causes for suicide in this population. METHODOLOGY: The bodies of women in the reproductive age who died as a result of fatal deliberate self harming were examined at Forensic Medicine Department Khyber Medical College, Peshawar from January 2017 to December 2017. RESULTS: Forty-four bodies of females were autopsied. 13 (30%) were adolescents between age 10-18 years. 31 (70.4%) were age between 19-48 years.33 of the victims committed suicide due to health related issues. 28 (64%) victims use poisons. In almost all cases (n=38) the incident happened when the victim was alone in house or left unattended. 30 (68%) victims were brought to hospitals for treatment. 13 were found to have recurrent attempt of self-harming.Suicide was associated with family and marital issues, and the methods of suicide included poison ingestion and hanging. CONCLUSION: Females have increasedtendency to commit suicide than males especially in their reproductive age. Low socioeconomic status, cultural norms unwanted pregnancies and ill health are the major causes.. KEY WORDS: Tresnds, Patterns, Suicide, Female, Peshawar
