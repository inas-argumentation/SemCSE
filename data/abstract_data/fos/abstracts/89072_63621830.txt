Research on Infrared Image Enhancement and Target Tracking Algorithm
Thermal infrared image has very high dynamic range, then how to map signals with wide range to 8 bits video signals with high-contrast and the latter deal with image tracking are an important technology in infrared image processing. Describes the principle of histogram-based HE algorithm、HP algorithm and PE algorithm, and provided the useful range of plateau values and the key steps of implementation with C language for PE algorithm, and describes the relevant questions about tracking algorithm respectively.
