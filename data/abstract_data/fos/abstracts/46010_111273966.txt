Feedlot Cattle Efficiency as a Function of Climate
ABSTRACT WEATHER records and feedlot cattle records for a 7-year time span were analyzed to determine which elements of the weather (temperature, wind, relative 'humidity, precipitation, and solar radiation) could be correlated to the expected changes in average daily gain and feed efficiency for feedlot cattle. The pre-diction equations developed were tested against performance of cattle from another feedlot in another climatic area..
