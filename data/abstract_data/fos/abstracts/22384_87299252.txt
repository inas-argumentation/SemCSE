Study on the technology of L-calcium lactate from oyster shell
The technology of L-calcium lactate from oyster shell was studied.The results showed that the optimum conditions are following: the oyster shell was ashed at 900 ℃ for 120 minutes,then put water(40.0 mL)into ashes contents(2.00 g).Finally,12.00 mL L-lactic acid was added into the lime and kept reaction at 60 ℃.After filtration,crystallization and washing,the produect was dried to white powder about 55 ℃.The yield was 92.45% and the content of L-calium lactate was 89.41% under this technology conditions.
