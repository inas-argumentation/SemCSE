Analysis on Precipitation Behavior of Al-Si-(Cu,Mg) Alloy During Aging Treatment
Age-hardening curves of Al-7Si-0.3Mg alloy and Al-4.5Cu alloy were plotted after age-treated.A precipitation process during artificial aging was studied using DSC and TEM.The results show that there are "two peaks" phenomenon on the two curves,the second peak value is slightly larger than that of the first peak.The translation of supersaturated solid solution to GP zone in the two alloys occurs at first,then the translation of GP zone to θ(Al2Cu) or GP zone to β(Mg2Si) turns up.The precipitate phase of Al-7Si-0.3Mg alloy is Mg2Si after aging for 4 h and Al-4.5Cu alloy is Al2Cu during artificial aging.
