From Peasant Struggles to Indian Resistance: The Ecuadorian Andes in the Late Twentieth Century (review)
Concrete local issues once again drove the demands, called the four "Ds" by the Comite Pro Rescate y Desarrollo de Vieques: Demilitarization, Devolution or return of the land, Decontamination, and Development. With its roots in the movement of the late 1970s, the current movement has already shown results. The U.S. Navy returned the lands it occupied in western Vieques. Whether it will clean up the toxics concentrated on the eastern part of the island remains to be seen.
