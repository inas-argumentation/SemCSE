Upper critical fieldHc3 of superconductors for all electron mean free paths
In this paper the ratioHc3/Hc2 is calculated for all electron mean free paths. Correction terms to the standard resultHc3/Hc2=1,695 are obtained both in the Ginzburg-Landau region and in the dirty limit at all temperatures. In the clean limit our results are identical with those obtained earlier byLüders.
