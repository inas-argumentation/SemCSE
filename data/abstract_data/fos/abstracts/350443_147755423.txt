Life Stress of Female College Students
The aim of this paper is to investigate the status of life stress of female college students. The research instr- ument was employed in this paper-the Life Stress Questionnaire for College Students was developed in the paper. The random samples consisted of 822 students, including 434 female college students from six colleges and universities, showing their life stress and psychology health state. the result shows that:The events with the highest frequency for female college students related homesick, missing their families, and the life event with the lowest frequency for female college students was the parents' divorce or their reorganized families. However life event with the lowest frequency caused the greatest subjective stress. There is no significant difference in the overall subjective stress between the female students and male students. All the average subjective life stresses of female college students'of nine categories are between "light"and "Medium." the top ten of compositive life stress of female college students focus on school work and personal development these two categories.
