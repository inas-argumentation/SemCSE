Gaussian Kernel Applied to Corneal Topography Image Processing
Corneal topography is one of the most important equipments in anterior-segment ophthalmology,particularly in the study of corneal shape.By capturing Placido image,detecting bright circles,corneal topography can be draw with relevant algorithm.A new approach to the extraction of bright circles from Placido images is proposed.The approach is based on computing a second order Taylor polynomial for each sub-pixel in the image by convolution with derivatives of a Gaussian smoothing kernel.Line points are found based on differential geometric properties of this polynomial: they are required to have a vanishing gradient and a high curvature in the direction perpendicular to the line.Accuracy coordinate is obtained and mean deviation of diopter is below 0.25D.
