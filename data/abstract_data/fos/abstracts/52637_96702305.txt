Raman spectroscopic studies of non-aqueous solutions
The techniques available for the application of Raman spectroscopy to the study of non-aqueous solutions are reviewed. Such techniques include operation at non-ambient temperature and pressure, and with corrosive liquids. The results from such studies are discussed under the headings: pure solvents and solvent mixtures; cation-solvent interactions; cation-ligand interactions; solvent-moderated ion-pair interactions.
