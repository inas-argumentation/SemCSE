THE PLASTIC DYNAMIC RESPONSE OF TWO WAY STIFFENED SQUARE PLATES WITH LARGE DEFLECTION SUBJECTED TO BLAST LOADS
The plastic dynamic response of built-in square plates with two way stiffeners under blast loading is discussed based on motion equations of both panels and stiffeners herein.The analysis indicates that the stiffened plate has three modes depending on the relative rigidity of the stiffeners and the peak of the load applied.In this paper,the global deflection mode was discussed only.The membrane solution for the built-in square plates with two way stiffeners(cross stiffeners and double cross stiffeners) was obtained by neglecting the influence of bending moment.The results were verified by the existing experiment results.It is shown that the present simplified analytical method can predict the permanent transverse deflection of the two way stiffened square plates subjected to blast loads with a reasonable accuracy.
