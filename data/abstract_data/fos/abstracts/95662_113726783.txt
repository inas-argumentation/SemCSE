The Study of On-line Insulation Measurement on Capacitive High-voltage Equipments
An insulation on-line detection system has been described in this thesis, by which the relative value of tanδ and capacitance of two equipments can be measured.This system can be used for measureins MOA full current and resistance current on-line.Field application showed that relative measuring method could gain more stable results for eliminating external affects easily, which can indicate the exact insulation status of measured apparatus. Further more, measurement of normal apparatus and apparatus with defective insulation has been introduced respectively in this paper.
