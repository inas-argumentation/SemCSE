Fate of 14C‐carbofuran in a rice‐fish model ecosystem∗∗
Abstract The distribution of 14C‐Carbofuran residues among the constituents of a model rice‐fish ecosystem was studied over a period of 90 days. Carbofuran was readily absorbed from the root system and distributed to all parts of the rice plant of the different constituents of the model ecosystem, rice plants contained the highest concentration of 14C‐residues. The results obtained indicate that carbofuran does not accumulate in fish to an appreciable extent. In water, the parent compound decreased appreciably with time and contributed about 10% of the total 14C‐residues after 30 days. Analysis of residues in fish, plant and soil showed, apart from the parent compound, the presence of several metabolites (60% of residues). Among the degradation products, 3‐hydroxy carbofuran and carbofuran phenol were identified as major metabolites.
