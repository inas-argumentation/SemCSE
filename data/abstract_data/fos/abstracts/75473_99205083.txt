Photocatalytic Degradation of Toluene in Gas-phase Using C-TiO_2 Nano-film
Preparation of C-TiO2(carbon doped TiO2) is described,involving flame CVD method and means for characterization of the photocatalyst.The study on photocatalytic degradation of toluene vapor was conducted using a tubular photo-reactor with C-TiO2 fixed inside;effects of initial concentration of toluene and the relative humidity were investigated.
