Constructing Campus Culture, Promoting Reformation and Development
The construction of the campus culture is the whole name of the material civilization and moral civilization. It shows the ability of the construction of the socialist advanced culture of the college. The result of the cultural construction could raise the effect of the college which is an important condition for the college to exist and develop.
