Summary of Data Compression Algorithm in Wireless Sensor Networks
First introduces the characteristics of wireless sensor networks and analysis the importance of data compression technology to wireless sensor networks,then summarizes in wireless sensor network data compression technology research present situation and introduces some of the wireless sensor network research results of a representative algorithm,by reading various types of literature,wireless sensor network data compression algorithms are divided into four categories in this paper: according to monitoring data in time and space on the existence of a correlation,separation of based on space-time correlation data compression algorithm;according to the data using a transformation to remove redundant information on temporal and spatial correlation,separation of based on wavelet transform data compression algorithm;in both centralized and decentralized information service can be implemented based on the principle,separation of distributed data compression algorithm;by conventional compression algorithm to be tailored and optimized,separation of improved traditional data compression algorithm;at the same time,this paper summarizes various algorithms suitable environment and its algorithm center thought,and finally discusses the of all kinds of wireless sensor network data compression algorithm in the defects of the present and discusses the future of all kinds of compression methods research direction.
