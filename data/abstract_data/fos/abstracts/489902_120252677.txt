Investigation of processes associated with photolysis of SPF3
The method of kinetic absorption spectroscopy was used in an experimental investigation of the kinetics of the processes accompanying the photolysis of gaseous SPF3. The rate constants of the deactivation of the sulfur atoms S(Z1D2) and of the quenching of the sulfur molecules S2(a1Δg) were determined. The rate constants of the reactions of SPF3 with sulfur atoms in the ground (33Pj) and excited (31D2) states were also obtained.
