Phase-rotation Based Blind Separation Algorithm for Multiple Co-Channel MPSK Signals
In this paper,the problem of blind separation of mixed high order modulation MPSK signals is considered.The properties of received MPSK signals are analyzed and an estimation method for the number of sources is proposed based on clustering.The number of sources is given after the received MPSK signals are preprocessed and punctured.Then after the phase rotation and eliminating common factors,the multiple co-channel mixed MPSK signals can be separated by using the algorithm for the blind separation of BPSK signals.Simulation results show that the proposed algorithm is effective.
