Metastability of the resonant Si level in Ga0.86Al0.14As
A helium gas pressure system was used to study the transport properties of Ga0.86Al0.14As in the temperature range 77-300 K and under pressure up to 14 kbar. The possibility of changing the pressure at low temperature permits one to fill heavily at zero pressure a high lying resonant Si level. The occupation of these states remains metastable up to 110 K, unless ionised by the light. Study of the time-dependent processes above this temperature enables the authors to estimate the energy barrier for thermal emission. They observe that the pressure dependence of this state is close to that of the X minimum.
