The requirement and the judgment standard for the academic dissertationof the graduate student majoring in management
In recent years, the quantity of the graduate students majoring in management and economics develop very fast in our country. But looking from the experience of directing and reading and appraising the thesis of post-graduate (especially the thesis of doctor), the quality of the thesis becomes a very outstanding problem.It should gradually exploring and definituding the basic requirements and judging standards on the research and writing of the management master's thesis.
