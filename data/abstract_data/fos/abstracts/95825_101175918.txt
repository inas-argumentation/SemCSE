Extraction of phycoerythrin from porphyra yezoensis by pulsed ultrasound
Extraction of phycoerythrin from porphyra yezoensis by pulsed ultrasound was studied.By single factor and orthogonal experiments,we achived the optimum conditions as follows:ultrasonic run time in a circle was 2s,ultrasonic interim time in a circle was 3s,ultrasonic power was 800W,extraction time was 60min,temperature was 20℃ and material/solution ratio was 30mg/mL.By the above condition,the extraction ratio of phycoerythrin was 3.249%,the purify standard(O.D561/O.D280) was 0.365.
