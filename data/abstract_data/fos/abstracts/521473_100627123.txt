Simultaneous determination of penicillion G and penicilloic acid in milk by UPLC-MS/MS
An ultra performance liquid chromatography tandem mass spectrometry(UPLC-MS/MS) analysis method was established for simultaneous analysis of penicillion G and penicilloic acid in milk.The milk sample was precipitated with acetonitrile and the supernatant was dried under nitrogen,the residue was dissolved in water and then extracted with n-hexane.The sample extract was separated by ACQUITY UPLC BEH C18 column,using a mixture of 0.1% formic acid in water with 1 mmol/L ammonium acetate and acetonitrile as the mobile phase.The detection limits of penicillion G and penicilloic acid was 1,2 μg/kg.And the limits of quantitation were 5,10 μg/kg.The correlation coefficient were above 0.999 within the linear range of 10～100 ng/mL.The average recoveries were 92%～103%,the relative standard deviations(RSD,n=3) were 2%～4%.
