Conjecture on Maximal Violation of T Invariance in Dilepton Production by Neutrinos
The conjecture is that $\mathrm{CP}$ and $T$ invariances are maximally violated by the weak currents associated with the new ("superstrange") class of hadrons suggested by dilepton production by neutrinos. This accounts for the phenomenon of $\mathrm{CP}$ nonconservation in ${K}^{0}$ and ${\overline{K}}^{0}$ decay, and the magnitude of ${\ensuremath{\eta}}_{+\ensuremath{-}}$ is consistent with a superstrange hadron mass within the energy range of the neutrino experiments. Tests of $T$ invariance in such neutrino experiments are suggested.
