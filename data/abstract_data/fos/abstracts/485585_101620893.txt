Study on starch microspheres prepared through two steps of bonding
The starch microspheres were synthesized from soluble starch through two steps of bonding, using SPAN-60 as Emulsifier. the mixture of Chloroform and Epichlorohydrin as oil phase. N,N'-methylene-bisacrylamide and Epichlorohydrin as crosslinker. The product was characterized by Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy(IR), Scanning electron microscopy(SEM) and granularity analyzer. The experiment showes that the main influencing factor to the average particle diameter of the microspheres is the dosage of N,N′-methylenebisacrylamide and the two phases volume Tatio of oil and water under the condition that the reaction time and the speed of agitation are invariant. The distribution of the average particle diameter of the microspheres is uniformity under the condition that reaction time is 2.5h, speed of agitation is 450r/min,oil and water phase volume Tatio is 3∶1, dosage of N, N′-methylenebisacrylamide is 0.4g. The microspheres are round, rough and porous which can be used as a kind of drug-carrier or adsorbents.
