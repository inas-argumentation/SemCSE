The development of biochemical engineering science in Europe
Biochemical engineering science is the fundamental research into all aspects of the interactions between engineering and other disciplines necessary to underpin the development of industrial-scale biologically-based processes. Its importance to European industry, justifying its incorporation into the Framework IV Programme, and the need to maintain a strong activity within Europe in this exciting field are emphasised. Some challenges and opportunities are discussed. The need for an integrated multidisciplinary and rational approach providing industry with strategies for process selection, design and operations based on generic principles and guidelines is highlighted.
