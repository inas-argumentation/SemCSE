Treatment of soap wastewater by UNITANK and contact oxidation
The UNITANK and contact oxidation were applied on treatment of soap wastewater in which main contaminations are lipin, glycerol, etc.. By examination, operational parameters optimization and analyzing the operation effect, the results demonstrated that the volume loading of 3 cells UNITANK is 1.5 kgCOD Cr /m 3·d, the removal of COD is about 80%, and the COD removal of contact oxidation is about 80%.
