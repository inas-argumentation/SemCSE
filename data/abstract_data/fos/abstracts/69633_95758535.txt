SEPARATION OF THE RECOIL PRODUCTS OF INORGANIC PHOSPHORUS OXYACIDS BY CROSS ELECTROPHORESIS
An attempt was made to separate the recoil products of phosphorus oxyacids (salts of hypophosphite, phosphite, orthophosphate and pyrophosphate) by a cross electrophoresis using zinc ions. This electrophoresis was found to have a general applicability to the isolation of a trace amount of a proper compound from a given matrix which forms insoluble complexes.
