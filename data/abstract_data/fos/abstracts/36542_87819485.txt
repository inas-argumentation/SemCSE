Cloning and sequence analysis of NAC transcription factors in Moso (Phyllostachys edulis)
NAC(NAM,ATAF1,2 and CUC2) proteins are plant-specific transcription factors,and have crucial roles in plant development,organ formation,hormonal regulation,biotic and abiotic stress response,defense,etc.In this study,with BeNAC1 as a bait gene,seven sequences which have high similarity with BeNAC1 were found in the Moso(Phyllostachys edulis) EST database through NCBI tblastn,and were named as PeNAC1,PeNAC2,PeNAC3,PeNAC4,PeNAC5,PeNAC6 and PeNAC7,respectively.The 7 transcription factors showed similar gene structure,contained 3 exons and 2 introns.The functional analysis showed that these NAC transcription factors may be involved in hormonal regulation,abiotic stresses(drought stress,salt stress,cold stress) and biotic stresses(insect) defense regulation,and some may be involved in leaf senescence regulation of moso bamboo.
