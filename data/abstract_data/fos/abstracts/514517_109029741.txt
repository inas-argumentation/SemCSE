VIBRATORY HARVESTING MACHINE FOR BOXTHORN (Lycium chinense MILL) BERRIES
Physical and mechanical characteristics of boxthorn berries, strength properties of berry–stem joints, and 
detachment efficiencies of berries with various vibration characteristics were analyzed. From results of the studies above, 
a mechanical harvester with a hand–held shaker, a collecting system, and a traveling system was developed and its harvesting 
rate was evaluated. The detachment force of the mature berries was 2.7 times lower than the immature berries, and the 
force–to–weight ratio was significantly different between the mature and immature berries among 12 varieties. The most 
desirable shaking conditions, which showed the highest detachment efficiency of the mature berries, were frequencies from 
94.2 to 125.7 rad/s, amplitudes from 25 to 35 mm, and a shaking time of 3 s with an experimental vibration system. The 
throughput capacity of the boxthorn harvester was about 4717 g/h, and it was around 4.2 times higher than hand picking.
