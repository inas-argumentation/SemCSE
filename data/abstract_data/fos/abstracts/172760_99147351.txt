Determination of Trace Vanadium(V) Using Polarographic Adsorptive Catalytic Wave of Vanadium-Morin-Bromate System
Using morin firstly as a complexing agent, a new polarographic adsorptive catalytic method for trace vanadium determination was proposed based on the catalytic cycle consisting of electrochemical reduction of vanadium(V) ion in vanadium–morin complex and synchronous chemical oxidation of the reduced product by bromate. Detection limit of this method was 2.0 × 10−9 mol L−1, which was two orders of magnitude lower than those obtained by reduction wave method.
