Study of Wear Resistance of Ni Based Alloy Tungsten Carbide Composite Coating Using Vacuum Fusion Sintering
The wear test of Ni based alloy tungsten carbide composite coating using vacuum fusion sintering method without lubrication is carried on a MPX 2000 pin on disc wear tester.The results showed that the coatings with low content of WC(25%、35%)have excellent wear resistance while the wear resistance of the coatings with high content(60%) is bad.The wear mechanism of composite coatings is abrasive wear.
