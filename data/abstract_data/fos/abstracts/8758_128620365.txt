The greenschist facies of an Archean assemblage near Wawa, Ontario
A stock of trondhjemite intrudes an Archean succession metamorphosed to the greenschist facies at Gutcher Lake, 30 km north of Wawa in Ontario. The stock is 4 km2 in plan and is partly enveloped by an aureole of epidote–amphibole hornfels up to 1 km wide. Within this aureole chlorite partly replaces biotite; chlorite, calcite, and quartz mantle hornblende and epidote; zoned amphibole has a rim of actinolite; and albite varies from 0 to 8% An. The stock has chlorite pseudomorphous after biotite, and feldspar is mottled by white mica and has a clear rim of albite. Fractures filled with quartz, calcite, ankerite, white mica, chlorite, pyrite, and native gold cross-cut the stock and its aureole. Wall rocks to these veins were modified by hydrothermal alteration with addition of Si, Fe, K, H2O + CO2, S, and Rb, leaching of Na, and a shift in Fe2+/Fet from ~0.66 to ~0.90.
