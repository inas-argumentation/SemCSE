Utilization of Mine Solid Wastes in Land Reclamation
With the analysis on ecological environment problems caused by mine exploitation,the paper introduces the significance of land reclamation and the current status of mine solid wastes disposal in China.The method using mine solid wastes for land reclamation,which is suitable for the mines of China,is summarized according to the successful experience of typical land reclamation in mining areas.The reutilization of mining castoff in land reclamation can bring significant environmental and economic benefits.
