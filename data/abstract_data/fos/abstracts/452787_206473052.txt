Comment on "Experiences of children treating functional bladder disturbances on schooldays" by Barbro Lundblad et al.
This is an extremely interesting and relevant study. The problems associated with compliance with training programmes within the school environment are well known amongst urology nurse specialists. What is needed is a wider appreciation of the issues surrounding school toilets by both the schools and local government. This study clearly demonstrates the fear and disgust experienced by children using toilets at school and also the long-term effect they have on children’s health. I was impressed with the method of collecting the data. Interviewing children in the home is the most effective way of gaining information, and the fact that the interviews were conducted as conversations would indicate that the information collected was reliable. The study discusses many different aspects of coping with bladder dysfunction at school including the need to be secretive, the feelings of being different and the apparent lack of understanding of teaching staff.
