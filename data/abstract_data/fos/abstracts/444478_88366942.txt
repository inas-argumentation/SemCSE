Study on the effect of supercritical CO_2 extraction parameters on the contents of functional ingredients
The effect of extraction pressure,temperature and time on the oil yield,fatty acid composition,and the contents of tocopherols and carotenoids of sea buckthorn whole berry oil extracted by supercritical CO2 were investigated.The results showed that the oil yield increased with increasing extraction pressure and first increased and then decreased with the rise of temperature.The fatty acid composition and four isomers of tocopherols displayed no significant difference at different extraction conditions.The contents of myristic,palmitic,palmitoleic acids and four isomers of tocopherols declined while stearic,oleic,linoleic and linolenic acids increased as the extraction time extended.
