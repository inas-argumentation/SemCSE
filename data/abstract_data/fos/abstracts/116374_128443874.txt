Geochemistry and petrogenesis of primitive alkali basalt from Mauritius, Indian Ocean
Intermediate Series lava from Mauritius composes a suite of primitive alkali basalt, basanite, and nephelinite, which display marked primary geochemical enrichment trends for K, Ti, P, Sr, Rb, Ba, and Zr and have probably been erupted unmodified from at least 45 to 60 km (15 to 20 kb). Younger Series lava, primarily alkali basalt with subordinate basanite, displays less well developed primary geochemical enrichment patterns that show scattering compatible with the overprinting effects of low-pressure olivine fractionation.
