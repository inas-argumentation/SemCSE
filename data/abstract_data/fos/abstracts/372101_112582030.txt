Research on Integrated Simulation of Fatigue Life for Cold Extrusion Part
The process of forming and fatigue destruction of cold extrusion part was considered as a system,using UG,MSC.Marc and MSC.Fatigue softwares as a platform,the problem of different software interface was solved reasonably,the integrated analysis system of fatigue life was made.Furthermore,the transmission shaft was taken for example to introduce integrated simulation.The method can help designers improve the quality of design.
