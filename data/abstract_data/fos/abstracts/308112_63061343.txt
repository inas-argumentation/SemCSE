Application of Project Teaching Method in Computer Network Technology Course in Higher Vocational Colleges
Computer Network Technology course in Higher Vocational Colleges with the concept of understanding of abstract and practical features,relying on classroom lectures are far less than ideal teaching effectiveness. Project-driven approach of teaching theory and practice organic integration,is useful in the classroom to help students develop creativity,to build better knowledge and application of social reality,so as to achieve the purpose of teaching. Takes network basic—network topology structure and its mapping as an example,the use of project-driven method of teaching design,and application of this method of teaching practice,to obtain a good teaching results.
