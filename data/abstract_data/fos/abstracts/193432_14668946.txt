Good l-filtrations for q-GL 3 (k)
Abstract Let k be an algebraically closed field of characteristic p, possibly zero, and G = q - GL 3 ( k ) , the quantum group of three by three matrices as defined by Dipper and Donkin. We may also take G to be GL 3 ( k ) . We first determine the extensions between simple G-modules for both G and G 1 , the first Frobenius kernel of G. We then determine the submodule structure of certain induced modules, Z ˆ ( λ ) , for the infinitesimal group G 1 B . We induce this structure to G to obtain a good l-filtration of certain induced modules, ∇ ( λ ) , for G. We also determine the homomorphisms between these induced modules for G.
