A Trans-Cam Mechanism for Harvesting Wet and Dryland Taro
AN improved version of a single operation, one-row harvester for removing complete taro plants from the ground is reported. This machine performed the same functions as a previously developed harvester, but had the following major improvements: 1 Vertical adjustment of digging auger. 2 Reduction of overall harvester weight by over 30 percent. 3 Reduction of rotor loads in pickup mechanism by 50 percent. Field trials demonstrated that the new version of the harvester performed satisfactorily, especially the redesigned pickup mechanism. This report gives major emphasis to the description of this new pickup mechanism.
