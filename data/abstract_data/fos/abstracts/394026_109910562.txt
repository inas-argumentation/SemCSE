Sugar Beet Response to Water Stress in Sandy Soils
LEAF xylem pressure data suggest brief reductions to about -14 to - 15 bars may be tolerable pro-vided average pressure over 10-20 day intervals is above — 12 bars. Seasonal average pressures of -15 and -21 bars were found to reduce yields to 0.6 and 0.1 that of a nonstress treatment, respectively. Water management criteria based upon allowable levels of leaf xylem pressure are suggested.
