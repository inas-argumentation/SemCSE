Structure and Development of Insect-gall Induced by Leaf-gall Midge,Asphondylia sp.
Conventional paraffin section and Olympus SZX-16 stereomicroscope were used to study the structure and development of insect-gall,induced by Leaf-gall midge,Asphondyia sp.The results showed that the gall-midge females in longan leaves laid their eggs near the vascular tissue of longan leaves,and when the larva hatched and began to feed on the leaf mesophyll tissues.This activity stimulated the formation of insect-gall.The insect-gall was increased with the larvae development.The anatomical structure of gall-room from outside to inside was outer epidermis,sclerenchyma layer as well as thin-walled cells layer.According to the change of gall tissue and anatomical features,the development of insect-gall was divided into 4 phases,including initiation phase,growth and differentiation phase,maturation phase and dehiscence phase.The internal structure of insect-gall was consisted of epidermis that provided protection for the growth of larvae and pupae of longan leaf gall midge,protective layer,nutritive layer,the main constituent of which was thin-walled cells,and also provide an ample and reliable source of food for growth and development of larvae.
