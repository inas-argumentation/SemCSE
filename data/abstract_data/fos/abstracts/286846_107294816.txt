BRIEF COMMUNICATIONS: Injection GaInPAs/InP heterojunction laser with 6-7° output divergence, emitting nonwaveguide modes
A heterojunction laser emitting at 1.255 m was constructed. The active layer was inclined at an angle of 13/sup 0/ relative to the resonator axis. This inclination suppressed excitation of waveguide modes and ensured generation of nonwaveguide modes with an output divergence of 6--7/sup 0/. The minimum threshold current density at room temperature was 18 kA/cm/sup 2/, which was only 3 times higher than in similar waveguide lasers.
