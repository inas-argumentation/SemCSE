The Coefficients of the Normalized Maximum Contrast as Statistics for Posttest ANOVA Data Interpretations
A fundamental fact of analysis of variance is that there exists at least one contrast significantly-different-from-zero according to the Scheffe multiple comparisons method, if the omnibus F-test is significant. The caveat to this rule is that the significant contrast(s) may not be of any scientific value or of any interest to the investigator. Furthermore, complex contrasts (contrasts other than simple pairwise contrasts) usually lack any real meaning with respect to the problem at hand. The intent of this paper is to introduce the concept of a normalized maximum contrast, the normalized contrast which is statistically the most significantly-different-from-zero, and to indicate its value to support the interpretations of an analysis of variance result.
