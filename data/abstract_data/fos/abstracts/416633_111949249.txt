An Application of Lime Soil Compaction Pile in Collapsible Loess Foundation Treatment
The paper analyzes and discusses lime soil compaction pile,construction,ramming process,compaction effect and bearing capacity of foundation during through foundation treatment and construction quality supervision and management,and discourses upon the reliability and practicability of the lime soil compaction pile in collapsible loess foundation treatment,by combining an example of application of the lime soil compaction pile in collapsible loess foundation treatment.
