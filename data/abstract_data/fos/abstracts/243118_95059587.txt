Vapor-phase sorption of p-xylene and water on soils and clay minerals
The competitive adsorption of p-xylene and water vapors on soil materials was studied to elucidate the mechanisms responsible for vapor-phase sorption in the unsaturated zone. Isotherms obtained from the adsorption of p-xylene vapors on oven-dried silica gel, kaolinite, and Webster soil were nonlinear over the range of relative vapor pressures investigated. Increasing the relative humidity to 67 and 90% resulted in dramatic reductions in p-xylene sorption and a shift to isotherms that were linear at p-xylene relative vapor pressure below 0.5
