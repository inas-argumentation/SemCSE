Study on Distribution of Magnesium in Ductile Iron
The distribution of magnesium in ductile iron and the mechanism of graphite modularization were studied.The experimental results show that the surface of ductile iron casting has a large amount of magnesium,and there is a gradient distribution of magnesium from outside to inside in the cross section.It can be explained that the heterogeneous distribution of magnesium is caused by the bilateral migration of the bubbles in the molten metal,the bubble migration is caused by the extrusion force which derives from the volume shrinkage of the liquid metal and the graphite expansion.
