Determination of Benzoyl Peroxide in Wheat Flour by Post-column Derivatization-High PerformanceLiquid Chromatography with Fluorescence Detection
A method for the determination of benzoyl peroxide in wheat flour by post-column derivatization-high performance liquid chromatography with fluorescence detection was developed based on the reaction that the p-hydroxyphenylacetic acid can be oxidized to the fluorescent dimer by benzoyl peroxide with hemin as catalyzer.The optimum derivatization efficiency was obtained with the hemin and p-hydroxyphenylacetic acid concentration at 8 μmol/L and 80 μmol/L,respectively,with the derivatization reagent pH at 10.5 and the derivatization temperature at 35 ℃.The linear range between 0.5 mg/L to 100 mg/L was observed for the established method.The sample detection limit was 1 mg/kg and standard spike recoveries were 98.5%-99.5% under the optimized conditions.The obvious advantages of excellent anti-jamming capability and high-sensitivity were observed comparing with other HPLC methods for determination of benzoyl peroxide.
