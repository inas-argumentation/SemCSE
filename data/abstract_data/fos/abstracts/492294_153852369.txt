No one's world: the west, the rising rest, and the coming global turn
Kupchan attempts to argue that the international order will soon be led by no one as multipolarity creates multiple conceptions of world order. Kupchan also rejects the “end of history” thesis and believes that the West’s path to modernity was a unique result of historical factors. Yet, Kupchan’s analysis occasionally fails to provide enough support for his claims and some of his recommendations for the West are misguided.
