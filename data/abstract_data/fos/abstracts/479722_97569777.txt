Adsorption of Water Vapor on Crystalline Phases of V-P-O
Adsorption of water vapor on crystalline compounds of V-P-O was examined by a TPD method. Water molecules chemisorbed on the V-P-O compounds composed of V(IV) ions such as α- and β-(VO)2P2O7 and VO(PO3)2, were desorbed at 400 °C. However, from compounds consisting of V(V) like V2O5 and VOPO4, only physically adsorbed water molecules were desorbed at 80–200 °C. It can be concluded that the valence of V ions is a key factor for the chemisorption of water vapor on V-P-O compounds.
