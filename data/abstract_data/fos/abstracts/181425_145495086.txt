Content Analysis of Basal Reading Texts for Normal and Retarded Children
MARLYS M. MITCHELL, Ph.D. GILBERT G. RAGLAND, Ed.D. University of North Carolina Studies have been made of the content of traditional basal reading series read by children in regular public school classrooms. However. no research has been done concerning content of the stories read by educable mentally retarded (EMR) children. Since a major instruc-' tional area for EMR children is reading, such lack of research effort is surprising and deplorable. Many special educators believe that the teacher of the retarded should place greater emphasis on reading than on any other subject (Smith, 1974). Robinson and Robinson (1 965) point out that regular graded materials are often inappropriate, uninteresting, or even humiliating to retarded children.
