On the Japanese Kwantung Army’s Intelligence War in the Soviet Far East
Harboring wild ambitions for occupying Soviet Far East area, Japan unfolded active intelligence war to Soviet Union after it captured Northeast China. For this purpose, the Kwantung Army worked out detailed intelligence collecting plan, making use of various means to collecting useful information. Large number of Chinese were sent to Soviet Union to gather information, becoming victims of Japan- USSR military wrestles. Kwantung Army was doomed to failure in spite of its efforts to avoid it.
