Screening of human neutralizing Fab molecules to spike protein of SARS virus from phage library
Objective To select human neutralizing Fab molecules to spike protein from human phage antibody combinatorial library. Methods The library was biopanned and the binding of human antibody to spike protein was detected by ELISA, and the neutralizing Fab was pre screened by the inhibition ELISA; furthermore, neutralizing test was utilized to confirm the neutralizing capacity of the antibody selected by inhibition ELISA, followed by nucleotide sequencing of the two Fab molecules. Results Phage antibodies to spike protein were specifically enriched and two Fab fragments selected were capable of inhibiting binding activity of monoclonal antibody 2C5 to spike protein, M1A and P8A. Neutralizing test confirmed partial neutralizing capability. Alignment of sequence showed that the two Fabs fragments represented individual clones. Conclusion Two neutralizing antibodies against SARS virus were obtained, which may be helpful for the study of passive immunoprophylaxis against SARS virus.
