On the Development of Tunxi Old Street in the View of Protecting Architectural Heritage in Tourism City
Though Tunxi Old Street has its own characteristics with a long history,it is now facing many problems in the protection of architectural heritage with the development of tourism,such as the destruction of the block structure and the living environment,the single internal function and the insufficient cultural characteristics.In order to develop the Tunxi Old Street,the government should increase its investment to improve traffic conditions around the Tunxi Old Street,provide a natural environment blending with the landscape,and guide the residents and operators to participate in the heritage conservation and development.Only by adhering to the principle of comprehensive development can the government develop the Old Street into a comprehensive recreation area.
