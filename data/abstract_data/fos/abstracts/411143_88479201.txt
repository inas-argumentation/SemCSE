Profile of NK and NKT cells in HIV/AIDS patients of China
Objective To study the changes of NK and NKT cells in HIV-infection. Methods Peripheral blood cells were stained with antibodies labeled with fluorescence and NK and NKT cells were counted with flow cytometry. Results The absolute counts of NK and NKT cells in HIV/AIDS patients was significantly lower than that of normal controls. The number of CD4+ T cells was remarkably less than that of normal controls; while CD8+ T cells was significantly higher than that of normal controls. The percentage of NK cells in HIV/AIDS patients was remarkably lower than that of normal controls. The absolute counts of NK cells in HIV/AIDS patients positively correlated to the number of CD4+ T cells, r= 0.289, P0.01 and the absolute counts of NKT cells positively correlated to the number of CD4+ T cells, also positively correlated to the number of CD8+ T cells. The NK and NKT cells in HIV infection and AIDS groups were lower than those of long-term nonprogressors and normal controls(P0.05, P0.01); while there was no difference in the counts of NK and NKT cells between long-term nonprogressors and normal controls. Conclusion The absolute counts of NK and NKT cells in HIV/AIDS patients were decreased by the infection of HIV and it correlates with disease progression.
