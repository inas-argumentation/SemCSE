Construction of Working Concurrent with Learning Computer Network System
Curriculum system is the basis and key of the major construction,which determines whether the higher vocational professional training objectives can be achieved.The compound and intersection of computer network affects colleges to determine the training objectives;it also impacts the employment of college graduates in E-commerce.After analyzing the current situation and problems of computer network curriculum system,discusses the basic principles of computer network curriculum construction,and proposes the work concurrent with learning program of computer network curriculum system construction.
