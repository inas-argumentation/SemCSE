Research on Ni Activation sintering Process for Mo-30% Cu Composite Material
Ni activation sintering Mo-30%Cu composite material was studied by the method of activation sintering of adding transition metal elements,and the sintering mechanism are analyzed.The results show that the method of adding element Ni accelerates the sintering process of Mo-30%Cu,increases the density.It is concluded that the formation of Mo excess space is the main reason for the increase of the density.
