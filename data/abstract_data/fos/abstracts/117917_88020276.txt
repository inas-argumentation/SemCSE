Isolation and molecular biological identification of a halophilic bacterium with yield of compatible solute ectoine
A halophilic bacterial strain SL39 highly producing compatible solute Ectoine was obtained from the soil of Pulandian salt pool, Dalian. The phylogenetic analysis based on 16S rDNA showed that strain SL39 was close to halobacillus trueperi with 100% sequence identity. The optimal temperature for the SL39 cell growth is 30℃ and pH is 7.0. It grows at the concentration of NaCl ranging between 0~25%(w/v). For its growth, the optimnm NaCl concentration is 5%(w/v). SL39 synthesized Ectoine in the culture medium with 2mol/L NaCl, the quantity of Ectoine synthesization is 304.8mg/L at 30℃, 48h.
