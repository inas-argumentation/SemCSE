Effect of Solution Temperature on Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of TC4ELI Titanium Alloy
The effects of solution temperature on the microstructure and mechanical properties of TC4ELI titanium alloy were investigated.The results show that with the increase of solution temperature,the quantity of equiaxed α phase of TC4ELI titanium alloy decreases and the quantity of transformed β increases gradually.And the tensile strength,yield strength and fracture toughness of TC4ELI titanium alloy increases with the increase of solution temperature.However,elongation after fracture and reduction of area decreases gradually.In particular,the reduction of area decreases most significantly.The crack growth rate of TC4ELI titanium alloy reduces with the increase of solution temperature.
