Application of Task Driven Teaching Method in Software Technology Specialty in Higher Vocational Education
At present, the higher vocational colleges have opened the software technology specialty, but usually some students have the weariness mood on a class or the professional in the actual teaching, and the teaching effect is not ideal. Therefore, uses task -driven teaching method to combine the pure theory teaching with the practical project or case. Analyzes the characteristics of software technology specialty in higher vocational education and some shortcomings of the traditional teaching method in software technology specialty in higher vocational education, mainly elaborates the implementation process of the task driven teaching method in software technology specialty and some matters needed to get attention in implementation, and explores a new idea for constructing the school-enterprise cooperation characteristic professional.
