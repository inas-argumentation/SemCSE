Analysis of internal transcribed spacer (ITS) sequence in common dermatopbytes
Analysis of internal transcribed spacer (ITS) sequence in dermatophytes and to identification and classification. We described a phylogenetic tree in order to study the relationship between them. Methods Sixteen strains from research center for medical mycology of Peking University were tested. The strains were cultured on PDA and other culture medium at 28℃ for 2-3 weeks, the morphological features of the colonies were examed. All strains were cultured in YPG medium, colonies were collected. Fungal general primers of ITS1 and ITS4 were selected to amplify ITS of 16 strains. After purification, the PCR products were sequenced directly and were analyzed through claswal on internet. Describing a phylogenetic tree referencing 6 strains sequences from NCBI. Results Eleven strains were identified for morphologic characteristics, which were 5 strains of Trichophy-ton, 5 strains of Microsporum, 1 strain of Epidemophyton and 5 unidentifiable strains and 16 sequences were obtained including Microsporum nanum that there was not full sequences of ITS on internal. Eleven strains were identified by two methods. One strain was identified by sequences as Chrysosporwn. But 4 strains can not be identified at last. According to ITS sequences dermatophytes were divided into 3 groups which were different from traditional classification. Conclusion Molecular method is sensitivity and fast. It needs supplement from morphologic and molecular methods. It may be a kind of identification and nomedature for dermatophytes, according to ITS sequences.
