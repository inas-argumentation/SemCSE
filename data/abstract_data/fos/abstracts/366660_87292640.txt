Preliminary studies on anti-fungal and radical scavenging activities of extracts from mycelia of the genus Paecilomyces spp.
Screening for anti-fungal and radical scavenging activities of extracts from mycelia of 29 strains belonging to 11 species of the genus Paecilomyces revealed that strain RCEF0866 of Paecilomyces gunnni had relative strong anti-fungal activity,the strain RCEF0866 and RCEF4118 had strong radical scavenging activity.At a mycelial concentration of 100.0 mg·mL-1,the ethylacetate extract of strain RCEF0866 had(18.1±1.7) mm di-ameter of inhibition zone against Candida albicans.At mycelial concentration of 5.0 mg·mL-1,the free radical scavenging activities of methanol extracts from mycelia of RCEF0866 and RCEF4118 were 82.75% and 79.53%,respectively.Anti-fungal TLC assay showed that the Rf value of the active compound from RCEF0866 was 0.90,which means that it is a low polar compound.DPPH-TLC test showed that the Rf value of the radical scavenger from RCEF0866 was 0.80,which also means that it is a low polar compound.
