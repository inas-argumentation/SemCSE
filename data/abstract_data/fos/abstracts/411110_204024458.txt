Distribution Attribution of Diameter Node and Width Diameter Nodefor Double-loop Networks
As for each double-loop network G(N;1,h),based on its definitions of diameter and wide diameter,and by aid of routing for diameter and wide diameter in its equivalent L-tile,it is founded that the sum of diameter node and wide diameter node for each double-loop network is respectively smaller than or equal to 2,and that the diameter node must locate in the first Quadrant,while the wide diameter node must locate on the x Axis or y Axis.As for double-loop network family G(N;1,2≤h≤N-1),by the increasing of step h,the value of its serial routing node has the characteristic of from small to big and then from big to small.By the aid of using vb6.0 to simulate the distribution diagram of diameter node and wide diameter node,it is founded that the value of diameter node and wide diameter node has the characteristic of fluctuating down,and that diameter node value is often bigger than wide diameter node value,and that they both reach biggest when step in the middle,and that as for one double-loop network,if there exist two diameter nodes then there must exist two wide diameter nodes.
