Statistical analysis on nationwide excellent doctoral dissertations
Based on the relevant data of national excellent doctoral dissertations during 1999-2008,this paper analyzes the degree granting institutions and disciplines distribution.Through the empirical study,the following phenomena are observed: from the degree granting institutions,the winners highly concentrate in Chinese Academy of Sciences,Tsinghua University,Beijing University,Fudan University and other prestigious institutions;from provinces distribution,excellent dissertations mainly happened to Beijing,Shanghai,Jiangsu,Shaanxi and other provinces;from disciplines distribution,the best thesises mostly focus on the disciplines of technology,science and medicine,moreover,literature,history,law,education and economics in the social science.As a result,it is unbalanced in the distribution of these papers because of Matthew Effect,it also determined by social and economic development and trend of discipline development in line with the development law of science and education.
