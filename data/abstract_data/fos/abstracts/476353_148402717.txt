St. Paul College of Macao and the beginning of Western-style higher education in China
St. Paul College of Macao established in 1594 is not only the beginning of Western\|style higher education of Macao, but also the first Western\|style university on the land of China. The establishment of this college promots the modernization of China's education and plays the role of a bridge in the cultural exchange between China and the West as well.
