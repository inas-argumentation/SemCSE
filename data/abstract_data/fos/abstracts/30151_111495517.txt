A Double Bus Switching Circuit Metering Failure Accident
Substation in double bus bar connection adopts metering voltage switching circuit,when voltage switching circuit appears accident,metering staff cannot make accurate analysis for it is out of their maintenance responsibility.In this paper,the metering voltage switching circuit operation principle is introduced,power calculation when Watt-hour meter in three-phase four-wire system is also introduced.Through the example of 220kV power substation metering failure accident discusses the situation analysis and processing of the accident,thus puts forward corresponding preventive measures.
