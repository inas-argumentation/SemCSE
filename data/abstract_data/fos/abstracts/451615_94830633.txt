Activity and selectivity in the electrophilic substitution of five-membered heterorings (review)
Data on the activity and selectivity in reactions involving the electrophilic substitution of five-membered heteroaromatic compounds with one heteroatom are discussed. Chief attention is directed to the reactions of pyrrole, furan, thiophene, and their substituted derivatives. The inconsistency in the change in the activity (reaction rate) — N>O>S — and in the positional selectivity (the α∶ß ratio) — N<S<O — that exists for all electrophilic substitution reactions in these systems is interpreted with allowance for the mechanisms of the reactions and the nature of the heteroatom.
