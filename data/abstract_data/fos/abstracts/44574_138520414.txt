Influence of Deformation Process on the Al-Ti-C Master Alloy
It was investigated that the influences of deformation process on the grain refining effect of Al Ti C master alloy. The results showed that the grain refining effect of Al Ti C master alloy is decreased after deformation with different methods, and the more the deformation, the more the decreases of the grain refining of Al Ti C master alloy. It was analyzed that the TiAl 3 phases are broken after deformation, and that TiC particles inside TiAl 3 are separated from TiAl3 phases, which results in the reduction of grain refining effect.
