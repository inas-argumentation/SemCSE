Tactical and technical shortcomings of the men’s basketball team of China from the perspective of basketball games played in Beijing Olympic Games
The author performed a comparative analysis on various technical and tactical indexes for the 6 games played by the men’s basketball team of China against its opponents as well as the 32 games played by top 4 men’s basketball teams in Beijing Olympic Games,and revealed the following findings: the men’s basketball team of China has made significant progress in recent years,and in terms of related indexes the differences between the Chinese basketball team and its opponents are gradually shrunk;however,compared with the winning teams,the Chinese basketball team still showed such signs as slow rhythm of offense and defense switching,poor ability of scoring at fast breaks and inside the ring,disconnection between players inside and outside the ring at offense,lack-ing in offensive power at defense,untimely position switching,inadequate ability of overall offense and defense co-ordination,and not adaptive to high intensity physical contests.
