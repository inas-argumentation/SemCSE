An Interdisciplinary Exploration into the Government Policy Process
This article attempts to summarize theoretical results of the policy process research from two disciplines---the Public Administration and Business Administration.It is found that both of them emphasize the action context and participants in the government policy process.The article proposes that policy process research should be carried on from the organizational behavior perspective, analyzing the interaction between government organizations at different levels on the one hand,and combining the integrate research of policy network theory in the field of public management and the cooperate political activity in the field of Business Administration on the other.
