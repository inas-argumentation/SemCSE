Ultrasonic wave assisted extraction of superoxide dismutase from porcine blood
A combined method-ultrasonic wave and thermal denaturation was used to extract superoxide dismutase from porcine blood.The amount of water,ultrasonic working frequency,ultrasonic power,the quantity of CuSO4,thermal denaturation temperature,and so on,were mainly investigated.The optimal extracting conditions were: the ratio of red cells to water 5∶1,ultrasonic working frequency 60,ultrasonic power 200 W,the amount of 5 mmol/L CuSO4 3 mL,thermal denaturation temperature 65 ℃,thermal denaturation time 15 min.The activity per unit volume was 1083.333 U/mL,the specific activity was 3997.539 U/mg.Ultrasonic wave assisted extraction was an effective technique for the extraction of SOD.
