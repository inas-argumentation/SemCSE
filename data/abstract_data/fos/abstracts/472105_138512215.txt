Solidification Structure of Fly Ash Foam Ceramic-aluminum Matrix Composite
The foam ceramic-aluminum matrix composite was fabricated by squeeze casting process,Al-Si and Al-23Si alloys were used as matrix.By optical metallographic,the influences of interface bonding of the composite and foam ceramic structure on the solidification were studied under different pouring temperatures.The results show that,as the pouring temperature increasing,the filling capacity of the aluminum alloy gets better with grain size growing up.Both eutectic and hypereutectic aluminum alloy can manufacture the composite with superior interface bonding.Foam ceramic structure can play a role in nucleation of two kinds of alloys solidification.
