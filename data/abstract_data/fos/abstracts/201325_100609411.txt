ON THE REACTIONS OF BENZO[ a ]PHENAZINE-5,6-DIONE 7-OXIDES WITH METHANOLIC ALKALI SOLUTION AND PYRROLIDINE
When treated with methanolic alkali, benzo[ а ]phenazine-5,6-dione 7-oxides convert into 11 Н -indeno[1,2- b ]quinoxalin-11-ones, whereas 11-hydroxy-11-(pyrrolidine-1-carbonyl)-11 Н- indeno[1,2- b ]quinoxaline 10-oxides form upon treatment with pyrrolidine.
