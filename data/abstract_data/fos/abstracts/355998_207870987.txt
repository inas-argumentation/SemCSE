On Approximation of $2$D Persistence Modules by Interval-decomposables
In this work, we propose a new invariant for $2$D persistence modules called the compressed multiplicity and show that it generalizes the notions of the dimension vector and the rank invariant. In addition, we propose an "interval-decomposable approximation" $\delta^{\ast}(M)$ of a $2$D persistence module $M$. In the case that $M$ is interval-decomposable, we show that $\delta^{\ast}(M) = M$. Furthermore, even for representations $M$ not necessarily interval-decomposable, $\delta^{\ast}(M)$ preserves the dimension vector and the rank invariant of $M$.
