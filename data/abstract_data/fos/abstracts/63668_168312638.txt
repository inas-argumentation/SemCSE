Problems in the Current Risk Management of Chinese Commercial Banks and Countermeasures to Them
Currently, compared with leadind foreign banks, China's commercial banks have great distance in solving credit risk , market risk, liquidity risk and operation risk. This problem will get more obvious with China's entry into WTO and New Basel Agreement going into effect in 2006. Only China's commercial banking industry seeks strategies in the corporate culture, risk management structure and system, as well as the application of risk management technologies can it survive in the competitive market.
