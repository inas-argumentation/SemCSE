Effect of Secondary Welding Thermal Cycle on Impact Toughness of Weld-coarse-grained HAZ in X80 Pipeline Steel
The change law of impact toughness and microstructure of weld-coarse-grained HAZ(WCGHAZ) under different secondary peak temperature in X80 pipeline steel was investigated by welding thermal simulation technology,modern physical testing technology and mechanical property testing.The results indicate that the toughness of WCGHAZ reaches the lowest and the local embrittlement tendency appears when the secondary peak temperature is in the(α+γ) range.The local embrittlement is caused mostly by M-A constituents with rich carbon.
