Study on the lithium solid electrolytes of Li 3N-Li 3Bi-LiCl ternary system — 2Li 3Bi · 3LiCl lithium solid electrolyte
Lithium solid electrolyte 2Li 3 Bi · 3LiCl was prepared and was characterized by X-ray diffraction and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS). The electrical conductivity of the prepared sample was determined from room temperature to 250 °C by PAR 368-1 AC-impedance spectrometer. The frequency measurement range was between 4 Hz and 100 kHz. The experimental results showed that the 2Li 3 Bi · 3LiCl lithium solid electrolyte has a high conductivity of about 1 × 10 −4 S/cm at 100 °C and 1 × 10 −2 S/cm at 350 °C.
