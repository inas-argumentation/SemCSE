The acclimatization on a resistant-oxygen strain and study on the culture condition of the Bifidobacterium adolescentis
We got a resistant-oxygen strain of Bifidobacterium adolescentis-Strain ad through acclimatizing the strain of Bifidobacterium adolescentis. The resistant-oxygen strain can grow in confining container with air well. Then we study on how to culture the strain with soya-bean milk as a main raw material. The optimal medium contained the following ingredients: on the base of 3Bx soya-bean milk we need to add 1% glucose,0.5% milk-peptide made by ourselves, 2.5% tomato extract and 2.5% carrot extract. The optimal pH 7.0, the vaccinated quantity 2%, fermentation temperature 37℃. The strain may be incubated anaerobically and motionlessly for 24～48 hours. The viable count of Bifidobacterium adolescentis can reach 6.9×109cfu/mL in the broth. The broth of the Strain ad is frozen directly without adding other protectants. The survival rate of the Strain ad will be increased if it is pre-heated for a period before freeze-drying. The viable count with water is 1.0×1010cfu/g. The viable count of the sample without water is 1.1×1010cfu/g.
