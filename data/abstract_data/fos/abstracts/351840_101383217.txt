Ultrafine grinding optimization of extraction of flavonoids from Linden bee pollen
Ultrafine grinding was used to treat Linden bee pollen for cell wall disruption, the extraction of total flavonoids was researched. The orthogonal experiment showed, the extraction temperature had the highest effect, followed by ethanol concentration and extraction time, the effect of solid-liquid ratio was the weakest. The optimal levels of the particle size of ultrafine grinding, ethanol concentration, solid-liquid ratio, extraction temperature and extraction time were 30 mesh, 80%, 1:40, 80 ℃, 2 h. Under the optimized extraction conditions, the content of total flavonoids was 2.58 mg/g. Using ultrafine grinding method, the broken rate of pollen was 90%, increasing the dissolution of flavonoids.
