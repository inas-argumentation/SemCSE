[Neurophysiological characteristics of juvenile schizophrenia patients examined at the very remote follow up stage].
OBJECTIVE
To determine neurophysiological characteristics of patients with long-term follow up (>20 years) and to find correlations between neurophysiological parameters and clinical features of schizophrenia.


MATERIAL AND METHODS
The patients were divided into three groups: with predomination of personality changes (group 1, n=17), with negative disorders (group 2, n=23) and with positive and negative disorders (group 3, n=40). A psychopathological method and electroencephalography with evoked potentials testing were used.


RESULTS AND CONCLUSION
In group 3, the statistically significant higher frequencies of theta-rhythm and lower of alpha-rhythm were found. Also, theta frequency correlated with PANSS positive scores. The significant intergroup differences by auditory oddball P300 were lacked. The findings are discussed in view of the hypothesis of theta-rhythm as a marker of hippocampal-prefrontal connectivity.
