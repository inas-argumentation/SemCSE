Application of Visualization Theme in Energy Management System
This paper introduces the application of visualization theme surveillance technology in EMS system.The application of 2d and 3d visualization technology for data display used in EMS are discussed,including bar chat,pie chart,flow line,contours,cylindrical/cone,space connecting line,etc.Based on this,the concept of visualization theme surveillance is proposed.And some themes such as voltage,tide,equipment load rate,N-1 analysis results are given the actual examples.The practical application shows that the visualization theme surveillance is helpful for dispatch personnel to find and solve problems.
