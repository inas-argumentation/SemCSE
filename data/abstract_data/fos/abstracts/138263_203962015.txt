INVESTIGATION ON ADDED RESISTANCE MODEL BASED ON ROLL MOTION ATTITUDE
With the increasing needs of fuel-efficiency and emission reduction,the research of added resistance’s effect on the ship navigation performance and control quality should be considered,when a ship motion control system is designed.The added resistance due to ship roll motion is analyzed based on potential flow theory and an added resistance model is presented based on the analysis.The roll motion added resistance is computed with hydrodynamic calculation software AQWA.The added resistance model is modified with the data from AQWA by a regression analysis method.The feasibility of model is validated by comparing the AQWA simulation results with the calculations of the added resistance model.Results provide a theoretical foundation for the study on the control strategy of ship anti-roll under the condition of minimum speed loss.
