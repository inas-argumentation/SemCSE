A Study of Titanium Alloy Integral Control Surface Based on Superplastic Forming/Diffusion Bonding
A study of titanium alloy integral control surface based on superplastic forming/diffusion bonding(SPF/DB) has been performed by targeting on some supersonic aircraft control surface with the characteristics such as small size, high demanding for aerodynamic configuration, heat resistance, mass coefficient, strength and stiffness, etc. The structure is optimized by using APDL to obtain the best grid size and wall thickness. Due to the specific characteristics of part shape and internal structure, the combination of superplastic forming/diffusion bonding (SPF/DB) is applied to achieve the development of the part. The test results show that the best process parameters of control surface forming by employing TC4 titanium alloy four-layer panel are: forming temperature 910°C, outer pressure 1.5MPa maintained for 80min~100min; inner pressure 1.5MPa~2.0MPa maintained for 15min. By part testing, it is found that the bonding rate of diffusion bonding position reaches over 90%, no pits in the cavity, and the formed control surface after machining and edge-trimming inconformity with the design configuration. In addition, thermal and mechanical test shows that the structure meets the requirements of heat resistance, strength and stiffness.
