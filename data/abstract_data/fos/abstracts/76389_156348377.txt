The Influence on Fruiting Cane and Fruit Quality of Loquat by Twice Cutting Back Pruning
In order to improve the extra grade quality fruit proportion of loquat,the influence on fruiting cane,fruit quality and production benefit of loquat by twice cutting back pruning were studied.The results showed that the side born branches would be induced into center branch after twice cutting back pruning.The center branches diameter,blades coarseness and weight were increased,and internodes became shorter,the quality of the fruiting cane were improved.Compared to the control,single fruit weight was increased 19.87 g by 34.69%,and spike weight was increased 55.72 g by 32.05%.The total sugar content of fruits was increased 1.23%,and extra grade quality fruit(more than 65.0 g)proportional was increased greatly,reached to 75.76%,was 2.8 times than that of the control,output value per hectare was increased 34 739.27 RMB by 61.14%.
