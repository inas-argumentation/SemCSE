Disappearance of Hall resistance in one-unit-cell-thick YBa2Cu3O7- delta : Evidence of free vortex-antivortex excitation.
Diagonal and Hall resistivities (p xx and p xy ) in the normal and superconducting states are studied in one-unit-cell-thick YBa 2 Cu 3 O 7-δ artificial materials. A number of transport properties in bulk YBa 2 Cu 3 O 7-δ are shown to be inherent properties of the individual conducting CuO 2 bilayers. Evidence of Kosterlitz-Thouless resistive transition as well as the excitation of free vortex and antivortex is presented
