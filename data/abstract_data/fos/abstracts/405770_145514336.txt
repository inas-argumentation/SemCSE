Leadership and the Perceived Effectiveness of Department Chairpersons.
In the last several years, considerable attention has focused on the role, function, and evaluation of the academic department chairperson [2, 5, 13, 17, 43]. This recent attention is long overdue. Although the department chairperson's position has been widely acknowledged as vital to the operation of colleges and universities, it has suffered from a general lack of attention from educational researchers.
