Considerations on the Equity and Efficiency in the Reform of the Distribution System of State-owned Enterprises
The reform in state-owned enterprises has entered a crucial period, the problem of the equity and efficiency caused by the reform of the distribution system and property right system in the distribution of enterprises has brought to people's great attention, and the contradiction caused by unfair distribution has been restricting the deepening of the reform of state-owned enterprises. The inequity in the reform of the distribution system of state-owned enterprises involves both the problems in theories and the problems in practice caused by the element distribution. The problem of equity should be seriously solved in the present stage. Giving consideration to both the equity and efficiency in the reform of the distribution system of state-owned enterprises will contribute to build a harmonious society.
