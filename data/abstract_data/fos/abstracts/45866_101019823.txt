Primary study on low temperature solid oxide fuel cell fabricated by hot-pressing
The performance of low temperature solid oxide fuel cells fabricated by hot-pressing technology was studied at temperatures below 650℃. The cells consisted of Ce_(0.8)Sm_(0.2)O_(1.9)-salt (Li_2CO_3+Na_2CO_3) composite electrolyte, Ni anode, lithiated NiO cathode and Ni-net current collectors. For a reported cell here, the open-circuit voltages were 0.863 and 0.873 V, the measured maximum power densities were 0.615 and 0.743 mW~ cm~(-2) at 600 and 650℃, respectively. In the best case, the OCV of hot-pressed fuel cell reached 1.006 V (650℃) with higher performance. The performance is significantly improved compared to those fabricated by cold pressing.
