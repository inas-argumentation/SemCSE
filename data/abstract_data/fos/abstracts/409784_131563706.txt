Removing algae by New-typed Algaecide Materials: Experimental Studies in Dianchi Lake
The experimental studies conducted at both bench-scale and in situ scale-up levels. The bench-scale study was with the lake water of 20 litre in the lab while the scale-up was conducted in an in situ enclosure with an area of 300 m~2 in Dianchi water body. The new light building algaecide materials tested have showed efficacies both for contaminants removal (i.e., COD, TN and TP) and algae removal. The enclosure experiment showed an evident decrease of algae concentration in the water after 10 days when the test materials were added.
