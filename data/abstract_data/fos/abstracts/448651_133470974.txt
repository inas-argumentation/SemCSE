The Significance of Biodiversity Conservation on Sustainable Development
The main reason of modern organisms extinct is the effect by human activities. The biodiversity conservation is very important on human beings and sustainable development. In order to keep sustainable development, the consciousness of biodiversity conservation should be raised. The laws and policies on biodiversity conservation should be established and perfected. The study on biodiversity conservation and sustainable use should be strengthened. And reasonable plans against the environmental pollutian should be made.
