Coupled-channel analysis of collisional effects on HFS transitions in antiprotonic helium atoms
Collisions of metastable antiprotonic helium with atoms of a medium induce transitions between hyperfine structure sublevels as well as shifts and broadenings of the microwave M1 spectral lines. We consider these phenomena in the framework of a model with scalar and tensor interactions between and He atoms.
