Competitive sports dissimilation from the perspective of Kant’s artistic aesthetical ideology
In order to specify the boundary between art and non art,Kant set fort 3 stipulations on art in his aesthetical masterpiece "Critique of Judgment",namely,unnatural,practical and skillful.According to his aesthetical theory,we can not only specify aesthetical principles for competitive sport,but also from his classification of artistic layers clearly identify the forms and tendencies which competitive sport is going to dissimilate,has dissimilated or will dissimilate into,thus providing a theoretical reference and practical idea for preventing,reducing or overcoming the sign of competitive sports dissimilation.
