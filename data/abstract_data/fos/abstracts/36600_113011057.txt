Finite Element Analysis of Dedendum Stress in Rotary Forging for Bevel Gear Cavity
The rotary roll for bevel gear is an advanced process for making gear,but the short service life of the die is its drawback,which was mostly attributed to invalidation of dedendum caused by splits.For improving the life-span of the die,the variation of dedendum stress in rotary forging for bevel gear cavity was studied by finite element analysis,and the influence of the change of dedendum stress at different points on the life of the die was discussed.
