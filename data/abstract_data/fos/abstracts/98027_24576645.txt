Intracellular Signalling Involved in Volume Regulatory Decrease
The following volume-sensitive channels are characterized in Ehrlich ascites tumor cells (EATC), (i) a tamoxifen- and AA acid sensitive, outwardly rectifying small anion channel (I<sub>Cl,vol</sub>) with low field anion selectivity (I<sup>-</sup>>Cl<sup>-</sup>) and moderate depolarisation-induced inactivation, (ii) a separate DIDS- and niflumic acid-sensitive organic osmolyte/anion channel (OOC) transporting predominantly taurine, and (iii) a clofilium- and Ba<sup>2+</sup>-sensitive, voltage- and Ca<sup>2+</sup>-insensitive 5 pS K<sup>+</sup> channel (I<sub>K,vol</sub>), resistant to a range of K<sup>+</sup> channel inhibitors including ChTX, clotrimazole, apamin, kaliotoxin, margatoxin, and TEA, and with a pH<sub>o</sub> dependence reminiscent of the two-pore domain background K<sup>+</sup> channels TASK. Cell swelling leads to an immediate and transient 3.3 fold increase in the rate of AA release resulting from activation of cPLA<sub>2</sub>α, which is found to be translocated to the nucleus upon cell swelling (probably to the inner nuclear membrane), where it is phosphorylated and activated by a G-protein coupled process. AA is a precursor for LTC<sub>4</sub>, which is transported out of the cell, where it is converted to LTD<sub>4</sub>, which activates I<sub>K,vol</sub>, and OOC, whereas I<sub>Cl,vol</sub> is activated via a different pathway. In the absence of an increase in [Ca<sup>2+</sup>]<sub>i</sub>, the unitary conductance, kinetics, and pharmacological profile are similar for I<sub>K,vol</sub> and the K<sup>+</sup>-channels activated by LTD<sub>4</sub>.Tyrosine phosphorylations are involved in the volume regulatory pathways and in defining the volume set-point. Tyrosin kinases appear to be involved in the signalling sequence leading to opening of the channels, and tyrosin phosphatases seem to be involved in closing of the channels. Finally a significant de-polymerization of F-actin is observed after cell swelling, the potential role of which in the volume regulatory mechanisms is under investigation.
