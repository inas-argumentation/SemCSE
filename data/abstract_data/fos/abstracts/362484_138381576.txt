Function of Rare Earth La in U71Mn Heavy Rail Steel
The function of rare earth(Re)-La in U71Mn heavy rail steel was investigated by using optical microscopy,scanning electron microscopy(SEM),transmission electron microscope(TEM),and full-automatic transformation test instrument(Formastor-Digital).The research results show that Re-La in U71Mn steel mainly exists in the form of Re inclusion and Re solid solution,along with the increase of adding amount of Re,Re inclusion and Re solid solution volume increase,and Re solid solution increases more notable.With the increase of Re content,the phase transformation points of the U71Mn steel have different degree on the decline,and the grains are gradually refined.
