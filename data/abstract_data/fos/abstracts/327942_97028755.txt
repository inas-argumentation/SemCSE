THE SMSI EFFECT ON THE ACTIVITY OF CO HYDROGENATION OVER Nb2O5-SUPPORTED Rh CATALYSTS
In contrast to the case of the TiO2-supported systems, the activities of CO hydrogenation on Rh/Nb2O5 catalysts in the SMSI state were two orders of magnitude lower than in the normal state. The product selectivity appeared to be similar between the normal and SMSI states, but to depend on the metal particle size.
