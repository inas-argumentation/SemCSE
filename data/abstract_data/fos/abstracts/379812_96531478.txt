Highly Thermally-Stable Diels-Alder Adducts of [60]Fullerene with 2-Cycloalkenones and Their Acetals
A [4+2] monoadduct with improved thermal stability was obtained from the reaction of [60]fullerene with 2,5-dioxaspiro[4.4]non-6-ene, a masked 4π-component, under high-pressure conditions (300 MPa) at 130 °C in toluene in 85% yield. Hydrolysis of the adduct gave the corresponding ketone, which was also obtained by the reaction of [60]fullerene with cyclopentenone. Even at 150 °C for 15 h, no cycloreversion of the adducts was observed.
