Contractional uplift of deep crustal rocks along the Legs Lake shear zone, western Churchill Province, Canadian Shield
The Legs Lake shear zone juxtaposes high-pressure (1.0+ GPa) granulite- and eclogite-facies rocks with low-pressure (~0.5 GPa) amphibolite- to granulite-facies rocks, and thus may represent an important crustal-scale, exhumation-related structure in the western Canadian Shield. Field mapping and structural and petrologic analysis document the deformation and metamorphic history of rocks within and adjacent to the shear zone. At least two important phases of deformation are recorded: (1) early oblique thrusting (D2) that placed high-grade rocks over lower grade rocks, and (2) more discrete and lower grade brittleductile normal faulting (D3) that may represent the later part of the exhumation history. The northwest-dipping shear zone consists of 58 km of mylonite in map view, which bounds the southeast margin of the East Athabasca mylonite triangle.
