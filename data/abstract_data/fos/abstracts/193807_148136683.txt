Cai Yuanpei and the institutionalization of Chinese modern university
Cai Yuanpei instituted University Regulations in 1912 and established the idea basis for Chinese modern university.Cai's ideal of founding Chinese modern universities became reality only after his reforming Peking University five years later.Cai was keen on reform in the early days of Peking University,but on the other hand he propagated and illuminated his ideas duly as an institutional entrepreneur.The institutionalization of Cai's ideas revealed the relative effectiveness between his idea and practice.
