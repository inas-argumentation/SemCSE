Studies on the Storability of Different Cassava Varieties
Used 17 varieties of cassava seed stem which stored in a cool and dry environment as materials.Weigh the seed stems regularly and measured their water content.Then planted the seed stems with different storage-time and water content.Investigated the survival rate and seedling growth indicators of different varieties after 60 days.The results showed that different varieties of cassava had different water content after storage,GR911 had the highest water content 68.5%,and the lowest were OMR36-40-9 36.5%and Guire 4 36.5%.Different cassava varieties had different rate of water loss in stage of storage.Water content of seed stem had significant correlation with seedling survival rate.The water content had significant correlation with the average number of germination,but had significant negative correlation with the length and number of bud.Guire 4,KU50,Shuwang 983,SC 8 and SC 9 had better storability than other varieties.
