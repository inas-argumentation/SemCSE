A Brief Analysis on Yuan Zhen's Making of Mandates——On the Relationship between Yuan Zhen and Bai Ju-yi and Ancient Prose Movement in the Mid-Tang Dynasty
Poems of Yuan Zhen and Bai Ju-yi have always been the focus of attention of scholars.However,their essays have been neglected again and again.In fact,both Yuan Zhen and Bai Ju-yi participated actively in the reform of the old malpractices of essays since the Qi and Liang Dynasties.Even in the making of mandates,they also broke through the long established pattern of rhythmical prose characterized by parallelism and ornateness,thus embodying a valuable reform spirit in style.Their far-reaching influence made a great contribution to the Ancient Prose Revival Movement initiated by Han Yu and Liu Zong-yuan in the Tang Dynasty.
