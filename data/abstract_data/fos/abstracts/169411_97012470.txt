Liquid-like model for the ultrasonic attenuation in superionic conductors
With due regard to diverse experimental evidence, a new model for the ultrasonic attenuation in superionic conductors is presented. Our formulation is based on the theoretical results of Rice and Allnat for the dense fluids. We have applied the model to superionic conductor α-Ag2Te and calculated the coefficients of viscosity and of ultrasonic attenuation. According to our model the main mechanism which is effective to the ultrasonic attenuation is the hard core collision between the mobile ions and the surrounding lattice ions.
