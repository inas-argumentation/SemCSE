On the Evaluation Index System of Tourist Experience Economy ——Taking Gudong Scenic Area of Li River at Guilin as an Example
Traveling is the most typical experiencing activity in the time of experience economy,and the demand for experiencing also gradually becomes the mainstream demand in traveling.Based on the core of the customer experiencing value,and combining the different perspectives of economics,cultural anthropology and sociology,this article tentatively selects 27 factors to build a tourist experience economic evaluation index system,with which an empirical analysis is made about the Gudong Scenic Area of Li River at Guilin with an aim to guide the construction and development of travel experiencing economy.
