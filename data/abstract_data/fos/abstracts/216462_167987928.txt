Theoretical research on the causes of formation of non-official mass sports organizations——Also on the main issues in follow-up demonstration research
The formation of non-official mass sports organizations is the process of self-organization of dispersed sports population developed form a disordered condition to an ordered condition,the product of contradiction between ever increasing demand for mass sports and insufficient supply by the market or government,and the process and result of interaction between dispersed sports populations as well as between sports population and the environment.The author also pointed out the limitations in theoretical research and the main issues in follow-up demonstration research.
