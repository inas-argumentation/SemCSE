Constrained layer damping of tubular truss members
An efficient technique is developed to model tubular truss members with a variety of constrained layer damping treatments along its length. The truss member is divided into sections with distinct damping treatments. Each section consists of concentric layers of an outer tube, viscous layer, and inner tube. The equations of motion of a typical section are obtained by Hamilton's principle. An exact solution to the equations is obtained and used to develop the dynamic stiffness matrix for a typical section. A novel condensation and assembly scheme is used to develop a superelement for a truss member comprising multiple sections with the damping treatments. The superelement is verified by comparison of results with an analytical solution and with a conventional finite element model for internal and external constrained layer damping.
