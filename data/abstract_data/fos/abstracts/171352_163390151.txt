The Awakening of the Females' Sense of American Minorities:——Analysis of Similarities between the Black Woman in The Color Purple and The Chinese-American Woman in The Kitchen God's Wife
After the mid-20th century,the American black female writer Walker Alice's The Color Purple and the Chinese-American female writer Tan Amy's The Kitchen God'Wife were two great novels.The two novels are about minority females' journey to personal freedom in the American white cultural background.There are some similarities between these two novels in embodying their themes,such as the similar background of oppression,the similar condition of being insensitive,the similar souce of revolting spirit and the similar road to freedom.However,inspite of similarities in their themes,these two novels also concern something that represent their own national spirit only.
