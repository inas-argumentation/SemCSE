2003 Report of the Board of Directors to the AACP House of Delegates
This report provides an update to the AACP House of Delegates on progress made in addressing the nine goals articulated in the House-approved AACP Strategic Plan (approved July 2000). It covers the period from July 1, 2002 to June 30, 2003. This has been a remarkable year of accomplishment for the Association.
