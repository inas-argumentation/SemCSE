Combat Capability Evaluation of Weapon System-of-Systems Based on Gray Hierarchy Method
For the purpose of evaluating the capability of weapon system-of-systems,analyzes the structure of weapon system-of-systems,and builds the guide line of capability evaluation of weapon system-of-systems.Aimed at the complex of weapon system-of-systems,presents a new evaluation model which combines grey theory with analytic hierarchy process for capability evaluation,calculates an example of the assessment model and the result shows that the model is reasonable,feasible and effective.
