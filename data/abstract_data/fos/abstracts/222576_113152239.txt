Die Casting Die with Inner Sliding Block Structure for Castings with Thread
Opening dies and ejecting casting from the die are the difficult points for the design of die-casting die for castings with thread.The casting described in this paper has three internal threads.Design of inner sliding blocks is made to open the die.While ejecting the casting by ejector pin,inner sliding blocks move in along the guide keyway on the core in the moving die and leave out the thread.The working principle is discussed;the die-casting machine is selected based on the swelling force calculation and the technical parameters are given.The die is very reliable,production efficiency is high,and the casting meets the quality requirement.
