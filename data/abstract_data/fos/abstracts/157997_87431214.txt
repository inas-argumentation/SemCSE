Analysis of SSR Information and Annotation in EST Resource of Caster Bean (Ricinus communis)
62 592 ESTs of Caster bean in the database of NCBI were downloaded and analyzed.After the preprocession,we got 11 708 non-redundant ESTs with total length about 921 kb.Total 2 471 SSRs distributed in 3 271 ESTs were detected,accounting for 21.10% of the non-redundant ESTs.The average length and distribution distance of the EST-SSRs were about 2.81 kb.Dinucleotide and trinucleotide are the main repeat types with similar frequency,accounting for 25.61% and 34.63% of all the SSRs.A/T(36.32%) and AG/CT(18.28%) are the most frequent motifs,575(23.3%),1 166(47.2%) and 1 817(73.5%) matched with genes with known function within COG,SwissProt and KEGG.These EST-SSRs will help to develop SSR markers with high polymorphism for peanut.
