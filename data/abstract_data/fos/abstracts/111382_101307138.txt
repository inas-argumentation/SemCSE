Study on Control of iil Uptake for Fried Foods
The technological parameters and processes of fried potato were comprehensively studied in the present paper and optimum technological conditions were found through the L9(34) orthogonal experiments:fried time 4 min， thickness 6mm， content of water 75％ ,10％ NaCl pretreatment.On the basis of studying fried principle and the main affect factors,new methods of control of oil uptake for the fried foods were advanced.
