Research and Implementation of Accelerating Arithmetic for Phase-field Microstructure Simulation
Having a great of the computing in phase-field microstructure simulation, an accelerating arithmetic based on the dynamic computing regions was designed in the simulation, which could reduce the computing time and the dependence of the simulation results on the hardware greatly, and the accelerating effect was evident. The dendritic growth of binary alloy is successfully implemented using the arithmetic. The computing results indicate that the precision of the arithmetic is reasonable, and its computing efficiency is high.
