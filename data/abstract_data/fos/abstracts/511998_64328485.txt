Simulation and Analysis of Backward Congestion in Reno and Vegas
Network congestion has become a bottleneck of network development and application. The congestion control algorithms that have been implemented in TCP are mainly Reno and Vegas. These two algorithms are both based on end-to-end congestion control scheme. They have heavily reduced traffic congestion in forward path, but cannot improve network performance such as throughput and packet loss ratio when traffic congestion happens in backward path. Based on the premise that packet loss is caused by network congestion, this paper uses OPNET modeler to simulate Reno and Vegas when congestion happens in the backward path. In the model, a single bottleneck network is built. The parameters such as throughput, packet loss ratio and congestion window (cwnd) are used to compare the performance of the two algorithms. After the simulation, we can conclude that in an unsymmetrical network, Reno is much more eligible and practical. When congestion happens in backward path, the throughput of Vegas will be greatly reduced, which makes Vegas not well compatible with Reno.
