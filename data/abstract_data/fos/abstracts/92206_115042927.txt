Management on the Rail of Electrolysis Travelling Crane in Jinlong Copper Company
Aimed at easy-loosing of rail back plate of electrolysis travelling crane, breakage of grouting course on the beam of railway, bad contact of rail welding coupling, and great vibration at the contact of coupling, Jinlong Copper Co., Ltd. made an reasonable analysis, and determined a reasonable management measure and carried into execution to greatly improve service life of railway of travelling crane.
