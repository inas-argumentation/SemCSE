Creating the Entrepreneurial University:The Formation Mechanism,Value Reconstruction and Path Choice
The main formation mechanism of entrepreneurial universities includes the interaction among universities,politics and market,changes of function of university,and power balance in universities.The values of entrepreneurial universities reflect the compatibility of the traditional academic value core,the idea of community responsibility and performance concept based on market value.Entrepreneurial universities could be reached by such four ways as the organic unity of knowledge innovation,technology transfer and organization transformation,the combination of university autonomy,academic freedom and social responsibility,the interaction of system environment and effective mechanism,and the strategy selection based on their transition.
