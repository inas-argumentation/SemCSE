On the Human Resource Construction and Management of the University Library
Through investigation and analysis of the university library human resource, this paper put forward the strategy of human resource construction and management of the university library in the our country knowledge economy ages. The library manager's high occupation sense of responsibility should be built. A library environment of study is advantageous to the manager's business ability and technique level. The library manager's service consciousness should be strengthened. The personalities of teachers and students should be respected. The manager of the library should go deep in the customer, promoting the blend between customer and manager.The personnel's structure should be adjusted.Enthusiasm of all levels of manager should be promoted. The assessing system should be established strictly.The encouraging mechanism should be practised, and the average allotment should be broken.
