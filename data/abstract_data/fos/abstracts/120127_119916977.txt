Nozzle characteristics in the transition regime between continuum and free molecular flow
Experimental measurements were made of the flow characteristics of a convergent-divergent nozzle, two convergent nozzles, and an orifice Data were obtained on the variations of the mass flow rate of argon with pressure ratio across the test specimens and the vaiiations of discharge coefficient with Reynolds number and Knudsen number The results are partially explained, and, in view of the scarcity of experimental data in this transition regime between continuum and free molecular flow, they should be of value to fluid dynamicists who are attempting to design in this flow regime and to those individuals who are attempting to theoretically analyze this flow regime
