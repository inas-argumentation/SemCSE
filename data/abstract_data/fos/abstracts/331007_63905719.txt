The Requirements for the Classification and Catalogue of Library at the Age of Network
The 21st centry is the age of network. Under the new condition libraries have new functions and new features: a large mount of information resources; the digitalization of information and the socialization of readers. The new age makes new requirements for the classification and catalogue of library work -- expanding the job of classification and catalogue, sharing resources, standardizing classification and catalogue, diversifying the productions of classification and catalogue.
