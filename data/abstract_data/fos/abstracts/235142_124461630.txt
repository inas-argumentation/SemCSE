Analysis of Calculus Rules on Fuzzy Temporal Relational Algebra
In order to resolve the temporal database more in line with the actual description of the abstract of the objective world,and that can effectively express and deal with complex object.By defining of a series of fuzzy temporal relation elements,we demonstrate the syntax and semantics of the fuzzy temporal relational algebra,and establish a fuzzy temporal relation algebraic theory of system info base on basic fuzzy temporal data model.Which avoid deficiencies in the temporal database,provide facilities for modeling of fuzzy temporal aspect of datum,and also strengthen the existing temporal database the ability to describe the temporal characteristic of fuzzy events.
