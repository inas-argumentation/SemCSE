On the flexural vibrations of circular and elliptical plates
In this paper we express R. D. Mindlin's version of plate flexure equations, which take transverse shear and rotary inertia into account, in general orthogonal curvilinear coordinates and then we specialize these to polar and elliptical coordinates in order to find the frequency equations for the normal modes of vibration of the circular and elliptical plates respectively. In particular, we wish to discover those of the eight natural boundary conditions for which the normal modes of vibration are expressible in terms of product functions.
