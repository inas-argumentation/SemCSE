Optimization of technology for alisma oriental(sam)juzep. resistant starch making
Alisma oriental(Sam)Juzep.contains abundant starch in the rhizoma,but they have always been ignored. In order to utilize the medicinal plant better,the research work about technology for Alisma oriental(Sam)Juzep. resistant starch making was consisted in this thesis.Through three-factor second order rotation combination experiment,the effects of the parameters which include starch concentration,autoclaving temperature and time to Alisma oriental(Sam)Juzep.Resistant Starch(RS)making were investigated,and the regression model with high reliability was obtained between the yield of RS and the factors.Results show that the three factors all have significant influence on the yield of RS.The influencing sequence from significant to common is autoclaving temperature,starch concentration and autoclaving time.The optimized processing parameters are 120℃of autoclaving temperature,30%of starch concentration and 45 min of autoclaving time。
