Effect of Relief Annealing on Metal Rubber Loss Factor
The effect of relief annealing on the static and dynamic loss factors of 304 metal rubber was discussed.The results show that the static loss factors are higher than dynamic loss factors by 3.8% and 6.4% before and after relief annealing,respectively.The static and dynamic loss factors of 304 metal rubber can be significantly changed by relief annealing.After relief annealing,the static and dynamic loss factors of 304 metal rubber can be increased by 10.0% and 7.1%,respectively.
