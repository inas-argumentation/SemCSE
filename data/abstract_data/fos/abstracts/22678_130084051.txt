Membrane bioreactor for domestic wastewater treatment and selection of membrane materials
A study on membrane bioreactor for domestic wastewater treatment and selection of membrane materials was introduced.The results showed that the membrane bioreactor has a high removal efficiency for COD cr (87%~98%)、BOD 5(88%~99%)、NH 3 N(89%~98%)、SS(100%)、E coli(100%)、turbidity(98%~100%).The treated water could be used again.The polypropylene membrane had far more water product and the resistance to contamination than the polysulfone membrane.
