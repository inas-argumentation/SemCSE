Weather Radar Signal Processor Based on TS101 Chip
Signal processor is an important part of weather radar,which implements all kinds of algorithms to obtain weather information,so it is important to develop a new generation weather radar signal processor.The signal processor adopts TigerSHARC series chip named TS101 which is produced by AD company.The article discusses particularly the hardware design of the signal processor which is based on the TS101 chip,and the software flow charts of signal processor algorithm of the weather radar.The weather radar signal processor shows its powerful computing ability,it can implement a complex algorithm to resolve range ambiguity and has a good processing effect.
