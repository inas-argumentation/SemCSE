Quantitative Study of the Correlation between Basic Research Input and Output of University in China:1991-2008
Research output is the ultimate indicator of basic research input.Based on the data of university basic research input and output from 1991 to 2008,this article illustrates current characteristics of Chinese universities basic research input and output,and simulates the original data type by using Matlab and the recursive model for various indexes of basic research input and output of universities in China.The result shows that there are stronger correlation between basic research input and output in universities but lesser correlation between growth rate of basic research input and its output in universities.Finally,base on the result of analysis,the article makes suggestions for basic research input and output of universities in China.
