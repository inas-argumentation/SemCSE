A Comparative Analysis of Two Synthetic Media for Suspended Particle-Attached Growth Anaerobic Fermentation
A comparative study was conducted to evaluate the suitability of two synthetic media for use in suspended particle-attached growth (SPAG) fermentation. The study evaluated the performance of 100 micron polypropylene felt and a reticulated nylon material in replicated 25 L fermenters. Media fill ratios (FR) of 7.5% and 15% were tested at hydraulic retention times (HRT) of 5, 3, 2 and 1 days. A 25 L, 0% FR SPAG reactor served as the control for this experiment. Screened flushed swine waste liquids were used as a methane substrate with loading concentration ranging from approximately 11 to 22 g VS/L during the 12-month study. Results from the study indicate that both media are suitable for use in SPAG fermenters, but that a combination of the two would be most desirable.
