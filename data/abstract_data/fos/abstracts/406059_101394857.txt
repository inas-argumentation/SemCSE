Preparation and Analysis of Non-covalent Complexes of Cyclodextrins with Piperazine Derivative Compound SIPI5357
The non-covalent complexes of α-cyclodextrin and β-cyclodextrin(α-CD,β-CD)with an antidepressant compound-aralkyl hydroxyl piperazine derivative,1-(1-chloro-2-methoxynaphthalene-6yl)-2-(4-(1-phenylethyl)piperazin-1-yl)pentan-1-ol dihydrochloride dihydrate(SIPI5357),have been investigated.The complexes of α-CD,and β-CD with SIPI5357 have been prepared and studied by electrospray ionization mass spectrometry(ESI-MS)and ultraviolet(UV)spectrometry.The mass spectrometry results revealed that SIPI5357(guest)and α-CD,and β-CD(host)formed 1∶1 complexes in water solution.Furthermore,the α-CD/β-CD-SIPI5357 complexes exhibited different features and properties in UV spectroscopy from the reactants,which are coincidence with the results of ESI-MS.According to the solubility measurement,the formation of the non-covalent complexes could prominently increase the solubility of SIPI5357 in aqueous solution that has great significance in the study of the antidepressant drugs.The formation constant of the complexes have also been estimated by phase solubility studies.The calculated apparent formation constants of SIPI5357-β-CD and SIPI5357-α-CD are 262 L/mol and 63.4 L/mol,respectively.
