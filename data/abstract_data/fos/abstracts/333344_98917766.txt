Experimental research on water heating scallop shelling
Scallop shelling has always been one of the difficulties on scallop processing. In this paper, the research is on the bay scallop shelling using the water heating method. Using the PID regulator to control the water temperature, and explore the bay scallop shell temperature's variation with the heating time and the water temperature. Exploring the biological characteristics of the bay scallop, and establishing its relevance mathematical model. The above studies provide a theoretical basis for set up the bay scallop shelling production line.
