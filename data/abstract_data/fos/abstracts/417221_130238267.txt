Mercury species in sediments polluted by different industries.
Sequential chemical extraction was used to study mercury species in sediments.Results indicated that mercury species were similar in sediments polluted by chemical industries,with mercury strongly binding with organic matter as primary and followed by species dissolved in alkali.Mercury in residual state was dominating in Wanshan mining area while species dissolved in alkali was dominating in sediments polluted by zinc smeltery,which indicated that mercury species varies largely with pollution source.
