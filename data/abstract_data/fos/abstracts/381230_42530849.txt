A comparative trial of desipramine and nortriptyline in depression.
A double-blind comparative trial of desipramine and nortriptyline in women suffering from depression showed that after 21 days, 79 per cent. of patients on desipramine and 70 per cent. of patients on nortriptyline had shown definite improvement. Nortriptyline was superior in reducing insomnia, but desipramine was superior in reducing anxiety and allowing increased work and activity. Side effects presented little problem. No significant overall advantage was shown for either drug.
