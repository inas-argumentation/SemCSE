Adaptive Channel Estimation for OFDM Systems Based on Delay Spread Detection
In this paper,an adaptive channel estimation which based on delay spread detection algorithm was proposed in OFDM system.We utilize the frequency-domain correlation of pilot to estimate the channel condition.Then we can real time track the channel parameters and adaptive adjust the number of pilot and Cyclic Prefix(CP) length in transmitter.Simulation shows that this algorithm can obtain the approximately statistical information of channel model's delay parameters,and it can optimize the redundant characters of systems.
