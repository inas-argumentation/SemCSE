Pain Sensitivity, Age and Activity Level in Chronic Schizophrenics and in Normals
The relationship between pain sensitivity and ward activity was tested using 18 chronic hospitalized schizophrenic patients. No significant relationship (linear or otherwise) was found. There was no significant relationship between age and pain sensitivity in the schizophrenic group, but a significant negative correlation between age and pain sensitivity was observed in a group of 56 normal Ss. The schizophrenic Ss did not differ significantly from the normals regarding pain threshold and pain tolerance.
