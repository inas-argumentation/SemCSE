MkNNI:New Missing Value Imputation Method Using Mutual Nearest Neighbor
As there may be noise in choosing the k nearest neighbors of missing data using the k-nearest neighbor imputation(kNNI) algorithm,puts forward a new imputation method,Mutual k-Nearest Neighbor Imputation(MkNNI),for missing values.Data for imputing missing value are the k nearest neighbors of the missing data and at the same time,their k nearest neighbors include the missing data.Thus,noise is effectively prevented in selecting nearest neighbors.We experimentally evaluate our approach and demonstrate that MkNNI is much better than the kNNI method in imputed accuracy generally.
