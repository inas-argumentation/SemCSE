Ein 1,5-Diazabicyclo[3.3.0]octa-2,7-dien aus Acetylendicarbonsäure-dimethylester und einem 3,5-Dihydro-4H-1,2,3-triazol-4-on
A 1,5-Diazabicyclo[3.3.0]octa-2,7-diene is Formed from Dimethyl Acetylenedicarboxylate and a 3,5-Dihydro-4H-1,2,3-triazol-4-one 
 
 
 
The 3,5,5-trialkyl-3,5-dihydro-4H-1,2,3-triazol-4-one 1 and dimethyl acetylenedicarboxylate (2) react slowly at 50°C in a 1 : 1 ratio to afford a 40% yield of a deep red crystalline product C18H24N2O8. 1H-, 13C- and 15N-NMR spectra and an X-ray diffraction analysis prove the structure 6 for the red product.
