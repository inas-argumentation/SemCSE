Simulation of Electrostatic Field of Electrical Upsetting Based on Numerical Manifold Method
Numerical manifold method was applied to analyze the electrostatic field.Numerical manifold formula of electrostatic field was derived.The boundary condition of the electrostatic field was disposed.Numerical manifold format of an electrostatic field simulation analysis was established.A program was scheduled to simulate the electrostatic field of the multi-physical fields of upsetting process.Comparing with the FEM method,the results show that numerical manifold method is rational and valid.This can provided a new calculating method for analyzing the electrostatic field of multi-physical fields.
