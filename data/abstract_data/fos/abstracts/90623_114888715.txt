The Principles of Magnetic Valve Controllable Reactor and its Simulation Research
Introduced the basic structure and working principle of the magnetic valve controllable reactor.The primary windings of the two identical transformers were connected sequentially and the secondary windings were connected reversely,then derived an equivalent circuit of the controllable reactor.And according to the varied work states,the corresponding electromagnetic equations were listed.At last through using the Matlab/Simulink constructed the simulation model of the controllable reactor and simulating received good simulation result.
