Kinetics study on the reaction of ammonium bifluoride and silicon
The research status of white carbon black and drained slurry is summarized.Two reaction-two cycle new fluorination process route of preparing white carbon black using silicon as material is introduced.Gas volume is determined by traditional drainage and transformed into concentration.Reaction kinetics of ammonium bifluoride and silicon,which contains drained slurry generated by cutting silicon crystal using a wire-saw,is studied.The results show that the first stage reaction of producing white carbon black production from drained slurry is a second order reaction[-r=k(CNH4HF)2],k50=3.47×10-4mol3/(L·s)and k60=5.62×10-4mol3/(L·s) at 50 ℃ and 60 ℃,the activation energies is 43.16 kJ/mol and the pre-exponential factors A is 3 288.85.
