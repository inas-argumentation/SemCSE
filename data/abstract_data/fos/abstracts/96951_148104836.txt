On the government's role in the co-education of industry university and research
Based on the specific culture of the system,the government should develop policies and regulations which adjust the interests of enterprises and stimulate the enthusiasm of enterprises,construct the platform of "the cooperation of industry,university and research ",and reform the system of cadre evaluation.In order to enhance the performance of policies and regulations on "the cooperation of industry,university and research ",the government should also strengthen enforcement of the policies and regulations,consummate the appraisal system of the policy,and improve the supervision mechanism.
