From status conferment to contract management——On labor institutional change for public HEls'teachers from a law perspective
Along with the reform of teacher employment system,the labor relation be- tween teachers and HEIs(Higher Education Institutes)has become more and more market-o- riented.Employment system of the teachers is changing from"allocated according to the plan"into"employing from contracts";the system of teachers'salary transforms from"fixed salary"to"flexible salary";the social security and welfare system has changed from"state protection"to"public operation".The labor relation between teachers and HEIs has changed:from administrative relation managed by identification into fair labor relation estab- lished by contracts.There is great disparity between market-oriented labor relation and the reality for the inadequate support from law,which severely affects the implementation of teacher'employment.
