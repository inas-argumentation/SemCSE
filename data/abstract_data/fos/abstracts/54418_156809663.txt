The Status Quo of Chinese Leisure Agriculture and the Influencing Factors for Its Development
Leisure agriculture is a new form of agricultural production which has great economic value and social benefits.The study shows that the development of Chinese leisure agriculture, with great development potential,has already taken shape;the distribution of the business entities across the nation is rather discrete,with those in East China aggregating while those in the Northwest dispersing;the development of leisure agriculture and socio-economic development has significant correlation,with regional GDP and average wage of staff having negative correlation with the number of practitioners in leisure agriculture but having positive relationship with the annual revenues of leisure agriculture;and the number of business entities and annual revenues of leisure agriculture has a positive relationship with the number of urban residents while having a negative relationship with the mileage of highway.It is proposed to increase the level of economic development,focusing on increasing income of urban residents,and continue to improve the leisure agriculture-related infrastructure.
