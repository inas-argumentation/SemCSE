Programming Research of High Speed Milling for the Die of Engine Lid
Viewing from optimizing the high speed machining technology,the strategy of high speed machining for mold with the Cimatron software is illustrated,which includes the choice of machining method,the cut-over and cut-off of cutter and the control of tool route manner etc..As an example of machining a female die cavity of engine lid,the machining strategy is made and cutting parameters is given to finish the program of high speed machining.The practices show that programme using the software can meet the requirements of high speed milling and overcome the shortcoming of the traditional cutting ways,thus shorten the cycle of design and production and increase the quality and production efficiency of products.
