Reconstruction and Operation of Treatment Technology of Food Processing Wastewater
Due to the increase in product variety and production scale expansion,wastewater treatment facilities of some food processing enterprise can not meet recent production,and affect the effectiveness of the sewage treatment operation.Therefore,according to the characteristics of wastewater quality and quantity,the hydrolysis acidification and contact oxidation as the main process added to existing wastewater treatment projects through reform and reconstruction.After engineering test and operation for half a year,the project has passed environmental completion acceptance.
