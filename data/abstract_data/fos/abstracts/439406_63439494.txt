Implementation of the Effects of Rainy Window with the Driving Simulation
Driving simulation,the analog of the natural environment can increase the user's sense of substitution,and improve the user's real experience.Usually driving simulation system is easy to ignore the effects of rain and snow on the windshield,the only traffic area of rain and snow environment simulation.Instead window rain and snow effects have a direct impact on the user driving experience,but not too much attention.Studies the law of motion of raindrops on the windows and simulation results.Because a large number of raindrops on the windshield of a vehicle,the complexity of the course of the campaign,which makes real-time simulation of raindrops become very difficult.In this paper,the problem of how to construct a simulation model as well as to reduce the computational complexity expand.We use a GPU Advanced shader Language,GLSL,using the Geometry shader optimization model rendering,instead of the traditional Billboard algorithm,so that the particles parallel computing,greatly improve the efficiency of computing and rendering.Authenticity using normal maps to simulate raindrops refracted reflection effect with high fidelity.
