Extraction of buckwheat protein by alkali solution acid precipitation and its amino acid analysis
The extraction conditions of buckwheat protein (BWP) by isoelectric precipitation method were systematically investigated. And the profile of amino acids of buckwheat protein was analyzed with autoamino acid analyzer. The result was showed that the pI of BWP was about 3.8. The optimum parameters were as follow: pH9.0, 50 ℃, 30 min, the ratio of solid to liquid 1∶15. The extraction yields of protein can be attained 72.25%. Also, the total amount of the BWP was 61.36 g/100 g. Threonine was the first limited amino acid.
