N-ALKYLATION OF TRIMETHYLSILYL DERIVATIVES OF ISATIN WITH HALOMETHYL KETONES
Trimethylsilyl derivative of isatin react with halomethyl ketones at the nitrogen atom in contrast to sodium salt of isatin, which reacts with these compounds at heterocyclic carbon atom. Author: M.
