On Several Issues about Enterprises' Internal Accounting Control
Along with the economic and social development and the scientific and technological progress,there has arisen a new objective requirement for the level of enterprises' internal accounting control.What is most important right now is to center on people,establish and perfect employees' credit insurance systems,practice systems of vacation and job rotation based on strengthening employee training and enhancing employees' qualities comprehensively,so as to implement enterprises' internal accounting control effectively.
