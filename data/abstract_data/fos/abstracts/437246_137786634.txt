Influences of Heat Treatment on Structure and Property of Medium-chromium Cast Iron
Influences of aging treatment,quenching temperature and tempering temperature on structure and property of medium-chromium cast iron were investigated.The results indicate that aging treatment can decrease or eliminate internal stress of specimen,which results in hardness falling and impact toughness rising.As adopting quenching temperature of 950℃ and tempering temperature between 350 and 400℃,medium-chromium cast iron acquires better structure and synthetic mechanical properties.
