Physical and mechanical properties of Catalpa bungei(Wanqing number 1)
In this study,Catalpa bungei(Wanqing number 1),planted in Qingyang and cultured by Anhui Academy of Forestry,Qingyang Forestry Bureau and Forestry and Landscape Architecture School of Anhui Agricultural University,was selected as materials to determine the value of air-dried density,oven-dried density and basic density;radial shrinkage ratio of oven-dried condition,radial shrinkage ratio of air-dried condition;tangential shrinkage ratio of oven-dried condition;tangential shrinkage ratio of air-dried condition;volume shrinkage ratio of oven-dried condition;volume shrinkage ratio of air-dried condition;the ratio of tangential shrinkage to radial shrinkage of air-dried condition;the ratio of tangential shrinkage to radial shrinkage of oven-dried condition;compressive strength parallel to grain,bending strength and modulus of elasticity in bending of Catalpa bungei.The results showed that:(1)The basic density was 0.444 g·cm-3,oven-dried density was 0.490 g·cm-3,air-dried density was 0.532 g·cm-3,the differentiate coefficients of them were small,the density was stable.(2)The radial shrinkage ratio of oven-dried condition was 4.0%,tangential shrinkage ratio of oven-dried condition was 5.7%,volume shrinkage ratio of oven-dried condition was 10.4%,the ratio of tangential shrinkage to radial shrinkage of oven-dried condition was 1.180,the capability of dryness was fine,it was difficult to distort and craze.(3)The compressive strength parallel to grain was,bending strength was 82.3 MPa and modulus of elasticity in bending was 10.03 GPa,the exact index was small than 5%,the results were credible,the properties was good,could be use for industrialization;the ratio of intensity and the ratio of modulus of elasticity were large,belonged to high quality grade,could use for bearing configuration material.(4)When compared with other familiar fast-growing trees,its density was larger,the shrinkage property was smaller,and the mechanical property was middle.
