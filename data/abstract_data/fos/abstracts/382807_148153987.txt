The Rational University,the Capital University and the Democratic University ——On the University Transformation from the Perspective of the Sociology of Knowledge
Advanced knowledge is the foundation of the university,and the outcome of the social construction.Hence,there are intrinsic relationships between the university,advanced knowledge and society,which is the thinking way of the sociology of knowledge.From the perspective of the sociology of knowledge,it puts forward that the society knowledge types were transformed from the metaphysics to scientific from the medieval times till now,and then it's going to be the cultural ones.Universal knowledge,liberal knowledge,capital knowledge and negotiatory knowledge are the forms of these knowledge types in the university.Along with the transformation of knowledge,the university was changed from the rational university to the academic capital university,and is to be the democratic university in the future.
