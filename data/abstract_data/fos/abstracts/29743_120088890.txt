Vapour pressure data for (2LiBr+ZnBr2)-H2O solutions
Abstract The rising costs of conventional sources of energy have resulted in a search for alternative, cheaper means of powering refrigeration systems. Solar energy, with its abundant supply in some parts of the world (especially within the tropics), is receiving some attention as an alternative means of powering refrigeration and air conditioning systems. Of all the conventional methods of refrigeration, the vapour absorption system has been found to be the most suitable for solar application. In this work, the vapour pressure for a novel working fluid consisting of 2 mol lithium bromide plus 1 mol zinc bromide as the absorbent and water as the refrigerant was measured. The result is presented on a Duhring chart and a method of calculating the solution enthalpy from the vapour pressure data and the specific heat of one concentration of the solution over the temperature range of interest is presented.
