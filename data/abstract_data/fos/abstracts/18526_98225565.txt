Direct Sol-Gel Replication of the Self-assembled Nanostructure Modified with H-bond Functionalities
The glucopyranoside-based amphiphile 1 formed nanofiber structures with 25–100 nm of diameters and several micrometer lengths in the presence of aminophenyl glucopyranoside 2, and their self-assembled nanostructures were successfully replicated into the silica tube by sol-gel polymerization of TEOS.
