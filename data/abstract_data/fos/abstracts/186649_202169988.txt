Comparative analysis of the changing trends in Jianghan lake group after "returning land to lake"
Jianghan Plain where Jianghan lake group locates in is an important production base of commodity,grain,cotton and oil,and is a typical ecological vulnerable area,with situation of severe flood and water logging.Jianghan lake group plays an important role in regulating and storing the flood for Jianghan Plain as well as Yangtze River.It is significant to analyze the evolution of lakes combined with the historical data.This paper does comparative analysis based on the previous studies on the lakes in this region and the remote sensing image data during 1995-2010.The results show that there are growing trends by number,area and water surface ratio of Ji-anghan lakes group after the implementation of the policy of returning land to lake in 1998.The sharply decreased area of lakes caused by over reclamation has been restrained,which fully reflects the positive impact of the im-plementation of land policy,while different counties have different characters.
