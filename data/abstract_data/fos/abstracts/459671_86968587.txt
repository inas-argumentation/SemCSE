Effects of NaCl Stress on the Biological Indices of Stylo Seedlings
The growth of stylo seedlings pot cultured in green house under different NaCl stresses was observed.The biological indices of the seedlings such as leaf harm level,the growth height,the biomasses above and under the ground,etc were determined,and the indices were analyzed and evaluated by using the interrelated analysis law of grey system.The wilting of leaves of stylo seedlings were found intensified,and the wilting rate of leaves of stylo seedlings increased with the salt concentration.The growth of stylo seedlings treated was partially inhibited or completely inhibited,and the height decreased significantly as compared with the control.A highly significant difference was found between the treatment and the control in biomasses above and under the ground of stylo seedlings.Twelvesstylo cultivars was found tolerant to salt for 12 in the order of Stylosanthes hamata cv.verano Stylosanthes guianensis cv.Reyan No.5Stylosanthes scabra cv.seca Stylosanthes guianensis GC1480Stylosanthes guianensis GC1463Stylosanthes guianensis cv.MineiroStylosanthes guianensis cv.Reyan No.7Stylosanthes guianensis GC1581Stylosanthes guianensis GC1517Stylosanthes guianensis cv.Reyan No.2.Stylosanthes guianensis cv.Reyan No.10 Stylosanthes guianensis cv.Reyan No.13
