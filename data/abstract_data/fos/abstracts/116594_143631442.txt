Poor, Nasty and Brutish: Guerrilla operations in America's first civil war
Guerrillas assumed a consequential role in the campaigns fought in the Carolinas during the American War of Independence. They attacked British detachments and outposts, held loyalists in check, and severed supply lines. They also collected intelligence for the regular forces. Though far better suited for insurgency than for participation in pitched battles, they nonetheless successfully fought in major actions, particularly at the Battles of Cowpens and Eutaw Springs.
