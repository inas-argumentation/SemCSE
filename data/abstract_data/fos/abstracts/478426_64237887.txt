Modified D_S Evidence Theory and its Application in Water Quality Assessment
Proposed a modified D_S evidence theory which can be used to combine efficiently highly conflict evidences.In developed method,a new weighted assignment algorithm is applied to classify the evidences in order to improve the combination results,at the same time,the Bayesian approximation method and a quick calculation method for combination of the modified evidences are introduced in order to speed up the process.The application of our proposed ways in water quality assessment under a framework with two-layer hierarchical structure shows that our new combination rule can combine highly conflicting evidences more quick and satisfactory.
