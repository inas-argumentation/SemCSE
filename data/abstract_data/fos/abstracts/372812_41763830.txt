"small" Accelerator, Radionuclide and Radiopharmaceutical Production
The scope of this discussion is limited to the proton/deuteron accelerators capable of producing the positron emitting isotopes carbon-11, nitrogen-13, oxygen-15, and fluorine-18. Attention is focussed on the production process from the selection of the target gas to the synthesis of the desired radiopharamaceutical. (GHT)
