The Ethics of Uterus Transplantation
Abstract Thousands of women experience uterine factor infertility (UFI). Some are born without a womb. Many of these women, whether suffering from congenital or acquired uterine infertility, retain a strong desire to be able to bear their own biological child. Here I will assess the ethics of two proposed models of human uterus transplantation (UT) to determine whether either approach is a morally superior alternative to gestational surrogacy.
