An amendment and extension of tide gauge data analysis for Churchill, Manitoba
An amended rate of land emergence at Churchill, Manitoba is given as 1.3 ft (39 cm) per century with 95% confidence limits of 1.68 ft and 0,85 ft (51.2 and 25.9 cm). It is based on a polynomial regression analysis of tide gauge data for 1940-1968 using the linear function as the most satisfactory. In a previous paper (Barnett 1966) tide gauge data for Churchill were reviewed critically and a linear regression analysis suggested emergence of the land at 2 ft (-61 cm) per century and uplift at 2.4 ft (-73 cm) per century after allowing for eustatic effects. Recently a machine-run check on the original manual calculation of regression has re vealed a fairly significant error in the slope of the line. Land emergence was only 1.3 ft (-41 cm) per century based on the same time period ( 1940-1964). The addition of the four years 1965-1968 brings the total time span to 29 y and the number of annual mean values to 27. The extended data were subjected to more rigorous statistical treatment to assess the validity of the new value. A polynomial regression program was used, which included an assessment of the probability that any relationship between tide level and time was due to chance. By analysis of variance the program also enabled a test of the suitability of the linear regression function: U, = 8.206 - .01264 t, where Ut = tide level in feet at time 't', as opposed to some higher order function. Furthermore, if the linear regression proved acceptable, a measure of its success in explaining the data was available. The hal step was to test whether the slope of the resulting regression line is different from zero in the statistical sense.
