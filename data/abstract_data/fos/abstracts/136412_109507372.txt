GRAIN MOISTURE MEASUI^MENT BY MICROWAVE HEATING
ABSTRACT A previously proposed method which indirectly determined grain moisture content (MC) by measuring its surface temperature after microwave heating was investigated. The effects of heating time, sample mass, and container size and shape on the temperature rise vs. moisture content relationship were studied for corn. A monotonic temperature rise vs. moisture content relationship existed at certain sample mass and container size combinations. Heating time affected the slope and intercept, but not the monotonicity. The methodology developed for corn was tested with soybeans and wheat.
