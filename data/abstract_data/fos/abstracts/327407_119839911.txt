Systematics of related high-spin isomers in 144Sm and other N = 82 nuclei

Abstract: An in-beam experiment with the reaction 122Sn(27Al,4np) at 127 MeV was performed at the Nordball multi-detector array in Roskilde. It provided evidence for a new high-spin isomer in 144Sm. This isomer with T1/2= (2.6 ± 0.5) ns at an excitation energy Ex= 9232 keV seems to belong to a family of isomers of similar configuration in neighbouring N= 82 nuclei.
