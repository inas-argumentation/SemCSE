Improvement on Foundry Process for Nodular Cast Iron Crankshaft with Heavy Section Used in Reciprocating Compressor
In order to resolve the defects such as graphite aberrance and shrinkage porosity in the nodular iron crankshaft with heavy section,the reasons for the formation of the defects were studied.The design of crankshaft foundry process was adjusted directionally.The control of melting,nodularization,modification technology,pouring technology,etc.were improved.The graphite aberrance and shrinkage porosity in the nodular iron crankshaft are removed,the qualified castings are gotten,and the differences of mechanical properties and metallographic structure between surface and central area are slight.
