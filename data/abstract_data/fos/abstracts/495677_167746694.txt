An Analysis of the Development Prospect of Agricultural Products Processing Industry in the New Period in Jiangsu
The agricultural products processing industry is the leading industry in the developed countries. It is also a sunrise industry in 21st century in china. In recent years, the value output of the agricultural products processing have raised continuously. But the whole level is not high. Not only has it the big gap with developed countries, but also fall behind the coastal provinces. In the new period, the development of agricultural producls processing industry in Jiangsu should coordinate the development of the urban area and the rural area,reinforce the macro-regulation of agricultural products processing industry, optimize the social environment, strengthen the innovation of the organizations, realize sound cycle of the industry chain, perfect the technique market, strengthen the technique innovation, and draw upon the strategy of group and brand.
