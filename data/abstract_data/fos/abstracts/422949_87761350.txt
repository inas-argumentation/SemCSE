Complete Genome Analysis of Coxsackievirus A16 SHZH00-1 Strain Isolated from the Mainland of China
Coxsackievirus A16(Cox.A16) is closely related to human enterovirus 71 and is often associated with outbreaks of hand-foot-and-mouth disease(HFMD).Cox.A16 infection is not always a benign infection, it can cause serious myocarditis and fatal shock.This study analyzed the complete nucleotide sequence of Cox.A16 SHZH00-1 strain isolated from Shenzhen 2000 in China. The results showed that the full genomic length of Cox.A16 SHZH00-1 is 7 410bp and the ORF is 6 579bp.The genome composition and construction are consistent with enterovirus. The nucleotide and amino acid sequences of Cox.A16 SHZH00-1, when compared with those of Cox.A16 Tainan-5079-98,G10, EV71 SHZH03 and BrCr strains, revealed that Cox.A16 SHZH00-1 is close to Tainan-5079-98,the nucleotide and amino acid identities are 97.5% and 98.8% respectively.Cox.A16 SHZH00-1 shares lower homology with G10,the nucleotide and amino acid identities are 79.1% and 94.5% respectively.Comparing Cox.A16 SHZH01 with EV71 SHZH03 and BrCr,the nucleotide identities are both less than 80%.
