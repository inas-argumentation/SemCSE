Design of Extrusion Die for BL-φ76-1 Aluminum Alloy Profile
A porthole compound die for a kind of given complex hollow aluminum alloy profile(BL-φ76-1)was designed.The structure and size of divergent hollows,divergent bridges,welding room,die core,bearing length,die hole,as well as clearance groove was designed according to the profile's characteristic.The results indicate that the design is reasonable,and can be used in production.
