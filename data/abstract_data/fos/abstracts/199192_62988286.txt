Checking Behavioral Subtyping Inheritance for Object-Z Specification via Generating Proof Obligation
Object-Z is a formal specification language with object-oriented extensions to Z,thus it has object-oriented characteristics. Behavioral subtyping inheritance is one kind of subtype inheritance.Subtype object holds the behaviors and properties of its superclass objects and the verified formal specification need not be verified again when behavioral subtype object substitutes for its superclass object.This paper defines behavioral subtyping inheritance for Object-Z.Especially,we present an approach to implementing behavioral subtyping inheritance and generating relevant proof obligations for Object-Z specification systemically.These proof obligations aim to give a justification whether the formal specification self is non-conflictive and whether the specification is developed according to the method of behavioral subtyping inheritance.Finally,we make use of the theorem prover Z/EVES to analyze and verify the proof obligations.
