What can we learn from Hong Kong government's appropriation strategies for higher education institutes?
Although higher education in Hong Kong is similar to that in mainland China in background and current systems,the former has tremendously improved the operational capability and competitiveness of its universities through the following strategies: it consciously adopts a disciplinary approach associated with student enrolment, personnel marketing strategy through wage reform of faculties and staff,and university autonomy strategy stimulated by associated funds.Drawing upon Hong Kong government's experience,we should expand our educational expenditure,make a rational plan of higher education development framework,cultivate an initiative sense of market competition for higher education institutes.In this way,we can improve the utilization efficiency of national higher education expenditure,creating a guiding appropriation behavior with a far vision.
