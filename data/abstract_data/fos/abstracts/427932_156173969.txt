An Analysis of and Countermeasures for the"North Paradox" Dilemma of China's Professionalized Sports
Unclear property rights and indistinction of operation body and management body have long been the main problems influencing the development of China's professionalized sports.The main reason lies in the government's adoption of both the duty of clarifying property rights of sports professionalization and the duty of controlling its development,falling into the North Paradox dilemma.An analysis of the paradox is provided,with an aim to promoting the healthy and sustainable development of China's professionalized sports.
