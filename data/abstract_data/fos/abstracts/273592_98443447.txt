EVIDENCE FOR THE ELECTRON TRANSFER FROM [Co(II)(en)2Cl]+ TO [Co(III)(en)2Cl2]+ IN METHANOL
The addition of [Co(II)(en)2Cl]+ accelerated the isomerization of cis-[Co(III)(en)2Cl2]+ to its trans-isomer in methanol. The reaction by use of [Co(II)(en)2Br]+ or [Co(II)(l-chxn)2Cl]+ instead of [Co(II)(en)2Cl]+ produced trans-[Co(III)(en)2BrCl]+ or trans-[Co(III)(l-chxn)2Cl2]+, respectively. These evidences demonstrated the inner-sphere electron transfer from Co(II) to Co(III) in the acceleration mechanism of the title isomerization reaction.
