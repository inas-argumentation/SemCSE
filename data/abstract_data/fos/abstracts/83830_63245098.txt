Research on Paralled of the Related Characteristic Set Method Based on Distributed Maple System
Characteristic set method is kernel of Wu's method,in order to improve the efficiency of Wu's method,analyses characteristic set method and polynomial factorization of Wu's method,uses coarse granularity parallel method,introduces a parallel method of characteristic set method and a parallel method of polynomial factorization based on distributed Maple,provides a method for studying of Wu's method parallel computing.
