The antimicrobial effect of airway epithelial cells transfected by elafin on Pseudomonas aeruginosa biofilm
Objective To explore whether airway epithelial cells(A549 cells)after transfected by elafin have antimicrobial effect on P. aeruginosa (Pa) biofilm. Methods To establish the Pa biofilm model in vitro, and proved by a rapid silver nitrate staining procedure and scanning electronic microscope (SEM). After cultivating the cells, the constructed pEGFP-N1-Elafin eukaryotic expression vector has transfected into A549 cells by Lipo-fectAMINETM 2000. Elafin transfected cells incubated by pig pancreas elastase (NE group) , the supernatant of Pa (Pa group) and Escherichia coli (E. coli group) respectively for 24 h. A549 cells transfected the vector without elafin gene as control group. After biofilm carriers put into each group and incubated for 8 h, we observed the structure of biofilm in each group by SEM, and measured the number of each group by MTT at 8 h. Results Both silver nitrate staining procedure and SEM demonstrated bacterial biofilm. The structure of BF in three groups was changed. The number of bacteria in both NE group [(3.238±0.316)×106 CFU/cm2] and Pa group [(3.317±0.247)×106 CFU/cm2] was decreased as compared to the control group [(5.946±0.453)×106 CFU/cm2]. The ratio of bacteria clearing in Pa group(44.2%) and NE group (45.6%)was higher than that of in E. coli group(13.6%) . Conclusion The airway epithelia transfected by elafin resist and clean Pa bioflim, meanwhile, the products of Pa are effective inducing factors on the expression of elafin.
