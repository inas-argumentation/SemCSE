Implementation of the Rolling Main Production Plan on ERP of Auto Parts Industry
Currently, ERP systems has been widely applied, ERP system, the main production plan decisions directly affect the operation of the entire enterprise. Based on the analysis of master production plan of ERP systems, proposes the rolling main production plan, describes in details in the ERP system, how to program to achieve the rolling main production plan. Applies these results, practices in an auto parts manufacturing sector, and achieves good results.
