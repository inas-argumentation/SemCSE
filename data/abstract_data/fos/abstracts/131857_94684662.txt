Reactivity of metallic glass fibres in simulated concrete pore solutions
Present work deals with electrochemical and microscopic studies on corrosion response of metal glass fibres (MGF) when incorporated to a concrete matrix. Tests have been carried out in simulated pore concrete solutions (alkaline with and without Cl - , SO 4 2- and neutral). Results have indicated the higher corrosion resistance than reinforcements under the same aggressive conditions. This better behaviour has been attributed to the amorphous microstructure of MGF and Cr content. Although certain attack has been detected in alkaline solutions and in presence of chlorides.
