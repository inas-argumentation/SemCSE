Study on the biological activity of anti-CD3/anti-CD20 diabody
Objective To study the biological activity of anti CD3/anti CD20 diabody. Methods The anti CD3/anti CD20 diabody was purified by affinity chromatography and analyzed by both Western blot and size exclusion chromatography. Its antigen binding activity was examined by FACS and rosetting assay; its biological activity was examined by 3H TdR incorporation and 51 Cr release test. Results The anti CD3/anti CD20 diabody bound to Jurkat cells (CD3 +) and Daudi cells(CD20 +) and block the sites where parent antibody bind to. Furthermore, it was found to have effective mitogenic activity and the effect on retargeting peripheral blood lymphocytes to lyse Daudi cells. Conclusion The biological activity of anti CD3/anti CD20 diabody is the same as its parent antibodies. The diabody proved to be a potent agent for retargeting peripheral blood lymphocytes to tumor cells expressing the CD20 antigen and then lyse them. [
