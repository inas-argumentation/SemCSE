Preparation and Photocatalytic Performance of Lanthanum-Bismuth Composite Oxide
Bi-La composite oxide was prepared by thermal decomposition of molecular precursor of BiLa(diethylene triaminepentaacetic)(NO3).3.5H2O at 500 ℃.Effect of calcination time on structure and photocharacter was studied by X-ray powder diffraction,scanning electron microscopy and UV-Vis diffuse reflectance spectroscopy.The photocatalytic performance of Bi-La composite oxide was discussed through the degradation of methyl orange wastewater under UV light irradiation.Results showed that sample calcined at 500 ℃ is a composite oxide compound of Bi0.775La0.225O1.5 with porous structure and photocatalytic activity of samples as-prepared was influenced by calcinations time.The samples calcined for 1h and 4h exhibit good photocatalytic activity.The degradation rates of methyl orange of 20 mg/L over prepared samples of 1 g/ L calcinated for 1h and 4h under UV-light irradiation for 4h were 95% and 91% respectively.
