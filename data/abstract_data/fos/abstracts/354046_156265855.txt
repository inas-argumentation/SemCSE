The Review of Local Department Budget Reform and Further Corresponding Suggestion
The reform of department budget is the basic request of fiscal management mode by the market economy mechanism,and the self-reform of public finance as well,inducing a change of budget management conception from stressing distribution to management.However,many difficulties are shown in the course of compiling department budget,which have disturbed the progress of further promoting the department budget regularly.Looking back on its last five-year reform by local governments,the text gives some suggestion to solve the difficulties mentioned,thus to perfect the department budget.
