A new family of probability distributions with applications to data analysis
Some compound normal distributions are obtained by ascribing to the normal mean a distribution closely related to the exponential distribution. The family of distribution functions found in this way can cope with skewed and long-tailed data. It has a location parameter which can be considered as a function of several covariates and a new set of parameters, making it possible to tackle regression problems. Maximum likelihood estimates of the parameters are obtained iteratively. The first four moments of the distribution, the maximum likelihood equations and three examples of application are given.
