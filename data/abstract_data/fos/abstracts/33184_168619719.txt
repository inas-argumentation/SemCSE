On the Relantionship between Economy Development and Modern Logistics Industry
Logistics industry is very important in the national economy,which has been the new increasing point of the economy and has been called the "accelerator" of the economy development.It makes positive efforts on the Chinese economy.This paper makes use of qualitative and quantitative analysis to research the relantionship between economy development and logistics industry of Taizhou city.Taking the GDP of Taizhou as the explained variable,the freight traffic and value added of the logistics industry as the explanation variable,with the mathod of econometrics,we recover that the correlation betweem economy development and logistics industry and put forward some advice for the future development of logistics industry.
