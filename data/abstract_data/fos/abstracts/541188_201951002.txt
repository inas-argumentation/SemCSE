Cloning and sequence analysis of {\sl S} gene of hantavirus strain isolated from {\sl Microtus fortis} in Zhejiang province
Objective The S gene of hantavirus strain ZT10 isolated from M.fortis in Zhejiang province was amplified by one step RT-PCR.Methods The product of 1.7 kb fragment was obtained as expected.The amplified fragment was then cloned into the pGEM-T vector and confirmed by PCR and sequencing.Results The result demonstrates that S segment of ZT10 is composed of 1753 nuclotides in length with a open reading frame encoding a protein of 429 amino acids.Comparison with that of SEO type viruses(SR-11,R22,Guo3,8610 strains) shows 87.9%-96.0% homology at the nucleotide level,96.9%-97.9% homology at the amino acid level respectively.Comparison with HTN type virus(76-118 strain) indicated that there was 72.0% homology at the nucleotide level,82.6% homology at the amino acid level.Comparison with other type hantavirus show lower homology at the nucleotide and amino acid level respectively.Conclusion The results of nucleotide and amino acid comparison indicated that ZT71 strain is SEO virus in molecular level.
