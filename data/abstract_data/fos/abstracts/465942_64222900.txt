Creating a Replicating Checkpoint Using a DHT-based P2P Networks
A replicating checkpoint mechanism for large scale computing resource sharing platform over the Internet was presented and constructed.With this technique any node in the computing platform can resume a failed task provided that the checkpoint file is available in the P2P infrastructure.Almost all interactions needed to replicate checkpoints are decentralized among the DHT(Distributed Hash Table),thus containing the load on the central supervisor.The DHT does not hold the checkpoints,but only pointers to the checkpoints.A standard operation in DHT returns an indirection pointer to the storage,instead of the storage itself.Worker nodes can take advantage of this P2P network to keep track,share,manage and reclaim the space of the checkpoint files.We used simulation to validate our system and show that remotely storing replicas of checkpoints can considerably reduce the turnaround times of the tasks.A good throughput of task and low overload were also obtained in this test.
