Introduction of Si–H Bond onto the Surface of Mesoporous Molecular Sieves and Functionalization by Hydrosilylation Reaction
The Si-H bond was introduced onto the surface of MCM-41 by reduction of the butoxylated MCM-41. The hexyl and phenylalkyl-functionalized MCM-41s were obtained by reaction of the Si-H introduced MCM-41 with 1-hexene or styrene.
