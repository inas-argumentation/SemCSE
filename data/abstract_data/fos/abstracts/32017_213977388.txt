Latest Progress on Advanced Bridgman Method-Grown K₂PtCl₆ Cubic Structure Scintillator Crystals
We are reporting on the latest growth and scintillating properties of a recently discovered intrinsic scintillating compound with the K<sub>2</sub>PtCl<sub>6</sub> simple cubic crystal structure: the Cs-based Cs<sub>2</sub>HfCl<sub>6</sub> (CHC) and Cs<sub>2</sub>HfCl<sub>4</sub>Br<sub>2</sub> (CHCB), both with the density of 3.9 g/cm<sup>3</sup>, as well as the Tl-based Tl<sub>2</sub>HfCl<sub>6</sub> (THC) and Tl<sub>2</sub>ZrCl<sub>6</sub> (TZC). This article presents a successfully developed process of growing crack-free, single crystals of 16 mm diameter to 1-in-diameter CHC by the Bridgman method. The energy resolution of 2.8% [full-width at half-maximum (FWHM)] at 662 keV has been obtained for small diameters and typical 3.5% (FWHM) at 662 keV has been obtained for 1 in <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\times 1$ </tex-math></inline-formula> in CHC. The light yield of 30 000 ph/MeV and the 3.8-<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\mu \text{s}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> primary decay time have been measured for CHC. CHC’s excellent linear response to gamma rays compared to those of NaI:Tl and BGO is also reported. The mixed-halides CHC-based compound CHCB performs with a shorter primary decay time of <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$1.8~\mu \text{s}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>. Introducing Tl, a heavier element than Cs, increases both Zeff and density of THC and TZC, and improves gamma-ray detection efficiency. Primary decay times are also reduced to about <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$1~\mu \text{s}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> for THC and <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$2~\mu \text{s}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> for TZC.
