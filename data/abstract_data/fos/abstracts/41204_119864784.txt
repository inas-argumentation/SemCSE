A Basic Program for Series Data Plotting on Triangular Diagram
Graphic triangular diagrams are used by geologists, chemists and others. They present the results of anlyses that can be expressed in terms of three end members (triangular coordinates) (Fig. 1) ; the sum of whose values will always equal 100, 10 or 1. The program for triangular diagram plotting provides us with a great convenience and accuracy. The results of series data plotting can be used to compare and contrast different mineral and rock compositions by using different symbols of the triangular diagram. The labels on the three end members can be entered from the keyboard making it possible to express any kinds of relationships. The graphic triangular diagram plotting is translated from Cartesian coordinates though the Cartesian coordinates are never seen during the whole procedures, therefore, points resulting from three data values have to be located in both the Cartesian and triangular coordinate systems.
