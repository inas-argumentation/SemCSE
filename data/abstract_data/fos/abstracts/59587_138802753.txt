Experimental studies on performance of cement-based straw fiber material
In order to alleviate China's energy crisis and relieve the pressure related to excessive abandoned-crop-straw on the environment,the performance of environment friendly cement-based straw fiber material were studied and discussed.The experiments show that the reinforced effect of straw fiber and the mechanical behavior of straw fiber reinforced cement-based material is improved by fiber modification and the cement matrix modifi-cation,which is effective in preventing degradation of straw fiber when exposed to alkaline medium.
