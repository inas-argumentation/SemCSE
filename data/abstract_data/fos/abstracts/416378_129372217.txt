Geochronologic Study of Granitic Rocks from the Okanogan Range, North-Central Washington
The results of rubidium-strontium, fission-track, and lead-alpha analyses are evaluated in terms of the regional and local geological framework of the Okanogan Range (in Washington and British Columbia). An Upper Cretaceous (81± m.y.) granodioritic-quartz monzomtic intrusive episode and a Middle Cretaceous (104± m.y.) period of isotopic homogemzation of a trondhjemitic batholith are defined. A period of Jurassic regional metamorphism and late-metamorphic quartz dioritic-granodioritic emplacement, although not established, is suggested; the minimum age of this orogenic phase is 129± m.y.
