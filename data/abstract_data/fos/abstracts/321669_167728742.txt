The Security Vulnerability of Chinese Accounting Software
In recent years, the function of Chinese domestic accounting software has been improving gradually, but few people cares about the security vulnerability of the Accounting Software which may cause the leak of the users' information and lead to fraudulent financial report. Based on the working experience of more than 10 years, this dissertation gets focus on 4 kinds of Chinese major accounting softwares (CK2000, Anyi2000, UFErp 8.12, King Dee 2000)to analyse the safety precautions of database and find out the serious vulnerabilities in order to remind the accounting software users of averting them carefully.
