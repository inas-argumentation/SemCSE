Optical Resolution of a Six-Coordinate Silicon(IV) Complex with a Tripodal Hexadentate Ligand
The reaction of silicon tetraacetate with a tripodal hexadentate ligand (H3L = 1,1,1-tris(N-salicylideneaminomethyl)ethane) afforded the colorless [Si(L)]+ complex. The X-ray analysis of the complex revealed that the Si–N bond distance, av. 1.926(2) A, is much longer than the Si–O bond distance, av. 1.723(2) A. The complex is stable toward hydrolysis and could be optically resolved by fractional crystallization of the diastereomeric salt with (−)-dibenzoyl-L-tartaric acid.
