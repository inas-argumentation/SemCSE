Influence of Titanium Alloying Methods on Grain Refinement Effect and Fading Behavior of A356 Alloys
The influence of titanium alloying methods and holding time on the grain refinement effect and its fading was investigated and compared by adding titanium by electrolysis,melting Al-10Ti master alloys and adding K2TiF6 respectively.The results show that the ability of grain refinement of A356 alloys alloyed by electrolysis is obviously higher than that of others when the alloys with the same titanium content(0.1%) during the short holding time.The primary dendrite length of A356 alloy by electrolysis is the most short.The fading of grain refinement effect takes place for all alloys when prolonging the holding time.The Si particles are obviously coarsened.But the A356 alloys alloyed by electrolysis have the most optimum fading resistance of grain refinement,while for the alloys alloyed by melting K2TiF6,the fading resistance of grain refinement is the lowest compared with others.
