Magnetic properties and the magnetoimpedance effect of nanostructured Fe73.5Si16.5B6Nb3Cu1 ribbons with induced magnetic anisotropy
The structure and magnetic properties and the magnetoimpedance effect of nanocrystalline Fe73.5Si16.5B6Nb3Cu1 alloy ribbons, obtained from the amorphous state by annealing under different conditions, were comparatively analyzed. Despite the similarity of the samples’ structural states and the processes of their quasi-static magnetization reversal, the features of the magnetoimpedance effect are indicative of significant differences in the processes of their dynamic magnetization.
