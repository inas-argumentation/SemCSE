Electron microscopic observation on the function of the antiviral capsule of traditional medicines on herpes simplex virusII
Objective To study the mechanism of the antiviral capsule of traditional medicines in treating genital herpes. Methods Using the transmission electron microscope, we observed the morphologic change of the viral particles with negative staining after the interaction of the capsule and herpes simplex virusⅡ. Results The results showed that the viral particles deformed, and the sizes the particles were not uniform, the envelopes were denuded or damaged. The viral particles were fused into masses, and the structure was blurred. Conclusion The antiviral capsule has significant damaging effect on the morphosis, superficial components and dispersion degree of the viral particles.
