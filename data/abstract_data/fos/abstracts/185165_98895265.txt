Effects of molybdenum on photosynthetic pigment,characteristics and water efficiency of flue-cured tobacco
Photosynthetic pigment,photosynthetic characteristics and water use efficiency(WUE) of flue-cured tobacco with addition of molybdenum nutrition on K326 were studied.The results showed that molyb-denum increased chlorophyll content of tobacco obviously.During the period between the 40 d and 55 d after treatment,the chlorophyll content of T1,T2 and T3 were higher than that of CK,and increasing range was T1 T2 T3.At the time of 55 d,the Pn(photosynthetic rate) of T1 was increased by 27.63% compared to CK.The Ci(intercellular CO2 concentration) was reduced by 14.41%.The Tr(transpiration rate) of T1 and T2 were compara-tively lower.The WUE of T1 and T2 were increased by 95.64% compared with CK.Molybdenum can improve the dry matter accumulation quantity.The recommended suitable dose of molybdenum is 1.35 mg per pot.
