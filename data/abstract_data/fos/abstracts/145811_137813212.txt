Preparation and Investigation of Ti6Al4V Pre-alloyed Powders
Ti6Al4V prealloyed powders were prepared by mechanical milling in alcohol media.The phase constituent and microstructure of the prealloyed powders during mechanical milling was observed by XRD,SEM and TEM.The results show that with the increase of ball milling time,the particle size and grain size becomes smaller,the extent of micro-strain is intensified;the powders ball milled for 60 h get effectly refining and solid solution.The particle size of powder ball milled for 90 h can achieve nano-meter level,about 50 nm size on average.
