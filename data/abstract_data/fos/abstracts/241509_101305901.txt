Determination of concentration of hydrolyzed polyacryamide with turbidity method
The mass concentration of partially hydrolyzed polyacrylamide(HPAM) is determinated by the turbidity method.The influences of reaction time,wavelength,temperature and salinity on the absorbance are investigated.The results show that the optimum test conditions are as follows: the reaction time is 25 minutes,wavelength is 472 nm,temperature is between 18~25 ℃.The monovalent Na+ and K+ and the divalent Mg2+ and Ca2+ have no effect on test result,while trivalent Al3+ has a great effect on the absorbance.When the mass concentration of Al3+ is greater than 80 mg/L,the absorbance value tends to stability.Under the optimum test condition,the standard curve of different ρ(HPAM) is drawn at different water quality conditions,and it is found that the influence on absorbance is different if water quality is different.The higher the salinity of the water the greater the change value of absorbance is.Actual water samples are simulated to determine the concentration of HPAM in oil field injected or produced waters.The research results show that the turbidity method has advantages of wider measuring range,higher precision and good reproducibility.
