Tetrathio-Derivatives of p-Quinodimethanes Fused with 1,2,5-Thiadiazoles. A Novel Type of Organic Semiconductors
Bis[1,2,5]thiadiazolo-p-quinobis(1,3-dithiole) (BTQBT) was prepared by a Wittig–Horner reaction of 2-dimethylphosphono-1,3-dithiole with the corresponding dione. BTQBT is a red-violet crystal with a high melting point and the conductivity is unusually high (10−3 S cm−1) as a single component. The X-ray structural analysis reveals that the molecule is completely planar and forms a sheet-like network by short S–S contacts.
