Two-dimensional electrons at a GaAs-AlAs heterojunction under hydrostatic pressure
The authors report an investigation under hydrostatic pressure from liquid helium to room temperature of the transport properties of a two-dimensional electron gas confined at a GaAs-AlAs heterojunction. Within 2 kbar at 4.2 K, carrier concentration measured by means of the Shubnikov-de Haas and Hall effects is smaller by a factor of 6 and Hall mobility is smaller by a factor of 2.5. An attempt is made to interpret this unexpectedly large effect by considering the relative movement of absolute band minima on each side of the interface (the Gamma valley for the GaAs channel and the X valley for the AlAs barrier). High-temperature Hall data are also reported and show dominant parallel conduction in the AlAs layer.
