Exploration of retrospective teaching by physical education teachers
Physical education teacher is one of the important factors that affect physical education teaching reform.A physical education teacher cannot simply depend on his/her own sports skills and basic teaching theories to engage in teaching,but should constantly retrospect in specific teaching environment,and only by doing so can he/she combine the theories with the practice effectively and become a problem solver and decision maker,so as to enhanced his/her own professional capability as a physical education teacher.
