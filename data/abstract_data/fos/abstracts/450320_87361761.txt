Research on Increasing Extraction Rate of Lipid of Seaweed by Using Cellulase
Using cellulase deals with milled powder of seaweed and controls the condition of enzyme decompound in reason,which can increase the extraction rate of lipid of seaweed in evidence.We obtain the optimal condition of enzyme decompound by optimizing experiment:dosage of Cellulase(Cellulase/Seaweed,%)remains0 727%,temperature55℃,pH5 5,reaction time of Cellulase is 4 hours.
