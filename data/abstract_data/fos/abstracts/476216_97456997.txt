Synthesis and properties of thiazole halohydrazones. 1. Synthesis and interaction of 2-α-chlorobenzylidenehydrazino-4-ethoxycar-bonyl-thiazole with triethylamine in the presence of dipolarophiles
In the interaction of 2-α-chlorobenzylidenehydrazino-4-ethoxycarbonylthiazole with triethylamine, a nitrilimine is formed as an intermediate that does not react with dipolarophiles, but rather undergoes intramolecular 1, 5-dipolar cyclization to form 3-phenyl-5-ethoxycarbonylthiazolo[2,3-c]-1,2,4-ttiazole.
