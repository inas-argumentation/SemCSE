Field Evolution of FFLO State in a Superconductor with Strong Pauli Effects
Fulde-Ferrell-Larkin-Ovchinnikov (FFLO) phase in the vortex state is quantitatively studied by selfconsistent Eilenberger theory in three dimensional (3D) space of vortex lattice and FFLO modulation. We estimate free energy to determine the FFLO phase diagram in T -H plane and stable FFLO wave number for typical and canonical example of 3D Fermi sphere with s-wave pairing. To facilitate the experimental identification of FFLO state, we investigate the field evolut ion of NMR spectra and flux line lattice form factors by neutron scattering in the FFLO vortex states. Possible applications to CeCoIn5 is mentioned.
