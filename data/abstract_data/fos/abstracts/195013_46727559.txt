The Association of Physicians launches new summer research studentships for medical undergraduates.
The Association of Physicians was founded in 1907 at the instigation of Sir William Osler. In the first minute book it states that he ‘suggested the formation of a National Association of Physicians, somewhat on the lines of the Association of American Physicians, which should both be a pleasant gathering and also should form the nucleus of a public for the magazine.’ The magazine in question being, of course, the Quarterly Journal of Medicine (QJM) .
