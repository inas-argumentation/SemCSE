Evaluation of reliability and M.T.T.F. of a complex system by Boolean Function technique
In this paper a complex system similar to a power plant has been considered. The object of the system is to supply power from a generator in a power house to a very critical destination, connected by cables and switches. The availability and M.T.T.F. have been evaluated by using the Boolean Function (B.F.) technique. The failure times of the various components of the system follow arbitrary distributions. Numerical examples and the graphs have also been given to highlight the availability and M.T.T.F. of the system at any time.
