The production and analysis of millet fermented rice
The millet as raw materials fermentation production sweet fermented glutinous rice, confirm the influence of fermentation strains, add quantity, fermentation temperature, time on the acidity (pH), sugar content, alcohol of millet fermented glutinous rice. Orthogonal test showed that the optimal process conditions of the millet fermented rice: the selection of yeast fermentation strains 1008, dosage 3 g/kg, fermentation temperature of 26 ℃, 4 days of fermentation. Under this condition, the millet fermented glutinous rice mash acidity (pH) is 3.9, sugar content is 12.8%, 7.3% alcohol, the sensory score higher, the product beige, soft and cooked rice texture, flavor pleasant fragrance, taste sweet and slightly acid, comply with the relevant food standards.
