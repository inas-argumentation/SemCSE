Maximum-Likelihood Estimation, from Censored Samples, of the Parameters of a Logistic Distribution
Consider an ordered random sample of size n from a logistic distribution with location parameter μ and scale parameter σ. Let the r 1 smallest and the r 2 largest sample values be censored, where r 1 ≥0,r 2 ≥0, and r 1 +r 2 ≤n −2. An iterative procedure for solution (on a computer) of the likelihood equations is outlined and illustrated by a numerical example involving Strontium-90 concentrations in milk. The information matrix is inverted numerically to obtain the asymptotic variances and covariances of the maximum-likelihood estimators of the parameters for censoring proportions, q 1 = r 1/n and q 2 = r 2 /n, at intervals of one-tenth. The means and variances of , and , together with the covariances of and , are reported for a Monte Carlo study of 1000 samples each of size n = 10. The mean square errors of the estimates are compared with the variances of the best linear unbiased estimators and with the variances of the maximum-likelihood estimators as given by the asymptotic formulas.
