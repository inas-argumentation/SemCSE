Evidence for fully gapped d-wave superconductivity in CeCu$_2$Si$_2$
The nature of the pairing symmetry of the first heavy fermion superconductor CeCu$_2$Si$_2$ remains an unresolved issue and is the subject of controversy. While it was generally believed to be a d-wave superconductor, recent specific heat measurements by Kittaka et al. [Phys. Rev. Lett. 112 067002 (2014)] showed evidence for fully gapped superconductivity, contrary to the expected nodal behavior. We report London penetration depth measurements [$\Delta\lambda(T)$], which also reveal fully gapped behavior at very low temperatures. We show that a fully gapped $d+d$ pairing state yields good fits to both the superfluid density and specific heat. Taking this and other factors into consideration, we propose this pairing state to be the superconducting order parameter of CeCu$_2$Si$_2$.
