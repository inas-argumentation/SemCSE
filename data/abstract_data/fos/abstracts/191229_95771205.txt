Potentiometric study of heteropolyanion formation from methylarsonate and molybdate anions.
The equilibria between CH3AsO32−, MoO42− and proton were studied by the use of a glass electrode in 1M Na(Cl) ionic medium at 25 °C. The data were explained assuming (H+)p(MoO42−)q (CH3AsO32−)r where (p,q,r) = (7,4,1), (8,4,1), (12,6,1), (13,6,1) together with (11,6,2) or (12,6,2).
