Study on Mechanical Properties of HP40 Alloy Modified by Al
HP40 alloy replacing Ni with 5%~20% (wt) Al was prepared by vacuum arc melting processing. The tests of normal temperature compression and mechanical properties were carried out.The results show that the strength and hardness of HP40 alloy modified by Al are markedly increased . With Al content increasing, the brittleness of the alloy is increased because dendrite (Ni, Fe)Al phase separates out and coarsens.
