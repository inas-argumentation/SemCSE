Study on the enzymatic hydrolysis processes on salty white of salt-pickled egg
Enzymatic hydrolysis of the salty white of saltpickled egg with the salinity up to 5% was investigated. Papain and Flavor Enzyme were chosen as biocatalysts for the reaction. Results showed that Papain and Flavor Enzyme should be added at the same time. The optimal technological parameters of enzymatic hydrolysis of salty white of saltpickled egg by Papain and Flavor Enzyme were:optimal temperature and time for pre-treatment were 90 ℃ and 30 min,Papain∶Flavor Enzyme=4∶6,concentration of substrate was 5%,E/S was 4500 U/g(Sub),pH 5.5,temperature was 50 ℃,time was 6 h,respectively.
