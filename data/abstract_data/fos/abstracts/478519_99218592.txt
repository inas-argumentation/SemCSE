Study on properties of biodiesel synthesized by catalytic esterification with sulfuric acid
Biodiesel is catalytic synthesized using variety of leftover oils of vegetable oil factory,waste products of high fatty acid plant and methanol as raw materials,and industrial sulfuric acid as catalyst.The effects of volume ratio of methanol to oil,amount of catalyst,reaction time and temperature on the esterification rate are investigated.The optimum reaction conditions are determined as follows: volume of methanol to oil ratio of 1∶1,amount of catalyst 0.6 g,reaction temperature 65 to 75 ℃,reaction time 6 h.Under these conditions,the esterification rate can reach 95.5%.The product is atmospheric distillated and washed,and crude diesel product is got.Then the crude diesel product is distillated in-0.095 to-0.085 MPa vacuum and 170 to 210 ℃ range of fractions is collected.The acid value of refined product is low,and the yield up to 90%.The basic properties of biodiesel,such as density,viscosity,flash point,etc.,are in line with existing US biodiesel standards,and close to those of mineral and petrochemicals diesel.
