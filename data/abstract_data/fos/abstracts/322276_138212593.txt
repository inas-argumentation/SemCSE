Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of ZM5+xRE Alloys
The effects of RE addition and solid solution treatment on the microstructure and mechanical properties of ZM5+xRE alloys were investigated.The result indicated that adding RE can obviously change the cast structure of ZM5 alloy: the rod-like RE phase separates out and the morphology of β-Mg17Al12 phase at grain boundaries changes from discontinuous network to granular phase and the α-Mg phase can be refined.After solid solution treatment,β-Mg17Al12 phase almost dissolves in α-Mg phase,large RE phase in rod form is broken and becomes sphere.The 0.8% Re addition content and T4(25 h at 410 ℃) treatment can largest enhanced the mechanical properties of ZM5+xRE alloy.
