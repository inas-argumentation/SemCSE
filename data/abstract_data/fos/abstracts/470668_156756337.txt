On the Teaching Reform of the Ideological and Political Education in Universities
As an important tache of the ideological and political education of university students,Two Courses(Marxism theory course and ideological morality course) play an un-measured role in improving students' theoretical culture and their distinguishing abilities in analyzing and animadverting,as well as in helping them erecting correct philosophy and value view.However,the impact of globalization and multivariate thoughts put forward new requests for the teaching of Two Courses which take university students as major school objects.Thus,how to endow Two Courses with new teaching content and mode has become a subject to which generally attention is given in recent years.As far as the new change of the course setup system and demands of two lessons,the author, integrating with her working practice,ponders on this task in teaching content and methods.
