Design and Application of Case-based Reasoning for Services Composition Template
The way towards industrialized service composition is a way of based on composition template.In the larger-granularity services composition approach for business-users the design of services composition template is a object-oriented decision-making,exploration and study activity with constraint. This paper made use of the expertsknowledge and experience effectively and presented a case-based reasoning approach to designing composition templates. The approach has been used in a services composition platform of City Emergency Oriented and illustrated the effect by a case study.
