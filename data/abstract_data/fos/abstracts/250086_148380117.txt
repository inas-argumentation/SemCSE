A new organization structure of education and research of Japanese Kyushu University and its inspiration
The Graduate School and Faculty organization system, which Kyushu University of Japan carries out, breaks with the traditional organization structure of university and achieves the gentle combination of the education function and research function with the two functions separating at the same university organization. The new organization structure of education and research can not only increase the adaptability and flexibility of the university organization, but also help to break the academic barriers, in order to realize the cross-discipline development of university organization. In addition, we can draw lessons from it and can apply it to our university reform and organization creation.
