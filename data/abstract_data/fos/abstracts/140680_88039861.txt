Effects of high temperature on physiological characteristics and grain yield of middle-season indica hybrid rice during flowering period
The physiological reaction,grain yield and their adaptability of middle-season indica hybrid rice under high temperature during flowering stage were determined,which was conducted with an artificial climate box.The results showed that,under high temperature,the content of malondialdehyde(MDA),free proline and soluble sugar in function leaves were increased significantly,while the chlorophyll content and photosynthetic rate of leaves were decreased.The great decline of yield(32%-44%) was mainly due to the seed setting rate de-creased(22%-30%) significantly,and then resulted from the grain weight reduced(8%-14%) obviously.A sig-nificantly positive correlations were found between photosynthetic rate,chlorophyll content,free proline content and grain yield,while MDA had a significantly negative correlation with grain yield.This paper discussed the physiological indicators under high-temperature in rice.
