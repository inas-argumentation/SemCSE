‘Egg-carton’ shaped plausible organo-sedimentary structures from the Archaean Dharwar Craton, India
Bizarre small bulbous ‘egg carton’ shaped organosedimentary structures/plausible stromatolites are reported from the Archaean Vanivilas Formation (> 2.75 Ga) of the Chitradurga Group (CG) of India. CG is a part of the Dharwar Craton, India (Seshadri et al. 1981). The top surface of the bed hosting these structures resembles the ‘egg-carton’ (Fig. 1 and Electronic supplement).
