Frequency properties of 4H-SiC thyristors at high current density
Frequency properties of 4H-SiC thyristors with forward blocking voltages of 400 V and 700 V have been investigated. The limiting operating frequency exceeds 500 kHz up to current densities of for 400 V structures and 750 A for 700 V structures. The experimentally measured limiting frequencies of 4H-SiC devices are compared with the theoretical estimations and the experimental data for rated Si thyristors.
