Application of SOLIDCast in the Casting Process Design of Spoke
The casting process of spoke was designed by using SOLIDCast.The filling and solidification processes were simulated by SOLIDCast and the formation of defects were analyzed.The casting process was optimized by adding chills and controlling of pouring time.The optimized process was put into production and the surface of castings produced was clean without defect.The optimized process is proved to be reasonable and casting processes simulated by SOLIDCast are accurate.
