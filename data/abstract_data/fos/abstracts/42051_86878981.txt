Impact of Sorghum Aphid, Melanaphis sacchari Population on Sorghum Yield
The substantial genetic variability for resistance to sorghum aphid was studied and the results indicated that genotypes 61526 and 61558 among aphid resistant lines, RSV 458, RSV 792 and RSV 807 shoot fly resistant lines, RS 29 and C-43 R-lines, RSE 3, Lakadi, M 35-1, local cultivars and Selection 3, Swati, and CSV 8R and CSV 216R national/state varieties had a low population build-up of aphids with < 2 score indicating their least preference with < 10 aphids per leaf. But considering the leaf chlorosis rating of < 2 score consistently during Rabi 2005/06 as well as Kharif 2006 season in the genotypes, 61526, RSV 161, C-43, IS 2312, RSE 3 and CSV 216 R were promising indicating good resistance with only a portion of the leaf found killed by the pest. Among these, 61526, RSV 807 and CSV 216R were most promising with a plant damaging score of < 3 invariably during Rabi 2005/06 and Kharif 2006 season implying that < 40% of the leaf area was dead.
