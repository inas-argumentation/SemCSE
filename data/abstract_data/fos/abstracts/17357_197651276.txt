Temperature activation of courtship behavior in the male red-sided garter snake (Thamnophis sirtalis parietalis): role of the anterior hypothalamus-preoptic area.
Adult male red-sided garter snakes (Thamnophis sinalis parietalis) received radio-frequency lesions prior to 17 weeks of low-temperature hibernation Animals found to have bilateral lesions of the anterior hypothalamus-preoptic area (AH-POA) failed to exhibit courtship behavior on emergence from hibernation Those individuals in which the major portion of the destruction was centered in the anterior POA also exhibited deficits in thermoregulatory behavior Animals that received unilateral lesions of the AH-POA initiated courtship behavior after controls and had an abbreviated period of courtship, these animals exhibited normal responses to thermal stimuli Male snakes with lesions outside the AH-POA courted normally and demonstrated no differences in thermoregulatory behavior compared with the surgical controls These results indicate that in male red-sided garter snakes, an intact AH-POA is critical for the integration of thermal stimuli that activate seasonal courtship behavior The anterior hypothalamus-preoptic area (AH-POA) is a major mtegrative site for external and internal stimuli regulating courtship behavior in male vertebrates (Crews & Silver, 1985; Ingle & Crews, 1985). Afferents of the major sensory systems important to sexual behavior converge in the AH-POA, this area also contains sex steroid hormone-concentrating neurons. It has been assumed, but not demonstrated as yet, that these sex steroid-concentrating neurons are also involved in the control of motor patterns associated with mating In all vertebrates exhibiting an associated reproductive pattern (Crews, 1984), lesions in the AH-POA result in the rapid decline and extinction of courtship behavior in adult males Furthermore, implantation of minute amounts of androgens directly into the AH-POA reinstates sexual behavior in castrated, sexually inactive males. In species exhibiting an associated reproductive pattern, there is a temporal association between maximum spermatogenesis, elevated circulating concentrations of androgens, and courtship behavior The red-sided garter snake, Thamnophis sirtalis parietalis.
