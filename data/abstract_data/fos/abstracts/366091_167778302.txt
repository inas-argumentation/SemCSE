The Implement and Perplexity of Enterprise Social Responsibility Standard under the Drive of Benefits
The influence of the implement of the social responsibility standard upon the business enterprise and economy society is increasingly obvious.The social responsibility standard is not compulsory standard still in our country,so most enterprises adopt the benefits judge rule to promote and carry out the social responsibility standard.at the same time this kind judgment under the benefits drives also gives enterprise perplexity whether implement the social responsibility standard,this article standpoint is: From the farsighted see It is necessity and urgencies for business enterprise to implement the social responsibility standard,and it is also the foundation that the business enterprise acquires the long-term and continuable developing competition ability.
