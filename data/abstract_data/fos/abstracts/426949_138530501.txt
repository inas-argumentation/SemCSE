Flow Stress Model of TC17 Alloy Under High Temperature Deformation
The isothermal compression te sts of TC17 alloy were conducted on Gleeble-1500 thermal-mechanical simulator machine.The results reveal that the flow stress of TC17 alloy decreases remarkably with an increase in deformation temperature or a decrease in strain rate.When the strain rate is relatively high(1.0 s-1and 10.0 s-1),the flow softening feature of flow curves is observed at α +β two-phase region,while the flow stress fluctuates after reaching a peak value at βsingle-phase region.The flow stress model for characterizing the flow stress was established by considering the effect of strain on the material parameters.The average relative error between the calculated and experimental results is 5.37%,which indicates that the flow stress model can provide theoretical basis for hot-forming processing of TC17 alloy.
