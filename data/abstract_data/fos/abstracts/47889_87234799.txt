Sieving of Selective Media for Beauveria bassiana
In the present paper,growth of Beauveria bassiana and 3 fungal contaminants on different media were studied.By measuring growing rates and CFU's of B.bassiana in different conditions of medial composition,fungistasis,and pH,selective media for B.bassiana were sieved.The result showed that the oat meal medium with dodine added is the best.pH adjustment gave a stronger inhibiting effect on contaminants than on B.bassiana.
