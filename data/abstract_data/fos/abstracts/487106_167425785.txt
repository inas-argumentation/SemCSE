Logic and Mechanism of Co-management of Commercial Bank Beneficiary
The model of beneficiary co management in commocial banks has amended the old one that focused on the priority of share holders. This new model emphasizes cooperating among borrowers, lenders, staff, senior management and supervision institution and participating in bank's value creating activities and management. In terms of China's financial and economic environments the option to improve management efficiency should be enhancing the mechanism and the approach with which beneficiary would be motivated to participate in management.
