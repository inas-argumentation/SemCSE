Experimental study of heat transfer augmentation versus pumping power in a horizontal R12 evaporator
An experimental investigation has been carried out to study the effect of ‘swirl generators’ inside a horizontal R12 evaporator. The swirl generators consisted of twisted stainless-steels strips with ratios (y) of 3.76, 5.58, 7.37 and 10.15. The variation of swirl flow heat transfer coefficients with pumping power has been studied. The ratio of swirl flow to plain heat transfer coefficient per unit of pumping power has been found to be more than one in several test runs. The data cover a heat flux range of 8000–13 600 W m−2, a mass velocity range of 7.0 × 105−14.0 × 105 kgh−1 m−2 and a vapour quality range of 0.30 to 0.75.
