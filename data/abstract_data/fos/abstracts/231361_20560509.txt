Community-acquired severity prediction-what are we predicting and why?
Dear Editor

I applaud the recent commentary by Dr Abers and Musher challenging the widespread use of the area under the receiver operator characteristic curve (AUC) to evaluate community-acquired pneumonia (CAP) severity indices. This statistic is very useful as a measure of discrimination for predicting an outcome (e.g. mortality) across a range of cut-offs, but says nothing about the clinical utility of the test.1 The clinical relevance depends critically on one question—what are we predicting and why?
