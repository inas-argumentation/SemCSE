Effect of Helicobacter pylori on cell proliferation, apoptosis of gastric epithelial cells in vitro
Objective Explore the influence of Helicobacter pylori to proliferation and apoptosis of gastric epithelial cells in vitro. Methods SGC-7901 cell proliferation was examined by immunohistochemical analysis of Ki-67 antigen after incubation with NCTC 11637. The effect of NCTC 11637 on cell apoptosis was evaluated by Hoechst 33258 fluorochrome staining and flow cytometry. Results Cell proliferation was enhanced when co-incubated with the H.pylori in low concentration (≤1.6×10 5CFU/ml) but inhibited in higher concentration (≥8×10 5CFU/ml) showing a dose-dependent picture. The co-incubation of SGC-7901 with H. pylori led to increase of cell apoptosis in a H. pylori concentration-dependent way. Conclusion The unbalance of cell apoptosis and cell proliferation was hypothesized to explain the histological outcomes after H.pylori infection.
