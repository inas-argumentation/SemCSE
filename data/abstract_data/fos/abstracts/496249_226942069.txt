Wild Cistanche deserticol Y. C.Ma crude polysaccharides enhance the efficacy of influenza virus vaccine in mice via subcutaneous immunization
Objective 
To investigate the potential effects of wild Cistanche deserticola Y. C.Ma crude polysaccharides (WCDCP) on enhancing efficacy of influenza virus vaccine (IVV) in a mouse model. 
 
 
Methods 
ICR Mice were subcutaneously immunized with 0.5 μg of IVV in combination with low, medium and high doses of WCDCP, respectively. Three control group including blank control group, control group and positive control group were set up by injecting mice with 0.9% NaCl, 0.5 μg of IVV without adjuvant and 0.5 μg of aluminum-adjuvanted IVV, respectively. Each mice were immunized twice with 14 days interval between prime and boost immunization. ELISA was performed to detect IgG, IgG1 and IgG2a specific for influenza virus in serum samples. Proliferation of lymphocytes was analyzed by MTT. CD3+ CD4+ , CD3+ CD8+ , CD4+ CD44+ and CD8+ CD44+ T lymphocytes were measured with flow cytometry. 
 
 
Results 
There was no significant differences in the growth of WCDCP-immunized mice of different groups (P>0.05). IgG antibody titers were significantly up-regulated in the three co-immunization groups of WCDCP and IVV (P<0.05), especially in the group with medium WCDCP, which was equal to the aluminum-adjuvanted group. Moreover, co-immunization with WCDCP and IVV significantly increased the titers of IgG1 and IgG2a antibodies (P<0.05), promoted the proliferation of lymphocytes (P<0.05) and up-regulated the percentages of CD3+ CD4+ , CD3+ CD8+ , CD4+ CD44+ and CD8+ CD44+ T lymphocytes in mice (P<0.01). 
 
 
Conclusion 
WCDCP-adjuvanted IVV could elicit stronger IgG responses and significantly increase the production of Th1 and Th2 associated IgG subtypes. WCDCP could enhance the efficacy of IVV by boosting humoral and cellular immunity responses, especially the T-cell mediated immunity, suggesting the potential use of it as a safe adjuvant. 
 
 
Key words: 
Cistanche deserticol; Adjuvant; Crude polysaccharide; Influenza virus vaccine
