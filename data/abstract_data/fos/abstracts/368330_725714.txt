Bilateral flank pain and epigastralgia in a young man with pulmonary tuberculosis.
A 28-year old, previously healthy man presented with bilateral flank pain and epigastralgia for 10 days, which increased in severity and frequency progressively. The pain was located on the level just above the umbilicus and was not related to respiration, active movements or food intake.
