Pipe Stiffness of Permanently Deflected Corrugated Plastic Drain Tubing
ABSTRACT THIRTY centimeter lengths of corrugated polyethylene drain tubing were permanently deflected in the laboratory using a parallel plate device and heat treat-ment. Permanent deflections ranged from 0 to 60 percent. The heat treatment itself did not affect pipe stiffness of the tubing. Following treatment, the 250 tests samples were loaded with a parallel plate device to deter-mine the force, F, required to deflect the tubing additional amounts, Ay. The ratio of F/Ay is defined as pipe stiffness. Pipe stiffness was found to decrease with an increase in permanent deflection, starting at approximately 10 per-cent permanent deflection for 102-, 127-, and 152-mm perforated helical-corrugated and 102-mm non-perforated ring-corrugated tubing. The pipe stiffness of 102-mm perforated ring-corrugated tubing decreased with permanent deflection over the entire range tested.
