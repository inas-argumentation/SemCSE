An application of the theory of social situations to repeated games
Abstract The paper applies the theory of social situations (J. Greenberg, “The Theory of Social Situations: An Alternative Game-Theoretic Approach”, Cambridge Univ. Press, New York, 1989) to infinitely repeated games with discounting. It is shown that every nondiscriminating conservative stable standard of behavior for the associated repeated game Nash situation, is a subset of the set of perfect equilibrium paths (PEP). Moreover, the set of PEP itself is a nondiscriminating stationary stable standard of behavior. Hence, stability refines perfection. I also introduce a new concept, coalitional PEP (CPEP), which is immune against correlated deviations of groups of individuals. Clearly, CPEP provides a further refinement of perfection.
