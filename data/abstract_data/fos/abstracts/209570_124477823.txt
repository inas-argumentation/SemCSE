X-ray laser Moire-fringe deflectometry diagnostic at low irradiance of pumping optical laser
Ne-like Cr X-ray laser was produced at low irradiance of pumping optical laser in Xingguang-II laser facility, and was used as probe laser of Moire-fringe deflectometry device. The clear XRL Moire-fringe without the object was obtained .The experimental method is described, the results are given and briefly discussed in the paper.
