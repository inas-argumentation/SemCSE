The Practice and Analysis of the Link from the Secondary Vocational Education to the Higher Professional Education
It is rather late for our country to realize and investigate the higher professional education. But the technical schools, secondary professional schools and adult institutes, owing to their characteristics, have had vocational quality in their education for a long time. This article presents the process of the three stages of the link from secondary vocational education to the higher progessional education in Tianjin. It also analyses the two styles of the link, gives proposals and suggestions, and approaches the thought in applying a policy to develop this link.
