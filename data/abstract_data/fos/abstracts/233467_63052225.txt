Research of TCP/IP Packet Header Suppress Algorithm in Broadband Wireless Access System
The background of this paper is WiMAX data link layer,analyze and verify detailed the PHS(packet header suppress) algorithm in Broadband Wireless Access System can carried out or not,and design a compression scheme under the IPv4. Using the ARQ information in the radio data link,we can do the header of TCP/IP packet with a greatest compression which is recoverable. Both launcher and receiver maintain some tables to keep the necessary information of the headers,helping to compress the header of the TCP/IP packets and improving the utilization of the radio bandwidth.
