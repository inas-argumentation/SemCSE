What Kind of University could Cultivate Innovative Talents
To cultivate innovative talents calls for series of requirements for universities' spiritual characteristics.Firstly,it calls for a university with the soul,which could have the ideal and height owe to its leading social progress.Secondly,it calls for a university with the horizon,which could have the wisdom and depth owe to its promoting students' development.Thirdly,it calls for a university with the mind,which could have the vigor and width owe to its encouraging diversity.Fourthly,it calls for a university with the backbone,which could have the courage and hardness owe to its refusing any attachment.Only with all these traits,could our universities truly cultivate innovative talents.
