From"There Are Laws to Follow"to"Three Are Good Laws to Follow" —Theoretical and Practical Significance of Jiang Zeming' s Thought of"Three Representations"for the Current Legislation in China
There have been two great leaps forward in the legislation of contemporary China:from the stage when there were no laws to follow to the stage when there were laws to follow,and to the stage when there are good laws to follow.This article holds that Jiang Zeming' s thought of"Three representations"is the criteria not only for the evaluation of socialist construction in every field,but also for the evaluation of the current legislation in China.Based on this point of view,the writer puts forward six ways out for the making of good laws.
