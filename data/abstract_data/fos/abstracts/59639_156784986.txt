Social capital,rent-seeking and tenure in academics——On faculty appointment system of scientific research staff in universities
The faculty appointment system in universities is a hotly debated focus in academic circles about whether to keep the tenure system or contract system.This paper makes a traced study on 107 advanced scientific research staff via Multilevel Data Analysis,the result of which indicates that acquiring social capital will have influence on output of the scientific staff and keep them in a state of high output in the professional career.We think this process is a kind of rent-seeking.It discovered that tenure system is in position to shorten this kind of rent seeking in universities.So tenure system is better than contract system.
