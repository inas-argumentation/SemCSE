On Discussion of the Logic Order of Constitutive Elements of Crime
Our traditional theory of Constitutive Elements of a Crime is virtually only to judge whether one act should be punished or not,not to reflect condemnation proceeding,because not only the acts of eliminating social hazards,but also those endangered by persons without criminal responsibility are excluded from traditional fields of our Crime Constitution Theory.Under the prerequisite of our traditional theory,It is little significance to study the logic order of Constitutive Elements of Crime.'Modified Four Elements' change the nature of Constitutive Elements of a Crime and take part in discussion of the logic order of Constitutive Elements of Crime.
