New heterogeneous mode of nitric oxide reduction by an in situ formed carbon
A highly reactive, carbonaceous reductant for NO is found on portions of heated reactor walls exposed to HC (hydrocarbon) + O/sub 2/ mixtures richer than optimum for homogeneous reduction of NO. This reductant reduces as much as 95% of the NO for prolonged periods. The duration of the reduction process is a function of the duration of deposition, temperature, residence time, and (O/sub 2/)/(HC) ratio, whereas the extent of NO-reduction is a function of the temperature, (O/sub 2/)/(HC) ratio, and residence time. In addition, an activation energy of 34 kcal is indicated. Residence times are compatible with those of automobile exhaust, and comparisons are made with previous observations of the SCR Mode in engine tests. The isolation, characterization, and products of the SC reductant are described.
