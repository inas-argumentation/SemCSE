Effect of a bias electric field on the dielectric properties of lead-free (Na,Sr,Li)NbO3 ceramics with diffuse phase transition
The effect of a bias electric field on the value and temperature dependence of dielectric permittivity ɛ of lead-free ceramics of the NaNbO3-Sr0.5NbO3-LiNbO3 system with diffuse maximum ɛ(T) (temperature Tm of which is strongly dependent on frequency) was studied. It was found that the ɛ(T) maximum decreases and diffuses as the electric field strength E increases, while Tm is virtually independent of E in the investigated range of E ≤ 8 kV cm−1. A similar effect of a bias electric field on ɛ and Tm was observed earlier in classical lead-containing relaxors of the PbMg1/3Nb2/3O3 type.
