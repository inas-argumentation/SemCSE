Analysis of the Efficacy of Stereotactic Cimformal Radiotherapy for Brain Metastases
Objective To evaluate the efficacy of sterotactic cimformal radiotherapy(SCRT) for brain metastases. Methods The data of 52 patienrs with brain metastases who took SCRT (the dose was 25~30GY)were analyzed retrospectively and 45 of them had taken whole brain irradiation(the dose was 35~40GY)before the SCRT.Results The median survival time was 13 months;one year survival rate was 42.3%; the tumor control rate was 92.3% after 3 months.Conclusion SCRT is effective and well-tolerateed for patients with brain metastases.
