Oviductus Ranae antioxidant activities component screening and preparation of antioxidant peptides from water-miscible proteins
In order to study on Oviductus Ranae antioxidant activitives component.Three sample include Oviductus Ranae powder,water-miscible proteins powder,non-water-miscible ingredients powder were prepared,and their clearance of hydroxyl free radicals,superoxide anion free radicals and DPPH· free radicals were determined.The different antioxidant activities among Oviductus Ranae powder,watermiscible proteins powder and non-water-miscible ingredients powder were evaluated.Results indicated that Oviductus Ranae's water-miscible proteins powder has a good antioxidant activity,under the same conditions,its antioxidant activity was the highest,in 10 mg/mL concentration,its clearance of hydroxyl free radicals,superoxide anion free radicals and DPPH· free radicals was 45%,37% and 71% respectively.And protease were used to prepare antioxidant peptides from water-miscible proteins.In 10 mg/mL concentration,the peptides prepared of neutral protease was the best,its clearance of hydroxyl free radicals,superoxide anion free radicals and DPPH· free radicals was 60%,45% and 82% respectively.
