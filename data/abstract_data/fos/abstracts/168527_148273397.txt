Teacher education reform in normal universities under the background of new curriculum reform
The development of a normal university is established in cultivation of high-qualified teachers for basic education.The reform of new basic education curriculum put forward unprecedented challenges,however,there was little change in the curriculum of teachers' education in normal universities in recent decades, and the content of teaching and basic education are disjointed,teaching concept and method is dated,so the graduates from normal university are not adapted to the reform of new basic education curriculum very well.So,we should renew educational conception,optimize the structure of teachers' educational curriculum,reform the model of teachers' cultivation,strengthen the cooperation and connection with basic education,form a new system of teachers' education including cultivation before work and training after work,in order to embody the characteristics of teachers' education in normal universities.
