FLUORESCENCE SPECTRUM OF PYRIMIDINE VAPOR FROM THE ZERO-POINT VIBRATIONAL LEVEL OF 1B1(n, π*) STATE
The fluorescence spectrum of pyrimidine vapor which originates from the zero-point vibrational level of the 1B1(n, π*) state has been measured. Most of the strong bands can be explained in terms of three totally-symmetric vibrations: v6a″, v12″, and V9a″. No phosphorescence was observed from pyrimidine vapor at various pressures.
