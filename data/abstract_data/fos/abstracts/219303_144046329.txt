Book reviews : Harley, B., Allen, P., Cummins, J., and Swain, M. (editors) 1990: The development of second language proficiency. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 248pp
The aim of this book is, according to the editors, ’to place the project in perspective by bringing together short summaries of the research on each issue and a set of discussion papers written by experts in the area of bilingualism and second language education’ (p. 1). The editors do not claim to have answered the questions that were set. On the contrary, their research has raised a great many more questions than were answered and will undoubtedly provide the impetus for years of work to come. The question set by this reviewer is: to what extent does this book give a balanced perspective of the project? Structurally the book is a frame story with each of the research questions addressed in Sections I, II, and III introduced by a discussion by the editors, a selection of two or three reaction papers which experts presented at a 1987 project symposium, and a brief response by the DBP team.
