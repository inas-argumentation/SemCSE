Studies on Serveral Bacteriosis and the Pathogeny of Grass Carp
Four seriously harmful bacterial diseases were reported and their occuring reasons for grass carp (Ctenopharyngodon idellus) were studied in Liuan city.The result showed that this kind of disease was mainly caused by feeding abundant powder fodder like rapeseed cakes,which directly caused water deterioration,rapid reproduction of Aeromonas,and reduced the disease resistance of grass carp.
