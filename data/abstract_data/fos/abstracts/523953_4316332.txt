Simulations, Measurements, and Sorting of THOMX Ring Bending Magnets
The THOMX facility is a compact X-ray source based on compton back scattering, under construction at LAL in Orsay, France. The lattice is based on a double bend achromat. The dipole design, the set-up, and magnetic measurement results are presented and discussed, as well as the sorting issue in preparation of the commissioning of ThomX facility.
