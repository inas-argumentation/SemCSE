A Practical Study of Promoting Popularization of Marxism by Using the Problem-based Teaching Method
The purpose of popularization of Marxism is to make the basic principles of Marxism the ideological weapon and action guide of the public.Achieving that goal depends on the methods of theoretical transmission,and in colleges that means it is essential to adopt teaching methods fitting in with the college students.The paper argues that the use of problem-based teaching methods can make teaching process a problem-solving activity,in which communication between teachers and students would be triggered by the problems and teachers could use the Marxist viewpoints and methods to analyze and solve problems.In the collision of ideas,the purpose of spreading Marxism,understanding,acknowledging and accepting Marxism could be achieved.
