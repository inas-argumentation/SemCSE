Evidence for an arid to semi-arid climate during deposition of the Cambrian system in central Texas, U.S.A
Petrographic evidence indicates that an arid to semi-arid climate existed in central Texas, U.S.A., during accumulation of the Cambrian System. This interpretation is supported by the presence of ventifacts, quartz with frosted surfaces, fresh well-rounded feldspars, paucity of clay material, and the presence of in situ and transported calcitized evaporites.
