Flux Pinning Analysis of Superconducting YBCO Foam Struts
Magnetization loops were measured on struts of superconducting YBa<sub>2</sub>Cu<sub>3</sub>O<inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$_{x}$</tex-math></inline-formula> (YBCO, 123-phase) foams taken from various positions of a 5 × 2 × 2 cm<sup>3</sup> large foam sample prepared at RWTH Aachen. From these data, flux pinning forces, <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$F_{p} = j_{c} \times B$</tex-math></inline-formula>, were calculated and pinning force scaling diagrams <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$F_{p}/F_{p,\max}$</tex-math></inline-formula> versus <italic>h</italic> = <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$H_{a}/H_{\rm irr}$</tex-math></inline-formula> were established. The scaling in the temperature range 60 K ≤ <italic>T</italic> ≤ 85 K was found to be well developed for all samples with peak positions, <italic>h</italic><sub>0</sub>, above 0.4, which is an indication of δ<inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$T_{c}$</tex-math></inline-formula> pinning. From microstructure analysis using the electron backscatter diffraction technique (EBSD), it is known that the struts are fully converted into the 123-phase with embedded 211 particles being relatively small. The variation of <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$F_{p}$</tex-math></inline-formula> depending on the position of the struts in the original sample directly reveals the changes of the texture as found employing EBSD.
