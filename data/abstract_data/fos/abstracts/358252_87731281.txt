Purification of RGL from Rice Germ by Affinity Chromatograph on a Chitin Column
Rice germ lectin (RGL) could be combined with N acetyl D glycosamine (GLcNAc) or its polymer specially.The affinity isolation of RGL from rice germ by affinity chromatograph on a chitin column was carried out in this paper.The isolated lectin had similar molecular weight,biological specificity and isoelectric point compared with the reported RGL.
