Study on Electrochemical Corrosion Behavior of Gray Iron in Acid Solution
The corrosion behaviors of various cast iron with different graphite shape were investigated in HCl solution at room temperature by weight losses and electrochemical techniques.The microstructure and composition of the tested samples were investigated by microscope.The results show that the corrosion resistance of the gray cast iron sample with graphite morphology of the center A-type and the edge D-type is better than others.The corrosion resistance of white iron is worst because its potential is higher than the base metal,easy to form micro-battery effect.The results of the polarization curves are in concordance with the results of weight loss test.
