Influence of Solution Treatment on Microstructure and Property of Boron Stainless Steel
The influences of solution treatment on the microstructure,mechanical properties and corrosion resistance of boron stainless steel were investigated.Borides have high thermal stability by thermo-calc calculation.The experimental results show that the precipitation amount of borides basically unchanges with different solution treatment.The proper heat treatment for this born stainless steel is solution at 1100 ℃ for 4 h then water cooling.The mechanical properties and corrosion resistance are relatively better because the accumulation of large blocky borides reduces and forms small block and dot borides in the grain boundaries.
