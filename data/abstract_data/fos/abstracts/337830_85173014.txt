Bioluminescence Activity of Coelenterazine Analogues after Incorporation into Recombinant Apoaequorin.
Of twenty-seven synthetic analogues of coelenterazine tested for bioluminescence activity with recombinant apoaequorin, eleven showed activity, but the best activity was obtained with celenterazine incorporated into wild type apoaequorin and into apoaequorin with all three cystein residues replaced by serine.
