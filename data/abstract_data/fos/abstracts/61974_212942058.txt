Nonlinear Dynamics of Extremely Short Solitons of the Generalized Reduced Maxwell–Bloch System
Abstract The effect of remote quantum states on two allocated atomic levels during the propagation of extremely short electromagnetic pulses in a medium is considered without using the slowly varying envelope approximation. A generalization of the reduced Maxwell–Bloch system is obtained that is integrable via the inverse scattering transformation method. Its soliton and breather solutions are found and studied.
