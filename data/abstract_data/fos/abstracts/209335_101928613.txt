Synthesis and reactions of 2, 3-dichlorothianaphthene-1-oxide
Oxidation of 2, 3-dichlorothianaphthene affords 2,3-dichlorothianaphthene-1-oxide. This reacts with methanol, ethanol, and piperidine to give 2-chloro-3-methoxy-, 2-chloro-3-ethoxy-, 2, 3-diethoxy-, and 2-chloro-3-piperidinothianaphthene-1-oxide.
