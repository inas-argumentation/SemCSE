Casting Defects Analysis and Its Improvement for Natural Gas Compressor Cylinder
RDS cylinder block castings are key components of natural gas compressors.Because of their complicated double-layer wall,uneven wall thickness and high bearing pressure performance requirements,the castings were often scrapped due to leakage and other problems caused by casting defects,such as porosity,crack etc.This paper analyzes the casting defects of cylinder block castings and the causes for leading to rejects.After adopting specific measures,the quality of casting has been significantly improved.
