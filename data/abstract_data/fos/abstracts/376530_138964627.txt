Research on Laser Induced Voltage Effect in Polycrystalline YBa_2Cu_3O_(7-x) Bulk
High temperature high quality polycrystalline YBa2Cu3O7-x superconductor bulk was prepared by solid state reaction method.The laser induced voltage(LIV) effect in YBa2Cu3O7-x bulk was investigated for the first time and LIV signal was observed with the angle of 20°.The amplitude voltage of 26.7 mV,the rise time of 30 ns and the full width of half maximum of 56 ns were detected when irradiated by a ultraviolet pulse laser with the energy density of 47 mJ/cm2.The peak voltage depends on and shows a linear relationship with irradiated laser energy density,which indicates that the thermoelectric anisotropy is responsible for voltage signals.The voltage in bulk is much smaller than that in YBa2Cu3O7-x thin film.
