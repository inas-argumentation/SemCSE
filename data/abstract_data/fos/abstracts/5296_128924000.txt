Seminar on the causes and mechanics of glacier surges, St. Hilaire, Canada, September 10–11, 1968: A summary
This summary of papers and discussion makes no real attempt to be comprehensive or complete, instead I have tried to select those contributions which seem to me to be most interesting, new, and significant. This summary is a personal view, and thus it suffers from personal bias and errors of omission and commission. I hope, however, that the more important highlights of this stimulating symposium will at least be identified. The papers clearly are of three main types, depending on whether the emphasis was on observational data, theory, or speculation on the large-scale effects of surges; I have grouped and discussed them accordingly.
