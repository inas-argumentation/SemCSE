Evaluation Analysis of Transformer Substation Radiation on Surrounding Environment
At first, this thesis screened out the indexes to evaluate the radiation that transformer substation has on the surrounding environment. Secondly, it conducted nondimensionalization and made grey relational analysis of the evaluation indexes and results to obtain the judgment matrix. Then, this thesis used fuzzy-analytical hierarchy model to obtain index weight and obtained the main evaluation indexes at last, providing theoretical foundation for the analysis of the radiation that transformer substation has on the surrounding environment.
