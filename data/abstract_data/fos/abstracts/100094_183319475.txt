Establishment of Sunan Soter Database and Its Application on Paddy Soil Productivity Evaluation
According to the methodology and technology of SOTER (Soil and Terrain Database) at a scale of 1∶1 000 000,a digital spatial database and a simple attribute database at a scale of 1∶200 000 was established in the south of Jiangsu province by integrating relief map, geology map and soil map with Arc/info software. Combined with other attribute database, an integrating SOTER Database was accomplished.Based on this database and some software,the evaluation of soil productivity was conducted by AHP method,and the classified map of agricultural soil productivity in Sunan area was drawn automatically by computer,which would be helpful to local government’s macroscopical decision making.
