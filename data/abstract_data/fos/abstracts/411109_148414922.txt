On Faculty Construction of Entrepreneurship Education in Higher Vocational Colleges
Nowadays,higher vocational colleges are actively promoting entrepreneurship education.However,the training for teaching staff is weak.The empirical survey shows college teachers and students share the view that entrepreneurship education plays an important role in student's successful entrepreneurship,but the effectiveness of the entrepreneurship guidance is not high.Therefore,some basic problems,such as lack of full-time faculty,the conflict between entrepreneurship guidance and academic development,loose faculty and lack of capacity for entrepreneurial guidance,hindered the construction of teaching faculty.To advance the development of entrepreneurship education,it should construct a team of full-time and part-time faculty,who has the definite obligation and be encouraged by the incentive policy.
