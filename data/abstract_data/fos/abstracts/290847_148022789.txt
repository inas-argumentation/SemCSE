Research on the Specialty Groups Establishment of Higher Vocational Colleges in the Coordination Perspective of Specialty Groups and Industrial Clusters
The specialty groups establishment and development of higher vocational colleges were influenced by such two factors as the internal driving force from the development demand of higher vocational education,and the external driving force from the external industrial economy environment demand.It's a new approach to establish specialty groups from the coordination perspective of specialty groups and industrial clusters,which could make higher vocational colleges integrate into the regional economy development and run school in an open-door way.Therefore,there're such specialty groups establishment principles as leading the industrial clusters development,serving the regional industrial clusters,arranging the specialty groups connection between different levels of vocational education,and combining the resource and the demand.
