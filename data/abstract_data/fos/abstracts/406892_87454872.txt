Cloning and Bioinformatic Analysis of the Gene HbRPS6-2 from Hevea brasiliensis
A high homologous RPS6 gene sequence was isolated by screening Brazilian rubber tree methylation filtration library.According to its sequences information,a novel full-length RPS6 termed HbRPS6-2 was obtained by rapid amplification of cDNA ends(RACE).The sequence contained 1 327 bp in length with a 483 bp open reading frame(ORF),flanked by a 357 bp 5'-UTR and a 475 bp 3'-UTR.It encoded 161 amino acids.No signal peptide and transmembrane structure were predicted within HbRPS6-2.The result of secondary structure predicted showed that the protein was classified as mixed type.There was a strong hydrophobic region in the N-terminal 18 amino acids of HbRPS6-2,but the entire polypeptide chain was hydrophilic.The result of phylogenetic tree indicated that HbRPS6-2 displayed a high degree of sequence similarity to PaRPS6,and had a low degree of sequence similarity to AtRPS6,ZmRPS6 and so on.
