Heterocyclic analogs of pleiadiene. 63. Synthesis and crystal structure of 1-aminoperimidines
By action of O-picrylhydroxylamine on perimidine and 2-methylperimidine, we have synthesized their 1-amino derivatives. An x-ray diffraction investigated of 1-aminoperimidine showed that the amino group in them is found in a pyramidal configuration with the axis of the unshared electron pair lying in the plane of the heterocyclic ring, oriented toward the μ-carbon atom.
