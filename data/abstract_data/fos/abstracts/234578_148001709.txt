On the Role of Moses Hess' Thoughts as a Bridge between Feuerbach's and Marx's Thoughts
Moses Hess,an important figure in the thought of Marx's philosophy revolution,plays a significant role in the formation process of Marx's historical materialism,in the successive combination of philosophy and economics,and in the promotion of internal displacement of political economics discourse with philosophical discourse.Feuerbach is an outstanding materialism philosopher,the basic core of his materialistic theory is one of the sources of the Marxist theory and its influence on the Marxist theory has been widely recognized in the world.The thoughts of these three people are not in a binary opposition,but a complex relation of seeking commonness while reserving differences,of the latter criticizing,inheriting and surpassing the former.Hess' thoughts form a bridge between Feuerbach's and Marx's thoughts because he criticized,inherited and surpassed Feuerbach's thoughts,and at meantime his thoughts also exerted an important influence on Marx's thoughts.
