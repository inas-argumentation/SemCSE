VACUUM THERMOLYSIS OF ARYL ACETATES. A MECHANISTIC STUDY
p-Tolyl and phenyl trideuterioacetates were thermolyzed in a vacuum. H1 -nmr of the produced p-cresol showed no deuterium incorporation at its ortho position, which disfavoured the 6-membered cyclic mechanism. Pressure-independence of the yield of phenol made the radical chain mechanism unlikely, too. A possible mechanism is proposed.
