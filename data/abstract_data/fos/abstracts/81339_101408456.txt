Study on Removal of Turbidity from Zhoushan High Turbidity Seawater by Coagulation Method
On the basis of investigating the particle size distribution and free settling characteristic of Zhoushan high turbidity seawater,the coagulation and sedimentation experiments were carried out by using FeCl3,PFC or PFS as coagulants,applying orthogonal test method,and the factors affecting the coagulation effect have been studied by range analysis method.The results showed that: silt is main composition of suspended matter in sea area of Zhoushan,which could not be removed only by free settling;the effects of four main factors on turbidity removal rate have been verified as follows: low stirring timelow stirring velocity fast stirring time fast stirring velocity;the best hydrodynamic conditions were that fast stirring time and velocity is 2 min and 300 r/min separately,low stirring time and velocity is 15 min and 60 r/min separately;PFS was the better coagulant,and its optimum dosage was 15～25 mg/L,the removal rate of turbidity exceeded 99% correspondingly.
