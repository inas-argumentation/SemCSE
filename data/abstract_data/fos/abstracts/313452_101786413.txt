Study on the vacuum freeze drying craft of Oviductus Rana
Oviductus rana was selected as the raw material of the study, then vacuum freeze drying method was adopted through the production exploration of freeze drying Oviductus rana, The result of experiments indicate that the economical and optimum quality freeze dring Oviductus rana production could be got under the following conditions: The pressure in drying room was 20Pa;The freezing velocity was 1.50℃/min;The heating temperature was 70~80℃,and got optimum reabsorbing quality 27.39%, protein content was 43.39%
