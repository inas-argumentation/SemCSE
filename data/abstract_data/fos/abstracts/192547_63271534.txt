Research on Knowledge Space Modeling Based on Granular Computation
All-Granular-Space,written as AllGS,and Super-Granular-Space,written as S__GS,of information system are built by use of Granular Computation,and then algebraic system,Boolean Algebra,and lattice are obtained.Taking advantage of the properties of lattice,structural model of AllGS,which is Super-Tree, written as Stree,is also obtained.Realization and design of KDD algorithms can be made easier with some properties of Stree and AllGS.All these work lay the theoretical foundation for further research on mechanism of KDD,and greatly rich the theoretic framework of geometry and algebra of KDD.
