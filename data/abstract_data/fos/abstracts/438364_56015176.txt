Quality of Life and Emotional Intelligence in a Sample of Kuwait University Students.
The Purpose of the study is to Describe of the nature of the relationship between quality of Life and Emotional Intelligence in a sample of Kuwait University students, as well as to identify the differences between males and females in the variables of the study, The study sample consists of 400 students from the University of Kuwait, (200) males and (200) females with average age of (21.38 0.72) years, and the study includes tools of emotional intelligence scale, and scale the quality of life, The findings reveal that the presence of correlated relationship has a positive statistically significant between the dimensions of emotional intelligence and the dimensions of quality of life. It turns out that there are statistical significance differences between males and females in the quality of life and emotional intelligence in favor of the female students. Keywords : Emotional Intelligence, Quality of life
