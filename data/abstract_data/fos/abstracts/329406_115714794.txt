Slow growth expected : talk digits with doc Koos
The key feature of this month's Dairy Digits is the sharp decrease in milk intake of 4,9% from January 2015 to January 2016. Last month's milk intake decreased by 3,5% compared to that of December 2014. This trend will continue and the Milk Producers' Organisation (MPO) estimates that total milk deliveries in 2016 will be at least 3% lower than in 2015. Total production in 2015 was still 5,5% or 163 million litres more than in 2014.
