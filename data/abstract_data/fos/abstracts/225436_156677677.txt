An Analysis of the Evolvement and Developing Tendency of Antidumping Theory
The paper argues that antidumping has passed the stage of maintaining fair trade and stays between the stages of special protection and strategic motive, with the evolving tendency to move towards the latter. It is suggested that our enterprisers shake off outdated conception and strive for their deserved benefits, the government set macro- strategies to support enterprises in information and policy, and the related sides including the government, enterprises and law circles join together for protecting our own interests in international trade.
