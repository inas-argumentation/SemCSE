Research on the Application of Flipped Classroom Model for Computer Programming Courses
The traditional way of teaching computer programming courses are mainly from classroom teaching by teacher, supplemented with students practice after school. It is difficult to understand and digest to first-year university students. They are fear of emotional for programming courses. Flipped classroom is emphasized teaching mode of student-centered,and teachers supplement's teaching mode. Flexible teaching methods, teaching environment is not fixed, can learn anytime and anywhere. Through by depth research to flipped classroom, introduces it that used in computer programming courses.
