Effects of Rumen-bypass Amino Acid on Rumen Fermentation,Milk Production and Components in Dairy Cows
Eight Holstein cows were used to evaluate addition of rumenbypass amino acid (Met+Lys) on the effects of rumen fermentation,milk production,and milk components.Results showed that dry matter intake (DM) of rumenbypass amino acid groups were not significant compared with that of the control groups.The milk yield and 4% FCM of rumenbypass amino acid groups were increased by 595%,and 409% compared with that of the control groups,but there was no significant difference (P＞005).The milk protein percentage were tended to decrease by the addition of rumenbypass amino acid supplement,but milk protein production increase compared with that of control groups (P＞005).The other milk components were not affected by rumenbypass amino acid supplement.The ruminal pH and ruminal concentration of NH3N,Total VFA,proportions of VFA of rumenbypass amino acid groups were not significant compared with that of control groups (P＞005).
