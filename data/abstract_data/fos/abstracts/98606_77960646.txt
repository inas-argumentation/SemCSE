[A data bank of poststroke patients: the factors affecting the efficacy of the rehabilitative process].
The authors provide the conclusions on the role of some clinical and psychological factors in recovery of postapoplectic patients. Most important of them are: early start of the treatment, the severity of the initial motor dysfunction, combination of some motor and higher cortical defects, multiple and bilateral focal lesions of the brain matter after repeat circulation affections, psychopathological disorders. The conclusions were made after the computer statistical processing of "rehabilitation sheets" compiled by the specialists of the neurological hospital department on the basis of formalized case records of 400 postapoplectic subjects. Each sheet in the data bank contained 1,000 clinical, laboratory and experimental-psychological characteristics.
