Research on HG-AFS determination of micro amounts of arsenic in baked food
The HG-AFS method was used for the determination of micro amounts of arsenic in baked food. The sample was digested with HNO3+HCIO4(4+1). Arsenic(Ⅲ) ion in the sample solution reacted with KBH4 to generate AsH3, which was led into the atomizer,and the micro arsenic was determinated by AFS using 5% HCI as acid medium, whereas 10g/L KBH4 together with 2g/L NaOH as reducing agent. Under the analytical conditions proposed in this paper,the limit of detection was 0.03μg/L, the recovery rates were 93.6%~101.3%,linear calibration curve for arsenic was measured within the range of 0~30μg/L, the correlative coefficient was 0.9997 and the RSDs of the applied stardard solution and standard materials were found to be 0.54%~1.77% and 1.27%~5.0% respectively. The method had been used for the determination of Micro Amounts of Arsenic in Baked Food and three standard materials with satisfactory results.
