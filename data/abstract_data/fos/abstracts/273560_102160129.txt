Headspace solid-phase microextraction and gas chromatographymass spectrometry analysis of aroma compounds in mango
Headspace solid phase microextraction combined with GC/MS was developed to analyze aroma components in mango juice,the parameters of SPME were studied.The result showed that the optimal extraction temperature is 45 ℃,absorption time is 30 min,ionic strength is 0.36 g/mL,desorption time is 5 min.This new method have well linearity connection in its measure range(r=0.9953～0.9975),and the relative standard deviations were 2.67%～5.88%.
