Image Matching Method Based on Wavelet Decomposition and Mutual Information
Presents an improved image matching method based on wavelet decomposition and mutual information.Firstly,decomposes the original images into several layers based on wavelet decomposition.Secondly,makes images gray-scale compression in order to reduce the computation parameters of the matching.Finally,the images are matched based on mutual information measure and DHS(Downhill Simplex).Experimental result shows that the image registration method can raise the matching speed under the same guarantee of matching.
