Comparisons of contents of total flavonoids from leaves of Apocynum venetum L. collected in Songnen plain and Yellow river mouth area respectively and their antioxidant ability
Ultrasonic extraction were applied to extract total flavonoids. This study determined the contents of total flavonoids and quercetin from leaves of Apocynum venetum collected in Songnen plain and Yellow river mouth area respectively. Spectrophotometric method has been used to measure total flavonoids, and the content of quercetin determined by HPLC. The antioxidant capacities of the extracts of total flavonoids from leaves of Apocynum venetum towards free radical scavenging were evaluated by using 1,1 -diphenyl -2 - icrylhydrazyl(DPPH) and hydroxyl radicals. The results showed that both leaves of Apocynum venetum collected in Songnen plain and Yellow river mouth area (2.900～4.531 mg/g and 5.23 mg/g respectively) posse- ssed higher amounts of quercetin and the contents of total flavonoids from theirs were of 5.428～8.071 mg/g and 12.110 mg/g respectively. In addition, leaves of of Apocynum venetum wildly growing in Songnen plain showed higher antioxidant activity as compared to that in Yellow river mouth area.
