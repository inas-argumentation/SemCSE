“Doughnut Augmentation”- Ieocaecal Segment with insitu appendix used as A simple but effective Bladder Augment/Substitute in very small Bladders
Purpose To devise a suitable technique of bladder augmentation/ substitution and appendicular mitrofanoff for very small scarred bladders, where implantation of the appendix into the bladder would be difficult Material and Methods The procedure was performed in 12 children, 9 with exstrophy, 2 with Urogenital sinus anomaly and 1 with inadvertently excised bladder during surgery for anorectal malformation. The augmentation/ substitution was performed using the ileocaecal segment and in situ appendix. The appendix was passed cephalad through the mesentry of the terminal ileum and attached to the umbilical area. The ileum was passed behind the appendix such that the ileocaecal segment after tubularization surrounded the appendix in the form of a “doughnut”, which provided effective antireflux mechanism as well as an effective support for the appendix for easy catherization. Results In all twelve cases the appendicular port was easy to catherize and did not leak. The overall RESULTS were very satisfactory though one child continues to leak per urethra. Urodynamic studies confirmed an effective augmentation. Conclusions The “doughnut” augmentation/ substitution is a simple and effective technique for children with small bladders specially in failed exstrophy.
