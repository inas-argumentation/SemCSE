Genetic analysis of PPO activity in the progenies from two wheat crosses
The enzyme polyphenol oxidase(PPO) activity of F3 progenies from wheat crosses Yannong 19×Annong 0016 and Yannong 19×Annong 98017 were measured with the methods of whole-meal and DOPA or catechol as substrate.The results indicated that PPO activity tested by catechol method is highly significant correlated with that by DOPA method and the correlation coeffient is 0.907 in the progenies from Yannong 19×Annong 0016.Therefore,these PPO activity assays were very useful for screening breeding materials and genetic analysis.PPO activity of the two crosses are controlled by two major genes and the heritability are high.The effects of first major genes is higher than that of second ones in cross of Yannong 19×Annong 0016,but for Yannong 19×Annong 98017,the effects of two major genes are equal.
