Influence of the A ion on the K-edge absorption spectra of ATiO3 (A = Ba, Sr, or Ca) crystals with perovskite structure
The near-edge structure of the Ti K-edge absorption spectra was calculated for BaTiO3, SrTiO3, and CaTiO3 crystals by the full multiple scattering method. The results obtained are in agreement with the experimental data. The numerical experiment revealed the mechanisms forming the structure of the main edge of the spectra.
