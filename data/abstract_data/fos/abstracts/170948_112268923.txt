The On-line Treatment of Turbine Lubrication Oil in Service
Following the oil deteriorated seriously,№.2 turbine oil of Huasheng Power Plant was deteriorared again in october this year.The Electric Power Testing Researcg Institute of Hubei Province, accompanying with Puasheng Power Plant,analyzed the fault and treated the deteriorated oil by way of on-line method adopting special absorbent for turbine oil and integral oil treating set,and soon afterwards T501 and T746 were added.The quality of treated oil conforms to national standard and approaches the new oil by testing common items and analyzing infrared spectrum, and the unit is normally in service now.
