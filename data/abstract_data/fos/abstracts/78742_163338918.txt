These few flaws do not reduce the remarkable value of La citta dei crucci. I totally agree with the author when he states that this study is not only a survey of Pistoia, but concerns Florence as well. Indeed, by reading these pages one learns much about the political strife and the structure of power in the capital city. William Connell's book is not only an interesting example of local history, but also a valuable contribution to one of the principal topoi of Italian historiography, state-building in the late Middle Ages.
