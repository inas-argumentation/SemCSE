The Views of College Newspaper Editors on their Readers, Newspapers, and News Priorities
The college newspaper is the primary mass medium of communication on most campuses. However, not enough is known about the characteristics and opinions of the editors. Data gathered from a mail questionnaire sent in the Spring of 1973 to a national, systematic random sample of editors in four-year colleges provided the following results. Editors tended to be white males of Christian heritage, left of center in their ideological persuasion, favorably impressed with the performance and impact of their newspapers, and strongly student-centered in their judgments about their readers and important content for their newspapers. In addition, it was found that editors of larger newspapers had a number of opinion differences from editors of smaller papers.
