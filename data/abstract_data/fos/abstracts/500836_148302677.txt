Analysis of the three major curriculum reforms in China’s universities
The three major curriculum reforms in China’s universities refer to the humanities education curriculum reform, the general education curriculum reform, and the quality education curriculum reform, dominated by academic elite spirits, university, government respectively. Humanities education advocates humanity; general education attaches importance to foundation; quality education pursues comprehensive development of man. For the lack of the humanism affection and the faith of university idea, the three curriculum reforms under the shackle of society-centered and pragmatism have been distorted into the supplement of vocational education and passive mend to the malpractice of education, haven’t led to the thorough transformation of the idea and practice of university curriculum.
