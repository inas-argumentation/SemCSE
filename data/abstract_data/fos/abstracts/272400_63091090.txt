Design and Implementation of Scalable SIP-based Multimedia Conference System
With the development of NGN and 3G IMS applications,SIP-based multimedia conference system has received more and more attention.At present,SIP-based multimedia conference products and related standards are not yet mature.To solve this problem,the standards of SIP-based multimedia conference system proposed by SIPPING and XCON working groups are studied.A SIP-based multimedia conference system is designed and implemented.The System has high scalability in that it is designed in a highly modular manner and different conferencing function is implemented with different module.The experiment results show that conferencing applications can be realized.The conference system complies with IETF standards and has interoperability.
