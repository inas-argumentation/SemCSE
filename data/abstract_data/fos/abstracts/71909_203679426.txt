Correctly Understanding the Logical System of Modern Economics——The Basic Object of Undergraduates’ Curriculum of Microeconomics
Microeconomics is the basis of modern economics. The basic object of curriculum teaching of microeconomics is to help students comprehend the logical system of modern economics. It is argued that, on the premise that correctly understanding the methodology and frame of modern economics, undergraduates' curriculum of microeconomics teaching should suitably arrange the material of the course, present the logical system of modern economics, and teach students to think like an economist.
