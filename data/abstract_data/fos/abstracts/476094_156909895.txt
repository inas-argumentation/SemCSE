On the Farmer's Payment Ability and Demand in the Economically Developed Areas
The common farmer family in the economically developed areas is easy to afford the expenditure of the public university.The expenditure of the private university is high,but most farmers' family can still afford it.More attention should be given to the family whose income is below average.The high demand to the education in the economically developed areas proves that education pays great roles in the human capital accumulation and that,education,as a part of culture capital,is important in the intergeneration transmission of family wealth and status.
