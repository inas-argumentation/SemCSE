Regulative effect of acupuncture on Arcuate nucleus of hypothalamus in type 2 diabetes mellitus
Objective To study the effects of acupuncture on the function of arcuate nucleus(ARC) of type 2 diabetes mellitus(T2DM), and further investigate central nervous functional mechanism of therapeutics by acupuncture.Methods Rats fed with ordinary wholesome rat-feed in the normal group. The successfully established experimental T2DM rats models were randomly divided into control group and acupuncture group. Rats in the acupuncture group were given acupuncture treatment for 28 days, and rats in the normal and control groups were put into fixation-machine for 10 minutes every day, lasting for 28 days. The levels of fasting blood glucose(FPG), fasting insulin(FINS), C-peptide(C-P), insulin sensitivity index(ISI), Homeostasis model assessment of insulin resistance and β-cell function (Homa-IR and Homa-β) as well as the frequency of spontaneous discharge of nerve cell in the arcuate nucleus of hypothalamus and the level of neurotransimitters in the nervous tissue of arcuate nucleus of hypothalamus in T2DM rats were observed with nervous electrophysiological and nervous biochemical technology before and after acupuncture.Results The frequency of spontaneous discharge of nerve cell in ARC of hypothalamus in control group rats [(3.59±0.92)Hz] were all markedly lower than those of the normal group rats [(10.32±2.95)Hz], the differences were significant (P0.01); but acupuncture group rats [(12.17±3.54)Hz] all markedly higher than those of the control group rats, the differences were significant (P0.01). The frequency of spontaneous discharge of nerve cell in ARC was negatively correlated with FPG, FINS, C-P, Homa-IR and positively correlated with ISI and Homa-β.Conclusion The good regulating effect of acupuncture on the function of ARC of hypothalamus might be one of central nerve mechanisms of correcting IR and abnormal metabolism in T2DM rats.
