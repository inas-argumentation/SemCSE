Analysis of Oscillation Frequency Characteristics in Large Area InterConnected Power Grids
Based on two-source system model analysis,this paper proposes the major factors that influence oscillation frequency characteristic variation of inter-regional interconnected system as the total system capacity,sub-capacity ratio,power exchange between interconnected power grids,reactance between the grids,etc.Based on the WAMS measured data,above conclusion is applied in analyzing the frequency variation on Central China-North China 1000kV UHV link line in two years of operation.
