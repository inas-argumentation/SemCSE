Preparation and Structural Characters of Natural Rubber Latex Graft-modified by Organosiloxane
Natural rubber latex modified by polyorganosiloxane was prepared by the method of graft-copolymerization. An attempt was made to study the effect of the coupling agents and dosages, NR latex concentration, dosage of initiator, reaction temperature, reaction time and the organosilixane agents and their dosages to the monomers conversion and monomers grafting percentage in graft copolymerization reaction. An orthogonal experiment was arranged to seek suitable technological conditions through statistical analysis of the available experimental data. The results showed that VD4 had higher monomers conversion and monomers grafting rate than D4 in graft copolymerization reaction. The A-171 was the best coupling agent as against the other agents: KH-550, KH-570 and A-151. The monomers conversion and the grafting rate were increased significantly with an increase in ratio of D4 or VD4/NR, in reaction time and in reaction temperature. The optimum technological conditions observed for modification of NR grafted by organosilixane were: NR latex concentration 50 %, reaction temperature 55 °C, reaction time 4h, the dosage of A-171 1.2 %, the dosage of organosilixane 15 % and the dosage of initiator 1.0 %. The IR proved that polyorganosiloxane was definitely grafted onto NR.
