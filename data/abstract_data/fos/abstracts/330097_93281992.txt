Random ac-networks in the theory of inhomogeneous ionic conductors
We investigate a three-component network to describe electrical properties of composite ionic conductors. A Monte Carlo position-space renormalization method is applied to calculate the conductivity and the dielectric response. Our results suggest that interesting dielectric behaviour should be observable near the onset of interface percolation. We also obtain the universal (complex) scaling function which governs the general critical dynamics in mixtures of a conductor and a dielectric insulator.
