Efficient higher order composite plate theory for general lamination configurations
An efficient higher order plate theory is developed. A composite plate theory for general lamination configurations is obtained by superimposing a cubic varying displacement field on a zig-zag linearly varying displacement. The theory has the same number of dependent unknowns as first-order shear deformation theory, and the number of unknowns is independent of the number of layers. The displacement satisfies transverse shear stress continuity conditions at the interface between layers as well as shear free surface conditions. The analytical solution for cylindrical bending is obtained for comparison with other theories
