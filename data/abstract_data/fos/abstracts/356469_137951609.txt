Effects of Electromagnetic Centrifugal Casting on Properties of High Chromium Cast Iron
In order to improve the mechanical properties of the high chromium cast iron,electromagnetic centrifugal casting method of the casting specimen was carried out experimentally to compare the conventional casting.The results show that after electromagnetic centrifugal casting,samples structure is refined,grains quantity increase;the grain size get smaller;mechanical properties increase.When the centrifugal speed is 1500r/min,magnetic induction of 0.5T,the electromagnetic centrifugal casting hardness of cast specimens is 55.3HRC,the hardness of 65.3HRC after heat treatment.Hardness increases by 10% than conventional cast samples,relative wear resistance β of cast sample is improved 5% than that of the conventional methods treated.
