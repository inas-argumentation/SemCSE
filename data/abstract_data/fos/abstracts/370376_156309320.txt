Experiential Education:an Effective Way to Improve Poor Students' Quality
Experiential education,creating situations and emphasizing "learning from doing",is one of the effective ways to improve the quality of poor students.Under the innovation situation of ideological and political education,to improve the quality of poor students,by summing up the quality status of poor students,the paper analyzes the effectiveness of experiential education,the principles of design and the key points of implementation targeted,and provides reference for long-term mechanism of impoverished university students.
