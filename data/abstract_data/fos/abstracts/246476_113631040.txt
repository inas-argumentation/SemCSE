Linear Wave Spectrum and Numerical Simulation Study Based On Radar Wave Information
The information of wave radar has high reliability and resolution characteristics. Process the, and then regard it as a target spectrum to simulate, we can get the sea waves closed to the actual. The simulation results are in good agreement with the theoretical value. Based on these, ship motion simulation studies will be done, which will help the driver to improve the safety of navigation. Introduction The waves have great effect on ship navigation and maritime operations. The previous studies are based on statistical wave spectrum, which has great difference with actual marine environment, and the forecast results cannot be used for navigation practice. Ship-borne radar wave measuring technology, emerging as a marine environmental monitoring method in recent years, provides real-time wave information [1] and a feasible way for the driver in determining the optimal course and speed and guarantee the safety of navigation. At present, study of measurement of radar wave mainly concentrated on the compare of measurement of wave radar and buoy observations [2], measured wave radar parameter adjustment and application analysis of measured wave radar information extraction software and hardware environment [3-4]. We believe that to be used for the study of navigation, we must firstly calculate the absolute spectrum according to the relative wave spectrum, and then provide a basis to ocean wave numerical simulation. The numerical waves can describe close to the actual wave, and the motions of ship model under such environmental conditions can reflect the real ship motion and dynamic response in real sea thus offer a feasible technical way for the prediction of navigation safety. Acquisition and Preprocessing of Radar Wave Measurement Information We collected the radar wave measurement information of the WaMoSII system on the ships: "Yuan Wang", "Yu Kun" and "ocean", including wave information of the Pacific, the South China Sea and the Indian ocean.
