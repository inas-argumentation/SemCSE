Study on Extruding Process of Magnesium Alloy Radiator
Extruding experiments for magnesium alloy radiator were performed using different extrude temperature.The results showed that AZ31B annealed at 400 ℃ for 20 h,extruding temperature 400 ℃,extruding velocity 1.0～2.5 m/min,AZ31B magnesium alloy has good thermo-extruding property and radiators with complex section can be obtained.
