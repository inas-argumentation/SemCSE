Comparative study the Elsholtzia bodineri Vaniot.'s quality in Jianshui and Shiping
This paper detected the polyphenol and flavones in tea of Elsholtzia bodineri Vaniot.to appraise it quality.The Spectrophotometry method was used in experiment,the results is that the content of polyphenol is 14.8 mg/g in Jianshui's Elsholtzia bodineri Vaniot.,which was higher than Shiping.The content of total flavones in the tea is 52.8 mg/g in Shiping,43.9 mg/g in Jianshui.The sum of the content of polyphenol and flavones in the tea is Shiping higher than Jianshui,so the quality of the Elsholtzia bodineri Vaniot.is Shiping's good.The result is one of the base for the Elsholtzia bodineri Vaniot.'s quality appraisement.
