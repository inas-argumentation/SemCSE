Research on Left-Handed Material Surface Wave Characteristics
Against the characteristic of electromagnetic wave propagating in left-handed material, researches on surface wave producing condition, draws wave vector equation of TE, TM polarization surface wave. Makes use of Maxwell equation and gets surface wave dispersive characteristics, energy flow characteristics. Complementary analysis showes that non-media interface can not transmit TE surface wave and TM surface wave, in the media on both sides of the interface in the opposite direction of the energy flow.
