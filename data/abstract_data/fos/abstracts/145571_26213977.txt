CLOSE IDENTIFICATION IN TWINS DISCORDANT FOR OBSESSIONAL NEUROSIS.
Case histories of two pairs of monozygotic twins are detailed, in which marked identification was a feature. In both instances only one of the twins developed a severe obsessional illness. This finding is presented as a significant item of evidence against the theory that the high concordance rate for psychiatric disorder in monozygotic twins can be attributed to mutual identification. The lack of precision in the literature in defining and assessing identification is mentioned.
