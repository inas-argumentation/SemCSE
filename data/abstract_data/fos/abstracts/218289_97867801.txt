Darstellung, Eigenschaften und Anwendungen von stabilen Polymeren mit ungepaarten Elektronen [Synthesis, properties and applications of stable polyradicals]
There are three known types of stable polymers with unpaired electrons: neutral polyradicals, polyradical ions and polymeric eh arge transfer complexes. The preparation, properties and reactions of the most important stable polyradieals are described and their e.s.r. spectra are discussed. By spin-labelling of polymers conclusions on their mobility and the chain entanglement can be drawn. Polyradical ions can be used as initiators for graft copolymerizations.
