Developments in functionalization of macrocyclic polyamines
The classical structure of macrocyclic polyamines (- cyclam 1) have been renovated by means of (1) conversion of amines into amides (& dioxocyclam); (2) replacement of N donors for S donors; (3) replacement of skeletonic C-H for C- F; and (4) attachment of intramolecular pendant donors. The characteristics of dioxocyclam 2 is best illustrated by its ability to incorporate Pt", while the 0x0-free cyclam 1 fails to do so. A new version of dioxocyclam 3 containing two S donors in place of N donors of 2 was designed, which aston- ishingly selectively accommodates noble metal ions Pt" and Pd", but not Cu", Ni", or Co" as 2 does. Furthermore, 3 detracts Pt" much more rapidly than 2 from cis- (Pt" (NH3 ) 2C12 ). The fluorinated polyamines show lower N basicities than nonf luorinated counterparts, and yet, the Cu" complex of 21c (CUH-~L) is more stable than that of 2. The fluorinated polyamine complexes stabilize metal ions with lower oxidation states than nonfluorinated counterparts. Intramolecular pendant donors greatly affect the redox and other chemical properties of the central metal ions, as well as the complex properties.
