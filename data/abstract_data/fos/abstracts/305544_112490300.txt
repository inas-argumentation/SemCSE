Numerical Simulation of Finite Element Based on Sheet Metal Stamping
Taking the forming process of a cylindraceous workpiece as an example, using finite element software MSC.Marc, the numerical simulation mode1 was established.The stress and strain distribution, frictional distribution and the relationship between friction coefficient and thickness of plate were analyzed.The result shows that the numerical simulation results accord with the actual circumstance, which means that using the finite element software MSC.Marc may simulate the process of sheet metal stamping correctly, also greatly increase calculation speed and calculation accuracy.The software has an important value for the engineering application of CAE technology.
