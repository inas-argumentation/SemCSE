The use of shape-selective stationary phases in GC. Part 1: Cyclodextrin phases
Cyclodextrin phases are one of the most inter- esting geometry selective stationary phase in GC. 'beyare outstanding in their ability to separate isomeric and enantiomeric compounds. Two examples of the separation of cyclic compounds on cyclodextrins are given. The combin- ation of chiral and achiral phases in series coupled columns is a useful tool for complicate separation and identification problems.
