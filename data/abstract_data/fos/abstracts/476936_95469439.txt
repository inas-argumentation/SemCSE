Thermogravimetric and microstructural studies on YBa2Cu3−xFexOy
Thermogravimetric and microstructural investigations on Fe-doped YBa 2 Cu 3 O 7 samples have been carried out, to study the accommodation of compositional variations as a function of the iron content. The results obtained allow us to conclude that the iron substitution is not isomorphous, the local anionic environment of the substituted positions being modified. The samples appear to be formed by disordered intergrowths between YBa 2 Cu 3 O 7 and perovskite-type blocks.
