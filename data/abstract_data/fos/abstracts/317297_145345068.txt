It is not enough to assert the pervasiveness of millennialism. That it was present is beyond doubt, but the extent to which it was an engine of the insurgency remains unclear. Moreover, the evidence for millennialism is much stronger for Upper Peru than for the northern movement. Nevertheless, scholars and students will greatly profit from the author’s discussion on this theme, the related “semiotics of rebellion,” and what he calls its “antinomies,” this last a shorthand to frame the abundant contradictions in the evidence.
