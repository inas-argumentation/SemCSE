The Change and Steadiness of the Shared Governance Mode in American Research Universities
University governance is a process of decision making on the major concerns of the stakeholders.The success of American research universities are benefited from their unique shared governance mode.Under the influences of the new managerialism and academic capitalism,the shared governance mode in American research universities is also changing which mainly presents the"changed"and"unchanged"gradual process that centers on the mission with the market mind on the basis of showing public reason.To be specific,highlighting the"public reason"eternal ideas through defining the"moral obligation"of the stakeholders,resolving the negative effects of conflict of interest by creating mutual trust"cooperation"organizational culture,responding to the market demand through establishing the"service"based on the university autonomy,all of which make the university governance effective in the end.
