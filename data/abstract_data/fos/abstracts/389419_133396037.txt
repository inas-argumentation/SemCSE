Analysis of Time and Space Distribution about Main Roads Noise in Chengdu
In recent years,with the rapid growth of vehicle population,traffic noise is becoming increasingly serious.The measurement of the traffic noise generally requires the instrument be fixed,which however makes it not cost-effective in monitoring at a large scale.Thus,the paper is aiming to evaluate the possibility of mobile measurement of sound level for monitoring traffic noise.Real-world experiments were designed and conducted in Chengdu using a conventional sound level meter.The measurements were also used for temporal-spatial analysis of traffic noise.Results showed that mobile measurement of sound level for traffic noise is plausible.The level of traffic noise varies by locations and road classes.For example,the sound level along the 2nd Ring Road is relatively high as 75 dB(A)for all three measuring time periods since the 2nd Ring Road is the center of the business centre of Chengdu.The 3rd Ring Road has higher sound level due to a higher speed limit and heavier traffic flow compared to other roads.
