Effects of bacteria-contaminated boar semen on the reproductive performance
Three types of bacteria (E. coli, Staphylococcus sp. and Pseudomonas sp.) in boar semen were introduced into the uterus with fresh semen to observe their effects on the endometrium and conception of the introduced sows at estrus and luteal phase.No bacteria were detected from the uteri of all sows when highly contaminated semen with bacteria were inseminated at estrus. The endometrial morphology of these sows appeared to be normal. The conception rate, number of embryos or fetuses in the uterus and the litter size at parturition were within normal ranges. On the other hand, a large number of bacteria were isolated from the uteri when highly contaminated semen with bacteria introduced during the luteal phase. Their uteri showed edema or pyometra. However, the inflammatory changes disappeared until the subsequent estrus.
