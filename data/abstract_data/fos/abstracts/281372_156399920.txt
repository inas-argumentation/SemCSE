Historical Evolution and Harmonious Development of the Relationship between Trade and Environment
The phases of"Trade = Economic Development","Trade = Economic Development+ Industry Pollution Control","Development = Trade Economy + Environment Protection"and that of "Interactive Development between Environment and Trade"formed historical evolution route for comprehensively harmonious development between trade and environment,in which some disharmony between ecological environment and trade development occurred both home and abroad according to some cases about their relationship.And how to deal with that relation exerts great influences on the long-term development of the country as well as the related areas.Thus,in terms of the dual character of their relation,the efficient developing ways should be focused on in studying the harmony of trade and environment.
