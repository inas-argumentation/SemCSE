Developments of Hot Simmering Bending X80 Pipes for Second West to East Gas Pipeline
According to requirements of the project to the X80 hot simmer elbow for the second west to east gas pipeline,the different Nb steel,Mo steel and the trunk pipe were developed by the heat simmer trial.After analyzing the test data,the mother tube was developed used to simmer elbow,and by optimizing the process parameters of the hot simmer,the development of the X80 hot simmer elbow for the second west to east gas pipeline was completed.
