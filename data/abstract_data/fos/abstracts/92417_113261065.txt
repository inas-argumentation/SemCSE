Study on Digital Waveform Control of CO_2 Welding in Short-circuit Process
CO2 welding inverter with waveform control was expounded,in which the current waveform control scheme and the structure of the system were introduced.According to the character of short-circuit transfer,the welding current was accurately controlled by this system,and the real welding voltage and current waveform was given.The result shows that,besides its simple structure and easy operation,the system has good controlling performance.
