Policy burden,adverse selection and soft budget constraint of HEIs
Policy burden that HEIs(higher education institutions) bear lead to the soft budget constraint(SBC) and the excessive loans of HEIs.Since information asymmetry and incentive are incompatible,policy burdens will lead to the adverse selection of university presidents,and hence the excessive loans and low efficiency.When HEIs bear policy burdens,government's restriction of the loan autonomy of HEIs usually is a suboptimal systematic arrangement.The key to hardening budget constraint of HEIs is to deprive them of the policy burden.
