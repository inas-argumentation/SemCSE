Synthesis and Unique Packing Structure by X-Ray Analysis of New Types of Stable Trithioles Bound to Fused Aromatic Systems
New types of stable trithioles bounds to fused aromatic systems have been synthesized and one of them, phenanthro[9,10-d][1,2,3]trithiole, has been characterized by X-ray crystallographic analysis, which has a unique helical packing arrangement.
