Effects of Mixing Treatment with High-low Temperature Melt on Solidification Microstructure of Hypereutectic Al-Si Alloy
The effect of melt mixing temperature and holding time on solidification microstructure of hypereutectic Al-20% Si alloy was investigated by melt mixing treatment with high-low temperature. During the process of melt mixing treatment, high temperature melt with low content silicon was poured into low temperature melt with high content silicon. The results show that hypereutectic Al-20%Si alloy possesses microstructure characteristic of hypoeutectic and hypereutectic Al-Si alloy and primary Si and α(Al) solid solution are meanwhile refined after melt mixing treatment.
