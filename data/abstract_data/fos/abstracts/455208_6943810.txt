Single particle properties of Λ hypernuclei in the density dependent relativistic hadron field theory
The density dependent relativistic hadron field theory is used to describe single particle properties of Λ hypernuclei. The discussion focuses on the spin-orbit systematics in the relativistic mean-field formalism by discussing general effects of the Λ continuum threshold and the delocalization of the Λ wave function. Theoretical predictions for the hypernuclear Auger effect are presented.
