Temperature variation of the “Kuroshio” and crustal movements in Eastern and Southeastern Asia 7,000
Abstract In eastern Asia, the temperatures of the “Kuroshio” (Japan Current) 7,000 years ago were, on average, 6°C above their present values. The changes of the Kuroshio can be correlated with crustal movements that occurred 7,000–5,000 years ago, and caused uneven uplift of marine terraces in eastern Asia.
