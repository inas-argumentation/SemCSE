Simply outstanding : new products
The recent DuPont Qualite Awards and South African Cheese Festival highlighted the various quality dairy products available to local consumers. Consumers should invest in a healthy diet by adding dairy to their shopping list as dairy products contain many nutrients with relatively few calories. Among these is iodine, a nutrient the body uses to regulate its metabolism. Research has shown that dairy products can aid in weight loss, particularly helping consumers to lose weight around the midriff.
