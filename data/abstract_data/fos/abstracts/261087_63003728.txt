Routing Optimization Algorithm for QoS Anycast Flows Based on Hybrid Strategy
In order to resolve the problem of Cross Service on QoS Anycast flow, a global routing optimization based on hybrid strategy is proposed after this paper presents the analysis of the causing process of the problem and its waste on resources of networks. Under constructing the question model of routing optimization, the expression is obtained about the QoS constraint and the optimal objective such as end-to-end delay, server load, networks traffic and routes adjustment. The hybrid algorithm is formed by the organic combine of GA, SA and TS. Then it is demonstrated that the important steps and process of this algorithm solution. Simulation results show that this algorithm can implement Anycast routing combinatorial optimization under QoS constraint and has a stronger stability and higher accuracy in contrast to the solution algorithms based on GA or SA.
