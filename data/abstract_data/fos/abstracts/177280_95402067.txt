Interesting Solvent Area in Crystal Structures of Two Natural Ergot Alkaloids
The structures of ergotamine bis(benzene) solvate ( 1 ) and ergocristine bis(benzene) solvate ( 2 ) are reported. Both structures crystallise in the P 2 1 2 1 2 1 space group with cell parameters: 1 , a = 14.2968(3) A, b = 15.4700(2) A, c = 17.8123(4) A, and V = 3939.57(13) A 3 ; 2 , a = 11.8358(2) A, b = 17.6469(3) A, c = 19.7125(3) A, and V = 4117.25(12) A 3 . Unexpectedly, despite the chemical similarity, structures of 1 and 2 significantly differ not only in the unit cell parameters, but also in the packing. Whereas in 1 solvent cavities are separated, there is only one unusual continuous solvent area in 2 filled with benzene, forming independent three-dimensional structure.
