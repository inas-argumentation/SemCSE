On the New Way of Task-Based Teaching Method Focused on the Topic
According to the new curriculum standard requirement, this article tries to explore the effective way to carry out the task-based teaching method, using "the topics" in the teaching material to promote the design, operation and finishing of the tasks in the English classroom instruction. This article also tries to analyze and discuss the feasibility and the experimental effect of the research on "the topics", as well as stating the questions appearing in the experiment of improving the task-based English teaching method by using "the topics" in hope of performing some assistant function to the present high school English classroom instruction reform.
