A LONG-RANGE ISOTOPE EFFECT ON A HFSC OF THE BICYCLO[2.1.1]HEXAN-5-YL RADICAL
The hyperfine splitting constant (hfsc) of H6endo of the bicyclo[2.1.1]hexan-5-yl radical increased from 28.22 G to 28.80 G upon the replacement of H5 with a deuterium atom, which is qualitatively explained, with the aid of INDO calculations, as due to the decrease of the out-of-plane mode vibration of the C5–H5 bond upon the replacement.
