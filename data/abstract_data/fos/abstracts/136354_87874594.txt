Preparation of Red Bean and Konjac Porridge with Nutrition and Health Function
This paper studies the processing technology of red bean and konjac porridge,the production formula was established by orthogonal trials and judgment of the sensory organ.The optimum production formula was determined as follows:amount of the red bean 44g,amount of the konjac 21g,amount of the sugar 32g, amount of the additives 0.35g. The optimum rate of additive was determined as follows:Xanthan gum to Arabic gum to CMC-Na to Sugar ester was 5∶3∶2∶6.
