SUBSTANTIAL EQUILIBRIUM SHIFT IN RACEMIC [Cr(ox)2(phen)]− ATTAINED BY THE PFEIFFER EFFECT WITH QUININE OR QUINIDINE HYDROCHLORIDE AS AN ENVIRONMENT SUBSTANCE IN WATER
The chiral equilibrium of [Cr(ox)2(phen)]− was found to be displaced greatly (ca. 18% O.Y.) in water toward its (+)546- or (−)546- enantiomer upon the addition of l-(8S,9R)-quinine or d-(8R,9S)-quinidine hydrochloride, respectively, which was attributed to the combined action of electrostatic and hydrophobic (or π-π) interactions between the complex and l-quininium or d-quinidinium cation.
