Pentaerythritderivate, II. Neuartige cyclische Nebenprodukte aus Pentaerythritablaugen
Derivatives of Pentaerythritol, II1). — Novel Cyclic By-products from Waste Liquors of Pentaerythritol Production 
 
 
 
The isolation of two novel pentaerythritol derivates is described. The structures were determined by 1H-NMR and 13C-NMR spectrometry.
