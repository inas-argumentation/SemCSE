Study on Paste Induction Boronizing Process of Q345B Steel
How to enhance the boronizing velocity of Q345B steel with induction boriding was studied.This experiment was based on single factor experiment method,and the influences of time and power of super audio induction boriding on the thickness of boronization layer were determined respectively.The microstructure of boronization layer and its hardness analysis were also provided.In the given experimental condition,the results show that the best process plan is induction heating power 4377.5 W(oscillating current 40 A),effective induction heating time 9 min,the biggest boronization layer thickness is up to 33μm,and boronization layer microhardness is 1400-2000 HV.
