On the Quality Requirements for Judges of the Grass-roots Court in the Rural Judicial Environment
The quality of the judges in the people’s court in villages and towns is decisive in the legal effect and social effect of judicature in rural area. Since the legal awareness and the ability to protect rights of the farmers is commonly low and the disputes in rural area are usually peculiar, the quality requirements for grass-roots unit judges should focus on different aspects compared with those of the professionalized judges, so as to make sure that the grass-roots judges can adapt to the judiciary work in rural areas much better. It is argued that the qualities essential for grassroots judges include the political quality of serving farmers, the humanity quality of having a good knowledge of customs and feelings, the professional quality of being good at civil law, and the professional quality of being expert in mediating and so on.
