A Study on Speech Errors of Chinese Non-English-Major College Students
On the theoretical basis of error analysis and interlanguage, this thesis designs an experiment and analyzes the errors made by the student test-takers so as to find about the main types and distribution of linguistic errors in oral English committed by non-English-major college students. Two speaking tasks of telling a story based on pictures and telling a story on a topic are assigned to 60 students (20 First-year undergraduates; 20 third-year undergraduates; 20 first-year postgraduates). It discusses the distribution of various types of errors and their distribution in three grades of students and two tasks. It would also trace the sources of the errors and provide some pedagogical suggestions for the prevention of errors in spoken English.
