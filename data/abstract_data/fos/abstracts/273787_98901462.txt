Effects of Application of Potassium Fertilizer Before Harvest on Storage Quality of Pineapple Fruit
In order to improve the storage quality of pineapple fruit,the effects of the application of potassium sulfate and potassium chloride before harvest on the content of total soluble solid,soluble sugar,titratable acid,vitamin C and soluble protein of storage pineapple(Ananas comosus cv.Comtede Paris)fruit in drip irrigation were studied.The results showed that an appropriate amount of potassium sulfate application before harvest could increase the content of soluble sugar,soluble protein,titratable acid and vitamin C in harvest.But it slowed down the reduction of the content of soluble solid,vitamin C and titratable acid and improved soluble protein content of storage fruits.However,potassium chloride supply before harvest reduced soluble solid content in harvest remarkably and had no effect on vitamin C content of storage fruits.Therefore,900 kg/hm2 potassium sulfate application before harvest could effectively improve the storage quality of pineapple fruits.
