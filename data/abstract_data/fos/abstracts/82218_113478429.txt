Design and Simulation of a New Short-circuit Current Limiter with Transformer as Core-construct
Short-circuit current will be greater and greater with power grid capacity increasing.Researching applied and reliable Short-circuit current limiter can resolve many problems because of fault current.This paper analyzes several current limiter schemes and puts forward a new current limiter,in which hard-core is consist of transformer.The current limiter consists of transformer,GTO and capacitor.Using Matlab to Simulate and select the parameters,the result shows that the limiter can reduce fault current from 13kA to lower 5kA.Reduction ratio is 70% and there is no hyper-voltage in limiting process.All these explain that this limiter has advantages such as simple structure,convenient control and easy implement.This limiter is a developing direction of current limiter.
