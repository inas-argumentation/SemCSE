Printing Industry and Stationery Ma nufacturing Industry in the Huaihe River Basin During the Song Dynasty
The Printing industry and stationery manufacturing industry were a important handicraft industry branch in the Huai-he River Valley during the Song Dynasty,and it was also a bright spot in the history of economic development in t he Huaihe River Basin.This Paper,from an angle of regional economic history and historical geography,discusses the developed traces and characteristics of printing industry and stationery manufacturing industry in the Huaihe River Valley during the Song Dunasty.
