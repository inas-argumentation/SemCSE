The Infrared Spectra of Triplet 4-Phenylbenzophenone
Microsecond time-resolved IR (TRIR) spectroscopy has been used to record the infrared spectra of the ππ* triplet states of 4-phenylbenzophenone and its 18O and 13C analogues. There is a definite shift in the frequency to lower wavenumber of the ν(CO) vibration. The intensity of the C-O vibration is similar in the ground and excited state. This is the first report of the infrared spectra of the excited triplet state of aromatic carbonyl compounds in room temperature solution.
