A comparative study on LAI of different urban forest communities in ring parks of Hefei city.
The ring park is a typical urban forest in Hefei city,WinSCANOPY instrument for canopy analysis was employed in the paper to measure LAI of urban forest stands with different vertical structure and species composition in the park.The result show that the average LAI of the park is 1.95.The four landscape spots of the park totally have 1.217 million m2 of leaf area,but the contribution rate of leaf area that 4 spots have separately is not close match to land proportion they occupied,which indicated that LAI of urban forest stand is interrelated with stand structure and species composition;some stands composed of canopy species such as Ginkgo biloba,Cedrus deodara,Sapium sebiferum and Pterocarya stenoptera that relatively have higher value of LAI;urban forest stands with conifer canopy species for either single vertical layer structure or poly-layer one have more LAI compared to stands with broadleaf species only.Effect of urban forest is correlated with LAI.It is logically to use LAI as an important index for urban forest assessment,so as to make area proportion of green land match their contribution rate of leaf area,and make full use of arban forest.
