Universality for generalized Euler products
In the paper, following ideas of Bohr and Bagchi, we present a new equivalent formulation of the Riemann hypothesis for the Riemann zeta-function ζ(s) in terms of approximation properties of certain Euler products (different from the Euler product representation for ζ(s)). This is established by a combination of Reich's universality theorem and results on the zero-distribution of certain Beurling zeta-functions.
