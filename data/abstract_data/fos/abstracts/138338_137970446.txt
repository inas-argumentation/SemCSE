Research on the Microstructure Transformation of SiC_p/Al Composite Based on Powder Metallurgy in Hot Plastic Forming
The law of the microstructure transformation of SiC p/Al composite based on powder metallurgy in hot plastic forming is investigated.The experimental results indicate that the plastic deformation of the composite is realized by the aluminium alloy matrix's plastic deformation.When the matrix is deforming,there is an interaction between the reinforcers and the matrix.On one hand,the interaction makes the reinforcers adjust their position by returning and become oriented in the direction of the matrix's deformation.On the other hand,the interaction makes the matrix's deformation restricted by the reinforcers,which causes the rate of the matrix's length to breadth different when the density of the reinforcers is different.Therefore,the reinforcers' distribution is found to be more homogeneous after the large deformation of the composite.
