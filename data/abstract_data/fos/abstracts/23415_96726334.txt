Modification de la nature de la conductivite electrique par creation de sites vacants dans les phases a caractere semi-conducteur du systeme Li2SSb2S3
In the system Li2S Sb2S3, correlation between crystal structure determinations and physical properties (electrical conductivity, optical transmission and ionic transport number) has shown, for the solid solution Li3xSb6−xS9, a different electrical behaviour whenx = 0.5 and 0.33. In both cases, crystal structure arrangement is close to Sb2S3 one, but if for x = 0.5 the electrical conductivity is electronic like in Sb2S3, for x = 0.33 it is mainly ionic. When the Li2S concentration is higher, Li3SbS3, the electrical conductivity becomes purely ionic in agreement with an iono-covalent arrangement quite different of Sb2S3 one.
