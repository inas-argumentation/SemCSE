SOM and Immune Clonal Selection Based Clustering Routing Scheme for Wireless Sensor Networks
This paper propose a novel clustering routing solution SICR,based on both SOM and immunity clone selection algorithm.During clustering phase,SICR takes key factors such as node density,residual energy and distance to the sink into account.Similar to self-mapping theorem,SICR adopts a novel algorithm of competition for cluster head to construct energy consumption-balanced structure.This structure can be effectively applied to heterogeneous energy scenario.Meanwhile,in order to reduce the reconstruction cost,self-adaptive mechanism has been introduced to maintenance phase: cluster head estimates current stability of the cluster based on each node′s residual energy and then adjusts cluster size accordingly to maintain the stability.Routing scheme can be divided into intra-cluster and inter-cluster communication: intra-cluster communication should be done based on topology inside the cluster while inter-cluster communication should be done based minimum spanning tree,constructed by clone-selection algorithm.Simulation shows that,compared with existing algorithms,SICR can lead to more balanced energy consumption between nodes and prolonged network life.
