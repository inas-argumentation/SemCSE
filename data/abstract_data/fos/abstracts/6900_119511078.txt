Kinetic coefficients for the active medium of a CO2 laser pumped by an ac electric field
A numerical solution of the Boltzmann equation for the electron distribution function is used to investigate the efficiency of pumping of the active medium in a CO2 laser by rf and microwave electric fields over a wide range of frequencies ω/p and electric fields E/N. The results are presented as a function of the scaling parameters E0/N, ω/p or E0/ω, and their range of validity is defined in terms of ω/p.
