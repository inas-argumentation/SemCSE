A Study of Several Psychometric Tests as a Basis for Guidance on the Junior High School Level
The general interest at the present time in the educational and vocational guidance of public school pupils led the writer of this study to undertake a testing program in the Junior High School of Mor gantown, West Virginia. It was thought that if we could have an evalua tion of each pupil's mental ability, the level of his present achievement in the fundamental studies of the school curriculum, some idea of his aptitudes in the several lines of work in which a large per centage of the adult population engages, and a pic ture of his personality and social adjustment, such data would serve as a basis for guidance. With the cooperation of the Principal of the school and the County Superintendent of Schools, a plan of testing was carried out during the sec ond semester of 1947-48. The tests were given to the seventh and eighth grades, but this report deals only with the eighth grade.
