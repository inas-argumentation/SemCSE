Voltammetry with an Electrode Surface Successively Renewed by Laser-Ablation
Voltammetric measurement was performed with a solid electrode surface which was renewed every several seconds by laser-ablation. The voltammograms obtained were, in appearance, quite similar to polarograms obtained with a dropping mercury electrode (DME). Oxidation waves of [Fe(CN)6]4− and L-ascorbic acid were examined by this voltammetry.
