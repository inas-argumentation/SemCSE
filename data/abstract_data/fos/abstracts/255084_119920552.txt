LETTERS TO THE EDITOR: New low-threshold noncentrosymmetric LaBGeO5:Nd3+ laser crystal
A new noncentrosymmetric LaBGeO5:Nd3+ laser crystal was produced. It has a low threshold for stimulated emission as a result of two lasing transitions: 4F3/2→4I11/2 and 4F3/2→4I13/2. The spectroscopic properties of this crystal are reported.
