The International conference on Advanced Materials Science and Engineering – Award Materials Congress 2020
The award to the experts who have made outstanding contributions to advancing in Materials Science and Nano Technology. It is the foremost prestigious award of the total conference and is labelled as scientific service action award. The eligibility criteria for achieving this award is that one should have minimum of 20+ years of experience in the Material Engineering, Nano Technology, Polymer Engineering, Bio Materials Green Technologies in public or private sector. The receiver of this award should have a dedicated fascination and should take initiative in researching the recent trends and developments towards the related subjects.
