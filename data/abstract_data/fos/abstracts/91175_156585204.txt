Research and policy analysis of state loan for college students
The state loan to college students as a public governance structure led by the government and established through the cooperation of the state, the banks, the institutions of higher education and the students and thus, based on the prior studies on the behaviors of these stakeholders, looking into the interactive relations among them is more possible to find a way towards the breakthrough of the stalemate, particularly, a way for the involved bear some kind of risk based on the reasonable benefit from the state loan.
