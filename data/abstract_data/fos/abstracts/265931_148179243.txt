The Interior Entrepreneur and His Characteristics:Inspirations for Entrepreneurial Education of Our Universities and Colleges
Innovative and entrepreneurial activity is the important power for the sustainable development in the existing enterprise,which comes from the interior entrepreneur. The interior entrepreneur has such characteristics,such as the innovative spirit,independent work and sustainable learning ability,and corresponding technical expertise,which offers a new idea for cultivating entrepreneurial education in universities and colleges.For entrepreneurial education in universities and colleges,it should change the situation which focus on training students' entrepreneurial skills,improve their ability for independent learning and lifelong learning,cultivate the entrepreneurial talents with professional skills,and encourage them to carry out entrepreneurial activity in the enterprise,finally becoming indispensable important force for the enterprise.
