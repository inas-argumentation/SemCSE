From the Bauhaus Re-Understanding the Relationship Between Art and Design
Established in Germany in 1919, Bauhaus design institute was the first institute in the world established to completely develop the design education, which carefully re-examined art and design relations in the teaching. Through the Bauhaus explored in design educations, we understand that art and design certainly are not each other separated, but are mutually connected. Design is a product of the combination of art and technology, which absorbs aesthetics elements from art and moves forward under the artistic impetus.
