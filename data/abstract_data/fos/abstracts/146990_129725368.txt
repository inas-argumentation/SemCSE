FORAMINIFERA OF TWO SUBMARINE CORES FROM THE NORTH ATLANTIC BASIN
The Foraminifera of two submarine cores from the basin of the western North Atlantic have been examined. Vertical faunal variations suggest two sub-Arctic faunas separated by a warm-temperature fauna, and these are beneath the present warm-temperate fauna. The Arctic faunas are interpreted as representing the two latesst ubstages of the Wisconsin. A post-Wisconsin fauna in one core suggests warmer surface waters than at present. Continuous sampling and quantitative study of the faunas are the most feasible methods for study of core faunas. A method of studying sizes of Foraminifera is described, and a possible correlation between sizes and ecologic factors is indicated.
