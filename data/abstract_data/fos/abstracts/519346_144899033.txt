In recent literature, Berting (1999), Hinitz and Stomfay-Stitz (1999), and Fennes and Hapgood (1997) have suggested that intercultural understanding and communication can be one of the bases for peace education within or between nations. However, in divided societies experiencing severe conflicts, developing multicultural education through social studies curricula is not an easy task. In this article, I discuss the challenges and problems of developing social studies curricula aimed at promoting intercultural understanding in Israel.
