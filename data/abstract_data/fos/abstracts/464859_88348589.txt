Preparation of antioxidant peptide from decapterus maruadsi protein by enzymatic hydrolysis
The enzymatic hydrolysis parameters for preparation of antioxidant peptide from Decapterus maruadsi protein were optimized by response surface analysis.Based on the results of single factor tests,reducing power and scavenging activity on hydroxyl free radical were chosen as the response values,the three responses,the influences of hydrolysis time,temperature,and water-material ratio for enzymatic hydrolysis by Alcalase2.4 were inspected.The results showed that the optimum hydrolytic conditions for reducing power was 50 ℃(temperature),200 min(time),2.6(water-material ratio).And for scavenging activity on hydroxyl free radical was 50 ℃(temperature),220 min(time),2.2(water-material ratio).Reducing power and scavenging activity on hydroxyl free radical under optimum conditions were 0.640% and 53.58% respectively.
