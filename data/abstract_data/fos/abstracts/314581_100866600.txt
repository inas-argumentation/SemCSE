The effect of IFN-γ on TRAIL-induced apoptosis in HBV related hepatocarcinoma cell line
Objective To explore the influence of IFN-γ on TRAIL (TNF related apoptosis inducing ligand)-induced apoptosis. Methods Apoptosis induced by TRAIL, IFN-γ, TRAIL plus IFN-γ was detected by flowcytometry, respectively. The expressions of membrane-bound TRAIL and its receptors were detected by flowcytometry and semi-quantitive RT-PCR respectively. The HBV particles secreted by HepG2.2.15 were detected by HBsAg and HBeAg detecting ELISA kit. Results HepG2.2.15 cell line was relatively resistant to TRAIL-induced apoptosis, but it could be made sensitive to TRAIL-induced apoptosis by IFN-γ (9.12% vesus 46.68%, P0.01). The expression of TRAIL and some of its receptors was upregulated by IFN-γ. The amount of secreted HBV particles was decresed by IFN-γ. Conclusion IFN-γ can sensitive HepG2.2.15 cells to TRAIL-induced apoptosis to a great extent and this may be realized by the upregulated expression of TRAIL and some of its receptors and the decreased secretion of the HBV particles.
