At times I questioned Ewing’s embrace of symbolism, such as the cat as a “symbol of the Virgin” (144) or the dog as a reference to marital fidelity (144), but Ewing is a brilliant connoisseur who describes De Beer’s virtuosic style, with its “distinctive system of light spotting” (147), “energy and verve” (154), inventive iconography, “obsessive attention to minute details” (176), and “exacting alignment” of forms with intersecting floor tiles (189). While scrupulously acknowledging the contributions of earlier scholars, Ewing produces a page turner, building layer upon layer, as he pushes his evidence to the limit and carefully, logically, builds his arguments. His book is certain to become the standard monograph on Jan de Beer.
