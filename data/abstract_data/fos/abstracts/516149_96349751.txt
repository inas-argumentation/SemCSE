The relationship between structure and optical properties in the mixed-ion system Na+/Ce3+/Nd3+β″-alumina
The introduction of Ce 3+ ions into the Na + /Nd 3+ β″-alumina system has been found to increase the pumping efficiency of the 1.06 μm line, which is fundamental to the unique lasing properties of this material. A single-crystal X-ray diffraction study of the mixed-ion system Na + /Ce 3+ /Nd 3+ β″-alumina has been undertaken in an effort to probe the possibility of obtaining structural information concerning the trivalent ion arrangement. Such information could then be used to make some crude deductions about the energy transfer efficiency of the Ce 3+ -Nd 3+ donor-acceptor system.
