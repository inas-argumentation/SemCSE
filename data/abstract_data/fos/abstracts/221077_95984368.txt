Dynamics of Gamma-X charge transfer in a double-barrier structure in the presence of pressure-induced Gamma-X mixing
With pressure-induced -X mixing, the temporal behaviour of charge accumulation inside the quantum wells of - and X-band states of a GaAs/AlAs symmetrical double-barrier structure was studied theoretically by numerically solving the time-dependent Schrodinger equation. It is found that the maximum charge being coupled to the AlAs X states from a charge initially photoexcited in the GaAs -sate quantum well will occur when the - and X-band energies are at resonance. This maximum charge is found to depend strongly on the barrier thickness of the double-barrier structure, reaching about 90% for barriers larger than 35 A. Variable frequency oscillatory behaviour of accumulated charge was also found, reflecting the coupling between the - and X-band states. The larger the frequency, the smaller the coupling strength between these states. Charge oscillation at resonance is found to cease for barriers smaller than 15 A. It is also found that changing the coupling coefficient not only changes the charge transfer but also affects its dynamics.
