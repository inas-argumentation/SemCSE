A Study of Social Insurance Pattern of Rural Migrant Workers
Rural migrant workers have played an important role in today's society,especially in the field of social security. On the issue of rural migrant workers' insurance,Shenzhen and Shanghai have set up concrete policies, thereby forming two patterns. Through a survey of the situation in Suzhou, the article points out three problems exiting in the system,that is, the problem in the implementation process,the problem of social security system and the problem of institutional environment. It is concluded that we should rely on the economic and cultural advantages of the local area to push forward the social security construction of rural migrant workers.
