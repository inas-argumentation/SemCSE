Faculty Composition, Institutional Structure, and Students' College Environment*
Each of several sets of variables—measuring aggregate student characteristics, institutional structural characteristics, and aggregate faculty characteristics—prove to have high correlations with students' assessments of the degree of their institution's climate of concern for them. Based on a nationally representative sample of 225 colleges and universities, and using stepwise multiple regression, the obtained Rs are, respectively, .77, .87, and .84. However, independent positive relationships are not found between the traditional measures of faculty “quality,” and the student-derived environmental measure.
