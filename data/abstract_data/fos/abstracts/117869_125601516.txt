Quasi-universal two-dimensional Gaussian paraconductivity fluctuations in layered superconducting cuprates
We investigate the conditions for the occurrence of two-dimensional Aslamazov-Larkin paraconductivity with a universal coefficient. We also analyze experimental resistivity data in underdoped La_{2-x}Sr_xCuO_4 samples establishing that the paraconductivity both in the absence and in the presence of magnetic fields calls for the presence of Gaussian superconducting fluctuations arising from weakly-coupled fermionic quasiparticles.
