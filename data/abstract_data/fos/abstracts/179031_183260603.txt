The Evolvement of the Connotation of "Quality" and the Significance of Cultivating Positive Activities Control Quality
The connotation of the concept of quality used to be very limited,referring only to the nature related to psychology and physiology.With its evolvement there comes sociality pedagogy or other meaning.At the beginning of 21st centuries there occurred researches about"the activities control quality".It emphasizes the significance of the activities control quality in the progress of cultivating the talents.Learning about the evolvement of the concept of"quality"through the narrow sense to the broad sense,the thesis defines the connotation of"pedagogy quality"afresh on the basis of critiquing the gain and loss of three pedagogy quality concepts which are popular in academic circles.Meanwhile,on the basis of the structure classification about pedagogy qualities,the thesis mainly explains the activities control quality,especially the putting forward of "the positive activities control quality" and its significance.
