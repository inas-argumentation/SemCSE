Dynamic-microwave extraction of Aquilaria oil in pilot experiment scale
Summary the vegetative state, quality evaluation, extraction, and separation of aquilaria, the essential oil from the aquilaria white wood was extracted by dynamic microwave extraction method (LDME). First, the extraction processing of Aquilaria oil in laboratory test was studied. Further, the extraction processing of pilot experiment of LDME was optimized, which studied the factor of the ethanol concentration, particle size, solid-lidquid ratio, material flow, and pre-heat temperature. The optimum process was obtained, which is the ethanol concentration 93%, particle size 40 mesh, solid-lidquid ratio1:8.5, material flux 78 L/h, preheat temperature 45 ℃, extraction times 2. Under the above condition, the extraction rate of materials is 6.9%, aquilaria oil yield was 0.68%.
