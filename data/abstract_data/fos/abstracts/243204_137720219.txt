Thermal Analysis for Technique of High Nickel Nodular Iron Technique
The paper has analyzed the characteristic points on the cooling curve (about) technique of spheroidizing and (inoculation) treatment for high nickel nodular iron by adopting thermal analysis method, which mainly (analyzed) the effect of the process and the influence from the declined spheroidizing. Meanwhile, with the (comparison) of metallurgical (structure’s) photographs, the paper has directly researched the transformation about the form of graphite during the (treatment) process. It is shown that spheroidizing effects are excellent when MgNi is as a spheroidizer. Furthermore, (spheroidizing) decline cant’t appear when it is poured within 4～6 minuts.
