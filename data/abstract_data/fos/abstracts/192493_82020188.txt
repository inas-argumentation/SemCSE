Isolation and identification of six phosphate-solubilizing bacteria.
Six isolated phosphate-solubilizing bacteria were obtained from the mud of Wangxiaoying sewage treatment plant.Based on the physiological and biochemical characteristics,the isolated S1,S2,S3,S4,S5 and S6 belonging to the genus Microbacterium,the genus Staphylococcus,the genus Pseudomonas,the genus Acinetobacter,the genus Flavimonas and the genus Cellulomonas,respectively.Compared with the six isolates,we can find that the isolate S3 had the strongest phosphate-solubilizing ability,its transparent rings on the plate showed that the ratio between the diameter of colony and that of colony transparent was 4.5;the degradation rate of lecithin was 66%.Further study by the 16S rDNA sequence analysis was done to identify the isolate S3,which belonged to the genus Pseudomonas alcaligenes.
