Chinese Students of Teachers College,Columbia University in the First Half of the 20th Century:Witness of a Doctoral List
In the first half of the 20th century,Teachers College,Columbia University had a special important status in the history of Sino-American educational exchange.A group of Chinese doctoral students graduating from TC played a positive role in promoting,and became the forerunners of the modern Chinese educational development and construction during the academic formation of modern education in China.They were engaged in a broad field of research,60% of whose doctoral dissertations were based on the problems of China education.
