Design of Pouring and Feeding in the Embeded Coal Hopper Casting of Heat Resistant Nodular Cast Iron
An embedded coal hopper casting,one of heat resistant nodular cast iron,is made of RQTSi-(5.5).The overall dimension,wall thickness and total weight are 2 400 mm ×1 000 mm ×(275 mm),25 mm,and 740 kg respectively.It is produced by a clay dry sand mould which is parted on the top of internal cavity,where the casting is mainly located in the bottom part and the gating system is overlapped on the top of core.The pouring design is adopted by the theory of large orifice discharge.The results are that a sprue with a size of 69 mm is located in the middle of runner;cross-section runners have sizes of 44 mm/54 mm×56 mm;six ingates have sizes of 110 mm/(130 mm)×6 mm;and two risers are located on the opposite of gating system.The size of riser was designed using moduli calculation method and the calculated result is that the riser has a diameter of 70 mm and 100 mm in height.The production shows that there is no defects and the production is reliable and stable,the overall yield of the casting is(93.7%).The practice proves that it is feasible to design gating system of heat resistant nodular cast iron casting by using large orifice discharge and moduli calculation method.
