Dissipation of phorate, disulfoton and endosulfan in/on soybean crop
Tnsecticidal schedules involving soil application of phorate, disulfoton and foliar application of endosulfan for control of insects on soybean crop have been assessed for toxic residues in a field experiment. No detectable residues of any of the chemicals used in this study or their metabolites (discerned through GLC) could be found in pods or the beans at harvest.
