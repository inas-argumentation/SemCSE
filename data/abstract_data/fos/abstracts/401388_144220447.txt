In the Eyes of Our Beholders: Some Evidence on how High-School Students and Their Parents View Quality in Colleges.
References to quality are ubiquitous on college campuses and in the discourse of individuals who are concerned about higher education. From college recruiting documents to the reports of national commissions, everyone is in favor of high quality. Quality has both its philosophical connotations and its practical implications. Recent advice to colleges and universities from marketing theorists stresses that an enhancement of quality is likely to accomplish more than price discounting will for an institution that seeks to improve its position in the market [ 16, 20].
