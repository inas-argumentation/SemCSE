On the Insurance Coverage of Agricultural Environmental Liability Insurance
With agricultural environmental pollution and destruction being intensified,environmental tort triggered by agricultural production stays widespread,and agricultural producers in disadvantaged status are unable to bear the corresponding liability for"homogeneous"damages.The presentation of agricultural environmental damage liability,is in accordance with socialized relief trend of environmental compensation.Insurance coverage is an important issue in agricultural environmental liability insurance system.From the perspective of theoretical and practical feasibility,it can be defined as the delivery liability including agricultural environmental tort liability,one's own field restoration liability,spiritual damages compensation liability and other necessary costs.
