Angular momentum coupling in molecular Rydberg states
The coupling of electronic and nuclear motions in the rovibronic levels of the lowest Rydberg states of methyl iodide is discussed in terms of a frame transformation between lab- and body- frame coordinate systems for an s Rydberg electron in the field of an electronically-degenerate, symmetric-top molecular ion core. It is shown that the r-dependence of exchange, spin-orbit, spin-rotation, and vibronic interactions, where r is the radial distance of the electron from the ion core, must be considered in order to provide a consistent analysis of the experimental data. Molecular constants, obtained by fitting one- and two-photon absorption spectra, show conclusively that the spin of the Rydberg electron is uncoupled from the molecular frame.
