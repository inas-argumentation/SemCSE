Application of hypobaric storage in chilled meat storage
The hypobaric storage was used for the first time in the storage of cooling pork,in this method,self-preservation storage decompression pressure testing machine was used to reduce the pressure in the environment to make the cooling pork maintain fresh.The scope of the pressure was:-0.1 ~-0.09 MPa,-0.05~-0.07 MPa,-0.03~-0.05 MPa,-0.01~-0.03 MPa,0~-0.01 MPa.The result indicated that the pork which stored in this method could maintain fresh for 16 days under 0~4 ℃.
