Effect of Inclusion and Banded Microstructure on Corrosion Performance of Pipeline Steel
The influence of inclusions and banded microstructure on the hydrogen induced cracking(HIC) performance for pipeline steel with different chemical composition was studied by analyzing metallographic microstructure, using electrochemical charge-to hydrogen test and HIC test. The factors for inclusions and banded structure of HIC property were analyzed. The nonmetallic inclusions which have a great influence on the hydrogen induced cracking sensitivity and resistance for pipeline steel were studied, which was the source of hydrogen induced cracking initiation. The cracks are easily induced and formed by the larger size inclusions, such as Si, Al, Ca and its composite oxide and so on. The band microstructure formed by the segregation of C, Mn and Si elements that is easy to cause high hardness during the banded structure and is gathering the places of hydrogen induced crack initiation and propagation pipeline, which has great deterioration on the hydrogen induced cracking resistance for pipeline steel.
