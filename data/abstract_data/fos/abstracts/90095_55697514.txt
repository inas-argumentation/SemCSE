Research on the Issue of the Distribution of Household Electrical Appliance Retail Chain Enterprises in China
The household electrical appliance retail chain enterprises in china develop rapidly, whilst the basic logistics facilities lagged behind. The slow development of logistics distribution blocks the further development of household electrical appliance sale and chain management. Therefore, effective measures shall be taken, according to the current situation of the development of logistics distribution for household electrical appliance retail chain management, so as to improve logistics distribution system so that the rapid growth of large household retail chain enterprises can be endowed with logistics system guarantee.
