Effect of Gradient Heat Treatment on Microstructure in Welding Interface of Ti_2AlNb/TC11 Dual Alloys
The gradient heat treatment was adopted in near isothermally forged Ti2AlNb/TC11 dual alloys which was obtained by vacuum electron beam welding.The weld interface structure before and after the heat treatment was observed by OM.The effects of gradient heat treatment on the microstructure were studied.The results show that after gradient heat treatment,the microstructure of heat-affected zone in Ti-22Al-25Nb alloy side changes slightly.The second phases are spheroidized and grown up.The basket microstructure transformes into two-state microstructure in heat-affected zone of TC11 alloy side.The microstructure of weld seam changes significantly.Lots of short rod and spherical second phases are precipitated and evenly distributed.This may be due to the alloying elements distributing uniformly after gradient heat treatment.
