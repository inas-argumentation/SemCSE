A Rorschach study of folie à deux of mother and son.
The Rorschach method can be of assistance in genetic studies of the personalities of several members of one family.

The test revealed in an objective way the similarity of the personality structure of mother and child at the beginning of treatment.

It helped in making the diagnosis of “folie a deux” and in the choice of treatment.

A re-test after a time of treatment checked the extent of improvement in child and mother objectively and allowed finer observations of the personalities.

It gave also some insight in the mental mechanisms underlying the improvement, and yielded valuable starting-points for analytical exploration.
