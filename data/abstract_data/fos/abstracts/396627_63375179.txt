Relevant Feedback Method for Image Retrieval Using Composite Score
A relevant feedback method for image retrieval using composite Score is proposed in the paper.First,the information,the retrieval satisfaction and feedback records from users,is stored into the feedback log database.Second,when a new retrieval is launched,the current feedback samples are inputted into the Soft Label SVM to get a classifier,together with expanded samples by analyzing historical feedback information to obtain.Finally,the weighted sum of three scores is calculated as the final score.The experiment result show that compared with existing relevant feedback method,the proposed method has significant improvement in feedback efficiency,the full use of the historical feedback,and feedback noise suppression.
