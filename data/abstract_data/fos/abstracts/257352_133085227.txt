In the writer's opinion, the zoning was caused by the action of acid mineralizing solutions of volcanic or subvolcanic origin on pre-existing rocks. The solutions became more acidic during the ascent as temperatures decreased from about 600•Ž. to 200•Ž. or 150•Ž.; they became more alkalic in lateral migration. Among the components of the original rocks, most of the Si, Al and Ti seems to have been concentrated in some parts of the altered aureole, altkalies partly or selectively removed, while other components almost entirely removed. H2O, C1, B, F and S are the components which are obviously added from the mineralizing solutions.
