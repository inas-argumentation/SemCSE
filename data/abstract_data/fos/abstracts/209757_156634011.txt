The Relationship between Farmers and Rural Cadres from the Perspective of Grassroots Social Management
In the circumstances of improving grassroots social management and service system,the relationship between the cadres and the masses in the countryside is an important component of grassroots social management.At present,there are some problems with such relationship,such as lack of trust,strained relation and poor political participation channel.There are some reasons behind this,including low quality of village cadres,dislocation of public opinion,lack of judicial protection and backward governance.It is suggested that,to solve the above-mentioned problems,it is essential to strengthen the education of rural cadres,improve their well-beings,increase their decision-making power,improve judicial protection and carry out elite governance in the countryside.
