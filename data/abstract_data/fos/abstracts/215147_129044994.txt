Oceanic pollen transport and pollen:dinocyst ratios as markers of late Cenozoic sea level change and sediment transport
Palynological studies of late Cenozoic cores from nine sites show large peaks in the ratio of pollen and spores to dinocysts (P:D) which reflect major increases in terrigenous sediment influx to the North Atlantic Ocean.
