Fast lithium-ion transport in composites containing lithium-bromide dihydrate
Composites containing lithium-bromide dihydrates and a second phase, either the monohydrate or dispersed Al 2 O 3 particles, exhibit an abrupt increase in the electrical conductivity at thedihydrate melting temperature T m = 43°C. Although the conductivity at T >62 T m is characteristic of a good liquid electrolyte -σ ≈ 0.1 mho cm -1 at 130°C with an activation energy E A = 13 kJ/mol and a Li + -ion transport number 0.9 ± 0.2, the composite pellet may be handled as a solid; it is brittle at 100°C. The possible significance of this finding for the design of high-power battery electrodes is indicated.
