The relationship between the universities and the university-managed technical firms and its change in 1990s:a sociological study based on the case of the technical firms managed by Peking University
In the 1990s,the development of the university-managed technical firms show the complex relationship among the universities,the firms and the governments.The need of the reproduction of the universities legitimated these firms,and the prestige hierarchy played a key role in the rapid development of the firms,and the hybrid system between the universities and the firms caused the phenomenon of strong firms and weak manager.The firms could not escape the control of the universities because of the institutions of the universities,but the hybrid system lead the interaction between the universities and the firms to lock-in in the later in the 1990s.Finally,the change of the conditions of the reproduction of the universities margined the firms in the universities.
