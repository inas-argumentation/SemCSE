Second-order quadrupole splitting of the55Mn NMR signal in KMno4 powder
In a KMnO4 powder sample the55Mn NMR signal has been observed using a FT pulse spectrometer at magnetic fields between 0.6T and 2.1T. A second-order quadrupole splitting has been found. The quadrupole coupling constant 3e2qQ/(2I(2I−1)h) is (240±10) kHz, the asymmetry parameterη is less than 0.05.
