Bio-hydrogen Generation through Dark Anaerobic Fermentation of Solid Waste
This paper is intended to summarize the recent development in biological H2 generation from solid wastes through dark anaerobic fermentation,focusing on search for the potential waste materials generating hydrogen,culture of microbes and up- to-date achievements at home and abroad in H2 production by fermentation of different kinds of wastes,including,wastes from agriculture and food industry. The latest advances in research of the combined dark/photo fermentation are introduced as well.
