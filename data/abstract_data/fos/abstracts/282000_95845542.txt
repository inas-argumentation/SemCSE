Exceptionally Symmetric Crystal Structure of (Pentabenzylcyclopentadienyl)(cyclooctatetraene)titanium(III)
Complex [Ti(III)(η 8 -C 8 H 8 )(η 5 -C 5 Bn 5 )] ( 1 ) was prepared by salt metathesis reaction of [TiCl(η 8 -C 8 H 8 )(thf)] 2 with LiC 5 Bn 5 and characterized by ESR and UV-near-IR spectra. Its crystal structure is symmetric with respect to a mirror plane involving the titanium atom and one of the benzyl groups.
