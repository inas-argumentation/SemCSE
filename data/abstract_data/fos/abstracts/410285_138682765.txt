Research of Fe_3Al Intermetallics and 304 Stainless Steel TIG Weld
The weldability tests were performed on Fe3Al Intermetallics and 304 stainless steel by using TIG welding with filler materials of 9Cr-1Mo based alloy,347austenitic stainless steel and Inconel 82 nickel-based alloy respectively,the joints also were studied. The results show that crack forms on welding zone ,crack also forms on heating affected zone of Fe3Al alloy .The molten metal of the pool coagulates along the base material of Fe3Al alloy with filler materials of 9Cr-1Mo based alloy and the microstructure phase of the joint is homogeneous .Fuzion zone can be found obviously when welded with filler materials of Ni based alloy at the side of Fe3Al base materials.
