Environmental Risk Zoning Based on Information Diffusion Theory
In view of the characteristics of the petrochemical industrial industry where multiple risk sources exist,a trial risk zoning is done for planning of a petrochemical industrial park using the information diffusion method.In this paper,the fuzzy diffusion model is built by the VBA program,which can be used to calculate the risk information matrix;and an intuitive environmental risk zoning map is worked out for the planned park by use of the cluster function of SURFER.
