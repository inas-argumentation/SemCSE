Influence of Sucrose on Spathe Color of Anthurium andraeanum ‘Dakota’
The influence of different concentrations of sucrose on the physiological characteristics associated with spathe color of Anthurium andraeanum Linden ‘Dakota’ was studied.The results showed that the color parameter a* and C* in the ear part of spathe was effectively improved,the contents of total anthocyanin,total flavonoids,soluble protein and soluble sugar increased,the incidence of " Green Ear" and the content of chlorophyll were reduced.Took all the physiological indices into consideration,it showed that the treatment of 0.05 mol/L sucrose in August to late September and the treatment of 0.15 mol/L sucrose in mid-September to the end of December had the best effects for the spathe color improvement.
