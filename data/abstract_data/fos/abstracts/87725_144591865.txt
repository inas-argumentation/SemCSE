Teacher Methodology and Transfer Potential in Language Arts Instruction.
The effect of teacher methodology in language arts instruction was considered relative to the performance of sixty-four ninth grade, track two, English students on a standardized achievement test and a standardized aptitude test. Results for the four classes evaluated suggest that despite differences in teacher approach to material, achievement over groups remained homogeneous. However, group scores, for language aptitude increased in direct relationship to teacher scores for application of general principles and ideas (as opposed to mechanics and specific skills).
