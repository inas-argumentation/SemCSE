On Significance of Mr.Pan Maoyuan in the Development of Higher Education Discipline in China
In the development of higher education discipline during recent 30 years in China,Mr.Pan Maoyuan has played the important role.It Mainly displays as follows:(1) the first "Higher Pedagogy" that edited by Mr.Pan Maoyuan has constructed the discipline system of higher pedagogy for the first time,and become the important symbol of higher education discipline establishment in China;(2) Mr.Pan Maoyuan founded the first institute of higher education research in China,took the lead to recruit the graduate students of higher pedagogy,impelled positively establishment of the Chinese Association of Higher Education and the National Higher Education Academic Society,which constituted the process of "the institution of training" and "the institution of research" in development of higher education discipline,and enabled the higher pedagogy to become an emerging area of knowledge.
