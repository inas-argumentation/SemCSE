OXYGEN ISOTOPE COMPOSITIONS OF SEAWATERS IN THE HUANGHAI(YELLOW) SEA AND THE BOHAI SEA
The characteristics of oxygen isotope compositions and distributions of the seawaters in the Huanghai(Yellow) Sea and the Bohai Sea have been investigated based on δ18O data meas- ured for 608 water samples. By using the property of tracing atoms, the mixing and the influencing range of the diluted freshwater of the Huanghe (Yellow) River and the exhibition of δ18O values in the movements of the Huanghai Sea warm current and its residual flows have been discussed. Particularly, the regional, vertical, seasonal and tidal changes of the δ18O values of the waters influenced by the Huanghe River have also been studied. A beneficial exploration was thus made for the application of stable isotope to the physical oceanography.
