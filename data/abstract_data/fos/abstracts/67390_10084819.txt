Phase diagram of the two component body-centered solid-on-solid model.
The (001) surface of a two component crystal of CsCl structure is described by a six vertex model with staggered vertex weights, which may be applicable to certain ionic crystals. Transfer matrix and finite-size scaling techniques yield a phase diagram with, besides previously known critical lines, a “critical fan” bounded by a line of Kosterlitz-Thouless points. This line is almost joint with a second order line with critical exponents which we find deviating from Ising values. Several thermal trajectories show inverse roughening transitions with reentrance into a disordered flat phase.
