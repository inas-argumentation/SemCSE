Mössbauer analysis of the solid-phase mechanical synthesis of nitrogen chromium-nickel and chromium-manganese austenite
Nanostructured austenite alloys lean-alloyed with nickel and manganese and precipitation-hardened with nitrides are prepared via solid-phase mechanical synthesis in a ball mill by milling powders of bcc iron-nickel and iron-manganese alloys together with chromium nitrides and subsequently annealing them in a range of temperatures near the upper boundary of α → γ transformations.
