A Study on Transferring the Rural Surplus Labour in China and Transforming Some Relevant Institutions
The large amount of rural surplus labour has always been the bottleneck for the further development of agricultural, rural and national economy. After studying the transferral patterns at home and abroad,the paper points out that the quasi－ transfer of rural surplus labour is incompatible with the situation of China from a long－ term viewpoint and the majority of rural surplus labour should be absorbed by developing township－ village enterprise and constructing small cities and towns.Meanwhile, the paper also explicates the necessity to transform some relevant institutions.
