Implementation of a Threshold Encryption Scheme
Proposes a threshold encryption scheme based on DES encryption algorithm and Shamir threshold secret sharing to achieve the complete text and images on the encryption and decryption. On the basis of the theory of algorithms, two kinds of information security algorithm are creatively combined together through the analysis of interface of algorithms in order to encrypt and decrypt images, with the pixel value transformed into string of binary, and Java language helps achieve the complete visualization of the process of encryption and decryption.
