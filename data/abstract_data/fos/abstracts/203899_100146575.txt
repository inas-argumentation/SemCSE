Comparision of nutritional compositions in muscles of Penaeus chinensis, Penaeus vannamei Boone and Penaeus japonicuss Bate
Nutritional components in muscles of Penaeus chinensis, Penaeus vannamei Boone and Penaeus japonicuss Bate were determined and analyzed, including the essential nutritional compositions, fatty acids and amino acids. Results showed that the contents of water and crude fat in Penaeus japonicuss Bate were higher than those in Penaeus chinensis and Penaeus vannamei Boone. The content of crude protein in Penaeus vannamei Boone was higer than that in Penaeus chinensis and in Penaeus japonicuss Bate. Seventeen kinds of amino acids were detected in three kinds of prawns. The totalcontent of amino acid was 78.89%, 80.67% and 74.57% and the total content of delicious amino acid was 30.83%, 31.52% and 29.11% respectively in Penaeus chinensis, Penaeus vannamei Boone and Penaeus japonicuss Bate. And essential amino acid index was 67.51, 70.36 and 63.21 respectively. Met+Cys, Ile and Val were the three lowest. The contents of palmitic acid and oleic acid were highest respectively in saturated fatty acids and monounsaturated fatty acids. The total contents of eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA) and docosahexaenoic (DHA) were 24.20%, 24.69% and 23.65% respectively in Penaeus chinensis, Penaeus vannamei Boone and Penaeus japonicuss Bate.
