Über den Einfluß der Coulomb-Wechselwirkung zwischen den Elektronen auf den Restwiderstand im Magnetfeld
Considering a gas of electrons, interacting viaCoulomb's law, containing randomly distributed, fixed impurities, it is shown that the calculation of the diagonal components of the static electrical conductivity tensor in an uniform magnetic field may be based on a quasi-particle-picture. The result holds at low temperatures in the first power of the density of impurities.
