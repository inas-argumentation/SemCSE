Study on the Jet Propeller Power System of the Engineering Dredger
By the analysis of presently existing problem of the engineering dredger practical application,according as the self_sail need of the engineering dredger,the propeller system is contrast analyzed and studied The paper presents the structure model on the propellant system of the engineering dredger At the same time making use of the analysis of hydrodynamics principium and jet basic theory,demonstrate that working principle and characteristics and give that calculation method And that structure design and characteristic calculation is processed
