Commentary to 'Putting the past behind us - Social stress induced urinary retention can be overcome'.
The authors have expanded upon their previous work with cyclosporine and stress-induced bladder dysfunction by looking at delayed administration of treatment and analyzing the long-term effects. Their data effectively demonstrates that after a period of stress, the administration of a well-tolerated dose of cyclosporine results in voiding pattern normalization.
