Study Status and Problem of Austenitic Stainless Steel with Ultra-fine Grain
The technology of ultra-fine grain austenitic stainless steel was introduced in detail, the relations of mechanical properties, intercrystalline corrosion, stress corrosion, pitting, crevice corrosion and grain size were summarized. In the end, the problems of researching ultra-fine grain austenitic stainless steel were prospected.
