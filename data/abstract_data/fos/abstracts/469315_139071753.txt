Mechanism Analysis on Center Layering in Tensile Fracture of High Strengthen Welding Steel HG70C
The hot rolled strip of high-strength welded structure steel HG70C arose serious center layering defect in mechanical property test.The test samples in different stress levels were designed.The tensile fracture's microstructure was analyzed by metallurgical microscope test and scanning electron microscope.The mechanism of tensile fracture was described.The research results show that excessive additional Ti forms inclusion,which generates crack by tensile stress.And the crack gradually extends with stress growing.
