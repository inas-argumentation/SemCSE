Effect of basal fruiting branch removal on SPAD values of the leaf subtending boll and cellulose synthesis in the premature cotton
In this study, two cotton cultivars("Quanyin-2", "Jiza-999") were used to research the effect of basal fruiting branch removal on SPAD values in the leaf subtending boll in the premature cotton and the related matter content during the cotton fiber development. The results indicated that the SPAD value in the treatment of removing two basal fruiting branches was higher than that of the control;meanwhile, removing basal two fruiting branches increased the transformation rate of sucrose and a higher transformation rate of sucrose was observed in the summer boll than in the autumn boll. With the growth of cotton age,the cellulose content in cotton fiber was higher than that in the control after removing basal fruiting branches. In conclusion, removing basal fruit branches could extend the functional period of the subtending leaf of the cotton boll and improve the transformation rate of sucrose in the cotton fiber; therefore, enhancing the biosynthesis of cotton cellulose.
