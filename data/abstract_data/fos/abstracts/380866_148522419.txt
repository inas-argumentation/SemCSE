Higher Education and Limited Choice of Rural Female——Narrative Analysis about a Rural Female Student of a Less Advantage College
Based on the analysis of narrative arise from a rural female student of less advantage college,it found that rural females have been born with double"ascribed"disadvantage according to their class and gender,which lead to the result that the field and capital endued by social institution and structure controlled their behavioral choice during the choice of higher education and the studying.Choice of behavior is the result of the double effects from individual and structure,and the restriction of choice resulted by structure is implicitly unfair.Thus,in such shackles of institutions,it should adopt the strategy to promote the educational equality from the perspective of psychological behavior of individual choice.
