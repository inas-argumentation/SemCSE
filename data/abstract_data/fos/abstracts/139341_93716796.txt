Analysis of the ideality factor in surface leaky HgCdTe photodiodes for the long-wavelength infrared region
The surface leakage currents in long-wavelength HgCdTe photodiodes have been calculated assuming the leakage is due to band-to-band tunnelling between the accumulated p-type surface and the adjacent region. Expressions describing the effect of the passivant's fixed charge density on the current - voltage (I - V) characteristics and ideality factor are developed and used to show that has a strong dependence on in a variety of situations. In the low forward bias region, the diode current can be dominated by bulk and surface tunnelling, in which case the current varies at a different rate than with thermal diffusion alone. As a result of this the versus V plot shows peaks and settles to the diffusion limited value of unity only for V > 8kT/q (50 mV) instead of the conventional 3kT/q limit, and these peaked regions can be used to identify the contributions of bulk and surface tunnelling. The experimental results agree reasonably well with the predictions of this model.
