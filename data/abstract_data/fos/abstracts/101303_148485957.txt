The Ningbo model of the campus zone establishment and management
To enforce the strategy of "booming the city by technology", Ningbo municipal government has successfully established the city's campus zone known as Ningbo model. The specialty of this model includes: an open campus in accord with open ideology; the share of resources promoted by multi\|system of school running; typical humanism atmosphere with multi\|functions; campus construction based on marketized managing system. The establishment of Ningbo campus zone has achieved its effect to enhance the development of Ningbo higher education and promote the urbanization of the city.
