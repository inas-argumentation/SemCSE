Study of the New Type Easy-cutting Brass
Based on Cu-Zn alloy with trace addition of other elements,a new type of easy-cutting brass was developed by using orthogonal experiment and optimized components design.Through the micro phase analysis,XRD and cutting properties test,the results show that: by adding some trace elements of Si,P,Al and La to the Cu-Zn-Si-P alloy,rigid γ phase can formed in the matrix of β phase.The γ phase distribute even in the alloy,so that the alloy has good mechanical property and the γ phase play an important role in breaking scrap during the cutting process.
