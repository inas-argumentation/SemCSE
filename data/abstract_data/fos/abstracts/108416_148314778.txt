Non-administration reform of higher education institutions: experiences abroad and strategies
The inefficiency of higher education reform is the result of s bureaucratic administration system in China. Experiences abroad such as economism, education evaluation system and education investment system, which are closely related to non-administration, can be the rationale, system assurance and economy assurance for the non-administration reform of higher education institution in China. The paper puts forward ideas of non-administration in institutions of higher education as follows: establishing the core of institution, making sure of staff's key roles, establishing independent value standard of higher education, speeding up internal reform, especially personnel system reform and logistics socialization.
