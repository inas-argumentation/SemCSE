Time-like detonation in presence of magnetic field
We study the effect of magnetic field in an implosion process achieved by radiation. A time-varying magnetic field is seen to affect the continuous transition of space-like detonation to time-like detonation at the core of implosion region. A constant magnetic field does not affect the detonation, and the effect of exponentially decaying field is not significant. The oscillating varying magnetic field has a significant effect in increasing the volume of the time-like detonation volume of the core of implosion and also modify the time of the implosion process. This transition can have significant outcome both theoretically and experimentally in the areas of high energy hadronization of quark-gluon plasma (QGP) matter and Inertial Confinement Fusion efforts of fuels.
