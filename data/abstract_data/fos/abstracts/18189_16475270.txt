Electromagnetic N-Δ transition form factors in a covariant quark-diquark model
The electromagnetic N-Δ transition form factors are calculated in the framework of a formally covariant constituent diquark model. As a spin-$tfrac {3}{2}$ particle the Δ is assumed to be a bound state of a quark and an axial-vector diquark. The wave function is obtained from a diquark-quark Salpeter equation with an instantaneous quark exchange potential. The three transition form factors are calculated for momentum transfers squared from the pseudothreshold (MΔ −MN)2 up to −2 (GeV/c)2. The magnetic form factor is in qualitative agreement with experiment. We find very interesting results for the ratios E2/M1 and C2/M1.
