Formation of methyl nitrite in engines fueled with gasoline/methanol and methanol/diesel
The concentration of methyl nitrite in the exhaust emissions from vehicles fueled with methanol and methanol blends was determined by gas chromatography with electron capture detection. The concentration was found to vary between 0.02 and 0.1 pm for methanol/gasoline blends and 0.02 and 5 ppm for a methanol/diesel-fueled engine. The amount of methyl nitrite in the exhaust emissions was reduced by the use of catalytic conversion system. Secondary formation of methyl nitrite during dilution of the exhaust emissions with air was found to be negligible.
