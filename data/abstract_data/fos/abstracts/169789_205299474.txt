Severe intestinal oedema due to nephrotic syndrome.
Nephrotic syndrome is defined by oedema, proteinuria (>3 g/24 h) and hypoalbuminaemia (<30 g/l). Nephrotic syndrome may occur as a result of primary renal or systemic diseases, commonly diabetes mellitus in the adult population. The cause of the oedema is not completely understood, but may result from loss of plasma oncotic pressure due to the hypoalbuminaemia or primary sodium retention in the collecting tubules due to abnormal tubular function.
