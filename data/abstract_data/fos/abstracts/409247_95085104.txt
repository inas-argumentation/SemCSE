Kinetics of formation of oxide superconductor phases : X-ray analysis
The rate of formation of EuBa 2 Cu 3 O 7− z is shown to be faster than that for HoBa 2 Cu 3 O 7− z , LaBa 2 Cu 3 O 7− z or YBa 2 Cu 3 O 7− z ·, this appears to be related to the structure of the rare earth precursor in the oxide mixture subjected to thermal treatment rather than its dispersion. At 973 K the effect of the oxygen partial pressure on the rate of this thermal transformation is rather complex, but at 1073 K the rate is independent of P O2 in the range 6–101 KPa.
