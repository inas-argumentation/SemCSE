Design and Technology Analysis of the Injection Mould for Press out Valve
Through analysis on the structure of press out valve and technology problems in injection forming,choosing the molding parameters of polystyrene,an injection mould structure use one module and four cavities were determined.The molding size was designed according to the average shrinkage ratio of polystyrene,demoulding mechanism of taper microgroove and arc side core-pulling mechanism was used in the mold.The design and machining technology process of its molding part,inject system and other make up,and working process of the mould are specified in detail.The forming trial of the mold was successful.The operation can be run reliably and flexibly,the precision of plastic components can achieve the requirements of the design and the outside surface is smooth.
