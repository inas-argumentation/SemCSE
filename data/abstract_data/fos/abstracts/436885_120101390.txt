Thermodynamic limits to thermal regeneration in adsorption cooling cycles
Abstract Energy and exergy models for ideal adsorption cycles with isothermal beds and no mass recovery are developed to predict the limits to COP enhancement using thermal regeneration. The models are applied to compare the performance of zeolite–water and silica gel–water adsorbent–refrigerant pairs over a range of maximum bed temperatures. The thermodynamic consistencies of several alternate adsorption property assumptions are quantified. Differences in adsorption characteristics between zeolite–water and silica gel–water result in a significantly larger potential to enhance COP by implementing thermal regeneration for zeolite–water.
