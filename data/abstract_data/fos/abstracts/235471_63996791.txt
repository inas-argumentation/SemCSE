The Design & Simulation of a Novel Ku-band Microstrip Antenna
There is a novel designation of Ku-band,high gain,wideband microstrip antenna with double-layer.The designation use cutting triangular notches on the patches and add a pair of metal-ridge on the upper patch.The simulated experiment proved that the relative bandwidth is 16.7%(VSWR≤2)and have gotten better impedance characteristic;when the centric frequency is 12GHZ,the gain is about 10.5dB.
