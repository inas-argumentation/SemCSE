Design of Environmental Monitoring System of Large Warehouse Logistics Center
According to the practical needs of the large warehouse logistics center,designs an intelligent environmental monitoring system.In the absence of operation or remote control mode,the system can collect in real-time and analyse to smoke in the storage center environment conditions,air quality conditions,temperature and humidity parameters,achieve the purpose of monitoring environmental conditions in a timely manner.The analysis of monitoring data,and storage optimization of materials has a certain significance.
