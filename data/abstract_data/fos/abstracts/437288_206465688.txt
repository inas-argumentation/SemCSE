Cardiovascular pathology: looking back on the first 25 years and forward into the future.
The publication of this issue heralds and celebrates 25 years of active publication of Cardiovascular Pathology (CVP), the official journal of the Society for Cardiovascular Pathology (SCVP). Over the years, the journal has remained committed to publishing peer-reviewed articles on all aspects of cardiovascular disease, with a particular focus on disease pathology and pathogenesis. I have the honor and privilege of serving as the third editor of CVP following the editorships of Dr. Stephan Factor, the founding editor (1992–2001), and Dr. Avrum Gotlieb and Dr. Jagdish Butany, who subsequently served as co-editors (2002–2011). While the editors have beenmindful that the content of the Journal should reflect the interests and expertise of the membership of the Society, the editors have aspired to have CVP recognized as an organ that publishes substantive, innovative, and authoritative work and is recognized as a premier, reliable source by the broad community of cardiovascular clinicians and investigators. Demonstrable progress in the pursuit of this goal has been achieved as reflected by increases in impact factor and other metrics of peer recognition of the content of the journal. CVP now has a quite competitive ranking among clinical subspecialty journals. In the last 24 years, CVP has published over 1300 items, including over 1200 papers representing a mix of original research articles, reviews, guidelines/consensus statements, and short communications (clinical case reports and images). The accompanying Table shows the number and relative frequency of publications on various topics. The Table documents that CVP has provided extensive coverage of all of the major areas of cardiovascular disease. The original research articles show a balance of studies of human disease and experimental models. In addition, these articles represent amix of descriptive andmechanistic information. This spectrum reflects a guiding principle followed by the current and previous editors that CVP should have an eclectic approach to publication of insights into the pathobiology and clinico-pathologic correlations of cardiovascular diseases. Over the years, CVP has consistently published papers on major areas, including atherosclerosis and related vascular biology, other vascular diseases, coronary artery disease, myocardial ischemia, rhythm and conduction disturbances and sudden cardiac death, cardiomyopathies, valvular and other structural heart disease, and congenital heart disease and developmental biology. In addition, not surprisingly for a pathology journal, CVP has publishedmany case series and case reports of cardiac tumors. Although just a few papers are devoted specifically to the validation and application of endomyocardial biopsy (EMB), the surgical pathology of EMB has figured prominently in the investigation of inflammatory heart disease and the clinical success and understanding mechanisms of the complications of heart transplantation.
