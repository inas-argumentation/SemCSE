Diffusion growth of Cu3Sn phase in the bump and thin film Cu/Sn structures
Monitoring the intermetallic interfacial growth in Cu-Sn interconnects is crucial from reliability point of view. In this work the increase of Cu3Sn thickness has been monitored during ageing for two different types of sample with flip-chip scale dimensions. The ageing was performed in the temperature range from 150 to 300 °C with different durations. Based on the thickness growth measurement, values of diffusion parameters were calculated. The obtained results exhibit that the processing route plays an important role in the growth of intermetallics in the Cu-Sn system.
