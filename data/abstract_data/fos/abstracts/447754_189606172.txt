SD Model Research on Science and Technology Resource Input and Output in Towns of Industry Clusters
Based on the system dynamics (SD) principle aspect, this text built up the mathematics model on the input and output of science and technology (S&T) resource in towns of industry clusters(TICs), used the Simulink of MATLAB to set up the homologous calculator model to imitate the real situation, and finally got the emulation result about the future development condition of S&T resource input and output in TICs. Through the deep analysis on the emulation result, this text also provided some practical, feasible and scientific basis for the design on the sustainable development of S&T resource input and output and the upgrading of TICs.
