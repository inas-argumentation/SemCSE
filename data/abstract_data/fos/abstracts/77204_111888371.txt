Application of Filter Noise by Wavelet Technic in Weld Crack Signal Process of Acoustic Emission
Aiming at forming mechanism and feature of low carbon steel weld crack,the filter noise of weld crack signal was investigated by wavelet technic.By wave and frequency analysis of extracting 110-190kHz crack signal,the results affirm the effectiveness of wavelet analysis in crack signal process,which provide some help for estimating weld structure perfection and active service.
