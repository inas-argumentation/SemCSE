A Novel Method for Synthesis of 7-Chloro-1H-Imidazo[5,1-c][1,2,4]Triazol-3-Amine from 4,5-Dichloroimidazole
There are many methods reported at this time for the construction of bicyclic nitrogen-containing heterocyclic compounds having a range of applied properties [1, 2]. Hence in the study [3] there was reported the possibility of preparing 1H-imidazo[5,1-c][1,2,4]triazoles by reaction of diazoimidazoles with diethyl malonate halo derivatives with subsequent intramolecular cyclization under the action of a base. We have found that treatment of 4,5-dichloroimidazole (1) with an equimolar amount of BrCN in the presence of triethylamine gives the 4,5-dichloro-1H-imidazol-1-yl carbonitrile (2) in 72% yield. The latter can react with hydrazine hydrate upon cooling to form the 4,5-dichloro-1H-imidazol-1-ylcarboxamidrazone (3). This amidrazone undergoes a smooth cyclization (method A) with refluxing for 4 h in acetonitrile in the presence of triethylamine, to give 7-chloro-1H-imidazo[5,1-c][1,2,4]triazol-3-amine (4) in 82% yield (calculated on the amidrazone 3 introduced into the reaction).
