Effects of Slow Release Fertilizers on the Growth,Development and Fruit Characteristics of Carica papaya L.
The effects of slow release fertilizers(SRF)on the growth,development and fruit characteristics of Carica papaya L.were studied by pot experiment.The results showed that fertilizers promoted the growth and development,improved the fruit physical and quality in Carica papaya L.The promoting effects of slow release fertilizers(SRF-3 and SRF-4)were superior to that of complex fertilizers(CPF).The difference of titratable acidity between treatments was un obvious,while the fruit transverse diameter,fruit longitudinal diameter,single fruit weight,output,whole sugar and Vc content between SRF-3,SRF-4 and CPF were obvious.The fruit number and fruit thickness between SRF-3 and SRF-4 were significantly different while that between SRF-3,SRF-4 and CPF was not significant.The difference of solid content between CPF and SRF-3 was not obvious,but the different between them and treatment of SRF-4 was obvious.150 g SRF-4 per plant had the best applying effects on the growth,development and fruit characteristics of Carica papaya L.
