Characteristics of Aniline Degradation on Platinum Electrode
Electrochemical characteristics of aniline on different conditions at platinum anode were investigated by cyclic voltammetry. Results showed there appeared oxidation peak in acid solution. At first cycle there was only one oxidation peak when peak electric potential was 1.0V while in later cycles there were two oxidation peaks when peak electric potentials were 1.0V and 0.5V.
