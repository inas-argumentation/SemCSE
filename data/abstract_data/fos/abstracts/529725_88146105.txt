The contents of nitrate, nitrite and Vc at different growth stages in leaves of 3 wild vegetables
The contents of nitrate, nitrite and Vc in leaves of 3 wild plants from Guangdong were determined. The results and conclusions were as follows: Callipters esculenta(Retz.) J. Sm. ex Moore et Houlst. in the phases of twist-leaves, rachis, young leaves and grown-up leaves, and Sonchus brachyotus DC. in the phases of seedling, vegetative and fruit, belong to the first class edible wild vegetables and they can be eaten anyway(in fresh, after salted or cooked); Callipters esculenta(Retz.) J. Sm. ex Moore et Houlst. in the phase of half grown-up leaves, and Sonchus brachyotus DC. in the primary flowering phase, belong to the second class and they can be eaten by cooked or salted. Sonchus brachyotus DC. in the phase of full-bloom, and Amaranthus spinosus L. in the phases of seedling, vegetative, primary flowering and fruit, belong to the third class edible wild vegetables and they must be eaten after cooked; In the primary fruit phase, Amaranthus spinosus L. belongs to the fourth class wild vegetables, it is unsuitable to be eaten normally.
