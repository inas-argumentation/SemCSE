Design and Applying of Slag Separating Device Used in Cupola-induction Furnace Duplex Smelting
It dealt with the necessity of applying the slag separating device in process of cupola induction furnace duplex smelting and the running principles of some of the devices. It introduced in detail the calculation of the major technical parameters of the slag continuous separating device and the construction design of the device, and it also introduced well the effects of applying the device and also the problems with applying the device.
