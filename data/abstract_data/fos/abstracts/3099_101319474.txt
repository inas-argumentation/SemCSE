Determination of Partition Coefficient of Alkaloids from Coptis Chinensis Franch by Thin Layer Chromatograph-Fluorometric Spectrophotometry
A thin layer chromatograph(TLC) coupled with fluorometric spectrophotometric method has been established to determine the partition coefficient of alkaloids extracted from Coptis chinensis Franch in solvent systems of high speed counter-current chromatography(HSCCC).After samples was equilibrated in solvent system,equal volume of upper and lower phase was spreaded with TLC method respectively and then extracted to measure the fluorescence intensity with fluorometric spectrophotometry(Lex 365 nm,Lem 409 nm) and to calculate the partition coefficient.A good linear relationship between fluorescence intensity and concentration of four kinds of alkaloid compounds in certain range was obtained.The average recoveries of epiberberine,coptisine,palmatine and berberine ranged from 94.7% to 104.1%.The range of relative standard deviations(RSD) in spiked samples was 1.9% to 3.8%.There was no significant difference in partition coefficient obtained with combined method,compared with HPLC.This method has high precision,broad applicability and consumes short time.
