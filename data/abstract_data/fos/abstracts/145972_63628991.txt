Research on the Commit Protocol for Grid Collaborative Design Transaction
According to the characters of Grid collaborative design transaction and the way of people's collaborative work in usual,a commit protocol for Grid collaborative design transaction and the recovery mechanism are proposed.This protocol cancels the vote stage of 2PC,and participators can decide commit or cancel by themselves,w ithout w aiting the commands of coordinators.It considers the time limits and quality requirements,allow s participators to make the decision by themselves to participate into the collaborative design or not,and enables them to exit the design in halfw ay.It can keep the unfixed atomicity of transaction.Finally,the correct of the protocol is proved by analyzing its atomicity,time-limited character and applicability,and its effectiveness and feasibility are illustrated by performance and experiments analysis.
