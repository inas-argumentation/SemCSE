High protonic conductivity of heavily KOH-doped ice
Heavily KOH-doped ice exhibits high protonic conductivity (10 S m −1 ). The KOH-ice conductivity in the high-temperature region is probably determined by the liquid electrolyte layer between grains of solid “pure” ice. Below 210 K, the temperature of freezing of the eutectic system 4H 2 O+KOH, the liquid electrolyte layer must be frozen, but a quasiliquid layer on the 4H 2 O+KOH eutectic system surface may exist down to 180 K.
