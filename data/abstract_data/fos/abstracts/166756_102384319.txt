Catalytic Oxidation Treatment of Reactive Brilliant Red Dye Wastewater by Chlorine Dioxide
ClO2 chemical oxidation system and ClO2 catalytic oxidation system were studied for treating reactive brilliant red dye wastewater with COD of 2700mg/L. Results showed that by adjusting pH to 10 and adding 800mg/L wastewater, an active time of 30min was the most effective and economical with COD removal efficiency over 63% and oxidation index of 2.18. When treating wastewater by catalytic oxidation of ClO2 and a special catalyst, adjusting pH to 10 and adding 1000mgClO2, an active time of 90min was the most effective and economical with COD removal efficiency up to 83.4% and oxidation index of 2.25. Results illustrated that ClO2 catalytic oxidation was an efficient method for treating refractory wastewater.
