EXPERIMENTAL STUDIES ON ASEISMIC PERFORMANCE OF T-SHAPE FABRICATED CONCRETE JOINTS WITH PRECAST EXTERNAL SHELL AND CAST-IN-PLACE CORE CONCRETE
In order to investigate the aseismic performance of fabricated concrete T-shape exterior RC joint with precast external shells and cast-in-place core,one new-type assembled exterior joint and one parallel joint were fabricated,and cyclic loading tests of the T-shape joints were carried out.The aseismic performances were investigated,including failure modes,hysteretic property,ductility,energy dissipation,stiffness degradation.The studies showed that the new-type assembled T-joint had excellent integrity and aseismic performance,similar to those of the cast-in-place RC T-joint,and could meet the aseismic-design requirement of RC frame structure.
