Origin and developing about "Wumei boxing" in female boxing style.
Based on their analysis of the historical origin and current status of Wumei boxing that integrates rare boxing style,female boxing style,and one of the second batch of provincial non-material cultural heritages of Fujian province,the authors put forward the following strategies for developing Wumei boxing;by relying on local universities and related governmental departments in charge,establish a Wumei boxing development studying and training base and a female boxing society to attract more females in schools,rural areas and communities to practice Wumei boxing;modify and innovate Wumei boxing and use it as a rural sport featured course to promote and popularize Wumei boxing in elementary and middle schools and universities;innovate Wumei boxing routines by referring to national Wushu competition rules;strengthen the protection of and support for folk Wushu inheritors,make inheritor protection scientific,systematic and standardized;establish friendships via Wushu competition,boost the development of Wumei boxing via traditional folk Wushu competition.
