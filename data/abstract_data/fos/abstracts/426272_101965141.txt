Changes of several nutrient components in pumpkin fruit after harvest
The change trends of major nutrient components of pumpkin fruit were different after harvest. In this paper,we studied the nutrient content of Lainong No.1 pumpkin during 0～80 days after harvest. The results shows that the content of water continuously decreased during the whole period,while the soluble protein and solid content kept elevating. The previous content of starch and carotenoid pigment were increased slowly,but later it then turned to downward trend. The soluble sugar had the similar trend with starch.
