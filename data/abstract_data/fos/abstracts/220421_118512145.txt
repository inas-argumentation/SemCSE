Untersuchung isobarer Analogresonanzen oberhalb derN=50-Schale mit polarisierten Protonen
In the region of isobaric analog resonances the analyzing power was measured in elastic scattering of polarized protons from the target nuclei88Sr,90Zr,92Zr and94Zr. From the analysis of the energy and angular dependence spin and parity values were determined for 19 pronounced resonances, isobaric analog to states in89Sr,91Zr,93Zr and95Zr.
