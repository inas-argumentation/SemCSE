Optimization of enzymatic extraction of polysaccharide from Laminaria japonica Aresch using response surface methodology and the antioxidant properties of the products
Cellulase-catalyzed hydrolysis was used to extract polysaccharides from Laminaria japonica Aresch and the antioxidant activities of the extracted polysaccharides were studied.The effects of cellulase concentration,the ratio of material to water,temperature,pH and time on the yield of polysaccharides were investigated by single factor experiments and response surface analysis.The optimal condition for the extraction was treating the material (a material-to-water ratio of 1:40) with 1.5% cellulase at 44 ℃ and pH4.0 for 2 h.Under this condition,the maximum yield of polysaccharides was obtained as (11.62±0.03)%.Moreover,the crude polysaccharides precipitated with ethanol demonstrated some antioxidant activity in the assays of ABTS radical scavenging activity and potassium ferricyanide reducing power.The result showed that the antioxidant capacity of the laminaran was greater than the fucoidan and alginate.
