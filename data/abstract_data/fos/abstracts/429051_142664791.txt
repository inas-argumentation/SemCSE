Mayas in Postwar Guatemala: Harvest of Violence Revisited (review)
This book is not an easy read. The main difficulty arises from the use of a wide variety of measurement units and the frequent shifting between these measures. For example, surface area is expressed variously as Cuban vara, caballeria, square kilometer, square Cuban league, and Corralera league. The same problem holds true for length, weight and volume. The author is in some ways hostage to his sources, which use these different measures and, to his credit, he does provide an appendix with equivalents. Nevertheless, it can be quite difficult to interpret comparisons based on different units of measure without a calculator. Overall, though, From Rainforest to Cane Field in Cuba is a well-crafted history of how Cuban forests were sacrificed to economic interests. It is more than an environmental history. The author recounts in detail the shifting debate over time between the competing interests of private property owners and the state. He demonstrates clearly what can happen when nonrenewable resources are at stake.
