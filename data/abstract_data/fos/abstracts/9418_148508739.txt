Carrying Out Psychological Healthy Education Among the College Students by Using the Method of Psychological Consultation
Psychological problems are generally existed among the current college students because of the factor of family, the college environment, the factor of social psychology and the factor of inheritance etc.. The psychological healthy education can be carried out among them by using the method of psychological consultation so as to change their pattern of recognition, enhance their self-confidence, master the way of self-management, increase the degree life-satisfaction and implement the value of life.
