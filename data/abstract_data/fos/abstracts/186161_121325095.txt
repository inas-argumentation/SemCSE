Elimination of Spurious Loss in Euler Equation Computations
The accuracy of a standard implicit conservative Euler equation solver is greatly improved by incorporation of an entropy-conservation equation, used in lieu of the streamwise momentum equation in all regions of the e ow except at shock waves. A slight modie cation is also required at stagnation points to maintain a well-posed nature. The overall method eliminates spurious entropy errors and, thus, reduces the grid density required to achieve any particular level of accuracy. Results arepresented for subsonic and transonicairfoil e ows. Although thederivation and resultsareforsteady,two-dimensionalEulerequations, theapproach hasno inherent limitationsthatpreclude extension to unsteady, three-dimensional, or viscous e ows. A brief discussion of these extensions is presented.
