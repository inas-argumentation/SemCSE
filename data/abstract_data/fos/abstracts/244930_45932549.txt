Some properties of functions associated with close-to-convex and starlike of order alpha
For analytic functions f(z) normalized with f(0)=0 and f^'(0)=1 in the open unit disk U, a new class A(@b"1,@b"2;@l) of f(z) satisfying some conditions with some complex numbers @b"1 and @b"2 is introduced. The object of the present paper is to discuss some properties for A(@b"1,@b"2;@l) of f(z) associated with close-to-convex and starlike of order @a in U.
