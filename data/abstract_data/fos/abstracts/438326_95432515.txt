ORGANIC CONDUCTORS: ELECTRICAL PROPERTIES OF HMTTeF (HEXAMETHYLENETETRATELLURAFULVALENE) COMPLEXES
Several charge transfer complexes of HMTTeF (hexamethylenetetratellurafulvalene) with TCNQs, p-quinones, and other π-acceptors were prepared and electrical resistivities were measured with compaction samples. Some of them revealed very low resistivities (ρRT<1Ωcm) and low activation energies(ea<0.01eV). Intermolecular interaction through tellurium atoms seems to play an important role in the conduction.
