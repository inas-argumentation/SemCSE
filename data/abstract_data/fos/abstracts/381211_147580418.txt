Discussion: Perceptuo-Motor Factors
Dr. Wedell cites increasing differentiation as a major reason for the low correlations. He notes the increasing role of cue selection and attention, the need to organize the sensory input data. He also cites the increasing dominance of visual perceptual organization over tactile information. hha t we seem forced to conclude is that tactile proficiency or deficit is very imperfectly related to proficiency at the visual level and presumably later, at the conceptual or language organization level.
