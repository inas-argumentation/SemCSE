Evaluating the Circadian Rhythm in Drinking and Voiding in Healthy Children, Using the Abc-Formula (Age-Related Bladder Capacity)
Purpose Voiding or bladder dysfunction in children is common seen in the daily practice. A protocol of drinking and micturition is helpful in diagnosing these dysfunctions. Up to now the Koff formula (bladder capacity (in ounces) = age (in years) + 2) has been established as gold standard. Despite its relevance, it shows certain weaknesses. It is often associated with not age-related bladder volumes and higher daily fluid intake. The aim of this study was to establish a logarithm for the daily need of fluid in healthy children using the ABC-formula (Age-related Bladder Capacity) (Age x 30) / 2 Material and Methods We examined prospectively a bladder diary in 60 children with no voiding or bladder dysfunction. These children were seen for other urological problems. We additionally asked for pooping habits. Three age groups of children were differentiated: Group 1: little children aged 5-7,(n=20) Group 2: small school children aged 8-10,(n=20) Group 3: big school children aged 11-15,(n=20) Results The results are represented in table 1.
