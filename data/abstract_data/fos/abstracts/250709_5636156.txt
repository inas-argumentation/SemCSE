Low Self-Esteem in Differentiating Fearful and Self-Conscious Forms of Shyness
Differences between fearfully and self-consciously shy individuals in self-esteem were investigated. Analysis indicated that fearfully (n = 45) compared to self-consciously (n = 40) shy individuals self-reported lower self-esteem. This result suggests that the role cf low self-esteem may be different for each shyness subtype. A possible explanation was proffered.
