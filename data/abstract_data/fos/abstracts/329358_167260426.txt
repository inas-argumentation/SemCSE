Research on Patent Application Competitive Power Based on the Soo Pat Database in Huizhou
Utilizes the Internet search database- the Soo Pat database of the State Intelligence Property Office of the P.R.C, establishes a patent application database about Huizhou by retrieving the patent documents applied by Huizhou dating from Jan. 1st 2004 to Feb. 31 st 2013.Then uses the patent information analysis platform system of the SIPO, carries out a refined and comprehensive analysis on the competitive power of Huizhou's patent application with respect to the application total number, the IPC distribution, the applier distribution, and the application trend of Huizhou. Retrieves the useful information from the patent documents and used for enterprises' economic benefits.
