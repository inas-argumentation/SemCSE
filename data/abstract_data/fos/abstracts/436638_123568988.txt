Knowledge Reductions Based on D-S Evidence Theory for Inconsistent Decision Table
For consistent decision table,the approach to knowledge reduction based on evidence theory is consistent with algebraic reduction. However,they do not comply with each other for inconsistent decision table. The difference between generalized decision reduction based on D-S evidence theory and algebraic reduction has been illustrated by an example. It is proved that generalized decision reduction is just equivalent to assignment reduction. A new approach to algebraic reduction based on evidence theory is proposed. Its validity is proved by theoretical analysis and an example is showed.
