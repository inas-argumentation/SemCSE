Study on the application of haccp in the foodstuff supplying station of P.L.A.Naval force
The foodstuff supplying station is a special food supplier for P.L.A.naval force. On the basis of food safety situation and the application of HACCP at home and abroad today, relating to task of making food in the foodstuff supplying station, this paper explored feasibility and significance to erect HACCP in the foodstuff supplying station, and the major working procedure was also discussed.
