Extraction and assay of antioxidant activity of crude polysaccharide from spent compost substrate of Cordyceps militaris
The optimal extraction technology by hot water was set up to prepare the crude polysaccharide from spent compost substrate of Cordycep militaris and the antioxidant activity of the crude polysaccharide was assayed by DPPH method.The results suggested that the optimal extraction technology was:ratio of dosage liqueur 1 ∶20, temperature 90 ℃ and 3 hours of extract time.The crude polysaccharide showed strong antioxidant activity.
