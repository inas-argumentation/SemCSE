Organic Manure Quality Evaluation of Wild Euphorbiaceae Green Manures
Euphorbiaceae is an important resource of green manures in the tropics and subtropics.Fifty-three germplasms of Euphobiaceae green manures collected in various localities from Hainan,Guangdong,Guangxi,Yunnan provinces were determined in terms of content of crude organic matter and mineral nutrients,and their organic manure quality was evaluated based on the analysis of the mineral elements content.The results indicated that the Euphobiaceae plant germplasms of 53 contained higher crude organic matter,and the average content was 89.2%,varied little,while the macro nutrients content varied substantially.Their nitrogen content ranged from 8.2 g/kg to 47.8 g/kg(the mean,23.2 g/kg),the phosphorus content from 2.9 g/kg to 26.6 g/kg(the mean,11.5 g/kg),the potassium content from 4.6 g/kg to 41.1 g/kg(the mean,15.3 g/kg),the calcium content from 2.8 g/kg to 30.9 g/kg(the mean,16.1 g/kg),the magnesium content from 1.1 g/kg to 8.0 g/kg(the mean,3.5 g/kg),the sulfur content from 1.0 g/kg to 7.5 g/kg(the mean,3.2 g/kg);The micro elements content varied even more greatly than the macro elements content,the mean iron content of all the accessions was 328.4 mg/kg,the mean boron content 26.6 mg/kg,the mean manganese content 313.3 mg/kg,the mean copper content 11.2 mg/kg,the mean zinc content 11.2 mg/kg,the mean molybdenum content 2.56 mg/kg;All the accessions of the Euphorbiaceae plant germplasm had a high quality when used as green manure,and all were categorized into the third or higher grades of green manure,most of which were listed in the second grade organic manure,and a few of which were listed in the third grade organic manure.
