ADSORPTION OF URIC ACID DERIVATIVES IN WATER BY POLY(VINYLDIAMINOTRIAZINE)THROUGH HYDROGEN BONDING
In aqueous solutions, poly(2-vinyl-4,6-diamino-1,3,5-triazine) efficiently absorbs uric acid, xanthine, and theobromine by using three complementary hydrogen bonds. The adsorption is selective and reversible, indicating a strong potentiality for the practical application.
