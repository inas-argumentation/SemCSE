Angular Distribution of Neutrons Following the Nuclear Capture of Polarized Muons
The angular distribution of neutrons following the nuclear capture of polarized mesons ( mu /sup -/) in S/sup 32/ was determined for the process: mu / sup -/ + p yields n + nu . The ( mu /sup -/) lifetime in S/sup 32/ was determined to be 0.46 plus or minus 0.05 mu sec. (C.J.G.)
