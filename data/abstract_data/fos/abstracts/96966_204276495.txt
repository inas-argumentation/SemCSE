Study on the Round Phase Formed after the Treatment of Double Glow Plasma Surface Alloying with Tungstern-Molybdenum
The various precipitation is formed after the treatment of double glow plasma suriface alloying with tungstenmolybdenum.In this paper one of the precipitation,the round phase,is discussed thoroughly.The following is the main work that has been done.Firstly,SEM analyzes its chemical composition.And also the SEM back scattering image shows its microstructure and the relationship with the μ phase.Secondly,its distributing feature is introduced.At last,the deposition theory of the round phase received careful study,which is better to clarify different reasons that lead to the brittleness of the alloying samples and to improve and consummate this novel technology.
