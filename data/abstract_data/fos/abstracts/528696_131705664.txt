Effects of Different Planting Time on Yield and Sucrose Content of Yuetang NO.60
Yuetang NO.60 was bre d by Guangzhou Sugarcane Industry Research Institute. It was an excellent sugarcane variety with desirable characteristics of early maturity,high cane and sugar yields,stable yield,good agronomic traits. The results of planting experiment of Yuetang NO.60 in different planting time in Xuwen,Suixi and Leizhou showed that the mean cane yield of Yuetang NO.60 was 120 t/hm2 above,sugar yield was all over20 t/hm2,sucrose content was all exceed 16.23%,and most of agronomic characters and ecomic characters were significantly better than those of the check variety ROC22,respectively. The difference among cane yield of Yuetang NO.60 in different areas was significant while the difference among sugar and sucrose content was not significant. Cane yield and sugar yield in Xuwen were the highest while sucrose content in Suixi was the highest.The difference among the cane yield and sugar yield of Yuetang NO.60 in different planting time were highly significant while sucrose content was not significantly. Ecomic characters were the highest planted in December and the lowest was in February. The results of large area planting test indicated that Xuwen,Leizhou and Suixi were suitable for growing Yuetang NO.60.
