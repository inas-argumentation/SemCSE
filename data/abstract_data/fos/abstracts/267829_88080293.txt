Induction of protocorm-like bodies from Dendrobium huoshanense and effects of different culture methods on protocorm multiplication
Effects of plant hormone combinations on induction of protocorm-like bodies from stem segments of Dendrobium huoshanense in vitro were studied and effects of four different culture methods on protocorm multiplication were compared.The results showed that 2,4-D was the key factor for the indution of protocorm-like bodies from Dendrobium huoshanense,the optimum medium for the indution was Knudson+2,4-D 0.1 mg·L-1+KT 0.05 mg·L-1.The main culture method was shaking-bottle liquid culture,sometimes solid culture method was used during protocorm multiplication for regulating protocorm growth.The protocorm-like bodies cultured by this improved method would be used for large-scale culture in bioreactor.
