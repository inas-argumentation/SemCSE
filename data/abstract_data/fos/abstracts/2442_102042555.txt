Chemiluminescence Behavior of Ce(IV)-Tween 40-Tryptophan System and its Application
It was observed that chemiluminescence intensity of the reaction of Ce(Ⅳ)with Tween 40 was greatly enhanced by tryptophan at acidic condition.Based on the observation,a sequential injection procedure with chemiluminescence detection was developed for the determination of tryptophan.The influence of several physical and chemical parameters was evaluated;the possible reaction mechanism was discussed.An intermediate product of Tween 40 oxidized by Ce(Ⅳ)was suggested as the emitter of the reaction.A sample volume of 70 μL with Tween 40 of 5%,a 80 μL of Ce(Ⅳ)solution of 1.0×10-3 mol/L,and a 1.8 mL/min of detection flow rate were employed for the determination.Under the selected conditions,an analytical range of 2.0×10-7-1.4×10-5 mol/L,a 3σ detection limit of 8.8×10-8 mol/L and a RSD of 1.3% at 6.0×10-6 mol/L(n=9)level was obtained.The method was applied to the determination of tryptophan in amino acid injection,beer and serum,with recoveries of 91.2%-102%.
