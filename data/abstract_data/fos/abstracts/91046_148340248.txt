On the Learning Competence of Students——A Category Expected to be Valued,Discussed and Utilized
Learning competence is the basic quality of learning system,while subject learning competence is the quality of subject learning system.Stronger learning system, makes learning competence more excellent,which gets learning quality and efficiency higher. By exploring the elements and roles of learning competence,teachers can insight into the learning competence and potential of students,and solve the problem of each student by the guidance of systematic viewpoint.Students can fully develop their potential after knowing the actual state of their own learning competence elements."Learning competence education" should be the main task of learning guidance,which is the indispensable fundamental part of the quality education in our country.Therefore,the requirement of improving the subject learning competence should be included in the course syllabus as a teaching task of main course in primary and secondary schools and basic courses in colleges.
