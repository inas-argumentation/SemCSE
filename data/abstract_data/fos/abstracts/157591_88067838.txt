DNA Immunization with Fusion Gene of Ubiquitin and Epstein-Barr Virus Nuclear Antigen 1
Two kinds of expression plasmids were constructed,one of which integrated with EBNA1(pCI-EBNA1),and the other with ubiquitin and EBNA1 fusion gene(pCI-Ub-EBNA1).After transfected into HeLa cell,indirect immunofluorescence assay and Western blot could detect the expression of EBNA1 gene in both plasmids.And then,these plasmids,respectively,were used to intramuscularly immunize Balb/C mice.The serum antibodies and cytotoxic T lymphocyte(CTL)response of the mice were detected.The results showed that there were not much more differences between the two plasmids in antibody response,but the fusion gene DNA induced stronger CTL response than single gene DNA did,which indicated that Ub conjugation did improve the cellular immune response to EBNA1 in mice.
