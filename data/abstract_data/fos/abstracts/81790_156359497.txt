A Comment on the Research Papers by College Counselors
In the present study, papers concerning team construction of college counselor from Chinese Science and Technology Periodical Database (www.cqvip.com) during the years from 2006 to 2008 are classified and analyzed. Based on the analysis of topic classification, research contents, and the types of the journals in which there papers are published, the author puts forward some related suggestions with a viewof promoting the level of study on the instructor troop construction in colleges and universities.
