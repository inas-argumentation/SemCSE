Phase transformations and ordering effects in nanocrystalline carbon-containing silicon films
The phase composition, surface morphology, and crystal structure of carbon-containing Si layers obtained on silicon surface by chemical conversion are discussed. The effect of structural ordering in the grains formed in the growth figures on an epitaxial surface has been studied for the 3C-SiC/Si(111) system. The possibility of Si1 − xCx → 3C-SiC phase transitions in a layer upon structure annealing during growth is considered.
