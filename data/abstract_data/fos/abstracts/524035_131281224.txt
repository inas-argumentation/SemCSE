An Analysis of the Aesthetic Significance of Geography of Geomantic Tower
Based on historical document,taking Zhenhu Tower in Feidong for example,the aesthetic significance of geography of Geomantic Tower is studied in this essay.The study shows that the ancient Chinese architectural culture and landscape planning are important parts of Chinese culture,and geomancy is one of theoretical supports on cultural content of Chinese ancient buildings.Tower is widely distributed in China with the meaning of geomancy.The research on geomantic tower is helpful to understand the aesthetic significance of the geography geomantic elements in the landscape planning.
