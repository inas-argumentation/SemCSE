A Harmonious Relationship between Man and Nature Embodied in the Ecological Civilization
During the development of human civilization,the relationship between man and nature went through from man's obedience to the nature to man's dominion over it.During the period of industrial civilization,man and nature were confrontational.Human regarded nature as the object of plundering,resulting in increasingly serious ecological crisis.Ecological civilization implies harmony and unity between man and nature.It recognizes that nature has intrinsic value.It implements the ecological production methods and consumption methods based on its ecological science and technology.
