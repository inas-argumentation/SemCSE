Research on Hot Stamping Performance for Pillar Strengthen Part
In the hot stamping forming high strength steel plate,as high temperature material will soften,if the pressure material way is not reasonable,it can cause uneven distribution of blank-holder force.The wrinkle,laping,crack and other forming problems are easy formed during the sheet metal forming.The reasonable determination of pressure material way during hot stamping forming is especially important to product quality.By using two differen pressure material way,hot stamping pillar strengthen part for automobile was numerically simulated.And through analysis of the simulation results,it was confirmed that B type pressure material way is more advantageous than A type in high temperature condition forming.It was solved that,the sheet metal produce problem of defect,as pressure material way is not reasonable in the hot stamping process.
