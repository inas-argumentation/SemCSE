Estimation of Yield and Nitrogen Removal by Bermudagrass and Bahiagrass
The objective of this work was to develop quantitative procedures for estimating annual yield, plant N concentration, and plant N removal as related to applied N, water availability, and plant species for bermudagrass and bahiagrass. The logistic equation provided excellent correlation (R > 0.98) between yield and applied N. An exponential equation was used to relate plant N concentration to applied N. For applied N of 170 kg/ha (150 lb/acre), plant N recovery ranged from 97% for bermudagrass with high rainfall to 58% for bahiagrass with low rainfall.
