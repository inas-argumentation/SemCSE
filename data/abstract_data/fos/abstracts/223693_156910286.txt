Social Progress and Innovation of Civil Principal Part in the Song Dynasty
In ancient China,the low class was exploited for a long time on account of strict class division.But in the Song Dynasty,the class division was loosened.As a result of social development,servants,tenants,craftsmen and merchants,who had always suffered from the exploitation before,were greatly upgraded in social and economical status,transferring from the objects of Civil Law to the subjects.This was significant for ancient China stepping into the later civilization.
