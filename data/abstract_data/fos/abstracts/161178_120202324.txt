Transient mass flow rate of a residential air-to-air heat pump
Abstract A system has been designed and built that will accurately measure the change in mass of each component of a residential heat pump or air conditioner operating in a transient mode. The mass rate of flow leaving the compressor is also measured as a function of time. Using this mass rate of refrigerant flow and the change in mass of each component as a function of time, the mass flow rate entering and leaving each component of the system as a function of time can be calculated. Unlike other literature cited that gives change in mass as a function of time for a heat pump operation in the transient mode, the work reported in this Paper continually measures this change in mass while the system is in continual operation. The results look very promising for a method to obtain much needed experimental data on transient mass flow behaviour.
