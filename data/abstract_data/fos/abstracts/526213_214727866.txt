Superfluid density in disordered superconductors
Recent experiments on disordered superconductors find that the superfluid density $n_s(T)$ (or stiffness) decreases dramatically and characteristically with disorder. We describe here an approach which includes the effect of electron pair phase in a gauge invariant manner, and use it to explicitly obtain $n_s(T)$ in the presence of (static) disorder. We compare our results successfully with experiment.
