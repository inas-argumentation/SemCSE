Effective approach of improving yield of the wind swept milling
s:The effective method of increasing yield of the wind swept milling are analyzed from the influence of six primary factors, such as the filling quantity of steel sphere, the diam of steel sphere and the ratio of sphere diam, the fineness of the end product mine, the temperature of come in and come out of the wind swept milling, the ability of wind swept (wind amount of exhaust fan) and the feed capacity.
