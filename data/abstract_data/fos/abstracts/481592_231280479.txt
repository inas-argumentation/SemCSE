A paired case-control study on interferon-γ gene polymorphisms and susceptibility to pulmonary tuberculosis
Objective To explore the possible relationship between the + 874 T/A single nucleotide polymorphisms of the IFN-γ gene and pulmonary tuberculosis in Chinese Han population,and the interwas conducted.The + 874 IFN-γ T/A polymorphisms were detected in 167 pairs of subjects by the methods of polymerase chain reaction with sequence specific primer(SSP-PCR)technique.Informations on related environmental factors of tuberculosis were collected using a questionnaire designed by ourselves.Univariate and multivariate conditional Logistic analysis were conducted in the end.Results The genotype frequency of AA (OR=2.584,95%CI:1.421-4.700,P=0.002)was significantly overrepresented in case group in univariate conditional Logistic analysis.The significant association remained after adjusting environmental factors between genotype AA (OR=2.544,95%CI:1.386-4.668,P=0.003)and the susceptibility to pulmonary tuberculosis.There were not interactions of multiplicative model between AA and TA and Bacille Calmette-Guerin (P=0.212),exposure history (P=0.380)or smoking (P=0.504).Conclusion The +874 IFN-γ/AA genotype might be associated with the susceptibility to pulmonary tuberculosis in Chinese Han population,and there might not be interactions between environmental factors and the genotypes.
