Recent results on the vibrational spectra of some inorganic compounds
During the past few years a considerable fraction of our research effort has been devoted to inorganic substances. This work has benefited greatly by the availability of two pieces of equipment: (i) a small grating infrared spectrometer capable of measurement down to about 70 cm-1; ~: ii) apparatus for obtaining the Raman spectra of coloured compounds. This paperwill describe some of the results of our studies.
