An Analysis the Value Composition and Price of Tea Based on the Value Thery of Labor
The value theory of labor is the scientific theory to analysis the formation of the value in goods. Applying the value theory of labor to analyze the phenomenon of tea price,we can draw the following conclusion:the value of tea is created by labor,the production price of tea is a converted form of its value;the production price of tea is the main internal determinant of its market price,and other factors including market supply and demand are the external causes leading to the fluctuation of the market price of tea.
