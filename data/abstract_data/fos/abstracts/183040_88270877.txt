The effect of aqueous enzymatic walnut oil with salt and the optimization of hydrolysis conditions
In the basic of aqueous enzymatic technology for walnut oil extraction.The paper explored the effects of different salts and concentration on extraction rate of walnut oil.The result showed that there is obvious interface between oil and water.It's easy to pour out the oil,the oil has good quality,the protein has lower degree of denaturation.So it is benefit for the comprehensive utilization of walnut oil and protein.Based on a single and orthogonal experiment.the optimum conditions by salt effect were as follows:dosage of the enzyme 1.2%,enzymatic hydrolysis temperature 55 ℃ and time 5 h,pH 6.5.Under these conditions,the free oil yield was 63.82%.
