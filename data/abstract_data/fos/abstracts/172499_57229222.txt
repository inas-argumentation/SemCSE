The Effect of Puerarin on Antagonizing TNF-αand Renin-angiotension-II in Insulin Resistant Hypertensive rats
Objective To study the effect of puerarin(Pue)on tumor necrosis factorαand rennin-angiotension-II in insulin resistant hypertensive(IRH)rats.Methods Feeding high fat-sugar-salt diets for 8 weeks to set up the model of IRH rats.And the rats were divided into 5 groups.The changes of blood pressure, contents of fasting blood glucose(FBG), fasting insulin(Fins), serum rennin, Ang-II, TNF-αwere measured.Results Compared with the model control, groups of Captopril(Cap), Pue80mg·kg-1 and 40mg·kg-1 could significantly decrease the blood pressure, contents of rennin and Ang-II(P0.05～0.01).Pue 20mg·kg-1 hadn't significantly difference between the five groups.Cap and Pue 80 mg·kg-1 could significantly decrease the Fins and TNF-α, improve the hyperinsulinmia, promote the insulin sensitivity index lowered(ISI)(P0.01)in IRH rats.And other two doses groups of Pue hadn't any difference in the three data(P0.05).Conclusion Pue can decrease blood pressure and enhance the insulin sensitivity, decrease the contents of rennin and angiotension II in IRH rats.
