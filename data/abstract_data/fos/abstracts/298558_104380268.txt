The effect of cooking methods on some quality characteristics of gluteus medius
Meat is generally eaten after undergoing some form of heating, such as boiling, grilling, and roasting. Quality characteristics of meat and meat products are affected by meat composition and method of cooking (Bosco et al., 2001; Fabre et al., 2018). Heat treatment is one of the most common methods to achieve microbial stability and diversity of meat products. Although meat is surrounded by hot air in dry heat cooking method, such as pan frying with oil, hot liquid or steam are applied to meat in moist-heat (hydrothermal) cooking. On many applications, combinations of dry heat and moist heat treatment are implemented together (Pearson & Gillett, 1996).
