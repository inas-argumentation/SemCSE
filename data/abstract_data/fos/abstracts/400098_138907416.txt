Simulation of Hot Rolling Process for 409L Stainless Steel
Rolling pressure is one of the rolling process important parameters,reduction rate and roller diameter are important factors in rolling process.Using the DFORM-3D software,hot rolling process of 409L stainless steel was simulated.The numerical models of reduction rate: 10%,20%,30% and roller diameter: 300,350,400mm were built.Changes of rolling pressure of the different parameters and the stress and strain distribution in the rolling process were analyzed.Results show that the rolling pressure,the stress and strain changes with reduction rate and roller diameter,which presents a certain law.
