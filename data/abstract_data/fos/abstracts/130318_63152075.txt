Channel Estimation for MB-OFDM UWB Based on Channel Shortening
A novel channel estimation algorithm is proposed for MB-OFDM UWB systems based on channel shortening.A channel shortening equalizer using the structure of Cyclic Prefix(CP) in MB-OFDM is designed in front of the system,for the sake of solving the problem that channel can not easily be estimated when the length of Cyclic Prefix is shorter than that of channel.The effective channel is estimated using the received signals of shortening equalizer and original channel can be estimated by deconvolution.Simulation results demonstrate better performance can be achieved.
