Resonant photoemission spectroscopy of Cu(InGa)Se2 materials for solar cells
The electron structure of CuIn1 − xGaxSe2 single crystals is determined via resonant photoemis-sion and the main regularities of its transformation upon varying concentration x from 0 to 1 are established. The dependence of the shape of valence band spectra on the photon energy is studied. Integral photoemission intensities are shown to be determined by atomic photoionization cross sections. Processes of the direct and two-step creation of photoelectrons accompanying photoemission and the participation of internal states in the spectra of electrons from valence bands are studied. Two-hole final states in photoemission are obtained upon threshold excitation of the Cu 2p level. The strong interaction of holes leads to the multiplet splitting of these states. Partial densities of the components’ states are determined using the energy dependence of atomic photoionization cross sections.
