On the Internationalization of Science and Technology
The internationalization of science and technology shows itself in the following aspects:the international cooperation of scientists and technicians is more active; the international action of science and technology between international companies is getting developed; and the international cooperation for important project between different nations is in a more deep-going way.The development of internationalization of science and technology results from many factors.Among these. the internationalization of economy is the most important pulling force, and the international net and information is the hard foundation of technology. Face to this situation, many nations are reforming their science and technology administrative system in order to improve their competitive ability.
