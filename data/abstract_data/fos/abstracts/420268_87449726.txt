Studies on the Isozyme of Peroxidase in Development of Osmunda japonica Thund
The peroxidase (POD) isozymes in the different development stages of Osmunda japonica Thund were studied using the method of PAGE and spectrophotometer.The results showed that the penoxidase isozymes bands became more and more with the development of Osmunda japonica Thund.In the prothallial and sporogonium period,their feature isozymes bands appeared stably.
