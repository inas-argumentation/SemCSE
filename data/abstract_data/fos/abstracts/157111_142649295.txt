Mexico, Nation in Transit: Contemporary Representations of Mexican Migration to the United States by Christina L. Sisk (review)
Christina L. Sisk’s brilliant book treats contemporary migratory movements of Mexicans to the United States as they relate to identity formations inside and outside of national boundaries. Her study comprises an introduction, six chapters, and a conclusion, wherein she explores the world of migrants traveling from different areas of Mexico to the United States. She focuses her analysis on migrant representations in music, literature, and film, arguing that these visual and auditory images relate to nationalist projects as well as to conceptualizations and constructions of Mexican identity or mexicanidad.
