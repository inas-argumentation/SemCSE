The multidrug-resistant mechanisms involved in overexpression of MexAB-OprM, MexXY-OprM active efflux systems in multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa
Objective To investigate the multidrug-resistant mechanisms involved in overexpression of MexAB-OprM,MexXY-OprM active efflux systems in multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa.Methods The expression of the mexA,mexX mRNA was examined by real-time RT-PCR to judge the expression of the MexAB- OprM and MexXY-OprM efflux systems.The controlling gene,mexR and mexZ,whose products downregulate the expression of the MexAB-OprM and MexXY-OprM respectively,were amplified by PCR.Then the amplified prod- ucts were sequenced by automated ABI PRISM 3700.Results Among 25 multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aerug- inosa,14 strains overexpressed the MexAB-OprM efflux system(56%),3 overexpressed the MexXY-OprM efflux system(12%).Three of them overexpressed both the two efflux systems simultaneously.Among 8 strains overex- pressing MexAB-OprM,5 carried mutations in mesR and had the amino acid substitution in MexR protein,1 had terminal cede at the position of amino acid 32 and 2 had intact mexR gene.All the 2 strains overexpressing MexXY-OprM carried mutations in mexZ and had the amino acid substitution in MexZ protein.Conclusion The overexpression of MexAB-OprM,MexXY-OprM active efflux systems in multidrog-resistant Pseudomonas aerugino- sa,which was caused by the mutations in mexR,mexZ respectively,was one of the multidrug resistance mecha- nisms.Other mechanisms also existed.
