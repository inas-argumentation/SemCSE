Study on extracting of grape seed oil and procyanidins (OPC′s) by supercritical fluid extract-CO_2
The optimal condition of SFE, with the parameters of extracting pressure of 30MPa, temperature of 45℃, moisture content about 6%,duration of 80min, were received in extracting grape seed oil by supercritical fluid extract-CO2. The extraction rate of procyanidins increased when methanol or ethanol was used as co-solvent, and the former was better than latter. However, the total extraction rate is still quite low. The result of TLC analysis showed that the extraction were mainly such monomers as (+)-catechin.
