Removing False Matching Points Algorithm Based on Feature Points
Accuracy of transformation matrix is usually affected by false matching points.More seriously,this status can cause failure of image registration.In order to remove false matching points,this paper proposes a algorithm based on the existence of false matching points in SIFT feature matching.True matching points of two registration images to be matched is similar in coordinate difference,and the coordinate difference of error matching points is different from the coordinate difference of true matching points.According to this discipline,false matching points can be removed by the difference between coordinate difference.Finally,the proposed algorithm is illustrated by different images.Experiment results show that error matching points in registration image are effectively removed and the complexity of time is significantly reduced comparing with RANSAC algorithm.
