Effect of Fertilization on the Wood Properties of Poplar 69
In order to investigate the effect of fertilization on wood quality of Poplar 69(Populus deltoides cv.Ⅰ69/55),the key wood properties (fiber length, fiber width, double wall thickness, lumen diameter of fiber, wall thicknesslumen ratio, microfibril angle and basic density) of 20 Poplar 69 trees of 12 years old from 4 plots subjected for different fertilization treatments were determined and analyzed.The results showed that fertilization treatments had significant effects on double wall thickness at 01% level, lumen diameter of fiber at 5% level, wall thicknesslumen ratio at 01% level and microfibril angle at 1% level. No significant effect of fertilization had been found on fiber length, fiber width and basic density.These results provided some information for the silviculture of poplar plantation.
