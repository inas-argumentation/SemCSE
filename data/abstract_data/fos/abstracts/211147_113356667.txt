Riser System of Flywheel Casting Modified by Proportional Solidification Contraction Modulus Method
Using the contraction modulus method to modify the riser system of Type K31×06-32 flywheel, the concrete countermeasures are as follows: we use a 140 mm ×350 mm duck month riser to replace original three 150 mm ×350 mm rises, runner is semi-circle and ingate is flat ladder-shape. The defects have not been found in casting. The yield ratio is increased from 82.7% to 92.9%.
