Sport: The way to modernization of Chinese and Chinese society ——A review of "Marrow of the Nation: A History of Sport and Physical Culture in Republic China"
The author reviewed Dr.Morris's new work "Marrow of the Nation: A History of Sport and Physical Culture in Republic China",and pointed out the following: Dr.Morris applied the theory of nationalism to analyze the sport and physical culture in Republic China and considered that sport played an important role in the formation of modern China and modern Chinese,which has provided a new way of thinking for the research of sports history and sports theories.
