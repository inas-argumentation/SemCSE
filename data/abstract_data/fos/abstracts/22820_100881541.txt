EXTRACTION EQUILIBRIUM CHARACTERISTICS OF DICARBOXYLIC ACIDS WITH TRIALKYLPHOSPHINE OXIDE AS EXTRACTANT AND 1-OCTANOL AS DILUENT
Extraction of oxalic acid, propandioic acid, succinic acid, maleic acid and malic acid with trialkylphosphine oxide(TRPO) dissolved in 1-octanol was studied.The experimental results showed that the complexing extraction equilibrium constants were dependent on pK a1 and hydrophobicity of the organic acids. By Fourier transform infrared spectroscopic (FTIR) analysis, it was found that the extraction process was mainly governed by hydrogen bonding solvation. There is hydrogen bonding between TRPO and 1-octanol, which retards the extraction process.A 1∶1 complex between TRPO and organic acids was formed according to mass action law, and a general expression of experimentally accessible extraction loading on the molar concentration scale was developed. Extraction equilibrium constants were obtained by correlating experimental data with the least square method. The calculated loading and organic acid concentrations were in good agreement with the experimental ones.
