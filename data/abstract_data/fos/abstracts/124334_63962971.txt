Implementation and Improvement on the IGI Measurement Algorithm of Network Available Bandwidth
This paper discussed in detail the IGI measurement algorithm and its implementation with C/C++.After analyzing the deficiency of this algorithm,a modified available bandwidth measurement algorithm was proposed.Through a huge number of network experiments it was found that the proposed algorithm of measurement could get measurement results more promptly,give more accurate estimation to network bandwidth, and yield more steady results.Furthermore,the proposed algorithm tends to be capable of rapidly reflecting changes of bandwidth and so improves the capability of tracing bandwidth changes.
