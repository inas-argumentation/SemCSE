IL-2 regulates CTLA-4 expression on human peripheral T cells
Objective To investigate the expression of CTLA-4 on human peripheral T cells and the regulatory effect of IL-2. Methods Before and after incubation of human PBMC with IL-2, both intracellular and surface expression of CTLA-4 in T cells were analyzed by Flow cytometry. Levels of CTLA-4 mRNA were determined by semiquantitative RT-PCR. Results There was no expression of CTLA-4 on the surface of human inactivated T cells. However, 7.6%-18.0% T cells expressed CTLA-4 intracellularly and the positive rate is some higher in CD4+ T cells than that in CD8+ T cells. The CTLA-4 del TM mRNA had a shorter half life than the full length CTLA-4 mRNA. The increased expression of CTLA-4 was dependent on the up-regulation of transcription of CTLA-4 mRNA in T cells induced by IL-2 and most of the T cells were CD25+. Conclusion CTLA-4 is mainly stored intracellularly to regulate the activation of human T cells. The immunosuppression effect of IL-2 may be correlated with the up-regulation of CTLA-4 expression on human T cell resulted from its stimulation on the transcription of CTLA-4 mRNA. [
