Application of Generalized Regression Neural Network in Predicting the Vibration Signal of Power Transformer
The vibration signals of power transformer are closely related to the operation conditions of winding and core.Based on the analysis of vibration mechanism of power transformer,the generalized regression neural network is applied to obtain the relations between the 100Hz component and voltage signal and current signal of power transformer,then the vibration signal can be predicted accordingly.The experiment platform is built to obtain the voltage,current and vibration signal to test the generalized regression neural network and to predict the vibration signal with the obtained neural network.The predicted vibration signals are fit well with the practical vibration signal,which verifies the effectiveness of the proposed method.Then the online monitoring of operation condition of power transformer could be easily realized.
