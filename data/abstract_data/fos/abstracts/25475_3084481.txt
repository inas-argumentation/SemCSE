Ground State Properties of Fluxlines in a Disordered Environment
A new numerical method to calculate exact ground states of multifluxline systems with quenched disorder is presented, which is based on the minimum cost flow algorithm from combinatorial optimization. We discuss several models that can be studied with this method including their specific implementations, physically relevant observables and results: (1) The N-line model with N fluxlines (or directed polymers) in a d-dimensional environment with point and /or columnar disorder and hard or soft-core repulsion; (2) the vortex glass model for a disordered superconductor in the strong screening limit; and (3) the sine-Gordon model with random pase shifts in the strong coupling limit.
