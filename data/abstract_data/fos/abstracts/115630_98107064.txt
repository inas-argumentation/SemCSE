Synthesis of 2,2-dialkyl(phenyl)-4-(2-hydroxyethyl)thiadiazinones
2,2-Dialkyl(phenyl)-4-(2-hydroxyethyl)thiadiazin-5-ones are formed as a result of the reaction of thioglycolic acid with 2-hydroxyethylhydrazones. The structures of the reaction products were proved by PMR and 13C NMR spectrometry and the mass spectra.
