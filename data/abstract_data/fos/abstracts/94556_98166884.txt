Electrical Characteristics of Ti/Al Ohmic Contacts to Molecular Beam Epitaxy-Grown N-polar n-type GaN for Vertical-Structure Light-Emitting Diodes
We investigated the electrical properties of Ti(30 nm)/Al(200 nm) contacts to molecular beam epitaxy-grown N-polar n-GaN with different carrier concentrations. Samples with carrier concentration of 1.2 × 1018 cm−3 showed nonohmic behaviors when annealed at 300°C, but ohmic at 500°C and 700°C. All samples with carrier concentration of 2.0 × 1019 cm−3 exhibited ohmic behavior. x-Ray photoemission spectroscopy (XPS) results showed that, for samples with carrier concentration of 1.2 × 1018 cm−3, the Ga 2p core levels shift to lower or higher binding energy upon annealing at 300°C or above 500°C, respectively. Scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM) results showed that, for samples with carrier concentration of 1.2 × 1018 cm−3, a wurtzite AlN layer (∼2 nm thick) formed at the metal/GaN interface when the samples were annealed at 500°C. An interfacial wurtzite AlN layer also formed upon annealing at 700°C, but its thickness was ∼4 nm. Based on the XPS and STEM results, the ohmic contact formation and degradation mechanisms are described and discussed.
