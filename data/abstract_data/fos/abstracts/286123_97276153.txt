Application of E.S.R. spectroscopy to the study of solvation
The use of e.s.r. spectroscopy as a tool to study solvation is reviewed. Specific attention is given to the detection of ion-pairs involving radical anions and cations. The effect of protic solvents on e.s.r. parameters for radical anions and neutral radicals is discussed especially for dialkyl nitroxides. The use of ionizing radiation to generate paramagnetic species in specific environments is also proposed as a useful method for studying solvation.
