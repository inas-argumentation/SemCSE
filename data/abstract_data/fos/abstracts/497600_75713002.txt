The study of T_H1/T_H2 and Tc1/Tc2 balance and cytokine changing in the pathogenesis of hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome
Objective To investigate the change of T H1 like and T H2 like cells balance and cytokine level in HFRS patients. Methods The levels of IFN γ, IL 4 and TNF α in serum were detected by sandwich ELISA. IFN γ + or IL 4 + T H cells and Tc cells in PBMC from febrile or convalescent phase patients were analyzed by immunofluorescence staining and triple color flow cytometry (FCM) after 4 hour PMA stimulation in vitro . Results The levels of IFN γ, IL 4 and TNF α significantly increased obviously in the serum of HFRS patients. The levels of IFN γ and IL 4 are 13.7 fold and 2.4 fold higher respectively than that of healthy individuals. FCM analysis showed that average percentages of T H1, T H2, Tc1 and Tc2 are 27.8%, 17.5%, 27.6% and 19.6% in PBMC from HFRS patients versus 12.4%, 14.5%, 7.1% and 7.0% in healthy individuals PBMC. The ratios of T H1 to T H2 and Tc1 to Tc2 are 2.10 and 1.70 respectively. The percentages of T H1, T H2, Tc1 and Tc2 in PBMC from febrile phase of severe patients showed more obvious change. Conclusions The levels of IFN γ and IL 4 in serum as well as the percentages of T H1, T H2, Tc1 and Tc2 in PBMC were all increased in HFRS patients especially in febrile phase. It suggested that cellular immunity in HFRS patients is shifted to T H1 type immunity.
