Study on Spheroidization Technology of Passivation Magnesium by Mean of Composite Package
Passivation magnesium granules is obtained when its surface is coated by one type of special heat resistant coating layer. As a spheroidizer, it is added to reaction room of spheroidization package, joined quantity by 0.3%. Iron liquid and passivation magnesium react through reactive room to complete spheroidization treatment, gets success used in production QT500 7. Comparing With irruptive method of rare earth magnesium , each ton of iron can save spheroidization treatment cost 45 Yuan.
