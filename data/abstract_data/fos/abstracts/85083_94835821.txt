Electric conductivity, dielectric permittivity and Raman scattering spectra of Li3Fe2(PO4)3 single crystals
Abstract The electric conductivity (σ), the dielectric permittivity (ϵ') in wide frequency and temperature ranges, and Raman scattering spectra in the range 20–1250 cm −1 have been investigated in Li 3 Fe 2 (PO 4 ) 3 . The anomalous behaviour of σ and ϵ' at 487 and 5 23 K and the change of the combination scattering spectra are connected with α↔β and β↔γ phase transitions. Relaxation and resonance type dispersions of σ and ϵ. In dielectric is connected with hopping conductivity of Li + ions. The jumping frequencies of Li + ions at 400 and 525 K are 5.4 × 10 4 and 2.3 × 10 7 Hz, respectively. Low-frequency vibrations of the crystal lat tice leads to resonance type dispersions of σ and ϵ'.
