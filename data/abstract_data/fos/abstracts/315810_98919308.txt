Laboratory study on removing polycyclic aromatics from extract oil of furfural refining vacuum distillate oil
Using furfural and assistant, the single stage extraction experiment is studied on removing polycyclic aromatics(PCA)from extract oil of Karamay vacuum fourth oil. The influences of the mass ratio of solvent to oil, extraction temperature, assistant dosage, extraction time and settling time on the yield and PCA content of refined oil are investigated by using single factor experiment. The study results show that the influences of extraction time and settling time on the yield of refined oil and PCA content are slight, and when the both are 20 min, the extraction process can achieve balance basically. Increasing the ratio of solvent to oil, extraction temperature and assistant dosage can reduce PCA content of refined oil. When mass fraction of assistant is 25%, extraction temperature is55 ℃, extraction time and settling time are 20 min, and the mass ratio of solvent to oil is 5, the PCA content of the refined oil is less than 3%, which conforms to EU requirement.
