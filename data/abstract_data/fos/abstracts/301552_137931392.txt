Effect of Morphology and Granularity Distribution of Molybdenum Powder on Strength of Green Compact
The bending strength of green compact made from four classes of molybdenum powder with different granularity was tested by three point bending method and the morphology of testing sample was observed.The effect of the morphology and granularity distribution of molybdenum powder on the strength of green compacts was studied.It is shown that the morphology and granularity distribution of molybdenum powder can be effectively controlled by adjusting two-reduction process.Under the condition of the same Fisher sub-sieve particle size,a powder consisting of non-single particle size,the higher the content of fine particles in the powder,the higher the intensity of the green compacts.
