A method to reduce contamination and uptake of lead by plants from car exhaust gases
Splashing and/or washing plant shoots with aqueous solutions of the chelates CaEDTA (max. 0·5%) or Na-polyphosphate (max. 0·5%) is an effective way to reduce contamination and uptake of lead by plants in areas where emission of airborne lead occurs. If the decomposition of chelates in the rhizosphere is prevented, they are also effective in reducing lead uptake by the plant roots.
