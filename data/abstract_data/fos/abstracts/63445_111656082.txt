Third Harmonic Voltage on Generators and Operating Values of Stator－Ground fault Protective Relay
This paper introduces and analyzes the 3ed harmonic voltages measured on generators.The transfonro' and PT will increase the 3hi harmonic voltage at the terminal of generator, and the high resistance connected to the neutral point will decrease 3 harmonic voltage on it was testified through the test .Some suggestions are given to the stator-ground fault protective relays based on the third harmonic voltages.
