Development and Application of a High Precision Vibration Feed Stream Inoculation Device
In order to improve the feed precision of vibration stream inoculation device,the feed precision and the overall accuracy improve through program-controlled weighing,on-line closed-loop feedback adjustments and the use of adjustable feed assembly,vibration damping base components,pneumatic sealing components,and other technical measures and the external shocks arising from the non-normal blanking in the non-working hours was also solved,thus avoids the inoculant waste.In November,2007,this project was put into production in WEICHAI POWER CO.,LTD and obtained the good results.The independent research and development project has applied or the national patent of invention.
