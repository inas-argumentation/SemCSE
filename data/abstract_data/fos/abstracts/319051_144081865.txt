Note on Wais Vocabulary Performance by Young and Old Adults
An analysis of the WAIS Vocabulary performance of 56 young adults (Mage about 19 yr.) and 39 old adults (Mage about 77 yr.), equated for educational level, showed that the average performance of the younger group was significantly higher. An examination of the responses of the two age groups to specific items suggested generational change in the degrees of understanding and the kinds of referents for several words.
