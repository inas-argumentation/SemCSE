On the Legal Issues Concerning China’s Import and Export Food Safety
The essay deems that food safety matter has become a global problem affecting people all over the world, which is worsened by the increasing international food trade. It is argued that China should establish and improve its import and export food safety system under the current situation. Then it is suggested that China enhance its international communication on food safety and system and aim at protecting global food safety. China should also establish the food safety system according to the principles of promoting international food trade and in favor of settling international food safety disputes.
