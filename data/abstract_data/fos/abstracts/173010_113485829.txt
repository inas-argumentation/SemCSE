Analysis and Countermeasure to Anomalous Partial Coking in Furnace Hopper of Tangential Circle Firing Boiler
It is often occurred anomalous partial coking in furnace hopper of tangential circle firing boiler with storage pulverizing system.The coke residue volume could rise to height mark and the boiler could recover after decoking when load down.During operation,only slight coking around the burners are found out through fire hole and the furnace temperature is even.After the bolier stops running,it is found the cokes stick to burner band are not serious and provided anomalous partial coking not from bad burning arrangement.By analyzing the coal-blelnding method,it is concluded that asymmetrical blending and blending ration change caused by startup or stopping pulverizing system are the main causes to form coke.The coke problem is solved after adopting corresponding measures.
