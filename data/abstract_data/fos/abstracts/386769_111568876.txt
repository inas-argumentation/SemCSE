Study on Application of Vibratory Membrane Technology in Waste Mineral Oil Disposal and Regeneration
In comparison of foreign and domestic design methods for treatment of waste mineral oil,it is found that at present the majority of processes cannot satisfy relevant energy saving and environmental requirements.In this paper a widely used,proven,reliable,environmental and energy saving new type waste mineral oil regeneration technology,i.e.vibratory membrane waste mineral oil treatment process,is introduced.This technology has already been widely used in Hong Kong and sees good application prospects.
