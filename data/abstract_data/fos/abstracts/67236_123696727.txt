Spectroscopy diagnosis of hydrogen plasma excited by helicon-wave
The hydrogen plasma was excited by the technology of helicon-wave plasma chemical vapor deposition(HWP-CVD).The characteristics of plasma have been investigated by using the vacuum ultraviolet-visible emission spectroscopy acquired in this experiment.The influence of experiment parameters on the relative density of excited hydrogen atoms has been analyzed from hydrogen Balmer lines,and their influence on the vibratinoal temperature of hydrogen has also been studied through the analysis of hydrogen Fulcher band lines.By analyzing the electron temperature,the electron density,the relative rate coefficient,the experimental results indicate that the states of low pressure hydrogen plasma can be diagnosed by the corona model the density of excited hydrogen atoms increases with increasing the input power and decreases with increasing pressure,while the vibratinoal temperature of hydrogen molecules increases first and then decreases with increasing pressure the electron temperature and electron density are the key factors for the variation of low pressure hydrogen plasma states.
