Optimal Futures Innovation in a Dynamic Economy: The Discrete-Time Case
We study the optimal futures innovation in a dynamic economy and investigate how the results obtained in a static economy should be modified due to the possibility of dynamic trading. We analyze three different notions of ex ante optimality, depending on the authority's ability to redistribute wealth among the investors and characterize the optimal futures innovation in each sense. Journal of Economic Literature Classification Numbers: D52, D60, G10.
