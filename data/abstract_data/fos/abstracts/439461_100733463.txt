Determination of 12 Isomers of p-Nonylphenol in Groundwater by Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry
To determine isomers of pra-nonylphenol in groundwater, separation conditions of gas chromatography-mass spectrometry( GC-MS) were optimized and 17 isomers were identified from a technical pra-nonylphenol reference material; mass ratios of the isomers were calculated by gas chromatography-flame ionization detection( GC-FID) and area normalization method; and 12 main isomers were detected using an internal standard quantitative method. Groundwater samples were prepared by adjusting solution's pH 2 and following liquid-liquid extraction procedure with dichloromethane. The results showed that linear correlation coefficients( R2) of the 12 isomers were all 0. 99 when the total nonylphenol concentrations were within200-1000 μg / L and 1000-8000 μg / L,respectively. For 1-liter groundwater,the method detection limits of the 12 isomers ranged 9. 01-41. 4 ng / L; the method recoveries ranged 69. 4%-129. 0% and relative standard deviations were 0. 6%-14. 0%( n = 5). The method is more sensitive and high reliable and has been used for the analysis of groundwater samples successfully.
