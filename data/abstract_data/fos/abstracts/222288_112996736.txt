A Discussion on Application of HDPE Membrane and Geosynthetic Clay Liner(GCL) in Environmental Protection
With the sustainable development of economy in China,to pursue green GDP has been placed on the agenda and to protect environment is one of the crucial work of state and all levels of government.It is more and more important that managing the relations between economic development and environmental protection,never exchanging the economic development by disastrous final of giving up environment and developing the economy necessarily under the preconditon of protection environment.
