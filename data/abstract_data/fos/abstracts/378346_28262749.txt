Electron delocalization and "aromatic" behavior in cyclic polysilanes
-The cyclic permethylpolysilanes (Me2Si) and other cyclic alkylpolysilanes have some properties whicI resemble those of aromatic hydrocarbons: they have strong ultraviolet absorption bands, form electron delocalized anionand cationradicals, give charge-transfer complexes with pi acceptors, and show substituent effects on disubstitution. The nature of chemical bonding responsible for these effects in polysilanes is discussed.
