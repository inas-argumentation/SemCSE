Ultrasonic-assisted Extraction Process of Ferulic Acid from Sapindus
The ultrasonic-assisted extraction process of ferulic acid was investigated from the flesh of sapindus by ethanol aqueous.The crushed samples were treated by different concentration of ethanol solution and ultrasonic treatment and then extracted by ethyl acetate.The content of ferulic acid was detected by HPLC.Improving the extraction efficiency of ferulic acid from the flesh of sapindus,the effects of ethanol concentration,ratio of solid to liquid,temperature,ultrasonic time and power on extraction yield were investigated.Based on the single factor results,the treatment conditions were optimized through orthogonal test.The results showed that the order of the primary and secondary factors for the rate of ferulic acid was the ratioethanol concentrationultrasonic timeultrasonic power.The optimal extraction conditions were 40% alcohol concentration,1 ∶ 5 solid-liquid ratio,45 min ultrasonic time and 160 W power,the extraction efficiency of ferulic acid was 0.034 2±0.001 2 mg/g on optimum technological conditions.The optimal extraction technology is reasonable,reliable.It is available reference to further research and comprehensive utilization of ferulic acid in Sapindus.
