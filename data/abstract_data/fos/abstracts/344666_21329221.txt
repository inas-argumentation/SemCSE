On computing of arbitrary positive integer powers for one type of tridiagonal matrices of even order
This paper is an extension of the work [On computing of arbitrary positive integer powers for one type of tridiagonal matrices, Applied Mathematics and Computation, to appear], in which the general expression of the l th power ( l  ∈  N ) for one type of tridiagonal matrices of order n  = 2 p  + 1 ( p  ∈  N ) is presented. In this new paper we derive the general expression of the l th power ( l  ∈  N ) for the same type of tridiagonal matrices of order n  = 2 p ( p  ∈  N ). Expressions of eigenvectors and Jordan’s form of the matrix, and of transforming matrix and its inverse are given, too.
