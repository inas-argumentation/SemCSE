Release of hexazinone from pronone 10g granules exposed to simulated rainfall under laboratory conditions
Abstract The release of surface coated hexazinone, 3‐cyclohexyl‐6‐(di‐methylamino)‐1‐methyl‐1,3,5‐triazine‐2,4(1H,3H)‐dione, from a new granular formulation, PRONONE 10G, was tested under simulated rainfall. The release of hexazinone from PRONONE 10G granules treated with 1.3, 2.5 and 5.1 cm of rainfall were 65.5, 87.4 and 91.5 % with CV of 8.7, 2.9 and 5.4 %, respectively. Comparing with the conditions tested in this laboratory study, the amount of rainfall required for a 90% release of hexazinone under field conditions is estimated to be less than 5 cm of intermittent rain complemented with overnight dew over a period of time. Based on a sample of the PRONONE 10G formulation, a single granule weighed 20.9 mg (11% CV, n = 600) and contained 10.7% hexazinone (15.6% C.V.), 7% higher than the label 10% hexazinone content of this granular formulation.
