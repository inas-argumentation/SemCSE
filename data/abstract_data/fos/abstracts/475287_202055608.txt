Numerical Simulation of Multistage Incremental Forming in Sheet Metal
Aiming at a cone with convex platform and inclination of 30°,incremental forming processes of multistage and single stage were simulated based on Abaqus.The thickness distribution and equivalent strain of each stage were obtained and the springback was also analyzed.The results show that the multistage forming quality was better than single stage and thickness was more uniform.The plastic deformation area of blank was increased and crack was delayed in multistage.In addition,the springback of multistage was obviously less than single stage.
