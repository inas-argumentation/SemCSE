TiO_2-SiO_2 Nanoparticles Synthesis and Determination of Ribonucleic Acid by Second-Order Scattering Method
The TiO2-SiO2 nanoparticles were synthesized by sol-gel method.The structure of the nanoparticles was characterized by XRD and TEM.The interaction of TiO2-SiO2 nanoparticles with ribonucleic acid(RNA)was studied.A new second-order scattering method for the determination of RNA has been developed based on the enhancement of second-order scattering of TiO2-SiO2 nanoparticles in the presence of RNA.Linear relationship between the second-order scattering signals and the concentration of RNA was observed in the range of 0.005-5 mg/L with a detection limit of 1.46 μg/L.The proposed method has been applied to the analysis of synthetic samples,the recoveries were in the range of 99.4%-103.9% with the relative standard deviations of 0.19%-0.24%.
