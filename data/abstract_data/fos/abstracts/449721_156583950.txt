Study on the Comparision of the Finance and Economics Universities Scientific Research Strength and Countermeasures of Promoting Our University Scientific Reserch Level
According to domestic Finance and Economics universities' nature of subject and characteristics of scientific research,and the quality identified of academic journals by relevant institutions,finance and economics universities' scientific research strength is compared by 11 Class A and 50 Class B journals selected as the domestic Chinese academic authoritative journals,which concluded that:(1) 2000-2009,the number of our university's papers published in the authoritative journals has showed an increasing trend year by year,especially after 2002,the rate of increasing is relatively large;(2) 2005-2009 in five years the mean number of class A and B papers published as a measure of research strength of the observations,according to three the number of clusters of Finance and Economics University of 30 cluster analysis between different groups found significant differences between university research strength.Finally,by analyzing the current main problems about our university's scientific research,countermeasures are putted forward to enhance the level of scientific research.
