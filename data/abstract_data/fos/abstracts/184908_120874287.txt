THE EFFECT OF CORRELATED ERRORS OF MEASUREMENT ON CORRELATIONS AMONG TESTS: A CORRELATION FOR SPEARMAN'S CORRECTION FOR ATTENUATION
An equation comparable to Spearman’s correction for attenuation, which does not depend upon the assumption that error scores are uncorrelated with true scores and with other sets of error scores, is derived. It is shown that when the assumption of zero correlation among these components is tenable, the equation reduces to the one given by Spearman.
