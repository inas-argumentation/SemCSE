Modules of constant Jordan type from Galois extensions of local fields
In this paper, we describe the Galois module structure of $J=\mathbf{K}^{\times}/\mathbf{K}^{\times p}$, where $\mathbf{K}$ is the maximal $p$-elementary abelian extension of a local field $\mathbf{k}$ containing a primitive $p$-th root of unity; in fact, we show that $J$ is a module of constant Jordan type, with stable Jordan type $[1]^2$, which, in a way, extends the result of Minac and Swallow. Also, we take profit from our proof and compute the cohomology groups of the Galois group $Gal(\mathbf{K}/\mathbf{k})$ with coefficients in $J$, which leads us to give a description by generators and relation of modules which are stably isomorphic to the Heller shifts of the trivial module.
