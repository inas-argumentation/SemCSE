Thomas Legler, Sharon F. Lean and Dexter S. Boniface (eds.), Promoting Democracy in the Americas (Baltimore, MD: Johns Hopkins University Press, 2007), pp. x+360, $27.00, hb.
Promoting Democracy in the Americas begins where Tom Farer’s formative book, Beyond
Sovereignty : Collectively Defending Democracy in the Americas (Baltimore, MD: Johns
Hopkins University Press), left off in 1996. Where the latter established the growing
role of international organisations in democratisation, Promoting Democracy reveals
how these organisations are now integral to understanding this process. Along with
the Organization of American States (OAS), the United Nations, the United States
and other major powers, non-governmental organisations and the spread of norms
all play a part in shaping the very nature of democratic institutions.
