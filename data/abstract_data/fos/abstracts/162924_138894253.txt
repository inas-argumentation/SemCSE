Preparation of Fe-based Bulk Amorphous Alloy by Suction Casting Method
With processes of arc melting alloy ingots,induction melting and conventional copper mold casting,a plate Fe-based bulk amorphous alloy in Fe-Co-Zr-Mo-W-B system of the thickness of 1mm was prepared.The surfaces of the samples have the typical metallic luster,and the fractures of the samples are agleam and have the typical glassy fracture.Tested with differential thermal analysis (DTA),the Fe-based amorphous alloy in study shows distinct glass transition temperature and large supercooled liquid region(T_g=884K,△T_x60K),which means the alloy has high Glass-Forming ability.
