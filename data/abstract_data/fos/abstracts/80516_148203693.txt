Academic Orientation in Cultivating the Master of Education in China and Its Countermeasures
Nowadays the cultivation of the Master of Education tends to be academic orientation in varying degrees,owing to the habitual effects of cultivating model of Master of Pedagogy.In order to highlight the practical and applicable cultivation features of Master of Education,it should stick to the practical training objective of professional degree education,by dealing with the relationships properly between practical orientation and academic orientation in cultivating Master of Education;perfect the curriculum teaching systems of training practical talents,by reinforcing the practical process of curriculum teaching;build double-quality teachers team,by emphasizing the supervisors’ practical quality as well as the academic quality.
