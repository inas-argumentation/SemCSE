The Educational Life of Solidarity and the Cultivation of Citizens' Public Spirit
Without reflection on solidarity,its educational meaning has been wear down and turned to be narrow and vulgar.Solidarity educational life is the public one based on fraternal spiritual.Therefore,it is the life of friendly,trust,and respect from each other.Solidarity means every student should develop their personality and ability,instead of self-abandonment or sacrifice in the name of collective goal.Solidarity dispels hostility between different groups by abandonment of the word us,instead of forgiveness inside and repel outside.Not like the gather strengthen in short in group activities,solidarity should be a lasting cooperation and forgiveness,and be a stable living condition.Solidarity educational life accepts the strangers by conception of others,and presents publicity of educational life,which could have the function of citizens enlightenment.
