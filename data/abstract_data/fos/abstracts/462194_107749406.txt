BRIEF COMMUNICATIONS: Efficient operation of a quasicontinuous dye jet laser with a high pulse repetition frequency
An experimental study was made of the operation of a quasi-cw dye laser pumped by the second harmonic of a cw-pumped YAG:Nd3+ laser. A high conversion efficiency (~20%) and a narrow spectral width of the emitted line (< 1 pm) were achieved. An analysis was made of the feasibility of using resonators with dispersive prisms as selective elements in such dye lasers.
