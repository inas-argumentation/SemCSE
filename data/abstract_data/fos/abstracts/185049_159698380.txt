New Ideas for a Public Service Government
To build up a public service government is both an inexorable trend in the development of history and an inner demand for better government services. It is the only way to further structural reforms and be in tune with the developments of economics and societies. We should have a better understanding about a public service government both in theory and in practice. The aim for a service government is to provide people public products and public services and to make the most of the limited public resources. It is a brand-new administrative pattern composed of some main factors such as law government, e-government and efficiency government. A public service government is changing its pattern from the government - centered to the public-centered.
