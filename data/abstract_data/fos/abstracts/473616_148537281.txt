Case study of the running of domestic and foreign multi\|campus universities
Since the mid of 1980s, with the amalgamation and the expansion of higher education institutions, multi\|campus universities have become more and more common. Thus at present, our overwhelming concern is how to plan the overall development, how to locate each campus, what mode of management to adopt. The introduction and analysis of the running of domestic and foreign multi\|campus university might be helpful to the above problems.
