Utilization of neutron diffraction and Mossbauer spectroscopy in the studies of the cathode for SOFCs
Neutron diffraction and Mossbauer spectroscopy were used to characterize the perovskite type cathodes, in terms of the crystal structure, oxygen occupancy, magnetic moment, and Fe valence state. It was found that a lower symmetry trigonal space group P-3C1 gave improved fits to the data including the matching of a peak not fitted in the R3¯c symmetry. A significant lattice distortion from rhombohedral to cubic was observed in La 0.60 Sr 0.40 FeO 3− δ (L6SF) quenched from 1200 °C.
