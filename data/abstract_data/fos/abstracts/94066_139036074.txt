Finite Element Analysis of Forward Extrusion Cavity Die for Deep Hole and Thin Wall Shell
Without the assumption of equally distributed load and thick-wall cylinder,the stress and deformation analysis were made on forward extrusion cavity die for deep hole and thin wall shell by FEM.Based on the result of FEA,a relation curve chart of shrink-fitting range,shear stress and outside diameter-b of prestressed ring was found.By this chart,the useful data of optimizing design will be found easily,which can provide the more accurate reference for the design of combination cavity die.
