Valuing theoretical research or applied research: the conceptual demarcation of founding and running universities
The emergence of local universities has significantly propelled the development of higher education since the 19th century . Meanwhile, a number of disorientations have appeared resulting from the mere imitation of the key universities’ developing ethos. The paper gives a profound analysis of the two different kinds of developing ethos in universities: value learning or technique. It is concluded that key universities should adhere to the ethos of the integration of learning and technique with stress on the former while local universities the integration of the two aspects with focus on the latter.
