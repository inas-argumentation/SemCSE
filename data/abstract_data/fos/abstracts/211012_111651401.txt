Design for Information Management System of Tailings Storage Security Monitoring
Tailings storage is a required facility for maintaining normal production of mine enterprises,information management system can be designed for data on-line collecting,off-line input,on-line analyzing and off-line analyzing in automatic manner,and this system has friendly human-machine interface,it allows the customs observe and analyze various monitoring data from the different aspects,it can search for information of sensor and basic data of tailings storage,the measured result can be shown in report and figure forms,it can be saved and printed,it also can send out the early-warning information in forms of sound,light and message to maintain the security of normal production of tailings storage.
