Konzentrationsanderungen bei blattfarbstoffen unter dem einfluss von luftverunreinigungen. Ein diskussionsbeitrag zur pigmentanalyse
The value of pigment changes as an early indication of the effects of air pollution on plants is discussed on the basis of experimental results and the literature. If variation of pH-values in the cell by SO 2 , HF or HCl remains unconsidered, changes in the concentration of chlorophylls as well as an eventual decomposition of these pigments are unimportant. It is therefore not possible to use such changes for the development of an indication technique to recognise influence of air pollutions. On the other hand, changes in the concentration of β-carotene following the action of pollutants is always significant, as is shown by the present results. It is suggested that this substance, and other yellow pigments occurring in the chloroplast, should be studied further in relation to the effects of different types of pollution.
