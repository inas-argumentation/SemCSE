The Effects of Succus Bambusae from Phyllostachys Pubescens and Phyllostachys Viridis on the Antitussive and Expectorant
Objective To investigate the antitussive and expectorant effects of succus bambusaes from P. pubescens and P. Viridis.Methods Citric acid-induced in guinea pig and ammonia-induced in mouse cough models were conducted to observe the antitussive efect of succus bambusae. Tracheal phenol red test in mouse and the tracheal mucociliary transport activity of rabbit trachea slice in vitro were used to investigate their expectorant effects.Results Succus bambusaes of P. pubescens and P. Viridis could prolong the incubation period of cough and reduce the frequency of cough and increase the tracheal excretion of phenol red and speed up the tracheal mucociliary transport, obviously compared with that of the control group.Conclusion Succus bambusaes of P. pubescens and P. viridis have antitussive and expectorant effects.
