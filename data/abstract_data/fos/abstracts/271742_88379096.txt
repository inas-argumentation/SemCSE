Analysis and Evaluation of the Nutritional Components in Aquilaria sinensis Seed
The nutritional components in the seed of Aquilaria sinensis were analyzed in this paper.The results suggested that there were high contents of nutrients such as crude fiber,crude fat,crude protein,and nucleoside.There were also abundant minerals and trace-elements.The seed of Aquilaria sinensis represented a potential nutritive supply with high application value,it could be used for oil,food,fodder and other industries.The results provided a scientific basis for further exploitation and utilization of the seed of Aquilaria sinensis.
