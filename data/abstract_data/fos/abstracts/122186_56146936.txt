Cotype and absolutely summing homogeneous polynomials in ${\cal L}_{p}$ spaces
We lift to homogeneous polynomials and multilinear mappings a linear result due to Lindenstrauss and Pe"czy nski for absolutely summing operators. We explore the notion of cotype to obtain stronger results and provide various examples of situations in which the space of absolutely summing homogeneous polynomials is dieren t from the whole space of homogeneous polynomials. Among other consequences, these results enable us to obtain answers to some open questions about absolutely summing homogeneous polynomials and multilinear mappings onL1 spaces.
