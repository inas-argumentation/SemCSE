Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Duplex Stainless Steel Under Different Heat Treatment Temperatures
Using the optical microscope, scanning electron microscope and electron microscope, the change of microstructures, mechanical and corrosion properties of duplex stainless steel under different heat treatment temperatures were analyzed. The results show that, by increasing the heat treatment temperatures, both hardness and strength of the steel increase, but the elongation decreases; by reducing Mo content, increasing the Cr and C concentration and increasing heat treatment temperatures, the mechanical properties and corrosion resistances of the duplex stainless steel largely enhance.
