Series hybrid power electric vehicle motor control system
In order to meet the transportation demands of Beijing Olympic Games,a concept of auxiliary power electric vehicle was put forward on the basis of hybrid electric vehicle.The protective circuit and driving motor′s control system were studied by using intel 80C196KC singleship as the main control unit and IGBT(insulated gate bipolar transistor) as the power device.The results indicated that the drive efficiency of vehicle loading power was increased and the driving course was prolonged significantly through adding auxiliary power unit.
