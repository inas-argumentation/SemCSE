Application of residue butylene dehydration-rectification purification process in 2 500 t/a methyl ethyl ketone unit
The residual butylene in discharge tail gas in 2.5 kt/a methyl ethyl ketone unit is recycled by dehydration-rectification purification process.The results show that the water content in treated residual butylene reduces to 0.000 6%,and there is no sec-butyl alcohol,sec-butyl ether or methyl ethyl ketone.The treated residual butylenes can meet the demand of feedstock of methyl ethyl ketone unit and can be recycled.
