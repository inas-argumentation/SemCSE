A Study of the Cooperation between Agro-technicians and Farmers Based on Evolutionary Game Model
In this study,we have investigated the cooperation between agro-technicians and farmers based on the assumption that both sides on gaming are rational.The results suggest that the gambling process has two possible evolutionary stable strategies.One is that both sides adopt"non-cooperation"strategy while the other is otherwise and leads to the optimal evolutionary stable strategies. Which evolutionary stable strategies the gambling process converges to depends on the total cost and incremental benefits.Therefore,gambling process will converge to the optimal evolutionary stable strategies if the cost is reduced and revenues are increased.
