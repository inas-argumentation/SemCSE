Observation of new nuclides37Si,40P,41S,42S produced in deeply inelastic reactions induced by40Ar on238U
Atomic numbers and masses have been identified for the fragments 10≦Z≦18 and 20≦A≦45 produced in the reaction40Ar(263MeV)+238U. Four new neutron-rich nuclides,37Si,40P,41–42S have been observed as a result of deep inelastic collisions. The nuclide identification combined two independant time of flight measurements as well as two (Δ E×E) informations and was quite unambiguous.
