Numerical algorithms for solving nonlinear L р-norm estimation problems: part II - a mixture method for large residual and illo-conditioned problems
The nonlinear least squares algorithm of Gill and Murray (1978) is extended and modified to solve nonlinear L р-norm estimation problems efficiently. The new algorithm uses a mixture of 1st-order derivative (Guass-Newton) and 2nd-order derivative (Newton) search directions. A new rule for selecting the “grade” r of the p-jacobiab matrix Jp was also incorporated. This brought about rapid convergence of the algorithm on previously reported test examples.
