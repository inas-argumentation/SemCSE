A Study on the New Model and Characteristics of Anhui Rural Folk-custom
Since the implementation of reform and opening policies in China,the countryside folk-custom has presented some new features,but there also exist some vulgar habits for some reasons.Therefore,the province should beef up its construction of folk-custom in the following aspects: strengthening the rural cultural infrastructure construction,enhancing rural cultural education,further improving farmers' ethical quality,developing folk-custom tourism,and mobilizing farmers' enthusiasm in folk-custom construction.
