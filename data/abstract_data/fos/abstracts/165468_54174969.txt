Research on Mechanical Parametric Design System Based on C-K Theory
C-K theory as an innovative design theory and combination the characteristics and deformation of the design. Into the product variant design will make the parametric mechanical design modeling more rigorous and standardized. The paper has carried on the second development on the Solidworks platform. On the basis of this, the modular intelligent design platform of NC grinding machine is realized. An innovative design method of GL-KXM series milling and grinding machine's parametric, series and intelligent assembly is presented.Realize the automatic generation from the user needs to the 3D entity model,and the feasibility of the method is verified by an example of software.
