Abrasive Wear Resistance of HVOF Sprayed Cr_3C_2-NiCr Coatings with Different Parameters
Cr3C2-NiCr coatings were prepared on low-carbon steel surface using high velocity oxy-fuel (HVOF) spraying technology with different parameters. The abrasive wear performance of the coatings and mild steel were studied by wet rubber wheel abrasive tester. The abrasive wear resistance of the coatings deposited under different conditions was researched. Cross section of the coatings was observed by metallurgical microscopy. Hardness of the coatings was tested by micro-hardness tester. The results show that wear mass loss of Cr3C2-NiCr coatings increases linearly with wear distance. Wear properties of the coatings are better than that of mild steel. The coatings deposited under different conditions shows dissimilar wear resistance. The coating sprayed in 38 L/min flow rate of fuel gas shows better wear performance and the laminated microstructure appears in the cross-section, and micro-hardness of the coating is better.
