Development Situation and Thinking of Landfill Ecological Restoration Technology
The construction and use of landfill not only take up a lot of land resources but also pollute the surrounding environment and residents.There are many different practices of ecological restoration at home and abroad.Plant repairing is the most common method.Covering over the landfill surface of different materials after the landfill is sealed to reduce the adverse effects on plants growth.Then select the appropriate plant species for ecological restoration.On the basis of the research in different cases at home and abroad we advance to clear the orientation of landfill restoration ecological firstly,then we can select the corresponding ecological restoration design and should strengthen the repairing process management at the same time.
