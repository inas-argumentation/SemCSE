Sunspot Equilibria and Efficiency in Economies with Incomplete Financial Markets: A Remark
I consider economies with incomplete financial markets and nominal assets. I construct an economy where some sunspot equilibria strictly Pareto dominate a subset of nonsunspot equilibria and are not Pareto dominated by any nonsunspot equilibrium. Equilibria in the nonsunspot economy are not Pareto ranked. Journal of Economic Literature Classification Number: D52.
