Geochemistry and mineralogy of Welsh early Llandovery (Silurian) sediments in relation to brachiopod assemblages (preliminary report)
Preliminary results are reported of a study of the chemistry (29 variates) and mineralogy (5 variates) of 99 samples of brachiopod-bearing (46 taxa) early Llandovery (Silurian) clastic sediments from Wales. The first three eigenvectors of the combined faunal/chemical/mineralogical correlation matrix are dominated by chemical variates; the first eigenvector is an index of chemical enrichment interpreted as related to original clay content. There is little evidence of correlation between brachiopod faunas and sediment chemistry/mineralogy.
