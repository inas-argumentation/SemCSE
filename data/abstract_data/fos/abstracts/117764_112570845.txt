Research on Capacity Increasing Modification of Lines from Three Gorge Power Station to Gezhouba Converter Station
With the development of power transmission between Hubei and Hunan province,the power lines from Three Gorges power station to Gezhouba converter station could be overloaded.Basing on present grids and planning near Three Gorges power station,the paper takes a study of capacity increasing modification of the lines.The achievements give references for project implementation.
