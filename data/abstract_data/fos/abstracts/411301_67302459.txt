Factors Affecting the Adoption of Knowledge Management Technologies: A Discriminative Approach
The increasing importance of knowledge management has prompted many researchers to examine facets of this important topic. However, the nature of the variables influencing the adoption process is not well understood. This study surveyed IT executives to determine the relationship among organizational, environmental, and technological variables and the adoption of knowledge management technologies. The results suggest that organizations most receptive to knowledge management technologies view information technology as strategic to their organization, have experience with user-centric technologies, and a commitment to relatively high IT investment in the past.
