Study on Fusing Genetic Algorithm into Ant Colony Algorithm
Propose a new ant colony system (ACS) for obtaining optimal value of continuous space. After comparing the advantages and disadvantages between genetic algorithm (GA) and ACS and analyzing their fusion basis, propose two new strategies of fusing GA into ACS: one is using genetic algorithm to obtain some rough solutions to a problem and then fast obtaining the more precise solutions Xbest by ACS, the other is that using crossover operator in GA generates two new travel paths in ACS in order to improve the global search ability of ACS. Based on above two ideas, two new hybrid ant colony systems based on GA are built, which are called GA-HACS-I and GA-HACS-Ⅱ. At last, verify the correction of GA-HACS-I and GA-HACS-Ⅱ from converge speed, hit rate and computational accuracy by test function Rosenbrock and test function Shubert.
