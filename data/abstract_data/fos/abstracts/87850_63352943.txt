Neural Network Modeling of Larval Survivorship of Spodoptera litura F.
BP neural network may approximate non-linear functions with desired accuracy. This paper deals with neural network modeling of larval survivorship of Spodoptera litura F. It was found that BP neural network could be effectively used to model the larval survivorship-time relationship. Some results for dynamic process of larval survivorship were obtained. Matlab codes for BP network model were given. The features of neural network modeling, and the factors influencing the efficiency and goodness of modeling were discussed.
