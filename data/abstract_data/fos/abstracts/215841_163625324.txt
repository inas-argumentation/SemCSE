The Influences and Transforming Effects of Loyal-Filial Concept on Chinese Ancient Novels
China is an ethics-oriented country, loyal-filial concept having long been rooted deeply among the Chinese and effected ancient novels. There are indications that the influences and transforming effects of loyal-filial culture on Chinese ancient novels are in-depth and comprehensive, because of which these stories have become more popular and widespread, as can be seen from the Chinese ancient myths.
