The Return of the Classical Tradition and the Reconstruction of Upbringing Education
Significance of upbringing education hasn't been understood completely.Upbringing education is important for passing on the cultural spirit of human beings and nations;it's also the requirement to develop the personality in the present Chinese society,and to form the spirit of citizens whose traditional values were depraved gradually.To claim the return of classical tradition and the reconstruction of upbringing education,agree with the internal demand of the time and nation's development,which is helpful to reconstruct the present educational spirit.
