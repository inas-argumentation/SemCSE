Beautiful Personality of Mao ZeDong's Poetry
The paper brings the basic esthetics principle-which style is like person to bear on Mao ZeDong's poetry, and believes that there is beautiful personality in Mao ZeDong's poetry, such as the splendid ideal and faith, the fine philosophic wisdom, the full-bodied love for the superb people, the vigorous and firm verve, the free and easy demeanour, and the simple and unadorned style.
