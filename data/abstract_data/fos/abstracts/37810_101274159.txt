Preliminary research on synthesis of cationic starch flocculant by dry way
The process for synthesizing cationic starch flocculant by dry way is studied. The results show that the optimum conditions are as followsthe mass fraction of water in reaction system is 35%, the mixing temperature of cationic etherifying agent and sodium hydroxide is lower than 10℃, the molar fraction of cationic etherifying agent to starch is 0. 35, the molar fraction of sodium hydroxide to cationic etherifying agent is 1. 4, the reaction temperature is 90℃, and the reaction time is 4 h. Under these conditions, the relative viscosity of cationic starch is 2. 0.
