The Influence of Optimization Technology on Search Engine
Introduces the relation between Website and search engine,and the effect factors of Websites construction on search engine,then discusses how to build a Website that is easy to search from following aspects: naming,title and the function of Tag.When search engine visits the Website,it can grasp the essentials of the page quickly,and grasp full information from page,so that users can acquire more information and relevant to the content of the site when search.
