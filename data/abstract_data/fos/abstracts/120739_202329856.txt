Efficiency Evaluation of Green Economy of Anhui Based on BCC-DEA Model
Energy crisis and environmental issue are the serious challenges in the process of China's economic development.As a province with huge energy consumption,the coarse developing mode has affected economic efficiency of Anhui.Based on the theories of classical production function,this paper carries out an empirical analysis on efficiency evaluation of Anhui's green economic efficiency through building a BCC-DEA model with a new input index — consumption of carbon.It shows that DEA efficiency basically keeps high level and regularly changes for every 1-2years.However,the DEA efficiency remains low in several years.In fact,the heavy fixed-asset investment in 2008-2010 reduces the efficiency.Finally this paper puts forward some policy suggestions such as maintaining the continuity and stability of economic policy,adjusting industrial structure,improving the capability of innovation and establishing the environmental accountability.
