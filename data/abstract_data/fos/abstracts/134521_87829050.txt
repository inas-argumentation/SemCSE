Study on transduction of mammalian cells with recombinant adeno--associated virus 2 {\sl in vitro}
A recombinant adeno-associated viral vector,rAAV2-Luc,which contained firefly luciferase gene,was constructed and prepared. The dose-effect relationship,the competitive inhibition of rAAV2,the antagonistic effect of heparin and the effect of butyrate on luciferase expression levels with rAAV2-Luc in vitro were studied.The results showed that,in a certain extent,the higher the MOI,the higher the luciferase expression levels.But the expression levels were decreased when the MOI was higher than 1×10~ 7. Butyrate could remarkably intensify the luciferase expression levels by rAAV2.The luciferase expression was specifically inhibited by heparin.The two kinds of rAAV2 containing different genes had remarkable competitive inhibition. This study has guidance in some degree to the study of gene transfer by rAAV2 vector.
