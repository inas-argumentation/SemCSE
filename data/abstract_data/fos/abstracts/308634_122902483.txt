Effect of strongly perturbed magnetosphere on the cosmic-ray cutoff rigidity
Comprehensive determination of the cosmic-ray cutoff rigidity in strongly perturbed periods has been performed for a number of stations located at different latitudes. To calculate the geomagnetic cutoffs by tracing the trajectories of cosmic-ray particles in the magnetic field of the magnetosphere, the model of strongly perturbed magnetosphere was used [1–3]. The diurnal anisotropy of the geomagnetic cutoff rigidity in the minimum of Dst variation in the geomagnetic field has been estimated.
