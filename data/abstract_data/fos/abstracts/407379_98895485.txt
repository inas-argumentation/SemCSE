Study on Biological Effects of Amphetamine on Growth of Microcystis
The kinetics process of the effects of Amphetamine(AMP) on the growth of Microcystis at different conditions were investigated. Results indicated that the growth of Microcystics can be prompted at lower concentration in basic condition, and it was inhibited at the higher concentration of AMP. The 24 h, 48 h, 72 h and 96 h EC_(50) of AMP to Microcystics are 24.52 mg/ L,16.51 mg/ L,10.76 mg/ L and 9.60 mg/ L,and toxicity of AMP to Microcystics increased with time.
