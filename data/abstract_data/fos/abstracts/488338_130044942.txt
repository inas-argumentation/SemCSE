SEMI-ANALYTICAL SOLUTION FOR ONE-DIMENSIONAL CONSOLIDATION OF LAYERED-SOIL WITH NON-DARCIAN FLOW
Based on the non-Darcian flow law proposed by Hansbo in layered-soil,the numerical solutions for one-dimensional consolidation of layered-soil was obtained under time-dependent loading by a semi-analytical method.The reliability of the semi-analytical solution for one-dimensional consolidation of layered-soil with non-Darcian flow was verified by comparing the results of double-layered soil with non-Darcian flow law by semi-analytical method with that by finite difference method.Finally,a calculation case of one-dimensional consolidation of triple-layered soil with non-Darcian flow law was analyzed.The results show that the rate of excess pore water pressure dissipation is different from that of the settlement during the consolidation of layered-soil with non-Darcian flow.
