Study on Microstructure of Grate Bars and Its Oxide Film
The phases of original grate bars consist of α-Fe,Cr7C3 and small amount of γ-Fe.There was network carbide on substrate.Oxide films of grate bars consist of FeCr2O4,Cr2O3,and Fe2O3.There were micro-cracks between oxide films and substrate.While,the oxide films are dense and thin when adding some Al into the original grate bars.
