Potential of water conservation in chestnut and tea mixed woodland
We studied the effect of different canopy densities on the potential of water conseravation in chestnut and tea mixed woodland.The result indicated that the maximum water-holding capacity in layer of deadwood and defoliation in different canopy density woodlands is 298.58～340.34%,thereinto,B5(pure chestnut woodland)B4(chestnut canopy density of 40%～50% in chestnut and tea mixed woodland)B3(chestnut canopy density of 30%～40% in chestnut and tea mixed woodland)B2(chestnut canopy density of 20%～30% in chestnut and tea mixed woodlands)B1(pure tea woodlands);Available water conservation capacity in layer of deadwood and defoliation is 9.14～18.85 t·hm-2,the maximum water storage capacity in forest soil is 2569.80～3531.60 t·hm-2,the maximum is B4,secondly is B3,the minimum is B5;Water conservation capacity in forest soil is 291.00～828.60 t·hm-2,the maximum is B3,next is B4 and the minimum is B5.From all of the water conservation capacity,smaller proportion of potential is in layer of deadwood and defoliation,from different density forests,the vast majority is in soil layer,B3 have the maximum water conservation potential and next is B4.
