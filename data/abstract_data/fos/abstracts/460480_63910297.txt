Real-time Rendering Method for Indoor 3D MMOG
As the scene of indoor3DMMOG is complex and the objects in the scene move frequently,the present methods cannot meet the requirement of real-time rendering.Thus,this paper puts forward a suitable fast-rendering method for the indoor3DMMOG.It adopts a new scene-structure which is a mixture of BSP-tree and dynamic-Octree,at the same time,it uses expanded bounding box rather than rendering the invisible dynamic objects to reduce the cost of the updating.The simulation results show that this method can accelerate the rendering of the scene of indoor3DMMOG,and be more suitable for the requirement of real-time rendering.
