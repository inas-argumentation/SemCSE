High Performance Liquid Chromatographic Method for the Determination of Allicin Using Ethylparaben as Substitute of Allicin Reference Standard
A HPLC method that use ethylparaben as substitute of allicin reference standard was established for the determination of allicin.A allicin solution(purity 99%)collected by preparative HPLC,was used as reference solution to determinate the corrective factor between alliicin and ethylparaben in a special HPLC system.The analyzing time of the roposed method was shortened from about 30 min to about 10 min.This shows ethylparaben is better than buthylparaben as a substitute of allicin reference standard.The method can be applied to the determination of allicin content range from 0.018 to 2.9 g/L.The method is simpler and more rapid and more promising than EP5.0 method.
