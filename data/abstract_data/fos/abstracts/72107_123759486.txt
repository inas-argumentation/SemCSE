Research on Motion Blur Image Fusion Based on Wavelet Transform
Because of relative moving between taking pictures and imaging devices,the obtained images are always blurred.To remove the motion blur and improve the image restoration,this paper applies the bilinear interpolation,the directional differentiation operator and the method in which the range of motion direction is identified roughly and then the precise value is determined.The method has many advantages,such as less computation,high precision,good stability and wide application.Because the pixels of blurred images are high relative,this paper adopts differential and self-related technologies to determine the blur extent accurately.The point spread function can be made by the motion blur direction and the blur extent,and then restored images can be obtained by different filter methods.A few restored images are fused by the different image fusion methods.The experimental results illustrate that the fusion images,which are obtained by the fusion method of the motion blur image based on wavelet transform,gain better objective evaluation and subjective visual effect.The proposed method has certain practical value.
