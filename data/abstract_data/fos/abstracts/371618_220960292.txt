Phosphorus characteristics and loss risk assessment under typical land utilization in the suburbs of Wuhan.
Phosphorus (P)as the restricting factor that leads to water eutrophication is always given to attention with the surface waters environmental problem getting obvious in China. Based on the field survey the soil phosphorus contents in the suburbs of Wuhan,the soil phosphorus characteristics under different land utilizations were studied and the loss risk was analyzed. Results showed that there was significant difference in the content of soil phosphorus under different land utilizations. As to various lands use type,the order of different soil phosphorus content is as vegetable soil paddy field the other dry lands nursery. In the upper soil layer of 0～20cm under different land use type,the content of total phosphorus was 255.4～1763.1mg/kg,and the mean was 975.4mg/kg. The content of Olsen-P was 1.3～164.2mg/kg,and the mean was 56.4mg/kg and the content of NaOH-P was 33.4～910.7mg/kg,and the mean was 204.1mg/kg. The content of Olsen-P was 56.0mg/kg was critical value that could be used to evaluate the loss risk of soil phosphorus. There were 34.9% in all soil samples that exceeded 56.0mg/kg and 93.3% of them were vegetable soil.
