Numerical Simulation and Optimization Research of the Casting Technology the Sheep Shearer Fork by Lost Wax Casting
The filling processes of different casting-technologies for sheep shearer fork in lost wax casting process are numerically simulated by ProCAST software.Casting defects such as shrinkage cavities and porosities are predicted by analysis of flow field and temperature field.Simulation results on various gating systems indicated that the optimized process designs were determined,which remarkably reduced the generation of shrinkage cavities and porosities.
