A note on the pseudo-fermion representation of a spin 1/2 or 1
Using Abrikosovs pseudo-fermion representation of a single spinS with pseudofermion energy λ=0 we describe a procedure, which allows calculation of thermal statistical averages using standard methods, including the linked cluster theorem of diagrammatic perturbation theory, for the casesS=1/2 andS=1. In particular we derive a relation relevant to the conduction electron Green's function in the Kondo-problem of dilute magnetic alloys.
