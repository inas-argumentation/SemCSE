Preparation of enzymatic degradation of bagasse enzymatic properties of xylooligosaccharides
Response surface optimization of hydrolysis conditions: The amount of enzyme 0.82%, crude xylan concentration 8.4%, hydrolysis temperature 46 ℃, hydrolysis time 4.5 h, XOS yield 43.55%. TLC analysis: Composition and structural characteristics shows xylobiose, xlyose. Infrared analysis shows structural features with XOS; HPLC shows products were xylobiose main content of 85%.
