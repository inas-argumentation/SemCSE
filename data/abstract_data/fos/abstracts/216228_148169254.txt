The value tropism of campus culture construction
To the essence, campus culture construction is a kind of value pursuit and creation. And the value orientation of campus culture has something to do with the following factors: campus tradition, function, trend of the era, vision of leadership, constitute of faculty, component of students, and so on. To grasp the value tropism of campus culture construction, which has special significance as the universities in our country are facing profound transformation at present, the author believes we should do like the followings: strengthen humanities atmosphere in the process of valueing the construction of campus hardware; hold the mainstream value in the process of value diversification; keep academic character in the process of social marketazation; retain the quality of personality in the process of standardization of education; maintain the quality of elite in the process of massfication; keep overall quality education in the process of specialization of disciplines; hold noble character in the process of secularization.
