Rapid detection of glucose with ultraviolet spectrophotometry
A new method for fast detecting glucose was established by using ultraviolet spectrophotometery.In the catalyzing of glucose oxidase,glucose can generate hydrogen peroxide,which is strong oxidizing,a yellow complex was generated by the redox reaction between the hydrogen peroxide and potassium titanyl oxalate(PTO).Results demonstrated that glucose concentrations were proportional to absorption values of the complex at the maximum absorption wavelength of 382 nm.A favorable linearity was presented in the range of 0.05 mg/mL to 0.75 mg/mL.The linear coeffciency was 0.996,the relative standard deviations are from 1.67%,And the recovery ratios are be tween 99.87% and 100.39%.This method was simple,reliable,and could be used for fast detecting glucose in samples.
