Establishment of the cell line producing a recombinant Epstein-Barr virus expressing gfp
Objective To establish a cell line which produces gfp recombinant Epstein-Barr virus. Methods Construction of an EcoRⅠcassette expressing humanised gfp and puromycin-resistance (pGFPURO). Construction of a vector for insertion of the gfp-puromycin cassette into EBV (pGFPUROEB). pGFPUROEB was electroporated into B95-8 cells and generated recombinant Epstein-Barr viruses by homologous recombination. Fluorescence microscopy and FACS were used to test for gfp expression. PCR and Southern blot were applied to detect gfp gene and recombinant virus DNA. Results Following puromycin selection, up to 80 percent of the transfected B95-8 cells expressed gfp (called gfpB95-8) and were shown by PCR to contain the gfp coding sequence. Southern blot analysis confirmed the presence of gfp recombinant Epstein-Barr virus genomes. Conclusion The results show that the cell line containing gfp-recombinant Epstein-Barr viruses has established. [
