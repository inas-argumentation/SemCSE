GAA substitution JMD on Anhui White Goat growth performance and blood biochemical index
A total of 120 younger Anhui White Goats were randomly divided into 4 groups. Coarse fodder was the straw and ensiling. Concentrate feed was used as food supplement. additive JMD was added into concentrate feed in a proportion of 1 to 1000 in treatment group to study its influence on white goat's growth performance and blood biochemical indexes. The result showed that between 30-day group and 60-day group, daily gain was significant difference(P0.01), and BUN( blood urea nitrogen) and ALP(alkaline phosphatase) were also significant difference(P0.05). The evidence indicated that average weight daily in Test A( with addition of JMD)was significant different with that in Control A(P0.01). The same as AST(P0.01) for body to absorb nutrient to promote growing in rational feeding time(30 days).
