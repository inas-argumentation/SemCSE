Design and Implement of Interactive Platform between Parents and Schools in Digitized Education
Discoursed the design and implement of interactive platform between parents and schools in digitized education, and this platform adopted building block design, distributed architecture, centralized management and advanced technology to realize some kinds of functions such as message-browsing and inquiry, net discussing, message-leaving and so on.Moreover, it provides the function of message management based on roles for administrator, so it is an interactive platform for communicating between parents and schools, which is perfect in function, expandable, low in use of resources, efficient and secure.
