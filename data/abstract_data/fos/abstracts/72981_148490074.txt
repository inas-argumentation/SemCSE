University town:new attempt of higher education development in developed regions of China
In recent years, university towns have been thriving in some developed regions in China. They are the outcome of sufficient participation and collective endeavor of multifarious aspects, among which government, universities and society play significant roles. University towns have been making multiple contributions to higher education's reform and development. Nonetheless, some prevalent problems occur such as planning, investment and management in the process of development. Therefore, principles like harmonious development, programming first, system innovation and scientific management should be followed in the development process of university towns.
