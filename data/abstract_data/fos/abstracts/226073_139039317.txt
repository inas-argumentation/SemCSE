Non-isothermal Crystallization Kinetics of Zr_(55)Al_(10)Ni_5Cu_(30) Bulk Metallic Glass with Different Microstructure
Zr55Al10Ni5Cu30 bulk metallic glasses with different microstructure were obtained by changing the melting time of the master alloy ingots,and the non-isothermal crystallization kinetics was investigated by differential scanning calorimeter.The results show that the two Zr55Al10Ni5Cu30 bulk metallic glasses with different microstructure have the similar apparent activation energy of crystallization.The reason is that the structure of the primary phase of the bulk metallic glasses is different from their local ordering structure,which results in little effect of the size of medium and short range ordering on the nucleation and growth of crystallization phase.
