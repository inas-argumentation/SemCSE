Investigation on Postembryonic Growth of Apis mellifera L.
In Jianghui region of Anhui province,the postembryonic growth period and weight of Apis mellifera L.were as follows:The queen was 15 38 days (355～376 h) and 231 8 mg,the worker bee was 20 49 days (477～498 h) and 109 3 mg and the drone was 23 87 days (560～583 h) and 189 5 mg,respectively.With the changes of anniversary seasons,the postembryonic growth course of three types of Apis mellifera L.existed differently.No siginificant defference of external form was found between zygotes and non zygotes.In larve period without closing cell,the difference of three types of differentiation and larva's growth existed due to the change at forage.The appearance and change of compocind eyes,abdominal jiont,sting and fine hair were important characteristics of worm stage.In larva stage without closing and early worm stage,bee grew with five ecdysises.
