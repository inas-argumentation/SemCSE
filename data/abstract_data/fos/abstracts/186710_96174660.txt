Li-diffusion and lattice relaxation in the 1-D superionic-conductor beta-eucryptite (LiAlSiO4)
Abstract The 1-d Li+ conductor β-eucryptite (LiAlSiO4) was studied by quasi-elastic neutron measurements. From the coherent scattering we can show that correlations between the Li+ ions are extremely important up to the highest investigated temperatures (T≤800°C). The diffusion of individual ions was studied by incoherent scattering. For the Li+ a jump diffusion along c with mainly a jump-distance of c/3 was confirmed.
