Purification and Characterization of Bactrocerin-2 from Oriental Fruit fly Bactrocera dorsalis Hendel
Bactrocerin-2(20-residues)was purified from the immunized pupae of oriental fruit fly Bactrocera dorsalis Hendel by cation-exchange chromatography,RP-FPLC and RP-HPLC.It was an isoform of Bactrocerin-1.Molecular mass of Bactrocerin-2 was determined as 2 311.51 u by MALDI-TOF-MS.Complete amino acid sequences was VTKTWIKVIRGIGKSKIKWA.It showed very high similarity to C-terminal sequences of Coleoptericin.The composition of amino acid residues revealed that Bactrocerin-2 was a hydrophobic,positively charged peptide.
