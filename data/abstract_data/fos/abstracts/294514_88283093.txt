Solid-state fermentation process of AMP deaminase produced by Aspergillus melleus
In order to investigate Solid-state fermentation process of AMP deaminase produced by Aspergillus melleus,optimization and regulation for the enzyme producing conditions.Through relevant experiments,it was achieved the optimum conditions are the initial p H5.0,inoculum 20%,temperature 30 ℃,stromal thickness 2 cm,Polystyrene 25%,the initial moisture content 70%,the batch replenishment quantity 0.5 m L/g,time 64 h.Under the above technological conditions of fermentation the enzyme activity of AMP deaminase can reach to 12000 U/g,And it was produced 100000 U/g commodity AMP deaminase,be placed in domestic to lead level.
