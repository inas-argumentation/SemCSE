The permutation module on flag varieties in cross characteristic
Let $$\mathbf{G}$$G be a connected reductive group defined over $$\mathbb {F}_q$$Fq (the finite field with q elements, where q is a power of the prime number p), with the standard Frobenius map F. Let $$\mathbf{B}$$B be an F-stable Borel subgroup. Let $$\Bbbk $$k be a field (may not be algebraically closed) of characteristic $$0\le r\ne p$$0≤r≠p. In this paper, we completely determine the composition factors of the permutation module module $$\mathbb {M}(\text{ tr })=\Bbbk \mathbf{G}\otimes _{\Bbbk \mathbf{B}}\text{ tr }$$M(tr)=kG⊗kBtr (here $$\Bbbk \mathbf{H}$$kH is the group algebra of the group $$\mathbf{H}$$H, and $$\text{ tr }$$tr is the trivial $$\mathbf{B}$$B-module). In particular, we find a large family of infinite dimensional absolutely irreducible abstract representations of $$\mathbf{G}$$G over $$\Bbbk $$k.
