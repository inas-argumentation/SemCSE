Educational Effects on Social Mobility in Rural China:Based on the analysis of empirical materials of status background
Education is a classical element in analyzing social position and social mobility while individual education level and their social status have a cause-and-effect relationship.As a result of the difference in economic status and the natural environment and so on,there exist many differences for the rural population in the aspects of the opportunity of accepting higher education as well as the realization of social mobility.These differences bear the characteristics based on historical culture and rural society.
