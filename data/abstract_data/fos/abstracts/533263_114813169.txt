Production Techniques of Germany YJ105A Towing Motor Bed
The difficult point of domestic production techniques of Germany YJ105A towing motor bed was very strict demand of material quality,and the factory standards on tensile strength and low temperature impact strength were higher then that in the standards of GB and ISO.By means of selecting fine raw materials and properly design chemical composition and strict conducting the nodularization and inoculation treatments of nodular iron as well as carefully processing the heat treatment,domestic production of YJ105A towing motor bed was successfully achieved.The results showed that the domestic produced YJ105A towing motor bed can completely replace the one import from Germany.
