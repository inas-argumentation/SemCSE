On the value orientation of university curriculum and harmonization of the relationship between various value orientations
The accordant viewpoint about the value orientation of university curriculum is “two kinds of orientation view”, that is “indvidual-based value orientation” and “society-based value orientation”. However, it could not expound the reality of the value orientation of university curriculum, and is also helpless for harmonizing the relationship between various value orientations also. A three kinds of orientation including the knowledg-based value orientation changes the frame of the value orientation of university course and makes the different value orientations a reasonable and integral structure. The key to harmonize the relationship between various value orientations is to deal correctly with the relationships between the demands of individual development and that of social development and knowledge development.
