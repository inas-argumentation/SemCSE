Failure Analysis on Spring Used in High-speed Railway Locomotive
Aiming at the fracture failure of spring used in high-speed railway locomotive,combining with the production process of spring coil company,the failure fracture was analyzed by scanning electron microscopy,metallographic examination and hardness testing.The results show that a second martensite appears on the flat system for the over heating and fast cooling in grinding process,which can play a major role in the failure.As a minor reason,there is alumina inclusion on the crack initiation which is 120 μm away from the surface.
