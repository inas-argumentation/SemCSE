On the succession of the middle vocational education and the higher vocational education
The succession of the middle vocational education and the higher vocational education includes layers of both form and content, with the succession of teaching programs as its nucleus. The formal succession of the middle vocational education and the higher vocational education should reflect the characteristics of occupation and promote the orientational functions of the ?°Qualification Certificate for occupational Post. The design of the teaching programs should be systematically taken into overall consideration in order to ensure the quality of the talents cultivation and the educational efficiency which would make certain guarantee to the successful realization of the succession.
