Research on Cutter Radius Compensation for 5-axis Peripheral Milling
Peripheral milling is a kind of 5-axis CNC milling mode.It mainly depends on the change of cutter′s posture.In order to deal with the problem of space cutter radius compensation for 5-axis peripheral milling,a new method was presented.This method is used in work piece coordinates system,giving full consideration to variable milling conditions,and is totally independent of the specific machine type.In this paper,an algorithm for radius compensation of a single block,a discriminant for the corner type and a transition processing method for the inside and outside corner are included.Besides,this paper also presents the theoretic analyze of the errors and the simulated results.The results indicate that the new compensation method can meet the system machining requirements.
