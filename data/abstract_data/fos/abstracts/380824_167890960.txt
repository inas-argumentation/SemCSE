Research on the Application of Enterprise Credit Management Based on Webwork+Spring+iBATIS
To establish the corporate credit management system supervised by Inspection and Quarantine Bureau is the only way to integrate with the international practices,strengthen the quality and security supervision of import and export products,and improve the international image and market competitiveness of China's enterprises and products.Based on the brief introduction to frameworks of Webwork,Spring and iBATIS,as well as the discussion of design ideas and module design,accomplishes the corporate credit management system.
