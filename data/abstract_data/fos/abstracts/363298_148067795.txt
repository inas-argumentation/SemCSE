Rethinking of the Views of Knowledge,Teacher-student Relationship and Teaching in the Case Method
Under the influence of the traditional views of knowledge,teacher-students relationship and teaching,teachers played a leading role in the precious case method.This not only caused the gradational and separate teacher-student relationship,but also made the case method become a closed teaching tool which was only used to teach knowledge by teachers.The case method under the new views of knowledge,teacher-students relationship and teaching was different.It emphasized that the knowledge was the product of co-construction,so teachers and students should organize the class together,and share ideas equally.This not only made teachers and students become the interconnected and equal partners increasingly,and co-create knowledge in the open cooperation and dialogue,but also made the case method become an extensive and profound revolution about the educational concept and practice.
