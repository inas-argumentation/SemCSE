Survival and development of Wushu with scientific spirit and humanitas spirit
The society must fellow the law, which is "survival of fittest", but some kinds of people, who engage in Wushu, may not do it. They attempted to hold the concepts of "handing down in a direct line from the master" and "orthodox school", by which the survival power of Wushu is threatened with the development of modern society.In the digital era of marketing economy,there is the new explanatory notes and conceptions of the survival and development of Wushu with the scientific spirituality and humanitas spirituality,which is the very urgent problem to be solved at present.The development of Wushu is in a dilemma.First,although Wushu has the value of keeping people being in good health,there is only a very small group of people to play it and even there is not enough theory on health to support it.The second,although Wushu has the value of entertainment,there are very few audiences to watch the Wushu routine performance in it's match.The third,although Wushu has the value of attacking,"art of composition" is scarce in the match.And the value,which is application of Wushu to the actual combat,is scarce.Conceiving the route of the development of Wushu is needed to be done.It is the kernel of thoughts on development of Wushu to embody the humanitas spirituality in eastern culture.It is the operating route for the development of Wushu to keep the "honest and simple" and to be the "gentle" in the modern society.
