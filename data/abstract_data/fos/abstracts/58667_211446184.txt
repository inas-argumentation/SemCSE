Beaten Down, Worked Up: The Past, Present and Future of American Labor
The American labor movement has faced many challenges in its history: while it has sought to represent the interests of its membership base by calling for higher wages and better working conditions, it has also faced criticism about the extent and character of its influence. New economic and political conditions resulting from changes in technology and globalization have also impacted the direction of labor movement policy, the overall health of organized labor, and the state of American workers.
