Experimental Study on Interface of Ni/Sn Solid/Liquid Diffusion Couple
Ni/Sn solid/liquid diffusion couples with curving interfaces were prepared by crucible-type mechanical method.The heat treatment of diffusion couples was carried out under different conditions in SK2 resistor furnace.The change of phase interface and composition of diffusion layers was analyzed by optical microscope and scanning electron microscopy.The results show that the thickness and the number of the diffusion layers increase with the increase of heat-treatment temperature and time.Intermetallic compounds(Ni3Sn4,Ni3Sn,Ni3Sn2) appear between the interfaces in the diffusion couples in order.
