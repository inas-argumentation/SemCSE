Optische Eigenschaften von Cadmiumselenid-Einkristallen im Ultraroten
The room temperature absorption- and reflection-spectra of CdSe single crystals containing electron concentrationsN between 5.3 · 1016 and 7 · 1019 cm−3 have been measured at wavelengths ranging from 0.5 μ to 12 μ. The spectral dependence of the absorption constantK is found to be K∝ λ2.5 ...3.6. An interpretation of the data in terms of the theories ofVisvanathan andFan is given. The effective mass m* of the conduction electrons in crystals containingN > 1018 cm−3 is calculated from the frequency of the reflection minimum. One finds: m*=f(N). An extrapolation forN=0 yields m*=0.08 m0; forN=7 · 1019 cm−3: m*=0.17 m0. Values of the effective masses in ZnO- and CdO-crystals are communicated.
