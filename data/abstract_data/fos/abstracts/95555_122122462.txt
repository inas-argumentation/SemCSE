Influence of Dynamic Processes upon Complete Fusion of Heavy Nuclei at Subbarrier Energies
Excitation functions for complete fusion of /sup 90/Zr + /sup 90/Zr and /sup 90/Zr + /sup 94/Zr have been found to decrease gradually over a broad energy range below the nominal barrier. The effective barrier for /sup 90/Zr + /sup 94/Zr was 10.5 +- 3.0 MeV higher than the nominal barrier implying a critical effective fissility of 31.5 +- 0.5. Although deep-interpenetration processes appear to limit fusion at near-barrier energies, weak-contact dynamics enable these cold, heavy nuclei to fuse with comparative ease at lower energies.
