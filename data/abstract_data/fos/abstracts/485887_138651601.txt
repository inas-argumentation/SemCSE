Effect of Deformation on Phase Transformation from Pearlite to Austenite in Low Alloy Steel
The effect of deformation on the phase transformation from pearlite to austenite in low alloy steel was studied by DSC. The results show that the phase transformation temperature and activation energy from pearlite to austenite in low alloy steel can be decreased through deformation, and the phase transformation time can be also decreased. As a results, It is helpful to phase transformation.
