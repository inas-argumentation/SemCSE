Contrast Study on Two Types of Metal Base in Slide Gate System
The indirect temperature/stress couples under two types of different structured metal bases were simulated by software ANSYS in the same liquid-steel pouring process.The results show that the maximum equivalent tensile stress value of No.1 metal base reaches 601 MPa in the diagonal direction,which is the main factor of base cracking,and crack easily takes place in the undersurface,where a obvious region of stress concentration exists in 45°direction;the maximum equivalent stress value of No.2 metal base is reduced to 397 MPa,which effectively avoids the cracks,the structure is more reasonable.
