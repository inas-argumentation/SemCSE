The introduction of Nash equilibrium offers a base to confirm the equilibrium of the oligopoly market, and on the Nash equilibrium, various manufactures will consider their competitors and suppose their competitors will do the same thing. In 1883, French economist Joseph Bertrand utilized the concept of Nash equilibrium in the Bertrand model established by him to study the price competition, afterward that model was broad used to explain and analyze phenomena and problems about price competition, and analysis and researches about dynamic price competition game were numerous.
