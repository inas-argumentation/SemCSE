Statistical Analysis between Output and Energy Consumption of Production Process in Iron and Steel Industry
On the basis of E-P theory of cleaner production, this paper verifies its reliability in terms of prediction of energy consumption using actual production data of output and energy consumption. And then the parameters of the E-P model are determinate by means of least square linear programming method. In addition, we use the actual data to further modify the E-P model in order to improve accuracy of model. It is proved that this model can forecast energy consumption kindly.
