Indoor WLAN Positioning and Tracking Scheme Based on Signal Propagation Characteristic
Indoor localization based on 802. 11 wireless networks has long been an import research field,which provides enabling technologies for location-aware mobile applications. One of the most challenging factors to radio-based approaches would be the heterogeneity of indoor environment that makes signal propagation complex and difficult for modeling. This work exploits the more deterministic characteristic of signal propagation when users are moving and conjointly leverage motion direction to reckon user azimuth relative to the AP it associates with. Together with distance between AP and user estimated through an empirical model,the accuracy of positioning and tracking is effectively promoted and the algorithm is inherently applicable for user tracking scenario. Results of extensive positioning and tracking experiments conducted in large scale production WLAN show that the proposed scheme could be rapidly deployed on off-the-shell mobile phones and WLAN infrastructures nowadays and achieved accuracy of 3. 7 to 5. 4 meters with probability 0. 7 without manpower intensive site survey,which is competent for general indoor location-aware applications.
