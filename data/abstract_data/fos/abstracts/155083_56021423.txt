Dendritic Polyelectrolyte-Metal Nanoparticle Complex Aggregates At The Air-Water Interface
Gold nanoparticles are bound to modified partially hydrolyzed polyacrylamide (MHPAM) chains in solution. However, it is interesting to observe that MHPAM-Au complex molecules in solution can be adsorbed to the air-water interfaces to form the dendritic aggregate morphology by addition of Au nanoparticles at interfaces as sorbents using Langmuir-Blodgett technique.
