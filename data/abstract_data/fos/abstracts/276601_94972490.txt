ANOMALOUS BIRCH REDUCTION PRODUCTS OF 1,1,4aβ-TRIMETHYL-2β-HYDROXY-8-METHOXY-1,2,3,4,4a,9-HEXAHYDROPHENANTHRENE AND UNUSUAL REACTIVITIES OF THE PRODUCTS
Birch reduction of the title compound 1 afforded a tetrahydroanisole 2 and its hydrogenolysis product 3. The methoxyl group of compound 2 is readily displaced by acetoxyl group with inversion of configuration by warming in acetic acid. Compound 3 is highly sensitive to oxygen; it is autooxidized to 8 on exposing to the air.
