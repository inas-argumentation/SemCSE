An Analysis of the Effect of the New Rural Cooperative Medical System in Alleviating Illness-related Poverty
Using China health and nutrition survey data,the paper analyzes the effect of the new rural cooperative medical system in alleviating illness-related poverty through comparing the rates of illness-related poverty before and after the implementation of the new rural cooperative medical system in the years of 2004,2006 and 2009.It is argued that the new rural cooperative medical system plays a certain role in alleviating illness-related poverty,but it has not achieved the desired effect theoretically.It is believed,however,that its ability in alleviating illness-related poverty will be improved by more capital investment from the government.
