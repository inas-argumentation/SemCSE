Design and Implementation of Laboratory Online Booking System Based on Campus Network
By adopting information and computer management technology,the system sets up a classroom teaching management platform.The system will publish current classroom's equipment information,classroom situation,opening and closing time and open projects,etc,on the Web.The system also uses online laboratory booking system for teachers and students to check the classroom's opening and closing situation at any time.By real-time access to the laboratory's materials,the system enables teachers and students to make a better use of the resources.
