A Discussion on Migrant Worker Flow in Spring Festival
Migrant worker flow in passenger transport during Spring Festival is the outcome of current changes taking place in China's social structure. As the main part in the transport,the appearance of migrant worker flow is the inevitable result of social economic development as well as an manifestation of the influence of traditional culture on behavior choice. The annual Spring Festival passenger transport brings various social problems. The core of all these problems is centralization of people rather than there are too many of them. Therefore,the solution to these problems is not simply the expansion of transport capacity. Instead,it needs comprehensive treatment based on the analysis of social,economical,and cultural factors behind migrant worker flow in Spring Festival passenger transport.
