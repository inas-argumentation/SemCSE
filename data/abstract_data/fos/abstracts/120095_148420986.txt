The Starting of Shanxi Education Modernization:Taking Shanxi University as an Example
The out-of-the-way common practice and deep-rooted traditional ideas made the interior provinces not only confront with difficult reform of the traditional educational mode,but also with the hard change of the traditional ideas.Besides,the interior provinces were forced by foreign powers,and struggling with the foreign forces for the education rights deepened the hardship.As China’s earliest university in interior provinces,Shanxi University became a miniature of the difficult starting of education modernization in China’s interior provinces.
