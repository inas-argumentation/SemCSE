Contrast and Research of MFT Logic for Homemade 600MW Supercritical Power Unit
As one of the main protecting measures, optimized MFT logic is important to the homemade 600MW supercritical power unit. In this article, the MFT logic of three earlier homemade 600MW supercritical power units were contrasted and their characteristics were analyzed. Furthermore, deficiencies in the logic were pointed out and some proposals were made. It is greatly beneficial to the similar power units′ design of MFT logic.
