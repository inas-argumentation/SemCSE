Photo-biological characters and its relationship with the yield of Annong 98005
The photo-biological characters and its relationship with the yield of wheat cultivar,Annong 98005 were studied in this paper.The results indicated that the flag leaves of the Annong 98005 had better ability of photosynthesis.The changes of Pn,Fv/Fm,ETR and the yield of flag leaves showed single peak type from booting stage to milk ripening stage.The Pn,Fv/Fm,ETR,yield and dry matter of flag leaves reached the maximum in the anthesis stage.In later stage after filling,Pn,Fv/Fm,ETR,ФPSⅡand the area of the flag leaves decreased slowly.
