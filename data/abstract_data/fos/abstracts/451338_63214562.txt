Design and Implementation of Embedded GUI
For the embedded devices are limited by the condition of hardware itself, so how to develop a GUI system, which adapts to the condition of product's hardware, becomes an important content in the development of embedded system. On the basis of multimedia application platform and the embedded system development's requirements, establishes a GUI system which according to this requirements. Puts forward and introduces the design and implementation of three subsystems of embedded GUI system, including graphics-subsystem, event-subsystem and object-subsystem.
