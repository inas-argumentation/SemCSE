Neutral Pion and η Production in Light and Heavy 3HeAu Collisions at $$\sqrt {{{s}_{{NN}}}} $$ = 200 GeV
Nuclear modification factor RAA of π0 mesons produced in 3HeAu collisions at a cms energy of 
$$\sqrt {{{s}_{{NN}}}} $$

 = 200 GeV is extracted from data of the PHENIX experiment at the RHIC. The mean RAA value for π0 mesons with pT > 8 GeV/c is found to be close to 0.8 for central 3He + Au collisions.
