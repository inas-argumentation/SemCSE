FORMATION OF DIPHENYL(9-PHENYL-10-ANTHRYL)METHYL, A POSTULATED INTERMEDIATE OF THE SIDE-CHAIN OXIDATION OF 9-BENZHYDRYL-10-PHENYLANTHRACENE, FROM 9,10-BNZHYDRYL-10-PHENYL-9,10-EPIDIOXYANTHRACENE IN LIQUID SULFUR DIOXIDE
By dissolving 9,10-dihydro-9-benzhydryl-10-phenyl-9,10-epidioxyanthracene in liquid sulfur dioxide, diphenyl(9-phenyl-10-anthryl)methyl was obtained, which was conveniently oxidized to diphenyl(9-phenyl-10-anthryl)methyl cation by introducing oxygen into this solution , and after treatment with ethanol 9-ethoxy-9-phenyl-10-diphenylmethylene-9,10-dihydroanthracene was isolated.
