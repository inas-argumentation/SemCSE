Item Convergent Validity: A New Method of Analysis for Frequency or Continuously Scaled Data.
A method of assessing item convergent validity of non-objectively scored items is presented. Developed within the conceptual framework of Campbell and Fiske (2), the new methodology is analogous to the technique developed by Krus and Ney (5) for objectively scored items. The methodology consists of computing prescribed point biserial correlation coefficients and then evaluating the coefficients with respect to three criteria. The utility of this methodology for item analysis and scale construction is demonstrated by its application to five-item subscale.
