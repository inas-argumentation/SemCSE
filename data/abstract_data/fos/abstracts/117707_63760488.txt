Switch-avoid Service Selection Algorithm in Mobile Environment
Service in the mobile environment has some characteristics of the traditional services such as Qo S( Quality of Service),but compared to the traditional service it will have its own characteristics. This paper focuses on the availability issue of service in the mobile environment. Because of the transmission mode of mobile service is in the form of wireless,the mobile service will have a certain transmission range,which makes the service availability is associated with the user's location and mobility. When a user' s location is beyond the transmission range of services his program use,the services will become unavailable relative to this user,leading to the failure of service executing,and the program need to switch to an available service,which brings extra cost. This paper put forward a service selection algorithm to avoid service switch( SASS),which satisfy the condition of avoiding service switching and seeking the service executing plan with global minimum cost. The paper provides two specific algorithm implementation including0 SASS and linear programming,and designs a simulation experiment to demonstrate the effectiveness of the SASS algorithm and analyzed the experiment results.
