Mechanism on Shallow Dissolving Air Flotation for Treatment of Micro-polluted Water in Closed Scenic Reclaimed-water
In view of the characteristics of pollutants for the scenic water, BOAF system was set up to purify the closed scenic water with source of reclaimed water in residential quarters. BOAF system includes a main process combining dissolving air flotation method with simple biochemical and two inside circulations of floating scum and dissolved water in the shallow dissolving air flotation device. Through the study of mechanism of shallow dissolving air flotation, the parameters which will affect efficiency of the device were analyzed. Results of the present study prove that BOAF system is a feasible method for removing pollutants in closed scenic water in residential quarters, and provides firmly technical support for the application and promotion of closed scenic water with source of reclaimed water.
