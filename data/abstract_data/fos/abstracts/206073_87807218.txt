Relationship between Physical Structure Characteristics and Nutrients of Hosts and Damage by Brontispa longissima(Gestro)
Resistance of 5 main hosts species to Brontispa longissima was measured in the laboratory.The relationship between physical structure characteristics and nutrients of hosts and damage by B.longissima were discussed.Host selective and suitable test results showed that in a free choice and forced choice conditions,the coconut leaf beetle could feed on different type hosts,but there were significant differences in preferences,the beetles more likely to feed on date palm and coconut in both cases.However the host suitability for coconut leaf beetle growth was different from the host selectivity preferences,the date palm was very detrimental to their growth and development,while Syagrus romanzoffiana was most favorable to beetles growth and development.Soluble sugar,soluble protein,crude fat and water content of different host were measured.The result showed that the nutrients and water content in different host had significant differences.There were no relationship between the nutrients and water content with beetle's host selectivity,only sugar content had correlation with the pupae weight of beetles and the correlation equation significant value was 0.0212.The hosts physical structure characteristics study results showed that as the palisade tissue got wider,the development of larvae also got faster.There was not no significant relation between epicuticle,hypodermi or palisade tissue and the host selectivity of B.longissima.
