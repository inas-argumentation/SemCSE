Cyclosporin A induces peripheral maternal-fetal immuno-tolerance of the abortion-prone murine model
Objective To testify if cyclosporin A (CsA) induces peripheral maternal-fetal immuno-tolerance of the murine abortion-prone model. Methods CBA/J female mice were injected i.p by 0, 5, 10, 15 mg/kg CsA on day 4 of gestation respectively after they were mated to DBA/2 as a abortion-prone model or BALB/c male mice as a successful pregnancy model. The resorption rate was calculated on day 14 of gestation, immuno-tolerance and IL-2 mRNA level were checked by mixed lymphocyte reaction and RT-PCR on day of 9, 14 of gestation respectively. Results CsA administrated on day 4 of gestation significantly reduced the embryo resorption rate of the abortion-prone model, and induced a significantly lower proliferation response and IL-2 production by maternal splenic immuno-competent cells to paternal antigen as compared to the control group. Conclusion Administration of CsA on day 4 of gestation, the duration of peri-implantation, could induce paternal immuno-tolerance, which lead to the embryo resorption rateof the abortion-prone model to normal level. We conclude that CsA is a potential medicine for the treatment of pregnancy loss.
