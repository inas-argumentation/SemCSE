In-company Situation and Development Strategy for Chinese Domestic Third-Party Logistics Providers (3PLPs)
With the rapid growth of logistics, it is important to gain further insight into the strategic benefits of logistics services. The study examines In-company situation of domestic 3PLPs. Based on the discussed background, the study analyses domestic 3PL companies in China Finally, the study recommends specific strategies to strengthen competitive power of global 3PLPs in China. This study provides a theoretical foundation for academics and also practical guidelines for logistics services industry
