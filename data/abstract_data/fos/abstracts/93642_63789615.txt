Performance Analysis of TCP Vegas Congestion Control Algorithm over LEO Satellite Networks
In this paper, we conduct comprehensive research of the TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) Vegas congestion control algorithms, and then we present a new improved algorithm, Vegas1 algorithm. The performance of TCP Vegas congestion control algorithms over Iridium-similar LEO satellite networks is analyzed. The Vegas1 algorithm is compared with old Vegas algorithms also. The difference between our work and previous simulation research work of others is that our research work concentrates on the whole network performance of various parameters, on the average numerical value. The result of our simulation shows that the Vegas1 algorithm is superior to the old algorithms. By comparison with the result of simulation using single link, using full network topology to simulate TCP congestion control algorithms has a positive meaning.
