A Novel Quantum Entanglement Wireless Relay Scheme and RQCP Protocol
Wireless quantum communication is one of the most important parts in the future quantum communication.Quantum entanglement relay is one of the key technologies in wireless quantum communication systems.However,there are so many kinds of interference and noise in wireless channel that it is very hard to realize the relay of the quantum states.To solve the problem,the two quanta relay schemes based on mobile terminals and base stations are presented firstly.The layered structure of wireless quantum communication and the quantum states routing protocol(RQCP) in wireless transmission are presented also.Theory analysis results show that the scheme above can transmit quanta states although no EPR pairs shared by source and objective nodes,and the time delay in transmission has nothing to do with the number of wireless hopping.It proved that the model and scheme are optimal.
