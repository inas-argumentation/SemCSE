ASYMMETRIC REDUCTION OF KETONES WITH A CATIONIC CHIRAL PHOSPHINE RHODIUM COMPLEX CATALYST
Methyl ethyl ketone, pinacoline and acetophenone are reduced asymmetrically to the corresponding alcohols with a chiral phosphine rhodium complex catalyst, [Rh(NBD) {(R)-PEtMePh}2]+PF6− (NBD = 2,5-norbornadiene).
