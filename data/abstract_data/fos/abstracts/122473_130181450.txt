Safety Evaluation of Underground Drinking Water in Small Towns Based Aggregative Index Method
Two most important indexes in the safety evaluation of underground drinking water are water quantity and quality index.Aggregative index method was applied in the paper to evaluate the present status of underground drinking water in these two aspects in the typical city named Shihezi.It is contributed to find the unsafe factors in the water supply and also provide the theoretical and decision-making basis for improving the security of water supply in small towns.
