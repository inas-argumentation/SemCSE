DRIVERS OF SINGLE PULSE SELECTORS BASED ON MOSFETS
The advantage of the single pulse selectors which is driven by high voltage and large current power MOSFETs is introduced. The characteristic phenomena are discovered when the electrooptic crystal is used as a fast electo-optic switch and the problems and resolvents of making the drivers by MOSFETsare described. The experiments performed by our MOSFET drivers display that the time of leading and trailing edges of the optical switch are 5ns.
