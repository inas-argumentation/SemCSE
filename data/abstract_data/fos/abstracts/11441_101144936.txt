Application research of the cultivated method of Pleurotus by response surface analysis using potato processing waste
The main purpose of this work was to optimize the cultivated method of Pleurotus by response surface analysis,which used potato processing waste.The optimal conditions of cultivation were obtained by response surface analysis as follows:cultivation with 9% potato processing waste in culture medium,at pH 7.0,20 ℃.The actual output of Pleurotus was 171 g,which was very closed to the theoretical value 174.555 g.It was not only enhanced the output of Pleurotus,but also utilized potato processing waste effectively.The products were high quality,plump,brightly colored and heavy fragrance.
