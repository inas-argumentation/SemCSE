New Approach for Medical Image Fusion Based on Orthogonal Wavelet Packets Decomposition and 2v-SVM
A new approach for medical image fusion based on orthogonal wavelet packets decomposition and 2v-SVM is proposed,aiming at the deficiencies of existing algorithms in characteristics representation and acceptance or rejection of information.The orthogonal wavelet packets decomposition divide image signal frequency bands into many levels and pick up the specific frequency components effectively,the node approach coefficients of the low frequency components are deposed using 2v-SVM linear fusion arithmetic operators proposed in this paper,combining the bigger node approach coefficients of the high frequency components in the two images to generate a new node coefficients matrix.Finally the fused image is obtained using wavelet packets reconstruction.By the experiment results,the approach is proved valid and advantaged.
