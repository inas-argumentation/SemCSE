Hierarchical Digital Map Data Management and Prefetching in Mobile Navigation System
Beginning with the hierarchical data management,this paper presents a data prefetching and updating strategy based on Hierarchical Hilbert Grid.Through the binary coding,the hiberarchy relationship and neighborhood relationship are described more exactly and flexibly.At the same time,the hierarchical data management is more suitable to the practical map display and can further decrease the cost of data prefetching and updating.The test result shows that such a strategy is more excellent than the ordinary prefetching methods.
