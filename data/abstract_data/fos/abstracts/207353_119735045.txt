Influence of Laser radiation on the collisional-radiative recombination and ionization coefficients
It is shown that irradiation of a plasma by a Laser beam lowers the collisional-radiative coefficient for recombination (α) and increases the one for ionization (S) compared to unirradiated plasmas. Numerical values ofα andS are given for a hydrogen plasma irradiated by CO2, Ruby, and Ar-Ion Lasers. The calculations are based on a collisional-radiative model in which photoionization and stimulated recombination intervene. Stimulated recombination has an important effect and can lead to saturation of free-bound transitions at high power density of the Laser beam as long as multiphoton ionization effects and plasma heating by inverse bremsstrahlung remain negligibly small.
