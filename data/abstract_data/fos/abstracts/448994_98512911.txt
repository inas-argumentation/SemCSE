Zur Synthese von Organo-Phosphor-Verbindungen unter Verwendung von P-substituierten Metallphosphiden
The preparation and properdes as well as the substitution, exchange and addition reactons of P-substituted metal phosphides are reviewed. Beginning with the P-H acidity of phosphine and primary and secondary phosphines their metallation with organametallic compounds, sodium amide, and alkali metals under suitable conditions is described. Then follows the preparation phosphides by cleaving the P-H and P-C bonds, namely of biphosphines and their sulphides, cyclotetraphosphines, tertiary aryl, and aralkyl phosphines with alkali metals or organometallic compounds. The properties of the P-substituted alkali phosphides are explained by bond conditions and molecular weights. The substitution reactions of alkali phosphides with alkyl or aryl halides, alkylene dihalides, ß-bromoethyl diethylamine, acid chlorides and acid imide chlorides are discussed in detail. The mechanism of the exchange reactions is described by terms of the nucleophilicity of the phosphide anions using the reaction with phenyl chloroacetylene as example. Also the possibility of building up a P-P bond by an exchange reaction is explained. An addition reaction ofP-substituted metal phosphides takes place by C=C, C=N, C=O double bonds, and epoxides, respectively.
