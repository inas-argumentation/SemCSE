Research on the Evolving Algorithm for Attitude Determination Based on Navigation Satellite
Attitude determination based on navigation satellites is a key technology in aviation,marine and land navigation. In the method using carrier phase difference,the solving of double difference integer ambiguity is important and difficult. This paper adopts Evolving Algorithm to search for the optimal integer ambiguity directly without the decorrelation of ambiguity,and calculates the baseline vector consequently. This method can improve the efficiency and real-time performance of integer ambiguity solving,so as to be applied in dynamic attitude determination. The experimental results show the validity of this method.
