Synthesis of 3-acylmethyl-1,5-dihydrobenzo[e]-2,4-dithiepins and 1,2-di(acylvinylthiomethyl)-4,5-dimethylbenzenes from α-acetylene ketones
Substituted 1,5-dihydrobenzo[e]-2,4-dithiepins and 1,2-di(acylvinylthiomethyl)-4,5-dimethylbenzenes were synthesized by the reaction between α-acetylene ketones and 1,2-dimethyl-4,5-di(mercaptomethyl)benzene in chloroform in the presence of K2CO3 or in methanol in the presence of triethylamine.
