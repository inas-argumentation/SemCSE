RAPD Analysis of the Pear Germplasm Resources in Protection District of Dangshansu Pear
The genomic DNA of 24 pear biotypes, which cultivated in Dangshan, Anhui Province, was amplified by the RAPD analysis by using the twelve arbitrary primers screened from 80 ones. The relationship between biotypes were studied by cluster analysis according to the RAPD patterns, which were coded in three ways: coding as 0-1, coding as 0-3 and the strong-band coding. The result showed that the cultivars, mutants and hybrids could be identified by using special bands. There was a close relationship between Pyrus bretschneideri and P.pyrifolia.The 0-3 coding could reflect more information than the other two coding ways.
