Ecology suitability evaluation of Alfalfa in Yellow river delta based on GIS——A case of Kenli county
Ecology environment database of Alfalfa in Yellow river delta(a case of Kenli county)was established on Arcview Gis and Arc/info technology.Spatial database was managed by Arcview Gis and shared with attribute database managed by Visual Foxpro through common field.According to homogeneous principles,the region was divided into 2312 grids(evaluating units),1 square kilometer each gird.Twenty-one indexes were selected and principle component analysis method was adopted in ecology suitability evaluation system, so that classified map and spatial distribution for ecology suitability of Alfalfa were generated.The value of social and economic factors,usually ignored in traditional evaluating system,was considered.Moreover,position indexes were introduced for region characteristic,all of which made the results more scientifically comprehensive.
