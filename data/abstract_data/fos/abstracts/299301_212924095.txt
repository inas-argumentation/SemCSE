Chapter 2 provides a close reading of some of the state-sponsored murals by the world-renowned artist Diego Rivera and his contemporary Jose ́ Orozco Clemente. Acknowledging that both artists have been studied exhaustively, the author’s analysis illustrates that “the two men communicated contradictory racial discourses as they disagreed about the proper place of the nation’s European and indigenous heritage within the official ideology” (28).
