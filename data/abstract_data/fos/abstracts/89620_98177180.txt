A new method of simultaneous measurement of tracer diffusion coefficient and mobility of alkali ions in glasses
A new experimental arrangement is presented which allows the simultaneous determination of the tracer diffusion coefficient and the mobility of alkali ions in glasses from a single sample. The basic idea is the study of tracer diffusion than electric field under special boundary conditions. The experimental procedure is described in detail and a mathematical analysis is given. As an example the experiment is applied to a commercial optical glass with high Na 2 O content.
