Construction and primary characterization of cDNA expression library of Periplaneta Americana nymrh
Objective To construct a cDNA expression library of Periplaneta Americana nymph. Methods cDNA fragments of Periplaneta Americana nymph were cloned into λExCell/NotⅠ/EcoRⅠ/CIP phage vector by directional cloning. The length of cDNA fragments was detected by PCR. Result The cDNA expression library constructed contains 1.29×10 6 recombinants, the efficiency of recombinance was 100%, and the average length of inserted cDNA fragments was about 1.12kb. Conclusion The library is qualified for further recombinant allergen study.
