Study on Springback Control of Automobile Front Fender Based on Virtual Drawing
Aiming at the springback after drawing of automobile front fender,virtual drawing was done based on the numerical simulation.The effect of die structure parameters on formability of automobile front fender was investigated.The results show that changes of die fillets radius in different positions make different degree springback and the influence is limited;die surface compensation is an effective method to control the springback of automobile front fender.The experimental verification is done,and the dimensional precision of practical production meets the design requirements.
