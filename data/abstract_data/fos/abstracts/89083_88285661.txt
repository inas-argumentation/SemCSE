The Adaptability of 5 Clones by Independent Selection in Hevea brasiliensis
To breed superior new rubber clones by independent selection,five clones were used as the materials in this experiment,and the characteristics of growth increment,latificer system and stress resistance were systematically determined for early prediction on the basis of investigation data over the years.The results showed that Reshi 87-1 and Reshi 87-4 performed well on the whole.It grew fast,and the ability of laticifer differentiation was superior to the control,and its resistance to wind damage and cold injury were fine.Reshi 87-5 showed a prominent stress resistance,but its ability of laticifer differentiation was weak.Growth increment and differentiation of laticifer system of Reshi 93-5 and Reshi 87-6 performed well while its resistance to wind damage and cold injury were poorer.Because of long growth period of rubber tree and complex ecological types of rubber panting areas in China,the above conclusion needs further verification.
