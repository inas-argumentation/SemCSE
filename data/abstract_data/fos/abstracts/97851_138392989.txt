Effect of Coiling Temperature on Microstructure Evolution of Low-alloy Wear Resistant Steel in Rolling
In the light of transformation rule of testing steel,the coiling temperature of low-alloy wear resistant steel sample subjected to deformation was simulated on Thermec Master_Z.The test result was verified on the steel plate rolled by experimental rolling mill and was applied into the industrial production of low alloy wear resistant steel after rolling.The thermal-mechanical simulation experiment shows that the microstructure is ferrite + pearlite and Vickers hardness is lower than 210 HV,when simulating coiling temperature is higher than 630 ℃.Bainite is found in the microstructure when the temperature is lower than and equal to 600 ℃,and as the temperature decreasing,the proportion of bainite and hardness of the sample increases.The rolling and coiling experiment of testing steel in lab shows that the microstructure is ferrite + pearlite and brinell hardness is lower than 220 when the coiling temperature is higher than 650 ℃.The industrial production shows that the brinell hardness of the coils is lower than 220 HB,while the average coil temperature is from 642 ℃ to 748 ℃,and there is little difference between head and tail of the coil.
