STATIC AND MODAL ANALYSIS OF ENGINE SUPPORTING BRACKET - A LITERATURE REVIEW
In an automotive vehicle, the engine rests on supporting bracket which is connected to the main-frame or the skeleton of the vehicle. Engine supporting bracket plays very significant role in reducing noise, vibration and harshness caused due to engine and thus has very effective role in improving vehicle comfort. This present work accounts for the investigation of engine supporting bracket by using ANSYS. This current article accounts for review of the work that has been previously done on engine supporting bracket. Further work includes Static and modal analysis of engine supporting frame and to investigate whether the current natural frequency of engine mounting bracket is lower than that of self excitation frequency of bracket. The ongoing work, therefore aims in reducing the undesired vibrations generated by the engine and road roughness can get directly transmitted to the frame through the supporting bracket. Keywords-- Static Analysis, Engine supporting bracket, FEA, NVH, Optimization.
