Markets for pollution control
The history of pollution control incentives, with evidence from the Environmental Protection Agency and the State of Wisconsin, suggests there is a future for marketable (or transferable) emission permits. Marketable permits have five advantages over effluent fees: (1) they eliminate uncertainty by having the environmental agency establish the total allowable emission level; (2) market forces will automatically establish the price of permits; (3) permits can be distributed initially to polluters to eliminate the additional cost for existing firms; (4) they allow flexibility in dealing with the spatial dimensions of pollution without discriminating; and (5) polluters will respond to the familiarity of permits better than to the imposition fees.
