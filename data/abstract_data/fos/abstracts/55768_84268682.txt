Horseradish peroxidase-mediated preparation of dimers from eugenol and isoeugenol
Coupling of eugenol ( I ) and isoeugenol ( II ) by means of horseradish peroxidase and hydrogen peroxide resulted in ~20% yield of dimeric compounds: 5,5’-diallyl-2,2’-dihydroxy-3,3’-dimethoxy-biphenyl ( III ) and 2-(4’-hydroxy-3’-methoxy)phenyl-7-methoxy-3-methyl-5-propenyl-2,3-dihydrobenzofurane ( IV ), respectively.
