Homologous Desensitisation of the Mouse Leukotriene B4 Receptor Involves Protein Kinase C-Mediated Phosphorylation of Serine 127
Murine leukotriene B<sub>4</sub> (LTB<sub>4</sub>) receptor (mBLT1) cDNA was identified by searching the EST database using human LTB<sub>4</sub> receptor as the query sequence. Expression of functional mBLT1 after injection of in vitro transcribed cRNA into Xenopus laevis oocytes was demonstrated as LTB<sub>4</sub>-evoked, Ca<sup>2+</sup>-activated Cl<sup>-</sup> currents recorded by two-electrode voltage clamp. From mBLT1-expressing oocytes, a dose-dependent relationship between the Ca<sup>2+</sup>-activated Cl<sup>-</sup> current and LTB<sub>4</sub> concentration was demonstrated with an apparent EC<sub>50</sub> of 6.7 nM. Following LTB<sub>4</sub> stimulation of mBLT1, we observed two transient, spatially distinct Ca<sup>2+</sup>-activated, inwardly directed Cl<sup>-</sup> currents in the oocytes: a fast peak current requiring relatively high LTB<sub>4</sub> concentrations, and a slowly progressing Cl<sup>-</sup> current. Nucleotides, PGE<sub>2</sub>, 12R-hydroxy-5, 8, 14-cis-10-trans-eicosatetraenoic acid, and LTD<sub>4</sub> did not activate mBLT1. U75302, specifically targeting BLT1, significantly reduced LTB<sub>4</sub>-evoked Cl<sup>-</sup> currents. Repetitive LTB<sub>4</sub> administration desensitized the LTB<sub>4</sub>-evoked currents. Activation of protein kinase C (PKC) by PMA addition completely eliminated the LTB<sub>4</sub>-evoked currents, whereas down-regulation of PKC by prolonged PMA exposure (20 h) impaired mBLT1 desensitisation. In addition, Ser-127-Ala substitution of the PKC consensus phosphorylation site on the second intracellular loop prevented the mBLT1 desensitisation. These data indicate that PKC-mediated phosphorylation at Ser-127 leads to mBLT1 desensitisation.
