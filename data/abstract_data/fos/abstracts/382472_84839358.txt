A Note on Key Agreement and Non-Interactive Commitments
We observe that any key agreement protocol satisfying perfect completeness, regardless of its round complexity, can be used to construct a non-interactive commitment scheme. This observation simplifies the cryptographic assumptions required for some protocols that utilize non-interactive commitments and removes the need for ad-hoc constructions of noninteractive commitments from specific assumptions such as Learning with Errors. ∗MIT.
