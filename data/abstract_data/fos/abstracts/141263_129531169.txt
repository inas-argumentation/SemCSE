Jurassic and cretaceous plate tectonic reconstructions
Five plate tectonic reconstructions are presented illustrating the breakup of Pangea and the evolution of the world's ocean basins during the Early Jurassic (Pliensbachian), Late Jurassic (Volgian), Early Cretaceous (Aptian), Late Cretaceous (Cenomanian), and latest Cretaceous (Maestrichtian). Indicated on the maps are the location of the spreading centers, intracontinental rifts, subduction zones, and transform faults which were active during each interval.
