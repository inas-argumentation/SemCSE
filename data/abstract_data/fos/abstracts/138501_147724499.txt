The Contents and Methods of the Situation and Political Education for the Students in Adult College
According to the ideological trend and character of the students' in adult colleges, we should pay much more attention to moral education and political education in the new situation, because some of the students have some changeable ideas on the judgments and choice of value, that is, "Both concern and indifference exist at the same time", "Hope is present in wondering", "Enterprise accompanied by hesitation", "Admission and disappointment are in turn".
