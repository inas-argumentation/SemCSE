Monetary Policy Coordination Under Asymmetric Shocks:A Stackelberg Model
In this paper,we analyzed the monetary policy coordination by a Stackelberg model under the hypothesis of asymmetric shocks.The analysis of the Stackelberg equilibrium solutions to the model shows that the asymmetric shocks have uncertain effect on the policy result of monetary policy coordination.In the area of monetary policy coordination,the preemptive superiority and strategy of the leader in the Stackelberg game cannot ensure him get certain policy gains from the monetary policy coordination.
