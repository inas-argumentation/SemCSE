Basicity of rotameric 9-(2-dimethylaminomethyl-6-methylphenyl)fluorene.
The pKa′ values of the conjugate acids of the title compound were determined as 5.85 and 6.45, respectively, for the sp and ap isomers by potentiometric titration in THF-water solutions. The difference was ascribed to the existence of N–H···π interactions in the ap rotamer on the basis of the equilibrium constants between and barriers to rotation in rotamers of the conjugate acids and the free bases.
