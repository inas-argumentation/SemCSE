Aerial photography as an aid to cropland erosion analysis.
COLOR and color infrared photography at the scale of 1:60,000 were used to determine cropland manage-ment (i.e., crop type, plowing, surface residue, and rota-tion) and supporting erosion control practices (i.e., con-touring, strip cropping, and waterways) for an agricultural watershed in Dane County, WI. Sediment loss predictions based on remote sensing airphoto inter-pretations were found to agree closely with field study predictions using Wischmeier's Universal Soil Loss Equation.
