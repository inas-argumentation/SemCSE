Effect of Zr Content on Heat Treatment Process and Properties of High Purity Copper Forge Piece
The change rule of heat treatment process and properties,when 0.1wt%～0.3wt% zirconium was added to high purity copper,was introduced.The testing results indicate that the different amount of zirconium has a little effect on the best solution temperature of high purity copper forge piece,but the best aging temperature of the alloy increases with the increase of the zirconium amount.The stability of smelting yielding rate of zirconium is poor in the industrial production.So batch heat treatment is not fit for high purity copper forge piece with zirconium,and the actual content level of zirconium is the base of mapping out heating process.
