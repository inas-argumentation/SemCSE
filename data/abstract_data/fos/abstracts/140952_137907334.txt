The Research of Coal Ash Mineral Behavior in Dynamic Coal Combustion
In a drop tube furnace and one-dimensional furnace,two kinds of coal are heated at 800 degree and 1000 degree. Scanning Electric Microscope (SEM) is used to observe ash samples from above canbustions. The conclusion is made that experimental results of coal ash mineral behavior obtained in static ash fusion measurement instrument can be used to explain that in dynamic coal combustion boiler.
