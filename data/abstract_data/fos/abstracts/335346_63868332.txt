Exploration and Practice of Task-Driven Method in Computer Networking Course Teaching
In view of the Computer Networking course,the traditional education methods excessively emphasizes the role of the teacher's classroom teaching,ignores the cultivation of the student's enthusiasm and creativity.In the teaching of the course,adopts task-driven teaching method to reform the curriculum structure,which aims at actual network project to expand the interpretation of knowledge structure,increases the proportion of experimental teaching,and fully arouses the student's enthusiasm.Results show that:compared with traditional teaching method,task-driven teaching method is of great help to train high-quality talents,is much more worthy to popularizing.
