A Textual Research on Xisai Mountain
The Xisai Mountain in the ci poem YUFU written by Zhang Zhihe refers to the one to the west of Huzhou in Zhejiang province, or to the west of Daye in Hubei province. Most of the people refer to both two places. According to CIPOEMS written by Zhang Zongsu, the Xisai Mountain must be at Huzhou.
