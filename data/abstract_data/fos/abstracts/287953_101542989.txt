Combination of Dispersive Liquid-Liquid Microextraction and Pre-column Derivatization-High Performance Liquid Chromatography for Determination of Bisphenol A in Water Sample
An analytical method,dispersive liquid-liquid microextraction-pre-column derivatization-high performance liquid chromatography,was developed for the determination of bisphenol A(BPA) in water sample.Several factors,influencing the performance of dispersive liquid-liquid microextraction(DLLME) such as extractant volume,disperser type and volume,water volume,pH,and ionic strength,were studied and optimized using interactive orthogonal array and mixed level design experiments.The optimized dispersive liquid-liquid microextraction conditions were as follows:the volume of extractant was 60 μL,the volume of dispersive solvent(methanol) was 0.4 mL,and the value of pH was fixed at 4.0.The optimal pre-column derivatization conditions were as follows:the concentration of P-nitrophenyl chloride was 2.0 g/L and volume was 0.1 mL,the derivatization time was 30 min.The linear range of the proposed method was 0.002-0.2 mg/L with r=0.9997,and the limit of detection was 0.007 μg/L at S/N=3.The extraction recovery ranged from 59.0% to 63.0% with relative standard deviation(RSD) between 2.5%-9.2%(n=5).The relative recovery(R) varied from 86.5% to 101.0%,and the RSD was in the range of 4.0%-11.9%(n=5).Meanwhile,the determination of BPA was not interfered by other estrogens such as estrone,estradiol,estriol,and 17α-ethynylestradiol.The developed method was simple and fast for the trace level BPA determination in water sample.
