Research on the Analysis of Seismic Response of Boron Condensate Cooler
Builds a computational model for boron condensate cooler by using the finite element method.And carries out the static analysis under deadweight,pressure and nozzle loads,modal analyses,and spectrum analysis under seismic loads.Obtains the stress of boron condensate cooler,and makes assessment on the load combinations and stress of boron condensate cooler under each using limit condition.The evaluation result demonstrates that the various components of boron condensate cooler can meet the requirement of the ASME Section III-ND NF codes.
