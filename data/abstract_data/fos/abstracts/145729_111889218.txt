Investigation of Gating System for Valve Castings in EPC Process
Combining computer simulation with orthogonal experiment,the effects of gating system,pouring way and the shape of in-gate cross section on the quality of valve castings were researched in EPC process.The results shows that the effect of pouring way on the casting quality is the most,the cross-section shape of in-gate is second,and the type of gating system is the minimal.Under negative pressure condition,the optimized method of EPC in valve casting is closed gating system,top pouring way and rectangular in-gate cross section.
