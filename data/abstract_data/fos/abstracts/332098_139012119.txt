Study on Microstructure of AlSi5/Galvanized Steel CMT Brazed Joints
ER4043(AlSi5) filler was deposited on galvanized steel surface using CMT welding method. AlSi5 filler was melted with only small amounts of melted substrate. The influence of welding parameters on the welding bead morphology and cross-sectional structure was studied. It can be seen that the appearance is uniform with bright metallic luster and spatters-free. Fe-Al intermetallic compound interface layer forms when the welding speed is 100 cm/min, wire feed rate is 4m/min and welding speed is 80 cm/min, wire feed rate is 3.5 m/min, respectively. The increase of wire feed rate and decrease of welding speed can accelerate the formation of Fe-Al layer. A zinc rich region is formed at the wetting tip of the bead. High zinc content of 40 wt % is seen in the region.
