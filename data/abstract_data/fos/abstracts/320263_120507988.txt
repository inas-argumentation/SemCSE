Relation of electric discharge parameters to short-pulse CO2 laser efficiency
A study is reported of the choice of the discharge and gas mixture parameters of an electron-beamsustained nanosecond CO2 laser with the aim of achieving maximum efficiency. An analysis is made of the dependence of the efficiency of such a CO2 laser on the composition and pressure of the gas mixture, properties and parameters of the discharge, electron and ion recombination coefficients, energy transfer between CO2, N2, and He levels, etc.
