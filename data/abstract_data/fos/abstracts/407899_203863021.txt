Biological Characteristics and Control of Onomarchus uninotatus on Jackfruit
The biological characteristics of Onomarchus uninotatus,a important jackfruit pest,was studied with laboratory observation and field investigation.The pest has 2 generations per year in Hainan and overwinters as nymphs or adults.There are two damage peaks in mid June to late July and late October to early December every year.This paper describes the morphological characteristics,feeding and damage,activity and habitats,molting and eclosion,mating and spawning in details,and proposing some prevention methods as put off egg branches and picking leaves with insect.
