Dual spaces generated by the interior of the set of norm attaining functionals
We characterize some isomorphic properties of Banach spaces in terms of the set of norm attaining functionals. The main result states that a Banach space is reexiv e as soon as it does not contain '1 and the dual unit ball is the w -closure of the convex hull of elements contained in the \uniform" interior of the set of norm attaining functionals. By assuming a very weak isometric condition (lack of roughness) instead of not containing '1, we also obtain a similar result. As a consequence of the rst result, a convex-transitive Banach space not containing '1 and such that the set of norm attaining functionals has nonempty interior is in fact superreexiv e.
