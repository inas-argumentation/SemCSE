The γ-Gauche Effect in the Se–Ge(α)–C(β)–C(γ) System as Detected by 77Se-NMR
The steric compression effect (γ-effect) of γ-carbon on 77Se chemical shift in mono- and diselenogermanes has been first evaluated by 77Se NMR spectroscopy. The γ-gauche effect falls in the range between −59.1 and −13.6 ppm (upfield shift).
