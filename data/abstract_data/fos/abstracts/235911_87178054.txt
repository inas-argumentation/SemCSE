Effects of whole peptidoglycans of Bifidobacteria on signal effectors produced by peritoneal macrophages of nude mice
Objective To study the levels of nitric oxide(NO), inducible nitric oxide synthase(iNOS) and cGMP produced by peritoneal macrophages of nude mice after stimulation by whole peptidoglycans of Bifidobacteria bifidum. Methods The content of NO, iNOS and cGMP derived from peritoneal macrophages of nude mice was detected by employing Griess reagent, laser scanning confocal microscopy and radioimmunoassay, respectively. Results The content of NO, iNOS and cGMP derived from peritoneal macrophages of nude mice induced by LPS in whole peptidoglycan injection group was significantly higher when compared with control group(P0 01). Conclusions Whole peptidoglycans of Bifidobacteria bifidum in synergy with LPS could activate macrophages to produce a large amount of NO, iNOS and cGMP. These signal effectors mediated many kinds of significant physiological functions of whole peptidoglycans.
