Phosphorus Distribution in Sediments of Dianchi Lakeshore Area
The surface and column sediments collected from lakeshore area of Dianchi,Yunnan Province were used to study the distribution and vertical changes of TP,Ca-P and Fe/Al-P.Results showed that the sediments of the lakeshore area had high TP contents,with the maximum up to 10604.64mg/kg and average TP content of 2452mg/kg.The phosphorus pollution of sediments was serious,and variation of sediments in different regions of phosphorus content was large.Ca-P contents were higher than Fe/Al-P in Dianchi lakeshore area,which are different from other lakes.The contents of various phosphorus forms in column sediments fluctuated largely in the vertical distribution,but the contents level of Ca-P and Fe/Al-P were consistent with TP.In total 13 sampling sites,Fe/Al-P contents at 12 sampling sites were lower than Ca-P, with ratio of Fe/Al-P content to Ca-P content less than 0.5.Ca-P is the majority of TP,and the risk of phosphorus release resulting from ambient acidification should be considered.
