Analysis of the Susceptibility of Heliothis armigera (Hübner) to Abamectin
The susceptibility of Heliothis armigera (Hubner) to abamectin was studied by leaf immersion method and spectrophotometry. The results showed that H.armigera in different areas had different susceptibility to abamectin. The resistance of H.armigera in Gancheng, Luodai, Mingshan and Dingan was 4.91 times, 3.21 times, 2.30 times and 2.65 times higher than that in CATAS in 2003 and 10.29 times, 10.75 times, 6.08 times and 3.70 times in 2004, respectively, based on the results of the LC50 of H.armigera in CATAS, and the resistance developed very rapidly. The bioassay of carboxylesterase indicated that the carboxylesterase activity in H.armigera increased significantly after treated by abamectin at levels of 18.00, 9.00, 4.50, 3.00 and 2.25 mg/g. All these results showed that significant increase in carboxylesterase activity in H.armigera was perhaps one of the main factors resulting in decline in susceptibility of H.armigera to abamectin.
