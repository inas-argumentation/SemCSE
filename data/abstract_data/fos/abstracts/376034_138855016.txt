Study on Synthesis of TiCuNiSnTa Amorphous Powders by Mechanical Alloying
Ti-Cu-Ni-Sn-Ta amorphous powders were synthesized by mechanical alloying. The effects of ball milling time on morphology,structure evolution,thermal stability of Ti-based composite powders,and the effects of components on the glass-forming ability were investigated. The results show that,milling time plays an important role in the formation of amorphous alloy and grain-refining. After ball milling,powders in the alloys disperse well and distribute uniformly. The composite Ti64Cu11.2Ni9.6Sn3.2Ta12 with wide supercooled liquid zone forms amorphous alloy. However,the amorphous transition of the composite Ti58.8Cu1.1Ni3.2Sn5Ta31.9 does not happen during milling.
