Research and Realization of Quadrature Digital Upconverter Based on FPGA
This paper introduces in detail the operational principle of every module of quadrature digital upconverter,and in accordance with the analysis of academic parameters,put forward the system scheme of quadrature digital upconverter based on FPGA.The scheme is based on the primary analog emitter in the lab,and quadrature digital upconverter device AD9957 replaces the analog emitter in the system.It presents the block of the hardware structure,the software flow chart and timing control chart,and descripes in detail the operational principle and process of the system.
