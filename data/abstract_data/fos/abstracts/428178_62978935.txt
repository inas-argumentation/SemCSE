The Study of Lexical Cohesion in English Texts
Lexical Cohesion is a device of greater importance to cohere an English text,compared with other devices.Reiteration and collocation are two types of lexical cohesion to complete text coherence by the selection of vocabulary.The paper illustrates the effect of lexical reiteration and collocation and interprets the special features of their forms.The study of lexical cohesion within a text is to help understand the communicative function of a text better.
