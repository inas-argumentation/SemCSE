Reform of assessment system and its effect on non-intelligent factors in physical education of undergraduates
The results of the experiment indicated that the reform scheme of assessment system: 1) was more capable of stimulating students’ learning interest in physical education course;2) was able to effectively enhance the ability of students to treat others correctly,to judge themselves objectively and to manage themselves;3) was beneficial to cultivating and enhancing the ability and spirit of students to learn cooperatively; 4) promoted the fulfillment and realization of multiple objectives of physical education course in colleges and universities, so as to achieve the goal of serving the assessment for course objectives.
