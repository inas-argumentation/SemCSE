234Th cycling in the upper water column of the northwestern Mediterranean Sea
As part of the DYFAMED program, a record of 234Th particulate fluxes in the water column has been obtained with sediment traps moored in the Mediterranean Sea near Corsica. During the same period, profiles of dissolved and particulate 234Th were determined at this site. The sediment-trap data show that 234Th particulate fluxes are closely correlated with biogenic particulate fluxes. The highest fluxes are observed during late winter and early spring, when primary productivity generally increases in these waters. Water-column data indicate that dissolved 234Th is always depleted relative to its present, 238U. This depletion is generally more pronounced near the surface. Particulate 234Th represents 5 to 50% of total thorium, which indicates a significant scavenging of dissolved thorium onto suspended particles. Furthermore, some situations display an important deficit of total thorium relative to its parent. This allowed us to calculate 234Th particulate fluxes, using a steady-state scavenging model in surface waters. Calculated fluxes agreed with sediment-trap data below the euphotic zone.
