Survey of sports lottery market in Ningbo
The questionnaire shows that most consumers have a limited knowledge about sports lottery. Only 18.3% of consumers have a good understanding, while 75.1% of them are just to take a chance to draw a lucky ticket or enjoy themselves, and merely 7.1% of consumers know that it is a key source to raise funds for sports. On the basis of statistics, we analyze the features of sports lottery market, and provide references to strategy-making for managing such market in Ningbo and Zhejiang province.
