Effect of Aging Treatment on AZ80 Magnesium Alloy Structure and Property
Through analysing of microstructure,micro-hardness measurement and fracture scanning,the microstructures,micro-hardness and tenaclty of AZ80 magnesium alloy at different aging temperatures were studied.The effect of aging temperature on property was analyzed.The results show that the aging temperature play a main role in AZ80 alloy.The optical aging temperature of AZ80 alloy is 170℃.
