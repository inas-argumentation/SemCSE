Idea of Leachate Treatment through Analyzing Landfill Pollutants Decomposition Ways in Landfill
The pollutants' composition and decomposition ways are analyzed by substance accounting.The research results indicated that COD flowing into leachate accounted for 2.3% of total COD of landfill.By making full use of landfill' treatment function,recirculation was optimal ways to treat leachate,and membrane technology was effective method to reduce leachate amount for recirculation.At the same time,focus during recirculation of leachate and concentration was put forward.
