Location-aware Middleware Combining Multi-positioning Techniques
Localization is one of key technologies for whatever ubiquitous computing or Internet of Things.In order to solve the problem that existing positioning techniques were mainly developed for a specific application and lack of generability,we built a common location-aware middleware named UbiRTLM(Ubiquitous Real-time Localization Middleware) in this paper.The application service was separated from location-aware technique,and UbiRTLM allows you add dynamically new positioning technique without changing the existing applications and services.The positioning precision and related functions were validated by experimental test.The results showed that: UbiRTLM can meet the precision requirement of most applications and handle related service requests.
