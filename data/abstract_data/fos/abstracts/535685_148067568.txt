The Unification of Knowing and Doing: The Prevention Logic of College Students’ Emergent Incidents
In the prevention of college students’emergent incidents,it is essential to not only know the meaning,features,changes and importance of this type of events,but to take the prevention through the whole process."Knowing" is the ideological basis,while "doing" is the practical carrier.Knowing and doing should be attached equal importance and be performed together.So the unification of knowing and doing is the prevention logic of college students’ emergent incidents.
