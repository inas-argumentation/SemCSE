Industrial application of domestic resin catalyst for esterification of maleic anhydride
The contrast tests are carried out for the comparison between the domestic resin catalyst and the imported resin catalyst.The results show that using the domestic resin catalyst in maleic anhydride esterification, the percent conversion, selectivity and service life can meet the process requirements, and some of the index number is superior to that of the imported catalyst. The industrial test of gradually replacing imported catalyst in the esterification tower with the domestic resin is carried out, and the loading plan of the resin in esterification tower is improved and optimized. The industrial application of the domestic resin catalyst can reduce the cost of production and make a better economic benefit.
