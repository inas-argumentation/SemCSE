Application Research of Support Vector Machine in the Synthetic Evaluation of Power Quality
In the environment of electricity marketization,synthetic quantified evaluation of power quality is an important basis for power quality appraisal and electricity energy pricing.This paper presents a new and more precise standard for the power quality evaluation index.Targeted at the disadvantage of existing evaluation methods,proposed a power quality synthetic evaluation method based on support vector machine(SVM) model.Calculation results show that the synthetic evaluation result of reflecting power quality from the proposed model is objective and authentic,and the structure of the model is simple.
