Effects of Limited Half-cone Angle and Interlayer Spacing on Quality of Incremental Sheet Metal Forming
The incremental sheet parts forming process is a flexible process which forms the sheet point by point based on numerical control technology.The forming principle of incremental sheet mental parts forming process,the limited half-cone angle and so on were presented through experimental analysis.It is proposed that the sheet metal forming point by point leads to the whole sheet deformation.The limited half-cone angle is related not only to the material but also to the thickness of the metal sheet.The interlayer spacing has not great impact on the limited half-cone angle,it relates to the part's surface quality,and with the decrease of interlayer space,the part's surface quality gets finer.
