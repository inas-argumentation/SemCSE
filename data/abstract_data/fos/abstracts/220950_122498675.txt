An experimental investigation of the pressure and concentration dependence of muonic Coulomb capture and cascade in gases
Muonic Coulomb capture ratios and x-ray intensity patterns in four binary mixtures of the gases N2, Ne, Ar, Kr, and Xe have been measured at three atomic ratios. An influence of the concentration has been established. The Lyman series intensity patterns of pure N2, Ne, Ar, Kr, and Xe were measured at pressures between 0.4 and 51 bar and found to depend on the pressure. Possible explanations are discussed.
