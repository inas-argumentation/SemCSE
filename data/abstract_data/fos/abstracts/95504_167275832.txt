Transfer of Manufacturing Industry of Japan and Korea:A Major Opportunity for Economic Internationalization in Jiangsu
In the 1990s,the main partners of foreign economic relations and trade of China shifted from Hong Kong and Taiwan regions to Japan and Korea. In recent years, the manufacturing industry of Japan and Korea has been in an accelerating trend of transfer to China. Jiangsu is a major province of manufacturing industry in China, and also one of the provinces closest to Japan and Korea. The transfer of manufacturing industry from Japan and Korea to China has provided a major opportunity for the economic internationalization of Jiangsu Province. We should grasp this opportunity to build up an internationalized manufacturing industrial base in Jiangsu, so as to speed up the progress of internationalization and modernization of the economy in Jiangsu Province.
