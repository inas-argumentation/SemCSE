Late pleistocene glacial sequence in the area of the high plain of Bogotá (Eastern Cordillera, Colombia)
Four main glacial events could be reconstructed on the basis of morphologic and 14C evidence in the parano regions surrounding the high plain of Bogota (Eastern Cordillera, Colombia). In the area four distinct morainic levels were recognized between ca. (2750-)3000 m and ca. 3750 m. Radiocarbon dates in combination with climatological data derived from palynological evidence indicate that morainic complex 4 was formed between ca. 13,000–12,400 yr B.P., complex 3 between ca. 18,000–14,000 yr B.P., complex 2 between ca. 22,500–19,500 yr B.P., whereas complex 1 may be of Middle Last Glacial age or of Penultimate Glacial age. The spatial distribution of moraines throughout the area clearly shows that limits of former glacier systems were strongly influenced by local climatological conditions.
