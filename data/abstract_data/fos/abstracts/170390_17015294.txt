Research on the Function Consistency of the Network Element based on Atomic Capability Architecture
When the functions of a network element (such as router, switch, etc.) based on Atomic Capability (ATC) architecture are reconstructed dynamically, the topology of ATC will be changed. The process of this reconstruction usually results in the fact that the ATCs being reconfigured cannot process data, and a large parts of the system services suspended and packets discarded. To solve this problem, from a theoretical point of view and under the definition of ‘ATC port status’ and ‘ATC work status’, two different methods to guarantee the consistency of the system during dynamic reconstruction are presented. Meanwhile, the issues of data packet loss and latency are analyzed through some examples. And the result shows that the methods can reduce data packet loss and latency during dynamic reconstruction
