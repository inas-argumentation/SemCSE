Research on an Algorithm for Real-time Visualization of Large-scale Terrain of TIN
How to efficiently process and display a large-scale terrain data is the key problem in the field of terrain visualization.In this paper,it is presented an algorithm for implementing large-scale terrain multiresolution model.Large-scale terrain is divided up through average division method,among which the problem of neighboring terrain blocks boundary while terrain blocks simplifying are solved in focus;Multiresolution TIN model is obtained through half-edge collapse and vertex split,and the method of vertex split is improved.The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method can render large-scale terrain in real time,improve the efficiency and visual effects of terrain walkthrough.
