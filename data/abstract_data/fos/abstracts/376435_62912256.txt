High-speed Bit Permutation Implementation Technology
To solve the problem for the slow to realize the displacement,this paper proposed bi-directional extraction for little-bit wide bit permutation by the characteristics of little-bit wide bit permutation,then,a generation algorithm is proposed for the control information of two-direct extraction. This paper proposed two-direct extraction and extraction-shift for large-bit wide bit permutation. Combined with the features of bi-directional extraction and extraction-shift,this paper designs extraction and bi-directional shift extraction unified hardware architecture based on i Butterfly network. Finally,the paper evaluates the method to achieve high-speed bit-replacement and hardware architecture. The results show that the structure can not only significantly enhance the bit permutation achieve efficiency,but also can support extraction,displacement,bi-directional extraction and other common operations with higher flexibility and handling properties.
