Determination of pesticide residues in agricultural products by HPLC with ultraviolet and fluorescence detection
A ultraviolet and fluorescence detection-high performance liquid chromatographic(HPLC) method for the determination of 12 pesticide residues in agricultural products has been developed.The samples were extracted with acetonitrile,cleaned up by NH2 solid phase extraction column.Chromatographic separation was performed on an Eclipse XDB-C8 column using a linear solvent methanol-water gradient.7 carbamate pesticides were detected by a fluorescence detector after online derivatization following ultraviolet determination of the other 5 pesticides,including imidacloprid,carbendazim,diflubenzuron,chlorobenzuron and phoxim,under optimized analytical conditions.In the linear ranges of concentrations,the correlation coefficients were between 0.9979 and 1.0000.The 12 pesticides were measured in cabbage,apple and mushroom samples at two spiked levels,the average recoveries(n=5) were in the range of 73.0%～108% with relative standard deviations of 0.2%～7.1%.The limits of detection(LOD) were 0.001～0.03 mg/kg.The method is rapid,economic and creative for the determination of pesticide residues in agricultural products.
