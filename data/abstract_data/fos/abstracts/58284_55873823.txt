Rare Errors in a Well-Characterized Electron Pump: Comparison of Experiment and Theory
By measuring each junction of an electron pump in a single electron box configuration, we determine all quantities needed to test the standard theory of pumping error and leakage. Background charges are determined with an imprecision of 60.01e. Electron temperature is measured to 40 mK. Measured charging energies show all junctions have nearly the same capacitance. We find agreement with theory at 140 mK, but a disagreement of many orders of magnitude at 40 mK. We suggest that the excess error and leakage at 40 mK are due to photon-assisted cotunneling processes not included in the standard theory. [S0031-9007(98)06149-3]
