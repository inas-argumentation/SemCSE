Does an Honor System Reduce Class-Room Cheating? an Experimental Answer
In 1948, one year before an Honor System was established at the Brigham Young Univer sity, the first experiment was made upon what will be referred to as Class A. This group of students will be considered the "Before" part of the total experiment. During the years of in troduction and revision of the Honor Code and System (1949-1953), three other classes (Class es B, C, and D) were studied. They constitute the "During" part of the experiment. Finally, five years after the inauguration of the System (1954), a follow-up study was made (Class E) which will be called the "Now" stage of the ex periment.
