A Preliminary Study on Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) of Kudzu
On the basis of past experience, and by way of scientific research and verification of practice, an initial research on growth condition, cultivars or types, seedling breeding, planting method, field management, disease and pest prevention, picking and processing, and quality standard monitoring of traditional Chinese medication of kudzu have been carried out. This research can provide scientific foundation for the production of (GAP′s) medicines, the conservation of wild resources, and the sustainable utilization of kudzu.
