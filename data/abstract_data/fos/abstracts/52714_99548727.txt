Determination of V_E in sea buckthorn seed oil by high performance liquid chromatography
A normal-phase high performance liquid chromatography(NP-HPLC)method and a reversed-phase high performance liquid chromatography(RP-HPLC)method were developed for the rapid determination of Vitamin E and its isomers in sea buckthorn seed oil.The linearity,precision,repetition,average recovery and stability were validated for each method.The results showed that both methods were simple,rapid and accurate for the analysis of vitamin E and its isomers in sea buckthorn seed oil.However,the content of VE and its isomers in the oil was higher by RP-HPLC than by NP-HPLC.
