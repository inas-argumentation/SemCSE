Effects of Isothermy and Continuous Cooling Transformation on Microstructure of Mold Steel
The microstructure of plastic mould steel was forged under sand cooling,and tempering at 350 ℃,and was studied under low cooling rate and isothermal treatment at different temperatures.The results show that a little ferrites appear when isothermal treatment is at 760 ℃,but large numbers of pearlite form when pearlite zone is thermal insulated.Bainite transformation occurs quickly.At later isothermal treatment period,the microstructure of the mold goes through isothermy and continuous cooling transformation is similar to tempering microstructure.
