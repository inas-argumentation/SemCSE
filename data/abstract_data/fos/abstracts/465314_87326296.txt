Proper Reserved Fruit Amount in Longan'Lidongben'
The effects of different reserved fruit amount on fruit quality,vegetative shoot quality and output benefit in Longan 'Lidongben' were studied in this paper The results showed that 60~70 reserved fruit amount and 32% fruit cluster number was the most suitable,with these conditions the single fruit weight was between 13.07～13.33 g,vegetative shoot coarseness was between 7.78～8.46 mm,vegetative shoot length was between 23.78～26.35 cm,compound leaf number was between 16.86～19.60 and the next year flower formation rate of shoot was 71.04～77.13%,which were similar to the CK(fine bearing basal shoot).The reserved fruit amount was a little higher for production at 38% and was a little lower at 28%,the reserved fruit amount which was at 42% and 48% would lead to alternate bearing.
