Defiance and Deference in Mexico's Colonial North: Indians under Spanish Rule in Nueva Vizcaya (review)
This volume is not just a narrow exercise in translation and linguistic analysis. It builds on seminal reinterpretations of the so called "spiritual conquest" created by scholars such as Louise Burkhart, and fits firmly into the growing stream of highquality studies which James Lockhart has called the new philology, studies which employ dense cross-disciplinary analyses of indigenous authored or influenced manuscripts to achieve a better understanding of the textures of native life under Spanish rule, and of the ways in which peoples such as the Nahuas came to grips with the new culture and faith which were confronting them.
