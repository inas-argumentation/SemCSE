The Research on the Adaption of SPS on Jiangsu Export Trade
Under world trade integration advancement,the new technical trade barrier gradually substitutes for traditional the non-tariff barrier.The Jiangsu agriculture is increasingly accepting the challenge of the SPS agreement.Food and the Draft animal exportation accounts for the total export the proportion unceasingly to drop with 2,004 ratios only had 1/3 of 1998.There are various reasons contribute to this:Subjectively,the quality of Jiangsu agricultural products do not stand up to the most rigorous test as well as the agricultural technology level is relatively low.Objectively,due to huge trade deficit and stemming from the need of protection domestic market,developed countries continually set barrier to the export farm produce of Jiangsu.In order to improve this situation,the enterprises of Jiangsu agricultural product exportation must start from improving the quality of agricultural product with the purpose of achieving the international standard.In addition,they should contact with guild where they can get some instructions.Finally,they should walk the path of the circulation agriculture and try their best in improving the agriculture ecological environment.
