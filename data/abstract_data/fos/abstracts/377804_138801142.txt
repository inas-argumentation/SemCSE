Several essential techniques of the automatic control system for PVC material producing
With the rapid development of PVC constructional material, the material production ability of constructional enterprise is impeding improvement of the whole production chain. Whereas the process of PVC constructional material production concerns with a wide scope of diverse sciences, such as conveyance and reserve of bulk material, automatically and precisely weighing of bulk material, speed mixing of several kinds of material and automatic control of the whole system under the abominable working condition, etc. are all essential aspects of the system.
