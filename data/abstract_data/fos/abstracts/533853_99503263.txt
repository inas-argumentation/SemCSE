Effects of Varieties and Environments on Quality in Soft Wheat
In order to determine relative contribution of variety and environment to variations in wheat processing quality, 18 Chinese wheat cultivars and experimental lines were evaluated in two environments (Hefei and Suzhou) in 2001. SDS sedimentation volume, amylose content, Polyphenol oxidase (PPO) activity, alkaline water retention capacity (AWRC) and swelling power were measured. The results indicated that the differences among the wheat varieties were highly significant in AWRC, SDS sedimentation volume and relative amylose content, significant in swelling power, and not significant in PPO activity. The differences between the locations were highly significant in AWRC, PPO activity and relative amylose content, not significant in SDS sedimentation volume and swelling power. The correlation analyses indicated that AWRC and relative amylose content were significantly influenced by environments and cultivars.Variety had a larger influence on SDS sedimentation volume and swelling power than genotype, but the variance in PPO activities between two locations appeared larger than the variation produced by the cultivars.
