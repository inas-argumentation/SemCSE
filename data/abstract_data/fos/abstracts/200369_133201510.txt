No. 31091. United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and Ethiopia
Exchange of notes constituting an agreement between the government of the united kingdom of great britain and northern ireland and the transitional government of ethiopia concerning certain commercial debts (the united kingdom/ethiopia debt agreement no. 1 (1992)). Addis Ababa, 11 august 1993 and 12 august 1993 [united nations, treaty series, vol. 1792, i-31091.]
