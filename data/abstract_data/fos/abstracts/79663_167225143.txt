Planning Proposals on Broadband Wireless Communication System Covered by the Sea
Maritime radio communication system is an integrated communications system,which requires to achieve real-time interconnection,interoperability,interoperability under the limit of extreme environmental conditions at sea.Routine services,such as voice,data,are require not only to provide,but also to transfer multimedia broadband services in the system,which needs to meet the requirement of interference,fading,security,the technologies of spread spectrum,adaptive and software radio need to be widely used.The capacity of the bottleneck on the surface coverage of base stations is analyzed when the principle of the wireless signal propagation and delay mechanism of TDD mobile communication system are cared for,and some suggestions and planning strategies are proposed on broadband emergency communications systems.
