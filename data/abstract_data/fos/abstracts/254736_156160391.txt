The difference between Chinese and western educational cultures is an un-neglectable issue in the development of modern scholastic physical education in China
By studying the causes that hinder the development of modern scholastic physical education in China,the authors pointed out that the difference between Chinese and western educational cultures had been neglected in modern scholastic physical education in China in the process of referring to the development of western education,which had caused the obvious lag of development of modern scholastic physical education in China.In the mean time,the authors offered some referable opinions on the development of modern scholastic physical education in China.
