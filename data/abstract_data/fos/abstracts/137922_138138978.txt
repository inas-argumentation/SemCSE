Microstructure Characteristics and Its Effects on Corrosion of 430 Stainless Steel Plate
The microstructure,inter-crystal corrosion characteristics and corrosion characteristics of 430 stainless steel plate hot-rolled and annealed at 950 ℃ for 5 min in the medium of acid corrosion under larger reduction deformation were researched.The results show that a significant layered corrosion phenomenon of hot-rolled 430 ferrite stainless steel appears in the media of 6%FeCl3 and 10%HCl,but uniformity corrosion in the annealing plate.There is obvious tendency of intergranular corrosion in the 0.5 mol/L H2SO4 + 0.01 mol/L KSCN solution,but eliminates in the state of annealing.Comprehensive analysis shows that a fracture morphology and corrosion characteristics of testing steel is induced by the band of carbon particles.
