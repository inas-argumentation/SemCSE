Adverse Reactions of 2176 Cases Clinical Compound Coptis Injection
Objective To clinically observe, generalize and analyze the manifestation and characteristics of adverse reactions of compound coptis injection.Methods To set up records of the clinical use of compound coptis injection of outpatient and inpatient department by special registration cards, then generalize and analyze the adverse reactions by th follow-up files.Results Adverse reactions occurred to 47 cases of altogether the 2176 patients who got the clinical usage of compound coptis injection.The adverse reactions were all mild including 35 cases of papular eruption, vascular stimulation and the rest of restlessness, itching, insomnia, short breath and drug fever.Allergic shock occurred to one case who was 79-year old and recovered after withdraw of drug and expectant treatment.Conclusion The clinical incidence of adverse reactions of compound coptis injection was 2.16%.Most of the adverse reactions are mild anaphylactic reactions.Shock occurred to only one patient.No patients died.It is secure to use the drug clinically.
