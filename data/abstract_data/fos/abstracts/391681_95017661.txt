Solid electrolyte HC sensor on gasoline engines
Abstract A sensor for detecting CH 4 in exhaust gases from gasoline engines has been constructed from two Pd | YSZ | Au cells, which are attached to each other with their Pd electrodes in the interior side of the assembly. When the sample gases consisting of CH 4 , O 2 and H 2 O were fed to the sensor element at 750°C, EMF values were generated from the one cell and then compensated by pumping O 2 electrochemically from the other cell. Currents applied to the latter cell linearly increased with CH 4 concentration, but were not influenced by changing O 2 concentration.
