Information Research of Power Grid Corporation Based on ERP
It will become to be business relationship between power grid Co., power generation Co. and the users with the further reform of power systems. To adapt to the changes, power corporations should form modern management model in order to enhance management, improve enterprise efficiency.As an advanced enterprise management model, ERP will bring magnitude charges in the application of power grid Co.The paper introduces the basis principles and the goals of ERP, then analyzes mostly running functions in power grid Co.,and discusses implement processes of ERP model.
