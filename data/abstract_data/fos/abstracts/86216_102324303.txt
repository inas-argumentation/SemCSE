Extraction on total saponins from Salicornia bigelovii Torr.
Total saponins were extracted from Salicornia bigelovii Torr..using ethanol as extraction solvent.The factors of the extraction were explored by single factor experiment and orthogonal array design methods.The research shows that the optimal conditions for extraction of saponins were:extracting 3 times(30 min) at 70 ℃ and 1:40 of solid/liquid ratio with 70 % ethanol.The yield can reach 2.04% under the optimum extracting conditions.
