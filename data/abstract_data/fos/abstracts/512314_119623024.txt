Cuspidal representations of GL(n,F) distinguished by a maximal Levi subgroup, with F a non-archimedean local field
Let ρ be a cuspidal representation of G L ( n , F ) , with F a non-archimedean local field, and H a maximal Levi subgroup of G L ( n , F ) . We show that if ρ is H -distinguished, then n is even, and H is isomorphic to G L ( n / 2 , F ) × G L ( n / 2 , F ) .
