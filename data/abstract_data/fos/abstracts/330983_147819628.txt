On state intervention of world class universities in western countries
State and government are all along the most decisive strengths to the prosperity and ruin of universities,the arising of world class universities in the western countries are not only the necessity outcome of the development of history,but also the outcome of constant intervening of states.This paper revealed the basic conflict of state and universities: freedom and control,corporation and opposition,and analyzed three state intervention model: intervention in process;intervention in target and omnibus intervention.
