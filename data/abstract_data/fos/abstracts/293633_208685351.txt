The application of mean averaging kernels to mean trace gas distributions
Abstract. To avoid unnecessary data traffic it is sometimes desirable to apply
mean averaging kernels to mean profiles of atmospheric state variables.
Unfortunately, application of averaging kernels and averaging are not
commutative in cases when averaging kernels and state variables are
correlated. That is to say, the application of individual averaging
kernels to individual profiles and subsequent averaging will, in
general, lead to different results than averaging of the original
profiles prior to the application of the mean averaging kernels, unless
profiles and averaging kernels are fully independent. The resulting error,
however, can be corrected by subtraction of the covariance between the
averaging kernel and the vertical profile. Thus, it is recommended to
calculate the covariance profile along with the mean profile
and the mean averaging kernel.
