Measures for Improving the Quality of Silver Jewelry Casting
Sterling silver jewelries often have defects such as gas hole,shrinkage hole and porosity,sand inclusion,oxidizing slag,etc.during casting.These defects mainly come from two aspects.One is the casting process especially some critical procedures such as gating design,investment and baking which have great effect on casting quality and should be strictly controlled.The other is related to the characteristics of silver.During solidification the solution of oxygen in silver is decreased sharply,this makes the casting easy to form gas porosity.Also,sterling silver is alloyed with copper which is easy to be oxidized and to cause oxide inclusion.The molten metal should be avoided to contact with air during melting and casting.Using protective measures such as vacuum,inert gas,reductive flame and boric acid coverage can effectively decrease casting defects.
