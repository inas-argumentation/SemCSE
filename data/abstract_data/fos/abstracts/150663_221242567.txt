Two orthorhombic crystal structures of a galactose‐specific lectin from Artocarpus hirsuta in complex with methyl‐α‐d‐galactose. Addendum
In the paper by Rao et al. [(2004), Acta Cryst. D60, 1404–1412] the Protein Data Bank reference codes were inadvertently omitted from the paper. The coordinates and structure factors for the structures reported in this paper have been depositied with the PDB and the reference codes are as follows: galactose-specific lectin at 2.5 A resolution, 1toq, r1toqsf, and at 3.0 A resolution, 1tp8, r1tp8sf.
