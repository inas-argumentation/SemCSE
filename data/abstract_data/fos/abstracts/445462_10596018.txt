Critical Phenomena for Electronic Structure at the Large-Dimension Limit.
We show that the symmetry breaking of the electronic structure configurations at the large-{ital D} limit is completely analogous to the standard phase transitions and critical phenomena in statistical mechanics. Electronic structure at the large-{ital D} limit exhibits critical points with mean field critical exponents ({beta}=1/2, {alpha}=0{sub dis}, {delta}=3, and {gamma}=1). The complete mapping is presented for the Hartree-Fock two-electron atom in weak electric field and the two Coulomb center problems. {copyright} {ital 1996 The American Physical Society.}
