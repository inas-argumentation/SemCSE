A method for obtaining psychophysical thresholds from the pigeon.
In several recent experiments, I have used a new and rather complex method to measure the pigeon's absolute visual threshold. The published reports of these studies (2, 3, 5, 6) include only brief summaries of this technique. The present paper describes the method in detail and outlines the reasoning behind its various aspects. For the sake of brevity and clarity, I do not attempt to discuss other applications of the technique. Also, I do not add to the previous apparatus descriptions (2, 3, 5), except where such addition is necessary to clarify procedural details.
