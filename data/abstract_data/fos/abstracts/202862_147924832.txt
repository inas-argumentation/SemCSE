On relations between the phenomenon of perishing young and the health education in academic schools
The phenomenon that the elite of the intellectual perish young has not been alleviated from the nineteen eighties till now.On the contrary, it has been growing from bad to worse.The phenomenon has transcended the field of pure pathology; therefore it is possessed with great social significance. The analysis of the dying young phenomenon shall provoke the profound thinking of the current health education in universities and colleges.It naturally follows that the improvement of mental health mainly lies in the health education to the full extent in every aspect.
