A New Type of Control Chart Limits for Means, Ranges, and Sequential Runs
Since most conventional control chart limits are designed so that the ratio of the expected number of false alarms to the number of samples tested is fixed in advance irrespective of the sample size, the ratio of false alarms to the number of articles tested varies with the sample size. In this paper, control charts are designed for which the expected ratio of false alarms to the number of articles tested is independent of the sample size. The power of the control charts in relation to sample size is investigated.
