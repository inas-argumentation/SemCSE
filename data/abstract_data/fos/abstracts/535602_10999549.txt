Distributed Systems Management - Overview of a COST-11 ter Project
The paper provides an introduction to and overview of one of the COST-11 ter projects. It sets out the scope and objectives of the work and summarises the three main threads of activity at the mid-point of the project: (1) a study of distributed systems architecture as it impacts management, noting that an object oriented model provides a powerful tool to specify management services; (2) a study of monitoring which has identified a hierarchical ordering of management activities; (3) a pilot implementation extending the scope of the Amoeba operating system kernel to operate over a wide area network. The paper identifies the relevance of this work to the development of OSI standards.
