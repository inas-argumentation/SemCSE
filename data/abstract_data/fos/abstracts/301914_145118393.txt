Psycholinguistic Abilities in Learning-Disabled, Emotionally Disturbed, and Normal Children
1This investigation was supported in part by projects 427, 912, and 918 from the Maternal and Child Health Service; Health Services Mental Health Grant HD-00324 from the National Institute of Child Health and Human Development; the State of Ohio Department of Health; and the National Foundation March of Dimes. The use of the concept &dquo;minimal brain dysfunction&dquo; to explain learning and behavior disabilities has increased, despite theoretical inadequacies and difficulties in definition by (Clements, 1966). A large number of special classes for children diagnosed as having &dquo;minimal brain dysfunction&dquo; or &dquo;minimal neurological handicaps&dquo; have been established; these are frequently called &dquo;learning-disabilities&dquo; (LD) classes.
