Application of SLS-RP Technique in Manufacturing Alkali-Corrosion Resisting Valve Pieces
Alkali-corrosion resisting valves is a type of industrial product that it is very necessary but there are not suitable products to alkali factory up till now.In order to develop a new type of alkali-corrosion resisting valves,in this paper,the integration technique about the combination of sintering alkali-corrosion resisting valve prototypes of PS-base powder material by SLS technique and manufacturing the valve castings by investment casting technique is introduced.Some problems about manufacturing of casting mold shell,especially post-processing,in the disposal of wax patterns,are researched.On the grounds of all above,the rapid casting process from three-dimensional digital model to metal parts is drawn up.
