Cultural Construction in New Countryside from the Perspective of Ecological Culture
The cultural construction in new countryside needs a comprehensive consideration of cultural background,cultural heritage,cultural carrier and cultural connotation.Although transplanting urban culture into countryside may bring new cultural elements at certain level,it cannot fundamentally raise cultural construction and revolution in countryside,while the rural cultural development maintains its own traditional living space and gesture. Understanding such a phenomenon needs rediscovering rural cultural landscape,analyzing culture transplant approach in the ecological culture system,and integrating highly confident urban culture and relatively conscious rural culture.
