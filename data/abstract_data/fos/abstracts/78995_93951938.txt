EDC·HCl and Potassium Salts of Oxyma and Oxyma-B as Superior Coupling Cocktails for Peptide Synthesis
Nowadays, DIC is the most widely used carbodiimide for solid-phase peptide synthesis, while EDC·HCl is mostly used only for solution-phase synthesis. In this paper, we report new coupling cocktails containing EDC·HCl in combination with potassium salts of OxymaPure and Oxyma-B (i.e., KOxyma and K-Oxyma-B, respectively).
