Nongovernmental Organizations and Food Safety of China
At present, food safety incidents in our country occur frequently .This requires us to think about our current state- monopoly food safety and inspection, prompts us to break the old management mode, develops new management ideas, and introduces social forces boldly such as nongovernmental organizations to participate in the supervision and management of food safety. Adhering to government-driven, we should guide non-governmental organizations to raise the consciousness of food safety, participate in and promote the establishment of a sound food safety system, re-supervise the superintendent of food safety, thereby decreasing food safety incidents and protecting consumers’health and safety.
