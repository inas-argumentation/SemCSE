Thixo-extrusion Forming of Semi-solid Magnesium Alloy
The thixo-extrusion die was designed and made. The influence of different process parameters on the deformation force was studied by thixo-extrusion experiments and routine extrusion experiments on AZ61 alloy. The results show that the thixo-extrusion deformation force decreases with the increase of extrusion temperature; the deformation rate increases with the increase of extrusion force. Using the constitutive relationship of semi-solid AZ61 alloy,the thixo-forming of semi-solid AZ61 alloy were simulated. Contrasted the thixo-extrusion experiment results and the simulation results, it is found that they coincide well.
