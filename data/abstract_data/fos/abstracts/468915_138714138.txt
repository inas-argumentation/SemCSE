Effect of Solidification Rate on Primary Phase M_7C_3 of High Chromium Cast Iron
The effect of solidification rate on the primary M_7C_3 phase of high chromium cast iron in-situ composite with the composition of Fe-3.35wt%C-27.5wt%Cr was studiedby the unidirectional solidification of liquid metal cooling.The results show the cross section morphology of primary phase M_7C_3-type carbides is hexagonal and hollow hexagonal at high solidification rate,and with decrease of solidification rate,the cross section morphology of primary phase tends to be in U and L shape.The hardness of hexagonal,U and L shape primary phase M_7C_3-type carbides is about HV 2500,but the hollow hexagonal is about HV 1550,which indicates that the primary carbide M_7C_3 has two growth directions during the process of unidirectional solidification.
