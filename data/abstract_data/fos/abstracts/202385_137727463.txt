PIEZOTHERMOELASTIC MODELLING OF WEAK INTERFACES IN PIEZOELECTRIC COMPOSITE LAMINATES
The constitutive laws at weak interfaces in piezoelectric composite laminates are multi-fields coupling.Interfacial elastic field is characterized by the relation between the interfacial displacement jumps and the interfacial transverse stresses.Interfacial temperature field is characterized by the relation between the interfacial temperature jump and the interfacial normal opening.Interfacial electric field is characterized by one assumption: electrically permeable,impermeable or semi-permeable.In this paper,the jumps of interfacial displacements,temperature and electric potential are obtained in linear forms.Based on forgoing assumptions,the piezothermoelastic coupling solutions of simply supported piezoelectric laminates with weak interfaces in cylindrical bending are deduced.The numerical examples show that the choice of interfacial electric assumptions has slight effect on the elastic field,but substantial effect on the electric field.Electrically impermeable assumption results in the maximum interfacial potential jump under all load cases.Some benchmark numerical results may be used as reference for further studies.
