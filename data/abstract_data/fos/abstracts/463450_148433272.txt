The change of the concepts of talent and education to deepen the higher education reform
Nowadays the economic situation and educational situation have taken great change. The development of higher education is confronted with new problems and challenges, which request us to change our concepts of talent as well as concepts of education to deepen the higher education reform. Professor Weng Shi Lie put forward his own ideas in six aspects. They are "The connotation of talent concept in new era", "The core of engineering education", "The Construction of university intellectual ecology", "The status of universities in the national system of creation", "Deepening the reform of higher educational management system", "Multi\|channel fund raising and multi\|faceted operation of universities".
