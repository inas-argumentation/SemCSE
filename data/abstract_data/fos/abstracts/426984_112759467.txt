Failure Analysis of Track-links of Bucket-wheel Coal-mining Machine
Track link is an important part of bucket wheel coal mining machine in open air coal mine. Its failure service may lead to catastrophic results. It will be a very serious security accident. Based on a failure analysis, overcoming the shortcomings of failed track links, referring to some foreign technical information, a new kind of steel was developed. Track links made of this new steel were put into operations in coal mine. Its operation was quite smooth. This means that the failure analysis made in this paper and the development of a new kind of steel was quite successful.
