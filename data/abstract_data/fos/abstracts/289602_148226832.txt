On HEIs' educational ideology:a 30-year retrospection and prospect
The Symposium on HEIs'(higher education institutes) educational ideology co-sponsored by Huazhong University of Science Technology,Hu'nan University and Nanjing University of Aeronautics Astronautics has convened for twelve times consecutively since 1986.During the past 22 years,The Symposium on HEIs' educational ideology,against the background of reform and opening up to the outside world and modernization,has closely followed Chinese reality and the trend of higher education development in the world,achieved significant theoretical fruits at ideology and conceptual level through free academic discussion and debate,iven full play to theoretical guiding in Chinese higher education reform and development.Great influence on the disciplinary construction and development of higher education has been achieved.
