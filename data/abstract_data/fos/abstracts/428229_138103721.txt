A Study on Pretreatment of Fly Ash from Incineration before Solidification with Cement
In order to stabilize and reuse fly ashes from municipal solid waste incineration(MSWI),different pretreatment procedures before solidification with cement are studied to find out the changes of resulted blocks in compressive strength and heavy metal leaching.Three pretreatment methods are investigated: 1) humidifying fly ash with deionized water and stored under anaerobic condition for 24h;2) treating fly ash with FeSO4 solution;and 3) no pretreatment.The results shows that under the same mixing ratio of fly ash and cement,pretreating fly ash with FeSO4 solution can obtain the highest compressive strength and lowest heavy metal leaching.It seems to be a promising way to dispose and reuse MSWI fly ashes.
