Effect of content of BaTiO3 surface cubic layer on the ionic conductivity enhancement in composite solid electrolytes
The effect of the content of BaTiO 3 surface cubic layer on the ionic conductivity enhancement of Na 4 Zr 2 Si 3 O 12 has been investigated. The cubic content increases with increasing applied stress prior to the firing and also with increasing of the cooling rate during the processing of BaTiO 3 . The conductivity enhancement depends on the cubic content, and the extrinsic factors to control the enhancement are discussed.
