Research Progress on Sugarcane Smut Disease and Sporisorium scitaminea
ugarcane smut disease, caused by the fungus Sporisorium scitaminea, is an important disease of sugarcane worldwide and the most serious sugarcane disease in China, leading to considerable yield losses and quality reduction in sugarcane. The pathogen characters, classification, physiological differentiation, genetic diversity and molecular detection technique of S. scitaminea, as well as occurrence, economic losses and control methods of this disease were reviewed. The future research strategies for sugarcane smut disease were also discussed in this paper.
