THE X-RAY ANALYSIS OF BIS(1-(2-THIAZOLYLAZO)-2-NAPHTHOLATO)COBALT(III) PERCHLORATE
Crystal structure of bis(1-(2-thiazolylazo)-2-naphtholato)cobalt(III) perchlorate was determined by X-ray analysis. A cobalt atom is surrounded octahedrally by two terdentate ligands in the mer configuration. Short intramolecular contacts of 2.2–2.3 A are observed between azo nitrogen atom(N(2)) and naphthol hydrogen atom(H(9)).
