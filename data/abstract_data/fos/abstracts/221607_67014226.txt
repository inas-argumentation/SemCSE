Optimization of Resources for Wireless Network Control System Based on Sending Control Strategy
This paper studies the wireless network control system, describes the current situation of wireless network control system and describes the general approach to optimize the network. Then put forward the resources to optimize the design of wireless network control system. Build a system model, and establish the corresponding scheduling problem, and achieve optimal design. Through validate with the results from optimization design by simulation. The results show that the estimation performance optimization algorithm liberal radio network control system designed in this paper is better than the best TDMA strategy.
