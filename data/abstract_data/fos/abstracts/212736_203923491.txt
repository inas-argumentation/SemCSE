INFLUENCE OF TEMPERATURE ON SOLVENT SELECTIVITY FOR EXTRACTIVE DISTILLATION
Extractive distillation is an important and useful technique for separating close boiling and azeotropic liquid mixtures.Solvent selectivity is the key to achieving success.In this paper, the relation of solvent selectivity versus temperature for different mixed systems(C_6H_6+C_6H_ 12+DMSO, C_6H_6+C_6H_ 12+DMF,EtOH+EA+DMF+DMSO, C_6H_6+C_6H_ 12+DMF+DMSO) was examined by the modified UNIFAC model,which is a well-tested method to select a solvent for extractive distillation.With increasing temperature,solvent selectivity was decreased.In addition, the influence of temperature on solvent selectivity was set forth for the first time by using the Clausius-Mosotti equation.
