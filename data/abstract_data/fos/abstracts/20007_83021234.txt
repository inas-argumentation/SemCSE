Preliminary study on DNA vaccine of Chinese HIV-1 strains.
For developing a HIV 1 vaccine against the prevalent HIV 1 strains in China, we constructed the DNA vaccine plasmids of the representative gag gene and gp 120 gene, and immunized the Balb/C mice. Preliminary results showed: (1) gag and gp120 DNA vaccine could induce HIV 1 specific cell mediated and humoral immunity; (2) Co immunization with gag and gp120 plasmids induced both gag and gp120 specific cell mediated and humoral immune response, and showed stronger than that of immunization with gag or gp120 alone; (3) The specific CTL induced by subtype B gp 120 could recognize gp120 antigen of subtype C, which showed a CTL cross reaction between subtype B and C. The preliminary results showed hope of developing HIV 1 DNA vaccine and worthy to continue further studies.
