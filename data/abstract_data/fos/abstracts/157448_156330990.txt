A Survey of "Ecological Imbalance" in College English Teaching in Some Universities and Some Countermeasures——Keeping Teaching Content and Teaching Object in Dynamic Balance
This paper tries to illustrate that unsuitable textbooks are the main cause of "the ecological imbalance" in college English teaching ,through the quantitative and qualitative analysis of the differences in English level,motivation,attitude,study resource between the 2 group students who use the same textbooks in Shanghai and Anhui.And this paper also presents some suggestions on how to solve the problem.
