Continuous tuning of the emission frequency of electron-beam-pumped lasers
An investigation was made of the possibility of using solid solutions for continuous tuning of the emission frequency of electron-beam-pumped lasers. A method was developed for growing ZnxCd1−xSe and ZnxCd1−xS solid solutions and a study was made of their laser characteristics. Continuous tuning of the emission wavelength extending over 270 A was obtained for ZnxCd1−xS samples with a composition gradient. The laser action threshold and the output power varied slowly within the tuning range.
