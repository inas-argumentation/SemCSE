Preparation of Mesoporous TiO2 Photocatalyst by Selective Dissolving of Titania–Silica Binary Oxides
Mesoporous TiO2 photocatalyst, which consisted of uniform mesopores (pore size about 4 nm) and with anatase framework and huge surface area as high as 329 m2 g−1, was prepared by selectively dissolving of silica in Ti–Si binary oxides.
