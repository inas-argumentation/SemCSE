Influence of Heat Treatment on Microstructure and Stress Rupture Property of Nickel-base Superalloy
The influences of three kinds of heat treatment processes on the microstructure and property of nickel-base superalloy were investigated,which are 1220℃×4h solution+1150℃×4h aging+870℃×24h aging,1220℃×4h solution + 870 ℃× 24 h aging and 1100 ℃× 4 h aging.The results show that the heat treatment can improve the stress rupture life at 1100℃/40MPa of the alloy remarkably,the stress rupture life can be enhanced from 24h to 65,64,and 53h respectively.The as-cast microstructure consists of γ,γ' and minor MC carbide,M3B2boride.The volume percent of γ' is 58%.After solution and two stage aging,MC presents on grain boundary as small particles.Meanwhile γ' has two sizes and morphologies.After the heat treatment of solution and single aging,MC dissociates into M23C6,which degrades the elongation of the alloy.The morphology of γ' is regular cubic,and is 0.4μm in size.Two scales and morphologies of γ' precipitate in the alloy if the heat treatment is directly aging,and the size of carbide decreases.
