Research Advances in Key Technology of Trust Model for P2P
Trust model is an important research topic for the P2P application. Recent research shows some intrinsic limitation in P2P trust model is proposed. In this article we discuss and classify some major algorithms proposed for trust model. The fundamental problems in trust model research are identified and the metrics for evaluation of trust model algorithms are summarized. Some topics are also discussed for their close relationship with trust model research. Finally, some possible directions for future research are discussed.
