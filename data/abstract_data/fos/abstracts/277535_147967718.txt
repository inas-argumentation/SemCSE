On soft budget constraint and financial decision-making right of public colleges
What the soft budget constraint means that asymmetry between financial control power and ultimate financial responsibility in the public colleges.It will lead to the colleges and their decision-makers not to be able to bear the finacial result.If we permit the colleges having the independent power of decision-making,the colleges will trigger over loan and over expenditure.This suggests that the public college's right get loan from the loan market should be strictly limited.In another words,the public colleges should not be able to have the decision-making power from the bank.
