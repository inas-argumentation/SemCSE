The New Trend of Transnational Corporations Transferring Their Manufacturing Centers to China
Transnational corporations have begun to bring China into the boundary of their global strategic arrangement since 1990s. The trend of transferring the manufacturing center to China has become more and more strong; the investment in the form of the merger and acquisition is increasing and the solely owned trend is obvious; the scale of investment and the level of technology are raised; the investment develops more to connected and systematical industrial chain; the location of the investment is concentrated; to capture the paramount of the markets is also one of the new trend of the investment strategy objective.
