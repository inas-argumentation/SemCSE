Building the Board of Trustees in Chinese Public University:From the Perspective of Corporate Governance Structure
Building the board of trustees in Chinese public university is a new exploration of higher education reform,whose aim is to improve the corporate governance structure of universities and establish the legal status.From the perspective of corporate governance structure,it should make the board of trustees in Chinese public university obtain the similar legal status of state-owned company,and make it become the enforcement organization of the government significant item decision-making and the decision-making organization besides the government,through defining its responsibilities and improving its operation mechanism.
