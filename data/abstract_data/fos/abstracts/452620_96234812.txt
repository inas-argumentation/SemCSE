Aniline polymerisation in HUP-like frameworks
Abstract The aniline intercalation in chemically dehydrated H 3 OUO 2 PO 4 · 3H 2 O (HUP) and H 5 O 2 Fe(SO 4 ) 2 · 2H 2 O(HFe), its protonation and the formation of oligomers and/or conducting polymers by CuCl contact have been studied by IR absorption, Raman scattering, X ray diffraction, and frequency dependent conductivity. The role of the atmosphere of handling and of the protons of the host framework are studied. A discussion of the polymerization mechanism is given.
