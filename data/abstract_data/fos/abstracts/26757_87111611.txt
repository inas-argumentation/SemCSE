Cloning and Analysis of the Gene Encoding Glycilin from Pigeonpea[Cajanus cajan(L.) Millsp.]
A special cDNA fragments with 833 base pairs(bp) long from the pigeonpea material was clone by means of designing degenerate primers homology to the sequences of other leguminous plants,RT-PCR amplification and agarose gel electrophoresis,blast tools.The full-length 1 595 bp cDNA encoding pigeonpea glycinlin(PPGLY)was cloned by RT-PCR,3' and 5'RACE technology.It contained a 1 551 bp(16 to 1 566 bp)ORF,encoding 516 amino acids with PPGLY molecular weight 57.718 ku of an isoelectric point(pI)4.68.The deduced amino acid sequence of PPGLY showed high homologies 83%,79%,77%,73%,67%,65% and 65% with Glycine max(1303273A),Glycine max(CAA55977.1),Lupinus angustifolius(AEB33710.1),Arachis hypogaea(AAU21493.1),Phaseolus vulgaris(ADR30064.1),Vicia fatal(CAA83674.1),Medicago truncatula(XP_003590690.1),respectively.
