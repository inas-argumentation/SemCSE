Talk about Cultivating and Developing Inner Sense of Hearing of playing the piano
It's very important for music teachers to lay emphasis on strengthening the cultivation and the development of students' inner sense of hearing. The main approaches we may use are:emphasizing the basic training of students' singing skills, listening comprehension of music and the appreciation of piano music works; Cultivating students to form the habit of listening and analyzing the music works consciously; developing their full imagination to piano playing; widening students' scope of knowledge, which is very helpful for them to learn more from other copy courses and improving students' aesthetic judgement.
