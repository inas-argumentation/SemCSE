Biodegradability of Wastewater Containing Tetrahydrofuran
The results to study tetrahydrofuran (THF) biodegradability show that anaerobic digestion could be inhibited a bit by addition of THF under the condition of single substrate. Anaerobic microbe was inhibited noticeably and gas generation reduced abruptly when THF concentration reached 200 mg/L, and increase of THF concentration had a helpful effect on generation of methane gas and biodegradability of THF under the anaerobic condition of co-substrate. Although BOD_(5)/COD in THF wastewater was 0.096, which was aerobic refractory organic pollutant, it hasn't inhibited microbe in aerobic sludge system because of its high volatility. The removal efficiency of COD_(cr) was up to 84% after an aeration of 12 hours.
