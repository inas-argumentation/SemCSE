Application of Plackett-burman design for determining key factors of ultrasnoic extraction of procyanidin from peanut red skin
Ultrasonic wave was used to extract procyanidin from peanut red skin in this paper, six influence factors of extraction were evaluated. On the base of single factors experiments, Plackett-burman design was applied to study the factors of ultrasonic extraction and screen out the key factors: alcohol concentration (P=0.02246), ultrasonic temperature(P=0.020182)and ultrasonic time(P=0.045504).
