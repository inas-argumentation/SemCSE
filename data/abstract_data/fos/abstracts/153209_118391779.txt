Microscopic description of γ-induced4He break-up processes
The theory ofγ break-up processes in the cluster model reaction scheme is developed and applied to the4He case. The large difference between (γ, p) and (γ, n) cross sections reported in the literature is not found, but the result of this calculation agrees well with (γ, p) measurements. Furthermore (γ, d) reactions are studied for low energies.
