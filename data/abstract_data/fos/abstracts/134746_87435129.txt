Mutation Breeding for Citrus reticulata Blanco of Few Seeds
Mature autumn shoots were taken from the adult'Nianju'(Citrus reticulata Blanco)trees and the leaves of the shoots were stripped.The shoots were irradiated with gamma rays from 60Co at the dosage of 0,4,6 and 8 kR.The survivor rates were 93.2%,68.4%,41.3% and 16.5% for the 0,4,6 and 8 kR treatment,respectively.The plantlets were grown in the orchard in May of 2006.Through fruit investigation for 2 years,4 few-seeded mutants of good characters were screened.The seed contents of the 4 few-seeded mutants were 3.3~6.6 seeds/fruit,which were remarkably less than 17.6 seeds/fruit of the control.The pollen alive rates of the 4 few-seeded mutants were 45.5%~68.8%,and pollen germination rates were 11.8%~16.7%,which were obviously lower than 97.3% and 54.1% of the control respectively.One of the main causes producing few-seeded fruit was higher rate of pollen abortion.
