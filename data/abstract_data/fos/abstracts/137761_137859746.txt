Effects of TiB_2 Content on Properties of TiB_2 /Cu Composite
The effects of TiB 2 content on properties of TiB 2 /Cu composite are studied . The results indicate that the hardness and strength of TiB 2 /Cu composite is increased while the conductivity is reduced with the increase of TiB 2 content, but the strength of TiB 2 /Cu composite is decreased after TiB 2 content is 2.0%, and the softening temperature is kept at 900℃.
