Limit theorems for age and density dependent stochastic population models
SummaryConsider a population consisting of m different types of individuals living in a fixed region with area A. We construct a stochastic population model in which the death rate is affected by the age of the individual and the birth rate is affected by both the age of the individual and the population density PA (t), i.e., the population size divided by the area A of the given region. (In the multiple case PA (t) is a vector.)We examine the dependence of the model on the parameter A and show that for large population in a large area (i.e., large A) the stochastic model can be approximated by a deterministic model given by a system of integral equations. The typical demographic and epidemic models are special cases of the limiting deterministic model.Under appropriate renormalization of the population size, we also obtain a central limit theorem.
