International Competitiveness Trade of China' Intermediate Goods——Based on Intra-Industry Trade
Intermediate goods trade has played an important role in China's total trade.For better understanding of international competitiveness of goods made in China,this paper will analyze the Intra-Industry Trade(IIT) of China's intermediate goods empirically.The result indicates that IIT index of intermediate goods has increased a lot with rapid growth of trade volume.The technology-intensive products has been gaining more and more proportion in the total trade volume.And also IIT has become its main trade mode.However the Low Quality Vertical IIT is the overwhelming part and it is urgent to improve the level of IIT.
