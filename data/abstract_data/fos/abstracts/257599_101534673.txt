Preparation of potassium sulfate from ferrous sulfate using extraction reaction process
The extraction reaction process of producing potassium sulfate with by product Fe(OH) 2 from ferrous sulfate and potassium chloride is studied. It is shown that the two step extraction reaction process is a better process for producing potassium sulfate.
