Synthesis and electrical conductivity of Sr- and Mn-substituted LaAlO3 as a possible SOFC anode material
Single phase (La,Sr)(Al,Mn)O 3 perovskites were synthesized and their electrical conductivity was studied as a function of temperature and oxygen partial pressure. The electrical conductivity was found to increase with Mn content, temperature and oxygen partial pressure, thus (La, Sr)(Al, Mn)O 3 may be regarded as a diluted system of (La,Sr)MnO 3 . The highest electrical conductivity at 810 °C in air and humidified Ar/4% H 2 was observed for (La 0.8 Sr 0.2 ) 0.94 Al 0.5 Mn 0.5 O 3 and reached 12 and 2.7 S/cm, respectively.
