The higher education massification in Latin America
The massification of higher education has been accomplished in 1960s and 1970s in Latin American countries. Primary policies and measures are: reform public higher education; develop private higher education of different levels; create new types of school running models. The effect of the massification of higher education in Latin American countries was obvious but also brought about problems of occupational stratification and low investment yields. The lessons in the process of massification as overdevelopment of higher education and neglect of elementary education also need our attention.
