Spatial correlation of Contarinia sp.and Erigonidium graminicola with cross-variogram
Investigations on the spatial correlation of Contarinia sp.and its natural enemy,Erigonidium graminicola,in Feidong county at different periods were conducted with cross-variogram.The results indicated that there is a siganificance spatial dependence between Erigonidium graminicola and Contarinia sp.from 21 May to 19 October except that on 27 August and 21 September.It was showed strongly that the following relationship of Erigonidium graminicola to Contarinia sp.in number and spatial especially on May 30 and October 19.It is demonstrated that Erigonidium graminicola is the dominant natural enemy to Contarinia sp.once again.
