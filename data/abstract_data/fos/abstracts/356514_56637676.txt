Study and Simulate the DVB-T System Based on OFDM Technology
OFDM(Orthogonal frequency division multiplexing) is the effective technology in the high-speed date transmission,due to its robustness against multi-path fading channel,its high bandwidth efficiency and simple realization,it has been regarded as one of the main candidate for the future mobile communication.This paper proposes a method of using OFDM technology to simulate the DVB-T system.Through the established transmission system model,using MATLAB to simulate the signal in ideal channel and Gaussian white noise channel.The simuliation proves that the OFDM technology can be an adaptive technology.It can improve the transmission rate of the DVB-T system and it is especially for the Gaussian channel.
