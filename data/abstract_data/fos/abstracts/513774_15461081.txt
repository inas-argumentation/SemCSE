A DESCRIPTION OF CHARACTERS ON THE INFINITE WREATH PRODUCT
Let S1 be the infinity permutation group and an arbitrary group. Then S1 admits a natural action on 1 by automorphisms, so one can form a semidirect product 1 oS1, known as the wreath product oS1 of by S1. We obtain a full description of unitary II1 factor-representations of o S1 in terms of finite characters of . Our approach is based on extending Okounkov's classification method for admissible representations of S1 ◊ S1. Also, we discuss certain exam- ples of representations of type III, where the modular operator of Tomita-Takesaki expresses naturally by the asymptotic operators, which are important in the theory of characters of S1.
