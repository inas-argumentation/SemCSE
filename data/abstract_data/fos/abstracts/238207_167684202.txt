A New Discussion of Scrambling for Registering Trademark
With the deepening of the knowledge economy, the trademark value is inscresingly taking root in the hearts of the people. The scramble for registering trademarks including well-known trademark, public figures and hot topics is becoming the present hot topic. This article draws up from the phenomenon of scrambling for registering trademark, discusses the goal of most of these behaviors, and analyzes our country enterprises in the trademark protection still remaining in question. Finally, the article pointed out that, each enterprise should have the multi- angles and multi-level realization of enterprise trademark protection, and build the foundation for enhancing the enterprises' competition strength in the market.
