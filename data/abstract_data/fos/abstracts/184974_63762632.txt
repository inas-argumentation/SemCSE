Trust-oriented Resource Trade Model in Virtual Computing Environments
Virtual users face increasing cheat and risk when executing resource trades,which is taken as a prominent feature of the market-oriented virtual computing environment.So this paper proposes a trust-driven resource trade model to assist users to trade and avoid being cheated.First,a trust mechanism based on the Dempster-Shafer theory is built for evaluating trust degrees which reflect user behavioral characteristics.In this trade model,one whole trade can be divided into many steps,and before each of which,the trust degrees of both parities are evaluated.The trade granularity of each step depends on the fluctuation of users' trust degrees between the current step and the previous one.This model supports the "pay-as-you-use" strategy which allows resource consumers to pay resource providers step by step.Simulations show our proposed model can ensure users to execute resource trades more securely and trustworthily,with costing a few more time.
