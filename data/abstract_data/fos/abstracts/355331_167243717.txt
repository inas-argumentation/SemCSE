Research on Electric Soft-Switch System and Security
The evolution from administrative switching and dispatching exchange to soft-switch system in electricity is an inevitable trend. The soft-switch system is the packet IP-based data communications system essentially, which can transmit data, voice and video and other information. But at the same time, because of its open and complex feature, soft-switch faces with a variety of security threats in the development of network hardware, protocol design and application software development. Analyzes the security threats faced by electric soft-switch, puts forward security schemes on business security, information security, equipment security and network security to ensure stable operation of power grid.
