Book reviews: Eubank, L., editor, 1991 : Point counterpoint. Universal Grammar in the second language. Amsterdam and Philadelphia: John Benjamins. 440 pp
In the past decade we have seen a growing interest of theoretical linguists in language acquisition research. Generative grammarians have argued that child language grammars are possible grammars and as such they should be subject to the principles and parameters of Universal Grammar (UG). Whereas this point of view is nowadays widely accepted as far as Ll acquisition is concerned, the ’applicability’ of UG to L2 acquisition is often challenged in the literature. Eubank’s volume contains a very interesting series of discussions about the UG issue in L2 acquisition. The papers were given at the Second Language Research Forum at UCLA in 1989.
