Relationship between Septobasidium felts and varieties and interior substance of peel of Castanea mollissima
In this paper,relationship between Septobasidium felts and varieties of Castanea mollissima was investigated,and the contents of nutrient,sugar,amino acids in bark of the trees were analyzed.The results show that the order of disease index of the five varieties is ErshuizaoMifengqiuYelicangDahongpaoZhandiban.In the interior substance of the barks,the content of sugar is positive and B is negative with disease index;the contents of the N,P and K are the highest in Ershuizao(the highestly-susceptible),the lowest in Zhandiban(the highestly-resistance) and irregular in other three varieties(middle-susceptible).The kinds and total contents of amino acids of the bark in Zhandiban are the richest among the five varieties.Among which,the content of arginine,is up to 367.1 mg·kg-1 and far surpass others.
