Locally Asymptotically Most Stringent Tests for Paired Comparison Experiments
Following the results of Bhat and Nagnur [2], the locally asymptotically most stringent test of the hypothesis of no preference in paired comparison experiments is derived for the models of Rao and Kupper [9] and Davidson [3]. It is shown that the asymptotic relative efficiency of these tests when compared to the test based on the likelihood ratio statistic is unity.
