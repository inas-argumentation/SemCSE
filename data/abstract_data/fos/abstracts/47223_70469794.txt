Nutritional Status of Hemodialysis Patients Receiving Recombinant Human Erythropoietin Therapy
The production of recombinant human erythropoietin (r-HuEPO) has provided a therapeutic agent to correct the anemia of chronic renal failure. The requirements of those nutrients crucial to erythropoiesis are likely to be increased. Elevation of serum potassium and urea attributed to improved appetite and decreased dialyzer efficiency have been reported along with rising hemoglobin values. The present study was undertaken to examine various nutritional parameters in patients receiving r-HuEPO therapy. Fourteen patients with a mean age of 42.8 years (range, 23–72) were studied before and after receiving r-HuEPO. Six patients were followed for 2 years and eight patients for 6 months. Routine biochemical, hematological, anthropometric monitoring, and assessment of protein catabolic rate (PCR) were performed.
