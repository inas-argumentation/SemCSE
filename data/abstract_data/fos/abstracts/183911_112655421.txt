Formation Mechanism and Prevention of Crack in Railway High-speed Truck Side Frame
The formation mechanism of the crack in high-speed truck side frame was studied,and the causes of cracking were analyzed.By the process measures of placing the cold iron in the hot section and corner parts of the casting,controlling the contents of sulfur,phosphorus less than 0.015% and carbon in the lower limit,increasing the concessions of molding material,controlling pouring temperature in 1 560 to 1 570 ℃,optimizing the heat treatment process,reducing the sand joints and so on,the cracks in the side frame castings can be effectively controlled.The cracks can be eliminated by mass production verification,and the result is satisfactory.
