Interior Link Delay Reference of Ad Hoc Networks Based on End-to-End Measurement: Linear Analysis Model of Delay
The interior link performance parameter is an important metric to evaluate the Ad Hoc networks, and moreover, to control and optimize the network performance parameters for special applications (i.e., OoS). The object of this paper is to infer the interior link delay in Ad Hoc networks. End-to-End measurement technology is introduced to our research for inferring the link delay probability distribution through End-to-End measurement based on linear analysis model. Simulation results indicate that this method is effective in small scale of Ad Hoc networks.
