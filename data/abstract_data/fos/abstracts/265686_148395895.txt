An open research field——Higher education in postmodernism perspective
From the view of postmodernism characterized by its openness, the paper revealed that higher education should be an open developing field which is realized through the openness of problems and researches. It is the openness of human beings that can maintain and enhance the open characteristics. The development of higher education pedagogy should insist on problem research and multi-mode practices. So this can develop the openness of researches and research culture itself. Then the openness of higher education research in China could be realized.
