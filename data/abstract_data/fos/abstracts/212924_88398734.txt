Optimization of Chromosome Spreading Technique in Cassava Root Tip Cells
Chromosome spreading technique is of great significance for chromosome and karyotype ploidy breeding,interspecific hybridization and germplasm analysis of cassava.Chromosome spreading of root tip cells of cassava(cult.Huanan 6)was performed using squash and enzyme digestion,low osmotic method,respectively.Some important factors during the preparation,such as chemicals and time for pretreatment,enzymolysis time,were discussed to optimize the chromosome preparation.Our results showed that the enzyme digestion,low osmotic method was the best one for chromosome preparation of the cassava root tip cells.The optimum pretreatment chemical was the mixture of the α-bromonaphthalene with saturated solution of p-dichlorobenzene and the mixture of the 0.1% colchicine with 0.002 mol/L 8-hydroxyquinoline(1:1),and the pretreatment time 2 hours,enzymolysis lasted 4 h at 37 ℃.
