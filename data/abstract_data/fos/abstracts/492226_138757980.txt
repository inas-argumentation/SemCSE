Computer Simulation of Deformation Process Based on Deform-3D in Equal Channel Angular Extrusion
The ECAE process was simulated by commercial DEFORM-3D software,and the results between the numerical simulation and experiment of load-stroke curves were compared,both were accordant.The distribution laws of the effective stress and effective strain during the extrusion process were obtained,the deformation mechanism on grain refining was initially studied,which provided the foundation for future research.
