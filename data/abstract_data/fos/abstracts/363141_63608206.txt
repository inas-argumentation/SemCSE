Design and Implement of the Texts Management Information System for based on C/S and B/S
Utilizing the technology of network and advanced database on the basis of analyzing the task and current situation develops the texts management information system. The paper introduces how to realize the principle and technology of C/S and B/S network model. We propose a new support system based on multi-model of C/S and B/S, and then introduce the design, its main functions, characteristics and implementation.
