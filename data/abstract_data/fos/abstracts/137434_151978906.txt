Curiously enough, the rise of clean elections in Latin America has played out against the backdrop of increasing irregularities in the American electoral system, most notably during the many irregularities that marred the 2000 election, especially in Florida. All of this should lead one to question how much legitimacy the United States has in advising Latin American countries on how to run free elections, and whether the former is now in need of foreign monitoring of its elections.
