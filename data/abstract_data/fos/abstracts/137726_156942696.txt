The Hygiene Administration System of Anhui During the Republic of China Period
During the Republic of China period,the politics of Anhui was in its transition process of modernization,of which the hygiene administration was also an important component.Under the leadership of Anhui provincial government,hygiene administration undertook an active exploration into modernization,making remarkable achievements.However,the political environment of Anhui in that time was the most changeful,and the development of hygiene administration was a circuitous and advanced process.Anhui's hygiene administration system played a positive role in promoting the practice and development of medical and health services,preventing and controlling epidemic diseases and protecting the majority of the people healthily in the period of the Republic of China.
