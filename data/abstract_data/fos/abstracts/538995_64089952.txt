Development and Application of ZigBee WSN Teaching Experimental Platform
Designs a kind of ZigBee-based wireless sensor network experimental platform to meet the needs of practical teaching and innovation training. Three types of nodes including coordinator, router and sensor end device uniformly employ JN5148 integrated solution. Designs a variety of sensor end devices to meet diverse application needs. The host computer connected with coordinator is responsible for sensor data aggregation and network status monitoring in real time. Practice shows that the platform is suitable for carrying out the stepped case teaching and project design training about wireless sensor network by significantly improving the students' understanding and application design level to wireless sensor networks and ZigBee.
