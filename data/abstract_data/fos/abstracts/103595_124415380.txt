One Feature of the Finite Dimensional Banach Space
This article introduces three concepts of absolute continuous functions in Banach space, that is, weak absolute continuous, absolute continuous, strong absolute continuous. It can be proved that the second concept is equivalent to the third one in Banach space. As its deduction, in Banach space the first one is equivalent to the third one too. This article also proves the first concepts is equivalent to the third one in some infinite dimensional space.
