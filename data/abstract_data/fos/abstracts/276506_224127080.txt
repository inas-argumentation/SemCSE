ELECTRON DENSITY DISTRIBUTION IN HETEROCYCLIC SYSTEMS WITH TWO VICINAL NITROGEN ATOMS. II. PYRAZOLE DIPOLE MOMENTS
The dipole moment of pyrazole is determined and found to agree well with that calculated vectorially. From vector calculation the angle between the dipole moment and the N-H bond is found to be 118° 15′. Authors: S. A. Giller, I. B. Mazheika, I. I. Grandberg.
