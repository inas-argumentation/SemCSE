Influence of Exportation Tax Reimbursement on Huangshan's Export Trade and its Countermeasures
China declared the adjustment of the export tax reimbursement of 2831 commercial goods since July 1, 2007, covering 37% of the goods with customs tax. Faced with the adjustment of the export tax reimbursement, Huangshan City will meet with a lot of new challenges and opportunities,which will surely have great positive or negative impact on Huangshan export trade. Under such cases, people should discard the belief of "Business Governing" that export is the source of wealth and speed up the transformation of economic development style, giving more priority to advantageous and special-featured industry so as to promote the foreign trade of Huangshan City.
