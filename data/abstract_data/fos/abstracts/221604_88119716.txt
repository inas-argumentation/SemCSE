Construction of live oral Vibrio cholerae vaccine candidates eliciting both antibacterial and antitoxic immunity
Objective Development of live oral vaccine candidates against Vibrio cholerae. Methods Mutant ctx gene (ctx *) and ct B gene were separately expressed in Vibrio cholerae with a chromosome plasmid balanced lethal gene expression system. The vaccine candidates against Vibrio cholerae containing ctx * or ct B gene were constructed (IEM104 and IEM105) and their immunogenicity were compared in rabbit model. Results The specific antibody titre of IEM104 were higher than that of IEM105 after vaccination. IEM104 could neutralize 5μg CT which was higher than that by IEM105 (2μg) in rabbit ligated ileal loop system. Conclusion IEM104 and IEM105 appeared safety and superiority of antitoxic immunity to IEM101 in animal model.
