Discussion on the Control of Privilege abut Administ Ative
Administrative contract is a good combination of free consultation and regarding executive and its opposite party.It is a flexible supervision style,which is being used widely in the field of administrative management.It is in its essence a kind of administrative behavior,and the result of executing power exercising.This article is discussion on the control of its privilege.By making a rule of administration procedure about its privilege,we want to protect people's right and perfect the procedure of judicial remedy.
