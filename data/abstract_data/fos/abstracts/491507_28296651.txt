A clinical trial with hydroxyzine (atarax) on a group of maladjusted educationally subnormal children.
1. A clinical trial with Atarax was carried out on 16 matched pairs of educationally subnormal children who were maladjusted and under-functioning.

2. Improvements as measured by behaviour and performances in class were noted in 14 out of 16 on Treatment and in 2 out of 16 in the Control Group.

3. White Cell Counts and Differential Counts were carried out periodically. In no case was there a degree of Leucopenia or Granulopenia present to warrant interfering with treatment. A transient Eosinophilia and increase in the monocytes did occur but cleared quickly.

However, the number of cases was small and it is felt this drug should be controlled with White Cell Counts until more information is available.
