Attempt to Discuss O'neil and Progress of the Traditional Tragedy
As a founder of American modern tragedy, O'neil wrote tragedies that modern people attempted but failed to regain human honour and happiness in an unacceptable world where "God is dead". He made a significant contribution to the foundation and development of modern tragedies on the basis of his own experience and practice. His tragedies underwent a substantial progress on the exploitation to the item of modern tragedy, the pursuit of esthetic effects and senses of tragic character compared with the traditional tragedy, which embody a conciousness of modern tragedies.
