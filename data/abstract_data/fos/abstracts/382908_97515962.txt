Synthesis and structure of electrolytic complexes based on α-hydro-ω-oligo(oxyethylene)hydroxy-poly[oligo(oxyethylene)oxydimethylsililene] and δ-MgCl2
An alternated copolymer with the formula α-hydro-ω-oligo(oxyethylene)hydroxy-poly[oligo(oxyethylene)oxydimethylsililene] and a molecular weight of 9860 was synthesized. Doping of this polymer with the anhydrous salt δ-MgCl 2 resulted in a new magnesium electrolytic complex poly[PEG400-alt-DEOS]/(MgCl 2 ) 0.26 . The structural hypothesis for the polymer was proposed on the basis of elemental analyses and molecular weight. Detailed 1 H-, 13 C-, and 29 Si-NMR spectral investigations fully confirmed the structure of poly[PEG400-alt-DEOS]. Mid-infrared region (MIR) FT-IR studies of the polymer showed that it presents (1) a sufficient number of terminal hydroxyl groups to confer a substantial degree of dissolution and salt-dissociation in the polymer complex; and (2) polyethereal fragments in a conformational geometry close to TGT (T= trans , G= gauche ). The conductivity against temperature plot for this very amorphous magnesium polymer electrolyte demonstrated that the material conducts ionically by means of two types of charge migration mechanisms.
