Industrial application of rotating packed bed( RPB) on H_2S removal from associated gas by iron-redox process
RPB is used for the removal of H2S from associated gas in industrial production by choosing chelated iron as desulfurizer. The RPB has been operating smoothly for over two years without leakage. Industrial research results show that the mass concentration of H2S in cleaned gas is less than 20 mg / m3,and in most cases,the monitoring value is less than 10 mg/m3. The industrial performance of RPB demonstrates that RPB desulfurization process has many merits,such as high removing efficiency,small equipment volume and easy operating. These advantages allow RPB to be qualified for desulfurization in various situations,such as onshore / offshore operating platform,frontier area in oilfield and natural gas process plant,etc. RPB desulfurization process provides a new solution.
