Design and Realization for Yantai Emergency Meteorological Service System
Yantai Emergency Meteorological Service System(YEMSS) is designed and realized specially for meet the demand of emergency meteorological service,on the emergency meteorological disaster and other significant emergent public events,which can be happened in the coastal and land in Yantai.YEMSS is composed of 6 sub-systems,included by conventional meteorological observation,live monitoring on actual location,video and teleconference and directing,weather forecast producing and distributing,communication,infrastructure build-up and power supply system etc.YEMSS can improve the live meteorological monitoring and service capabilities,provide the scientific basis of government decision-making in emergency rescue,to avoid or mitigate losses caused by meteorological disasters and other significant emergent public events,protect people's lives and property.
