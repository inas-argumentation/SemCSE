Stochastic Fault-tolerant Control for Networked Control Systems with BIBO Stability
For a discrete time networked control systems with time-delay,date packet losses,wrong-sequence and so on non-ideal elements,the stochastic fault-tolerant control problem of sensor or actuator failures is investigated under the system having mean square bounded input bounded output( BIBO) stability. Due to the sensor and actuator failure or not are random,in view of this consider, the independent Bernoulli random sequences as switching matrices are introduced,which improves the 0,1 switch matrix. For reducing the conservatism,by decomposing connect weighting matrix,using discrete Newton-Leibniz formula,combining with Lyapunov stability theory and linear matrix inequality( LMIs) method,the mean square BIBO stability conditions are obtained for the system has sensor or actuator failures,based on this stability conditions,we can solve the feedback control gain matrix. At last,the stochastic fault-tolerant controller is designed.
