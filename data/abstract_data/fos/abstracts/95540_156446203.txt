The change of value viewpoints of physical education and the essence,function and purpose of physical education during the new period
The value viewpoints of physical education is closely related with the essence,function and purpose of physical education.In this paper,the value viewpoints of physical education is the main clue,the relations among the value viewpoints and the essence,function and purpose of physical education have been dialectically analyzed,so that the new point of cut-in has been provided for the construction of basic physical education theory in China,and the academic basis and practical instruction have been offered to the deep development of physical education reform in China.
