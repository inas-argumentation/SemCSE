PHOTOGRAPHY OF COSMIC RAYS IN A LUMINESCENT CHAMBER
A method is described for photographing minimumionizing cosmic rays in a sodium iodide crystal using available electronic and optical components. With modifications the apparatus can be used for luminescent chamber experiments in high-energy particle physics. The system consists of photomuitiplier, Nal crystal, and three image intensifier tubes optically coupled to each other and to the crystal and film with refractive lenses. (W.D.M.)
