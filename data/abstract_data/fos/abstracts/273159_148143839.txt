Study of appointment and salary distribution system of local higher education institutions——A case study of Foshan University of Science and Technology
By studying the case of appointment and salary distribution system reform of a local higher education institution,the paper explores such issues as how local universities should organize their positions in a scientific and reasonable manner,how to strengthen their appointment evaluation system,how to implement the new distribution system,and so forth.The ultimate issue is whether the reform can better exploit the potential of the faculty so that the quality of cultivating professional talents and the quality and effectiveness of running the university can be improved.
