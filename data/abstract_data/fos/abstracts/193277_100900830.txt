Preliminary Study on the Activated Carbon from Bamboo Shavings
The activated carbon from bamboo shavings shows different quality by the methods of steam activation, phosphoric acid-spinning stove and phosphoric acid-microwave. The iodine number by steam activation is the biggest,but the decolorizing capacity of caramel adsorption is small. The three indicators of two kinds of chemical methods are all high. The decolorizing capacity of metylene blue and caramel adsorption exceed the national standard.The ash can be reduced greatly by the way of washing with hydrochloric acid.
