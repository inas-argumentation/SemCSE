From Balanced Development to Pursuing Excellence——Review of "Excellent Initiative" of German Higher Education
In order to cope with the new situation when the elite education was paid more attention with the booming of the world higher education,Germany proposed and put in to practice "Excellence Initiative" during the recent years.Therefore centralizing and investing more funds and finer material resources in scientific researches in elite universities,Germany put top universities prior to average ones rather than treated them on an equal basis as in the past.All efforts were aiming at recovering Germany's dominating position in the field of higher education.Although "Excellence Initiative"is facing many problems,such as stakeholders' game playing,the traditional view of balanced development,funding shortage,its strategic significance is exceeding the practical significance to intensify the competition among the universities.
