Land Lost from Production Under and Around Electrical Transmission Line Structures
ABSTRACT AN evaluation of land lost from production under and around electrical transmission structures, using low-altitude photography, is reported. Results from a total of 2803 structures from 17 line segments in the Upper Midwest are included in a data base. Data from each structure include the area lost from production and per-tinent variables about the structure and its location. Wide variation exists in land lost from production within any line segment. The area lost from production increases for H/K Frame structures as the size of the structure increases. For Lattice structures land lost is relatively constant for structures with base dimensions greater than 9 m. Machinery size variation between geographic areas results in significant differences in the proportion of towers farmed through.
