Research of Multiple Queries Processing in Wide Area Sensor Databases
Wide area sensor databases are an internationally hotspot research area at present.The query processing technology in wide area sensor databases is analyzed in detail in this paper.Focusing on the query redundancy problem among multiple queries,a multiple query processing architecture is proposed and then every module and its algorithm are analyzed.Theoretical analysis and experimental results demonstrate that the method can not only reduce the users' query response times and expedite the speed of the queries effectively,but also decrease the messages transferred in the sensor network and improve the utilization ratio of the network bandwidth dramatically.
