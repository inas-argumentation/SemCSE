A window to look at the picture of information technology in American Higher Education — ——EDUCAUSE's Missions and Implications
EDUCAUSE is the most influential professional organization in the field of information technology in higher education.Brief history of EDUCAUSE shows that the early dominant concept in higher education is computing,which contains two domains of administrative computing and academic computing,later replaced by the emerging up-to-date concept of information technology.What we can learn from EDUCAUSE include its focus on macro-policy and strategic issues,its commitment to change the teaching and research modes,and its emphasis on partnership with industry and foundations.The establishment of China EDUCAUSE could facilitate the innovation and development of information technology in higher education.
