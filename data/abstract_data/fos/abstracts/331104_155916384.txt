Reform of Congress: David B. Truman (Ed.), The Congress and America's Future--A Discussion
T HE EDITORS of the POLITICAL SCIENCE QUARTERLY are pleased to be able to present reviews of a significant new work on congressional reform-David B. Truman (ed.), The Congress and America's Future (Englewood Cliffs, N. J., Prentice-Hall for The American Assembly, 1965; vi, 185 pp., $3.95)-by important leaders of both the Democratic and Republican parties. Senator A. S. Mike Monroney, Democrat of Oklahoma, is co-chairman of the Joint Committee on the Organization of the Congress. Senator Thomas H. Kuchel, Republican of California, is a member of the Committee on Appropriations.
