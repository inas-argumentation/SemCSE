Detection of Human Herpesvirus 8 Infection and the Virus Variation in Patients with AIDS in Guangzhou Region
In order to find out the human herpesvirus 8(HHV-8) infection in patients with AIDS in Guangzhou,to elucidate the possible risk of AIDS related Kaposi′s sarcoma in the patients and to explore the possible genetic variation of HHV-8 in this region,n-PCR was used to detect the HHV-8 DNA in the saliva of the patients with AIDS,and then the PCR products were sequenced by ABI 3100 system.Results showed that the HHV-8 positive rates in AIDS group and the control group were respectively 20.0% and 0.0%.The difference between the 2 groups was very significant.No DNA sequence difference was found in the 261-511 base range,as compared with that of the published one(NC 003409).The HHV-8 infection rate was quite high in AIDS patients of Guangzhou region.
