Policy analysis and institutional research:construction of mid-level theory in the research of China's higher education
Institutional Research(IR) is the most dynamic research direction of China's higher education research,and many important issues of contemporary higher education can be discussed within the framework of IR thoroughly and effectively.As a form of research between abstract unitary theory and specific empirical description,IR comes so relevant to mid-level theory.The research object of IR is institutional transformation;the core topic of IR is the policy process of institutional transformation;one main paradigm of IR is system analysis;and the basic approach of IR is from individual case study of single institution to multiple cases of area study and homo-type study.
