The patterns of institution innovation of higher education:cases from America
Higher education institution refers to behavior rules of higher education system or university organization,and complicated relationship among its relevant subjects.Institution innovation of higher education includes eight patterns: the pattern of stage innovation;the pattern of structure innovation;the pattern of benefit consensus and the pattern of benefit relevance included in the pattern of benefit;the pattern of situation innovation,the pattern of interaction innovation,the pattern of goal innovation,and the pattern of member loyalty which are included in the pattern of situation.Institution innovation is under the control of its subject elements: benefit relationship,ability level,cognition level,and emotion.
