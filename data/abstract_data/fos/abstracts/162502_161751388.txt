In his preface to this superb new edition and translation of Vida’s Christiad (1535), James Gardner describes Christian epic’s once-powerful appeal: ‘‘it is almost as if Vida were rewriting Scripture itself, translating it out of what was felt to be the crude and demotic idiom of Saint Jerome’s Vulgate into the more ‘elevated’ language of Vergilian Latin’’ (x).
