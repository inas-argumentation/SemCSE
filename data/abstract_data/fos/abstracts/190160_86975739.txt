Community of enemy insects of Dendrolimus punctatus in Beauveria treated forest
Investigation on the community of insect enemies of Dendrolimus punctatus showed an abundant community existed in a forest treated with Beauveria bassiana inoculatively.The total numbers of insect enemies differed among development stages of D.punctatus.The number reached the peak at the stage of larva period of D.punctatus with 9 species of insect enemies.Four species and 1 species were found at the stages of egg and pupa,respectively.The community of enemy insects showed temporal characters.The abundant community of insect enemies and temporal suppression mechanism against D.punctatus played important roles on sustainable control of D.punctatus.
