Effects of Laser Cladding Parameters on Macro-dimensions of Laser Cladding Layer
The effects of scanning speed and powder feeding rate on width,height and contact angle of laser cladding single layer were studied.The results show that the width and height decrease with the increase of the scanning speed.The width changes little with the change of powder feeding rate and the height increases with the increase of powder feeding rate.The contact angle changes with the change of the ratio of the height and the width.
