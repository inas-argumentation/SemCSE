Study on antibacterial action of coprircus comatus and the preservation of cherry
The antibacterial action of the fruiting bodies from coprircus comatus was studied by the methods of agar diffusion and solid dilution.The result showed that the extract of coprircus comatus could inhibit the growth of escherihia coli,staphylococcus aurens,bacillus subtilis.Tests of cherry preserving were carried out by using compound liquid coating membrane which consisted of coprircus comatus.The weight-loss,rot rate and Vitamin C(Vc)were determined.It was shown that the fresh fruit rate of the cherry was 24% higher than that of the control.
