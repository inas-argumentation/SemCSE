Faculty engagement in the internationalization of the curriculum
The internationalization of the curriculum is the key of internationalization of higher education.The internationalization of the curriculum means to integrate the idea of internationalization into curriculum design and implementation.But faculty engagement is essential to the internationalization of the curriculum.Their attitude and ideas,their knowledge,experiences and methods of internationalization influence directly the policy making,the plan designing and implementing of curriculum internationalization.To guide and encourage faculty engagement in the internationalization of the curriculum,universities should make policy,adjust plan,give recognition,provide chance and add experience about internationalization.
