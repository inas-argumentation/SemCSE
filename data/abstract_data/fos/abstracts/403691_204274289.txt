Effect of Substrate Pre-deformation on Stress and Strain Fields of Stainless Steel Test-piece by Laser Solid Forming
With relevant orthogonal test and hole-drilling method,the effects of substrate pre-deformation on residual stress and strain of 304L stainless steel test-piece by laser solid forming(LSF) with 316 powder were researched.The results show that the pre-deformation has greater effects on the roughness,next is laser power and speed.The stress fields in the substrate after pre-deformation has a great effect on the final shape.It is found that the residual tensile stress σx first increases and then decreases,but the σZ has an opposite trend.
