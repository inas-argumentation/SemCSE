Synthesis of 5-(4,6-dimethyl-2-pyrimidinyl)-1,3,4-oxadiazole-2-thione and its alkylation, aminomethylation, and acylation
The reaction of the hydrazide of 4,6-dimethyl-2-pyrimidinecarboxylic acid with potassium ethylxnthate gave 5-(4,6-dimethyl-2-pyrimidinyl)-1,3,4-oxadiazole-2-thione. The alkylation of this product in an alkaline medium proceeds at the sulfur atom, while the aminomethylation and acylation proceed at the nitrogen atom. The major criterion for the structure of the S- and N-derivatives is the chemical shift of C(2) in the 1,3,4-oxadiazole ring in the 13C NMR spectra.
