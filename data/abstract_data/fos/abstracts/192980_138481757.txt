Microstructures and Phase Diagram Calculation of Al-Li-Mg-Si Alloy
The typical cross-section phase diagram of the alloy AL-Li-Mg-Si was calculated, and the microstructures of the alloy were analyzed. The results show that the main precipitated phase is the phase δ under the equilibrium state, and a large number of Si-rich phases of Mg2Si and AlLiSi are formed. But the actual precipitation is mainly metastable phase δ′,which can inhibit the formation of Mg2Si, but increase of Mg content will promote the precipitation of Mg2Si.
