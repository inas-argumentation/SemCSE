The influence of reserpine on a disturbed male ward and a method for assessing this.
1. In order to show the influence of reserpine on the atmosphere of an agitated male ward of a psychiatric hospital, we made a comparison of that, before and after, reserpine treatment.

2. Toward this purpose we used a method permitting the expression of the clinical behaviour in a graph.

3. As a result of our investigation we found a remarkable decrease in the paranoid attitude, the anxiety and the aggressiveness of the patients. Improvement in the quality of social adaptation is facilitated by the above changes in the clinical state and can now be achieved more easily by psychotherapy, a fact on which we will report at a later date in greater detail.
