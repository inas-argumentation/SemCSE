Association between activation-induced cytidine deaminase gene polymorphism and asthma in children
Objective Activation-induced cytidine deaminase (AICDA) is a RNA-editing deaminase that plays an important role in class-switching of immunoglobulin. The aim of this study is to determine whether the AICDA gene 8408 C/T polymorphism is related to asthma in children and total plasma IgE. Methods A case-control panel was chain reaction and undertaken to study the polymorphism of AICDA which was detected with polymerase chain reaction-restriction fragment length polymorphism (PCR-RFLP). Results The 8408 T allele was more commonly found in asthma children (χ2=7.736, P 0.01). Conclusion The 8408 T allele of AICDA is associated with asthma in children. [
