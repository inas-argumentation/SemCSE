Research of OFDM Synchronization Method Based on Conjugated-symmetrical-structured Training Sequence
The performance of OFDM synchronization method based on conjugated-symmetrical-structured training sequence is studied,and the channel environment in which such kind of method can work well is given: in the channel environment where the first path is the dominant path and its power is much stronger than other paths,such method can get the timing position and integral frequency offset correctly;In the channel environment where there is not a dominant path,the accuracy of timing estimation will be reduced and the correctness of the integral frequency offset cannot be guaranteed.
