Changing the Concept of Development and Innovating the Pattern of Development——Highlights in Proposal on the Eleventh Five-year Program
The prominent character in Proposal on the Eleventh Five-year Program is that it attaches great attention to fully carrying out the scientific concept of development.It proposes to maintain smooth and steady operation of the macro-economy.Building a new socialist countryside is the basic strategy of overall coordinating of urban and rural economic and social development.It proposes to adjust and optimize industrial structure and upgrade growth pattern with increasing independent innovation as the core link.The realizing of social fairness is the key to build a harmonious society.
