Review of ASHRAE symposium session on the application of adaptive control to real HVAC applications
Abstract ASHRAE Symposium PO-86-5 was held in Portland, Oregon, USA, 22–25 June 1986. The session was sponsored by TC 1.4 Control theory and application, with E. Gut as the Chairman. This Paper gives a short review of the following papers: 1. 1. Adaptive control implementation issues: S. G. Brandt, Honeywell Commercial Division, Arlington Heights, Illinois, USA. 2. 2. Self-tuning digital integral control: M. J. Pinnella, Aerospace Corporation, Redondo Beach, California, USA; E. Wechselberger, University of Illinois, Urbana, Illinois, USA; D. C. Hittle, USA Construction Engineering Research Laboratory, Champaign, Illinois, USA; C. O. Pedersen, University of Illinois, Urbana, Illinois, USA. 3. 3. Automated controller tuning for HVAC applications: C. G. Nesler, Johnson Controls, Milwaukee, Wisconsin, USA.
