Analysis of S460M Steel Plate TEKKEN Weldability Test
In order to analyze the weldability of S460M steel plate,S460M steel plate was welded by arc welding method at room temperature(26 ℃),preheat 50 ℃,preheat 100 ℃,and carried out TEKKEN weldability test and test of maximum hardness in weld heat-affected zone.From the test results,welding specimens have not cracks under the conditions of room temperature,preheat 50℃,preheat 100℃,and when the steel plate is preheated to 100℃,maximum hardness in weld heat-affected zone is about 328 HV and cold crack tendency is little.The results show that the arc welding performance of S460M steel plate is good.
