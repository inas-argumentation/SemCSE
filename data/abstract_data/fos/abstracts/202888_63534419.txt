Research and Design of the Wireless Sensor Network Routing Protocol Based on Non-Uniform Clustering
For the current uniform clustering routing protocol exists between the cluster and the cluster energy imbalance and other issues first choice is unreasonable,an improved scheme,eventually forming a new wireless sensor network clustering routing protocol inhomogeneous UCRP,the agreement fully considers the residual energy of sensor nodes,based on residual energy select cluster head,and improves LEACH routing protocol in single hop communication energy consumption big disadvantage.Campared the NS2 simulation platform for protocol simulation,the simulation results,UCRP algorithm with the original LEACH protocol,the results show that UCRP algorithm is well-balanced network energy consumption and prolong the survival time of the network.
