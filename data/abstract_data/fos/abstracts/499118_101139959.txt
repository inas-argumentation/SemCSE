Simultaneous Determination of Ethanol,Sugar and Organic Acids in Kluveromyces marxious Broth by High Performance Liquid Chromatography-Ultraviolet/Refractive Index Detector
A high performance liquid chromatographic method was developed for the simultaneous determination of ethanol,sugar and organic acids in Kluveromyces marxinus broth by UV and RI(Refractive index)detector.Aminex HPX-87H organic acids analysis column was used for separation.The mobile phase was composed of sulfuric acid and water,and the concentration of sulfuric acid was 0.01 mol/L(pH 1.4).The flow rate was 0.5 mL/min.The broth(containing glucose,fructose,ethanol,acetic acid,glycerol,and lactic acid) was separated by the dual-channel detector within 25 min.The method showed high average recoveries for the samples ranged from 95.8% to 109.6% and good precision(RSD) ranged from 0.3% to 4.0%.The results show that ethanol,sugar and organic acids can be determined rapidly and effectively,and is capable of being used for real-time analysis of metabolic components in fermentation broth.
