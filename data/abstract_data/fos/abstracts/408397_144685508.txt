Although confusion is central to Geisler’s POST theory and 3rd Curriculum, for the purposes of this text, the confusion negatively impacts the effectiveness of his larger arguments and ideas. Paul R. Geisler’s text, Higher Education at a Crossroads helps perpetuate some of the many concerns that scholars have debated regarding the current state of higher education by adding a unique perspective. In doing so, Geisler discusses some valid issues and raises important concerns that can be difficult to understand because of the style he utilizes to express his ideas. Although his suggestions for a remedy seem plausible, his description about how to actually implement these ideas still needs more detail and elaboration to be effective. Overall, Higher Education at a Crossroads attempts to offer a practical solution to some of the problems facing higher education and allows for a foundation from which researchers can build.
