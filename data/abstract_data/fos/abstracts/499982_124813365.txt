British Social Trends since 1900: A Guide to the Changing Social Structure of Britain.
John Bull's Island Immigration and British Society, 1871-1971 Colin Holmes Three main themes come under discussion: which immigrant and refugee groups came to Britain and why? What were the major distinguishing features of the economic and social history of such groups? How were these newcomers received by British society? The result is a major historical survey which synthetizes and evaluates existing research and adds valuable new material. 420 pp. 1988. ISBN 0-333-28209-4. $39.50 (cloth). 0-333-28210-8. $19.95 (pap.)
