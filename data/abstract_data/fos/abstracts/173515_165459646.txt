The approach of I gesuiti e i papi is completely different—it does not provide a general overview, but instead offers seven original essays on just as many case studies from the sixteenth to the twentieth centuries from the same diachronic perspective, showing that the archives are filled with documents just waiting for historians. The Jesuits and the Popes, written in the brilliant, elegant style that characterizes all of John O’Malley’s work, is accompanied by a series of illustrations representing the Society’s long historical journey, from the painting of Ignatius kneeling in front of Paul III to the photographs of Pope Francis’s visit to Saint Joseph’s University in Philadelphia in September 2015.
