Research for Consistency Initialization of Redundant Array of Independent Disk
When RAID storage volume is newly created,the data in RAID is initialized.The process involves not only formatting the RAID storage volume to clear any previously data,but also initializing any redundancy information required by the associated RAID management level.Otherwise data in RAID stripe will not be consistent,and RAID cannot rebuild from failure for member disk.This paper investigates others' initialization methods,and proposes a new method for improving initialization performance.It makes RAID initialize and I/O request processing parallel,and reduces initialization time efficiently.Testing results reveals a moderately successful performance improvement of the new initialization method.
