Effect of Arabic Gum on the Sedimentation of Al_2O_3 Suspension
The ultra-fine alumina suspension was prepared using arabic gum as a dispersant,the effect of a dispersant additive,agitation time and pH value on the sedimentation of a-Al2O3 suspension was studied,Zeta potential of suspension at different pH value was tested and the sintered body was analyzed.The results show that the most desirable suspension conditions were 24 h for agitation time and 0.8 wt% of Al2O3 for the arabic gum concentration.Zeta potential measurements show that the addition of Arabic gum shift the isoelectric point to the left,as well as a dramatic change in the absolute zeta potential,and pH value change has no effect on sedimentation of Al2O3 suspension and Zeta potential.
