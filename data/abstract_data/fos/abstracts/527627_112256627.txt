Evaluation of Static Strength and Plasticities of 9Cr-1Mo-V-Nb-N Steel Pipe on Optimization Process Parameters
In this paper the static strength and plasticities of 9Cr-1Mo-V-Nb-N thick-wall seamless steel pipe ware measured at room temperature and 566 ℃,which were heat treated by using optimizing process parameters of 1 055 ℃ normalization and 765 ℃ temper. The effect of sampling positions and sampling directions on the static strength and plasticities were respectively evaluated by using statistic test t and standard deviation s. The results showed that the static strength and plasticities and standard deviation were superior to those of 9Cr-1Mo-V-Nb-N thick-wall seamless steel pipe heat-treated by using original process and were about equal to those of imported P91 thick-walled seamless steel pipe. So these reveal that the manufacturing techniques of 9Cr-1Mo-V-Nb-N thick-walled seamless steel pipe in our country were ripeness gradually.
