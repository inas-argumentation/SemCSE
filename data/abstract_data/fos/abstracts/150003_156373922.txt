Route Choice of Relationship Construction between Collective Economic Organization and Village Committees
For relation between village-level collective economic organization and village committees,the law generally provides that village committees execute economic management function in case of no collective economic organizations established. For those established,collective economic organization is equivalent to or beneath village committees,or even replaced by village committees. Although they generally exist in the form of two for one in practice,transition from consolidating to separating is a rational route choice in the relationship construction. In order to endue the village committees with essential economic management function and at the same time cater to the needs of rural economic development,the economic management function of village committees shall go macroscopic and indirect,which in law perfection can be expressed as follows:supervise the collective economic organization on the implementing relevant law,regulations,resolution and statutes as well as carrying out financial system,take part in the important decision-makings of collective economic organization,and reflect villagers' opinions and come up with development suggestions.
