Study on new detective technology of the organic pesticide residues in fruits and vegetables
Organophosphate pesticides belong to esters compounds,organophosphorus pesticides can be hydrolysis in alkali solution,and easily generates dichloro acetaldehyde.It is a specially good reaction produced pink compound that dichloro acetaldehyde reacts with resorcin,then a new detective technology that the residue content of organophosphorus pesticide in fruits and vegetables was established by spectrophotometer.The experimentation results show that the most absorbing wavelength of the pink compound by dichloro acetaldehyde reacted with resorcin is at 480 nm absorbance,the prime conditions are pH11,appropriate resorcin solution,and calefaction temperature 60 ℃ by water bath,this new detective technology has a good linear relationship in the range of 0.08~16 mg/kg and the detection limits of 2.7×10-2 mg/kg,the relative standard deviations were 0.60%.The method was proven to be high select,simple,nicety and low cost.
