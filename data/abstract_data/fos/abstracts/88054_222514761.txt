1-(HETERYLOXY)SILATRANES
Novel heterocyclic 1-derivatives of silatranes have been obtained. The optimal synthetic method for the indicated compounds is transesterification of tetraethoxysilane by an equimolar mixture of triethanolamine and a heterocyclic (or aromatic) hydroxyl-containing compound. Authors: I. Iovel, L. Golomba, J. Popelis, S. Grinberga, and E. Lukevics.
