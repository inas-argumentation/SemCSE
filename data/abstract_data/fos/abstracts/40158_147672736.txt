A STUDY OF THE PERSONAL-SOCIAL AND INTELLECTUAL CHARACTERISTICS OF A GROUP OF STATE COLLEGE STUDENTS PREPARING TO TEACH
THE PURPOSE of this study is to seek infor mation about the personal-social and intellectual characteristics of students preparing to teach in a Wisconsin state college. It is hoped that the study will provide information not merely about the characteristics of the students at any given time, but information about the stability of the data col lected with reference to these characteristics and how they are interrelated.
