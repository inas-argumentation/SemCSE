Study on Hot Forming Die and Temperature Control System for High-strength Steel
The key technologies of hot high-strength steel forming include die structure design and temperature control system design. Quenching and cooling rate of sheet metal after heating deformation is the key of success or failure in the process, therefore developing a die to realize the optimum quenching and cooling process and the corresponding temperature control system is urgent. Taking a box-shaped part with flange as the study object, the fillet radius and clearance for punch and die of hot forming die were calculated firstly. Then the concrete structure of hot forming punch, die and blank holder and the cooling water way were designed. Finally the automatic control scheme and device of temperature control system for hot forming die were developed.
