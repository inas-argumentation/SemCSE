Effects of pre-harvest salicylic acid treatment on storage quality of muskmelon fruits
Plants of muskmelon were sprayed with 0.2, 1.0, 5.0 mmol/L of salicylic acid at four times of plant melon: young fruit period, fruit enlarging period, netting period and 48 h before picking, respectively. To study the different concentration of pre-harvest salicylic acid effect of solution to post-harvest physiology. Post-harvest melon storied at low temperature (7 ℃), regularly measured the firmness of fruits, weight-loss rate, soluble solids, titratable acid, ascorbic acid content, incidence of disease and disease index of muskmelon. The results show that 1.0 mmol/L of salicylic acid can be better to keep the firmness of melon fruit, inhibit the increasing of weight loss rate; can efficiently inhibit the soluble solids, titratable acid, ascorbic acid content degradation; can inhibit the incidence of disease and disease index of muskmelon.
