Strengthening Top-down is Necessary to Improve the Effectiveness Education of Ideology and Politics in Universities
In the thirty years of reform and opening-up,many achievements have been obtained on the ideology and politics education in universities of our country.However,the practical effects are not always satisfying due to the lack of a bridge between theory and practice of the education of ideology and politics.This article believes that the introducing top-down concept of the management in large projects would help the construction of the bridge between theory and practice of the ideology and politics education,inject the scientific thought of development into the strategical planning of ideology and politics education,and improve the education earnestly and consistently.This article starts with the discussion of what top-down of the ideology and politics education in universities is,then analyzes the importance of introducing top-down in full details,and points out the key points in the top-down of the ideology and politics education in universities.
