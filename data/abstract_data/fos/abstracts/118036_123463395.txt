How to Define the Concept of the Personal Property
What is can be called the thing and what is can be called the "thing treated" of the real property law? The answers to the question will directly affect the definition of the personal property. The definition of the thing should be based on the objective existence of the object, and the definition of the thing treated should be characteristic of its subjective existence of the object. Based on of those, it can define the concept of the personal property in two ways: one is the subtraction principle, means that the personal property is something residual after the extension of the thing concept detracting the real property; another is the imitation principle, means that the personal property still including the thing treated of the real property law.
