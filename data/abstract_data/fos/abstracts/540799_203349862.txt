True, false, paranormal and ‘designated’?: a reply to Jenkins
Jenkins (2007) charges that the language advanced by Beall (2006) is either expressively impoverished, or inconsistent. We argue that Jenkins’s objections are based on unreasonably strong constraints on formal theories of truth. Our primary concern is not to defend the ‘paranormal’ framework advanced by Beall (2006), but to respond to a common – and implausible – ‘revenge’-style charge directed at a certain class of formal theories of truth and paradox. In §1, we outline Beall’s language; §2 outlines Jenkins’s main criticism and the resulting dilemma. In §3, we give our two-fold reply to Jenkins.
