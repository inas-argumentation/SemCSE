Effect of Yttrium on Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Ti600 Alloy
The effects of rare earth element(Y) on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti600 alloy were studied by OM and SEM measurement.The results show that Y can refine microstructure.It has little influence on the strength at room temperature,but can improve plasticity.In addition,Y can change the distribution of S2-(Ti、Zr)6Si3 and α2 precipitated phase and dislocation's configuration in matrix,so it can improve the thermal stability and creep resistance.
