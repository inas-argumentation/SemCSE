Effect of Enzymes Preparation Supplementation in Diets with Wheat on Digestion and Microflora in Growing Pigs
One handred and eight heads of crossbred Duroc×Landrace×Yorkshire piglets were selected randomly and divided into 9 treatments(3 duplicates per treatment,4 piglets per duplicate with the male/female 1∶1) to evaluate the effect of wheat ratio(0%,25%,50%) and NSP enzymes supplementation(0,0.025%,0.05%) on nutrient digestibility and rectal pH and microflora of growing pigs by 2×3 factorial design.The results showed that:(1)Dietary wheat ratio had significant effects on nutrient digestibility and rectal pH and microflora of growing pigs(P0.05).Digestibility of CF,CP,DM and GE for growing pigs decreased by 23.10%(P0.05),16.50%(P0.05),13.14%(P0.05),8.66%(P0.05) and rectal Bifidobacterium and Lactobacilluys numbers decreased by 31.95%(P0.05),35.46%(P0.05),respectively,but rectal E.coli and Salmonella numbers were 173.84%(P0.05),156.81%(P0.05) higher in diet Ⅲ with 50% wheat than in diet Ⅰ without wheat respectively. There were significant differences between diets with 25% wheat and diets with 50% wheat in all the above index.(2)NSP enzymes supplementation had significant effects on nutrient digestibility and rectal pH and microflora of growing pigs(P0.05).Digestibility of CF,CP,DM and GE for growing pigs increased by 46.19%(P0.05),10.88%(P0.05),11.68%(P0.05),5.18%(P0.05) and rectal Bifidobacterium and Lactobacilluys numbers increased by 43.02%(P0.05),33.80%(P0.05),but rectal E.coli and Salmonella numbers were 52.14%(P0.05),62.11%(P0.05) lower in diets with 0.025% NSP enzymes than in diets without NSP enzymes respectively.However,there were no significant differences between the two levels(0.025% and 0.05%)of NSP enzymes in all the above index.Therefore,NSP enzymes supplementation in diet with wheat can increase nutrient digestibility and improve rectal microflora of growing pigs.Based on the above results,it is suggested that the optimum wheat ratio inclusion in swine diet is about 25%,and the optimum enzyme preparation supplementation level is 0.025%.
