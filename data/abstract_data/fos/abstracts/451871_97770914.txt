PREPARATION, CHARACTERIZATION, AND INTERCALATION OF (PBS)(TS2)2 ; (T=TI, NB, TA), NEW TERNARY SULFIDES WITH LAYERED COMPOSITE CRYSTAL STRUCTURE
A few (PbS)(TS2)2; (T = Ti, Nb, Ta) type of ternary sulfides has been prepared. On the basis of powder X-ray diffraction, it is suggested that they have layered composite crystal structure in which a two-atom-thick PbS layer and two adjacent three-atom-thick TS2 sandwiches are stacked alternately. Intercalation of hydrazine into the sulfides has been observed.
