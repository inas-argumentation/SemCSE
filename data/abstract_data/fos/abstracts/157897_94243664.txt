Self-diffusion and ionic transport of sodium and cesium ions in particles of hydrous zirconia
Self-diffusion of Na+ and Cs+ in hydrous zirconia heated at 50, 200 and 400°C was studied as a function of ion exchange capacity. The mobility of Na+ and Cs+ decrease with increase of heating temperature and ion exchange capacity. At the same capacity, D of Na+ is higher than that of Cs+ at all heating temperatures. The diffusion constants, activation energies and entropies of activation were determined. In light of these parameters, the mechanism of isotopic exchange is elucidated.
