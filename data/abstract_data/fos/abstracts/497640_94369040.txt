BRIEF COMMUNICATIONS: Generation of light in epitaxial cadmium sulfide films
Lasing was observed in single-crystal (0001) CdS films grown on (0001) Al/sub 2/O/sub 3/ substrates and excited with N/sub 2/ laser radiation. Lasing emission occurred at 492 and 497 nm and the thresholds were 0.3 and 0.75 MW/cm/sup 2/, respectively. The half-width of the bands was approx.1 nm and the divergence of the radiation was approx.20/sup 0/. It was assumed that the mechanisms responsible for lasing were the exciton--phonon interaction (492 nm) line and recombination in an electron--hole plasma (497 nm).
