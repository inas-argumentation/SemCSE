Study on extracting the essential oils from jujube
Ziziphus jujube cv.Jinsixiaozao produced in Dezhoulaoling was used as raw material,the essential oils from jujube was extracted using two methods,the influence of various factors on the yield was studied.The results show that the yield of essential oils is higher using ethanol as solvent,and optimal parameters for extracting the essential oils from jujube are as following∶ the ratio of raw material weight(g) to solvent volume(mL) 1∶4,extracting time 20 min,extracting temperature 78 ℃ and steam distiltation time 10 min.
