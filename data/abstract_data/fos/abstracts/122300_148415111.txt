Moral Construction from the Perspective of Marxist Epistemology
Marxist epistemology emphasizes practice,so moral construction should also stress the importance of moral practice.The morality in moral construction should be a unity of moral sentiment,moral ideology and moral behavior.Moral sentiment is the foundation and a kind of sensible understanding;moral ideology is a kind of rational understanding related to morality;and moral practice is the embodiment of morality.Accordingly,evaluations of morality should base on moral practice instead of conjecturing moral sentiment and moral ideology.
