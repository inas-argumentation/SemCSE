Physiological reactions of different Eucalyptus cultivars under low temperature stress
In order to explore the adaptability of cold resistance of introduced eucalyptus, their annual shoots in vitro were used as material to investigate the effects of low temperature(0℃,-5℃,-10℃,-15℃ and-20℃) on electrolyte leakage, soluble sugars, free proline and chlorophyll fluorescence parameters of 4 kinds of eucalyptus; simultaneously, chlorophyll fluorescence parameters of 4 kinds of eucalyptus were further studied under low temperature stress(0℃) for different treatment time(2 d, 4 d and 6 d). The results indicated that the electrolyte leakage rates of 4 kinds of eucalyptus shoots were rising with a fall in temperature, meanwhile, the fitting of Logistic equation to LT50 derived 4 kinds of eucalyptus showed that LT50 of Hehongan was minimum; the content of soluble sugar increased at first and then decreased with the fall in temperature, and the difference between the contents of soluble sugar in 4 kinds of eucalyptus was significant. Pro(proline)of 4 kinds of eucalyptus shoots was rising with a fall in temperature; the fall in temperature along with the prolong of time under 0℃ stress, Fo was up gradually, Fv/Fm, ФPSⅡ and qP were declined. Integrated indicator value of cold resistance of each provenance was obtained based on the function value. The cold resistance order of different provenances was Hehongan﹥DH-32﹥U-6﹥GuangLin 9.
