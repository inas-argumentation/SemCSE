Effects of Processing Parameters on Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Zirconium Alloy
The microstructure,mechanical property and texture of Zr alloy under different deformation,Q value and heat treatment process were investigated.The results show that ZrSn alloy under large deformation recrystallizes completely when soaking for 3.5 h at 510 ℃.With the increase of heat treatment temperature,the extension strength and yield strength decrease,but the speed decreases a bit slowly.The plastic property increases slowly.In order to get appropriate texture,it's better to choose Q value greater than 1.2 or eaqual to 1.8.
