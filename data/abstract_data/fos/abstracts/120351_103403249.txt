RECYCLIZATION OF 4-ALKYL(ARYL)-2,6-DIAMINO-3,5-DICYANO-4 H -THIOPYRANS WITH 2-AMINOETHANOL AND (2-FURYL)METHYLAMINE
Recyclization occurred on interacting 4-alkyl(aryl)-2,6-diamino-3,5-dicyano-4 H -thiopyrans with 2-aminoethanol and (2-furyl)methylamine, and resulted in the formation of 4-alkyl(aryl)-2,6-bis-(R-methylamino)pyridine-3,5-dicarbonitriles. Authors: V. D. Dyachenko and T. A. Rylskaya English Translation in Chemistry of Heterocyclic Compounds , 2013, 49 (6), pp 897-901 http://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10593-013-1323-0
