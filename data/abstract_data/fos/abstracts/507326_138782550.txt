Research on Low Temperature Brittleness of 51CrV4 Spring Steel
Using 51CrV4 spring steel as research object,its low-temperature brittleness was studied.The results show that the impact energy of 51CrV4 spring steel is 20-25 J at-60℃——0℃,and it decreases with the decline of temperature,the impact energy maintains in a high level at-60℃ and no brittle fracture happens.The impact fracture of 51CrV4 spring steel is mixed type fracture,with cleavage,tears and dimple aggregation characteristics.By the spectrum analysis,51CrV4 spring steel contains small amounts of non-metallic inclusions made up of silicon and oxygen elements,which belong to the silicate inclusions.
