Microstructural Characteristics and Carbonitrides Precipitation of 830 MPa High Strength Finished Twisted Steel Bar During Surface Ultra Fast Cooling Process
The carbonitrides precipitation morphology,strength modes and microstructural characteristics of 830 MPa high-strength finished twisted steel bar during surface ultra fast cooling process were studied.The results show that precipitation phase has three forms that is square,sphere and flake,and the structures are all(TixV1-x)(CN).Core educts of high-strength finished twisted steel bar are more,disperse and fine,the forms are flaky/fine flaky,and the size is about 50 nm;for edge educts are relative less,square/sphere,and size is about 100 nm;Typical structure characteristics are the core is tempered sorbite and the edge is ferrite and pearlite,make use of dispersion precipitation,interphase precipitation and the precipitation on dislocation line inner ferrite on the basis of structure strengthening,make up for strength differences between the region and the core,ensuring the stability of high strength finished twisted steel bar.
