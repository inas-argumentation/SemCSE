Investigation of neodymium silicate glass diffused waveguides
Planar waveguides were fabricated from GLS2 and GLS3 laser glasses and KGSS026 glasses with high concentrations of neodymium ions by diffusion of K/sup +/ and Ag/sup +/ ions. The optical stability of the K/sup +/ waveguides was superior to that of the Ag/sup +/ waveguides. Amplification of laser radiation was achieved in flashlamp-pumped GLS3 glass K/sup +/ waveguides and the gain was approx.0.08 cm/sup -1/.
