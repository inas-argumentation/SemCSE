The Possibility of Efficient Mechanisms for Trading an Indivisible Object
We study a trading problem in which the seller of an indivisible object races at least two potential buyers. Each trader′s valuation is privately known and regarded by the others as an independent random variable. We assume that the highest possible buyers′ valuation is greater, by an arbitrarily small amount, than the seller′s highest possible valuation. We show that for a family of distributions forming an open set in the space of probability distributions, there exist individually rational, ex post efficient, Bayesian mechanisms for trading the object. In addition, we show that any such mechanism is equivalent to a simple bidding game. Journal of Economic Literature Classification Numbers: D82, C72.
