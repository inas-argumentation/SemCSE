Characterization of clay and pillared clay catalysts
High-resolution solid-state NMR ( 29 Si and 27 Al), as well as Fourier transform IR and X-ray diffraction are shown to be of considerable value in characterizing pillared clays and various un-pillared clays consisting of one or other of the following interlamellar ions: Li + , Na + , Cs + , NH + 4 and Al 3+ . The catalytic activities of clays prior to and after pillaring are briefly discussed.
