Economic Power, Financial Instability, and the Cuomo Report
Economic power and endogenous financial instability are fundamental aspects of the modem capitalist economy. The existence of administered prices and wages, validated by endogenous money expansion, implies that product prices include customary, discretionary "costs," which contain significant elements of Veblenian waste in corporate invidious distinction.
