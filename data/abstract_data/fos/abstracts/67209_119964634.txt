Comment on 'Equilibrium configurations and energies of the rotating elastic cable in space'
I N Ref. 1, it is suggested that a thin elastic rod rotating in space can have stable equilibrium configurations that contain intersections or loops. The conditions for stability, which are neglected by Wang, are given here together with a derivation of a simplified version of the energy equation. Unfortunately, these results cannot be used to demonstrate the stability of Wang's looped configurations because they are found to be unstable. For the rod to be stable, the configuration must be at a local energy minimum for any small perturbation of the shape that maintains the angular momentum.
