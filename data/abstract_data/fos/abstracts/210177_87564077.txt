Metabolite Contents and Correlation Analysis in Yam Tuber
The differences of metabolite contents and correlation were analyzed by multi-variable statistical methods in GH No.2,GH No.5,G16 tuber during harvest period.The results indicated that the contents of water content,soluble sugar,sucrose,malic acid,tartaric acid,saponin and titratable acid in G16 were significantly higher than those in GH No.2 and GH No.5,but that of reducing sugar and soluble protein in G16 were significantly lower than those in GH No.2 and GH No.5.There were significant correlation between malic acid,tartaric acid and titratable acid,there were some different correlationship among other metabolites in different Varieties.Four common factors were extracted from all measured indices in 3 cultivars,the cumulative variance of the common factors accounted was 98.15%,93.11%,94.03%,respectively.Acids was the No.1 common factor,the others were discordant in varieties.These results indicated that there wer differences in contents,correlation,key factors of metabolites in different varieties,and resulted in different quality effects.Acid substance played a key role in substance metabolism and quality information in yam tuber.
