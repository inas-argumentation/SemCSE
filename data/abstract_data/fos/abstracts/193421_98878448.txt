Nondestructive testing of jujube water in south Xinjiang by NIRS
Low testing speed, low precision and sample damage is one of the major disadvantages of traditional moisture measurement of jujube during acquisition and processing. Detecting technology with rapid, simple and without damnification is necessary. NIRS(Near infrared spectroscopy technology) has the advantages with rapid, simple and without damnification. Fresh jujubes were randomly divided into two groups. The first group was used to establish the calibration model of moisture, the other is verification model. The drying of jujubes was obtained by standard bake method. Spectral data was pretreated by Savitzky-Golay and MSC. The calibration model of moisture was constructed by PLS. The lowest value of SEC is 1.0667. The RC, RPDC reach the highest value 0.98683, 5.6258, respectively. The main factor is 6, the verification deviation range-1.94~2.22, and mean variation is 0.41%. The result indicated that moisture measurement of jujube in south Xinjiang with NIRS is available.
