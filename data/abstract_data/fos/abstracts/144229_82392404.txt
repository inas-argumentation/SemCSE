Effects of Eimeria acervulina infection on partial blood physiological and biochemical parameters of chickens.
One hundred and twenty chickens of 28-day-old were used to investigate the effects of E.acervulina on partial serum physiological and biochemical parameters.The results showed that the quantity of erythrocyte,the contents of hemoglobin,calcium,sodium,magnesium,serum glucose,serum cholesterol,serum total protein and the activities of superoxide dismutase,glutathione peroxidase,catalase decreased compared with control group.The quantity of erythrocyte decreased significantly on the 6th and 8th day(P0.05);the contents of hemoglobin decreased significantly on the 4th and 8th day(P0.05);the calcium level decreased significantly on the 8th day(P0.05);the magnesium and serum total protein level decreased significantly on the 8th and 10th day(P0.05);the activities of superoxide dismutase decreased significantly on the 2nd and 4th day(P0.05).The contents of potassium increased slightly,but without significant difference compared with the control group.The alkaline phosphatase levels decreased firstly,then increased to the level of the control group.
