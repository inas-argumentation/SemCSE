Research progress of tea catechins oxypolymers
Since Roberts discoveried theaflavins,people have already made a series of encouraging progress to tea catechins oxypolymers.This article does the survey and summary about domestic and international research of tea catechins oxypolymers,including its biological activities,the method of its extraction,pre-paration,separation and purification,for the purpose of providing a new way for tea catechins oxypolymers research.
