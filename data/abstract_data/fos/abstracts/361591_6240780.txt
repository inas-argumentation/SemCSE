Degeneracy of the lowest Landau level and quantum group of the quantum Hall effect on the Poincare half plane
It is shown that the presence of the quantum group symmetry suq(2) in the quantum Hall effect on the Poincare upper half plane governs the degeneracy of the lowest Landau level.
