Features of isotherms of the surface tension of a bentonite water suspension
The results from experimental studies of the concentration and temperature dependence of the surface tension of aqueous suspensions of bentonite are presented. The studies are conducted using the pendant drop method on a DSA-100 tensiometer. The maximum error of measuring the surface tension is 1%. The characteristics of isotherms of the surface tension of aqueous suspensions are analyzed. An attempt is made to explain the nature of low surface tension isotherms of a suspension.
