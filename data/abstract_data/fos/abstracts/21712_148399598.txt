Turn to research of education being concerned about life
The researchers have always been tryig to approach the science of education by applying empirical studies in the research of education. In the middle period of the 19th century of the west, an intense argument between scientific research and humane research has been caused. However, empirical studies, which treat"human beings"in the same way as other "non-human substances", have lost its vigor in the research of education. With vivid human activities as its direct focus, the research of education demands a turn from "being regardless of life" typical of empirical studies to "being concerned about life". This is a "paradigm of education research based on understanding"of humanities.
