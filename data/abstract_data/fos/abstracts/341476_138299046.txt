Effect of Mg Content on Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Hypereutectic Al-18% Si Alloy
With the view of improving morphology, SEM and optical microscope were used to observe the changes of the microstructure on hypereutectic Al-18% Si casting alloys with different content of Mg, and relative mechanical properties were tested. The effects of intensifying treatment had been investigation. Experimental results show that the effect of Mg in hypereutectic Al-Si casting alloys is come true by heat treatment. With Mg increasing in alloys, strength will increase, but elongation decline. To improve morphology and increase the mechanical properties of hypereutectic Al-18% Si alloys,the content of Mg should be limited within 0.5%-3.0% .
