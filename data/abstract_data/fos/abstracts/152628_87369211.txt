Effect of tea polyphenols on fresh-keeping refrigerated fillets of fresh Ctenopharyngon Idellus
In order to study effects of tea polyphenol on fresh-keeping refrigerated fillets, fresh Ctenopharyngon Idellus were slaughtered and washed, and then dipped in 0.25%, 0.50%, 1.00%, 1.50%, 2.00% tea polyphenol solution for 5 min, then kept in 4 ℃ after vacuum packaging. Sensory assessment, pH, total volatile basic nitrogen(TVB-N) and bacterial counts were determinated to evaluate the fish quality. The result indicated that tea polyphenol solution could restrain bacteria growth, reduce TVB- N, and delay the decline of sensory score. In summary, tea polyphenol solution can inhibit bacteria growth and anti - oxidation of fat, and delay the spoilage of refrigerated fillets fish.
