Pesticides residue analysis of pure tea produced in Xishuangbanna state
Pesticide residue analysis of 13 pesticides for 400 samples of pure tea produced in Xishuangbanna state including 285 samples of non-fermentation tea and 114 samples of fermentation tea were detected by gas chromatography. The result showed that maximum residues of benzene hexachloride, chlorophenothane, permethrin, bifenthrin, cypermethrin, flucythrinate, esfenvalerate, deltamethrin, acephate, dimethoate, fenitrothion of non-fermentation tea were 0.113 mg/kg, 0.126 mg/kg, 0.844 mg/kg, 1.090 mg/kg, 0.304 mg/kg, 0.242 mg/kg, 0.508 mg/kg, 0.1 mg/kg, 0.032 mg/kg, 0.037mg/kg respectively and detection rate were 26.3%, 3.2% , 2.1% , 13.0% , 17.9% , 3.5% , 2.8% , 0.35% , 4.9% , 5.3% , 5.0% . Maximum residues of benzene hexachloride, chloro-phenothane, bifenthrin, cypermethrin, flucythrinate, esfenvalerate, deltamethrin, acephate, dimethoate, fenitrothion of fermentation tea were 0.135 mg/kg, 0.103 mg/kg, 0.195mg/kg, 0.124 mg/kg, 0.492 mg/kg, 0.138mg/kg, 0.060 mg/kg, 0.024 mg/kg, 0.004 mg/kg, 0.001 mg/kg, 0.005 mg/kg respectively and detection rate were 42.1%, 12.3%, 7.9%, 19.3%, 19.3%, 4.4%, 8.8%, 0.88%, 2.6%, 1.7%, 2.5%. respectively. Dichlorvos and quinalphos of non-fermentation and fermentation tea were not detected.
