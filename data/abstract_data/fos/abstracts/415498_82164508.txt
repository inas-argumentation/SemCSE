Preliminary identification of a pathogenic bacterium in Phalaenopsis amabilis
One bacterial strain was isolated from infected leaves of Phalaenopsis amabilis from Taiwan.The strain was Gram negative,polar flagella,oxidase positive,catalase positive and could cause sunken spot on pileus.The results of pathogenicity,morphological characters,cultural characters,physio-biochemical properties and the phylogentic analysis based on 16S rDNA suggested that it was a strain of Pseudomonas tolaasii.
