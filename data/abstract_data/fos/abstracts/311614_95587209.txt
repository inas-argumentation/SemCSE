ENANTIOFACE-DIFFERENTIATING EPOXIDATION OF (1-PHENYLALKYLIDENE)MALONONITRILES WITH MOLECULAR OXYGEN, CATALYZED BY OPTICALLY ACTIVE TERTIARY AMINES
It has been found that (1-phenylalkylidene)malononitriles are asymmetrically oxidized with molecular oxygen to optically active oxiranes in the presence of chiral tertiary amines such as nicotine and d- or 1-α-(N,N-dimethyl)phenylethylamine.
