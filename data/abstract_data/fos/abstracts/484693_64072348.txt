Internet Integrated Customer Relationship Management a Key Success Factor for Companies in the E-Commerce Arena
The purpose of this paper is to analyze the application of the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) in New Economy, which is characterized by the advancement in the information technology. The impacts of the Internet on CRM are assessed in detail in order to reveal the new opportunities and challenges related to CRM in the business settings. In addition, this paper addresses the relationship between CRM and other E-commerce applications, including ERP, SCM and Data Warehousing. Such addresses indicate the role of CRM in the E-commerce arena. Based on the analysis, CRM's future implications in the Internet era to be discussed.
