Letters: Eye of the beholder.
Dear Editor: I read (ES&T 1998, 32(13), 302A) that EPA will soon present the Agency's Science Advisory Board (SAB) with its updated Dioxin Risk Assessment (DRA). The exaggerated human cancer claims EPA made in the 1994 DRA died with my 1995 SAB presentation, of a comparative analysis of the two major epidemiology studies on dioxin, including the single study EPA itself relied on. A highly specific and testable pattern of cancer decreases (lung, primary liver, total cancers) and increases (soft tissue sarcoma, non-Hodgkins lymphoma) found in the low-dose men of the Seveso population was repeated and amplified in the male population of one of the NIOSH plants. The chance of finding such a predicted pattern of effects repeated to the degree that it is in any of the 12 NIOSH plants is p < 0 001
