Effect of 1-MCP and silicon gum film modified atmosphere packaging on quality retention during storage of green pepper
The effects of 1-MCP treatment, silicon gum film MAP on quality retention and physiology property of green pepper during storage at(8±0.5) ℃ were investigated. The results showed 1-MCP treatment and MAP could inhibit the degradation of chlorophyll, decline of Vc and increase of EC, maintain the quality of green pepper. After 28 days, the green pepper treated with 1-MCP and packaged with silicon gum film, commodity rate of green pepper was 90.2%, showed the better effect. However, with time progresses, CO2 concentration increased and high relative humidity in silicon gum packages, the rot rate increased, 1-MCP treatment effect is significant, the commodity rate of green pepper increased 14.78% compared with CK after 42 days.
