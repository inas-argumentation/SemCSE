Comparative Analysis on the FDI Location Selection of Eastern and Western Area in Mainland China
This paper studies the location selection factors of Foreign Direct Investment in mainland China by combining qualitative analysis with quantitative analysis.Using data from 1996 to 2005 of 11 provinces of eastern area and western area respectively,the empirical results suggest that FDIi,t-1,AGDPi,t,WAGi,t,TIEi,t,EDUi,t are important factors in eastern China,and FDIi,t-1 Gi,t,are important factors in western China.The conclusion provides valuable empirical bases for appropriate FDI policies of eastern area and western area respectively.
