Describes efficient data replication providing solutions for telecommunication that solves the problem existed in enterprise information systems in the data management and introduces the overall system architecture,data replication software,data replication scale,data replication channel optimization and deployment of data access security measures and optimizing of the real-time data replication project of BSS system core database of certain telecommunication enterprise provincial branch,and the effectiveness of project implementation.
