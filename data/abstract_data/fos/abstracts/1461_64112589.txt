MAOCW:One New Type of CSCW System based on Mobile Agent and Its Application
With the development of computer network,distributed systems are being widely applied.But the entity of traditional C/S frame lacks of cooperation,which restricts the development of distributed systems.However,Mobile Agent is characteristic of self-determination,mobility,cooperation,intelligence and so on,which provides a new idea.There fore this paper puts forward a new-pattern system based on Mobile Agent,namely MAOCW,which can solve the problems in C/S.MAOCW disposes interacted and cooperative problems between different entity in CSCW,which advances work efficiency and quality.
