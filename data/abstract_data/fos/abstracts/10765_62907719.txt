Improved Research of P2P Model of CAN
This paper proposes an improved method of CAN(Content-Addressable Network). with the architecture of CAN , the method exploits the architecture of binary tree to make resources' configuration become reasonable in the distribution, and adopts backward-studying algorithm in routing algorithm. So the improved CAN becomes more effective. Experiments are imitated to perform by exploiting C++ and net-imitated tools(NS2). Experiment results indicate that the improved CAN improves resource orientation and error solution, and makes net resources' distribution become more reasonable and improves routing function. So it is worth popularizing and applying in unstructured P2P network.
