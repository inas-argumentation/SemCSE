Long-time asymptotics of the zero level set for the heat equation
The zero level set Z(t) := {x ∈ Rd : u(x, t) = 0} of a solution u to the heat equation in Rd is considered. Under certain conditions on moments of the initial data, we will prove the set Z(t) in a ball of radius C √ t for some C > 0 converges to a specific graph as t → ∞ when the set is divided by √t. Solving a linear combination of the Hermite polynomials gives the graph and coefficients of the linear combination depend on moments of the initial data. Also the graphs to which the zero level set Z(t) converges are classified in some cases.
