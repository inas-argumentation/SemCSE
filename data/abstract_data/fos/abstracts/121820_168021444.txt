The Problems about State-Owned Corporations' Inside Control——A Analysis Framework
Inside control in big corporations are common phenomena because of the information asymmetry.But there are not a lot of problems in west countries about inside control because they have good corporate governance structures.Based on the economic transformation in China,the inside control in state-owned corporations have something specialness.This paper analyzes the main causes about state-owned corporations'inside control,and we think that the structure about inside control and outside control should be instituted in state-owned corporations.
