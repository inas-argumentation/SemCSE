Design and Implementation of Optimum Investment Project based on MATLAB Optimization Toolbox
In the field of finance, optimization design and MATLAB optimization toolbox have inestimable prospect of application. This paper firstly presents the conception and the process of optimization design,and analyses the advantage of MATLAB optimization toolbox in solving optimization problem. Then this paper designs and implements an optimum investment project through establishing mathematical model, choosing an algorithm named Sequential Quadratic Programming, utilizing a nonlinear restraint function in MATLAB optimization toolbox and programming in MATLAB.
