Perhaps the most important conclusion to be taken from this book is not the argument that early modern theater is a product of new knowledge practices, but the corollary conclusion that theater became valued as a site of knowledge production. Turner’s work offers a powerful revision to how we understand early modern stage practice. At its best, The English Renaissance Stage allows us to see into the intellectual toolkit that created the “golden world” of Renaissance drama. ELIZABETH SPILLER Texas Christian University
