Simulation of Plastic Metal Flow Field in Friction Stir Welding
According to hydrodynamic theory,a finite element model of the three dimensional flow field for plastic metal was established,the three dimensional velocity and flow line trajectory of plastic metal were obtained by numerical simulation.The results show that,the upper welding metal is laminar flow and the lower metal is turbulent flow.The plastic metal around pin moves,upwards,its peripheral metal moves downwards.
