Study on Structure and Wear Resistance of Ni-based Alloy Spraying Coating with Adding Nanometer CeO_2
Ni-based self-fluxing alloy powder with various percentage of nanometer CeO2 were sprayed on the substrate of Q235 steel by subsonic flame spraying. The microstructure, micro-hardness and wear resistance of the spraying coatings were measured. The effects of nanometer CeO2 on the microstructure and wear resistance of Ni-based self-fluxing alloy coatings were studied. The results show that the microstructure, micro-hardness and wear resistance of the coatings can be improved by adding appropriate quantity of nanometer CeO2.
