Study on Fingerprint of Ginkgo Leaves by Pressurized Capillary Electrochromatography
The pressurized capillary electrochromatography(PCEC) fingerprint of Ginkgo leaves was established to evaluate the quality of Ginkgo leaves. 29 co-possessing peaks were selected as the fingerprints of Ginkgo leaves,and rutin was selected as the internal reference peak. The pCEC fingerprints of 10 batches of samples from different regions in China were obtained,and 9 fingerprints showed good similarities with the coefficients from 0.900 to 0.991. The method presented has good precision,reproducibility and stability with the relative standard deviations (RSDs) less than 2.6% for the relative migration time and less than 3.5% for the relative peak area. With low consumption of sample and reagent,good reproducibility and high resolution capability,this pCEC method is suitable for the quality control of Ginkgo leaves.
