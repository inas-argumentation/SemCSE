Research on Homogeneous Structure of Manufacturing Industry from the Angle of the Division of Labor
After near thirty years development, the Yangtze River Delta region (includes Jiangsu, Shanghai, Zhejiang) has been one of the places with fastest economy development and strongest economy power in China. Meanwhile, the economic integration in Yangtze River Delta region, especially the industry integration was proposed twenty years ago. In this paper, from the labor division angle the author has analyzed the homogeneous phenomenon of manufacturing industry in Yangtze River Delta region based on amounts of basic researches. By means of demonstration analysis with computation measures, the author has advanced relevant policy suggestions.
