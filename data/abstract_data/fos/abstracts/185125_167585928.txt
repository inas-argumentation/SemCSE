An Empirical Study of Rural Public Service Efficiency and Its Influencing Factors——Taking Northern Jiangsu as an Example
This article analyses the rural public service and its influencing factors by taking Northern Jiangsu as an example.The result indicates that the efficiency of the rural public service in Northern Jiangsu showed a trend of fluctuation and increase,there is a non-linear relationship between the efficiency and the equity of the rural public service,and the level of urbanization and market have positive effect,while the effect of the latter is not significant,which may be related to the imperfect market mechanism at present.The government should handle both the relation of fairness and efficiency and that of government supply and market supply correctly,thereby establishing a perfect supply system.
