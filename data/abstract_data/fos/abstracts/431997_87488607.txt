Determination of ESBLs and antibiotics susceptibility test analysis for avian pathogenic Escherichia coli from the disease duck
The productions of extended-spectrum lactamases(ESBIs) of 32 Escherichia coli isolated from duck were collected from seven provinces in China,and their antibiotics susceptibilities were investigated.The results showed that there were 7 ESBLs-producing isolates among all the 32 isolates E.coli,the ESBLs positive rate was 21.87%.The susceptibility rate of isolates producing ESBIs against third generation cephalosporin and semisynthetic penicillin were lower than 42.9%,but the resistant rates were higher than 28.6%,the resistant rates against Amoxicillin,Ampicillin and Ceftiofur were 100%.All the E.coli isolates were sensitive to imipenem,meropenem and cefepime.The susceptibility rates of all the pathogenic Escherichia coli to the combinations of beta-lactams with inhibitors such as amoxicillin-clavulanic acid,ampicillin-sulbactam,cefoperazone-sulbactam and so on,were all higher than it to β-lactam antibiotics alone.ESBLs-producing isolates revealed multi-resistance,beta-lactam antibiotics combinations with enzyme inhibitor can antagonize the resistance.
