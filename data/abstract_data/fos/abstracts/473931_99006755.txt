Chemical characterization and water absorption of artemisia seed polysaccharide
Artemisia seed polysaccharide (ASP) has great potential as a stabilizing agent, a water retention agent or a filmogen because of its high viscous and viscosity. Its molecular weight was determined by light scattering. The chemical structure was studied by chemical and spectroscopic techniques, e.g. methylation, GC, GC-MS, IR, NMR, enzymatic hydrolysis etc. The results demonstrate thatASPwas comprised of six kinds of monosaccharide: L-Ara, D-Xyl, D-Lyx, D-Man, D-Glc, D-Gal, in approximately molar ratios 1: 5: 2: 28: 4: 14. Its molecular weight was around 1.42x10 5 g/mol. Furthermore, the possible glucosidic linkages of ASP are described. The water absorption was determined.
