GC determination of fatty acids in fermented slurry from steamed sponge cake
Lipids were extracted with ultrasonic wave by using ether from fermented slurry during steamed sponge cake processing.Different components of fatty acids were methylated.Methylated lipids were then determined by GC equipped with 0.25 mm ×30 m× 0.25 μm capillary column.The results indicated that the main fatty acids were polyunsaturated fatty acids(PUFA) in fermented slurry,among which was 93.9%,The main PUFA was linoleic acid,which was 56.8%,and then 7,10-octadecadienoic acid and 8-octadecadienoic acid was 11.3%,18.4%,respectively.
