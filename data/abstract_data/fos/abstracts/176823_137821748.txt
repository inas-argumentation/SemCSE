Effect of Boronization Technology on Microstructure and Properties of Boronizing Layer on 45 Steel
The effects of boronizing temperature,holding time and formula on microstructure and properties of boronizing layer on 45 steel were investigated.The results show that,in the given conditions,the microstructure of boronizing layer is dense,boride wedged base in the shape of spider,which combines with base firmly;as the temperature heightens,boronizing thickness is significantly added and when the time extends,boronizing thickness is also significantly increased.Compared with the time,the impact of temperature factors on the layer thickness is greater.The surface hardness of 45 steel is greatly improved for the exist of FeB;the corrosion resistance of boronizing layer is excellent which can resist hydrochloric acid,sulfuric acid and sodium hydroxide.
