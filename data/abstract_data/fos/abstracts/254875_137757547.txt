Effects of Heat Treatment and Cooling Way on Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of High-boron High Speed Steel
The effects of cooling way and heat treatment on microstructure and properties of high-boron high speed steel were studied.The results show that the size and thickness of the quartz sand mould is bigger because of its eutectic carbide is thicker and the segregation is aggravating.The carbon-boron compound in graphite mould is fine and uniformity.Owing to residual austenite decrease,while quenching at 1050 ℃ and tempering at 525 ℃,the steel has high hardness and excellent wear resistance.
