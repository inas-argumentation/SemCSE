Study on Gender Differences in Scientific Reasoning Ability of College Students between China and the U.S.A
The Lawson Classroom Test of Scientific Reasoning(LCTSR)is used to test scientific reasoning ability of college students in China and the USA.The comparison shows gender differences in the USA are much more significant than those in China in the total average score.Their gender differences exist in all of six dimensions and most test items.Though Chinese female students and male students have achieved considerable results in scientific reasoning and scientific knowledge compared with international researches,the low proportion of women in scientific careers in China are completely consistent with international phenomena,which should arouse the concern of educational researchers.The factors that discourage women from entering science careers should be researched and some effective interventions should be implemented.
