In fact, in some cases, the repetitions show how different authors interpret the same phenomena or institutions, for example, the movement to seek the “land without evil” (a.k.a. “intact soil, where nothing has been built” or for example, virgin land [pp. 291, 317]). The volume also treats us to differing views on the Peruvian institution called SINAMOS of the 1970s and the very controversial Summer Language Institute. These comments should not distract unduly from what is otherwise an exhaustive research summary, which at times is elegantly written. It provides not only an outline of “what happened, where, when, and by whom,” but also illustrates different approaches to the reconstruction of the past. Chapter authors also include notes on what topics merit further research. For these reasons, I believe that the volume will serve as a convenient source for lecturers, first stop for many investigators, and guide to scholars, in general, for generations to come.
