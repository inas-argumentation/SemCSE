Synthesis of Binuclear Palladium(II) Complexes of Bidentate N(21),N(22)-Bridged Porphyrins
N(21),N(22)-(etheno)-bridged porphyrins were metallated with PdCl2 to give binuclear porphyrin complexes with (μ-dichloro)dipalladium(II) structure in which the terminal PdCl2 moiety is coming close to the porphyrin plane.
