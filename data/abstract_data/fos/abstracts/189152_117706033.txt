Junction-Dependent Spectroscopic Signatures of Larkin-Ovchinnikov Superconductors from Hybridized Midgap States
We have developed a discrete-lattice approach to calculate the conductance spectra between a normal-metal and an $s$-wave Larkin-Ovchinnikov (LO) superconductor, with the junction interface oriented along the order parameter (OP) modulation. As a result of Andreev reflection and the periodic OP sign reversal, a hybridized midgap-states band is formed amid a momentum-dependent gap. This band-in-gap structure and its anisotropic properties give rise to distinctive features in both the point-contact and tunneling spectra as compared with the BCS and Fulde-Ferrell cases. These spectroscopic features can serve as distinguishing signatures of the LO state.
