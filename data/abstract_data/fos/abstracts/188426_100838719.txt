Mass Spectroscopy for Investigation of Transition Metal Tantalum and Yttrium-Carbon Clusters
Metal-carbon clusters ion signal of the transition Tantalum and Yttrium were obtained with differential sampling system and the resonance enhanced multi-photon ionization technique. The second-harmonic output of a Nd: YAG laser is used to ablate the surface of rotating metal rod. The laser vaporization of the metal rod with helium carrier-gas contained 3% acetylene. Metal-carbon mixed clusters were formed through plasma reactions between laser-ablated metal atoms and C2H2. Cluster species underwent a supersonic expansion and formed a collimated beam with skimmer and entered into ionization region of a time-of-flight ( TOF) mass spectrometer,and then ionized by 193 nm excimer laser. The results showed that the intensities of ion signal of odd Tantalum-carbon chains were smaller,while the intensities of ion signal of even Tantalum-carbon chains were bigger. The Yttrium carbide cluster ions YC m+ ( m = 1-4) ,Y2C n+ ( n = 2,4,6) and YCnH 2+ ( n = 2 6) were also observed.
