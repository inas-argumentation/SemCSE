How to Deal with the Inefficient University Classroom Teaching: From the Students' Perspective
The ideal classroom teaching equilibrium is teachers enjoying teaching,students studying hard,and teacher-student interacting well.However,there were many negative phenomena in the university classroom teaching.Based on the information obtained from the interviews,students arrived in class and studied conscientiously generally when facing the high effective classroom teaching.At the same time,students were more likely to stay in the classroom when facing the inefficient classroom teaching.Students thought that some measures should be taken,such as promoting teachers to improve the teaching quality,guiding students to increase learning inputs,implementing some exit mechanism for students,to reverse the poor state and improve classroom teaching quality.
