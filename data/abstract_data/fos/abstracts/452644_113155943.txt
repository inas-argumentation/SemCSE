Wrinkling Research on Auto Inner Door in Sheet Metal stamping Forming
It was found that forming limit diagram used in CAE did not well evaluate and predicate of wrinkling defect in the production of inner plate auto door. Through the observation of wrinkling position in actual production and strain trajectories of corresponding materials in the CAE simulation,considering the principle and mechanism of uneven stretch wrinkles, the original evaluation criterion was modified which could also apply to the compressive stress wrinkling and uneven stretch wrinkles. Finally process optimization was guided by using the modified wrinkling evaluating criteria in CAE software. The experimental results show that process optimization guided by modified criteria can improve and eliminate the wrinkling effectively,which has certain accuracy and reliability.
