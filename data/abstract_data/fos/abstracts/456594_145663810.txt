A multicultural personal growth group as a pedagogical strategy with graduate counseling students

Students' levels of ethnic identity and social-cognitive development did not change; however, 90% (n = 18) of the students reported valuing the group experience and appreciated the group leaders facilitation of the pedagogical strategy.
