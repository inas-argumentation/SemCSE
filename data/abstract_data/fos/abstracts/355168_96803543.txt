Determination of the activities of Cr and Cr2O3 in slag-metal systems of ferrochromium smelting composition using stabilized zirconia electrolytes
This paper describes attempts to determine the activity of chromium in chromium-rich, liquid-iron chromium alloys, and that of chromium oxide (Cr2O3) in ferrochromium slags at 1650°C by use of the following cells: (i) Cr; Cr2O3∥ZrO2(CaO or Y2O3)∥(FeCr)alloy; Cr2O3, and (ii) Cr; Cr2O3∥ZrO2(CaO or Y2O 3)∥Cr; (Cr2O3 + slag). Measurements have been made of the activity of chromium in the FeCr system, and that of Cr2O3 in the Cr2O3MgOSiO2 d;Al2O3 slag system at 1650°C.
