Analysis of Impacts Recorded With An Instrumented Sphere
The transportation and handling of agricultural produce can result in various degrees of damage due to impacts. This research covers the techniques and software used for the analysis of impact data (vector accelerations) recorded by a microcontroller based Instrumented Sphere (IS) of 140 mm diameter and 1.44 kg. mass. The IS stores data for impacts which are above a predetermined threshold and formats the data into records consisting of triaxial acceleration values along with the time of occurrence. Some of the techniques used in the analysis include: digital filtering, peak detection, numerical integration and coordinate rotations. The analysis techniques were also applied to some IS data collected from a commercial apple packing line. The maximum possible drop heights derived from the IS data, using the analysis techniques, rarely exceeded 0.1 m and corresponded with packing line transfer points. or serviced..
