Screening for a vanadium-enriched yeast and primary optimization of cultivation condition
From 5 strains of yeasts,Saccharomyces cerevisiae Meyen ex Hansen),which was suitable for V-rich yeasts fermentation was selected.RCEF1023 was selected for optimization cultivation through fermentation test.The result showed that the highest V content of the strain was obtained in the condition of 48 h at 28℃ with 10%((V/V)) inoculum volume,when grown in 50 mL culture fluid containing 40 μg·mL~(-1) V~(+5) in 500 mL shake flasks.Under the optimized cultivation conditions,the biomass(dry weight) of 1 L fermentation reached 6.63 g,the enrichment quantity of vanadium was about 587.6 μg·g~(-1).
