Study on Thermal Fatigue Characteristic of Fe-based Infiltrated Layer on Copper Substrate
Fe-based infiltrated layer was prepared on copper substrate through vacuum infiltration casting technology and thermal fatigue property of the Fe-based infiltrated layer was investigated. The results show that a certain extent oxidation happens on the surface of infiltrated layer when thermal cycling times are less than 20, and no micro-cracks are observed. While micro-cracks appeared at the interface between the infiltrated layer and the substrate and on the surface of the infiltrated layer when the thermal cycling times are beyond 60. The thicker the infiltrated layer, the earlier the time of micro-cracks appearing in different extent.
