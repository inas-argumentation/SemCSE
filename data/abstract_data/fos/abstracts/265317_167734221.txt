A Study on Compensation of Administrative Expropriation
The administrative expropriation,the principles of compensation for administrative expropriation and the system of our current administrative expropriation is discussed. Some suggestions are proposed to further improve our system of administrative expropriation which include the scientific definition of the public interest, the formulation of the impartial standards of compensation and suitable procedures of administrative expropriation, and the improvement of the reasonable mechanisms of compensation after the event.
