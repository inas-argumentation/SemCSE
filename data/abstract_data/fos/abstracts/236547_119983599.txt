Model-space dependence of nuclear observables
The dependence of calculated observables such as binding energies, spectra, static moments, transition rates and spectroscopic factors on the choice of the shell-model space is investigated for somesd-shell nuclei. It is found that not each extension of the model space necessarily leads to an improved description of nuclear properties. In some cases an extension may involve inconsistencies in the corrections resulting from single particle-hole excitations. A successful description may be expected when up to three-particle, three-hole excitations are taken into account.
