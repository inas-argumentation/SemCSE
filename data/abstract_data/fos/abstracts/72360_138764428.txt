Optimal Design of Cast Mouth for Aluminum Roll-casting
The overall structure,internal cavity,feeding flow system and diffluent blocks of the cast mouth were designed and its materials was selected by analyzing and studying the production principle of aluminum continuous roll-casting machine and the structure and properties of typical cast mouth.A practical and efficient cast mouth was obtained by means of AutoCAD2008 to design geometric modeling.The advantages and disadvantages of secondary and three-level diffluent blocks of cast mouth were compared,as well as the feasibility of surface coating on the materials of cast mouth was analyzed.
