Engineering Design of Municipal Solid Waste Sanitary Landfill in Haining of Zhejiang
The main design content and the outline of Yuanhua solid waste sanitary landfill engineering in Haining of Zhejiang is introduced.A series of reasonable and feasible proposals are put forward combining the transportation manner,the anti-leachate in the landfill and the methane discharge in view of the ground character of a mine being out of date.
