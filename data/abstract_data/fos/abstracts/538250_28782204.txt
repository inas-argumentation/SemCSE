Human Rights, and the Destructive Communications and Actions of Differentiated Society
According to Gunther Teubner, the formulation and implementation of human rights in law is a way of realising, through law, law-transcending demands of justice by human beings in danger of being harmed by communications in modern society.1 Modern society, which tends to destroy the integrity of the body and mind of human beings, and thereby at the same time threatens important presuppositions of its own self-reproduction, is described as a set of autonomous communication systems.
