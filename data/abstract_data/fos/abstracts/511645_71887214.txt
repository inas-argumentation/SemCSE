Bone Marrow Transplantation for Beta-Thalassaemia major
The treatment of β-thalassaemia major by the new therapeutic modality of bone marrow transplantation has recently been carried out in Kuwait. A report on the first 2 children is given, illustrating the factors affecting the outcome of marrow transplantation for this disease. One child has remained in excellent health with normal haematological indices for more than 45 months after bone marrow transplantation. The advisability of adopting marrow transplantation in lieu of the conventional therapy for thalassaemia major is discussed.
