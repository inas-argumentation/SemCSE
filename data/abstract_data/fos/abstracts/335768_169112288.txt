Research on Design and Implementation of Accounting Computerization System in Small and Medium-sized Enterprise
The accounting informationization platform can provide relevant, accurate, reliable and timely information for users in the information age. Based on the actual situation of small and medium-sized enterprises in China, this paper analyzes and discusses the feasibility of constructing a big data platform, and applies for the cloud computing technology and B/S structure model to construct an accounting information platform with the functions of comprehensive financial monitoring, comprehensive financial decision and comprehensive financial forecasting, and then introduces specific cases to verify the superiority of accounting data analysis platform in the financial application.
