STUDY ON CONSOLIDATION CALCULATION OF LOW-GRADE CONCRETE-CORED SAND-GRAVEL PILE COMPOSITE FOUNDATION
A low-grade concrete-cored sand-gravel pile composite foundation was a new soft ground improvement method.A consolidation calculation model considering impeded boundary conditions was presented.While considering the hole pressure continuous problem of an overlying soil layer and a reinforce field,according to consolidation theory of double-layered soft clay composite ground,the consolidation problem of a low-grade concrete-cored sand-gravel pile composite foundation is translated into a consolidation problem of an equivalent double-layered foundation,and corresponding analytical solutions are solved.The comparison between the calculated results and the filed measured data showed that the calculated excess pore water pressure,the consolidation degree and the settlement-time relationship were consistent with those of filed data respectively.
