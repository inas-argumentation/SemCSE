Establishment and Application for Molecular Detection of Chilli ringspot Virus
Chilli ringspot virus(ChiRSV)is a novel virus discovered in 2007 in chili pepper.ChiRSV was first detected on Capsicum chinense Jacp.Cv.Yellow Lantern in Hainan province in 2009 by the authors.The RT-PCR detection method was established in this research,which including specific primers designing,annealing temperature testing.This method showed good amplification reaction,and the sensitivity was as high as 12.5 pg virus RNA.RT-PCR detection of field samples demonstrated the sensitivity and specificity of the method in this paper,indicating the possibility of the spread of ChiRSV in Hainan Province.
