On Syntagmatic and Paradigmatic Relations and English Writing
Syntagmatic and paradigmatic relations between words are seldom covered in the present English writing teaching in China; however, majority of the mistakes the Chinese students have made in English writing are related to collocation and choice of words. In view of the above- mentioned facts, this paper discusses syntagmatic relation in terms of conceptual meaning collocation, affective meaning collocation and stylistic meaning collocation, as well as paradigmatic relation in terms of synonym and hyponymy, and suggests that the teachers of English writing attach importance to the teaching of syntagmatic and paradigmatic relations to heighten the students' writing ability.
