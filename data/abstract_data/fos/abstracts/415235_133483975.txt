The Eco-Tourism Environment Quality Evaluation System Based on Fuzzy Theory
Eco-tourism is a new direction of the modern tourism development, while maintaining a good ecological tourism environment is the key to maintain development of eco -tourism. From the complexity of the interaction between the impact of the tourism environment quality evaluation factors and diversity of different sensory tourists, researches on building an eco -tourism environment quality evaluation system based on fuzzy comprehensive evaluation model, and realizes the evaluation process through computer program, takes Tianyan Shennongjia primitive ecology tourist area as an example, carries on the quality synthetic evaluation to its eco-tourism environment quality.
