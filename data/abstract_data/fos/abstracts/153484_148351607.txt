From certainty to uncertainty——On the modern transformation of traditional knowledge instruction
Knowledge indicates the traits of both certainty and uncertainty.Oriented merely towards certain knowledge,traditional knowledge instruction demonstrates various limitations in standpoints of instruction and instruction modes;in order to transcend traditional knowledge instruction,modern knowledge instruction should accept the uncertainty of knowledge,embody the reality of knowledge increasing and life growing,and realize the transformation from the inheriting instruction to developmental instruction,from the maintenance study to innovational study.
