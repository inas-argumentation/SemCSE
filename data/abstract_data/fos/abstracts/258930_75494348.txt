Problems existing in physical education teaching materials and methods adopted in middle schools in China and countermeasures therein
The study of physical education teaching materials and methods in China is divided into 3 stages,namely,study beginning stage(before 1982),a study stage during which physical education teaching methods were focuses on(1982-1998),study focusing on physical education teaching materials and methods(after 1998).There are many problems with the contents of physical education teaching materials and methods adopted in middle schools,such as to many repeated contents of scholastic physical education science,repeated contents of teaching materials and methods for individual sports events,and basic conceptions and design ideas which are not in conformity with the physical education(and health) curriculum standard.The author considered it necessary to reexamine the subject orientation and content system of physical education teaching materials and methods adopted in middle schools.
