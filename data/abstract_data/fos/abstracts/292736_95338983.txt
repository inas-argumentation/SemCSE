A structural model for fluoride ionic transport in Ba1−xHoxF2+x solid solutions (x⩾0.1)
A combination of experimental techniques, viz. electrical conductivity and single crystal X-ray structure analysis has been employed to study the ionic transport in the fluorite-type solid solutions Ba1−xHoxF2+x. The new heterovalent substitution scheme based on the structure and ionic conductivity data is also given. The results obtained are explained in terms of “defect regions” model involving Ho6F36−37 clusters.
