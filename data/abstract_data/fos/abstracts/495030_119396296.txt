Superconductivity with angular dependent coupling: stripes, Coulomb repulsion and enhanced Tc
We have analysed a simple analytically solvable model with an angular dependent coupling λ ( ϕ ) represented by a square-well form. We have found that the introduction of the Coulomb repulsion λ C , increasing the “contrast” | λ |+| λ C |, or the depth of the angular modulation of λ ( ϕ ), leads to a remarkable enhancement of the superconducting critical temperature T c . This effect can be optimized by combining attractive ( λ λ >0) interactions appearing, for example, along stripes and perpendicular to them in high- T c superconductors.
