Cenozoic volcanism of the Candelaria region, Nevada
Tertiary rocks of the Candelaria-Mina region of the western Great Basin (Fig. 1) occur in five distinct sequences, each separated by a known or probable regional unconformity. One sequence, the upper Oligocene tuff of the Candelaria Hills, is of particular interest because of its remarkable lateral variations in constitution and thickness in the Candelaria Hills. Such variations point clearly to local differential subsidence during deposition about 25 to 22 m.y. B.P. The Oligocene-Miocene boundary is assumed to be 22.5 m.y. and the Miocene-Pliocene boundary, 5.1 m.y. (Berggren, 1972).
