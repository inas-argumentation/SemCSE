Superionic conductors Li3M2(PO4)3 (M==Fe, Sc, Cr): Synthesis, structure and electrophysical properties
Abstract Superionic conductors Li 3 M 2 (PO 4 ) 3 have been prepared by solid-phase and hydrothermal synthesis, from flux and melt. Single crystals were used in precision structural and electrophysical measurements. The specific features of atomic structure of α-, β-, and γ-phases of Li 3 M 2 (PO 4 ) 3 have been determined. The ionic and electronic conductivities of ceramics and single crystal Li 3 Sc 2 (PO 4 ) 3 have been investigated. The influence of isomorphic displacements on phase transition temperature and the values of conductivity have been established.
