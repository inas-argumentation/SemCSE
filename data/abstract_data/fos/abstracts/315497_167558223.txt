Discuss the System Barriers and Breach Strategy about Circular Economy
Circular economy is called substance closure economy simply and is different from traditional crude economy comparatively speaking.Circular economy is a kind of ecology economy with the basic character of resource thrift and circular utilization.Developing circular economy will meet some barriers such as marketing mechanism barrier,property right system barrier and policy supporting barrier under marketing economy.The system innovation and breach strategy must be needed in order to overcome the system barriers of circular economy development.
