Isometric extensions, 2-cocycles and ergodicity of skew products
We establish existence and uniqueness of a canonical form for isometric extensions of an ergodic non-singular transformation T . This is applied to describe the structure of commutors of the isometric extensions. Moreover, for a compact group G, we construct a G-valued T -cocycle α which generates the ergodic skew product extension Tα and admits a prescribed subgroup in the centralizer of Tα.
