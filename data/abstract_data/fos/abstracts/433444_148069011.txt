Three Missions of the Education Evolutions for PhD Degree Level in Canada:Interconnectedness,Policies,Quality
As the most strikingly distinguished country which excelled in the educational modes of PhD degree level,Canada,as a leading country in global educational process,had made the series progress in the educational revolutions of PhD degree level.From the results of serial education reform,there were such multiple cores attribute to Canada's long-term outstanding education of PhD level,as the quality assurance,the policies and the interconnectedness.It would benefit the transformation of education mode of PhD degree level in China.
