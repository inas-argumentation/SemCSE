Design and Realization of Power Marketing Data Warehouse
Lots of historic data have accumulated after many years' information development of electric power industry. How to utilize these data effectively is an important problem to study and solve for continued information development in the near future. Describes system design and realization process integrally from the perspectives of system infrastructure design, system function design and system realization process, based on electric power marketing data warehouse system of Zhongshan bureau power supply burean.
