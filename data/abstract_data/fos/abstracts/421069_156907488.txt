On Rights and Obligations of the Contract Parties When the Discrepant Documents Dishonored
At present, a large proportion of international settlements is completed by letters of credit for their characteristics. But, because of the late development of LC, there are some distortions about them theoretically and practically in our country. Through analysis of a case of Chinese Supreme Court in 1996 and theories as well as cases of Common Law on LC, this article elaborates on the relationship between the contract parties after the issuing bank dishonored the discrepant documents that the seller presented in international trade.
