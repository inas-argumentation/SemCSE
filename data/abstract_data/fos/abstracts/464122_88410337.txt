The Cloning and Expression Analysis of Sucrose Transporter Gene ShSUT4 in Sugarcane
A new sucrose transporter gene was isolated from sugarcane,named ShSUT4,GenBank accession numbers GQ485583.1.Sequence analysis showed it encodes 501 amino acids,including conserved domain of GPH superfamily and 12 transmembrane α-helices.Expression analysis indicated that ShSUT4 expressed in root,stem and leaf of sugarcane.Moreover,the expression amount of ShSUT4 in mature stems of 9 sugarcane cultivars was different,which was positively relative to sugar content in the detecting internodes of 8 sugarcane cultivars.It is speculated that ShSUT4 might involve in the sucrose regulation in sugarcane.
