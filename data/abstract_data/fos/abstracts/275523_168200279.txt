Analysis of Issuing Convertible Bonds of Chinese Listed Companies——An Comparative Study with Issuing Common Stock
This paper wants to explain the convertible bonds issuing motive of Chinese listed companies by analyzing the financial indexes of listed companies,which have announced issuing convertible bond and SEO between 2001 and 2006.The results of the single factor and Logistic regression analysis suggest that:(1) the preferences for choosing convertible bond was related to assets-liabilities ratio and MV/BV strongly and negatively;(2) the preferences for choosing convertible bond was related to the size of company,growth opportunity and volatility of cash flow strongly and positively;(3) there was insignificant relation to age of company,physical assets ratio and profitability of company.
