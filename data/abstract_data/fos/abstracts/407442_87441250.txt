Construction of Infectious Chimeric cDNA Clone of Sindbis Virus by Triple Fusion PCR
Using triple fusion PCR method,i.e.long PCR-based fusion of three DNA fragments simultaneously,we assembled DNA fragment E_3E_26KE_1′3′UTR which included E3,E2,6K,3′UTR of XJ-160 virus and E1 of YN87448 virus.Ligating the fusion fragment to the backbone of infectious full-length cDNA clone of XJ-160 virus through Xba I and Xho I enzyme sites,we constructed the chimeric viral cDNA clone which exchanged the E1 glycoprotein of XJ-160 virus and YN87448 virus successfully,and named it pBR-XJ160Y E1.After linearizing pBR-XJ160Y E1 and in vitro transcripting,RNA transcript was transfected into BHK-21 cell via liposomes.Cytopathic effect(CPE) was commonly observed 36h after transfection.The protein expression of chimeric virus was identified by indirect immunofluorescence analysis.Isolated RNA of the chimeric virus in the culture media after passaged five times in BHK-21 cell proved that the virus came from pBR-XJ160YE1 by RT-PCR and DNA sequencing.The infectious chimeric viral cDNA clone can act as a molecular biology tool for studying the relative function of E1 glycoprotein of Sindbis virus and the molecular bases of one way serology reaction between XJ-160 virus and YN87448 virus.
