An Integrated High Efficient Method of Ascertaining the Format of an Loaded Optical Disc
Optical driver will integrate all kinds of module for dealing with CD,DVD,HD and BD disc.With the compatibility improving and the optical disc type having gone up,it is difficult to ascertain all kinds of Optical Disc.This paper proposes a high efficient method of ascertaining the format of a loaded optical disc,and provides the processing of implement.Testing results show that this method can make optical driver discriminate the optical disc type efficiently and set OPU power,channel parameters and servo parameters,thereby booting and reading the data on a loaded optical disc rapidly.
