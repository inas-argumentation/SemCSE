"Diversified","disordered" and "inundated" phenomena in the understanding of sports tourism and analysis of their causes
The author analyzed a number of phenomena existing currently in the understanding of sports tourism,and revealed the following findings: in China,the orientation of sports tourism is too "diversified";the classification of sport tourism is too "disordered";the attribution of sports tourism events is too "inundated".Main causes for the occurrence of the said phenomena are obviously sentimental understanding and lacking in the spirit of exploration.
