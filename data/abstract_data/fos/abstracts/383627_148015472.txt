The university character between the elite education and the mass education
Educating the man to be perfect is the traditional core of university idea from the classical time. When the elite higher education is changing to the mass higher education, it is necessary for the university to adapt the vocational education for the individual. This leads to the surrender of the university character rather than the remain of the educational tradition. The building of our universities’ character is not only meeting the challenge of the massification, but also the building of our humanity tradition themselves. Now we should emphasize the location of the university. Meanwhile, we should make sure of the targets for the different university style and their different direction for the teacher groups.
