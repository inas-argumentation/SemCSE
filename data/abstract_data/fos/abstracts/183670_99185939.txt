INVERSE ELECTRON DEMAND DIELS–ALDER REACTIONS WITH AMINOMETHYL DERIVATIVES OF 3-ARYLHYDROXYCOUMARINS
The reactivity of Mannich bases derived from 6- and 7-hydroxycoumarins was studied in inverse electron demand Diels–Alder reactions with enamines. The reactions were shown to proceed by a cycloaddition mechanism followed by transformation of hemiaminals and resulted in the formation of heterocyclic pyrano[2,3- a ]xanthene, pyrano[2,3- b ]xanthene, and pyrano[3,2- b ]xanthene. Authors: Galyna P. Mrug, Kostyantyn M. Kondratyuk, Svitlana P. Bondarenko, Mykhaylo S. Frasinyuk*
