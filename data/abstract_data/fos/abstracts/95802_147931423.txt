A Brief Discussion of Economy Thought And Its Contemporary Enlightenment of Wang Fu's “On Hermit”
Wang Fu's discussion about economy is an important part of his thought in “On Hermit”, this article has a briefl discussion about his thought of the enriching people and how to enrich people, and combinates the thought with his thought of the people as foundation. After that, this article debates on the law of the development of the agriculture、industry and business. In the end, this article briefly discusses about the contemporary enlightenment of its thought.
