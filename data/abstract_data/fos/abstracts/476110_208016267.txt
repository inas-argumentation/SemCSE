Prof. Boyle Assumes Role of Linacre Book Review Editor
A professor of philosophy at St. Michael's College, University of Toronto, Joseph Boyle, Ph.D., is the new book review editor for Linacre Quarterly. He received his doctorate in philosophy from Georgetown University, with specialization in logic and metaphysics under Germain Grisez, and did post-doctoral work on intention and double effect at Brown University. Professor Boyle has taught at St. Fidelis College, Herman, P A; Aquinas College, Grand Rapids, MI; The College of St. Thomas, St. Paul, MN; the Center of Thomistic Studies, The University of St. Thomas, Houston, TX in addition to St. Michael's. He was elected vice-president / president-elect of the American Catholic Philosophical Association in 1987 and is on the board of editorial consultants of The New Scholasticism and the board of editors of The American Journal of Jurisprudence. He has co-authored four books, and has written numerous articles and reviews, mostly on ethical topics, for various professional journals. He gave recent presentations at the International Congress of Moral Theology in Rome, in April, 1986, and at a conference on Ownership and Distribution of the World's Natural Resources at the California Institute of Technology in October, 1987, in addition to participating in many workshops, seminars and symposiums.
