Yet during most of Faulkner's Questioning Narratives it seems as though Minter has decided not to read any Faulkner other than what he knows he likes and as though the writing of his Cultural History of the American Novel, the chapter headings of which also promise "brief appearances" by various figures, has taught him to substitute notes and encounters for organized, sustained analysis. His admiration of Faulkner's work is obvious and genuine, but this book doesn't adequately explain its sources.
