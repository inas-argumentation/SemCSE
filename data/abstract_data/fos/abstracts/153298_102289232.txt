Effects of metal ion on physicochemical properties of mung bean starch by sedimentation
The quality of starch is affected by water. Effects of Ca2+, Mg2+ and Al3+ on mung bean starch. During the preparation of mung bean starch by traditional method, different metal ion solutions were added. Results indicated: metal ion solutions reduced ash content and chroma and swelling power and peak viscosity of starch. CaCl2 and AlCl3 solutions-isolated starches had significantly(p0.05) higher solubition ability, had lower break-down viscosity and higher short time retrogradation properties.
