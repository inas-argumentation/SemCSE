Taking Advantage of the Resources from Community Colleges,Running the Demonstrative Aged Schools
Developing the education for the aged is a requirement for improving the lifelong education and building a study-oriented society. Owing to the exuberant need in our country, the education for the aged has a vast room for development. Running the school for the aged, Nankai Community College has brought its social functions into full play, realized the goal of sharing its resources, and facilitated its own development. From now on, with the development in terms of both intension and extension, it will run the demonstrative school for the aged more successfully.
