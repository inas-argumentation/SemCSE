Thoughts about the humanistic dilemma in sports technicalization
The author discussed the fact that kinetic damage is escalated by the development of sports technicalization,expatiated on the reality that sports technicalization neglects humanistic consideration,analyzed the root causes for the humanistic dilemma in sports technicalization,pointed out that the three major reasons for causing the dilemma are the utilitarianism in sports development,overriding theoretical rules preset by sport,and technical destruction and reconstruction of sport,which result in the denial of ethical values of sport.Basing on such a study,the author put forward some basic ideas to clear the humanistic dilemma in sports technicalization,such as rebuilding the conception of ethical values of sport and the behavior code for realizing such values,and reestablishing the system for evaluating the ethical values of sport.
