Exact quantum states of the Holstein-Tavis-Cummings model
As explored recently in many experimental and theoretical works, matter-light coupling can affect molecular structure, leading to cavity catalyzed chemistry. As a cavity couples simultaneously to $N$ molecules, understanding this requires understanding collective behavior. We show how the exact eigenstates of the $N$-molecule problem can be found in polynomial time. Using such exact solutions, we construct a simple ansatz which, in the one excitation sector, accurately reproduces both the minimum energy and the full quantum state of the $N$-molecule problem.
