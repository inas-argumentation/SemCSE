Study on preparing of Conjugated linoleic acid from sunflower oil
Linoleic acid content of sunflower oil is high. It is good material for Conjugated linoleic acid. Conjugated linoleic acid has good physiological function. The reactive mechanism of conjugation catalyzed by alkali and the affecting factors such as time and amount of alkali were discussed. The optimal conditions were obtained by orthogenesis test, which provided a conjugation yields above 90%.
