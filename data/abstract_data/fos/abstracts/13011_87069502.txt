Antimicrobial activities of Actinidia arguta polysaccharides
Make use of polysaccharides in Actinidia arguta in own laboratory,for the research experiment of its antimicrobial activities.The results showed that Actinidia arguta polysaccharides could inhibit the growth of all of the following microorganisms:Bacillus subtilis,Escherichia coli,Staphylosocus aureus,Rhizopus,Aspergillus niger,Aspergillus oryzae,Penicillium sp.and Saccharomyces cerevisiae,and the effect is significant,the inhibiting effect enhances along with the increase of concentration of polysaccharides,the corresponding minimal inhibitory concentrations varied from 10 mg/mL to 25 mg/mL.However,no inhibitory effect on G.tropicalis was observed.Temperature and pH value also have certain effect on it,the inhibiting effect enhances along with the temperature up,partial acidic pH value conducive to enhance the inhibiting effect,in range of pH value 4～5,the inhibiting effect is better.
