Construction of Interactive Laboratory Based on the Simulation Software
In the learning of electrical and electronics engineering professional,experiment teaching is very important.Aiming at the defects of the traditional experimental teaching,puts forward the interactive laboratory based on simulation software platform,which is based on simulation software as the main tool,with the traditional hardware experiment,the experiment content is more rich,flexible,and reasonable use of laboratory resources and teachers' resources,saving the cost of experimental teaching,and can achieve very good experimental effect.
