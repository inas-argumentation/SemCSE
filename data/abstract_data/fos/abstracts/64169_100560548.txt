Research progress on mass-transfer model calculation of structured packing
The mass-transfer calculation methods and models for structured packing are summarized.The theoretical foundation and applicability conditions of these models are analyzed and compared.Some modification and extension of traditional mass-transfer models for structured packing are concluded,and the empirical correlation formulas for effective specific surface area that has effect on mass-transfer performance are discussed.The exploration and improving direction for research of mass-transfer performance of structured packing is pointed out.
