Residues of organic chlorine pesticide and their distribution characteristics in arable land soil of Chaohu Lake region
By grid method,soil samples were taken from the arable land of Chaohu Lake region during June and July in 2010.The distribution characteristics and composition of residues of organic chlorine pesticide were studied by gas chromatography.The results showed that the total OCPs concentration value was 0.11-32.24 ng?g-1,and the average concentration value was 3.71 ng?g-1.The detection ratios of HCHs and DDTs from samples were 100%.In which,HCHs residues ranged from 0.24 to 4.58 ng?g-1 with the average concentration of 1.39 ng?g-1,and DDTs residues ranged from 0.36 to 32.24 ng?g-1 with the average concentration of 7.87 ng?g-1.The HCHs residues were mainly composed of β-HCH,in which the order of all the constituents was β-HCH α-HCHδ-HCHγ-HCH.The DDTs residues were mainly composed of DDE and DDD,in which the percentage of DDE was over 50% as the main composition of DDTs residues.The analysis of the pollution resource showed that HCHs residue α/γ value ranged from 0.95 to 3.12,The(DDD+DDE) /DDTs ratio was greater than 0.5 and the DDE/DDD ratio was greater than 1.In contrast with the related data,residue of organic chlorine pesticide in arable land soil of Chaohu Lake area was relatively at a low level.
