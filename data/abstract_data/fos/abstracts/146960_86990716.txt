Study on divergence of vacA gene amongHelicobacter pylori
Objective In order to find the divergence of vacA (vacuolating cytotoxin gene A) among H. pylori strains and explore the relationship between divergence of vacA and of VacA (Vacuolating cytotoxin A). Methods vacA gene of 17 H. pylori strains were amplified by PCR using 4 primers which were designed in our laboratory and matching each other. Results Four kinds of amplifications including different regions of vacA gene were obtained for every H. pylori strains. But the length of amplifications of 17 strains for each pair of primer were variable and the region corresponding to VacA was not found through comparing the length of amplification among tox + H. pylori and tox - H. pylori . Conclusions There is very significant divergence of vacA gene among H. pylori strains and the region of vacA gene which has relationship with VacA was to be determined. It is assumed that vacuolating activity is positively related to quantity of VacA secreted.
