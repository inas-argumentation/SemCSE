H_2S Removed by γ-FeOOH Added by Alkaline Additives
The lepidocrocite(γ-FeOOH)for removing H2S was prepared by oxidation method under mild conditions,and characterized by XRD,FTIR,TEM,SEM and TG/DSC.The experimental results showed that the pure γ-FeOOH samples synthesized in this method exhibited higher activity for removing H2S.By adding alkaline additives such as KF,K3PO4 and NaOH respectively,the breakthrough capacity of γ-FeOOH was enhanced under the same activity less than 0.1×10-6.Compared with γ-FeOOH/K3PO4 and γ-FeOOH/NaOH,the γ-FeOOH/KF exhibited the highest breakthrough capacity of 330 mg/g,for removing H2S.
