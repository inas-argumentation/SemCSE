An Analysis of the Influencing Factors on Farmers' Need for Specialized Cooperatives: Based on Questionnaire Survey of Grape Fruit Farmers in Hu County of Shanxi
Based on the survey data of 180 households of fruit farmers in Hu County,Shanxi Province,a typical area in China to produce grapes,with SPSS soft and Logistic Model,the willingness of fruit farmers to join in specialized cooperative economic organization was investigated.The results show that the four variables significantly influenced the willingness of the fruit farmers,namely,education level,grape planting scale,difficult degree of receiving capital,degree of grape price change.Other eight factors did not show significant impact,such as sexes of householder,age,status of being village cadres or not,labor number in one family,planting years,the ratio of the income of the grape to the household total income,range of grape selling,and degree of difficult grape selling.Some advices are put forward accordingly,including attention to the farmer's cultural education and technical training,appropriately expanding grape planting scale,and providing grape production capital supporting and market information service.
