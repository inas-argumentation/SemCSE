The refoundation of the symmetric equilibrium in Schumpeterian growth models
We provide a refoundation of the symmetric growth equilibrium characterizing the research sector of vertical R&D-driven growth models. We argue that the usual assumptions made in this class of models leave the agents indfferent as to where targeting research: hence, the problem of the allocation of R&D investment across sectors is indeterminate.
