Mercury and chlorinated hydrocarbons in plankton of Lake Päijänne, Finland
Plankton was sampled for residue analysis as different size fractions in the years 1972–1974 from four sub-areas of Lake Paijanne, Finland. Of the residues studied PCB was the greatest contaminant, the dry weight content being, on average, 290 μg/kg. The corresponding content of total mercury was 178 and of DDE + DDT (= Σ DDT) 91 μ g/kg. Annual and regional differences were generally small. The Σ DDT content of the greatest size fraction (350 μm) was, on average, higher than in fractions 100, 50 and 25 μm. Correlation between the contents of PCB and Σ DDT was highly significant. The mercury contents were generally of the same order as marine values but smaller than those in polluted regions elsewhere. The contents of PCB and Σ DDT were generally lower in Lake Paijanne than in marine plankton.
