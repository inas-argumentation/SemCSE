Cross-sensitivity Analysis of Extended Octagonal Ring Transducer
Two extended octagonal ring transducers were designed and fabricated for measuring vertical and horizontal loads on the wheels of a model tractor. The transducers were calibrated extensively and their cross-sensitivities were analyzed to accurately determine the loads on the wheels. The vertical sensitivity was affected by varying horizontal load directions. The horizontal cross-sensitivity was larger than the vertical cross-sensitivity. A statistical model was developed to reduce the measurement error caused by these cross-sensitivities.
