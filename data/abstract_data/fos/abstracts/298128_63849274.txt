Research on User Profile Based on Time Element Interest Rate Quantification Method and VSM Extension
In order to make information searching much more convenient,an automatic and intelligent document recommendation method based on user profile which is established from the contents of documents that the user reads and the behavior performed while reading them is being used widely.In this paper,the establishment of user profile is introduced in detail based on the others′ research and a reasonable quantification method of interest rate based on time element is brought out.Also,the VSM(Vector Space Model) text expression method is extended accordingly and the concept of theme interest rate is raised.Experiments show that the user profile established in the above way can distinguish and represent the user′s interest well and exhibits a high rate of accuracy.
