Influence of Planting of Sugarcane Seeds Carrying Smut Fungus on Cane Yield and Sugar Content
Sugarcane smut is one of the most serious diseases in sugarcane production. It will seriously affect the cane yield. In the study, sugarcane seedcanes carrying smut pathogen were planted while healthy seedcanes were used as the control in spring and summer in 2012. Smut disease, effective stalks, height, diameter and brix were checked. It was revealed seedcanes carrying smut pathogen would lead to severe disease, and the seedcanes carrying smut pathogen should be planted in spring and winter to delay the occurance the disease and further alleviate loss.
