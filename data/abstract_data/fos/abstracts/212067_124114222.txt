Interference Power Invariant Anti-interference Algorithm for Smart Antenna Systems
In this paper,a blind interference blocking algorithm for smart antenna systems is proposed.The algorithm estimates the interference-blocking weight vector from the covariance of received signals.Since the estimation of the covariance doesn't require time reference,the proposed algorithm is applicable to the environments where interference power is too high to establish reliable time synchronization.The performance of the algorithm is also shown to be stable under various interference and noise power.This property is not shared by most other anti-interference methods,and it has been verified by the simulations.
