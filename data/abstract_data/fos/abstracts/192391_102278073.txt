Study on the microwave synthesis of seselin
Used umbelliferone and 3-chloro-3-methyl-1-butyne as feed stock,the seselin is synthesized by method of microwave irradiation in two steps.In each step,a different microwave condition is given.The experiment results show that MW-synthesis can not only greatly reduce the reaction time,but also reduce the consume of the expensive material campared with traditional conditions,it's a better way to synthesize the Seselin.
