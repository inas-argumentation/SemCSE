Photographs were purchased with the help of a grant from the Research Council, University of Massachusetts, Amherst. This article was accepted for publication in May, I988. A final draft of the text was sent to the editor in September, 1989. Since that time two further interpretations of the Amolfini painting have come to my attention. One by Linda Seidel, " 'Jan van Eyck's Arnolfini Portrait': Business as Usual?", appeared in Critical Inquiry I6 (I989): 55-86; to my mind, it repeats the anachronistic mistake of several authors by attributing specific documentary significance to the image. At the Historians of Netherlandish Art conference, Cleveland, 29 October, I989, Margaret D. Carroll gave a talk entitled "Speculum Mercatorum: Jan van Eyck's Marriage of Arnolfini"; I have great sympathy with Carroll's reading of the multi-faceted nature of the image's meaning.
