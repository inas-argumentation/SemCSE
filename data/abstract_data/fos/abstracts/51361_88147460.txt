Analysis of nutritive components of pleurotus tuber-regium(Fr.) sing
According to the national standard analytic methods,the contents of several nutritive components of dry pleurotus tuber-regium(Fr.) sing were assayed.The results showed that the content of protein was 15.60% in the edible fungus,fat was 2.50%,fibre was 6.30%,thick polysaccharides was 10.8%,and total candy was 34.60%.The content of K,Ca,Fe,Zn and Se in minerals elements were relatively high,and were respectively 22.4g/kg,768mg/kg,157mg/kg,105mg/kg,0.05mg/kg.The content of total amino acid was 14.9%.Eight kinds of essential amino acid comprised over 46.10% of all the amino acids,and content of LLE,LYS and LEU were relatively higher.
