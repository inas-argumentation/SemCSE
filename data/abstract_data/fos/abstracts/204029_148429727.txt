Causal analysis of the professionalization of American university faculties and its inspiration to China
During the process of the professionalization of university faculties in America,four factors have advanced faculties' development,namely,high standard for teachers' entrance system,effective guarantee for professional organization of university faculties,the implementation of peer review,and the open,mobile and competitive academic labor market.The professionalization of faculties in universities of China can learn from that: developing high sense of academic community,establishing powerful professional organization,improving academy-oriented evaluation system and making academic labor market more mobile and attractive internationally.
