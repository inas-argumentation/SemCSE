Dirt and Desire: Reconstructing Southern Women's Writing, 1930-1990 (review)
Publishing the Family has many virtues, and not the least among them is that not only is it a great book, it's a great read. Howard's prose is brisk and straightforward, careful of complex ideas yet accessible to a range of readers. Publishing the Family is a book to be reckoned with. It should prove an invaluable model and resource for scholars of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, for historians and literary critics, and for scholars of print culture more generally.
