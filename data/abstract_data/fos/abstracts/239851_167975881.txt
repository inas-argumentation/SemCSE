A Study on Wholesale and Retail Pricing Model in China's Electricity Industry
The reform of electricity price is the center and the difficulty in the electricity market reform,particularly in the reform of wholesale and retail pricing.First this article proposes four electricity industry patterns which are more and more market-oriented in turn.And then it points out that the third pattern "wholesale-competition" is most suitable for our country in the background of the present pricing system.Finally,it expounds the wholesale and the retail pricing model of this pattern,as well as the workable method of charge calculation with specific example.
