The Evaluation System and the Model of Controlling of Degree of Difficulty of the Test Paper
This paper has given a kind of assessment index system of the difficulty of the examination question and the degree of difficulty of test paper,Have discussed the control method of examination question and the degree of difficulty of test paper.And a kind of control model and realization that has given the difficulty of test paper way of thinking.As well as this difficulty control technology in the intelligent type automatic generation of test paper in realize.Have offered theoretical basis for the intelligent control of the difficulty of test paper in the type automatic generation system of test paper.
