The paleogeography of arctic shelf and coastal zone of eurasia at the time of the last glaciation (18,000 yr B.P.)
Abstract Paleogeographic maps showing reconstructed environments of the Eurasian Arctic shelf and coastal zone during the last glacial maximum (18,000 yr B.P.) were compiled within the project IGCP-200 “Sea-level correlations and applications”. The reconstruction shows conspicuous difference between eastern and western sectors of the Arctic shelf of Eurasia. Large glaciers existed in the west while the eastern Arctic shelf was an emerged ice-free lowland dominated by tundra landscapes and built of unconsolidated sediments with a variety of genesis.
