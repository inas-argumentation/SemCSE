Effect of Process Parameters on Electromagnetic Separation of Inclusi ons From Al-alloy
The experiments of electromagnetic separation of inclusions from molten aluminum alloy were conducted by a separator. The relationship between t he coil current and the magnetic flux density has been established. The experime ntal results show that,the higher the current is,the higher the separation effic iency is;with the increasing of processing time, more efficient results of separ ation are obtained.
