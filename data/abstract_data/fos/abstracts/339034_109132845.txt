Evaluation of interaction of wood framing and metal-cladding in roof diaphragms
A scaled-down (test panel) and full-scale post-frame building were extensively instrumented to determine the 
interaction between the wood roof framing system and the metal-cladding membrane. The behavior of the system was 
analyzed more from a global rather than localized viewpoint. The measurements provided these insights: (a) contributions 
of interior purlins in resisting diaphragm forces, (b) interaction between the two roof halves, (c) interaction between the 
roof purlins and the metal cladding, and (d) load sharing between frames including end-wall frames.
