The Methods Based on Career Management for Retaining Talents of Small and Medium-sized Enterprises
The small and medium-sized enterprises have received much attention as they have become the main strength which has supported our national economy.They have been honored as the hiding champion of Chinese Economic Developmeent.But the lose of talents has become the manager's most headache topic.To solve this problem is especially important to the core competitive competecy of enterprises based on human capital and sustainable development.The essay puts forward the methods for retaining talents of small and medium-sized enterprises,which based on Career Management,namely retains talents with career management method,preserving enterprise's core competitive competency,provides the firm with the safeguard for the development of mid-and small-scale enterprises.
