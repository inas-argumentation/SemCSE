Exploration on campus-industrialpark linkage mechanism in higher vocational education
The construction of campus-industrialpark linkage mechanism,based on the interaction between higher vocational education and regional economy,would affect the sustainable development of higher vocational education.After the period of superficial passive acclimation,the construction of campus-industrialpark linkage mechanism needs to be dominated by active adaptation,and oriented by initiative,which can realize the long-acting linkage between campus-industrial park.
