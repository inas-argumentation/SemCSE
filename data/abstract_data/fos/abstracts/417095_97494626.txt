Novel Alkali Metal Ionic Liquids: N-Ethylimidazolium Alkali Metal Sulfates
Novel ionic liquids containing alkali metal ions as component were prepared by the neutralization of N-ethylimidazole with various kinds of hydrogensulfates (MHSO4; M=Li, Na, or K). All salts were obtained as transparent viscous liquids at room temperature. These ionic liquids showed ionic conductivity higher than 10−4 S cm−1 at room temperature, reflecting low glass transition temperature of below −60 °C. This is the first report on alkali metal salts which are inherently liquid state at room temperature.
