“Histopathological findings post Dextranomer/Hyaluronic acid implants in Children with Vesicouretral Reflux”
Abstract Purpose With Deflux being widely used as the only approved implant for the treatment of vesicouretral reflux in the United States, and being comonly used in Europe as an alternative to other implant materials .the literature is still (lacking or deficent) in documenting histopathological changes in humans after implants. Material and Methods A retrospective review of patients undergoing uretroneocystostomy after failed dextranomer/hyaluronic acid (DEFLUX) implants to capture demographic and clinical data of those patients. In addition, histopathological specimen review with further testing of excised ureteral segments looking at inflammatory changes of the surrounding tissue was conducted. Histopatholoical changes were correlated with the clinical findings looking at volume, duration of implant and degree of reflux. Results A total of 12 patients, (17 ureters) were eligible for the study. Mean age at implants 53 months, mean age at uretoneocystostomy was 60 months. Mean & Median time from implant injection to excision were 10 & 7 months respectively. All ureters showed giant cell reaction with variable severity, all were encapsulated, all showed the presence of mast cells range (6-26 by hpf) and eosinophils range (1-30 by hpf). Atypia was not present in any specimen, marked to moderate fibrosis was present in all ureters. there was no coreraltion between the histopathological and clinical findings in regard to volume and degree of reflux.no signs of nuclear atypia or high turn over was noted. Conclusions Dextranomer Hyalornic acid is stable and safe after 5 to 39 months of followup of intra/suburetral injection.no signs of nuclear atypia or high nuclear turn over was noted.There is no corelation between volume of implant used and degree of inflammation.
