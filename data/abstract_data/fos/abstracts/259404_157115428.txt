The major shortcomings of the present volume are a lack of overall integration around a well-defined theme as well as the relative absence of literature citation and review which detracts from the volume's potential value as a comprehensive statement of the bearing of current scientific knowledge about aging on the formation of public policy. The reader gets his money's worth of original ideas, however, while at the same time receiving an overview of the frames of reference employed by the various social sciences in their approaches to the phenomena associated with aging.
