Disposition characters and association of tree species in classical gardens in Shouxihu lake of Yangzhou.
The Shouxihu lake classical gardens in Yangzhou city were selected as study cases in the paper,plant communities were classified into 3 site categories: plant communities of the small yard with constriction,marsh plant communities along the main circuit of lake surface and terrestrial plant communities.The sampling method of Vegetation Ecology was employed in the paper to investigate species composition and tree health index,and species association was accounted as well.The results showed that there were 67 woody species reported in samples,and the health situation for total trees could be evaluated as good.Sixteen species-pairs showed a significant positive correlation such as Salix matsudana-Prunus persica,Zelkova schneideriana-Sapium sebiferum,Ginkgo biloba-Sapium sebiferum,Pinus massoniana-Zelkova schneideriana,Acer palmatum-Acer palmatum Thunb,Viburnum macrocepharum-Forsythia viridissima,Osmathus frograns-Celtis Petrandra;but another 12 species pairs showed negative correlation such as Zelkova schneiderian-Acer buergerianum,Sabina chinense-Althaea rosea,Salix matsudana-Buxus sinica,Magnolia denudate-Pyracantha fortuneana,Pterocarya stenoptera-Acer palmatum f.rubrum,Sapium sebiferum-Prunus persica,Gingko biloba-Prunus yedoensis.This conclusion provides an ecological understanding for planting design in gardens.
