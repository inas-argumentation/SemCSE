A TiO2/CNTs Nanocomposites Enhanced Luminol Electrochemiluminescence Assay for Glucose Detection
A novel luminol electrochemiluminescence (ECL) strategy based on TiO 2 /CNTs nanocomposites for detection of glucose was developed. First, the TiO 2 /CNTs nanocomposites were prepared by a sol-gel method and modified on the glass carbon electrode. The ECL signal could be greatly enhanced after the electrode was modified with TiO 2 /CNTs nanocomposites, which finally resulted in the increased sensitivity. Glucose oxidase can react with glucose to form H 2 O 2 , and then the H 2 O 2 reacts with luminol to produce the ECL signal. Thus the above system is proved to be efficient for glucose detection. The modified electrode exhibited excellent ECL signals and gave a good linear range over 1.0 × 10 –7 –5.0 × 10 –6 M with a detection limit of 5.2 × 10 –8 M towards glucose detection. This strategy was successfully demonstrated as a sensitive, rapid, simple and cost-effective method for the determination of glucose. Meanwhile, the TiO 2 /CNTs nanocomposites offered a novel material for the signal enhancement in electrochemiluminescence sensor.
