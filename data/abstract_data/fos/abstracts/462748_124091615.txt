MATRICES THAT COMMUTE WITH CERTAIN HAUSDORFF MATRICES
We prove that if an infinite matrix A satisfies AC = CA where C is either a Cesäro matrix Ca or a Holder matrix Ha each of order a = 1,2,3, or 4, then the matrix A is triangular, and hence is a Hausdorff matrix. We prove also that corresponding to each real a > 4 there exists a non-triangular matrix A with rows in (.i that commutes with Ha , and that corresponding to each integer a > 4 there exists a non-triangular matrix A with rows in that commutes with CaIn addition, we prove results concerning infinite matrices that commute with Ha when Q is a fraction of a certain kind or with the Euler matrix {E,q). 1991 Mathematics Subject Classification: 40G05
