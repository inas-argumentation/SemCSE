Research and Application of Job Scheduling Optimization in the Field Work
Takes some relative research and the analysis on field work's model with corporation farm transportation and harvest problem, and then designs multi-objective models according to the cost of operation and the working cycle. The farm job scheduling is optimizesed by inte-grated design's multi-objective GA PARETO solution, and is compared with the production practice, which gets a good simulation effect, and has the high application value on the de-sign in the field work question.
