The Transient Diffusion and Absorption of Ammonia in Roughages
ABSTRACT WHEN NH3 is applied to corn stover or straw, both the digestibility and crude protein content of these materials are improved for ruminant feed. The mathe-matics of the diffusion of NH3 and its simultaneous ab-sorption by such porous media as corn stover or straw is developed. An effective coefficient of diffusion of NH3 which takes into account both the tortuosity of the porous medium and the affinity of NH3 to the porous medium, was found from experimental data.
