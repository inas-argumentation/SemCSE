Investigation of lck on IL 12 signal transduction in tubular epithelial cells
Objective To investigate the effect of lck pathway activation on IL 12 signal transduction of the tubular epithelial cells. Methods Murine tubular epithelial cells(TEC) were stimulated with IL 12 or IL 2, expression of lck mRNA was determined by in situ hybridyzation. When TEC was cultivated with lck specific inhibitor PP1 to inhibit lck activity, effect of lck on IL 12 signal transduction of TEC was measured by autoradiography and immunoblot assay, respectively. Results lck may regulate the lck/c jun signaling pathways in TEC. Further, inflammation of TEC mediated by the activation of lck pathway is related to the expression of c Jun promoted by IL 12. Conclusion lck play an important role in the signal transdution of IL 12 in tubular epithelial cells. [
