Effects of Inclusions on Impact Properties of A357 Alloy
The effect of inclusion content on the microstructure and mechanical properties of aluminum alloy was investigated in this paper.The different inclusion contents were introduced into alloy melts by adding its scraps.The impact properties of alloy materials were tested.The micro and macro fracture morphologies were observed and the EDS analyses of micro-areas were also carried out.The experimental results show that with the increasing of inclusion content,the impact energies of alloy materials and the load capacity decrease.The fracture morphologies gradually appear more cleavage feature.The gathered inclusions and the extraneous phases are more in alloy with the increasing of inclusions,which influences the homogeneous microstructure of the alloy.And the inclusions become the crack sources during impact process.
