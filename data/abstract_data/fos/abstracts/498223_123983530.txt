Excitation functions of the reaction $sup 12$C + $sup 13$C
Excitation functions have been measured for the $alpha$-particle, proton, and elastic channels of the reaction $sup 12$C+$sup 13$C at several angles in the energy range E/sub lab/=16--28 MeV. Observed structures cannot be due to molecular-type doorway states as is the case for the sub-Coulomb resonances of the reaction $sup 12$C+$sup 12$C. (AIP)
