The Distribution of Education Departments during the Period of the Republic of China and Its Characteristics
During the period of the Republic of China,the geographical distribution of education departments was well-distributed but unbalanced.The number of education departments increased rapidly,while they were mostly distributed in comprehensive universities and independent colleges.However,education departments were distributed sparsely in the normal colleges.From the temporal distribution,it showed the characteristic of great difference before and after the Anti-Japanese War.These characteristics were formed as the result of government policies,distribution of universities,and social needs.Nowadays we should inherit and innovate the distribution of education departments rationally,and pay attention to the government regulation.Meanwhile,we should also adjust the distribution flexibly according to the needs,and develop a good standard and make a reasonable assessment.
