The quark compound bag model: Defining the admixture of six-quark bag in the deuteron
The probability of the six-quark bag part of the deuteron is defined within recently formulated quark compound bag (QCB) model. An upper limit of about 1 % for the admixture of the confined bag in the deuteron is found for the QCB potential supplied by the long range Paris interaction. The six-quark bag corrections to the static multipole moments of the deuteron are estimated to be ≲1 % forμd and ≲6% forQd.
