Irradiance scintillations of a partially coherent source in extremely strong turbulence.
Northwestern University, Department of Electrical Engi­ neering & Computer Science, Evanston, Illinois 60201. Received 30 December 1978. 0003-6935/79/081133-03$00.50/0. © 1979 Optical Society of America. Recent theoretical work has shown that the scintilla­ tion index for an extended strongly turbulent medium satu­ rates to a constant of unity. These studies have been con­ cerned with the wave propagation of initially perfectly co­ herent or completely incoherent radiation. As perfect co­ herence is only an idealization of a real source, for many ap­ plications such as large laser source apertures or reflectance, it may be necessary to account for the effects of the degree of coherence of the source. Recently second-order mutual co­ herence functions have been formulated for partially coherent sources. For irradiance scintillations the fourth-order mutual coherence functions are needed. In this Letter we extend Fante's result to the general case of spatially partially coherent radiation propagating in a strongly turbulent me­ dium. Fante has shown that the scintillation index ap­ proaches unity for the two extreme cases of perfect coherence and complete incoherence. The extended Huygens-Fresnel principle together with the paraxial approximation is used, and Gaussian field statistics are assumed. A compact mathematical form, in which a pa­ rameter ρs (characteristic coherence length) is introduced, describes the general coherence distribution of the source.
