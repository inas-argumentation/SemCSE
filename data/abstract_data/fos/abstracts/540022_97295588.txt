Recyclization of 7-hydroxy-3-(4-phenyl-1,2,4-triazol-3-yl)-chromones using binucleophiles
The recyclization of 2-R-7-hydroxy-3-(4-phenyl-1,2,4-triazol-3-yl)chromones using the binucleophiles hydroxylamine, hydrazine, or guanidine gave isoxazoles, 2-aminochromones, pyrazoles, or pyrimidines with a 4-phenyl-1,2,4-triazol-3-yl substituent.
