Hadamard And Fejer-Hadamard Type Inequalities for Convex and Relative Convex Functions via an Extended Generalized Mittag-Leffler Function
In this paper, we will prove the Hadamard and the Fejer-Hadamard type integral inequalities for convex and relative convex functions due to an extended generalized Mittag-Leffler function. These results contain several fractional integral inequalities for the well known fractional integral operators.
