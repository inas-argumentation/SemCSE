FORMATION OF THE HE2(C,D,E,F,C,D,E,F) STATES BY ION-ION NEUTRALIZATION REACTION OF HE2+ WITH C6F6- IN THE HELIUM FLOWING AFTERGLOW
The ion-ion neutralization reaction of He2+ with C6F6− has been spectroscopically studied in a He flowing afterglow. The He2(C,D,E,F,c,d,e,f) states in the energy range of 19.22-20.62 eV were produced. There was a great similarity in the product electronic state distribution between the He2+/C6F6− and He+/C6F6− reactions leading to He2* and He*, respectively.
