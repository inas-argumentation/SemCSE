2,1-Benzothiazine 2,2-Dioxides. 3*. 4-Hydroxy-1-Methyl-2,2-Dioxo-N-(1,3-Thiazol-2-yl)-1Н-2λ6,1-Benzothiazine-3-Carboxamides – a New Group of Potential Analgetics
We have developed an effective synthetic method and prepared several 4-hydroxy-1-methyl-2,2-dioxo-N-(1,3-thiazol-2-yl)-1Н-2λ6,1-benzothiazine-3-carboxamides and a few structurally related heterocyclic arylamides. The structural features of one of the substituted thiazolyl-2-amides obtained have been investigated. Compounds with a high analgesic activity have been identified within this group by pharmacological screening.
