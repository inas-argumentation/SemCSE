Wireless Sensor Network Protocol Based on Energy Balance
The cluster division algorithms mentioned in the LEACH protocol of wireless sensor networks can effectively reduce the power consumption of the communication node and extend the life-time of network.In order to more effectively reduce the energy consumption of nodes,extend the life-time of the network,it can adopt the energy balance thoughts in the cluster division algorithms,meanwhile the cluster-head node send data to sink node through a routing tree.Finical,experiment results showed that the wireless sensor network based on the energy balance algorithm performs better and has more life-time.
