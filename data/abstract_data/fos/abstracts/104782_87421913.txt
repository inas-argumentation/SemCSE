Synthesis of long-chian esters of p-hydroxybenzoic acid
The long-chian esters of p-hydroxybenzoic acid, such as heptyl ester of p-hydroxybenzoic acid, octyl ester of p-hydroxybenzoic acid, undecyl ester of p-hydroxybenzoic acid and lauryl ester of p-hydroxybenzoic acid, were synthesized by a direct esterification from 1-heptyl alcohol (1-octyl alcohol, 1-undecyl alcohol, 1-laurl alcohol) and p-hydroxybenzoic acid as catalyst in benzene. And the final products were got by a recrystallization using hexane. The yields were 83.1%, 85.4%, 90.3% and 94.6%. The optimum condictions for the reaction as follow: p-hydroxybenzoic acid and alcohol in the mole ractio of 1:3, reaction time 4 h, reaction temperature 130~140 ℃, p-toluenesulfonic acid and p-hydroxybenzoic acid in the mole ratio 0.07:1.
