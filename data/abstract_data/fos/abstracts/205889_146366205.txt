The Role of Truth Commissions in the Search for Justice, Reconciliation and Democratisation: the Salvadorean and Honduran Cases
The truth commissions in El Salvador and Honduras attempted to
 offer the transitional civilian governments a way to balance their moral and
 legal obligations with practical political constraints; namely to reconcile calls
 from different sectors of society to either punish or forgive those responsible
 for past human rights violations; and to establish new and independent institutions
 without precipitating a backlash from vested interests which could derail
 the whole democratic transition. This paper examines the parameters in which
 these truth commissions operated, and evaluates the role they played in trying
 to facilitate the democratisation processes in Honduras and El Salvador.
