Effect of Surfactants on Detection and Separation of 4-Cl-7-nitrobenzofurazan Derived Amino-acids by Chip-based Capillary Electrophoresis
The effect of various surfactants and solvents on the fluorescence properties of the 4-chloro-7-nitrobenzofurazan(NBD-Cl) derived amino-acids was studied.The experimental conditions for the derivatization reaction between NBD-Cl and amino acids,and those for chip-based capillary electrophoresis separation-laser induced fluorescence detection(CCE-LIF) of the amino acid derivatives were optimized.It was found that above their critical micelle concentrations,cationic surfactant CTMAB sensitized the fluorescence intensity of NBD-Cl derived arginine,glutamic acid,and aspartic acid,while non-ionic surfactant Brij-35 did the fluorescence intensity of the NBD-Cl derived phenylalanine and arginine.It was observed that six NBD-Cl derived amino acids of arginine,phenylalanine,alanine,glycine,glutamic acid and aspartic acid were CCE separated within 70 s in a 15-mmol/L pH 9.2 borate buffer containing 5 mmol/L Brij-35 and 20% acetonitrile additives,the resolution of the partially overlapped alanine and glycine derivatives being 0.91,and the detection limits of the six derived amino acids were 0.27-1.6 μmol/L.
