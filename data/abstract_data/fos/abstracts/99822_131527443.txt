The Evolution of the Relations between Tang Dynasty and the Northwest and the Northeast Inferior Powers in the Late Seventh Century
The Tang Dynasty was one of the great dynasties,which,for its prosperity and strength,greatly influenced its neighboring regimes,especially those in the northwest and northeast,and solved the problems puzzling the Central Plains Dynasty and the regimes in the northwest and the northeast.The Tang Dynasty properly handled its relations with the various political powers in the northwest and the northeast and finally established a suzerain-vassal relationship with them,putting them under its continuous control.
