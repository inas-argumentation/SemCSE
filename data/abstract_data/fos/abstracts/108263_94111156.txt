Low-temperature hydrothermal formation and ion exchange of hydrated sodium manganates
Abstract Hydrothermal treatment of α-Mn 2 O 3 with 5 M NaOH at 155°C leads to the formation of phyllomanganates structurally related with birnessite by a dissolution-disproportionation process. Further treatment of these products which causes the gradual exchange of Mn by Na ions, is accompanied by an increase in the unit cell-volume of the solid, the average Mn oxidation state and the 1-D character of the particles. After 51 d of hydrothermal treatment, the degree of Mn/Na exchange allows the formation of phases containing interlayer Na exclusively.
