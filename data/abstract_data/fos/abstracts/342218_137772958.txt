Study of Tension-tension Fatigue Properties of Cr4 and Cr5-type Steels
The tension-tension fatigue properties of Cr4 and Cr5-type steels for back-up rolls were measured by using the group test method,the microstructure of the steel was also studied by means of XRD,SEM.The results show that the fatigue stress of Cr5-type steel(659MPa)is obviously better than Cr4-type steel(438MPa),when the median life gets to 10~6 cycles.
