Effect of copper stress on catalase activity of carp(Procypris mterus)
The aim of this experiment was to examine the effects of copper stress on the catalase(CAT) activity in blood and liver tissue of Carp(Procypris mterus).Carps were treated with 0,0.067,0.084,0.101,0.118 and 0.134 mg·L-1 of Cu2+ for periods of 0 to 11 d under visible light condition.The results showed that highest CAT activities in blood and liver tissue were observed in the lower Cu2+ concentrations treatment(0.067 mg·L-1)and CAT activities of this treatment were increased with extension of exposure time.However,CAT activities in blood and liver tissue in other treatments(0.084,0.101,0.118 and 0.134 mg·L-1 Cu2+ concentrations)were decreased with Cu2+ concentrations and exposure time(P0.05)and tended to be stable after 5 days.The experiment suggests that CAT activity can be affected by Cu2+ concentrations in water,and a low dose stimulation followed by a high dose inhibition.
