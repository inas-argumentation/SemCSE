Development prospect of the course "Management of Seashore Sports Recreation" in social sports specialty
The author gave thought to the development prospect of the course "Management of Seashore Sports and Recreation" in the first social sports specialty in China established by Guangdong Ocean University.The main reasons for sustainable development of the course "Management of Seashore Sports and Recreation" are that there is a huge sports recreation market and that China is short of sports recreation operating and managing professionals;the main assurances for sustainable development of the course "Management of Seashore Sports Recreation" are state policy guidance for sports industry,sufficiently supplied resources and complete professional arrangements;the main advantage for sustainable development of the course "Management of Seashore Sports Recreation" is that Guangdong Ocean University not only has a superior geographic location and natural resources but also has its own subject advantages and oceanic features.
