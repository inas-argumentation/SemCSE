A Pitch Extraction Method with High Frequency Resolution for Singing Evaluation
This paper proposes a pitch estimation method with high frequency resolution suitable for singing evaluation. Karaoke scoring function embedded in commercial karaoke machine is often pointed to conflicting with human feeling. One of the reasons for the poor evaluation is “low frequency resolution” of conventional pitch extraction method. Professional singers and musicians have sharp hearing for music and singing voice. They recognize that singer’s voice pitch is “a little off key” or “be in tune”. In the same way, high frequency resolution is necessary for pitch extraction method embedded in karaoke scoring devices. Fig. 1 shows “Musical-interval resolution” of several pitch extraction methods. The pitch extraction method utilizing FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) that is embedded in conventional karaoke scoring has very low resolution. Autocorrelation method can detect 50 [Hz] pitch with high resolution (Δc 1.8 [cent]) if vocal signal is sampled at 48 [kHz]. By this method, however, 1000 [Hz] pitch is detected with low resolution (Δc 33.3 [cent]). This paper compensates the frequency resolution in high frequency band by using the resolution in low frequency band of autocorrelation function.
