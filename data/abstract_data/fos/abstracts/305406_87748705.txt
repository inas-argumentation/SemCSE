Effects of pCEP4-PTH gene plasmid on production performance and calcium,phosphorus metabolism in layers
One hundred and fifty New Yangzhou layers of 56-week-old,were randomly divided into three treatments,each treatment had five repeats,and one repeat had 10 birds,to study the effects on production performance,eggshell quality,calcium and phosphorus appearance metabolic rates by injecting pCEP4-PTH gene plasmid into the crural muscle of layers with a dose of 0,200 and 400 μg,respectively.The results showed that pCEP4-PTH gene plasmid enhanced the production performance;Injecting dose of 200 μg obviously improved eggshell strength(P0.05),injecting 400 μg had no effect on eggshell quality obviously;pCEP4-PTH gene plasmid might be expressed well in layers,the level of PTH elevated significantly(P0.05 or P0.01),and in 400 μg dose it elevated extremely significantly(P0.01);Injecting dose of 400 μg could promote the secretion of endogeny CT and E2(P0.05),which was disadvantaged to display the biology effect,BGP and AKP in the two treatments were not significantly different from that of control group,serum calcium,phosphorus and calcium,phosphorus metabolism in the two treatments all increased,which indicated that pCEP4-PTH gene plasmid could improve the Absorption of calcium,phosphorus but would not be harmful to bone.pCEP4-PTH gene plasmid improved the production performance and eggshell quality,promoted calcium and phosphorus metabolism,but did not affect bone.
