A Switching Vector Median Filter Based on CIELAB Color Space
Vector median filter (VMF) is a kind of traditional and highly effective vector filter which is mainly used to remove impulsive noise from color images.However,it fails to distinguish thin lines and boundaries from impulsive noise,and usually filter them out because it regards them as some noise.In this paper the original RGB-image is first converted to the CIELAB space,then by means of imitating Laplacian operators,a new color impulse detector is presented.Combining this color impulse detector with the traditional VMF,a new kind of switching vector median filter (LABSVMF),is proposed.The experimental results show that the proposed filter,not only can effectively preserve the fine details,but also provides better filtering performance compared to the traditional VMF and some of the representative vector filters and recently developed vector filters.
