On Chinese traditional education for developing sociability and its contemporary significance
In Chinese traditional education ideas, conscientiousness, agreeableness, cultivation oneself to be good to the group always shared a very important status. In the Republic of China Period, education for developing sociability was put into educational policy. In modern society, education for developing sociability is embodied in the education goal of Hong Kong, Marco, Taiwan and Singapore. Nowadays strengthening and endowing it with new connotation are beneficial to calibrate deviation, enrich theories and promote all-round development of people.
