Determination of chilli products of sudanI,II,III,IV by UPLC-MS/MS
To established a fast and accurate method for determination chilli products of sudanⅠ, Ⅱ, Ⅲ, Ⅳ illegally added to chilli product. The samples were extracted by acetonitrile and determined by ultra performance liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (UPLC-MS/MS). The calibration curve of these four compounds showed a good linearity in the range of 0.0～200 ng/mL for sudanⅠ, Ⅱ, Ⅲ, Ⅳ and the correlation coefficients were better than 0.9911. The limits of detection(LODS) of the method were 1.0 μg/kg for sudanⅠ, 2.0 μg/kg for sudanⅡ, 2.0 μg/kg for sudan Ⅲ and 4.0 μg/kg for sudan Ⅳ, respectively. The average recoveries are higher than 75% and the relative standard deviations(RSD) of the method were higher than 10%. The method was sensitive and accurate and it could be used for the detection of illegal added industrial dyes such as sudanⅠ, Ⅱ, Ⅲ, Ⅳ.
