Cloning and Expression of HbSERK1 from Hevea brasiliensis
The full-length cDNA encoding somatic embryogenesis receptor-like kinase,designated HbSERK1,was isolated from rubber tree.HbSERK1 was a 2 159 bp long containing a 1 482 bp open reading frame encoding a putative protein of 493 amino acids.The deduced amino acid sequences of HbSERK1 shared 91%,90%,88%,88%,87% and 86% identity with the somatic embryogenesis receptor-like kinases from Populus trichocarpa,Vitis vinifera,Daucus carota,Solanum tuberosum,Arabidopsis thaliana and Oryza sativa.HbSERK1 contained a conserved intracellular serine/threonine protein kinase domain.After about 20 days of culture on the embryo induction medium,HbSERK1 was expressed in calli,while HbSERK1 was little expressed in the calli growing onto calli induction medium,leaf and latex.It was suggested that HbSERK1 maybe take part in somatic embryogenesis during in vitro culture.
