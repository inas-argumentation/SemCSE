Isotropic nmr shifts in the 'solid' and 'liquid' states
The derivation of the expressions for the isotropic shift of nmr signals in paramagnetic molecules is reconsidered. lt is shown that the reaction of the electronic s pin system to the thermal motions of the molecules should be taken into account. This leads to the result that the isotropic shift in liquids is independent of the relative magnitudes of the tumbling time and the electronic relaxation time. Hence, the temperature dependence of the correlation times cannot be responsible for non-Curie behaviour of the shift. F or the case of free rotating molecules this result is proved in a way analogaus to Van Vleck's treatment of the Langevin-Debye formula of the electric susceptibility. The relation with the phenomenon of motional narrowing is discussed.
