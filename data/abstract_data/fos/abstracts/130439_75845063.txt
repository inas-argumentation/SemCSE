Study of the Effect of Laughing Gas Combined Lidocaine in Artificial Abortion
Objective To study the effect of laughing gas combined with lidocaine in artificial abortion of gravida in early pregnancy.Methods 160 cases of unigravida were randomly divided into group A(simple laughing gas group) and group B(laughing gas combined with lidocaine group).Observed the effect of anesthesia,the extent of cervical relaxation,incidences of syndrome of artificial abortion,amounts of bleeding and the rates automatically away from the bed 3 minutes after the operation between the two groups.Results There were significant difference in the effect of anesthesia,the extent of cervical relaxation and the incidences of syndrome of artificial abortion between the two groups(P0.05).Group B was better than group A.There were no significant difference in the amounts of bleeding and the rates automatically away from the bed 3 minutes after the operation.Conclusion The inhalation anesthesia of laughing gas combined with local anesthesia of lidocaine has an exact effect on artificial abortion section and is easy and convenient.
