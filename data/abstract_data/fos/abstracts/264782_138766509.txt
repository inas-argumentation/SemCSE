Solution and Its Principle of Macro-deformation of Diffusion Bonding Jionts
The pressure is the most important parameters for the diffusion bonding within the limit temperature range, a larger pressure are usually required to ensure the rapid diffusion bonding, so the macroscopic deformation has become the major problem for diffusion bonding. Two diffusion bonding techniques: transient liquid phase diffusion bonding and hot isostatic pressing diffusion bonding was brought in and the macro-deformation of the diffusion bonding jionts was solved. Transient liquid phase diffusion bonding technology realizes rapid welding weldment at a lower pressure to ensure non- macro-deformation, hot isostatic pressing diffusion bonding with a homogeneous pressure process to ensure that welding macro-deformation is not realized in a larger pressure.
