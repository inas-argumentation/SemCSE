A viewpoint of education in post modernism
The reflection of the contemporary educational problem from the perspective of post modernism should establish a series of new education ideas and research fields : 1. a view of educational philosophy of the non-determinism, 2. non-logic cognition in learning research, 3. the physiological psychology in education research, 4. theory of the study self-organizaion, 5. caring for education and humanitarianism, 6. community's network and the development of education for all and life-long education.
