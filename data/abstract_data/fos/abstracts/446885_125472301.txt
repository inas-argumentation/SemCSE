Research on Components Characteristics of Single Container Friction Hoist
To improve the system of a single container multi rope friction, from the point of view of safety conditions, based on Euler formula, combined with dimensionless method, the static tension ratio analysis and research into the system, draws a single container friction hoisting system Components characteristics of static tension ratio C, in order to explore components configuration of the system and anti-skid, provides a simple, convenient, and reliable theoretical basis.
