Singularities in boundary value problems for an abstract second-order differential equation of elliptic type
In this work we give an alternative approach to the study of some singular boundary value problems for a second order differential-operator equation in the space of Holder continuous functions. We prove that the solution may be broken down explicitly into the sum u=u"R+u"S of a regular part and a singular part under some natural assumptions on the data. We then give a complete analysis of u"R and u"S by using the operational calculus.
