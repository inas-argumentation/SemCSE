The Legislative Perfection of Civil Judicial Jurisdiction concerning Foreign-related Ship Collision
Marine casualty dispute caused by ship collision is an important type in maritime civil disputes.On account of the complexity,national sovereignty and national differences of civil judicial jurisdiction legislation,it is significant to enhance the foreign-related civil judicial jurisdiction legislation of ship collision.And there are some deficiencies in our law,as the concept of ship collision is confusing,the point of contact is improper,and the legislation of new Civil Procedural Law on the subject is imperfect.Therefore,to improve the foreign-related civil judicial jurisdiction legislation of ship collision,we should unify the concept of ship collision,improve the connection point,and value the related legislation under the general idea of"sovereignty maintenance and risk sharing balance".
