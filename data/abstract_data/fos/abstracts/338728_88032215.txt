Study on invitro antioxidant effects of extractive from Malus hupehensis (Pamp.) Rehd
Objective: Discussion invitro Antioxidant Effects of extractive from Malus hupehensis(Pamp.) Rehd. Methods: Choosing DPPH free radical and hydroxyl free radical reaction system to determinate invitro Antioxidant Effects of extractive from Malus hupehensis. Results: Ethanol total extractive from Malus hupehensis, of which the EC50 of elimination of the DPPH free radical is 0.20 mg/mL, and major component of Phloridzin from Malus hupehensis is 0.75 mg/mL, Elution of 50% ethanol is 0.38 mg/mL, and Elution of 20% ethanol is 1.2 mg/mL. The major components from Malus hupehensis, Phloridzin, Elution of 20% ethanol, Elution of 50% ethanol, and ethanol total extractive have ability to inhibite the formation of hydroxyl free radical. Conclusion: Phloridzin from Malus hupehensis, as one of the predominant active ingredients, have satisfactory antioxidant activity. Furthermore, Malus hupehensis have a role to remove DPPH free radical and inhibite the formation of hydroxyl free radical.
