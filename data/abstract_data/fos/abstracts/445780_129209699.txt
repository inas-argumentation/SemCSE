The effects of surface mining of coal on water quality near Grande Cache, Alberta: Discussion
Introduction Water Quality Mining hydrology in general and the environmental consequences of mining for hydrologic systems in particular are the focus of increased attention by legislative bodies, regulatory agencies, and scientific organizations in Canada and in the United States. The paper by Hackbarth (1979) constitutes one of the few studies published so far in this field in Canada. It provides badly needed background data on some of the effects of resource development on nearby surface and groundwater, based on a collection period of 7 years (1972-1978).
