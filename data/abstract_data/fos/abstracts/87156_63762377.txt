Calibration of Projector Brightness Curve Based on HDR
Multiple projectors seamless splicing correction process is mainly the projector geometric correction and color correction. Multiple projectors color correction requirement is calculated for each projector color curve, and it's the same color to make the corresponding input output so as to achieve the overall color of unity. Proposes a method based on camera HDR, first finds the camera's brightness response curve, then the brightness of camera response curve based on the camera's input is the output of the projector, the projector's brightness obtained indirect response curve. Experimental results show that the luminance graph of HDR measurement, compared with using the light meter, at 10% or less difference between the results.
