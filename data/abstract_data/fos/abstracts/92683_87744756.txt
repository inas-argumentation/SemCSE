Establishment of AFLP reaction system and its application to analysis of genetic diversity for oil tea(Camellia olifera Abel.)
Amplified fragment length polymorphism(AFLP) reaction system was established for Camellia oleifera Abel..Four AFLP primer pairs were tested and selected.The genetic diversity was analyzed based on the primer combinations.A total of 23 loci with unambiguous amplified fragments were revealed per primer pair with a number of polymorphic loci(N) of 18,a percentage of polymorphic loci(PPL) of 79.13%,an effective number of alleles per locus(ne) of 1.320,an expected heterozygosity(He) of 0.184,and Shannon's information index(I) of 0.267.The results indicate that AFLPs are suitable markers for the research of C.olifera,trees of the species pos-sess a relatively high level of genetic diversity.The present study provids theoretic and technical support for the further population genetic study of C.olifera.
