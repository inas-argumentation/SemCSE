TRITHIOPEROXYBENZOATO(η3-DITHIOBENZOATO)OXOMOLYBDENUM(IV), MoO(S3CPh)(η3-S2CPh): SYNTHESIS AND X-RAY MOLECULAR STRUCTURE
The titled novel complex was obtained by the reaction of sodium molybdate(VI) dihydrate with diethylammonium dithiobenzoate in an acidic aqueous medium. An X-ray structure analysis of this complex has revealed the presence of trithioperoxybenzoato and η3 -dithiobenzoato ligands both coordinated to oxomolybdenum(IV).
