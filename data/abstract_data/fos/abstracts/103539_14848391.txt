A convexity property for the SO(2,ℂ)-double coset decomposition of SL(2,ℂ) and applications to spherical functions
Abstract.We make a fine study of the SO(2,ℂ)-double coset decomposition in SL(2,ℂ) and give a full description of the intersection of the various cells with the complex crown Ξ of SL(2,ℝ)/SO(2). A non-linear convexity theorem is proved and applications to analytically continued spherical functions are given.
