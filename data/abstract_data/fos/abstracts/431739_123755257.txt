An Analysis on Cognitive Motivation to Classify the Conversational Implicature into Generalized and Particularized Subtypes
The paper watchfully illustrates the concrete practice and its equivalent results of dealing with the argument on how to classify conversational implicatures in classical Conversational Implicatures Theory, and meanwhile it points out the defects and deficiencies of such practice, based on which, we suggest certain cognitive factors be involved into the process of classification. Then starting from the concept 'Scale of Contexts' with a combination of two cognitive principles, we reanalyze the motivation for the binary classification mentioned above, thus indicating it is necessary to make further explorations on it by taking this opportunity.
