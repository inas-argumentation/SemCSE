A New Type of Higher Education's Quality Assurance:Monitoring Evaluation
Monitoring evaluation is a new type of higher education's quality assurance with the emergence of China's new normal of high education's development.In order to present higher education's status directly and provide an objective basis for multi-agent value judgments and scientific decision-making,monitoring evaluation will take use of modern information technology to collect and analyze the related information.The connotation and characteristics of higher education's monitoring and evaluation include:monitoring teaching process regularly,showing the quality of education by data,feedbacking and improving teaching quality and multi-agent value judgments.It should clear the implementation path from constructing institutions,following principles,constituting indicators and improving mechanisms.
