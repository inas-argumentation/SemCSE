Dimensional Analysis and Wafering Cotton Stalks
ABSTRACT PROPER design of wafering equipment for cotton stalks requires studying the effect of the process parameters on the energy required and on the wafer den-sities inside the die under maximum load and outside the die after the load was released. Experiments were per-formed using the dimensional analysis approach to relate the process variables as well as the material properties to both densities and energy requirements. General correla-tion models are given and a discussion leading towards obtaining optimum wafering conditions is presented.
