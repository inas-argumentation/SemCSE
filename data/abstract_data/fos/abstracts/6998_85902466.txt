Dissipation of Methamidophos Residues on Cauliflower
Three sprays of 0.03% and 0.05% methamidophos were given at the rate of 0.33 and O.55 kg ai/ha at the time of pest population build up. Samples of leaves and flowers/curds were collected at 0,3,7, 14 and 21 days after spray application. The residues were estimated by bioassay and chemical methods.
