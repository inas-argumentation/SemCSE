Technical progress for combined control of sulfur dioxide and nitric oxides
Sulfur dioxide and nitric oxides are mainly derived from flue gas, and the flue gas treatment is one of the important missions of environment protection.The advantages and disadvantages of the traditional methods which remove SO_2 and NO_x from flue gas by wet desulfurization and NH_(3) selected catalytic reduction are described.Some technologies for combined control of SO_2 and NO_x are introduced concerning their current progress, basic principle, process characteristics and applications.
