The role of speech expressive means in Spanish poetics
In recent years, the interest of researchers towards the problems of modern poetry has steadily increased. Poetic language became a research object of many scientists of the national and the foreign linguistics. The majority of the linguists who study a poetic language believe that the linguistic analysis of the poetic text can be carried out in accordance with different principles. However, the specific nature of the poetic language should be always taken into consideration.
