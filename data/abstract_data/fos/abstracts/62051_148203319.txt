Conflict and Moderation: On Internal Contradictions in Southwest Associated University——Also on the Essence of the Spirit of Southwest Associated University
Jointly running-school of Southwest Associated University was a forced action during the Anti-Japanese War,which produced many contradictions.Different background and tradition,division of labor and arrangement,and a sense of belonging were the historical reason,personnel reason and psychological reason of the contradictions.Implementing disciplined management,arousing patriotic sentiment and forming the style of running school were measures of resolving the contradictions.It can provide some examples and revelation for resolving contradictions of our universities.
