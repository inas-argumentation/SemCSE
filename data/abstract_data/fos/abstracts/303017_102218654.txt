Experimental research on brine pretreatment of herring fillet stored under controlled freezing-point temperature
Determination of NaCl concentration in different sample pre-treatment of experimental herring stored in freezing-point temperature(-0.8±0.2) ℃ changes in the total number of bacteria, physical and chemical indicators of TVB-N, pH value and sensory characteristics. The three experimental groups were used 1% of NaCl pre-treatment, 3% NaCl pretreatment and no-pretreatment method for evaluating the quality of change of herring stored in ice -temperature environment. The first two processing methods of TVB -N content respectively achieve 22.43 mg/100 g and 20.41 mg/100 g stored 22 days under ice-temperature, while not to do pre-processing of the herring fillet storage for 18 days reaches 19.43 mg/100 g. The results show that the temperature of the herring ice storage prior to saline pre-treatment, which can effectively maintain the quality during storage, the method will help inhibit the herring's growth of bacteria and TVB-N production, to extend the ice-temperature preservation time.
