Health Related Quality of Life (Hrqol) in Children After Major Urinary Tract Reconstruction
Purpose HRQOL scores, obtained using the 23 item Pediatric Quality of Life Inventory (PedsQL), (Varni et al. Medical Care 1999; 33,2, 126-139), assess the physical, emotional, psychosocial, social and school functioning of patients. Patients and their parents completed this tool following major reconstruction of the lower urinary tract. Material and Methods 44 patients were reconstructed by a single surgeon between 2002 and 2008 (mean follow up 50 months). PedsQL questionnaires were sent to families for completion by both the child (self-report) and a parent (proxy-report). 35 were completed (response rate 79%) and 30 were suitable for analysis. All patients had undergone Ileocystoplasty and Mitrofanoff formation. 5 also had an ACE procedure and 10 a bladder neck reconstruction. Results were compared to age matched normal controls and other chronic childhood diagnoses [diabetes, asthma, inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) and cancer]. Within our group we considered the effects of age (15 pts 12 years), gender (16 M, 14F), diagnosis (15 neuropathic bladder, 8 anorectal anomaly, 6 exstrophy, 5 others), surgical procedure and outcome.
