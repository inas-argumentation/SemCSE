Thermochemical investigations on methyl-substituted benzoic acids: I. Toluic acids
The thermochemical study of the methyl-substituted benzoic acids has been undertaken as a contribution to the basic thermodynamic information on pure organic compounds. This paper contains the thermochemical data of the three first members of this series: the toluic acids. The unit of energy upon which values reported in this paper are based is the absolute joule. The relation 1 cal = 4·1840 J is used. The molecular weight of carbon dioxide is taken as 44·011.
