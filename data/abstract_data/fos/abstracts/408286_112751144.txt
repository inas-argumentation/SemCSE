Characteristics of Casting Process and Process Optimization
Frequently-used many methods of casting process optimization are not able to meet the requirement of production.Through analysis of characteristics of casting process problem,it is recognized that casting process problem is a complicated technology problem,in which some conflicts exist.Applying the theory of solving inventive problem to casting process optimization of the magnesium alloy manifold tubes,the inner shrinkage cavities of castings were eliminated,and the technical requirements of production were satisfied.
