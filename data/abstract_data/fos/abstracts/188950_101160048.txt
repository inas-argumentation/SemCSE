An Integrated System for Mass Spectrometry Based Metabonomics Data Analysis
Mass spectrometry(MS) data processing and analysis have become one of the bottlenecks influencing metabolomics applications.In this paper we introduce a software system,MS-IAS(mass spectrometry based integrated analysis system),for processing and analysis of MS-based metabolomics data.It contains various kinds of feature selection,clustering and classification algorithms.Several multiple statistical analysis methods are integrated in the system to compare the selected features,aiming at finding the potential biomarkers.The visualization tools are also very convenient in MS-IAS and helpful to the analysis and interpretation of the data.To demonstrate the usefulness of MS-IAS,one human liver disease metabolomics dataset was used.40 ion features were defined by the two feature selection methods in the system.5 fold cross validation accuracy rates show the validation of the selected features.The results show the potential of MS-IAS to become a general system for MS data processing and analysis in metabolomics.
