Development of Portable Insulin Pump and its Data Communication Design
This paper introduces a portable ultra-low power portability insulin pump based on MSP430.The author mainly discusses using RS232 interface of the MCU to communicate with PC,and implement the system software in system programming.And also,all historical records stored in flash can be sent through the RS232 interface to the PC,and processed by the computer software,the doctor can instruct the patient to adjust the dosage according this,and at the same time,insulin pump operational problems are also sent to the manufacturer for product quality tracking.
