Nucleation and stability of a microparticle system in an ion-irradiated material
The features of formation of a microparticle system in an ion irradiated material has been analytically described taking into account the size factor for the interfacial energy. The calculation performed has revealed the physical nature of the phenomena corresponding to the phase transition in both homogeneous and heterogeneous regimes. The results of the calculation are in good agreement with the experienced data.
