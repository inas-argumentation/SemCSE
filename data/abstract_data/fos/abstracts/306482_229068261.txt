METHODOLOGICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR RESEARCHING SOCIO-ECONOMIC PREFERENCES OF YOUTH
The article reflects the methodological recommendations of the study of the socio-economic preferences of youth. The methodology of conducting systemic monitoring of the socio-economic intentions of various layers of youth is presented. The monitoring was created on the basis of the following blocks: observation of socio-demographic processes, standards, making long-term forecasts, regulation of socio-economic preferences. During the monitoring, attention was paid to the socio-demographic characteristics ofyouth in order to develop appropriate proposals
