A Carrier Phase Recovery Method Based on Adaptive Step
Recent years,a method of digital carrier synchronization is presented,which is based on the theory of maximum likelihood estimation and iterative calculation.In this paper,a novel carrier phase recovery method based on the adaptive step algorithm is introduced,corresponding to the disadvantage of the fixed step in the old method.Using this algorithm,the phase of the received signal can be tracked quickly because of the adaptive step.Therefore,it is suitable for those coherent communication systems which need fast synchronization.Simulation results show that,in the case of using the adaptive step the phase tracking speed of the new method is increased about 2 times in comparison with the constant step,even in a low SNR,a satisfied phase tracking result is obtained.
