Optimzing acid extraction technology of gallic acid from fruit hull of Camellia oleifera Abel
Camellia oleifera Abel is China's special oil plant.The fruit of Camellia oleifera Abel is composed of fruit hull and seed,and the fruit hull accounts for about 60% wet weight of the fruit.Using the fruit hull of Camellia oleifera Abel as material,the yields of acid extracting gallic acid from the fruit hull of Camellia oleifera Abel were studied.Using HPLC as detection method,the yields of gallic acid which were extracted by different kinds of acid were analyzed,and the results turned out that hydrochloric acid was the best acid.Based on the three single-factor tests of the concentration of hydrochloric acid,extraction time and the ratio of solid to liquid,orthogonal experiments were determined,optimize extraction process.Through orthogonal experiments,the influencing factors of gallic acid were:the concentration of hydrochloric acidextraction timethe ratio of solid to liquid.The optimum technology for gallic acid was:the concentration of hydrochloric acid was 0.75 mol/L,the extraction time was 3 h,and the ratio of solid to liquid was 1:40.Under this condition,the yield of gallic acid was 8.94 mg/g.
