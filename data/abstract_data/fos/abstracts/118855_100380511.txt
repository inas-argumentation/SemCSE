Detection of octachlorodipropyl ether residue in tea adsorbed from the combustion of the mosquito coils by gas chromatography
A gas chromatographic method was developed for the determination of octachlorodipropyl ether(S421) residue in tea.The proposed method was used to study the adsorption effect on S-421 from the combustion of the mosquito coils by tea.S-421 was extracted from tea samples using n-hexane,purified with celite 545-sulfudc acid(1 g-0.4 mL) using hexane as an eluent,and then analyzed by gas chromatography using DB-1701 capillary column(30 m×0.32 mm×0.25 μm) for separation and electron capture detector(ECD) for detection.The method of external standards was used in determination.A good linear correlation for S-421 was obtained over the range of 0.010~0.200 mg/L(r=0.9992).Average recoveries of S-421 from tea spiked at three concentration levels of 0.04,0.10 mg/kg and 0.16 mg/kg ranged from 92.4% to 96.9% with relative standard deviation less than 4.0%.The limit of quantitation(LOQ) was 0.01 mg/kg.The proposed method was easily operative and accurate for precise determination of S-421 residue in green tea with satisfactory results.It was found that one of the main sources of S-421 in tea may be the adsorption from the combustion of the mosquito coils.
