Polyfunctional pyrazoles. 3.* Synthesis of 3-(3-aryl-4-formyl-1-pyrazolyl)propionic acids and their amides
Abstract3-(3-Aryl-4-formyl-1-pyrazolyl)propionic acids were synthesized by the reaction of 3-aryl-1-(2-cyanoethyl)-4-formylpyrazoles with concentrated hydrochloric acid. They were converted into the corresponding amides by the carbodiimide method.
