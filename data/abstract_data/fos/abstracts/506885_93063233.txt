PREPARATION OF RUTILE POWDERS BY VAPOR PHASE REACTION OF TiCl4–H2–CO2 SYSTEM
Titanium dioxide powders of 98 % rutile content were obtained by vapor phase reaction of TiCl4–H2–CO2 system at temperatures above 1000 °C. The average particle sizes of products were 0.11∼0.66 μ. The sizes decreased with increasing reaction temperatures.
