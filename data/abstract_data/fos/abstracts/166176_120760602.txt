Eine Bestimmung der effektiven Zustandsdichte des Bandes für überschüssige Defektelektronen in Anthrazen
Steady-state space-charge-limited current measurements were used to obtain the effective density of states in the band appropriate to excess hole motion in anthracene. Since the energetic trap density decreases exponentially with the energy distance from the band, the traps are filled by the space charge up to a certain quasi Fermi level. The thermal activation energy of the current should be a kind of spatial avarage of this level. This energy was measured and used to calculate the effective density of states. The result, 4·1021 cm−3, is close to the double molecular density which is 8,4·1021 cm−3. Further, a discussion on traps is given in an appendix.
