Realizing of the Outpatient Navigation Enquiry System on the Touch Screen
This paper introduces the method of designing and realizing of the outpatient navigation enquiry system of Hospital Information System(HIS)for Guangdong Maternity and Child Care Hospital.The system processes quering with the multimedia touching screen,orient public,outpatient may know the basic circumstances of the hospital,the characteristics of each branch,the introduction of specialists,healthcare knowledge etc by this system.Furthermore,people can enquire the price of the Material medica and items of diagnosising,treating and checking up the hospitalization expenses etc.
