Nonhomothetic preferences and exact price index numbers
In this paper, we suggest a way of combining the statistical and the economic approach to price index theory by imposing a specific exactness condition. We propose the application of a reference level function mapping the utility levels attained in base and comparison periods into a reference level for the price comparison. This concept allows us to derive exact price indices for utility functions that are not necessarily homothetic. In addition to deriving new exact statistical price indices, we show that there exist utility functions for which the Marshall-Edgeworth index and the Walsh index, respectively, are exact.
