REACTION OF 1-(R-SULFONYL)INDOLES WITH N,N-DIBENZYL-β-AMINO ALCOHOLS
The reaction of 1-(methylsulfonyl)‑ and 1-(phenylsulfonyl)indoles with N,N-dibenzylamino alcohols leads to the formation of a mixture of isomeric 1-(beta-aminoethyl)indoles. Authors: A. V. Kurkin, A.
