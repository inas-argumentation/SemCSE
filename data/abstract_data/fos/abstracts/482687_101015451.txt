Study on microwave extraction and purification of polysaccharides from Panax
Developed a microwave assisted extraction method to extract polysaccharides form Panax Ginseng. The extracted condition with best optimal process was confirmed as follow: microwave heated 6 minutes, with ratio1∶50 of solid(g)-liquid(g) and power 450 W, through testing on single factor and orthogonal. The result showed that the extracted yield of polysaccharide was 30%. Infrared spectrum analysis showed that Panax polysaccharides was a model of β-pyran. Microwave assistant suggests a higher extraction efficiency, less energy and time comparative with the traditional direct heating extraction.
