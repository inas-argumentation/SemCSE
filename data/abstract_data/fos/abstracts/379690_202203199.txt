Experimental Investigation on Preparation of SiC_p/Al Composites by Severe Plastic Deformation
35vol% SiC particles and Al powder mixtures were successfully fabricated into metal matrix SiCp/Al composites at 250℃ by two typical severe plastic deformation methods named equal channel angular pressing(ECAP) and equal channel angular pressing and torsion(ECAPT). Through the qualitative analysis of microstructure and the quantitative calculation of quadrat method, the effects of different techniques, sample positions and deformation stages on the distribution of SiC particles were acquired. The results show that: the amount of shear strain and the mobility of metal matrix have the most important influence to the uniform distribution of SiC particles. The distribution of SiC particles after 1 pass of ECAPT is much more homogeneous than that of 1 pass of ECAP, which is equivalent to that of 2 passes of ECAP. With the deformation of ECAPT going on, the distribution of SiC particles is more and more homogeneous; especially from the compaction stage to ECAP stage, the distribution uniformity of SiC particles is greatly improved.
