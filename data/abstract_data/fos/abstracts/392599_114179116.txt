Study on Fault Diagnosis Method for Transmission Network
Fault diagnosis is crucial for reducing the outage time and enhancing power supply reliability. In this paper, a novel fault diagnosis method based on forward and backward inference is proposed for transmission network. Using the information of protective relays and circuit breakers from SCADA, the proposed system can find out all the lines which are most possible to have a fault and ranking them by the corresponding possibilities. Moreover, the inference process is simple and easy to be realized. Computer simulation results demonstrate the validity of the proposed method.
