Research on Microstructure and Properties of Hot Cutting Heat Affected Zone of Q345E Low Alloy Steel
The microstructure and properties of Q345E low alloy steel hot cutting heat affected zone were studied through different thickness hot cutting heat affected zone width,microstructure,hardness and cutting surface morphology analysis.The results show that the width of Q345E low alloy steel heat affected zone upper surface is larger than lower surface.Plasma cutting heat affected zone significantly reduces,cut face except oxide skin cracking,there are no cutting cracking in matrix.The microstructure of material is ribbon distribution ferrite and pearlite,flame cutting has first eutectoid amorphous research microstructure with a small amount of ferrite pearlite between inclusion.Plasma cutting edge over research microstructure is the obvious plate martensite,annealing zone is ferrite and pearlite.
