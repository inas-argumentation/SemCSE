On Dirichlet type spaces, α-Bloch spaces and Qp spaces on the unit ball of Cn
Abst rac t : In this paper, some function spaces on the unit ball of C are studied. We obtain three integral characterizations of Dirichlet type spaces DT. We study the inclusion relations among DT,B, and Qp in the case of η > 1, and show the strictness and best possibility of these inclusions.
