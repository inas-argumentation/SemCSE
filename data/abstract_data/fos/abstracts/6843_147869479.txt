A Study of Ecological Relationship of Grassland from the Perspective of Symbiosis Theory:A Case Study of Gannan Grassland
The symbiosis theory was first used in biology,and later is applied to economics,sociology,anthropology,etc.because of its comprehensive adaptability.It is a new try to apply the symbiosis theory to analyze the ecological relationship of Gannan Grassland.According to the symbiosis theory,it is claimed that people,domestic animals and grassland are the basic symbionic units in ecological relationship of grasslands,but the core part lies in people.The traditional nomadic livelihood mode is a typical mutually beneficial and symbionic mode,while the modern grassland livelihood mode,i.e.,grassland household responsibility system,is a typical symbionic laterality mode.The symbionic environment of grasslands includes both the natural and the cultural environments.Based on the theory,it is concluded that the return of Tibetans to traditional ecological ethnics,the cooperation and the building of herder are the most reasonable and productive method for administering the ecological system of grasslands.
