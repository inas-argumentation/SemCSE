To compare the protective effect of fructose 1,6-diphosphate and coenzyme Q_(10) on adriamycin-induced cardiotoxicity in rats
Objective :To compare the effect of fructose 1,6- diphosphate (FDP) and coenzyme Q10(CoQ10) on adriamycin-induced cardiotoxicity in rats, and investigate the mechanisms. Methods :The study of isolated perfused rat hearts was performed, these hearts were randomly divided into four groups: control group, adriamycin group, adriamycin + FDP and adriamycin + CoQ10 group.The level of contractility, coronary fluence, electrocardiogram, content of malondialdehyde, SOD and GSH-Px were monitored. In addition,the changes of myocardial ultrastructure were assessed by means of transmission electron microscope. Results :In comparison with adriamycin group, myocardial contractibility and coronary fluence were improved and arrhythmia was reduced in FDP and CoQ10 groups, especially in FDP group. Moreover, the content of malondialdehyde was lower, the activities of SOD and GSH-Px were higher, and the ultrastructure in FDP and CoQ10 groups was better. Conclusion: FDP and CoQ10 have protective effects on adriamycin-induced cardiotoxicity, and FDP is better than CoQ10.
