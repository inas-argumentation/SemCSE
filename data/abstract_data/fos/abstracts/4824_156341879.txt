Extending Family Order against the Background of Pan-politicization——Take the Two Times of Renewal of the Sun's Genealogy of Le'antang in North Jiangsu as an Example
The study of the two times of renewal of the Sun's family tree of Le'antang in the north of Jiangsu province in less than 20 years shows that it is a peculiar phenomenon in China to extend family order by renewing pedigree since 1980s. Renewal of pedigree is more than nongovernmental practice. With the influence and promotion of many factors against a background of pan-politicization,the changes in the contents of the genealogy deeply reflect the social changes affected by pan-politicization for several decades. Pan-politicization has a profound historical resource and cultural tradition.Both the orientation of the influence and the factors initiated by family tree renewal are pluralistic.Mutual influence of Pan-politicization and pedigree renewal is sure to exist for a long time.
