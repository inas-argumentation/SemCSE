Reflection and innovation of methods in institutional research
As far as research methods are concerned,the main problems in IR(institutional research) include the confusion of natural and social properties,the separation of data phenomena and inner meaning,the inconsistence of researchers' expression and researched persons' comprehension,the equation of response effect and real thought.With these problems to solve,the basic approaches for IR innovation are: encourage the transformation of researchers' roles,research value location and research means,realize the combination of "object research" and "subject research",the combination of "phenomenon description" and "meaning comprehension",the combination of "explicit cognition" and "implicit experience".
