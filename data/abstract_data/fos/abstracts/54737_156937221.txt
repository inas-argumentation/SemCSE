On Chinese Education and Cultivation of Humanistic Quality in Application-oriented Universities
Humanistic quality-oriented education consists of humanities education,social sciences education and natural sciences education,so that targeted college students can develop in a comprehensive way.Application-oriented universities need to emphasize educations of emotions,morals,sense of responsibility and values which are very important for cultivation of humanistic quality.Meanwhile,practicability should also be emphasized,which means application-oriented universities should focus on training and improvement of language application ability of college students.
