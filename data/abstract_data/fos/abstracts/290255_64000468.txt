A Security Authentication Mechanism Based on Behaviour Trust in Wireless Sensor Networks
Considering that in many cases,a sensor behaviour is both spatially and temporally correlated,proposes a distributed and localized behaviour trust mechanism based on similarity.This mech-anism realizes behaviour trust authentication by using statistical hypothesis test for matching the reading sequence of sensors and statistical characters of the behaviour.The simulation result shows that the mechanism can reduce the network traffic,can detect as much as 93% of the not trusted sensors when 10% of sensors are not secure.
