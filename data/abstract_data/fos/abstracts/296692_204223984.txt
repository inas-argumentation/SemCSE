Preliminary Study on Dyeing Wastewater Treatment Using Aged-Refuse-Aquatic-Plant Bed
Aged refuse is used as the matrix of aquatic-plant bed to replace the function of soil in the matrix.Selecting eichhornia-crassipes and alternanthera-philoxeroides as the plants of aquatic-plant bed,artificial aquatic-plant bed is built to treat simulated dyeing wastewater made from dyeing water solution,and a comparison on the degradation of dyes under natural conditions is conduced in the paper.
