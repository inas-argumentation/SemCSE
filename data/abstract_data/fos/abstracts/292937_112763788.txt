Effects of Bending on Plate Shape and Residual Stress in Straightening Process by 17-Roll Straightener
Effects of bending on 6mm thick plate shape and residual stress in the straightening process of 17-roll straightener were studied by ABAQUS/Explicit. The results show that when the bending at entrance is 7.2mm and the bending at export is-2.6mm, the plate shape after straightening is fine and residual stress distribution is uniform. Bending of 2, 4 roller of 17 roller straightener should be increased properly, so that the plastic deformation ratio closes to the extremity, and ensures the straightening quality
