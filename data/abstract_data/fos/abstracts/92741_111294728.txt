Prediction of dairy cattle housing costs with different cleaning systems
This article presents a spreadsheet application that, as an aid to prediction of investment costs deriving from the 
construction of dairy cattle housing, calculates cost per stall and cost per unit area of usefully occupied space as a function 
of the number of stalls for any combination of seven common freestall barn designs with any of four different types of cleaning 
system (tractor scraper, automatic scraper, flush system, and slotted floor). In the range of 20 to 200 stalls, the least expensive 
cleaning system is always the tractor scraper, and the most expensive is almost always the slotted floor, which raises total 
cost by up to 40% with respect to the tractor scraper. The least expensive designs generally consisted of three or six rows of 
stalls, except for the design with two rows of stalls arranged tail-to-tail when the number of stalls was <58 for the automatic 
scraper, or the design with four rows of stalls arranged tail-to-tail when the number of stalls was <52 for the tractor scraper.
