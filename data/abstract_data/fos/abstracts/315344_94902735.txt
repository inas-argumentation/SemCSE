SELECTIVE PERMEATION OF CARBON DIOXIDE THROUGH CELLULOSE NITRATE/POLYETHYLENE GLYCOL BLEND MEMBRANES
Permeabilities for N2, O2, and CO2 through cellulose nitrate/polyethylene glycol blend membranes were determined as a function of film composition. Not only the permeability but the permselectivity for CO2 increased with an increase in the fraction of polyethylene glycol. It was suggested that the intermolecular force induced by polar groups in the film and the role of polyethylene glycol as a plasticizer may be responsible for such a behavior for CO2.
