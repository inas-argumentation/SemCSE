Analysis of the takeoff technique of ZHENG Xin-juan as an excellent female high jumper in China
By applying the method of 3D kinematical analysis,the authors studied the takeoff technique of ZHENG Xin-juan as an excellent female high jumper in China,found that she had such shortcomings as too small H1 value and too large H3value,and irrational coordination between the stretching of the takeoff leg and the swinging of the arms,and thought that the coordination between stretching and swinging and the technical move of bar clearance are the keys to enhancing her performance.
