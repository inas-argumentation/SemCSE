Study on Properties of Electro-less Ni-P-Diamond Composite Coating
The metallographic organization , microhardness , adhesion and wear resistance of electro-less Ni-P-diamond composite coating were studied. The results show that the coating with more diamond particle and even distribution particle can be gotten with suitable technologies. The hardness of the coating is 1850HV or so , the adhesion between coating base is good and the wear ability is higher than electro-less Ni-P coating adhesion.
