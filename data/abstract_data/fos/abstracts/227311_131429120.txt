Study on the Release Law of Formaldehyde With Urea-formaldehyde Resin Adhesive Added to Attapulgite
The problem of formaldehyde emission always exsiting in wood-based panel has become the focus of attention,since the formaldehyde emission of wood panels,since national sanitary standard on formaldehyde emission of wood-based panels and finishing products was forcibly implemented.In this dissertation,the comparative study was carnied out on the release law of formaldehyde with urea-formaldehyde resin adhesive seperately added to attapulgite or flour in short-term and long-term by dryer measure formaldehyde method.The result showed the formaldehyde emission in each day changed with a gradual decrement trend,and to be stable in 5 days later.The former formaldehyde emission was lower.The formaldehyde emission would be decreased with urea-formaldehyde resin adhesive added to attapulgite layed for a long term,but the effect will be limited one month later.
