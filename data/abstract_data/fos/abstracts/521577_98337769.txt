Hydroboration of alkenes and alkynes with sodium borohydride catalyzed by titanium complex.
The titanium complex prepared from Cp2TiCl2 and NaBH4 in THF in the presence of crown ethers efficiently promotes the catalytic hydroboration of alkenes and alkynes with NaBH4 to give sodium alkyl- and alkenyl-borohydrides which are converted into alcohols and alkenes, respectively.
