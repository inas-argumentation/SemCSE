Theoretical Study on Fundamental Performance of Pulsed Liquid Jet Pump Device with Gas-liquid Piston
Based on the fluid dynamics theory,the performance equations of liquid jet pump with gas_liquid piston are derived The influence of the main factors on the performance of this kind device is analyzed in the two different processes of refilling and driving Compared with the perormance equation for pulsed liquid and steady liquid jet pump device,the correlation of fundamental performance between them is stated
