A NOVEL SULFENYLATION REAGENT. SYNTHESIS AND REACTIONS OF N-(ARYLTHIO)ISATINS
N-(Arylthio)isatins (1) were prepared by treatment of arenesulfenyl chloride with isatin. The compounds 1 reacted with organometallic compounds, thiols, and sodium cyanide to give sulfides (2), disulfides (3), and thiocyanates (4).
