Immobilization of PLA_1 by chitosan/sodium alginate microcapsales
PLA1(phospholipase A1) was immobilizied by the methods of microcapsule. There studied the meathods of immobilization,immobilized carriers and dependence between recovery of enzyme activity and conditions of immobilizied PLA1. The results showed that the condition of microcapsule for immobilizied PLA1 were the chitosan(DD%=85%,Mw=1.3×105)concentration 1.5%,alginate concentration 3%,glutaradehyde concentration 1%,Ca2+ concentration 0.5moL/L,pH=6,Finally getting recovery of enzyme activity 81% and T1/2=350h.
