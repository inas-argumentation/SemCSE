The examination of the paintings’ compositions, symbolic attributes, and the sitters’ postures and gestures leads Dorn to conclude that these portraits are conscious self-representations of the diplomat for a contemporary audience that would understand their intrinsic symbolic meaning. The following three chapters center around specific subject matters of portraits of diplomats encountered by Dorn during her research. Chapter 5 focuses on diplomats who belonged to the clergy. Chapter 6 examines how early modern treatises on diplomacy (Castiglione) and rhetoric (Quintilian) affected the depiction of diplomats. Chapter 7, in turn, surveys the various relations between art and diplomacy in the early modern period and how they were expressed in eighteenth-century portraits. In chapter 8, Dorn broadens the scope of her research by considering portraits of outer-European diplomats. Here she argues that portraits of the Safavid envoy Sir Robert Shirley and the Ottoman diplomat Mehmed Said Efendi should be understood as a deliberate appropriation of contemporary conventions used for portraits of diplomats by these ambassadors in order to present themselves to a European audience. In her conclusion, Dorn summarizes the outcomes of her research before extending her approach into nineteenthand twentiethcentury examples. Because it is a very ambitious survey of both the period and the topic, the reader might feel at times that a closer examination of some of the concepts, such as early modern European notions of the Orient (chapter 8), would have been beneficial. However, this is compensated for by the richness of Dorn’s analyses of the portraits under consideration. This book is a must read for anyone interested in early modern portraiture or the relationship between art and diplomacy. Dorn successfully demonstrates how thinking about portraits of diplomats as a new subgenre of portraiture can be a productive way to stimulate new research into early modern art and diplomacy. This makes her first published book a significant contribution to the field.
