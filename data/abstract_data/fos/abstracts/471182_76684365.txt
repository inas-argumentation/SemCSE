Clinical Analysis on 319 Cases of Colorectal Carcinoma in the Elderly
Objective To investigate the clinical features of colorectal carcinoma in the elderly.Methods The clinical data of 319 cases(over 65 years old)admitted from Jun.2000 to Jun.2005 were analyzed retrospectively.Results 72.7% of the patients had preoperative comorbidities.Postoperative complication rate and mortality were 31.9% and 3.6% respectively.Preoperative comorbidities, age and emergency operation related to postoperative complications and mortality(P0.05).Conclusion More attention to the elderly, early diagnosis and active treatment of comorbidities are essential to lower postoperative complications rate and mortality.
