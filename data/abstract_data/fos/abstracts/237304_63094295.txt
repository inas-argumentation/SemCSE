Research on Improved HMIPv6 Handover Based on Prepared Allocation Care-of Address Pool
We proposed an improved HMIPv6 protocol,called PA-HMIPv6(Prepared Allocation-HMIPv6),which introduces a mechanism of access route pre-choice and care-of address pool in access routers and mobile anchor points.Access routers obtain handover information in advance by multiple connections,which implement care-of address configuration and duplicate address detection operation.The process of initialization and update of address pool,which completed before mobile node handover,seek to minimize the handover delay.The simulation results have demonstrated that PA-HMIPv6 protocol can effectively reduce the handover latency and packet loss rate.
