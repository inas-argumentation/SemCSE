NINTH VAVILOV CONFERENCE ON NONLINEAR OPTICS, NOVOSIBIRSK, JUNE 16-18, 1987: Sub-Doppler studies of Rydberg states of ammonia using multiphoton excitation
The band systems of NH3 and ND3 were recorded using multiphoton ionization and their rotational structures were analyzed. The vibronic band of ND3 was also recorded Doppler-free using the method of two-photon excitation with counterpropagating beams. Selected lines were examined in magnetic fields of up to 10.5 kG. Measurements of the Zeeman splittings for these lines yielded the electron orbital magnetic moment. The total internal angular momentum, deduced from the rotational analysis, was then analyzed into its separate electronic and vibrational components.
