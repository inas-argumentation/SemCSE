Di-electrons from η -meson Dalitz decay in proton-proton collisions
Abstract.The reaction $\ensuremath pp\rightarrow pp\eta\rightarrow pp\gamma e^+e^-$ is discussed within a covariant effective meson-nucleon theory. The model is adjusted to data of the subreaction $\ensuremath pp \rightarrow pp\eta$ . Our focus is on di-electrons from Dalitz decays of η -mesons, $\ensuremath \eta\rightarrow\gamma\gamma^* \rightarrow\gamma e^+e^-$ , and the role of the corresponding transition form factor $\ensuremath F_{\eta\gamma\gamma^*}$ . Numerical results are presented for the intermediate-energy kinematics of HADES experiments.
