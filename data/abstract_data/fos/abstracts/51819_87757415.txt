Effects of lasR rhlR defects on chronic Pseudomonas aeruginosa lung infection in rats
Objective To understand the importance of quorum sensing (QS) system in Pseudomonas aeruginosa (Pa) biofilm lung infection, the pathogenic effects of the wild-type Pa PAO1 in vivo were compared with QS double mutant PAO1 lasR rhlR. Methods Rats were challenged intratracheally with alginate embedded strain PAO1 and the mutant of QS in the concentration of 10~9 CFU/ml. One and two weeks after intratracheal chal- lenge with Pa, parameters were evaluated. Results One week after challenge, significantly milder microscopic and macroscopic lung pathology (P0.001) and lower lung bacterial load (P0.001) with lower IL-4 (P0.01), IL-10 (P0.05) production in the lungs were found in the lasR rhlR mutant group compared with the wild type PAO1. In the PAO1 group, the lung IL-10 level in week 2 was higher than that in the mutant group (P0.001) and also higher than that in week, 1(P0.01). Conclusion The wild type PAO1 could cause persis- tent and acute lung infection, which induced a T_H2-1ike cytokine response. The pathogenic effects of the lasR rhlR mutant were obviously milder than that of the wild type PAO1 in the animal models. So the functional lasR and rhlR genes of Pa play an important role in the establishment and persistence of the chronic lung infection.
