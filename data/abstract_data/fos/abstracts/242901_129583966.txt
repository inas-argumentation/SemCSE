Precambrian Zircon Age of Orthogneiss in the Shuswap Metamorphic Complex, British Columbia
Pb-U age determinations carried out on zircon from granodiorite gneiss of the core zone of Thor-Odin gneiss dome have provided isotopic evidence for involvement of Proterozoic basement rocks in the Mesozoic structures of the Shuswap Metamorphic Complex. The study has revealed that the zircons originally crystallized ago and suffered an episodic loss of lead ago.
