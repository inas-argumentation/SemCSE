Making the World a Media Space with Internet (Ubiquitous Media Space)
Users ofconventional digital videoconferencing tools are typically limited to a single static view of their environment with no opportunities to move about or to obtain a general mental model of the communication space. The Ubiquitous Media Space (UMS)offers a viable solution to these problems, using the Internet, while observing the restrictions of limited bandwidth.
