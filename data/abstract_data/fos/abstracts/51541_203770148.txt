Study on Serum Levels of FSH,LH and T in Male Rabbits
Blood samples were collected from jugular veins by surgery in male rabbits. The serum levels of follicular stimulating hormone (FSH), luteinizing hormone (LH) and testosterone (T) were measured by radioimmunoassay (RIA). The catheters, from which the blood samples were taken every 20 minutes for 10 times, were separately inserted in jugular veins of 5 healthy male rabbits. The results showed that the concentrations of serum FSH, LH and T were (244.95±45.49) IU/L, (433.64±188.44) IU/L and (132.46±92.24) nmol/L, respectively. Obvious fluctuation were observed in LH and T levels but not in FSH level.
