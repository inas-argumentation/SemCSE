Research on Information Entropy of Rough Functional Dependency in RRDM
The rough functional dependency theory play an important role in the theory set of rough relational database,and the rough sets data analysis is an important method for calculating several kinds of information uncertainty including rough databases. In this paper,the rough functional dependency of RRDM and its examples are studied by use of the rough sets theory. The binary relation between database tuples is firstly constructed according to the semantic connection between dates of RRDB,and its properties are also researched. Based on these,the paper propose a new definition of the concept of rough functional dependency of RRDM from the viewpoint of information entropy,its properties and some inference rules based on rough functional dependency are discussed finally.
