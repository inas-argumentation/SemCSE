Influence of Raw Material Mixture Ratio on Microstructure and Property of Nodular Graphite Cast Iron
Under the same casting process,the nodular graphite cast irons with the same chemical composition were prepared by the different mixture ratio of pig iron,scrap steel,silicoferrite and electrode graphite.The influences of raw material mixture ratio on the microstructure and mechanical properties were studied after the same low temperature normalizing.Based on the study results,the reasonable proposals of raw material mixture ratio were put forward for practical production needs.
