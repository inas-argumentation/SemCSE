Thermo-economic analysis of a novel regasification system with liquefied-natural-gas cold-energy
Abstract In this study, to improve the efficiency of the organic Rankine cycle (ORC)-style cold recovery system, a partial cold-energy recovery (PaCER) system was developed. The developed system additionally generates high-pressure natural gas that is sent to shore after regasification and supplied to duel fuel (DF) engines. To verify the efficiency of the developed system, the results of energy, exergy, and economic feasibility analyses were compared with existing methods at a specific seawater temperature. The results indicate that the PaCER system increases energy efficiency by as much as 5.8% and exergy efficiency by 2.4%–9.27%, based on a seawater temperature of 20 °C, compared to the 1ORC system. Further, it increases annual total net income by USD 1.86–5.49. In 3PaCER systems with added stages, energy and exergy efficiencies are even higher, with annual total net income of USD 10.97, which represents an increase of USD 7.34 compared to existing 1ORC systems.
