Impact of ERP on Traditional Accounting Business Process
According to the shortcoming of traditional accounting business process,the paper in detail illustrates the impact of ERP on accounting business process on the base of collecting information,interal control and change of management function.And by comparing the difference of accounting business process model of ERP and Event Approach,the paper indicates the shortcoming of accounting business process of ERP.Finally it indicates the theory of event approach will be the direction of future AIS.
