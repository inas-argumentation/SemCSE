Investigated synthesis of galato-oligosaccharide from whey mist by immobilized lactase
The optimum conditions of production of galacto-oligosaccharide were investigated using Immobilized lactase acts on desalting whey mist,and thin-layer chromatography estimates the quantity of galacto-oligosaccharide.The result showed as follow: The primary factors of this reaction were desalting whey mist concentration and time,while the lest one was pH.The synthesis of galacto-oligosaccharide amount was 29.78%,when time was 36h,temperature was 55℃,whey mist concentration was 50%,pH was 5.0,and the enzyme amount was 40U/g.
