Research Progress on Preparation Technology of Magnesium Alloy Sheet
With the gradual expansion of the field of application and the increase in demand for magnesium alloy, the sheet preparation technology is being paid more and more attention. The magnesium alloy sheet rolling technology in current application was introduced. The characteristics, advantages and disadvantages of the various rolling technologies were analyzed and discussed. The development status of China's magnesium alloy sheet production was described and the development direction of China's magnesium alloy sheet was presented.
