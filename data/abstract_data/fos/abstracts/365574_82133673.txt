Cloning and RNAi of Bmugt2, an UDP-glucosyltransferase gene in Bombyx mori.
Glucosidation plays a major role in inactivation and excretion of most compounds.We report here the characterization and RNAi of an UDP-glucosyltransferase gene in silkworm,namely Bmugt2.The coding sequence is 1578 bp in length(GenBank accession number: FJ237534) and can be translated into a protein of 525 amino acids.The deduced protein has about 30% overall amino-acid identity with other members in UGT family.RT-PCR result shows that the gene has high expression in silk gland in all the tissues tested and the expression was suppressed distinctly by corresponding dsRNA through microinjection into silkworm.However,the RNAi of the gene has no obvious effect on the cocoon color.
