Monjas y beatas: La escritura femenina en la espiritualidad Barroca Novohispana, siglos XVII y XVIII (review)
Overall, this eloquent study of various aspects of the colonial marriage market represents a quantitative and qualitative analysis that considerably goes beyond existing research. The work underlines once more the significance of regional studies for the history of women and men in Latin America. Through a successful combination of structures and individual fates it offers an interesting and informative access to the (gender) history of this border region.
