The Reflection and The Improvement of Chinese Criminial Investigation Procedure
Criminial investigation procedure of our country carries the characteristics of authoritarianism,even super-authoritarianism, which needs reforming and improving. From the view of contents and nature, this proposes that investigation procedure is both administrative and judicial. After reflecting the current situation of investigation procedure in its nature,this paper puts forward that the main disadvantage of Chinese criminial investigation procedure lies in the lack of judiciality,in particular,the serious lack of the rule of law. The author of this paper thinks that construction of investigation legalization should be strengthened; "investigation without law" and the investigation right of procuratorial organ should be established. Meanwhile,it is necessary to strengthen the supervision right of procuratorial organ. Criminial investigation and custody should be departed from each other. These methods would improve our country's criminial investigation procedure and make it legal and resonable.
