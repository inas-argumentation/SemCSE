The Social Transferring Period and the Transformation of Left-behind Women’s Role and Status
Driven by urbanization,a lot of men in the countryside have been looking for jobs in cities;in addition,the urban-rural dualistic structure leads to the division of cities and countryside,thus forming the new group of left-behind women in rural area.Their status has been transformed from a secondary status to a key position in the aspects of politics,agricultural production,social and family life.The role changes bring them heavy life and mental pressure.This paper makes an attempt to provide some feasible suggestions for the new problems confronted by the left-behind women in the procession of role change,such as providing the left-behind women more help,strengthening the construction of community safety,releasing their life pressure,enriching their cultural life,and improving their ability to get rich and so on.
