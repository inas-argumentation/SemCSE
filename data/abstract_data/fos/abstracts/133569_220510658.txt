Proton–proton fusion in new pionless EFT power counting
The zero-energy astrophysical S-factor for the fusion process $$p+p\rightarrow d+e^{+}+\nu _{e}$$ p + p → d + e + + ν e is calculated using pionless effective field theory ( $$~/\!\!\pi $$ / π EFT). In the present study, the order-by-order results are reproduced according to a new suggested power counting. The short range interactions in the S-wave pp scattering at leading order and the corrections in the next-to-leading order including the Coulomb interaction are introduced. In addition the Coulomb interaction between the incoming protons has been considered. The new nuclear $$~/\!\!\pi $$ / π EFT amplitude is compatible with renormalization-group invariance.
