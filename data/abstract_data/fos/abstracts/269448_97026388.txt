Synthesis and transport studies of one-dimensional ion conductors: AxGa4+XTi1−xO8 (A = Li, Na, K)
Abstract Ion exchange reactions of the sodium-ion conductor Na 0.7 Ga 4.7 Ti 0.3 O 8 were investigated. Exposure to molten LiNO 3 at 440 °C for 72 h resulted in a 2% decrease in the unit cell volume, whereas exposure to molten KNO 3 under similar conditions had very little effect on the lattice parameters. The electrical conductivity of unexchanged Na 0.7 Ga 4.7 Ti 0.3 O 8 ranged from ∼ 10 − 7 S/cm at 300 °C to 10 − 3 S/cm at 1000 °C with an activation energy of 0.75 eV. Ion exchange with LiNO 3 resulted in an increase in conductivity whereas exchange with KNO 3 resulted in a decrease. The differences in conductivity are attributed to differences in the size of the mobile cation.
