On the Practical Dilemma and Logic Route of the Academic-oriented Governance Structure of Higher Learning Institutions
The value purport and demand of improving the structure of governance of higher learning institutions lies in courting university spirit,showing academic value,ensuring university autonomy and demonstrating academic character.But the gradually administration led to the shrinking academic power,which caused administration-dominated governance structure.Constructing the academic-oriented governance structure should be the inevitable choice and logic route of governance structure in higher learning institutions in China.In the constructing the academic-oriented governance structure,we should establish the supreme decision-making position of academic committee in the academic affairs,and build a discipline-based and research-dominated and academic-oriented of grass-roots academic organization,and intensify service function of executive power,and a supervising organ should be established and completed in higher learning institutions and that a full play be given to the role of the representative assembly of teachers and staffs and the students' union in running the institutions.
