Effect of Thermal Method of Reclamation on Technological Property of Silica Sand
The gas evolution capacity, proportion of gravity to volume, grain size and tensile strength from four sources, and the influence of thermal method of reclamation on them have been examined. It has been concluded that the above-mentioned technological properties differ with different sources and the changing rule of the properties varies with reclamation times. It has been proved that high-temperature baking has no negative effect on the properties but in favour of the quality of moulds and castings, which provides reliable technical evidence for foundry sand reclamation.
