Study on Microstructure and Properties of ZK60+Y Magnesium Alloy After Plastic Deformation
A melted ZK60+Y magnesium alloy was extruded at 380℃,then parts of the extruded sticks were rolled into the sheet with 3mm thickness.The microstructure and mechanical properties of the alloy were studied and analyzed by optical microscopy,X-diffraction,tensile test and transmission electron microscopy(TEM).The results show that there are lots of precipitates in the microstructure of the rolling sheet,whose size includes nano grade.The yield strength,tensile strength and extensibility of the rolling sheet are all lower than those of the extruded sticks,and the values are 263 MPa,235 MPa and 15%,respectively.
