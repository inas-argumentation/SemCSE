Failure Analysis on Joint of Boiler Reheater Connecting Tube
By the methods of microscope analysis,mechanical analysis,chemical analysis,metallographic examination and SEM,the joint of boiler reheater connecting tube(12Cr1MoV) in which cracks occur many time was analyzed.The results indicate that the crack of boiler reheater connecting tube joint is the reheating crack and the primary season in which the cracks occur is excessive stress.
