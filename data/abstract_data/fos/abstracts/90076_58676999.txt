Research on the Fitness Function in Papers Analysis Based on Evolutionary Computation
Computational intelligence which based on data is the focus in the field of intelligence research, and evolutionary computation as an important branch of computational intelligence has wide applications. The fitness function is the key factors which determines the performance of evolution. Researches on the applications of evolutionary computation in papers analysis, and focuses on the construction of the fitness function which depends on different evolutionary stages. This construction method can ensure the population diversity and accelerate the evolution rate, thereby enhance the performance of evolution.
