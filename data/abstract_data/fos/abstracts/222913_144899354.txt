Predicting Educational Suitability of Children in an Assessment Unit.
The criterion problem for ascertainment of children in an assessment unit and their placement in suitable special education systems in investigated. Twenty-four variables were analysed by the Principal Component Method and then rotated by Varimax and Promax Criteria. Seven factors are identified. The investigation indicates in particular the importance of basic school skills, intellectual ability and placement recommendations, social adequacy, and physical appearance in deciding placement. It also supports the need for a prolonged and comprehensive assessment before recommending placement for children in an assessment.
