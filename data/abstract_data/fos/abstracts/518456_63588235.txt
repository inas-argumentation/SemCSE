Hardware Architecture for Packet Re-Ordering in TCP
TCP(Transmission Control Protocol) is the fundamental protocol in current Internet. For the high performance severs′ requirement, the hardware architecture to implement TCP is researched and developed by many international cooperatives and institutes. In this paper, the hardware architecture to reorder the mis-order packets was discussed. High scalability and performance was reached in this scheme, while the ZBT SRAM was used as the memory and the pipeline is used to computation. The principles and data structure of the design were presented in this paper. And the performance and critical parameter configuration were analyzed too.
