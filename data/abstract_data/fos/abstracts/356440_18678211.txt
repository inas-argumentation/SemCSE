Magnetic field reconnection: A first-principles perspective
Recent satellite missions and computer simulations of charged-particle dynamics in Earth’s magnetosphere are helping unravel the mysteries behind the breaking and reforming of magnetic field lines and the concomitant acceleration of electrons to high energies.
