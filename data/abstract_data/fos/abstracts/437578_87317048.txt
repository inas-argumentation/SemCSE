Analysis of Floral Characteristics of Three Prunus domestica×armeniaca Cultivars
In order to investigate the factors that affect fruit set ratio in Prunus domestica ×armeniaca ‘ Fengweimeigui',Prunus domestica ×armeniaca ‘Weidi',and Prunus domestica ×armeniaca ‘Konglongdan',we investigated the floral characters through different types of flowers,pollen quantity,correlation of the length of pedicel and pistil,pollen viability and controlled pollination tests.The results showed:(1)The percentage of long pistil of ‘Konglongdan'was higher than that of ‘Weidi' and ‘Fengweimeigui'.The difference of different types of flowers and the length of pistil in the same species was significant.(2)The length of pedicel of flowers that bore fruits was longer than that of flowers which had long pistil and did not bear fruit,and the latter one was longer than that of flowers which the pistil aborted.The length of pedicels and pistils had significant positive correlativity(p 0.01).(3)The flowers with aborted pistil had more pollen than that with long pistil,however,within the same floral type,‘Eeidi' had more pollen than ‘Fengweimeigui',and ‘Konglongdan' had the least pollen among the three species,the difference of the same floral types between species was significant.(4)The pollen of ‘ Weidi' did not have viability,but the pollen viability of ‘Fengweimeigui' was higher than that of ‘ Konglongdan'.The pollen viability of long pistil flower was higher than that of aborted pistil flowers.The different extracted solution from style played a role in promoting the pollen germination,although the promoting degree was different due to different floral type.(5)The results of artificial pollination showed that the matting system of ‘Weidi' was compeletely out-crossing,while ‘Fengweimeigui' and ‘Konglongdan' was mainly outcrossing with partial self-compatibility.The result would provide a theoretic basis for interspecific pollination and fertilization of plumcot.
