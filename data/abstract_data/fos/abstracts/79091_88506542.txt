On the eigentheory of operators which exhibit a slow variation
A class of linear operators which exhibit slow variation is considered. If the kernel of the operator is K(x — y, \e(x + y)), e the parameter of slowness, then its Wigner transform is defined to be K(p, q) = J K(u, q)exp(-iup) du. The eigenvalues of such operators are shown to follow an area rule: if the curve Xn = K(p, q) contains the area s/(A) = (2n + 1 )ne then Xn is an eigenvalue. Forms for the corresponding eigenfunctions are also obtained. Classical WKB theory is shown to be a special case and other examples are given.
