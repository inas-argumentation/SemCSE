Technological Status of Copper,Lead,Zinc,Iron and Sulfur Recovery from Cyanide Tailings
It is significant to recover and utilize cyanide tailings because it often contains many kinds of valuable elements such as gold,silver,copper,lead,zinc,iron,sulfur and so on.The technological status of technology for copper,lead,zinc,iron and sulfur recovery was introduced and the technical innovation direction for efficient utilization and pollution control of cyanide tailings was put forward.
