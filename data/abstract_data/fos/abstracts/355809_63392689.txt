Constructing a High-Speed and Secure Campus Area Network
Campus network is an important indicator scale of a school information technology and modern management level. A high-speed, steady, and secure campus network is one of the vital base establishment for sponsor schools. Discusses the high-speed campus area network design scheme and system security management strategy based on FDDI ring structure and the three-tier exchange VLAN technology in allusion to issues of programming and management about the upgrade reconstruction of the campus network.
