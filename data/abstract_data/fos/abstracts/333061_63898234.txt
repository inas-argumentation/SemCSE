Novel Method Based on Fuzzy 2DPLA for Application of Face Recognition
In this paper,a novel method based on fuzzy 2DPLA is proposed which combines fuzzy set,two dimension,PCA,and LDA methods.This method first integrates fuzzy set into 2DPCA and 2DLDA;what′s more,it reduces dimension through horizontal and vertical directions of the image matrix using Fuzzy 2DLDA and Fuzzy 2DPCA,respectively,which makes data better robustness and self-adaptive;Last but more importantly,it uses the F-norm classification measure based on the matrix instead of the 2-norm based on the vector.In experimental phase,Yale Face Database B,ORL and FERET Database are used by the test.Experimental results demonstrate that our method has better robustness and higher recognition rate than the state-of-art methods.
