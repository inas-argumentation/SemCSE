Design of Vehicle ABS Rolling Drum Inert Testing Bench
The status of domestic vehicle safety testing equipment and the working principle of ABS are simply reviewed in this paper. At present, the domestic vehicle safety testing equipment cannot evaluate the brake performance of ABS. Basing the object's kinetic energy can be expressed by the object's moment of inert, we designed a testing bench which can simulate the running state of vehicles. The testing bench not only can evaluate the brake performance of ABS, but also substitute the vehicle safety testing equipment, improving testing efficiency, and have a good market.
