Relationship between activities of lymphocytes and apoptosis by Triptolide
Relationship between activities of lymphocytes and apoptosis by Triptolide$$$$ DAI Chunsun, YANG Junwei, CHEN Zhaohong, et al. Research Institute of Nephrology, Jinling Hospital, Nanjing University School of Medicine, Nanjing 210002 【 Abstract 】 Objective To study the mechanisms of Triptolide on immunosuppression by means of observation on Triptolideinduced apoptosis of activated lymphocytes. Methods Spleen cells of BALB/c mice were stimulated by mitogen such as PHAP, PMA and interleukin2 (rhIL2). Activity and apoptosis of lymphocytes were measured with 3 HTdR incorporation, and with DNA fragment determination and membrane damage, respectively. Results Triptolide could induce apoptosis of activated lymphocytes in dosedependent fashion, but not resting lymphocytes. The degree of susceptibility to apoptosis was correlated with the levels of cell cycling as measured by 3HTdR incorporation during S phase; and DNA fragmentation was associated proportionally with time elapse after adding Triptolide. The number of cells in S phase was proportional to the amount of apoptotic cells induced by Triptolide. Conclusion These data suggest that the suicide of activated cycling T cells induced by Triptolide may be one of the mechanisms for the immunosuppression. 【 Subject words 】 Triptolide Apoptosis Lymphocytes
