The Calculation Model of the Attenuation Due to Clouds or Fog and the Analysis of its Characteristic
This paper focuses on the available calculation model of the millimetric wave propagating attenuation due to clouds and fog in atmosphere.It's difficult to calculate its attenuation accurately because of the diversity of the kind of clouds and fog and the inhomogeneity of the particle density in real environment.Real- time observation method is generally used in calculating the attenuation due to clouds and fog,butit is disadvantage for quantitative analysis.So we use the Rayleith scattering approximation to calculate the attenuation due to clouds and fog in a certain area according to its weather data.At last,we simply analyze its results.
