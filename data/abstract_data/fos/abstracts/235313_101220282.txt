Study on synthesis and aroma of alkyl 2-methoxybutyl sulfides
Six alkyl 2-methoxybutyl sulfides were obtained by refluxing 1-alkylthio-2-butanols with iodomethane for 3～4 hours under the action of sodium hydride in tetrahydrofuran.The alkyl groups included ethyl,n-propyl,n-butyl,n-hexyl,n-octyl and furfuryl.All products synthesized above were identified by IR,MS and 1HNMR.Alkyl 2-methoxybutyl sulfides possess a sweet,vegetable,onion,garlic and radish aroma.
