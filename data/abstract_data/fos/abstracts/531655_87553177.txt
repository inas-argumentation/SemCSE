Low Phoshorus Stress on the Physiological Metabolism of Hevea Seedling
By hydroponic culture method,the effects of 31mg/L(CK)、10m g/L、5 mg/L、1mg/L、0mg/L different phosphorus(P) stress levels on the physiological metabolism of Hevea seedling were studied.The results showed that the soluble sugar content(SSC) in root,stem and leaf were decreased with the P level.The soluble protein content(SPC) of stabilization leaf and root were increased under low phoshorus stress,but the SPC of young leaf and stem were decreased.The chlorophyll content was increased under low phoshorus stress.The nitrate reductase(NR) activity and root activity were decreased with the P level.
