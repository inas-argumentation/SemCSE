Study on enzymatic hydrolysis of mutton and synthesis of mutton flavor aroma
The enzymatic hydrolysis of mutton using Flavourzyme(F) and Protamex(P) were studied res-pectively.Furthermore,the synthesis of mutton flavor aroma of enzymatic hydrolysates through Maillard reaction was studied.The results showed that the optimal hydrolysis condition of mixed enzyme are:F:P=1:1,45 ℃,pH 7.0,substrate content 30%,enzyme concentration 0.8%(E/S).With the 50 g enzymatic hydrolysates got under the optimal hydrolysis condition of mixed enzyme and added HVP 8.0 g,glucose 2.0 g,xylose 1.0 g,alanine 0.6 g,I+G0.8 g,suet 4.0 g,L-CysHCl 0.5 g,microzyme 5.0 g,VB 10.5 g in 110 ℃,30 min,the ideal mutton flavor aroma can be synthesized through Maillard reaction.
