Reliability Technology of Coalfield Fire Zone Monitoring System of Wireless Sensor Networks
The reliability technology is the key of the monitoring system's realization in coalfield fire zone.Time synchronization and the data transmission from the monitoring node to the center are two center taches of reliability.Most of the current time synchronization algorithms are computationally intensive and ask too much of hardware.A low power cost time synchronization algorithm based on error correction via linear fitting and local clock self-correction is proposed,which is simple and easy to implement on single chip microprocessor.A self-adapting heartbeat mechanism based on Winsocket has designed for keeping the reliability and stability of the TCP/IP remote communication.This self-adapting heartbeat mechanism can request to reconnect with remote clients or server when network is at the jam and inform or at the disconnection.The testing of the system running status indicated that the time synchronization algorithm can effectively reduce clock errors among the nodes of wireless sensor networks which are caused by time deviation,and the heartbeat mechanism had been improved the data communication's reliability and stability of the monitoring system and realized the system's maintenance-free.
