Zhu Ke-zhen'idea about faculty as university soul and its contemporary value
Zhu Ke-zhen'theory that teacher is the soul of university reflected his teacher-originated and scholar-originated value tendency.Both of student'growing up and college'development rely on teachers and famous teachers.This idea is different prominently from today's official-centered and government-centered value tendency in our univercity.According to the idea,he made Zhejiang University change from local university into a national famous university.The idea is beneficial to the development of university and faculty building in modern China.
