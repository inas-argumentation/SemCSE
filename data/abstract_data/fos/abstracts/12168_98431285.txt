Halide-Bridged Zigzag Chain of Tetrakis(acetamidato)dirhodium Cationic Radical Assisted by Hydrogen Bond
The reaction of a Rh25+ cationic radical salt, 2,2-cis-[Rh2(acam)4(H2O)2]ClO4 (Hacam = acetamide), with sodium halides in aqueous solution gave crystals of [Rh2(acam)4Cl]n·7nH2O and [Rh2(acam)4Br]n, both of which consist of zigzag chain of (–Rh–Rh–X–)n, which is supported by hydrogen bond between amidato–NH and amidato–O atoms for the chain of the bromide and between O atoms of amidato bridges through interstitial water molecules for the chloride chain.
