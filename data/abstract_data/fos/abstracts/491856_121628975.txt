Investigation of the diffusion of titanium into LiNbO3 in the fabrication of optical waveguides
A study was made of the dependence of the transverse refractive index distribution in Ti-diffused LiNbO3 crystals on the diffusion time. A mathematical model was proposed for the process of titanium diffusion into LiNbO3. A method was developed for predicting the characteristics of optical diffused waveguides fabricated in different ways. Data were obtained on the dispersion of the waveguide parameters in the wavelength range 0.44–1.15 μ.
