Validity of the PPVT, Knox Cubes and ITPA with Aphasic Adults
Ratings of the abilities for oral self-expression and for understanding others were obtained for 20 aphasic male patients in a VA general hospital from nursing personnel sufficiently well acquainted with the patients to feel confident in their ratings. The 25 raters' combined judgments resulted in rkks (Ebel, 1951; Winer, 1962, pp. 96-104) of 94 for self-expression and .81 for understanding others, with the two ratings correlating .73.' Ss ranged in age from 56 to 78 yr., the mean being 69.5 yr. (SD = 5.6 yr.). Peabody Picture Vocabulary Test IQs ranged from 55 to 111, with a median of 83 ( M = 58). The PPVT and the ITPA Auditory Decoding demonstrated reasonable correlation (.63 and .58) with self-expression but not with understanding others. The most likely explanation for this consistent effect appears to be the lower degree of reliability achieved in the ratings of understanding, with consequent attenuation of the validity measures.
