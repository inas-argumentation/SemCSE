In memory of Olga Casals
Olga Casals was born in T etouan (Morocco) in 1957. She moved to Zaragoza (Spain) at the age of 15. She received her degree and Ph.D. in Telecommunications Engineering in 1983 and 1986, respectively, from the Polytechnic University of Catalonia (UPC) in Barcelona, Spain. After her Ph.D. she joined the Department of Computer Architecture as an Associate Professor where she became a Full Professor in 1994 and served as the head of the department between 1998 and 2001. In 1988 she founded the ‘‘Computer Network’’ research group and built a team focussing on the analysis, design and evaluation of broadband communication networks. At the beginning Olga focused her group on ATM performance issues (between 1988 and 1995), mainly in the context of the European RACE project ‘‘Technology of ATD’’ and its follow-up projects. Later on, between 1992 and 1998, she did innovative work on the analysis and design of MAC protocols for optical access networks. More recently, she directed her group’s focus in the area of wireless and mobile networks. She had many plans in this area, which unfortunately she was not able to realize. Olga was able to direct her research towards fields and problems of high importance and relevance, both for the academic and the industrial world. In all these areas, she has published many papers in international telecommunication and networking journals (Computer Networks, Journal of Operations Research, Performance Evaluation, Telecommunication Systems, IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking, etc.) and presented her research in several international conferences.
