An in-mass transducer for measuring the static pressure ratio (K) in grain storage bins
A transducer was developed for measuring the static pressure ratio (K) within a grain mass. The transducer consisted of three round, fixed edge diaphragms which were mounted on a wire frame to measure pressures vertically, horizontally, and at a 45° angle. The diaphragms of the in-mass transducer (IMT) were calibrated with a water column and in a calibration bin filled with soybeans which had an inflatable, flexible diaphragm to exert uniform overburden pressures.
