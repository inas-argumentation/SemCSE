Digital Depth Inspection and Monitoring Based on CAT for Communication Technology
This paper has deeply investigate the digital inspection and computer monitoring based on digital and CAT technology. Three main research aspects are discussed in detail for digital depth-inspection based on CAT, including: Digital inspection instrument, Communication interface of depth-inspection and Quality monitoring of depth-inspection result. In this approach, we fabricate a special apparatus with digital indicator—depth meter, and monitoring software is developed by ourselves with visual c. Then, this paper has also put forwards a factual case with CAT measuring system, and results indicate that this depth digital inspection and monitoring approach is effective and convenient. Introduction As the main trend of quality inspection for manufacturing factory, digitalization has got great achievement, including display, interface output and computation processing, etc. because of the easy-to-use of inspection with instrument and the easiness of cooperation with computer processing, which eliminates manual error and decreases tiredness of manual labor. [1] Computer aided testing CAT has got great progress with the developing of computer technology. Main points refer to three aspects in product quality testing: (a) computer application including data acquisition, digital display of inspection result and data processing of quality testing, farther more, digitalization of whole procedure of quality inspection with computer, (b) integration of quality inspection and result monitoring, (c) the visualization and intelligence of inspection results upon computer. [2-4] Computer monitoring of inspection results in product quality testing has also got great progress with the development of CAT technology. Based on CAT technology for measuring system, product quality monitoring with Shewhart chart can improve greatly efficiency and effectiveness. [2] With the development of advanced communication technology, interface communication, computer communication and data communication has become new research and developing point especially in measuring system, and more and more application are taken effect in quality inspection and monitoring.[5,6] Composite of Digital Depth Measuring System Generally, depth inspection is a usual inspection item in quality testing.
