A Discussion of the Naming of Four Singing Styles in the National Youth Singing Competition and its Provisions about the Songs for Competition
The National Young Singer TV Grand Prix sets up four singing styles, namely, the original eco-singing, bel canto, ethnic singing and pop singing. It is argued in the paper that it is improper to name them this way and four names such as China soil wind singing, the European classical singing, contemporary country and contemporary pop vocal singing style should be adopted instead.
