N-Cyano-N′-diethylaminophenyl-1,4-naphthoquinonediimines: New Chromophores for Second-order Nonlinear Optical Materials
New chromophores for second-order nonlinear optical materials, N-cyano-N′-diethylaminophenyl-1,4-naphthoquinonediimines, were proposed. The electro-optic poled polymers showed large second-order nonlinear hyperpolarizability (μβ) in a constant electric field at incident irradiation wavelength of 1.3 μm, being 4 - 6 times than that of commonly used Disperse Red 1.
