Out of the Plight of Growth-type Universities: The Strategy Analysis of the Developing Path from Growth-type Universities to Mature Universities
Facilitating a large number of growth-type universities into mature universities,will not only promote universities' development,but also be helpful to advance the quality of China's higher education.However,there seemed to be a plight which made the growth-type universities remain at the same level for ever.Thus it's necessary to analyze the advantages and disadvantages of growth-type universities,so that they could make the right decisions and reach a new stage.
