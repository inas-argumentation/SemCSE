Research on the synthetical evaluation system of students' PE grades in college and university under idea of new curriculum
Based on "The National Programme for Education of College PE"made by Ministry of Education,adopting the methods such as documental data,"Delphi"and statistics,the authors eatablish a set of synthetical evaiuation system on the grade of college PE.It also designs a kind of new system of assessment to the college students' PE grades which which embody demands of College PE.It provides a good specific operation method for the college sports teachers to evaluate the grade of College PE,it also offer parameter of quantization and foundation of theory in the new era for the refom of college PE.
