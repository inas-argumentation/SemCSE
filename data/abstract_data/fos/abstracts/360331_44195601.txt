(23) The initial design of such detectors made use of inert inserts within the liquid argon, both as electrodes and as converters for electromagnetic and/or hadronic cascades. It was recognized early that these inserts degrade the energy resolution of such calorimeters. The first liquid argon shower counters were made with many thin converter sheets in order to minimize this effect.
