Teaching Reformation Practice of Computer Application Basic in Vocational College
Computer Application Basic is not only one of the most basic selected courses,but also a very practical course in vocational college.However,the traditional teaching approaches have failed to meet the current needs in teaching the course.With many years' teaching experience,puts forward some innovational teaching methods such as playing games,hypothetically situational setting,task-based,enlightenment,case study and grouping teaching,etc.
