Active Attack Security Based Extensible FGS Encryption Algorithm
Attacking actively to the relativity of temporal and spatial with the baselayer selective encryption algorithm,this paper finds that the relativity of interframe is very serious in the encryption video sequences,the effect of encryption is unreached,and proposes an active attack security based extensible FGS encryption algorithm and employ a strategy of encrypting both base layer and enhancement layer.Based on the experiments on sufficient QCIF video sequences,this encryption algorithm both base layer and enhancement layer show good effect in the encryption speed,encryption effect and play-back quality.
