Hereditary Transmission of AZ91D Magnesium Alloy
The magnesium ingots from the three factories A,B and C are re-melted in this experiment.The microstructure of both un-re-melted and re-melted magnesium in got are discussed in the present article,so does that of the added recycled scrap magnesium alloy.The results indicate that the microstructure of re-melted magnesium alloy takes on a hereditary transmission similar or resembling to that of feed materials.The characteristics of the short range order structure atomic group in alloy is the same as or resembles to that of feed materials in its melting process. The hereditary transmission of magnesium alloy can be explained by the atomic conglobation theory.
