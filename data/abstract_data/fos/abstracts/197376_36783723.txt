Profound hyponatraemia following a tramadol overdose.
Sir,

Hyponatraemia is a common electrolyte disturbance associated with prolonged hospitalization and increased mortality. Among numerous aetiologies, tramadol is not routinely included in the list of causative medications. This case highlights an important biochemical side effect, and symptoms that may be easily overlooked without appropriate investigation.

A 56-year-old man presented to hospital 12 h after an intentional overdose of 60 tramadol (50 mg) tablets and 20 co-dydramol (10/500) tablets. The past medical history included COPD, treated with spiriva and seretide inhalers. There was no other current or previous medication use.
