Studying the ferromagnetic–paramagnetic phase transition in thin films of L10 FePt1–xRhx
FePtRh films with different Rh concentrations (FePt1–xRhx) are fabricated by magnetron sputtering. The magnetic structure and ferromagnetic–paramagnetic phase transition in thin films of L10 FePt1–xRhx with different Rh concentrations (0 ≤ x ≤ 0.40) are studied. It is demonstrated that thin films of FePt1–xRhx with 0 < x < 0.34 are in the ferromagnetic state with high magnetocrystalline anisotropy energy at room temperature, while similar films with 0.34 < x < 0.4 are paramagnetic.
