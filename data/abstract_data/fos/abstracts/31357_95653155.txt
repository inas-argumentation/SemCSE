Synthesis of 3,6,7-triazabicyclo[3.3.0]octenes by 1,3-cycloaddition with N-arylpyrrolidin-2-ones
The 1,3-dipolar cycloaddition of diazomethane to 1-(4-R-phenyl)-5H-pyrrolidin-2-one was investigated. Some properties of the triazabicyclo[3.3.0]octenes, including the results of an x-ray diffraction analysis, were studied.
