Design and Implementation of Mobile GIS Application Software Based on Component Technology
With the development of transportation and communication,Mobile GIS will be the trend of GIS development. To solve these problems, component technology is used in this thesis. By reconstructing architecture of Mobile GIS system with component technology, the complicated Mobile GIS system can be divided into some functional components. And by reusing and integrating these functional components, it is possible, with reasonable cost, to rapidly develop GIS application software for various mobile devices.
