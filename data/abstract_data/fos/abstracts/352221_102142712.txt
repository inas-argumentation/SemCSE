Synthesis and characterization of polyaniline-poly(propylene oxide)prepared in BMIHSO_4 ionic liquid
Polyaniline-poly(propylene oxide)(PAN-PPO)was prepared in an ionic liquid 1-butyl-3-methylimidazolium hydrogen sulfate(BMIHSO_4) by cyclic voltammetry.In comparison with polyaniline(PAN),the polymerization rate of PAN-PPO in BMIHSO_4 was much higher than that of PAN.In addition,PAN-PPO was characterized by FTIR spectrometry and scanning electron microscopy(SEM).The result of FTIR showed that aniline and propylene oxide have been copolymerized in the ionic liquid BMIHSO_4,and SEM photograph showed that PAN-PPO was composed of nanofibers with diameter about 80 nm.
