An Approach to Quantify Fragments for Placement of Reconfigurable Hardware Task
The fragments of placement for reconfigurable hardware task in reconfigurable computing are one of critical factors to affect task to be placed successfully and system resource utilization.An approach named of QFTD that aims at quantifying the fragments of task placement was proposed in this paper,and its kernel is to abstract the fragments in a 3D angle,then to quantify the fragments′ value of task waiting to be placed.Emulation results showed that when applied to placer it can markedly reduce task rejection ratio and increase the chip resource utilization.
