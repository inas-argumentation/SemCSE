Study on the enzymatic processing technology of instant ginkgo powder
Gingko resources are extremely rich in China.The ginkgo seed contains protein,fat,carbohydrate,amino acids and the trace elements.It has high medical and edible value.However,the processing products of ginkgo fruit are not very much in the market.That the study on instant powder of ginkgo is not only benefit to increase the types of healthy products,but also benefit to solve the problem of the preservation.The starch was hydrolyzed by enzyme under the condition of pH6.0.By the single factor and orthogonal experiment,the optimistic conditions for starch hydrolysis was determined,which was 80 ℃,30 min.The application experi-ments were carried out for further studies on the instant ginkgo powder.
