Immunomodulatory activity of extracts from mycelia of a strain of Hirsutella sp.
In the process of a screening for bioactive metabolites of entomogenous fungi,the fermentation broth of a strain of Hirsutella sp.RCEF0851 exhibited strong immuno-enhancive properties.Therefore,we launched a systematic studies on immunomodulatory activities of different extracts from mycelia of RCEF0851 by using real time qPCR method.The results showed that the ethyl acetate extract could enhance gene transcription of TNF-α,but had no effect on gene transcription of IL-1β and IL-6,water and methanol extracts could up-regulate gene transcription of IL-1β,IL-6 and TNF-α.This means that the extracts of RCEF0851 have immuno-enhancive activities.
