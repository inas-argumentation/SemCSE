Event-area Based Energy Aware Routing for Wireless Sensor Network
In this paper,an event-area transmitting model is proposed based on the property of practical application in Wirelss Sensor Networks(WSNs).An Event-Area Based Energy Aware routing(ABEA) is proposed in this paper.ABEA chooses the node with higher residual energy in the event region as a root.Sensor nodes within the region form an aggregation tree rooted at that node according to their hops to root and remaining energy.The aggregated data are transmitted to the sink along an energy aware path.Simulation results show that ABEA not only can achieve less energy consumption and smaller transmission delay,but also balance the energy consumption over the whole network.
