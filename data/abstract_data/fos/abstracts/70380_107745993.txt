LASER APPLICATIONS AND OTHER TOPICS IN QUANTUM ELECTRONICS: Some applications of a high-sensitivity optical projection system in physical experiments
An experimental investigation was made of some potential applications, in physical experiments, of a high-sensitivity system for amplification and phase conjugation of weak optical radiation. The results demonstrated, in particular, the feasibility of recording the spatial structure of radiation experiencing spontaneous scattering in various media and a study was made of interference of weak optical beams with a complex transverse structure.
