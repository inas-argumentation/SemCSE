But what is the process by which the NSF decides to fund one proposal and reject another? How open is the competition for ideas in economics within NSF? To understand the NSF funding process, I decided to investigate whom NSF funds and how its external review panels, which make decisions about research underwriting, are constructed. I came to this set of questions out of firsthand experience with NSF proposals that I was asked by the NSF to review before their submission to review panels.
