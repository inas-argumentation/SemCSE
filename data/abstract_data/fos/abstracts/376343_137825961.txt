Study on Surface Defects of SPA-H Steel Slab by Compact Strip Production
For the production of continuous casting and rolling SPA-H steel slab by CSP(Compact Strip Production) factors effecting longitudinal cracks and other defects were analyzed,including protective slag,composition of steel,heat flux through the mold,drawing speed etc.Based on the analysis it was suggested that steel carbon is controlled to 0.06%,slag moisture ≤0.5%,and using electromagnetic braking technique and secondary cooling in the steel slab production. After taking these measures the continuous casting slab surface quality is improved and scrap rate is reduced by 4%.
