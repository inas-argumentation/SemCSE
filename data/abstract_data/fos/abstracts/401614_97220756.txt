Isomerizations of citronellal to isopulegol and geraniol to linalool catalyzed by solid acids and bases.
Citronellal was isomerized to isopulegol over SiO2–Al2O3, TiO2–ZrO2, FeSO4, NiSO4, Ti(SO4)2, Zr(SO4)2, and Al2O3, with the selectivity higher than 91%. Geraniol was also isomerized mainly to linalool over SiO2–Al2O3 and those four metal sulfates, and the selectivity on SiO2–Al2O3 and FeSO4 was higher than 81%.
