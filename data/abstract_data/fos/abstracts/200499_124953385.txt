Experimental studies of relativistic backward wave oscillator with resonant reflector
Experimental studies of relativistic backward wave oscillator(BWO) with a resonant reflector are presented. The resonant reflector is a circular wave guide with longitudinal irregularity. The device emits TM 01 microwave at 8.874GHz. The power of BWO is about 170MW at applied magnetic field B z =0.7T, and 370MW at B z =1.7T, both with microwave pulse duration of 10 ns. Experimental results show that BWO with resonant reflector works well at low applied magnetic field, and primary experimental studies will be the foundation of further research work.
