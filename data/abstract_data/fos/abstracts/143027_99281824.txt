Synthesis and evaluation of molecularly imprinted polymers using malachite green as template
Using malachite green(MG) as template molecule,the molecular imprinted polymers(MIPs)was synthesized adopting molecular imprinting technique.The affinity of polymers synthesized under different functional monomers,template-functional monomer ratio,crosslinkers and functional monomer croslinker ratio was discussed.The structure of polymers were analysed by IR.Scatchard analysis indicated that one equivalence binding site formed in the molecular imprinted polymer between the reaearching concentration scope.
