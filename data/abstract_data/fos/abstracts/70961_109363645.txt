Network reliability and availability analysis to minimize downtime costs for communication networks
Abstract There is an increase in the number of communication networks having link (arc) failures and repairs. One needs to analyze the network in terms of failure and repair rates in order to optimize system configuration or maximize system availability. The downtime costs for these communication networks can be substantial. Two techniques for analyzing a communications network, a method developed by Pulat (1986) and Monte Carlo simulation, are introduced and compared. Both techniques are applied to a simple communications network involving series and parallel repairable arcs.
