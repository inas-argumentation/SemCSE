The research of tumor vaccine with apoptotic tumor cell and tumor cell lysate
Objective To find apoptotic tumor cell and tumor cell lysate as antigens, presented by DC, and to induce special CTL in vitro.Methods Mature DC loading with apoptotic tumor cell and tumor cell lysate to stimulate autologous T cells in order to induce special CTL and to detect special lysis of corresponding target cells by LDH. Results ~137Cs and MMC can induce tumor cell apoptosis with percentage as 93.0%±6.20%, 88.70%±8.28% separately. Apoptotic tumor cell induced by ~137 Cs or MMC and tumor cell lysate were used as tumor antigen to stimulate autologous T cells. The percentage of CD4~+/IFN-γ~+, CD8~+/IFN-γ~+ and cytotoxicity of T cells stimulated by apoptotic tumor cells increased as compared with tumor lysate.Conclusion Apoptotic tumor cell can induce special CTL and potentially function the candidate of tumor vaccine.
