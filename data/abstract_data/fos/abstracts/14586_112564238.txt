The Reform and Innovation of Practical Teaching on Database Technology for Non-Computer Majors
The practical teaching is very important and essential during the database technology teaching, so how to improve the quality of practical teaching is particularly critical for the non-computer major students. Through analyzing the present domestic situation of database practical teaching, and proposes a lot of educational reform measures such as the innovation teaching mentality, the reform teaching method, the conformity course content, the innovation inspection way and so on, these measures have obtained the better teaching effect in reality.
