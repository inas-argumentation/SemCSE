Reaction of 5,5-dimethyl-4-cyanomethylene-1,3-oxathiolan-2-one with amines and ammonia
A simple method for the synthesis of 5,5-dimethyl-4-cyanomethylene-2-cyanomethyl-2-[1-methyl-1-(carbamoyloxy)ethyl]-1,3-dithiolanes was developed on the basis of the reaction of 5,5-dimethyl-4-cyanomethylene-1,3-oxathiolan-2-one with amines (primary and secondary) and ammonia.
