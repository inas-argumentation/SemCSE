An Empirical Equation for Predicting Soluble Phosphorus Loads from Agricultural Watersheds
AN empirical equation has been developed to predict soluble reactive phosphorus (SRP) loads from agricultural watersheds. The equation was derived from regression analysis of data obtained from nutrient loadings studies conducted in the Midwest and similar climatic regimes. The factors significantly affecting SRP loading included precipitation, area, slope, soil type, land use, and farming practices. Consequently, the equation is capable of assessing the impact of changing land use and management practices on SRP loads. On the basis of annual land use data from 1976 to 1978, the equation was used to predict SRP loads from agricultural watersheds in Washington County, Wiscon-sin. Predicted loads were compared with monitored loads from the watersheds over the same time period. Predicted and observed loads were primarily a function of land use.
