A Discussion on Initial Planning of Nanchang Cuilin Shanzhuang Residential Quarter Project
The influences of cultural meaning, landscape design, residential quarter's style, road system arrangement and functional design defect in the initial planning of a project on the development and construction of the project are discussed from the point of view of a construction company, taking the initial planning of Nanchang Cuilin Shanzhuang Residential Quarter as an example. It is proved from practice that a construction company should make an overall consideration on project's construction and operation with the developer in initial planning of the project so as to optimize design and save cost for good economic benefits.
