The only lost solutions of the axially symmetric potential flow equation are thus seen to be a rectilinear flow along the axis, a .two dimensional line source on the axis, and the Taylor Maccoll flow past a cone. The first two flows may be superimposed, but the result is of little interest. Only the third conical flow is an essentially three dimensional flow pattern. In conclusion it can be stated that all the lost solutions of axially symmetric irrotational compressible flow are already well known solutions. This result was apparently known to Bateman; however, it does not appear in any of his writings.
