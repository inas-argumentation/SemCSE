Chemical properties of ylidene derivatives of azines. 7. Conversions of 5-methyl(phenyl)-substituted 1,2-dihydro-2-pyrimidinylidenemalononitriles by nitric acid
It has been shown that the reaction of 5-methyl(phenyl)pyrimidinylidenemalononitrile with nitric acid leads either to a 2-pyrimidinecarboxylic acid or to a pyrimidinylidene-α-carboxamido(α-carboxamido, α-nitro)-acetonitrile depending on the conditions.
