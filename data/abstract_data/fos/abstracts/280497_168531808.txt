Analysis of the characteristics of relocation of excellent gymnasts in China
Having studied the characteristics and trends of relocation of excellent gymnasts in China,the authors put forward the following opinions: 1) the main way for gymnast relocation is vertical relocation;2) at different stages of development there are different forms of gymnast relocation;3) there is a lack of international relocation of coaches for the national team;4) excellent gymnasts have more ways and independency to make career switching;5) the sign of "culturally lowered relocation" is conducive to the popularization of gymnastics.The authors also put forward measures for development in an action specific way.
