Reaction of o-phenylenediamine with isatins
It is shown that the reaction of o-phenylenediamine with isatins in the general case leads to mixtures of indolo[2,3-b]quinoxalines, 3-(2'-aminophenyl)-2(1H)-quinoxalinones, and spiro[2H-benzimidazoline-2,3'-indolin-2'-ones]. Spiro[2H-benzimidazole-2,3'-indolin-2'-ones] were isolated in individual cases.
