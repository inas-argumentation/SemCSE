Clinical Effect and Nursing Observation of Recombinant Human Interferonα-2b Combinated with Acyclovir Treating on Herpes Zoster
Objective to determine the clinical curative effect of recombinate human interferonα-2b and acyclovir treating on herpes zoster.Methods patients are divided into two groups,In the group of treatment, 200 mg acyclovir tablets are orally taken,4 times a day, and the recombinant human interferonα-2b is spread on the affected parts , 4 times a day, with some proper nursing practice; In the group of comparison, the patients only take acyclovir tablets. Observe the condition of pain relieving, incrustation, decrustation, curing etc.Results the curial effect of the group of treatment is better than the group of comparison, and the time of pain relieving , incrustation, decrustation is also shorter than the group of comparison, P0.01.Conclusion the treatment of recombinant human interferonα-2b combinated with acyclovir treating on herpes zoster is more effective, and can reduce the incidence rate of lingering effects of nerval pain.
