Limb Impact Harvesting, Part II: Experimental Approach
ABSTRACT THE dynamics of limb impact harvesting was studied for two apple cultivars (Mcintosh and Red Delicious) using tri-axial accelerometers and high speed photo-graphy. The fruit was found to move very little, if any, prior to detachment and was released within 0.1 s whenever the detachment occurred. A verification for the finite element model developed in Part I is provided.
