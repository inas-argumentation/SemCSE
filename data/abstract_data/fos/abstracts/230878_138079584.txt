Analysis on Interfacial Characteristics of FeB-Q235 Composite Prepared by Suspension Casting
The element diffusing and segregation in the interface of FeB-Q235 composite prepared by suspension casting was studied by optical microscope,SEM and EDS.The experiment results show that,the element moving distance is short,the producted high-boron phase and impurity distributes in the interface of FeB-Q235 composite.The high-boron phase in the interfacial of FeB-Q235 composite can be reduced by normalizing.But normalizing doesn't change the shape and distribution of impurity.After normalizing,the size of crystal near the interfacial of FeB-Q235 composite is smaller than that of the matrix.
