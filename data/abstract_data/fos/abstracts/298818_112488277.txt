Technical Study on Semi-aerobic Landfill in Refuse Treatment
imulated semi-aerobic landfill column is designed based on the principle of semi-aerobic landfill.An orthogonal test is arranged as per L4(23)orthogonal test table,with hydraulic load and recirculation frequency as two factors.The test results show that the hydraulic load is the critical factor in the removal of ammonia nitrogen of simulated semi-aerobic landfill column and the recirculation frequency is the secondary factor.Semi-aerobic landfill is an energy-saving refuse treatment method worthy of popularization and accordant with environmental protection as it can obviously shorten the stabilizing time of refuses.
