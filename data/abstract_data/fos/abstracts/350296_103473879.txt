Enantiospecific Total Synthesis of (+)‐3‐epi‐Epohelmin A Using a Nitrogen‐Substituted Donor–Acceptor Cyclopropane
An enantiospecific total synthesis of (+)-3-epi-epohelmin is described using nitrogen substituted D-A cyclopropane. The chiral cyclopropane was prepared by a highly diastereoselective intramolecular cyclopropanation of vinylogous carbamate. The regioselective ring opening of D-A cyclopropane and further utilization of ring opening product gave 3-epi-epohelmin A.
