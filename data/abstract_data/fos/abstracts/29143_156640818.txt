Measure on VaR of Shanghai and Shenzhen Stock Index Futures 300 Based on Monte Carlo Simulation Method
The VaR will be estimated,based on Monte Carlo simulation method.By analyzing,the value is 367,200 Yuan when you take long product and 334,200 Yuan,when you take short product,based on history data,holding one day,at 95% confidence level.All the investment will be in the risk,when holding period is more than ten days,weather you take long or short product.So it is necessary that the investor should alert the risk of this species and have anti-risk mechanism to control it.
