The Effects of Student Teaching and Pretesting on Student Teachers’ Attitudes
The study was conducted to examine the effects of a student teaching experience and the pretesting of attitudes on student teachers’ attitudes. A Solomon three-group design was used to control for the effects of pretesting. Results suggested that pretesting of attitudes is detrimental to attitude improvement. Student teachers who were not pretested showed significant improvements in their attitudes. Appropriate designs for assessing changes in attitudes were discussed.
