Amorphous alloy deposited on support and its hydrogenation activity
The supported amorphous catalysts, Ni-P-SiO 2 and Co-P-SiO 2 , with large surface areas were prepared by depositing amorphous Ni-P and Co-P alloys on silica. The surface areas are about 80 m 2 /g. XRD, DSC and SEM methods were used to verify the amorphous state of the samples. The catalytic activities of the amorphous alloys and their crystalline counterparts are compared for the hydrogenation of styrene. In order to recognize the active sizes, XPS and TPR were used to analyze the chemical state and the results were compared with the hydrogenation activities of the catalysts. TPD and TPRS were used to study the adsorption of hydrogen and carbon monoxide and their interactions. A mechanism for the hydrogenation of carbon monoxide is suggested.
