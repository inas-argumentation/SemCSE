An operator related to the Cosserat spectrum. Applications
Summary If G ⊂ Rn (n ≥ 2) is either a bounded or an exterior domain, weak Lq-solutions of Δv = ∇p in G, v|∂G = 0 are considered. Let Zqp := div v for p ∈ Lq(G). Restricted to a certain closed subspace B(0)q(G) ⊂ Lq(G) made up of harmonic functions, the operator (Zq − ½I)|B(0)q(G) is compact. On one hand this forms the basis for the investigation of the Cosserat spectrum [8]. On the other hand it admits an easy proof of a useful decomposition theorem for the Sobolev space Ĥ•1,q(G)n. As a corollary existence and a priori estimates for Stokes' and Lamé's equation follow immediately. Finally we investigate the problem whether there exist eigensolutions v to the Cosserat eigenvalue λ = 1 such that div v is harmonic. This depends on topological properties of G.
