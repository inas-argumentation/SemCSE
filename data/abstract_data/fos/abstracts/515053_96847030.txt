Conformational Behavior and Epoxidation of the Dolabella-3E,7E-dienoid
Conformational analysis of the dolabellane diterpenoid, 6β-acetoxy-12β-hydroxydolabella-3E,7E-diene containing 1,5-undecadiene, has been achieved by means of molecular mechanics calculations, 1H NMR, and CD spectrometry. The epoxidation products of the diene can be rationalized in terms of the calculated equilibrium conformations.
