Evaluation on Environmental Quality of Soils in Farmland of pepper
Contents of heavy metals Hg,As,Cr,Pb,Cu,Cd and pesticides HCH,DDT of pepper in Hainan and Yunnan provinces were detected and analyzed.The environmetal quality was evaluated by the methods of single quality index and comprehensive quality index.Results showed that the average content of heavy metals Hg,As,Cr,Pb,Cu,Cd and pesticides HCH,DDT were all under the threshold set for"Farmland environmental quality evaluation standards for edible agricultural products".The soil environmental quality in the studied were all classified as classⅠand suitable for the plantation of pepper free of pollution.
