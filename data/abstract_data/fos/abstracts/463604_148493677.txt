Rethinking on "Faculty Governing School Affairs"and "Faculty Governing Academic Affairs"——Discussion with Professor Zhao Mengcheng
There's an argument that faculty should govern school affairs or govern academic affairs in universities.In fact,"faculty governing academic affairs" is the reasonable extension of essential connotation of professors,while "faculty governing school affairs" oversteps the essential connotation of professors.Because "faculty governing school affairs" is the product of particular historical circumstances,it's unsuited to copy in reality.Educators running school affairs,the committee of faculty governing academic affairs,and administrators running administrative affairs are the inner conditions for building the system of faculty governing academic affairs.Meanwhile,government should streamline administration and delegate power to the lower levels to create outer conditions for faculty governing academic affairs.
