Gel permeation chromatography clean-up for the determination of benzo (a) pyrene in edible vegetable oils by high performance liquid chromatography with fluorescence detector
This study developed a reverse phase HPLC method for determing benzo (a) pyrene in edible vegetable oils. After samples were pretreated by gel permeation chromatography, benzo (a) pyrene was separated on a Phenomenex Luna℃18 (4.6 mm×250 mm, 5 μm) using a mobile phase composed of acetonitrile and H 2 O (91:1, v/v) at a flow rate of 1 mL/min and detected using a fluorescence detector set at λex=384 nm, λem=406 nm. At the spike levels of 2.0, 10.0, 25.0 μg/kg, the average recovery for benzo (a) pyrene was 98.7% with the RSD of 3.4% (n=6), the limit of detection was 0.3 μg/kg, and the linear range was from 0.1~20.0 μg/L (R=0.9992). The results of benzo (a) pyrene determination in four different edible vegetable oils samples by this method were almost the same as those determined by GB/ T 22509—2008. The method was fast, easy and accurate. Therefore, the method developed can be use to determine benzo (a) pyrene in edible vegetable oils.
