Scintillation and Optical Properties of ${\rm BaBrI}\!:\!{\rm Eu}^{2+}$  and ${\rm CsBa}_{2}{\rm I}_{5}\!:\!{\rm Eu}^{2+}$
This paper reports on the scintillation and optical properties of the recently discovered europium doped BaBrI and CsBa<sub>2</sub>I<sub>5</sub> scintillators. Single crystals of BaBrI and CsBa<sub>2</sub>I<sub>5</sub> were grown from the melt by the vertical Bridgman technique. Crystals were doped with 8% and 4% Eu<sup>2+</sup> for BaBrI and CsBa<sub>2</sub>I<sub>5</sub> , respectively. These crystals have higher optical quality and present improved scintillation performance compared to the previously reported values. An estimated light yield of 97,000±5,000 and 102,000±6,000 photons per MeV (ph/MeV) of absorbed gamma-ray energy is now measured for BaBrI:8%Eu<sup>2+</sup> and CsBa<sub>2</sub>I<sub>5</sub>:4%Eu<sup>2+</sup> , respectively. A better energy resolution has also been observed. BaBrI energy resolution for the 662 keV full absorption peak (FWHM over peak position) decreased to 3.4±0.2% and CsBa<sub>2</sub>I<sub>5</sub>:4%Eu<sup>2+</sup> energy resolution decreased to 2.55±0.2% . Additional measurements show both scintillators with an excellent light yield proportionality. Finally, based on optical and x-ray excited emission spectra and decay curves, energy transfer mechanisms in BaBrI and CsBa<sub>2</sub>I<sub>5</sub> scintillators are briefly discussed.
