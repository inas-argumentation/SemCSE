Fertile medium of kiwifruit wine yeast by response surface methodology
The effects of proliferated role by fertile were studied. The result of JMP and Box-Behnken response surface methodology(RSM) indicated:glucose,(NH4)2HPO4,VB1,VB1 and pantothenic acid have very significant influence,MgSO4 has significant influence. Optimum fertile medium formula of cell proliferation is glucose 8.39 g/L,(NH4)2HPO4 0.53 g/L,MgSO4 0.07 g/L,VB1 1.75 mg/L,VB2 0.60 mg/L,pantothenic acid 0.57 mL/L,the prediction value of biomass reach to 3.01785×108 cfu/mL.
