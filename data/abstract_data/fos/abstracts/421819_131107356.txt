Survey of Tianjin Literature and Art and the Celebrities in Tianjin Cultural Circles
Survey of Tianjin Literature and Art is a collected writings of the works written by celebrities in Tianjin cultural circles. It shows the situation of development and celebrities of cultural circles in this area. On the occasion of the founding of Tianjin city, it is necessary to sum up the result of predecessors and propagate the achievement of the culture in Tianjin history to improve the researches on local history in order to promote the construction of the modern spiritual civilization in Tianjin.
