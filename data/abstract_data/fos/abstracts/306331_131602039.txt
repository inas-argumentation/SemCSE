Biomass Distribution of Young Eucalyptus Forest Under Different Soil Preparation
The survey of young eucalyptus distribution of biomass under different site preparation modes showed that the average individual biomass for each tree in the three kinds of soil preparations was of significant differences. The average individual biomass for each tree was in the order: total reclamation soil preparation(8.72 kg/plant) furrowing soil preparation(7.93 kg/plant)digging hole soil preparation(7.01 kg/plant). There also existed a significant difference between undergrowth biomass. They were in the order: digging hole soil preparation(5.38 t/hm2)furrowing soil preparation(4.03 t/hm2) total reclamation soil preparation(2.12 t/hm2). The biodiversity of digging hole soil preparation and furrowing soil preparation was superior to total reclamation soil preparation. Combining with the trees and undergrowth biomass concluded that the total forest biomass of furrowing soil preparation was the largest(15.73 t/hm2), followed by digging hole soil preparation(15.15 t/hm2) and total reclamation soil preparation(14.15 t/hm2). Considering various indicators, furrowing soil preparation was more conducive to sustainable management of eucalyptus plantation.
