Protective effects and molecular mechanisms of Mycophenolate mofetil in the treatment of MRL-lpr/lpr mice
Objective To evaluate the efficacy and explore the underlying molecular mechanisms of Mycophenolate mofetil(MMF) in the treatment of MRL-lpr/lpr mice. Methods 12-week female mice were given MMF (90mg·kg -1·d -1, po), methylprednisolone(MPS,25mg·kg -1·d -1, ip)or cyclophosphamide(CTX,25mg·kg -1·w -1, ip) for 6 months. Proteinuria was determined, and suppressive effect of the drugs on serum anti-dsDNA antibody level was measured by radioimmunological assay. Immunohistological analysis was used to quantify the expression of plasminogen activator inhibitor-1(PAI-1) and intercellular adhesion molecule-1(ICAM-1) in renal tissue. ICAM-1 gene expression in renal cortex was examined by RT-PCR. Results Mice treated with MMF, MPS or CTX had their weight increased more than those in the control group (P0.05) (MPSCTXMMF). Compared to the ones in the control, mice with MMF, MPS or CTX treatment had a lower levels of proteinuria (P 0.01) (CTXMPSMMF), anti-dsDNA antibody (P0.01) (MMFMPSCTX), and mortality (MMFMPSCTX). Pathological microscopy showed that these three drugs ameliated tubulointerstitial intrafiltration of inflammatory cells and glomerular injuries (P0.01) (CTXMPSMMF). Immunohistological results revealed that expression of PAI-1 and ICAM-1 in the renal cortex of mice treated with the drugs was reduced compared to that of the control. ICAM-1 mRNA was less in the renal tissue of mice treated with the three drugs (P0.01) (MMFMPSCTX). Conclusion MMF reduces proteinuria, prolongs life span, ameliates renal injuries in lupus MRL-lpr/lpr mice. This protective effect might be explained by its immunosuppressive action, which leads to reduced-expression of PAI-1 and ICAM-1 in the kidney of MRL-lpr/lpr mice. MMF also has the advantages of routine therapy of MPS or CTX in prolongation of life span in this model.
