The interrelationships between mental and somatic disorders in those who cleaned up the accident at the Chernobyl Atomic Electric Power Station
: Psychic and somatic disorders are characterized for 120 subjects who have worked in radionuclide-contaminated territories. All the examinees had both psychic and somatovegetative abnormalities. A relationship has been established between some psychic disorders, syndromes, somatovegetative disorders and the intensity of radiation exposure.
