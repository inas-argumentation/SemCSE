High-speed Machining of Hardened Steel
High-speed machining has become an important cuttingmeasure. There are still many problems in its applications, especial-ly in the high-speed machining of some difficult to cut materials,such as hardened steel, which have taken a very vital role in vari-ous industries because of its excellent performance. In this paper,the general of hardened steel high-speed machining is introduced.Several important factors affecting the cutting process such as cut-ting tools, process parameters, tool path are analyzed.
