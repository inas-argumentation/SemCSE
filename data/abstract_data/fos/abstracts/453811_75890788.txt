Study on the correlation between allergic rhinitis and enviroment in petrochemical works
Objective Examining the T cell subpopulation, IgE, IgG, IgM, IgA and studying the relation between the immunofunction and incidence of the allergic rhinitis in the petrochemical workers. Methods Examining index of the subpopulation (CD3, CD4, CD8 and CD4/CD8), IgG, IgM, IgA and IgE for 398 employees who work at the noxious post in the petrochemical plants, 70 employees at non noxious post and 109 peasants in the control group, who live in the village with no industrial pollution, 50 kM far away from the petrochemical areas. Results Index of the major contamination in petrochemical area, masculine rate of allergic rhinitis in the petrochemical workers evidently higher than that in rural area; CD3, CD4, CD8 and CD4/CD8 and IgE level have signifitant difference. Conclusion Environmental pollution and invasion of the venomousness injurant is the important reason relating high incidence of allergic rhinits in the petrochemical workers, and the mechanism is immunofunction disorder.
