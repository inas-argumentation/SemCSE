Grey incidence analysis between main physical properties and chemical indexes in flue-cured tobacco leaves of Hunan Province
Nine different genotypes of flue-cured tobacco were collected from Hunan Province to study the correlation between physical properties and main chemical indexes by using the method of grey incidence analysis.The results indicated that the correlation between main physical properties and chemical indexes were significant different with leaf positions.In all physical properties indexes,the closest relation degree to total sugar content in cutters and lower leaves was leaf width/leaf length,and in upper leaves was leaf thickness.The closest correlation to total nitrogen and nicotine content in cutters and upper leaves,and the nicotine content in lower leaves was stem ratios;the closest relation degree to total nitrogen content in lower leaves was leaf length,and the relation degree to margin of two sugar,reducing sugar/nicotine,total nitrogen/nicotine were different with leaf positions.The leaf thickness of upper leaves was the key factor to the main chemical indexes,leaf width/leaf length deter-mined the intensity of tobacco plant photosynthesis and the accumulation for cutters and lower leaves,so the ratio reflected the quality of tobacco leaves to a certain extent indirectly.
