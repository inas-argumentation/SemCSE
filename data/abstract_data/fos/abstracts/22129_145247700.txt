Organizational Documents as Indicators of Effectiveness of Educational Planning
Many of the educational agencies now engaged in long-range planning have had to undertake training programs in order to teach their personnel how to plan. When evaluating these programs’ impact on the planning process, educators should study the relationship between the surface quality of the plans drafted during and after training and the attitudes of personnel toward those plans. Based on their assessment of a training program in educational planning, the authors contend that the better the plan, the more favorable the attitudes towards it and the greater the chances for lasting improvements in the agency’s planning capability.
