Analysis of poliovirus type 1 wild strains by restriction fragment length polymorphism assay
The dominant wild strains of Guangdong province isolated in 1992 and 1993 belonged to the same category, but different from those isolated in 1989, 1990 amd 1991, The wild strains in Guangdong province isolated in 1992 were of the same category as those isolated in Hainan, Shanxi and Henan provinces. It seemed that the cpidemic strains of Guangdong and Hainan procinces in 1992 were from Shanxi or Henan province, because many people of Henan and Shanxi provinces went to work in the southern provinces in recent years. On the other hand, the viruses isolated from Yili prefecture in the northern part of Xingjiang were different from those isolated from Kashi prefecture in the western part of Xingjiang. Difficulty of communication between the two prefectures may have limited the circulation of polio stranins on the local area only. In 1992, there were poliomyelitis outbreaks in Jiangxi and Guangdong provinces, but the strains isolated in Jiangxi province were different with those isolated in Guangdong province. It showed that there were different strains causing endemic disease in the two provinces in the same year.
