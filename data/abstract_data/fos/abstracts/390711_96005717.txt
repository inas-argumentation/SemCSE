AMINATED POLYSTYRENE-BOUND Rh CLUSTER CATALYSTS FOR THE WATER GAS SHIFT REACTION
Various aminated polystyrenes are synthesized by treatment of chloromethylated polystyrene with amines. The aminated polymers are examined as an insoluble support for Rh6(CO)16 in the water gas shift reaction (WGSR). Diethylenetriamino polymer is the most effective support for the WGSR. This heterogenization does not lower the WGSR activity in homogeneous systems.
