Friction Properties of Cr_3C_2-Ni Coatings Prepared by Laser Cladding
Cr3C2-Ni composite powders were synthesized by powder electroless plating method and performed as precoated layer in laser cladding in order to obtain antifriction coatings on Q235 steel plate. Dry friction properties of the laser cladding coatings were investigated. The wear morphology and hardness of the coating was observed and tested,respectively. The results show that,the coatings have uniform and dense microstructure,and relatively high hardness as well. The laser cladding coatings perform stable friction state and friction coefficient is 0.44. The good properties benefit from the carbide and metallic binding phase.
