Investigation of shanxi rural physical culture and correlated suggestions
Investigations were conducted, by means of questionaire and interviewing, on the farmers of twenty and over in 1816 households in eleven regions and cities of Shanxi (involving 21 regional and county committee for physical culture). Present conditions of rural physical culture, including physical consciousness, sports activities and forms, management, fund and economic condition, have been analyzed carfully. Some tentative suggestions have been put forward to promote the development of physical culture in rural areas.
