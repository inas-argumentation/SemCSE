Nano-Cross-Junction Effect on Phonons in Silicon-Nanowire-Cages
Based on the bottom-up approach and planar nano-cross-junctions (NCJs) of silicon nanowires (SiNWs), we take a step further and propose a Silicon-nanowire-cage (SiNWC) structure constructed by SiNWs and NCJs. Thus, the one-dimensional (1D) SiNWs is turned to a three-dimensional (3D) bulk material which is easy to be applied. It is known that SiNWs have low thermal conductivity due to the surface scatterings and size confinement. We perform equilibrium molecular dynamics (MD) simulations to study the thermal conductivity of SiNWCs and found an ultralow value, 0.173 W/m-K, which is even one order lower than SiNWs. To find the mechanism of reduction, we compared the phonon modes in SiNWCs with those in SiNWs. It is found that a new phonon localization effect induced by NCJs is effective in reducing thermal conductivity. Our work is motivated by experiments and may help in synthesis of new materials in the next step.
