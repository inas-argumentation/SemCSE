Interaction of chloride ions with nonionic surfactant as mediated by inorganic cations incorporated in surfactant micelles
The line width ΔνCl of 35Cl NMR was measured for n-C12H25O(CH2CH2O)pH (p = 5, 6, 7, 8) / water / XCl (X = H, Na, Li) systems. The 17O NMR line width ΔνO due to water was also examined. The broadening of ΔνCl by introduction of the nonionic surfactant to aqueous XCl solutions was explained by the enhanced interaction of the chloride ions with surfactant micelles to which inorganic cations were incorporated.
