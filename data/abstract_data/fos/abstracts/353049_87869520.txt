Analysis of Genetic Similarity Among 44 Parents of Japonica Hybrid Rice by Simple Sequence Repeats(SSR)
Genetic similarity of 44 parents of Japonica hybrid rice were analyzed by 158 alleles detected from 58 pairs of primers.The genetic similarity coefficients of the 44 parents of Japonica hybrid rice ranged from 0.54 to 0.96.Furthermore,all the varieties were classified into 4 groups,at the level of genetic similarity coefficient 0.71 according to cluster analysis by an un-weighted pair-group average method with arithmetic mean(UPGMA).Most of the varieties were classified into the fourth group.The fourth groups were classified into 5 subgroup at the level of genetic similarity coefficient 0.75.All the Male sterile lines and maintainer lines were classified in the E subgroup of the fourth groups.
