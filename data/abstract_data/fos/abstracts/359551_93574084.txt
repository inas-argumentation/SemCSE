Synthesis and Characterization of Bisumanenylidene
Bisumanenylidene, consisting of two sumanene components bridged by a double bond, was synthesized by McMurry coupling of monooxosumanene. Calculations and low-temperature 1H NMR spectroscopy experiments suggest that bisumanenylidene is likely to be dominantly present in the C2h symmetric structure rather than the C2 symmetric one. Evidence for the extension of π conjugation in bisumanenylidene was shown by absorption and emission spectroscopy.
