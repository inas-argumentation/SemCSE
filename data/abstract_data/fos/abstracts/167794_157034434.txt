Rethinking about Article 13 of China’s Law of Bills
Bills defense and restrictions to bills defense are important legal systems in law of bills, which fully embodies the roles of law in balancing the interests of all parties,stabilizing society and regulating on social relations. The two systems are based on the provision of Article 13 of China’s Law of Bills,which adheres to none- causation theories of law of bills. Based on an analysis of the provision of Article 13 of China’s"Law of Bills",the paper presents some suggestions about the legislation of Article 13.
