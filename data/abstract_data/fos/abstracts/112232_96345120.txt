Time-Resolved Emission Anisotropic Studies of Silylene-Diphenylene Copolymers: Energy Migration along the σ–π Conjugated Chain
The excited-state dynamics of silylene-diphenylene copolyemers, –[(SiMe2)m–(C6H4)2]n–, m = 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6, was studied by picosecond time-resolved emission anisotropy. The emission depolarization within 100 ps suggested the effective energy migration along the σ–π conjugated silylene-diphenylene chain.
