Effects of grain yield and quality of strong gluten wheat and interaction in combined application of nitrogen and potassium
The pot experiment was adopted to study the effects of combined application of N and K on grain yield and qualities of strong gluten wheat.The results showed that the effects of N and K combined application on grain yield and quality of wheat were obvious.Application of N and K at an appropriate rate and ratio enhanced spike number of wheat,plant height,spike length,grains per spike and 1000-grain weight.The grain yield,protein content and yield,wet gluten content,sedimentation value increased with the increase of N and K application rates.There was significant positive interaction between N and K on grain yield and qualities of strong gluten wheat.
