Effects of nitrogen application on grain yield and quality of Wanmai 48.
The new weak-gluten wheat variety of Wanmai48 was selected to research the effects of topdressing amount and application time of nitrogen fertilizer on the production and main quality characters from 2005 to 2006.The results showed that:(1)In this test conditions,the yield reached the highest when topdressing nitrogen was 184.5 kg·hm-2,and topdressing at the reviving stage,setting stage and jointing stage,the maximum output reached 6062.5,6472.0 and 6883.0 kg·hm-2,respectively.(2)When basic pure nitrogen was 135 kg·hm-2,just under the no topdressing condition,the grain protein,wet gluten content and other quality indicators accorded with the national quality standard of weak-gluten wheat.The dough stability time and water absorption also accorded with the standard.The values of Alveograph parameters P and L and P/L and W values under all treatments of topdressing at the reviving stage,reached the standard of weak-gluten wheat.When the amount of topdressing nitrogen at the setting stage was less than 114 kg·hm-2 and below 90 kg·hm-2 at the jointing stage,the W values reached the standard of weak-gluten wheat.
