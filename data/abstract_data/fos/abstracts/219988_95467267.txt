Photoluminescence and raman scattering from superlattices made by phase-locked epitaxy
The precision of layering of crystals grown by the Phase-Locked Epitaxy (PLE) method, which controls the molecular beam by monitoring the reflection high energy electron diffraction (RHEED) intensity, is investigated by photoluminescence from quantum-well structures and Raman scattering from superlattice structures. Peak energies and peak widths in the photoluminescence spectra show that the quantum-well widths are precisely controlled to one monolayer.
