Simulation Research on Dynamic Recrystallization of Blade in Die-forging Based on Cellular Automata
The 3D numerical simulation of blade die-forging was performed,and the stress,strain,strain rate fields as well as temperature field were obtained from the simulated results.A two-dimensional dynamic recrystallization model was adopted by cellular automata,which simulated work harden,dynamic recovery,nucleation,grain growing and etc.The variation of dislocation density,grain shape,orientation and mean size of recrystallization grain could be calculated during the whole deformation process by this model.The microstructure evolution under different strain and temperature was analyzed.The simulated results agree well with growth kinetics of dynamic recrystallization.
