The Research about Construction of University Students Ability Appraisal System
To train the sports ability of university students is a demand of the reform in present sports education,it is a need of implementing quality-oriented education too,therefore how appraise university students' sports ability overall and effectively becomes a problem urgently solved.So this text will proceed from angles of pedagogy,psychology and sociology,adopt the law of documents,questionnaire investigation method to explain the intension of sports ability,then research construction of appraisal system about university student sports ability,in order to offer the theoretical foundation for reform of the current student's sports appraisal system,and offer reference to the reform and innovation of sports course.
