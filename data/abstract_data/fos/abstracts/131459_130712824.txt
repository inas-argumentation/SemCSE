Effects of different mulching on soil temperature and corn yield-increasing effect in Hexi irrigated area
A field experiment was conducted to study the effects of different mulching patterns on soil temperature, soil moisture, maize growth and development yield and WUE. The results indicated that different mulching patterns showed significant temperature--increasing effect and moisture--preserving effect. The corn yield and WUE treated with different plastic films and mulching patterns were all significantly higher than those of CK. The sandy covering could significantly increase WUE of the plant, temperature of the surface soil, improve the corn yield, and achieve the maximum economic benefit, but it increases the difficulty to publicize the operation. In conclusion, the soil covering method can provide protection for high yield of Spring Maize in Hexi irrigated area and provide a basis for extending it to a large area plantation.
