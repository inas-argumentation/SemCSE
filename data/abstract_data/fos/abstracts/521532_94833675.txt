Preparation of crystalline ZnSnO3 from Li2SnO3 by low-temperature ion exchange
Polycrystalline zinc stannate (ZnSnO 3 ) has been obtained for the first time by an ion exchange reaction between solid α-Li 2 SnO 3 (low-temperature modification) and a melt of ZnCl 2 .KCl at 350°C for 48 h. The X-ray diffraction data show that the phase obtained has an ilmenite-type structure (space group R-3, a =5.2835 A, c =14.0913 A, Z =6). The success of the synthesis strongly depends on the structure type of the solid precursor and the thermal conditions of the ion exchange reaction.
