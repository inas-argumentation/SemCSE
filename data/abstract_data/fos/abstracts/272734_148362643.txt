The Ecological View in Lady Chatterley's Lover
In Lady Chatterley’s Lover,Lawrence puts forward the ecological view that nature and man are equal subjects,man belongs to and relies on nature.Through Connie’s coming to nature and melting to nature,nature’s positive effect on man is presented obversely.While through criticizing Clifford's speech and behaviour,the danger of destroying nature is raised.In this way,Lawrence shows the influence of nature reversely.Then the conclusion is submitted that nature is the subject and man should return to nature.
