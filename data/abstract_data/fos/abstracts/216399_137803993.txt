STUDIES ON DYNAMIC RESPONSE OF CONCRETE FILLED STEEL TUBE UNDER LATERAL IMPACT LOADING
The experimental and theoretical studies on simply supported beams of concrete filled steel tubes with different casing hoop steel coefficients were completed. The experimental researches were done through a DHR-9401 drop hammer test machine. During testing, residual deformation modes, the time history curve of impact loads were recorded. On the basis of the experimental results and consideration of the local deformation effect on the integral bending deformation, the relationship between local damage and integral bending beam deformation is established, the dynamic transverse deflection is achieved and it is in the fair agreement with the experimental one.
