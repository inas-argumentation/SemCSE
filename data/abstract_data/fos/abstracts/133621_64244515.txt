Design and Implementation of Random Questions Algorithm of Test Database System
According to the characteristics of boutique Website of vocational education,presents the network of education adapted to the knowledge point of the core algorithm for automatic test paper.The algorithm makes the difficulty proportional and number of question type as the main control objectives to provide for the topics of knowledge points test paper,and applies the appropriate strategy for optimization of stochastic algorithms composing test paper results.Experiment shows that the test paper efficiency,success rate,and knowledge point coverage of the algorithm which based on a different strategy for automatic generation of test paper are ideal
