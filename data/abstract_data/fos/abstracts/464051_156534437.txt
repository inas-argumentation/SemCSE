On the reform in socialization of logistics service in Jiujiang University
A series of mechanism which conforms to the educational and market nature has been established in Jiujiang University by reform and innovation.A new structure including standard management,distinct property ownership,invigorated property,fair competition,flexible operation and priority to efficiency has been formed and the two goals of economic benefits and social benefits have been reached.Therefore,an innovative route with charateristics for university logistics service has been created.
