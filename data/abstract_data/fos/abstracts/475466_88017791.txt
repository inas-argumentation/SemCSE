siRNAs Specific to Subtype H5 Avian Influenza Virus Delivered by Attenuated Salmonella typhimurium Strain
For developing attenuated Salmonella typhimurium as a vector expressing siRNAs delivery system for the control of avian influenza virus(AIV)infection,three short interfering RNAs(siRNAs)specific to the nucleocapsid protein(NP)gene,polymerase A(PA)gene and hemagglutinin(HA)gene of H5 subtype avian influenza virus respectively were designed based on the sequence of the AIV viruses.The corresponding DNA fragments were synthesized,annealed and ligated to the downstream of the mouse originated U6 promoter of the pSIREN-ASD ZsGreen vector.As control,negative fragments were also made in the same way.Recombinant plasmids were transformed into an attenuated S.typhimurium strain X4550 and positive clones were selected.The transformants were identified and sequenced and four clones of them carried the correct inserts.They were designated as X4550(PAZ-NP),X4550(PAZ-PA),X4550(PAZ-HA)and X4550(PAZ-NC).Then the inhibitive effect on replication of subtype H5 AIV were examined in vitro and in vivo.Firstly,we examined their inhibitive effects on MDCK cells and CEF monolayers.Purified plasmids(1ng)were transfected before or after the cells were infected with subtype H5 AIV(50 PFU).No cytopathic effect(CPE)and virus titer were detected in cell cultures transfected with Retro-NP,Retro-PA,Retro-HA before virus infection.On the contrary,the CPEs were observed after 48 h and the HA titers in the cell culture supernatants reached 24 in the controls.Secondly,groups of 4-day-old chickens were administered orally with 1×109CFU recombinants respectively.The chickens were challenged with subtype H5 AIV(105 ELD50)1d,7d,14d after bacteria inoculation by oculonasal route.The observation lasted 15 days after challenging.The swabs of cloacas were collected from survived chickens for bacteria and virus isolation.The motality of the chickens reduced 23%-50% by the siRNAs delivered by these recombinants.No virus was isolated when the experiment finished.All these results indicated that the Salmonella based delivering systems can release harboured DNA to express siRNAs specific to H5 AIV successfully in vivo,and elicit protective responses.
