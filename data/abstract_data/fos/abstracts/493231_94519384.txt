Structure and Stereodynamics of N-9-Triptycyl-O-phenylhydroxylamine, an Unusual Product by the Reaction of 9-Nitrosotriptycene and Phenyllithium
Reaction of 9-nitrosotriptycene with phenyllithium gave N-9-triptycyl-O-phenylhydroxylamine (TpNHOPh). The free energy barrier to rotation about the Tp-N bond is 10.9 kcal mol−1 and that to the N-O topomerization is higher than this.
