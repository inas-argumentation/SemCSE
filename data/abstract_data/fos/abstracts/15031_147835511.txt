Society-based-education:the dominant value orientation on the target of talent education in higher vocational colleges
Society-based-education and individual-based-education are two kind of tendency for talent training of vocational colleges.The contradiction and the integration of these value orientation determines that society-based-education should dominate talented persons education,which is beneficial not only to fulfill the mission which higher vocational colleges should undertake,but also to advance development of individuals in vocational colleges.
