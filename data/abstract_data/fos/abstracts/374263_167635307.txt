Investigation and analysis of leisure sports activates of employees of private enterprises in developed areas in Zhejiang
The authors investigate the situation of leisure sports activates participated by part of employees of 4 municipal private enterprises in Wenzhou,Hangzhou,Ningbo and Taizhou, and revealed the following findings: In relation to the employees' participation in leisure sports activates, the consciousness is not strong,the conditions are not matured,the funds,playgrounds and leisure time are not adequate or matched, the motive is weak compared with the general masses; the employees spend most of their leisure time on reading or watching TV; regional features exist in the employees' participation in leisure sports activates,mainly influential competitive sports,national traditional sports,practical sports and leisure entertaining sports.
