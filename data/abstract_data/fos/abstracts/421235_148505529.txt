On the Theory of Political Socialization for German College Students and Its Inspiration
The theory of political socialization,one of the fundamental theories of political education for college students in contemporary Germany,has absorbed and innovated from the theory in America.It not only emphasizes citizenship studies and social communication of political culture,but pays attention to the leading role of the government in the whole process.Moreover,it constructs the national resource system of political education.To a certain degree,it will enlighten the reinforcement and improvement of the construction of the political education resources in China in the new era.
