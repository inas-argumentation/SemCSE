System changes in China's massification of higher education
Since the expansion of admission for higher education in 1999,statistical data has shown some differences between central higher education system and provincial one.The central higher education system has obtained elite characteristics in terms of more graduate education,more comprehensive institutions,and more resource per student.In contrast,the provincial higher education system has contributed significantly to achieve the objective of higher education massification in terms of more students enrollments,more vocational institutions,less resource per student.All these will make the basis of multiple modes and stratifications of higher education in China.
