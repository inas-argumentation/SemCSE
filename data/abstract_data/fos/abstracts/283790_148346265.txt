University moral & civil education on undergraduate level in USA today ——Curriculum and teaching
The curriculum is the primary media of educating the student. However a lot of contents of the citizen and moral education are basically the intellective activities of cognitive, perception, explanation, judge etc. The curriculum case in American universities combines the morals and citizen education in the whole studing experience of undergraduate, that is, inside the course of general education. At same time, the abundant extracurricular activities are important part of moral and civil education in American universities, and have their own unique characters.
