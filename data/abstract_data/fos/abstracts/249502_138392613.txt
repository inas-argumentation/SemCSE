Main anatomical characteristics of Bamdusa pervariadilis × Dendrocalamopsis daii
In order to improve the level of high value-added processing and utilizing for sympodial bamboo,the main anatomical characteristics of B.pervariadilis × D.daii have been studied with the microscopic image analysis method.The results show that the parenchyma cell,fiber,vessel and sieve tube are 55.8%,37.8%,5.6% and 3.6%,respectively.The radial width,tangential width and frequency of vascular bundle are 778.445 μm,700.162 μm and 1.512 individual·mm-2,respectively.The average values of fiber length,width,lumen diameter,double-wall thickness,length-width ratio,double wall thickness-lumen diameter ratio and lumen diameter-width ratio are 1 673.993 μm,15.364 μm,5.811 μm,9.552 μm,109.104,2.230 and 0.367,respectively.
