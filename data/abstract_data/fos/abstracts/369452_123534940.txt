Almost Sure Convergence of the Series of Gaussian Markov Sequences
The necessary and sufficient conditions for the almost sure convergence of the series of multi-dimensional zero-mean Gaussian Markov sequences are found. Besides, the necessary conditions for the almost sure convergence of the series of regressive sequences with operator coefficients in Banach spaces are studied. By obtained general results, we find the criterion for the almost sure convergence of the series of Gaussian m-Markov sequences of random variables.
