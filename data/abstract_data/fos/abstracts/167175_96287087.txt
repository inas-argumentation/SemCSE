Remarkable Changes in the (N)CH3 Stretching Wavenumbers of Amines on Protonation
Remarkable changes in the (N)CH3 stretching wavenumbers have been observed for amines on protonation. These C–H stretching wavenumbers are related linearly to the corresponding C–H bond lengths calculated by the ab initio method. The (N)CH3 stretching bands are useful for distinguishing between free and protonated amines.
