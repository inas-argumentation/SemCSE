Analysis and Research on the Strategy of Disaster Evacuation System
When an emergency disaster occurs,taking timely evacuation strategy could greatly improve the efficiency of the evacuation.Proposes three evacuation strategies based on the computer simulation model,also analyzes and compares,the experimental result shows that the leadership behavior is the most superior among three evacuation strategies,followed by the strategy to help conduct evacuation,and the evacuation strategy of random behavior is the worst.Puts forward a dynamic group algorithm based on A* algorithm.Under the leadership of the multi-leader,uses the algorithm to simulate the evacuation.We can find that the efficiency of evacuation and the number of the leaders belong to non-linear relationship.Setting up a certain number of leaders is in favor of evacuation,but the excessive number of leaders leads to confusion.
