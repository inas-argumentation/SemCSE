Discussion on the Study of "Hu" in "Ma Shi Wen Tong"
"Hu" is one of the function words which are fully discussed in Preposition Part and Auxiliary word Part in "Ma Shi Wen Tong". The preposition word "Hu" can be used to express comparison, precede objects and indicate passivity etc. The auxiliary word "Hu" can be used not only to assist a sentence, but also assist a word. The study of "Hu" is an important achievement in the study of function words in "Ma Shi Wen Tong".
