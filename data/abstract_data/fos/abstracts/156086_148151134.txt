On the present situation and problems of BSO (basic scholastic organization) of higher education ——Questionnaire findings report of the 231 institutes of higher education
According to the result of the questionnaire about 231 colleges in China, we consider that the actuality of BSO have 5 problems as follow: Firstly, the types of BSO lacks diversity and some are not appropriate; Secondly, the systems of BSO are similar, stiff and lethargic; Thirdly, the functions of BSO are marrow, lack of vitality; Fourthly, The conditions of running are not smoothly; Lastly, the construction measures are not effective. In order to adapt to the social, economic and scientific development, perfect or expand the self\|function of the higher education and increase the energy of the higher education, we should give deeply reform to BSO, making them down in the direction of the rationality, diversification, convenience, and information highway.
