Strategies for establishing the system of lifelong learning in China
Establishing the system of lifelong learning is an important aim of our country's education for the 21st century. It is also one of important objectives for constructing the full prosperous society in our country. By utilizing the advanced experience of developed countries and according to our country's actual situation, establishing the lifelong learning system may focus on the following aspects: the guarantee by law, the regulation by policies, the raising of funds, the support of modern technology, the participation of all social departments and the stimulation for learning.
