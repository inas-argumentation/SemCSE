Nutrient components in fruit of Trichosanthes kirilowii Maxim
The main nutrient components among the pericarpium, seeds and pulps in fruit of Trichosanthes kirilowii Maxim were analyzed. The result showed that fruits were rich in lipids, protein, minerals, vitamins, polyphenol, flavonoids. In the seeds, the content of crude fat, protein, Fe, Cu, Zn and other trace elements were the highest, which were 25.00%, 9.46%, 73.19 μg/g, 7.65 μg/g and 42.48 μg/g respectively. Fruits were also rich in Vc, the content of which were 95.92 μg/g, 29.45 μg/g and 15.45 μg/g in pericarpium, seeds and pulps respectively. The contents of polyphenol and flavonoid were highest in pericarpium, which were 8.76 mg/ g and 3.99 mg/g respectively. According to the nutritional feature in fruit of Trichosanthes kirilowii Maxim, we may develop different products.
