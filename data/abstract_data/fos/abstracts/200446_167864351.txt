The Accounting Confirmation,Measurement and Disclosure of Haze Problem
To cope with the serious environmental problem in China,the study of environmental accounting proves to be effective.We should clarify the major causes of haze problem along with its definition,and conduct the environmental accounting study with reference to the American environmental accounting.The research employed a 3-step accounting confirmation method,and the three major calculating methods(replacement cost method,the opportunity cost method and the human cost method)are adopted when we calculate the cost of haze.The combination of voluntary disclosure and obligatory disclosure in terms of environmental accounting information disclosure will ultimately benefit the policy-making process of accounting information users.
