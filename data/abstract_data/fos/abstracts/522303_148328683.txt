The restructure of the government public power& the governancein higher education of China
The relationship between government and universities will change from government to governance,and this is the important current of the reform in higher education.If we want to get the aim,we must change the strong control complexion between government and universities,decompose and restructure the public power of the government.The course of the restructure includes feebleness,differentiation,transformation,aggrandizement and legalization.
