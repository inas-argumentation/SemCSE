Cluster Core Expansion through Incorporation of Transition-metal Fragments or an Alkyne Molecule into an Incomplete Cubane-type Fe2RuS4 Cluster
Treatment of Cp S 2 2 Fe 2 S 4 (Cp S 2 = 1,3-C 5 H 3 (SiMe 3 ) 2 ) with [CpRu(MeCN) 3 ] + in acetonitrile at room temperature led to the formation of an incomplete cubane-type cluster [(Cp S 2 Fe) 2 -(CpRu)S 4 ] + (1). Further treatment of 1 with [Cp'Ru(MeCN) 3 ] + (Cp' = Cp, Cp*) produced [(Cp S 2 Fe) 2 (CpRu)(Cp'Ru)S 4 ] 2 + . Cluster I reacted with DMAD to give the 1:1 adduct through formation of three sulfur-carbon bonds.
