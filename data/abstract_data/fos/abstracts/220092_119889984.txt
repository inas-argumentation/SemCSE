LEMS: application of the method to study the static quadrupole moment of the isomer in 179W
The method of the level mixing spectroscopy (LEMS) was applied for the first time for the study of the static quadrupole moments of high-K isomers in the A ≈ 180 mass region. Results from a preliminary experiment for the static quadrupole moment of the (750 ns) isomer in 179W give a limit for its upper value Q < 7b, corresponding to a quadrupole deformation of β2< 0.343.
