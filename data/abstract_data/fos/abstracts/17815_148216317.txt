On Power and Academic Profession Stratification——The Empirical Study on the Power Influence Faculties’ Rank Promotion
Based on empirical survey,this article investigates the influence on the faculties'rank promotion from administrative power and academic power of universities.The research finds that the influences from administrative and academic power on rank promotion of different levels of faculties are different,and the effects are also different.The study confirms that administrative power of universities has an important influence on faculties’rank promotion.Therefore,it is necessary to clarify the boundaries of administrative power and academic power,and to restrict the administrative power’s influence on faculties'rank promotion,and make full use of academic power in institute and department.
