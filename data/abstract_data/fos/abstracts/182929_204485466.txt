The Analysis of Economic Operation between Jiangsu and Zhejiang Province under the Background of Financial Crisis
The financial crisis makes a serious impact on export-oriented economy of the developed coastal areas,especially for the province of Jiangsu and Zhejiang.However,due to the economic structure between Jiangsu and Zhejiang is different,so the degree of impact is vary.In this paper,we adopt the method of empirical analysis to research the difference of the two province,such as the development type of the export-oriented economy,foreign trade,industry,finance and taxation,etc.Finally,we provide some practical advice,including the industrial upgrading,industrial transfer and the modern service industry,etc.
