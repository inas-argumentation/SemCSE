To do scholarly research and to cultivate human beings must rectify the prevailing practice
Universities should stick to the idea of "doing scholarly research as root and cultivating human beings as base"; students must possess a fine atmosphere of learning; teachers ought to obtain healthy practice of doing scholarly research and cultivating human beings; management personnels should have good style of management. Thus, we should fully set up the four practices as: highly responsible, practical and realistic, harmonious but different, and updating with the time. In order to rectify the prevailing practice, universities must deepen reforms, carry out the system of "under the leadership of Party committee, the management of executive branch and the educating of professors", stick to the principle of "governing the school according to law, virtue and democracy", try the best to prosper healthy practice and standardize management.
