Characterization of Activation Metabolism Activity of Indoxacarb in Insects by Liquid Chromatoraphy-Triple Quadrupole Mass Spectrometry
A new method for measuring indoxacarb N-decarbomethoxyllation activity catalyzed by insect crude enzymes, was developed by triple-quadrupole LC-MS/MS.The metabolite, N-decarbomethoxyllated indoxacarb( DCJW),was determined using electrospray isozation in positive ion mode.The quantitative and qualitative analyses were performed in MRM.The relative standard deviation( RSD)( n = 5) of DCJW was2.1%,indicating the metabolite had a good chemical stability in 30 h after the sample preparation.Five injections of DCJW standard solution in acetonitrile,five injections of the same sample in one incubation( substrates plus enzyme samples),and five injections of 5 separate metabolic reactions with the whole body of a susceptible strain of Plutella xylostella( L.) were individually injected into LC/MS system,and the RSD was 0.7%,1.1% and 2.7%,respectively.The average recoveries of 440,880 and 2200 pg DCJW added to incubation mixtures were 96.1%- 102.9%,and the RSD of recoveries of three added levels ranged4.8%- 9.4%.The limits of quantification and detection were 0.1 pg and 0.01 pg,respectively.A good linearity was achieved in the range of 46- 2310 pg( R2= 0.9996).This method with high sensitivity and simplicity was applied to the assay of indoxacarb N-decarbomethoxyllation activity of insect metabolic enzymes.A comparison of indoxacarb N-decarbomethoxyllation activity of an avermectin-resistant strain and a susceptible strain of Plutella xylostella( L.) was carried out using this novel LC-MS/MS method.The indoxacarb N-decarbomethoxyllation activity in the resistant strain was 3.43-fold of that in the susceptible strain,implicating that negative cross-resistance might exist between indoxacarb and avermectin in Plutella xylostella( L.).
