Novel high-performance Si-SiO2@Fe/C composite anodes from low-cost and industrial AlSiFe alloy powders
Using industrial, low-cost and easily available AlSiFe alloy powders as the precursor, the porous composite of silicon and silica containing iron with conductive carbon shell (Si-SiO2@Fe/C) are prepared by alkali etching combined with following pyrolysis process, in which the silicon nanoparticles are evenly embedded in the porous layered silica templates containing iron confirmed by scanning electron microscopy and X-ray diffraction analyses. The remained iron and fabricated porous structure as well as the coated carbon layer in the composite can not only alleviate the volume effect of silicon particles, but also enhance the conductivity of the electrode materials, which can result in excellent cyclic stability (830 mAh g−1 after 200 cycles at 200 mA g−1 with coulombic efficiency >99%), remarkable rate capability (reversible discharge specific capacity of 950, 900, 800, 650 and 500 mAh g−1 at 100, 200, 500, 1000 and 2000 mA g−1, respectively), low transfer resistance (35.71 Ω after 200 cycles at 200 mA g−1) and enhanced diffusion coefficient of lithium ions (increasing from 4.453E−15 cm2 s−1 to 2.945E−13 cm2 s−1 after 200 cycles). These outstanding results can ensure the targeted Si-SiO2@Fe/C composite as a promising anode for lithium ion batteries.
