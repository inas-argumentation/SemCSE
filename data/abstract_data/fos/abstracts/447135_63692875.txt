Simple Discussion on Heuristic Teaching Applied in the Design and Analysis of Algorithms for Computer Major
For the difficulties that students,in Computer Science major,would have while learning Design and Analysis of Algorithms,proposes heuristic teaching.In order to introduce the application of heuristic teaching in Design and Analysis of Algorithms,describes the teaching procedure of a problem named Maximum K Product as an example,which is solved by dynamic programming.Result of the application shows that the heuristic teaching has good effect.
