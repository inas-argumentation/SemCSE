Effect of B_4C Content in Plating Solution on Microstructure and Property of Electroplated Iron Coating
Iron coating was formed using electrochemical deposition technology and adding nonmetal insoluble solids B4C with high hardness.The surface evenly degree,hardness and corrosion resistance and wear resistance were studied by changing the concentration of B4C.The results show that the micro-cracks reduce,the hardness,corrosion resistance and wear resistance increase with the increase of B4C concentration.
