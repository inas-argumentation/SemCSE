Independent college is a new type of private college with Chinese characteristics
Chinese independent college is the product of both the socio-economic development and the higher education development,which promotes the development of the massification of higher education.Independent college is part of the private higher education and divided into two types: type A and type B.The type B independent college is "state-owned and locally governed" which is suitable to Chinese national conditions and is a new type of private college with Chinese characteristics.Several oppositional views appearing in society are one-sided and untenable.We should positively support for independent college and develop independent college steadily.
