Synthesis of α-arabinose nucleosides from 6-substituted uracils
The reaction of silylated 6-substituted uracils 5 with methyl 2,3,5-tri-O-benzoyl-α-D-arabinofuranoside (3) in the presence of trimethylsilyl trifluoromethanesulfonate (TMS triflate) afforded a mixture of the corresponding protected N1,N3-bis(arabinofuranosyl)uracils 6 and the 6-substituted 2,3,5-tri-O-benzoyl-α-D-arabinofuranosyluracils 8. Deprotection of 6 and 8 gave the corresponding α nucleosides 7 and 9. The reaction of silylated 6-ethyl- and 6-methylthiouracil with 3 afforded a mixture of S- and N3-glycosides 10 and 11. The glycosides 10 were deblocked by treatment with methanolic ammonia to obtain the deprotected α nucleosides 12.
