Effects of Se and S Application on the Uptake and Accumulation of P in Tobacco
The research was carried out in pot experiment and the results showed that the influence of selenium and sulfur on the uptake and accumulation of P changed with the growth period and organs of tobacco. P contents in leaves and stalk at early stage increased with the increase of selenium and sulfur addition.There was synergistic interaction between selenium and sulfur on P contents.In the absence of added SO 4 2 ,phosphorus contents of mature tobacco leaves were increased with increase of SeO 3 2 , while in the presence of added SO 4 2 ,P contents in tobacco leaves were decreased with the additions of SeO 3 2 .There were antagonistic interaction between SeO 3 2 and SO 4 2 in the distribution of P in tobacco leaves.Significantly positive correlations between the accumulation of P,S and Se in tobacco,and negative correlation between S content in the leaves and P content in stalk at mature stage were found.
