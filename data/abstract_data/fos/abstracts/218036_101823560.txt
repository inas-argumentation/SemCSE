Manufacture of vegetable and fruit moon cake stuffing with low sugar
The manufacture of white gourd and honey-filled moon cake stuffing and the study of the stuffing' prescription were introduced in the paper, such as the main materials, technique character, key point and the quality of stuffing. The kind of stuffing are mainly made of white gourd, honey, maltosyl and peanut oil. The moon cake is light sugar and fat with delicate fragrance.
