Exploration into a sustainable development mode-benign interaction between investment and school running——A case study of Hunan International Economy College
The fundamental factor that constricts the sustainable and healthy development of civil-run colleges in China is the conflict between extensive need of investment funds in schools and the lack of investment initiatives and the limit of investment capability.The history of private colleges'development shows that the mode heavily relying on school fees should not be adopted nowadays.In order to solve the problem,we must change our mode that the colleges should shift from being a fund consumer to being a fund growth player.Only in this way can the development of schools and enterprises be sustainable and reach the stage of benign interaction between investment and school running.In this aspect,Hunan International Economy College stands out as a good example,gaining obvious achievement.
