Countermeasures for the Lacking of Impetus for Environmental Public Litigation
Under the background of frequently-occurred environmental pollution,China has made a series of researches on environmental public interest litigation.However,there is still a considerable gap between the implementation effect and the expected result,lacking of impetus being one of the important manifestations.The underlying reasons are listed as follows:the legal system is not perfect;the court is reluctant to accept the public interest litigation cases;judges dislike such cases;the cost limits the litigation;and public participation becomes empty words.Thus,we must put forward countermeasures and suggestions from the following aspects,such as,complementing the substantial law of environmental public interest litigation,setting up a systematic procedure mechanism of environmental public interest litigation,reforming the system setup of environmental courts and tribunals and encouraging the public participation.Moreover,we should regard improving the basic theory of the public interest litigation as driving force,take the easing of judicial practice as hybrid power,and consider the public participation as fundamental force,so as to establish a systematic environment public litigation dynamic mechanism and get out of the impetus-lacking predicament.
