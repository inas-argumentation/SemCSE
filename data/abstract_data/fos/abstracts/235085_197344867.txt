1H and 13C NMR Spectra of Technically Important Sodium 1- and 2-Aminonaphthalenesulfonates
The 1 H and 13 C NMR spectra of twenty-one technically important sulfonated 1- and 2-naphthylamines have been measured in deuterium oxide. The substituent chemical shifts (SCS) of a sulfonic acid group on naphthylamine skeleton have been calculated from the chemical shifts assigned.
