The Study of Skin Allergy and Acute Toxicity of Blumea Balsamifera Oil
Blumea balsamifera oil is the yellow oil from Blumea balsamifera(L.) D C. The allergy and acute toxicity of external application of B. balsamifera oil were evaluated for clinical application. The regular methods of skin allergic experiment and acute toxic experiment were used in the study. In the allergic experiment, guinea pig was used as the experimental animal, and B. balsamifera oil was applied externally on the intact skin to evaluate the allergy. The allergic situation of guinea pigs were observed. In acute toxic experiment, rats were used as the experimental animal, and the B. balsamifera oil was applied externally on the intact and damaged skins to evaluate the acute toxicity, respectively. The acute toxic situation was observed. The results of the experiment showed that the external application of B. balsamifera oil was no allergic response on the intact skin of guinea pigs, and also no acute toxicity on the intact and damaged skins of rats.
