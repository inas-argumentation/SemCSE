EPC Casting Process and Numerical Simulation of Connection Cylinder
Based on the analysis of the structure and size of connecting cylinder,the EPC casting method was determined to obtain connecting cylinder casting.Then according to the characteristics,experiences and actual production condition of EPC casting,EPC casting process of connection cylinder was designed.The solidification process of the casting was simulated by View-Cast,and the reason of casting defects was analyzed.Based on the casting defects obtained by simulation,the riser and gating system position were optimized and the new casting process was put forward.Thereby the waste products reduces and the product quality improves.
