On the Performance Influence Factors of Training Talents in the Enterprise Graduate Workstation and Its Evolution Mechanism
The structural equation model analysis on dynamic co-evolution mechanism effect among different combinations of variables showed:the overall knowledge had indirect effect on talents training;there existed a workstation environment path dependence;furthermore,internalization of implicit knowledge was the core elements of culture;among various factors of talent cultivation,the first responsibility of university teacher's role was not due to the culture environment change and enterprise mentor participation;there was a significant difference in the single dimension factors,such as university tutor,enterprise R D activities tutor,organizational environment,and its independent utility decreased;performance of personnel training in different combinations of variables displayed different dynamic co-evolution mechanism,and there was a significant difference in effect.
