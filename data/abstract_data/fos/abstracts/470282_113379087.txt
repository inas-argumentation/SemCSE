A New Type Adaptive Algorithm of Ground Distance Protection
Based on the single-phase earthing model,this paper calculates the additional impedance caused by load currents and different impedance angles between two sources when grounded through transition resistance,and revises the criterion of the traditional distance protection by use of it.Accordingly operation criterion of adaptive ground distance protection is obtained.It can compute more accurately the resistance component and reactance component of fault line impedance,which makes resistance line and reactance line of quadrilateral characteristic distance relay easier to set,avoiding shortening protection area because of declining reactance line and avoiding complicate analysis of resistance line dodging load performance.Thereby overreaching and under-reach problems of distance relay are solved.Simulation results prove the proposed adaptive criterion possesses good performance and improves reliability and sensitivity.
