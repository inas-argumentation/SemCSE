BIOGENETIC TYPE SYNTHESIS OF 10-BROMO-α-CHAMIGRENE
Bromonium ion induced cyclization of methyl 2,3-cis-farnesoate (III) was achieved by the action of 2,4,4,6-tetrabromocyclohexadienone (II) to give monocyclic bromoester (IV), which was converted into dl-10-bromo-α-chamigrene (I), a naturally occurring brominated sesquiterpenoid.
