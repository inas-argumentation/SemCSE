Chemistry of hetero analogs of isoflavones 26. Synthesis of 2-alkyl derivatives of 3-(thiazol-2-yl)-and 3-(benzothiazol-2-yl)chromones
Treatment of substituted 2-(2,4-dihydroxyphenacyl)thiazole and 2-(2,4-dihydroxyphenacyl)benzothiazole with propionic and isobutyric anhydrides gives the substituted 2-ethyl-and 2-isopropyl-7-acyloxychromones. The use of pivaloyl chloride gives 1-pivaloyloxy-2-hetarylstyrene derivatives.
