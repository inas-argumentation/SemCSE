The effect of the substitution of B2O3 to P2O5 on Tg and electrical properties of 0.65 ((1+x) Ag2O P2O5) 0.35 AgI glasses
Abstract Glasses of the series 0.65 ((1+ x ) Ag 2 O y B 2 O 3 (1− y ) P 2 O 5 ) 0.35AgI (0⩽ x ⩽0.6 and 0⩽ y ⩽0.2) have been prepared. Their glass transformation temperature, T g , electrical conductivity, σ, and conduction activation energy, E σ and preexponential factor, σ 0 , have been evaluated. The substitution of B 2 O 3 to P 2 O 5 has been found to be useful in making both T g and σ to increase. The results have been interpreted on the basis of a structure composed of AgI microdomains in a phosphate or borophosphate matrix and on the basis of the diffusion path model for ionic conduction.
