Research and Progress of Hot Stamping of Ultrahigh Strength Steel
Hot stamping technology is one of the important way to reduce the autobody mass and increase the impact resistance and crash performance.Based on the requirements of the hot forming steel sheet and hot stamping tool's material and design,the research and development progress of ultrahigh strength steel are summarized,the research status and main direction of hot stamping technology are analyzed.The key points of hot stamping of ultrahigh strength steel are discussed.These provide instruction for the application of hot stamping of ultrahigh strength steel sheet in automotive industry.
