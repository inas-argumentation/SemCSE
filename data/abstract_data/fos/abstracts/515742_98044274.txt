Surface structures of fixed Cr catalysts and their catalytic activities for CO oxidation and C3H6 hydrogenation.
Two types of active fixed Cr catalysts(Al2O3 and SiO2 supports) and surface structures of active sites are reported. Coordinatively unsaturated Cr2+ ions with three vacant positions in the “virgin” fixed catalyst showed a high activity for C3H6 hydrogenation Five-coordinated Cr3+ species with suitably strong exchange interaction between Cr3+ in a Cr3+–O–Cr3+ cluster of the “rearranged” fixed catalyst created by surface reconstruction were found to be active sites for CO oxidation.
