Effect of clomiphene on serum LH levels in immature rats primed with PMS (authors transl)
Previous reports found that a single injection of clomiphene blocks PMS-induced ovulation in immature rates up to 24 hours. To measure the time of the LH surge for ovulation PMS (3IU) was subcutaneously injected to female Wistar rats at 10:00 a.m. on 24-26 days of age. Clomiphene-citrate: 2.5 mg/100g b.w. in 30% ethanol-saline solution was subcutaneously injected 6 hours after PMS. At autopsy ovarian and uterine weights were recorded and oviducts were examined for ova. Serum LH levels were measured by radioimmunoassay. Ovulation rates were 5/5 in PMS-treated control group. 0/5 in clomiphene-treated group 72 hours after PMS. After 96 hours 5 out of 6 treated with clomiphen ovulated after PMS. After 48 hours of PMS injection significant decreases of uterine weights were observed in clomiphen groups. Highly elevated levels of serum LH were observed during 54-62 hours in PMS-treated controls and during 78-79 hours in the clomiphene group indicating a 24 hour delay of LH discharge in the latter. The anti-estrogen drug may affect estrogen-receptors in the CNS-pituitary axis responsible for the LH surge on the second day-afternoon in immature rats. (Authors Modified) (Summary in ENG)
