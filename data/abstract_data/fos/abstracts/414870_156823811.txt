A Survey of Chinese Traditional Music Education in Regular Institutions of Higher Education in Anhui
Through a survey of Chinese traditional music education in 16 regular institutions of higher education in Anhui,the author has found in it various problems concerning the proportion of Chinese traditional music in the music courses offered in schools and college students' attitude towards and their level of cognition on Chinese traditional music.These problems bring the author into the thought of the situation of Chinese traditional music education in regular institutions of higher education,and lead the author to put forward some constructive ideas and suggestions from the aspects of curriculum,teaching content,teaching plans and teaching programs.In the paper the author furthermore discusses how to make efficient use of regular institutions of higher education to conduct Chinese traditional music education and to pass on the magnificent heritage of Chinese music culture from generation to generation.
