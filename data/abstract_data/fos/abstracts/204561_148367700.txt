Tuition fee and financial aid policy to foreign students
China's higher education service to foreign students can be regarded as private goods because it possesses little attributes of public goods, thus its price cannot be based on the cost\|compensation policy. Institutions of higher learning should be allowed to decide the tuition fee and make profits according to market needs, its quality, educational ideas as well as regions and majors. The potential external benefits can be distributed; accordingly, the public\|private expense distinction between foreign students should be abolished. The tuition fee and financial aid to students from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan should be reconsidered.
