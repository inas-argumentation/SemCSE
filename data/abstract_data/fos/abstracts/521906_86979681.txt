Effects of 1-MCP on the Physiology of Harvested Indian Jujube
Indian jujube(Ziziphus mauritiana)fruits of the cultivar'Gao lang No.1'were harvested at about 80%-matured stage and were exposed to 1 μL/L\ 1-methylcyclopropene(1-MCP).The fruit were then stored at(25±1)℃ and 60%～65% relative humidity and later assessed for physiological characteristics.1-MCP treatment delayed the weight loss and decay incidence.Treating had a positive effect on the firmness of fruit and retention color.Treatment increased the activities of superoxide dismutase(SOD),catalase(CAT)and peroxidase(POD),and reduced malondialdehyde(MDA)content.
