Position Information and Office Information Data Transmission System Based on GPRS/CDMA
Build into an opportunity for the high-speed development and establishment of GPRS/CDMA 1X network of the mobile data communication service,this paper proposes a kind of wireless data transmission and process system of position information and office information based on GPRS/CDMA. The system, which is based on the public mobile network platform of China Mobile and China Union, realizes the data transmission and process of ships and vehicles' position fixing,supervising and controlling,navigation information and office information.
