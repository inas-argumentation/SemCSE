Research on the stability of extracted anthocyanin from vitis
Effect of different concentration of hydrogen peroxide,asco bic acid,sodium sulfite,potassium sorbate and sodium benzoate on the stability of extracted anthocyanin from vitis were studied.It was found that vitis anthocyanin can be rapidly oxidized by hydrogen peroxide,asco bic acid and sodium sulfite decreased the stability of vitis anthocyanin,and the higher the concentration they were,the more rapid the decline speed was,low concentration of potassium sorbate and sodium benzoate affected vitis anthocynin little.
