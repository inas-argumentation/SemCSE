Research on Teaching Reformation of Fundamentals of University Information Technology
Based on investigation of information technology relative courses of high school in Fujian proving and the Fundamentals of University Information Technology in Fujian province both within and outside,combines the own actual work to put forward the reformation of teaching way and method,and update teaching content according to the existing problems in teaching of Fundamentals of University Information Technology,and improve teaching quality and effect in computer in order to fit in with the needs of the society.
