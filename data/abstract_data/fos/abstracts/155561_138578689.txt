Study of Microstructure and Properties of Mg-RE(Ce Nd Y)-Zn-Zr Alloys
Microstructure and properties were investigate in the Mg-RE(Ce Nd Y)-Zn-Zr alloys by using Optical Microscopy(OM),Scanning Electron Microscopy(SEM) and X-Ray Diffraction analysis. The structural feature and fracture behaviour of the investigated alloys were revealed. The proof strength at room temperature of the three alloys were 251.3MP,275.5MPa and 253.3MPa respectively for the as-extrusion+T2 treatment(+T6 treatment). The proof strength of the 2~# and 3~# alloys at elevated temperature can been maintained 187.3MPa at 150℃ and 219.6MPa at 250℃ respectively.
