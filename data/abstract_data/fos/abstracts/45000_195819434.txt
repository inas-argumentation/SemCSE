New horizons in catalysis using modified and unmodified pentasil zeolites
Know-how obtained from petrochemical processes on the catalytical properties of pentasil zeolites is being used for selective organic syntheses in the chemistry of intermediates. The isomerization properties are demonstrated by doublebond migrations in olefins with carbonyl groups and by the aldehyde/ketone rearrangement. The dehydration properties are used in the conversion of aldehydes to 1, 3-dienes, in aldol condensation reactions and for the preparation of dicarboxylic acid nitriles from acids and ammonia. Therefor this paper concentrates on hydrocarbons with carbonyl groups.
