Stem curve equations for Pinus massoniana standing trees
This article is based on data analysis of twenty-five Pinus massoniana of stem part,to establish Pinus massoniana standing trees stem curve equations and evaluate them.The results indicate that quartic poly-nomial as stem curve equation is more suitable than cubic polynomial in simulating stem base shape,and calcu-lating stem volume;the breast-height form factor of Pinus massoniana is 0.499.The research results could offer theoretical basis for revising and updating management numerical tables of pinus massoniana.
