Minor EBB-structures and shell orientations on a tidal beach (bay of Arcachon, France)
Minor sedimentary structures occurring on a small tidal flat at La Pointe, Bay of Arcachon (Gironde, France) are analyzed in the light of their usefulness for interpreting ancient environments, particularly as indicators of short subaerial periods in the history of a sediment. The structures may be grouped into the categories: ripple breaching, rill marks, ripple erosion, wave sweeping, and “dribble” marks. The relative abundances of pelecypod shells differ significantly in various parts of the beach, reckoned in a straight line perpendicular to the shoreline. The ratio of concave-upwards to concave-downwards oriented valves is unlike for different species across the beach.
