"Associative concept learning in animals" by Zentall, Wasserman, and Urcuioli: a commentary.
This opportunity to comment on the article by Zentall, Wasserman, and Urcuioli allows me to thank them for their impressive body of work. Their pioneering efforts have influenced the careers of a large number of researchers who have been interested the analysis of stimulus control (me included).
