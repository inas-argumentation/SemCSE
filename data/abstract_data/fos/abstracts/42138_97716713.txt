Photochemical Isomerization of trans-Dichlorotetrakis(dimethylphenylphosphine)osmium(II)
Photochemical isomerization of trans-dichlorotetrakis(dimethylphenylphosphine)osmium(II) to its cis-form was confirmed by NMR and IR spectroscopy. The quantum yield of isomerization was 0.9 at 410 nm which was much larger than that (0.03 at 410 nm) of trans-dichlorobis(diphenylphosphinoethane)osmium(II).
