A review of studies about the chemotrophy and lipid-lowering activity of Opuntia Dillenii
This article summarizes the progress of study about the chemotrophy and lipid-lowering activity of Opuntia Dillenii. Opuntia Dillenii, which contains affluent aminoacid, trace element, protein,vitamin, mineral matter and so on,can be used as greens and medicine. Opuntia Dillenii has the effect of lowering the blood fat, blood sugar and resisting tumor.
