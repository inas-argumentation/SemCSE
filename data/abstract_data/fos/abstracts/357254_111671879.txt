Numerical Simulation of Loose Tooling Forging Forming Process for Grinding Roller Shaft
The grinding roller shaft is a key part of ATOX grinding roller,which has a specific shape and is a represent of large forgings.Because it is difficult to form,the loose tooling forming process is used.The forming of square part of grinding roller shaft was mainly researched.It is difficult to forge because of the corner which limites metal flowing.The simulation software DEFORM-3D was used to simulate the forming process of square part.The appropriate process was obtained through improving method repeatly,which made the square part form well.The eligible forings also proved the rationality of the technical process.
