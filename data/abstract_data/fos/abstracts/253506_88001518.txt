Comparison of separation and purification methods of soybean Kunitz-type trypsin inhibitor(KTI)
Soybean meal was used as the raw material. With the crude extract preparation of soybean Kunitz-type trypsin inhibitor(KTI) and chromatographic methods, a higher efficiency separation and purification method of KTI was studied and compared with the one-step method. Two methods were performed. One was that a series of chromatographic methods including ion-exchange, affinity chromatography and Sephadex filtration; the other was that one-step affinity chromatography purification. The results showed that, from 10 g soybean seeds, through multistep, purification factor of 72.39 times of the KTI was obtained while using one-step purification, the purification factor was only 30 times. A series study of KTI showed that the inhibitor has a relative molecular weight of 19.4 ku by SDS-PAGE. After comparing analyzing the above result, we found purification factor of multistep is much higher than that of one-step method, suggesting that multistep method is superior to one-step purification.
