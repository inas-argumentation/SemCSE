Social Work Embedded in Chinese Academic Advising:The Cause,Path and Guarantee
Academic advising was an important measure to help students solve the learning problems,to promote students' development,and to improve education quality.Based on the limitation of students' affairs working-pattern and the characteristics of social work,it put forward the idea that academic advising should use the experience of social work.With the embedment of social work,it would advance the embedded development of academic advising through the construction of the guarantee mechanism of regulations,professionals and advising center,to realize its objects and functions.
