Optimization for Extraction of Proanthocyanidins from Grape Seeds Using Response Surface Method
The extraction technology of proanthocyanidins from grape seeds was studied using response Surface Method(RSM),and acetone concentration,solid-liquid ratio,temperature and time were selected as experimental the factors.Based on the analysis of significance and interaction among different factors,the optimal condition of extraction technology was obtained as followed: acetone concentration was 75%,solid-liquid ratio was 1 ∶ 10,time was 44 min and temperature was 51 ℃,and the predicted value and measured value of proanthocyanidins yield was 2.90% and 2.88%,and the purity was 55.92%.
