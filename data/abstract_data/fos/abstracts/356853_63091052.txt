Resources Optimization Allocation Strategy Based on Cross-layer Bargaining Game Over Cognitive Radio Network
How to reasonably allocate the limited spectrum is the one of core issues in cognitive radio network. Researched on the requirement of the physical pow er control and M AC layer bandw idth allocation,a multiple constraint cross-layer optimization model for maximizing the system throughput is proposed in this paper. And then BPAA-CLBG algorithm( Bandw idth and Pow er Allocation Algorithm-Cross Layer Bargaining Game) is given based on the model. Theoretical analysis and simulation experiment show that the algorithm can effectively improve the frequency efficiency and the throughput of the system is obviously improved compared w ith the existing algorithms.
