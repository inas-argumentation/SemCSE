A Study of Coordinated Development among Industrialization,Urbanization and Agricultural Modernization in Anhui Province Based on Efficiency Measurement
Taking thedevelopment efficiency of 16 cities in Anhui Province in industrialization,urbanization and agricultural modernization(short for"Sanhua")as the starting point,the paper measures with a HR model the efficiency coordination index.The results reveal that there is a serious regional imbalance in industrial efficiency in the province;the efficiency of agricultural modernization is generally at a low level;and with respect to most of the cities studied,the development of Sanhua is in low efficiency and lack of coordination.Then,from modern agricultural perspective,an exploration of Sanhua coordination influencing factors is carried out via the Ologit model.Empirical results show that the improvement of agricultural mechanization and industrialization,embodied in the wide use of machinery and other industrial inputs,is an important entering point for the efficient and coordinated development of Sanhua.
