Biomass and its influencing factors of Platyclatdus orientalis plantation in the limestone mountains of Xuzhou.
Biomass and productivity are critical parameters for assessing forest carbon sequestration capability and the quantitative estimates are required for forest biomass carbon accounting.We studied the 50-year-old Platyclatdus orientalis plantations in the limestone mountains of Xuzhou,and the results showed that:(1) Whole-tree was in a range of 8.88-80.71 kg.Allocation of biomass to organs followed a sequence of trunkbranchrootleaves or trunkbranchleavesroot;(2) Significant correlation was found between the whole tree biomass and aboveground biomass,followed by trunks and aboveground biomass.Correlation between organ biomass and tree height was lower than that with diameter at breast height and D2H;(3)Biomass for stands of 8 plots was in the range of 28.92-75.42 t·hm-2,with the average value of 54.35 t·hm-2;(4) Biomass for stands were positively correlated with the north aspect index,while other factors took no effect on it.The response curves of biomass or aboveground biomass to stand density were both unimodal.Biomass and aboveground biomass had the maximum values when stand density was closed to 2600 trees·hm-2.
