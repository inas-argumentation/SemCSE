Study on Social Work Specialized Practice Educational Model
In the social work specialized practice education,Practice,an important component of the specialized practice educational plan,is a learning mode which other courses can't substitute for as classroom instruction,if no social work practice,there is no way to achieve personnel training objectives of social work specialized education.this paper analyze the form,content,the roles and responsibilities of social work practice education from the characteristics,the objectives and principles,and proposed suggestions for social work specialized practice educational model aiming at the current difficult position.
