Conservation of Rare and Endangered Plants in Dabashan Nature Reserve
Based on the book of Chinese plants (Vol. 1 and Vol. 2) and the national important wild conservative plants list,there are 39 families, 66 genera , 77 species of the rare and endangered plants in Dabashan Nature Reserve, including 1 family ,1 genus,1 specie Pteridophyte, 5 families ,8 genera, 12 species Gymnosperm and 33 families ,57 genera and 64 species Angiosperm.In terms of growing types, the rare and endangered plants in Dabashan Nature Reserve consist of 45 arbors, 3 shrubs, 21 herbs and 8 vines. The features of the rare and endangered plants in this area are ancient origin ,obvious endemism ,complicated elements. Some proposals for pretecting these resources are presented according to their growing types,distribution and endangered causes.
