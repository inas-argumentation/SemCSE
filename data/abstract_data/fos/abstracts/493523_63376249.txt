Study on the cluster analysis of physical workload of university's physical education
The rationality of the physical workload of university physical education (PE) is a main factor that evaluation the quality and scientificity of PE In order to discuss the distributing and trait of physical workload of university PE more accurutey, this paper collected one hundred and sixty thousand data from four hundred and fifty eight marked students in thirty universities in China.The authors try to use the methods of K mean cluster analysis to value the heart rate of PE, and build a series of mathematical models. In order to get the result more finely and accurately,the authors use the software of physical workload intensity data analyze system of university to carry out analyzing respectively.It provides a reference for evaluation of physical workload of university PE.
