Analysison Explosion Hardening Mechanism of High Manganese Steel
The diffraction pattern and microstructure of unexploded and exploded high-manganese steel were analyzed by XRD(X-ray Diffraction) and HREM(High Resolution Electron Microscopy).The results reveal that the microstructure of exploded high manganese steel is still austenite,without martensitic transformation,and also indicate the microscopic mechanics of explosion hardening is that the interior of the exploded high manganese steel is impacted by high energy blast wave of explosion,lattice constant increases and the lattice distortion is aggravated.Large high density faults and dislocations appear,which can hinder the dislocation movement,then resulting in the hardening of high-manganese steel.
