BRIEF COMMUNICATIONS: Fluctuations of the intensity of radiation emitted by multimode gas lasers due to a nonequidistant mode distribution
An experimental study was made of the influence of the mode composition of the radiation emitted by freely oscillating helium-neon lasers on the intensity fluctuations. These fluctuations were measured at the wavelength of ? = 0.63 ? in the frequency range of 0.5?100 kHz. There was a correlation between low-frequency fluctuations of the intensity and the envelope of high-frequency fluctuations near intermode beat frequencies.
