Comparison of fractal and pore models for electrolyte/electrode interfaces
Fractal and pore models connecting the fractional-power frequency dependence of the impedance of electrolyte/blocking electrode interface to the interface microstructure are compared. It is demonstrated that (a) Liu's fractal model is similar to a pore model because of the way the fractal interface is constructed; (b) the validity of the relation between the fractal dimension D and the impedance frequency exponent n derived by Liu depends crucially on the way the fractal dimension is defined; and (c) the relation between D and n derived by Nyikos and Pajkossy is questionable because their claim that all fractal interfaces have a fractional-power-law impedance is inconsistent with our numerical results.
