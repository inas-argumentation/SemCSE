Enlightenment on the Changes from the Stock System to the State-owned System
The 16th Committee of the Party offered the main realization form from stock system to state-owned system. This theory has offered the significant discovery about the realization form of ownership. The enlightenment is that we should approach the realization form of ownership, summarize the practice of reformation and research the way of resolution of the problems in order to have more knowledge about the character, action and its significance to carry out the principles of the 16th Committee of the Party of China.
