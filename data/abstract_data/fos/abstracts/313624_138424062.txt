Processing Technology Analysis of Cold Bending Pipe for Oil and Gas Pipeline
The principle and process of cold bending pipe by the vertical hydraulic cold bending machine were introduced.The influence of the processing technology on appearance quality and mechanical properties of cold bending pipe was analyzed.It is believed that the essence of cold bending technology is a superposition process of some forming angle within the unit step.Reasonably controlling the various components of cold bending machine and forming angle within the unit step,the imperfection such as ovality,wrinkle,flaw of coating can be effectively reduced.The mechanics experiments indicate that the cold bending deformation has less affected on tensile strength.The inside yield strength in longitudinal direction of the cold bending pipe reduce and the outside yield strength rise.Comparing with unformed pipe,the low temperature toughness of cold bending pipe is reduced and ductile brittle transition temperature is raised.
