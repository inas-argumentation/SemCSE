Effect of Rare Earth La Modification on Microstructure of A356 Aluminium Alloy
The effects of the holding time and the stirring time on the microstructure of A356 alloy modified by rare earth La were researched.The results indicate that when the holding time is 90 min,most of the eutectic silicon become small granular,and the α(Al) grains are refined,the best modification is achieved.The holding time is greatly shorten by stirring.The microstructure is as fine as only insulating for 90 min when the A356 alloy is stirred for 3 min and insulated for 10 min,and the holding time in modification is shorten for 80 min.
