On Hochschild Cohomology of a Self-injective Special Biserial Algebra Obtained by a Circular Quiver with Double Arrows
We calculate the dimensions of the Hochschild cohomology groups of a self-injective special biserial algebra Λ s obtained by a circular quiver with double arrows. Moreover, we give a presentation of the Hochschild cohomology ring modulo nilpotence of Λ s by generators and relations. This result shows that the Hochschild cohomology ring modulo nilpotence of Λ s is finitely generated as an algebra.
