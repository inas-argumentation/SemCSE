Investigation into poverty-stricken undergraduates' employment vision in ten provinces of western and middle China
The study is based on a questionnaire survey of 9641 undergraduates of 17 higher education institutes(HEIs) of ten provinces in Western and Middle China.The results showed that there aren't significant differences between poverty-stricken and non-poverty-stricken undergraduates in employment expectation and cognition in Western and Middle China.The results are contrary to authors' expectation,and are also different from most present research results.Higher education can narrow the gap of knowledge,ability,and social capital between the undergraduates coming from the poverty and other families,thus the impact from family economic condition will decrease with higher education.Comparatively speaking,higher education is more significant for undergraduates' individual development than family economy.
