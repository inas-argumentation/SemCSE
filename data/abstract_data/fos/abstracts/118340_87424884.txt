Advance in variations of polyphenol oxidase(PPO) activity and genetic properties in wheat varieties
Polyphenol oxidase(PPO) in grain is regarded as a major factor resulting in time-dependent darkening of wheat end products,particularly for Asian noodles.In this study,a wholemeal assay for PPO activity was used and the results were as follows:coefficient index between two crop seasons(2004 and 2005)was 0.839 in PPO activity of 131 wheat varieties,and that between F2 single plants and F3 lines was 0.727 in the combination Yangmai 158×Huaimai 18,both were highly significant.PPO activity tested by catechol as substrate was significant correlated with that by L-DOPA(r=0.907) in the progenies from Yannong19×Annong0016.The genetic analysis indicated that PPO activity of the combinations was controlled by two major genes.Based on the cDNA sequence(GenBank Accession Number:AY515506),8 pairs of primers were designed using the software'Primer premier 5.0'.One of the markers designated as STS01 can amplify a 560 bp fragment in cultivars with low PPO activity,while no PCR fragment was detected in cultivars with high PPO activity.To understand the relationship between STS01 and PPO18,which was developed by Institute of Crop Science,Chinese Academy of Agricultural Science,allelic variations in both STS01(L1 and H1) and PPO18(L2 and H2) loci were detected.The results suggested that two markers were genetically independent and the allelic variation of two PPO genes could explain 63.06% of phenotypic variance for PPO activity of 131 Chinese wheat cultivars through two crop seasons.The tests for PPO activity demonstrated that the variations were highly significant in wheat varieties,and there was great potential for the genetic improvement.The varieties with low PPO activity included Yu02321,Ningmai9,02P157,02Y151,34-th195,Ning0076,Emai19,et al.
