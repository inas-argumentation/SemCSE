Study on Tempering Brittleness of Flux Cored Submerged Welding Wire W110 Used for Roller Building up Welding
The tempering brittleness of flux cored submerged welding wire W110 used for roller building up was studied.The composition of deposited metal of flux cored submerged welding wire W110 was 0.3%～0.45%C,12.5%～14.5%Cr,1.0%～1.6%Mn,0.5%～0.8%Mo,0.8%～1.5%Ni,and 0.5%～1.2%Si.As-welded deposited metal of submerged welding wire W110 was tempered at different temperatures.By hardness testing,impact toughness testing and micro-analysis of fracture surface,the properties of the samples after tempering were studied.The results show that,within different tempering temperature range,the hardness of the tempered samples is not in agreement with their impacting energy,but which are nearly identical with the fracture surface aspects.The tempering brittleness temperature ranges of W110-deposited metal are decided by impact toughness values of tempering samples,that are 100～150 ℃ and 450～550 ℃.
