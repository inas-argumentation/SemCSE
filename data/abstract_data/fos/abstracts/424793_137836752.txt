Review on Modification Mechanism of Primary Faceted Crystal during the Solidification Course
The growth process of primary faced crystal,the effectof different modification on the morphology and the mechanical properties in different alloys are reviewed.The nucleation and growth mechanism and the effect of the different modificators on the morphology of primary faced crystal are introduced.Finally,some suggestions are proposed on the study of the primary faced crystal phases and it is hoped that it could provide the reference for this kind of alloy research and the application.
