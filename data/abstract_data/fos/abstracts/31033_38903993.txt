Catholic Hospitals, are Mergers the Way to Go?
Mergers of Catholic hospitals with non-Catholic entities are becoming increasingly common. For example, within the Archdiocese of Chicago, there are nineteen Catholic hospitals (Kenedy Directory 2013). Sixteen (Table 1) are involved in mergers, acquisitions, or relationships with six other corporate entities. These include (Table 2) Ascension, Presence, Trinity, Catholic Health International, Franciscan, and Mt. Sinai.

At a time when Catholic hospital identity is in question, these corporate relationships pose troubling dilemmas. Are these hospitals first Catholic or are they first businesses? Both purposes: Catholic or business, are usually compatible, and even happily complimentary, but there can be conflicts. It is worthwhile to discuss Catholic hospitals as spiritual witnesses and/or business resources for patients, pastors, and staff.
