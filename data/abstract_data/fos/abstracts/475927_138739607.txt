Effect of Aging Treatment on Hardness and Electrical Conductivity of Polybasic Copper Alloy
The effects of aging treatment and deformation on the hardness and electrical conductivity of polybasic copper alloy were studied by electric conductivity instrument,hardness tester,metallograph and TEM.The results show that the hardness and electrical conductivity of the alloy can be greatly improved by deformation and aging treatment,and the satisfied hardness and electrical conductivity of polybasic copper alloy,120.5 HV and 75.5%IACS respectively,can be achieved by the treatment with 40 % deformation after 930 ℃×1 h solid solution,then aging at 450 ℃ for 1 h and 80 % deformation after 930 ℃×1 h solid solution,then aging at 500℃ for 2 h.
