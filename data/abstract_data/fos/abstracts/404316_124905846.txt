The Application of Watershed Algorithm based on Mathematics Morphology to Segment the Water System Images
This paper gives the method that deal with water system remote sensing images. It bases on the Mathematical Morphology. It is generally called ″Watershed″. But it is different from the traditional division arithmetic. It gains the conclusion though selecting the lowest points and centering these points dilating to get the division areas. In the course of dilation, it depends on the eight adjacent areas. It not only makes the efficiency improved, but also shortens time. Then. This method can apply to the image with large quantity data.
