Process for the Extraction of Polyphenol from Agrocybe Aegeritawith Organic Solvent
The process for the extraction of Polyphenol from Agrocybe Aegerita with organic solvent was studied.The influences of the extraction temperature,ethanol concentration,extraction time,material-solvent ratios on the yield were investigated.The process was optimized as the following: extraction temperature of 80 ℃,ethanol concentration of 50%,extraction time of 3 h,material-solvent ratios of 1:30.The extraction rate was 14.96%.
