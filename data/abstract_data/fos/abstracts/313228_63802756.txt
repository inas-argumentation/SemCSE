Reliability Assessment of Object Recognition Based on Epipolar Constraints
Based on epipolar constraints,a method of reliability assessment of object recognition is proposed in vision-aided terminal guidance system which used inertial navigation system (INS). When sensor noise comes into play,the extra value had to be added to maintain equality of the epipolar constraint is called a residual,which can be computed from the information of INS and images. When the error of the camera attitude,which proposed by INS,is lower than 0.3 degree,the value of residual can reflect the error of object in images. The results of theoretical deduction and experiment indicate that the residual,as reliability assessment of object recognition,has several desirable properties.
