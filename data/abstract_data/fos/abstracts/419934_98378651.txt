Research on the chemistry of pyrazolidine. 25. Alkylation of 4-cyclohexylidene-1-phenyl-3,5-dioxopyrazolidine
Products of alkylation in the 2 and 4 positions, as well as at the C3=O group, were obtained by the reaction of 1-phenyl-4-cyclohexylidene-3,5-dioxopyrazolidine with alkyl halides in the presence of sodium methoxide. The reaction of sodium deuteromethoxide with 1-phenyl-4-cyclohexylidene-3,5-dioxopyrazolidine was studied.
