A Novel Photocatalysis of Phosphorous Porphyrins for Hydration of Benzonitrile
Under irradiation with visible light, oxophosphorous porphyrins [(EtioP)PR(=O), 1 (R = Ph) and 2 (R = Et); EtioP = etioporphyrinato I] and phenyl(methoxy)phosphorous porphyrin chloride [[(EtioP)PPh(OMe)]Cl, 3] catalyzed hydration of benzonitrile to benzamide in alkaline methanol.
