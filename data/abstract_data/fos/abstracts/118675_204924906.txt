Stellar systems following the R1/m luminosity law.
The structural and dynamical properties of isotropic spherical galaxies with surface luminosity profile following the R 1/m -law are studied. For these models the luminosity density, the potential, the velocity dispersion and other related deprojected properties are presented in detail, together with analytical expressions for their asymptotic behavior at small and large radii. The parameter m for the computed models covers the range [2, 10]
