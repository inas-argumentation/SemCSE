Industrial-worthy plasma torches: State-of-the-art
Plasma arc torches are being applied today as unique heating tools in industrial processes. The many benefits of using plasma torches are being demonstrated in different production plants and prototype equipment around the world. And the successful demonstration of this versatile, low-mass, controlled high-enthalpy, selective-atmosphere, heat source is generating new applications. Industry today is benefiting from plasma heating and will in the future derive more benefits from this proven heating technology. There are many different types of plasma arc torches available. Single torch power ratings range from 20 kilowatts to 60 megawatts. The plasma heaters at the low and high ends of the power range are employed in the universities and in the national research laboratories for basic scientific studies. The industrial sector, with its emphasis on longevity and economy, has adapted only the plasma heaters in the range from 20 k~ to 8 mW.
