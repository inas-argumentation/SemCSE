Effect of Porosity of Powder Liner on Penetration of Shaped Charge Jet
The shocking temperature rise of the shaped charge with powder liner before collapse was calculated based on the Herrman equation of state.Shaped charges with the charge diameter of 28 mm were used to fire at the 603 armor steel target,the penetration-standoff curves(P-S) of the shaped charge liner with the porosity of 12.2% and 10.3% were measured and compared.The results show that proper porosity is helpful to lengthen the jet break up time and penetration depth.
