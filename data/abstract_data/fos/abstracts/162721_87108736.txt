Determination of γ-amino butyric acid in the germinated brown rice by HPLC with dansyl chloride as derivation reagent
A precise and fast high performance chromatography(HPLC) with UV detection method for determination γ-amino butyric acid(GABA) in germinated brown rice was developed by precolumn derivatization of GABA with dansyl chloride(Dansyl-Cl). GABA was determined on intertsil ODS-C 18 colunm(4.6 mm×250 mm, 5 μm), GABA was determined with UV detector at 386 nm. Good linearity was observed within the ranges from 0.005 mg/mL to 0.1 mg/mL with equation of Y=7358070X+8842.82692(R2=0.9993), and the detection limit was 3.461 ng. The RSD of peak area was 1.51%, and the recoveries were 98.62%~101.29% for GABA. The method is easy to operate, and high degree of recovery and precision. GABA content in 20 types of germinated brown rice(22.68~88.36 mg/100 g, dry weight) were determinated by the method, and the result showed that GABA content in germinated brown rice varies with different varieties, among that Zhong jia zao 17 and Zhe fu 802 are significantly higher than other varieties, so it is tried to be the raw materials of germinated brown rice by-products in the practical production.
