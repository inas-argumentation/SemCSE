Effect of alternating current electric field treatment on the storage quality of tomato fruits
The effect of the high voltage electric fields on the storage quality of "Shanghe219" tomatoes (Lycopersicom esculentum) was investigated. With the weight loss and decay index of tomatoes fruits as evaluation indexes, the storage quality of tomato fruits which were pretreated by ACEF(-150 ~150 kV/m for 15 min and 30 min)and static electric field (SEF) (-150 kV/m for 15 min and 30 min) were compared. The results showed that the ACEF treatment was more effective than the SEF in preserving tomato qualities. Meanwhile, tomato fruits’ color change, titrable acidity, content of proline and lycopene were measured during the storage. The results showed that the ACEF pre-treatment (15 min) significantly delayed the color change from green to red of tomato fruit, slowed down the increase of titrable acidity and lycopene, moreover, elevated the accumulation of proline.
