New derivatives of imidazo [5, 1-b] benzothiazole
Abstract1-Cyano-3-phenylimidazo[5, 1-b]benzothiazole (II) has been obtained from 1-formyl -3-phenylimidazo[5, 1-b]benzothiazole (I) and from 1-brono-3-phenylimidazo[5, 1-b]benzothiazole (III) and has been converted into the corresponding amide (IV) and thioamide (V). New 1-alkyl-3-phenylimidazo[5, 1-b]benzothiazoles (VI) have been synthesized.
