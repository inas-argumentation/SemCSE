State Evaluation of Oil Circuit Breake Based on Gray Relation and Chromatography Analysis
Based on gray correlation theory and chromatography, the gray correlation and chromatography methed is presented to assess the oil situation in the high voltage circuit breaker. The method of evaluation of the use of state gives the basic steps high voltage circuit breaker can be as filling as a supplement to the operation of evaluation methods. Practical examples have proved the effectiveness and feasibility of the method.
