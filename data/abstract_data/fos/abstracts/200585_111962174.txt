Study on Spiral-Ribbed Tube Cold Reheater in 400t/h Coal-fired Boiler
The testing and study on spiral-ribbed tube cold-reheater in400t/h coal-fired boiler in Hunan Jin zhushan Power Plant was present in this paper.The spiral-ribbed tube cold reheater was made to replace naked pipe,it re-duced the abrasion of the tube cold-reheater,the space of examining and repair is increased for cold reheater.
