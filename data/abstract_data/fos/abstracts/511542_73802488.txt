SU‐AA‐350‐01: Student Meeting
The annual AAPM student meeting welcomes current and prospective trainees in medical physics. Speakers will address issues of importance to trainees, including the impact of the 2012/2014 residency requirement for the ABR, and what the AAPM is doing to increase the number of residency positions. Breakfast will be provided. Come and meet your fellow students, and have a great start to the AAPM meeting!
