Kinetics of Adsorbing Cu~(2+) and Zn~(2+) by Chitosan and Its Copolymer
Kinetic characteristics of adsorbing Cu2+ and Zn2+ by chitosan(CTS) and chitosan/sodium alginate copolymer(CTS-AGS) were studied by electric conductivity.Results showed that the adsorption was chemical process with single molecular layer as main characteristics.Through calculation of adsorption rate constant under different conditions and analysis of SEM different before adsorption and after adsorption,the adsorption mechanism was discussed.
