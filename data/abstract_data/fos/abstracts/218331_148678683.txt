The Effect of Law Divorce for Parents’ Responsibility to Children
The divorce happens between a husband and a wife does not decrease the responsibility to responsible for the children’s life. The goal of this research is to know how far the parents’ rsponsibility to children after divorcing and finishing of parents’ responsibility problems to the children after the divorce. This research used normative research method. The discussion explaines that a divorce will not be a handicap for the couple who had divorced to fullfil the neccessity of the children that must be filled. The parents arerequaired to maintain, educate, and protect the children having in the marriage. The cost of children maintanance is borne by the father, the mother holds the under age children’s custody. If the parents’ responsibility to the children after the divorce becomes a problem it can be finished as a family. If it can not be thus it can be finished through the court. Keywords: Parents’ responsibility, Deforce, Parents and children.
