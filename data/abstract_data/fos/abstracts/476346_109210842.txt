Changes in electrical characteristics of AlTi contacts on silicon
The electrical characteristics of AlTi metallization on p, n, p+ and n+ silicon were studied. The contact specific resistance or the electron and hole energy barriers were determined. The conductance-reverse voltage measurement technique was used to reveal the contact non-uniformities (microplasma-type effects). SEM micrographs provided a picture of the contacts surface geometry. All these results showed that when the Ti barrier disappears due to the AlTi interaction (during the post-metallization heat treatment), the entire contact behaviour resembles that of an Al/Si contact. This drastic change of the contacts properties, as well as the surface degradation, are therefore confirmed by a consistent investigation of the electrical characteristics. The paper also presents the results of some investigations on Ti and Ag-Ti metallization systems.
