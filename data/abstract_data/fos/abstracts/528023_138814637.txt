Research on the Criterion of CET Position of Aluminum Alloy in Continuous Casting
The columnar to equiaxed transition (CET) is a kind of microstructural transformation in certain solidification, which should be controlled. Combining the methods of the orthogonal experimental analysis,numerical simulation and curve fit, the influence of the technical parameter of continuous casting on the CET position of billet has been analyzed and a criterion is put forward as follows:G~(0.8072)/R=0.469 when the fraction of solid is equal to 0.3, where G and R represent temperature gradient and cooling rate respectively.
