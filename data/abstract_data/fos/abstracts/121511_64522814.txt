Research of Multi-objective Optimization Model for Distributed Economic Dispatch of Power Grids
Traditional economic dispatch model has single target,large amount of calculation,optimization ineffective and many other defects.On the point of the subarea division and coordinated calculation view,a distributed economic dispatch model of power grids based on multi-objective optimization is established in this paper.It has three objective functions and takes into account the maximum benefits that brought from the difference of sale and purchase price,both the minimums of generation costs and pollution emissions at the same time.A new multi-zone distributed computation method based on approximate Newton direction is proposed latter.The improved distance differential evolutionary algorithm is carried out in each sub-region independently.Then the global revised calculation is conducted by asynchronous iteration.Finally,the simulation result shows that it can achieve good application effect when the method is used for distributed economic dispatch optimization.
