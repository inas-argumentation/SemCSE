Analysis of fatty acid compositions of ningxia Solanum pseudocapsicum L. seed oil
Objective:To analyze the fatty acids composition of Ningxia Solanum pseudocapsicum L. seed oil. Methods:The seed oil was extracted by soxhlet method. The fatty acids compositions were analyzed by gas chromatography-mass spectrometry(GC-MS) and their relative contents were calculated by the peak area ratio. Results:The extraction ratio is 33.06%,the iodine value is 90.4 gl/100 g,20 compounds were identified,among which 6 fatty acids existed in Ningxia Solanum pseudocapsicum L. seed oil,the main fatty acids are linoleic acid(30.64%),oleic acid(19.49%). The total content of the unsaturated fatty acids is 50.13%. Conclusion:Ningxia Solanum pseudocapsicum L. Seed is a natural resources obtaining linoleic acid.
