A survey on factors of graduate students' scientific research competence structure and enlightenment
In this survey, 14 normal factors of scientific research competence structure were selected, and 126 supervisors of graduate students from 15 provinces were surveyed through questionnaires. On the data processed by computer in level-analysis way, the conclusion can be made as the follows: The basic factors of scientific research competence structure required by all the graduate students of arts, science and engineering have some common traits, with some concrete differences existed. The basic factors of scientific research competence structure required by all the graduate students of arts, science and engineering listed in the top five are the abilities of creativity, logical reasoning, data collecting and process, problem solving, and verbal expression.
