Fouling and slagging dynamic processes in boiler with electron probe microanalyzer
Two pieces of typical slags specimens were sampled from 0.25 MW test furnace wall to represent fouling and slagging, respectively.Each mineral element dynamic distribution was analyzed along the slag sample’s thickness direction by using electron probe microanalyzer.Enrichment of iron,sodium and sulphur was the substantial reason leading to forming slag’s initial layer, while enrichment of sodium,silicon,aluminum and sulphur was the most outstanding trait of the fouling sample’s initial layer.Iron and calcium played much more important role in the course of slagging than fouling.Silicon and aluminum showed identical distribution regularities in two individual samples.
