Research of Core Making Technology in Sodium Silicate Cold Box Process
It is a latest cold box core making technology that with the help of special gas generator it makes menthyl formate and air blow into core box to achieve the sand core hardening bonded by sodium silicate.This technology possesses many advantages,such as high efficiency,energy saving,low-cost,friend environmental,etc.Based on the laboratorial research,a batch of sand cores was made and castings were poured successfully in factory,that proved the practicality、reliability and applicability of this technology.This technology is very meaningful for improving present formidable working conditions of cold box core making technology.
