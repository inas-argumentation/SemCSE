Microstructure Analysis of Piercing Plug Made of 20CrNi3MoW Steel
The microstructure and hardness of piercing plug made of 20CrNi3MoW steel were studied. The results show that the original microstructure is granular bainite and some acicular martensite, retained austenite; The microstructure and property of plug is improved after normalization. The surface oxide film of plug should be symmetrical and compact. We should provide seemly oxide process to get more FeO layer. Finally, combined to the producing practice, improvement measures were put forward.
