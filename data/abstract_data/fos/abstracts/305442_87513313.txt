Improved Method of Microtubule Cytoskeleton Immunofluorescence for Banana Leaf and Its Appliance
A staining method was optimized to visualize the microtubule cytoskeleton of banana leaf.It was revealed that 60 min fixation with fixative containing 3.7% paraformadehyde,1% trixon x-100,1% glycerol and 1% DMSO was suitable for maintaining the microtubule organization;and 30 min digestion could facilitate antibody penetrating cell wall;further,the response of microtubule was observed when challenged by fusaric acid;and microtubule underwent fragmenting,indicating that cytoskeleton was involved in the interaction between banana cell and Fusarium oxysporum f.sp.cubense.
