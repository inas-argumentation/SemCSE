A Simple Alcohothermal Synthetic Route to High Surface Area Zirconia Aerogel
A novel alcohothermal process has been successfully developed to prepare the thermally stable tetragonal zirconia aerogel using a cheap inorganic salt as the starting material. The results showed that the as-prepared zirconia aerogel has a high surface area and a well-developed textural mesoporosity, which is highly attractive for potential applications in heterogeneous catalysis.
