Study on the latest organize structure of city's sport department of China
There are some different "each does things in his own way" and function overlapping in the recently organize structure of city's sports department of China.The functions of information and control are not strengthened and combined according to development of the position. Therefore we suggest that the functions of the Chinese city's sports department can be combined into five parts: sports train,sports competition, sports industry, sports information and health, sports security and supervise,sports administration.
