Image Fusion Algorithm Combined with Orientation Information Measure and NSCT
It easily happened that the edge information lost and the effect of anti-noise was weak if the traditional image fusion algorithms were applied.In order to preserve the edge information in the source image and improve the noise immunity,a new image fusion algorithm based on orientation information measure and NSCT was presented.The algorithm firstly recovered source image by using Orientation information measure,and then performed it as low frequency and high frequency parts by using NSCT decomposition,finally,fused the low frequency part by using Orientation information measure weighted rule,and the high frequency part by using this paper′s improved region energy weighted rule.The algorithm maked full use of the good information retention ability with NSCT and the good anti-noise performance with orientation information measure,such that the fused image had good results.Experiments show that,this algorithm can preserve the edge information with strong noise resistance,can be widely applied in multi-focus image fusion.
