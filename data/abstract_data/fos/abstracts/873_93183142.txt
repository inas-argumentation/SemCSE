Removal of phosphate and ammonia nitrogen from hogpen wastewater by steel slag and zeolite.
Removal of phosphate and ammonia nitrogen from hogpen wastewater was investigated.Phosphate was removed by steel slag,and ammonia nitrogen was removed by zeolite modified by NaCl.Removal efficiency of phosphate and ammonia nitrogen by mixed materials of steel slag and zeolite was studied.Results showed that steel slag and zeolite can remove effectively phosphate and ammonia nitrogen from hogpen wastewater.When ratio of steel slag to zeolite was 4:1 in mixed materials,removal efficiency of phosphate and ammonia nitrogen were 75.87% and 89.71% respectively.After secondary treatment,the concentration of phosphate and ammonia nitrogen in hogpen wastewater can satisfy the discharge standard.
