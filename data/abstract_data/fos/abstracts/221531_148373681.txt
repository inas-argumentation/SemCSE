The path selection of governance structure change of China's university
Carding and analysis the path selection of our country university governance structure change since the Qing Dynasty,we found that path selection is lack of support of governance philosophy,and it was mainly compulsory institutional change and has no consistent goal.These have lead to some problems in our country university governance structure:governance body is single and governance structure is lack of efficiency,and so on.Building a university governance structure which conforms to Chinese realities,the needs of social development and university logic,requires to reconstruction modern university governance philosophy,to overcome the path dependence in the process of university governance structure,and pay attention to the timely transformation between compulsory institutional change and induced institutional change.
