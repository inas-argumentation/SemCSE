A study on HLA-A、-B genes associated with Guillain-Barre syndrom
Objective To explore the relations between susceptibility to AMAN and frequencies of HLA A? B alleles, and to identify the characteristics of the patients with AMAN in immunogenetics. Methods A case control research was done among 33 AMAN and 132 health individuals. DNA was extracted from peripheral blood leucocytes by improved fast salting out. HLA A? B alleles were typied by DNA based technology, PCR sequence specific primers(PCR SSP) method. Results HLA B15? B35 frenquencies showed a significant increase in patients with AMAN with relative risk(RR) of 4.09 and 7.08 respectively and corrected probability(Pc)as 0.015 and 0.0008 respectively. Conclusion HLA B15? B35 may be related to susceptibility to AMAN.
