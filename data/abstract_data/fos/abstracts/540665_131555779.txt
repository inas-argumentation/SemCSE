Effect of Fertilizer Application on Growth and Production of Cashew without Management
At the cashew plantation of Clone FL30 where no management had been done for 5 years in Dongfang City, Hainan Province, a NPK fertilization experiment with L9(34) orthogonal design was conducted to study the effect of fertilizer application on cashew growth and production from 2005 to 2007. The results showed that application of nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium fertilizers improved cashew growth and production significantly. During the initial flower stage, and the full flower and fruitsetting stage, N was an important nutrient element to increase cashew production and had significant positive correlation with N content of cashew leaves; P and K fertilizers did not increased production obviously when N was applied at a low level.
