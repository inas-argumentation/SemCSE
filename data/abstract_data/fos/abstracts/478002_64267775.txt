Study of the Power Supply Noise Effection on Radio Fuze Based on Established Module of Mechanism and Systemic Identification Method
Discussed the electrical source processing circuit (integrated manostat) of the radio fuze by adopting established module of mechanism and systemic identification method, and focus on exploring the electrical source system's ability to response to and restrain the noise. Finally, the paper draws its conclusion as: power supply noise can heighten the fuze's height of bursting, even causes early bursting as the value of the noise is high enough.
