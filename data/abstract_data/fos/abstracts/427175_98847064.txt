Effect on long-term potentiation by HIV-1 V_3 loop in rat hippocampus in vitro
Objective To explore the effect of gp120 V 3 loop on synaptic transmission and plasticity in the CA 1 region of rat hippocampal slices. Methods We recorded the field excitatory postsynaptic potentials(f EPSP) and investigated effect of long term potentiation by gp120 V 3 loop in rat hippocampus in vitro . Results The results showed that HIV 1 gp120 V 3 loop inhibited the LTP induced and maintance of CA 1, V 3 loop had no effect on basal synaptic transmission. V 3 McAb of V 3 specific antibodies can reverse the inhibition effect, but IgG can′t reverse it. Conclusion This inhibition of LTP by gp120 V 3 loop may contribute to the HAD (HIV 1 associated dementia) pathogenesis.
