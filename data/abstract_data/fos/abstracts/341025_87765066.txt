The effect of decoloring agent on the extraction of inulin
The effect of decoloring agent, temperature and time on the extraction and refinement of inulin were studied. Results showed that decoloring agent and temperature were correlation strictly to the extraction ratio of total sugar and reducing sugar, while the dosage of decoloring agent and processing time did not. Among the decoloring agents (AlCl3, infusorial earth and polyethylene), the filtrate treated with 1% polyethylene at 90 ℃ for 10 min is of clarification and has high-transparency.
