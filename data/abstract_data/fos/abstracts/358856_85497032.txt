Proof of Clozel's finiteness conjecture of special exponents: A Reduction Step
In this paper, via Casselman--Tadic's Jacquet module machine, we establish a ``product formula'' of the cardinality of the Jordan--Holder series of generalized principal series. As a byproduct, we prove Clozel's finiteness conjecture of the exponents of discrete series, i.e. the so-called special exponents under the assumption that it holds for the relative rank-one case.
