A Ultra-wideband Antenna with Opening Annular Coplanar Waveguide
UWB antenna used for short- range and high- speed wireless commnication requires not only smaller size,but also smaller return loss and good omnidirectional. Structure of feed waveguide with annular opening coplanar can enhance current path and realize the miniaturization of antenna. Gradient structure with fork main radiating element owns the capability for creating homogeneous radiation of full band and high frequency in all directions particularly. The result shows that antenna in 3. 1 GHz ~ 10. 9 GHz owns return loss less than- 10db and VSWR less than 2. Figture of H flat is still nealy a circle at 9. 5GHz. The structure of proposed antenna is a miniaturizated one and it performs excellently in low profile ultra- wideband as well.
