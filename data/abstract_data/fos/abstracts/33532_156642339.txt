On Establishing New System of Judicial Expertise
As the legal evidence which determines facts, judicial expertise plays an important role, even a decisive role in the impartiality of lawsuit, arbitration and enforcement of administration law. However, our current system of judicial expertise has many disadvantages that lower its public credibility and fail to attain the objective of judicial impartiality. This paper dis-cusses that we should establish new system of judicial expertise fitting in with the needs of the development of our judicial re-form.
