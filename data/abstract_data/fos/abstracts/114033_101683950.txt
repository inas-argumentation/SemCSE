Study on the rheology and the influence factor of low-concentration carrageenan
This paper studied the rheological properties of low-concentration carrageenan with different concentration, shearing rate, temperature, pH or stirring time that would affect the viscosity of gum solution. The results showed the viscosity of low-concentration carrageenan was increased with concentration. It became pseudo-plasticity fluid and its viscosity was fluctuated with temperature. At low concentration, the viscosity of carrageenan solution was distinct increased when its pH value was under 5.0, the viscosity of low-concentration gum solution was constant when its pH value in range of 5.0~10.0 and its viscosity was gradually reduced when its pH value up 10.0. The viscosity was influenced with different stirring action.
