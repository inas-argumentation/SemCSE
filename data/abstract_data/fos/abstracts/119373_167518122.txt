On the Conflict of Interest in Water Control during Kangxi and Qianlong Period
The Qingkou area became the key point during the course of harnessing Huanghe River,Huaihe River and the Grand Canal since 1194. Three factors such as grain carriage,the upstream regions and the downstream regions have been taken into consideration by the rulers and the ministers of water resource. As for the rulers,the grain carriage was of course the most important. The present study reveals the conflict of interest between the Central Government and the local governments in the water control activities.
