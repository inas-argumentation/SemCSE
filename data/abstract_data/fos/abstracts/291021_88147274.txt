Construction of Staphylocoecus epidermidis icaC deletion mutant and the phenotype changes of the mutant
Objective To construct Staphylococcus epidermidis (Se) icaC deletion mutant (△icaC strain) for acquiring the mutant and wild-type strain with the same genetic background except icaC gene, and thus to find the function of icaC gene in biofilm formation process of bacteria. Methods Plasmid pBT2-△icaC was constructed for homologous recombination in Se by inserting erythromycin resistance gene (ermB) and two icaC-flanking fragments into vector pBT2. Se1457 with recombination plasmid was continuously subcultured at 40℃un-till the△icaC strain was selected. The growth curve, biofilm formation and polysaccharide intercellular adhesion synthesizing were compared between Se1457 wild and A△icaC strain. Results The icaC gene was deleted by homologous recombination in Se1457 strain. Compared with wild strain, the growth of the△icaC strain showed no change, while the biofilm formation and the extracellular polysaccharide intercellular adhesin declined dramatically. Conclusion Se1457△icaC strain is successfully constructed. Though the deletion of the icaC gene has little effect on Se growth, the biofilm formation and the extracellular polysaccharide intercellular adhesion both dramatically decline in the△icaC strain.
