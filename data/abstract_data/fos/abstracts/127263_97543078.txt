Radiometrische plutoniumbestimmung neben grossem uranüberschuss in den organischen phasen des purex-prozesses
For the control of the plutonium content in the organic phase of the Purex Process, the plutonium must be determined in the presence of uranium in a plutonium --uranium ratio of 1: 10⁶. A radiochemical method was developed for plutonium determination in TBP containing 0.2M HNO₃ and 0.35M UO₂ (NO₃)₂. For plutonium separation, the reextraction by means of an iron(II) amidosulfonate solution, oxidation by a sodium nitrite solution, and further extraction in a 0.5M TTA solution in xylene were carried out. For a 10⁻⁶M plutonium solution, a reproducibility of plus or minus 3% was obtained by this method. (M.C.G.)
