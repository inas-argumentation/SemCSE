Quasi-Physical and Quasi-Human Algorithm for Protein Structure Prediction in an Off-Lattice Model
A three-dimensional off-lattice protein model is studied.The model has two species of different amino acids, hydrophobic and hydrophilic.Enlightened by the law of reciprocity among things in the physics world and human society, a related quasi-physical and quasi-human algorithm is formulated.Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method is quite effective in solving the off-lattice protein structure prediction problem. Compared with the results of all instances in literatures, the proposed algorithm can get better results with respect to not only the lowest energy but also runtime. For three of these instances,the algorithm finds new lowest energy configurations different from those in references.
