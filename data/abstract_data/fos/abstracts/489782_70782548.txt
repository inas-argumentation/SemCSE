Review of the selection of physical education curriculum contents by new China in 60 years
The author reviewed documents regarding the selection of physical education contents by new China,and put forward the following opinions after having retrospected based on his review: the relation between optional contents and required contents was not well handled at all historical stages of new China;unilaterally emphasizing optional contents or required contents is inadvisable;only by appropriately combining both contents can the comprehensive and primary development of students be boosted.
