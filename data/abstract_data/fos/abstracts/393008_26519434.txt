Some subclasses of meromorphically multivalent functions associated with a linear operator
Let Σ p denote the class of functions normalized by f ( z ) = z - p + ∑ n = 1 ∞ a n z n - p ( p ∈ N : = { 1 , 2 , 3 , … } ) , which are analytic and p-valent in 0 | z | 1 . Making use of a linear operator, which is defined here by means of the Hadamard product (or convolution), we introduce some new subclasses of the meromorphically p-valent function class Σ p and investigate their inclusion relationships and convolution properties. Some integral-preserving properties are also considered.
