An analysis of the condition of development of non-Olympic events in university student sports competitions in China in recent 20 years
The authors carried out a statistical analysis on the condition of development of various event branches of university student sports associations in China, the condition of event setup by all previous national university student games, the condition of development of non-Olympic events in national university student sports competitions after 2008, and revealed the following findings: in major sports associations and sports games in China, Olympic events still had an advantageous status; the proportion of non-Olympic events increased year by year, and its increasing speed had exceeded Olympic events'; non-Olympic events in single event competitions developed more rapidly in recent years, having been basically provided with the performance to compete with Olympic events equally. Non-Olympic events are numerous, features respectively packed, inheritable and time specific; their competition forms are diversified, prone to be accepted by the university student group. Universities had advantageous conditions for boosting the development of non-Olympic events, will become an important innovative development path for the development of non-Olympic events in China in the future.
