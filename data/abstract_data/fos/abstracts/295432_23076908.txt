Periodic Peak Modulations in Bi-2212 Stacks Coupled to a Submicrometer Hole
Coupled stacked Josephson junctions (SJJs) in high-T<sub>c</sub> superconducting (HTS) Bi<sub>2</sub>Sr<sub>2</sub>CaCu<sub>2</sub>O<sub>8+d</sub> (Bi-2212) are fabricated with a refined coupling configuration of a hole. Two types of coupled SJJs with a completely perforated hole of 0.1 mum x 0.2 mum and 0.2 mum x 0.4 mum were fabricated by using a 3-D focused ion beam (FIB) etching method. Those SJJs exhibited clearly oscillated critical current I<sub>c</sub> under external magnetic fields H<sub>e</sub>. With applying H<sub>e</sub>, the clear periodic modulation patterns of I<sub>c</sub> are obtained and well fitted to the calculated value from the hole area of 0.02 mum<sup>2</sup> , DeltaB(= Phi<sub>0</sub>/S) , where S is hole area. The width and uniform transport properties of SJJs are confirmed by flux flow resistance R<sub>ff</sub>-H<sub>e</sub> measurement with a bias current I<sub>b</sub> of 1 muA at 10 K. We also investigated external field angle thetas<sub>i</sub> dependence of junction resistance R<sub>j</sub> at 1 T and 10 K. When a constant H<sub>e</sub> of 1 T and a I<sub>b</sub> of 1 muA are applied to the sample with changing thetas<sub>i</sub> , unspecified equidistant and periodic peak structures in SJJs were induced. Those peaks dependence on thetas<sub>i</sub> under H<sub>e</sub> were experimentally observed by measurement of the R<sub>j</sub>. We assign the origin of the peaks to the interrelations between the periodic mechanical vibrations of sample stage and the quantized flux behaviors around a submicrometer hole. Those peak structures of I<sub>c</sub> and R<sub>j</sub> could be excited by H<sub>e</sub>,thetas<sub>i</sub>,I<sub>b</sub>, and some external signals from the peculiar properties of SJJs coupled to a hole.
