High-voltage Inverter in Datang Luoyang Thermal Power Plant Boiler Fan
This paper introduces the Datang Luoyang thermal power plant No.5 boiler induced draft fan and primary air fan of technical parameters,operation characteristics and energy saving potential,introduces high-voltage variable frequency speed regulation system and its application in the 5th unit boiler fan,comparing on the energy saving efficiency,declares the prospect of high-voltage inverter in application.
