Intercalation compounds of zirconium phosphates with substituted pyrazoles and imidazoles and their ac conductivity
Intercalation compounds of α-zirconium phosphate with methyl and ethyl derivatives of pyrazole and imidazole have been prepared and characterized by XRD, TG and impedance measurements. Composition data and interlayer distances ( 10.3⩽d( A )⩽11.6 ) suggest that the guests are accomodated in the interlayer region as a monolayer of molecules lying flat or slightly tilted with respect to the surface of the α-layer. The temperature of thermal de-intercalation is always much higher than the boiling point of the pure guest. The ac conductivity has been measured in a dry nitrogen atmosphere from 100°C up to a temperature within the phase stability range. The replacement of pyrazole and imidazole with the corresponding mono- or di-substituted derivatives decreases the conductivity by at least one order of magnitude and increases the activation energy; furthermore, the conductivity of the di-substituted imidazole intercalates is lower than that of the mono-substituted ones. Comparison of conductivity data and pKa values of the protonated guest shows that, the steric hindrance being the same, the conductivity increases with decreasing the basic strength of the guest. The conductivity of intercalates of γ-ZrP with imidazole and pyrazole has also been investigated.
