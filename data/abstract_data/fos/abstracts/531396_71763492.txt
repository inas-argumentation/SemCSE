Functional up-regulation of toll-like receptor 4 and 1 in ischaemic skeletal muscle
assessed by myogenin Western blot. Results: Simulated ischaemia significantly reduced the proliferation rate of myoblasts (Pb.001). Pretreatment with IGF-1 did not affect the proliferation rate of myoblasts in simulated ischaemia (in both MTT assay and cell counting techniques) and did not significantly increase the expression of myogenin in ischaemic myoblasts. Conclusions: IGF-1 had no impact on the proliferation or differentiation of myoblasts when exposed to ischaemic conditions in vitro. Ischaemia seems to have an detrimental effect on the function of myoblasts, and thus, the potential to stimulate their function in vivo appears limited.
