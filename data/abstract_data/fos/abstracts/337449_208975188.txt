Maximum Power Control Method of Concentrated Solar Flexible Collector
Concentrated solar power (CSP) system plays very important role in the thermal energy production because of its reproducibility. Therefore, CSP technology is widely used in steam turbine units and cogeneration units. These applications must have thermal storage to permit them to run continuously in night. For this demand, the CSP which is a kind of new control system is studied. Firstly, a design scheme of CSP such as flexible collector, absorber is presented. The CSP system can control the absorption temperature, obtain the maximum CSP temperature. So as to improve the overall production efficiency of the device. After that, the efficiency of CSP is discussed. The application effect of dynamic sensor is further emphasized.
