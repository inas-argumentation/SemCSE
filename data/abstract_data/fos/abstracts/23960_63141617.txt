Research on Teaching Method of Computer Fundamentals in Vocational College
According to the present situation of the higher occupation technique education,discusses how to adjust the contents of Computer Fundamentals,and how to innovate in teaching methods of the contents of Computer Fundamentals,using arts of teaching before and after classes,and combines various teaching methods,which founds on the actual state of vocational higher education.It shows the effect of changing teaching methods by using the list of the score of the CCT about each year.
