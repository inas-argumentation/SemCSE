An Analysis of the Development of Farmers' Cooperative Economic Organizations in Anhui Province
Based on the analysis of the reality of Anhui Province and the origin and characteristics of farmers’ cooperative economic organizations,the paper discusses some issues concerning the improvement of growth environment,the present situation and progress of organization development and the problems in it,thereby putting forward some corresponding solutions and suggestions. It is pointed out that the development of farmers’ cooperative economic organization is the benefit interactive link between various social economic subjects,however facing all sorts of problems and difficulties. The findings of the present study help to get a clearer idea in the further analysis of farmers’ cooperative economic organizations.
