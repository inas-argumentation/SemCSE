Multi-objective Risk Decision-making Analysis on Corporate Merger and Acquisition
MA is an important strategy for corporate to grow rapidly. More and more corporate and researchers pay attentions to MA. There are many researches indicating that more than three tenth MA cases are unsuccessful. There are many kinds of reasons for unsuccessful MA. This paper applies a risk decision-making model to MA. A multi-objective risk decision-making model is established to resolve the problem of MA decision-making.
