Numerical Simulation and Experimental Verification on Temperature Field of CO_2 Welding Based on SYSWELD Software
Based on software SYSWELD,the temperature field in low alloy structural steel using CO2 arc welding was simulated.Combining the welding parameters,the heat source was modified with the tool furnished by the software,the distribution map of temperature field and the temperature variation curve of characteristic points were obtained.Comparing the simulation results with the experimental results,it is demonstrated that the established model can preferably simulate the temperature field,which provides a foundation for analyzing stress,strain and so on in welding process.
