Murder followed by suicide in those who murder police officers.
According to the annual FBI summary (FBI, undated), from 1974 to 1978, 558 law enforcement offirers were murdered in the USA. and 729 offenders were identified. Of these offenders, 3 496 ( 2 8 ) committed suicide afterwards, a figure comparable to the percent of murderers ~n general in the USA who commit suicide after murdering (Wolfgang, 1958). The suicide rate of murderers of policemen is roughly 3430 per 100.000 per year as compared to a general suicide rate in the USA of 12.5 per 100,000 per year. The 28 murderers who committed suicide after murdering policemen were compared with the total sample of murderers of policemen. Of the murder-suicides, 65% were white (vs 56% of the total sample of murderers). All the murder-suicides were male ( v s 96% of the total sample of murderers), and 66% were 1 8 to 30 yr. of age (vs 60% of the total sample of murderers). All of the murder-suicides used firearms for the murder, and 26 of the men killed themselves using a gun. (At least 4 used the policeman's gun.) One shot into dynamite, and one hung himself in his jail cell after arrest. The majority of the murdersuicide situations were not related to serious crimes. Seven involved traffic stops, accidents, or disabled vehicles. Five were family disturbances. In only six cases was the offender actually involved in an armed robbery or burglary. In addition, one was hijacking an airplane and one was a sniper. Interestingly, six of the offenders killed, wounded, or threatened a close relative as well as killed a policeman. In these cases, the policeman seemed to have been killed because he was there while the violence was being discharged. A few of the murderers appeared to have grievances against particular policemen or the police in general, since they sought out the home of a police officer or the police station in order to kill the policeman. Those involved in crimes may be defending themselves and trying to escape capture. Several appeared to kill themselves while being hunted or when cornered. As arrest was imminent, they commitred suicide. Some of these murder-suicides may be victim-precipitated homicides (i.e., provoking the police into killing them) and commit suicide when the police do not kill them. Most, however, appear on superficial examination to be quite similar to the total sample of police murderers and not unlike murderers in general in the USA.
