Analysis of immunogenicity of recombinant human papillomavirus type 6 virus-like particles
Objective To investigate the immunogenicity of recombinant human papillomavirus type 6(HPV-6) virus-like particles(VLPs). Methods HPV-6 L1 VLP(L1-VLP) and L1+L2 VLP(L1+2-VLP) were prepared from Sf9 cells that were infected with L1 recombinant baculovirus or co-infected with additional L2 recombinant baculovirus, and were characterized by SDS-PAGE, Western blot and electron microscopy. The L1-VLP and L1+2-VLP were used to immunize BALB/c mice, and the humoral and cellular immune responses were detected. Results L1-VLP and L1+2-VLP were both spherical particles with a diameter of approximately 50nm. L1+2 VLP comprised L1 and L2 in the molar ratio of 4∶1. The titers of serum antibodies in groups vaccinated with adjuvant L1-VLP and L1+2-VLP were above 1∶10?000 as detected by ELISA, which was higher than that in groups without adjuvant (1∶2?000), L1+2-VLP induced antibodies specific to L2 antigen. The serum antibodies mainly recognized confirmation-dependant HPV-6 epitopes, showing cross-reactivity to HPV-11 but not to HPV-16. Specific splenic lymphocytes proliferation after in vitro re-stimulating with HPV-6 L1 VLP was observed in 3H-TdR incorporation test for immunized mice (P0.01), but not significant difference between L1-VLP and L1+2-VLP immunized group (P0.05). The stimulation indexes(SI) were 6.4, 6.2 and 1.1 for L1-VLP, L1+2-VLP and control group. In vitro re-stimulation also specifically induced significant increase of IL-2 and IL-10 secretion in the splenic lymphocytes from VLP-vaccinated group (P0.01). However, no significant increase was found for IFN-γ. Conclusion The prepared recombinant HPV-6 VLPs were highly immunogenic inducing both cellular and humoral immunity in mice, and can be used to explore preventative vaccine against HPV. [
