Photoluminescence of monolayer to submonolayer thick Ge1-zCz on Si(001)
A detailed photoluminescence (PL) study elucidates the epitaxial limits for nominally pure and optically active Ge1-z Cz layers on Si(001) substrates. The C content is varied from z = 6% to z = 100% and the layer thicknesses are changed from 0.05 monolayers (ML) to 3 ML. With increasing C fraction the thickness for which the PL signal can be detected gradually decreases. Excitation dependent PL experiments reveal a type I band edge alignment for z 29% and a type II alignment for z 12%.
