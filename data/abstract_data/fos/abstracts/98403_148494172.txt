The Reform of the Graduate Entrance Examination and Enrollment System: the Progress,Debate and Reflection
The graduate entrance examination and enrollment system could be divided into such two forms as the primary system and the secondary system.After the reform and opening up,to maintain the rationality of the primary system,the government adjusted the secondary system continuously,adopting the autonomous enrollment,recommendation enrollment and so on.Nevertheless,the questions caused by the primary system and the reform of the secondary system still aroused widespread controversy.In the future reform of the graduate entrance examination and enrollment system,the government must persist the primary system based on examination,reform the secondary system under the premises of abandoning the principal contradiction theory,overcome anxiety of reform for reform's sake,and reform as far as possible in the open environment.
