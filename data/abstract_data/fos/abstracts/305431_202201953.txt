Preparation and Properties Investigation of Cu-P Amorphous Foil Ribbons
The amorphous foil ribbons and the as-cast solder of Cu68.5Ni15.7P6.5Sn9.3 which have the same compositions were prepared by means of rapid solidification and ordinary casting method respectively.The amorphous ribbons would not be broken if folded overlappedly. Two kinds of filler metals are brazed with copper by vacuum brazing under 660℃?670℃?680℃?690℃ and the holding time is 5min?10min?15min?20min .The results show that the melting point of Cu68.5Ni15.7P6.5Sn9.3 amorphous foil ribbons is lower than ordinary filler metal with DTA,and wettability of amorphous foil ribbons is better than ordinary filler metal under the same brazing parameters.
