Solar radiant heating of a rotating solid cylinder
In an earlier paper, Olmstead and Raynor (1) have considered the temperature distribution in a solid cylinder exposed to solar radiation. They obtained a solution using the method of Green's functions and numerical examples were presented utilizing asymptotic expansions of the modified Bessel functions appearing in the formal solution. In the present note, a Fourier series expansion of the radiation input function is used in conjunction with a simple product solution to obtain the results in a straightforward manner.
