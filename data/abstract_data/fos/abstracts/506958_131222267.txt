Natural convection in a vertical annulus filled with porous medium with inner heat source
In order to study the hydrodynarnic and heat transfer characteristics in porous medium with inner heat source, the natural consection in a vertical annulus filled with saturated porous medium is numerically analyzed when the top and bottom in adiabatic condition, and temperature difference existed in inner and outer wall. Effects of Rai/Ra, A and k on the fluid field, temperatur field, local and average Nusselt numbers are investigated. The results show that two reverse circulations appear near the inner and outer wall,high temperature region of (θ1 and low temperature region of θ1 occur in the temperature field at relatively high Rai/Ra andA, and the region ofθ 1 expands with increasing Rai/ Ra and A. The local Nusselt number on inner wall increases at first and then decreases along the axial direction, the zero point moves to lower as k decreases and A increases. In the other hand, the local Nusselt number on outer wall increases rapidly on the upper of annulus. The average Nusselt number on the inner wall decreases linearly as Rai/Ra increases.
