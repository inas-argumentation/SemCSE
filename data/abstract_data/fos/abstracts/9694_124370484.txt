An Application of the Frequency-Domain Criteria for Dissipativity of a Certain Third Order Non-Linear Differential Equation
The generalized Theorem of Yacubovich on the frequency-domain method is applied to a certain third order non-linear differential equation. Uniform dissipativity is proved for this equation.
