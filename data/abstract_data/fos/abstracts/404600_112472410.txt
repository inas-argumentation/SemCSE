Trial and Application of Primary Frequency Regulation Function for Hydro-turbine Generator Unit in GZB Hydropower Plant
As the rapid development of grid,the demand for safety and stability of grid is increasingly higher for power system.Due to the need of Central China Grid,Gezhouba Hydropower Plant takes the responsibility of primary frequency regulation.This paper described how to achieve the function of primary frequency regulation by hydroturbine governor in Gezhouba Hydropower Plant.After the test,it also intensively analyzed the relationship and influence between the regulative parameters of hydro-turbine governor and primary frequency regulation of the unit.And finally the function of primary frequency regulation was realized successfully in Gezhouba Hydropower Plant.
