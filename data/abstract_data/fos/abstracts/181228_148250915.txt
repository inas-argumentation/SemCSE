The Boundary of Educational Administrative Power——On Education Administrative Power Boundary
A number of Chinese education problems are caused by the power of education administration.Clearing the boundary of educational administrative power is the key to promote education administration functions,to enhance the effectiveness of educational administration,and to promote the education development.In this paper,the boundary of education administration power is summed up as the boundary of law,profession and value.Imperfect law,deficient educational administrative system,and unsound public participation decision-making mechanism,caused educational administrative power beyond the boundary of law,profession and value.So it needs to improve the educational law,reform the educational administration,and strengthen the juridification,democracy and scientization of educational administration.
