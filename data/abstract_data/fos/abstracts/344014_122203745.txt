Dielectric and polarization properties of KH2PO4 single crystals doped with Li, Na, and NH4 impurities
The dielectric and polarization properties of potassium dihydrogen phosphate (KDP) single crystals doped with Li, Na, and NH4 impurities have been investigated in the temperature range 70–150 K. It is found that the domain freezing temperature in the lithium-containing sample exceeds that for nominally pure KDP. Disappearance of freezing is observed in the ammonium-doped crystal.
