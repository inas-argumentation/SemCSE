A Study of Risk Prevention and Control System for Folk ROSCA
Rotating Savings and Credit Association(abbreviated as ROSCA),as a Chinese traditional form of folk financing,has such functions as promoting folk financing and supporting funds for the construction of countryside and medium and small size enterprises under the current economical construction.Because of the lack of the law relating to ROSCA,however,some ROSCA developments have been abnormal,which has brought about the failure of ROSCA on a large scale and influenced local financial security and social stability.China may construct the system for administrative regulation over folk ROSCA,the system for the guarantee of parties’ liabilities in folk ROSCA,and the system for the scale and the control of performance period in folk ROSCA,so as to lead and normalize folk ROSCA development and,thereby,to exert fully the active role of,besides the prevention of the risk on,folk ROSCA.
