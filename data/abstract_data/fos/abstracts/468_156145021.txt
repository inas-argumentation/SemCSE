Strengthening the Construction of University Students’Communities and Constructing Harmonious Campus Culture
In recent years,university students’communities have gradually grown in scale. In the management of the students’communities,we still have a lot of problems and shortcomings that are limiting the development of students’organizations. In order to better strengthen the management of students' communities, the paper explores ways to make our students' communities activities become more and more full of vitality, including strengthening ideological and political education, increasing input and guidance, establishing strict management system and so on. By doing these, we will construct harmonious campus culture.
