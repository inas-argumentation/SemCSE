Discussion on the Development Future of the Internet of Things in University
Introduces the background of our IOT proposed,the concept,the output and employment prospects about the IOT.Mainly introduces the current domestic on the IOT offered in university.Moreover,researches on the core technology of the IOT from three layers: the feeling layer,the network layer and the application layer.Also analyses the IOT Engineering Training Program.In view of the current development status of the IOT,proposes the difficulties in offering the IOT in university,including the teachers' building,the teaching materials' construction,the hardware equipment and the establishment of experimental environment.Finallly,summaries the development future of the IOT in university.
