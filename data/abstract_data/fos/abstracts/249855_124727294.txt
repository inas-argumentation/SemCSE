The Experimental Research on the Performance of Jet Pump and Pipe Friction on the Condition of Steady and Unsteady Flow
This paper studies the performance of jet pump and the functional loss of pipe related to the frequency on flow,and compares with the steady flow.The results show that the performance of jet pump on unsteady flow(f=1～2Hz)is better and more efficient than the performance on steady flow.There is more frictional loss of the pipe on unsteady condition,when the frequency is increased.
