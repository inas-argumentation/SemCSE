Mathematical Models and Dynamic Simulation for Cartridge Valves
This paper takes an investigation into the dynamic charac-teristics of hydraulic cartridge valves as an independent component.Based on their internal structure and practical application details,two groups of mathematic models for cartridge valves have been developed.With relevant circuits,all of the models were simulated for their ap-plicability.The result gives useful evidence for further theoretical and experimental investigation on the cartridge valve systems.
