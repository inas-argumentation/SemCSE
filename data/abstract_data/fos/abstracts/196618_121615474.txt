The calculation of optical data from electron energy loss measurements
A method is given for the calculation of — Im 1/ɛ from electron energy loss data. The observed energy loss spectrum, and its variation with the angle of scattering, is corrected for plural electron scattering (including elastic-inelastic events). The method requires no assumptions about the behaviour of the angular variation of the energy loss spectrum, but the angular variation of the zero loss (unscattered and elastically scattered) electrons is required.
