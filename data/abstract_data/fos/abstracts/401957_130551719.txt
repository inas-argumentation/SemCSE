Regionalization of Ecological Function and Its Effect on the Development of Eco-Anhui
Based on the analysis of some concepts of ecological function regionalization,we discussed its method, content and application in Anhui Province. Ecological function regionalization is a kind of geographical regionalization on the basis of ecosystem sensitivity, process and effect of ecosystem stress, ecosystem service importance, and integrality, continuity, homogeneity of a region. The ecological function regionalization of Anhui Province would be carried out under the guide of the determination of provincial eco-environmental problems and ecological fragile region and the related importance levels of different regions in the aspect of ecological security.
