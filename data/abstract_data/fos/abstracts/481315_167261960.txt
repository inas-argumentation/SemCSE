Legal Regulation on Plan of Development——Experience and Comparison of German Legislations on Space Plan
As a modern administrative means,plan would satisfy the reasonable allocation of resources by rational means and rescue the defection of the market.At present,China lacks effective legislations on plan and thus bring about many contradictions.This paper introduces different opinions on legislative modes in China on Plan Law,and on the basis of making comments on comparing legislations on space plan of Germany,it sets up the basic structure of Chinese Plan Law in the near future.
