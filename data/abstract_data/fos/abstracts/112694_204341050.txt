Harvard University's capital sources,financing mode and its inspiration
Harvard University's sources of capital have highly taken on the characteristics of marketizing,privatizing and commercially operating.The endowment's absolute predominance role in Harvard University,have embodied the development trends on the financing of the higher education institutions(HEIs) in U.S.A.Its excellent financing mode which contains the pluralistic financing approaches,the highly effective organization function system,the financing team composed of professional experts and volunteers,and the modernized financing artifice,has shed Chinese universities lights on both the external environment cultivation and the internal financing capability construction.
