Comparison of three kinds of farm produce culture media to the growth of drosophila.
To study the effects of the three different culture media on the growth of drosophila melanogaster(fruit flies),fruit flies were divided into different groups and the effects of bran,banana,Indian meal culture media in the diet on development and reproduction of fruit flies(Drosophila melanogaster)were investigated.The results showed that bran culture medium is fit for the growth of drosophila,which is of jarless physics and lower price,is a kind of suitable farm produce culture medium and Indian meal culture medium is optimal choice to rear the drosophila under the condition of the short of bran.
