Investigation of a Written Form of the Mini-Mult
This study examined the relationship between an oral and written form of the Mini-Mult. When 47 undergraduate college students were administered these two forms along with the standard MMPI, significant correlations were obtained between each of the forms and the full MMPI on all of the scales. When the data were analyzed from the standpoint of relative accuracy in predicting profile elevation there was agreement between the 2 forms in 43 of 47 instances. Correlations between the oral and written forms of the Mini-Mult and the full MMPI were not significantly different from each other with the exception of scale K.
