Housing Price Fluctuation and Monetary and Credit Policy Macro-Control
The paper compared the growth rate of housing prices with growth rate of GDP and resident income and discovered that housing price fluctuation is more volatile than that of GDP and resident income,and worked out the fluctuation range of housing price by HP Filter.It is claimed that the main monetary and credit policies used in the real estate macro-control since 2003are:adjusting the deposit reserve ratio,interest rate and capital ratio of project,restricting the loan qualification and differentiating housing credit policy.There are some factors restricting macro-control effect,such as policy defect,poor execution of local government and commercial bank and the peculiar real estate environment.This paper also proposes some policies and suggestions to help government control real estate market.
