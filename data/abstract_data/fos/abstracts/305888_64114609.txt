3D Face Expression Animation Utilizing a Bilinear Analysis Based Approach
Animating realistic 3D facial expressions is an important research field of computer applications.In this paper,a novel technique is presented for expression synthesis.Based on the statistical analysis for the segmented 3D faces,bilinear statistical models were built to separate the respective identity and expression attributes.Moreover,this model was developed to be muscle-driven.By changing the muscle parameters,the statistical expression parameters were changed correspondingly to generate different expressions.With the help of a 3D Morphable Model,arbitrary frontal 2D or 3D faces could be automatically fitted to our bilinear model.Experimental results show that this technique can simulate various realistic face expressions.
