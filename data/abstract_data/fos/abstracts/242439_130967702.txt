Interpretation of New Humanism in French Environment Law
In the social development, humanism was gradually replaced by new humanism as harmonious relationships of human and nature was established in the new humanism.From the aspect of evolvement and development of historical connotation of humanism, new humanism in French environment law was analyzed and interpreted.It was found that new humanism which represented with ecological humanism as basic theory of environment law in China is the necessary selection of historical development.
