A Discussion about Solutions to the Employment Problem of China
Employment is one of the bread-and –butter issues requiring urgent attention and careful handling. With the accelerating of the urbanization, a large number of surplus labors have appeared in rural areas of China. At the same time, in the course of the deepening reform of state-owned enterprises,   there are more and more laid-off workers, so the problem of unemployment sticks out. In this paper, the authors first analyze the industrial and the employment structures of China, and then propose a facile solution to the problem of employment.
