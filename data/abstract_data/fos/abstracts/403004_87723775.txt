Mitigating Effects of Leaf Spraying with Four Plant Growth Regulators on Rape Seedling Under Low Temperature Stress
Using rape "Zhouyou 589" as the material, the effects of plant growth regulators on the growth of rape seedling under 4 ℃ low-temperature stress was studied by hydroponic culture. The results showed as follow: plant growth regulators application on the leaves significantly increased the plant height and root length, improved seedling growth and increased fresh weight per plant, while the contents of photosynthetic pigment, soluble sugar and proline, the root activity and the autioxidant enzyme activities were significantly increased. The content of malonaldehyde(produce of membrane lipid peroxidation) was significantly decreased when the rape seedlings were suffered from low-temperature stress for 4 d. The results showed that SA, BR, ABA, 6-BA could effectively keep cytomembrane stabilization and reduce the damage from membrane lipid peroxidation, by which could mitigate the harmful effects from low-temperature stress on plants, and promote the growth of soyben, and the effect of ABA and 6-BA were relatively better.
