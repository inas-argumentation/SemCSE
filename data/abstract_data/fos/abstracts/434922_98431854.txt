Dielectric susceptibility of amorphous III - V compound semiconductors
We analyse, for the first time, the effect of bond angle disorder on the dielectric constant of a model amorphous III - V compound semiconductor. We adopt a linear combination of hybrids method developed by us. We introduce distortion in bond angles and construct an orthonormal basis set for the disorder network to obtain an expression for in terms of bond-angle distortion parameter . We analyse with increasing . Our results agree well with experiment.
