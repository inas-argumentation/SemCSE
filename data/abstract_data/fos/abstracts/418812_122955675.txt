Neuromuscular resetting and entrainment
In a recent work (Oguztoreli and Stein, 1979) some of the theoretical characteristics of the interacting resetting and entrainment oscillations in an experimentally based model of the mammalian neuromuscular system has been investigated. The present work is devoted to the numerical solution of the nonlinear integrodifferential difference equation which describes the complex nature of these oscillations.
