Study on WC-Co Cemented Carbides Synthesised by Thermal Mechanical Alloying
The raw materials W, active carbon, Co powders were in-situ synthesised by thermal mechanical alloying(TMA) to prepare WC-Co cemented carbide. The effects of TMA parameters on phase, microstructure and mechanical properties were studied . The results show that microstructure of WC-Co cemented carbide are fine-clubbed. Longer balling time and higher synthesis temperature accelerates the reaction activeness and the transformation from Co3W3C to WC, thereby improves the densification and the microstructural homogeneity. But too higher synthesis temperature will make WC grain grow up. Balling time mainly influences the reaction degree, whereas the synthesis temperature mainly influences densification. The density, hardness and bend strength improve with balling time prolonging and synthesis temperature increasing.
