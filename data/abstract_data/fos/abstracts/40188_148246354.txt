A Study of Social Support Network for Community Correction Subjects——From the Perspective of System Theory in the Field of Social Work
Based on the survey materials from two communities and one county of H City in Anhui Province,this article is aimed to analyze the current conditions of social support network for correction subjects from the perspective of System Theory,and indicates that we can help correction subjects regain and reconstitute social support network on three different levels:personal network,mutual aid network and community network.
