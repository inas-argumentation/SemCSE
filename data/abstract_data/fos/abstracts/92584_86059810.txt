An Action Threshold for Helicoverpa armigera Hb. Based on Pheromone Trap Catches in Cotton
Sex pheromones can be used for monitoring and management of Helicoverpa armigera Hb. populations. Studies with sleeve type pheromone traps indicated that 22 moths/trap/night correspond to 10% damage' to the reproductive parts in cotton by H. armigera. Based on the pheromone trap catches, action for controlling this pest should be initiated when the moth catches exceed 7 moths/trap/night.
