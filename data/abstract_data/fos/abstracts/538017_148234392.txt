Strengthening the competitiveness of universities: experience of Korea
For strengthening the competitiveness of universities in Korea, Korean government has been carrying out many measures and strategies in the reform of higher education since 1990s’, which include drafting developmental plan and projects; increasing financial support on both national universities and provincial universities; reforming structure of inner organization and establishing the new model of management; launching evaluation of universities and improving appointment of academic system; enhancing international cooperation and exchange and so on. The purpose of this article is to introduce the current situation of universities in Korea in an era of reform in order to help Chinese readers not only to deeply understand the development and reform of universities in Korea, but also provide some experiences for the reform of universities in China.
