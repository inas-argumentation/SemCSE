Ionization energy of the Si acceptor on AlxGa1-xAs
Using Hall effect and photoluminescence investigations the impurity incorporation was characterized on n-type MBE-grown AlxGa1-xAs:Si. Although the compensation ratio was small, silicon was also observed as both a donor and an acceptor in low doped material. Starting from the value in GaAs (EaSi(GaAs)=34.8 meV) the activation depth of the isolated silicon acceptor with respect to the valence band increases in the range 0<or=x<or=0.4 corresponding to the relation EaSi(x)=34.8+47.3x+4.65*102x3.5 (EaSi in meV). The activation depths of the five-site acceptors carbon, silicon and germanium are correlated as a function of the chemical composition and compared with the theoretical predictions of Baldereschi and co-workers (1980). The excellent material quality is evidenced by the small FWHM=1, 4 and 5 meV of the donor bound excitons for the x-values 0.09, 0.25 and 0.38 respectively. Especially for x=0.09 the radiative decay of donor and acceptor bound as well as free excitons were observed.
