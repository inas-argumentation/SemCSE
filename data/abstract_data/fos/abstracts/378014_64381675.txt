Research and Design of Wireless Communication Experiment System Based on ARM and GPRS
Studies the wireless communication experiment teaching system based on ARM and GPRS.The design of the system combines the needs of teaching and practice of wireless communication, and uses the advanced technologies of communication industry and electronic professional. There are three parts in the system.The software designing includes data acquisition terminalside software and control center server-side software.The system uses client/server mode to realize communication between data terminal and control center.As a wireless communication experim ent teaching system,it is convenient for technical persons to rapidly understand and master the GPRS wireless communication technology.
