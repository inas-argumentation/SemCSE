Research on Technology of Producing Granular Ferroalloys Using Continuous Casting with Crystallizer
Main means of producing granular ferroalloys are moulding and manually crushing in P.R.China, and are granulating with water and casting with spheric agglomeration device and molding with mould in oversea countries, but these methods can not be spreaded due to the technological or economical reason. A number of tests were made on multi-ferroalloys applying continuous casting and making granule equipment with crystallizer in this investigation, and it has achieved preliminary success. The key technology for producing manganese silicon ferroalloy with low strength and high brittleness adopt low resistance self-lubricated crystallizer and control temperature of casting blank below 750 ℃ on outlet of crystallizer, and using these oscillating parameters of amplitude in the range of 0.8~1.2 mm and frequency in the range of 80~100 min-1 on crystallizer
