Research on Opening Experimental Teaching Based on Learning by Doing
In order to meet the needs for innovative engineering talents in development of the industry,it is essential for full development of student's engineering quality by strengthening the experiment teaching.Learning by doing advocates learning by practicing,founding and accumulating knowledge by practicing.Proposes open experimental teaching model based on learning by doing,constructs open experimental teaching system,which aims at engineering capability development and focuses on multi-level projects,explores assurance jobs for implementing open experimental teaching,furthermore provides an important way to cultivate engineering quality of students.
