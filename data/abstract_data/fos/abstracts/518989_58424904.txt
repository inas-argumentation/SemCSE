A study of the reclassification of physical education teaching skills from the perspective of new curriculum reform ideas
Scientific and rational classification of physical education teaching skills helps physical education teachers understand teaching skills profoundly, thus enhance teaching quality. Current classification of physical education teaching skills has such problems as overlapping, epochal indistinctiveness, disciplinary indistinctiveness etc. According to new curriculum reform ideas and physical education class teaching activities, the author put forward a new classification: learning guidance skills, content arrangement skills, activity organization skills, load adjustment skills, help and protection skills.
