Simulation for Smoke and Obstacles Interaction Using the Overlapping Grids
It is time-consuming to simulate the smoke that interacts with large numbers of obstacles in the high resolution grid.To further improve the real-time of the simulation and retain the local visual details around obstacles,we combine the overlapping grids method with the node-grouping method.The node-grouping method is used to handle the boundary condition of obstacles,and each obstacle is surrounded by a local grid.In each time-step,we solve the N-S equations for each grid and transfer information between the local grid and the global grid.The experimental result shows that our method could obtain competitive local visual details around obstacles with the node-grouping method,and the simulation speed is almost three times faster than that using the node-grouping method only.
