Consequently, the effect of using the PIQ as a covariate assumes unique variance in the Block Design test instead of regarding it as one measure of abstract thinking. In other words, using covariance to &dquo;partial out&dquo; the level of functioning represented by the PIQ may well partial out the dependent variable.
