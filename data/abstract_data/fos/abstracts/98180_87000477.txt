Extraction of total dietary fiber in peanut meal by two enzymes method
Extraction technics of total dietary fiber in peanut meal,as a material,by two enzymes method was discussed.Enzyme concentration,hydrolysis time,temperature of papain and glucoamylase effect on extraction rate of total dietary fiber was explored by Single-factor experiment.The results showed that the best extraction conditions of papain was 50 ℃,concentration 8% for 4 h,of glucoamylase was 60 ℃,concentration 1.2% for 1 h.Extraction rate of soluble dietary fiber was 40.45%.
