Simultaneous Determination of Five Bile Acids in Pulvis Fellis Suis,Pulvis Billis Bovis,Pulvis Fellis Caprinus and Pulvis Fellis Galli by High Performance Liquid Chromatography-Charged Aerosol Detector
A high performance liquid chromatography-charged aerosol detection( HPLC-CAD) method was established for the simultaneous determination of five bile acids in pulvis fellis suis,pulvis billis bovis,pulvis fellis caprinus and pulvis fellis galli. Components were separated and analyzed on a Waters HSS T3 column( 100 mm × 2.1 mm,1.8 μm). Acetic acid-acetonitrile-water( 0.1∶ 30∶ 70,V/V) and acetonitrile were selected as the mobile phase A and B with a flow rate of 0.3 mL / min. CAD was equipped as the detector with the atomization temperature of 35. 0 ℃ and nitrogen gas pressure of 2. 413 × 105Pa. Under the selected chromatographic conditione,the limits of detection and quantification of each bile acid were 6 ng and 30 ng, respectively,while the linear range was 0.6-300 mg / L. The spiked recoveries( n = 6) were 85.0%-97.1%. The method was 3. 0 times more sensitive than HPLC-Evaporative Light Scattering Detector( HPLC-ELSD) and had a much wider linear range.
