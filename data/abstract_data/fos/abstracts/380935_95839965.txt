Obtaining a divided uranium oxide from the thermolysis of UO2 (NO3)2.6H2O : outstanding role of the residual pressure
It is shown (using Controlled transformation Rate Thermal Analysis) that the residual pressure during the two last dehydration steps of hexahydrated uranyl nitrate determines the surface area of the final UO 3 (2 to 45 m 2 g −1 as this pressure drops from 100 to 5×10 −2 mbar) due to the formation of two different nitrates.
