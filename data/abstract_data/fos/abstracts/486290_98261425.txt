Numerical calculation of gas breakdown in ultrafast gas switch
The closing process of a peaking switch was divided into two phases, gas breakdown and voltage collapse. A model for the discharge in the first phase was established and the development of discharge was numerically simulated. The results with pressurized nitrogen show that the switch delay increases with the increasing of gas pressure and the breakdown voltage increases with the decreasing of the rise time if breakdown of the gap takes place on the leading edge of the charging pulse. The results with hydrogen show good agreement with that from experiments, which confirms the correctness of the model and calculations. (3 pages)
