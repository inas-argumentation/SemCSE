Prediction Techniques for Traction Using Field and Laboratory Data
ABSTRACT T RACTION tests were performed with several soil types and conditions varying from acceptable to very adverse, using different load conditions and machine configurations. Comparisons were made for different tires in terms of obtainable pull at different slip rates. Soil properties were determined at different moisture contents and were used to predict maximum pull obtainable in the field. These were then compared to the field data. Sinkage modulii and the^ sink-age coefficient were determined, and these coefficients were used to evalu-ate the performance of the tire in soils varying from very weak to me-dium firm. Such behavioural studies helped to compare the results with other investigations and to evaluate traction theory.
