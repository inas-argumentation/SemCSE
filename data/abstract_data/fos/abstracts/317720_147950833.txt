The Survey on the University Students' Social Practice and Its Countermeasures
The empirical results showed that there were unitary form,monotonous content,unsatisfied implement and unsound implement system in the university students'social practice.In order to strengthen and improve the university students'social practice,it should guide the value orientation of social practice,with the object of serving the community;expand the content and form of social practice focused on the job training;perfect the implement system through the construction of mechanism;enhance the assessment of social practice with the standard of employment reserves.
