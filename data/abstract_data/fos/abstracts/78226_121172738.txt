A relativistic supermultiplet theory and its connection with the “hybrid groups“
The relativistic extension ofWigner's supermultiplet theory proposed byBudini andFronsdal is discussed. It is shown that the predictions one can get with this model are exactly the same as those emerging from the “hybrid group” of the previously discussed model3. The model will give new relations, however. These will connect processes involving the lowest supermultiplets (the only ones studied so far) with processes involving higher supermultiplets.
