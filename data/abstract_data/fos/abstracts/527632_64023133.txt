Fully Distributed Node Discovery Model with High Topology Consistency for DVE
Due to the lack of global management of central server,node discovery becomes a challenge for P2P-based massively distributed virtual environment(DVE).In order to solve such problem,an analytical framework for node discovery with high topology consistency is proposed.According to the framework,based on the respecting of global connectivity property,local awareness property can be maintained by fulfilling three topological requirements with different strengths,which are AOI neighbors′ encapsulation property,neighbors′ encapsulation property and weak areas periodically checked property.And a fully distributed node discovery model with high topology consistency named SimWorld is designed founded on the framework.The results of the experiments show that the topology consistency of SimWorld model approximates to 100%.
