Synthesis of all of the possible stereoisomers of 4-benzamido-3-hydroxy-2-(δ-carbomethoxybutyl)thiophan
The stereospecific synthesis of the four possible isomers of 4-benzamido-3-hydroxy-2-(δ-carbomethoxybutyl)thiophan (III–VI) was accomplished from 4-benzamido-3-oxo-2-(δ-carbomethoxybutylidene)thiophan (I) using the stereospecificity of the reduction of the oxo group of I, the stereospecificity of the reduction of the exocyclic double bond of 4-benzamido-3-hydroxy-2-(δ-carbomethoxybutylidene)thiophan (II), and inversion of the hydroxyl group of III and IV. The configurations of the stereoisomers obtained were established by PMR spectroscopy.
