The Impact of Family Background on Occupying Student Union Cadres of University Students
A statistic comparative analysis on family background between all the students and Cadres of Student Union(CSU)in X university reached the following conclusion:The proportion of CSU from cities,with higher Social Economic Status(SES)and better educated parents,is higher than those from rural areas,with lower SES background and little educated parents.The cultural character of students who come from the upper middle class,city,high-income families and parents of better educated families is coincidence with the quality of CSU.So students coming from the upper class and middle class have more possibilities to occupy CSU than lower class students.In addition,the alienation and rejection to this organization of lower class students themselves aggravates this situation.
