Influence of Agglomerated-particle Transnaturing on Ti(C, N)-TiB_2 Multi-phase Ceramic Coatings Sprayed by Reactive Flame
One of the critical factor to influence the quality of the reactive flame sprayed Ti(C, N)-TiB2 multiphase ceramic coatings is the agglomerated state of the spray particles. Chemical plating Ni and sol-gel cladding TiO2 techniques were applied to transnature to the Ti-B4C-C-Al agglomerated particles, so as to enhance the SHS(self-propagating High-temperature Synthesis) reaction of the agglomerated particle unit. The influence of capsule transnaturing on the SHS reactive flame sprayed Ti(C,N)-TiB2 multiphase ceramic coatings was studied. It is showed that capsule transnaturing to the agglomerated particles is the effective method to improve the qualityof the reactive flame sprayed coatings. The better the effect of the capsule transnaturing is, the more sufficiently the reaction carries on and higher degree of conversion of the reactive products is achieved. It can also effectively protect Ti from being oxidated during the course of spray and take some influence on the microstructure and tissue distribution of the coatings. So the micro-hardness and compactness of the coatings are improved.
