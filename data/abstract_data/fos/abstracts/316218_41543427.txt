Sweat-patch test for alcohol consumption: rapid assay with an electrochemical detector.
The sweat-patch test monitors the amount of alcohol consumed over a number of days. A new method is described for rapid assay of ethanol in the sweat-patch, using a hand-held electrochemical detector (Alco-Sen-sor III). The head-space assay technique is sensitive and highly linear, and may be used in an office setting with no additional laboratory equipment. Using this method, it is possible to obtain clinically useful information at the time of an office visit concerning the patient's alcohol consumption during the preceding week.
