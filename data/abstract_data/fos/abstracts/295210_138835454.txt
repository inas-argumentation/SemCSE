Study on Effect of Degree of Supercooling on Solidification Structure of 430 Ferritic Stainless Steel Based on 3D-CAFE Model
The solidification structure of 430 ferritic stainless steel was simulated with 3-D CAFE model.The influence of the maximum degree of supercooling in nucleation on solidification structure was studied by changing the maximum degree of supercooling in nucleation from 2.20 K to 3.35 K at 0.05K intervals.The simulation results indicate that the proportion of columnar grain zone increases,and the proportion of equiaxed grain region decreases,the chilling outside layer of fine equiaxed grain zone widen and equiaxed grain area shrink with increase of the maximum degree of supercooling in nucleation.At the same time,the solidification structure of 430 ferritic stainless steel was investigated by laboratory experiment.The experimental results show the solidification structure of casting blank is almost spherical equiaxed grains when casting billet cool in furnace from liquidus temperature.The experimental results of laboratory experiments are consistent with the simulation results of the 3D-CAFE,which indicates that 3D-CAFe model can play a good role in guiding the study of CET transformation process in laboratory.
