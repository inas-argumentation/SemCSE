One-dimensional diffusion with the diffusion coefficient a linear function of concentration: reduction to an equation of the first order
The problem considered by Stokes1, of one-dimensional diffusion from an initially sharp boundary between two semi-infinite columns of liquid, the diffusion constant being a linear function of concentration, is discussed. It is shown how the differential equation may be reduced to an equation of the first order.
