Bulk Density Changes Effected by Insect-Produced Dust in Sound Wheat, Grain Sorghum and Corn
DUST produced by Rhyzopertha dominica (Fabricius) was added to undamaged wheat, sorghum, and corn to investigate its effect on the bulk density of these grains. The effect of various amounts of insect-produced dust on the bulk density differed significantly depending on the grain and moisture content. The reduction in bulk density in grain containing 0.4% dust varied from approximately 0.5 to 1.0%. Because of this dust-produced effect, a weight loss estimation method based on changes in bulk density substantially overestimated weight loss from R. dominica in wheat. Repeated manipulation consistently increased the bulk density of wheat, decreased that of grain sorghum, and had little effect on that of corn.
