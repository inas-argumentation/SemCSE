Polymerization of Aniline on Orthanilic Acid Functionalized Glassy Carbon Electrode and Its Application for Electro-oxidation of Ascorbic Acid
Electro-polymerization of aniline(AN) on orthanilic acid(ABS) functionalized glassy carbon electrode(GCE) and its electrocatalytic oxidation towards ascorbic acid(AA) were studied.After ABS was covalently grafted on GCE via electro-oxidation,the polymerization of AN was performed on ABS/GCE,then polyaniline(PAN)-ABS composite membrane modified GCE(PAN-ABS/GCE/CME) was obtained.PAN-ABS/GCE/CME exhibited good electro-catalytic response towards AA oxidation in 0.1 mol/L PBS pH 6.8 due to the doping of sulfonic acid groups in ABS.The anodic peak potential of AA shifted from 0.39 V at GCE to 0.17 V at PAN-ABS/GCE/CME with an enhanced current response.A linear calibration graph was obtained over the AA concentration range of 5.0×10-4-1.65×10-2 mol/L with a correlation coefficient of 0.9973,the detection limit(3δ) for AA was 7.2×10-6 mol/L.The modified electrode showed good stability and reproducibility.
