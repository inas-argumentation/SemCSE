Udder health (part 10) : prevent and control coliforms : technology
Coliform mastitis is one of the most difficult diseases in the modern dairy industry to treat. Although less than 1,5% of clinical mastitis cases in South Africa is currently caused by coliforms, this proportion is increasing. Coliform mastitis increases as dairy management improves. In this edition we continue to look at the prevention and control of coliform organisms, as well as mastitis treatment.
