Brief Discussion of the Financial Support to Medium and Small-sized Enterprises
In the article, we start from the financing feature of medium and small-sized enterprises and analyse the solution to asymmetric information and mechanism of financial system which lead to medium and small-sized enterprises' financing difficulties. We also present a view that an effective mechanism of financial system is a multi-gradational system with can meet the demand of different-sized enterprise financing, and an effective mechanism of financial system which can promote the development of medium and small-sized enterprises is a pluralistic financial system in the financial market of medium and small-sized enterprises' layer.
