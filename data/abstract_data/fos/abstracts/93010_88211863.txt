Effect of special blockers and activators of ion channels on Bombyx mori paralysis induced by ENF peptides
ENF peptides from the hemolymph of lepidopteran insects are conserved in the primary structure and have such biological functions as paralysis,stimulating hemocyte spreading,growth-blocking,but the mechanism remains unknown.In this study,we investigated the paralytic mechanism induced by AnyPP(Antheraea yamamai paralytic peptide) with the specific activators and blockers of ion channels.The results indicate that IbTX(iberiotoxin),the blocker of BK channel,reduces the duration of paralysis.It is suggested that BK channel is possibly involved in the paralytic effect induced by AnyPP.While the ligation experiments show that nervous system might not be responsible for the paralytic activity of AnyPP.
