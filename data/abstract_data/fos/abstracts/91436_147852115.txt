The establishment and development of the pedagogy at the Christian colleges of the modern ages of China
The subject pedagogy was on of the earliest subjects established at the Christian college of China. The subject formed its own characteristics during its establishment and development. Teachers for the subject had once been part-time priests, who were replaced by the full-time teachers later on. Its curriculum tended to be practiced and localized. The subject was taught with diversified and scientific teaching methods, emphasizing the teaching practice and educational measure and cultivating both erudite and specialized talents. All the characteristics made it a leader of all subjects and promoted the development of Chinese educational subject, exerting a greater influence on the modernization of Chinese education.
