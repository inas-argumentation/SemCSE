Twenty year storage test of Ag/RbAg4I5/I2 solid state batteries
Solid state active primary batteries based on the Ag + ion conductor RbAg 4 I 5 were developed 20 years ago. Silver anodes were coupled with iodine-complex cathodes of Me 4 NI 5 and Me 4 NI 9 . Batteries were tested after 20 years of storage and still delivered 90% of their initial capacity confirming that the average self discharge amounted to about 0.5% per year.
