The Study of Existing Problem and Countermeasues Towards Rules of Origin in China
Rules of Origin ( ROOs) are important rules in both international trade and customs administration. Concerning the economic and trade interests of every country, ROOs are taken considerable attention by every country and have become an improtant component of their trade policies. However, ROOs are used in a very limited way in China, tariff or import nationali-ty statistic, while ROOs have been full used as "gray" policy measures by some developed countries and regional economic groups such as America, EU, etc. So it leads to a lot knottiness duo to other countries' discrimination. It is exigent for China to enact and use improved ROOs to resist protectionism, to protect our own interests.
