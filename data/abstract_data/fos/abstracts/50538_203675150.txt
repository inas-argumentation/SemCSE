Research and Practice of Course Teaching Reformation of Computer Professional in Application-Oriented Institutes
According to the student's training target and characteristics of application-oriented institutes,analyses the problems of teaching model about computer professional courses at present,puts forward a new teaching model that combined the task-driven teaching method with the experience type teaching method closely,designs structure diagram of the new teaching mode,and discusses the application of the new teaching model in the computer network course teaching.The results show that the new teaching model can fully mobilize student's learning initiative and improve the classroom teaching effect.
