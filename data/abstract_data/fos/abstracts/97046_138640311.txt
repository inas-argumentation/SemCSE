Study on Thermodynamic Behavior and Rolling Technique of Rod for TC4 Titanium Alloy
TC4 alloy was compressed on Gleeble-1500 thermal simulator, and the deformation behavior was also analyzed under different temperatures and strain rates. It was ascertained that the optimal deformation beginning temperature was 950℃, and the strain rate was 1 s-1.
