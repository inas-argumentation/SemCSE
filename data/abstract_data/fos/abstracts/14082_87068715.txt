A comparative study on post-harvest nutritional quality of different olive varieties
To compare the post-harvest storage effect of different olive varieties,take the common olive varieties in the east of Guangdong Province:Xiayuan,Dingxiang,Xiangzhong as experimental material. Stores for 9 d under the normal temperature,determines the changes of the outward appearance quality,water content quantity,weight loss rate and nutritional quality (the soluble solid content,vitamin C,protein content,organic acid content) everyday. The results showed that:after 7 days'storage,the three species' outward appearance quality drop obviously,their difference isn't obvious,the average good fruit rate is 34.7% ,the average water contain quantity is 73.5%,the average weight loss rate is 31.1%. Overall sensory appraisal,in the early time the best fresh food is Xiayuan,Dingxiang is the worst,But in later period with the Xiangzhong best and the Xiayuan of the worst. The vitamin C content changes significantly,Dingzhong takes the biggest drop (84.44%),Xiayuan shows the smallest decline (61.4%),the soluble solid content assumes the trend of escalation,rose by an average of 5.89%,the protein and the organic acid content assume the declining trend,dropped by an average of 18.25% and 0.81%,the difference of the species is not obvious.
