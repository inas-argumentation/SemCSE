A performance comparison of a closely-coupled and a loosely-coupled architecture
This paper presents a VAXcluster and an Ether-net-based distributed system architecture. A VAXcluster represents a closely-coupled distributed system; the Ethernet-based system is a loosely-coupled distributed system. The distinguishing feature of a VAX-cluster is that it has a shared file system. The models of an Ethernet-based system and of a VAXcluster are presented. A study of different architectures for various numbers of hosts, fileservers and diskservers is given. A performance comparison of a VAXcluster and an Ethernet-based architecture is made for different workloads.
