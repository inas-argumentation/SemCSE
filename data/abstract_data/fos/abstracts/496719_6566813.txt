As part of the effort to bring medical and dental education together, our dental school has recently implemented a new integrated clinic to teach dental students primary care medicine with a focus on oral health. We are using the primary care rotations for students in a dental setting as a platform for change in our approach to patient care, and we are hopeful that interprofessional educational experience could help provide the tools for our future health care professions to serve the comprehensive health needs of our patient population and furthermore the general public.
