DISPROPORTIONATION OF CF3Cl CATALYZED BY Al2O3·Cr2O3
A mixed oxide of Al2O3 and Cr2O3 (Al2O3· Cr2O3) showed catalytic activity higher than that of Al2O3 or Cr2O3 in the disproportionation of CF3Cl. Maximum conversion (> 30 %) was obtained when the reaction was carried out over the mixed oxide (atomic ratio of Cr/Al:1/9) at 450°C.
