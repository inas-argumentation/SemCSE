Observation of a transverse voltage in the mixed state of YBCO thin films
The occurrence of a transverse voltage peak has been observed in YBCO thin films. This phenomenon occurs under conditions of temperature and current in the earth's magnetic field such that the films are in the mixed state. The magnitude of the peak was found to scale with both temperature and current, and exhibited a Sin(/spl theta/) orientational dependence measured between the film-substrate and the position of the measurement electrodes on the film surface. These observations are similar to observations made in conventional superconducting thin films such as Nb, NbN, NbCN, and PbBi.<<ETX>>
