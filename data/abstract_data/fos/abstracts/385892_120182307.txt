Probability inequalities for quadratic forms
Jensen [SIAM J. of Appl. Math., 1982, 297-301] has obtained Chebyshev inequalities for joint distributions of k quadratic forms. In this paper Jensen's bounds are sharpened under α-unimodality assumptions to yield Gauss-type inequalities. Jensen's applications In ballistics, statistical quality control, and analysis of variance arereconsidered.
