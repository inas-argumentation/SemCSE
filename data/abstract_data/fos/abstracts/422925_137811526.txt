Research on Microstructure in Thick Plate of 9Ni3CrMoV Steel
The microstructure of 9Ni3CrMoV steel in different position along with the plate thickness was studied by optical microscope with a kind of new corrosive 725-6,and compared with the observed results by TEM. The low carbon tempering lath-martensite and bainite were clearly observed in optical microscope, and the content of martensite along with plate thickness could be measured accurately, which accords with the results of TEM analyses.
