Application of the Grey Relational Analysis on the Yard Long Bean in Facility
Six cultivars of yard long bean were evaluated by using the relation degree of grey system.They were analyzed in terms of 10 traits including yield,bean-pod character,disease resistance.The results showed that "Zajiaozhenzhuxiabao" and "Yuebaoyoudoujiao" were closed to ideal cultivar,and its comprehensive characters were the best.It also showed that the ranking order by the grey relation degree was consistent with the actual observation.
