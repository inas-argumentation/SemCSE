A Summary of Next Generation Dispatching Automation System
At present with the development of computer hardware and software,network,database,intelligent control technology and the demand of power market,it is necessary to build up next generation dispatching automation system.New system have new propertie of adopting component,internet,artificial intelligence and considering security of power system in the circumstance of power market.From aspect of fundamental technology,architecture,relations with power market management system and security of power system,the paper gives a brief depict about new generation dispatching automation system.
