Effects of Forming Velocity on Knuckle Hot Extrusion System
Aiming at widely existing problems such as low die service life in knuckle hot extrusion process,the effects of various forming velocities on knuckle hot extrusion system about pressure and temperature are studied by FEM simulation.The results show that the faster the velocity of forging equipment,the higher the maximum extruding force,maximum principal stress,maximum part temperature and maximum die surface layer temperature.It provides a reference on improving extrusion die life based on forming velocity perspective.
