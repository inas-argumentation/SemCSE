Energy Saving Experience for 2×300MW Thermal Power Commissioning Project
Some energy saving experiences used in a 2 ×300MW thermal power commissioning project are introduced.These experiences mainly are using industry water instead of open water as cooling water for closed cooling water system,so circulating pump power consumption is saved;using steam feedwater pump for start pump to save motor driving feedwater pump power consumption and application of the plasma ignition technology to save ignition oil.These experiences can also speed up the commissioning progress under special conditions.
