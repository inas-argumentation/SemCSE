Major ion composition and chemical associations of inorganic atmospheric aerosols.
Atmospheric aerosols were sampled and analyzed for H/sup +/, NH/sub 4//sup +/, Ca/sup 2 +/, Mg/sup 2 +/, Na/sup +/, K/sup +/, SO/sub 4//sup 2 -/, Cl/sup -/, and NO/sub 3//sup -/. Gaseous ammonia was also measured. The analyses demonstrated that these ions are the main constituents of the particulate water-soluble fraction of the aerosols sampled. Ion balance studies showed that the main compounds present were (NH/sub 4/)/sub 2/SO/sub 4/, NH/sub 4/NO/sub 3/, and NH/sub 4/Cl in polluted aerosols and NaCl and MgCl/sub 2/ in maritime aerosols. Backward air mass trajectories for collected samples indicated that NH/sub 4/Cl is primarily produced locally, NH/sub 4/NO/sub 3/ has a local to medium-distance origin, and (NH/sub 4/)/sub 2/SO/sub 4/ has been transported over long distances.
