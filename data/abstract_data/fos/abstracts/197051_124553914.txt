Oscillations in the excitation function for complete fusion of $sup 16$O+$sup 12$C
The total fusion cross section for the $sup 16$O+$sup 12$C system has been measured in the ranges 13 MeV< or =E/sub c.m./< or =27 MeV. The cross section is found to increase with bombarding energy up to E/sub c.m./approx. =17 MeV but to stay roughly constant at higher energies, in qualitative agreement with predictions of current models. Superimposed on this average cross section behavior unexpected oscillations are observed with a period of about 3--4 MeV (c.m. energy). (AIP)
