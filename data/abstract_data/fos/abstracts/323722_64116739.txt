QAM Resource Allocation Algorithm Based on Utility Max-min Fairness
This paper introduces the architecture of a project extending the downstream bandwidth of access networks by adopting IPQAM devices,and researches the QAM resource allocation problem in such an architecture.Considering the bandwidth requirements of services as bandwidth bounds and utility functions,we researched the Utility Max-min(UMM) fair allocation,and designed a new algorithm for UMM fair bandwidth allocation in a single link.This algorithm is more accurate than the algorithm based on piecewise linear functions,and their time complexity are similar.Based on this UMM allocation algorithm,considering the bandwidth use ratio and utility fairness,we designed the QAM channel selecting algorithm and the bandwidth allocation algorithm on a single channel.As the simulation results show,our UMM allocation algorithm is quite accurate,and our QAM channel selecting algorithm achieves good fairness among different channels.
