Research on Urban-Rural Marriage and New Urban Immigrants
As the result of reform and opening up,and farmers' frenzied hunt for work in cities,massive urban-rural marriages appear.Different from the previous ones,today's new urban-rural marriages has created a new immigrant group: city wife from countryside.These women settle in cities through the marriage with urban men,and therefore change their rural household registration into registered permanent urban residences.The article analyzes the form,structure and development of urban-rural marriage in new era,and points that,as new immigrants,city wives from countryside need to pay more efforts to acclimatize themselves to city life and also to be accepted by the city.
