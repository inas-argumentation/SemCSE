On the crystal structure of hollandite-like TixV5S8, evidence for one-dimensional cation order
A single crystal structure refinement of Tl 0.83 V 5 S 8 corroborates the centrosymmetric space group C2/m being most probable for TIV 5 S 8 type phases. Diffuse X-ray scattering proves one-dimensional ordering of the thallium atoms within the not fully occupied channels of the structure. A model for the cation order based on a restricted, commensurate Frenkel-Kontorowa chain is proposed.
