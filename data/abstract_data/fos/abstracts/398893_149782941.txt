Hybrid plasmonic nanosystem with controlled position of nanoemitters
Quantum dots optically excited in close proximity to a silver nanowire can launch surface plasmons. The challenge related to this promising hybrid system is to control the position of nanoemitters on the nanowire. We report on the use of a two-photon photopolymerization process to strategically position quantum dots on nanowires at controlled sites. A parametric study of the distance between the quantum dots and the nanowire extremity shows that precise control of the position of the launching sites enables command of light intensity at the wire end through surface plasmon propagation.Quantum dots optically excited in close proximity to a silver nanowire can launch surface plasmons.
