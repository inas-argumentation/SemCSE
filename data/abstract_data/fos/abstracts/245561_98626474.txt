Reaction of 2-mercaptobenzimida(thia)zole with 1,4-naphthoquinone and its oxide
The reaction of 2-mercaptobenzimida(thia)zole with 1,4-naphthoquinone proceeds as nucleophilic 1,4-addition to give the corresponding thioethers. The reaction of 1,4-naphthoquinone oxide with 2-mercaptobenzimidazole leads to benzimidazonaphthothiazole, while the reaction of the oxide with 2-mercaptobenzothiazole leads to a thioether as a consequence of the addition of the mercapto-substituted heterocycle at the oxide ring.
