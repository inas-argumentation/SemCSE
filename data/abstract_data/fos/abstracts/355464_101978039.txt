isolation, purification and structural characterization of neutral heteropolysaccharide from bletilla striata
Crude polysaccharide was prepared by ethanol precipitation followed the hot water extraction Bletilla striata stem and deproteinized with Sevag method.It was further isolated by ion-exchange chromatography on DE-52 column and gel filtration on Sephadex G-100 column.One fraction was obtained and was homogeneous identified by GPC and gel filtration. Its molecular weight was about 99.658 ku estimated by GPC.Its compositions and structures were determined by TLC,chemical analysis and spectroscopic methods(FT-IR,()~(13)C-NMR,()~1H-NMR).The results showed that it was neutral heteropolysaccharide with the composition of β-(1→4)mannose,β-(1→4)glucose, and α-(1→6)glucose residues in molar ratios of 6.8∶3.2∶1.5.
