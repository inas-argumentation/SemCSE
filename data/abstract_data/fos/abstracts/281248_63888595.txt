Addressing Scheme for 6LoWPAN Networks Based on Geographic Polar Coordinate Grid
Because of the resource constrained nodes and multi-hop topology,traditional IPv6 addressing scheme in 6LoWPAN netw orks w ill cause a large overhead. This paper proposes an addressing scheme for 6LoWPAN based on Geographic Polar Coordinate Grid( GPCG),including an IPv6 address autoconfiguration method and a routing algorithm. This scheme could achieve stateless address autoconfiguration as w ell as address compression. In addition,a node could select route based on the address w ithout storing much routing state information. Simulation results reveal that the address autoconfiguration method proposed in this paper outperforms existing netw ork address configuration methods on terms of both protocol overhead and latency. Moreover,simulation show s the routing algorithm based on the addressing scheme also has a good performance.
