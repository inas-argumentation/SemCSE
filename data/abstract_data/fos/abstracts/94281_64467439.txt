Rendering Pipeline Optimization of Deferred Shading
Traditional method of GPU rendering is forward rending.In the current field of real-time rendering,a new pipeline called deferred shading utilizes multiple render target of feature GPU to realize two-stage pipeline processing.In this way,all of the visibility tests are done before per-pixel lighting calculation,which minimizes the pixel fill rates.However,deferred shading requires a large amount of video storage space,and can't render translucent objects efficiently.To solve the technique limitations when applied,proposes some feasible solutions.
