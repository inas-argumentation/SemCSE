Pt-doped and SnO2-templated TiO2: properties and reactivity towards CO
Pt-doped and SnO 2 -templated TiO 2 can be prepared by sol-gel methods and are very active in CO oxidation especially at low p CO and high O 2 :CO ratios. It may also have activity in CO oxidation promoted by water vapour at moderate temperatures of reaction. Scanning calorimetry and residual gas analysis suggest that O finds it relatively difficult to establish itself on a surface precovered with CO and it may be that water-generated OH groups at the Pt-oxide interface accelerate this particular process.
