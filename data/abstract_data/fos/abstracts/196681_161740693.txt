Comics, as a hybrid medium, have a special potential to address issues of alterity, or as Thomas concludes his essay, "The fragmented, invisible art makes visible the invisible citizens of the world" (172). As comics themselves become visible to the broader scholarly world, work like Multicultural Comics demonstrates their power to approach people and subjects differently than other media, creating complex and illuminating narratives about those that too often remain unseen.
