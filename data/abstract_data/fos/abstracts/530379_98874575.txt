Synthesis of 3-chloro-2-methylaniline by high selective hydrogenation with ligand modified Pt/C catalyst
The Pt/C catalyst is modified by nitrogenous organic compounds.The effects of modifier on the restrain dechloridation performance of the catalyst and the reaction conditions on the selectivity to arylamine are studied.The results show that the selectivity to 3-chloro-2-methylaniline reaches above 99.90% at the conversion of 100%.The catalyst can be recycled more than 20 times without notable decrease of catalytic activity and selectivity.
