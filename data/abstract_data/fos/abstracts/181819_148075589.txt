Main Measures for Profession Morality Construction of Publishing Industry
The profession morality construction of publishing industry is a very important task.The paper discusses the main measures to enhance the profession morality construction of publishing industry,containing including the profession morality construction into the enterprise culture construction so as to make the enterprise system rules regulate the profession morality behaviors,combine the profession morality construction with the citizen morality construction,create good interaction and harmonious atmosphere to provide the desirable environment for the profession morality construction and open the courses of profession morality education,etc.
