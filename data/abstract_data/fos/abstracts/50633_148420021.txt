Research on the legal relationship between Chinese public higher educational institutions and their teachers
With the deepening of the reform in higher education system and personnel system for teachers, the previous relationship between public higher educational institutions and teachers has gradually been broken. Through signing up the letter of appointment, teachers started to enjoy a personnel relationship protected by law. This is a special administrative legal relationship. Public higher educational institution teachers are no longer civil servants but possess the identity of administrative employees. The teacher appointment agreement is neither the civil agreement regulated by "Contract Law" nor the labor agreement regulated by "Labor Law", but rather possess the character of administrative agreement.
