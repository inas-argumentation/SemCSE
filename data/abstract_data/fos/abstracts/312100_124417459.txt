DESIGN OF A OPTICAL KLYSTRON ON HESYRL STORAGE-RING
AbstractHe Duohui and Jia Qiqia(Heifei National Synchrotron Radiation Lab.,Heifei, 230020)The structure of the Optical Klystron(OK)on HESYRLSR has been discribed in thepaper. The main structure and dimensions of the OK are shown in Fig.3 together withthe direction of each permanent-magnet block and directions of axes.The magnet materialis NdFeB with a remanent field higher than 1.17T. The number of magnetic periods N_u in asingle undulator section(US)is 12 with the period λ_s=4a=7.2cm. The length in the zdirection of dispersive section (DS) is 2.1 6cm, and the total length L of the OK is 196.2cm.The gap of three sections can be changed either in synchronism or independently. Authorsprovide a code for computational 2-D optical klystron magnetic field. The verticalcomputational magnetic field B_y in HESYRLSR OK along the axis, with gap in the US of4cm and in the DS of 6cm, are shown in Fig.7.
