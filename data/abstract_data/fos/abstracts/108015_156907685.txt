The Behavior and Psychology of Country Fair Trade——A Field Investigation in Some Town Markets of Chaohu City
Through the field investigation in s ome town markets of Chaohu City,the a uthor finds that the peasants' behavior in market trading is subject to rationality.On the one hand,rationality of economic man takes advantage of living ratio-nality and develops the modes of dimi nishing risks through the agents' experience and personality and experts;on the other hand,the living rationality is challenged by the rationality o f economic man through cheating both the ac-quaintances and the strangers.Such behavior is supported by the social psychology in the countryside.The article is supposed to provide a practical th eory for the planning of constructin g towns in Anhui Province.It is also i ntend-ed to throw light on the modernity in t he peasants and social structure in t he rural areas.
