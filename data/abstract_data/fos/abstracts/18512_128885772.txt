Comments to ‘A reply to “Relative sea level during the Holocene in Uruguay”’ by Bracco et al. (2013)
Criticisms of Bracco et al. (in press) about Martinez and Rojas (2013) are commented. We still claim that the sea level curve shown in the latter paper is the first statistically, testable curve produced for Uruguay. Previous attempts were made by hand. With the exception of some typographical errors correctly signaled by Bracco et al. (in press), it is demonstrated that their assumptions about our work are wrong and subjective.
