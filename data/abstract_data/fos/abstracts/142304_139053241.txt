Comparative Analysis on Microstructure of Brazing Diamond by Ti-containing Filler
Taking Ag-Cu-Ti and Ti-Zr-Ni-Cu as fillers separately to braze diamond,the strongly bonding between diamond and steel was realized. Then the brazed samples were tested through SEM and EDS. The results show that taking Ag-Cu-Ti as filler a little lump-shape TiC engenders,which distribute,discontinuously in the diamond surface, however, taking Ti-Zr-Ni-Cu as filler there is much cobble-shape TiC,which distributes continuously.
