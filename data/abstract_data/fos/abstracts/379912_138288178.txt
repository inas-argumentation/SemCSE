Boronitriding used in valve core to improve corrosion resistance
The boronitriding possess has advantages of fast speed, deep case layer, excellent property, cheap cost, and bright surface, etc. Because of nitrizing firstly, the compactness of the Fe 2 B phase on the surface is improved effectively, the compactive carbon nitrogen compounds are formed on the surface, and the boronic compounds layer is deepened; so the wear resistance, corrosion resistance and hot fatigue resistance are improved. The steel 45 after boronitriding could be substituted for stainless steel to make the valve core for the hydrochloric acid, which can use for a long time without obvious corrosion, and the effect is remarkable.
