With careful attention to the structure, cultural context, and performance potential of Shakespeare’s dramatic verse, Lerer traces intertextual contentions that movingly illuminate these still comparatively underappreciated plays. The claim that a similar interpretation is evident in traces of original reception, such as Simon Forman’s brief notes or marginal annotations by the anonymous Mesei Folio reader, is less compelling. It is unfortunate that the argument about hypothetical reception lead to Pericles and Two Noble Kinsmen, which are not in the Folio, being relegated to a short epilogue on editorial desires for authenticity—where they get suggestive readings deserving of greater expansion. Lerer concludes that readers who seek “authentic Shakespeare” are searching for “something lyrical” (202). Certainly that is not what all readers seek, but in Lerer’s captivating readings one finds it with haunting beauty.
