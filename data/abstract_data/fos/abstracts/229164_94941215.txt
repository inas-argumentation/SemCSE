Preparation of organocyclosilazanes by ammonolysis of dialkyldichlorosilanes
A study is made of ammonolysis of dialkyldichlorosilanes of the RR'SiCl2 series, where R = Me, Et, and R′ = Me(CH2)n (n = 1, 3, 5, 7, 8). With the lower members of the homologous series it takes place in accordance with a previously known equation. Ammonolysis of dialkyldichlorosilanes with big R′ radicals on the silicon atom gives both cyclic and linear ammonolysis products.
