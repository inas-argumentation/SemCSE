An Oligopsonistic Model of Wage Determination in Agrarian Societies
The theory of rural wages in less developed countries is itself only partially developed. Three major explanations have been put forward but none of these can account for observed variations in nominal wages over time, over regions, and across individuals. It is, therefore, time to take a fresh look at the problem and to develop new models which are more in accord with the data. This task is undertaken in section II where a model of heterogeneous labour is used to analyse rural labour markets and explain the wage payments system. Section I contains a brief summary of existing theory and its deficiencies. In order to derive the main properties of the model quickly formal proofs of assertions are relegated to the technical appendix. and most of the discussion of the assumptions and detailed explanations are left to section III. This section also contains general conclusions as well as a discussion of some of the more controversial issues.
