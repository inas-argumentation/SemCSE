An Empirical Study of Training Mode Reform and Employment Probability for Graduates of Higher Vocational College
By making use of a national representative dataset collected in 2012,this study applied the hierarchical linear model to examine the effect of teaching mode reform on the employment probability of graduates of higher vocation college.However,only few schoollevel factors and individual human capital factors had significant effects.The results implied that employment rate was no longer an imperative challenge faced by higher vocational colleges,and employment quality should be the next focal point.
