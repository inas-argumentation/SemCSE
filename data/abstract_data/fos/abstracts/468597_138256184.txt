Effect of Q-I-Q Heat Treatment Technique on Microstructure and Strength-toughness of GDL-1 Steel
The GDL-1 bainite steel was heat treated by a novel technique Q-I-Q(quenching-isothermal-quenching).The effects of technique parameters on mechanical properties were studied by changing temperature and time in isothermal process.The results show that the microstructure of the GDL-1 steel by Q-I-Q heat treatment is composed mainly by martensite and a few bainite,as well as small amount of retained austenite.The maximum impact toughness is 108.5 J which is derived by preserving in the temperature of 320 ℃ for 30 min.And the maximum tensile strength is 1614 MPa which is derived by preserving in the temperature of 340 ℃ for 3 h.The optimal temperature for highest strength and toughness changes from 320 ℃ to 340 ℃.
