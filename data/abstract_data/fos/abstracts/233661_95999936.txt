Structural investigation by X-ray diffraction of GaAs epilayers and AlAs/GaAs superlattices grown on Si by MBE
The structural properties of GaAs and AlAs/GaAs superlattices on (100) silicon oriented 2 degrees off towards are investigated by X-ray diffraction measurements and by X-ray topography. The average defect density in the epilayers, determined from the linewidth of the GaAs peaks, is found to reach a minimum of 5*107 cm-2 for 3 mu m thick GaAs layers. Additionally, there is a relationship between the dislocation density and the tilt angle between the GaAs layer and the Si substrate. X-ray topographs reveal a fluctuation of the lattice constant in the GaAs epilayer which forms a cellular structure of cell diameter approximately=10 mu m. The X-ray diffraction patterns of AlAs/GaAs superlattices exhibit satellite peaks indicating the expected strain modulation. However, the crystalline quality of the constituent AlAs layers is inferior to that of the GaAs layers, leading to inferior structural properties of the entire AlAs/GaAs superlattice.
