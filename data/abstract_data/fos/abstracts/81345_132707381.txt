Acoustic Environmental Performance Survey on Urban Roads with Hydrophobic Asphalt Pavement
Traffic noises of five roads in Hangzhou were monitored before and after hydrophobic asphalt pavement reconstruction,and traffic noise annoyance from the residents who lived near roads was surveyed.A contrast of after reconstruction with before reconstruction showed that Leq reduced 0.2 ~ 8.7 dB and average equivalent sound level reduced 2.4 ~ 6.0 dB,which indicated that hydrophobic asphalt pavement had obvious noise reduction effect.The level of traffic noise annoyance from residents in social surveys was reduced significantly.
