The production of MBP-specific T lymphocyte lines in Wistar rats
Objective As a technical plateform of T cell vaccine preparation, we made up a MBP specific rat T cell lines. Methods Wistar rats develop EAE model was established by immunization of guinea pig spinal cord homogenate(GPSCH) in CFA and pertussis vaccine(PV). LNC was stimulated by GPMBP in every two weeks, and selected by 3H thymidine incorporation. The surface phenotype of the MBP line was determined by FACS. The cytokines mRNA expression was assayed by RT PCR. Results A single injection with GPSCH in CFA and PV induced EAE in Wistar rats. GPMBP specific T cell lines were produced. Their phenotype were CD4 +. The mRNA expression for IL 2 and IFN γ was higher than that for TNF α. Conclusion EAE could be induced in Wistar rats. The surface phenotype of the T lymphocyte lines was CD4 +, and showed a T H1 cytokine profile.
