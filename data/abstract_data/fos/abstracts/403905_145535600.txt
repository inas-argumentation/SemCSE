Extraterrestrial intelligence: the debate continues. the author comments
The above criticisms of my views can be divided into two classes: (1) those of a technical nature, and (2) those which deal with the sociology of possible extraterrestrial intelligent beings. Most of these criticisms I have discussed in the three Quarterly Journal articles of which the PHYSICS TODAY paper was but a precis. Here I will reply briefly to those criticisms that were answered in the Quarterly Journal; my critics are referred to those papers for more detail on the points in question.
