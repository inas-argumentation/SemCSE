Investigation on Contemporary Vietnamese University Students Inheriting the Confucian Ethics
Based on the data analysis of investigating contemporary Vietnamese university students inheriting the Confucian ethics,it found that the Confucian ethics were recognized broadly by contemporary Vietnamese university students;Vietnamese students expressed carrying forward the Confucian ethics could offer spiritual and intellectual support;they gave a high appraisal to the moral cultural contribution.Most of them supposed that the Confucian ethics would still be an important part of university students’moral system in the 21st century;besides,they also rethought and questioned the adaptability of the Confucian ethics in the contemporary Vietnam society.
