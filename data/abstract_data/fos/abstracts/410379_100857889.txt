Photocatalytic Mechanism and Effect Factors of Acid Orange II in Fe~(3+)/UV/O_2 Process
The photocatalytic mechanism and effect factors of acid orange Ⅱ(AOⅡ) in Fe3+/UV/O2 process were studied.The results showed that degradation of the dye was attributed to the oxidation of ·OH from photocatalysis in the presence of Fe3 +.The ferrous ion was photo-oxidized to ferric ion by dissolved oxygen,which was beneficial for the sustainable degradation process.Moreover,the optimum condition was that the initial pH value was 3 and the air flow rate was 1.0 L/min.Acid orange Ⅱ was degraded completely within 120 min and part of N molecule was transformed to N2,but only 35.77 % of TOC was removed in solution which indicated acid orange Ⅱ molecule could not be mineralized completely in Fe3+/UV/O2 process.
