Measurement on Phase Transition Temperature of Titanium Alloy BT25
The phase transition temperature of the titanium alloy BT25 was measured by the methods of calculation, thermal expansion, DSC and continuous rising temperature metallographiy. The results show that the phase transition temperatures obtained by the four methods are 1 023.2, 1 029.0, 1 029.8, and 1 029.0 ℃, respectively.
