How far are China's top university discipline fields from the world class——Meditation on discipline ranking of universities in the world
This study analyzes the position of top Chinese universities in the world and their performance in various academic indexes.It is found that in natural science and engi- neering fields,some famous universities from mainland already had relatively large amount of publications,but the proportions of papers in great impact journals are still low,and are lac- king in original achievements of great influence and top scholars.The study also reveals that in comparison with world class universities,fields of life science and clinical medicine in Chi- na still stand at a beginning stage.As for social science fields,the international impact of top Chinese universities is still negligible.Suggestions are made accordingly:rationally view the position of our top universities and its current discipline state in the world;stick to the confi- dence of building world-class universities and disciplines and further expand key input to sev- eral top universities;confirm the idea of intensive development so as to realize the rapid change from quantitative accumulation to quality improvement.
