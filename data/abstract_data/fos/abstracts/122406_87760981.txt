Simultaneous extraction of phytosterol and V_E from corn germ oil deodorizer distillate
Corn germ oil deodorizer distillate is rich in phytosterol and VE. The extraction, separation, analysis of phytosterol and VE from corn germ oil deodorizer distillate were investigated. The conditions for the extraction of phytosterols and VE were studied by experimenting with the steps that included crystallization, recrystallization, saponification and extraction. Studies have shown that the yield of sterol was 70% and the purity was 83.2% when using acetone-methanol mixed solvent, liquid ratio 3:1, the crystallization temperature of-4 ℃, crystallization time 12 h, the best recrystallized reagent was Acetone. And by using high performance liquid chromatography(HPLC) method to determine the composition of the extract of phytosterol is campesterol, stigmasterol and β-sitosterol, the ratio of 1:7:6. The influences of alkali dosage, saponifying time, saponifying temperature, on the yield of VE were investigated, and through single factor and orthogonal experiments to determine the optimum conditions for extraction VE, alkaline to material(w:w) ratio of 3.5%, saponification time 4 h, saponification temperature 55 ℃, the yield of VE was 54.5%. All the above established technics for separating and purifying of phytosterol and VE.
