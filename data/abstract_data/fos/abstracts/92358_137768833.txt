Effects of Solidification Rate on Microstructure of Al_3Ti/Al Composite
In-situ Al3Ti/Al composite was fabricated by direct melt reaction method in Al-Ti system.The microstructure of the composite at various solidification rates was studied by XRD and SEM.The results show that the morphology and size of Al3Ti particles are obviously affected by various solidification rates.The morphology of Al3Ti particle is needle-like with the length of 50～150 μm in the composite which is fabricated by sand mould at low solidification.While it is short rod-like and nearly 10 μm in size when the composite is casted by cooper mold at rapid solidification.But in the thinnest of the composite,the Al3Ti particle is four-angle star in shape and below 10 μm in size.Additionally,the crystal grain size of the composite decreases with solidification rate increasing.
