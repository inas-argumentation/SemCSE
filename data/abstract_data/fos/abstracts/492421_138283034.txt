Effect of Cr on Properties of Fe-C-V-B Surfacing Alloys
The hardfacing was used for depositing Fe-C-V-B alloy with and without Cr on Q235 steel.The effects of Cr on the properties of surfacing alloys were investigated by optical microscopy,scanning electron microscopy and X-ray diffractometer,together with the abrasion resistance evaluated by wear mass loss and the analysis of worn morphology.The experimental results show that the certain Cr content can significantly improve the abrasion resistance of Fe-C-V-B surfacing alloys,increase the bond strength between the hard phase grains and the matrix.
