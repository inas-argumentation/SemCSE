History revision & future prospects for orientecring sports
The appearaance of the word"Orienteering"signifies the beginning of Orienteering Sports, with a history of more than one hundred years by now.It has won wide popularity in the Continents of Europe,Australia and also in North America, More and more people begin to pay their attention to it,even in China,Orienteering Sports are being held in various kinds of schools in recent years.With the efforts of all Orienteering Sports Organizations,the moment for the membership of Orienteering Sports into the Olympic Family will come soon.
