The palaeotectonic status of the Malay Peninsula
Abstract Van Bemmelen (1949, 1954) and a number of other geologists have promoted the concept that the orogenic belt which forms the Malay Peninsula is a southwesterly outgrowth of “Cathaysia”, “Laurasia” or the “Indosinian massif”. Recent investigations, however, indicate that the peninsular tract formerly bordered the Indian shield, from which it became detached as Gondwanaland disintegrated. The Bay of Bengal appears to be a sphenochasm formed by the same phase of continental drift.
