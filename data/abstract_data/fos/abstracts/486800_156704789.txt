Resistance against Corruption by Means of System:the Successful Exploration and Historical Experience of Deng Xiao-ping
The resistance against corruption by means of system is the core of Deng Xiao-ping's ideas about resistance against corruption. In the course of the exploration,Deng Xiao-ping made a great contribution to resistance against corruption by means of system. His successful experience could be generalized as follows:first,strengthen democracy and perfect legal system;second,initiate the supervision with system as its carrier;third,reform the traditional political system. Deng's idea provides important inspirations for the Party to execute the state power and strengthen the capacity of preventing corruption,namely:to set up the authority of system;to have a scientific and reasonable system;and to pay great attention to the implementation of system.
