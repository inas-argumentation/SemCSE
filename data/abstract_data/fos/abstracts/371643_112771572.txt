Comprehensive Evaluation of Casting Quality based on the Modified Fuzzy Analysis
A fuzzy evaluation method of casting technological quality based on fuzzy analysis was presented. Taking evaluating factors such as shrink soft, crackle, gas hole, inclusion fuzzy parameters, casting technological quality was evaluated by using fuzzy math theory. Simple factor evaluation was taken according to subordinated function before comprehensive evaluation was taken according to weight, a difficultly quantitative evaluation was changed into quantitative result by using fuzzy transform. Meanwhile, the weight defining method was improved in order to avoid to consistency check and the evaluative matrix was improved so as to develop distinguishing rate.
