Radiative characteristics of buried mesa stripe heterolasers emitting at 1.5 μm
An experimental investigation was made of changes in the angular distribution of the radiation at the moment of appearance of self-modulation pulsations in buried InGaAsP/InP heterostructure lasers emitting at 1.5 μm. This was attributed tentatively to "leakage" of the output radiation at technological irregularities in the active medium.
