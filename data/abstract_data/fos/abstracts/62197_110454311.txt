Status and Verification of Nebraska's Corn Growth Model—CORNGRO
A computer model, CORNGRO, was developed which simulates corn growth as influenced by soil water availability. Specific details of plant growth modified from an earlier version of CORNGRO (Childs et al., 1977) are presented and further elaboration is made on some concepts previously presented. Verification of the model against carefully monitored field conditions is presented. Grain yields were accurately predicted for limited irrigation field plots for several years at two locations in Nebraska. Additional improvements can be made in the model's present procedures for predicting leaf expansion, tasseling and maturity dates, and depths of root water extractions; however the model can be useful in developing limited irrigation management practices.
