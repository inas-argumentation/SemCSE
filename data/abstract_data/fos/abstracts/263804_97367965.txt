Synthesis of N-hydroxymethyl derivatives of 5-substituted tetrazoles
Mixtures of isomeric 1- and 2-hydroxymethyl derivatives are formed in the reaction of tetrazole and 5-alkyltetrazoles with formaldehyde in aqueous media, while only one isomer is formed from 5-phenyl- and 5-trifluoromethyltetrazoles. The structures and ratios of the isomers were determined by PMR spectroscopy.
