Research on Highly Available Services in Ad Hoc Networks
Ad-hoc networks are used more and more widely in military and civilian applications due to the rapid organizing of networks and the outstanding ability of destruction-resistance and self-recovery. Technology of re-construction and self-recovery is very important for the availability, destruction-resistance and robustness of a network. Based researching reconstruction and self-recovery for the Ad Hoc network, The server group management algorithm is proposed. This algorithm uses active replication policy to guarantee the consistency of the status of servers and maintains the server group by adding, replacing and deleting servers to reduce service interruptions. It guarantees that certain service is still available while some nodes that supply such service failed. The results of simulation and data analysis show that the algorithm improves availability and guarantees reliability, destruction-resistance and robustness of the Ad Hoc network.
