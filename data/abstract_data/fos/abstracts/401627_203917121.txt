Optimization for Chemical Degumming Technology of Raw Banana Fiber
Chemical degumming of banana fiber was performed with alkali and the factors affecting degumming were investigated,using the amount of residual gum and residual lignin as the indices of the result of degumming.Single-factor analysis was adopted to find out the parameters range during degumming and then orthogonal experiment was used to determine the optimal parameters for chemical degumming.The results indicated that the amount of residual gum and residual lignin were mainly affected by time of boiling-off,Na2SO3,Na5P3O10 and NaOH concentration in the first boiling and NaOH,H2O2 concentration,time of boiling-off were main factors in the second process.The optimal degumming of the first and the second boiling was determined as fellow:14 g/L NaOH,150 min,3% Na2SO3 and 2.75% Na5P3O10;14 g/L NaOH,8% H2O2,120 min,respectively.Under these conditions,the amount of residual gum reduced from 27% to 8.85%,the amount of residual lignin was 4.68%.This indicates that chemical degumming method is good for the extraction of banana fiber.
