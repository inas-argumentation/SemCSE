An Analysis of the Environmental Governance Information Disclosure of Listed Companies of Heavy Pollution Industry in Anhui Province:Based on Empirical Data from theYear of 2001 to 2012
Taking listed companies in the heavy pollution industry in the year 2001to 2012as an example,the analysis of environmental governance information disclosure in prospectus and the company's annual report shows continuous improvement of the disclosure content and extent,with qualitative description as the main form and a big increase in quantitative analysis,which is the rusult of mandatory regulations after a series of policies and regulations have been enacted.However,there is lack of disclosure motives and salient impression management behavior in their disclosure of environmental governance information when companies normally tend to disclose positive information that is advantageous to establish good images.The level of economic development of the regions where listed companies are located has little to do with the environmental governance information disclosure,which is influenced to some degree by the company size,competitive advantage and industry categories.
