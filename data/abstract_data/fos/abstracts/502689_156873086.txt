Evolutionary Economics, Rational Expectations, and Monetary Policy
The main corpus of positive economic theory in the premodern computer era and of the "pure" rational expectations theory may be characterized by scientific method in the tradition of classical (or Newtonian) mechanics. A good bit was achieved in economics in that tradition, but impediments to the movement of economic theory along the lines of a modern science have been brought to the forefront of economic research with the support of the modern computer. The impediments concern the absence of specific time frames, emerging events and outside induced changes, Bayesian learning, sequential variation, and an uncertain and only partially foreseeable future. These are symbolized by the introduction of unidirectional (or entropy) time, as distinct from sidereal time (as in the apparent motion of fixed stars).
