The Study of the Actualities of the Option Market in Our Country
The growth in the national economy needs the support of the option market, but the growth in the option market is still in its infancy and exists quite a lot problems, such as the unfavorble legal system building, a single variety, underdeveloped futures exchange and broker, as well as the control of the investor and so on. The text, aiming at the questions, advances that the development of the option market need to strengthen management macroscopically and to perfect the market's structure microcosmically.
