Application of the Abrikosov pseudofermion technique to a homogeneous spin system
We construct a thermodynamically consistent approach to a pseudospin many body problem under the observance of spin conservation starting from the RPA of the free energy. For this purpose the Abrikosov method of spin representation is extended to many directly interacting spins. We consider the region of validity of the linked cluster theorem (LCT) and give a comment on a recent controversal discussion between Keiter and Stedman concerning the formal analogous problem of indirect spin coupling via conduction electrons.
