New derivation of the symmetry energy for nuclei beyond the {beta}-stability line
We suggest the procedure of direct derivation of the symmetry energy from the shift of neutron-proton chemical potentials {Delta}{lambda}={lambda}{sub n}-{lambda}{sub p} for nuclei beyond the {beta}-stability line. We observe the presence of anomalous strong (about 15%) shell oscillations at the symmetry energy coefficient b{sub sym}. Our results do not confirm the existence of exceptionally large values of the symmetry energy coefficient at mass number Aapprox =100 that was reported in Ref. (2). Using the fitting procedure, we evaluate the volume b{sub sym,vol} and surface b{sub sym,surf} contributions to the symmetry energy. We estimate the experimental value of surface-to-volume ratio as r{sub S}/V=|b{sub sym,surf}|/b{sub sym,vol}approx =1.7 for the fitting interval A>=50.
