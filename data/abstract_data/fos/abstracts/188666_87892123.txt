Studies on Main Factors Influencing Differentiation of Adventitious Buds of Mulberry Leaves
The effects of four kinds of plant regulators (IBA,NAA,BA,KT) and agar on adventitious buds' differentiation of mulberry leaves were investigated using Orthogonal design method.The best differentiation of adventitious buds was obtained on MS+0 2 mg/L IBA+2 mg/L BA+5 mg/L KT+6 g/L agar.
