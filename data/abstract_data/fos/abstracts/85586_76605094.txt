Study on expression of survivin,estrogen receptor and progesterone receptor in endometrial carcinoma
Objective: Combined with the pathological characteristics,to study the expression of survivin,estrogen receptor(ER)and progesterone receptor(PR) in human endometrial carcinomas(EC).Method: Archived tissue samples of 60 EC and 34 paracarcinoma were selected and immunohistochemically analyzed for survivin、ER and PR expression.Results: The positive rate of survivin was significantly higher in EC than in paracarcinoma(65% vs 8.8%)(P0.01),but ER(50% vs 94.1%) and PR(48.3% vs 85.3%) reverse were(P0.01).Both survivin and PR expressions were associated wi th clinical pathological characteristics of endometrial carcinoma such as surgical-pathological stage,histological grade,myometrial invasion,lymph node metastasis.(P0.05).ER expression was associated with histological grade and myometrial invasion(P0.05).Survivin expression was negtively correlated with ER and PR in endometrial carcinoma tissue(r=-0.314,P0.05;r=-0.479,P0.01).Conclusions: Survivin,ER and PR may play an important role in the development of endometrial carcinoma.The lower expression of ER and PR in endometrial carcinoma indicates that endometrial maligant cells lose their initial function after their canceration.It is more helpful for diagnosing,directing treatment and evaluating the prognosis to detect the expressions of surviving,ER and PR in human endometrial carcinoma together.
