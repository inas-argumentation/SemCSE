Model Study of Hot and Dense Baryonic Matter
The properties of baryonic matter have been investigated at finite density and temperature using different models. The variation of baryon masses and fractional number densities with baryon density and temperature obtained from different models have been compared. The quark hadron phase transition have been studied using Chiral Colour Dieletric (CCD) model in the quark sector. No phase transition have been observed for the different variants of the Zimanyi-Moszkowski models below T=60 MeV.
