MOSPOWER—The challenge to power bipolars☆
Abstract A new VMOS technology has been developed which is likely to open up new fields in high speed high current switching as well as audio and R.F. communications. The inherent differences between VMOS and standard MOS technologies are discussed and the electrical qualities of VMOS compared with those of power bipolars. Applications foreseen for these devices are numerous and some of the most obvious are discussed. With the advantages it has to offer, it will undoubtedly become a serious contender to power bipolar technology.
