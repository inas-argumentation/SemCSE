A Case Study of Teaching Literary Theory——Leaving Home and Going Far Away at the Age of 18 under cultural poetics
Taking Leaving Home and Going Far Away at the Age of 18 as an example from the view of a literary theory, this paper tries to reveal the fact that the quality of teaching literary theory can be improved by combining the teaching of literary theory with literary reading. With the view of cultural poetics, the methodologies to study the literature comment are proposed. Firstly we begin with interpretation of literary text to the exposure of poem meaning; then we study the appreciation of beauty of the literary text; finally we can reveal the cultural spirit for realistic demand by clarifying the study of text. According to the method, this paper studies narrating way of Leaving Home and Going Far Away at the Age of 18, pursuit of the appreciation of beauty, and cultural sprit of humanism. When the students learn the theory of cultural poetics, they also knowhowto go on with the criticism of the literary text.
