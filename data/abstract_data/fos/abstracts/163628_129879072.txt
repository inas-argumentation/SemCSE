Drill hole predation on fossil serpulid polychaetes, with new data from the Pliocene of the Netherlands
The fossil record of drill holes in shelled invertebrates is primarily focused on bivalves and gastropods as prey. This study shows by means of an examination of serpulid polychaetes from the Pliocene of Langenboom (The Netherlands) and a review of the literature that drill holes could be much more common in fossil tube-bearing serpulid polychaetes than previously known.
