Study on the enzyme hydrolysis technology of Oviductus ranae with viscosity as the index
Objective:To establish the optimal enzymatic hydrolysis technology of Oviductus ranae.Methods: The monofactor test and the L9(33) orthogonal test are used to select the best technique condition with viscosity as the index,while the enzyme dosage,the enzymolysis temperature and the enzymolysis time as the observation factors.Results: The optimum enzymatic hydrolysis technology is obtained as follows: the enzyme dosage is 120 U/g,the enzymolysis temperature is 65 ℃ and the enzymolysis time is 40 min.Conclusion: The viscosity of Oviductus Ranae reduces from 3127 mPa.s to 8.86 mPa.s and the production dissolubility and flowability are both better.The enzymatic hydrolysis technology is simple,stable and feasible.
