The status quo of HongKong sport and its developing trend
China has resumed its sovereignty over Hong Kong in 1July 1997.the research include personal interview,group discussion and literature review,in order to give a comparetivly comprenensive analysis of the status quo of Hong Kong Sport.The research shows that after the hand over,Hong Kong sport is still mainly managed by sport organizations.school sports has been greatly improved,community sports develops quickly,elite sport standard has been obvirously raised,the transaction of Hong Kong sport with other contries is increasingly freqent,spaces are still not enough for school sports and sports science curriculum is not paid enough attention.The return to motherland and the applicant of Asian Games in 2006 provide proper opportunity to the development of Hong Kong sport.
