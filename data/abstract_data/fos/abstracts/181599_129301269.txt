A thin predominantly cold-based Late Miocene East Antarctic ice sheet inferred from glaciovolcanic sequences in northern Victoria Land, Antarctica
Glaciovolcanic sequences are widespread in northern Victoria Land and their interpretation enables estimates of past ice thicknesses and other defining parameters of the coeval ice sheet to be made with considerable certainty. The terrestrial sequences are mainly Late Miocene in age (c. 12–5 Ma) and conform to five main types: aa lava-fed deltas, volcanic sheet-like sequences, a glaciolacustrine sequence, scoria cones and tuff cones.
