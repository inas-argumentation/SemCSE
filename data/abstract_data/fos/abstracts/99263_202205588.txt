Microanalysis of Explosive-welded TA2/316L Composite Plate Interface
The microstructure,ingredient and microhardness of explosive-welded 316L/TA2 composite plate interface were studied by using SEM,EDS and microhardness instrument.The results show that the interface exhibits wave shape.There is a melting zone which is about 5μm between 316L and TA2 join interface,and a whirlpool molten block in the wave tail,there is microcrack and blowhole inside of the molten piece;the matrix structure the nearby interface generates violent plastic deformation;one side of titanium has adiabatic shear band;the atom nearby the interface has diffusion phenomenon.The microhardness of interface region is improved notably.
