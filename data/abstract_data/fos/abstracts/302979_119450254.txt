Josephson Effect In The Cuprates: Microscopic Implications
In the tunnel limit, the current-phase relation of Josephson junctions can be expanded as I (o) = I 1 sin o +I 2 sin 2o. Standard BCS theory predicts that I 1 R N ~ Δ/e and I 2/I1 ~ D, where R N is the resistance of the junction in the normal state, Δ is the superconducting gap, and D ≪ 1 is the junction transparency. In the cuprates, the experimental value of I 1 R N (I 2/I 1) is much smaller (larger) than the BCS prediction. We argue that both peculiarities of the cuprates can be explained by postulating quantum fluctuations of the pairing symmetry.
