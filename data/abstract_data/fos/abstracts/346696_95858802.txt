Physical characterization of lithium phosphoantimonite glassy system
Glasses of different compositions with P2O5 and Sb2O3 as glass formers, Li2O as network modifier and LiCl as dopant were prepared. The effect of another glass forming oxide on the binary system of Li2OP2O5 is clearly seen by the increase in bulk density, refractive index, and oxygen packing density. The increase in molar refraction is also observed due to the composition effect.
