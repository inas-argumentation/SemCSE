Potassium Uptake Difference of Different Sugarcane Varieties
The potassium(K)uptake characteristics and K utilization efficiency of different sugarcane varieties under K levels of 0.02mmol/L and 2mmol/L were studied in greenhouse sand culture experiment.The result showed that different K levels had significance on K uptake of different genotypes of sugarcane.K application could promote root and shoot growth,reduce root/shoot ratio and increase photosynthetic rate,which is in favor of biomass accumulation;Low-potassium stress could promote root growth of YT159,which increased the absorption area and the effectiveness of potassium in rhizosphere.With the increased levels of K,root growth of YT2 and ROC22 grew,while root growth of YT159 was inhibited.Biomass,root morphology index and photosynthetic rate of YT159 were the highest compared to YT2 and ROC22 under the two levels of K,meaning YT159 was the K efficient variety.From low to high K treatments,YT159 had a small increase in biomass and its well-developed root system enabled it to a normal growth.Therefore,YT159 was also the low K tolerant variety.
