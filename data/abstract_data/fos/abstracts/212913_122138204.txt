Exploring the limits of superlattice miniband engineering using inverse scattering
We show how inverse scattering theory can be used to design superlattice structures with specified energy versus crystal momentum dispersion relations. We illustrate the method by calculating the superlattice unit cell required to maximize the curvature of the lowest miniband near the centre of the minizone. This type of structure should have a high electron drift velocity and might therefore be useful for high frequency applications.
