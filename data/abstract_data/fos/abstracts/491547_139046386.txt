Mechanical Properties of Ti-6Al-4V Alloy Based on BP Neural Network Under Thermal Cycling
The tensile strength and tensile ductility of Ti-6Al-4V alloy were measured under thermal cycling temperatures.The results show that the tensile strength of the alloy is significantly influenced by thermal cycling temperature,but not as well as the tensile ductility.Using BP neural network theory and method,a BP neural network model on the mechanical properties of the alloy was established under different thermal cycling temperatures.The calculation results show that the prediction error is within 5% with high accuracy and the method can be used to research on Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
