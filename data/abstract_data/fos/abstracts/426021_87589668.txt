Primary studies on bovine sperm proteome by two-dimensional gel electrophoresis and mass spectrometry
In order to lay a technological foundation for studying physiological function of spermatozoa,proteomics research of bovine spermatozoa with two-dimensional gel electrophesis and MALDI-TOF-MS were established.The heat TRIzol method and two-dimensional gel electrophoresis(2-DE) were used to separate the total proteins of bovine X and Y spermatozoa,ImageMaster 6.0 software was applied to analyze 2-DE images,and proteins were identified by both MALDI-TOF-MS and 2-DE.As a result,16 proteins in the spermatozoa were identified.So it provided a basis for detecting and analyzing the differential proteins of the X and Y bovine spermatozoa.
