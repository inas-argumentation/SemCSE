Antioxidant activity of the roots of Siraitia Grosvenorii
Antioxidant capacities of 4 solvent extracts in Siraitia Grosvenorii roots extracts were determined by the formation of DPPH,ABTS and FRAP methods.It was found that they also exhibited scavenging activities on the three radicals,and the chloroform extract and methanol extract had significant antioxidant activity among the four extracts.The results of the antioxidant effect of S.Grosvenorii roots provide a scientific basis to the research of active constituents and the further exploitation of S.Grosvenorii.
