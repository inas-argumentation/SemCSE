A Pilot Study of Nitrogen and Phosphorus Removal for a Wastewater Reclamation Process
A new wastewater treatment process——nitrogen and phosphorus removal(NPR)——is summarized and a pilot experiment has been conducted to study the new process on the basis of the conventional activated sludge and bio-film process.The study shows the effluent can meet the standard for reclaimed wastewater and could be used as irrigation water and be reused in some industrial sectors.
