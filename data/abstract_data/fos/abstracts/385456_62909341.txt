Formal Verification of SpaceWire Decoding Circuit in HOL4
SpaceWire is a high-speed communication bus protocol in harsh environment,and the decoding circuit is the key circuit of its receiver.There is important and practical significance for formal verification of the circuit.Theorem proving,one method of formal verification,models the circuit which is to be analysed and combine theorem prover to verify and reason the properties of the model strictly.Based on the higher-order logic theorem prover HOL4,this paper applied theorem proving to verify the SpaceWire decoding circuit.Firstly,the paper extracted the relevant properties by SpaceWire standards and used higher-order logic language to model them.Then analyse the VHDL design codes of decoding circuit and model them logically by the relevant predicates according to the realized function of the codes.Finally verify that the model of the circuit design can satisfy the properties in HOL4.This paper also gave the method of formal modeling and verification.
