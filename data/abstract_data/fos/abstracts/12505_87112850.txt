Effect of dextran molecular weight on its antioxidant activity by chemiluminescence
Several dextrans with different molecular weight was obtained based on fractional membrane filtration and other measures.The free radical scavenging abilities of dextrans and glucose towards reactive oxygen species(ROS) was determined by chemiluminescence technique.The result shows that, the scavenging reaction kinetics of dextrans varied with molecular weight evidently in removing hydrogen peroxide(H2O2) and hydroxyl radical(·OH), where sample"Dex-13"and glucose exhibit the strongest and weakest antioxidant activity respectively.However, in the superoxide radical anion(O2-·) assay, molecular weight affect uncons-picuously on scavenging rate of dextrans, which show lower antioxidant activity than glucose in this assay.
