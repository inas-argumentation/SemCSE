ARM Based Drunk Driver Identification with Tracking System
1,2 Department of ENTC, SSGMCE Shegaon Abstract— The most of the accidents in every country are caused by the drunken driving. But still in many places the rules are being violated, hence to avoid these situations we need a more efficient system which will be firstly able to verify that whether the drive is in intoxicated situation or not. In this paper we are introducing a method that when the drunk driver is found then the vehicle ignition function should get disabled and a massage in the form of vehicle location will be sent to the driver’s relative whose number is pre-defined in the system. Keywords— Alcohol detection system, Vehicle controlling system, ARM Controller, GPS, GSM.
