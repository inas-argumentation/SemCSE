Skyrmion dynamics in a chiral magnet driven by periodically
varying spin currents*
In this work, we investigated the spin dynamics in a slab of chiral magnets induced by an
alternating (ac) spin current. Periodic trajectories of the skyrmion in real space are
discovered under the ac current as a result of the Magnus and viscous forces, which
originate from the Gilbert damping, the spin transfer torque, and the β-nonadiabatic torque
effects. The results are obtained by numerically solving the Landau-Lifshitz-Gilbert
equation and can be explained by the Thiele equation characterizing the skyrmion core
motion.
