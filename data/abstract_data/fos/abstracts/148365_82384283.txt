Selection of substrate for cultivation of Begonia semperflorens Link et Otto cv.Cocktail series
In order to select the optimum planting substrate,four types of substrates(including M1(soil),M2(peat∶perlite 2∶1),M3(pottery grain),and M4(vermiculite∶slow-release fertilizer 25∶1) were used in this study.The growth indices(including plant height,crown diameter,biomass,root length etc.),flower trait index(including flower number) and contents of C,H,N and S were investigated for determination of the optimum substrate for Begonia semperflorens Link et Otto cv.Cocktail series,and the results are as follows:①Judging from the observation of growth stage of vegetation and reproduction,M2 was the best one for the increase of height,crown diameter,and floret number.②The measurement of wet and dry above and below-ground weight of Begonia semperflorens and root length showed that M2 was the best to increase the above-and below-ground biomass and root length.③Begonia semperflorens growing in M2 showed higher contents of C and S than in M1.The medium M2 is the optimum material for soilless culture of Begonia semperflorens from the point of appreciating characters and nutrient feature.
