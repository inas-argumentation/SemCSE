Contemporary Culture Development of China in the Globalization Progress
In the multi-national circumstances,cultural clash and harmonious also can't be avoided. how to handle the relation of each race of world culture will affect the cultural destiny of every country.From this angle of view of the cultural clash and harmon,this text has a foothold to turn the progress in the world,and analyzes the contemporary development of Chinese traditional culture.We debate that how the Chinese traditional culture develops from the aspects of conflict and merger.
