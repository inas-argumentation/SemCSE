Green Casting Preferred Way to Short-flow Duplex Melting Process
The importance of blast and medium-frequency induction furnace duplex short-cut melting process for green casting was described. As an example of producting cast iron in machine tool, the the process of research and production of blast and medium-frequency induction furnace duplex short-cut melting was introduced. The results show that the data of blast furnace and medium frequency furnace duplex melting process is the preferred way for green casting from the energy-saving emission reduction to achieve economic benefit analysis.
