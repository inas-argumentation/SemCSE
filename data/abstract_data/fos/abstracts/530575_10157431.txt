Coating Conditions of SCO Single Buffer Layer via Slot-Die Technique
A Sm0.2Ce0.8O1.9-x (SCO) single buffer layer was prepared by a polymer-assisted chemical solution deposition method and a slot-die coating technique for YBCO coated conductors. The smooth, uniform, and crack-free SCO buffer layer with sharp (200) c-axis orientation and high texture has been obtained by controlling the coating conditions, i.e., the viscosity of solution, the moving speed of tape, and the release rate of solution.
