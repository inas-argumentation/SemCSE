Influence of nonstoichiometry on the oxygen self-diffusion in Co1$minus;xO single crystals
Abstract 18 O self-diffusion has been investigated as a function of oxygen partial pressure and temperature in Co 1− x O single crystals. The results show that the dominating defects of the oxygen sublattice are doubly charged oxygen vacancies V o at low oxygen partial pressures and singly-charged oxygen interstitials O' i at high oxygen partial pressures. The contribution of V . o and O″ i has also been estimated.
