Research on Ion Plating Surface Modification of Dental Prosthetic Material
TiN coating was formed on titanium alloy (Ti6Al4V) surface by employing multi-arc ion plating, which is used as dental prosthetic material. The structure and anti-bacterial adhesion property of the coating and substrate was investigated and analyzed respectively, The results show that TiN coating was built up mainly by pure TiN phase with 111 preferred orientation, compared with the untreated sample, TiN coating can reduce the bacterial adhesion on titanium alloy surface significantly.
