MEASUREMENT AND CALCULATION OF HYDRATE FORMATION CONDITIONS FOR GAS MIXTURES CONTAINING HYDROGEN
Hydrate formation conditions of twelve synthetic hydrogen + hydrocarbon gas mixtures (H_2 + CH_4, H_2 + C_3H_8, H_2 + CH_4 + C_3H_8, H_2 + CH_4 + C_2H_6 + C_3H_8) were measured in a sapphire cell using the "pressure search" method. The experimental temperature range was 274―280 K, and the pressure range was 0.65―7.0 MPa. The hydrogen content in the gas mixtures varied from 22% to 92%(mol).By using PR equation of state and suitable mixing rule, the Chen-Guo's hydrate model was extended to calculate the hydrate formation conditions of the hydrogen-containing systems. Satisfactory agreement between experimental data and calculated results were achieved.
