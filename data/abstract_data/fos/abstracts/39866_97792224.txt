Self-enhancement of A-centre annealing in silicon
Thermal annealing of the Ec - 0.18 eV level in Czochralski-grown silicon crystals irradiated by 60Co gamma rays has been investigated using Hall effect measurements. An anomalous annealing kinetics has been found, in which the centre disappearance probability is increased with isothermal annealing time. A tentative kinetic model of the process is proposed.
