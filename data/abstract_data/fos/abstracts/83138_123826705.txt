Abelsche und Taubersche Sätze für Laguerre-Reihen
In this note we will prove Abelian and Tauberian theorems for Laguerre expansions and derive relations between the asymptotic behavior of a function and the asymptotic behavior of the coefficients of its Laguerre series. To this end we will see that Laguerre series are asymptotically of Hankel-transform type. General kemel results for this transformation are well known.
