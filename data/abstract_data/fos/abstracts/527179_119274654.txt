Magnetic moments of (JP = 3/2+) heavy baryons using effective mass and screened charge scheme
Magnetic moments of heavy charmed baryons with JP = 3/2+ are predicted employing the concept of effective quark mass and screened charge of quark. We also extend our scheme to predict the 3/2+$ \rightarrow$ 1/2+ transition magnetic moments. A comparison of our results with the predictions obtained in recent models is presented.
