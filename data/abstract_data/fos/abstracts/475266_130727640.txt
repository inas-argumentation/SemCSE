Effect of Tillage Pattern on Root Growth of Different Rice Cultivar During Seedling Standing Period
The Effect of tillage pattern on root growth of different rice cultivar during seedling standing period was studied.The results showed that the no-tillage and cast transplanting rice root number,whiter root number,root activity and SOD activity increased significantly or greatly significantly during late seedling standing period.However there were no significant differences in the rice ratio of root to shoot dry weight,shoot dry weight and biomass per plant,root length per plant and root hair zone length,root primary branches,root second branches compared with conventional tillage and cast transplanting rice.Liangyou Peijiu had positive effect on root growth and activity,the differences were significant compared with Jinyou253.
