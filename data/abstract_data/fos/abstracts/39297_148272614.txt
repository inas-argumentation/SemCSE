On the cultivation of native-oriented personnel in West
The situation of education in Western part of China, especially the current training mode of universities and colleges, can hardly meet the need of personnel according to the Western part of China and the brain drain of the western region is extremely serious. So, it is necessary to optimize the structure of education in the region, adjust the curricula of the universities and colleges, cultivate native-oriented personnel, make the various measures of goverment, and create the social environment beneficial to the personnel to be localized.
