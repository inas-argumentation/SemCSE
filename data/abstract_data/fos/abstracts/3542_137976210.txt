Effect of Solution,Deformation and Aging on Properties of Cu-0.65Cr-0.35Zr Alloy
The effects of solution,cold deformation and aging parameters on the mechanical properties of Cu-0.65Cr-0.35Zr alloy were analyzed.The results indicate that the relatively higher electrical conductivity and hardness is achieved after solution,cold deformation and aging processes.The electrical conductivity and hardness is higher respectively than 75% IACS and 147 HB after solution at 1000 ℃ for 1 h,60% cold deformation and aging at 500 ℃ for 4 h～6 h;upon aging at 500 ℃ for 2 h,the electrical conductivity reaches about 72%IACS and hardness 163HB.The tensile strength reaches 532 MPa and elongation 9.2%;the tensile strength of 80% deformed alloy reaches 585 MPa,the elongation is about 16.3%.The resistance to softening at elevated temperature is above 500 ℃.
