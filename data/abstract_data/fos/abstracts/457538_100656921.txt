Analysis of Deoxyribonucleic Acid Interstrand Cross-links Induced by Nitrosourea with High Performance Liquid Chromatography-Electrospray Ionization Tandem Mass Spectrometry
DNA interstrand crosslink induced by nitrosoureas was investigated by high performance liquid chromatography-electrospray ion mass spectrometry(HPLC-ESI-MS/MS) ,with the synthesized l-N-(2′-de-oxycytidyl) -2-N-(2′-deoxyguanosyl) ethane(dG-dC) as standard.Calf thymus DNA was treated with 1,3-bis-(2-chloroethyl) -1-nitrosourea(BCNU) .The DNA samples were denatured,enzymatically digested,centrifugal filtered,and then separated on a C 18 reverse column with ammonium acetate aqueous solution and acetonitrile as mobile phase.Samples were ionized by electrospray ionization(ESI) in positive mode.First,qualitative analysis was carried out by MS1and MS2 full-scan.Then the content of dG-dC in DNA samples were quantified by monitoring the ion transition of m/z 521→289 with selecting reaction monitoring mode. This research provided an effective method for the qualitative and quantitative analysis of DNA interstrand crosslink.
