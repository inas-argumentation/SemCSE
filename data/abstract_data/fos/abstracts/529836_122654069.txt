Structure of the nucleon from electromagnetic form factors
.Recent experimental data on the ratio of electric to magnetic elastic form factors are reviewed in light of a model of the nucleon with an intrinsic (quark-like) structure and a meson cloud. The analysis points to the astonishing result that the proton electric form factor vanishes at Q2~ 8 (GeV/c)2 and becomes negative beyond that point. The intrinsic structure is estimated to have a r.m.s. radius of ~ 0.34 fm, much smaller than the proton r.m.s. radius ~ 0.87 fm. The calculations are in perfect agreement with the proton data, but deviate drastically from neutron data at Q2>1 (GeV/c)2. Relativistic invariance is a crucial ingredient responsible for the vanishing of $G_{E_{p}}$. Symmetry, rather than detailed dynamics, appears to be a determining factor in the structure of the nucleon. Scaling appears to occur at much larger values, Q2≥ 30 (GeV/c)2, than previously thought.
