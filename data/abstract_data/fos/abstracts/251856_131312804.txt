Screening of Upland Rice Germplasm with High Soil Phosphorus Efficiency
An experiment was carried out to screen upland rice germplasm with efficient intake of soil phosphorus(P)on the substrate of the typical P deficient soil in Hainan Island.RSW(relative dry weight of shoot),RTW(relative dry weight of tillers),RTN(relative number of tillers)and RPW(relative plant dry weight including shoot and root)were found to be good parameters for screening at the seedling stage,while RTN,RAPN(relative available panicle number),RPR(relative panicle rate),RGSR(relative grain setting ratio)and RGYPP(relative grain yield per plant)were reliable and effective parameters for screening in the course of the whole life cycle of the germplasm.Multi-correlation analysis of these parameters of the upland rice germplasm showed that BXLD and AX128 were soil P efficient upland rice germplasm,while QX128 and RD-2 were sensitive to soil P deficiency.
