On the Forms of Employer's Discriminatory Retaliation and Related Legal Remedies
Employer's discriminatory Retaliation is a kind of independent type of employment discrimination which has unique forms,mainly including unjust dismissal,demotion,lowering labor remuneration and welfare treatment and so on.Although the law of our country has made prohibitive provisions,it lacks regulation system.For lack of remedies,the existing laws cannot provide the ideal remedy means and relief.Employees can not get effective remedies after they are discriminated.To solve this predicament,it is necessary to seek legal remedies,regulating employer's labor practices by laws,protecting the legitimate rights and interests of workers,and regulating labor-capital relations.
