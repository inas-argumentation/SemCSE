Effects on sterilization and sensory quality of cakes by irradiation
In order to extend the shelf-life of cakes, they were irradiated by 60 Co γ-rays at 0, 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 kGy, and the moulds count, aerobic plate count, coliforms, pathogens and sensory characteristics were investigated during different storage times of 0, 30, 60, 90 d, respectively. These results showed that the survival of microbe decreased with increasing irradiation doses. The 100% mortality of moulds and pathogens were achieved after irradiated at 1 kGy, and aerobic plate count was greatly reduced to 5.6%. The moulds were inhabited for more than 90 d by irradiation at 1 kGy at room temperature. Irradiation would not change the sensory quality of cakes at 1~3 kGy. Therefore, 60 Co γ-rays irradiation was an effective sterilization method on cakes.
