Analysis on Fabrication Process of K_xNa_(1-x)NbO_3 Lead-free Piezoelectric Ceramic
(K,Na)NO3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramics(KNN) were fabricated by solid-state synthesis and sintering at standard pressure.The actual densities of the resultant piezoelectric ceramics were measured by Archimedes' principal,the phase structure was analyzed by XRD,the surface morphologies were observed by SEM.The effects of composition,temperature,heating time on the sintering characteristics were analyzed.The results show that the as-prepared KNN is solid solution with perovskite crystal structure,the replacement of K with Na in cubic NaNbO3 leads to the lattice distortion and the increase of lattice constants resulting in a inclined density and enlarged grain size;the prolonging sintering at elevated temperature within a suitable range facilitates the crystal growth;overheating at high temperature speeds up the evaporation of Na,K and lessens the ceramic densification.
