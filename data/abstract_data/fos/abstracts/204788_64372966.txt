High-speed multi-channel real time data collection system based on FPGA in agricultural by-products processing
A high-speed multi-channel data collection system based on FPGA was introduced. The main components of the system and the implementing method of FPGA were described. EP2C5T144 was used for the hardware of system to carry out the function of the control, multiplexer data conversion and memory of the high accurate A/D converter TLC549. VHDL hardware description language was used for software of FPGA. The system has the characteristics of the high speed, high reliability, simple construction and tiny volume. It can be used for the data collection of quality monitoring in agricultural product and by-product processing, and promote quick, intelligent, dynamic and portable detection. It can provide technical support for the rapid detection of the harmful residue in the process of safe production and consumption of the agricultural product and byproduct.
