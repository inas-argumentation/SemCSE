Effect of Several Plant Volatiles on Antenna Electrophsiological Activity of Brontispa Longissima (Gestro)
The antenna electrophsiological activity of Brontispa longissima treated with 19 plant volatiles was studied by using the electroantennogram (EAG) technique. The results showed that all these plant volatile compounds stimulated B. longissima's EAG response and that the female and male adults gave similar EAG response. The former 6 compounds that induced highest EAG value were geraniol, 1-hexanal, Z-2-hexen-1-ol, 1-heptanal, 1-hexanol and 1-octen-3-ol. The male had higher EAG response to 1-hexanol, E-2-hexenal and 1-pentanal than the female but lower EAG response to the latter 16 compounds. However, only the EAG response to 1-hexanal was found to produce obvious difference between the male and female adults. The relationship of these results with the host selection of Brontispa longissima was discussed.
