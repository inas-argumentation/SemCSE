The role of high-pressure cooking method in the extraction of corn germ protein
This experiment choose oil-extraction corn germ meal as raw materials. After high pressure steam treatment to the germ meal, then using cellulase enzymes to hydrolysis the materials. By measuring the sugar content of the liquid, cellulose hydrolysis rate of the residue and the protein content of final product, to investigate the effect of high pressure cooking method in protein extraction. The results showed that after treatment by high pressure steaming, the effect of extraction of protein digestion is rather to the immersion 48 h. After the treatment, both content of protein can reach 42%. And the high-pressure steam treatment reduced the processing time significantly, better than the soaking treatment of raw materials.
