Design and Implementation of the Online Virtual Bookshelf System of the Library
Analyzes the problems of low recall and low precision because of reader's own ability in the process of use the library,designs and realizes the online virtual bookshelf system.The virtual bookshelf system finds similar collection of books by the reader query results.According to the order of the physical shelves,and get the book's cover by the Douban API calls,this can give a way to scroll through more related books that reader interested in.Taking the case study of the SulcmisIII's OPAC,gives an integration and implementation case of the system and OPAC retrieval system.
