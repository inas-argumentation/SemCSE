Responsible for country: the mission embodied in university
In modern times,the property and fade of the university is closely related with the fate of the nation.The university plays an inevitable important role.The complete mission value is embodied in emphasizing knowledge finding,broadcast,and also the combination and unification of social progress and innovation.This is decided by the primary feature of the university.Therefore,the university should put more energy on the responsibility of subject building and scientific research,on the nurturing of the talents,on the moral self-regulation and cultural pioneer.
