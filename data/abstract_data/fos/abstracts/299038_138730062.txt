Study on High Temperature Oxidation Properties of Aluminized Coating on K4104 Superalloy
The aluminized coating on the surface of K4104 superalloy were prepared by using slurry process.The isothermal oxidation bahavior of these coating was investigated at 1000℃ in air for 200h.The surface morphology of the aluminized coating were studied by using SiRion SEM.The results indicate that the aluminized coating on the surface of K4104 superalloy has transformed into compact oxidized film α-Al2O3 in the process of high temperature oxidation and aluminized coating has excellent high temperature oxidation resistance.
