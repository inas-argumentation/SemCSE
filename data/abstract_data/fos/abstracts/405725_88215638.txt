The emulsibility of octenyl succinic starch ester and the effect of emulsifying conditions
The effect of emulsifying conditions on the emulsifying properties is studied in this paper,the viscosity of emulsion,the medium(including NaCl),the pH of medium,the temperature and so on.The result shows that,the emulsion has better stabilization when the viscosity of the emulsion,the pH of medium and the quantity of NaCl rises while the temperature drops.It will offer the basic theory and conduct for starch industry.
