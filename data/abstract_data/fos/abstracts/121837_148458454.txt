On the correlation of social atmosphere with contemporary university students' values
Forming and change of the contemporary university students' values is under the influence of various factors,among which social atmosphere claims an important place.Based on the sampled investigation and empirical study of university students in Guizhou,the authors found the correlation between the social atmosphere and the forming and change of contemporary university students' values is tight.Therefore,we must build up healthy social atmosphere to guide university students to establish sound values.
