Confidence Intervals on Total Variance in a Regression Model with an Unbalanced Onefold Nested Error Structure
Abstract In applications using a linear regression model with nested error structure, interest often focuses on inferences concerning total variance of the response variable. This article considers alternative methods for constructing confidence intervals on the total variation and compares them in a simulation study. We also examine the ability of PROC MIXED of Statistical Analysis Systems (SAS) to generate confidence intervals that maintain the stated confidence coefficient. A numerical example is provided to demonstrate computation of the methods.
