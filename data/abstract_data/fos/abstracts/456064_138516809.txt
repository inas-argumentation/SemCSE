Effect of Welding Methods on Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Welded Joint of 2205 Duplex Stainless Steel
2205 duplex stainless steel tube was welded by metal arc welding(SMAW) and gas tungsten arc welding(GTAW).The microstructure and mechamcal properties of the welded joint were analyzed.The results show that austenite phase in GTAW welded joint is more than that in SMAW welded joint.The comprehensive mechanical properties of GTAW joint are better than that of SMAW joint
