On Degenerating of the Representative of People' s Interest——The lessons and enlightenments given by the Communist Party of the Soviet Union in the respect of representing the most broad people’s interest
The cause of falling from power of CPSU is that CPSU,the representative of the people's interest,has become a group of bigwigs,who seek personal interest at the great expense of the broad people’ s interest.From the whole course of obtaining interest,we can see that in order to really represent most broad people’ s interest,the communist party must set up the system of interest's expression,the system of political participation and the system of supervision and restriction.Essentially,only reforming the political system of highly centralized power and changing the highly centralized leading of the party can the communist party become the faithful representative of the people’ s interest.
