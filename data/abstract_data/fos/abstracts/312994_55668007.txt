Effect of different beta-glucan preparation pretreatments on fortified bread quality
One of the substances that could enhance bakery products in their functionality is beta-glucan. This is a polysaccharide that can be found in cereals, like oats and barley (Wang et al., 2016). Beta-glucan’s health benefits were proved by the European Food Safety Authority (2011) with claims that beta-glucan is recognized as an agent that could lower the blood cholesterol and postprandial glucose response.
