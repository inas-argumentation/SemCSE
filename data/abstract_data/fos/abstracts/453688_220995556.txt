Analysis of the classification of sport disciplines and suggestions for adjustment.
The authors analyzed and adjusted 4 level 2 disciplines under sport sciences,and put forward the follow-ing opinions: "Sport Humanistic Sociology" and "Sports Human Body" are two important foundational disciplines in the sport science system;the contents of "Physical Education and Sports Training" and "Traditional National Sports" need to be regrouped;"Physical Education","Competitive Sports" and "Leisure Physical Exercises" will be developed as the sport disciplinary system.
