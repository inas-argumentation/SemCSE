STEREOCHEMISTRY OF ELIMINATION REACTIONS OF HALOHYDRIN DERIVATIVE AND vic-DIBROMIDE BY BUTYLLITHIUM
Elimination of 1-bromo-2-methoxy-1,2-diphenylethane with n-butyllithium yielded stilbene in completely stereospecific cis manner in non-polar solvents. A solvent-dependence of the stereochemistry of the elimination reaction was observed.
