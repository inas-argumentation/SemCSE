Low Cost Exhale Breath Analyzer for Asthma Detection
1, 2, 3 8 th semester, Department of Information Science and Engineering, NIE Mysore-570008, Karnataka 4 Asst. Prof., Department of Information Science and Engineering, NIE Mysore-570008, Karnataka Abstract: Asthma is a respiratory related disease which one out of five people suffer from. Asthma can be caused due to genetic or environmental conditions. There are many devices being used in detecting asthma. Since, these are very complex and expensive; we propose a system of nano biosensors that diagnose asthma using the concept of data mining in the most cost efficient ways. We fabricate a low cost bio-sensor using nano-materials which detects the presence of exhaled gases (nitrogen oxide, carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, acetone, ammonia) in the ppb (parts per billion) level. Sensor is prepared using ITO (Indium Tin Oxide) substrate spin coated with ZnO (Zinc Oxide) layer prepared by sol-gel technique and thermal evaporation. Pt (Platinum) electrodes over the ZnO layer measure the change in potential/resistance occurred by different ppb (parts per billion) concentrations of exhaled gases. This is followed by transferring the data to a system for data mining and analysis using WEKA software. By analysis we find the ‘safe’, ‘moderate’ and ‘critical’ values of gases’ concentrations for which the person is considered to be in riskless, average and dangerous stages of asthma respectively. Subsequent remedial actions can be taken thereafter.
