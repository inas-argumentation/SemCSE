Legal Considerations on the Safety Supervision of Genetically Modified Organisms in India
The rapid development of transgenic technology makes India become the world's second largest cotton producer and exporter,and produces huge economic,social and ecological benefits.And India's supervision over the safety of genetically modified organisms becomes increasingly rigorous and scientific.The country has formulated a legal supervision framework made up of basic regulations,technical guidelines and the protection of legislation,which form a special framework in which three layers of management systems act in close coordination.Complete approving system,specialized consulting departments and highly effective supervising agencies are the embodiments of the functions of India's safety supervision departments to genetically modified organisms,which we believe will benefit for the function building of related departments in China.
