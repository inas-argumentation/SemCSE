Thought on the Reform of the Adult Higher Vocational Education
The third plenary session of 16th CPC Central Committee presents the requirement of building lifelong education system and boosting the people's all-round development. The national vocational education will face both opportunity and challenge. Adult higher vocational education must deepen the reform, cultivate new education thought, unite the cognition, study the reform orientation and reform goal of the adult higher vocational education, explore several relationships such as theoretic teaching and practical teaching, science spirit and cultural etc.
