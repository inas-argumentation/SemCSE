Should Women Support the Privatization of Social Security
years ago very few people took proposals to privatize social security seriously. Today such proposals are being given very serious consideration. Attention to this issue has increased with the recent release of three alternative proposals for reform by the Advisory Council on Social Security, two of which call for partial privatization.1 In view of this, it is time to remedy the near silence with respect to the implications for women. Many privatization proposals, including those recently made by the Advisory Council, would have bad economic consequences for women. The Maintenance of Benefits plan, the least controversial of the three proposals by the Advisory Council, suggests that 40 percent of the Social Security Trust Fund be invested in the stock market.
