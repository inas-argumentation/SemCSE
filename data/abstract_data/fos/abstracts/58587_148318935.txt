On the transformation of nationwide Traditional Minority National Games into "Traditional Chinese National Games"
From the perspective of carrying forward traditional Chinese national sports as well as of Traditional Minority National Games(referred to as TMNG for short) as a sort of attachment to ideology,the author dissected irrational things in the form of TMNG:the author first pointed out that TNNG is not a complete whole and transcultural comparison valid competition from the perspective of carrying forward traditional national sports,an-thropology and macroscopic background of traditional Chinese national sports,then pointed out the potential possi-bility of TMNG in maintaining national unity and "state recognition" from the perspective of the generation of de-velopment of a national state and Chinese national form,and lastly put forward some preliminary ideas about con-structing "Traditional Chinese National Games".
