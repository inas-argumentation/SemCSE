The Activities of State Agencies in Providing Information to Prospective Students.
Access to educational opportunities depends, in part, on students having adequate information about the alternatives that are available to them. State postsecondary agencies are attempting to improve the availability of this information to students by assuming an increasing role in coordinating and disseminating information on educational alternatives within their state. However, the extent and comprehensiveness of these information activities appear to vary widely across states. Since this information dissemination role is a recent activity for most state agencies, these agencies are largely unaware of what other states are doing. This article reports a national study that surveyed the extent and comprehensiveness of publications provided by state agencies to inform prospective students about higher education opportunities within each state. In particular, the study investigated three questions. To what extent do state postsecondary education agencies provide prospective students with information on the higher educational opportunities available to them within each state? What kinds of information are provided? How comprehensive is it in areas students say are important?
