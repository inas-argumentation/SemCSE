Research in Synchronization of Frequency Hopping Satellite Communication
Synchronization is the key technology of FH communication.Consider the account of need for anti-jamming,introduce frequeney-hopping into Satelite communication.Studied the characteristics of FH satalite communication synchronization,According to the complexity of equipment and low-capture time synchronization requirements,synchronization header + TOD synchronization method will be introduced to the frequency hopping satellite communication systems,studied the synchronous serial format,synchronization header structure,frequency synchronization algorithm,design the initial synchronization program.
