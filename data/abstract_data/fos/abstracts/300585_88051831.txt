Expression of the α-subunit of human acetylcholine receptor in E.coli DH5α
Objective Through the co expression of acetylcholine receptor (AchR) α subunit and chaperonin to raise the amount of soluble protein and decrease inclusion body. Methods Plasmids pPR506 containing the gene of α subunit domain of AchR and pGE60 containing the gene of chaperonin GroESL were co transferred in E.coli DH5α and co expressed through induction of IPTG. Results Compared the co expression product with that of the engineering bacteria containing pPR506 only,the former was more in the form of non inclusion.Because the chaperonin has a function of refolding recombinant protein.After separation and purification of the expression products by FPLC,the electrophoric patterns of the various eluted peaks are not different with the culture products on SDS PAGE. Conclusion It is pointed out that chaperonin facilitates the correct refolding of recombinant protein and in turn increases the soluble protein.
