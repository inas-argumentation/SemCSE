Determination of Polyamines by High Performance Liquid Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry with Fluorescence and Atmospheric Pressure Chemical Ionization Detection
A simple and highly sensitive method was developed based on the derivatization of polyamines utilizing1,2-benzo-3,4-dihydrocarbazole-9-isopropyl chloroformate(BCIC) as the novel pre-column derivatization reagent followed by high performance liquid chromatography with fluorescence detection and online mass spectrometric identification.Optimum derivatization was obtained by the reaction of polyamines with BCIC at 40 ℃ for 10 min in the presence of sodium borate(pH 9.0) in acetonitrile solvent.Derivatives were sufficiently stable to be efficiently analyzed by high performance chromatography without pretreatment.On a reversed-phase XDB-C8 column,five kinds of polyamines derivatives were separated in conjunction with a gradient elution with a good baseline resolution.The precise determination of polyamines from urine sample was obtained by post-column online mass spectrometric identification with atmospheric pressure chemical ionization source under positive mode detection.The established method exhibited excellent reproducibility and recovery.Linear responses were observed with the correlation coefficients of 0.9996.The detection limits(at signal-to-noise of 3∶1) were 5.3～9.9 fmol.
