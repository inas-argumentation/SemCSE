Development of Long-Length BMO-Doped REBCO Coated Conductors by Hot-Wall PLD Process
Fujikura Ltd., Chiba, Japan, has developed stable and high-throughput production techniques for REBCO coated conductors (CC) by using large-area ion-beam-assisted-deposition (IBAD), and hot-wall type pulsed-laser-deposition (PLD). Recently, we have been attempting to dope BaMO<sub>3</sub> (BMO, M: Zr or Hf) to REBCO layer in order to enhance in-field critical current density (<inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$J_{{\rm{c}}}$</tex-math> </inline-formula>). In the course of optimizing deposition conditions of PLD for BaHfO<sub>3</sub> doped EuBCO films on IBAD templates, “high <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$J_{{\rm{c}}}$</tex-math></inline-formula> condition” and “high growth rate condition” were found. For samples prepared under these conditions, the magnetic field angle dependence of <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$I_{{\rm{c}}}$</tex-math> </inline-formula> and REBCO thickness dependence were investigated. Finally, we succeeded in fabricating a 300-m-long CC with uniform <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$I_{{\rm{c}}}$</tex-math></inline-formula> distribution at the high-growth-rate condition. The longitudinal <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$I_{{\rm{c}}}$</tex-math> </inline-formula> homogeneity (defined as standard deviation divided by average) at 77 K evaluated by magnetization method was 2.6%, which is comparable to nondoped conductors. Transport in-field <italic>I</italic><sub>c</sub> for the both end points were 1756 and 1786 A/cm at 30 K, 2 T, respectively.
