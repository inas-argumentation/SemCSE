RWTCA:Building Certification Authority Based on Real World Trust Relationships
Public key infrastructure (PKI) provides a security guarantee for the rapid growing of network applications. However,in traditional PKI systems,for some reasons as the only trusted party CA verifies the entire end-entities' identity,it may issue certificates with spurious identity. By modeling the trust formula of certificate contents,authors present a new CA system based on RSA algorithm with non-centralized structure:RWTCA. The new system,based on real world trust relationship and built on some authorities of certificate contents,can issue standard X.509 certificates and has good reliability and flexibility.
