Study on Adhesiveness Between Fe/Cr_3C_2 Composite Coating and 16Mn Steel Substrate
Taking 16Mn steel as substrate material,Fe/Cr3C2 composite coating was sprayed on its surface,and aluminum was sprayed on the top of the composite coating by electric arc.The heat treatment of coating was carried out at 850℃ with different time.The metallurgical structure,hardness,adhesion of the coatings were analysed and researched.The results show that the adhesiveness between Fe/Cr3C2 composite coating and 16Mn steel with holding time of 32h and 48h at 850℃ are better.
