Removal of Ammonia Nitrogen by Zeolite Enhanced SBR
A new process of zeolite enhanced SBR for microbial denitrogenation was presented, and results indicated that zeolite could enhance the ability of microbial denitrogenation of SBR process, and enhancement was more effective at low water temperature. The efficiency of NH_ 3-N removal was assessed with different alkalinity and influent NH_ 3-N concentration.
