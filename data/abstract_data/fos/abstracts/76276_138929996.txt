Study on Impact Abrasion Performance of High Strength Steel
The impact abrasion performance of martensite steel was studied.The results show that carbon content has a great influence on abradability.When the carbon content is 0.30%,the steel shows a wonderful wearing resistance of shock resistance.With ballistic work 1J,wearing 1h,the wear mass loss is 48.1 mg,but that of 45 steel which rubs against the tested material reaches 79.5mg.In addition,increasing the ballistic work can aggravate the fatigue wear.The wear mass loss of tested material with 0.30%C is lower than other tested steels under different ballistic work,so the martensite steel with 0.30%C has a good abradability.
