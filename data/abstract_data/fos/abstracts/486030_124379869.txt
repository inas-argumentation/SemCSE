Nominal level variable and ordinal level variable of regression analysis in the research of physical sociology
regression analysis is the one of the most important methods which analyze many factors possessed causation in the sociology field.Few people apply the method to the practice of physical sociology.It is meaningful to study and master the method which can improve the level of application and research.This article will introduce the measurement of the social variables,the transform of nominal level variable and ordinal level variable and the regression analysis of nominal level variable and ordinal level variable.The purpose of the article is to guide the application of the regression analysis.
