Regularity of the symbolic calculus in Besov algebras
We consider Besov and Lizorkin–Triebel algebras, that is, the real-valued function spaces B p,q(R )∩L∞(R ) and F s p,q(R )∩L∞(R ) for all s > 0. To each function f : R → R one can associate the composition operator Tf which takes a real-valued function g to the composite function f ◦ g. We give necessary conditions and sufficient conditions on f for the continuity, local Lipschitz continuity, and differentiability of any order of Tf as a map acting in Besov and Lizorkin–Triebel algebras. In some cases, such as for n = 1, such conditions turn out to be necessary and sufficient.
