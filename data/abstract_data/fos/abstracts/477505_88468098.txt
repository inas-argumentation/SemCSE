Pharmacological research on water-soluble protein of Oviductus Ranae
Objective:To study the effect of water-soluble protein of Oviduetus Ranae on anti-fatigue,hypoxia-tolerant and hypolipidemic.Methods:To determine time of swimming with heavy burden,weight change,hypoxia tolerance time under normal pressure.Utilize ELISA to determine the content of serum total cholesterol(TC),an triglyceride(TG) and high density lipoprotein cholesterol(HDL-C).Results:It appeared from the data that water-soluble protein of Oviduetus Ranae could significantly prolong not only the time span of swimming with heavy burden but also the survival time against hypoxia under normal pressure(p0.05).Simultaneously they could reduce the content of serum total cholesterol and triglyceride of rats.Conclusion:Water-soluble protein of Oviduetus Ranae possess hypoxia-tolerant and hypolipidemic effect.
