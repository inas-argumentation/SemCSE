THE CONSTRUCTION STRATEGY OF GREEN CAMPUSTAKING THE CAMPUS CONSTRUCTION OF SHENYANG JIANZHU UNIVERSITY FOR EXAMPLE
A green campus construction should focus on an ecological campus environment construction and the humanities environment construction.The campus environment construction mainly includes the ecological planning,the design of ecological architectures and the design of ecological landscape.The campus humanities environment construction mainly includes the construction of humanities environment and the extension of the concept of environmental protection.This article will elaborate the construction strategy of a green campus in two aspects including the construction of the ecological environment construction and the humanities environment construction,and takes the campus construction of Shenyang Jianzhu University for an example to illustrate some valuable construction strategies of the green campus.
