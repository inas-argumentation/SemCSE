Research and Development in Strengthening and Toughening of Magnesium Alloys
The current situation and recent development of the research on the magnesium alloys' strengthening and toughening from semi-solid formation grain refinement strengthening,inoculation grain refinement strengthening,rapid solidification grain refinement strengthening and heat treatment strengthening were introduced in this paper.The processing characteristic and mechanism of these different strengthening and toughening were analyzed emphatically,and the existing problems and future research direction were pointed out.
