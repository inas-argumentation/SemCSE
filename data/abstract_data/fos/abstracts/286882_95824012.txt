Conversion of some substituted 1,2,4-triazole-3-thiones
An N-phenyl substituent and an N-allyl substituent in the 4 position of some substituted 1,2,4-triazole-3-thiones do not affect the course of cyanoethylation and alkylation reactions. Cyanoethylation leads to N-substituted derivatives, whereas alkylation gives S-substituted derivatives.
