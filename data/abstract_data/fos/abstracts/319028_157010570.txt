A Review of the Citizenization of China’s Migrant Farmers
Currently,the research on the citizenization of migrant workers has been expanded from macro-level into micro-level,and the research methods have been changed from literature research to empirical research.However,the system and standard of the research need to be improved.Therefore,starting from the current situation of citizenization of migrant workers and its dilemma,influence and methods,this paper will have a general review about the existing research,which will contribute to summarizing achievements,discovering problems,then digging the new subjects worth further discussing.
