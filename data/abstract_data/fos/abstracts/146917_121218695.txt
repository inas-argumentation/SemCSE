Research program for the radioactive beams of the ACCULINNA-2 separator
The ACCULINNA-2 separator is intended to provide beams of exotic nuclei with Z = 1−30, energies of 10–50 MeV/amu, and intensities that allow one to study excitation spectra and decay dynamics of neutron- and proton-drip nuclei. Examples from studies of the 10He and 6Be nuclei are presented.
