Isolation and characterization of high salinity aniline-degradation bacterial strains.
5 high salinity aniline degradation bacterial strains which are capable of degradation of aniline and wastewater were isolated. The strains grew fast,with short delay period and stable period as well as optimal temperature of 25℃ and pH close to neutral. The tolerance to salt was up to 18% and to wastewater up to 30%.
