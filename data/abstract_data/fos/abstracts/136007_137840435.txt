Study of Laser Strengthening on Inner Surface of Concrete Duct
The microstructure,mechanical behavior and the abrasive resistance of the laser claded Mn-Cr-WC-Fe alloy layer was analyzed.The results show that the structure in the areas of the overlap of clad is finer and even than that of the non-overlap area; the hardness of the clad is obviously higher than the base metal; the hardness of the phase-changing zone is also higher;the abrasive resistance of the claded sample is better than that of uncladed sample. Because of the existence of Mn in the clad layer , the occurrence of dislocation strengthening during the abrading process results in work-hardening so as to greatly improve abrasive resistance.
