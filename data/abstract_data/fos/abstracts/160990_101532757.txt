Contrasting analysis on principal ingredients of ginger essential oil from different ways of extraction
The paper introduces the conditions of extraction and the analytic results on principal ingredients of several kinds of essential oil of ginger,which are gained by vapor distillation and by two conditions of super-critical CO2 extraction respectively.It is found that the latter two includes zingiberol series compounds the highestcontent is 26%,which represent the special sapor of ginger well,and that there is relationship between thequantity of zingiberol and time.This is not only one of the characteristics and advantages of supercritical fluidextraction,but convenient to ascertain the operation time.
