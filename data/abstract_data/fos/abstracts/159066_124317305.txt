RESEARCH OF THE REAL TIME PROCESSING TECHNOLOGY FOR HIGH SAMPLING FREQUENCY SHACK-HARTMANN WAVEFRONT SENSOR
High sampling frequency Shack-Hartmann wave front sensor is used to measure the dynamic wavefront error in adaptive optics system. Its output signal must be processed by wave front processor.In this paper, the method of real time processing for high sampling frequency Shack-Hartmann wave frontsensor was discussed. As an example, the results for 2900 Hi Shack-Hartmann wave front sensor was given.
