GROWTH-REGULATED PEPTIDE-α (GRO-α) PRODUCTION BY ORAL KERATINOCYTES: A COMPARISON WITH SKIN KERATINOCYTES
Growth regulated peptide (GRO-α) is chemotactic for neutrophils. It also stimulates keratinocyte proliferation and migration, and angiogenesis in cutaneous wound healing. We compared GRO-α production by normal human skin and oral keratinocytes, and the effects of cytokine stimulation. Resting keratinocytes produced little, if any, GRO-α.
