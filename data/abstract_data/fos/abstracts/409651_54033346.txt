Asymptotic analysis of the one-dimensional Ginzburg-Landau equations near self-duality
It is known that when the Ginzburg-Landau parameter κ = 1/ √ 2 the one-dimensional Ginzburg-Landau equations exhibit self-duality and may be reduced into a pair of first order ODE. The present asymptotic analysis initially focuses on infinite samples of superconductors for which |κ − 1/ √ 2|  1. It is shown that when the value of the applied magnetic field at infinity lies between κ and 1/ √ 2 a superconducting solution exists. It is later shown, for arbitrary values of κ, that no solution, other than the normal state can exist for applied magnetic field values which lie outside the above interval.
