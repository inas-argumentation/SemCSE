The Influence of Development Priority Zones Construction on the Development of New Su-Nan Mode and Its Strategy
In accordance with the requirements of National the eleventh five-year plan and those of the planning of development priority zones demanded by the State Council,the planning of national development priority zones construction has been completed.According to the requirements of the document Technique Regulations of Provincial Level development priority zones issued by the Committee of Development and Reform,each province,city and autonomous region must accomplish the planning of provincial-level development priority zones in 2008.The important decision involving regional economic and social development certainly will have a significant impact on the development of new Su-nan mode.Based on a preliminary analysis of the background of development priority zones construction and the barriers it confronts,the economic and social development of Su-nan district,the problems that development priority zones construction encounters,this essay advances some basic ideas to optimize the development of Su-nan district.
