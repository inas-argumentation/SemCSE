Identification of canine distemper virus receptor in cell lines.
The cell membrane from permissive Vero cells was solubilized by SDS or RIPA lysis buffer, and then subjected to the virus overlay protein blot assay (VOPBA) to identify CDV binding proteins. The results showed that there were two groups of cellular protein that could bind CDV in Vero cells, one with high molecular masses of 127kD, 120kD, 110kD and the other with relatively low molecular masses of 27kD, 30kD. The characteristics of these proteins remain to be studied further.
