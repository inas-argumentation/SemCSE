Analysis of non-structural protein NSP2 gene sequence and protein structure of human group B rotavirus strain WH-1
Objective To clone and sequece the non-structural protein NSP2 gene of human group B rotavirus (GBRV) strain WH-1, compare identity to other GBRV and predict the secondary structure of its mRNA and protein. Methods The NSP2 gene of GBRV strain WH-1 was amplified by reverse trarnscription polymerase chain reaction (RT-PCR) and ligated to plasmid pUCm-T and transformed into E.coli competent cells One-shot Top-10F′, the NSP2 gene of GBRV strain WH-1 on the cloned plasmid pT-NSP2 was sequenced, the identity to other GBRV was analyzed by software GeneBee, the secondary structure of WH-1 NSP2 mRNA was predicted by software RNA structure 3.7 and Rnaviz 2.0, and the protein of NSP2 was analyzed by PredictProtein. Results The nucleotide sequence of WH-1 NSP2 showed high identities (more than 90%) to that of other human GBRV such as ADRV in China, while identities tothat of animal GBRV such as IDIR(Rat) was 79%. The secondary structure of WH-1 NSP2 mRNA was constructed by 28 stem-loop structures. The polypeptide encoded by WH-1 NSP2 contains 301 amino acids. The protein contains two potential N-linked glycosylation sites and some phosphorylation sites. Comparied with other GBRV WH-1 NSP2 demonstrated 98.4% and 97.7% of amino acids similarity with ADRV and CAL-1 respectively, but only 89.0% with IDIR. Conclusin GBRV WH-1 and ADRV might originate from a common ancestral virus.
