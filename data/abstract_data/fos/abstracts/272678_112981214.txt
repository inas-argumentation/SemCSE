Repairing Process for Bending Deformation of Long Side Plate of Aluminum Reduction Cell Shell
Due to high temperature and poor heat dissipation,bending deformation happened on the long side plate of aluminum reduction cell shell and stress concentration occurred in the central region of the long side plate. Through introducing repairing processes for aluminum reduction cell shells,proper repairing process for the cell shells was summarized. The results show that the repairing process can decrease the repairing time,increase productivity and reduce the repairing cost. The conclusion can provide base for repairing such similar parts.
