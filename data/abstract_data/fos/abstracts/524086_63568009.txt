Research on Frequency Synthesizer of High Speed and Wide Band Based on DDS Hybrid PLL
The thesis introduces the development of frequency synthesis.To meet the new requirements of the systems like frequency-hopping communication,a logical scheme of DDS+PLL is advanced,fully considering the indexes such as bandwidth、switch time、spectrum scattering and so on.Then a simulation model is built in Matlab+Simulink.The systematic analysis of the simulation results will give a reference to the later research and design of high-powered synthesizer.
