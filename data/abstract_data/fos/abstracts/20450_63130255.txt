Weighted Cooperative Spectrum Sensing Technology Based on Signal to Noise Ratio
Cognitive radio is considered as an potential technology for efficient utilization of under-utilized spectrum when primary users are absent.However,the detection performance degrades for only one node because of the complexities of the wireless environment.Conventional cooperative method can improve the detection performance in some sense,but,not optimal.Given the reasons mentioned above,we propose a weighed cooperative method based on signal to noise(SNR) to boost the probability of detection,then validate the method in Additive White Gaussian Noise and Rayleigh fading channel individually.Simulation results show that the proposed method improves spectrum sensing performance clearly,and have a good ability to eliminate the effect of fading channels.
