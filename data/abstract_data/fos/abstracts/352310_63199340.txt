Implementation of Software Simulation for SST39VF040 NorFlash Chip
The data structure of the chip was defined according to the hardware structure of SST39VF040 NorFlash chip which is based on the embedded development platform.After analyzed the reading,writing and erasing operation of the chip of work procedures through its work principle and set up a corresponding FSA(Finite State Automata) model.The software simulation of the function of the chip was designed and accomplished utilizing this model and definition of the data structure.Testing result shows that simulation of NorFlash can support Flash Bootloader,at the same time it has achieved significant results in reducing development costs and improving development efficiency.
