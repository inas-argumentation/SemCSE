Research on Medium Access Delay of Ad Hoc Based on Logical Slot Time
Based on the exponential backoff mechanism of DCF and the two -dimensional Markov model,analyzes the interval of random backoff process and logical slot time is presented.Using this method,logical slot time is divided into three parts and the average medium access delay of DCF,under traffic conditions that correspond to the maximum load,is equal to the average length of logical slot time multiply by the average back off number which after successful transmission.The simulation results show that the method is very accurate.
