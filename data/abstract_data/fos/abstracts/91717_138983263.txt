Microstructure of Ni-based Alloy Coating Cladded by Laser with Adding Rare Earth
Ni-Cr-B-Si alloy powder with adding rare earth was cladded by CO2 laser on the surface of 45 steel, then Ni-based coating which is combined in metallurgy with the matrix and has good performance was obtained. Microstructure ,microhardness and phase of the composite coatings were investigated by SEM,XRD and EDS. The results show that microstructure of the coating consists of plane, dendrite and cellular, and the coating has higher hardness. So the laser cladding process of rare earth transmutation can improve the performance of low-carbon steel.
