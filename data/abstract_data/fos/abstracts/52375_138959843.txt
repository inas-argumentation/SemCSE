Study on Microstructure and Properties of Commercial Pure Titanium by Equal Channel Angular Pressing at Room Temperature
A single equal channel angular pressing (ECAP) at room temperature was successfully carried out in commercial pure (CP) Ti at an internal angle Φ=120° and an outer curvature angle Ψ= 20°,then the as-pressed CP Ti was annealed at 200～500℃ for 0.5 h. The microstructure and mechanical properties of CP Ti were studied. The results show that, after a single ECAP, deformation twins are produced , and grains break into segments and forme shear bands; yield strength and microhardness significantly enhance, while maintaining good ductility; microhardness slightly decreases below 300℃annealing , then microhardness decreases sharply above 300℃annealing .
