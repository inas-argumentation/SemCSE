Phosphorus Speciation and Phosphorus Release from Surface Sediments in Nansi Lake
This paper relates to an investigation into phosphorus in the surface sediments which were sampled from different areas of Nansi Lake, a large lake in north China. Speciation study shows iron-bound phosphorus (Fe-P) is the main form accounting for 89%; on the other hand, organic phosphorus less than 0.9%. Phosphorus release experiment was conducted in the lab with simulated condition in terms of temperature, pH and perturbation of lake water.
