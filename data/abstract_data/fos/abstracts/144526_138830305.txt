Effect of Surfactant on Microstructure and Properties of Ni-P-nano-Si_3N_4 Composite Coating
The effects of surfactants on the surface morphology,deposition rate and hardness of AZ31 Ni-P-nano-Si3N4 composite coating were investigated.The results show that uniform structure,highest hardness and deposition rate of the composite coating can be obtained by anionic surfactant.
