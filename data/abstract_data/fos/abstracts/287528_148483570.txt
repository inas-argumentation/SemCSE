To Person's Liberation——Recalling China's educational values in 30 years of reform and opening up
Educational Value Orientation is the soul of educational reform,playing a directional role in development of education.Recalling the development process of China's education in 30 years of reform and opening up,from 1980s' taking economic construction as the dominant,to 1990s'founding people as subject of microscopic education field,and then to the beginning of the 21st century to establish the educational value of caring life and the pursuit of education equity,education reform constantly moved towards the liberation of people.By making scientific development as guidance,which is established by the Party's 17 largest,people's ontological status in education will be further consolidated,and ultimately make education truly become a people's liberation cause.
