Heat Capacity of the ? Phase of He^{4}
The properties of the phase of He/sup 4/ were investigated to determine the lattice heat capacity difference of the bodycentered-cubic and hexagonal-close -packed phases, the isotope effect in the body-centered-cubic phase, and the heat capacity of -He/sup 4/. Considerable information was gained on the detailed behavior of He/sup 4/ along the various phase boundaries. (C.E.S.)
