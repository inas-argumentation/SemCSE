On Some Major Issues concerning Rural Land Reform
The system of"Dabaogan"was initiated by the farmers of Xiaogang Village under the stress of survival environment,which was upgraded as the direction of the rural reform in the Third Plenary Session of the Eleven Committee of the Communist Party of China.The success of the urban stateowned land reform makes it an urgent issue to break the ice of rural construction land reform,with a purpose to make the rural construction land realizable assets.Accordingly,this paper interprets the issues of rural land reform of the Third Plenary Session of the Eleven Committee of the Communist Party of China,holding that we should not be so anxious to deepen the reform of agricultural land management system,but to adhere to the Chinese characteristics principle when launching rural land system reform and take the road of comprehensive reforms.
