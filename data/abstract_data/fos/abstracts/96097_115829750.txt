Graphene Based Topological Insulator with Conical Singularity in the Presence of Intrinsic and Rashba Spin Orbit Couplings
In this article, we derive the modified Kane-Mele Hamiltonian for a graphene based topological insulator with a conical singularity and spin orbit coupling (intrinsic and Rashba). It is shown that there exist the Kramer's degenerate midgap localized spin fluxon states around the defect which are robust to the addition of the Rashba spin orbit interaction.
