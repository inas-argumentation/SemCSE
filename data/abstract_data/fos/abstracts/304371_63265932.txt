Resources′ Dynamic Organization Supported Grid Dependent Tasks Rescheduling Mechanism
To solve the low efficiency and triggered frequently problem of grid dependent tasks rescheduling,this paper proposes an resources′ dynamic organization supported grid dependent tasks rescheduling mechanism,which takes the resources′ dynamic organization as the core,and classifies the resources by performance with clustering analysis method based on the resources′ dynamic evaluation results,and filters the strong dynamic resources from the performance similar resource group,in order to reduce resources while increasing the filtered resources′ stability.Experiments show that the rescheduling algorithm based on the proposed mechanism could maintain the performance advantage for the static scheduling strategy compared to the dynamic,thus show that the proposed mechanism is effective to resolve the low efficiency and triggered frequently problem faced by grid dependent task rescheduling.
