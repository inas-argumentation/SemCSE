Study on antioxidant activity of Momordica polysaccharide
The antioxidant activity of Momordica polysaccharide which was purified through deproteinization was studied.The antioxidant activity of the polysaccharide was evaluated by determining the scavenging effects with NBT illumination and Fenton reaction in vitro.Furthermore,the contents of SOD,CAT,MDA in serum,liver and cerebrum were detected in vivo.The results showed that Momordica polysaccharide had ability to scavenge(O2-.) and(.OH) effectually,and the antioxidant activity was directly related to its concen-tration in vitro.Momordica polysaccharide could obviously enhance the contents of SOD and CAT in serum,liver and cerebrum,and it could descend the levels of MDA to some extent.Momordica polysaccharide exhibited the distinctive antioxidant function.
