In Vitro Fertilization: Truth and Consequences
In 1978 the birth of Louise Brown, the world's first test-tube baby, captured worldwide attention. Over the past 20 years, the use of in vitro fertilization (IYF) has increased rapidly. By 1994, approximately 31,000 babies had been born in the United States after fertilization in vitro.1 Tbis paper begins with a description of IYF as it is practiced today. The Church's teaching on IYF is then briefly reviewed, followed by an overview of arguments used to support IYF. A final section examines some of the unto\",ard consequences of IYF which are often ignored by its proponents.
