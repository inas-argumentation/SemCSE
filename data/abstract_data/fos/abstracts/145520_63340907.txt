IEBTrust:a Trust Model Based on Individual Experience and Behavior
In distributed networks,the subjective characteristic and the objective characteristic of one peer is asymmetric.Existing trust models could not solve the problem,that the peer has different trust evaluation results because of different interactive experience and behavior in different situations.Thus,considering differences of individual behavior,experience factor is introduced.Then,a trust model based on individual experience and behavior is proposed.Experimental results show that the proposed new model has more improvement on the algorithm of feedback trust value.
