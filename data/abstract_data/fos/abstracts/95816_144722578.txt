Futures Lost: Nostalgia and Identity among Italian Immigrants in Argentina (review)
It would be difficult in this short review to comment on the nuanced nature of Roldan's descriptions. For instance, there are the stories of guerrillas in action in Bajo Cauca, one of which involved two women, Angela Rosa, the innocent lamb, and La Cucaracha (the cockroach), one of the few woman leaders of vicious guerrilla bands. In this regard the analytical contrasts set up by Roldan are quite inspiring. For example, there is the gang type in the East versus the more popular deeprooted guerrillas in Uraba, and particularly in "exceptional" Urrao. These disparities are carefully backed by hard research and pertinent commentary on the social and cultural milieu. In many ways Roldan's book is also human geography at its best.
