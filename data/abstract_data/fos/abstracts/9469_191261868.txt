The Mapmaker’s Quest provides the best summary available of Renaissance cartography for specialists and non-specialists alike. Some readers might quibble with the points and themes he chooses to emphasize. Buisseret almost offhandedly dismisses the importance of the printing press, noting that “the advent of printing and literacy [chiefly had] a multiplier effect upon developments whose origin came long before the great efflorescence of printed maps” (177–79). But most, surely, will be thankful that this complex subject has been treated comprehensively and authoritatively, yet in a digestible and pleasurable prose. JAMES AKERMAN Newberry Library
