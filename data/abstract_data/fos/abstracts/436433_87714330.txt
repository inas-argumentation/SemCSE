Influence of exosome secreted by tumor cells on the proliferation and cytotoxicity of CIK cells
Objective To analyze the influence of malignant cancer cells on the proliferation and cyto- toxicity of cytokine induced killer (CIK) cells. Methods Supernatant from gastric cancer cell line BGC-823 and colon cancer cell line HCT-8 was added into the culture system of CIK cells. The proliferation of CIK was analyzed by BrdU and MTT method. The percentage of the major effect cells (CD3~+ CD56~+ cells) were enumerated by flow cytometry. The cytotoxicity was evaluated using LDH method. To analyze the mechanisms of the tumor cells affect- ing CIK, the exosome of BGC-823 cells was isolated and tested by electron microscopy. Results The addition of the supernatant from cancer cell culture system decreases the proliferating rate of CIK cells and the prevalence of CD3~+ CD56~+ cells. The cytotoxicity of CIK cells against other similar cancer cell lines was also decreased. Exo- somes, small (40-90 nm in diameter) single-membrane bound vesicles were extracted by ultra-speed centrifugation from the supernatant of BGC-823 cells, and were identified by electron microscopy. Depletion of the exosomes from the supematants of BGC-823 cells decreases their suppressive capability to the proliferation rate of CIK cells. Con- clusion Cancer cells can inhibit the biological activities of CIK cells. The exosomes secreted from cancer cells may act as an important factor in this course.
