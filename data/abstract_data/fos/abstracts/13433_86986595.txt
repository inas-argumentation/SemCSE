Effects of the scallop skirt peptide extract in regulating blood lipid and anti-oxidation of rats with hyperlipidemia
Rats with hyperlipidemia were made by feeding high fat forage in order to evaluate the effects of the scallop skirt peptide extract in regulating blood lipid and anti-oxidation.Results showed that compared with the model control group,media and high dosage group of scallop skirt peptide extract could significantly reduce the TC,TG,LDL-C levels of serum and the MDA levels of liver,while the activities of antioxidative enzymes including SOD and GSH-pX also increased significantly,although the effects of both the media and high dosage groups were similar.The groups with low dosage of scallop skirt peptide extract showed no significant changes on the above indexes compared with the model of control group.This study indicated scallop skirt peptide extract with certain dosage had the effects of lipid adjustment and anti-hyperoxidization,and could also strengthen the antioxidative effect of liver thus to protect liver.
