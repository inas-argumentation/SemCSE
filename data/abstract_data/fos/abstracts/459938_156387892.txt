On the Weakening of Social Responsibilities of Multi-national Companies in China and its Countermeasures
While promoting economic globalization,transnational corporations are playing an increasingly important role in the world economic activities and become the biggest profit-reaper of globalization,leaving also a profound influence upon the host countries.During the past twenty eight years of China's opening to the outside world and reform,more and more transnational corporations come to invest in China.They brought with them modern ideals of enterprise management,company managerial structures and enterprise cultures,doing great contribution to China's economic development.However,recently transnational corporations have shown some weakening in some areas of social responsibilities,such as product quality,taxation operation,and labor relationship.The present paper analyzes the countermeasures to improve those aspects of weakening and tries to find out ways to ensure the result of Double-Win between transnational corporations and China.
