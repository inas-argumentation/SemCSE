Separation of Some Metal Thiothenoyltrifluoroacetonates and Their Pyridine Base Adducts by Reversed Phase HPLC
Separated were 1,1,1-trifluoro-4-mercapto-4-(2-thienyl)-3-butene-2-one (thiothenoyltrifluoroacetone, STTA) chelates of Zn2+, Cu2+, Cd2+, Ni2+, Co3+ by reversed phase high performance liquid chromatography (RP-HPLC). The elution of the STTA chelates of Cd2+, Zn2+, and Ni2+ was delayed by the addition of pyridine, α-picoline, or γ-picoline, indicating the formation of the adducts.
