Finite Element Simulation of Deformation Behavior of Preform Designed Workpiece During Equal Channel Angular Pressing
The plastic deformation behavior of preform designed workpiece during equal channel angular pressing was investigated using 3-D finite element analysis,and the effect of slant angles on the deformation behavior was also studied.The results indicate that one of the preform shapes slanting at the back part of the workpiece is the best design,which achieves homogeneous deformation,reduces strain concentration and eliminates wrinkle.
