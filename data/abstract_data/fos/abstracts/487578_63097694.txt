Extraction Exploration of Facial Cognitive Behavior Feature
To describe more facial display,this paper proposes the concept of 'facial cognitive feature',which is based human cognitive experience. The main contributions of this paper are included: 1) Presents the computational model for the analysis of facial cognitive feature and extract 11 common facial cognitive features based on the action units( AUs) from facial action coding system( FACS). 2) Gives a detailed solution to detect facial cognitive behavior features. First,AUs are analyzed by threshold decision. Then HMMs are used to detect facial cognitive behavior feature. Experimental results verity the performance of our solution.
