Research and Analysis of the Development of Digital Hainan Regional Tourism Geography Information System
Introduces the relevant technologies of tourism geography information system development,and analyses and designs Hainan tourism geographic information system structure,database,WebGIS technology,system module.Using Internet,GIS,ASP technology to build Hainan regional tourism geography information system is not only for the construction of the informatization Hainan tourism offers a new thought and solution,but also to play a key role in the construction of Hainan island of international tourism.
