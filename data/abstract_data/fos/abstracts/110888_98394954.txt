Synthesis, Structure, and Fluxional Behavior of κ1-O-Enolatoiron(II) Complexes Derived from 1,3-Dicarbonyl Compounds
Highly nucleophilic κ 1 -O-enolatoiron(II) complexes trans-FeH(OCR 1 CHCOR 2 -κ 1 O)(DEPE) 2 (1a: R 1 = R 2 = Me, 1b: R 1 = R 2 = OMe, Ic: R 1 = Me, R 2 = OMe) are prepared by the reaction of Fe(N 2 )(DEPE) 2 with 1,3-dicarbonyl compounds.
