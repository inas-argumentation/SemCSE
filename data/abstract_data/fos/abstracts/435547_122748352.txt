Editors' Report for 1996
Applications and Case Studies. This section received 125 new submissions during the calendar year 1996, compared with 127 in 1995. In all, 175 manuscripts were considered at least once in 1996, including 50 re-submissions of manuscripts considered in previous years. By the end of 1996, 34 of the submitted manuscripts had been accepted, 31 had been invited to revise and resubmit, 40 had been rejected without external review, 39 had been rejected after review, 1 had been withdrawn, and 4 had been transferred to Theory and Methods. The acceptance rate (ignoring manuscripts under revision) was 30%. The acceptance rates for 1995 and 1994 were 22 and 25% respectively.
