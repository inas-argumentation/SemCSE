Misunderstood Issues for Sludge Treatment and Disposal and Technical Countermeasures
Through the analysis of the current situation and trends of sludge treatment and disposal indicates that some issues such as responsibility for dealing with sludge,sludge control systems and related standards exist in current sludge treatment process.The misunderstood issue is raised with current sludge disposal programmes.Then the sludge disposal methods can be analysed adequately so that we can find an economical,viable and convient technical countermeasures and final treatment way for the sludge.
