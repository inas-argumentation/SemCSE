Consideration of Strong & Smart Grid
The interpretation and cognition to the concept and content of smart grid in worldwide was introduced,the necessity to build strong smart grid and required qualification was studied.To build a strong smart grid is the tendency to meet the future needs of human society development.The future strong smart grid will provide economic development a more stable and reliable ensurement,provide better quality of life for people as well as flexible and personalized services while protect the environment,save energy and make greater contribution.
