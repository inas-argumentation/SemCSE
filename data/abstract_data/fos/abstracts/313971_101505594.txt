Causes analysis of producing of polymers in butadiene extraction plant and its measures
The polymers are easy to produce in butadiene extraction plant because of the properties of butadiene and extraction solution DMF, so the equipment, pipes and valves are plugged, and the operation load and period are restricted. The characteristics and harmfulness of polymers are analyzed theoretically, the causes of producing of polymer are summarized, and the measures are put forward combining with the production practice.
