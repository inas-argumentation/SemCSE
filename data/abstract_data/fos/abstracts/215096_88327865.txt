Study on pigment extracting condition from Acanthopanx senticacosus harms fruit
The material of the study is acanthopanx senticacosus harms fruit.The researches indicate:the pigment of acanthopanx senticacosus harms fruit belong to water-soluble anthocyanin pigment.It has a absorb apex at 525nm and is red liquor at acid condition.The odd factor test' results indicate:temperature,alcohol concentration,pH have notable influence to extracting pigment,pectic enzyme,ultrasonic,microwave can improve extracting effect,but the impact is not prominent.The orthogonal test indicate:the best extracting conditions are temperature 80℃,ethanol concentration 95%,pH2.0.The sequence of the factors affecting extracting pigment from acanthopanx senticacosus harms fruit is pH,ethanol concentration and temperature from greatness to smallness.
