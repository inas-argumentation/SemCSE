Thought on Democratic Administration in Adult Colleges
In the course of the reformation from the current system of state recruitment to the system of the unit recruitment adult colleges meet some problems on deep educational reformation and the immediate interest of the teachers and staff members. It is very important to enhance the democratic administration in adult colleges. At present its situation is not suitable to the educational reformation and development. Some people misunderstand the democratic administration. So we should improve the cognitive ability, clear up the misunderstanding and realize the"Three Representatives" which is one of the main tasks in deepening reformation in adult colleges.
