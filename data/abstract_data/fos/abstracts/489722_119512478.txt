The indirect interaction between nuclear or ionic spins and the Ruderman-Kittel-Kasuya-Yosida approximation
The matrix elements describing transitions between two excited states near to the groundstate of the free electron gas might considerably change the strength of the interaction between two nuclear or ionic spins as found in the R.K.K.Y. theory. To investigate this effect the third order correction to the groundstate energy was calculated. The calculation was performed with the assumption that the groundstate of the free-electron gas was non-degenerate and unpolarized. The hamiltonian describing the interaction between the electron system and the nuclear or ionic spin system was taken in the so-called R.K.K.Y. approximation, meaning that the interaction strength between the spins of the electrons and the spins of the nuclei or ions is taken independent of the momenta of the electrons before and after scattering. Our calculations showed that the third order correction tends to infinity. The density functions of electrons with spin up or down and the total density function up to second order also diverge.The only convergent result obtained is the polarization density up to second order.Therefore it is concluded that the usual R.K.K.Y. result is not correct.In order to get rid of these divergencies the upper limit ∞ in the integrals was replaced by akcut-off. It was possible to obtain an asymptotic form of the third order correction for largekc.o. The present work strongly suggests that higher order terms has to be taken into account in order to give an adequate description of the indirect interaction between nuclear or ionic spins.
