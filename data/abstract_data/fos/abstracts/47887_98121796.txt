Dependence upon oxygen potential of the interdiffusion in single crystalline UO2-(U, Pu)O2
The chemical diffusion coefficient of the metal atoms, D was measured in single crystals of UO2 and (U, Pu)O2 at 1600°C and at variable oxygen potential (different gas mixtures of CO and CO2). D depended strongly on the oxygen potential ΔG(O2): it decreased by more than 3 ordeers of magnitude between ΔG(O2)=-20 and -120 kcal mol−1. The dependence on the U/Pu ratio was much less pronounced. The diffusion mechanism (change from vacancy to interstitial mechanism with or without cluster formation) is discussed and the implications for other kinetic processes are described.
