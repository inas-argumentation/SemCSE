Sally Patricia Allwork (June 14, 1938–July 12, 2009)
After a short illness of rapid onset, Sally Allwork passed away on the July 12, 2009. Sally achieved her ultimate position as a morphologist despite the lack of an initial traditional university education. Her mother was Italian in origin. She left school at the age of 16 after taking her 'O' level examinations. She became an Almoners' Clerk at The Central Middlesex Hospital, continuing her studies in the evenings so as to obtain the necessary qualifications to become a laboratory technician.
