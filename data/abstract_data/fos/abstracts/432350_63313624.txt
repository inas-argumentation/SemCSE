Analysis of single mode laser with chirped gratings
Using the wavetheoretical analysis, this paper investigates the lasing characteristics of three sectioned single-mode DFB semiconductor lasers with chirped gratings. Analytical expressions are derived for the threshold gain and lasing wavelength of chirped DFB lasers. Numerical results show the sectioned chirped gratings have significant effects on the fine tuning of lasing wavelength and the improvement of the single mode gain margin to the DFB lasers.
