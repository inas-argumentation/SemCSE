Release policies with random software life cycle and penalty cost
In this paper, we discuss software release policies for a flexible reliability growth model based on a non-homogeneous Poisson process (NHPP) with a random life cycle and a penalty cost. The optimization criterion is that of minimizing the cost, subject to achieving a given level of software performability. Here cost also includes the penalty cost which is incurred by the manufacturer for not delivering the software at the scheduled delivery time. The flexible model is a fairly general model and covers a variety of known models under different conditions. In each case, we show that there exists a finite and unique release time. Numerical results are also presented.
