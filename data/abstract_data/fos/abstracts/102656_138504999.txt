Effects of Overtemperature and Holding Time on Melt Quality in Short-cut Melting Process
According to the characteristics of short-cut melting process,the effects of overtemperature and holding time on iron melt quality were studied by thermal analysis,metallographic analysis,fracture analysis and mechanical property measurement.The results show that the relative purity and high quality iron liquid can be obtained by controlling thermodynamics and dynamics conditions when the overtemperature in short-cut melting process is in the range of 1510～ 1540 ℃ and holding time is in the range of 10～15 min,which can greatly eliminate the inheritance of blast furnace iron liquid.
