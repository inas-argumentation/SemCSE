Effects of soil treatment with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1 on capsicum growth.
The growth-promoting effects of soil treatment with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1 on capsicum and their mechanisms were investigated in this paper.The results show that capsicum grows much better in the soil with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1 than in the soil without treatment.Compared with those without Trichoderma harzianum TH-1,the fresh weight of capsicum and the content of chlorophyll increase significantly with the average increase over 22.26%,and the nitrate-reductase activity in the capsicum leaves in soil with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1 increases by 68.12%.Capsicum grows much better in the unsterilized soil with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1 than in the sterilized soil with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1.Compared with the capsicum in sterilized soil with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1,the fresh weight of capsicum and the chlorophyll content and the nitrate-reducase-activity in capsicum leaves in the unsterilized soil with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1 increases obviously by 18.42%,15.9% and 26.28%,respectively.The amount of Actinomycete,Lactobacillus and photosynthetic bacteria in soil with Trichoderma harzianum TH-1 increase by 64.71%,77.27% and 58.62%,respectively,while the amount of yeast decreases by 52.62%.
