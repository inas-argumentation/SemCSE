Structural petrology along a transect across the Thompson Belt, Manitoba: dip slip at the western Churchill–Superior boundary
Early Proterozoic rocks and Archean gneisses of the Thompson Belt, east of the Churchill Province – Superior Province boundary, record the history of the Hudsonian Orogeny in this area.
