Isolation of the lactic acid-forming bacteria from the yoghurt and the analysis of the ratio to bacillus and coccus
By the concentration gradient dilution method,the lactic acid-forming bacteria from the yogurts sold in the campus were isolated,the isolated was analysed according to the candy fermentation experiments and the physiological and biochemistry analysis.And the ratio to bacillus and coccus were carried under the method of plate culture count.The data showed,the isolates were lactobacillus bulgaricus and streptococcus thermophilus.The ratio to bacillus and coccus of Xi You yogurt and Uygur yogurt were 1∶2 and 1∶1 espectively.The data indicated the Uygur yogurt was of heavier and sourer properties,while the Xi You one has the better flavor.This study would provide the scientific clue for the people in the campus.And it'd also improve the study of the native microorganisms.
