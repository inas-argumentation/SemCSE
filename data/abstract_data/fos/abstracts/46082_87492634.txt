Improved Method on Establishment of Banana Embryogenic Cell Suspension Cultures
Development of banana embryogenic cell suspension cultures is constrained by factors such as low induction frequencies of embryogenic callus,long induction period,and loss of embryogenic competence.In this paper,we reported on the improvement of induction conditions of embryogenic callus for the establishment of an efficient and reliable method of banana embryogenic cell suspension,using immature male flowers of "Musa spp.AAB cv"."Dwarf Brazilian" and "Musa spp.AAA cv""William" as initial explants.Three conclusion points could be drawn from the present work:(1)light conditions was good for banana embryogenic callus inducing;(2) adding Casein and Proline in the M1 medium could help to improve the induction rate of embryogenic callus;(3) using microwell plates instead of Erlenmeyer flasks to initiate cell suspension cultures not only enhanced cell division and proliferation,but also reduced the contamination potentials.
