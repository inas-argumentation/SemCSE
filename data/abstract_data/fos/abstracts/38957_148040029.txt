The evolution of the rules of martial art routine competition and its effect on the development of martial art
The authors studied the rules of all previous martial art competitions and analyzed the characteristics and contents of evolution of the rules,and drew the following conclusions: 1) technical structure of the movements has changed as a result of technical guidance,which,however,has a tendency to deviate from the nature of such a sport;2) the gradual establishment of difficulty movement system has raised the level of standardization of judgment criteria;3) the development of performance techniques has enhanced the appearance of the movements and increased the difficulty of the techniques;4) the evolution is lack of guidance for offense and defense consciousness as well as intrinsic spirit,and neglects technical style.
