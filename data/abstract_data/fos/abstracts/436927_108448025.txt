Soil reacting forces for two, tapered bentleg plows.
The soil reacting forces for tapered bentleg plows with two cutting widths were determined for a field soil. A split-plot experimental design was used with the plows in the main plots and with the presence or absence of a prior tilling plow, three rake angles and two tillage depths in the sub-plots. Twisting of the tilling interface limited the soil reacting forces of the wider plow. Penetration was optimum at the intermediate rake angle. The lateral reacting force for the plows would necessitate the use of right and left hand or dual plows
