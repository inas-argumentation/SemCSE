Decoloring method of the crude polysaccharides from Enteromorpha linza
Activated carbon,hydrogen peroxide and ADS-7macroporous anion exchange resin were respectively used in decolorization of the crude polysaccharides from Enteromorpha linza.The decolorization effect of activated carbon was found to be the best one through single factor experiment.Then the orthogonal experiments were used to find the optimal conditions in activated carbon method,which were as mass fraction of activated carbon 6%,temperature 35 ℃,decolorization time 40 min.
