On the Cultivation Pattern of International Accounting Personnel in China
This paper mainly explores the cultivation pattern of China’s international accounting personnel in three aspects.First, detailed analysis on necessary qualities of international accounting personnel is conducted in knowledgestructure, comprehensive ability, English communication ability as well as moral quality; second, the importanceof cultivating international accounting personnel is elaborated on from the angles of the nation and enterprises;finally, the cultivation paths of international accounting personnel are explored in two aspects includinguniversity basic education and in-service training.
