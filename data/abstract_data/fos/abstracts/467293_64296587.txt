Design and Implementation of Electronic Lock System Free from Power Supply
Presents electronic lock system which is free from power supply,the lock system contains three core components,including electronic lock,electronic key and lock manager device.The electronic lock makes use of the power of the open door behaviors of users and collect the energy,the circuit transforms the energy to electronic power and uses the power to supply the whole electronic control system.The electronic lock system avoid the defect of those electronic lock system with the external power supply or battery-powered feather,it solve the problems which have limited the wider application of electronic locks.And because the electronic lock system also has high security and high ease-to-use features,it has a good application prospect.
