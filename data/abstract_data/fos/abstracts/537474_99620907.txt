Statistical analysis of gatch hydrogenation unit material balance and overall energy consumption
Through the statistical analysis of gatch hydrogenation unit material balance and overall energy consumption in Luoyang Petrochemical Company,the product yield,comprehensive energy consumption structure and the change are presented for enterprise to optimize production,reduce processing cost. The feedstock is mixed wax oil and hydrogen; the main products are refined wax,dry gas from hydrogenation and acid gas from hydrogenation,byproduct diesel oil and naphtha. Under the unit load of 162 136 ~ 201 298 t,the overall yield of products is about 99. 9% and increases when the handling capacity becomes larger.
