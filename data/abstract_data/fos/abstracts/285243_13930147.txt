Updated dispersion-theoretical analysis of the nucleon electromagnetic form factors
Abstract.In the light of the new data on the various neutron and proton electromagnetic form factors taken in recent years, we update the dispersion-theoretical analysis of the nucleon electromagnetic form factors from the mid-nineties. The parametrization of the spectral functions includes constraints from unitarity, perturbative QCD, and recent measurements of the neutron charge radius. We obtain a good description of most modern form factor data, with the exception of the Jefferson Lab data on GEp/GMp in the four-momentum transfer range $Q^2 = 3 \ldots 6$ GeV2. For the magnetic radii of the proton and the neutron we find rMp = 0.857 fm and rMn = 0.879 fm, which is consistent with the recent determinations using continued-fraction expansions.
