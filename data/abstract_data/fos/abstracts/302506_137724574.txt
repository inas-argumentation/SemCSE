Study on Repeated-impact Fatigue Properties of 38CrMoAlA Nitrided Steel
The repeated-impact fatigue crack initiation life,rupture life and crack propagation rate of 38CrMoAlA steel were studied.The results show that nitriding at low impact energy has little effect on crack initiation,and nitriding at high impact energy can enhance repeated-impact fatigue crack initiation life and rupture life.While the effects of the impact energy and nitridation on the crack propagation rate are not obvious.The crack propagation rate of nitrided specimen is lower than that of unnitrided specimen at the same impact energy and stress strength factor range(ΔK).
