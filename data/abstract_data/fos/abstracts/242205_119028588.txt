Effects of different densities and nitrogen application techniques on yield and population quality of super-high-yield wheat.
Using Yannong19 and Xinong979 as material,with split-split plot design,we studied the effects of 3 densities and 3 ratios of base and top-dressing nitrogen fertilizers on the yield and population qualities of super-high-yield wheat.The results showed that:the tiller number at maturity are 621.71×104 hm-2 and 650.66×104 hm-2,the panicle setting rates are 39.53% and 41.73%,the LAIs at late filling stage are 0.95 and 1.13,the dry matter accumulation are 16925.37 kg·hm-2 and 17419.75 kg·hm-2 for Yannong19;the tiller number at maturity is 647.76×104 hm-2,the panicle setting rate is 43.49%,the LAI at late filling stage is 1.11,the dry matter accumulation is 18937.85 kg·hm-2 for Xinong979.
