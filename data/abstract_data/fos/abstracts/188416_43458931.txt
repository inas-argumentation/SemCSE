Confidentiality and the Role of the Addiction Specialist Team in Liver Transplantation Procedures. Commentary on Donnadieu-Rigole et al., 2017, Follow-Up of Alcohol Consumption After Liver Transplantation: Interest of an Addiction Team?
For patients suffering from end-stage alcoholic liver disease (ALD), liver transplantation (LT) is often the only remaining, life-saving intervention. During the last two decades the number of transplantations that have been performed in the context of ALD has being growing steadily both in the United States and Europe. For example, 30-50% of all liver transplantations in Europe are currently done for this indication (Bruha et al., 2012).
