Progress on Safety Evaluation of Transgenic Soybean
Transgenic soybeans have been promoted rapidly in the world since the first transgenic glyphosate resistant soybean varieties cultivated commercially in 1996.With the tremendous economic benefits,its potential risk of food and environmental safety was of widespread concern.This article discussed the advantages of transgenic soybeans,food and environmental safety,and provided a reference for the commercialization of transgenic soybeans in China.
