Promoting the Growth of University Students by Career Development Education
The career development education refers to the extension and development of career guide work and vocational guide education,including the professional life plan education,the occupational psychological evaluation and the profession consultation,profession choosing guidance and education of starting a new business and so on.This passage emphasizes on the problems of university students' career development education,proposes the goal and the principle of career development education by combining the present domestic and foreign situations,analysizes and discusses content establishment,implementation way and channal,work system and the team construction,the safeguard system of the career development education with the aim of promoting the growth of the university students.
