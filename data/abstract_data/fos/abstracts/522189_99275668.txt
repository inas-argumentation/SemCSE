Determination of chlorogenic acid in Burdock Oligosaccharide Granules by HPLC
In order to determine the content of chlorogenic acid in Burdock Oligosaccharide Granules, an external standard method by HPLC(high performance liquid chromatography) with Discovery YILITE using C18 column(5 μm,4.6 mm×250 mm) was adopted. The mobile phase consisted of A(acetonitrile: 0.4% phosphoric acid solution,10:90) and B(acetonitrile: 0.4% phosphoric acid solution, 60:40),and the flow rate was 1.0 mL·min-1. The detection wavelength was 327 nm, and the column temperature was 30℃. The results showed a good linearity between the peak area and chlorogenic acid content in the range of 2.50 to 80.03 μg·mL-1(r=0.9999).The average recovery rate of chlorogenic acid was 98.8%, and RSD was1.34%(n=6). In conclusion, the method is simple, accurate, sensitive and reproducible, and provide experimental evidence for internal quality control of Burdock Oligosaccharide Granules.
