Near Three Gorges Region of Power Grid Relevant Operation Problem Research
There still exist two problems requiring prompt solution currently in the near Three Gorges region power grid.One is restricted by thermal stability in the low water period of Douli-Jiangling transmission lines' s sections,which hinders the resource optimal allocation in trans-provincial area.The other is the short circuit current of power grid near Three Gorges region approaching exceeding the standard and getting worse.The paper takes an intensive study in the problems of the deficient power flow transmission capacity of Douli-Jiangling transmission lines' s sections and the higher short circuit current of the power grid near Three Gorges region,analyzes the reason caused the above-mentioned problems,and proposes year by year resolution to the above-mentioned problems taking account of the development of the power grid.
