Discussion on the Purification Method of Electrolysis Aluminum Liquid for Producing Aluminum Rolling Ingot
The authors analyzed the shortages and special feature of making Al rolling ingot directly from electrolysis aluminum liquid,especially focus on the inclusions and poresity defects in the final products which could not be ignored.According to the present conventional process,the purification technology in front furnace was put forward.Some purification technologies at domestic and abroad were also introduced.The degree of purification of Al liquid got raised obviously by restricted process controlling and several purifying methods performed in furnace and on production line.Thickness of plates produced by rolling reduced from original 0.35～1.2 mm to the foil of 0.026 mm now.
