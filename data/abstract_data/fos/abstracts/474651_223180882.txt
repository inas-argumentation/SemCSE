Co-production of carbapenem-hydrolyzing enzyme KPC-2 and ArmA 16S rRNA methylase in pan-drugs resistant Enterobacter cloacae
Objective To investigate the production of carbapenemase and 16S rRNA methylase in five isolates of pan-drugs resistant E. cloacae recovered in Ruijin hospital. Methods MICs of the five isolates to 10 antibiotics were determined by E test. Six kinds of 16S rRNA methylase genes and a series of 13- lactamase genes were amplified by PCR. Shotgun cloning was performed to detect carbapenem resistance determinant. The conjugal transfer of carbapenemase gene and 16S rRNA methylase gene was performed in broth culture with E. coli J53 as the recipient. Pulsed-field gel eleetrophoresis （PFGE） was carried out to analyse the genotyping. IEF was performed to detect β-lactamases. Southern blot was performed to determine the location of carbapenem resistance determinant. Results The MICs of 10 antibiotics were 〉32 mg/L. Four β-1actamases with pIs of 5.4 （ TEM-1 ）, 6.7 （ KPC-2 ）, 8.2 （ SHV-12 ）, 8.4 （CTX-M-14） were determined. The insertion sequence in the recombinant plasmid was blaKPc.2 flanked by a transposon, blaKpc_2 was located on a large non-eonjugative plasmid whereas anna was located on an other conjugative plasmid. PFGE patterns of 5 isolates were identical. Conclusion KPC-2 was responsible for carbapenem resistance in pandrugs resistant Enterobacter cloacae. There was no relationship between blaKkpc-2 and armA. Although pandrug resistant Enterobacteriaceae remain rare, the emergence of this group of organism merits monitoring.
