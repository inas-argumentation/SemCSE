Television Communication in Chengcun Village——An Ethnographic Study about the Media and Country
In the changing process of traditional culture,we can not ignore the power of mass communication.In this research,the methods of ethnography are adopted in the study of communication in rural areas.Based on related data and field work in the Chengcun Village located in the west of Fujian province,the article touches such issues as mass media(mostly TV) developing in the village,interaction between mass media and villagers,and the roles of mass media in the social transform in the countryside,sketching a picture of interaction between mass media and Chengcun villagers and conflict between tradition and modernity.
