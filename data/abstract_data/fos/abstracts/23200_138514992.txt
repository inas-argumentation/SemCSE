Preparation of Purification Agent and Undercooling Mechanism for DZ125 Superalloy
The undercooled DZ125 superalloy was investigated by the method of glass and molten salt purification combined with cycled superheating,the mechanism of purification by compound salt was discussed.The experimental results show that when the B2O3 glass purifier is used,the undercooling is just 58 K;while the method of molten compound purification combined with cycled superheating is used,a high undercooling degree of 180 K is obtained.
