Image Super Resolution Reconstruction Algorithm Based on Contourlet
Proposes a new method of super resolution based on Contourlet.Uses Contourlet to decompose the image to get information in different directions of image,cuts the image to little patch and finds the best match in the dictionary,thus can get the coefficients that we have lost.With these coefficients we can use Contourlet to compose the original image that we are excepting.The final image is more faithful to the original one because we use all directions other than four directions,it's much better than other methods.
