On Extended Rate of Convergence Results for the Invariance Principle
Let {X i i = 1, 2, 3, …} be a sequence of independent and identically distributed random variables for which EX i = 0, var X i = σ2, E|X i |2 +a = b 0. C[0, 1] is the space of continuous real-valued functions on the interval [0, 1] with the sup norm topology. We define a “random broken line” as follows. \(\begin{array}{l} \xi _n (t) = \sum\nolimits_{i = 1}^k {X_i /(\sigma n^{\frac{1}{2}} )}\quad \quad \quad \quad \quad \quad {\rm for}\ t = n^{ - 1} k \\ \xi _n (t) = \xi _n (n^{ - 1} k) + n[\xi _n (n^{ - 1} (k + 1)) - \xi _n (n^{ - 1} k)](t - n^{ - 1} k) \\ \quad \quad \quad \quad{\rm for}\ n^{ - 1} k \leqq t \leqq n^{ - 1} (k + 1),1 \leqq k \leqq n. \\ \end{array}\)
