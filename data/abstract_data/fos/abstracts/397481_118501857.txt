D-annihilators, weakly n-angulated categories and derived equivalences
In this paper, we introduce D-annihilators and weakly n-angulated categories, and give a general construction of derived equivalences between the quotient rings of endomorphism rings (moduloD-annihilators or proper D-annihilators) of certain objects involved in a sequence in an additive category or a weakly n-angulated category. This method generalizes the main results in [HX11], [HKX13] and [Che13].
