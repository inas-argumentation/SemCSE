Study on Pseudo-carburizing Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of New-type Bainite Carburizing Steel
The microstructure and mechanical properties of the new type bainite steel were studied by different pseudo-carburizing processes and the microstructure abtained by industrialized carburizing was also studied.The results show that carburizing bainite steel has better combination of strength and toughness by normalizing heat treatment and different pseudo-carburizing heat treatment,the microstructure obtained by pseudo-carburizing process and non-carburized layer in carburizing treatment has not excessive grain growth and grain coarsening,the testing material has better strength and toughness by pseudo-carburizing and carburizing treatment at 920℃×10h+880℃×1h air-cooling+680℃×3h air-cooling+ 880℃×1h air-cooling+200℃ tempering.
