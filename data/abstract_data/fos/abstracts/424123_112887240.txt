Finite Element Analysis of Welded Joints with Multi-site Damage
The J integral values in the vicinity of the crack tip in welded joints were numerically analyzed by using the plane stress elastic-plastic finite element method.The effects of some parameters on the J integral values in detail were explored.The results indicate that multi-site damage have a great influence on the J integral values.So it is essential to reinforce quality control and improve welding procedure in order to ensure the structural strength of bogies.
