Integrated Dynamic Scheduling Algorithm Based on Group Optimization for Multiprocessor Real-Time Systems
A integrated dynamic scheduling algorithm that is based on group optimization is developed to schedule the task sets combining hard and soft real-time tasks in multiprocessor real-time systems.Starting with an empty partial schedule,each step of the search in our algorithm extends the current partial schedule by adopting an optimization assignment strategy for a group tasks selected from all pre-scheduling tasks and a QoS(quality of service) degradation policy for soft real-time tasks.To evaluate the performance of our algorithm,an intensive simulation is made to analyze the impact of several task parameters on its scheduling success ratio.The simulation results show that our algorithm can offer superior scheduling success ratio than that of Myopic algorithms.
