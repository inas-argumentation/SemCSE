Simultaneous Determination of Zoalene ant Its Metabolite in Chicken Tissues by Ultra Performance Liquid Chromatography
A method was developed for the simultaneous determination of zoalene and its metabolite(3-Amino-5-nitro-o-toluamide,ANOT) in chicken tissues by ultra performance liquid chromatog-raphy(UPLC).Samples were extracted twice with acidic phosphate buffer solution and then the extracts were adjusted to pH 7.0 and purified through HLB cartridge after centrifugation.The residues were eluted from cartridge with 0.1% formic acid solution/acetonitrile(80 ∶ 20,V/V).The samples were analyzed by UPLC with photodiode-array detector(PDA) and quantified with the external standard calibration curve method.The detection limits of ANOT and zoalene were 55.0 and 25.0 μg/kg,respectively.The mean recoveries of zoalene and ANOT in chicken tissues were 79.2%-88.1%,the intra-assay relative standard deviations were 2.7%-5.2% and the inter-assay relative standard deviations were 4.1%-6.9% at spiked levels of 1500-9000 μg/kg.There was a good linear correlation(the calibration coefficient is above 0.9998) between the peak areas and concentration of zoalene and ANOT in the range of 20 to 2000 μg/L.
