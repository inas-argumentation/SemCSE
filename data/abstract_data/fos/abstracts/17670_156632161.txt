A Speculation on the Issue of Income Gap from the Angle of Political Economics
The issue of income distribution is an important issue in the field of Political Economics.Special attention must be paid to the big gap between high income and low income.Here,I'd like to give a complete analysis of the increasing income gap since the open policy and economic reform came in action, especially in recent years.This article also talks about the consequence due to the big gap.In conclusion,a lot of strategies and suggestions are proposed to cope with this kind of problem.
