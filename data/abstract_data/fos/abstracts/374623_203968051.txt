Experimental Study on Hydrogen Generated by Anaerobic Fermentation of Kitchen Waste under the Action of Aged Refuse
Two groups of tests were carried out to explore the effects of adding aged refuse with a landfilling time of 10 years on the hydrogen production process by anaerobic fermentation of kitchen waste.In the first group of test,impacts of the inoculation quantity of aged refuse on the hydrogen production of kitchen waste were preliminarily studied.It was found that the concentration of hydrogen production of kitchen waste was increased after adding the aged refuse(increased from 24% to 30%);in the second batch test.The second groups of tests were also carried out to analyze the co-effects of aged refuse and municipal sludge on the hydrogen production.It was seen that the specific hydrogen production ratio and the maximum concentration increased from 64mL/gVS to 102mL/gVS,and 33% to 37%,respectively after adding aged refuse,due to the coordination mechanism of aged refuse and municipal sludge.
