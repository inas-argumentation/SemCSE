Effect evaluation of the application of scientific supervision mode for port catering sector during the important international events
Through the analyzing the exsiting food hygiene supervision mode and the function actuality of HACCP system in catering sector,choose two demonstration companies to establish the four-side scientific supervision mode in the base of ISO 22000 Food Safety Management System,it focus on the important international events,and evaluates the application effect of scientific supervision mode,designed to improve and enhance the airport's restaurants port of food safety management system to create demonstration bases and scientific regulation and the experience promotion.
