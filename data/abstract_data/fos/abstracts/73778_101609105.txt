Reaction of dialuric acid with hydrazine derivatives
In the condensation of dialuric, thiodialuric, and acetyldialuric acids with hydrazine derivatives, the ketone character of the CO group in the 2 position is manifested only in the case of acetyldialuric acid; the hydroxy or mercapto group in dialuric and thiodialuric acids is substituted to form 5-substituted derivatives of these acids.
