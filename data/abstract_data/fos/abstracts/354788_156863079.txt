The Current Dilemma of Agriculture Trade Liberalization and the Corresponding Causes
In the increasingly quickening liberalization course of international commodity trade,the liberalization of agriculture trade goes along relatively tardily.The trade protections in agricultural products remain quite serious and current Doha negotiation is proceeding roughly.This paper reviews the historical negotiations on agriculture trade liberalization and explores the economical,social and political causes which have blocked the standing process of the liberalization.Perspectives of international agriculture trade liberalization and revelations to our country are put forward as well.
