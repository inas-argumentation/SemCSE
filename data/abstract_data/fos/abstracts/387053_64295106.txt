Design of High Precision Electronic Scale Based on 24-bit A/D Converter
Adopts AT89C52 microcontroller as the main control chip,combines pressure sensor,analog to-digital converter,and liquid crystal display(LCD) to make up a high-accuracy and multifunctional digital electronic scale.44 matrix keyboard makes operation convenient.LCD is used to display weighing weight,unit price,total price and other information.Thermal receipt printer prints out the rated information.The electronic balance has functions of weighing,keyboard input,automatic measurement,information display and bill printing.The design has such characteristics as small-size,low-cost,high-accuracy and high-reliability.
