Study on High-temperature Mechanical Properties of GCr15 Steel
The hot tensile test of GCr15 steel was conducted by thermal simulator Gleeble-1500.The high-temperature mechanical properties of the slab was studied at different temperatures.The change of tensile strength and reduction of area with temperature was analyzed.The fracture morphology of the specimen was observed by SEM.The results show that,the temperature area of good plasticity is 800-1200℃.The first temperature area of brittleness is 1200-1350℃,and the third temperature area of brittleness is below 800℃.The zero plastic temperature is 1300℃,and the zero strength temperature is above 1400℃.The anti-oxidants can improve the elongation of GCr15 steel.The elongation of the samples after double-fined treatment and repeatedly coated with anti-oxidant can reach 324.5%.
