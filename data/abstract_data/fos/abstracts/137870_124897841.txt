STUDY OF THE MECHANICS OF I(5~2p1/2) BY PHOTOLYSIS OF CF_3I
WT5”BZ]The fourth harmonic of the YAG fundamental 1.064μm is used to generate the electronically excited iodine I(5 2p 1/2 ) through the photolysis of CF 3I.The direct detection of the decay of the fluorescence at 1.315μm has been made to investigate the spontaneous radiation life time of I(5 2p 1/2 ) and the quenching rate constants of the electronically excited iodine atom by CF 3I、O 2 and He. The results are: 0.037±0.005s,80.69Pa 1 s 1 ,299.97Pa 1 s -1 and 29.33Pa -1 s -1 respectively. [WT5”HZ]
