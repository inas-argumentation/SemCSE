Reflections on Carrying out Consumption Reverse Logistics
It is important to carry out consumption reverse logistics for circular economy. At present, the system of consumption reverse logistics has not yet been established in our country. The main reasons are the lack of strong implementation and the market failure. With regard to carrying out consumption reverse logistics, government should be dominating, who should restrain various links including producing, circulating, consuming, and should implement appropriate measures, intensify education and scientific research investment, and set up our system of consumption reverse logistics step by step.
