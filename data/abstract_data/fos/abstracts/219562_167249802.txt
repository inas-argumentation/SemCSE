An Analysis of the Factors Obstructing the Fusion of New-generation Migrant Workers into Urban Lives
First appearing in the "central number one file" in 2010,the term "new-generation migrant workers" is both a new highlight and a new research direction.In order to understand the obstacles inflencing the fusion of current new generation into city lives and explore the effective countermeasures,a survey has been made among some migrant workers in Xiamen and Zhangzhou and related literature and reports have been reviewed.It is found that the factors obstructing the fusion of the new generation of rural migrant workers into cities are multifaceted,including system,concepts,skills and so on.To make the new generation migrant workers melted into the city,not only should the workers themselves make efforts,but also the government should give great impetus to the institutional reform and perfection,gradually opening the cities' gates to the new-generation migrant workers.
