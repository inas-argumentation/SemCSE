Vocational Education Promoting the Surplus Rural Labor Force to Shift in Order
How to meet the needs of market economy, shift the surplus rural labor force in an orderly and resonablely manner, and turn the advantage of size of population into a resource advantage and economic advantage in the present stage, is concerned the further development of rural economy of our country and great strategic question of the social stability. But for various reasons, the rural transfer of surplus labor is slow and even presents the backset, Its amphibious flow type but not transformation of the immigrating type is undoubtedly a multifactor result that acts on together, but the rural laborer's education level is an important restriction factor. So to develop modern vocational education and to reduce the supply of low-quality labor force are the important way to solve rural transfer of surplus labor.
