PSOSZ: Group Command Transmission Algorithm Based on Internet
It is a problem that the best-effort service model of Internet causes easily resources competition,which will generate GCT( Group Command Transmission) transmission failed giving rise to part of commands lost. To attack the problem,this paper firstly builds EPSN( Effective Path Statistics Network) based on Internet,and transforms the problem GCT based on Internet into the problem GMCMOOP( Group Multi-Constraints Multi-Objective Optimization Problem) based on the EPSN. Aiming at the problem GM-CMOOP,this paper proposes PSOSZ( Particle Swarm Optimization based on Sole Zone). The algorithm makes use of PSO to searching GMCMOOP problem solutions according to sole search space tactic. The experiment shows that the algorithm,under the condition of scale of group commands between the maximum amount 175 and the minimum amount 75,the transmission success rate of GCT,relative to classic routing algorithms such as DSA and Yen,has better algorithm performance,according to scale of EPSN changing based on Internet. Meanwhile,the algorithm has better performance than Particle Swarm Optimization according to error rate.
