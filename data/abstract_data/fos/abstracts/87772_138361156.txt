FRP corrosion resistance: the role of the glass fibre type
The resistance of FRP laminates to corrosion attack in aqueous solutions of both NaOH and H 2 SO 4 is investigated. A two level fraction factorial experiment is carried out to study the effects of the glass fiber type (E vs. E-CR), the resin type (isophtalic vs. bisphenol A), the laminate construction (mat/mat/mat vs. mat/woven roving/mat), the exposure media type and the acid/base ion concentration (1N vs. 2N)
