A study of British youth sport organizations from the perspective of civil society
Based on the stage specific characteristics of teenager growing up,youth sport is the starting point of en-hancing the physical constitution and health level of the whole nation.By basing theoretical background on civil so-ciety,the authors examined British youth sports organization running modes:the joint development of individual rights and public areas has brought tremendous development spaces to the public sports life of teenagers;British "Youth Sport Trust" as a social organization is highly regarded by the British government and society;as analyzed from the perspective of civil society,the most distinctive characteristics of this organization are to boost the boom-ing of social groups,to blend in school physical education gradually,to expand leisure spaces constantly,and to en-able teenagers to have the right to participate in sports activities.
