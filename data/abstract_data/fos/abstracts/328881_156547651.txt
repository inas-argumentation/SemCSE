The Legislations of Abandonment Crime of Germany and Japanese Criminal Law and Its Enlightenment to the Chinese Relevant Legislation——In view of one judicial case
According to Chinese Criminal Law,the essence of crime of abandonment is an infringement on the right of family member.Lacking the explicit provision,some offences of abandonment,which do not happen between the members of a family,may not be punished,and the protection function of penal law won't be realized.It is necessary to learn from the relevant provisions of Germany and Japanese Criminal Law to perfect our legislation of crime of abandonment.
