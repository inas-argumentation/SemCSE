Treatment of Chinese medicine pharmaceutical wastewater by hydrolysis acidification + sequencing batch reactor activated sludge(SBR) process
The design and debugging of hydrolysis acidification + SBR process to treat Chinese medicine pharmaceutical wastewater is described.After a period of commissioning,it shows that this process operation is flexible and easy to management,the treatment effects are stable,the quality of the effluent reached the second grade of the Integrated Wastewater Discharge Standard(GB8978-1996) and the Wei River System(Shaanxi section) Integrated Wastewater Discharge Standard(DB61/224-2006).The process meets the big fluctuation of Chinese medicine pharmaceutical wastewater.
