Thought for Constructing and Developing of Chinese Government Website
Analyses the actuality of Government Website in China, points the problem of constructing and developing of Chinese Government Website. Being a member of constructing Government Website, to solve those existing problems, brings forward some research and thought, comprise giving publicity to people, advancing innovation of government system and improve the information developing level of civil servants、starting from service, bringing along social information, taking collectivity grasping of development, paying attention to security of network and information.
