How to extract isoscalar transition rates from alpha particle scattering experiments
Various methods proposed for determining isoscalar transition rates are briefly discussed and demonstrated by an application to 146 MeVα-particle scattering from16O for excitation energies up to theE2 giant resonance region. Satchler's theorem relating the moments of a folded potential to moments of the nucleon distributions has been empirically studied for various cases of 104 MeVα-particle scattering.
