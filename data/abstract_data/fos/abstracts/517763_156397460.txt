Pull and Push:The Dual-drive of Migration Employment for Post-graduates
In the background of the great expansion of higher education and rapid development of China's economic,lots of college graduates prefer to working in metropolises such as Beijing,Shanghai,Guangzhou and so on,rather than undeveloped districts in the middle and western.The results show that both reservation wage and expected wage can improve the probability of post-graduates' actual migration employment significantly which means that it is the double-drive,the pull power by high expected wage and the push power by high reservation wage,that stimulates post-graduates' migration to metropolises for employment.Based on such empirical results,the authors suggests that the employers of middle and western districts should focus on creating rewarding opportunities for career development of the high-quality human resources as much as they can,rather than sticking to the stereotypes of merely raising wages,thus making it clear that even though they cannot take advantages of starting wages here,however,the future developing perspective and stage are much greater and brighter than those in metropolises.
