A Modified Algorithm of Image Segmentation for Bearing Surface DefectsBased on K-means
To realize the detection of the Roller bearing automatic completely,the method for Roller bearing surface defect by digital image processing w as researched.A method for Roller bearing detection under complex background is proposed on the basis of two dimensional multi-wavelet and K-means algorithm.The feature parameters of detection image region were extracted by two dimensional multi-wavelet.According to features of the surface defect,the method extracts it from the input images by means of a modified K-means dynamic clustering analysis algorithm.The experiment results prove that the FKM method takes on an applicable value,because the accuracy is also improved as well as it's computing velocity.The influence of background,light illumination,angle and altitude on detection is very small and the method has good robustness.
