The role of guide-book to the classics in the teaching of the humanities in universities
The set modes of modern science have led the teaching of humanities in universities to the Essence and restoration deviation, resulting in the rejection of reading the original works by various modes of outlines or summaries. Text reading is not only a training of academic history and knowledge of certain majors but also a training of more general humane method and quality. The ability of reading and studying the classics is a fundamental standard to evaluate an art student; and the possession of satisfactory course system of guide\|book to the classics is one of the fundamental standards to evaluate the teaching of humanities in universities.
