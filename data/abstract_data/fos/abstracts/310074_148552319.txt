On the Construction of the Future City from the Ecological Perspective
As the deterioration of environment has threatened our living space,more and more people began to realize the importance of protecting our environment.With their unique view on ecological civilization,Eco-Marxists have made great contribution to the construction of eco-city.They advocate the cyclical mode of green economy,on the basis of scientific innovation,of humanistic planning of the city,and of rational consumption.Under the general background of environmental protection from all over the world,it is of great significance for China to build ecological city.By keeping the value of environment in due respect,we can achieve rapid economic development without destroying our environment and consequently be able to build a brand new ecological city,where the conflict between man and nature has been eventually resolved.
