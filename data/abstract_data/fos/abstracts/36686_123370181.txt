Using ion post-treatment technique to improve laser-induced damage threshold of thin films
ZrO_2 thin films were deposited on K9 glass substrates by e-beam evaporation method and some samples were treated with low energy O~+_2 after deposition. Surface weak absorption was measured using surface thermal lens technique, and micro-defect density was measured using optical microscope. The experimental results indicate that the absorption of the samples decreases from 1.147×10~(-4) to 9.56×10~(-5) after post-treatment, and the micro-defect density reduces from 18.6/mm~2 to 6.2/mm~2 compared with the un-treated samples. Anti-laser-irradiation test indicates that the laser-induced damage threshold are improved from 15.9 J/cm~2 to 23.1 J/cm~2 comparing the ion-post treatment samples with the untreated samples. By analysis of the defect density, weak absorption, and damage morphologies of the samples, it is found that the reduction of the micro-defect and sub-micro-defect density after ion treatment, so the reduction of the average absorption, and the enhancing of the adhesion of films and substrates are the main reasons that the laser-indeced damage threshold of the films increases.
