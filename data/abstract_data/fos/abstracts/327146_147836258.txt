From the Unit System to the Project System:The Strategic Transformation of Higher Education Key Construction
The strategic transformation of higher education key construction has been changing from the unit system to the project system.The key construction of universities has obvious characteristics of"the unit system"."The 211 project"and "the 985 project"has part of the characteristics of"the project system".And"the 2011 plan"has significant features of"project system".The basic idea of strategic transformation of China's higher education is to weaken or stop the implementation of"the unit system"and deepen and expand the implementation of"the project system".In the process of transformation,it should expand international vision,draw lessons from international experience,strengthen the system construction,and intensify the innovation of mechanism and system.
