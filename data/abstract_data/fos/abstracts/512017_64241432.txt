Approach for Hardware Device Failures Checking
The reliability of hardware devices is of critical importance to the overall stability of operating systems.Devices assume that hardware devices always work normally,but this assumption may cause driver or system crashes,since hardware device failures occur not infrequently.Hardware device may generate interrupt-related bugs,such as stuck or missing interrupts.This kind of bugs can make the kernel of the operating system work abnormally.Thus,checking interrupt-related bugs can significantly improve the reliability of operating systems.This paper introduces an approach for interrupt-related bugs checking.It can detect stuck interrupts and missing interrupts.This approach can reach the target with low cost and high accuracy.
