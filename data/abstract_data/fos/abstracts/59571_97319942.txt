NMR study of Ba2+ ion motion in one-dimensional ionic conductor with hollandite-type structure
Ionic motion of a divalent cation, Ba 2+ , in a single crystal of Ba−Al-priderite was studied using 27 Al as an NMR probe. Several paris of satellite peaks due to electric quadrupole interaction were observed superposed on broad satellite tails on both sides of the main peak of 27 Al. These peak pairs indicate the existence of some stable three-dimensional configurations of Ba 2+ ions in the structure, and the broad shoulders show a random substitution of Al 3+ for Ti 4+ sites. The temperature dependence of the spinlattice relaxation time T 1 * measured in the temperature range from 161 to 1176 K was analyzed by a curve fitting method on the assymption that there are two types of Ba 2+ ions. An activation energy of 0.47 eV was obtained for the motion of Ba 2+ ions which are easy to move, and a broad distribution of activation energies spread over a range from 0.95 to 2.45 eV was obtained for the motion of Ba 2+ ions which form a three-dimensional honeycomb framework and are hard to move.
