Long-lasting effects of IMHV lesions on social preferences in domestic fowl.
Imprinting leads to strong filial attachment to the mother. Later it influences mate choice. Although nothing is known about the brain regions involved in adult sexual preferences, a particular region of the domestic chick's forebrain (IMHV) has been implicated in filial imprinting.
