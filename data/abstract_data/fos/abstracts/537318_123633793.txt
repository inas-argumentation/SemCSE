0+ analogue state in 118Sb from 117Sn(p,nγ) reaction
The analogue of the 0+ ground state in 118Sn has been observed in the compound nucleus 118Sb through 117Sn(p,n γ) 117Sb reaction. The neutron decays of this analogue resonance have been studied from the deexciting γ-rays of the residual nucleus 117Sb. From off resonance excitation functions, spin assignments have been made to states in 117Sb, on the basis of Hauser-Feshbach formalism. The resonance parameters of the isobaric analogue resonance have been determined, including the total, proton and neutron decay widths.
