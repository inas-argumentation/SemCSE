Towards a Self-adaptive,Efficient and Low-cost Maya-pyramid Like P2P Overlay
Locating content in an efficient and low-cost way without centralized control is one of the challenges within P2P computing. The paper presents a Maya pyramid like P2P overlay (MPO) where update and backup only take place in two neighboring levels or layers so that the maintenance cost is less, differing from the recursive method in traditional hierarchical P2P overlay structure. Unlike normal redundant mechanisms for solving the single fault problem: Tri-Information Center (Tri-IC) mechanism is presented in order to improve robustness by alleviating the load of cluster heads in a hierarchical P2P overlay. A source ranking scheme is proposed in order to discourage free riding and to encourage frequent information exchanges between peers. Evaluation proved that MPO is robust, highly efficient and of a low-cost.
