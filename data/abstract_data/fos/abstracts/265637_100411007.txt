Simultaneous determination of six polyphenols in tobaccos by high performance liquid chromatographic method
A high-performance liquid chromatographic(HPLC) method is applied into simultaneous determination of six polyphenols present in tobacco samples.Three channels of diode array detection are simultaneously employed in the detection,which ensure reliability of results.The mobile phases,which are maintained at a slightly weak acid state,can easily separate the six polyphenols at a gradient condition.During extraction,accelerated solvent extraction(ASE) apparatus,one of the most powerful extraction system,is introduced into the experiment,which performs at 40℃,and uses anhydrous methanol involving 0.5% ascorbic acid(w/v) as extraction solvent,for extraction of target analytes in order to avoid its degradation and keep a higher extraction efficiency.A lab-made clean-up cartridge is used for cleaning up of extracts,which can effectively eliminate the effluence of the interferents on determination and lipophilic compounds on analytical column.The proposed method is very suitable for determination of polyphenols in tobaccos because recoveries for all analytes exceed 84%,and relative standard deviations(RSDs) are lower than 5%.
