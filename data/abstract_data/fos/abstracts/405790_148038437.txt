On the Statute Content of the Higher Vocational Institution
The statute of the higher education institution falls within national laws and regulations,and acts as the supreme law and the guiding principle in the system of higher education institution.The Higher Education Act and Interim Regulations on Developing the Higher Education Institution Statute offer the reference and basis for the statute content arrangement.The higher vocational institution as a special type of higher education institutions,its statute content should reflect the common requirements of higher education institution statute,and also highlight its characteristics.The framework of the statute should include such seven aspects as the legal characteristics of institution,the school-running idea,the school-running mission,rights and obligations of stakeholders,the basic system,inheritance and innovation thoughts of campus culture,management of people,money and material.
