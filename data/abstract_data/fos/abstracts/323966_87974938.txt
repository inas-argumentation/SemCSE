Research and development of reaction oxygen species in fruits quality deterioration during post-harvest periods
This paper reviewed the related concepts of the reactive oxygen species(ROS) including the varieties,generation and metabolism,as well as its biological effects in post-harvest of fruits.Moreover,the relationships among ROS metabolism,senescence,disease and chilling were discussed.We pointed out a forecast and outlook for the role ROS metabolism ragulation played during fruits storage,which provided an significant theoretical base for the post-harvest storage of fruits.
