The restoration and recreation of education: on education itself
Since the problem of the present education is deviated from education itself,education must be restored and recreated.Therefore education is able to refresh its vitality and vigor.Education itself is the essence of the education.Education should treated people as the real people who have their humanities.Thus education can appeal and cast people's spirit.Realizing the education itself must dependent on the teachers who make use of their whole life to motivate,liberate and promote the life of the students.
