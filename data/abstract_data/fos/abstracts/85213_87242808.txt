Seasonal dynamic of pests and natural enemy communities in chestnut orchard
Based on the nutritional relationship and system taxonomy,the arthropod communities of chestnut orchard were divided into four sub-communities,which were phytophages,predators,patasitoids and neutralities.Every sub-community was divided into different functional groups.A number of communities were used to explore the structure and the seasonal dynamics of community.The results showed that there were 178 species belonging to 101 families.The relative abundance of phytophages was 0.9330,from April to October,which was 0.7903~0.9698;in November which was the lowest(0.1509).The relative abundance of predators was 0.0406,from April to October which was 0.0202~0.1954;in November which was the highest(0.8409).The dominant concentration of arthropod communities was 0.2564.The dominant concentration of sub-communities was phytophagespredatorsparasitoids.The diversities and evenness of phytophages showed the same tendency with the total community,from low to high,then low and high again.The diversity and evenness of predator sub-community was mainly steady,with occasionally reducing.
