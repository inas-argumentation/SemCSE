Research on Teaching of University Computer Basic Course
Indicates a few layers and the teaching point of the university computer basic course, from the point of view of teaching methods, studies and puts forward: to know the creation of the computer from the technical evolution and its development, from the completion of the homework mission to know the computer software, from scientific and technical incompleteness to know the computer virus, from predictability to know the database management system, from the human information communication to know soul of the Internet, from the file layout to know the multi-media technology.
