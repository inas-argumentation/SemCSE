HPLC-DAD determination of two lactone form statins and their related substances
To investigate the hydrolysis of two lactone form statins,high performance liquid chromatography-diode array detection(HPLC-DAD) method was developed to determine lovastatin and simvastatin and their related substances simultaneously.Waters Symmetry C18 column(4.6mm×150mm,5μm) was used and the mobile phase was a mixture of acetonitrile-sodium dihydrogen phosphate(60:40),flow rate 1.0mL/min,detected at 238nm.Under proper conditions,lovastatin and simvastatin could be hydrolyzed to their corresponding β-hydroxy acids,respectively.The linear ranges of four related substances were determined by HPLC-DAD method.The method is simple,quick and accurate,can be used to the quality control of synthetic simvastatin as well as the lovastatin fermentation process.
