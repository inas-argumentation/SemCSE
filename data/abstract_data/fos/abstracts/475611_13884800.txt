DUAL SUPERFLUID-BOSE-GLASS CRITICAL POINT IN TWO DIMENSIONS AND THE UNIVERSAL CONDUCTIVITY
The continuum version of the dual theory for a system of two-dimensional quantum disordered bosons with statistical particle-hole symmetry and at $T\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}=\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}0$ admits a new disordered critical point within the renormalization group calculation at fixed dimension. We obtain the universal conductivity and the critical exponents at the superfluid-Bose glass transition in the system: ${\ensuremath{\sigma}}_{c}\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}=\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}0.25(2e{)}^{2}/h$, $\ensuremath{\nu}\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}=\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}1.38$, and $z\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}=\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}1.93$, to the lowest order in fixed-point values of the dual coupling constants.
