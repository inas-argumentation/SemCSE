An Empirical Study on the Relationship between Independent Directors' Background and Bank Performance:Evidence from 16 Listed Chinese Banks
This paper did an empirical analysis on the impact of independent directors' background on bank performance based on the data of China's 16 listed banks from 2008 to 2012.It shows that education background of independent directors does not have any significant positive effects on bank performance,yet the highly-educated independent director,especially with a PhD degree,can improve commercial bank's risk management and reduce non-performing loan ratio.The overseas experiences of independent directors have significant positive effects on bank performance.The independent directors who have the experiences in colleges and scientific research institutions,financial institutions or top management can improve bank performance significantly.The multiple directorships of independent directors have negative effects on bank performance,while it is helpful to improve bank performance with appropriately prolonged independent director's tenure. Women independent directors have negative effects on bank performance,and the age of independent directors has no significant effects on bank performance.
