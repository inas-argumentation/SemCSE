Study on Hot Extrusion Forming Process of Thin-wall Tee for Nuclear Power Station
Production technologies of thin-wall and thick-wall tee are different. The thin-wall tee is usually made by hot extrusion forming process with repetitious hardening in one die. Through a mass of technological tests, the key parameters of hot extrusion forming process for thin-wall tee were obtained: the wall thickness of the raw materials of thin-wall is chosen according to the designed wall-thickness of the tee and the raw diameter is calculated by equal altitude method; it is suitable that each pass reduction of thin-walled tee is about 2 times the reduction of the original material thickness; it is appropriate that quenching water depth is as high as bottom-die's sideline.
