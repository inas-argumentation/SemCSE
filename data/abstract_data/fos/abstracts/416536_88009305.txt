Effects of different extract oil way to draw the corn germ meal protein
The experiment uses corn germ meal which are extracted from different oil extraction ways of compression,solvent immersion method and supercritical CO2 extraction,and draw protein from them.Hydrolysis of samples by cellulose amylase and α-amylase hydrolysis,then concentrates preparation through the isoelectric point of protein extraction technology.The results show that the supercritical CO2 extraction of corn germ meal are most conducive to germ protein concentrate extraction,the following is solvent extraction method,and the last is the compression.
