Effects of green tea leaf shaking technique on oolong tea quality.
In order to investigate the effects of green tea leaf shaking technique on oolong tea quality,the same batch of fresh tea leaves was shaked respectively with various times(two times,four times and six times) in spring,summer and fall with green tea leaf cooling(liangqing) as the control.All groups were manufactured into oolong tea with the traditional technology at the same processing conditions.GC-MS analysis results have showed that the quantity of aroma components and the amount of essential oil of the fresh tea leaves by six shaking in three seasons were better than others as well as the order as six timesfour timestwo times.Moreover,The results of sensory evaluation also showed that the quality of oolong tea by six shaking was better than that of others and the fall-season oolong tea with six shaking was the best.
