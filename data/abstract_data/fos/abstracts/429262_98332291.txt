Bisindoles 34. Preparation and reactions of dehydrazides of the benzo[e]pyrrolo[3,8-g]-indole and indolo[4,5-e]indole series
Summary2, 9 -Di (carbohydrazide)-1 H, IOH-benzo[elpyrrolo[3, 2-g]indole and 2, 9-di (carbohydrazide)-3H, 8H-indolo[4, 5-e]indole have been prepared. Nitrosation of the dihydrazides has been effected. The aryl azides do not undergo the curtius rearrangment.
