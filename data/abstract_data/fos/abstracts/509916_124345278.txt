Brief reference is made to three-dimensional solutions in rectangular coordinates. Introduction. Vector and scalar wave equations appear in electrodynamics, in elastodynamics, and in many other problems involving radiation of energy. During an attempt to formulate, in closed form, the natural modes of elastic vibration of an isotropic solid cylinder of traction-free surfaces and finite dimensions, the writer uncovered some non-separable, simple harmonic solutions that are not described in the standard works [1-8] on partial and ordinary differential equations. This brief paper is a presentation of these solutions in circular cylindrical coordinates, as befit the geometry of the elastic problem, and the paper concludes with some remarks about non-separable solutions in rectangular coordinates.
