Effect of high voltage electric field on SOD activity by ultraviolet spectra
After the Superoxide dismutase(SOD) was treated with different strength of high voltage electric field,activity of SOD and ultraviolet spectra were measured.The study showed that the activity of SOD was decreased by the certain strength of electric field.The ultraviolet spectra showed that conformation was greatly changed by high voltage electric field.The activity decrease possibly was caused by the conformation variations of SOD.The result of this study has important meaning to explain the macroscopical electrostatic biological effects.
