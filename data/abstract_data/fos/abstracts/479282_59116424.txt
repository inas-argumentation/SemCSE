Are You Ready for Self-CIC? Subtitle: Assessment Instrument to Check the Patient's Readiness and Ability for Self-CIC Program
Purpose One of the main tasks for a paediatric urological nurse engaged in children with Meningomyelocele is the urinary continence management. The nurse is responsible to teach the self-CIC program to the child and his parents. The first step in the program is to identify the child's and family's readiness and ability to learn the self-CIC. Therefore the nurse has to know the essential elements for a successful CIC. The aim of this small literature review was to research the main prerequisites a child has to present for a successful learning of self-CIC. Material and Methods This small literature review was conducted in the database Medline(R) and in the urological paediatric Literature. Following key words have been used: paediatric, children, nursing, CIC, urinary incontinence, key elements, assessment, spinal cord dysfunction, neuropathic bladder, patient education, bladder management, motivational components, skills, ability, instruction. Results The result of the small review is an assessment instrument for children and families starting with the self CIC-Program. The assessment explores four main categories: motivation, cognitive ability, physical mobility and manual ability. Conclusions This instrument is used since about six month in the University Children's Hospital in Zurich and should be evaluated in 2008.
