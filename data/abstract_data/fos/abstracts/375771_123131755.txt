Charge-independent effective interactions for shell-model studies in the 0g9/2-1p1/2 space
:The matrix elements of the effective Hamiltonian in the 0g9/2-1p1/2 space are determined by a least-square fit to the energies of 477 levels of nuclei with 38≤Z≤50 and 47≤N≤50. The results of the calculation are found to be in better agreement with experiment than those obtained with previously determined interactions.
