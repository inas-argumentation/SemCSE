An empirical study on the assessment system for university middle-level official based on mutil-criterion group decision-making
The assessment system for university middle-level official was built according to the assessment feature of university middle-level official.The weights of assessment indexes were determined by analytical hierarchy process and group decision-making.An empirical study on the assessment for a university middle-level official was given by using mutil-criterion group decision-making based on TOPSIS,and the assessment results can disclose exactly the achievements for university middle-level official.This method had reference value due to its strong maneuverability and agility.
