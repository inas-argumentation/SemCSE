The long term mechanical behaviour of filled and unfilled injection moulded thermotropic liquid crystalline polymers
Injection mouldings of thermotropic liquid crystalline polymers (TLCPs) have complex, highly anisotropic layered structures, and correspondingly large spatial variations in orientation and mechanical properties. The room temperature axial mechanical properties of standard tensile test specimens moulded from TLCPs are remarkable (ten-fold and two-fold increases in modulus and strength respectively, compared with conventional resins).
