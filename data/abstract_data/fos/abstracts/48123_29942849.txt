An uncommon cause of melena--left gastric artery pseudoaneurysm.
A 40-year-old man presented to the emergency department with abdominal pain and melena. His past medical history was significant for several episodes of chronic alcoholic pancreatitis over the past 5 years. He had undergone endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography and pancreatic duct stenting 1 month prior.
