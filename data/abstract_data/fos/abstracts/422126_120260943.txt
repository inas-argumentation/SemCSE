Adiabatic wall focusing of intense ion beams for the ignition of thermonuclear microexplosions
It is shown that an intense can beam can be adiabatically and spherically symmetric focused onto a center of convergence by a Prandtl-Meyer flow inside a curved concave wall. The method can be extended to more than one wall and several beams and can be applied to the ignition of thermonuclear microexplosions.
