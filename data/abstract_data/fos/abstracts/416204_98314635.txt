The Liquid-Phase Photolyses of Tetra- and Octa-CDDs and Their CDFs in Hexane Solution
The liquid phase photolyses were conducted with a hexane solution of tetra- and octa-CDD and their CDFs at 254 nm. Their first-order rate constants obtained were as follows(× 10−5 s−1): 2,3,7,8-TCDD, 29.2; 1,2,3,4-TCDD, 13.1; 2,3,7,8-TCDF, 46.0; octa-CDD, 17.7. The PCDF photolyses of 2,3,7,8-tetra- and octa-congeners compared to their CDDs were more rapidly, as well as the photodestructions of 2,3,7,8-chlorine substituted DD and DF which are more highly toxic substances.
