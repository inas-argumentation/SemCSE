1H, 2D and alkali n.m.r. studies of alkali naphthalene ion pairs
1 H, D and alkali resonances have been performed on alkali naphthaJene ion pairs in ethereal solution. Special attention is devoted to the electron spin relaxation time Te. It proved to be proportional to the inverse of the ion pair concentration, but not to the viscosity divided by the absolute temperature, as is predicted by the Pake-Tuttle relationship. An explanation for this behaviour is given in terms of polymolecular collision processes. The alkali resonances provided information on the sign of the alkali hyperfine splitting constants and on the contribution of the various relaxation mechanisms to the line widths. A line width analysis demonstrated that the ion pairs can be considered as static ion pairs, except for sodium naphthalene, where ion pair equilibria play an essential role.
