Study on synthesis of p-aminophenol from catalytic hydrogenation of the nitrobenzene
The synthesis process of p aminophenol from catalytic hydrogenation of the nitrobenzene is introduced, and the factors for effecting synthesis process are approached. The best synthesis process condition is determined. In the condition that the reaction temperature is 85℃, the concentration of sulphuric acid is 17%, the volume ratio of sulphuric acid and nitrobenzene is 6.6∶1, the pressure of hydrogen is 0.2MPa, the reaction time is 2 hours, the conversion of the nitrobenzene to p aminophenol reaches to 81.9% with selectivity of 98.9%.
