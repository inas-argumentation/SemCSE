In keeping with East Anglia’s sacramental theology, the Digby Magdalene undergoes, not a mere change of personality, but rather transubstantiation. Her outward appearance remains the same, but, “like the transubstantiated bread of the Sacrament,” her body becomes “the locus of her new sacred power” (217). As a dramatic embodiment of the mystery at the heart of Christian faith, the Digby Mary Magdalene encapsulates the wonder and richness of late-medieval English religious drama.
