Comments on the papers by Elliott and Wakefield, Wartenberg, Stein et al. and Steward and John
Helen Dolk (London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine) In this section, two papers present the authors' views on why, when and how to investigate clusters (Elliott and Wakefield, and Wartenberg) (with emphasis on chronic diseases) and two papers present examples of cluster investigations (Stein et al. and Steward and John). Both examples concern clusters detected following public concern about local environmental exposures. Epidemiologically, a cluster investigation tends to follow two main phases.
