A Study of the Components of the Good Faith Obtaining System
This paper discusses the good faith obtaining system. As one old and important part of civil law, its components have already become complete. But with the development of social economy, the three components of this system, including previous owner, buyer and object, have changed. They should also be applicable to real estate and moveable property mortagage. Based on our study, the author thinks there are conflicts between the good faith obtaining system and the guaranteeing law.
