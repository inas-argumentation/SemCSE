The Genetic Characterization of Echovirus 13 Isolated in China
2 strains of enterovirus were isolated from Fujian Province in China in 1998 and 2000.The 2 isolates,Fujian98-1 and Fujian00-1,were Echovirus 13 identified by WHO panel of enterovirus sera using neutralization assay.The complete VP1 sequences of the 2 isolates were successfully amplified by RT-PCR with two enterovirus general primer pairs.The whole coding region of VP1 of the Fujian98-1 and Fujian00-1 were sequenced and analyzed.861 nucleotides of VP1 coding gene of the 2 Fujian isolates were compared with other Echovirus 13 isolates available from GenBank.The highest nucleotide homogeneity of Fujian98-1 and Fujian00-1 were 80.0% and 88.0% with French strain CF1083-91,and 75.8%,77.9% with Echovirus 13 reference strain(Del Carmen)respectively.The two strains from Fujian Province were thus clearly identified as Echovius 13 that agreed with neutralization result.The homogeneity analysis showed that the 2 Fujian isolates shared 79.6% similarity in nucleotide level and 93.4% in amino acid level.Phylogenetic tree based on complete VP1 sequence assigned the two isolates to different branches,which indicated there were at least two transmitting chains of Echovirus 13 prevailed in Fujian Province.We also found there were 3 genotypes(A,B and C)of Echovirus 13,the diversity of complete VP1 sequence among them was more than 20%.In order to confirm the genotyping,a phylogenetic tree based on the 5′ terminus of VP1 gene was constructed,including 26 strains of Echovirus 13 from different countries and different times.As the result,the Fujian strains,Fujian98-1 and Fujian00-1,still belonged to genotype B and genotype C respectively.With one exception,the diversity of genotype cluster from each other was more than 15%.So far,this is the first report about sequencing data and analysis of VP1 gene of Echovirus 13 isolated in China.
