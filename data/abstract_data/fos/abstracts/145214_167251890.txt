The perspective for competition - power of Guangdong
The paper researches the competition characteristic of Guangdong in Nation Games and Olympics Games completely by using event-group as unit and time as background.The result indicates: in the proceed of the number of 4-9 Nation games,physique dominated event-group and difficulty and beauty expression event-group are the traditional superiority event-group.And the competition power of physique dominate event-group is the foundation that made of the all competition power,analyzing the lasting and newly project that willbeeome the subdue project,it is found that the long-distant swiming and shooting project are the first projects,and the second projects are long-distant running,table-tennis and Judo project; and only lifting and diving is subdue project in Olympics Games.And all these point out that the necessary of stall rationally on the basis of the characteristic of event-group.
