Relationship of Motor Development to Visual-Spatial Cognitive Growth
1The study was conducted in cooperation with the Los Angeles Child Amputee Project. 2The author is grateful to Ronald W. French and James Scott for collecting the data for this study. The assertion that perceptual-motor development underlies conceptual growth is central to the theoretical formulations of Piaget (1971) and to the remediation rationales and techniques advocated by Barsch (1965), Delacato (1966), Kephart (1971), and Getman (1962). The medical and educational literature is replete with articles discussing the results of inconclusive experiments and arguments supporting and denying this assertion.
