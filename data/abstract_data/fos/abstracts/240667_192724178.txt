A Study on Poetry Development and Developing the Useful and Discarding the Useless of the Old Standard and Rhythm Poem
The development of poem form of our country is a course of inheriting and developing, which include The Book of Songs, The Chu Songs, the Tang Dynasty Poems, the Song Dynasty Songs and the Yuan Dynasty Music--the standard and rhythm from nothing to birth, from strict to loose. However, after the Ming Dynasty and the Qing Dynasty, the poetry development did not form a new pattern which can be narrated in the history, but becomes more and more depressing nowadays. It has two reasons. One is the colloquialism poem abandon the technique of expression and the standard and rhythm of the old poem completely; the other is the old poem affirmed completely its standard and rhythm. Neither of them takes the attitude of developing the useful and discarding the useless. Based on the analysis of the reason of the depressing state of the new poem and the old poem and based on the requirement of epoch, the author discusses the fundamentals and method of creating poems with the new standard and rhythm.
