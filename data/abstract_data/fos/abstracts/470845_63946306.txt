Research on Information Security Risk Assessment by AHP Approach
Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) is a quantitative method to solve problems of multi-objective decision-making, which can be used to analyze the risk assessment of information security systematically and quantitatively. With the theory of information security, AHP models can be built. With the AHP software, Expert Choice, calculation and analysis of AHP can be finished, then find where the risks are from, and make an effective solution.
