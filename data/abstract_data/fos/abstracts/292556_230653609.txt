Three-Dimensional Printing of Tetrafunctional Polylactide Using Ciprofloxacin Derivatives as Photoinitiators
Hydrophobic derivatives of the antibacterial drug ciprofloxacin are synthesized and used as photoinitiators of two-photon polymerization. A way of selecting 3D printing parameters and the effect they have on scaffold topography are described for photosensitive compositions based on tetrafunctional poly(D,L-lactide) and ciprofloxacin derivatives.
