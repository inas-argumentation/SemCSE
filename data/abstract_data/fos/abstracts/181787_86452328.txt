A Novel Hilbert Fractal Antenna For RFID Tag
With the rapid development of RFID technology,finding for some antenna structure with size reduction characteristic becomes the actual needs of RFID design,Hilbert fractal structure is an effective solution on the design of miniature antenna.In this paper,the basic principle of Hilbert fractal antenna is introduced,and then it uses the Hilbert fractal structure in meander line dipole antenna.The proposed antenna has the physical size of 25.6mm by 16.2mm,which is designed for UHF RFID systems,to have the operating frequency bandwidth of 870MHz~960MHz.The antenna performs well at the desired frequency of operation,and it can be applied to the security RFID tags in wine.
