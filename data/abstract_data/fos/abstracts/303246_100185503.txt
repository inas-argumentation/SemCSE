Syntheses of Diruthenaborane Clusters, [{Cp*Ru(μ-H)}2B3H7], [{(Cp*Ru)2(μ-H)}B4H9], and [(Cp*Ru)2(μ-H)(PMe3)(μ-η4-B2H5)]
A diruthenapentaborane cluster [{Cp*Ru(μ-H)}2B3H7] (1) was synthesized by the reaction of [Cp*RuCl]4 with BH3·THF. Compound 1 undergoes cluster expansion by further interaction with BH3·THF to give [{(Cp*Ru)2(μ-H)}B4H9] (2). Reaction of 1 with PMe3 leads to degradation of the cluster framework to give [{(Cp*Ru)2(μ-H)}(PMe3)(μ-η4-B2H5)] (3).
