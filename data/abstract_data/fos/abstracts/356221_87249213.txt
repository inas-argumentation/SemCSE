Morphology study of acetylated starch granules with chemical gelatinization
Two acetylated potato starch samples,with degree of substitution 0.0472 and 0.0560,were separated by sieving into three different size groups(30 μm,30～50 μm,50 μm).The samples of a uniform granule size(30～50 μm) were treated with 4 mol/L CaCl2 at 20 ℃.The morphological changes of the granules were observed with polarized light microscope.The result illuminates that acetylated potato starch granules could be gelatinized by 4 mol/L CaCl2 from the periphery to the core in a controllable way.Chemical gelatinized starch at the periphery was machanically removed from the remaining granule.There were a few sagging areas on the surface of the remaining granules,indicating the structure of the granule was heterogeneous.Chemical gelatinization could be an effective method in studying the granular structure of acetylated starch.
