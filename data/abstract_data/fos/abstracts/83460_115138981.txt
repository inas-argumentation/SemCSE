Coating based on breadfruit starch (Artocarpus altilis) for fruit conservation: influence of glycerol, sorbitol, and mannitol as plasticizers
There is a need to replace synthetic products as plastic coatings based on petrochemical materials with coatings based on natural products that are renewable or biodegradable for improving storage of plant products like fruits. The use of natural products as material for coating may decrease production costs, as well as being abundant in nature and renewable. Coatings based on polysaccharides are a versatile alternative to applications in the food industry, not just aiming to improve food conservation (Falcão-Rodrigues et al., 2007; Cazón et al., 2017; Nawab et al., 2017), but also to produce an environmentally friendly product.
