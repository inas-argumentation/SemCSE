Optimization of Structure Arrangement of Anode Preparation System in Copper Electrolysis
As key equipment in tankhouse,the performance and speed of anode preparation system directly affect availability and current efficiency in tankhouse.The authors analyzed the current structure arrangement of anode preparation system inside and outside China and have put forward a new arrangement of production lines in parallel connection in view of the shortcomings in existing structure arrangement of anode preparation system inside and outside China.The optimized arrangement effectively improves the availability in tankhouse and extends the service life of milling cutter and therefore will be applied widely in the future.
