Effect of Water Stress on Endogenous Hormone Content in Litchi Leaves
The 2-year-old seedlings of two litchi varieties (Litchi chinensis Sonn.), one grow-ing on the river side (considered drought-sensitive) and the other on the hill (drought-resis-tant) were used to observe the effect of water stress on endogenous hormone content in litchileaves. Under water stress the content of ABA in litchi leaves increased in both varietiesbut more in the drought resistant vanety, and the content of IAA and GA1+3 in litchi leavesdecreased in both varieties but mure rapidly in the drought-resistant variety. The ZR contentin the litchi leaves, however, declined in drought-resistant variety but increased in thedrought-sensitive one.
