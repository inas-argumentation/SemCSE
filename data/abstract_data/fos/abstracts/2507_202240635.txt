Effective University Governance:The Experience of Western Universities and Their Inspiration
To improve the effectiveness of university governance is the pursuit of the governments and the universities all over the world.In order to ensure effective university governance formally,the principle of democratic participation allocation is used in the Western universities for responding to democratic demand.At the same time,in order to ensure effective university governance in essence,the principle of efficiency is run in the university decision-making body size and decision procedure.There are four key problems to solve in China universities.Firstly,it should expand the composing of the Party Committee members.Secondly,it should define the conference procedure of the Party Committee clearly.Thirdly,it should reform the composing of academic committee members,and its conference procedure be defined distinctly.At last,the conference program of the principal office should be defined expressly.
