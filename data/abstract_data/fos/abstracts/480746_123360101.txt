Submillimeter-band mixer utilizing superconducting point contacts
An investigation was made of the mixing of a 74 GHz klystron signal with HCN laser radiation (891 GHz) in superconducting Nb-Nb point contacts at 4.2°K. Anodized high-resistivity contacts were found to be efficient and stable mixers. A model was developed to explain the characteristics of the mixing of harmonics.
