Grid-based Uneven Clustering Routing Protocol for Wireless Sensor Network
The hot pot problem in wireless sensor network( WSN) can be solved by uneven clustering algorithm. In this paper,a gridbased uneven clustering algorithm( GBUC) for target tracking is proposed. The grid of netw ork area is divided by concentric-circles and rays. Based on the uneven clustering,the path of moving target can be predicted macroscopically. Furthermore,the node energy management is classified based on the prediction. This management makes some nodes sleep to reduce energy consumption on the premise of ensuring the tracking accuracy. Simulation results show that this new algorithm provides longer lifetime than the classical clustering algorithms,and the reduction of energy consumption reaches 63% more than LEACH.
