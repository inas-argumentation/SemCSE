Nonetheless, they generally agree that museums, archives, and the current work of scholars are resources that have great potential for improving the quality of history education. Artifacts and archival materials, thought-provoking concepts, and indepth studies from the work of practicing historians can broaden teachers’ knowledge, increase their skills in the interpretation of history, and help them to enrich and enliven curricula. The problem is how to make those resources available in a way that fits curricular needs and time constraints. The Progressive Industry and Its Legacy project’s main goal is helping secondary school teachers to improve the quality of history education in the classroom.
