Analysis of the Campus Network Community by Using SAS Time Series under Network Environment
The technology of data mining is the result of current information development, and it mainly uses all kinds of data channel to mine the latent knowledge and information, so emphasis more on the process. Now more and more latent knowledge need to mine in order to find more useful information. Makes an analysis for campus network community due to technology of SAS time series, investigates the station of campus network community, and will improve the management level of campus network according to these theoretical basis and decision support, it also gives a reference to information construction in colleges and universities.
