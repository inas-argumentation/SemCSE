Exploring Confucius’ Tragic Consciousness and His Transcending of It from The Analects of Confucius
This paper studies the text of the classical Confucian works The Analects of Confucius which embodies the core of Chinese traditional culture. We can find that the tragic consciousness and the spirit beyond such tragic consciousness are contained in Confucius’ philosophic thoughts from the three aspects of life, realization of individual value and emotional ethnics, that is, to stubbornly do something even though you know it is impossible. Just because of the influence of the spirit, Chinese people’s tragic consciousness gets self-digested to certain extent, and thus it casts the self-improving character of Chinese people that combines both hardness and softness.
