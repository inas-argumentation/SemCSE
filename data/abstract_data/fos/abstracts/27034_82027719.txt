Genotyping of hepatitis A virus strains recovered from different geographical regions in China.
In order to clarify the distribution of hepatitis A virus (HAV) genotypes in China, 17 HAV strains were isolated from fecal and serum specimens of hepatitis A patients in different geographical regions including Hangzhou Zhejiang, Qidong Jiangsu, Tongling Anhui, Kunming Yunnan and Shanghai. The nucleotide sequence of VP1/2A region of the 17 HAV isolates were tested by RT nPCR and direct sequencing. The nucleotide sequence diversity among all 17 HAV strains were less than 15%, but that in half of the isolates were greater than 7.5%. The results showed that epidemic or sporadic HAV strains isolated in China may belong to HAV genotype I, subgenotypes IA and IB.
