A homogeneous Markov model for phased-mission reliability characteristics
This paper deals with the analysis of a homogeneous Markov model for phased-mission reliability characteristics. A possible interpretation for the system is that of a satellite containing two computer units. The satellite is launched in phase 1, deployed in phase 2, and remains on station during phase 3. Depending upon the operational readiness of the satellite, it can be launched into one of two orbits, representing primary and secondary objectives, respectively. Repair is available during phase 1 and phase 3 of the secondary objective as long as one unit remains operational. The failure and repair rates of both the units are assumed to be constant. Distributions of the time to phase change are negative exponential. Using the regenerative point technique, various reliability measures are obtained.
