Emission Spectrum of the C2 Radical Embedded in Superfluid Helium around 1.5 K
The C2 d3Πg-a3Πu (v′−v″) Swan band emission spectrum induced by the laser ablation of graphite in the superfluid helium around 1.5 K, was observed. Only the vibrational progression of Δv=v′−v″, where Δv less than 0 are observed, indicating fast (τ<100 ns) vibrational relaxation in superfluid helium. This result also indicates a unique characteristic of superfluid helium as a circumstance of chemical processes.
