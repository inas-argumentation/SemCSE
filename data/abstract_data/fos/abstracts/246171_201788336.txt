This reader, at least, could hope that the author’s experience with epidemic disease might have led to a more thorough discussion of just what these epidemics entailed scientifically and medically, thus making more intelligible the racially repressive details that he privileges. But the questions he asks and the ways he answers them sensitize us to the scapegoating and medical victimization of a population associated with the eruption of devastating illness.
