Potassium nutrition on photosynthesis and chlorophyll fluorescence in Actinidia chinensis Planch leaves
We adopted four kinds of potassium level on Actinidia chinensis Planch cv “81-5”.The results showed that the NPK2 level increases content of chlorophyll,raises net photosynthesis rate(Pn),stomatal conductance(Gs)and water use efficiency(WUE)notably and reduce Chl-a/Chl-b ratio,transpiration rate(Tr),intercellular CO2 concentration(Ci)in A.chinensis Planch cv “81-5” leaves.The NPK2 and NPK3 level enable the Pn to reach the peak early in one day concentration and delay the “midday depression” of the plant effectively.The NPK1 and NPK2 level improve the value of Fv/Fm and Fv/Fo,activate the activity of electron transmits and the potential activation of PSⅡ and improve the efficiency of PSⅡ originally light energy transform effectively.Lacking or low potassium level weaken the ability of dissipating on absorbing the surplus light energy through the photochemical reaction way and non-radiation energy way.
