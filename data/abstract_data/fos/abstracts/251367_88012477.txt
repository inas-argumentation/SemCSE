Isolation and purification of the liver protective compounds in submerged fermentation by Cordyceps
The pharmacology experiment showed that the substance from the mycelia of fungal fermentation pretreated in the model in vivo can reduce the level of ALT,AST,LPO, suggesting a significant protective function to liver. The bioactive substances were purified through adsorption chromatography, gel chromatography and so on. The purity tests of HG-1 and HG-2 by TLC, analytical HPLC and chemical methods proved that they were both single substance.
