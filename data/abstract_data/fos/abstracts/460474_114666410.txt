Fault Line Selection Method Based on Multiple Rejudge in the Resonant Grounding System
This paper aimed frequent occurrence of resonant grounding system phase-to-ground fault and difficult to locate the fault line,proposed a new method applying multiple rejudge technology to select the fault line.This method is combining two basic location methods,namely the zero sequence current phase comparison method and residual increment method,through the adjustment of reactance value of extinction coil to multiply rejudge the selection results in order to locate the fault feeder line.Through the EMTP simulation analysis and actual tests show that the method of fault feeder judgment is accurate,and has high robustness.
