Multifunctional Spin-carrying Anthraquinone Derivatives
Several nitroxide radicals derived from 2,3,6,7-tetraalkoxy-9,10-anthraquinone core have been prepared and their magnetic, redox as well as battery properties are studied. Notably, one of the derivatives with a mono-PROXYL-radical substituent exhibits a fairly stable multistep charge-discharge process and a heat-responsive magnetic behavior.
