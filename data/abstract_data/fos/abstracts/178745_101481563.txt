Study on o-chloroaniline production wastewater treatment with Fenton reagent
Fenton reagent is used to treat o-chloroaniline production wastewater with oxidation method.The influences of H2O2,Fe2+ dosage and pH value of reaction system on removal rate of COD are studied.Through the experiment,the optimal conditions are indicated such as pH=3,FeSO4·7H2O dosage is that the Fe2+ mass concentration in waste water is 0.56 g/L,and the dosage of H2O2 with 30% mass fraction is 18 mL in 1 L waste water.Under these optimal conditions,COD removal rate is 72.9%.
