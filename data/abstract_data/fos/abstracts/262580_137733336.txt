Effect of caldum phosphate cement on TNF-α and IL-6 expression in rabbits
Objective: To investigate the effect of calcium phosphate cement (CPC) on IL-6 and TNF-αexpression in rabbits. Methods: Models of hip arthroplasty were prepared in rabbits. ELISA was used to detect the expression of IL-6 and TNF-αat various times after implantation of bone cement. Results: Slight up-regulation of TNF-αexpression was detected at the time of reaming as compared with the control, while rapid increase of both TNF-αand IL-6 was observed from the end of surgery to 24h post-surgery. Conclusion: Implantation of CPC induced temporal expression of IL-6 and TNF-αin rabbit serum, indicating a possible role of CPC in evoking the immune response.
