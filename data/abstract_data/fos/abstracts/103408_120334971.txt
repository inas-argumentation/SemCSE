Falling liquid films in absorption machines
Abstract The absorption machines of a water-lithium bromide system have recently been established as heat source equipment for residential and industrial use, which includes refrigerating machines, heat pumps and heat transformers. Several advanced cycle machines have also been proposed and tested. All of the absorption machines consist fundamentally of four kinds of heat exchangers: evaporator, absorber, generator, and condenser. Horizontal or vertical types of falling film heat exchangers are usually employed, since the pressure drop which causes an undesirable change in the fluid temperature is relatively small. The horizontal type is popular at present, while the vertical type is being developed promisingly. The purpose of this paper is to survey the literature giving information on heat and mass transfer enhancement in the heat exchangers of absorption machines. Emphasis is placed on the hydrodynamic characteristics of falling liquid films in absorbers and generators.
