Optimization of green tea processed cheese processing conditions
Using natural cheese and green tea powder as raw materials, a green tea flavored processed cheese and its key parameters of processing were investigated. Processing conditions are the main factors on the quality of green tea processed cheese. The experiments showed the optimal processing conditions were: to melt at 85 ℃ for 5 min and stirring at 300 r/min, then cool it fast. The final products have good texture and excellent flavor.
