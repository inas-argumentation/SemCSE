The Measurement of Voidage of Nonuniformly Fluidized Bed by Means of the Wall Pressure Signal
By the wall pressure signal, ∆ p x , from a nonuniformly fluidized bed is here termed the excess pressure at a wall of open column over the atmospheric pressure measured at a distance x from distributor. The relation was investigated between the wall pressure signal, ∆p x , and some specifically defined voidages of a so-called standard bubbling bed characterized by the fact that the ratio of the experimentally determined pressure drop for minimum fluidization, ∆p 0,mf , and of the theoretical pressure drop, ∆ p t , is approximately equal to the unity.
