Municipal Sewage Treatment by Process of Improved Oxidation Ditch and Advanced Treatment Technology
Taking the urban sewage treatment plant in Luohe city as an example,process principle and operational characteristics of the improved oxidation ditch in municipal wastewater treatment were introduced simply and its operating conditions and treating effect were analyzed.The results show that removal rates of the main indicators of effluent,such as CODCr,BOD5,SS,NH4+-N,TN and TP,are 92.62%,95.51%,97.82%,90.53%,72.95% and 95.67%,respectively.Effluent quality reached the standard of "Pollutant discharge standard of urban sewage treatment plant(GB18918-2002) Table 1 Standard A" and met the requirement of water reuse completely.
