Constructing "Wenzhou model" of entrepreneurship education of undergraduates
Nowadays,the entrepreneurship education carried out by Chinese higher education institutes is still underway.Most borrowed from overseas experience and less based on our facts.Based on graduates' major facts and Wenzhou local facts,we make the advantage of Wenzhou to carry out entrepreneurship education in Wenzhou,leading inside requirement of undergraduates' entrepreneurship.Borrow from entrepreneurship spirits of citizens of Wenzhou,and train on entrepreneurship consciousness of undergraduates;borrow from entrepreneurship experiences of citizens of Wenzhou,and improve entrepreneurship ability of undergraduates.Combined with major and based on situation of Wenzhou,build a special office to construct "Wenzhou model" of entrepreneurship education and form a unique entrepreneurship education.
