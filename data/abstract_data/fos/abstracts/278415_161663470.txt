But, again, Tichi exerts so much of her energy making her case for design that she neglects content. After all, Williams' poems are finally "machines" made of words. Tichi's study is both delightful and exasperating. She is at her best detailing the effects of the industrial revolution on popular culture. Here she is original, provocative, and always interesting. Her discussion of the literature, however, suffers from too narrow a focus and from a failure to examine properly the issues she raises.
