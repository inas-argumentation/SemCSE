On curriculum arrangements og educational courses in higher normal education institutions based on teacher professional development
Educational courses in normal college have some problems such as unreasonable curriculum structure,incomplete curriculum content,not ideal results of teaching practice and so on."Academic nature" of curriculum offering and deeper effect of scientism and technological rationality are main reasons for the above problems.To design educational courses,high normal education institutions should consider practical demands of educational reforms which are implemented in elementary,secondary and high normal schools;and educational courses should be favor of integration process between pre-cultivating and post-training of teachers.Therefore,to improve teacher professional development,the higher normal education institutions should design conditional educational courses and practical educational courses.Conditional educational courses can improve theoretical quality of normal school students and strengthen their consciousness of educational study;and teaching practice can cultivate their professional techniques and promote the level of their professional development.
