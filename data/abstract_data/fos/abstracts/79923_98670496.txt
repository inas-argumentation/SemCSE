Comparative analysis of the surface mobility of protons and other single-charged cations on the surface of rutile-type oxides
Quantum mechanical modelling of the transport of sodium, lithium and hydrogen over the surface of oxides of group IV elements having rutile-type structure, showed that the structure and geometry of the conducting channels are primarily dependent on the nature of mobile cations, whereas energy barriers for transfer via these channels depends on both the size of the elementary cell of the oxide and the radius of the cation.
