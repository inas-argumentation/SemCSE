DSR Protocol with Small-world Characteristics and Collision Avoidance
Proposed an improving DSR protocol,called M-DSR protocol.At first,the scope of the routing request packet is limited to a certain area in the M-DSR protocol,and then it gradually expands the scope of the routing request in accordance with the actual situation.Meanwhile,it can avoid conflicts in accordance with the performance value of the node.The simulation results show that the M-DSR protocol can increase the speed of the routing request,reduce redundant information and avoid conflicts in local network,especially in the overhead routing load.
