Spurenanalyse von Cu in ZnS-Phosphoren mittels Neutronenaktivierung
The applicability of neutron activation analysis to the determination of copper impurities in ZnS has been investigated. The sensitivity of the method has been determined and the method of chemical separation, which is neccessary after irradiating the samples, is outlined.Because of the disturbing nuclear reaction Zn-64 (n, p) Cu-64 the sensitivity of the method does not only depend on the absolute amount of copper atoms available but also on the concentration of Cu in zinc sulphide. Using a thermal neutron flux of 3×1011 cm−2 sec−1, a ratio of thermal to resonance flux (gold)Φth/Θ=30 and a copper concentration of 1×10−4g Cu/g ZnS, it is possible to determine amounts of Cu as small as 3×10−8 g. Increasing the neutron flux means to improve the sensitivity of the analysis. Increased moderation of the neutron flux makes the analysis of ZnS with smaller copper contents possible (limiting value when using the Berlin Reaktor BER: 5×10−7 g Cu/g ZnS, withΦth/Θ=8000).
