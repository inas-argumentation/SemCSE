An Empirical Exploration into the Construction of Featured Database of Dabie Mountains Road of Anhui Agricultural University
Based on the researches on the significance and feasibility of constructing featured database of Dabie Mountains Road done in an earlier stage,the paper analyses the critical factors of constructing the database from the angles of the running platform of the featured database based on TPI system,the constructing principles,standards and norms,and the collection,filtering and integration of target data.
