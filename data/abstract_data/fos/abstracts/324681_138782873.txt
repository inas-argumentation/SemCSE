Reason Analysis on Micro-cracks in Medium and Heavy Plate of Q345A Steel
Micro-cracks in the plate have three kinds of abnormal microstructure,which are pearlite,bainite and martensite banded microstructure.The concentration of carbon and manganese was found in the abnormal microstructure by energy spectrum analysis,that is,the segregation of composition forms in the abnormal microstructure,the interfaces between steel basic phase and abnormal microstructure or inclusions such as MnS and nitride are the sources of micro-cracks,which can result in cracks by the tensile stress during cooling and straightening.
