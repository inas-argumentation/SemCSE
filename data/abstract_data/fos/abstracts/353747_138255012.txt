Effects of Heat Treatment Process on Mechanical Properties of 20CrNi2Mo Steel as Heavy Gear Material
Aiming at heat treating distortion for 20CrNi2Mo steel as heavy gear material,the effects of heat treatment on mechanical properties of 20CrNi2Mo steel were studied.The results show that the elongation,reduction of area and impact toughness are barely changed in new process,but the tensile strength,hardness and heat treating distortion are improved obviously by the new heat treatment process.It is feasible to use the new heat treatment for 20CrNi2Mo steel instead of the old heat treatment,which can lay down the foundation for expanding the spectrum of 20CrNi2Mo steel application.
