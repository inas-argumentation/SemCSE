Research on Network Individualized Teaching of Visual FoxPro Database Program Design
Making good use of network teaching platform is the important measures to improve teaching quality nowadays, but there are many problems with most network courses in individualized teaching. The key point to carry out individualized teaching is to organize curricula knowledge and navigate students' intelligence effectively. Puts forward the idea that ontology conceptual concept should be used to organize curricula knowledge of Visual FoxPro Program Design, on the basis of it, proposes a kind of intelligence navigation algorithm.
