Study on Transient Liquid-phase Bonding of TP304 Steel
TP304 steel pipes were joined by Transient Liquid Phase(TLP) bonding process using BNi2,Fe_(78)Si_9B_(13) and FeNiCrSiB filler in argon atmosphere. The microstructures and properties, as well as the element distribution of the joints were examined. TLP bonding parameter was determined.The results show that the tensile strength and bend strength at room temperature of the joint with BNi_2 and FeNiCrSiB filler is equal to,or greater than that of the base metal.
