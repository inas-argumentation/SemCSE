Globalisation, health policy and the exercise of power--a second opinion on Gilbert and Gilbert (2004).
Globalisation implies increased interconnectedness between economies. The resulting change in governance patterns is often utilised by powerful actors in the pursuit of their own interests. Thus, the effects of globalisation cannot be understood without exploring the exercise of power. The impact of globalisation on health policy in South Africa is no exception.
