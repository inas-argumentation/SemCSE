Flow Through Earth Dams with Spatially Random Permeability
Even though soil shows the highest variability of any engineering material, the analysis of flow through earth dams typically proceeds deterministically and results can sometimes be quite misleading. In fact it is well known that soil permeability varies randomly in space and an improved earth dam model should incorporate this variation. In this paper the soil permeability in an earth dam of typical geometry is viewed as a spatially random field following a lognormal distribution with prescribed mean, variance, and spatial correlation structure. The statistics of flow and free surface drawdown through the dam are computed using Monte Carlo simulations and observations are made on the statistics of the flow rate and downstream exit-point.
