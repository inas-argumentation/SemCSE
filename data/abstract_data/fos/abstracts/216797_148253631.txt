A Social Semiotic Approach to Multimodal Discourse Analysis of News Cartoon:A Case Study of News Cartoon‘Interior Decoration'
Based on Halliday's Social Semiotics and Kress Van Leeuwen's Visual Grammar,this paper makes a multimodal discourse analysis on a news cartoon ‘Interior Decoration'.Adopting this theoretical framework,this qualitative study reveals that the three meta-function of systemic grammar,namely ideational,interpersonal and textual function,can be applied to non-linguistic symbols.Language symbols and non-linguistic symbols can complement each other,building the meaning of multimodal discourse together.
