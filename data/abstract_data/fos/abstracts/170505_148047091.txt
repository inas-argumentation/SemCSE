The Connotation of Relationship between Academic and Politics and the Tendency of Research in a "Low-cabon" Era
Since the second world war to the end of the cold war,an interactive relationship always existed between science technology(ST) and politics.After the end of the cold war,we gradually entered a so-called "low-cabon" era,with the strong international political and economic colour,"low-carbon" is endowing current and future ST with some new political connotation.Around low-carbon idea,to establish some preferential topics,change the research paradigm and the ways of cooperation,and promote the transformation of academic organization,is an inevitable choice for us to meet challenge of the low-carbon era,safeguard national political and economic security,and establish the international competitive advantage in the future.
