Research on oil extraction methods in peroxide value determination of microencapsulated evening primrose oil
In order to determine the peroxide value of microencapsulated evening primrose oil,extraction oil methods of alkaline ether,trituration and ultrasonic were studied.The results show that the rate of extraction oil of alkaline ether method is the highest(94.69%)in three methods,but the peroxide value of microencapsulated evening primrose oil was higher than evening primrose oil;trituration method is the lowest(40.68%),and the results were not stable;ultrasonic method is moderate(59.63%),this method has good reproductivity and high accuracy.Peroxide value of microencapsulated evening primrose oil was approach to evening primrose oil.The optimum time of ultrasonic was 3min.
