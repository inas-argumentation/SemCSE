Implementation of Spectrum Monitoring and Analyzing System Based on Virtual Instrument Technology
With the fast development of the virtual instrument technology,it has been applied to various communication signal processing applications except for traditional measurement and control field. According to the ideas of the virtual instrument,a simple spectrum monitoring and analyzing system is implemented based on NI PXIe hardware and Labview development software. Functions of spectrum monitoring,analyzing,recording and remote control are integrated into the system. Compared with traditional spectrum monitoring system,the system proposed overcomes the weaknesses of high operation cost and low flexibility. Experiments verify the functions of wideband spectrum monitoring,single signal analyzing,waveform recording and remote control,which show the effectiveness of the proposed system.
