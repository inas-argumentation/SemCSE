Manufacture and test of common oil supply system forsynthetic gas compressor unit and ammonia compressor unit
The structure and principle of common oil supply system which was used to lubricate and control for synthetic gas compressor unit and ammonia compressor unit in 300kt/a ammonia synthesis plant were introduced. The manufacture and assembling process of pedestal oil tank,oil cooler,oil filtrator and oil supply pipes of the system were discribed. The procedure and method for washing and test of the system were given.
