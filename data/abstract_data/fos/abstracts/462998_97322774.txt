Diffusion along grain boundaries and dislocations in oxides, alkali halides and carbides
Abstract The interpretation of experiments, from which parameters characterising dislocation and grain boundary diffusion can be deduced, is described. The relevant parameters are a diffusion coefficient for the boundary (or dislocation) and the effective width of the boundary (or radius of a dislocation) within which the diffusion coefficient is significantly different from the bulk lattice. The experimental data for these short-circuit diffusion parameters in several classes of binary compound are reviewed and the trends compared with the well-established data for metals. Attempts are made to explain some of the discrepancies which exist in the literature. Atomistic models of dislocation and grain boundary diffusion and possible future developments are discussed.
