Research on VRML Network Interaction and Its Application in Design of Oil Pipeline
Takes building oil pipeline virtual scene as an example,analyzes the interactive functions of virtual reality modeling language(VRML) and the implementation process systematically,including the basic interaction of VRML,the interaction between VRML and JavaScript,the interaction between VRML and Java.It makes VRML have more powerful programming capabilities,and it is more flexible and convenient to interact and control the scenes and models,and realizes controling the VRML scene at the Web page effectively at the same time.
