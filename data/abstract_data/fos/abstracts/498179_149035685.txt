Control and Competition: The Architecture of Boathouse Row
Vol. CXXX, No. 3 ( July 2006) This paper began as an independent study project during my masters program in art history at Williams College. I would especially like to thank Professor Michael J. Lewis for his insightful remarks, as well as Professor Charles Haxthausen and members of the masters class of 1996. In addition, I greatly appreciate the contructive criticism of the anonymous readers for the Pennsylvania Magazine of History and Biography, whose comments helped me refine and sharpen my arguments. Finally, this paper would not have been possible without the kindness of many members of the clubs of Boathouse Row and without the housing support of Seth Rabinowitz during my many months of research.
