Condensation of pentafluorobenzaldehyde with o-aminothiophenol
The condensation reaction of pentafluorobenzaldehyde with o-aminothiophenol is shown to lead, depending on the conditions used, to the formation of 2-pentafluorophenylbenzthiazoline or a fluorine-substituted derivative of cyclophane and cannot serve as a method for preparing 1,2,3,4-tetrafluorodibenz-[b,f][1,4]thiazepine.
