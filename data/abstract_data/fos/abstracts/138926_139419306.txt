Dielectric and electrical properties of phosphate glass
Measurement of the dielectric properties of different substances has been the subject of many researchers and various models have been proposed to interpret the experimental results.Dielectricmeasurements on ionicmaterials give useful information about dynamic processes involving ionic motion. In this paper the dielectric and electrical properties of SLBAP glass specimen wasmeasured in a frequency range 100Hz to 1MHz and over the temperature range from303 to 543K.
