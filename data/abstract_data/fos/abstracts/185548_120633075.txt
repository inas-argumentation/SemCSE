Bayes estimation of the reliability of a system under a random environment governed by a dirichlet prior
We consider a system of components whose lifetimes are independent and identically distributed as exponential random variables. The parameter of the exponential distribution is affected by environmental factors specified by a random variable η, whose uncertainty is described by a Dirichlet process D(α), with parameter α. Under this prior, the Bayes estimator of the reliability of the system is derived using the Monte Carlo simulation and the estimates of the parallel and series systems are compared for specific choices of the lifetime distributions and the environment. The analysis extends the results of the earlier work of the authors for the case of a single component to that of a multi-component system.
