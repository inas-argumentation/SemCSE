Nutritional Components of 9 Kinds of Artificial Cultivated Wild Vegetables in South Fujian
The basic nutritional components of 9 kinds of artificial cultivated wild vegetables in south Fujian region and their compositions of amino acids were systematically analyzed.The results showed that the nutritional components of 9 kinds of wild vegetables were quite different.Their edible portion were rich in crude protein,crude fat and Vc,K,Ca,Na.They had high content and reasonable ratio of amino acids.The contents of total amino acids,essential amino acids and delicious amino acid in Brassica oleracea var acephala,Kalimeris indica,Ocimum basilicum and Mentha haplocalyx Briq were significantly higher than that of other wild vegetables.
