Impact of Cathode Carbon Block Material on the Thermal-electric Field in Aluminum Reduction Cell
The choice of cathode carbon block material is relevant to the economic benefit of aluminum reduction cell.In this paper,a mathematic model of 180 kA aluminum reduction cell was developed by finite-element analysis software ANSYS,and impact of semi-graphitic,semi-graphitized and graphitized carbon material on the thermal-electric field in the cell were compared.Calculation results show that the ohmic voltage drop of the cathode are 0.286 V,0.226 V and 0.142 V respectively,and the heat loss are 333 kW,339 kW and 346 kW respectively.So the best carbon material to save energy is graphitized carbon,and the second-best one is semi-graphitized carbon.
