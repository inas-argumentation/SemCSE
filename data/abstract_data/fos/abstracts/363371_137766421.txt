Research on Welding Quality Control of Explosion-containment Vessel Made of Stainless Steel Clad Plate
The explosion-containment vessel consisting of the l6MnR wall with 20 mm thickness and 0Cr18Ni9 wall of 4 mm thickness were used for the synthesization of nano-powder material.In the process of making explosion-containment vessel,the key techniques with respect to weldability,welding method,welding material,welding bevel design,welding process evaluation and welding quality examination of the explosion-containment vessel made of stainless steel clad plate were discussed from the physical-chemistry and mechanical properties of l6MnR and 0Cr18Ni9.
