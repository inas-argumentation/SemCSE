The Historical Evolution of the Evaluation Methods in the Quality Assessment of American Doctor Program
The origin and history of the development of Quality Assessment of Doctor Program from 1925 to 2007 is divided into four historical phrases.Based on the analysis of a series of events of Doctor Program Assessment,we argue that the reputation ranking was a completely subjective method from the beginning of Doctor Program assessment;meanwhile the objective items for assessment were gradually designed and involved into the assessment events within the years passing by.These two methods made up each own advantages and disadvantages,and developed into this systematical assessment methods for American Doctor Program nowadays.
