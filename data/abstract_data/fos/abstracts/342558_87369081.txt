Study about the effect on activity of lipoxygenase in defatted soybean flour by drying heating
Lipoxygenases in defatted soybean flour were dealed by dry heating treatment. The effect of heating temperature, heating time and water content in flour on enzyme activity were compared and analyzed, mostly. The inactivity of heating time and water content in flour on Lox-1 and Lox-2 was severely. But the effect on the Lox-1 was greater than that on the Lox-2. The activity of isozyme Lox-3 was the least in them, but it had more resistance to dry heating treatment.
