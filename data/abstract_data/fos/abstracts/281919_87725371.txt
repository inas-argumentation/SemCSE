Optimization of submerged fermentation medium of coprinus comatus
Used extracellular polysaccharide and biomass as indexes, optimization of fermentation medium was studied. The results showed that the optimum medium composition for the submerged fermentation of Coprinus comatus was as follow: 4% maize amylum, 0.2% soybean cake meal, 3% sucrose, 0.1% KH2PO4, 0.1% MgSO4, 0.05% CaSO4, 0.5% corn steep liquor.
