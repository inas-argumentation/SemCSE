Construction of eukaryotic expression plasmid of murine TLR3 and identification of its biological activity
Objective To Clone and express murine TLR3 and identify its biological activity. Methods Murine TLR3 cDNA was cloned by PCR and inserted into pBabe-puro plasmid. After been transfected, recombinant TLR3 expressed in L929 cell was assayed by Western blotting and its induction of IRF3 nuclear translocation after stimulation with polyI∶C was investigated by IHC. Results The MW of recombinant TLR3 was almost the same as anticipated value and TLR3 activated the IRF3 after being recognized polyI∶C. Conclusion The recombinant TLR showed biological activity, which laid the basis to investigate its signaling pathway and its impact on antiviral immunity.
