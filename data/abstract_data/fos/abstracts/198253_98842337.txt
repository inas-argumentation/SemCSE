Synthesis of n-butyl acetate by transition metal monosubstituted tungstosilicic heteropoly-anion layered compounds catalyst
The transition metal monosubstituted tungstosilicic heteropoly-anion layered compounds of Zn2Al-SiW11M( M = Co2+,Cu2+,Ni2+) are synthesized by inserting K8[SiW11MO40]( M=Co2+,Cu2+,Ni2+) with Keggin structure to Zn2Al clay using ion-exchange method. The layered compounds are characterized by XRD,IR and UV. The results indicate that with the heteropoly-anions displacing the NO3-ions in the clay,the layered compounds still have Keggin structure. n-Butyl acetate is synthesized using the layered compounds as catalyst,and the catalytic activity of layered compounds is investigated. The results indicate that the layered compounds have good catalytic performance over the esterification.
