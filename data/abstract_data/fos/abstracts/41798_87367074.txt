Effects of Sanchi extract on the activation and proliferation of the mouse lymphocytes in vitro and NO secretion by peritoneal macrophages
Objective To investigate the effects of Sanchi extract (SE) on the activation and prolifera- tion of murine lymphocytes and NO secretion by peritoneal macrophages, and so to elucidate the molecular and cel- lular mechanisms of immunosuppressive effect of SE. Methods Fluorescence conjugated monoclonal antibodies and flow cytometry were used to detect the expression of CD69 by T cells in response to concanavalin (Con A) and lymphocytes proliferation stained by CFSE. Murine peritoneal macrophages were stimulated with LPS or lympho- cytes culture supemate (LCS) and the concentration of NO was determined by Griess reagent assay. Results Af- ter 6 hours culture, the activation rate of T cells in Con A group was 59.79%, and the activation rates in SE 50μg/ml and SE 100μg/ml groups were 46.50% and 37.73%, respectively (P0.01). After 72 hours culture, the concentration of SE of 12.5～100μg/ml significantly inhibited proliferation of mouse lymphocytes in response to Con A. Additionally, SE(12.5～100μg/ml) inhibited secretion of NO by peritoneal macrophages that stimulated by LPS or lymphocytes culture supernate. Conclusion SE inhibits activation and proliferation of the murine lym- phocytes and NO secretion by peritoneal macrophages.
