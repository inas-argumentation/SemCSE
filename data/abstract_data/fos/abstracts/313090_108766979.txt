Richard’s Equation Approach to the Design of Evapotranspiration Beds
Evapotranspiration (ET) bed design commonly uses a simple mass balance (SMB) approach to determine adequate depth and area for on-site wastewater treatment systems. The SMB approaches tend to oversimplify interactions among water, soil, and plants. This article proposes an alternative approach to ET bed design that utilizes the Richards equation simulation model and a well-known groundwater modeling computer program. Results indicate that this approach more accurately represents an actual system because it includes consideration of the dynamics of the soil-water-plant system.
