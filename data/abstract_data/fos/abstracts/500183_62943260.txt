Discussion on Teaching of Cultivating Non-Computer Major's Interest in Visual Basic
Because of the non-computer professional students' lack of interest in Visual Basic learning,it is difficult to effectively achieve the teaching goals.For this purpose,this paper puts forward the ideas of teaching,including explanation of the concepts by analog method,comprehension of knowledge by designing of cognitive conflict,and happy learning by interesting cases.The teaching experience has shown that these ideas have obtained favorable teaching effect for reasons of the students' high interest in Visual Basic learning.
