Study on influence of processing technologies on saponins content of bitter melon juice
The effects of processing technologies(blanching,preparing and sterilizing) on the content of sapo-nins of bitter melon juice was studied.The result indicated that the quantity of saponins was higher when bitter melon was blanched at 70℃ for 4min.The loss of saponins was reduced when bitter melon juice with pH 4 was sterilized at 80℃ for 15min.
