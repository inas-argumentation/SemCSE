Determination of of anthocyanin in S. tianschanica by spectrophotometry
The method for determination of anthocyanin in S.tianschanica was set up in which reference substance was anthocyanin.Methods: n-BuOH:HCl(95∶5) were chromogenic agent and the detection wave-lengths were at 550 nm.Result: anthocyanin in the leaf of S.tianschanica was 1.03%(RSD% =2.4) and anthocyanin in the fruit of S.tianschanica with the linear range of 0.04~0.16 mg/mL(R2=0.9997).Conclution: This method is simple,convenient,rapid and suitable for determination of anthocyanin in S.tianschanica.
