Vapor species from fused Li2O-B2O3, Na2O-SiO2 and Na2O-P2O5 systems for thin film manufacturing by PVD
The estimation of partial pressures of various vapors is believed to be very important for precise control of the composition of thin films of superionic conductors manufactured by PVD, CVD and other elegant processes. The intention of the present work is to estimate partial pressures of various vapor species which could be evaporated from fused Li 2 O-B 2 O 3 , Na 2 O-SiO 2 , and Na 2 O-P 2 O 5 systems. The estimates were made using the Gibbs free energy of the pure substances and the activities of the components. From the calculated results, the following is known: for the Li 2 O-B 2 O 3 system, the vapor pressure of the complex oxide LiBO 2 is greater than the others, and the ratio of J Li to J B remains 1.0 at 1200K. In the case of evaporation of the Na 2 O-SiO 2 system, the vapor pressure of sodium is always the greater. The ratio of J Na to J p strongly depends on the composition and temperature of the evaporation source of the Na 2 O-P 2 O 5 system.
