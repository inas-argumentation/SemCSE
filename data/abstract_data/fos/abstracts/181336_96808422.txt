Eine effiziente und stereoselektive Synthese von D‐erythro‐Sphingosin
An Efficient and Stereoselective Synthesis of D-erythro-Sphingosine 
 
 
 
A highly stereoselective 3-step synthesis, leading in 81% overall yield to D-erythro-sphingosine (8), is described. Several further examples for the nucleophilic addition to the serinal derivative 1 are given.
