Traveling wave solutions in a two-group SIR epidemic model with constant recruitment
Host heterogeneity can be modeled by using multi-group structures in the population. In this paper we investigate the existence and nonexistence of traveling waves of a two-group SIR epidemic model with time delay and constant recruitment and show that the existence of traveling waves is determined by the basic reproduction number $$R_{0}.$$R0. More specifically, we prove that (i) when the basic reproduction number $$R_{0}>1,$$R0>1, there exists a minimal wave speed $$c^*>0,$$c∗>0, such that for each $$c \ge c^*$$c≥c∗ the system admits a nontrivial traveling wave solution with wave speed c and for $$c<c^*$$c<c∗ there exists no nontrivial traveling wave satisfying the system; (ii) when $$R_{0} \le 1,$$R0≤1, the system admits no nontrivial traveling waves. Finally, we present some numerical simulations to show the existence of traveling waves of the system.
