Silver(l) Ion Selective Electrode Based on Dithlamacrocycles
Poly(vinyl chloride) membrane all solid state electrode based on the macrocyclic polythiaether 6-oxa-3,9-dithiabicycle[9,3,1]-pentadeca-1(15)-11,13-triene, has been developped. The electrode exhibits a Nernstian linear response to silver(l) ion from 10−6 to 10−1 M, good selectivity (no response for Hg(ll)) and short response time (<10 s).
