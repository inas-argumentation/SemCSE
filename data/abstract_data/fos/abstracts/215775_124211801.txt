Investigation of high power sub-nanosecond electrical pulse generated by GaAs photoconductive switches
In this paper, experiments of a lateral semi-insulating GaAs photoconductive semiconductor switch triggered by nano-second and pico-second and femto-second laser pulse are reported. The switches were insulated by solid multi-layer transparent dielectrics. Dark insulating intensity of the 8mm-gap GaAs switch reached 30kV. Jitter-free current wave form of the 3mm-gap and 8mm-gap GaAs switches were observed when triggered by the laser pulses. The current could be as high as 560A when the 3mm-gap switch was under the voltage of 2kV and triggered by an 8ns and 1 2mJ laser pulse. The same device also revealed good temperal characteristics when triggered by a pico-second laser and 10 8 repeat frequency laser pulse strings. The shortest rising time the 3mm-gap switch response is less than 200ps which is limited by the coaxial cable.
