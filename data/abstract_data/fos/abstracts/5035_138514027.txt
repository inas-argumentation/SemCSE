Low Pressure Sand Mould Casting Technology for Heavy Aluminum Alloy Casting
A low pressure sand mould casting technology for heavy aluminum alloy components, such as thin wall shell of high pressure electric equipment, power apparatus, et al, is introduced.. The metallurgical quality of Al-Si-Mg alloy is improved by optimizing alloy composition and purifying molten alloy.. The self-developed low pressure equipment has the characteristics of crucible sealing, simple structure, easy manufacturing, low investment and better flexibility. Casting mould can be made of dry clay, resin or water-glass bonded sand. Positioning accuracy of casting mould is increased obviously by the combination of iron chill and metallic core. Subsequently, the wall thickness of cast components has small deviation. Over ten years production practices have validated that the technology can produce better castings with dense structure and good airtightness, the mechanical properties and electric conductivity of castings produced can meet the requirements of specification. The products have already been exported to other countries.
