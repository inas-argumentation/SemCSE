Some Renewal Theorems with Application to a First Passage Problem
Let X i , i=l, 2, 3, … be a sequence of independent and identically distributed random variables with EǀX i ǀ 0. Write \(X_i^ - = - \min \left( {0,X_i } \right),S_n = \sum\nolimits_{i = 1}^n {X_i } \) and \(M_n = \max\nolimits_{1 \leqq k \leqq n} S_k \). In this paper we shall discuss the asymptotic behabiour as x → ∞ of the sums \(\sum\nolimits_{n = 1}^\infty {a_n \Pr \left( {S_n \leqq x} \right)} \) and \(\sum\nolimits_{n = 1}^\infty {a_n \Pr \left( {M_n \leqq x} \right)} \) for certain classes of positive coefficient sequences {a n } and use the results on the latter sums to investigate the behaviour of the first passage time out of the interval (− ∞, x] for the process s n as x→∞.
