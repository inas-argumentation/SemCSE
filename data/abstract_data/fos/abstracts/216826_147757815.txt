On the Methiod in Teaching Basic Law Courses in Higher Professional Colleges
Basic law course is an important component part of moral education course of higher vocational college. Its nature determines that its teaching task lies in improving college students' law quality, and that its teaching method must be innovative as well. According to the teaching practice, the writer concludes the " Three Practice Teaching Methods" of basic law course, namely "Case Training Method", "Simulating Experiment Method" and "Social Practice Method". The article respectively introduces the concrete contents, operating gist, teaching effect and points for attention of the " Three Practice Teaching Methods". 
