Throughout his works, Bekele Mekonnen thus explores the use of uncanny parodies to articulate the various faces of destruction. His work however should be seen not in terms of the lamentation of poverty and its consequences, but rather as the symptom of the uncertainties generated by new global situations. His work should be placed within this wide perspective. It could also be seen as the personal protest of an abstract identity that is galvanized within the power dynamics of the world in which he is living. Born in 1964, Bekele received his formal training in sculpture from the Fine Arts School in Addis Ababa and Moscow State Fine Arts Institute of the former Soviet Republic. He was the Director of the Fine Art School between 2000-2003.
