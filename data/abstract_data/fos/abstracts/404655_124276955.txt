A Deducing Method of Generator Matrix and Simpest Information Restoration Matrix of Punctured Convolutional Codes
Punctured convolutional codes are usually used in satellite communication system.For information interception and signal analyzing of satellite communication system,it is usable if knows the generator matrix and information restoration matrix of Punctured convolutional codes.In this paper,a simple deducing method of generator matrix is intruduced by using simple physical concept..By using elementary transformation based on degrading the degrees of generator polynomial matrix step by step,a deducing method of the simplest information restoration matrix and the check matrix is presented.This method is suitable for all code rate punctured convolutional codes and practical for information interception and signal analyzing of satellite communication system.
