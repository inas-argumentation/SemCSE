Mercury Removal Performance and Resistance to Sulfur Characteristics of Mn-Nb Composite Catalyst
Manganese-Niobium(Mn-Nb) composite catalyst was prepared on the carrier of titanium dioxide(Ti O2),effects of reaction temperature and hydrogen chloride(HCl) concentration on the catalytic removal efficiency of zero-valent mercury(Hg0) were investigated,as well as the resistance to sulfur characteristics of Mn-Nb composite catalyst was studied. Results showed that Mn-Nb composite catalyst achieved the optimum activity at 300 ~350 ℃,capacity load of Mn-Nb played a great role on the removal efficiency of Hg0,which could reach 90.90% at capacity of 7%(w)Mn-2%(w)Nb. Cerium oxide modifying enhanced the resistance to sulfur,which could inhibit activity of mercury removal of Mn-Nb composite catalyst,and it achieved the highest activity after dipping cerium oxide of 1.5%(w).
