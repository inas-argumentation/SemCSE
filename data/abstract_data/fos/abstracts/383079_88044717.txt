Identification of Transgenic Bt Cotton by Using Kanamycin in Cotton Field
A simple,cheap and efficient technique for identifying transgenic Bt cotton by using kanamycin as an identification marker in the field experiment was tested.The result showed that kanamycin could be used to test whether or not a plant was resistant.In the field experiment,a writing-brush was soaked with 4 000 mg·L~(-1) kanamycin solution and scribbled on the second neonate expanded leaves at seedling and bud stage.As a result,seven days later,all of leaves of the susceptible plants showed apparent yellow patches whereas the leaves of the Bt resistant plants remained green without any symptom.Furthermore,the reaction of plants against the kanamycin corresponded to the result which was infected by bollworm in the field.
