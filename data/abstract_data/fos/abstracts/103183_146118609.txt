Design of Digital Filter for Digital SQUID With Sub-Flux Quantum Feedback Resolution
Digital SQUIDs with the single flux quantum (SFQ) feedback have attracted much attention because of the feasibility of realizing a wide dynamic range and high slew rate. In order to realize higher resolution, we have studied the performance of an advanced, digital SQUID configuration offering sub-flux quantum feedback. Combining the sub-flux quantum feedback to a feedback loop and the direct SFQ feedback to a main loop triggered by carry SFQ pulses from an up/down counter. After optimization of the circuit, we confirmed the maximum magnetic field amplitude exceed 1 <inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\mu$</tex-math></inline-formula>T, the magnetic field noise is lower than 100 pT/<inline-formula><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}$</tex-math></inline-formula> with a signal band frequency of 1.22 MHz. Using a first-order sinc filter, we could implement the digital SQUID magnetometer at low-temperature with reasonable noise reduction performance and the binary data rate reduction in order to transfer the data from low temperature circuit to the room temperature electronics.
