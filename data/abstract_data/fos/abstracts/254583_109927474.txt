Influence of the separation energy on the radius of neutron rich nuclei
In this work we extend the model of Hefter, de Llano and Mitropolsky to nuclei composed of a core and a weakly bound cluster. This enables us to describe in a satisfactory way the nuclear radii, in particular as nuclei approach the neutron drip line. It allows also qualitative predictions for the width of the momentum distributions measured in fragmentation reactions.
