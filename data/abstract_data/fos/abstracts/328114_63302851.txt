Solutions for Open Enterprise Application Integration System
With the inceasing development of computer technology, e stablishing Open Enterprise Application Integration System has been an important solution for enterpr ises to establish new systems rapidly to integrate different, distributed application. By contrasting the tradi tional EAI Solving scheme (CORBA)with the solving scneme based on the new technology of Web Service, this paper discusses how to establish Open Enterprise Application Integration System and points out the advantages of Web Service Solving scheme.
