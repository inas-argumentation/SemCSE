Separation of the insoluble substance in concentrated apple juice
A comparative study of the efficiency of four different technologies on the separation of the ultrafi ltration tank substrates and the insoluble substance in enzymatic juice was carried out.Experimental results showed that:the horizontal spiral separator reduced the insoluble substance in the ultrafi ltration tank to 10%.The average fl ux of the disc separator on the insoluble substance in enzymatic juice was 22.7 m3/h which 11.8% higher than the normal technology.The average fl ux of the combined process(horizontal spiral separator and disc separator) on the separation ultrafi ltration tank substrate was equivalent with the normal technology when the ultrafi ltration run for 20 hours.The plate-and-frame fi lter press with 400 mesh can remove the insoluble substance in the enzymatic juice,and reduce the insoluble matter in the tank to 2%.The average fl ux of the plate-and-frame fi lter press was 30.4 m3/h,which 50.78% higher than the normal technology.
