High Temperature Corrosion Behavior of Al_(0.5)FeCoCrNi High Entropy Alloy
High-entropy alloy Al0.5CoCrFeNi was prepared by arc-melting and casting method(Subscript indicated molar ratio).The corrosion behavior of high-entropy alloy in 75wt%Na2SO4+25wt%NaCl mixed molten salt at 800 ℃and 900 ℃ was studied.The microstructure,morphology and composition of corroded specimens were analyzed by scanning electron microscopy(SEM) coupled with electron diffraction spectrometry(EDS) and X-ray diffractometry(XRD).The results show that the high-entropy alloy has serious mass loss in the molten salt.With the temperature increasing,the corrosion resistance declines.The corrosion products could be divided into multi-layer.The loose external layer contains NaFeO2 and other oxides.Moreover,the alloy suffers from serious internal oxidation and sufidation,and the internal corrosion layer contains Al2O3,Cr2O3 and Cr5S6 sulfide.
