Correlation between T cell receptor repertoire and cytokines expression with tumor progression and metastasis of gastric cancer
Objective To study the changes of host immune response against tumor with the progression and metastasis of gastric cancer. Method The expression of 24 families of T cell receptor β chain variable region (TCRVβ) and the cytokines profile in isolated CD4 + and CD8 + subsets from cancer tissue, residual benign tissue and peripheral blood of patients with gastric cancer were tested by a highly sensitive radio labeled semi quantitative RT PCR technique. Results The number of expanded T cell clones in both CD8 + and CD4 + cells from the peripheral blood of patients with lymph node metastases was reduced more than that of those without metastasis ( P 0.001, P 0.05). The number of expanded clones in the CD8 + subpopulation from peripheral blood was decreased more in patients with infiltration of the full thickness of gastric wall than that of those involving only the mucosa or submucosa ( P =0.05). The mRNA levels of IL 6, IL 8, TNF α in CD8 + T subset as well as the level of IL 4 in CD4 + T subset from cancer tissue (0.43±0.17, 0.42±0.11, 0.18±0.05, 0.08±0.03) were significantly higher than that of residual benign tissue (0.08±0.02, 0.17±0.05, 0.08±0.03, 0.01±0.00) in the cases with lymph node metastases ( P =0.040, P =0.020, P =0.017, P =0.034); However, in those cases without metastasis, no significant difference was found between cancer tissue and residual benign tissue. Conclusions The number of expanded T cell clones and cytokines expression in patients is correlated with progression and metastasis of gastric cancer and both fewer expanded T cell clones and the shift to T H2 of T H1/T H2 in cytokines expression suggest a suppressive immune status in advanced gastric cancer.
