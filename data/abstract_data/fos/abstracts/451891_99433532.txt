Measures and ideas for long period operation of delayed coke installation
Coke oven is a key device in delayed coke installation. It is considered that coking of coke oven pipe mainly relates to the properties of raw materials, the heat intensity of the surface of radiation pipe, the arrangement of the radiation pipe, the temperature controlling of the oven outlet, and the flow velocity and flow pattern of the agent in oven pipe, etc. Based on the delayed coke principles, the measures for retarding the coke formation velocity and prolonging the continuous production period of coke oven are reasonable design and careful operation. Accordance with the problems that exist generally in coke installation such as coking of coke oven pipe, coking of fat gas line, and coking of bottom of fractional column, etc, some experiences are found out and good effects are obtained. \[
