A characterization of the room index by means of functional equations
By solving a system of functional equations we characterize the classical room index which is useful in architectural problems concerning room lighting. In order to decide the best lighting for a room most architects base their solutions upon the well-known room index R{x-y-z) = l§+Tv (1) where x and y stand for the length and width of the room (orthoedric shape), z indicates the distance form the light to the surface to be illuminated (floor, table, etc.) and c is a constant. The room index (1) has been adopted in the manuals made by factories (Philips, Osram, etc.) following the tables produced by the International Commission on Illumination in order to determine several light factors in rooms [3, 4], Our aim in this paper is to characterize (1) as solution of a system of functional equations which represent natural conditions to be satisfied by a room index, establishing on the way a theoretical framework which justifies the empirical room index (1).
