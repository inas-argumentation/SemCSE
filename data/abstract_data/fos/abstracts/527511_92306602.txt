Rapid growth of single-layer graphene on stainless steel substrates
We report a rapid method for the synthesis of single- to multi-layer graphene on commercially available stainless steel sheets using direct thermal chemical vapor deposition with aliphatic alcohols as the carbon source. We also studied the influence of various elemental species present in stainless steel on graphene growth. It is shown that while graphene growth occurs on FeO rich regions, it is retarded where Cr and Mn are present.
