Proton dynamics in phase II of CsHSO4 studied by 1H NMR
Proton dynamics in phase II of CsHSO 4 have been studied by 1 H NMR. 1 H spectral line shapes were traced between 210 and 470 K, and 1 H spin-lattice relaxation curves were measured between 310 and 470 K, taking into account the thermal history of the sample. The NMR results demonstrate that proton transfer between two neighboring SO 4 tetrahedra takes place much more easily than the reorientation of the SO 4 tetrahedra in phase II. Thus, the SO 4 reorientation limits the rate of the proton transport. Correlation times of the SO 4 reorientation are estimated from the second moments of 1 H NMR spectra. The obtained parameters are τ 0 =1.5×10 −9 s and E a =29 kJ/mol. The 1 H spin-lattice relaxation in phase II is caused by motions much faster than the SO 4 reorientation.
