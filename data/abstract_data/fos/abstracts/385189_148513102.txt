The Policy of Creating the World-class University in France and Its Characteristics
The policy of creating the world-class university by French government in2010 was born in the unique context of French political,economic,legal and cultural.The contents of this policy covered the key elements of construction goals and tasks,project collection and standards,selection and review,and funds allocation,etc.This policy had unique French characteristics as it led the modernization transformation of higher education and research institutions in France,promoted the building of collaborative innovation platform based on the regional economy,led the development of science and technology cooperation projects and their parallel projects and promoted the powerful combinations between those world-class institutions and advanced disciplines.
