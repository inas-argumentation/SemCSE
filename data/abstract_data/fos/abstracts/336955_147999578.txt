Dao of university and utensil of university——Explanation of traditional spirit of university
Dao and utensil is not only the primary question of traditional culture of China,but also the primary question of traditional university of China.For a long time,utensil of university is separated from Dao of university,the whole function is damaged badly,and the ideal and spirit of university is injured.The spirit of university embodies the soul and basic character which is the base of exist and development of university.With the coming of time of multiple,comprehensive and individualism,people put forward the historical mission including the integration of Dao and utensil,humanities and science,academics and utensils,thinking science and technological science.Therefore,we are able to reconstruct the spirit of modern university which can realize the unification of science and utility.
