Meson-exchange corrections to the O+→O−-beta decay of16C
The fo-value for the β-transition16C(O+,gst)→16N(O−, 120 keV) is calculated within an extended shell-model space. It is reduced by a factor of 2.5 compared to the result obtained without explicit treatment of configuration mixing. The exchange current contribution enhances the one-body axial charge density by ∼50%. It is argued that the experimental data can well be described when using Woods-Saxon single particle wave functions.
