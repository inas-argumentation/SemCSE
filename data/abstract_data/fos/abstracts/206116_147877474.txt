Experience of Zhejiang higher education from the development of independent colleges
It has been 10 years since the establishment of Science and Technology College,Ningbo University in April,1999.During this period,independent colleges in Zhejiang province have got through three developing stages.They evolved some distinguishing features,such as regular operation,flexible cooperative models of school-running,distinctive developing styles,etc.The developing process of independent colleges in Zhejiang provides some feasible experience for higher education reform in China,which is running colleges with the direction of Zhejiang Spirit,adopting the progressive reforming mode,keeping good interaction between local governments and colleges,promoting colleges and society/market to share mutual complementary advantages,and achieving the targets of quantity expansion and quality improvement ultimately.
