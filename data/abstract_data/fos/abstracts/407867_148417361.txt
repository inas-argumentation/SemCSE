On the scale economy and knowledge alliance in higher institution
The result of empirical analysis on the scale economic situations of our country since 1995, shows that, with the extreme enlargement of the scale of higher education institution, the higher education institution has already been uneconomical for scale by the transition of the scale economy gradually. Under this condition, it is not a reasonable choice to pursue excessively the enlargement of higher education institute. At the same time, it is a kind of better system arrangement to set up the knowledge alliance of higher education institutions.
