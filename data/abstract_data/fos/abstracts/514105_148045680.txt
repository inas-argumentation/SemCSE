On the creative spirit of academic in the modern university
The modern university should become the unique organization for academic creation.According to the nature of the university,experiences of successful universities,and the requirement of establishing the first-class university,there is an urgent need to promote the creative academic-spirit in universities.In order to carry forward the creative academic-spirit in universities,we must thoroughly implement the scientific concept of development,and have the emphasis on the role of scientific research as the starting-point,the building of the common-knowledge courses as the basis,the establishment of humanistic spirit as the focus,and the reformation of evaluating methods as our core task.
