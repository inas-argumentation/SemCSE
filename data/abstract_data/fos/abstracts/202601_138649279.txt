Research of Microstructures and Yield Properties of Pipeline Steels at Different Deformation Conditions
The effects of deformation conditions on the microstructures and yield properties of X70, X80 and X100 pipeline steels were analyzed using optical microscopy and mechanical test. The results show that, at 500 ℃ deformation temperature, the fine matensite-austenite island(M-A island) and lower banite can enhance the yield properties of X100steel; at 800 ℃ deformation temperature, X70, X80 and X100 steels have similar yield properties.
