Correlation between ESR and luminescence of MFX: Eu
The electron spin resonance(ESR)and lumiuescence properties of MFX:Eu(M=Ca,Sr,Ba;X=Cl, Br,I)and correlations between them have been investigated.It has been found that the ESR parameters are cor- related to the luminescence properties.The Hamiltonian parameter b_2~0 increases as the crystal lattice constant a in- creases,while the hyperfine parameter ~(151)A_decreases.As temperature decreases,the b_2~0 and the emission intensity of d→f transition of Eu~(2+)decrease,while that of f→f transition of Eu~(2+)increases.It is demonstrated from the ESR studies that phonon action has a strong affection on the probability of d→f and f→f transitions.As the chemical bond ionicity P of M—F or M—X bonds increases,g factor increases,while the d→f emission of Eu~(2+)shifts toward the blue.Both the ESR signals and luminescence intensity of Eu~(2+)in samples synthesized in reduction atmosphere are much stronger than those of Eu~(2+)in samples prepared in air.It is revealed from both ESR and luminescence results that the main products created by X-irradiation are[Eu~(2+)+h~+]complex and F centers.It is easy for Eu~(2+)to he reduced,but difficult for Eu~(2+)to be oxidized.
