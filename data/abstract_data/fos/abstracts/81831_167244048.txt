On Television News Spot Report
The spot report represents the developing direction of the news. The spot report of the foreign news organization has already combined organicly with the rolling news broadcast, the 24-hours live broadcast and the later producing flow. The spot reports of TV station in our country are increasing. But there are few striking spot reports. How to be out of the wrong way? The author holds that the reporters' experience should be enriched and the notion of the news and the news system should be reformed.
