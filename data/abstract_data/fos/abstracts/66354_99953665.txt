Processing technology of salted plum lactobacillus beverage
Salted plum lactobacillus beverage was made using salted plum juice as ingredients. The results showed, salted plum juice greatly increased the acidity and salted plum flavor. The optimal technology of salted plum lactobacillus beverage were as follows: mixed with salted plum juice 20%, fermentation milk 30%, sugar 6%, pectin 0.2%, CMC 0.25%, glycerin monostearate 0.03%. The beverage was light reddish brown, with the characteristics of salted plum and fermented milk flavor, and the quality index conformed to the national hygienic standard for lactobacillus beverage. The acidity of beverage was 64.5 °T, and the rate of DPPH· radical scavenging was 66.18%.
