Environment Monitoring Technology on Sensor Network and Early-warning System in High Risking Area of Black Water Cluster TAI Lake
Aiming at the problem of high risking area of black water cluster in Tai Lake,the framework of real-time monitoring network and emergency pre-warning system was proposed,which based on environment monitoring technology on sensor network.Combing the current mechanism research of black water cluster,it gave the details of function realization on water quality monitoring subsystem,monitoring subsystem and basic data management subsystem.Meanwhile,the early warming system including the function of ecological perception,multi-network,intelligent processing and integrated management was built,which melted the network,wireless sensor and ecological perception into environment monitoring technology.Thus,the entire framework of the system provided practical ideas to further improve the monitoring and earlywarning system about black water cluster in Tai Lake.
