Compositional dependence of the ionic conductivity of Na2.2Zn0.9(MoO4)2 with Zn partially replaced by Sc or Cd
The systems Na2.2Zn0.9(MoO4)2-Na2.2Cd0.9(MoO4)2 and Na2.2Zn0.9(MoO4)2-Na2.5Sc0.5(MoO4) 2 have been investigated by means of X-ray powder diffraction, thermal analysis and impedance measurements. Complete solid solubility between the end compositions was observed in both systems. The ionic conductivity increases by one order of magnitude when all the Zn atoms are replaced by Sc or Cd. The Cd compound is the best conductor, with σ=1.6·10−5 (Ω cm)−1 at 575 K. The activation energy, which decreases from 0.85 eV for Na2.2Zn0.9(MoO4)2 to 0.72 eV and 0.66 eV for the Sc and Cd analogues, respectively, was found to be correlated with the unit cell volume.
