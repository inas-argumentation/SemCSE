Lower Devonian flora from Beartooth Butte, Wyoming
Fossil plants are extremely rare in the Paleozoic system throughout the Cordilleran region of North America. This is primarily due to the preponderance of marine sediments. The discovery of a small, but well preserved, Devonian flora from an estuarine deposit at Beartooth Butte, Wyoming, is consequently of interest. This flora is particularly significant as an extension into western North America of the primitive Psilophyton flora of the Lower Devonian, which constitutes the oldest terrestrial vegetation thus far known, and which is recorded from only a few scattered localities, chiefly in western Europe, Australia, and eastern Canada.
