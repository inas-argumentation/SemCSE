Optimization for the enzyme hydrolysis of fenugreek neutral heteropolysaccharide and the effects of its products on reproduction of Bifidobacterium adolescentis
This paper studied the enzyme hydrolysis conditions of fenugreek neutral heteropolysaccharide and the effects of its products on reproduction of Bifidobacterium adolescentis.The orthogonal test was used to optimize the reaction temperature,reaction time and enzyme content,using the yield of oligosaccharide as an index,the products of two schemes with different hydrolysis efficiency and different molecular weight components,showed significant effects on the growth of B.adolescentis.The results showed that the No.5 scheme(9 h reaction time,50 ℃ reaction temperature and 6% enzyme content) was the optimal condition for enzyme hydrolysis of fenugreek neutral heteropolysaccharide and its products had better effects on the growth of B.adolescentis.Molecular weights of two components,which were from the products separated by Sephadex G-150,were 4.5×10~4,7.5×10~3 respectively,and the small one had better effects on B.adolescentis growth.
