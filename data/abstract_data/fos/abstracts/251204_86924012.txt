Clearance of blood HBsAg and HBV by bispecific monoclonal antibody heteropolymers
Objective To determine if heteropolymer containing mouse monoclonal antibodies to CR1 cross-linked with mouse monoclonal anti-HBsAg (antigen-based heteropolymers, AHP) by goat anti-mouse antibodies can bind corresponding HBsAg on erythrocyte CR1 and promote the antigen clearance from the circulation, and to investigate the clearance mechanism. Methods AHP was constructed by cross-linking mouse anti-HBsAg to mouse monoclonal antibodies to erythrocytes CR1 with goat anti-mouse monoclonal antibodies. Four tubes containing 500μl blood from hepatitis B patients were mixed with 500μl AHP. After incubation for 2 min, 5 min, 10 min and 30 min at room temperature (25℃), the samples were centrifuged, the supernatant isolated and assayed for HBsAg and HBV binding activity. As control groups, monoclonal antibody of CR1 was substituted with mouse anti-human CD2, and HP was replaced by PBS. The quantity of HBsAg and HBV bound to erythrocytes were assayed by RIA and fluorescence quantitative PCR(FQ-PCR). The changes of the number of CR1 expressed on erythrocytes was assayed. Results 95% of HBsAg and 51% of HBV specificially bound to human erythrocytes via the complexes from plasma in five minutes. The number of CR1 expressed on erythrocytes lost in this process, erythrocytes hemolysis was not found. Conclusion HP cross-linked with biotin-streptavidin replaced by second antibody can quickly bind plasma antigen on erythrocytes, and facilitate the clearance of antigen.
