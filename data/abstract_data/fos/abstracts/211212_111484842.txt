COMPLEX RESPONSE OF A HIGH-PARAMETER TURBO ROTOR MODEL UNDER THREE NONLINEAR FORCES
The dynamic equations of a flexible symmetrical rotor of a single span under three nonlinear forces,the oil film force,the airflow force and the seal force are established.Considering the parameters of high speed,large airflow velocity and a small seal clearance,the dynamic behavior of the system is studied with a numerical integration method by the software of Matlab.The results show that: with the change of rotary speed,the system shows a complex dynamic behavior containing periodic movements and quasi-periodic movements.With the change of airflow velocity,the instability of the system will occur suddenly,and windows of period-6 and period-3 movements occur after that;the amplitude of period-3 movement increases suddenly and the window becomes high.With a small seal clearance,the system has quasi-periodic movements,in which the period-1 movement is contained.
