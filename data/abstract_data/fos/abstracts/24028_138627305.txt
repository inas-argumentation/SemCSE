Analysis on Structure and Anti-corrosion of Arc Sprayed RE-Zn-Al Coating
Using cored wires and arc spraying technology to manufacture rare earth zinc aluminum alloy coating,the microstructure and anti-corrosion property of the coating were studied by X-ray diffraction,corrosion experiments.Test results show that the rare earth zinc-aluminum alloy coating has dense structure,and integrates with the substrate closely;there are some spinal oxides with well anti-corrosion property in the coating.During corrosion,the self-sealing characteristics of the coating prevent corrosion reaction from expansion,resulting in improve of corrosion resistance.
