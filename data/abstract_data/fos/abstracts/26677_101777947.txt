Synthesis of a New Acridone Derivative and Study on the Electrochemical Behavior of Its Interaction with Protein
Potassium ethoxide was used as dehydrogenation reagent,and 10-methyl-acridone was synthesized from acridone.Then,a kind of new acridone derivative-10-methyl-3-nitro-acridone(MNA) was synthesized by nitration of 10-methyl-acridone.The electrochemical behavior of the interaction of MNA with proteinum was investigated by cyclic voltammetry and a differential pulse voltammetric method has been proposed for the determination of the protein with MNA.In pH 7.0 phosphate buffer solutions(PBS),an electrochemically non-active complex was formed via intercalation of MNA with protein.On addition of protein into the MNA solution,the oxidative peak current of MNA decreases.The decrease of oxidative peak current of MNA was proportional to BSA and HSA concentration in the range of 0.4-14 mg/L and 0.04-0.28 mg/L,respectively.This method had been applied to the analysis of HSA samples.
