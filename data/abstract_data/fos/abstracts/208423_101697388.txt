Study on processing technology of fruit and vegetable beverage of blackberry
To compare both methods of producing crude juice of blackberry and 4 methods of defecation and 14 kinds of clarifying agent, and in the end compounded a stable beverage. The optimum digesting condition of crude juice were investigated by using the orthogonal design. The results show that at 27℃, pH3.13, 0.06% of enzyme to digest for 4h are optimal. The concentration of compound clarifying agent and stabilizer are more than 0.050% and 0.015% respectively.
