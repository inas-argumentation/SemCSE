Microstructure and Microhardness of ZrO_2/Ni60A Composite Coating Formed by Feeding Powder Laser Cladding on Circular Surface
The better processing property coating was formed by laser cladding self-fused Ni-based ceramic powder of ZrO2 composite on the surface of 304 SUS,and the cladding experiment with parameter variations were presented.The influences of the laser processing parameters on macroscopic view,microstructure and micro-hardness of the laser cladding layer were investigated.The results show that the better coating can be obtained when laser power is 1.5 kW,and the layer has the trend of refined framework with the growing of scanning speed;the microhardness will be higher and distribution from substratum to surface with little fluctuate by optimizing scanning speed.
