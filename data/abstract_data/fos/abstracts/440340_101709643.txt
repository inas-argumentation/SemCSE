Fermentation conditions of purple sweetpotato wine
Brewing with the new meterials is an important and effective way to develop the wine with new tastes.In this paper,a new brewing process with purple sweetpotato as the main meterial was studied.Firstly,primary fermentation time,technique,temperature,pH,inoculation volume and liquid volume were optimzed with a series of single-factor experiments.Secondly,the key parameters including fermentation temperature,pH,inoculation volume and liquid volume were futher optimized with the Box-Behnken central composite design experiments.Results showed that the optimal fermentation process was as follows:fermentation temperature 27.9 ℃,pH3.81,inoculation volume 3.97%,fermentation volume 60.99%;An optimized conditions during the store-wine period(after-fermentation) was 14 ℃ for 60 days.The alcohol content of the wine was 9.8 °.Purple sweetpotato wine produced with the new fermentation process,was clear,transparent and tasty.Moreover,this wine was characterized with the purple potato aroma.Hence,purple sweet potato wine has a certain commercial potential.
