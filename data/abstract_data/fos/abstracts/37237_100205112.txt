Purification of cordycepin from Cordyceps militaris with macroporous resin.
Seven types of macroporous resins were used to separate cordycepin from Cordyceps militaris in order to select the best resin.Effect of pH on static adsorption and methanol concentration on static elution were investigated.Dynamic adsorption curve and elution curve of cordycepin was studied.The results showed that AB-8 resin was suitable for adsorbing and purifying the cordycepin from Cordyceps militaris.When the pH value,adsorption temperature,concentration of sample,the volume of sample of resin bed and the flow rate of the upstream solution was 5.0,20℃,0.15 mg·mL-1,10 times and 1.8 BV·h-1,respectively,the resin had better adsorption capacity.When the eluant was 15% methanol and the volume of the eluant was 2.5 times of the resin bed,the resin had better desorption capacity.
