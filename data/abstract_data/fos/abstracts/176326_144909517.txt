Mad Jesus: The Final Testament of a Huichol Messiah from Northern Mexico (review)
demonstrates the real possibility of doing sound social history of medicine using archival sources in Costa Rica. The author has made a remarkable effort to base her narrative and conclusions on library and archival sources. The book is presented by Steven Palmer, author of a number of sound articles and books on the modern history and history of medicine in Costa Rica. Readers interested in the history of Central America and in the history of medicine in Latin America more generally will be very much interested in this study.
