Analysis of Reusing PHS Resources in Mobile Network
Through the analysis of PHS station sites,equipment,supporting facilities and all kinds of resources,discusses the methods and ideas of reusing various resources of interest PHS.It also introduces mobile network reusing the PHS resources to solve the proposal of urban depth coverage.Rational use of these resources will not only meet the energy-saving emission reduction called for national advocacy,but will also make sense for saving network construction costs of operators and speeding up the 3G network coverage.
