Corrosion Resistance of Mg-4Zn-1Ca Biological Alloy in Simulated Body Fluid
The Mg-4Zn-1Ca ternary alloy ingot was prepared by permanent mold casting with vacuums protection atmosphere, The corrosion behavior of as-cast Mg-4Zn-1Ca alloys in SBF solution(simulated body fluid) were investigated by optical microscopy, scanning electron microscopy, corrosion weight loss test and electrochemical experiments. The results show that the corrosion rate of Mg-4Zn-1Ca alloys decreases with the increase of corrosion time; and the oxide film will lose protective capability after 60 min. The mean corrosion rate of as-cast Mg-4Zn-1Ca alloy declines from 10.91mm/a to 4.447 mm/a after immersion test for 5h.
