Effect of ultrasonic-ozone treatments on microorganisms in pear juice
A study was made on the effects of ultrasonic-ozone treatments on the yeast and mold in Pear juices(pH5).With different ultrasonic power(200～800W),ozone concentration(0.02～0.14mg/kg) and treatment time(35～210s).It is indicated the effect of sterilization by ultrasonic-ozone is better than that of ultrasonic or ozone only.
