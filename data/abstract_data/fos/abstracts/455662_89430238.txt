Study on Zymotechnique of cyclodextrin glycosyltransferase by Bacillus alkalophilus MS-5
Objiective:To make the cyclodextrin glucosyltransferase-producing strain of Bacillus alkalophilus MS-5 to adapt to the needs of industrial production. Methods:Fermentation of strain MS-5 was carried out in 5L-fermentor and the fermentation process was optimized in accordance with the laws of the fermentation. Results:During the exponential phase the dissolution oxygen of medium was very low. The pH of medium dropped at the prophase and increased after the metaphase while the CGTase concentration increased continually. During the stationary phase,the dissolution oxygen,pH of medium returned to their original level,but the maximal yield of CGTase yield was obtained at the time. By increasing the dissolution oxygen of medium at the exponential phase,the time to reach enzyme activity peak was shortened by 50%. Conclusions:In 5 L-fermentor,the strain MS-5 could produce as much as 6032.5 U/mL.
