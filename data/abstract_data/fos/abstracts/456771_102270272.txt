Study on the process and properties of selective oxidized cotton fiber by sodium periodate
In this paper,dialdehyde cellulose was prepared by periodate selective oxidation of secondary hy-droxyl groups of cotton fiber.The preparation process was optimized by orthogonal design-direct analysis.The results showed that the optimum preparation process was determined as sodium periodate 2 g·L-1,oxidized at 40℃ for 2 h,taken dialdehyde content and strength loss rate as indices.It can be obtained that after periodate treatment,new activated aldehyde groups were generated on cotton cellulose molecular chain while the crystallinity of cot-ton fiber was decreased by FI-IR and XRD(x-ray diffraction) analysis.Compared with native cotton,elastic de-form and water absorption of cotton fiber oxidized by optimum process increased,and its initial modulus de-creased a little,so the oxidized cotton fiber softened slightly.
