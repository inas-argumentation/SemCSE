Palettisation et stockage en entrepôts frigorifiques
The article first sets out to define the role of public cold stores, placing emphasis on the importance of handling operations. Various storage modes employed are then discussed: bulk storage, batch or individual storage, ie storage to enable easier access to each independent load. A description is given of various stowage systems, more or less automatic, and solutions adopted in order to reduce lost space whilst still maintaining the possibility of providing access to each load unit. The complementary services provided by public stores are also described: packaging, preparation of orders, distribution etc. Maintaining stock-accounting is particularly interesting, with data processing becoming widely used in the management of stocks and it has been extended to the control of storage operations themselves, whether for pallet stowage or in order preparation areas.
