Application of Copper Pyrithione Suspension Agent and Its Moulding Control Activity
A better formula of the copper pyrithione suspension concentration as wood moulding control was studied.The results showed that the preferable wetting-dispersing agents for 20% copper pyrithione suspension concentration were 602#,33# and 500#.The results also indicated that it had a good effect of moulding prevention against common wood fungi(Aspergillus fumigatus,Penicillium purpurogenum,Botryodiplodia theobromae Pat)at about 1% concentration.
