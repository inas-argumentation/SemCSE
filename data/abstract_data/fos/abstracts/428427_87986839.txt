Efficacy of 18 Plant Volatiles Regulating Behaviours of Chaitophorus salinigra
Chaitophorus salinigra is one of the most serious pests in gardens.Eighteen plant volatiles were selected as the source of flavor to test the aphid selectively behavioral responses with Y-olfactometer.The results showed that the aphid choice behavior of(Z)-3-Hexenol was very significant(p 0.01),while(Z)-3-Hexenyl Acetate,(E)-2-Hexenal,Linalool,Benzaldehyde and(E)-β-Ocimene was significant(p 0.05).Mixed volatiles composed of(Z)-3-Hexenol,(Z)-3-Hexenyl Acetate,(E)-2-Hexenal,Linalool,Benzaldehyde and(E)-β-Ocimene with the same concentration revealed the aphid choice behavior was very significant(p0.01).The aphid traps using the 6 kinds of volatile components and the blend as the attractants,combined with the jasmine yellow glue board showed that jasmine yellow sticky board attached green leaf alcohol(Z)-3-Hexenol had more significant effect.Two days and 6 days later,the control effect was 46.65% and 31.58%,respectively,while jasmine yellow sticky board attached blend volatiles had the most significant effect.Two days and 6 days later,the control effect was 53.50% and 41.09%,respectively.The control efficacy was as good as other biological control ways.
