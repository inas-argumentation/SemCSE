A study of HLA-DQA1*0301 and *0103 hereditary susceptibility between lacunar stroke and essential hypertension
Objective To analyze the hereditary susceptibility by genotyping of HLA DQA1 alleles according to trait of multiple genes leading to diseases in lacunar stroke(LS)and essential hypertension (EH). Methods The allelic typing of HLA DQA1 was done by PCR SSP in 62 cases of LS,52 cases of EH and 64 cases of normal individuals. Results The frequency of HLA DQA1*0301 was obviously higher in groups of LS and EH patients, and the frequency of HLA DQA1*0103 was significantly lower in LS and EH patients. Conclusion HLA DQA1*0301 allele might be a correlative gene with hereditary susceptibility of LS and EH, whereas HLA DQA1*0103 allele might be a protective gene in the patients with LS and EH.
