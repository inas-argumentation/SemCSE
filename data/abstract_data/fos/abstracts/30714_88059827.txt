Determination of Blood Glucose by Fluorescence Capillary Analysis Based on Glucose/Glucose Oxidase/H_2O_2/L-tyrosine/ Horseradish Peroxidase Reaction System
A novel fluorescent reaction system of glucose/glucose oxidase(GOD)/hydrogen peroxide/L-tyrosine/horseradish peroxidase(HRP) combined with a fluorescence capillary analysis(FCA) was established for developing a sensitive glucose enzyme FCA(GE-FCA).After studying the new reaction,the optimum conditions were as follows: concentrations of GOD and HRP were both 2000 U/L,concentration of L-tyrosine was 1.0 mmol/L,pH of phosphate buffer solution was 8.5,reaction temperature was 35 ℃ and reaction time was 20 min.The GE-FCA′s linear range was 5.0-140 μmol/L,relative standard deviation was less than 4.0% and detection limit was 2.65 μmol/L.The method has been successfully applied for the determination of blood glucose and the recovery was in the range of 98%-104%.Compared with routine methods,merits of the GE-FCA was simple in operation,less in sample volume and dosage of chemical as well as enzymic reagents(only 9 μL),wide in linear range,strong in anti-interference ability and low cost,it will be easily popularized.
