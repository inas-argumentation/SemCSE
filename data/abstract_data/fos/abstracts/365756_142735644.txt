Identification of Factors Responsible for Conflict among High School Students
The Development of a nation depends upon the development of its children. According to Kothari commission report (1966) “The destiny of India is being shaped in its classroom”. Every student is facing two environments, namely home and school. In these environments they are facing many problems. Particularly, during adolescence period every individual is facing the problem. It is the period of rapid revolutionary changes in the individual’s physical, mental, moral, spiritual, sex and social outlook.               During this epoch the adolescence are not excel in their studies and is likely to be affected by the environment.
