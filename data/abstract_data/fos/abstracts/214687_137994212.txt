Investigation on Thermal Conductivity of Mo-Cu at Low Temperature
The thermal conductivity of Mo-Cu prepared by melt infiltration at 350-20 K was measured.The results show that the thermal conductivity(TC) of the alloy is 200 W/(m·K) at 350 K.The TC decreases with the temperature reducing.The TC elevated below 200 K and achieved a maximum of 305 W/(m·K) at 20 K.The microstructure of the sample which corroded by nitric acid was observed by SEM.The results show that the Mo has been sintered into skeleton structure in the preparation process,which is the reason for the TC elevation at low temperature.
