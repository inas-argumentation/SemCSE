Studying the Orientation of the Database Center in Guangzhou Social Security Information System
Cooperation of many official departments, interlink of different database, and inter-departmental data sharing are the problems with which will be confronted in the Guangzhou Social Security Information System (GZSSIS). A simple and effective way to solve these problems is to found the database center. The orientation will take effect in the operation designing and system designing, and the implementation of database center. So, the orientation must be confirmed before we begin to found the database center. According to the experience of the GZSSIS, the database center is designed and founded to complete shared data management and data exchanging.
