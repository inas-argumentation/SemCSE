Wärmeleitung abgeschreckt kondensierter Silberschichten
Silver films quenched by evaporation upon a cooled substratum have a very low thermal conductivity of electronic origin (1.8 K<T<20.4 K). The temperature proportional part obeys Wiedemann-Franz's law. The quadratic component depends on the structure expressing deviations from Matthiessen's rule. The lattice component of thermal conductivity is estimated at 1% of the bulk value.A low temperature thermopile is constructed by evaporation. It combines high sensivity (150 μV/K) with a high thermal resistance (> 0.2 K/μW) within the He- and H2-range.
