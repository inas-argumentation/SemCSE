Research on the Clustering Algorithm on Video Semantic Scene Extraction
Clustering of video data is an important issue in video abstraction,browsing and retrieval.How to get the high-level scenes becomes an important problem.In this paper,considering the similarity between the frames using comprehensively ratio rate basing on frames block and boundary information basing on wavelet change,we propose a novel shot clustering algorithm combing the editing feature of the video and the criteria of choosing representative shots.The simulated experiment results demonstrate the effectiveness of our method for the dialogue-dominated video,the precision and the recall of this algorithm are higher than the WBS algorithm about 8.7% and 28.4%,respectively.
