Effects of Microstructure on Delayed Fracture of Ultra High-strength Steel
The U-bent experiment was adopted for investing the effects of microstructure on delayed fracture properties of 1500 MPa ultra high-strength martensite steel.The results show that the grain size of martensite,the internal stress of steel and the carbon contents of martensite reduce with the tempering temperature increasing,which improves delayed fracture resistance of ultra high-strength martensite steel.But,when the tempering temperature reaches 400 ℃,martensite has transformed to tempered troostite,which decreases delayed fracture resistance of steel.
