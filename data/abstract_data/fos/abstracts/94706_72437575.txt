Multicentric preliminary report for endoscopic treatment of Vesico-Ureteral reflux using Polyacrylate-Polyalcohol Copolymer Particles in Glycerol and Water (Vantris)
Abstract Purpose This polyacrylate-polyalcohol was developed in the laboratory, pursuant to ISO10993-1:2003 Standards, both in vitro and in animals. The substance is a synthetic, non-absorbable type, with low bulking reaction, manually injectable. A preliminary report of patients with at least 1-year follow up is presented. clinical, ultrasound, scintigraphy, urine, laboratory were performed by means of cysto-video-urodynamics. Material and Methods Out of 74 patients injected with this substance, 52 have been followed up for more than one year. The average injection age was 38 months and the number of injected ureters was 75, 35 right and 40 left. Presenting 3 grade V, 11 grade IV, 34 grade III units and 27 grade II units, a close circuit TV system and 23-gauge needles were used. The volume that was injected manually was: 0.2 mL-1 mL per unit with a median of 0.66 mL. The echographic and cysto-video-urodynamyc and clinical follow-up was performed between 16 and 24 months with a mean follow-up of 20 months. Results There were no echographic changes. The complications were in 3(5,7%) patients with urinary infections, 3(5,7%) with fever and 3(5,7%) with lumbar pain after the injection. Antibiotics were stopped after 10 days of treatment. In 67 (89,3%) kidney units, reflux was eliminated, 5 (6,6%)Kidney units, reflux was improved to grade I and in 4(5,3%) Kidney units, the implantation failed with 2 grade III and 2 grade IV. Conclusions In this number of patients, polyalcohol-polyacrylate has shown to be easy to work with. Given the characteristics of the material, since this is a permanent material, it has presented no complications and thus becomes an attractive material as the first choice for permanent treatment of vesico-ureteral reflux.
