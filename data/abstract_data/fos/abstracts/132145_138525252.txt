Microscopic Analysis on High-temperature Oxide Film for Grid Plate of Grate-kiln
The high-temperature oxide film for grid plate with more than 18 months life was analyzed and studied by XRD,SEM and EDS techniques.The results show that the oxide film contains SiO2 layer and Cr2O3 layer and has excellent compatibility and continuity,and it is thin and imbedded in the backing.Besides,nickel-rich layer with stable high-temperature chemical properties lies between Cr2O3 layer and the backing.It plays a complementary role which makes the compatibility,continuity and adhesiveness of the oxide film even better.Thus,the grid plate has excellent oxidation resistance and longer service life than other similar products.
