Study on Microstructure Changes of T91/G102 Welded Joints after Long-running
After a long time running,the microstructure changes of joints welded with T91/G102 dissimilar steel have been analyzed. The results show that the long-running has little effect on the microstructure of T91 side,but visible effects on the microstructure of G102 side:after a long time running, in G102 side,the original lath-shape bainite features in base metal disappear, recrystal grain forming and carbide accumulating and growing,original small granular bainite features normalizing zone in G102 side remaine except a certain extent of recovery; recovery and recrystallization take place in overheating zone, the smaller grains form, and the original grains boundaries become more clearly.In welding zone the lath-shape microstructure reduces, and equiaxed microstructure increases.The microstructure on the other side away from the fire is smaller than that of the side closed to the fire, and the obvious columnar grains form in some parts of the weld zone on the side away from the fire.
