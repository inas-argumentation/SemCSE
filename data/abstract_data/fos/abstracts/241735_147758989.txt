On universities' presidents and liberalism education in China's modern times
At the beginning of the 20th century, China's modern universities tended to hold liberalism education, partly owing to the influence of western liberalism theories, and partly to the earnest practice of some famous presidents. This tendency was reflected in every specific field in education, such as the inclination to advocate individuality as the goal, sticking to academic freedom in the course, and insisting on professors as the administrators. It is especially progressive in its opposition to the feudal education restraints on individual's development. However,it was doomed to fail in the old semi-feudal and semi-colonial society due to the environment and the weakness in itself as well.
