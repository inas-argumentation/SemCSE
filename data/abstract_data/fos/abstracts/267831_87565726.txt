Application of response surface method for optimal concentration of culture medium for enzymes of degrading cholesterol
A medium for enzymes of degrading cholesterol was optimized.At first,the effect of NH4NO3,Tween-80,KH2PO4,yeast extract,MgSO4·7H2O and cholesterol on enzymes of degrading cholesterol was evaluated using a fractional factorial design,and secondly the path of steepest ascent and the response surface analysis were used to approach the optimal concentration of the medium composition.The results show Tween-80 increase the activity of enzymes,yeast extract decrese it,and optimal concentration of them is 0.905mL/L and 5.694g/L respectively.The enzyme activity of degrading cholesterol is 1.956×10-3U/mL under optimized medium.
