Gaining Control: Direct Suzuki Arylation of Dihydroazulenes and Tuning of Photo- and Thermochromism (Eur. J. Org. Chem. 6/2011)
The cover picture shows different states of a dihydroazulene/vinylheptafulvene photochromic system that was prepared by a Suzuki cross-coupling reaction. The color of the system is controlled by light, heat, acid, and base. Details are discussed in the article by M. A. Petersen, M. B. Nielsen et al. on p. 1033 ff.
