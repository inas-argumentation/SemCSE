Comparison of Structure and Performance about Two Kinds of Microstrip Dual-band Bluetooth Antenna
The dual-band Bluetooth antenna operates in two bands 2.4 ～ 2.5GHz and 5.15 ～ 5.35GHz respectively,two kinds of microstrip dual-band Bluetooth antenna are investigated.the straight arm inverted F antenna and the twist arm inverted F antenna are simulated,the antennas have been also tested,results showed that two kinds of structures are suitable for Bluetooth communications.
