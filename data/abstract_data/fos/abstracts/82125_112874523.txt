Study on the Project of Hunan Power System Interconnected with Central China Power System in the Eleventh Five-Year Plan Period
500 kV trunk network of Hunan power system will be developed rapidly in the Eleventh Five-Year Plan Period. It's guarantee for Hunan power system running economic and safely that three chunnels deliver electricity from west to east of Hunan province and three chunnels from north to south of Changjiang River be built on forming the 500 kV loop of endure system.In order to coordinate electricity transmitted from Three Gorges to Hunan, the third 500 kV tie-line is built from Qianjiang in Hubei province to Yueyang in Hunan province is better than from Jingzhou to Yiyang through investigation. The goal network of Hunan power system is imagined in this paper.
