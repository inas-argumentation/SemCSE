On-line Hyphenated Chromatographic Electrochemical Instrument for Improving Accelerated Oxidation Degradation Test
In order to improve the accelerated oxidation degradation test,the method by using an on-line hyphenated chromatographic electrochemical instrument( ECI) was established. Tert butyl hydroquinone( TBHQ) was selected as modal drug to optimize several conditions factors of ECI including applied potential of Columba electrochemical detector,stretch time of high voltage switching six-way valve and flow rate. The optimized conditions were obtained after all above investigations were finished. The oxidant of TBHQ was identified by the high preference liquid chromatography-diode array detector. The optimized conditions including applied potential of Columba electrochemical detector,stretch time of high voltage switching six-way valve and flow rate were 150 m V,30 s and 0. 2 m L / min,respectively. The oxidant of TBHQ was preliminarily identified as tert butyl quinine( TQ). The method based ECI was on-lined and automated and will be very useful in improving accelerated oxidation degradation test.
