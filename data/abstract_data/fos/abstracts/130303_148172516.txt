The scientific connotation and disciplinary construction of Higher Educatoin
Higher Education is not only an independent education catalog with rich connotations, but a broad practical field. Its main contradictory is between the development of one's personality and the social request. Its basic rule is focused on two aspects: the cultivating rule of advanced special talents; the developing rule of higher education. So the ultimate aim of the disciplinary construction of higher education is to eventually form its independent and scientific theory system through widely question\|based research and multi\|disciplinary research.
