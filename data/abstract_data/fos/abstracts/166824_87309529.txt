Research on protoplast fusant of Ganoderma lucidum using biochemistry identification method
Ganoderma lucidum Meida and the Ganoderma lucidum Xinzhi were used as starting strains and their protoplasts were fused by chemistry method,biochemistry identification method was used to detect the results in this paper.The results showed that extracellular polysaccharide content of fusant was increased 332.80% and 22.86% compared with Ganoderma lucidum Meida and Ganoderma lucidum Xinzhi separatedly,intracellular triterpenoid content was increased 36.21% and 113.16% compared with Ganoderma lucidum Meida and Ganoderma lucidum Xinzhi separatedly,extracellular triterpenoid content was increased 23.17% and 46.31% compared with Ganoderma lucidum Meida and Ganoderma lucidum Xinzhi,protein content of extracellular was increased 51.91% and 33.56% separatedly.Agarose gel electrophoresis experiments was used to detect DNA of fusant and parents.The result revealed that DNA of fusant sped slower than the parents'.It proved that the DNA molecular weight of fusant was greater than parents' and parental genetic material had been recombined.
