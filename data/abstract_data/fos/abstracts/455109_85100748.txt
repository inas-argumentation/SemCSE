Metabolism and thermotolerance function of trehalose in Saccharomyces : a current perspective
The aim of this review is to update the regulatory mechanisms involved in trehalose metabolism as well as provide an insight into the application we have given to the acquired knowledge in investigating the transduction of the glucose signal across the membrane and the effects caused by heat shock . Furthermore, the role this disaccharide might play in the acquisition of thermotolerance by yeast cells will be discussed.
