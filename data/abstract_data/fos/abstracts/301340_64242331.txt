Performance of SISO-OFDM Systems Based on Turbo Product Codes over IMT-2000 Channels
Turbo product codes(TPCs),which combine the properties of turbo codes and product codes, are an advanced channel coding scheme in broadband mobile communication systems.The encoding and decoding of turbo product codes are introduced .Then a novel SISO-OFDM system based on turbo product codes is proposed. The system performance in IMT-2000 channels for a uplink mobile user is studied. Simulation results show that Bit Error Rate (BER) of 10 -6 is achieved over IMT-2000 channels at Eb/N0 15dB.Further MIMO(Multiple-Input Multiple-Output)-OFDM systems research will be based on this work.
