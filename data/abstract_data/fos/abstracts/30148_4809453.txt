Maximum allowable limits in biological materials in the prevention of inorganic lead poisoning
In order to prevent poisoning, it is necessary to have clearly in mind what one wants to prevent. There is great confusion concerning the concept of poisoning, especially in medical circles not in direct contact with occupational medicine. What one physician calls" intoxication", " poisoning", " pathological state", "disease", is considered by another merely as a consequence of an allowable" high intake", " impregnation". Therefore it is necessary to specify that degree of reaction of the organism which is not allowable and which should be prevented.
