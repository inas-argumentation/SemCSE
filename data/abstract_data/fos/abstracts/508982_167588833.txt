On the Study of Rural Pension Insurance System under the Background of Aging Population
Rural problems have been attached importance by the CPC and its government,even the whole society.Against the background of aging population,the problem of rural pension insurance has received popular social attention.Constructing and perfecting rural pension insurance system is considered important measures to plan social development,promote social harmony,improve farmers' living standard and build a moderately prosperous society in all aspects.This paper gives detailed analyses of the present situation and developing tendency of aging population,rural pension insurance and problems they bring in China.It also introduces correspondingly specific solutions to rural pension insurance.
