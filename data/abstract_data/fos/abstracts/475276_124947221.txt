Studying of Computation Complexity Reduction of Rate-distortion Motion Estimation Using Integer DCT Transform
Rate-distortion is one of the key tools minimizing the motion-compensation prediction error. But for the high computation complexity of it's distortion criterion ,it hasn't been widely used for a long time. In this paper , integer DCT is used to calculate the texture factor of distortion criterion replacing of the DCT transform in order to increase the computation efficiency. The proposed optimization formula is derived in detail by paper, and the validation of this approach is shown by several simulation results.
