Causes of future Mediterranean precipitation decline depend on the season
•We aim to determine the causes of the Mediterranean precipitation decline throughout the year. An ensemble of 2 regional climate simulations is used to simulate the end of century precipitation change (1971-2000 vs. 2070-2099) assuming RCP8.5. The ensemble is based on the GCMs MPI-ESM-LR and HadGEM2-ES and the mean is analyzed. By performing simulations with modified lateral boundary conditions we can assess the importance of four different drivers of climate change.
