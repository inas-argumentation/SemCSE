A Study on Enterprises' Employment Strategies with Labor Contract Law
The issuance and implementation of Labor Contract Law and Implementing Regulations of Labor Contract Law have exerted a direct effect on the establishment, fulfillment, change and termination of enterprises’ labor contracts, hence influencing their employment forms and costs. Therefore, it has been an urgent and practical issue for enterprises’ human resource management how to adapt their employment system to the new labor regulations. From the perspective of human resource management, this article aims at exploring into some possible legal problems during the course of recruiting, employing and dismissing employees as well as coming up with some suggestions on how to adjust and perfect the present employment system.
