Thermal and electrical properties of rapidly quenched glasses in the systems Li2S_SiS2_LixMOy (LixMOy = Li4SiO4, Li2SO4)
Abstract Superionic glasses were prepared in the systems (100 − x ) (0.6 Li 2 S · 0.4 SiS 2 ) · zLi x MO y ( Li x MO y = Li 4 SiO 4 , Li 2 SO 4 ) by twin-roller rapid quenching. Glass transition temperatures ( T g ) and crystallization temperatures ( T c ) were determined for the glasses. The difference between T g and T c , which was one of the measures of glass stability against crystallization, was maxmized at the composition with 5 mol% Li 4 SiO 4 . The conductivity at 25 °C (σ 25 ) was as high as 10 −3 Scm −1 for the glass with 5 mol% Li 4 SiO 4 . The doping of Li 2 SO 4 , however, did not improve the glass stability against crystallization nor σ 25
