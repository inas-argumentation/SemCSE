Teachers' profession development in institutes cooperation perspective: analysis of empowerment and discipline
The cooperation partnership between university and primary and secondary school becomes a more and more important measure to advance teachers' development. Theoretically, the cooperation is good for the development of teachers' knowledge, ability, attitude and emotion. Teachers can feel the empowerment in the cooperation. But the sophisticated reality often changes the effort of teachers' empowerment in university into the discipline of teachers. Cognizance of the dilemma between the two values is important to discuss the cooperation.
