Enzymatic hydrolysis of kitchen garbage for microbial oils production.
As to the available components in the kitchen garbage,complex enzymes were employed to hydrolyze kitchen garbage,and the optimum hydrolysis conditions of kitchen garbage for microbial oils production were determined as follows: 8 u.g-1 of amylase,200 u.g-1 of glucoamylase,50 u.g-1 of protease and 50 u.g-1 cellulose be added at the same time,pH 5-7,50-60℃ and hydrolyzing for 2 h.After adjusted appropriately,the hydrolyzate of kitchen garbage could be used as culture medium for the strain G9 of Geotrichum robustum in microbial oils production,and the lipid yield of strain G9 could reach 9.74 g.L-1,which means every tonne of kitchen garbage could be converted to near 20 kg lipid.
