Transgenic Tobacco Plants Resistant to Potato virus Y(PVY) via dsRNA-mediated Virus Resistance
In order to obtain transgenic tobacco plants resistant to Potato virus Y(PVY),sense and antisense fragments of 3' end of gene HC-Pro of PVY were amplified respectively to construct an inverted repeat plant expression vector.Tobacco(Nicotiana tabacum)cultivar Yunyan 85 was transformed via Agrobacterium tumefaciens mediation,and T0 generation transgenic tobacco lines were acquired by resistance screening and resistance identification.T0 resistance transgenic tobacco lines were propagated.It was found that T1 progeny of partial resistant lines generated a certain proportion of segregation of resistance to susceptibility by resistance identification.T1 generation tobacco plants generating segregation of resistance to susceptibility were detected by real-time PCR.It was found that RNA accumulation level of gene HC-Pro in transgenic resistant tobacco plants was significantly lower than that in susceptible transgenic tobacco plants,and it was illustrated that gene HC-Pro generated RNA silencing in transgenic resistant tobacco plants.In our research work,gene HC-Pro was silenced by using dsRNA technique and T1 generation transgenic plants of tobacco cultivar Yunyan 85 resistant to PVY was obtained.
