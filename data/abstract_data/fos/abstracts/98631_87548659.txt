Protoplast Occurrence and Regeneration of Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. cubense
The effects of some factors on the occurrence and regeneration of protoplasts of Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. cubense were studied. These factors include the appropriate hypha time for isolating protoplasts, the concentrations of enzyme, digesting times and temperatures, and osmotic pressure stabilizers. The purified protoplasts were regenerated on potato dextrose agar medium containing 0.8 mol/L sucrose, and the regeneration rate was 22.5 %.
