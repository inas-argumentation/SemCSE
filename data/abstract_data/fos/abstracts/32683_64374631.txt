Thin-server Using Search Algorithm for Keyword Matches in P2P Systems
P2P is a key technology in the future network,and the search algorithm is the master factor of determining the performance of the P2P network.This paper based on some existed search algorithm,and described a thin-server based search algorithm,in which the server got rid of the burden of responding the huge number of queries but only took charge of establishing and maintaining the search-trees,and the task to search is distributing to the nodes of the network.The algorithm keeps the information of the topology of the network while the search queries are processed by the nodes,thus brings the high percent success of search recalls,high message efficiency,nodes equality and easy maintenance.
