FLOW EVAPORATION HEAT TRANSFER AND PRESSURE DROP IN HORIZONTAL MICRO-FIN TUBES
To investigate the effects of micro-fin geometry and operating conditions on flow evaporation performance of micro-fin tubes, tests were conducted on four micro-fin tubes having different micro-fin geometries and one smooth tube having 9.52?mm outside diameter (OD). Two of the micro-fin tubes had 9.52?mm OD and the remaining two had 7.0?mm OD.The refrigerant used was R22. The experiments were carried out for 90-400?kg·m -2·s -1 refrigerant mass fluxes at 7?℃ evaporation temperature, 15%-20% inlet vapor quality, 5-6?℃ outlet superheat degrees. The variations of evaporation performance of micro-fin tubes with refrigerant mass flux were obtained. The effects of micro-fin geometry and tube diameter on performance were discussed. The two 9.52?mm OD micro-fin tubes yielded 130% and 180% greater heat transfer coefficients than the smooth tube, while the inside surface areas of the two micro-fin tubes were only 40% and 70% greater than that of the smooth tube.
