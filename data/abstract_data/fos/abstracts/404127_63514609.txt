Study on Model of Production Process Management System Based on IDEF &UML
A hybrid modeling method of IDEF and UML was proposed, which can effectively avoid the shortcoming of setting up model for information flow with IDEF and inaccuracy syntax with UML. IDEF0 method was used to determine the system function model, the IDEF1x supports information model and the UML aids to design an object-oriented software. With this method, a model of production process management system based on the components was designed as an example, and the system is maintainable, extensible and reusable.
