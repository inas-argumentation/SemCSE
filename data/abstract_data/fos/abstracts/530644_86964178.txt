Molecular Markers and the Linkage Map of Avr-Pid2 in the Rice Blast Fungus Magnaporthe oryzae
Cloning and variation of avirulence genes will provide important information to support rice resistance gene deployment.The progeny isolates of FJ81278 and GUY11 were inoculated on a transgenic line with Pi-d2 and its parent variety TP309 to assay their virulence.Results showed that the isolate FJ81278 had the Avr-Pid2 gene,and the segregation ratio of avirulence and virulence on Pi-d2 fit 1:1,suggesting it was a single independent locus.By further genetic analysis,two SSR and PATE markers were found to tightly link to the avirulence locus.The genetic distance between the marker and the AVR gene was estimated to be 6.1 cM,13.0 cM respectively.These two markers delimited the Avr-Pid2 in a range of 500 kb in chromosome 7,which provides a solid foundation to clone the Avr gene.
