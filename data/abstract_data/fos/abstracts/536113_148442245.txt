On Mozi's Business Ethics Thought and Its Modern Values
Mozi was an eminent thinker in our ancient country.His business ethics thought was abundant.The thought embodies:(1)the utililarianism view of unite righteousness and benefit;(2)the production view based on the agriculture;(3)the consumption view based on the thrift;(4)the respect on person with ability.(4)the moral evaluation view of unite motivation and impact.The thought has an important inspiration for socialism market economy.
