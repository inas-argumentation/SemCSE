Effects of pretreating procedure on preparation of Ca_3Co_4O_(9 +δ) powder
Pretreating procedures of dry gel affect the properties of Ca3Co4O9 + δpowder synthesized by citrate acid sol-gel method. The Ca3Co4O9 + δgel is prepared through sol-gel method with deionized water as solvent,citric acid as chelate,and metal nitrates as starting materials. Polycrystalline Ca3Co4O9 + δpowder is prepared via calcining the dry gel,and the Ca3Co4O9 + δwith different grain sizes is prepared by controlling the calcining time. The obtained Ca3Co4O9 + δpowders are analyzed by XRD,SEM,TEM,SAED and Marlvern granularity analysis. The results show that the Ca3Co4O9 + δpowder is single phased composition with good crystalline quality and flat-shaped powder microstructure,the crystal size is about 300 nm,the grain size ranges from 300 nm to 1. 5 μm,and the grain size increases with increasing the calcining time.
