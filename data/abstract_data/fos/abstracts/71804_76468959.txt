Effect of dexamethasone on the expression of Toll-like receptor-4 and -2 mRNA in splenic macrophages of allergic asthma mouse model
Objective To detect the expression of gene Toll-like receptor-4 (TLR4) and Toll-like receptor-2 (TLR2) mRNA and the effect of dexamethasone(DM) on them in splenic macrophages of allergic asthma mice. Methods With the fluorescent indicator SYBR-greenⅠ and the house-keeping gene HPRT, LightCycler real-time PCR assay was applied to test the threshold cycle times(Ct) of cDNA of TLR4, TLR2 and HPRT gene to get Ct2, Ct4 and CtH respectively. The value of relative quantity of mRNA level was calculated by the ratio of Ct2/CtH, Ct4/CtH. To compare the value of relative quantity between the control mice and allergic asthma mice. BALB/c allergic asthma mice were injected with DM, and the mRNA from splenic macrophages was isolated and examined for the expressions of TLR4 and TLR2 mRNA. Results There is difference of Ct2/CtH in splenic macrophages between the control and allergic asthma mice(P0.05), and the expression of TLR2 mRNA in the splenic macrophages of allergic asthma mice was down-regulated; there is difference of Ct2/CtH in splenic macrophages between allergic asthma mice and allergic asthma DM mice(P0.05), and the expression of TLR2 mRNA in the splenic macrophages of a allergic asthma DM mice was up-regulated; there is no difference of Ct4/CtH in splenic macrophages between the control mice and allergic asthma mice(P0.05); there is difference of Ct4/CtH in splenic macrophages between allergic asthma mice and allergic asthma DM mice(P0.05), and the expression of TLR4 mRNA in the splenic macrophages of a allergic asthma DM mice was up-regulated. Conclusion There is no change of the expression of TLR4 in allergic asthma mice and recognising PAMP is not down-regulated. The expression of TLR2 in allergic asthma mice was down-regulated, and the expression of TLR4 and TLR2 was up-regulated by DM.
