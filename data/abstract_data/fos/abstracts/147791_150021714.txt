Evaluation Of Crystallization Kinetics Of Fe85Zr3.5Nb3.5B7Cu1 Amorphous Alloy By Non-Isothermal Analysis
The crystallization processes for the amorphous Fe85Zr3.5Nb3.5B7Cu1 alloy were investigated using no-isothermal method. The crystallization data are analyzed in terms of a modified Kissinger’s equation to determine the activation energy (E) and the Avrami exponent (n). The obtained activation energies for the first crystallization is 271±19kJ/mol, with the crystallization kinetics proceeding by a three-dimensional diffusion controlled grain growth on pre-existing nuclei, where n=1.3.
