Effect of arsenic on tea plant growth and its absorption and accumulation in tea plant
Adopting a method of Hydroponics in laboratory,toxic symptom under the situation of different concentrations in As3+ intimidation,and the effect on the growth tea shoot of 3 kinds of cultivars,as well as the absorption of arsenic and accumulative characteristic in the organs were analyzed.The results show that when the intimidate concentration of arsenic exceeds 1mg·L-1,tea plant presents serious hazard symptom,meanwhile intimidate of arsenic has negatively effect on the germination and growth of tea shoot remarkably.Different tea cultivars indicate at a certain extent with different abilities of anti-arsenic,as Fuding white teaMingshan 213Mingshan 131.Arsenic contents in the organs were inclined to a trend with the increase of arsenic treatment concentration,and arsenic content in various organs and arsenic concentration in nourish liquid present remarkable positive correlation.The distribution of arsenic in tea plant shows a regularity,that is in radicelthickset rootstalkfresh stalk leafnew shoot.When arsenic intimidation concentration is more than 1 mg·L-1,the arsenic content in the one bud with two leaves of fresh tip may exceed 2 mg·kg-1.
