Comparison of Anglo-American classification of higher education policy——To take British binary higher education system and the master plan for higher education in California for an example
British binary higher education system and the California Higher Education Master Plan are the typical cases of higher education classification with government intervention appeared in the 1960s,but both the results of the implementation,effectiveness and ultimate fate are very different.These two policies as typical cases for a comparative study of higher education,has an important inspiration for the world higher education,especially for China developing the policy of higher education classification.
