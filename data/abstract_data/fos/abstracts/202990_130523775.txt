Cutting Propagation of Mallotus furetianus(Miq.) Muell.Arg
Mallotus furetianus is a wild tea plant of great development value.Cutting propagation of M.furetianus was conducted with young lignified shoots(1～2 years old)of M.furetianus naturally grown in Wuzhishan as the cutting material and sand as the culture medium.The shoot fragments of different size(length of about 10 cm,diameter of 0.20～0.40,0.45～0.70,0.75～1.00 cm)were soaked in Gen Wang,IBA,NAA,IAA using different concentrations(100,400,800 mg/L)of different time(0.5,2.0 and 8.0 h).The results showed that the treatments of the cuttings with length of about 10 cm and diameter of 0.45～0.70 cm produced the best results;the highest rooting rates(50% or more)produced with Gen Wang or IBA;Of the different concentrations of growth regulator treatments,800 mg/L of IBA was optimal with the rooting rate of 60% and average root number of 13;and the best rooting treatment was dipping or soaking the base of cuttings in the solutions for 0.5 h.
