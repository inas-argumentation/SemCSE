Effect of Northern Climatic Condition on Performance of Furan Resin Self-hardening Sand
Aimed at the characteristics of northern climate,it is cold and dry in winter,and warm and more humid in summer,the effects of the hardening characteristics of resin sand and the environmental condition of temperature and humidity on the performance of the resin sand were experimentally studied.The results show that the intensity of sand resin has a great relationship with environmental temperature and humidity;the higher the temperature,the faster the harden;but when the temperature is too high,the final strength reduces.The greater the humidity,the slower the harden,the final strength also decreases.
