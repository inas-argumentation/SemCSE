Fractional calculus and some properties of certain starlike functions with negative coefficients
A certain subclass T"@c(n,p,@l,@a) of starlike functions in the unit disk is introduced. The main object of this paper is to derive several interesting properties of functions belonging to the class T"@c(n,p,@l,@a). Various distortion inequalities for fractional calculus of functions in the T"@c(n,p,@l,@a) are also given.
