Autoimmune hypoparathyroidism associated with pulmonary tuberculosis.
A 53-year-old man with a history of bilateral cataract surgery at the age of 32 years with ‘a negative work-up’ and no family history was referred for fatigue, intermittent fever, night sweats and cough for the last 2 months. At examination, he appeared fatigued and febrile (38.6°C). He also complained with perioral and limb paresthesiae, and muscles cramps of recent onset. Trousseau and Chvostek signs were both present. Blood cell count showed a hemoglobin level of 8.6 g/dl with a mean corpuscular volume of 73 fl and 54 000 reticulocytes/mm3, a white blood cell count of 6100/mm3 with 73% neutrophils and 15% lymphocytes and the platelet count was 363 000/mm3. Immunophenotyping of lymphoid cells showed 738 CD4+ cell/mm3 and 276 CD8+ cell/mm3 and a normal level at 9% of CD25+ CD127 low/reg of CD4 cell count. Human immunodeficiency virus testing was negative. Serum creatinine and liver function tests were normal, serum albumin was 32.8 g/l (normal 39–46 g/l). Serum total calcium level was 1.07 mmol/l (normal 2.16–2.52 mmol/l) and phosphate level 1.44 mmol/l (normal 0.85–1.31 mmol/l).
