Study on anti-oxidative action of the taurine-contained beer
The anti-oxidative action of taurine-contained beer in mice was studied. 48 KM mice(10 months old)were randomly divided into four groups: senior control group, ig ordinary beer; taurine-contained beer groups, ig taurine-contained beer at a dose of 10g/L, 5g/L, 2.5g/L respectively, the other 12(8-weeks-old) KM mice(young control group)were given ordinary beer. After 30d, the contents of lipofuscin of the liver and MDA, SOD and GSH-PX activities were measured. The results show that taurine-contained beer reduces the level of MDA and lipofuscin significantly, and also increases the activities of SOD and GHS-PX. Taurine-contained beer may have anti-oxidative and anti-senile ability.
