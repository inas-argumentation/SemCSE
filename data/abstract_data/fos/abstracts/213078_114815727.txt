Personnel Cultivation Model of Art & Design Major for Non-arts Matriculates
It is a common situation in China for non-arts matriculates to be admitted as art design majors. How to improve designing ability of those students is a common concern of many universities in China. Based on teaching practice and experience of the authors, this paper analyzes comprehensive ability of these non-arts matriculate and presents several thoughts about teaching aim of this major and the organizing of the teaching process, hoping to find a reasonable personnel cultivation model for non-arts matriculates.
