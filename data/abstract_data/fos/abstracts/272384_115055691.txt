RELIABILITY INDEX ANALYSIS ON ANCHOR-NET-SHOTCRET SUPPORT STRUCTURE OF TUNNEL UNDER NON-SYMMETRICAL CONDITION
Based on the analytical solution of elasticity and plasticity theory of circular tunnel surrounding rocks,the analytical model of reliability index on an anchor-net-shotcrete support structure of a tunnel under a non-symmetrical condition is built.Considered the high non-linearity of functions,the reliability index computation is adopted by the Monte-Carlo and Rosenblueth methods.The calculation results show that the reliability of a support structure is a common action result of surrounding rock,anchor,net and shotcrete.Reliability index decreases with the increase of the variation coefficients of the crustal stress,cohesive force,inner friction angle,thickness of a spraying layer,and the extension strength of an anchor and an anchor-reinforced force.Reliability index increases with the increase of a lateral pressure factor.The reliability index rapidly decreases with the increase of crustal stress.
