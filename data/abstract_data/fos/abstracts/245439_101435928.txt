Technical revamping of 100t/h wastewater stripping unit
Present situation and complete checking of wastewater stripping unit are introduced. Main controls parameters of the unit are analyzed. A new type of vertical mass transfer, with high efficiency CTST trays are chosen in capacity expansion revamping of stripping unit. The throughput of wastewater is enhanced from 100t/h to 140t/h and the unit runs all right. Normal producing and living environments of the refinery are protected.
