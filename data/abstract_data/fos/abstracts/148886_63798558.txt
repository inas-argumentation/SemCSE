Geography Information System in Power Quality Based on Web
This paper introduced the management system of the paper quality which is integrated with the WEB service、GIS,MapInfo,spatial database,using MapXtreme as the GIS platform.The designs and implementations of the function of each part of the system are discussed in detail,including the hardware equips,the design of the function mold and software construction,space database.The target of this system is to establish a gather system regarding database as the core,network as the sustaining calculator,using advanced calculator technique and mature software to make the management of the electric power quantity more science more on time,more accurate.
