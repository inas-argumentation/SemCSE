Joint User-antenna Power Allocation Algorithm Based on SLNR for Multi-user MIMO Downlink Channels
In multi-user MIMO downlink channels,power allocation between antennas or users can improve sum capacity.Based on iterative water-filling algorithm,a joint user-antenna power allocation algorithm is proposed for multi-user MIMO downlink channels.Before iterative water-filling,the proposed algorithm allocate power between users based on SLNR at first.The simulation result shows that sum capacity of the proposed algorithm is better than the iterative water-filling algorithm and the user selection based iterative water-filling algorithm.
