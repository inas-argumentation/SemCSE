Urbanization of the Ecdemic Rural Labour ——A Realistic Choice of Dwindling Economic Difference Among Regions in Jiangsu Province
Noticing the abnormal change of the difference of regional economy of Jiangsu Province,we analyzed the close relationship between Jiangsu regional economy difference change and difference change in urban and rural areas,and conclude that the ecdemic urbanization of people in the countryside is a realistic choice of dwindling regional economy difference.
