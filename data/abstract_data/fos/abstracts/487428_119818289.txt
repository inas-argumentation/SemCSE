Experimental determination of the Rydberg constant using two-photon spectroscopy of atomic hydrogen
Doppler-free two-photon absorption was used to observe the 2S–8D and 2S–10D transitions in atomic hydrogen and deuterium with a relative width of ~ 1.8 × 10− 9. A comparison between the wavelengths of these transitions and that of an iodine-cell-stabilized He–Ne laser yielded a new value ofthe Rydberg constant R = 109737.31569 ± 0.00006 cm− 1.
