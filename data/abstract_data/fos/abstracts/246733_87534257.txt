Efficacy of trehalose in cryopreservation of chicken spermatozoa.
In order to evaluate an effectiveness of trehalose in cryopreservation of chicken spermatozoa, semen was diluted with extender containing different levels of trehalose and glycerol, and was frozen in pellets. After thawing at 37°C, the sperm motility, oxygen consumption rate (OCR) of sperm, lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) ac-tivity of extracellular medium and sperm cell volume were measured. 1) When the semen was frozen without glycerol, the postthaw spermatozoa at 0% trehalose (100% glucose) was completely immotile, but the motility of frozen spermatozoa increased as trehalose levels increased. 2) With increasing trehalose levels, the OGR of freeze-thaw spermatozoa increased and the postthaw cell volume of spermatozoa and the LDH ac-tivity in extracellular medium of freeze-thaw semen decreased in the absence of glycerol. 3) When the semen was frozen in various levels of glycerol, the 100% trehalose level gave the best postthaw recovery in the sperm motility and OGR, and the lowest LDH activity. 4) We concluded that the trehalose is a novel cryoprotectant for chicken sperm.
