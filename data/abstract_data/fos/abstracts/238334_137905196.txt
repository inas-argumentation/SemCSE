Effect of Aging on Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Mg-6Gd-3Y-0.4Zr-1Zn Alloy
Mg-6Gd-3Y-0.4Zr-1Zn alloy was prepared by solvent protection.The microstructure and mechanical properties of the alloy after solid solution treatment,hot extrusion and aging at 200℃for 54 h were studied by OM and SEM.The results show that the sample aging at 200℃ for 54 h,the second phases in alloy sample have better refinement and distribution more uniformity than those in solid solution state alloy,the hardness and yield strength is increased by 25.87% and 2.3% respectively,the tensile strength is decreased by 2.4%,the elongation is decreased from 10.1% to 10%,but still has good plasticity.
