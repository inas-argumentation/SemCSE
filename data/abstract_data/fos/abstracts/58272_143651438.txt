Many chapters would provide excellent study group readings and generate discussion with faculty members who wish to embark upon experimentation in the curriculum or their teaching. The powerful examples of metaphor are especially impressive and provide a sensual and visual dimension beyond typical rationalism for mentally envisioning experimentation and innovation. Reinvention or any serious reconsideration of existing educational models is most certainly not for the exceedingly cautious or those who may be motivated by instant gratification. These stories of courageous people and programs identify significant landmarks to place on our map of innovations in higher education, as well as offering navigational tools for those who wish to continue the exploration. Extensive and useful references (in many chapters) nicely complement the powerful stories and legacies of gutsy, perilous struggles over eighty years. Finally, Reinventing Ourselves achieves a primary outcome of any good book; one leaves stimulated toward higher and better questions rather than answers.
