Historic Changes and Strategic Transformation of Jiangsu Economy in Past 60 Years
Since the foundation of new China 60 years ago,Jiangsu economy has seen historic tremendous changes from semi-colonized and semi-feudal economy to socialist economy,from plan economy to socialist market economy,from closed and semi-closed economy to open economy,from the province of agriculture to the big province of industry and big province of economy,as well as from poverty to general well-off.In these 60 years,the operation and development of Jiangsu economy has experienced two historic periods of plan economy and socialist market economy,two three-year adjustments and eleven five-year plans,and its economic development has obtained glorious achievements in expansion of total amount,industry growing up,material property increase,comprehensive financial strength,urban and rural improvement,as well as in increase of people's daily life.In these 60 years,the operation and development of Jiangsu economy having suffered from nine fluctuation periods,has unevenly marched on,being ironed with the main national melody,yet dotted with its own characteristics.In these 60 years,the operation and development of Jiangsu economy and its reform and opening up,have accumulated rich experiences and provided a lot of enlightenment.Looking forward,Jiangsu economy has a bright and beautiful future,yet with challenges and opportunities co-existing,therefore,necessity should focus on the conduct of scientific developmental view,on taking pains to achieve strategic upgrading and transformation of economy oriented to the economy of consumption pull,service economy,self-innovative economy centered on original innovation,and healthy and mature socialist market economy.
