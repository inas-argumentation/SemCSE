The analysis and enlightenment of the aesthetic psychological tendency of faculty and students of higher education institutes
Research shows that,compared with whole citizens,faculty and students of higher education institutes(HEIs) have stronger aesthetic need,and they pay more attention on goodness,verity and harmony.For aesthetics and aesthetic education faculty and students' cognizance approach to the essence.Compared with students of HEIs,faculties are more rational on aesthetic ideals,and pay more attention on harmony.The carrying out of aesthetic education of HEIs should make students' need basic point,lay stress on education in cultivation;should use noble-minded sentiment to mould students,improve students' aesthetic ideals;should increase aesthetic knowledge education,strengthen faculty and students' basic theories.
