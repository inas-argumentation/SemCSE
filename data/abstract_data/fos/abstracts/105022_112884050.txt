The Improvement of the Teaching Method of Electronic Major
For the sake of the development of the electronic technology and the more severe competition, the teaching method should be improved so as to raise the teaching quality and cultivate the practical talents that adapt to the feature of vocational technology education. The heuristic teaching should be paid more attention and thus activate the creative ability of the students. Meanwhile, under the principle of more practice and less theory, the teaching material should be used with pertinence so that the teaching quality can be enhanced.
