Humanism in Colleges’ Ideological and Political Education
Humanistic thought is the philosophy which recognizes the value and dignity of people and regards the human as the yardstick of all things, taking the humanity,subjectivity of people and interests of people as the starting point and destination.Based on the analysis of present situation and the existing problems in colleges' ideological and political education,this article elucidates the significance and the implementation of effective methods of ideological and political education in colleges.
