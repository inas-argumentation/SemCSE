Numerical Simulation of Foundry Technology for Zinc Ingot Mold Based on Proportional Solidification Theory
Based on the proportional solidification theory,the pouring plan and the gating system design calculations for zinc ingot mold casting were prepared.Then the numerical simulation of zinc ingot mold by use of Pro / E and Procast software was conducted.By comparison,the results indicated that the two are consistent.The possible casting defects can be predicted by simulating and the casting process optimization obtained.
