Organosilicon and organogermanium derivatives with silicon-metal and germanium-metal bonds
In recent years, interest has grown in the chemistry of bimetallic organometallic compounds containing metal-metal bonding between atoms of different metals. Compounds of this type are very different in their chemical properties from the "ordinary" organometallic compounds. In this connection it is interesting to compare the reactivity of the bimetallic organometallic compounds with that of the correspondgin "ordinary" (i.e. without Simetal or Ge-metal bonds) organometallic compounds, although our data on the chemical properties of the former are far from being complete.
