A Study of Agent-Oriented Software Engineering Technology
Agent-oriented software engineering is one of the most important new techniques in the fields of both Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence. Compared with existing development approaches, Agent represents high- level abstraction of active entities. It provides a new method to do with the analyzing and designing of compli- cated distributed software system. This paper presents the Multi-Agent based software engineering by a detailed study of the main methodologies and technologies of Agent-oriented software engineering.
