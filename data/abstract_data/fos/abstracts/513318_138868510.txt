Research on Microstructure and Hardness of Tin-base Babbitt Alloy Made by Laser Remelting
The experiment of laser remelting on the surface of centrifugally cast tin-base babbitt alloy was made. The microstructure and hardness of laser remelting layer were studied. By laser remelting, the casting defects, such as coarse chemical compound, segregation and gas cavity can be removed. The microstructure of laser remelting layer is tin-base solid solution with Sn Sb and Cu6Sn5. The average hardness of laser remelting layer increases by 25% than that of centrifugally casting sample. By laser remelting, the metallurgical quality and performance of babbitt alloy greatly increase. So, the laser rapid remelting technology greatly increase the service life of sliding bearing and has great application value in the manufacture of sliding bearing.
