Eco-compatible synthesis of novel 3-hydroxyflavones catalyzed by KF-impregnated mesoporous natural zeolite clinoptilolite
A series of novel 3-hydroxyflavones were designed and synthesized under efficient and green one-pot three-component aldol condensation/intramolecular cyclization reaction of 1-[6-hydroxy-2-(prop-1-en-2-yl)-1-benzofuran-5-yl]ethanone, substituted benzaldehyde, and hydrogen peroxide in the presence of potassium fluoride /clinoptilolite nanocatalyst in water at room temperature.
