Study on Warm Deep Drawing Process of Magnesium Alloy Cross-shaped Cup
To research the forming property of the complex piece of magnesium alloy during warm deep drawing,taking Benchmark 2#(Numisheet2011) as an example,the warm forming finite element model of the cross-shaped cup was built with ABAQUS.Through the thermo-mechanical coupling analysis,the strain-effective nephogram,stress-effective nephogram and temperature distribution of the blank during the warm forming process and the curves of the punch force with stroke were obtained.The simulation results and experimental results were compared,and they have a good accord.
