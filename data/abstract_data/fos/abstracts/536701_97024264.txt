A modified charge-voltage technique for interface-state measurements in metal-oxide-semiconductor capacitors
The charge-voltage technique of Ziegler and Klausmann (1975) for evaluation of the surface potential and interface states in metal-oxide-semiconductor capacitors has been modified. Rather than using static voltages applied to a series combination of the MOS capacitor and a fixed capacitor, for which an electrometer must be connected in parallel with one of the capacitors to measure the voltage across that capacitor, two separate measurements with slow voltage ramps are employed. The first measurement concentrates only on the MOS capacitor and the second on the series combination of the MOS with a fixed capacitor. The need for the voltage-measuring electrometer is avoided as are the many errors arising from the use of this electrometer. Thus, the modified charge-voltage technique avoids many of the pitfalls of the original and is much easier to implement in practice.
