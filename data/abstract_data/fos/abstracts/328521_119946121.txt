An Evaluation of Some Tests of Trend in Contingency Tables
Consider an r × c contingency table under the full multinomial model in which each classification is ordered. The problem is to test the null hypothesis of independence against the alternative that all local log odds ratios are nonnegative with at least one local log odds ratio positive. A number of tests have been proposed for this problem, including the Goodman–Kruskal gamma test; a family of linear tests studied by Agresti, Mehta, and Patel; and a test based on “C–D,” the difference of concordant and discordant pairs in the table. In this article we show that all of these tests can be improved on in some sense for most cases. In fact the preceding tests sometimes are inadmissible in a strict sense. Furthermore, we show by example that in some cases improved tests can yield substantially improved power functions.
