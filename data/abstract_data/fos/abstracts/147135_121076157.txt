Sulfide compounds MexMn1 − xS (Me = Cr, Fe, V, Co): Technology, transport properties, and magnetic ordering
Electronic transitions of the metal-insulator type and magnetic transitions of the antiferromagnetic semiconductor-ferromagnetic metal type are established in cation-substituted manganese sulfides MexMn1 − xS (Me = Cr, Fe, V, Co), synthesized on the basis of MnS. The effect of negative colossal magnetoresistance is found and the regularities of change in the conductivity type and magnetic order are analyzed.
