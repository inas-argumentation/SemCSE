Vacancy diffusion in the intercalation electrode Li1−xNiO2
The rate of lithium diffusion in the layered intercalation compound Li1−xNiO2 has been investigated. The variation of the chemical diffusion coefficient, DLi, for lithium and the self diffusion coefficient, DLi, for Li+ ions, with lithium content is presented. The maximum value for DLi=2×10−7cm2s−1 and is amongst the highest observed for a lithium intercalation compound. Within the composition range studied, 0.5<(1−x)<1.0, the ions are transported by a vacancy mechanism. A vacancy diffusion coefficient, Dv, is defined and its variation with lithium composition described. In addition, large thermodynamic enhancement factors are observed.
