Research of Multi-passes Drawing Process of Battery Shell and Its Finite Element Analysis
In view of the complexity of forming and technological design during multi-passes drawing of deep square box,the deformation progress of the battery shell from cylinder part to deep square box was numerically simulated based on experience design and computation.Using finite element analysis software MSC.Super,the influences of drawing deformation and main parameters on drawing forces were studied,the guide function of numerical simulation result in the multi-passes drawing and the technological design was verified.
