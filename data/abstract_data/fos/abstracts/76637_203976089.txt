New Die Design of Precision Forging Process for Gear Blank Based on Dividing Flow Method
Based on the simulation software DEFORM-3D,the influence of die structure on metal flow direction,speed,effective strain and die load was analyzed,a kind of new die structure named the stepped dividing top die and lower die with ribs groove was put forward,which could greatly improve ribs filling situation of gear blank.Through actual practice,the reliability of the numerical simulation was verified.
