Breeding and Selection of Arachis pintoi cv.Reyan 12
Arachis pintoi cv. Reyan No.12, first introduced from the Center of International Agriculture Tropical (CIAT), Columbia in 1991, was planted in pot and observed in greenhouse during 1992~1993, and then transplanted into field in 1994~1996 to evaluate its adaptability and released for extension. Observations showed it was widely adapted, persistent and productive, tolerated shade and long dry season. During 1997~2000, a comparative trial was conducted with other 8 accessions of Arachis spp., and a regional trial from 1998 to 2003 and a production trial from 1999 to 2004 were arranged in Hainan, Guangdong, Guangxi and Yunnan. The preliminary results showed that Arachis pintoi cv. Reyan No.12, prostrate and stoloniferous, grew densely upto 20 cm and had a wide adaptability with high forage yield and nutrition. It grew best in moist, well drained and moderately fertile soils and produced high quality feed with higher dry matter yield and nutritional value over a long growing season. It flowered after planted less than one month and the flower stage persisted for more than 10 months. It had excellent virescence effect. It has been and can be widely used as a pasture plant, a cover crop in plantations, a ground cover on roadsides and steep slopes, and an ornamental plant in South China.
