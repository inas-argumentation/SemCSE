Direct versus thermal particle emission in high-energy heavy-ion collisions: Multiplicity-selected proton inclusive spectra
Based on the two-component model (direct plus thermal) introduced in a previous article we calculate and discuss proton-inclusive energy and angular distributions associated with low- and high-multiplicity selected events. In particular, we study proton production from Ne on U and Ne on Al at 400 MeV/N, and from Ar on KCl at 800 MeV/N.
