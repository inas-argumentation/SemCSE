Expression of PPAR-γ,TLR4 and activation of STAT1 signal protein in rats with peritoneal dialysis related acute peritonitis induced by LIPS
Objective To survey the expression of peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor-γ （PPAR-γ） , Toll-like receptor 4 （ TLR4 ） and the activation of signal transducer and activator of transcription 1 （STAT1） signal protein in Spragne Dawley （SD） rats with peritoneal dialysis-related acute peritonitis in- duced by lipopolysaceharide （LPS） and to reveal initially the mechanism of LPS in inducing peritoneal dialy- sis-related acute peritonitis. Methods Thirty SD male rats were randomized and divided into five groups （ n = 6） according to the time points by LPS stimulation： control group, injected with 4.25% dextrose perito- neal dialysate （PDF） via abdominal cavity（90 ml/kg） ; LPS 4 h group, injected with LPS （1 mg/kg） via abdominal cavity followed by PDF injection; LPS 8 h group, LPS 12 h group and LPS 24 h group, injected with LPS via abdominal cavity 4, 8 and 20 h followed by PDF injection, respectively. The rats were killed after injection with PDF for 4 h. The protein expression of PPAR-γ in peritoneal mesothelial cells was examined by immunohistochemistry staining. The mRNA expression of PPAR-γ and TLR4 were determined by RT-PCR. The proteins expression of PPAR-γ, TLR4, p-STAT1 and STAT1 were analyzed by Western blot. IL-6 level in abdominal dropsy was determined by ELISA. Peritoneum tissue was stained using Masson. Number of leucocytes in abdominal dropsy was also counted. Results Immunohistochemistry staining shows that PPAR-γ was constitutively expressed in peritoneal mesothelial cells. After stimulation by LPS, the PPAR-γ mRNA expression in peritoneum tissue of SD rats was decreased at 4 h （ P 〈 0.05 ） and increased at 8 h （P〈0. 01 ）, but no significant difference was observed at 12 h and 24 h when compared with control group. The expressions of PPAR-γ protein as well as TLR4 mRNA and protein were markedly increased at 4 h （ P 〈 0.05 ） and lasted for 24 h （ P 〈 0.05 ）. There is a positive correlation between PPAR-3, and TLR4 protein expression （r = 0. 5936, P 〈 0. 001 ）. After stimulation by LPS at d h, p-STAT1 expression in- creased markedly and the high expression of p-STAT1 lasted for 24 h （P 〈 0.01 ）. The level of IL-6 in ab- dominal dropsy was obviously increased after stimulation by LPS at 4 h and 8 h. Masson staining shows that peritoneum tissues in all groups stimulated by LPS appeared dropsy. There was no significant difference in leucocyte counts of abdominal dropsy between control group and LPS stimulation groups. Conclusion High expression of PPAR-γ and TLR4 together with activation of STAT1 in peritoneum tissue may participate in the pathogenesis of peritoneal dialysis-related acute peritonitis induced by LPS in rats.
