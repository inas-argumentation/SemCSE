Research on Cooling Characteristic of A New Type of Aqueous Polymer Quenchant KHP-W
The statistic cooling characteristic of a new type of aqueous polymer quenchants(KHP-W) was researched by using testing instrument.The results show that starting temperature and concentration have great effects on the cooling characteristic of KHP-W. The temperature at Vmax (TVmax) improves with the increase of temperature and concentration, but the maximum cooling speed(Vmax) and the cooling speed at 300℃ (V300 ) reduce. When the temperature is over 60℃,the cooling characteristic of KHP-W is changed obviously because of resolution inversion.
