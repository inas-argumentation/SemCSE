ELECTRON PARAMAGNETIC RESONANCE OF TRIMETHYLENEMETHANE FORMED BY RADIOLYSIS OF METHYLENECYCLOPROPANE
Trimethylenemethane was formed by the radiolysis of methylenecyclopropane at 77°K, and its epr spectrum was studied. From the ΔMS=1 spectrum epr parameters were determined as Tzz=9.17±0.05G, D=(2.37±0.01)×10−2 cm−1 and E = 0. The ΔMS=2 transition showed seven hyperfine lines with a separation of 9G.
