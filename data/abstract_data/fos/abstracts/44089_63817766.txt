Research of Abis Interface in cdma2000 system
On basis of deep research on 3gpp2 Abis interface model, the 3G air interface protocols and signal flow chart, the characteristic and shortcoming of existing Abis model are analyzed. According to the characteristic of IP core network, Abis interface based on IP is investigated well, and then Abis interface communication protocol including message types defining, data format defining and signal flow are worked out.This protocol has been realized by software in cdma2000 system, which is validated primarily in actual product and run environment.
