Determination of 4 Phytohormones in Latex of Hevea brasiliensis by HPLC
Four hormones,namely Zeatin(ZT),Gibberellin(GA),Auxin(IAA)and Abscisic acid(ABA),in rubber(Hevea brasiliensis)latex were isolated and purified in the following order,first extracted with 80% ice-cold methanol extraction,centrifugated,then the supernatant from centrifugation evaporated to the aqueous phase under vacuum condition.After pH value adjusted,the extraction was further purified through PXC pre-column.GA,IAA and ABA were eluted followed by ZT.The ZT was detected with isocratic elution,the mobile phase was methanol and 0.5% acetic acid(20/80 v/v),the analysis time was 10 min,peak time was 5.426 min,and the recovery rate was 87%.The GA,IAA,and ABA were detected with gradient elution,the mobile phase was methanol and 0.5% acetic acid(20/80 v/v),the analysis time was 34 min,the peak time of GA,IAA,and ABA was 13.377,16.581,and 18.988 min and the recovery rate was 70%,82%,and 92%,respectively.The content of the 4 phytohormones could be correctly assayed since the concentration of impurities were reduced and the hormones were detected separately.The results showed that the method operated simply gave a high reproducibility and was suitable for the study of the isolation and detection of phytohormones in rubber tree latex.
