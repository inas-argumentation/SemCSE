Fault indicators and the unavailability of redundant circuits
Abstract This paper considers the effect of two types of fault indication scheme on the unavailability of redundant circuits. Expressions are derived which can be used to predict the unavailability of the redundant circuit as the efficiency of the fault indication scheme varies. It is shown that the usual assumption that repair is started as soon as a fault occurs can seriously overestimate the reliability of the system if the fault indication scheme is not working efficiently. The equations can also be used to obtain the conditions required for an efficient indication scheme. The results of some simulation studies are also presented.
