A survey of the present physical condition of the intellectuals in the cities and towns in Henan and their participation in physical exercises
The survey of the present physical condition of the intellectuals all around Henan Province and their participation in physical exercises indicates that the in service intellectuals in China are in poorer health and they turn to be among the weak groups in the movement of body building for all.Therefore,a vigorous development of the intellectuals' physical condition,which can build up their health will help the implement of the building China on science and education strategy and will thus promote the rapid and stable development of the county's economy.
