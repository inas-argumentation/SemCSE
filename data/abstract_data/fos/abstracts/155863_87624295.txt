The relationship of phenols and antioxidant capacity in barley and malt
This study analyzes and compares the antioxidant capacity of seven kinds of barley and its malt. The evaluation method is DPPH radical scavenging activity, ABTS radical scavenging activity, reducing power. And measuring the content of total polyphenol and ten monophenol. The study found that the antioxidant activity are significantly different between varieties of barley and malt. Correlation analysis shows significantly associated with the total polyphenol content and DPPH radical scavenging activity. The role of the monophenol capacity is different, which catechins is the strongest.
