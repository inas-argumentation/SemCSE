LETTER TO THE EDITOR: Laser diode pump sources for blue up-conversion fibre laser
High-power InGaAsP/InP lasers emitting at 1130 - 1140 nm and 1220 nm have been fabricated by single-step growth on a non-planar InP substrate. The structure seems to be a suitable pump source for a fibre laser. Stable fundamental transverse mode operation was obtained up to an emitted power of 120 mW. The narrow and almost circular beam enables efficient (60 - 70%) coupling into a single-mode fibre.
