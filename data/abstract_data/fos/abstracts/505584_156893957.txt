Analysis of China's Economic Growth Path Based on Neo-Classical Economic Growth Model
In order to analysis the tendency and the evolution process of China's economic growth and get clearly which stage is China's economic staging in,meanwhile find out how the next stage China's economic sustainability will be,we constructed a theoretical framework of reform evolution and devided China's economic growth path into 4 stages.Simultaneously,we also constructed a econometrics model which was mainly based on Neo-Classical Economic Growth Model to simulate China's economic growth path but by loosing some constrain conditions.The conclusion was that China's economy was still in the Economy Take-off stage and the sustainability of next stage will remain in the high speed.The conclusion was proofed by the empirical data from promoting the deepening of reform as a whole and perspective of Institutional Economics.
