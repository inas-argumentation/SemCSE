GEOMETRIC NON-LINEAR ANALYSIS OF FLEXIBLE SUPPORTING SYSTEMS FOR POINT-SUPPORTING GLASS BUILDING
A calculation method for flexible supporting system with large displacements and deformations is developed so as to determine the tangent stiffness and internal stress vector. The formulation is established for a ?D-bar?element and a ?D-beam?element. A program is developed and two typical flexible supporting system models are studied. The results lay down the basis for further research work.
