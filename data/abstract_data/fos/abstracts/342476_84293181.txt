Analysis of a stable polymorphism arising in a selection-migration model in population genetics
SummaryThis investigation studies the dynamic behaviour of a population in which there is a geographic variation in fitness of a one-locus, two allele trait. The results derived here complement certain results by W. H. Fleming who described the existence of a stable polymorphism when the ratio of selection intensity to dispersion rate exceeds a critical value. Here an approximation to the polymorphic state is obtained, and the evolution of other initial states to it is studied.
