Design of Inversion Mobilization Heuristic Algorithm of Optimal Binary Tree
In terms of building optimal binary tree with n leaf nodes,proposes a novel method.And the method determines nodes position in optimal binary tree according to inverse principle of mobilization algorithm and by using the change of weighted value produced by the mobilization of nodes position.This algorithm is totally different from previous huffman algorithm in the operation process of optimizing binary tree.The inversion mobilization algorithm's steps are concise,and its speed is fast.In the end,the empirical result indicates the effectiveness of the algorithm.
