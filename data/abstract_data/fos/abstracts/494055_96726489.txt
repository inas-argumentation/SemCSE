Study on sodium fast ion conductors in the Na1 + 3xAlxHf2 − xSi2xP3 − 2xO12 system
Natural layered aluminosilicate pyrophyllite Al2[Si4O10](OH)2 has been used as a starting material for preparing Nasicon-type fast ion conductors in the Na1 + 3xAlxHf2 − xSi2xP3 − 2xO12 system. Most of the synthesis reactions were performed at 900–1150 °C and with increasing value of x, lower reaction temperatures were used. A single NASICON phase with a C 2 c space group has been found in the composition range x ≤ 0.6. The volume of the unit-cell in this composition range increased with increasing x. The initial composition with x = 0.6 possesses maximum ionic conductivity which reaches values up to 1.22 × 10−2 S · cm−1 at 350 °C, with an activation energy of 26.3 KJ/mole.
