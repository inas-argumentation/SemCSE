Non-equilibrium noise in mesoscopic systems: effects of transit times
The current correlation function and noise spectrum for a finite quasi-one-dimensional system, biased by an external voltage, are calculated. The time-dependence of the correlation function is analysed in terms of the transit times of the carriers through the system. For a multi-subband system in the degenerate limit, the low-frequency spectral density exhibits a step-like shape. Under the presence of an applied voltage the step height is reduced to one half of its equilibrium value.
