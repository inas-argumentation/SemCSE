Synthesis and Properties of Tetrakis(alkylseleno)tetrathiafulvalene
A series of tetrakis(alkylseleno)tetrathiafulvalene (TSeCn-TTF; n = 1–18) has been synthesized. The melting points of this series showed the same dependency on the length of alkyl chains as the sulfur and the tellurium analogues. The g-values of the TYC1-TTF cation radicals (Y = sulfur, selenium, and tellurium) are compared.
