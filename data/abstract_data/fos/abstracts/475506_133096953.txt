Geotextile Containment for Environmental Engineering
The paper reviews the operating principle,types and selection of geotextile containment.Special attention is given to the application of geotextile containment in dewatering pretreatment of sludge,such as civil sludge,industrial and chemical sludge,polluted river sludge,etc.Domestic and foreign engineering applications are introduced and broad application prospects of this technology in sludge treatment are anticipated.
