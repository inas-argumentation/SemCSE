Okay, Let's Tell It Like It Is (Instead of Just Making It Look Good)
SOL GORDON, Ph.D. Syracuse University I am grateful to all the respondents for their thoughtful comments. The criticism that I was not specific enough is justified. Let me briefly make a few specific comments about the mentally retarded-the most &dquo;neglected&dquo; of all groups. The mentally retarded are not exceptional in their sexual impulses. In the past we &dquo;all&dquo; joined everybody else in a crusade against these impulses ; now a few pioneers are saying to all of us: The mentally retarded are entitled to the expression of impulses, as are all human beings. Like the rest of us, they are entitled to be sex-educated, and this education must not make the presumption that only containment of sexual impulses is valid and permissible. In all likelihood, what &dquo;we&dquo; (the so-called normal) have most in common with the mentally retarded are hang-ups, guilt, and fantasies about and/or desires for sexual expression.
