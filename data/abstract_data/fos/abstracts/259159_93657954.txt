Fluorescence Spectra of 6-Substituted 2,3-Dicyano-5-[4-(diethylamino)styryl]-7-methyl-6H-1,4-diazepines in Solid State
Intense fluorescence of 2,3-dicyano-5-[4-(diethylamino)-styryl]-7-methyl-6H-1,4-diazepines in solid state could be successful in improving interference in cofacial molecular stacking between the chromophores by the bulky 6-substituent.
