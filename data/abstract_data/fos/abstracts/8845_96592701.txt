Complex formation of 1-vinylnaphtho-[2,3-d]imidazole with hydrogen chloride and organic electron acceptors
The reaction of 1-vinylnaphtho[2,3-d]imidazole with hydrogen chloride, alkyl halides, tetracyanoethylene, 2,4,7-trinitro-9-fluorenone, and p-chloranil giving the corresponding quaternary salts and charge-transfer complexes has been studied. The structure of the synthesized compounds has been confirmed from elemental analytical data and from IR and UV spectrometry.
