On the lmprovement of the People's Supervisor System
The people's supervisor system is an important measure launched by the Supreme People's Procuratorate in response to pushing ahead the judicial system reform.It adequately embodies the notions of right restricting power,sovereign of people,public participation in judicature and judicial democracy.The effect of the people's supervisor system has been noticeable in the past three years.But there are several defects in practice.Effective measures should be taken to improve this system.
