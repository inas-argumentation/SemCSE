Entrepreneurial university: challenge and opportunity of research university
In the late 20th century, the innovational and entrepreneurial activities of some research universities gave birth to a brand\|new university mode \| entrepreneurial universities. The entrepreneurial universities aim at improving national competitiveness, productivity and the entrepreneurial and innovative spirit of the country and nationality, and developing the national and regional economy. The "social services" of entrepreneurial universities reconstruct and promote the form and content of the third function of universities, and rebuild the spirit and feature of traditional research universities.
