Effects of Ti on Vermicularizing Result of RE-Mg Alloy
Aiming at the narrow addition amount of vermicularizing alloy and the difficulty to control in the treatment process of vermicular graphite cast iron,the influences of the adding amount range of vermicularizing alloy on microstructure and performance after adding the appropriate content of anti spheroidizing element Ti in the alloy were studied.The results showe that when the vermicular cast iron contains 0.28% of titanium,the suitable addition amount of rare earth magnesium alloy increases from 0.83% ~ 0.94% to 0.8% ~ 1.0%,almost doubled,and it can still obtain satisfied vermicular rate.The ferrite content of matrix microstructure and the hardness increase.But the increasing ranges are smaller.It proves that adding the appropriate anti spheroidizing elements in the process of vermicularizing treatment can make the production of vermicular graphite cast iron stable and easy to control.
