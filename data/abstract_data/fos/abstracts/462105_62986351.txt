Research on a User Motivation Model Based Search Engine
Proposed a user motivation model based search engine and a solution is proposed for improving the construction efficiency of the user behavior model.User motivation models are established between the users and the search engine.The user motivation models are used to assist the web search engines,so that the efficiency and accuracy rate of information retrieval can be improved.The user motivation model combines similar behavior models of the users based on the theory of anthroponomy and displays the returned results according to the clustering results and representation to achieve personalization.Experiments show that the returned results by the search engine based on the user motivation model can better meet the requirements of the users.
