Dynamical DX centre breakdown in submicrometre AlGaAs/GaAs structures
Dynamical electrical breakdown in the depleted region of diodes has been investigated in the nanosecond timescale. A persistent conductivity due to Si-related DX centre impact ionization in high electric fields was observed after the onset of the breakdown. From the present investigation it follows that DX centre breakdown begins at electric fields of ( and is related to impact ionization of DX centres by free hot carriers.
