Alpha-gamma decay studies of 258Db and its (grand)daughter nuclei 254Lr and 250Md
Abstract.The superheavy isotope 258Db was produced in the 209Bi(50Ti, 1n)258Db reaction at GSI, Germany. A detailed $ \alpha$α-spectroscopy study of this isotope and its $ \alpha$α-decay daughter products was performed. Two long-lived $ \alpha$α-decaying states in 258Db are observed. A spin-parity of $ 0^{-}$0- is tentatively assigned to the ground state for which a half-life of $ 2.17 \pm 0.36$2.17±0.36 s is determined. Spins and parities of either $ 5^{+}$5+ or $ 10^{-}$10- are proposed for the isomeric state found at an excitation energy of 51 keV with a half-life of $ 4.41 \pm 0.21$4.41±0.21 s. The $ \alpha$α -decay daughter isotope 254Lr displays a similar behavior. A half-life of $ 11.9 \pm 0.9$11.9±0.9 s is determined for the 254Lr ground state which is tentatively assigned a spin-parity of $ 4^{+}$4+. The isomeric level of this isotope placed at 108 keV with a half-life of $ 20.3 \pm 4.2$20.3±4.2 s is tentatively assigned a spin-parity of $ 1^{-}$1-. Two long-lived $ \alpha$α-decaying states are observed in the granddaughter isotope 250Md . The ground state has a half-life of $ 59.5 \pm 9.1$59.5±9.1 s. A half-life of $ 42.4 \pm 4.5$42.4±4.5 s is measured for the isomeric state positioned at an excitation energy of 123 keV. Partial, tentative level schemes for 254Lr, 250Md and 246Es are proposed based on the $ \alpha$α-$ \gamma$γ coincidences.
