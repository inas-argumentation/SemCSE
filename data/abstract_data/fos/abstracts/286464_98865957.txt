Effects of Amendments and Compound on Rape Growth and Physiological Metabolism in Cd Polluted Garden Soil
The effects of fungi residues and compound on rape biomass,the uptake of cadmium by rape,enzyme activity and chlorophyll fluorescence parameters were studied using pot experiments.Results showed that the control treatment concentration of SOD,POD and CAT were the highest.The chlorophyll fluorescence parameters of F0 was increased,Fm,Fv,Fv /Fm and Fv /F0 were decreased.These showed that the rape under stress and the biomass decreased.The rape stress would be reduced and the yield would be increased by adding fungi residues and compound.The high fungi residue treatment showed the highest yield by 7.5times compared with the control treatment.The amount of absorbed Cd in rape was decreased after the fungi residue and compound application.Lime application showed the best effect to decrease the Cd content in rape leaf.The treatment of lime and high fungi residue co-application wouold lead to Cd content reduction by more than 81.9% compared with the control treatment.The amount of Cd in the rape after adding lime or lime and high fungi residue co-application was within the food safety standard.
