On How the Financial Management in Colleges and Universities Adapts to the Development under the New Circumstance
With the deepen reform of the public financial system, the idea of the financial management in colleges and universities has changed deeply in the aspects of arranging the expenses, organizing the income, drawing up the budget, controlling the oblitation and fund management; the financial management have developed greatly in the function, the transformation of the role and the work focus;the service awareness and methods of the financial management have also changed; the financial operating and monitoring system has established and improved step by step.
