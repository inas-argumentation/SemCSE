A Voice Noise Denoising Algorithm Based on Wavelet Transform
The received signal tend to be interferenced by radio voice noise.With the prosperous development of radio industry,the energy of noise is increasing to influence the transmitting speed and quality of communication system.Due to the non-smooth of voice noise,traditional denoising methods are not available.This paper introduces an voice-denoising algorithm based on Wavelet transform to lower the energy of voice noise added on received BPSK signal.The result shows us that this algorithm has an excellent denoising ability and improves the SNR/BER performance of communication system.
