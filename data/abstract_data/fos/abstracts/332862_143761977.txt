A Tyrian dilemma: Guarantors in peace processes
This article attempts to make sense out of a difficult and, in the author's opinion, understudied concept ‐ that of guarantors in peace agreements. The trials and tribulations of the god Tyr are used as an example of the problems confronting guarantors in peace agreements. The article offers categorizations of the guarantor's relationship with the parties and with the agreement itself. After various classifications have been put forth, examples are employed to exhibit how this has been tried and tested in reality. Finally, some tentative conclusions will be put forth so that more work can be done to understand this complex subject better.
