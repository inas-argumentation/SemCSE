Analysis of Phylogeny of Representative Species in Four Families
Phylogenetic analyses of the chloroplast 16 ~23S intergenic region sequences were conducted for 29tropical representative crops from Euphorbiaceae, Leguminosae, Musaceae and Caricaceae. The results indicated that 16~23S intergenic region sequences had high homology and conservatism. Phylogenetic analysis showed that the evolution of 29 tropical crops was in full compliance with the Engler( 1897) classification. The level of sequence variation was low. Further researches about sequence differences among 11 varieties of cassava demonstrated the difference at the interspecific level in cassava was very low, only SC8 revealed minor difference. Although the chloroplast 16~23S intergenic region gene sequences could be used to identify the 29 crops of the 4 families, the level of sequence variation was low. So the 16~23S intergenic region is not quantified as candidate DNA barcodes.
