Phosphate-solubilizing Mechanism of PSFM and Preliminary Study on Solubilization Capacity of Insoluble Phosphates
One phosphorus solubilizing fungal strain named PSFM was isolated by biological enriching using organic phosphobacteria liquid medium and then screened via plate dilution method using inorganic phosphofungi solid medium.It was identified as Aspergillus niger by means of morphological observation and ribosomal spacers ITS amplified sequence analysis.The tested strain PSFM was evaluated in liquid medium containing Ca3(PO4)2 as the only P source under temperature 28 ℃ and shaking speed 180 r/min in different time and on solid plate.The results showed that,It was found that the phosphorus solubilizing fungal strain had strong capacity to solubilize phosphate.In liquid culture,the strain PSFM had a dissolving phosphorus capacity of 511.28 mg/L in the frist 144 h,and the capacity descended with continuing culture.The changes in pH value and in phosphor content were determined when the strain was grown in liquid medium cultures.The pH would decrease to 2.03 after 144 h culture and continue to maintain a low level.Its growth curve was studied and it was found that the mycelium was the maximum when shaking culture was for 6 d.
