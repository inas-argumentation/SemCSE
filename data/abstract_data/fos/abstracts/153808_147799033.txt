The Analysis on Practical Enviroment of Information Management and Chinese Institutional Research
The information management,which is based on the development of information technology,is the basis and important content of the institutional research.Institutional research is introduced into Chinese institutions in the era of information technology.At present,it is a problem that if the enviroment of information technology can meet the development of institutional research in Chinese institutions.This paper analysis the responsibility of two different departments of Chinese institutions,the gap of the information management and information technology in IR from the institutions of U.S.A,and the enviroment of information enviroment of Chinese institutions.
