THE AXIAL ACCELETRON - A NEW HIGH POWER MICROWAVE SOURCE
A new high power microwave source, the axial acceletron is presented in this paper.Compared with the radial acceletron, an acceleration region is added after the diode/oscillator and radial acceleration is changed to axial acceleration. The bunching and radiation of the electron beam in foe device areinvestigated. It is found that the electron beam may approach ideal bunching, each harmonic current of thebeam may reach about 5.6 times of the initial direct current in the nonrelativistic regime. High power microwave pulses with tunable frequency can be generated by post-accelerating the electron beam and carefullydesigning the extraction cavity.
