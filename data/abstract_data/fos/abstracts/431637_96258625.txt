Synthesis of Mono‐ and Sesquiterpenoids, XVI Synthesis of (‐)‐Pereniporins A and B, Sesquiterpene Antibiotics from a Basidiomycete
Starting from (S)-3-hydroxy-2,2-dimethyl-1-cyclohexanone (5), the enantioselective total syntheses of (-)-pereniporins A (1) and B (2) were achieved in 33 steps (0.6% overall yield) and in 28 steps (1.8% overall yield), respectively.
