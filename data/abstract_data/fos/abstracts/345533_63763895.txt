Analyze the Performance of HARQ in Satellite Communication System
The three modes which are ARQ、FEC and HARQ are usually used to ensure the free error,but because of the shortage of ARQ and FEC,people advanced a new system-HARQ,the application of HARQ technique is successful in the 3G mobile communication system and play an important role in improving the data transmission and throughput.However,the research of this technique is not in-depth in satellite communication system,this paper mainly compare the performance of three error-control modes by emulation to provide a foundation for apply this technique in satellite communication.
