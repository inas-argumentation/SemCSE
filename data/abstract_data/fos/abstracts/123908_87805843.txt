Effect of non-surgical intra-uterine insemination on fertility at first estrus synchronized with prostaglandin F2.ALPHA..
An experiment was conducted to investigate the synchronization of estrus with Prostaglandin F2α (PGF2α) in Australian Merino ewes at unknown stages of the estrous cycle and the aspects of non-surgical intra-uterine insemination with frozen-pelleted ram semen.One hundred and ninty-five out of 232 ewes (84.1%) treated intramuscularly with 16 mg of PGF2α showed estrus within five days after treatment. Ewes in estrus were inseminated once with either 0.1 ml of fresh-undiluted semen or 0.3 ml of frozen-thawed semen. Intra-uterine insemination without surgery was successful in 33 out of 78 ewes (43.0%). Non-return rates obtained on 21 days after the last insemination date were as follows: normal insemination with fresh-undiluted semen, 48.6% (17/35); normal insemination with frozen-thawed semen, 27.0% (10/37); deep-cervi-cal insemination with frozen-thawed semen, 37.8% (14/37); and intra-uterine insemina-tion with frozen-thawed semen, 54.5% (18/33). There was no significant difference in nonreturn rates between the normal insemination method with fresh-undiluted semen and intra-uterine insemination method with frozen-thawed semen. (Japan. J. Anim. Reprod. 23 (3), 11620, 1977).
