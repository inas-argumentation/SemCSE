Francisco Solano López and the Ruination of Paraguay: Honor and Egocentrism (review)
All students of Paraguayan history must come to terms with the life, death, and memory of Francisco Solano López since no other man in the history of the nation is the subject of so much love and so much hate by Paraguayans and foreigners alike. James Schofield Saeger has made his judgment on the life of Solano López: he was nothing more than a tyrant with unparalleled ambition and power who willfully set the Paraguayan nation ablaze during the War of the Triple Alliance (18641870). Saeger posits that Solano López’s lack of human empathy clouded his ability to effectively understand behavior and emotion in others. Furthermore, he charges that Solano López, while casting himself a hero, was in actuality a coward. This harsh assessment is based on intense research and thoughtful consideration.
