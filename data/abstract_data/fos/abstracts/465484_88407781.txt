Preparation of Artificial Diet and Trial Rearing on Antheraea yamamai
The paper mainly explores the differences between artificial diet rearing and oak leaves rearing of Antheraea yamamai. The effects on silkworm dominant physiological characters and the growth are also investigated. In this test we try to find the optimum raising way and artificial diet recipes which not only decrease the rearing costs and increase its economical effects, but also promote the development of the artificial diet rearing technology in China.
