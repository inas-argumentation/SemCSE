Ga1-xinxas - inp abrupt heterostructures grown by MOVPE at atmospheric pressure
High quality InP and Ga1-x InxAs layers have been grown on InP substrates using MOVPE growth at atmospheric pressure. Excellent material quality has been obtained using triethylindium and trimethylgallium sources(n = 1.7 1014 cm-3, μ = 106 000 cm2V-1s-1 at 77 K for InP andn = 1 − 3 1015 cm-3, μ= 75 000 cm2V-1 s-1 at 77 K for Ga1-xInxAs). The InP/Ga1-xInxAs interface width obtained is very small (10 Å). The first PIN diodes grown by the process exhibit excellent characteristics.
