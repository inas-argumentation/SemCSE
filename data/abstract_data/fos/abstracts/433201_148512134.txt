Alliance or colonization: the relation between university and enterprise fromtwo-fold perspectives
There are two kinds of explanation about relation between university and enterprise.One is that economics is regarded as "economic theory of knowledge based on knowledge",another explanation is based on the ideas of university coming from sociology.These two kinds of explanation take place in different discourse space,what they followed is different logic and operating mechanism.It indicate that university is under the double structurization and pressure between pursuing truth and pursuing interest.How should the university face its own "awkward predicament",that are strengthening the alliance and cooperation with the commerce and keeping itself unique and pursuing.This is not only the realistic choice that the modern university should make,but also the theme that studing of modern university need to think.
