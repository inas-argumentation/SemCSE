Study on affect of the embryo-oil preparation to the isoflavone and saponin with the ultrasonic extraction
The article focuses on the affect of the embryo-oil preparation to the isoflavone and saponin.With the ultrasonic extraction(450W),the best condition was 50℃,solid-liquid ratio1∶12,and distilling 40min.The exceeding oil ratio is 10.02%,the extraction ratio of isoflavone is 1.37%,and the extraction ratio of saponin is 3.56%.The content of linolenic is 23.8%.
