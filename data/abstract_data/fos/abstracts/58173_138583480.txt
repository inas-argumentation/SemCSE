Research on Wear Resistance of Al-Si-Fe Alloy Coating Prepared by Thermal Spray
The coating on AM50 surface was prepared by using thermal spray and thermochemical reaction method.The coating combination was tested by using thermal shock,the phase composition and substrate were analyzed by X-ray.The wear resistance of the coating was measured by ML-100 wear testing machine.The results show that the coating contains the second phase Al8Si6Mg3,Al3.21Si0.47,Al9Fe0.84Mn2.16Si and Al3.2Fe.The strength containing transition layer coating is better than that of non-bonding coating.The abrasire wear resistance and adhesive wear increases of the coating increase 2 times and 1.7 times than that of AM50 matrix.
