On the Determination of Cash Flow in Investment Project
The cash flow of an investment plan plays very important role in the evaluation of project investment because it is the reference of the financial feasibility of this plan and its good and bad when it is evaluated and can affect the evaluation result directly.When determining the cash flow,one should not only think of the decision-relevant cost such as opportunity cost,but also obey the accounting rules and the accounting system of our country's enterprises.The cash flow table and the cash flow graph can give a help in this process.
