Study on the Project of Power Transmission from Sichuan to Hunan Power Grid
In order to exploiture the hydropower of Sichuan,the electricity of Sichuan will be transmitted to central china power grid while the electricity of three gorge and jinshajiang river being transmitted to hunan power grid.It is suggested that building the third 500kV tie-line of sichuan and chongqing power grid interconnected with central china power grid instead of the line ofhunan power grid interconnected with central china power grid which would realize the electricity transmission from sichuan to hunan power grid.It indicate that the project will increase the ability of electricity transmission from Sichuan and make for exploituring the hydropower of Hunan and Sichuan.
