The Content of Amino Acid of Chinese Bayberry Fruit and Its Nutritive Evaluation
Using the egg amino acid as a standard and WHO/FAO reference model of essential amino acid(EAA) as an appraisal criterion,the nutritional value of the flesh fruit proteins in ten cultivars of Chinese bayberry were evaluated by fuzzy pattern recognition and ratio coefficient of amino acid(AA).The results showed that the Chinese bayberry was rich in all kinds of AA,and EAA accounting for 28.2%～35.2% of the total AA.Lysine(Lys) was the highest in EAA content,accounting for 25.2%～26.7% of the total EAA,secondly for leucine(Leu),accounting for 20.8%～21.4% of the total EAA,methionine(Met) was the lowest,accounting for 2% below of the total EAA.Through the amino acid fuzzy pattern recognition evaluation,‘Erse’ and ‘Wandao’ Chinese bayberry were more close to the standard amino acid,with the best nutritional value.
