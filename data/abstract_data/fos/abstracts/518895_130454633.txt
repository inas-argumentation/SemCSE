Proceedings of the First Annual Meeting of the Mineralogical Society of America, held at Chicago, Illinois, December 29,1920
Session of Wednesday, December 29, 1920 The Mineralogical Society of America held its first annual meeting in Rosenwald Hall, University of Chicago, Chicago, Illinois, on December 29, in affiliation with the Geological Society of America.
