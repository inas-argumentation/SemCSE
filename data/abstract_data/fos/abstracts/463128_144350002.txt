Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale Performance of Familial and Undifferentiated Mental Subnormals
Patients with the diagnoses of familial and undifferentiated mental retardation are often treated as a homogeneous group in research on mental subnormality, particularly in psychological studies where intelligence is a variable. This study investigated the homogeneity of subtest performance of familial and undifferentiated retardates1 on the Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale ( WAIS) . From a larger pool of WAIS data, 40 familial and 40 undifferentiated Ss were selected at each of three age levels (16 to 24 yr., 25 to 34 yr., and 35 to 44 yr.) and individually matched for age, sex, and Full Scale IQ. The total sample was thus set at 240. Critical ratios between the mean subtest scores of the two diagnostic groups were calculated at each age level.
