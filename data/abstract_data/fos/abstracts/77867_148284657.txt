The influence of the rationalism and utilitarianism on the development of modern higher education
Rationalism and utilitarianism has played great roles in the development of modern higher education.We can see the influences in thoughts,policies,organizable behaviors,teaching,researches and allocation of resources.On one hand,the two ideas are contradictable.Rationalism disdains the influences of social development on universities,pursues universities' independence and academic freedom,while utilitarianism advocates universities to be adapted to society,serve society and pursue practical value.On the other hand,the both fuse mutually.Realistic difficulty causes rationalism to be far away from social development and national construction no more,while utilitarianism gradually adopts university-running ideas of university-basis.The two ideas impel the development of higher education and society together.
