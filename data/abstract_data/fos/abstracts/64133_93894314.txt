The appearance of an orthorhombic BIMEVOX phase in the system Bi2MgxV1 − xO5.5 − 3x/2 − δ at high values of x
The structural and electrical behaviour of an orthorhombic BIMEVOX phase Bi 2 Mg 0.3 V 0.7 O 5.395 −  δ is discussed. This composition lies close to the BIMEVOX solid solution limit in the pseudo-binary system Bi 2 MgO 4 –Bi 2 VO 5.5 −  δ . Unlike most other BIMEVOXes, the BIMGVOX system exhibits an orthorhombic polymorph above the composition limit for stabilisation of the tetragonal γ-phase. On heating, Bi 2 Mg 0.3 V 0.7 O 5.395 −  δ exhibits phase separation at around 650 °C, which is reversed at 840 °C. On cooling, the BIMEVOX phase is maintained throughout to room temperature. Small changes in structure and conductivity are evident between 500 and 700 °C and suggest a structural and or compositional change in the vandate layer of the BIMEVOX phase at these temperatures.
