Failure Cause, Frequency, and Repair for Forest Harvesting Equipment
ABSTRACT TIME between failure and time to repair distributions for rubber-tired feller bunchers, rubber-tired grapple skidders, and mobile chippers are presented. There is a considerable amount of variability among machines of the same type. These differences indicate that a machine's availability is heavily dependent on its working environment, the operator, and maintenance practices. A tabulation of the reasons for failures is also given.
