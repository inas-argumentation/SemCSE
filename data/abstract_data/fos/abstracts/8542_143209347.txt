What Is a Competence-Based Curriculum in the Liberal Arts?
A competence-based curriculum is defined and its key characteristics listed. A concept of liberal education is outlined. The compatibility of a competence-based curriculum with the goals of liberal education is discussed. Key behavioral outcomes of a competence-based curriculum in liberal education are described. Critical objections to competence-based learning are noted and analyzed. A model of a competence-based curriculum in liberal education is offered with key components of that model described. Conclusions about the role of assessment in competence-based liberal education are drawn. The potential contribution of competence-based liberal education to the life of contemporary college students is discussed. The seventies bring a time of reappraisal to American higher education. The current decade follows the turbulent events of the sixties which have been likened to those forces which shook higher education just prior to the Civil War. Today's faculty, largely shaped and informed by the "Academic Revolution," are beseiged by critics who denounce the narrowness of faculty educational perspectives. This denunciation is not unlike that made of faculty in the mid 1800s for a similar provinciality of outlook.
