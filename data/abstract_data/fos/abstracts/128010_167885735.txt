Analysis on the Quality of China-coop' Enterprises in China
The article analyses the difference of the enterprises of China-coop among 30 provinces,au-tenuous regions and municipalities by using the methods of factor and cluster analysis.And alyses the facts which would affect the financial quality,such of the inner operating facts,construction and outer facts,and how to deduce the difference of China-coop economics.The result of this research manifests that the inner operating facts,construction and outer facts almost are correlated with financial quality in static.
