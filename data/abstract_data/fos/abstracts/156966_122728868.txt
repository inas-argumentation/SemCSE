Inviscid Solution for the Secondary Flow in Curved Ducts
This paper presents an analytical formulation and a numerical solution for three-dimensional rotational flow in curved ducts. The formulation is based on calculating the through flow vorticity and velocity components from the momentum equation, using a marching technique. The secondary velocities are determined from the simultaneous solution of the continuity and vorticity equations. These equations are transformed through the use of the streamlike function formulation into a single Poisson equation with Dirichlet boundary conditions. A numerical solution is obtained for the flow with large inlet velocity gradient in a 90-deg curved duct with constant radius of curvature. The results are presented and compared with the existing analytical and experimental data.
