Synthetic nuclear Skyrme matter in imbalanced Fermi superfluid mixtures
Cooper-pair formation in a system of imbalanced fermions leads to the well-studied Fulde-Ferrell-Larkin-Ovchinnikov (FFLO) superfluid state. In ultra-cold atoms it is possible to realise the more general case of a fermionic imbalanced mixture for example of Lithium-6 and Dysprosium. We show that superfluid states of mixtures can be very different from the FFLO states, but closely connected with the ostensibly unrelated nuclear Skyrme matter. The system spontaneously forms three-dimensional textures, which represent a synthetic realization of crystals found in the Skyrme model of densely packed baryons.
