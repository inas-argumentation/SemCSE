A study on the research performance evaluation of researchers in higher education research in Mainland China
In this paper,the authors applies Hirsch Index(h Index) method to evaluate the research performance of researchers in higher education research in Mainland China during the period of 1998 to 2007,by analyzing the citation data of papers published by 15 academic journals,which are from Chinese Science Citation Database in CNKI in higher education research of Mainland China.As a result,we select forty-seven scholars,the most influential ones in higher education research in Mainland China during the period 1998 to 2007, which can reflect research present situation in higher education in Mainland China in last decade.
