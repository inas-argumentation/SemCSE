Design and Implementation of Voice Communication System Based on Link Stability for Ad Hoc Environment
In some special fields such as military、emergency rescue、disaster relief、anti-terrorism,voice communications without infrastructure are really needed.This paper combines technologies of Ad Hoc network、embedded system and multimedia development to complete a set of voice communication system,which includes control center and Ad Hoc embedded terminals.By means of selecting the route based on stable link and RTP real time transporting,the latency and jitter were effectively decreased to guarantee the QoS(quality of service) of the voice communication system.Field tests show that this system is easy to build and strongly adaptive to mobility.The performance of the system is stable,and voice communication is generally fluent.All of these meet the design requirements.
