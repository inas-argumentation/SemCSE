Discussion on the Safe Operation and Disaster Recovery and Backup of Dispatching Automation System in Ji′an District Dispatch
With the development of the power grid and the promotion of the unattended substation,the requirement of the operational reliability and data security of dispatching automation system is increasing,the safe operation on the master-station and substation system of the Ji'an district dispatching automation system are discussed in this paper.The main structure of the disaster recovery and backup of the district dispatching automation system in Ji'an 220kV substation is listed,with the situation of its actual operation,the following work on the construction of the system is also pointed out.
