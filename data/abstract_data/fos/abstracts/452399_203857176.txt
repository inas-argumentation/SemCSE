Preliminary study on ferment condition of L-Lactic acid by new type of rhizopus oryze
Its growth and ferment conditions of new tyte of rhizopus oryze were investigated. In optimal condition, the mutant was fermented in the 15L auto-fermentation tank, continuous feed ammonia to control pH value from 5.5 to 6.0, the total acid and the L-Lactic acid weight percent were up to 9.5% and 9.0% respectively, and it could tolerance 1.6% NH+4.
