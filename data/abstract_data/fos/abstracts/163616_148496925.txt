On the pedagogy localization for a hundred years
Pedagogy is imported at the beginning of China as a discipline,people say vividly it is"a thing from abroad ",the introduction of pedagogy and Chinese pedagogy nearly grows together,Chinese pedagogy has gone through six stages.Solving Chinese pedagogy localization should notice Chinese education reality,use the scientific research approach,deal with the relationship between academic research and ideology correctly,recommend pedagogy being studied originally,handle relationship between spreading of nationality's cultural educational traditions and foreign education theory well.
