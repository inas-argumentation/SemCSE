The88Sr(d, n∼p)88Sr reaction
The88Sr(d, n∼p)88Sr cross section has been measured at 170 ° for deuteron energies from 7.5 to 10.0 MeV, and the total88Sr(d, n∼p) cross section obtained. The total cross section is compared with results of coupled-channel Born approximation calculations using both empirical and shell-model-reaction-theory form factors to describe the decaying isobaric analog state. It is shown also that the new data and calculations are consistent with earlier88Sr(d, p)89Sr data.
