CHEMISTRY OF 2-SUBSTITUTED BENZIMIDAZOLES. 1. 5-AMINO-2-METHYL(ARYL, ARYLALKYL, PYRIDYL)BENZIMIDAZOLES
A series of 2-substituted benzimidazoles was synthesized. These products were consecutively converted into 5-nitro- and 5-amino-2-substituted benzimidazoles. Authors: K. D. Ambacheu, V. G. Pleshakov, B. S. Baatkh, V. P. Zvolinskii, M. D. Kharlamova, A. A. Obynochnyi, and N. S. Prostakov.
