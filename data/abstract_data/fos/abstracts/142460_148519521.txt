The Appearance of the Team of College Women Teachers and Its Early Develpoment in China
This paper conducts a comprehensive study on the appearance of the team of college women teachers in higer education of our country as well as its early development,including the contributions made by the foreign college women teachers,the emergence of the chinese college women teachers and the development of the team of college women teachers in the era of the National Government.While revealing the fact that the team of college women teachers is continuously enlarging in our country,the paper devles into the contributions,distribution,origin of the team of college women teachers,together with the existing problems and the main restraint factors.
