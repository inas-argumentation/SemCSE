Corrosion of bond coat Ni22Cr10AlY-alloy in sulphur-containing atmosphere at temperatures of 1173 and 1273 K
This paper deals with the corrosion behaviour of Ni22Cr10AlY alloy, used as a bond coat in thermal barrier coating systems, exposed in ‘pure’ SO 2 at 1173 and 1273 K. Two types of alloys were used: (i) of high porosity and large grains, (ii) of low porosity and small grains. The oxidation rate of Ni22Cr10AlY in SO 2 decreased with decreasing reaction temperature and depended on the type of material. The oxide scales formed during oxidation of Ni22Cr10AlY alloys had complex microstructures and compositions.
