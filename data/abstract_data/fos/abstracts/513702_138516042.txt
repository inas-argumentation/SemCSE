Insert moulding : Automatic manufacturing of rubber-to-metal and rubber-to-plastic moulded components
This report describes recent developments in automated manufacturing of engineering plastics components involving rubber-to-metal and rubber-to-plastic bonded parts. Examples are based on Engel machinery and experience. Specific features mentioned include tiebarless machines, software for cure time prediction, automated work cells, and microprocessor control systems. Specific examples of automated parts manufacturing are given.
