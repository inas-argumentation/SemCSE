Identification and Biological Characteristics of mango Fruit Rot Pathogen
The pathogen of mango fruit rot was identified and the biological characteristics of the pathogen were studied.The pathogen was identified as Pestalotiopsis mangiferae(Henn.) Steyaert based on morphological characteristics and phylogenetic analysis of rDNA ITS sequences.The optimum temperature for mycelial growth of P.mangiferae was range from 25 ℃ to 28 ℃.The optimum temperature for conidial germination was 32 ℃,and the fatal temperature was 60 ℃ for 10 min.The optimum pH value for mycelial growth was ranged from 4 to 5,and the optimum pH value for conidial germination was 4.Mannitol was the best among the tested carbon sources,but maltose was not propitious to mycelial growth.The best nitrogen source for mycelial growth was beef extract and peptone,but urea was not propitious to mycelial growth.Complete dark or alternative culture for 12 h under dark and light was the best for mycelial growth.
