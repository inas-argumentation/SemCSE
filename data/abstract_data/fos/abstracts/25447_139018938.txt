Effect on Second-Phase of Inter-Magnetic Shielded Steel in Different Curl Temperature
The effect of coiling temperature on second-phase of inter-magnetic shielded steel was observed and analyzed by transmission electron microscope (TEM) and scanning electron microscope (SEM). The results show that most of precipitates are MnS particles and precipitated in initial stage of hot rolling, aggregated and grew in coiling procedure. Higher coiling temperature is beneficial to coarse MnS particles, and beneficial to ferrite grains growth and magnetism improvement. There were more large particles in head site than that in rear site of the strip under the same coiling temperature. The inclusion content is very low and irregular distributed, and coiling temperature has no effect on inclusions.
