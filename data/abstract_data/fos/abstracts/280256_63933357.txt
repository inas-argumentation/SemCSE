Practical Teaching Reformation of Webpage Design and Making Course Based on PBI
According to the problems that commonly exist in the teaching of Webpage Design and Making course,such as short of occupation ability training,takes the practical teaching reformation as the breakthrough point,according to the characteristics of project driven teaching mode,summarizes the implementation of the reformation through case demonstration,project task and team division of labor,project implementation and process guidance,comprehensive evaluation,teaching effect,etc.,these five steps,and points out the problems during the implementation of project driven teaching method.
