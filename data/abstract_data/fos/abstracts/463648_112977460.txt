Measurement and application of condensed mode steam quality
According to the principle of energy conservation and the measurement principle of condensed mode steam quality,a measuring method of condensed mode steam quality was proposed,and the steam quality meter of condensed mode was developed with this method.Quantitative analysis and experimental verification of the precision of the steam quality meter were made,and the results showed that the precision of this meter could reach 98%,and the matching model to increase precision was also presented.Furthermore,the field mounting project of this steam quality meter was designed and the experiment indicated the accuracy and practicality of online measurement of this meter.
