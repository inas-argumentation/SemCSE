On the Common Factors of MAO Ze-dong and DENG Xiao-ping Breaking through Dogmatism
The history of the Communist, has seen the mistake tendency to dogmatism for several times. It is not accidental that MAO Ze-dong and DENG Xiao-ping could break through dogmatism in the different history period. It cann't separate with their resemble character factor. Among them, the great life ambition and resolute conviction is the latent factor; to foothold practice and not to follow like sheep is the decided factor; dare to do and have no selfishness and fearlessness is the essential factor.
