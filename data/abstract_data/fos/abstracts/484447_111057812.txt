Exhaust gas recirculation for control of updraft gasifier grate temperature.
THE oxidation zone temperature in an updraft gasifier is usually sufficiently high to cause slagging of the ash. Exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) in a fan motivated system was tested and shown to reduce grate temperatures for updraft gasification of wood chips without significant effect on system efficiency. A simple damper controlled EGR duct design was found to provide uniform grate temperature over a wide range of gasification rates.
