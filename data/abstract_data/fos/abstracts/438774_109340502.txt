The Micro-Circuit Module—A versatile interconnection packaging system
The Micro-Circuit Module is an all-electron beam welded, hermetically sealed interconnection packaging system which is compatible with all approaches to microminiaturization. The form factor of the Micro-Circuit Module is a rectangular parallelopiped—0·390 × 0·390 × 0·8 in. (maximum)—with 20 external leads which protrude from a glass-to-metal header at the base of the assembly. The system has evolved through the developmental stages and a pilot production facility has been established for the assembly of 100 modules per 8-hr shift. The primary equipment of the facility is an electron beam welder which has been provided with an electronic control for programming of beam deflection and gating. The electronic programmer has been complemented with mechanical transfer machines which bring the workpiece under the beam for automatic ribbon welding and header-to-can welding, which is carried out automatically. The suitability of the Micro-Circuit Module for the interconnection and packaging of actual functional equipment has been demonstrated by the fabrication of 10-stage binary dividers, sense amplifiers, servo amplifiers and other digital and analog modules.
