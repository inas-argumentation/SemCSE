Study on the Double Pulse Horizontal Position MAG Welding Process
Since the surface tension of molten metal can not resist the molten pool gravity during the process of horizontal position fusion welding,it is easy to cause these defects such as undercut and overlap. The horizontal position fusion welding technology is studied by double-pulse MAG welding process. The results show that the download flow caused by gravity can be controlled by controlling thermal input into the molten pool,thus realize the control of welding seam.
