Isothermal Deep Drawing Process of AZ31 Mg Alloy Sheet
The isothermal deep drawability of Mg-3Al-Zn(AZ31) magnesium alloy sheet was investigated. The results show that the AZ31 sheet has a perfect deep drawability under 200 ℃ in the conditions of suitable blank hold force and good lubricate. The maximum limiting drawing ration value reaches 2.0 at 150 ℃ and the blank hold pressure value reaches maximum value at 100 ℃. The evolution of blank hold pressure is determined by the effect of work hardening and softening during deep drawing process.
