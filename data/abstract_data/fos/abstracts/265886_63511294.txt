Research on the High Performance Multimedia Object Storage System
This paper analyzes multi-users concurrent access patterns and performance of multimedia applications,explores the higher-performance architecture of data organization in multimedia object storage System(MOSS).MOSS,adopted two-level architecture,is to use the upper layer with the RAID5 data organization for making use of high disk utilization and the under layer,referred to as hot data disk.The hot data disk,as a secondary storage area to optimize the performance of multi-users concurrent access,puts different objects into corresponding disks in order to distribute various requests largely.Under the four users′ request environment,the MOSS shows 90% higher performance as compared to existing RAID system through simulates test system.As we known,the performance of existing RAID system drops obviously according by the increasing users and continuous requests scheduling.MOSS gets larger speedup and 550% higher performance than sixteen users′ circumstances.
