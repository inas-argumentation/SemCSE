Application of Extended Octree Model in the Three-dimensional Display System of Tool-path
The extended octree nodes are added into the tradtional octree model,and the data structure of blending octree is introduced into the three-dimensional display system of tool-path in the NC machining;then the method of converting the B-rep model of the part into the corresponded extended octree model is described,and the Real-time simulation of the machining process of the part is implemented by means of the extended octree model;finally,the feasibility and validity of the algorithm in this paper is testified by implementing a NC machining program.
