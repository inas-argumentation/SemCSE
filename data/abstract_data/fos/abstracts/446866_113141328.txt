Practice on Innovation of Fume Exhaust System of Jinlong Flash Furnace
Jinlong Company takes full advantage of opportunity of boiler innovation for renovation and optimization of fume exhaust system.On the basis of analyzing the problems existed in FF fume exhaust system before innovation,the paper emphasizes innovation and technical improvement of electrostatic precipitation,exhaust fan,flue dust delivery system,settling room and duct.After innovation,FF fume exhaust system will be operated in stable state,various parameters and index will be achieved at a better level.
