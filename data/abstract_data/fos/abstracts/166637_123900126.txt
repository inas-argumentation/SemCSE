New Improvement of Set Partitioning in Hierarchical Trees (SPIHT) Algorithm
Proposed some modifications to the SPIHT algorithm. It is based on the idea of the insignificant correlation of wavelet coefficients among medium-and high-frequency subbands. In this scheme, insignificant wavelet coefficients that correspond to the same spatial location in the medium subbands can be used to reduce the redundancy by a combined function proposed in associated with the modified SPIHT. In high-frequency subbands, the modified SPIHT proposes a dictator to reduce the interband redundancy. Experimental results indicate that the proposed technique improves the quality of the reconstructed image in terms of both the peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and the perceptual results over the original SPIHT at the same bit rate.
