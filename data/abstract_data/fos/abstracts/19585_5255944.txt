MrNomogram: a web-based multivariable pediatric renal nomogram.
UNLABELLED
Multivariable Pediatric Renal Nomogram, or MrNomogram, is a web-based clinical tool for the evaluation of pediatric renal length. Unlike other available age-adjusted renal nomograms, MrNomogram takes into consideration the fact that the renal length is influenced by multiple demographic variables. It provides a more accurate prediction of pediatric renal length, given the patient's demographic characteristics, such as age, gender, height, etc. (
