Faculty governance in PKU during the presidency of CAI Yuanpei: problems and changes
Faculty Governance dates back to the administrative pattern of universities in the Middle Ages and Peking University during the presidency of CAI Yuanpei carried out the idea first in Chinese modern universities.Despite the legitimacy difficulty it encountered,the idea of democratic governance,scholarly professors and the individual personality of President CAI,all provided conditions for the realiation of this system.As a result,its execution secured professors' leading role in making decisions for the university affairs.However,due to the different ideas of governance,JIANG Menglin put an end to the institution of "Faculy Govrenance".
