Adenovirus mediated pGH gene expression and its growth-stimulating function in rats
Recombinant adenovirus vectors (MTCD-rAdV) that contain an expression cassette of porcine growth hormone (pGH) cDNA under the control of sheep metallothionine (MT) promoter was generated by homologous recombination. pGH expression was readily detected in the culture medium of rAdV infected CHO cells 48 hours post infection. To test the growth promoting function of the rAdV-pGH in vivo, 0.5 ml MTCD-rAdV (TCID_(50) 10~(-10)) was infused throug cardiac injection into rats blood. Under the intermittent inducement with ZnSO_(4), the body weight gain of rats injected with MTCD-rAdV was significantly (P<0.05) higher than that of the control group (injected with virus as control) within 19 days after injection. Therefore, it is evident that adenovirus mediated pGH gene transfer has a significant effect in promoting rats growth.
