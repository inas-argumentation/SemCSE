A Quantitative Study on Scale of Eco-environment Impact Assessment Based on Fractal Theory
Taking a project of ski field in Shennongjia,Hubei Province as an example,a quantitative study on scale of eco-environment impact assessment based on fractal theory was proposed.Two circular areas centered the project whose semi-diameter are 8km and 30km respectively are lined out as two research areas which are divided into 18 and 62 zones with measurement in 500m.The border length,acreage and fractal dimension of various ecological patches in different zones are calculated with GIS technique,and regression analysis is carried out to result two scale-free areas,which indicates the impact scale of eco-environment is 78.5km2.The study offers a method for quantitative assessment of eco-environment impact scale for the construction projects.
