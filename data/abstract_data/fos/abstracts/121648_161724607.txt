Ernest Hemingway and the Discipline of Creative Writing, Or, Shark Liver Oil
A couple of years ago six young men submitted six stories in one day in my class. Every story was laid in a bar, every one involved a girl, wanton but wistful, with whom the hero was involved, every one contained an impressionistic passage during which the hero studied his drunken countenance in the wavering bar mirror, and every one was written in a tough, bare, corner-of-the-mouth style. —Wallace Stegner, "The Anxious Generation"
