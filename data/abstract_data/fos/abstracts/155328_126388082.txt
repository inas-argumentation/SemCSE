An Improved Electrical Hygrometer and Its Applications
The electric hygrometer consists of an aluminum tube coated with polystyrene and wound with a bifilar winding of bare palladium wire. The unit is then coated with a thin film of polyvinyl acetate with an amount of lithium chloride in it depending upon the humidity range to be covered. The electrical resistance of the film between the two coils is a function of humidity. If appreciable temperature changes are involved, a correction is applied. The thin aluminium tube enables the unit to quickly assume the temperature of the air as it must if measuring relative humidity. The palladium wire prevents an increase in resistance caused by a film which forms on the surface of other types of wires. The polystyrene forms an excellent water-resistant surface of high electrical resistance for the wire and water sensitive film, which prevents a hysteresis effect caused by the absorption of water by glass and other materials originally used as bases.
