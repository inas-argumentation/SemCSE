Entrepreneur Education: New Responsibility and Mission of Higher Education
Entrepreneur education is a kind of education which aims to develop students' basic qualities of undertaking an enterprise and cultivate students'capabilties to take on an enterprise.Entrepreneur education is a historic mission undertaken by higher education institutions to build an innovative country.It serves as an effective method to solve the problem of employment and takes the realistic responsibility to promote the development of college students' own characteristics.The realistic channels of entrepreneur education in higher education institutions are as follows: 1) to strengthen organized leadership;2) to reform the pattern of cultivating talents;3) to construct the platform of entrepreneur education;4) to provide system guarentee.
