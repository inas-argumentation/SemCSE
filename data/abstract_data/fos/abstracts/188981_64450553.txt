Design and Implement of LF Modules in Dual-frequency E-tag with Adjustable Communication Distance
Based on the radio frequency identification technology,this paper proposes a project of long distance dual-frequency E-tag with adjustable communication distance.According to the principle of low frequency magnetic communications,a transmitting module and a receiving module at the frequency of 125kHz are designed.In the transmitting module,the gain of the amplifier can be controlled by adjusting the rheostat Rw and it also can control the antenna's transmitting power and communication distance;In the receiving module,the signal received is amplified by the ultra-low noise,ultra-low distortion operational amplifier ISL28190,of which the gain could get up to 40dB.By using the two modules,the peak-to-peak amplitude of the signal at the output of the receiving antenna could get up to 5mV at the distance of 5 meters from the transmitting antenna.
