An autoradiographic study in μ-opioid receptor knockout mice
An autoradiographic study was carried out to study the opioid binding sites in a μ-opioid receptor knock-out mouse line whose exon 2 and 3 were deleted. μ-Opioid binding sites were undetectable in this knock-out mouse line while the binding of the other two types of receptors were unaltered. Our results suggest destroying functional μ-opioid receptor does not affect the expression of the other two opioid receptors.
