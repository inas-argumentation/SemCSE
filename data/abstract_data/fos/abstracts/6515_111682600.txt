Reinforcement Method of Extrusion Die Work-position of Tube
Aiming at the rupture defect of extrusion die work-position of tube,a practical improving technique about the die structure was put forward.The die structure was changed from integral structure to two part: the original die part and the mandrel reinforcement part.The mandrel type was double cuniform.The two parts were well integrated by plastic-working,press-working and heating expanding and cooling contracting.Through the actual production verification,the die's rupture phenomenon disappears and the die's service life greatly increases.
