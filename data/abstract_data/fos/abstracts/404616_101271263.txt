Application of ELISA technology on food safety detection
Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay(ELISA) is an immunoassay technology established on the combination of the highly specific antigen-antibody reaction and the efficient catalysis of enzyme.In this paper,the application of ELISA on food safety detection is reviewed,including pesticide residue,animal drug residue,forbidden drugs,genetically modified foods,pathogenic microorganisms,biotoxin and others,and the existed problems and corresponding methods to solution are proposed.
