Research on Low Temperature Toughness of Welded Joints of 10CrNi3MoV Steel
By observing the microstructure of welding joints,and analysis of low-temperature impact energy,the series of temperature impact and the morphology of impact fracture,it can be obtained that the impact energy of the welded joints on the platform of the fusion line of 10CrNi3MoV steel is above 140J.There is a stable platform at-35℃ and 50% impact energy transition temperature is-70℃.The impact energy dispersion is relatively large at-50℃ that average value is 109J.The average of impact energy at-70℃ is above 80J and the ductile-brittle transition temperature is below-70℃.
