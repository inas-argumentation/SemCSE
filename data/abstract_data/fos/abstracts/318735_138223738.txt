Effect of Copper-coated Graphite on Property of Graphite-silver Matrix Composites
The interface between graphite and silver as well as distributed form is a very important factor to affect the property of graphite-silver composites.The graphite-copper-silver composites were made from copper-coated- graphite powder.By means of the analyses of SEM and metallograph and physical tests,the microstructure,properties of composites made from copper-coated-graphite powder were studied.The results show that when molding pressure and sintered temperature are optimal ,the composite has homogeneous structure and high density with scattered-graphite in the silver.This structure makes the electrictal conductivity and hardness impove remarkably.
