Research Status on One-dimension Metal Oxide Nano-materials
The common preparation methods for one-dimensional metal oxide nanomaterials were reviewed,and on the basis,the performance and preparation of ZnO nanotubes,nanowires,TiO2 nanotubes and Al2O3 nanowires were elaborated.The current research status of several other kinds of metal oxide nanomaterials were introduced.The existing problems in the preparation process of one-dimensional metal oxide were broached,and a further outlook for the development trend was put forward.
