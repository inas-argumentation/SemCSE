Antioxidant activity of Lactobacillus rhamnosus strains’ constituents
The antioxidant activity of Lactobacillus rhamnosus strains B7, B8, B10, B44 were determined by using DPPH· method, superoxide dismutase(SOD), glutathione peroxidase(GSH-PX) and catalase(CAT) activity of the four strains’ fermentation broth, cell and cell-free extract were analyzed, and their DPPH·scavenging ratio of strains’ constituents with after enzyme inactivation were compared. The results showed that the four strains have high antioxidant activity, contain all the three enzymes, and the strains which enzymes have inactivated also can scavenged DPPH·. The results also showed that fermentation broth has the strongest DPPH· scavenging ability, then followed by cell-free extract and cell, and the tendency consistent with the DPPH· scavenging ability before enzyme inactivation. Therefore antioxidant activity of the four stains should not only be related to antioxidant enzymes, but also with other substances.
