Study on Growth and Development of Sexual Gland of Wannanhua Boars
The results of this study on growth and development of sexual glands of Wannanhua boars indicate that the testis weight is from 0 43 to 0 50 g at birth,compared with from 92 33 to 97 75 g at 240 days The epididymis weight is from 1 12 g at 45 days, compared with 34 50 g at 240 days The growth of testis volume (y) with days (x) can be presented by a model of y=a+bx+cx 2 in which a,b and c capture the model parameters,respectively The weights of prostate gland, vesicular gland and cowper's gland are 0 21, 0 13 and 0 26 g at birth,compared with 8 27, 182 00 and 153 83 g at 240 days respectively,which can be described by exponential growth curves There is a significant relationship between the growth and development of sexual glands and the levels of hormone (GH and T) in blood serum of Wannanhua boars
