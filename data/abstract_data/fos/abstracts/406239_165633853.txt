Wittgenstein’s famous rejection of metaphysics is less a dismissal of the various ideas that term is often taken to include than a claim that they reside not in some transcendent realm of thought but rather in the everyday, reimagined as a “scene of significance” (125). Pointing us toward this conclusion, the essays in this collection help us to see both modernism and Wittgenstein anew.
