Shell construction, life habits and evolution in the gastropod Velates
Abstract The gastropod Velates is unique among the Neritidae in its large size and shell shape. Shell morphology in Velates was achieved by addition of shell material on the outside, combined with extensive resorption and remodelling on the inside. This process is highly specialised, but not qualitatively different from shell growth in “normal” Neritidae. This likely allowed a rapid evolution of Velates from rock-dwelling Neritidae, triggered by a shift to life habits as an infaunal, possibly suspension-feeding, soft-bottom dweller.
