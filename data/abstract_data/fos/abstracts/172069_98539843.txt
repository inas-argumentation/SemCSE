Spin-flip tunnelling between quantum wells with a semimagnetic barrier
Non-resonant tunnelling transitions between quantum wells accompanied by spin-dependent exchange scattering of the electron are considered theoretically for structures consisting of two semiconductor quantum wells separated by a semimagnetic barrier. The transitions both with and without spin flip are shown to be equally important for the transverse current in an electric field. The contribution due to spin-flip scattering gives rise to an additional step in the voltage - current characteristics when a magnetic field is applied.
