Reduction of Carboxylic Acid with 2-Propanol over Zirconia-Titania
The reduction of long carbon chain carboxylic acids or the esters with 2-propanol proceeded efficiently over zirconia-titania at atmospheric pressure, and the corresponding alcohols were obtained. Methyl 9-octadecenoate was reduced to give 9-octadecen-1-ol in a 58% yield.
