Research of effectively purgative components of SCI in semen cassiae
Study the effectively purgative components of SCI in semen cassiae. SCI was determined by high performance liquid chromatography(HPLC) on a Kromasil C18 5μ(250mm×4.6mm), using acetonitrile-5%methol(60∶40), a flow rate of 1.0mL/min, the detective wavelength was 280nm and was analyzed by chemical methods too; Study the purgative effect of the different components in SCI on mice by noting the water content of dejecta. The emodin, chrysophanol and physcion were separate completely within 30min under this chromatographic condition. Fibrins and water-soluble polysaccharides were determined by chemical analysis. Animal experiments proved that different components all had purgative function only little different. The purgative function of anthraquinones in SCI is mainly lax, going with constipation, while polysaccharides and fibrins have better and stabile purgation. And the best effect realizes when the two combines. They have synergetic effect.
