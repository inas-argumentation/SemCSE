Remeasurement of the lifetime of the 41+-state of156Gd and recalculation of itsg-factor
The half-life of the 41+-state of156Gd has been remeasured by the delayed coincidence technique. The excellent time resolution which can now be achieved by use of small BaF2 detectors allows a more reliable determination than with previously applied methods. The resultT1/2(156Gd,41+)=108(2) ps is smaller than previously published data, but it fits well into the systematics of theB(E2) values of the rotational transitions of this nucleus. A recalculation of the previously measuredg-factor of the same state givesg(156Gd,41+) =0.327(19).This value is still smaller than theg-factor of the 21+ state, but the magnitude of the reduction can now easier be interpreted by nuclear structure calculations.
