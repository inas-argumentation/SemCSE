Synthesis,Characterization and Antioxidation Activity of Germanium(IV)-Quercetin Complex
Quercetin(Qu) is a kind of natural flavonoids with diverse biological activities.By optimizing the experimental conditions,the quercetin and germanium(Ⅳ) complexes,QuGe and QuGe2,were synthesized.The structures of the complexes were characterized by UV/vis spectroscopy,IR spectroscopy,1H NMR,elemental analysis,HPLC,and electrochemistric method.The results showed that the chelation sites of QuGe complex were the 3' and 4' hydroxyl,while the QuGe2 complex 4-carbonyl and 3-hydroxy except 3' and 4' hydroxyl.Moreover,the antioxidation activity and its mechanism of the complexes were also discussed.The result revealed that QuGe and QuGe2 complexes exhibited more potent antioxidation effect than quercetin.
