Research on Wood Recognition Based on Bag of Words
Leads Bag of Words into the field of timber image recognition,and introduces its implementation in the timber identification.First uses SURF to extract feature points,and makes these feature points clustering and gets the heart of class.On the basis of the heart of class,gets the vector column chart of various training species and species images to be identified.Using the classifier to classify the vector column chart description of the timber image,will improve the efficiency of timber identification,so as to provide a more reliable method for timber expertise to more accurately identify the species.
