Study on effects of reducing sugar content of sugar can molasses with phosphoric acid pretreatment
The article studied the pretreatment of the sugar with phosphoric acid method. Through the single factor and orthogonal experiment,the best pretreatment conditions of the sugar can molasses were ascertained:the molasses were diluted with water at the ration of 1:1,and then were heated up and filtered. The filtrate was laid still for 14 h after adjusting pH to 3.0 with concentrated H3PO4. After adjusting pH to 7.0 with Ca (OH)2again,and was heated for 30 min between 65 ℃ and 70 ℃,the ABS value of the reducing sugar content in the filtrate is 2.995.
