On the Self-Organization and Orderly Development Of Chinese Higher Education System
Higher education system is a self-organization system.The university can carry through self-regulation and self-regeneration according to the internal and external change,so as to make itself constantly adapt to the requirement of the development of economic society and the development of human beings.Since the reform and opening up,with the gradual expansion of autonomy in Chinese universities,the abilities of universities' self-regulation and self-development are becoming gradually strengthened.However,compared to foreign universities,the abilities of Chinese universities' self-regulation and self-development need to be further improved,and self-regulatory mechanism needs to be further reformed and improved.The autonomy of school-running should be further enlarged,and the initiative and consciousness of the university should be brought into play.Only in this way can the vitality of school-running be strengthened,and the self-organization and orderly development of higher education system can be achieved.Therefore,the construction of great power in higher education could be promoted.
