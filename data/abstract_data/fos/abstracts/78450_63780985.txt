Buiding the Patterns of Prediction about the Logistics Adopting the Technology of Neural Network
The article studies theories and methods adopted in the realizing of intelligent systems about the ogistics. It tries to buid up the patterns of prediction about the logistics, adopting the technology of neural network. Frist, it points out the level structure and the precess of discovering knowledge about the patterns of prediction for the logistics. Then, it designs analyses them in the calculating methods of BP network. Finally, it does the experiments on some cases and analyse the results of these experiments.
