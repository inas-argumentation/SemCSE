An evaluation of information technology use among first year pharmacy students
SUMMARY This analysis of student attitudes towards computing and experiences of IT in the first year of this pharmacy degree course has indicated that:• on entry, most pharmacy students have only a basic experience of computing resources; • the use of IT resources by students is likely to be enhanced by the provision of suitable training as part of the undergraduate course; • students find CAL material more beneficial than other IT resources during the first year of the course; • mature students are more willing to embrace the use of learning technologies for active learning and as a consequence benefit to a greater extent than those students entering university directly from schools and colleges; • specific areas of this course, such as stereochemistry, are enhanced to a greater degree by IT than others. To further the implementation of IT for student learn-ing we recommend that first semester curricula should include an element of IT training. This would include basic word-processing, data handling, electronic commu-nication and the use of CAL and the Internet. Such train-ing would provide all students with the necessary tools to assist them with their future studies and careers.
