Rate Based Proportional and Integral Controlled Active Queue Management
Proportional integral(PI) controller of control theory is introduced into AQM.Although the performance of PI AQM is superior to that of random early detection(RED),its convergence speed is slow especially when the target length of queue is smaller.The testing method of choosing parameters can′t guarantee transient and stability at the same time.This paper proposes a Rate based Proportional and Integral(RPI) according to the aggregate packet arrival rate in the router.RPI adopt appropriate dropping probability by the strictness degree and the trend of change.Verified by using NS-2 simulations,RPI can achieve faster convergence speed and smaller queue oscillation than PI.
