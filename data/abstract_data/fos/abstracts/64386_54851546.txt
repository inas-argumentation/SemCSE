The Schroeder-Bernstein index for Banach spaces
In relation to some Banach spaces recently constructed by W. T. Gowers and B. Maurey, we introduce the notion of Schroeder–Bernstein index SBi(X) for every Banach spaceX. This index is related to complemented subspaces ofX which contain some complemented copy of X. Then we establish the existence of a Banach space E which is not isomorphic to E for every n ∈ N, n ≥ 2, but has a complemented subspace isomorphic to E2. In particular, SBi(E) is uncountable. The construction of E follows the approach given in 1996 by W. T. Gowers to obtain the first solution to the Schroeder–Bernstein Problem for Banach spaces.
