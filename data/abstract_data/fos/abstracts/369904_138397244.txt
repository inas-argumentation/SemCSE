Discuss on Evolution Low of Semi-solid Microstructure of Different Steel Materials
Three kinds of semi-solid specimens of steel materials were produced by homemade equipment,the evolution mechanism of the semi-solid microstructure of different steel materials was researched.The results show that the evolution mode of different steel materials is quite different.The primary phase of the semi-solid microstructure of 1Cr18Ni9Ti is nearly spherical;and that of 60Si2Mn can bend,that of T10 is easy to break.
