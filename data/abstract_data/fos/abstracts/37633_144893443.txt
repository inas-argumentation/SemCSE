More Than Just Pictures: Using Picture Story Books to Broaden Young Learners' Social Consciousness
Abstract When they had all gone Little Rabbit crept into the middle of the empty clearing. I should have tried to help the other rabbits, he thought. If only we creatures had stuck together, it could have been different. Sadly, Little Rabbit left the clearing. He'd go tell other forest creatures about the Terrible Things. He hoped someone would listen. (Eve Bunting, Terrible Things, 1980, unpaginated).
