Determination of Cenozoic sea-temperatures using Hiatella arctica (Linné)☆
Abstract Hiatella arctica ( Linne ) has had a world-wide distribution from the Early Tertiary through to the present. Study of Recent Hiatella has shown that features such as numerical distribution, shell form and growth are temperature-guided. Studies on fossil Hiatella reveal a similar pattern. Shell size of Recent populations has been directly compared with temperature by means of graphs and the results have then been quantitatively applied to fossil samples. Not only can the mean yearly temperature for various Cenozoic localities in central and western Europe be deduced but an estimate of the yearly extremes can also be made.
