Research on Weighing Strategy of Vehicle Entering Plant Based on Fuzzy Operation Time
In order to solve the problem of congestion and disorder in the process of weighing and handling of cement vehicles entering the plant, a new scheduling strategy based on fuzzy operation time is proposed. Firstly, the modeling and analysis of the problem, and then proposes a genetic algorithm to solve the problem. The algorithm was improved and the different algorithm and the improved algorithm optimization results was compared. The results show that the proposed scheme can effectively solve congestion problems and increase weighing efficiency of vehicle entering plant.
