A Broad-Leaf Contour Extraction Technology in Complex Background
To extraction the leaf contour from an image is an important process in recognize plant by image.Proposes an algorithm based on integrated technology.First,selects the target area and finds the edge by Canny operator.Second,finds all the lines,builder the relationship between line numbers and line length,finds the threshold by second derivative.Finally,deletes the line that length less than threshold,Remainder is the contour of target.Experimental results of broad-leaf image shows that this algorithm is feasible and effective.This algorithm can filter the background noise and branch edges of leaf internal.
