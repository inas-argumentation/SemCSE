Finite element model to determine the shape factor for soil air permeability measurements
A finite element model was developed to simulate air flow through soil. The model was used to find the shape factor, A, for an air permeameter used to determine soil air permeability. The finite element model yielded more accurate results than an electrolytic model. It satisfies the boundary condition for the flow equation better, especially for measurements in the field. The ratios of shape factor to probe inside diameter (A/D) were determined and plotted as a function of the ratios of probe inside diameter to probe insertion depth (D/H). The finite element model can be used to predict shape factors for any soil insert dimensions. It is more flexible than the electrolytic model or calibration in determining soil air permeability.
