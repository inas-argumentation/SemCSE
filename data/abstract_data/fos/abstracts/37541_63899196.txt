Design and Implementation of a New Kind of Computer-Room Enviroment Automatic Monitoring System
Introduces a new kind of computer-room enviroment automatic monitoring system with capacity of real time detecting voltage,leakage of water,noise,smoke,temperature as well as humidity.It includes SMS alarming,net alarming,sound alarming,light alarming,GPS funtion and has the merit of multi-function,reliable alarming,easy expansibility and so on .Describes the composition,function and design of software and hardware of the system and explains the experimental results and its using effect.
