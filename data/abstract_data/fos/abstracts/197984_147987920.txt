The costs and benefits of world-class universities
It is a high cost to carry a university into the ranks of world-class institutions. The author argues that putting too much stress on attaining world-class status may harm an individual university or an academic system. Meanwhile he as well recommends a more realistic and objective strategy, which is for focusing on building "national" or "regional-class" academic institutions with closely related to economy and social of the country or this region, and not blindly pursuing world-class status.
