Retrospect and Prospect:College Students' Employment Research over 10 Years
The problem of college students' employment has caused widespread concern in the academic field since 2001.Research on this issue in the recent decade are mainly concentrated in college students' employment difficulties,the factors affecting college students' employment,the quality of college students' employment,college students' employment integrity,college students' employment education,and college students' career guidance.There are more to expand in details,such as the college students' employment institutions,college students' employment system,college students' employment ideal and statistics research of college students' employment.
