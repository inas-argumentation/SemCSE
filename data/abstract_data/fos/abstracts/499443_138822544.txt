Effect of Al on M_2C Eutectic Carbides in High Speed Steel
The effect of Al on as-cast microstructure,morphology and property of M2C eutectic carbides in M2 high speed steel was investigated,and the mechanism of morphology evolution was discussed as well.The results show that Al can increase the solubility of alloying elements in matrix,decreases the amount of ledeburite eutectic and refines the ledeburite network.Al can change the morphology of M2C carbides which is transformedfrom the curved rodlike to the lamellar.Meanwhile,the concentration of alloying elements in M2C increases while that decreases in the matrix between carbides.The lamellar M2C is more difficult to break and granulate during heat treatment,which is not favorable for the improvement of carbide morphology.It is suggested that the morphology of M2C depends on the distribution of alloying elements and the constitutional supercooling caused by Al.
