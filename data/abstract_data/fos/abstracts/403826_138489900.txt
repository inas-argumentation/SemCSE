Simulation on Filling Process of Semi-solid Aluminum Alloy ZL201 Stent
The constitutive relation of the semi-solid aluminum alloy ZL201 was established by multiple nonlinear fitting on the experimental data of the hot compression. And the filling process of the alloy stent was simulated by the finite-element software AnyCasting. The results show that the apparent viscosity of the alloy decreases with the increase of the shear rate. The lava shows a feature of shear thinning and fills with laminar flow mode, thus high-quality casting can be obtained.
