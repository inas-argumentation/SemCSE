Study on enzymatic hydrolysis and membrane technology of preparing active peptides
This study mainly do research on membrane work process on preparing active peptide through enzymatic hydrolysis method and other technologies,optimal processing conditions for protein hydrolysate are as follows:dosage is 2%,a ratio of fish to water meat is 1∶4 and enzymatic time is 3 h. Active peptide products can be obtained from fish flotsam like the head and cauda of fish,fishskin,scale,and more than 86% peptides are the bioactive peptide that can be easily absorbed by man.
