Removal of cadmium from Chlamys ferrari skirt border enzymatic hydrolysate by cross-linked chitosan resins
In this paper,chitosan was used as raw material for preparing the five kinds of cross-linked chitosan resins,chitosan resin(CTS),chitosan bound Ce resin(CTS-Ce),chitosan-tea polysaccharide bound Ce resin(CTS-TPS-Ce),chitosan-tea polysaccharide resin(CTS-TPS)and chitosan bound Zn resin(CTS-Zn).The effects of the different kinds of chitosan resins on the removal of Cd from Chlamys ferrari skirt border enzymatic hydrolysate were studied and their effects on the beneficial ingredients such as proteins,amino acids and trace elements(Zn,Fe,Ca and Mg) were analyzed.The results indicated that CTS-Zn was useful for the removal of Cd from Chlamys ferrari skirt border hydrolysate and the removal rate was more than 80%.In contrast,the adsorption effects of CTS-Zn on proteins,amino acids and the trace elements were less than that on Cd.
