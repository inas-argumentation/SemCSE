Kirillov–Reshetikhin crystals, energy function and the combinatorial R-matrix
We study the polytope model for the affine type A Kirillov–Reshetikhin crystals and prove that the action of the affine Kashiwara operators can be described in a remarkably simple way. Moreover, we investigate the combinatorial R-matrix on a tensor product of polytopes and characterize the map explicitly on the highest weight elements. We further give a formula for the local energy function and provide an alternative proof for the perfectness.
