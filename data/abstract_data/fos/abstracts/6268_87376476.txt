TNF-α and IL-1β secreted from LPS activated activated macrophages down-regulated by over-expression of GFP-hDaxx fusion protein
Objective To study the effect of over-expression of GFP-hDaxx fusion protein on the content of TNF-α and IL-1β secreted by the LPS activated macrophages. Methods The eukaryotic expression vector of GFP-hDaxx fusion protein was constructed by hDaxx cDNA fragments subcloned to pEGFP. Twenty four hours after transfection of pEGFP/hDaxx into macrophages, the expression of GFP or GFP-hDaxx was examined with fluorescent microscopy. The macrophage culture media were replaced with a medium containing LPS. During further incubation period after changing media, the content of TNF-α and IL-1β in the media were tested quantitatively with EIA kit at 0.5?h, 4?h and 12?h, respectively. Results Transfected into macrophages, the pEGFP/hDaxx over-expressed GFP-hDaxx and GFP that located respectively in nuclei and cytoplasma. And the secretions of TNF-α and IL-1β reduced significantly at 4?h and 12?h. Conclusion The over-expression of hDaxx fusion protein can down-regulate the secretions of TNF-α and IL-1β in the LPS activated macrophages.
