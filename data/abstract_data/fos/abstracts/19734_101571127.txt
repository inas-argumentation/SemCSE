Preparation of ZnO Nanorod Arrays by Chemical Bath Deposition Method
Ordered ZnO nanorod arrays were fabricated by chemical bath deposition method using Zn(CH3COO)2·2H2O and C6H20N4 as precursors.The influence of the deposition condition,such as the concentrations of reaction and seed solution,on the morphology,grain size and crystal structure of ZnO nanorod,were investigated by FESEM and XRD.The results indicate that the diameter of ZnO nanorods increases with the increase of concentration of reaction solution.The nanorods grows perpendicularly to the substrate along the(002) crystal facets.UV-Vis spectrum of ZnO nano-rods arrays show an intense absorption at 300-400 nm wavelength and a weak absorption in 500-700 nm wavelength range.The dominant UV band emission of ZnO was observed at approximately 384 nm wave length.
