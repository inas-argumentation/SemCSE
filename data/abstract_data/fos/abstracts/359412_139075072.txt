Study on Performance Comparison Between Electroless Ni-P and Ni-Ce-P on 27SiMn Steel
In order to improve the corrosion resistance and wear resistance of 27SiMn alloy substrate of the mining hydraulic support,the electroless Ni-P and Ni-Ce-P coatings were prepared on the alloy substrate,respectively.The surface morphology and phase analysis of the coating were characterized by OM,SEM and XRD,meanwhile,the hardness,wear resistance and corrosion resistance of the coating were analyzed comparatively.Compared with the electroless Ni-P coating,the surface of Ni-Ce-P coating becomes more compact and smooth,and the thickness of Ni-Ce-P is about 1.8 times higher than that of Ni-P coating,which indicates that the addition of Ce element can increase the plating velocity and improve the coating quality.The XRD results reveal that both of them have a typical amorphous structure.The hardness and wear resistance of the coatings are higher than those of Ni-P coating.The polarization curve measurement in 3.5%NaCl show that there are not only passivation behaviors in anodic polarization curves of the two coatings,but also the corrosion resistance of the Ni-Ce-P coating is superior to that of Ni-P coating.
