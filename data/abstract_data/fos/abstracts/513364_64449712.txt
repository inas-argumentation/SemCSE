Linearly Reasoning Method Based on EFTPN for Fuzzy Temporal Knowledge and its Application in Analyzing Operations of Train Group
To deal with the fuzzy temporal knowledge reasoning problems in analyzing operations of train group, in the paper we combining with linear logic and EFTPN, put forward a linearly reasoning method based on EFTPN. This method not only can accurately reason and validate the fuzzy temporal knowledge, but also is more efficient, namely solve the inference problems within linear time complexity; Furthermore, it is suitable to more situations, and can solve complicated problem of temporal knowledge reasoning in other researching fields.
