Analysis on Effect of Local Heat Treatment on Mechanical Properties of Clinched Joints
Two kinds of clinched joints with different substrates were taken local heat treatment. The influences of local heat treatment on stretching mechanical properties of clinched joints with different substrates were studied. The results show that the tensile strength of clinched joints is improved by local heat treatment. Failure displacement also increases when the joints have local heat treatment. Energy absorb ability of clinched joints has a big range promotion with the extending of tensile strength and failure displacement. The resistance of clinched joints tensile failure is heightened on account of the improving of substrate strength, which eventually leads to lock parts of the clinched joints in some degree of torn. For this reason, the comprehensive mechanical performance of the clinched joints is promoted.
