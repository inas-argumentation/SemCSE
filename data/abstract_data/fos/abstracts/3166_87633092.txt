Effect of puerarin on serum uric acid in hyperuricemic rats
Recently, hyperuricemia is the increased risk of metabolic syndrome. This study explored the effect of puerarin on serum uric acid concentration and pathways in hyperuricemic rats. This research monitored indicators of hyperuricemic rats who intaked puerarin, such as serum uric acid level, oxidase activity of xanthine and uric acid excretion, etc via animal model of hyperuricemic rats, biochemical, metabolic experiment method, etc. The results showed that puerarin in reasonable dosage were beneficial to improve health status in patients with hyperuricemia by reducing the body's blood uric acid levels via inhibiting generating of uric acid, promoting excreting.
