Informatics analysis of distribution characteristics for SSE in ESTs of Salvia miltiorrhiza.
In this study,10228 expressed sequence tags(ESTs) of Salvia miltiorrhiza in the database of NCBI were downloaded and processed in order to develop the functional molecular marker.And 5073 unique sequences with total length 292.561 kb were obtained after the removement of the ESTs with redundances and low qualities.Finally,in total 628 SSRs were detected in 538 ESTs which mainly distributed in 401～600 bp and 601~800 bp sequence extent,accounting for 10.4% of the non-redundant ESTs.And the distribution distance and the average length of the EST-SSRs were about 0.47 kb and 14.47 bp.Dinucleotide are the main type repeats,accounting for 72.45% of all SSRs.And trinucleotide are the secondary type repeats,accounting for 26.75% of all SSRs.(AG)n,(AT)n,(AC)n,(AAG)n and(AGC)n are the most frequent motifs.The results will contribute to the design of EST-SSR primer and development of SSR markers with high polymorphism for Salvia miltiorrhiza.
