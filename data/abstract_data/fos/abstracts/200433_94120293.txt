Improvement of the thermal shock resistance of β-Al2O3 ceramics for sensor applications
In recent years, much progress has been made in the production and evaluation of ceramic sensors for the on-line testing of the aluminium composition before casting. However, the use of these sensors in such applications is often limited by their poor thermal shock resistance. The purpose of this paper is to communicate the initial findings of research aimed at improving the thermal shock resistance of β-Al 2 O 3 , ceramic used for the detection of sodium in aluminium. In order to improve the thermal shock resistance of β-Al 2 O 3 , Al 2 O 3 and ZrO 2 fibres have been incorporated into β-Al 2 O 3 . Other second phase materials including Al 2 TiO 5 and SiC in a β-Al 2 O 3 matrix have been tested, but their chemical stabilities during sintering are poor. WC is stable in a β-Al 2 O 3 matrix if it is pressure-sintered in argon.
