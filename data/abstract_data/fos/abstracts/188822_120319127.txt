Heavy-ion L=1 transfer: A sensitive test of reaction theory
Angular distributions presented for reactions $sup 40$,$sup 42$,$sup 44$Ca($sup 13$C, $sup 14$N)$sup 39$,$sup 41$,$sup 43$K (transferred L=1) at 68- MeV bombarding energy are observed to oscillate out of phase with exact, finite- range, distorted-wave-Born-approximation (DWBA) calculations. It is shown that differential cross sections for L=1 transfer reactions are particularly sensitive tests of the DWBA. Simple arguments follow which indicate that the difficulty in fitting present data lies with our understanding of the reaction mechanism. (AIP)
