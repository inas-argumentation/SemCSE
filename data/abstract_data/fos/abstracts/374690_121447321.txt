Investigation of a plasma mirror of an electron-beam-controlled CO2 laser with heating radiation power densities of 1011–1012 W/cm2
It is shown that when an electron-beam-controlled CO2 laser operates in a simple single-stage scheme with a plasma mirror, the laser radiation is absorbed efficiently in the plasma. For a heating radiation power of several tens of gigawatts and a power density at the target q~1011–1012 W/cm2, the electron temperature and density in the plasma mirror were Te~200 eV and Ne~1019 cm–3. Helium-like Mg XI ions were obtained.
