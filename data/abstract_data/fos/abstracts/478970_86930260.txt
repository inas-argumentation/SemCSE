Influences of reaction parameters upon carboxymethylation of cassava starch through a rapid semidry process
Reaction parameters affecting DS and RE in carboxymethylation of cassava starch were studied, including reaction temperature, time, stirring time under room temperature, NaOH, sodium monochloroacetate(SMCA), and ethanol dosage. It was observed that the optimal reaction temperature was 120℃. With the elongation of reaction time, DS and RE increased, and became constant afterwards. DS and RE ascended according to the increase of NaOH and SMCA dosage, however RE declined at high SMCA dosage.Scarcity of ethanol was not capable of preventing gelatinization which led to an undesirable destruction of the granular structure of starch molecules, while abundance brought on falls of DS and RE values. Within 20min of stirring time under room temperature, DS and RE increased significantly, and turned steady henceforth.This novel process is chiefly appreciated for retrenchment of organic solvent, environment protection if applied to mass production, convenience and high efficiency
