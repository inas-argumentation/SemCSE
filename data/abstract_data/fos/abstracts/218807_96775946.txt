A “high resolution” NMR study of ionic transport in the hydrate of ammonium ferrocyanide
Ammonium ferrocyanide monohydrate has been studied by NMR at 270 MHz and 500 MHz. Two chemically shifted (separation = 3.55 ppm) signals observed are attributed to the NH4+ and H2O protons. From the temperature dependence of the line-widths and the chemical shifts, both NH4+ and H2O are concluded to take part in diffusion.
