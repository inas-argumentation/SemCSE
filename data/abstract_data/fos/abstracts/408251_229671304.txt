Progress in global drug resistance patterns and drug resistance mechanisms of Mycoplasma pneumoniae
Mycoplasma pneumoniae (M.pneumoniae) is one of the predominant pathogenic bacteria causing community acquired pneumonia. An epidemic of M. pneumoniae infection occurs every three to seven years worldwide. It can cause a variety of clinical symptoms. Since 2000, macrolide-resistant M. pneumoniae strains have become increasingly common in many countries around the world and the drug resistance rate has reached as high as 100% in some area, which has posed a great threat to human health. This paper will review the status and mechanism of drug resistance in M. pneumoniae. 
 
 
Key words: 
Mycoplasma pneumoniae; Global drug resistance; Drug resistance mechanism
