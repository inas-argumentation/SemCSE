Effect of Quenching Temperature on Mechanical Properties of Pipeline Steel with Excellent Deformability
A new type of pipeline steel with excellent deformability containing bainite and M/A constituent can been obtained through the hotting online partitioning(HOP) process.The influences of quenching temperature on mechanical properties of the pipeline steel were investigated by means of microscopic analysis and mechanical property test.The results show that with the increase of quenching temperature,the volume fraction of bainite decreases and M/A constituent increases,so the strength,hardness,impact toughness and Y/T ratio of the experiment steel decreases and the elongation after fracture and uniform elongation both increases.When the quenching temperature is 350 ℃,the experiment steel has the stronger strength,toughness and excellent deformability.
