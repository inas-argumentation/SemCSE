Twin Rolls Thin Strip Continuous Casting Technology
Twin rolls thin strip continuous casting process is a leading technology in metallurgy industry.It has characteristics with a shortened procedure,low energy consumption,high metal yield,short production cycle,and can reduce rolling mill pass,effectively raise rolling mill operation rate.The development of twin rolls thin strip continuous casting technology in china and abroad was introduced in this article.The process and microstructure research and mathematical modeling were also introduced.The results showed that twin rolls thin strip continuous casting technology simplified metallurgical thin material production process,saved equipment investment and played an important role in the production of high speed steel, nonferrous metals and stainless steel.
