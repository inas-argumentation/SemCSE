Quartz Crystal Au Electrode-Based Electrochemical Immunosensor for Rapid Detection of Escherichia coli O157:H7
An electrochemical immunosensor with a quartz crystal Au electrode was developed for rapid detection of Escherichia coli O157:H7 using electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS). It was based on the immobilization of antibodies onto a monolayer of protein A on the quartz crystal Au electrode surface. A three-electrode system was utilized in the EIS measurement, and cyclic voltammetry (CV) was also used for further confirmation. The results indicated that the impedance was increased due to the immobilization of antibodies and the binding of E .coli O157:H7 cells onto the Au electrode surface, which was directly measured with EIS in the presence of [Fe(CN) 6 ]3 -/4- as a redox probe. The label-free immunosensor showed good selectivity to E. coli 0157:H7. While E. coli O157:H7 concentrations ranged from 1.6 X 10 3 to 1.6 X 10 6 cfu mL -1 , a linear relationship between Aimpedance (Z cells - Z antibody ) and the logarithmic value of E. coli 0157:H7 concentrations was found with a correlation coefficient of 0.98. The detection limit of the immunosensor can reach 10 3 cfu mL -1 .
