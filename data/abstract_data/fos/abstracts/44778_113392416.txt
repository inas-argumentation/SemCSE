Forming Process and Experimental Verification on Small Complex Parts
According to the structural characteristics of small complex parts and technical requirement,focusing on how to reduce production costs,improve work efficiency,and simplify the die structure,a set of the blanking,punching,bending and other processes consisting of stamping process plan was formulated,and then each process of die structure was designed.The final test proves that the use of this technology scheme and die structure can significantly improve efficiency product quality of the small complex parts production.
