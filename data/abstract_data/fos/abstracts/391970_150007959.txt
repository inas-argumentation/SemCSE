Chapter 6 concludes this part, with a discussion of the expansion of state forestry in the 1970s, and in opposition to this emergence, increasingly explicit calls for community control of forests for the benefit of rural people and for protection of forest ecosystems. This was the groundwork for the expansion of community forestry at the end of the twentieth century.
