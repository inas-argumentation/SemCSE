The Social Concern of Pedagogy Research since the Reform and Opening-up in China——Taking the Educational Papers in Xinhua Digest as an Example
Selecting the general catalogue in Xinhua Digest since 1981,it analyzed the influence and the social concern of the discipline of pedagogy.The study showed that there was obvious fluctuation from the perspective of the social concern,and that the educational papers in Xinhua Digest had attracted more social concern since 1997.The papers covered many major topics,but most of papers were concentrated on how to satisfy the social requirement and how to improve the social development,with the problem-centered characteristic.The theory consciousness and the discipline developmental consciousness of pedagogy researchers played an important effect on the social concern of pedagogy.To improve the social concern,it needs to think highly of the discipline construction of pedagogy.
