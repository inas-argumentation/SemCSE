Applied Value of Heparin in Fetal Growth Restriction
Objective To study the clinical significance of heparin for the treatment of fetal growth restriction (FGR). Methods 108 patients of FGR were randomly divided into two groups, study group (n=54) and control group(n=54). Low molecular dextran and heparin were used in the patients of study group, while low molecular dextran and tanshinone were used in the control group. The height of uteras, the length of femur,the parietal diameter and the biophysical score were compared between the two groups after the treatment.Results Compared with control group, the increase of height of uteras , length of femur and parietal diameter was significantly quick (P0.05), and the improvement of the biophysical score was more obvious(P0.05) in the study group.Conclusion Small dose herapin can be used in the treatment of FGR , and it is safe and effective.
