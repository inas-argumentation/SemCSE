Influence of Natural Enemies on Population of Wheat Aphid and Greenbug
Based on the grey system theory,the relationships between total predacious number of enemies and wheat aphid,and the relationships between enemies and their superior natural enemies were studied.The results showed that Sgrphidae,Propylaca japonica,Aphididae and Erigonidium graminicola were the natural enemies which greatly influenced the populations of wheat aphid and greenbug.
