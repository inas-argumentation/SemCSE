Measurement of spin-dependent total cross-section difference ΔσL in neutron-proton scattering at 16 MeV
A new measurement of ΔσL for longitudinally polarized neutrons transmitted through a polarized proton target at 16.2 MeV has been made. This complements our previous measurement of ΔσT at the same energy and after a phase-shift analysis improves the precision of the previous value of 3S1− 3D1 mixing parameter ε1. The measurement yields the value ΔσL = (− 55 ± 20 ± 7) mb. The result of a simple phase shift analysis for ε1 is presented and compared with the theoretical potential model predictions and other experiments.
