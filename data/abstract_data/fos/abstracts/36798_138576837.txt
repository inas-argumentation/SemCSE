Recrystallization Process of Alloy Ni5W
The recrystallization process of the alloy Ni5W was investigated. The results show that the hardness of the alloy baseband decreases with the increase of annealing temperature. The initial recrystallization temperature of the alloy Ni5W is around 700 ℃, and the motivation of the large-angle boundary results in the formation and growth of the recrystallization crystal nucleus. The cube crystal nucleus has a size advantage during the nucleation process. The average grain size of the baseband decreases in the following order: the surface, section surface and section core of the alloy Ni5W.
