Effects of Pouring Temperature on Interface Bonding Properties of Cu/Ni Water Jacket Material
A new type of Cu/Ni water jacket embedded pure nickel tube was prepared by sand casting.The interface bonding properties between the matrix and the embedded nickel tube at different pouring temperatures were mainly analyzed by OM,SEM,micro-hardness testing and shear strength testing,such as interface atomic diffusion,micro-hardness,bond strength,etc.The results show that the thickness of interface diffusion layer and micro-hardness increase with the increase of pouring temperature from 1 100 ℃ to 1 300 ℃.When the pouring temperature is as high as 1 300 ℃,there are many tiny pores attached to the surface of the nickel tube,which leads to the decrease of the interface bonding strength.The best interface shear strength about 162 MPa was obtained when the pouring temperature is 1 200 ℃.
