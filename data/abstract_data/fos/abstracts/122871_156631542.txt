Developmental and Regional Differences of Agricultural Export Trade in Anhui Province
Since China joined the WTO,agricultural export trade of Anhui Province has developed rapidly,but there are many obvious developmental and regional differences in the province.Facing the situation of economic growth expected to continue to slow down,agricultural export trade of Anhui Province need to improve the quality of development,enhance core competitiveness,and further explore and utilize advantageous resources.Therefore,this paper collates the export trade structure,comparative advantages and competitive advantages of Anhui agricultural products since China joined the WTO,and analyzes the regional trade differences with the location entropy index,revised Theil index and other measures.The results show that the export trade volume of Anhui agricultural products is at the middle level in the country,the number of products with comparative advantages is small,and the overall competitiveness of the products is weak.Finally,this paper suggests developing processing trade,opening up the path of connotative development for quality promotion and taking other measures to ensure a sound development of Anhui agricultural export trade.
