Experimental Study on Casting in Place Surface Hardening by V-EPC Process
In this paper the casting in place surface hardening process by V-EPC was studied on the base of experiments, the main influential process factors of compositing layers were found out, and the optimised process parameters had been proposed, then some other experiments were carried out on the base of initial experiments. The results show that, at the 1420 ℃ pouring temperature, the reinforced particles of SiC is in 0.4 mm grade and under the vacuum degree of 0.04 MPa, the thickness of compositing layers can reach up to 4 mm.
