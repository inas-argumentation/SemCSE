Antibacterial effect of the extract from Artemisia rupestris L.
The in vitro antimicrobial activities of water and ethanol extracts from Artemisia rupestris L.on ten bacteria were investigated.And the antibacterial active components were selected by the tube preliminary test.The results indicated that the water and ethanol extracts inhibited significantly the growth of a wide range of bacteria.The antimicrobial efficacy of absolute ethanol extracts from Artemisia rupestris L.is remarkable and its inhibitory effect on G+ is better than G-.A preliminary test of chemical constituents showed that its active components may be conmarins,glycoside and flavone,etc.
