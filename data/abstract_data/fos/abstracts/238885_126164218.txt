Floquet insulator of Proximitized Graphene with Pseudohelical Edge States
Strongly optical irradiation on graphene or graphene family 2D materials induces Floquet insulators with topological edge states. We study the Floquet insulator states of graphene on realistic substrates which induce staggered intrinsic spin-orbital coupling. With appropriate combination of optical frequency, amplitude, wavenumber and the spin-orbital coupling strength, the first order electron-photon coupling induces dynamical gap that surround the intrinsic Fermi level. Within the gap, the pseudohelical edge states at the zigzag edge remain protected and gapless. Additionally, the zigzag edge of semi-infinite graphene supports the valley-centered helical edge states within the bulk gap. For the zigzag nanoribbons with finite width, the valley-centered helical edge states are gapped out by the avoid band crossing due to finite size effect. Thus, the pseudohelical edge states and the Floquet side bands induced by higher order electron-photon coupling are the only conductive bands near to the intrinsic Fermi level. We studied the spin polarized conduction through the zigzag nanoribbons with finite irradiated section.
