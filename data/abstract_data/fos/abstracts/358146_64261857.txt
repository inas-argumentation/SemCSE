Efficient Hill-climbing Algorithm for Learning Bayesian Networks
This paper presents an efficient hill-climbing approach for learning Bayesian Networks,HCBest algorithm.When a circle emerges in the process of evolving networks,the algorithm delete the edge that can best improve the score of the learned network until there is no circle.The experiments show that,HCBest not only can be used as an independent approach for learning Bayesian networks,but also can be applied in other complicated meta heuristic approach as a local search algorithm.HCBest can achieve good quality network structure with higher metric score.In the aspects of simplicity and robustness,the algorithm also performs well.
