CLASSIFICATION OF 3-GRADED CAUSAL SUBALGEBRAS OF REAL SIMPLE LIE ALGEBRAS
Let ( g $$ \mathfrak{g} $$ , τ ) be a real simple symmetric Lie algebra and let W ⊂ g $$ \mathfrak{g} $$ be an invariant closed convex cone which is pointed and generating with τ ( W ) = − W . For elements h ∈ g $$ \mathfrak{g} $$ with τ ( h ) = h , we classify the Lie algebras g $$ \mathfrak{g} $$ ( W , τ , h ) which are generated by the closed convex cones C ± W τ h ≔ ± W ∩ g ± 1 − τ h $$ {C}_{\pm}\left(W,\tau, h\right):= \left(\pm W\right)\cap {\mathfrak{g}}_{\pm 1}^{-\tau }(h) $$ , where g ± 1 − τ h ≔ x ∈ g : τ x = − x h x = ± x $$ {\mathfrak{g}}_{\pm 1}^{-\tau }(h):= \left\{x\in \mathfrak{g}:\tau (x)=-x\left[h,x\right]=\pm x\right\} $$ . These cones occur naturally as the skew-symmetric parts of the Lie wedges of endomorphism semigroups of certain standard subspaces. We prove in particular that, if g $$ \mathfrak{g} $$ ( W , τ , h ) is non-trivial, then it is either a hermitian simple Lie algebra of tube type or a direct sum of two Lie algebras of this type. Moreover, we give for each hermitian simple Lie algebra and each equivalence class of involutive automorphisms τ of g $$ \mathfrak{g} $$ with τ ( W ) = − W a list of possible subalgebras g $$ \mathfrak{g} $$ ( W , τ , h ) up to isomorphy.
