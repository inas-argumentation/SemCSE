The Risk Control of Human Resources Out-sourcing for Small-and-medium-sized Enterprises
Under the background of economic globalization,the problem of SEM's Human Resources mangement had been a bottle-neck to restrict their development.With the increasingly fierce market competition,human resources outsourcing is becoming a strategic choice for SMEs as the business resources outsourcing is becoming more and more popular and the needs of constructing core capability in Enterprises are increasing.However,how to avoid control the risk during the process of HRO becomes a burning issue.
