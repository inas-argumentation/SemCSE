The identical bands in177Ta
The [402]5/2 bands in177Ta which are “identical” to the neighboring even-even176Hf groundstate band have been extended to higher angular momentum. These bands in the two nuclei are seen to diverge from each other in the region of the first i13/2 neutron alignment. The lower observed crossing frequency for the [402]5/2 bands indicates a lower deformation for these bands compared to176Hf. Extensions to theh9/2 [541]1/2 yrast band are also reported.
