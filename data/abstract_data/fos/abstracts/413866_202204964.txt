Optimization Design of Sprue Push in Low Pressure Casting Aluminum Alloy Used in Wheel Hub of Car
Through the experimental comparison, sprue pushes which made by metal happened deformation and wear after erosion of high temperature molten aluminum in the low pressure casting mold, and the temperature of molten aluminum has greatly effect on it, so it is difficult to adjust casting process. If ceramic were used to make sprue pushes, it will have advantages of high temperature resistance and corrosion resistance, positive effect in the process of feeding in casting solidification. The production efficiency and rate of finished products were increased significantly, but also the utilization rate of materials and service life of products were improved greatly.
