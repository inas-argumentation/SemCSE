Study on Electroless Plating Ni-P Alloy Surface Stabilizing Agent of Cast Iron
As Ni-P electroless plating solution has poor stabilization,larger consumption of nickel ion,the author investigated the stability of Na_2S_2O_3, Pb(AC)_2,stabilizing agent A and B,and the plating rate,stability of plating solution,coating porosity and erosion resistance were used as evaluating indicator,and the lactic acid was added as complexing agent.Experimental results indicate that compare to single stabilizing agent,compound stabilizing agent has higher plating rate,better stability and lower porosity.Plating solution and coating have the best performance when adding Pb(AC)_2 and stabilizing agent A to plating solution,and the stability of plating solution is up to 35 000 S,the plating rate is 30.1 μm/h,the coating porosity is 0.78/cm~2,the corrosion time is up to 856 h when the erosion resistance of coating was investigated by salt spray tests.
