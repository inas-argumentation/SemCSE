On the Relationship between Harmonious Urban Culture and Construction of Harmonious Cities
Culture is the basis of morality,and urban culture is a harmonious city's basis. Looking for the intersection of urban culture and urban construction would do good to improving the quality of urban people and enhancing a city's comprehensive competitiveness. The researches about the construction path of harmonious urban culture should reflect the unity of theories and practice,revisit the image of urban culture in a down-to-earth manner,and explore the cultural construction of a harmonious urban reform and development in multiple perspectives. The harmonious urban culture creates a compatible and open atmosphere of urban culture for the harmonious urban construction,so as to make the harmonious city be an ideal home for people living and working in it.
