Application of Finite Element Simulation in Changing Streamline of Forging
The numerical simulation and finite element technology about forging forming obtains universal application overseas in recent years. The Chinese forging domain is also attempting to apply this technology to guide production development and optimize design. Based on the factors of influencing forging performance, using the Qform forging simulation software, the optimization plan for the forging die design was proposed. Thus, the forging streamline is changed, the forging performance improves, the die design time reduces greatly, the experimental times and experimental cost reduces.
