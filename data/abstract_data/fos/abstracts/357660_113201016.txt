Reformation and Exploration on Software Engineering Practice Curriculum Based on CDIO
Software Engineering is a core curriculum at professional high grade stage in computer and related majors.It emphasizes on the ability's comprehensive training and system upgrade for students' software development and engineering practice.Based on the reality of the situation of the Xinjiang University Software Engineering curriculum setting and implementation,in accordance with the CDIO engineering education concept,constructs the "three stage" ladder model,hierarchical software engineering practice teaching content system,forms a variety of teaching methods,the training of engineering practice ability as the core,a multi-dimensional teaching quality monitoring and evaluation system for the protection of practice teaching reformation,and gradually forms a "student" as the center of Engineering practice teaching mode of Software Engineering,and strives to improve the overall teaching level and the quality of student learning.
