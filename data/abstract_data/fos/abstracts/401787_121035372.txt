Harmonic generation in coherent two-photon interaction between high-power optical pulses and resonant media
A theoretical investigation is made of collinear propagation of high-power optical pulses through a medium under conditions of two-photon resonance ω0±ωs≈ω21 (ω0 and ωs are the pulse frequencies, ω21 is the frequency of an atomic transition in the medium). It is assumed that the condition for coherent interaction τ0, τs < T2 is satisfied (τ0 and τs are the pulse durations at the entrance, T2 is the phase relaxation time of the medium). By means of numerical integration of equations for the fields and the medium, it is shown that efficient generation of the harmonics ω1 = 2ω0 ± ωs, ω2 = 2ωs ± ω0 is possible under short and ultrashort pulse conditions.
