Engineering Application of Fugitive Gas Desulphurization with Activated Coke in Copper Smelting Process,Guixi Smelter
The paper has introduced the principle and process flow for dry process of flue gas desulphurization with activated coke and elaborated equipment scale-up and equipment design and operation.The results show that the industrial demonstration unit constructed is stable in operation,simple for operators,highly automated,and highly efficient in desulphurization and sulphur resources reutilization,realizing integrated functions of water-saving,desulphurization and sulfur recovery,as well as good economic benefits.Therefore,it is suitable for large-scale industrial promotion and industrialized development.
