A Theoretical Discussion and Value Analysis of Consumer's Right to Know
Consumer's right to know stipulates that consumers have the right to require the sellers to inform them of the real information of purchases or use of a commodity or a service,and they also have the right to require the state organs or public organizations to provide consumer information.The content of consumer's right to know include the right to know and the right of being told,and the object is the true,overall,appropriate and effective consumer information.The main body of obligation of consumer's right to know includes sellers,government,consumer organizations and public media.Consumer's right to know,a kind of right stipulated in economic law,is very valuable in protecting the consumer interests,the healthy development of market economy and the equity and integrity of the society.
