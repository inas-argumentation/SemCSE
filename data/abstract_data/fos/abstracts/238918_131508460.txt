Investigation on resources of ancient and famous trees in Jiuhua Mountain Scenic Resort of Anhui Province
The comprehensive and systematic investigation on ancient and famous trees in Jiuhua Mountain was first done by GPS,increment borer,steel tape,ribbon tape and other measuring equipments in November,2006.The tree species,quantity,distribution,growth condition,age and grade were studied,and their scientific and utilization value,protection and management were discussed.The results showed that there are 360 ancient and famous trees,belonging to 33 species,29 genera,22 families;there are 7 famous trees,belonging to 3 species,3 genera,3 families;and there is an ancient community which is climax community of Cyclobalanopsis myrsinaefolia in Jiuhua Mountain Scenic Resort.
