THE CASE FOR UNIDROIT
SUBTITLE: AN INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON UNIDROIT WILL CONVENE IN SOUTH AFRICA NEXT MONTH. THATCHER STONE, PARTNER AT ROSENMAN & COLIN, ARGUES THAT RATIFICATION OF UNDROIT (THE UNIDROIT CONVENTION ON INTERNATIONAL INTERESTS IN MOBILE EQUIPMENT AND THE AIRCRAFT PROTOCOL) WILL LEAD TO A MORE TANSPARENT FRAMEWORK FOR CROSS-BORDER AIRCRAFT FINANCING.
