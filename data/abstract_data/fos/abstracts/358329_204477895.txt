The hope for imminent victory was omnipresent in the pupils’ enigmas. Second, the author proposes a new approach. Traditional historiography tends to highlight an opposition between elaborate royalist pictures and naïve, emotion-based pictures by the Catholic League. However, as Buttay shows us, the Jesuit works were far from being simplistic, so it is more appropriate to distinguish “between narrative pictures and pictures termed as enigmatic” (330). This is a very important book, both for the Jesuit enigmas that have now been decoded and made accessible and for new directions of research concerning militant education during the early modern period.
