DIRECT SPECTROPHOTMETRIC DETERMINATION OF THE TOTAL AMOUNTS OF RARE EARTHS IN IRON AND STEEL USING p-AMINOHIPPURIC ACID CHLOROPHOSPHONAZO
In a medium of 0.1N HCl p-aminohippuric acid chlorophosphonazo reacts at once with rare earths and a stable blue complex is formed. Absorbance ma ximum are measured at about 680nm. The molar absorptivities(La-Lu, Y)are 7.27×10~(-4)-10.22×10~4·mol~(-1)·cm~(-1). Beer＇s law is obeyed over the range of 0—20 μg RE/25 ml. EDTA is used as the main masking reagent to prevent the interferences from matrix iron and other ions. Ti and Nb are masked with mandelic acid plus H_2O_2, and tartaric acid respectively. The method is simple, rapid, accurate and reliable, and can be used to determine the total amounts of RE in various irons and steels.
