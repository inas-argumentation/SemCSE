Measurements of Dissolved Organic Nitrogen with Electrodialysis Pretreatment
When the DIN/TDN ratio of water sam ple is larger than 0.6, the quantification of dissolved organic nitrogen(DON) will be greatly affected. This paper investigate the accuracy of the DON determination after using the electrodialysis pretreatment to remove the inorganic nitrogen ions. The results show that DOC loss was lower when using the CJM-2 type ion exchange membrane and the DOC loss decreased in the samples after the appropriate acidification. For example, the DOC loss of Yangtze River water sample was 5.3%. At the optimal operating conditions, in 40 minutes, the ratio of desalinization of the synthetic water sample can reach 97%. In 15 minutes, the DIN/TDN ratio of the water sample was less than 0.6, the determination of the concentration of DON when measured with and without electrodialysis pretreatment were compared after electrodialysis, the measured values were closer to the real values, and the standard deviation(n = 4)was decreased from 18.65% to 3.25%.
