Discussions on Maximum Impact Distance of Non-coal Mine Tailings Dam Failure
The maximum impact distance of tailings dam failure is estimated by 40 times or 80 times of dam height(40H,80H) in tailings safety pre-assessment reports and basic design documents of some construction projects.But through our analysis on relevant documents of the state safety and supervision bureau and the comparison between the actually measured maximum distance in dam failure events and the value estimated with the said method,it is concluded that it has no legal basis to use this method and this method does not conform to the actual situation of tailings dam failure.
