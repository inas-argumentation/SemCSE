Potentiometric sensors based on mixed ionic-electronic conductors instead of solid electrolytes
Abstract A new method is suggested for determining chemical potential differences by potentiometric measurements. It uses mixed ionic-electronic conductors, MIECs, instead of solid electrolytes. The galvanic cell includes the sample, a reference electrode, two MIECs and a power supply. The procedure of measurement is different from the usual potentiometric one. Sources of various errors and their effects are analyzed. The method should overcome another problem in the existing potentiometric method, namely, that of material permeation by ambipolar motion through the solid electrolyte during prolonged measurements.
