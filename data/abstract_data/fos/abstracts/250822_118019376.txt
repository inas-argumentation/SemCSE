BRIEF COMMUNICATIONS: Mechanism of enhancement of light scattering during optical recording in As2S3 films
An experimental study was made of the kinetics of light scattering during exposure of films As2S3 to monochromatic and white light. It was established that the experimentally observed considerable enhancement of light scattering was due to the coherence of light. Hie maximum enhancement depended on the initial degree of scattering and on the film thickness. The surface layer of a film acted as the source of the strong initial scattering in the case of films whose thickness exceeded 7 μ.
