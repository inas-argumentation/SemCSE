Making technics of capsule from cactus polysaccharide
Orthogonal test were performed to extract polysaccharides from extract cactus juice,and the polysaccharides extracted were prepared to polysaccharides capsules.The experimental results showed that 5 times concentrated cactus juice,a mixture of ethanol-cactus juice(1:3) and temperature of-18℃ were optimal for highest yield.The method of preparing polysaccharides capsules were as follows:6%～8% HPMC were dissolved into 60% ethanol as coating fluid,coating at 35～40℃,filling the capsules after drying for 2h.
