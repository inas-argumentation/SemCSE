Effect of CtpA protein encoded by at3g57680 gene on the function of photosystem II in Arabidopsis thaliana
Electron microscopy technique,Northern blot,Blue native gel,SDS-PAGE gel and Western blot technique were used to analyse at3g57680 gene knocked mutant and wild plant of Arabidopsis thaliana.The results were as the follows:(1) The chloroplast in mutant plants and wild type plants were all in flat shape and had similar size;the granas and matrixs of chloroplast were uniformly distributed;the number of granum-thylakoids staek were similar in the mutant plants and the wild type plants.(2) The transcript levels of psbA psbB,psbC and psbO were the same in the mutant and wild type plants.(3) The composition and expression of thylakoid membrane-protein complexes and their corresponding sub-units were similar between the mutant plants and the wild type plants.Furthermore,the expression of D1 protein also remained consistent.Therefore,the CtpA homologue protein encoded by at3g57680 gene has no effect on the chloroplast microscopic structure,D1 protein maturity modification,photosystem II protein complexes and its subunits in Arabidopsis thaliana under normal growth condition.
