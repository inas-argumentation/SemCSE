Influence on the stimulated-emission cross section of Nd~(3+) concentration in laser glasses
Six types of Nd~(3+) laser glasses of N31 phosphate laser glasses are experimented and analyzed by Judd-Ofelt model. the experiment result shows that when the Nd~(3+) concentrations increases, the stimulated-emission cross section of the Nd~(3+) laser glasses decreases. therefore, the influence of Nd~(3+) should be considered in laser system design.
