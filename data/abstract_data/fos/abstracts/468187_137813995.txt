Effect of V Addition on Performance of High Cr Cast Iron Lining Board
In order to improve the brittleness of high chrome cast iron, the modification was carried out using ferro-vandium.The influences of V content on hardness, impact toughness and wear resistance of high chrome cast iron were studied.The results show that the hardness of high chrome cast iron increases with the increase of V content and the impact toughness firstly increases and then decreases.The impact toughness is highest when V content is 0.8wt%.The wear resistance changes in accord with impact toughness.
