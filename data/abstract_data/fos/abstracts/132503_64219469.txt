Highly Scalable Video Codec Based on RDWT-Subband Motion Compensation
With development of technology, video codec demands not only the compression efficiency, but also other important functionalities, such as scalabilities. This paper presents a highly scalable video codec based on RDWT (Redundant Discrete Wavelet Transform)-Subband motion compensation, which first spatially decomposes the input video group of frames, then in motion compensation temporal filtering uses all phase RDWT subbands as candidates and selects one best-matching RDWT subband as references to improve the efficiency of motion compensation. In order to keep the scalabilities of wavelet coefficients the codec also utilizes an amended 3D SPIHT method with new scan order. The experimental results prove that the codec can produce encoded stream with higher PSNR and highly scalable functions.
