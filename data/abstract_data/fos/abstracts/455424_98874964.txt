Study on extraction conditions of flavonoids and alkaloids from mistletoe
To study the extracting technology on flavonoids and alkaloids from mistletoe by ultrasonic.The results showed that the optimal conditions was as follows: extracting agent 80% ethanol,extracting 15min,MTS ratio 1:40,the extraction rate of flavonoids is 1.37%;extracting agent 1.5%,extracting 30min,MTS ratio 1:30,the extraction rate of alkaloids is 1.73%.
