Mesoionic compounds with a bridged nitrogen atom. 4. Thiazolo[3, 2-a]quinolinium 1-oxide derivatives
It is shown that diverse 2-substituted thiazolo[3,2-a]quinolinium 1-oxides can be obtained from 2-quinolylmercaptoacetic acids. The structures of the synthesized compounds were confirmed by the PMR and mass spectra.
