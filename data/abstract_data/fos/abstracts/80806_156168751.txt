Innovation in Financial Statistics Education from View of Evolution Tendency of Statistics
To make our student qualified for the 21st century, we must have advanced methods of education, especially in financial statistics education. In this article, several crucial problems are put forward in financial statistics education based on evolution tendency of statistics, all of which need to be improved as soon as possible. The problems are being analyzed from four aspects, including teaching goals, teaching methods, teaching strategies and examinations, proposals are also carried out to improve education quality.
