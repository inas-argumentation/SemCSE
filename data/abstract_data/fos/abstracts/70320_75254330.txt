Study on association between polymorphism of HLA classI and classII genes and Behcet′s disease
Objective To explore the predisposing role of HLA classⅠ and classⅡ genes in Behcet′s disease(BD). Methods HLA classⅠ and classⅡ genes were studied by LABType~ TM SSO in 40 patients with BD and 100 healthy controls. Results The gene frequencies of HLA-B*51(30% vs 12%, χ~2=5.312, OR= 3.143 , P0.05) and HLA-DRB1*14(22.5% vs 8%, χ~2=4.354, OR=3.339, P0.05) were significantly higher in BD patients than those in controls, while gene frequency of HLA-DRB1*15(10% vs 30%, χ~2= 5.175 , OR=0.259, P0.05) was markedly lower in BD patients. Conclusion The results suggest that HLA-B*51 and HLA-DRB1*14 might be the susceptible genes of BD patients, while HLA-DRB1*15 might be the protective gene of BD patients.
