Effect of Processing Parameters on Electrospark Strengthened Layer on White Cast Iron
The electrospark strengthened processing was used to treat white cast iron, the effects of capacity and specific strengthening time on the electrospark strengthened layer on white cast iron were analysed. The results show that the surface roughness of the electrospark strengthened layer increases with the increase of capacity value (between 30 μF and 300 μF) all the time, but the thickness and microhardness increase initially and become stable gradually later. By using the same capacitance value, the surface roughness and microhardness of the electrospark strengthened layer don't increase with the increase of specific strengthening time, but they have slightly changes. Furthermore, in the process of the electrospark strengthening, the electrospark strengthened layers with the better surface quality and the higher thickness and microhardness are gained through the re-strengthening process.
