Effect of Compound Modification on Morphology of Inclusions and Property of Wear-resistant Low Alloy Cast Steel
The influence of compound modification(RE-Si-Ca-Mg) on the inclusions morphology and property of dual phase wear-resistant cast steel was investigated.The results show that compound modification can influence notably the morphology and distribution of inclusions in dual phase wear-resistant cast steel,which can greatly improve the impact toughness of the steel.
