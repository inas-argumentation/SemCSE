Non-blocking Bidirectional Optical Network-on-chip Via Component Sharing
In Network-on-Chip(NoC),the transmission via photons,instead of electrons,offer inherent advantages in terms of higher speed and lower power consumption.It introduces new on-chip architecture,namely Optical NoC(ONoC).In this paper,we propose a new bidirectional wavelength routing router architecture,which routes the optical signals by their wavelength and allows simultaneous bidirectional transmission by shared ports and channels.With extensive analyses,we prove that the proposal is practical and can be efficiently realized with current CMOS technology.Compared to traditional optical networks,the proposed router architecture dramatically increases utilization rate of optical network and decreases the hardware consumption and shorten the transmission depth of light by fully utilizing bidirectional wavelength routing method and the wavelength division multiplexing(WDM) technique.
