Systematical investigation on the stability of doubly heavy tetraquark states
We systematically investigate the stability of the doubly heavy tetraquark states $$[Q_1Q_2][\bar{q}_3\bar{q}_4]$$ [ Q 1 Q 2 ] [ q ¯ 3 q ¯ 4 ] ( $$Q=c$$ Q = c and b , $$q=u$$ q = u , d and s ) within the framework of the alternative color flux-tube model involving a multi-body confinement potential, $$\sigma $$ σ -exchange, one-gluon exchange and one-Goldstone-boson-exchange interactions. Our numerical analysis indicates that the states $$[bb][\bar{u}\bar{d}]$$ [ b b ] [ u ¯ d ¯ ] with $$01^+$$ 01 + and $$[bb][\bar{u}\bar{s}]$$ [ b b ] [ u ¯ s ¯ ] with $$1^+$$ 1 + are the most promising stable states against the strong interaction. The states $$[cc][\bar{u}\bar{d}]$$ [ c c ] [ u ¯ d ¯ ] with $$01^+$$ 01 + , $$[bc][\bar{u}\bar{d}]$$ [ b c ] [ u ¯ d ¯ ] with $$00^+$$ 00 + and $$01^+$$ 01 + , and $$[bb][\bar{u}\bar{d}]$$ [ b b ] [ u ¯ d ¯ ] with $$01^-$$ 01 - as stable states are also predicted in the model. The dynamical mechanism producing those stable states with tetrahedral structure are discussed in the model. The states $$[bb][\bar{u}\bar{d}]$$ [ b b ] [ u ¯ d ¯ ] and $$[bc][\bar{u}\bar{d}]$$ [ b c ] [ u ¯ d ¯ ] with $$IJ^P=12^+$$ I J P = 12 + would not be stable although they are, respectively, below the $$\bar{B}^*\bar{B}^*$$ B ¯ ∗ B ¯ ∗ and $$D^*\bar{B}^*$$ D ∗ B ¯ ∗ thresholds because they can decay into D-wave $$\bar{B}\bar{B}$$ B ¯ B ¯ and $$D\bar{B}$$ D B ¯ through the strong interaction. The states should have narrow widths if they really exist as our model prediction.
