Effect of Reduced and Delayed Fertilization on Growth and Yield Formation in Rice
Using nitrogen strategies to increase yield and reduce fertilizer pollution to environment in Hainan was studied.The cultivar of Teyou669(indica)was used to study the effect of reduced and delayed fertilization and local fertilization on the growth and yield in rice.The results indicated that rice using reduced and delayed fertilization was fewer tillering,dwarfer plant,smaller leaf area and less dry matter accumulation at tillering stage and booting stage.Leaf area decreased slowly and dry matter accumulated fast at productive phase.High panicle bearing tiller rate and seed setting rate compensated few tillering.The reduced and delayed fertilization could obtain higher yield than the local fertilization.
