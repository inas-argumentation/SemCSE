Convergence radii for eigenvalues of two-parameter Sturm-Liouville problems
The n th eigenvalue μ = μ„(Α) of the differential equation —y" + q(x)y = (μ + A r ( x ) ) y subject to self-adjoint boundary conditions admits a power series expansion in powers of A for sufficiently small |λ|. It is proved that the convergence radii of these series grow at least like η log η as η tends to infinity if q is integrable and r is of bounded variation. It is shown by an example that the growth rate of η log η cannot be improved without imposing stronger smoothness conditions on r. 1991 Mathematics Subject Classification: 34 Β 24, 33 E 10
