Apoptosis of human premyelotic leukemia cells induced by an extract of Tripterygium Wilfordii Hook F in vitro
Objective To investigate the effect of an extract of Tripterygium Wilfordii Hook F (TWH) on inducing apoptosis of human premyelotic leukemia cells(HL 60). Methods HL 60 cells were cultured with TWH and prednisone in a different concentration, the changes of morphology, cell cycle and DNA ladder pattern by electrophoresis were analyzed. Results Typical morphologic changes such as cell blebbing, cytoplasmic and nuclear condensation and apoptotic bodies were found in HL 60 cells cultured with TWH; This apoptosis inducing effect was stronger on cells at proliferative stage and was time and dose dependent. Prednisone is found less effective on inducing apoptosis of HL 60 cells than TWH; There was also no synergistic effect between TWH and prednisone. Conclusions TWH possesses apoptosis inducing effect on HL 60 cells. This effect of TWH is probably related to its immunosuppressive and clinical therapeutic effect on autoimmune diseases and its side effects.
