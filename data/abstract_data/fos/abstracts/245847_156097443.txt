On the American Subprime Mortgage Crisis and its Warning and Hint to China
The article expatiates on the present American subprime mortgage crisis which began in the early 2007,analyses its coming cause and also makes some comments on the dealing tactics from US and the world over.This paper mainly introduces the reaction and affection from China.Figures show that the present American subprime mortgage crisis growing more and more serious but the increasing stock holding in a large scale in China is really hard to understand.The impact of such crisis to China is embodied in the shrinking value on the held stocks of American subprime lending,the sharp influence of American dollar depreciation to China's exporting business,and the great pressure caused by weak American dollars to China's inflation.The paper relates that the main warnings and hints from the present American subprime mortgage crisis are as follows:we should increase the mortgage quality and prevent the overheating property sector and establish grade-divided credit system and push forward steadily the mortgage asset-securitization.
