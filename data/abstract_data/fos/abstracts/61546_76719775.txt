Creating of lala band calisthenics form and the research of its aerobic exercise prescription
The aim is to try to utilize the properties of "pull and elasticity" to establish a popular calisthenics exercise form. The LaLa Band's rebound is used to regulate partial body's load and increase effective chest expanding so that muscles can operate alternate active and passive action for the purpose to increase the stimulating frequency of the ex- ercise to chest muscle mass. The exercise intensity is supervised by the HR measured by the Polar Advantage NV (produced by Finland), the target heart rate of the whole set of exercises is from 70% to 80% of the maximum heart rate（HR max), which equals to 70% VO2max.
