The "unusual " isotope shift in high-temperature superconductors can be explained by the usual theory of the electron-phonon interaction
J. W. Goethe-Universitt Frankfurt am Main, Theoretische Physik,Robert-Mayer-Str. 8, 60054 Frankfurt/Main, Germany(Dated: February 2, 2008)We show that recent ARPES results on the ”unusual” oxygen isotope shift in the real part of theself-energy in the optimally doped Bi2212 samples [1] can be qualitatively (and semi-quantitatively)explained by the theory of the electron-phonon interaction (EPI) elaborated few decades ago. How-ever, for a quantitative analysis of the ARPES spectra it is necessary to know the momentumdependence of the EPI, the Coulomb contribution at high energies and the background due toimpurities and defects.
