Isolation and Identification of Bacillus thuringiensis Strains from Coconut Grove of Seaside,Hainan Province
One hundred and forty-four soil samples were collected from coconut crove of seaside,Hainan Province,and 10 Bacillus thuringiensis isolates were obtained.We found there were long bipyramidal crystal,short bipyramidal crystal,square crystal types in the 10 Bt isolates.Comparison of plasmid DNA patterns by agarose gels electrophoresis was used to discriminate the types of the isolates,and the diversity of these isolates was prominent.cry1,cry1I and cry2 type genes were found in these isolates by PCR-RFLP method,but the cry gene-types of three isolates BH10,BH17,and BH21 were not known.SDS-PAGE results showed that the 10 wild isolates could express Cry proteins with 130 kD molecular mass,and 2 isolates could express both 130 kD and 60 kD protein.Bioassay results of 10 isolates indicated that six of them were toxic to Brontispa longissina.
