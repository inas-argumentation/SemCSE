Design of Car Equipment of Driver Training Management System Based on Fingerprinting and IC Card
Provides the project of driver training management s ystem on-board equipment design based on fingerprinting and IC card.The system's core is microprocessor SPCE061A which control the management of driver training.The system gets trainee's fingerprint by FDA01 based on optical fingerprint recognition and establishes identity by comparing the fingerprint stored in IC card.Static information and dynamic information produced in training will be stored in IC card.The design puts forward a efficacious solution to guarantee the correctness and accuracy of information of driver training management.
