Qualitative Effects of Radiation Damage on Equipment and Components at the R.P.I. Linac
The R.P.I. Linac was designed and has performed as a high powered accelerator. It was tested in excess of 50 kw of average power at 50 Mev. The shielding and facilities1 were designed for operation with such an accelerator. The question of the selection of many small components and equipment to operate in such an environment was and still is quite difficult. Usually radiation resistant devices are either not existant or very expensive. We chose the pragmatic approach by saying, we would choose the best known device to satisfy the particular function within the usual bounds of radiation resistance2, not particularly exotic items and make the replacement rather easy when failure occurs.
