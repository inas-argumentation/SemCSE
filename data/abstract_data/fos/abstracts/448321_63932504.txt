Protocol Analysis of Agent-Based Approaches Towards Mobile Multicast
In this paper, a survey on mobile multicast protocols based on Multicast Agent (MA ) is given. Then it provides a comprehensive analysis of existing mobile multicast protocols based on MA from the view of Architecture, Group Management Packet Delivery and Handover, also the advantages and shortcomings or problems are anatomized in depth,and then a comprehensive comparition is given. In the end, an indepth summary and analysis on multicast based on MA is given, and the future trend and emphasis are discussed.
