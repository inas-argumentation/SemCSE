Cloning and Function Analysis of 5' Regulatory Region for HbSRPP from Hevea brasiliensis
In rubber tree,small rubber particle protein(SRPP) is a key protein involved in rubber biosynthesis.The 5' regulatory region of HbSRPP from Hevea brasiliensis was cloned using GenomeWalker strategy.TATA box,CAAT box and other core configurations were found in this promoter region.Several sequences similar to eukaryotic cis-regulatory elements were found in the 5'-UTR proximal 5' flanking sequence of HbSRPP.The plant expression vector with 5' regulatory region of HbSRPP was constructed and transgenic tobacco plants were obtained by Agrobacterium-mediated transformation.GUS activity analysis revealed that the fragment of 5' regulatory region of HbSRPP driveed expression of the GUS gene transgenic tobacco plants.
