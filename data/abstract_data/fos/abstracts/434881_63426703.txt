Segmentation of Video Object Plane ConsideringShot Break and Object Disappearance
Segmentation of semantic video object plane (VOP) through video sequence is a key to standard MPEG-4 withcontent-based video coding. This paper analyses weakness of segmentation approach in T.Meier's paper,whichapproaches based in Morphological Motion Filter and Change Detection Mask can't detect independent motioncomponent (IMC) when shot breaks, and match of Hausdorff distance is invalid when object disappears. Thispaper has overcome the weakness by detection of shot break and object disappearance. The approach of shotbreak is simple, computationally cheap and robust. Disappearance of object is decided by nonexistence of slowchange component.
