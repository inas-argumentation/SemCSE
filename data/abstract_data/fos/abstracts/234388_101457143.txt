Adsorption of aromatic compounds of multicomponent system on the sediments of the Yellow River
Baotou Channel of the Yellow River has been heavily polluted by industrial discharges and aromatic compounds pollutants are found in the water body.In present paper,adsorption of aromatic compounds on sediments sampled from upward reaches of Baotou Channel the Yellow River was studied.The factors that may affect the adsorption of three aromatic compounds in multicomponent system,i.e.,phenol,aniline and chlorobenzene,were also investigated.The results show that the isotherms of phenol,aniline or chlorobenzene are all nonlinear and adsorption isotherms are dependent on the concentration of adsorbents.The absorption amounts of aromatic compounds by surface sediments are influenced by pH, ionic strength and content of organic matter in sediments.
