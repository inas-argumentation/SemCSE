Finite Element Simulation on Ship Plate Steel in Rolling Process
In order to acquire the Zener-Hollomon parametric equation,the ship plate steel under different deformation conditions was researched by using a Gleeble-3500 thermo-mechanical simulator.The commercially available FE analysis software DEFORM-3D was used to simulate the multi-pass hot rolling process based on actual parameters of production line.The results show that both the simulated multi-pass rolling force and temperature field of the steel were approximate to the measured values in the two producing processes.So the simulation results prove the accuracy and the validity of hot rolling model,and which can provide an important reference to establish rolling process parameters in factory.
