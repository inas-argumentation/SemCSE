PHOTOCHEMICAL REACTION OF 3-HEXENE-2,5-DIONE WITH TETRAMETHYLETHYLENE: STEREOSPECIFIC SYNTHESIS OF TRANS-1,2-DIACETYL-3,3,4,4-TETRAMETHYLCYCLOBUTANE
Photochemical reaction of 3-hexene-2,5-dione (1) with tetramethylethylene has been found to lead to stereospecific formation of trans-1,2-diacetyl-3,3,4,4-tetramethylcyclobutane. No evidence for oxetane formation has been obtained.
