Study on Feasibility of Flow-pushing SBR
The flow-pushing SBR device is designed on the basis of the partial improvement of SBR structure,appealing to the demand of domestic sewage treatment in our country's small and medium cities.According to experiments,the condition of operating the flow-pushing SBR is exploited and the efficiency of the organic matter degradation is proved.Meanwhile,the effectiveness of the nitrogen and phosphorus removal is also testified.The results indicate that the flow-pushing SBR achieves the objective of the elimination of NH3-N and TP,with the highest removal efficiency of 92% and 98.76% respectively.Additionally,the removal ratio of TN is 73%,COD is 90%.The above progress is attributed to the special operating mode of the modified SBR technique.
