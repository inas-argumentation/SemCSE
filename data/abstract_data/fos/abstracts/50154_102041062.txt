K_xNa_((1-x))NbO_3 Powders Synthesized by Microwave-hydrothermal
Using Nb2O5,NaOH and KOH as raw materials,KxNa(1-x)NbO3 powders were microwave-hydrothermal synthesized.The influences of nKOH/nNaOH and synthesized temperature on the morphology and composition of the powders were studied.The results show that the composition is closed to the phase boundary(MPB) when molar ratio of K+ / Na+ in solution precursor is 5 ∶ 1.The crystal plane spacing is shifted towards left with the increase of molar ratio of K+/ Na+.
