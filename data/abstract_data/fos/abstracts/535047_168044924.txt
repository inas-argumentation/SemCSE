Some Relevant Problems of Independent Directors Composite in Listed Companies
The main characteristic of independent directors is most of them are college teachers and specialists with same majors, good education and good titles. But they are lack of business experiences, attendance, independence and diversification. The undeveloped manager market, unwillingness of listed companies for directors working, the relation between privates, well - known people 's effect and the desire of good reputation are the causes. It is urgent that right persons can select the appropriate persons as the independent director and the proper motivation and restriction system should be established.
