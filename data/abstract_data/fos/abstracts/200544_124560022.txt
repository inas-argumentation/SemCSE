THE POLOIDAL TOROIDAL DECOMPOSITION OF DIFFERENTIAL FORMS
The poloidal-toroidal decomposition for vector fields in spherically symmetric domains in R is extended to differential forms of arbitrary degree q in arbitrary dimensions. The decompositions are derived both in the context of distributions and in the context of ZZ-Sobolev-spaces. MOS classifications: Primary 46E30 , Secondary 46E35 , 35Q60 , 35Q35 , 78A30
