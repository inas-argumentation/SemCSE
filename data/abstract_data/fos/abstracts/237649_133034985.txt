Ecological Jiangsu Construction and Environmental Law Perfection
Since 1996,Jiangsu has strengthened the environmental legal system construction.Provincial people's congress has promulgated "Water prevention and cure byelaw of Taihu Lake in Jiangsu province".Provincial committee and government have taken the environmental problems as an assessment standard of governors,and proposed the ecological construction goals of Jiangsu province.In this paper,it systematically discussed the inner relationship between ecological Jiangsu construction and environmental law perfection and the international experiences of environmental legal system construction.The problems are analyzed in the light of the present situations of ecological construction in Jiangsu.The corresponding strategy is proposed in the end.
