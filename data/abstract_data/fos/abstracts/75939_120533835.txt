Population inversion of the vibrational levels of N2O behind a shock wave front
Calculations have demonstrated the possible establishment of a population inversion due to the 040–0001 and 050–0001 vibrational transitions in the N2O molecule behind a shock wavefront propagating through a mixture of N2O, N2, and He gases. The absolute values of the inversion attain ~1015 cm−3 although its zone of existence is generally narrower than one centimeter. A rough estimate of the gain due to these transitions yields (1–4)×10−2 m−1.
