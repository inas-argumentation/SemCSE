An Analysis of the Academic Influence of Editorial Friend——Based on CSSCI Data from 2003 to 2008
Taking advantage of CSSCI database,the paper analyzes the impact factor,authors,papers of Editorial Friend which were published between 2003 and 2008.It is found that the papers are of high quality,the citation of the periodical is extensive and has distinguishing characteristics,and the continuity and stability of the papers are very good.Meanwhile,the periodical has stable author group.It is a high quality periodical in the field of editing and publishing field.
