Integrated criteria of gamma-ray burst spectral hardness
Most gamma-ray burst (GRB) spectra are well described by Band’s model with the parameters α, β (spectral indices in the low and high energy bands) and Epeak (energy of a spectral peak). GRBs (gamma-ray bursts) have been observed in different energy ranges. In some of them, the β parameter describes the spectral shape in regions of up to several hundreds of MeV or covering several orders of magnitude (e.g., GRB100724B and GRB080916C, respectively). In addition, spectral hardness parameter H32 (the ratio of total counts in the 100–300 keV and 50–100 keV energy range), which was used earlier for classifying events as hard and soft, and for categorizing different GRBs groups based on their hardness and duration distributions (the subgroup of intermediate bursts), fails to provide a complete description of all spectral properties in events with Epeak > 300 keV. In this work, we introduce new integrated spectral criteria that can be used in a wide range of energies for data analysis. These criteria and the spectral parameters of gamma-ray bursts, taken from BATSE and Fermi catalogues, are analyzed. The results obtained via data processing show that the new criteria allow GRB classifications that include discrimination of intermediate burst subgroups.
