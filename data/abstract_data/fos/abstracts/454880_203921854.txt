Determination of 11 Kinds of Ether Herbicide Residues in Vegetables by Gas Chromatography-Negative Chemical Ionization Mass Spectrometry
An offline solid phase extraction(SPE)-gas chromatography(GC)-negative chemical ionization mass spectrometric(NCI/MS) method was proposed for analyzing 11 kinds of ether herbicide residues in vegetables.Herbicide residues were extracted from vegetables sample with acetonitrile,then the extract was cleaned up by Envi-Carb SPE column connected to Alumina Neutral SPE column.Analytical screening was determined by gas chromatography-negative chemical ionization mass spectrometry-selected ion monitoring,and quantitative with external standard method.Linearity of all herbicides is good in the range of 10 to 200 μg/L,and the LOQs were under or equal to 1 μg/kg.The results gained from method were accurate and stable,and the recoveries of all herbicides were 70%-120% at four spiked level,4,10,20 and 40 μg/kg,and the relative standard deviations were below 10.3%.The method is reliable and stable,and is so selective that there are no interferences in determination of ether herbicides residues in all kinds of routine vegetables.
