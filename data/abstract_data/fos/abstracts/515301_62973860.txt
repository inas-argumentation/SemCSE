Simple Discussion on Network Teaching of Computer Basic
Analyzes the defects such as large differences of students,low rate of resource sharing and lack of teachers in the teaching of Computer Basic.Describes the characteristics,advantages and major teaching of network teaching in details and proposes that the network teaching model is a useful supplement for traditional teaching mode.So that the self-learning of students and interactive teaching by teachers and student the truly be unified,and provides an idea for the teaching reformation of Computer Basic course for the university.
