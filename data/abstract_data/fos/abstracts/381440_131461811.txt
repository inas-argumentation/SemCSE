The Research of LPG/Diesel Dual-fuel Engine of Bus
In order to attain the automobile emission standard of the P.R.China,the diesel buses' fuel systems have been converted to dual-fuel system.The constructions and characteristics of the mechani-cal and electrical control type have been discussed.Before and after the conversion,a YC6108Q engine has been tested on an engine test bench,the mixing ratio and working relationship map of the LPG/D has been got.During control process,the system is running according to the test results.The effect is fine after the conversion.
