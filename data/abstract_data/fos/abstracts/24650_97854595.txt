Ion exchange of potassium hexatungstate (K0.30WO3.15) by protons
Potassium ions in K 0.30 WO 3.15 , a compound whose structure resembles that of an orderly hexagonal tungsten bronze, are exchanged by protons in aqueous acids, in a topochemical reaction that yields K 0.3− x H x WO 3.15 · n H 2 O ( x ⩽0.26). These hydrated compounds retain the HTB structure. Moreover, electron diffraction patterns show the absence of streaks associated with to the ordering of K + ions, probably as a result of the low K + population in the tunnels. IR and thermal techniques showed deprotonation with formation of monoclinic WO 3 to take place above 250°C.
