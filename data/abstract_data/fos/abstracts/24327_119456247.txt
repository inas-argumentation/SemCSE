Nonlinear Seebeck effect in a model granular superconductor
The change of the Josephson supercurrent density js of a weakly connected granular superconductor in response to externally applied arbitrary thermal gradient ∇T (nonlinear Seebeck effect) is considered within a model of 3D Josephson junction arrays. For ∇T>(∇T)c, where (∇T)c is estimated to be of the order of ≃104 K/m for YBCO ceramics with an average grain size d≃10 μ m, the weak-links-dominated thermopower S is predicted to become strongly ∇T-dependent.
