The Research of Common Wireless Location Technology of Communication
Along with the development of mobile communication technology,more and more users want to mobile communication system can provide location-based services.Especially along with the third generation mobile communication system development,positioning business has become widely and prospect of one of the important business attraction.Based on the introduction of principle of each common wireless location technology,this thesis has compared the advantages and disadvantages between them,analyzed the causes of the positioning error causes and improvement methods,summarized the wireless location technology applications,at last,the common principles and the prospects of these methods are summarized,thus it provides new ideas for other location-based technology research and development.
