Analysis on the Rationality of our University's Teaching Appraise System
In recent years,our university has developed all kinds of works on the evaluation of higher education,and has made positive progresses.We concentrate our efferts on the evaluation of teaching quality all along,the level of teaching quality improved notability.But,whether the install of the teaching quality system is reasonable,whether the results reflect the level of teachers truly,is a question.This article will expound in the reliability of result of every guideline,the test of capacity error,and the rationality of every guideline's weight.
