Evaluation of Digital Learning Process Based on Naive Bayesian Method
Learning process evaluation can monitor all steps of learning in real time, and give full play to instructive function of fallback and adjustment, motivation and promotion. Because bayes method combines prior information with probability distribution of samples data, it is of high scientific to apply bayes method to evaluate digital learning process, and it is a beneficial exploration of education administration combined with data mining technology. The results of experiments show that the evaluation conclusions are objective, consistent and highly accurate.
