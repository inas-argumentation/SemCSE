Characteristics of Photosynthesis and Water Physio-ecology of Calophyllum inophyllum Linn.
The changes of leaf photosynthesis and water physio-ecology parameters of Calophyllum inophyllum Linn on the low hill land in Hainan Island were observed under various intensities of photosynthesis active radiation(PAR)and different CO2 concentrations(Ca)at the temperature of 30 ℃.Regression equations were established based on the observations.Under 400 μmol/(mol·s)Ca,the regression equation between net photosynthesis rate(Pn)and PAR is Pn=-1.3×10-6 PAR2+4.89×10-3 PAR-0.409 02 with a regression coefficient(r)0.921 4,and the critical points for light saturation,light compensation,apparent quantum yield(AQY)are 1 880.77 μmol/(m2·s),85.59 μmol/(m2·s)and 0.016 44 mol/mol,respectively.The regression equation between transpiration(Tr)and PAR is Tr=-6.709 8×10-8 PAR2+2.8×10-4PAR+0.296 12 with r=0.899 8.The relationship between water utilization efficiency(WUE)and PAR showed parabola trends with r=0.760 1(n=39).Under 1 000 μmol/(m2·s)PAR,the regression equation between Pn and Ca is Pn=-5.73×10-6Ca2+0.016 1Ca-0.976 5 and r is close to 1.0,and the CO2 saturation point,compensation point and carboxylation efficiency are 1 404.89 μmol/mol,62.01 μmol/mol and 0.015 98 mol/mol,respectively.The Tr showed negatively exponential down trend with increase in Ca:Tr=1.180 4 e-0.000 9Ca,and r=0.980 3.The WUE showed linear up trend with an increase in Ca,and the coefficient r is close to 1.0(n=36).
