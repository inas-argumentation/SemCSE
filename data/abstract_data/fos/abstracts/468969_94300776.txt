Electrochemical properties of junction between protonic conductor and oxide ion conductor
A dense and tightly contacted thin layer of SrZrO 3 -based solid solution could be formed on yttrium stabilized zirconia, YSZ, by high temperature chemical reaction. A water vapor concentration cell using this SrZrO 3 -YSZ junction showed a stable e.m.f. when the SrZrO 3 side was exposed to wet air, whereas it exhibited no e.m.f. when the SrZrO 3 was exposed to dry air, suggesting protonic conduction in SrZrO 3 . An oxygen concentration cell showed lower e.m.f. than that of the theoretical at 600°C. A hydrogen–oxygen fuel cell showed asymmetric behavior regarding the used gases. Higher current could be drawn when the SrZrO 3 film was exposed to hydrogen. Higher current passed through the junction when a positive voltage was applied on the SrZrO 3 side. This rectifying behavior did not appear in dry argon.
