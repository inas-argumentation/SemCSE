Imperative Operational Semantics in Type Theory
Type theory provides expressive and sound logics,which are well understood and relatively easy to be implemented since they are based on a small set of rules.The goal in this paper is to develop operational semantics in pure type systems. It is a straightforward implementation,the derivation system is formalized as inductive relations by directly describing the derivation rules.Type theory is naturally well-suited for the proof of purely functional programs.It can be shown that type system is also a good framework to specify and prove imperative programs.
