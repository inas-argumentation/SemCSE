Motivic zeta functions of infinite-dimensional Lie algebras
The paper shows how to associate a motivic zeta function with a
large class of infinite dimensional Lie algebras. These include
loop algebras, affine Kac-Moody algebras, the Virasoro algebra
and Lie algebras of Cartan type. The concept of a motivic zeta
functions provides a good language to talk about the uniformity
in p of local 
p-adic zeta functions of finite dimensional
Lie algebras. The theory of motivic integration is employed to
prove the rationality of motivic zeta functions associated to
certain classes of infinite dimensional Lie algebras.
