Design of Die Casting Mould with Circular Arc Corn Pulling by Hydraulic Pressure , Rack and Spur Gear Mechanism
A practical structure of die casting mould with circular arc corn pulling by hydraulic pressure, rack and spur gear mechanism for die casting faucets is presented, and a description is made on the feed system, the operating principle and the struc- tural features of the mould. A sector gear and rack mechanism is adopted in the mould for side circular arc corn pulling of the faucet castings, at the time, the corns pulling of the faucet castings in other two direction are respectively finished by the correspondent two mechanisms with angle pins and slides.
