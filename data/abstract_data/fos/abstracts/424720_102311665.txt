The Clinical Study of Combined Use of Benazepril and Antisterone in Treatment of Ventricular Arrhythmias of Chronic Heart-Failure
Objective To study the clinical therapeutic effectiveness of the depressor of angiotensin sulfotransferase of benazepril and receptor antagonist of aldosterone of antisterone in treatment of chronic heart-failure of ventrieular arrhythmias.Methods Selected of 98 inpatients with ventricular arrhythmias of chronic heart-failure,at the foundation of anticardiac failure in the meantime applied benazepril 5～10mg/d and antisterone 20～40mg/d,before 4 wks and after 4 wks of taking medicine separately measured the concentration of serum kalium ion, the concentration of serum magnesium ion, 24h dynamic state of electrokardia-gram as well as the level of plasma renin activity.angiotensin II.aldosterone and endothelin, moreover, for these results compared and also evaluated the clinical classification of New York heart association.Results After 4 weeks of taking medicine the cardiac function improved obviously,elevation of the serum kalium ion, elevation of the serum magnesium ion,the ventricilar premature best decreased obviously,the plasma renm activity.angiotensin II.aldosterone.endothelin reduced remarkably.Conclusion Benazepfil and antisterone can restrain the chronic heart-failure, reduce the making ganglionerous excitation systematic activity ofrenin-angiotensin-aldosterone, improve on cardiac function,elevate the serum kalium ion.serum magnesium ion, decresae in ventfieular arrhythmias, reduce the permcions level and reduce cardiac sudden death rate.
