Detail Fatigue Rating of Casting Aluminum Alloy A356.0
Detail fatigue rating is the inherent characteristic value of fatigue performance in a structure,which has a wide range of applications on the design of fatigue life and durability analysis in the civil aircraft.According to the test criteria in HB7110-94 by metal DFRcutoff,the fatigue property of A356.0-T6 alloy was tested on the detail fatigue rating.According to the experimental results,the calculated DFRcutoff is 139 Mpa.No apparent fatigue fracture is revealed from the scanning electron microscope analysis The cleavage surface and second cracks are found,which suggest that the alloy has anobvious characteristic of brittle fracture In addition,dendrites,blowholes,inclusions and shrinkage porosities are found on the fracture.The poor quality of casting is the key factor to affect fatigue propertyof alloy A356.0.
