Characterisation of Se implanted layers for GaAs FETs
Results on doping profile, uniformity of doping and mobility for Se implanted LEC grown semi-insulating GaAs substrates are presented as a function of Se ion energy and annealing temperature. SiO2, RF sputtered Si3N4 and Ga2O3+Al are used as encapsulating layers. Surface effects (stress and damage) on doping efficiency of implanted Se ions are also included.
