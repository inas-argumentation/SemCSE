Evidence for the Decay B0s → D(*)sD(*)s and a Measurement of ΔΓCPs/Γs
We search for the semi-inclusive process B 0 s → D ( * ) s D ( * ) s using 2.8 fb -1 of pp collisions at √s = 1.96 TeV recorded by the DO detector operating at the Fermilab Tevatron Collider. We observe 26.6 ± 8.4 signal events with a significance above background of 3.2 standard deviations yielding a branching ratio of B(B 0 s → D ( * ) s D ( * ) s ) = 0.035 ± 0.010(stat.) ± 0.011(syst.). Under certain theoretical assumptions, these double-charm final states saturate CP-even eigenstates in the B 0 s decays resulting in a width difference of ΔΓ CP s /Γ s = 0.072 ± 0.021(stat.) ± 0.022(syst.).
