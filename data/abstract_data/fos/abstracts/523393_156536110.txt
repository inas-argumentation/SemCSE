Development of Sports Activities and College Students' Abilities of Human Relationship
Human communication ability is one of modern people's necessary abilities. This article, embarking from the existing problem of the contemporary university student human communication ability, elaborated the promoter action of sports to the human communication ability of the university student, which mainly displays: Provides the opportunity for the human communication; raises the necessary good individuality quality of human communication; improves the human communication ability; refines the manners and the demeanor in human communication and so on.
