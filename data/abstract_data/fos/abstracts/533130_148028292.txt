On Higher Educational Institutions' Resource Allocation
From the perspective of leadership in universities,the article analyzes the limitations in institutions' resource Chinese universities encounter obstacles in integrating internal resources allocation.Universities become inactive in economic activities;the efficiency of resources utilization is relatively low.The article providing some constructive suggestions as well;adopting strategic management;combining resources allocation with strategic plans; establishing rational resources allocation system and resources sharing mechanism.Disciplinary of strategic plans should be insisted,and specific implementation mechanism should be set up with explicit objectives and clear-cut responsibility;constructing rational decision-making system,implementation system and supervision system,and provide the guarantee for the realization of the university objectives.
