Research on molecular mechanism of new Chinese cabbage cytoplasmic male sterility RC_7
The special sequence related to CMS was amplified in the new Chinese cabbage CMS RC7 by PCR amplifying,and its homology is 100% with orf138 published in NCBI.This not only indicated that RC7 has the similar sterility with Ogu-CMS,but the gene related to CMS is relative conservative.The result both established the theory for full utilization of new variety RC7 and enriched the CMS theory.
