Effect of Suspension Casting on Microstructure and Properties of Fly-ash Floating Beads Particle Reinforced Al Matrix Composite
The effects of suspension casting on the microstructure and properties of fly-ash floating beads particle reinforced aluminum matrix composite were studied.The results show that adding suspending agent with the same composition to aluminum can improve the hardness of aluminum matrix composite containing 5vol% fly-ash floating beads particle.When the addition temperature is 800 ℃ and addition quantity of suspending agent is 3%,the mechanical properties(σb,δ) decrease.The hardness and wear resistance of fly-ash floating beads particle reinforced aluminum matrix composite can be increased by 13.10% and 20.10%,the mechanical properties(σb,δ) are decreased by 23.80% and 43.54%.
