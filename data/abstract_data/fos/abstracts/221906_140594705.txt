Effects of limestone dust accumulation on lateral growth of forest trees
Four species of trees from two sites in southwestern Virginia were used for annual ring measurements to determine the effects of limestone dust on lateral growth of forest trees. The sites were similar in all respects except dust accumulation. One site, the control, had essentially no dust accumulation, whereas the other had heavy dust accumulation from nearby limestone processing plants. A reduction in lateral growth of at least 18% was shown for Acer rubrum, Quercus prinus and Q. rubra. Lateral growth of Liriodendron tulipifera, however, increased by 76%. Prior to this study, dust accumulation had not been correlated with a significant increase in lateral growth of trees.
