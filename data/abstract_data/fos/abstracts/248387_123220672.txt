Investigating and optimizing electrophysical processes for joining tensomodule elements based on a silicon-on-insulator heterostructure by thermoelectrically stimulated bonding
The thermoelectrically stimulated bonding (in an electrostatic field) of the chip of a strain-sensitive pressure cell with a glass reference element to form the strain-sensitive module (tensomodule) of a thermally resistant pressure sensor is examined. The strain-sensitive pressure cell is based on a silicon-glass-silicon heterostructure.
