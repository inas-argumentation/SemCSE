Enantio- and stereoselective synthesis of the right-half segment (1, Cl-Cg) of macrolide antibiotic Rhizoxin is an antitumor antibiotic isolated as a toxin from the culture broth of Rhizopus chinensis which is a potent pathogen of rice seedling blight.* We have been investigating the total synthesis of this novel macrolide antibiotic based on the retrosynthetic analysis shown in Scheme 1. This paper describes the synthesis of the right-half segment (1, Cl-Cg) of rhizoxin by enzymatic approach using PLE.
