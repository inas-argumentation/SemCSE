Effect of 1-methylcyclopropene on the postharvest physiology of green pepper
In order to investigate the effects of 1-methylcyclopropene(1-MCP)on the postharvest physiology of green pepper,green peppers were treated with 1-MCP of different concentrations and stored at(10±0.5)℃ for 40days.The results showed that 1-MCP significantly depressed respiration rate,decreased ethylene production and concentration of alcohol of fruits.Among the different concentration of 1-MCP,1000nL/L of 1-MCP had the best effect.In short,1-MCP can delay ripening and prolonged storage time of green peppers.At the same time,respiration rate,ethylene production and concentration of alcohol of green pepper had apparently correlation.
