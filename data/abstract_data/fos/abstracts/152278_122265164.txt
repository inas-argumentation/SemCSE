Empirical Likelihood for Partially Linear Models with Missing Responses: The Fixed Design Case
Suppose that we have a partially linear model with independent zero mean errors ε i , where {x i , t i , i = 1,…, n} are non random and are observed completely, and {Y i , i = 1,…, n} are missing at random (MAR). Empirical likelihood (EL) ratio statistics on the regression coefficient β and the nonparametric function g(t) for fixed t ∈ (0, 1) are constructed based on the inverse probability weighted imputation approach, which asymptotically have χ2-type distributions. These results are used to obtain EL-based confidence regions on β and g(t). Results of a simulation study on the finite sample performance of EL-based confidence regions are presented.
