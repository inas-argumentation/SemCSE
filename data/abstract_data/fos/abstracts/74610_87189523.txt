A primary analysis about the distribution of lymphocyte subpopulations and its relationship to Ag NORs′ content of patients with convalescent SARS
Objective To investigate the immune state of the lymphocyte subgroups and its active state in the patients with convalescent SARS by testing their distribution of the blood lymphocyte subgroups and to analyze relationship with the Ag NORs′ content. Methods The peripheral lymphocyte subgroups of the patients were tested by flowcymetry and the Ag NORs′ content of the T cells were tested by KL immune picture analysis system. The relationship of the two items was analyzed. Results The number of the total T lymphocytes and CD4 + subgroup was almost normal, but that of CD8 + increased. The ratio of CD4 +/CD8 + lowered, which less than 1 was 37.4%. The activated T lymphocytes were mostly CD8 + cells. The number of B lymphocytes was normal, but that of NK cells was lower in patients than that of control apparently. The number of CD4 +, CD4 +/CD8 +, CD19 +CD5 + decreased and that of CD8 +, CD3 +HLA DR + increased in the severe cases. The number of CD3 + HLA DR + rose in those older than 50 years old or treated by high dose hormone. The content of Ag NORs(IS%) was in normal range, but the IS value of the patients showed an abnormal distribution. The IS value in 44.99% patients was lower. The content of Ag NORs statistically correlated to CD3 +, NK, CD3 +HLA DR +, CD3 +CD25 +. Conclusion The immune function of the patients with convalescent SARS trended normal, but some of them had an abnormal counting of lymphocyte subgroups and lymphocyte activity. So, these patients should be followed up for a longer period to find out the secular effect of SARS virus on the immune function after their clinical symptoms had been improved.
