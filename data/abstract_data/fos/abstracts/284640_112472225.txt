Fault Analysis of Pole II Thyristor Valves in Gezhouba Converter Station
The fault of poleⅡ thyristor valves happened on 28th Dec.2008 in Gezhouba converter station is introduced in this paper,relative inspections and tests subsequently taken are also presented,the detailed analysis of fault cause is given.It is concluded that the degradation of voltage withstand ability and the increase of leakage current of some thyristors resulted from thyristor aging is the main cause of the fault.Moreover,the relative problems of thyristor aging are illuminated and some constructive preventive advices are also proposed.
