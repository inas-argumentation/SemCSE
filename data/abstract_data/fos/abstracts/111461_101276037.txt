Study on inorganic microparticulate flocculants
The preparation of microparticulate flocculants on the base of sol with P,Mg,Zn,Mn and Co as assistant components and their flocculating performances are presented.With percentage turbidity removal of an aqueous kaolinite suspension as criterion,two more excellent flocculants NSAFMnS and NFAC are selected.Treating the sewage from Zhuangxi United Station,Shengli Oil Field,which has turbidity of 130.47 NTU and contains 24.13 mg/L of oil,by using flocculants NSAFMnS and NFAC at dosage of 20 mg/L,the removal rate of turbidity reaches 75.89% and 78.19%,and the removal rate of oil substances reaches 78.52% and 81.02%,respectively.In contrast,the removal rate of turbidity and oil are 49.11% and 57.14% in case of using a commercial polyaluminum under similar conditions.The structures of flocculants are identified by SEM.
