Academic nature and its strengthen strategy of university instruction
Academics is the special systemic knowledge while university is the main place of producing and disseminating it. Instruction is inherently unified with scientific research, and both give priority to special personnel training. In light of university teachers' career development and instruction itself, university instruction should be based on research, which presents many characteristic, such as innovation, specialization and exchangement, and so on. At prsent, conceptions shouldbe ugraded to establish the acadmic status of university instruction; the construction of system should be strengthened to engage teachers in instruction; the academic trait of university instruction can be reinforced through furthering its research.
