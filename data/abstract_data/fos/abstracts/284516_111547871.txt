A New Method of EHV Transmission Lines Protection Based on Wavelet Transform
The analysis of power system transient signals is the basis of fault diagnosis and the transient protection theory.Wavelet transform is a powerful mathematic tool for transient signal analysis.In this paper,a novel high speed distance protection scheme for EHV transmission line protection based on transient signals wavelet transform is presented.The fault distance is estimated from the travelling waves directly.The relaying signals are processed using the wavelet transform instead of conventional filtering.At the same time,a new algorithm wavelet transform is proposed.The simulation results of ATP-EMTP and Matlab/Wavelet Tool-box show that the novel theory and the proposed methods are working fast and reliable.
