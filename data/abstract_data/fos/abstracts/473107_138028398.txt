The Study on the Overcooling Austenite Isothermal Transformation Diagram and Tempering Technology of Circular Chain Steel in Coal Mine Machine
The critical points and the overcooling austenite isothermal transformation diagram of ciroular chain steel in coal mine mine machine were measured by using automatic transformations testing instrument (formastor digital).The tempering technology were studied.The results showed there is not pearlite area in this steel and the microstructure were tempering martensite and bainite after quenching and tempering at mid temperature.
