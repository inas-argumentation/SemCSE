Chinese Newly-founded Colleges: Rise,Predicament and Choice
Newly-founded colleges has become important members among China's higher education institutions.However,with the rapid development of Newly-founded colleges,they actually confronted many internal and external problems to solve.These problems restricted their further development,and made each college caught in a realistic predicament.In fact,transformation development is the rational choice for Newly-founded colleges to step out of the plight and realize its own value.
