Appraisal of experiment and theory in the spectra of complexes
The methods ofcrystal field (CF) and ligand field (LF) theories have now been quite generally applied to a study of the spectra of transition metal complexes for a little over ten years, and it seems to be a suitable time and occasion to appraise the progress marle during that time. Many of the predictions marle by the use of these theories have been in the nature of suggesting the assignments for very many absorption spectra and it is at least pertinent to inquire just how firmly these assignments stand in terms of spectroscopic theory as distinct from Iigand and crystal field theories.
