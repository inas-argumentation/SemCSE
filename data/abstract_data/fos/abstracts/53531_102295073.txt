Headspace Gas Chromatographic Method for the Determination of Partition Coefficient of Organic Surrogates Between Paperboard Packaging Materials and Air
Partition coefficients of organic contaminants between paperboard packaging materials and airs,Kpaper/air,is a very impotent physicochemical parameter to study the potential migration of organic surrogates in paperboard package into food.In this study,headspace gas chromatographic method(HS-GC) for the study of Kpaper/air is reported.The Kpaper/air is derived from the volumes and the ratio of the peak areas of the paper samples from two independent headspace GC measurements of the two vials at equilibrium rather than the specific concentration of the contaminants in the paper matrix.Mathematical precision analysis and experimental verifications indicate that the accuracy of the method and the volume ratio,x,of the paper samples in the two testing vials is a key parameter.The method is a rapid,accurate and relatively simple to measure Kpaper/air.
