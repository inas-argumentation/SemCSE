BRIEF COMMUNICATIONS: Laser-plasma spectra of ions of elements from Fe to Br with 15-24 lost electrons, recorded in the 50-150 Å range
Plasma was formed by focusing neodymium laser radiation of 5 GW power on solid targets made of elements between Fe and Br. Radiative transitions of the 2s22pt–2s22pt+1 type were recorded for F-, O-, and N-like ions as well as spectral lines or anomalous intensity due to 4f–5g transitions in Na-like ions. Intercombination transitions in O-like ions Fe XIX, Ni XXI, Cu XXII, and Zn XXIII were observed.
