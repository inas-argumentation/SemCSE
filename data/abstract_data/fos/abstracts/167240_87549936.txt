The Observation of Mycorrhizal Microstructure with Scanning Elcetron Microscope(SEM)
This paper is to explore the observation condition of mycorrhizal microstructure with SEM and observe them in order to make a supplement of identification methods to mycorrhiza and mycorrhizal fungi. The results showed that the light brown and mature fine root with 1~2 mm diameter should be selected. The microtubule and hartig net images could be obtained clearly and identifiably by being treated with different Ca(OH)2 concentrations and times. The structure of microtubule was certain stability and could be used to identify mycorrhiza and mycorrhizal fungi.
