A postmodernistic review on the relation between the theory and practice in education
Due to the dualism of essentialistic thinking-pattern,it is still a vexed question that the theory and the practice separate from each other in educational research.The key to solve the problem is to convert the thinking-pattern.Postmodernism provides a complex and multiple perspective on the relation between the theory and practice in education.In the postmordernistic perspective,there is no exclusivity in the educational theory,no absolute objectivity in the educational practice ane no permanent method in the education research.From postmodernism,we can learn that the theory is not onefold,the pactice is not self-objective.Educational research has not fixed way,the relation is not so bad as we think usually.At the same time,postmodernism provides us a new practical way to integrate the theory and the practice more diversely and tightly.
