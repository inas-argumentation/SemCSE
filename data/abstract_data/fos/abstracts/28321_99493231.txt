Studies on the Effects of Selected Pesticides and Surfactants on Photodegradation of Choropyrifos
Effects of 9 kinds of pesticides and 6 kinds of surfactants on photodegradtion of chloropyrifos on glass surface were studied under high pressure mercury lamp Two of the pesticides, namely pirimicarb allethrin, exhibited the marked photosensitive effects, and the marked photoquench effects were observed for lindane, bifenthrin and fenitrothion, respectively The three of surfactants, which were 2201, 0201and JFC, accelerated intensively the photolysis of chloropyrifos
