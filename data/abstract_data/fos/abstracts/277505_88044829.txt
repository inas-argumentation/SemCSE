Reviewed of allergen-cross-contamination management guides of Chinese major trading nations or regions
Cross-contamination in food production strictly effects the accuracy of food allergen labeling,which greatly threaten the health of food allergy group.In this article,the guidelines to prevent cross-contamination in food industry were reviewed,in which Australia and United Kingdom were distinctly?illuminated.To implement a good performance in managing allergen cross-contamination in Chinese food industries,the difficulties and the coping strategies were proposed.
