Chemiluminescence method for the determination of human serum albumin by highly sensitive chemiluminescence probe-fluoresceinyl cypridina luciferin analog
Highly sensitive chemiluminescence (CL) enhancing method was developed to detect human serum albumin(HSA) based on the reaction of fluoresceinyl cypridina luciferin analog (FCLA)-O-1(2). Experiments revealed that chemiluminescence could be greatly enhanced by the addition of HSA. Owing to the enhancing effect of HSA, under the optimal conditions, the enhanced CL intensity was linear for a HSA concentration of 5 x 10(-9) -8 x 10(-8) mol/L, with a detection limit of 0.017 mg/L. The method has a low cost and a higher sensitivity than thase of fluorescence spectra analysis, synchrony fluorescence scanning technique, rayleigh light scattering technique. Determination results for HSA in urine were consistent with clinical ones. Additionally, the mechanism of enhanced CL by HSA was discussed.
