Study on enzymatic clarification of pomegranate juice
The effects of dosages of pectinase, temperature, time and dilution ratio of juice on the clarification of pomegranate juice were discussed. On the basis of single factor, the results showed that the pectinase usage was 1830~2550U/mL, temperature at 40~55℃, time at 1.5~2.5h, dilution ratio of juice at 0~5, Transmittance of clarified pomegranate fruit juice was over 59.1%. Through orthogonal test, the optimal conditions were determined as follows: dosages of pectinase at 1830U/mL, temperature at 50℃, time at 2h and dilution ratio of juice at 0.
