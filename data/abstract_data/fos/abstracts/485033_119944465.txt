The mean velocity of particles ejected from a polycrystalline copper surface during the bombardment with high energetic Ar+-ions
The measurements are made with the aid of a sensitive ballistic microbalance. The mean velocity decreases rapidly above 150 keV with increasing energy and reaches a constant value at about 800 keV. The exact analysis of the motion of the balance exhibits a strong influence of condensation phenomena and of the bombarding ions on the experiments.
