Application of Chitosan and Chitosan Derivatives into Processing of Mung Bean Sprout
Effects of chitosan and chitosan derivatives on yield and quality of mung bean sprouts were studied.The results indicated that all chitosan and chitosan derivatives increased yield and quality of mung bean sprouts. Among chitosan and chitosan derivatives,chitosan glutamate performed best.With 0.05% chitosan glutamate concentration and 8 h immersing time,total weight of bean sprouts was increased by 10%, Vitamin C content increased by 61.93% and hardness, thickness, length of sprouts were increased at different levels.
