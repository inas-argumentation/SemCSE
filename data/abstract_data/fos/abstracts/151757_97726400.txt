Conductivity of (Na+-K+)-β′'/β-Al2O3 ceramic electrolytes
The impedance spectra of (Na + -K + )-β′'/β-Al 2 O 3 ceramics have been measured between -100°C and 300°C. The total conductivity of the ceramics can be described in terms of conductivity of the β″- and β-Al 2 O 3 grains and their boundaries. The ionic conduction mechanism in the mixed-alkali/mixed-phase β″/β-alumina electrolytes is discussed.
