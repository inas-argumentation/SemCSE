The Role of the Graduate Record Examinations in Graduate Admissions.
It is well known that practically all young people aspiring to college take at least one of the two major college admission tests, a phenomenon explained by the fact that most four-year institutions require prospective students to submit test scores with their applications [6]. In addition, much has been written about how these tests are used in admission decisions at institutions with competitive admissions [10] as well as at the less selective ones [11]. To add to our empirical base, the American Association of Collegiate Registrars and Admissions Officers and the College Board conducted a 1980 national survey of admission test requirements and uses [2]. At the graduate level, less information has been available about either admission test requirements or their use. ' Information about graduate program test requirements has been collected, but the data usually have been reported in formats and sources (for example, graduate program guides and directories) that do not improve our understanding of graduate admission test requirements on a national level. Similarly, some research has been done on test use at the graduate level, but it has dealt generally with single disciplines at specific institutions (for example, [4]).
