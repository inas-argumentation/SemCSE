Preliminary study on inhibitory mechanism of pyrolin against Alternaria alternate
Study on inhibitory mechanism of pyrolin against Alternaria alternate from Alternaria alternate growth, action mode, cellular membrane permeability and protein content. The result showed that the action mode of pyrolin against Alternaria alternate is inhibition, but not germicidal action. Alternaria alternate growth was decreased with the increase of pyrolin concentration, the inhibition rate of 2.5 mg/mL pyrolin reached 100% , the cellular membrane permeability increased, and the protein inhibition rate was 52.63% while the concertration of pyrolin was 0.313 mg/mL.
