Determination of Barium in Silico-Barium Alloy
In the medium of NaACHAC, pH6.0～6.5 buffer,with ammonium triacetic acid as masking agent for Fe(Ⅲ),(NH_4)_2Cr_2O_7 was added to precipitate the barium by forming BaCrO_4,thus separating Barium from interferences of coexisting elements.BaCrO_4 are dissolved in hydrochloric acid,(NH_4)_2Fe(SO_4)_2 standard solution titration CrO_42-,can indirectly determine Barium content.
