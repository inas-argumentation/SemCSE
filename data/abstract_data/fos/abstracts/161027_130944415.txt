Performance of a New Refrigerant-CMR-02 Compared with R12 and R134a Used in Automobiles' Air Conditioning
CMR-02 is a new type of refrigerant. By comparison and analysis of main parameters of performance and comparison experiment of compressor functions of CMR-02 as well as R134a and R12,it was showed that if using CMR-02 replaces R12 in automobile air conditioners,the cooling capacity per mass improves remarkable by 68.10% and cooling capacity per volume increases by 14.38%,which satisfies the environment criteria with low cost and as a transitional substitute of R12.
