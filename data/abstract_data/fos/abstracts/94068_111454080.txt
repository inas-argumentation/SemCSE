Analysis and Application of the Power System of Hydraulic Pile Dvers
This paper first illustrates the control system of hydraulic pile divers, establishes the differential equation of the hydraulic servo and determines the transfer function. Secondly, the solutions to the differential equation are obtained by means of Laplace's transform method. Thirdly, the dynamic response of the hydraulic power system is analyzed. Finally practical applications to construc- tion projects are demonstrated
