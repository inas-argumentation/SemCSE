On the Relation among the Government,Institutions of Higher Learning and the Market after China's Entry into WTO
For higher education,it has brought about both the challenge and the opportunity since China entered into WTO.In the era of economic globalization,the market competition of the international higher education becomes more drastic.We should hold the opportunity,meet the challenge, deepen the government reform and transform the government function,which makes institutions of higher learning international,industrial,popular and diverse. Thus we can speed up the development and strengthen the competition of the institutions.
