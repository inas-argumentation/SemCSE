Extraction optimization and free radical scavenging of flavonoids from Physalis alkekengi var. francheti calyx
The optimum extraction technology and free radical scavenging ability of flavonoids from calyx of Physalis alkekengi L. var. francheti were performed for future development and utilization. The optimum parameters of extraction were determined by response surface methodology. The free radical scavenging ability was examined by two method of free radical scavenging assays with Vc as control. The results show that the optimized condition was as follows: ethanol concentration of 71.08%, extraction temperature of 78.46 ℃, extraction time of 6.62 h, ratio of solvent to material of 19.80 mL/g and the flavonoids yield is up to 4.65%. The results of free radical scavenging ability tests demonstrated that the flavonoids from Physalis alkekengi L. var. francheti calyx has remarkable free radical scavenging ability.
