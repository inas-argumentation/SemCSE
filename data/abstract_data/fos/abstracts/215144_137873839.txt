Effect of Si Content on Microstructure and Internal Friction of Fe-Al Based Damping Alloys
Three kind of alloys with different Si content were experimentally made and internal friction and microstructure of the alloys under annealing 2h at 900 ℃ conditions were studied. An internal friction peak was observed in the present alloys. The Q- 1 peak increases and then decreases with the increase of Si, which was found to be directly attributed to the precipitation intermetallics from the aluminium alloy matrix. The internal friction mechanisms of the alloy were put forward.
