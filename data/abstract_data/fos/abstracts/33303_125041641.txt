Spectroscopy of the 10Li isotope in stopped pion absorption reactions on a 14C radioactive target
A search for the 10Li formation was performed in missing mass spectra measured in stopped pion absorption reactions 14C(π−, pt)X and 14C(π−, dd)X. Three excited states of 10Li were found. The broad state with a resonance energy of ER = 6.13 ± 0.11 MeV has the highest excitation energy in comparison with levels observed earlier.
