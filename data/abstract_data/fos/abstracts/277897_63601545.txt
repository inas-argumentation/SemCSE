The Introduction to the Security Policy in SQL Server
The paper presents the safety management strategies of database security design, such as database integrity, data backup and resume, preventing illegal intrusion, data audit and data encryption. It presents the safety management strategies of large scale relational database system based on client/server in Microsoft SQL Server. According to the safety questions while developing MIS and from the point of view of safety designing in applying systems,it puts forwards the design and realiza- tion of database security in management information systems.
