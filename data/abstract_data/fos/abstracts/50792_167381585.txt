Discussions on Application of Normal Distribution in Performance Evaluation
The performance evaluation is a new human resources management in the normal distribution,which can reduce the disadvantages of egalitarianism.The person to be assessed should be the one engaged basically in the same work,and preferably the person to assess is to be the leader who knows best all the persons' performance to be assessed.In practice,the implementation of performance evaluation will encounter various problems.Therefore,it is necessary to present an objective analysis of the environment and the needs of enterprise development,the right perspective to evaluate the performances,and the optimization and adjustment in practical work.
