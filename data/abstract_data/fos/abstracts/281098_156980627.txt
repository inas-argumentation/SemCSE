On the Relationship between Urban-Rural Income Gap and Urbanization of Guangdong
This essay establishes time series by selecting the data of the urban-rural income gap and urbanization level of Guangdong province.Through the co-integration test,error correction model and grey relevance analysis,we can get the following results: Guangdong urban-rural income gap and the urbanization level are positively correlated,and there exists long-term co-integration relationship between them;the grey relevance between them is high in the process of development.
