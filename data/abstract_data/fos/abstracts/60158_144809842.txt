Migration, Mujercitas, and Medicine Men: Living in Urban Mexico (review)
Valentina Napolitano has written a complex, intellectually stimulating ethnography of life in Polanco, a low-income neighborhood on the urban periphery of Mexico's second largest city, Guadalajara. Her informants are women and men, young and old, whose lives highlight multiple themes, including the rural to urban migration experience; popular religiosity; alternative forms of medicine; and coming of age rites of young women.
