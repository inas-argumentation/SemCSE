My View On Path Innovation of Training Economic News Talents
Financial media heat and "economization" of mass media arouse great demand for economic news talents and media talents.Since financial institutions could take advantages of economic management,economic news talents training should seek the meeting point between journalism and subjects related to economic management.At the same time,convergence between general journalism curriculums and curriculums about economic news should be well handled.Moreover,we should strengthen practice teaching and employment orientation,give prominence to specialty of economic news,and avoid "generalization","soften" and "rigidification".
