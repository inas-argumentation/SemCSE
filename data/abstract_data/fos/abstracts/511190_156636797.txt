Ruling by Law and Social Morality Environment in the Present Period of China
The current social morality environment system in china consists of the level of subjects'understanding of morality, the unity of the legality and morality of the subjects'acts and the evaluation environment of social morality ruling by law in certain society has intrinsic requirement on its moralty evaluation environment. As for the present ruling by law in china, the requirement manifests in the consistency of morality, the advancement of morality and the identity of the goal of morality and that of ruling by law.
