Improving Bonding Strength of Arc-spraying Chromium Coatings by Arc-ultrasonic
A new method to improve the bonding strength of arc-spraying chromium coatings by arc-ultrasonic is presented. As the heat resource of arc spraying, the arc emits ultrasonic excited by high frequency modulation. Arc-ultrasonic improves the atomization of spraying materials and the bonding strength of spraying coatings, and the bonding strength increases with ultrasonic frequency and excitation voltage.
