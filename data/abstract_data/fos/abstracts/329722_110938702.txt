Transient Soil Moisture Movement Through Layered Soils Using a Finite Element Approach
ABSTRACT A numerical model has been developed to study tran-sient vertical moisture flow through homogeneous and layered soils under isothermal conditions using a finite element approach. The flow system was divided in-to a number of elements, and the principles of Darcy-law flow and mass balance were applied to each element to obtain the soil moisture conditions at each time. The validity of the model was established by comparing the results with solutions of published models, and good agreement was obtained. This modeling procedure does not utilize the traditional differential equation of flow and the basic principles of flow appear explicitly in the computation procedure.
