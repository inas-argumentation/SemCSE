Design and Development of a Knowledge Management Modeling Tool Based on Meta-Model
Knowledge management modeling is the foundation to build knowledge management system for an organization.Because of the complexity of knowledge management modeling,the modeling tools for knowledge management is necessitous now.The requirements for organizational knowledge management were analysed form two levels of knowledge object and management.Based on this,a knowledge management meta-model was suggested which includes four sub-models: organizational strategy submodel,organizational anatomy sub-mpdel and organizational knowledge map sub-model.In order to develop modeling tools of knowledge management,an object-oriented modeling language(OML) for knowledge management corresponding to the meta-model was suggested.A modeling tool for organizaional knowledge management was implemented.
