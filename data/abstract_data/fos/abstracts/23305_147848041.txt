The developmental route and orientation of nongovernmental higher education in Zhejiang
Two running styles of higher education exist in Zhejiang: minban (people-run) institutes and state-owned people-run institutes. Among them, second level colleges of the latter have many characteristics. With the increase of private finance and the accumulation of experience in running higher education, minban (people-run) general higher- education institutes will become the mainstream in nongovernmental higher education in Zhejiang.
