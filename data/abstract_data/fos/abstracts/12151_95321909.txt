Synthesis of substituted 2-amino-5,6-dihydro-4h-1, 3-oxazines
4,4,6-Trimethyl-2-alkyl(aryl)amino-5,6-dihydro-4H-1,3-oxazines were synthesized via two methods: amination of 4,4,6-trimetiiyl-2-methylthio-5,6-dihydro-4H-1,3-oxazine and cyclization of N-aryl-N′-(2-methyl-4-hydroxy-2-amyl)-S-methylisothiourea.
