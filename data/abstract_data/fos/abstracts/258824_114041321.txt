Reflection and Thought on the Networking Construction of the Management of University Textbooks
With the rapid development of high education and the improvement of teaching and learning reform in China,the information system of the management of university textbooks based on traditional management mode cannot keep up with the requirement of the new situation.The paper analyzes the present status and development foreground of the networking construction of the management of university textbooks,probes new management mode which is suitable for cultivating students' individuality,and studies the ways of networking construction of the management of teaching materials in universities from the perspective of contemporary reform and development of high education in China.
