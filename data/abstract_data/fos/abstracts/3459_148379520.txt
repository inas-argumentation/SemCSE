Analysis of Organizational Communication Satisfaction for University Faculty Members
A total of 492 university faculty members participated the survey.They were required to evaluate their feelings of these items according to their experiences during the past year.Findings indicated that,there were significant differences between organizational communication pre-expectations and its real satisfactions among faculty members,namely the former were distinctly higher the latter.Neither gender nor educational degree was markedly correlative with faculties' OCS(organizational communication satisfaction),while age,length of service,monthly salary and professional title were strongly interrelated with the satisfactions.Length of service was the most important influencing factor to faculties' OCS.Age took the second place.The rest of characteristic variables had no significant influence to faculties' OCS.There were significant positive correlations among all aspects of the organizational communication satisfactions and the job satisfactions.
