Verification of“ Three Dates and One Certificate”in Personnel Files
Employees'“ three dates and one certificate (date of birth,date of joining Chinese Communist Party,date of first employment,and certificate of highest education degree)” are the basis of human resource information management as well as important references for further decision- making processes.Verification of the“ three dates and one certificate” by the human resource department is a very serious work which requires great care and strict methods of relative policies.It influences directly on each employee's individual interest,and also has effect on the quality and efficiency of the human resource management work. But recently for some employees,the records of the“ three dates and one certificate” are not consistent with fact or historical ones,for such problems,the author proposes some detailed verification methods to distinguish the true ones. These methods not only aim to safeguard individual employee's personal interest, but also try to eliminate the“ alias” problems in the personnel files and keep the seriousness and correctness of these files.
