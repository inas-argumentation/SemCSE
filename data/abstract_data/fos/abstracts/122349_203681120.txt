A New Design of Intelligent Streetlight Control System Based on Wireless Sensor Networks
This paper introduces a new design of intelligent streetlight control system based on wireless sensor networks to solve the problems that a serious waste of the traditional street power and the reality of single control modes.The system gathers information by deploying wireless sensor street nodes to realize the real-time acquisition of natural light intensity and the vehicles moving situations,which wirelessly transmits to the control center.And the control center deals with the information by analysis,computation,feedback,storage and display.Depending on Address Identification Control Method by independent design,we can automatically open the appropriate streetlights according to the position and moving speeds of the vehicles and close the streetlights timely after they pass by.It is a set of energy-efficient,scientific and rational,safe and reliable adaptive control system,which offers some guidance to the design of streetlight control system.
