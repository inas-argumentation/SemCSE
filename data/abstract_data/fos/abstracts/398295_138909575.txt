Research on Removing Process of Valve Sleeve′s Casting Defects Based on Computer Analysis Technique
The influences of compression and die-entrance angle on the removing of shrinkage cavity and shrinkage porosity were simulated by using the Deform-3D software based on computer analysis technique.The entire removing of the cast defects such as shrinkage cavity and shrinkage porosity in the copper valve sleeve was researched during the plastic deformation condition.Through comparing the relative densities before and after the plastic deformation,the removing results of the defects under different conditions were obtained.
