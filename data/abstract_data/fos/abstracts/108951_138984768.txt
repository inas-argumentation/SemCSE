Effect of Suction-casting Voltage on the Glass Forming Ability and Mechanical Properties of Zr_(63)Cu_(17.5)Al_(7.5)Ni_(10)Fe_2 Amorphous
By adding minor amount of cheap element Fe into metallic glass based on Zr65Cu17.5Al7.5Ni10 composite,bulk metallic glass(BMG) in diameter of 3 mm was successfully synthesized by copper mold suction casting method at different voltages.The effect of suction-casting voltages on the microstructure,glass forming ability,compressive mechanical property and fracture features of Zr63Cu17.5Al7.5Ni10Fe2 bulk amorphous were investigated by means of X-ray diffraction(XRD),scanning electron microscopy(SEM) and computer controlled electronic mechanical testing system.The results show that the property of the amorphous is closely related to suction-casting voltage.
