The interaction of V2O5 and Nb2O5 with oxide surfaces
The interaction of vanadium oxide and niobium oxide with Al 2 O 3 and TiO 2 supports is examined with Raman spectroscopy. The Raman spectra of the supported metal oxides reveal that the strong interaction of the metal oxides with Al 2 O 3 and TiO 2 supports results in the formation of two-dimensional surface metal oxide overlayers. The surface vanadium oxide overlayers are unstable to high calcination temperatures and readily undergo solid state transformations. The surface niobium oxide overlayers are much more stable to high-temperature calcination treatments and retard solid state transformations of the mixed oxide systems.
