Influence of Three Gorges-Gezhouba Joint Operation to Gezhouba Hydropower Station
Introduces the Three Gorges-Gezhouba joint operation system of cascade hydropower stations.From the changes of regulation of incoming flow,the improvement of the utilization efficiency of water resource,generating efficiency increase,the changes of properties of flood season scum and the cooperation of Yangtze shipping,the influences of Gezhouba hydropower station were analyzed in terms of the joint operation mode since the impoundment of Three Gorges Project in 2003.
