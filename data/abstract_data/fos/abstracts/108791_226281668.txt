Hyperspherical few-body model calculation for cascade $\Xi$ exotic hypernuclei with reference to nuclear driplines
In this paper, we presented the ground state energies calculated for some single and double cascade hyperon(${\Xi}^{-}$) hypernuclei. For hypernuclei of the type $_{\Xi}^{A_c+1}$X we applied $\Xi+^A_c$X two-body model and for hypernuclei of the type $_{\Xi\Xi}^{A_c+2}$X we adopted $\Xi+\Xi+^A_c$X three-body model. A two-term Gaussian (Isle type) potential with adjustable parameters is chosen as the $\Xi$-nucleus interaction while a two-term Yukawa-type potential is chosen the $\Xi\Xi$ pair. In the three-body model calculation, we adopted the hyperspherical harmonics expansion (HHE) formalism. The resulting two- and three-body Schr{o}dinger equations subject to appropriate conditions have been solved numerically to get the ground-state energies and wavefunctions.
