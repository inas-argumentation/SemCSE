Effect of Heat Treatment on Residual Austenite of High Vanadium High Speed Steel
The amount of residual austenite in high vanadium high speed steel was measured by ferromagnetism method.The effect of heat treatment technique on the amount of residual austenite was studied.The results show that the amount of residual austenite rise when the quenching temperature increase,but the amount of residual austenite decrease when the tempering temperature increase;At the quenching temperature (900～1100℃)when the tempering temperature is lower than 450℃,the amount of residual austenite has slight variation.But the amount of residual austenite sharply decreases if the tempering temperature reaches 550℃.
