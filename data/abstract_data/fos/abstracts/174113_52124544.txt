Fabrication and Characterization of SQUIDs With Nb/NbxSi1- x/Nb Junctions
Superconducting quantum interference devices (SQUIDs) with 3 <italic>μ</italic>m × 3 <italic>μ</italic>m or 4 <italic>μ</italic>m × 4 <italic>μ</italic>m self-shunted Nb/Nb<italic><sub>x</sub></italic>Si<sub> 1−</sub><italic><sub>x</sub></italic>/Nb Josephson junctions are designed and fabricated. By adjusting the Nb content and thickness of the Nb<italic><sub>x</sub></italic>Si<sub>1−</sub><italic><sub>x</sub></italic> barrier, the parameters of the SQUIDs can be easily tuned. The optimal parameters for the barrier are found to be a thickness of around 15 nm and 7% Nb content. The <italic>I–V</italic> curve, <italic>V–Φ </italic> curve, and noise spectrum are measured for the SQUID. The high frequency noise for the SQUID is only about several hundred nΦ<sub>0</sub>/√Hz. Another advantage for these devices is found, that it is not sensitive to the static electricity.
