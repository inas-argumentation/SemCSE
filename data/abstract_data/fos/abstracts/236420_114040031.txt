Study on Resonance Phenomena Caused by Energization of Transformer
This paper presents an analysis of high frequency resonance caused by switching on the unload transformers,including its equivalent circuit,basic equation,resonance condition,origin of harmonic energy and its analytical methods.The typical resonance phenomena from EHV transmission system are studied by means of EMTP and the simulation results are shown in the paper.The potential problem caused by energization of transformer and its countermeasure for different system conditions are discussed in this paper.
