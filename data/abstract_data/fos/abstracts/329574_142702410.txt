Evo’s Bolivia: Continuity and Change by Linda C. Farthing and Benjamin H. Kohl (review)
The rise to power and subsequent presidencies of Evo Morales have fascinated the world, compelled by the story of the man who Linda Farthing and Ben Kohl dub “the most famous Bolivian ever” (p. 1). Socialist, anti-imperialist, and indigenous, Evo, as he is universally known, has intrigued observers both foreign and domestic, as he has set about implementing his plans for the complete reinvention of his country. This book recounts the recent history of Bolivia under Evo, exploring the goals, programs, and challenges of this reinvention. It is a book as compelling as the man it places at its center, highly readable and engaging. With its detailed reporting, multiplicity of voices, and balanced perspective, Evo’s Bolivia will surely be considered the go-to text for understanding the transformations of Bolivian society under this charismatic and powerful president.
