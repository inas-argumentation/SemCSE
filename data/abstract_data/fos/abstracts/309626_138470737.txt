Analysis on Thermal Stress of Bi_4Ti_3O_(12) Ferroelectric Films Based on ANSYS Numerical Simulation
The thermal stress of Bi4Ti3O12thin film after annealing was simulated by ANSYS software. The influences of thickness of BTO film on the thermal stress were researched. The results show that when the thickness of BTO film is 0.3 μm, intrinsic compressive stress is induced after annealing processing. When the thickness of the film varies from 0.1 μm to 1 μm, the thermal stress transforms from the maximum compressive stress to tensile one and increases with the thickness increasing.
