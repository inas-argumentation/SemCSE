Geographical Tracing of Wuyi Shuixian Tea with Multiple Fingerprints
Wuyi Shuixian Tea(Camellia sinensis L. cv. Wuyi Shuixian) is an important tea variety for Wuyi Rock essence tea. Based on the different micro eco-enviroment, there are two kinds of production areas, named famous arears and normal areas. In this article, the quality compounds were compared between these two areas production. The results showed that there were extremely significant difference(p0.01) in Ni, Ba, Mn, significant difference(p0.05) in Cr, K and Cd, but no obvious difference(p0.05) in tea polyphenols, caffeine, crude fiber, total amino acids, theaflavins, thearubigins, theabrownin, amino acids composition, tea polyphenols composition, F, Fe, Ca, Mg, Al, P, Cu, Zu, Sn, Pb, Na, Mo, Co, and As. The multiple fingerprints were applied to establish discriminal model. Mn, Ni, Cd, Ba and Cr were involved in the model, for their Pearson chi square conefficient greater than 0.95. The accuracy of geographical tracing prediction model was 88.24%. The results also indicated that mineral fingerprints were suitable for geographical tracing of Wuyi Shuixian tea.
