An Empirical Analysis of Relations between Fund Flows and Stock Returns
This article researches the relations between fund flows and stock returns through constructing the variable of flow's model,finding that there is a negative relation between fund flows and stock returns.The higher of the flow,the lower of the stock returns,Vice versa.Comparing the flowing effect with the value effect and the momentum effect,we find that they are influencing factors which does not contain mutually,this emphasized the stability between fund flows and stock returns.Last,we explain this Phenomenon,and give some suggestions.
