Deep Potentials of the NN Interactions with Forbidden States
The three variant of the potentials with tensor part and ioao containing unobservable forbidden states and having one fitted parameter are considered. Parameter of such potentials is fixed only on the base low energy NN characteristics and deuteron binding energies. The NN characteristics at high energies, deuteron properties and formfactors up to 6 Fm-1 is appear in the good consent with experimental data.
