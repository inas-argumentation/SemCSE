Ecological Adaptation of Vietnamese Coconut Resources in Hainan
The collection and evaluation of germplasm were the basis of modern breeding,The evaluation of coconut germplasm resources of Vietname will provide valuable information for breeding new coconut variety.According to the discriptors standard for germplasm resources of coconut,some indices were measured.The result of statistical analysis showed that the stem thickness,petiole color,trunk growth vigor were the important indices to distinguish the tall with dwarf germplasm resources;floral colors,the number of female flowers could be as the critical factor for morphological evaluation on germplasm resources;according to fruit color,the germplasm resources were classified into redness,brown,ivory,the germplasm resources were divided into aromatic and sweetness on the basisi of special flavour of coconut water and content of soluble solids.
