Microstructure and Properties of 20MnSiCrV Corrosion Resistant Rebar in Continuous Cooling Transformation
Using dilatometric change referring microscopic test and hardness measurement, the Gleeble-3800C thermal-mechanical simulator was utilized to measure different dilatometric curves for overcooling austenite of 20MnSiCrV corrosion resistant rebar with different cooling rates. The continuous cooling transformation curves(dynamic CCT curve) for 20MnSiCrV steel were obtained and compared with 20MnSiV steel. The results show that the addition of Cr and the reduction of Mn content in 20MnSiCrV steel can lead to the transrformation temperature of ferrite and pearlite rising and the phase transition zone of bainite enlarges. The proper microstructure and properties can be obtained when the range of controlling cooling rates is 0.5-3 ℃/s. All those provide references for developing controlling cooling process.
