Exploitation and Restraint on Agricultural Economy in South of Shanxi by Immigrants in the Qing Dynasty
As the result of a large number of immigrants' moving in,many mountains were exploited widely in south of Shaanxi in the Qing Dynasty, cultivated areas increased,the degree of exploitation and utilization for wasteland exceeded any period in history,the speed of economy development was accelerated apparently,and promoted the development of economy in south of Shanxi objectively.But at the same time,since immigrants' excessive increasing and crushing exploitation of wide scope,they destroyed forests and opened up wasteland unlimited,enlarged the cultivated areas blindly,then the natural environment of south of Shanxi was destroyed seriously,the natural calamity occurred frequently,and the continuous development of economy of south of Shanxi was influenced and restricted at last.
