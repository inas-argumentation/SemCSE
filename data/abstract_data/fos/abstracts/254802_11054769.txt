Pressure as a kinetic parameter in mechanistic studies of chemical reactions induced by flash photolysis and pulse radiolysis
A detailed outline is given of how pressure can be used as a mechanistic indicator in kinetic studies of chemical reactions in solution that are induced by flash photolysis and pulse radiolysis. These techniques have mainly been applied to tlie study of organometallic and bioinorganic reactions including processes such as ligand substitution, binding of small molecules, formation and breakage of metal-carbon bonds, 0-elimination and electron transfer.
