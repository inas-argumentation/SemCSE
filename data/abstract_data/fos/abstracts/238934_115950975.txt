Hartree-Fock-Rechnungen mit Paritätsmischung in den Einteilchenfunktionen
A mixture of one-particle states of opposite parities introduced into the framework of the Hartree-Fock theory leads to an additional spin-dependent potential in the Hartree-Fock equation, which provides aj-splitting similar to the shell modell prediction. This splitting depends strongly on the strength of the nucleon-nucleon tensor force. For an effective two-body potential with a tensor part like the Hamada-Johnston potential the calculated splitting agrees in sign and magnitude with the observed values. The calculations are carried through for the nuclei He 5, C 12, N 15, O 16 and O 17.
