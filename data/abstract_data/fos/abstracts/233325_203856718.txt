Cloning of the major gene segments of group B rotavirus ADRV and RDRV and their comparison
Cloning of the major gene segments of group B rotavirus ADRV and RDRV and their comparison$$$$ YANG Hongyan*, JI Shaozhong, KANG Zhigang, et al. *Institute of Epidemiology and Microbiology, Chinese Academy of Preventive Medicine, Beijing 102206 【Abstract】 Objective To compare the major gene segments of group B rotavirus adult diarrhea rotavirus (ADRV) and infant rat diarrhea rotavirus (RDRV) which share the same RNAs migration pattern on polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis (PAGE). Methods The full length cDNA of ADRV gene 4, 5, 9 were produced by reverse transcription and polymerase chain reaction (RTPCR) from dsRNA of ADRV with terminal primers of each gene segments, respectively. And the above cDNAs were cloned. The dsRNA of RDRV was amplified successfully with the terminal primer of ADRV gene 9. The molecular weight of the RDRV PCR product was same as that of ADRV gene 9s 814bp (the RNA templet was called RDRV segment 9 temporarily) and the clone was demonstrated. The four ADRV gene 4,5,9 and RDRV gene 9 full length cDNA probes were made from aimed digested gene segments by labelling with Digoxigenin. Genic identity was studied with Dot and Northern hybridization. Results The full length cDNA clones of ADRV gene 4,5,9 and RDRV gene 9 were obstained. The hybridization signals of ADRV and RDRV RNA homologizing dots and fragments were all positive and the every negative control was negative. Conclusion RDRV share high identity with ADRV identified with Dot and Northern blot hybridization using the probes of ADRV gene 4,5,9 and RDRV gene 9. 【Subject words】 Adult diarrhea rotavirus Rat diarrhea rotavirus Genic identity Gene,viral
