Comparison between Private Education Promotion Law and Private School Law in Taiwan
Based on a comparison between Private Education Promotion Law and Private School Law in Taiwan, analyze the difference between the two laws in the legislative purpose, definitions of private education and private school, nature, application scope, the related rules for the boards of regents, fees, taxes, property rights as well as the rights and interests of faculties. Thorough comparison, provide theoretical basis for further improvement of Private Education Promotion Law.
