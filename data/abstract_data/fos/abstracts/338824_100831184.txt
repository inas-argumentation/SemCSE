Mechanical Model of Micro-particle on Surface Adhesion
Mathematical model of micro-particle adhesion is the basis for comprehension and control of micro-particles which greatly affect industries,agriculture and people's health.Many studies regarding to adhesion including physical adsorption such as Van der Waals force,electrostatic force,magnetic force and capillary force were summarized,and actual application of various models was described.An example was given to illustrate simple coupling of several forces to micro-particle.The future studies were also prospected.
