Analysis of Student's Absorption Amount of Classroom Knowledge
Increasing absorption rate in classrooms is an important way to improve classroom teaching. In this article, a mathematical mode of dynamic with the method of differential is set up. This kind of mode can be used to describe the teaching process. The article also gives full explanation about the factors and inner links, which affect the absorption rate in classrooms. At the end, the article gives a certain analysis of these factors with boundary layer and the ways of increasing absorption rate and its theoretical basis.
