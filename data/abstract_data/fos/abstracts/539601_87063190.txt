The mechanism about the proliferation of Schwann cells affected by β1,4-galactosyltransferase-I
Objective: To study the mechanism about the proliferation of Schwann cells affected by β-1,4-galactosyltransferase-I(β-1,4-GalT-I). Methods: The changes of prolife ration related signaling molecules like cyclin D1?p16INK4a and p27Kip1 in Schwann ceIls transfected with β-1,4-GalT-I plasmids of various concentrations were studied by Western blot, and p19ARF was studied by Northern blot. Results: The expression of cyclin D1 in Schwann cells decreased when the concentrations of transfected β-1, 4-GalT-I expression plasmids increased, while expression of p16INK4a and p19ARF had opposite change. The expression of p27Kip1 had no obvious change. Conclusion: β-1, 4-GalT-I affects the proliferation of schwann cells through cyclin D1?p16INK4a and p19ARF-associated signal pathway.
