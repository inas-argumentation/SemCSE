The transition of the idea of modern university in China——From Jingshi Daxuetang Zhangcheng to Daxue Ling
The Jingshi Daxuetang established in 1898 displayed the form but not the real- ity of a university.The so called"form"refers to the fact that it borrowed relevant system of a modern university and introduced relevant instruction contents from Japan indirectly. While,"reality"so called was still under the influence of traditional ideas,not free from the old-fashioned bureau nature.The phenomenon mentioned above can be reflected in Qinding Jingshi Daxuetang Zhangcheng(regulations of Peking University made in 1902)and Jingshi Daxuetang zhangcheng made in 1904.Not until the appearance of Daxue Ling chiefly made by Cai yuanpei at the beginning of the Republic of China can the university idea of modern u- niversity see the transition from tradition to modern.
