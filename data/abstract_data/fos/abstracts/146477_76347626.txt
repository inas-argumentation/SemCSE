The immune regulation of Dermatophagoides farinae extract induced inflammatory reaction in asthma mice after exposure to endotoxin
Objective To explore the effect of exposure to endotoxin during different phase on the in- flammatory reaction in allergic asthma mice. Methods Sixty mice were divided into four groups: asthma group using Dermatophagoidesfarinae (Der f) allergen to sensitize and challenge, control group with saline instead of al- lergen, experimental group A (pro-sensitization LPS+Der f allergen) and experimental group B(post-sensitization LPS+Der f allergen). Isolating the lung of mice and making the pathological section stained by hematoxylin and eosin. Counting the total and differential cell in BALF. Measuring the concentrations of cytokines and specific im- munoglobulin by ELISA. Results Compared with asthma group, experimental group A had a less total cell and EOS, lower IL-4 and IL-5, a higher IL-12 in BALF(P0.05), lower sIgE and higher sIgG2a in serum(P0.05), but experimental group B only had a higher IL-4 in BALF(P0.05). Conclusion The exposure to LPS during pro-sensitization can inhibit the infiltration of inflammatory cells and down-medulate the T_H2 response of al- lergic asthma, but that during post-sensitization could not have the function. It is suggested that the early exposure to endotoxin could have a protective effect on allergic asthma.
