Research on agility of the college education service
Since the college education in our country has come into the stage of "the popularization education" and the employment problem has outstandingly appeared,agility of the college education service should attract people's attention.We believed that,we can further expand the choosing range of the curriculum through the suitable detention of the time of choosing major in the current situation,reasonably reduce the teaching interval,reduce the reaction time by fully using the modern information technology,release teaching management jurisdiction and reduce response links.Then we can practically enhance the agility of the college education service.Therefore,it is advantageous for the university student,the society and so on to be satisfied.It is also advantageous to improve the core competitive power of university itself.
