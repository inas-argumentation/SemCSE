Subject Honorification in Korean: In Defense of Agr and Head-Spec Agreement
In this paper, I claim that a traditional insight that Korean subject honorification involves syntactic agreement is basically correct, contrary to Kim and Sells (2007), although I follow their position that previous accounts which are based on an [Hon] feature on a noun cannot be maintained due to numerous counterexamples. In order to capture the insight and explain the counterexamples, I propose that what is involved is agreement in terms of a [Speaker Honorification] feature under a Spec-head configuration and its interactions with some non-linguistic conventions concerning honorification. I further propose that the agreement host is not in Spec, but in head, since the feature is interpretable on -si/o, while it is uninterpretable on a noun. This leads me to propose that -si/o forms Agr in Korean and head-Spec agreement is involved in Korean subject honorification.
