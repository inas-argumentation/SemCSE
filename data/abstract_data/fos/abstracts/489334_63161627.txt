Aspect-Oriented Formal Method of Heterogeneons Dynamic Distributed Real-Time Systems
Formal Method is based on the mathematical,it contents precise mathematical meaning that can be used as methods of scientific research and verification. The development of Distributed Real-Time Systems is very complicated and can't use one development method for analysis,design and implementation. Because of Formal Method contents characteristics of mathematical analysis and certification,we can develop the system under different aspects which can use different formal languages to achieve,and then weave these areas into the system.
