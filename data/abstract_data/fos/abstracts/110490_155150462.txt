Influence of Slight Substitution (Mn/In) on Thermoelectric and Magnetic Properties in Chalcopyrite-Type CuInTe2
Alternative energy sources such as thermoelectric materials can play an important role to deal with waste heat problems. Currently new and improved thermoelectric materials are in high demand for energy harvesting from waste heat. In this research we synthesized and investigated thermoelectric and magnetic properties of CuIn1-xMnxTe2 where x = 0.0, 0.01, 0.02. All samples were densified by spark plasma sintering. Powder x-ray diffraction confirmed phase pure tetragonal structure for all nominal compositions with space group I–42d. Electrical resistivity showed a slight decrease upon Mn-doping, while the Seebeck coefficient was not reduced by doping, resulting in an enhanced power factor. Furthermore, relative power factor with respect to x = 0 as a function of temperature revealed the nature of the conduction mechanism changes after 450 K. It is observed that power factor enhancement was significant in the extrinsic region. Magnetic susceptibility verses temperature graphs showed paramagnetic behavior with a large negative Weiss temperature, which suggest relatively large antiferromagnetic interactions. Thermal conductivity decreased by 15–20% upon Mn-doping. Therefore, Mn-doping gives a totally positive effect on the figure of merit (ZT). Highest ZT value of 0.82 was observed for x = 0.02 at 854 K, which is close to 30% higher than non-doped sample.
