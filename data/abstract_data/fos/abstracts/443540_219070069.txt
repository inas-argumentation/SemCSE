Predicting the Floods that Follow the Flames
Postwildfire Hydrology Workshop What: More than 40 participants, representing government agencies in research, training, and operations; nongovernmental organizations; emergency management; and academia, met for two days in person and online to discuss the monitoring technologies, forecasting tools, and community mitigation efforts in preparation for flash flooding, severe erosion, and debris flows that often follow wildfires. When: 2–3 October 2019 Where: Norman, Oklahoma
