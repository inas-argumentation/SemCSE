Development of Flow Behavior in Molten Pool of Twin-roll Casting
Through an overview of the development of flow behavior in molten pool of twin-roll casting,it was pointed out and analyzed that the structure and size of the outlet,height and fluctuations of pool level,casting speed,impact depth of flow and turbulence conditions of solidification zone are the main factors resulting flow behavior in molten pool.And According to these reasons,Twin-roll casting quality was improved by optimizing molten pool technology.
