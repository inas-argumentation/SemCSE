Study on Application of Decoppering Technology in Tin Solder
In electronic and photovoltaic industry,to achieve the product requirements,Tin solder should be plated on copper substrate surface.In the actual production,hot-dip tin can be mostly used,due to high temperature in operating,which can cause thermal erosion on copper,increase copper content in the Tin tank,and affect the performance of the physical,chemical and use,ultimately the welding technology becomes unstable.As to this problem,the decoppering technology was studied,and different methods for decoppering were proposed.
