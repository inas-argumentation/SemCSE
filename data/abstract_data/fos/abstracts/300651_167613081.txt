Research on the Countermeasures of broadening the financing channel of medium and small-sized enterprises in our country
Medium and small-sized enterprises are the most active main economic body in our national economy development currently. They are most possible to organize the production in accordance with the comparison advantage because of the small scale and the flexible mode of operation and have become the important parts of our country's economy development. The medium and small-sized enterprises promote the progress of science and technology, the increase of employment and the expansion of export, but meanwhile they are now facing the problem of financing difficulties and this becomes the bottleneck that restrict the their development. Thus, how to broaden their financing channel becomes the problem that should be solved soon.
