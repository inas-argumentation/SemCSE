On the Loopholes of China's Witness Testifying System in Civil Procedure
In civil proceedings,witnesses' testifying in court is the basic requirement for achieving judicial justice.Due to the incompleteness of China's legislation of evidence system as well as some subjective and objective factors of witnesses,the phenomena of witnesses' not appearing in court or perjuring in court are not rare in judicial practices.Therefore,it is necessary to improve the system of testifying in court through defining the rights and obligations of witnesses,establishing a compulsory testifying rule,and standardizing procedures of testifying in court.
