FSTM:a Subjective Trust Model Based on Fuzzy Theory for P2P Network
The feature of the peer-to-peer system such as user anonymity,open nature makes that it's hard to establish the trust among participants by the objective confirmation mechanism.This paper presents a new P2P reputation system based on the fuzzy set theory.It employs fuzzy operators to deal with the issues of trust vector's transfer and coalition,and then apply them to calculate the transitive closure of global trust relation matrix.Plus,with the help of distributed hash tables,the trust value can propagate easily.Experiments show that the model can improve the rate of successful barter greatly,and the computing cost of the whole peer-to-peer network is comparatively less.
