Superplasticity of Ti-6Al-4V Butt-welding Plate by High Energy Beam Welding
The superplasticity of Ti-6Al-4V butt-welding plates by plasma arc welding(PAW),electron beam welding(EBW) and laser beam welding(LBW) were studied through uni-axial hot tensile tests and simple bulging tests at superplastic forming temperature 925℃.The results show that both of butt welded plates by LBW and EBW possess good superplastic ability,the superplastic capability of LBW is slightly better than that of EBW.The butt welded plate by PAW doesn't possess superplasticity during uni-axial tensile test,however,it can be bulged in the superplastic condition.The higher the input energy density is,the quicker the cooling rate of weld metal is,and the smaller the weld microstructure is too.Finally,according to the experimental results,m values,the strain rate sensitive index for Ti alloy butt-welding plates of three kinds of high-energy beam welding,are evaluated respectively by finite element method.
