Solutions of Faddeev-Yakubovsky Equations in Configuration Space for the 4N Scattering States
The Faddeev-Yakubovsky equations in configuration space have been solved for the four nucleon system with special interest in their scattering states. We present results concerning the structure of the first 4He, scattering states in different (T,S) channels including the first inelatic threshold N+3N→NN+NN and the n-t cross section using realistic potentials.
