Determination of Microcystins in Cyanobacteria Using Immunoaffinity Chromatography Column and High Performance Liquid Chromatography/Mass Spectrometry
A method was present to detect microcystins(MCs) in cyanobacteria by using home-made immunoaffinity chromatography(IAC) column,solid-phase extraction(SPE) and high performance liquid chromatograph/mass spectrometry.There was much matrix interference in sample if it was only concentrated by SPE column,which could interfere with the determination of MCs(MC-LR especially) in cyanobacteria.Most of interference could be removed and accurate results could be obtained if it was cleaned by IAC column after concentrated by SPE column.LOD was 2.5 μg/L,and the linear range was from 5 μg/L to 500 μg/L.Recoveries of MC-RR and MC-LR were higher than 84%,and RSDs were lower than 5%.The method was sensitive and accurate for quantification of MCs in cyanobacteria,and it could be applied in other samples which was similar to cyanobacteria.
