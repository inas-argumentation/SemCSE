Research on Security Issues and Preventive Strategies of Small Business Website
Along with the development of network technology, Website to the role of enterprises is in-creasingly obvious, but the attacks have intensified on the site. Aiming at Web security prob-lems of small and medium-sized enterprise' Website, analyses existing security policy and at-tack to the application layer, puts forward some corresponding solutions and security strategy tomake Website reduce attack, and provides technical support.
