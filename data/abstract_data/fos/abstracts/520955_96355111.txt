Phase transitions and oxygen content in the Nd2−xSrxNiO4+δ system
The Nd 2− x Sr x NiO 4+ δ (0⩽ x ⩽1) system prepared under different surrounding atmospheres has been studied by means of pow X-ray diffraction and thermogravimetric analysis. The accurate control of oxygen content in this system leads to three different K 2 NiF 4 -related structural types which are also dependent upon the Sr content.
