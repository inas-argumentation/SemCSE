The expected professional position and the individual needs of higher vocational education
The social needs of higher vocational education includes economic needs and individual needs; the latter has more direct influences on the recruitment of higher vocational schools. Because of social stratification, the individual needs of each student focuses on trying to optimize his or her expectation of professional position. Three factors are crucial for the expectation of professional position: chances for graduates choosing their jobs, employment rate, and evaluation of vocations. Now the expectation of professional position of higher vocational schools is rather low in general, so that there is lack of attraction to students. We can enhance the expectation of professional position of higher vocational schools through conducting propaganda among the masses, adopting "Order-type programs", and linking up special courses with undergraduate courses.
