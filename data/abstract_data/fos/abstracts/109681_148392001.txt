On student evaluation of teaching and improvement of the teaching quality assurance system in HEIs
As a fundamental policy for the teaching quality assurance system in HEIs,student evaluation of teaching is utilized to voice students' opinion about teaching,and establish the objective of serving students.However,there are still some problems in practice,to name just a few,students often substitute others to make their evaluation,and formal significance has been taken more importance than the innermost significance,evaluation results are often misused,and some loopholes exist in the design of evaluation system,etc.Therefore,we should establish correct view of student evaluation of teaching,construct scientific system of student evaluation of teaching,and draw upon the sophisticated experience from abroad so as to perfect the system of student evaluation of teaching,and the quality assurance system of university instruction in China.
