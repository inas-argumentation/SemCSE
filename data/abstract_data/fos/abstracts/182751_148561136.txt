Root-seeking——Analysis of Song of Solomon written by Toni Morrison
In Song of Solomon, the author tries to find a way out for black American people. As a symbol of his nation, the protagonist Milkman is faced with different ways of his father, his aunt and his friend Guitar. Influenced by the great woman Pilate, he finally returns to his ancestor's land and realizes the value of his national culture. His choice conveys the author's opinion: the black American people should base on their traditional culture to get the strength and confidence to build up their own nation.
