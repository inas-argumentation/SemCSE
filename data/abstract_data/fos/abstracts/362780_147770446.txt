Positioning and development of humanities in universities of science and technology
Developing appropriately in the universities of science and technology is necessary.The development of humanities could suit themselves-position and level rather than pursuit the large scale including all humanities.The key measures are as follows:The humanities should show the characteristics of their own engineering and get support from their own advantage science and technology subjects;They should highlight the integration and correlation of subjects and show the advantage of cross-edge;They should set up some scientific humanities majors and courses to meet the demands of national and local economic construction.
