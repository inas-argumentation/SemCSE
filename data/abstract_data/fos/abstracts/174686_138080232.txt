Effects of ZrO_2 on Solid-state Boronizing of TC4 Titanium Alloy
Effects of adding ZrO2 to solid boronizing agent(B4C and SiC) on surface morphology,phase constitute,thickness,hardness,layer-substrate bonding force and friction wear properties of the boronized layer on TC4 alloy were investigated.The results show that the adding of ZrO2(4.0wt%) to the solid boronizing agent changes the phase constitute of the boronized layer(the phase of adding ZrO2 is composed of TiB,Ti2B5,TiC and Ti while the other is composed of TiB2,TiB12 and TiN),improves the thickness and hardness(35.0% and 6.99%,respectively),raises the layer-substrate bonding force(41.17%) and improves the spalling resistance and crack resistance during the friction wear process.
