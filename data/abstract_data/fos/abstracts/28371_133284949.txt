Evaluation of Academic Influence of Agricultural Science and Technology Journals--a Case Study of Chinese Journal of Tropical Crops
The keywords fr equency, citation frequency, download frequency, the paper statistics column article citations, download frequency and impact factor that CNKI retrieval for China Journal of Tropical Crops from2010-2014 were used to analyze the academic influence of the columns of agricultural science and technology periodicals. The results showed that China Journal of Tropical Crops covers rubber tree, cassava, sugar cane,banana, rice, litchi, longan, mango and papaya. Crop nutrition, cultivation and breeding, plant physiology and biochemistry, biotechnology application, tissue culture, crop diseases and insect pests and their control,environmental protection, agricultural products processing and analysis were the main columns. The impact factor of the review column in China Journal of tropical crops was the highest. The biological technology section had more papers. Based on the above findings, suggestions such as planning quality columns, strengthening editorial column of soliciting contributions, inviting authors for the review column, creating characteristic columns, making good use of new media according to the analysis result were supposed to reflect the special features and publication purpose of the periodical and to provide reading guide for the authors.
