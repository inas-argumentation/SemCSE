Cloning and biologic activity analysis of novel interferon alpha subtype gene of woodchuck
Objective To investigate the function of interferon alpha(IFN-α) in the woodchuck (Mar-mota monax) model of hepatitis B, the woodchuck IFN-αfamily gene(IFNA) was cloned, and analysis cellular immune status of woodchuck with chronic woodchuck hepatitis virus(WHV) infection. Methods Peripheral blood mononuclear cells(PBMC) of naive and chronic WHV infection woodchuck were cultured, then investigate antiviral biological activity of culture supernatants from PBMC stimulated with different concentration Poly I-C. Plasmids harbored woodchuck interferon alpha gene with different genotype were generated using molecular cloning technology. All sequence were sequenced and subtyped. Biologic activity of interferon was detected by viral protection assay. Results The data indicate that we obtain 10 novel IFNA from 36 wIFNA sequences, including 8 functional sequences and 2 pseudogene sequences. There are lower titer of interferon from PBMC of chronic infection woodchuck than that of naive woodchuck(P 0.01). Conclusion The cellular immune function of woodchuck with chronic WHV infection is impaired. The novel IFN-αgene of woodchuck was cloned and characterized to provide a tool with which to test the antiviral effect of new forms of IFN-αdelivery and to improve the efficacy of current therapies by woodchuck HBV model.
