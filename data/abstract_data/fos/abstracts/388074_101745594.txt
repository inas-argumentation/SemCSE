Selective Catalytic Reduction of NO_x by NH_3 Used in Diesel Vehicles
Basic principles of the selective catalytic reduction(SCR)of NOx by NH3 were introduced.In order to develop SCR catalysts used in diesel,the disadvantages and advantages of TiO2 and zeolite catalysts as well as problems and corresponding measures with respect to NOx abatement from diesel by TiO2 and zeolite catalysts were discussed as well.
