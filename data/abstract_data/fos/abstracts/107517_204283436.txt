Influence of Heat Treatment Technology on Tensile Property of 30CrMnSi Steel
The influence of heat treatment technology on tensile properties of 30CrMnSi steel at room temperature was analyzed by using electronic testing machine.The hardness change law was finded by rockwell hardness test.The surface fracture morphologies were analyzed by SEM.The experimental results show that,after quenching+ mid-temperature tempering and quenching+high-temperature tempering,the steel has the high hardness,high plastic-toughness and good integrated mechanical properties.
