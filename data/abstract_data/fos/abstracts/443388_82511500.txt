Identification of a Zucchini Yellow Mosaic Virus Isolate from Siraitia grosvenori(Swingle) C.Jeffrey in Guangxi Province and Preparation of Coat Protein Antiserum
A filamentous virus was found that causes rugose and mosaic symptoms in Siraitia grosvenorii (Swingle) C.Jeffrey in Yongfu county of Guangxi Province. Based on its biological,morphological and cytopathological characteristics,isolate LGL-1 was primarily identified as a member of family Potyviridae.Using the degenerated primer of family Potyviridae,the 3′-terminal sequence of isolate LGL-1 was determined.BLAST search confirmed that it was an isolate of Zucchini yellow mosaic virus(ZYMV).Phylogenetic analysis based on the coat protein nucleotide sequences of ZYMV isolates confirmed the existence of three main groups.LGL-1 belongs to the newly reported Group III which is only found in China so far.A polyclonal antiserum of isolate LGL-1 was raised against the expected coat protein.Indirect ELISA result suggested that ZYMV was the main potyvirus harmful to the production of Luohanguo plants in Guangxi Province.The differences between this antiserum and the antiserum prepared by using purified viral particles in ELISA were also discussed.
