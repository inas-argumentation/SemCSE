Mutation Induction of EMS in Vitro Cultured Oncidium and RAPD Analyzing of Regenerated Plantlets
Pieces of thin cell layers(TCLs) of Oncidium protocorm-like bodies(PLBs) were treated in vitro with EMS for the induction of mutation and it was found that mutagen EMS had some TCLs got browning and died.The regenerated PLBs grew slowly and the number of regenerated plantlets were reduced.There were obvious injured effects on PLBs,but less injured effects on plantlets.The injured effects got more and more serious with higher concentration of mutagen and longer time treatment.The results of 10 primers of RAPD reaction indicated that the molecule maps of RAPD had some polymorphisms which mean the sequence of DNA had changed.The ratio of polymorphisms got higher with higher concentration of mutagens and longer time treatment.However,the ratio of RAPD polymorphisms had no obvious raised when the concentration of EMS reached to 0.4%.The concentrations of 0.4% EMS treatment are suitable dosage for Oncdium chemical induction in vitro.
