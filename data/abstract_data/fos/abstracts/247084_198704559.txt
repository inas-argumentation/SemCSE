Surviving the ups and downs
The volatility of the milk price, driven by supply and demand, is of huge concern to milk producers. It is hoped that we are now at a turning point. Although global prices remain low, milk price increases have been observed from some processors. We trust that the price will soon be increased to a viable level for all producers. The milk price has its ups and downs, influenced by macro-economic factors about which producers can do little. The price certainly matters, but so do the farmer's other expenses.
