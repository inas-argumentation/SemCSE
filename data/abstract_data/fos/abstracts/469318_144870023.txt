One question that kept coming up for me is the extent to which this type of tourism may result in exotifying resistance, revolution and authenticity as experiences that are only available abroad, rather than as goals, projects, and political ideals that can be nurtured and found "back home." Indeed, the author's interviews with tourists open up interesting insights about the ideological and political "work" that this type of tourism entails, not only for locals but for the tourists themselves. Yet, it is the influence of tourism in helping to fashion Latin American nations and histories that is the core of the book, and in this regard we see many examples of how these tourist strategies have contradictory effects. Sometimes, tiiey help to revalorize histories and traditions at the very same time that these are appropriated for international consumption, and the author seems to delight in locals' ability to profit and take charge of their culture for tourist ends. Sex and romance tourism, however, is overtly troubling, because of the perils of sex trafficking and sexual predation of youth.
