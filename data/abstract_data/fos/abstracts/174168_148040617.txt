On integration mechanism in multidisciplinary higher educational research
Multidisciplinary research is considered a unique methodology in higher educational research.However,there is little attention on the properties and limitations of this methodology.Researchers use it in their studies according to their own understanding,interests and abilities.Higher educational multidisciplinary study is at primary stage for a long time and it simply borrows other disciplinary views to explain higher education issues.On the one hand,it weakens the effect of multidisciplinary study.On the other hand,it may bring some deviations from research goals of higher education.Integration mechanism of multidisciplinary research aims at pushing multidisciplinary research from primary stage to advanced stage,making higher education research serving theory and practice better.
