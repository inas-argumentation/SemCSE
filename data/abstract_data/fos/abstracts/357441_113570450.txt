Analysis of Special Operation Mode of Ground Wire Laying
According to construction of ground wire laying of #30～#45 tower in 220kV Pingjiang line,research on the electric distribution during the construction period was carried on.Special operation mode ensuring power grid operating safety was schemed,power load analysis and risk assessment were presented according to the scheme.Finally,considering potential problems,a pre-planning was schemed to ensure the equipment safety and 220 kV power supply system reliability during the construction period.
