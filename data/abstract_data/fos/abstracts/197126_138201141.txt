Gas Interspace Pressure in Liquid Metal Flowage Frontier in Evaporated Pattern Casting(EPC)
The gas pressure(P膜) in the front of liquid metal flowage in Evaporated Pattern Casting is figured out that it is directly proportional to the product of vertical path(S直) area and the flow velocity when liquid metal flows in it,pouring temperature(T膜),density of model(ρ模),thickness of covering material(H).It is inversely proportional to vacuum(P真),level perimeter of casting(S) and permeability(K)increasing.And it is validated by production practice.
