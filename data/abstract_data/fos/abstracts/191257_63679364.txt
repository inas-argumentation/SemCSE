Improved Algorithm of Mining Fuzzy Multilevel Association Rules Based on Implication Degree
Aimming at the deficiency of fuzzy multilevel association rules mining algorithm, introduces implication degree, implement the fuzzy multiple-leveled association rules mining algorithm base on implication degree. Implication degree instead of confidence dgree and using fuzzy implication operator,implication degree is further derived by support degree,thus the program execution time was effectively shortened. The experiment results prove that the efficiency of fuzzy multilevel association rules mining algorithm improved by using the method of implication degree instead of confidence dgree.
