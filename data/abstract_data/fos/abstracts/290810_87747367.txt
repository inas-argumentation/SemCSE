Identification and inheritance analysis of transgenic maize by PFGE
The foreign gene(Bar and B.t) was successfully transferred into maize seed by the PFGE(pulsed field gel electrophoresis,PFGE) method.T_0 and T_1 generation plants screened with PPT were further confirmed by PCR.The results revealed that transformation frequency of T_1 generation was much lower than PCR positive frequency of T_0 generation.The both changing frequencies were correlative significant(r=0.9874).T_1 maize plants tested by PCR were confirmed by Southern blot.The results demonstrated that foreign gene was single copy in transformants by PFGE.The exogenous gene inherited from T_1 generation to T_2 generation and showed typical Mendelian segregation in the T_2 generation.
