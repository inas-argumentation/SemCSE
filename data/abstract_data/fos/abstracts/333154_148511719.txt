On the tridimensional structure model of the university students' mental quality
This paper constructed and discussed the structure of the university students' mental quality through combining the empirical method of questionnaire survey and theoretical analysis about connotative meaning of mental quality.The university students' mental quality is the three-dimensional structure,which is made up of cognitive trait,personality and adaptability.The cognitive trait and personality dimensions constitute the basic plane of the university students' mental quality,while adaptability dimension is the integrated representation of cognitive trait and personality in the university students' social environment.
