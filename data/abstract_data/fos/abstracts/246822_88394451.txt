The Dynamic of Population,Virulence and Sporulation of Beauveria bassiana Survived in Soil
The CFUs (colony forming unit) of Beauveria bassiana that survived in soil decreased slowly within the first two months, and the CFUs under different treatments of 1 g, 5 g and 10 g decreased by 50%, 60% and 64%, respectively, but after the first two months, the CUs dropped sharply in a rate of 85%-95% every month. The CFUs of Beauveria could be monitored in a level of 10~(3)-10~(4) CFU/sample after 1 year survival. Too high quantity of spores was detrimental to the long survival of Beauveria bassiana. The virulence and sporulation of Beauveria isolates kept relatively stable in soil.
