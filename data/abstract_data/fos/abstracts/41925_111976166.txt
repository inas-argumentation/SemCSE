Building the College Campus Culture of Taking Innovative Study as Orientation
Particularity that university students include value orientation of study, attitude towards study and study the behavior etc. in innovative study. It is a need of the social development to build culture of the campus for the orientation with innovative study, need of growing up by oneself that students too. In view of the above, this text offer suggestions for campus culture of orientation to learn to build: ( 1) incorporate the culture of the campus of the innovative study orientation; (2 ) train the innovative study behavior of university students; ( 3) form such practicality contents as the resultant of forces of the orientation campus cultural construction of innovative study.
