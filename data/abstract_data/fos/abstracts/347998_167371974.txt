The Applying Technique of Wireless Public Telephone in Passenger Train
According to a recent survey,the passengers transported by trains is over one billion person-time at every year,communications demand in passenger train is latent huge communications market.In order to develop this passenger train communications market so that we can provide the commercial communication services for passengers in the trains.The applying technique of wireless public telephone in passenger train is always stressed by the leaders of the Ministry of Railways and also discussed as a task by chinatietong.This paper studied deeply the applying technique of wireless public telephone in passenger train.
