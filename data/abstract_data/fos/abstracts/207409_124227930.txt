Mathematical simulation of the physical process to fabricate hollow glass microspheres by liquid droplet method
The physical process to fabricate hollow glass microspheres(HGM) by the Liquid Droplet Method in a 7-meter-high multi-zone high temperature furnace has been modeled mathematically. And the model quantitatively describes the whole process, through which the main parameters of the droplets/membrane spheres/HGM including the velocity, the sizes, the glass-forming oxide mass, the wall-thicknesses, and the inner gas pressures of vapor and air inside can be calculated. Calculation has been done under a series of operation conditions, especially at different purge gas rates, initial concentrations of the blowing agent and those of glass-forming oxides in the glass-forming solutions, and sizes of the initial droplets. The calculated results are to some degree in good agreement with the experiments carried out under the same conditions as the calculation. It is proved that the model can help technicians to select the operation conditions to fabricate HGM in different scales of diameters and wall-thicknesses.
