The Development of the Region Trade under the WTO Multilateral Trading Systems——Concurrently Discuss the Influence on China
In January 2007,all the contracting parties of the WTO agreed to reorganize the Doha Round negotiations,which was not successful finally because the developed country did not budge on the issue of the SSG.The multilateral trade of the WTO suffered a serious setback.Meanwhile,the regional trade got rapidly increase,the multilateral trade of the WTO would become marginalized.
