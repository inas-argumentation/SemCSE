Establishment of Management System on Maintenance of Power Plant Equipment
Based on the experience of both domestic and abroad power stations in management, an modernization management system on maintenance of power plant equipment is established ,which has achieved good effect. In the system, all sorts of the tasks of power plant equipment (defect, preventative maintenance, outage) are connected with the support items (material, technical data ) so as to make the information all-sided, timely and accurate, shorten the transfer time, improve the efficiency and give powenful bases to the managers in making correct decision.
