Chemical laser based on CO+0. 5O/sub 2/ oxidation reaction in a supersonic stream
Laser radiation of lambda=10.6 ..mu.. wavelength was emitted from a supersonic stream as a result of burning of a CO+0.5O/sub 2/+He+H/sub 2/ mixture. It was established that the excited CO/sub 2/ molecules were formed as a result of a chemical reaction. A comparison was made of some of the characteristics with those of a gasdynamic CO+0.5O/sub 2/+N/sub 2/+H/sub 2/ laser. (AIP)
