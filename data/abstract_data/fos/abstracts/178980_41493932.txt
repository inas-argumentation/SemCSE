Measuring quantity, frequency, and volume of drinking.
This paper is based on the first sessions of the same name that were held on May 12,1997 at the International Workshop on Consumption Measures and Models for Use in Policy Development, Bethesda, MD. The session chair was Jurgen Rehm; presenters were Susan Bondy, Deborah Dawson, Kaye Fillmore, Thomas Greenfield, Deborah Ha-sin, Jurgen Rehm, Anders Romelsjo, and John Searles; and discussants were Eckart Kuhlhorn and Linda Sobell.
