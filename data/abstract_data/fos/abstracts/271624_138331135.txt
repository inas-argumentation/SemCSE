Effect of Heat Treatment on Strengthening Level of Acicular Ferrite Pipeline Steel
The effects of heat treatment on strengthening level of acicular ferrite pipeline steel were investigated by OM, TEM, SEM, tensile test and impact test. The results show that: A non-quenched aging process is applied to improve the strength level of a commercial acicular ferrite pipeline steel by analyzing the Larsen-Miller parameter describing the combined effects of the annealing temperature and time, which still remains the substantial acicular ferrite microstructure. This non-quenched aging promotes the further precipitation of the carbonitride dispersion of acicular ferrite microstructure. The strengthening obtained by non-quenched aging for acicular ferrite pipeline steels provides a lower economical cost approach for developing the high strength level of pipeline steels.
