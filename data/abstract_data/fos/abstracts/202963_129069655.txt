Evaluating Interrill and Rill Erodibilities for Soils of Different Textures
ABSTRACT Soil loss rates were evaluated on row-sideslope and row-furrow plots for three soils: one relatively high in sand, one in silt, and one in clay. Interrill erosion rates were measured on plots constructed from a 0.91-m section of furrow. Rill erosion rates were determined on separate 9.3-m sections of graded (0.035) row furrows by apportioning the total soil loss with respect to contributing areas. The interrill and rill erodibilities of each soil were then computed from the erosion rate data. Comparison of the erodibilities showed that the relationship among soils' rill erodibilities may vary greatly from both the relationships among their interrill erodibilities and among their site-specific USLE K-factors..
