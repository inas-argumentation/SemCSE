Messung von Oszillatorenstärken im Singulettsystem des neutralen Magnesiums
A novel type of confined arc has been constructed. Its high magnesium density (up to several 1015 cm−3) provides some unusual spectroscopic features. Relativef-values have been measured for the lines 31P-n1D,n=4...9 and 31P-n1S,n=5,6. By separate comparison with the line 33P10-33P2, these values were placed on an absolute scale.
