Study on Reaction-processed Particle Reinforced Al-Si Alloy Matrix Composites
Melting-reaction method was employed to fabricate Al_2O_3 particle reinforced Al-Si alloy matrix composites based on the chemical reactions between aluminum and SiO_2 powders.The results show that the reinforced particles obtained in composites are about 0.2~0.5μm and are distributed almost homogeneously in the Al-Si alloy matrix;the micro-hardness of composite increases significantly with the increase of volume fraction of Al_2O_3 particle. The chemical reactions between aluminum and SiO_2 are analyzed according to the microparticle-reaction model.
