Growth and characterization of tris-thiourea strontium chloride single crystals
Tris-thiourea strontium chloride was synthesized and single crystals were grown by the slow solvent evaporation technique using aqueous solvent. The solubility curve was obtained as well as the grown crystals were characterized by thermo-gravimetric analysis.Applying Coats and Redfermrelation to the thermo-gram the kinetic and thermodynamic parameters were calculated.
