Biomimetic catalysts for selective oxidation in organic chemistry
Major advances have been made towards efficient catalytically active model systems for cytochrome P-450 during these last ten years. Some recent developments of this field are described. This include (i) examples of application of these biomimetic systems to compounds of biological interest, (ii) the use of Mn-porphyrins to catalyze the transfer of a nitrogen atom into organic substrates, and (iii) the preparation and catalytic properties of metalloporphyrins supported on mineral matrices.
