Tissue Culture and Rapid Propagation Research on Immature Male Flower of Banana
The banana immature male flowers were used as the explants for tissue culture and rapid propagation in this study.The results indicated that the best way for explant processing was that the immature male flowers were sectioned as 1.2 mm thickness within 24 hours without disinfects processing.The results also indicated that the best inducing medium for callus induction was MS medium with 0.2 mg/L TDZ,0.2 mg/L Zeatin,1.0 mg/L biotin,100 mg/L glutamine,40 g/L sugar(pH5.3).The best medium for bud differentiation was MS medium with 4.0 mg/L 6BA,0.3 mg/L NAA,30 g/L sugar(pH5.8).The best medium for root differentiation was MS medium with 2.0 mg/L IBA and 0.2 mg/L NAA,30 g/L sugar(pH5.8).After acclimatization,the culture-bottle seedlings were transplanted into the base materials of coconut tree branny.The survival rate was up to 100%.The reproduction technology improved the efficiency of embryo callus induced by banana callus and banana propagation coefficient,providing prerequisite for banana genetic improvement and large-scale production.
