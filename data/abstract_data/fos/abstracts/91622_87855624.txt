Hereditary Tendency of Aril Flavonoid in Longan Hybrid Progenies and Superior Selection
The inheritance and changes of aril flavonoid content in 94 longan hybrid progenies of'Lidongben'(♀)×'Qingkebaoyuan'(♂)were studied.The results showed that aril flavonoid content was accorded with the pattern of quantitative character and continuous variation genetic character.There were super parent plant lines in hybrid progenies by cluster analysis based on different content,such as'96-125','96-75','96-141',which belonged to the group of high aril flavonoid.Its average inheritance of transfer ability was 96.48%.The general hybrid progenies shows mid-parents inheritance tendency,but there were also some lines belonged to low and high parent inheritance tendency,both of which were 12.77%.
