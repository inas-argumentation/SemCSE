Die Reaktion Li7(p,α) He4 mit polarisierten Protonen für Protonenenergien zwischen 1 und 3,2 MeV
The reaction Li7 (p, α) He4 has been investigated with polarized protons using the reaction C12(d, p) C13 as a proton polarizer. The right-left asymmetryR under 45 degrees was found to be less than 0,5. The caseR=0 for all proton energies is not excluded.
