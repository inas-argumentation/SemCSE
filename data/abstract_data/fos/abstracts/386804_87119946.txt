Study on waste edible oil to produce stearic acid and oleic acid
Waste edible oil is decolorized by bleaching earths, then being saponified. The solution, got from saponification, is changed into precipitate of lead oleale and lead stearate. Using methanol to seperate lead oleae and lead atearate, then after a series reaction with hydrochloric, acid oleic acid and stearic acid are obtained. The purity of stearic acid is 83%, and oleic acid is separated completely by inspection.
