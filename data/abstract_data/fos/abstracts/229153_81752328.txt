Post Traumatic Diffuse Neurofibroma in the Foot: An Unusual Presentation
Received: June 23, 2018 Revised: October 15, 2018 Accepted: October 30, 2018 Abstract: Background: Neurofibromas are benign peripheral nerve sheath tumors that present as solitary or multiple lesions. They can present alone or as part of the disease process called neurofibromatosis type 1. The etiology behind solitary neurofibromas is still poorly understood, however, trauma has been proposed to be a possible cause.
