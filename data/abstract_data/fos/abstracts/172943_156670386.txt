An SVAR Analysis of the Effect of Fiscal Expenditure for Supporting Agriculture on the Income and Consumption of Rural Residents
The relationship of fiscal expenditure for supporting agriculture and the consumption of rural residents has long been a controversial issue within economic realm.Based on time series data from 1978 to 2007,this paper applies the model of Structural VAR to explore the relationship between fiscal expenditure for supporting agriculture and the income and consumption of rural residents in China from the macroscopical and dynamic perspective.The dynamic analysis of Structural VAR suggests that the fiscal expenditure has varied influences on the income and consumption.In the short run,it boosts the income but exerts crowding-out effect on the consumption;however,in the long run,it would not only increase rural residents' income but also promote rural residents' consumption.
