Astrovirus infection in association with acute diarrhoea in Henan and Beijing of China.
Human astrovirus (HAstV) is a new family of RNA viruses. Seven HAstV isolates were identified in fecal specimens from acute diarrhea children in Zhengzhou, Henan 1990 and in Beijing 1996 by EM, RT-PCR and sequencing of PCR products. The viruses were approximately 28nm in diameter with five-or six-point star surface. The phylogenetic dendrogram generated from comparison of the 348-nucleotide sequence within ORF2 of the genome demonstrated that HAstV-1 was the prominent serotype in Zhengzhou 1990 with another one HAstV-5 strain, but serotype HAstV-5 was found transmitted in Beijing in 1996. The clinical manifestations of HAstV infected patients were discussed. The results may further help our understanding of the epidemiology of HAstV.
