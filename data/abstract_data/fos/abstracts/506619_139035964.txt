Study on High Cycle Fatigue Behavior of Micro-alloyed Pipeline Steel X80
The high cycle fatigue behavior of micro-alloyed pipeline steel X80 was studied under different fatigue experimental conditions, the S-N curve was drawn and the fatigue limit was obtained. It is found that the different microstructure has great effect on the fatigue limit and the S-N curve of micro-alloyed pipeline steel X80 through the analysis of microstructure, the mechanical properties testing and high cycle fatigue test. At the same time, the fatigue fracture morphologies were observed and analyzed. The results show that the microstructure can be refined with the decrease of finishing rolling temperature. The fatigue limit of micro-alloyed pipeline steel X80 is improved.
