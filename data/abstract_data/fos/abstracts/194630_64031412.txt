Optimization of Strictly Nonblocking Multicast Three Stage Clos Network
One of important problems in designing multicast three stage Clos network is how to provide strictly nonblocking state while keeping minimum hardware cost.In this paper,a new method for optimizing the network hardware cost is proposed.The optimized hardware cost of strictly nonblocking multicast three stage Clos network is given under two conditions: fan-out capacity in all stages and no fan-out capacity in middle stage.Our analysis shows that the optimized hardware cost of strictly nonblocking network is efficiently reduced,even lower than that of its wide-sense nonblocking counterparts in some cases.In addition,the optimized network does not need specific routing algorithm which consumes large time while keeping strictly nonblocking.
