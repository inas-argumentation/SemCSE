Certificateless Two-party Authenticated Key Agreement Protocol With Provable Security
This paper studies two-party authenticated agreement protocol in the certificateless public setting.Most of existing schemes either are proved insecure or have computation burden.Basesd on twin Diffie-Hellman problem introduced by Cash,Kiltz and Shoup,an efficient certificateless authenticated two-party key agreement protocol using bilinear pair is proposed.To construct an effective decision oracle for twin Diffie-Hellman problem,generic method of extracting partial private key is proposed.the method,with one master key and two hash functions.is applied to the design of protocol.When compared with other schemes with provable security,the proposed scheme,with the same message bandwidth,achieve efficiency in computational cost.The security of the proposed scheme has been proven in the random oracle model without Gap assumption.
