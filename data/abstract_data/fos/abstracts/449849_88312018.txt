The longest exon of CAP59 gene of C.neoformans and its application in detecting and typing C.neoformans
Objective Potential application of the longest exon of CAP59 gene in the detection and typing of C.neoformans. Methods Primers were designed and the fragment was amplied according to the longest exon of CAP59 gene. The sequence of five serotypes of standard C.neoformans was analysed one by one in GenBank. Their phylogenetic tree was analysed with Phylip3.6a3. Some seotyped isolates of C.neoformans and other fugi were collected to test primers′s usefullness in detecting and seotyping of C.neoformans. Results There are differences of the longest exon of CAP59 gene among five serotypes of standard C.neoformans isolates. The serotying result of clinical isolates with the longest exon of CAP59 gene was all consistent with immunological serotyping. Conclusion The gene amplied by primers, P1: 5′-GAG TGT CTC CGC AAC CCG CA-3′ and P2: 5′-CCT ACT CTG CCA AAT CAA CTC-3′ is able to type and detect C.neoformans as a molecular too.
