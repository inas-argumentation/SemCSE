Punch Planting of Vegetable Seeds—A Progress Report
ABSTRACT A two-row punch planter was designed, developed, and tested with lettuce seed. This planter has a notched seed wheel that rotates in a vertical plane to individually meter coated vegetable seeds. Seeds are transferred by magnetic force from the seed wheel to magnetic punches. The seed coatings contain an iron oxide, Fe304, which makes them attractive to magnets. The punches firmly imbed the seeds into the soil and ideally, the holes remain uncovered. Tests showed that the punch planting system resulted in a shorter time between planting and emergence than when a conven-tional planter was used.
