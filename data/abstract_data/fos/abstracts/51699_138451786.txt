Fracture Analysis on Drain Bracket of Aircraft
The failure mode and cause of aircraft bracket was studied by macro and micro observation,EDS analysis and metallurgical structure examination.The results show that the fracture of the bracket is exfoliation corrosion crack.Local coating on the bracket was damaged,and the bare regions contact directly with the waste water,which is the main cause for the exfoliation corrosion of the bracket.In addition,the deformation structure in the S direction decreases the corrosion resistance of the material.According to the results above,some measures for the safe of aircraft were put forward.
