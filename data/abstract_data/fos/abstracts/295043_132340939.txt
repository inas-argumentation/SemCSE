Studies on Fiber Morphological Features of Economic Plantation Walnut Grown in Two Kinds of Silvicutures
The fiber formal features of economic plantation walnut planted in Fenyang Walnut Seed Garden of Shanxi province of China were studied in this paper. The results were as follows:(1)Contrasted with the walnut trees cultivated with fertilization,graft and shear measures, each average of fiber feature of the walnut trees without such measures were bigger,only fiber tangential diameter and double cell wall thickness of which differ significantly.(2)For the two kinds of silvicultures,the radial variation patterns of fiber length, fiber length diameter ratio, fiber tangential diameter and double cell wall thickness were analogous:from pith to bark, first increase moderately, after a few years undulate.(3)For the two kinds of silvicultures,the patterns of the longitudinal variation of the fiber tangential diameter, fiber length and fiber length diameter ratio were alike,and the patterns of the longitudinal variation of the double cell wall thickness and fiber wall diameter ratio were different. So the average of the main fiber formal features and the patterns of the variation of the two kinds of silvicultures were alike.
