Current situation of physical health and physical exercising with respect to enterprisers of private enterprises in Wenzhou area
The author investigated and analyzed the health(illness) condition,current physical condition,living style and physical exercising with respect to 87 ages 28～59 enterprisers of private enterprises in Wenzhou area,and revealed the following findings: The physical health condition of enterprisers of private enterprises in Wenzhou area was not optimistic.The top 6 major illnesses are high blood fat content,high blood pressure,diabetes,urinary system,fatty liver and chronic hepatitis B(the average illness rate per person is up to 3.13 times).The overall acceptable rate in physical checkup is 67.83%,which is lower than the acceptable rate of 71.40% for the whole country and the acceptable rate of 79.70% for Zhejiang province.Most enterprisers of private enterprises had a poorly managed daily living style,and seldom participated in physical exercising.The author suggested that enterprisers of private enterprises in Wenzhou area and Zhejiang province and even China should improve their bad daily living habits,pay attention to knowledge about physical health and hygiene,and participate in physical exercising actively.
