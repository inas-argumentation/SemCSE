Cardiotoxicity associated with an intentional overdose of maprotiline and perphenazine: a case report.
Cardiotoxicity is a life-threatening complication of an intentional overdose of cyclic antidepressant and phenothiazine drugs. This case report illustrates the severe conduction abnormalities that can accompany a massive ingestion of maprotiline and perphenazine. The patient's condition was further complicated by the unexpected finding of hypothermia.
