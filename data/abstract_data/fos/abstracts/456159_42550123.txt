Complete genome sequence of nucleoside producing strain Corynebacterium stationis ATCC 6872.
Corynebacterium stationis ATCC 6872 has been the subject of intense study in recent years with respect to various important industrial applications, such as nucleosides production. Here, the complete genome sequence for ATCC 6872 is presented. This bacterium harbors a 2,853,666bp circular genome that comprises 2487 coding CDS.
