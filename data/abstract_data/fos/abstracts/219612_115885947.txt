Erzeugung polarisierter Elektronenstrahlen durch optisches Pumpen
The valence electrons in sodium vapour were aligned by optical pumping to a polarization between 5% and 15%. An electron beam of 10−12 A current, 10−2rad divergency and 1 mm diameter has been obtained by ionisation of the sodium vapour with uv-light. This electron beam was polarized up to 2% as measured by Mott scattering.
