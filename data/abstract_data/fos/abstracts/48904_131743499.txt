Treatment of Wastewater Containing Acid-red Dye by Fly Ash and Fenton
Fly ash from a coal-fired power plant was applied with Fenton method, i.e. H_(2)O_(2)+FeSO_(4), for treating dyeing wastewater containing acid-red dye in a laboratory bench-scale. The experimental results showed a removal of more than 97% for CODcr and the decolorization above 99%.
