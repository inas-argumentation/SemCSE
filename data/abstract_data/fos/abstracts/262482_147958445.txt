The role of research universities in the U.S. R&D performance
Research and development(RD) has materially contributed to the innovation and economic growth in the United States.Research universities are committed to RD,particularly to effective basic research.The research universities accounted for 6% of all higher education institutions,but they got the majority of federal RD investment and expenditure;published a large number of scientific papers;produced more than 60% of science and engineering doctoral graduates and over 90% patents of colleges and universities.These all show that RD in research universities is a good combination of knowledge production and talent training;also is an effective way to carry out their duties and serve the community.
