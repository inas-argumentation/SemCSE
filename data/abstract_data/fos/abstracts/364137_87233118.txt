Cloning and Bioinformatics Analysis of a Tonoplast Aquaporin Gene LlTIP2 in Lilium longiflorum
Aquaporins,belong to major intrinsic proteins(MIPs)superfamily,are channel proteins of biological membranes including plasma membrane and tonoplast,which highly facilitate the transportation of water across these membranes.A tonoplast intrinsic proteins(TIPs)gene(LlTIP2,GenBank accession number: JN085950)was isolated from Lilium longiflorum leaves by RT-PCR and RACE.The full cDNA sequence of LlTIP2 was 986 bp,containing 208 bp 3'-UTR,34 bp 5'-UTR,and 744 bp open reading frame,which encoded 247 amino acids,proposed as defining the specificity of aquaporins.Two highly conserved NPA(Asn-Pro-Ala)motifs of aquaporins and the MIP family signature sequence SGGHVNPAVTFG can be found in the deduced protein.Homology of amino acids sequences between LlTIP2 and TIP2s from Gossypium hirsutum,Hordeum vulgare,Lolium perenne,and Triticum aestivum was 77%,74%,73% and 72% respectively.Phylogenetic analysis indicated that LlTIP2 was a new member of TIP2 subfamily.
