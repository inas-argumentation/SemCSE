Energy saving technology on shell and tube methanol synthesis unit
Aim at shell and tube methanol synthesis reactor can not large scale,catalyst loading low,and short cycle defects for catalyst change,the methanol synthesis tower is modified.The results show that the production capacity of single-column is increased;the catalyst replacement cycle is improved.The separator of the methanol synthesis unit is modified,the results show that the separation efficiency of methanol is improved,the methanol content in circulating gas is reduced,and the one-way conversion of CO and the net alcohol is improved.Through modification of the methanol synthesis reactor and the methanol separator,the overall production capacity of the single-family is increased by 10%than the original equipment,and the production cost is reduced effectively,without affecting the device's safety and operational stability.
