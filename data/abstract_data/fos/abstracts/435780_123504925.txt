A Traceable Threshold Proxy Signature Scheme Based on Double Sub-secret
In the light of the problems that the existing threshold proxy signature schemes do not have traceability and cannot prevent the proxy from abusing signature,a threshold proxy signature scheme based on double sub-secret is proposed in this paper. From the Computational Diffie-Hellman( CDH) problem and bilinear pairings,the scheme can restrict and track the initiator of the signature by introducing blind factor and proxy sub-secret. It can make the signature have the security nature of strong unforgeability,non-repudiation,traceability and resist collusion attack. Vandermonde transform and other mathematical tools are used to analyze correctness,efficiency,and security of this signature scheme. The detailed analysis shows that this scheme is secure and effective.
