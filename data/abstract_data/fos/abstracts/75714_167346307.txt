On the Value Orientation in Township Government's Function Transformation under the Background of Constructing New Rural Community
Under the background of constructing harmonious socialist society and new socialist countryside,there are mutual restrictions and promotions between the construction and development of rural community and the function transformation of basic level township government.Based on the theories on the relationship between country society,government market,equity efficiency,and governance government,the paper aims to explain the internal tension between rural social changes and function transformation of township government,thereby demonstrating the value orientations of servicesation,commercialization,marketization,socialization,and transparency in the function transformation of township government,which help to make township government be a service-oriented,saving-based,guiding-type,creative,and sunshine-type government at the basic level.
