Reflection Spectrum of Solid Nitrogen in the Vacuum Ultraviolet
The reflection spectrum of solid ${\mathrm{N}}_{2}$ at 4.2\ifmmode^\circ\else\textdegree\fi{}K has been measured over the wavelength range 550-1550 \AA{} for the first time. These data reveal that Rydberg states thought to perturb excited vibrational levels of the $b^{1}\ensuremath{\Pi}_{\mathrm{u}}$ state are present though not directly observable in the solid.
