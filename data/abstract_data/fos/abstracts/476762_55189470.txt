Mechanistic studies of the photogeneration and photochemistry of ortho-xylylenes
A variety of ortho-xylylenes have been generated photochemically using ketone precursors via either CO elimination from appropriately substituted 2-indanones or y- hydrogen transfer in ortho substituted aromatic ketones. The solution lifetimes of these xylylenes range from nanoseconds to minutes and their decay involves a rich variety of intra- and intermolecular processes. Examples are presented where the effects of xylylene irradiation ranges from a dramatic acceleration of the reaction to examples where the effect of irradiation is to change the product ratios or to a decrease in yields through promotion of the back reaction.
