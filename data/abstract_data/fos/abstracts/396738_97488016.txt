Electron crystallography, by X. Zou, S. Hovmöller and P. Oleynikov
This book is without reference to diffraction characterisation of twinning, which is a limitation obviously. More generally, I would also mention that this book has the unusual feature that the contents are at the back of this book, near to the subject index. This book really does deserve to be translated into English and the publisher CNRS Editions is again commended to do this, as did the review we cite in (1). Or there again, this book is a good reminder encouraging those like myself to improve our French; this book will deliver a great reward to us as we learn more about the detailed history and descriptions of les macles.
