Study on Electrical Sliding Wear Behavior of Cu-Cr Alloy
The electrical wear behavior of Cu-Cr precipitation strengthened copper alloy was investigated using electrical sliding-wear tester.The worn surface of the alloy was analyzed by scanning electron microscopy(SEM) and energy dispersive X-ray spectrometry(EDS),and its abrasion mechanism was discussed.The results indicate that the wear rate of Cu-Cr alloy increases with the increase of sliding distance.The wear rate of the alloy decreases with the increase of Cr-content.The wear rate of Cu-0.65Cr and Cu-0.76Cr alloy is 2 ～3 times that of Cu-0.86Cr alloy.The adhesive wear is the dominant wear mechanism during electrical sliding wear.
