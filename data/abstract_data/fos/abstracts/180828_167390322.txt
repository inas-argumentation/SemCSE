Basic Requirements for Higher Vocational Teachers in Enforcing Quality Education
With the knowledge economy times coming, the quality education will be carried out com- prehensively and in the meantime the higher vocational education will be vigorously developed in our coun- try To realize the training aims the quality education lies in the teachers. It is necessary for us to have a teachers' contingent of higher quality if higher vocational education and the quality education are merged. This article presents the basic requirements for higher vocational teachers in enforcing the quality education.
