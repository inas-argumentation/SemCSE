BULL ATTACK AS A CAUSE OF SPINAL CORD INJURY IN BANGLADESH - DOES IT REALLY MATTERS?
Bangladesh, a developing country of South-Asia region, despite being burdened with a population of about 160 million [1, 2, 6] has attained compelling achievement in health sector [3]. Evidence from available literatures, trauma of different modalities along with some nontraumatic causes are the principle element of SCI in Bangladesh. Specialized center like Centre for Rehabilitation of the Paralyzed (CRP) is a wellrenowned institute which plays a pivotal role in rehabilitation of the SCI patients in Bangladesh [2]. A recent study reported that approximately 390 patients per annum with injury to the spinal cord were admitted to the CRP [2].
