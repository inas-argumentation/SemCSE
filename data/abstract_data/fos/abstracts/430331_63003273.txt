Approximation of Many-Valued Functions by a Sand Glass Type Neural Network
Approximations of continuous many-valued functions were tried using sand glass type neural network of 5 layers. In the examples of success in identity mapping learning to sample points on the curve, the output value of the third layer unit changed with a specific value to a sample point and did not overlap in the whole range although there were cases that it changed discon tinuously. Approximation of several many-valued functions were realized by propagating the third layer unit value along the behavior to the second half part of the network except narrow areas between sample points correspondent to the discontinuous areas of third layer unit values.
