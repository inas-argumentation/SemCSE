Chemical control of green peach aphid, Myzus persicae (Sulzer) on potato in north-eastern plains.
Field experiments conducted at Patna (Bihar) revealed that single application of foliar systemic insecticides (0.03 and 0.05%) and granular system1c insecticides (@ 1.0 and 1.5 kg a.i./ha) alone in different protocols did not protect the crop from Myzus persicae(Sulzer) for the entire cropping season.
