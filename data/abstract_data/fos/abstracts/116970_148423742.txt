Some conceptions of optimization for ideological and political education of undergraduates
Based on the background of a complicated,variable domestic and overseas environment and the optimization for ideological and political education of undergraduates,this article proposes the following several conceptions:(1)strengthen local party committee and government's leadership over university students' ideological and political education work;(2)grasp two courses teaching,for each one has its strong points and each one is indispensable;(3)focus on "cultivate";(4) make university students' ideological and political education assimilated into life.
