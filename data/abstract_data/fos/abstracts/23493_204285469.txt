Performance of Risers Covering Flux for Large Castings
The influence of SiO2,Fe2O3 on the thermal conductivity,melting point of risers covering flux were studied in the paper and the effect of dilative graphite on expansibility and heat-insulating property were also investigated.The high temperature microstructures and the thermal insulation mechanism were researched by scanning electron microscope(SEM).Based on comprehensive comparative analysis above,the riser covering flux for large steel castings can be optimized.
