Generation–recombination reduction in InAsSb photodiodes
Generation?recombination processes in a narrow band gap active region of infrared photodiodes are studied theoretically and experimentally. Thanks to an analysis of the transport in InAsSb photodiodes as a function of temperature, we demonstrate that these processes can be reduced by controlling the doping of the active region. The first Auger-dominated detector in this spectral range is shown, with negligible diffusion and SRH generation?recombination processes.
