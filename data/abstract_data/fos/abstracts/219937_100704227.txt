Research Progress on Synergism Antioxidation Mechanism of Tea Polyphenols and Vitamins
A large number of epidemiological surveys and studies in vitro and in vivo have shown that tea polyphenols and vitamins are important antioxidants.They have strong biological activities such as antioxidant,reducing damage from free radical,sterilization and cancer prevention.The synergy of these two substances and their mechanism are summarized in this review,in order to provide theoretical basis for further study and developing safer and more efficient antioxidants.
