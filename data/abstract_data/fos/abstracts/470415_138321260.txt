Study on Combination Intension of Self-lubricant Coating by Flame Spraying
Self-lubricant (SL ) coating was prepared by oxy-acetylene flame spraying using Ni/C. Design scheme was optimized by orthogonal test, the combination intension was measured by pull-extend test and optimal machining parameters were found when the combination intension of the coating was better. The best parameters are oxygen pressure 0.5 MPa, air pressure 0.4 MPa, acetylene pressure 0.1 MPa, spraying distance 100 mm. The combination intension of SL coating by flame spraying is 6.3 MPa under the parameters, which meets the demand of combination intension of SL coating.
