Intercouple Dating on a College Campus.
Four-hundred-eighty-six university students completed a confidential questionnaire designed to assess a multitude of aspects of their current and past dating relationships. The 24-item questionnaire was designed to highlight the prevalence and rate that individuals were involved in InterCouple relationships; these being interfaith intercultural and/or interracial romantic relationships. 66% of the participants reported being involved at some time in an InterCouple relationship. The overall results of the survey and the importance to college personnel and students are given. (authors)
