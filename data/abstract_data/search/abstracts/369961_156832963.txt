Research on the Valuation of Real Options Approach in Forestry Companies
Forestry Companies have many uncertainties caused by the operation risks and price fluctuations of forestry resources,so real options approach is a good method to make valuations in the companies.According to the characteristics of China's forestry companies,the authors formulate the enterprise valuation model in continuous time,and discuss the parametric variables and valuation methods in the model.By doing a case study on a listed forestry company,the authors find that compared with the valuation results of Net Present Value model,the real options approach could evaluate all the real option value of the forestry company.Through sensitivity analysis,the authors figure out the parameters which affect the value of forestry companies most.Thus the shareholders and managers could make optimal strategies for the company.
