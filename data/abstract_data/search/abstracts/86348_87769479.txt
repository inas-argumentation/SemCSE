A Field Study of the Flight Behavior of the Tiger Swallowtail Butterfly
During the summer of 1957 a preliminary effort was made to mark Tiger Swallowtail butterflies (Papilio glaucus glaucus L. and, after releasing them, to see if any of the marked specimens could be observed at the same location on subsequent days. On August 28 eight males were marked with rubber name and date stamps, using black ink. The yard in southern Maryland where the insects were marked was watched on the following days. One of the marked specimens returned on the second day, and two additional ones were recaptured on the fifth day after being marked. These results were very intriguing and led to a more thorough study in the summer of 1958 to gain information on the behavior of the Tiger Swallowtail butterfly.
