Clinical observation of Yitikang capsule on type 2 diabetes millitus
Objective To observe clinical effect of Yitikang capsule on type 2 diabetes millitus. Methods 155 patients with type 2 diabetes millitus were divided into treatment group (n=125) which was treated by Yitikang capsule, and control group (n=30) which was treated by shen qi jiang tang capsule. Blood glucose, HbA1c, blood lipid, hemorheology and blood insulin level fasting and postprandial 2hr in both groups were detected. Results Levels of Blood glucose of Fasting and postprandial 2hr, HbA1c, cholesterol, triglyceride, hematocrit and fibrinogen were significantly lower (P0.05) than those in control group. Conclusion Yitikang capsule can produce obvious effect on the treatment of type 2 diabetes millitus.
