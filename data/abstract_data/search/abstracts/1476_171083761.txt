A Study of the Feasibility of Combining Traditional Green Earthen Buildings with Modern Architecture
Environmental problems have become increasingly more serious these days. In this paper we examine a possible solution based on traditional "Green earthen buildings" approach, an architecture which has not spread widely until now and where more attention is required.
