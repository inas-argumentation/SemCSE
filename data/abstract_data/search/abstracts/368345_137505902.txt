High strength and formable Mg 6.2Zn 0.5Zr 0.2Ca alloy sheet processed by twin roll casting
Abstract Twin roll cast and hot rolled Mg 6.2 wt%Zn alloys microalloyed with Zr, Ca, and Ag show tensile yield strength exceeding 300 MPa in the T6 (peak aged) condition with reasonable formability in the T4 condition. The addition of Zr and Ca plays a critical role in the development of weak textured recrystallized microstructure in Mg 6.2 wt%Zn alloys so Mg 6.2Zn 0.5Zr 0.2Ca (wt% alloy shows equivalent mechanical properties with Mg 6.2Zn 0.5Zr 0.2Ca 0.4Ag (wt% alloy even without expensive Ag.
