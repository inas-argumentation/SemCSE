Adoption and diffusion of internet banking: case of Tunisian banking sector
This paper deals with the adoption of Internet banking in the Tunisian banking industry. The aim is to identify factors that accelerate or slow down the adoption process. The literature review enables identifying a set of variables: organizational, individual and structural. The research methodology used within this study is the case study. Five case studies in banking sector were conducted. The sample is shaped by banks that adopted the Internet Baking as an innovation. The analysis allowed the determination of the related dimensions of the aforementioned variables (competition, perceived benefits, and organizational compatibility) Indeed, this research has revealed some variables that hinder the adoption of technological innovations.
