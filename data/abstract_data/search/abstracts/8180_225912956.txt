"A Study On Customer Preference Towards E Tailing In India"
The Internet has changed the way of the traditional method of customers shopping andpurchasing goods and services. In this quickly changing age and time, going 'digital' is the newpattern. Each brick and mortar shop are currently attempting to make itsonline nearness to remain in front of the challenge. consumers utilize the internet for onlineshopping, yet in addition to look at costs, the product feature and after sale service facilities. Etailingalso called online shopping, is a structure of electronic Commerce which enablesConsumers to legitimately purchase goods or services from a seller over the Internet utilizing aninternet browser. Customers are obtaining the goods and services online in light of the fact that itspares time, and more determination, for goods are accessible as compare with offline shopping.Furthermore, most the significant thing there is no compelling reason to go anyplace one can getgoods at his/her home. The main objective of this research study is to examine the consumerpreference of e tailing. The present research paper has utilized the quantitative technique to studyconsumer preference towards, E tailing. The information was gathered through Survey. Thisexamination likewise expects to discover the key factors like age, gender, and different paymentmethod that influences consumer preference towards e tailing.
