The Economic Value of Data A New Revenue Stream for Global Custodians
Among the newer revenue streams upon which some firms have embarked is to monetize the large volumes of data assets they hold on behalf of their clients to meet regulatory mandates the maintenance of which carries a major technology price tag for custodians. Leveraging this data in aggregated form to offer time series analysis, predictive analytics, business intelligence and visualizations provides significant insights to decision makers at custodian firms and their operations teams. This is made possible with technology innovations in big data management and analytics.
