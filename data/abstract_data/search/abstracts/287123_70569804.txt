AANP Foundation announces Scholarship Grant Program 2010 award recipients
Formed by the American Academy of Nurse Practitioners (AANP) and beginning operations in March 1998, the AANP Foundation is an independently incorporated 501c3 charitable nonprofit organization. AANP Foundation goals are to provide scholarships for NP and Doctoral preparation; to award grants for research/projects contributing to improved public health; and to promote international/intercultural exchange between NPs and others. With the close of the 2010 Funding Cycle, the AANP Foundation is honored to have awarded $759,650 worth of scholarships and grants to members of AANP. We hope members will celebrate with us and support these programs by attending our first annual Honors Dinner, Thursday, June 23, 2011 at the AANP Annual Conference in Las Vegas.
