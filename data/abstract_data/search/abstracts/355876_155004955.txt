The wider acceptance of non alignment and the loosening of alliances are two points of the same continuum, the motivations and elements of which call for an unprejudiced appreciation. Non alignment, instead of being a transitory phenomenon born essentially out of a negative response to bloc politics, derives its inspiration from values which have been dear to states and to the individuals comprising them and which run counter to the spirit of the alliance systems of contemporary international politics.
