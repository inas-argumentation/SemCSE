The Regional Heterogeneity Impact of Digital Inclusive Finance on Urban Rural Income Gap in China: Empirical Research Based on Provincial Panel Threshold Model
This paper selects provincial data from 2013 to 2018 to construct a static panel model and a non linear panel threshold model. Empirical research finds that digital inclusive finance has a convergence effect in narrowing the urban rural income gap, but this effect has regional heterogeneity, from strong to weak in the west, east and central. In addition, we find that the deeper the degree of openness, the stronger the convergence effect of digital inclusive finance in narrowing the urban rural income gap. Therefore, this article puts forward a series of policy suggestions such as accelerating the construction of digital infrastructure, innovating financial development models, intensifying opening up, and improving the rural financial system in order to achieve the coordinated and sustainable development of urban and rural income.
