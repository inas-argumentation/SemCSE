Design of High Performance IIR filter Using Vedic Multiplication Method
Digital filters can be found in most of the electronic circuits. One major application of these filters involve an input signals that are sampled and converted to the real time signal to the modern computers. This paper describes the high performance infinite impulse response (IIR) filter for the implementation of digital signal processing (DSP) applications. Among the various architectures of filtering a signal, we have chosen transposed direct form II cascaded structure to implement IIR filter due its low round ff noise and low quantization errors. The IIR filter is designed with the Vedic multiplication technique. It reduces the computational delay and hardware to minimize the number of iterations and enhances the performance of the DSP processor. The filter is designed on Xilinx software and different frequency responses are evaluated in Matlab at different filter orders.
