An acquisition method of distance information in direction signs
In this paper, we propose a method to acquire the distance information in the road signboard quickly and accurately. The proposed method consists of recognition of signs, preprocessing process to acquire distances, and obtaining distance information. In recognition of the sign, color detection including gamma correction is used to solve various noise. In order to acquire distance information easily, we applied gradient correction using linear factor and resolution correction using fast Fourier transform. In the process of acquiring distance information, the area is highlighted through morphology operation and template matching is used. Through this process, we obtained the distance information of the road sign and output the distance to the junction. It can be used in a system that can process in real time with emphasis on the improvement of the computation speed. As a result, it has an average speed of 0.46 seconds per frame and a similarity value of 0.65 even in accuracy. And the recognition part of the road sign is implemented by hardware.
