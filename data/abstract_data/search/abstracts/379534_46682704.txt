Get ready for your closeup: HTM professionals have key role with electronic health records.
If you belong to one of the thousands of hospitals that have begun implementing an electronic health record (EHR) system, you know firsthand what a major undertaking the project is. But perhaps you belong to the other 60% of hospitals in the United States who are still in the planning phase or are hoping this latest "fad" will just go away. If you belong to this latter group, get ready for a surprise, because whether you like it or not, your hospital will have some form of an EHR in the next five to 10 years. Despite the ever increasing rate of EHR adoption in U.S. hospitals, some still believe that it does not impact healthcare technology management (HTM) departments. As the vice president of Clinical Engineering Service for a large multistate health system that is more than halfway through its EHR implementation, I can assure you that this is not the case. This article offers up some of our recommendations and lessons learned from an HTM perspective.
