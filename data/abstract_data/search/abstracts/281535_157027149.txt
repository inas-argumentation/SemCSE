Ways to Cultivate the Self awareness and Develop the Capacities of Primary and Secondary School Students
The meaning of capacities is the intelligence to be developed and the potentiality for personality development, which is the new formulation in the field of education for all around development.Practice has proved that the adoption of the ways such as autonomous learning,autonomous management,incentive evaluation,enhancing students' level of self awareness,and making use of the active impact of psychological hint and so on,can improve the education environment and fully arouse students' enthusiasm,which is an effective way for the development of capabilities of primary and secondary school students.
