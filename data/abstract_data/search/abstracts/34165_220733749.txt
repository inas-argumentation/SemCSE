Transfer Learning Method Evaluation for Automatic Pediatric Chest X Ray Image Segmentation
Pneumonia is highly dangerous for children and is the disease that most kill for those under 5 years. For rapid diagnosis, it is common to require chest x ray, which is a difficult image for reading and interpret, requiring an expert physician. It is important to provide computational methods to help exam interpretation or enhance important information. This paper proposes an approach of transfer learning to automatic semantic segmentation of lungs in a pediatric chest x ray image based on knowledge acquire in other datasets. The results are promising and reaches 0.917 of Dice using U Net combined with InceptionV3 in a chest x ray dataset without lung annotation.
