A Study of Execution Oriented by Value Innovation Strategy:Based on Empirical Investigations from the Perspective of Process Management
The process management has three components:the value innovation strategy orientation,the execution process and the affecting relationship between customer value and enterprise value.Through a deep empirical analysis of the relationship between the three,this paper finds that the value innovation strategy orientation is a necessary but not sufficient condition for innovating customer value and improving enterprise performance.The process management ability is not simply playing a regulating role between value innovation strategy orientation and customer value performance,its influence is perhaps stronger than that of value innovation strategy orientation.Human resource management has significant positive effects on customer value performance,but not playing its role through taking the internal customer value performance as the intermediary variable.This study points out that process management capability is an important factor in realizing the object of improving enterprise performance through innovating customer value in the value innovation strategy.
