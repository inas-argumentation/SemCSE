Analysis of plant configuration of the hospital in landscape design The New District of Wuhu Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine landscape design for example
The outdoor environment of hospital is an important part of hospital environment,it is the daily work process of medical staff have to experience the environment,but also of patients and hospital environment.In recent years,with a new awareness of modern medical model transformation and the relationship of people to the environment and human beings,we are increasingly aware of the help and support of plants on rehabilitation,and even many diseases through long time contact with the natural environment,and achieve the therapeutic effect of drugs can not match.So in the hospital outdoor landscape design,selection of appropriate plant and make the correct arrangement of configuration of these plants,it is particularly important.In this paper,taking Anhui province Wuhu Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine hospital district landscape design process as an example,elaborated the hospital plant landscape building theme,selection of plant species,plant allocation principle and configuration,and strive to build ecological garden hospital of Wuhu Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine.
