HOW TO WRITE MATHEMATICS
As a mathematics major, you need to know many things in order to get started in the world of mathematics. These things include mathematical concepts, definitions, theorems, and proofs. Equally important is the knowledge of how to write your ideas, solutions, and proofs. Some students are surprised by this. They ask, "Why do I need to learn to write if I am a mathematics major?" The answer is that writing is important in just about all fields, and certainly in mathematics. You need to learn to write so that others can follow your work. The goal of mathematical writing is clear communication of mathematical ideas. Mathematical writing is accurate, precise, and concise. In general, writing is a skill that can and should be worked on, and it is a skill that can be improved with practice. Writing about a subject goes hand in hand with learning about the subject. Mathematics is a challenging field in which these general principles of writing are of critical importance.
