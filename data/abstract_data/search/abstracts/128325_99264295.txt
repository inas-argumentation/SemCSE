Nghien cuu thanh phan va dieu che Phytosome Saponin toan phan cua cu cay Tam that (Panax Notoginseng) trong o Tay Bac Viet Nam
Tom tat Tam that Panax notoginseng (Burk. F. H. Chen) mot loai duoc lieu quy, la cay dac huu cua vung Tay Bac, cho nang suat tot va gia tri kinh te cao. O nuoc ta, cho den nay cac cong bo ve thanh phan hoa thuc vat, hoat tinh sinh hoc va tac dung duoc ly cua cay Tam that con it va tan man; chua co nghien cuu he thong ve hoa thuc vat lam co so du lieu cho viec phan tich, kiem nghiem nguon duoc lieu quy nay cung nhu de phat trien cac ung dung cua Tam that lam thuoc duoi cac dang bao che hien dai. Voi thuc te do, chung toi thuc hien nghien cuu thanh phan saponin Tam that bang cac phuong phap phan lap sac ky va phan tich cau truc dung pho khoi va cong huong tu hat nhan. Nghien cuu da ghi nhan duoc 5 hop chat saponin bao gom ginsenoside Rc, Rd, Re, Rb1 va Rg1 tu phan doan giau saponin cua cu Tam that Tay Bac. De phat trien cac dang thuoc hien dai, co sinh kha dung cao, nghien cuu cung dat van dieu che phuc phytosome cua saponin toan phan cua Tam that. Tu phan doan saponin, da dieu che duoc phuc phytosome voi hieu suat cao la 88,76% Ket qua phan tich cho thay ham luong saponin tao phuc la hon 70% Day la cong bo dau tien o nuoc ta ve huong nghien cuu nay. Nhan ngay 26 thang 9 nam 2015, Chinh sua ngay 07 thang 11 nam 2015, Chap nhan dang ngay 25 thang 6 nam 2016 Tu khoa Tam that, Panax notoginseng saponin, phytosome, Tay Bac.
