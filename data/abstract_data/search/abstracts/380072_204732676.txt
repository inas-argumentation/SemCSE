After the trade war, where the path leads to Sino US relations? Innovation, China's new future
The initiation of the Sino US trade war twitches on March 22, 2018 after US president Trump imposed tariffs on Chinese imports. Due to this the economic condition of both countries have been impacted but at the same time growth of China's product value have also increased inevitability of trade war. This study employs the empirical evaluation and analysis based on the 30 years of data collected from the World Bank and CEIC. To analyze the economic data to know the GDP of both countries and Sino US economic problems the ARIMA time series model is used. To ensure the stability of the ARIMA time series model the ADF test is done. Based on the results, we came in verdict that interests of cooperation between China and the US are greater than the interests of confrontation and conflict. At the same time from the comprehensive analysis, we alleged that future development of China and US depends on Sino US cooperation. China's independent innovation is a suitable path under the premise of win win cooperation between China and US.
