Effervescence, Resonance and Emotive Practice on Social Media: Public Expressions of Heartbreak Among Young Filipino Twitter Users
This chapter examines a popular hashtag among Filipino Twitter users, #WalangForever (there is no forever) that is based on a film of the same title. #walangforever has become a discursive space where Twitter users make witty references to the movie as a way of expressing heartbreak. At one level, the public expression of sentiments appears as a virtual form of collective effervescence. However, digital ethnographic analysis reveals that the hashtag generates resonance, momentary forms of belonging and intimacy (Miller 2015) based on shared sentiments about modern love. The ways Filipino Twitter users express hurt and bitterness suggest the emergence of emotive practices, particularly hugot (to pull) The chapter discusses how hugot, the public expression of intense yet repressed feelings, constitutes ephemeral intimacies on social media.
