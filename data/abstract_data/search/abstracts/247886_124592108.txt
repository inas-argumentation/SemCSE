THE OSCILLATION OF QUASI CONTINUOUS FUNCTIONS ON PAIRWISE ATTAINABLE SPACES
We introduce a class of attainable and pairwise attainable spaces which includes metrizable spaces. On this classes we develop a new technique of constructing of functions with given oscillations. We characterize the oscillations of quasi continuous functions. In particular, we prove that the function g defined on a pairwise attainable space is the oscillation of some quasi continuous function f if and only if g is nonnegative upper semi continuous function with a meager support.
