Influence of tin (IV) doping on structural and optical properties of rhombohedral barium titanate (BaTiO3)
The impact of tin (IV Sn 4+ doping into rhombohedral barium titanate (BaTiO 3 towards the enhancement of second harmonic generation (SHG) was studied. XRD analysis confirms retention of rhombohedral symmetry in Sn 4+ doped BaTiO 3 UV Vis absorption study reveals that Sn 4+ doping has no significant effect on the optical band gap of BaTiO 3 Raman spectrum portrays that undoped and Sn 4+ doped BaTiO 3 nanopowder exhibits Raman active modes of rhombohedral symmetry. The studies displays that Sn 4+ doped BaTiO 3 has created non centrosymmetry, which could be utilized for enhancement in SHG and possibility to improve the efficiency in rhombohedral BaTiO 3 based solar cells.
