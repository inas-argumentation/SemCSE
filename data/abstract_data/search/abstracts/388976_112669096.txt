The Application of the DNDGN Multi functional Laser Coal Inventory Instrument in Thermal Power Plant
This paper sums up the practical application of DNDGN multi functional laser coal inventory instrument in Yangcheng International Power Generation Ltd.Co. describes the components, operation principles and installation procedures of the system in detail, and connecting with the actual coal inventory data of the company's No.4 coal field, makes the analysis on coal inventory accuracy. The practical operation shows that the system can quickly?finish the automatic measurement for open air coal storage in coal fired power plant with DNDGN multi functional laser coal inventory instrument,and can efficiently solve the problem that the accurate coal storage can't be obtained commonly in thermal power plant.
