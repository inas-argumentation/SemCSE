Differences dans les systemes de gestion des proprietaires dirigeants, entrepreneurs et operateurs de PME
Following an empirical study of the managerial systems of 116 small business owner managers, the subjects were divided into two groups. A total of 74 were classified as entrepreneurs, and the remaining 42 as operators. The managerial systems of all the owner managers in the sample were analyzed from the perspective of Fayol's P.O.C.C. elements (planning, organizing, commanding, and controlling) Each of the two groups had an activity system of its own. For the entrepreneurs, the activities in the system were visioning, designing, animating, monitoring, and learning, while for the operators, they were selecting, performing, assigning, allocating, monitoring, and adjusting. The text describes, explains, and discusses the findings. Resume L'objet de cette recherche consiste a mieux comprendre ou se situent les systemes de gestion des proprietaires dirigeants, entrepreneurs et operateurs de PME, en relation des elements du P.O.D.C. planifier, organiser, diriger, controler. A partir de l'etude empirique des systemes d'activites de 116 proprietaires dirigeants de PME dont 74 entrepreneurs et 42 operateurs, un modele de gestion est degage pour chaque categorie. Les activites du processus de gestion de l'entrepreneur sont: visionner, architectures animer, verifier et apprendre. Pour l'operateur, ces activites sont: choisir, accomplir, assigner, allouer, verifier, ajuster. Ces activites sont decrites, expliquees, discutees.
