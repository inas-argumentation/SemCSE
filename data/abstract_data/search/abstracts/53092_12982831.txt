Fingerprint analysis and multi component determination of Zibu Piyin recipe by HPLC with DAD and Q TOF/MS method
Abstract Zibu Piyin recipe (ZBPYR) a traditional Chinese medicine formula, is used for curing dementia caused by diabetes. For quality control of ZBPYR, fingerprint analysis and qualitative analysis using high performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) with a diode array detector, and confirmation using HPLC coupled with electrospray ionisation quadrupole time of flight tandem mass spectrometry (HPLC Q TOF MS) were undertaken. HPLC fingerprint consisting of 34 common peaks was developed among 10 batches of ZBPYR, in which 7 common peaks were identified in comparison with the authentic standards and detected simultaneously. Furthermore, these seven compounds were verified by HPLC Q TOF MS methods. The method can be applied to the quality control of ZBPYR. Graphical abstract
