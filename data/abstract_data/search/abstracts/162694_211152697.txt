The purpose of this study is to explore the relationship among the willingness of the organization change, organization communication, and organization learning. Two hundred and five effective surveys were received by employees of M Team companies in machine tool industry. The data was analyzed by confirmatory factor analysis, reliability analysis, using the SEM model to measure the relationship amongst the constructs. Results showed that the willingness of organization change has a positive influence on organization communication and organization learning. And then, the mediating effects of organization communication towards the willingness of organization change can positive influence organization learning. As a result it is suggested that interaction of the employees within the organization in order to communicate with and consolidate the understanding of vision, and to improve the willingness of organization change, organization communication, and organization learning.
