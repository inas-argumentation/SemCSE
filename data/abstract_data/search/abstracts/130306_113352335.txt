Study on the Measurement of Shielding Effectiveness of EMI Gasket Using Transfer
This paper begins with brief description of several measurement methods of Shielding Effectiveness (SE) of Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) Gasket. It discusses the measurement principle of SE of EMI gaskets, the structure of the required measure system and computational method with the transfer impedance measurement system according to the principle that energy penetrating the EMI gaskets is inversely proportional to transfer impedance of EMI gaskets.
