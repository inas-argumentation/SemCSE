Targeting Induced Local Lesions IN Genomes and Its Application in Plant Genetics and Improvement
TILLING(targeting induced local lesions in genomes)is a novel reverse genetics technology that provides rapid and effective characterization and targeting screen for mutations.The methodology,technical core and advantages of TILLING were introduced and its applications in mutation research,reverse genetics and functional genomics,SNP(Single Nucleotide Polymorphism)detection,germplasm enhancement and analysis,and crop genetic improvement were reviewed.
