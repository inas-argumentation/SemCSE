On finite plane deformations of compressible isotropic hyperelastic solids
SummaryThe purpose of this paper is to obtain solutions for the displacement and stress field of two boundary value problems, of practical importance, for finite plane deformations of compressible isotropic hyperelastic materials, of harmonic type, with elliptical boundaries. With the use of the asymptotic analysis and appropriate Laurent's series the solutions are obtained in the form of integral.
