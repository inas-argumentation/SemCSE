A POST COLONIAL STUDY ORIENTALISM AS PORTRAYED IN THE KARATE KID MOVIE A GRADUATING PAPER Submitted in Partial Fulfillment of the Requirements for Gaining the Bachelor Degree in English Literature
The Karate Kid is a movie that tells about the struggle of a boy named Dre who must move from America to China with his mother. In China, Dre felt cannot stand and uncomfortable since he got beatings and disruption continuously by Cheng and friends. Then Dre met Mr. Han and he got help from Mr. Han. After that Dre started to learn about Kung Fu from Mr. Han. Using great effort Dre eventually won the competition of Kung Fu by beating Cheng and his friends, Dre also received recognition from them and not disturbed anymore. The film itself is the West movie and directed also by Western. What's interesting about this movie is how this film depicts the character and characterization of the West and the East side, and how the director showed the superiority of the Western powers towards the East through Dre's character who could win the tournament of Kung Fu. Hence, the researcher wants to examine the portrayal of Orientalism that appears in the movie. This research uses a qualitative method and using library research method. The researcher applies Post colonial theory with the approach of Orientalism by Edward Said. Orientalism itself is thought that the West has the authority to provide stereotypes and construct the East. The purposes of these things are to show the domination of the West superiority to the East. The result from this research is about how the West constructs and depicts the East and the West itself through this film. This study also shows the gap that occurs in the film using a binary opposition. Then all closed by showing the portrayal of Orientalism in The Karate Kid movie.
