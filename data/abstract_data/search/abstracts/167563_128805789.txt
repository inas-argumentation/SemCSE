The Purpose of this study is investigate the trend of sports injury status in the Korean Foot Ball players during the Asian Games, East Asian Games and Olympic Games from 1986 to 1998. The subject of this study is total members of Korean Delegation Foot Ball Players. The situations of the Sports injury is divided into 4 cases with 14 Lesions and the mechanism of injury is divided into 4 mechanisms according to the previous investigations by the researchers. 1. The Korean delegation Foot Ball players of recent Asian, East Asian, Olympic games are 195 and there was no remarkable difference in Sports injury variations between the years. 2. Ages of the participants was vary from 19 to 33 years old. The most frequent age distribution was from 21 to 25 years old, is 132(67.7% players. The distribution is tend to closer by the year. and it says the amateur football players' age was restricted for the purposes of separation from professionalism. 3. Sports injuries situations during the practice was 76 players(39.0% during the games was 108 players(55.4% during the physical training was 61 players(31.3% out of the games was 30 players(15.4% 4. The severity of the sports injuries was classified into light, moderate, and severe. light cases were 165 cases (84.6% moderate cases were 26(13.3% severe cases were 6(3.1% 5. The mechanisms of injury is classified with 4 types rotation, strain, traumatic and mixed types in sum traumatic type was 36 cases(18.5% rotation type was 55 cases (28.2% strain was 130 cases (66.7% and mixed type was 73 cases (37.4% 6. The biggest incident rate was ankle and knee joint with 65 cases(33.3% next was lower back area with 63 cases(32.3% lower leg with 27 cases(13.8% foot area with 22 cases(11.3% in row. 7. The trend of incident rate in practice, during the game and out of the game was in younger age, and the proportions incident rate of before 1990 was high, it is thought to be the stage of sports injury care was not developed well compare with after 1990.
