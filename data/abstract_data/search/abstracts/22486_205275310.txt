Re: Remodelling of nasal mucosa in mild and severe persistent allergic rhinitis with special reference to the distribution of collagen, proteoglycans, and lymphatic vessels.
This article is very interesting as the authors provided the first evidence that airway remodelling may occur also in the upper airways of patients with allergic rhinitis (AR) [1] In fact, this elegant study clearly demonstrated that there is an altered distribution pattern of collagen, proteoglycans, and lymphatic vessels in patients with persistent AR in comparison with normal subjects. In addition, this study showed that the severity of rhinitis may affect the degree of remodelling. The authors concluded that those observed changes could potentially modulate the remodelling of nasal mucosa.
