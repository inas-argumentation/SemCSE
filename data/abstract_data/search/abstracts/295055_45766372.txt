Optimizing Compilers for Modern Architectures: A Dependence Based Approach [Book Review]
O ptimizing Compilers for Modern Architectures: A Dependence Based Approach, Randy Allen and Ken Kennedy. Although modern computer architectures designed with high speed microprocessors offer potential performance gains, their complexity makes it increasingly difficult to produce efficient code. The authors assert that compilers can play a vital role in addressing this issue. They base their methods on data dependence, a fundamental compiler analysis tool for optimizing programs on high performance microprocessors and parallel architectures. Allen and Kennedy provide a broad introduction to data dependence, the many transformation strategies it supports and its application to optimization problems. They apply their techniques to several languages, including Fortran 77, C, hardware description languages, Fortran 90, and High Performance Fortran. Worked examples demonstrate each compiled transformation while two case study compilers implement the theories described in each chapter.
