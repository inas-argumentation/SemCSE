Portable multifunctional solar energy heat preservation water cup
The utility model discloses a portable multifunctional solar energy heat preservation water cup which comprises a cup lid, a cup body and a base. The cup lid is located on the cup body, the base is installed at the bottom of the cup body, a heating plate, a portable power source and a solar energy controller are arranged in the base, the heating plate is connected with the portable power source, the solar energy controller is connected with the portable power source, the outer side of the cup body is wrapped with a solar energy panel, a vacuum insulation layer is arranged between the solar energy panel and the cup body, the solar energy panel is connected with the solar energy controller, and a power control switch is further arranged on the base. According to the portable multifunctional solar energy heat preservation water cup, electric power storage can be conducted through solar energy based on the portable power source and the heating plate, the portable multifunctional vacuum water cup has a charging function for simple electronic equipment, the heat preservation water cup has a heating function, a solar energy charging function and charging and discharging functions of the portable power source, and the heat preservation water cup is convenient to carry.
