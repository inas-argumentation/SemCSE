SRC 1091.001 Final Deliverable Report for Oct 2006: Highly Scalable Multilevel Placement Algorithm for Mixed size with Complex Constraints
2 Summary This report summarizes the work on robust techniques for multilevel mixed size placement with complex constraints: routability and thermal. Also, we have developed the rst analytical work for the heterogeneous placement problem for FPGAs and structured ASICs. The mPL package has been augmented by a fast, Poisson based numerical PDE (partial dieren tialequation) solver in order to support global renemen t of a given placement consistent with accurate, nonconvex models of constraints and objectives. Numerical results show that our latest placement tool{mPL6 produces the best solutions comparing to available academic placers. Recently, it has produced the best quality of solutions in the ISPD'06 placement contest [9] where the main objective of the contest addresses the routability capability of placers. On the IBM version 2 examples [10] mPL6 produces results that can be successfully routed using an industrial router with 5 13% shorter wirelength than other academic placers. For thermal driven placement, mPL6 can reduce the maximum temperature by 11% with only 3% wirelength increase. Finally, we present mPL H, a heterogeneous placement algorithm for FPGAs which is built based on the generalized force directed formulation in mPL6. Compared to a leading edge commercial FPGA placer, Quartus II v5.0 by Altera Corporation, mPL H produces 3% shorter half perimeter wirelength, and 2% shorter routed wirelength on average. Furthermore, mPL H shows better scalability when the examples becomes sucien tly large. When compared to Quartus with its timing driven mode turned on, mPL H produces 11% shorter half perimeter wirelength, 4% routed wirelength, with a penalty of 12% in maximum frequency. Overall, compared the mPL3 at the beginning of this project, mPL6 delivered over 30% wirelength reduction on real industrial circuits. This delivers over one technology general improvement (over 30% as promised in our original proposal.
