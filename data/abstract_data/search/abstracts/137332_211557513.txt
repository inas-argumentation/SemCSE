Measurement properties of the Patient Reported Outcomes Information System (PROMIS(r) Itch Questionnaire: itch severity assessments in adults with atopic dermatitis
Little is known about the validity of numeric rating scales (NRS) and verbal rating scales (VRS) for itch and itch frequency for assessing itch severity in atopic dermatitis (AD) We evaluated the Patient Reported Outcomes Information System (PROMIS(r) Itch Questionnaire (PIQ) itch severity assessment, including multiple NRS, VRS and frequency of itch assessments, in adults with AD and compared their performance.
