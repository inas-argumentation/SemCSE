Crystal Structure of the Isotactic Alternate Copolymer between Carbon Monoxide and Styrene
The crystal structue of a highly isotactic optically active alternating styrene carbon monoxide copolymer, obtained with a chiral cationic Pd(II) bioxazoline complex, is determined and refined from powder X ray diffraction data with the Rietveld method. The unit cell is monoclinic, P21 space group, with a 8.367 A, b 7.574 A (chain repeat) c 5.47 A, b 110.0deg, and Z 2 monomeric unis with a density of 1.32 g/cm3. The observed chain conformation has a repeat period almost identical to that observed in syndiotactic copolymers in spite of the different symmetry connecting the monomeric units, s(2/1) instead of tc. The different symmetry connecting the monomeric units along the chain influences significantly the reciprocal orientation of the phenyl groups within each chain and between different chains.
