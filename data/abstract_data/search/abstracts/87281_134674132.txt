Automatic stable lifting type clothes drying rack
The invention discloses an automatic stable lifting type clothes drying rack. The automatic stable lifting type clothes drying rack comprises a lifting rack, a clothes drying rod and an electric lifting mechanism. The electric lifting mechanism comprises two first winding wheels on the left and the right, the first winding wheels are respectively connected with two pull ropes, the other ends of the pull ropes are respectively connected on the left side and the right side of the clothes drying rod, the lifting rack comprises a slide rod, two slide sleeves sleeve the slide rod, two sides of the clothes drying rod are hinged to one ends of two lower connecting rods respectively, the other ends of the lower connecting rods are hinged with the lower sides of the slide sleeves respectively, the left side and the right side of the electric lifting mechanism are respectively hinged to one end of an upper connecting rod, and the other ends of the upper connecting rods are hinged to the upper sides of the slide sleeves respectively. The automatic stable fliting type clothes drying rack is simple in structure, the pull ropes are identical in lifting speed, balance of stress on the clothes drying rod during lifting is guaranteed, and inclination and shaking are avoided.
