An Analysis of the Profit Model and Its Financial Evaluation of GOME Retail Holdings Limited under the "New Retail"
As the Internet becomes more popular among Chinese citizens, Ecommerce is developing. In the meanwhile, the traditional retail industry has been greatly affected, so the transformation is urgent. In 2016, Jack Ma proposed a new concept of "New Retail" and then the government encouraged it. After that, retail companies began to transform. With the development of AI, cloud computing and big data, several companies explored new profit model, seeking online and offline development. GOME Retail Holdings Limited is one of the leading transformed retail companies, which is representative. Based on the profit model proposed by Guohong Zhen, this paper takes GOME as the research object, analyzing its profit model under the new retail. By comparing its financial evaluation before and after transformation, the paper analyzes its advantages and disadvantages and makes some suggestions for the company. Other companies in retail industry may gain some implications and find out how to survive and develop well in the trend of new retail.
