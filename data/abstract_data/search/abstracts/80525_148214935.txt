Interpersonal Metafunction's Analysis of Obama's Inaugural Speeches
Based on interpersonal metafunction perspective of Systematic Functional Linguistics and data of Obama( 2013) 's inaugural speeches,this paper probes into the reflection of interpersonal meaning in the light of mood,modality and personal pronouns. The author concludes that: Obama uses many declarative clauses,properly makes use of "can""will"and "must",flexibly adopts personal pronouns "I"and "we" By means of interpersonal metafunction perspective,to some degree the readers can understand the purpose and real intension of Obama's inaugural speeches deeply and profoundly.
