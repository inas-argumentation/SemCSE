Exploratory Factor Analysis
Exploratory factor analysis (EFA) is a very popular statistical tool that is used throughout the social sciences. It has proven useful for assessing theories of learning, cognition, and personality (Aluja, Garcia, Garcia, 2004) for exploring scale validity (Manos, Rachel C. Kanter, Jonathan W. Luo, Wen; and for reducing the dimensionality in a set of variables so that they can be used more easily in further statistical analyses (Mashal Kasirer, 2012)
