Study on nursing of patients with diabetic nephropathy hemodialysis
Objective:To investigate the care measures to reduce complications during diabetic nephropathy (DN) hemodialysis. Methods:To conduct the retrospective analysis on 63 cases of DN patients with hemodialysis complications from April 2007 to October 2009 in our department.Results:12 cases of hypoglycemia occurred in hemodialysis period,24 cases of hypotension,16 cases of hypertension,9 cases of varying degrees of bleeding,2 cases of mild infection of puncture. After a positive symptomatic treatment and care,the patient's condition was improved. Conclusion:Suitable diet care,psychological care,skin care,medication nursing and case observations can help to reduce complications in the patients with DN in hemodialysis,extend the lives of patients and improve their quality of life.
