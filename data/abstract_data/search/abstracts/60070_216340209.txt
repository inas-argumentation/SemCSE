Effect of warm mix rubber modified asphalt mixture as stress absorbing layer on anti crack performance in cold region
Abstract Reflection cracking is one of the most prevalent distress phenomena occurring for semi rigid base asphalt concrete pavement especially in cold region. In this paper, the effect of warm mix rubber modified asphalt mixture (WMRMA) on cracking resistance were investigated by tensile stress restrained specimen test (TSRST) and finite element (FE) analysis method. WMRMA exhibited good low temperature performance in the case of blending and compaction temperature fall of 30 degC after incorporating SK I warm additive. Moreover the FE analysis also proved that WMRMA can significantly improve the force conditions at the bottom of asphalt surface layer owing to stress absorbing layer acting as an anti crack performer compared to other hot mix asphalt mixture. Thereby, WMRAM is suitable for using as a stress absorbing layer to counter road cracking in cold region.
