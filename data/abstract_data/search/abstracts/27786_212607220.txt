A Survey on Methods of Abstractive Text Summarization
Text summarization is the process of extracting the important information which gives us the overall idea of the entire document. It is a tedious task for human beings to generate an abstract manually since it requires a rigorous analysis of the document. In order to ease human efforts and to reduce time, automatic summarization techniques prove to be very useful. Text summarization has two techniques viz. Extractive summarization and abstractive summarization. Extractive technique[1] is the one in which we generate a summery by using the relevant sentences in the document as they are, whereas in abstractive summarization technique[2] we form the sentences on our own and then combine these sentences to form an abstract. In this paper, an overall idea about the extractive methods is presented and we focus on the abstractive summarization methods.
