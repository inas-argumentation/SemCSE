Constitutionalising Racism: The British Nationality Bill 1981
It is the aim of this section to include summaries and comments on recent developments in law and policy in the social welfare area. This covers short notes on changes in the law or in administrative practice, as well as discussion and criticism of future plans or political strategies. In this issue we look at the government's proposed new nationality legislation, and at the operation ofthe Manpower Services Commission's Youth Opportunities Programme. In future issues we hope to discuss recent developments in health, education and social services policies; and we would welcome contributions of 2000 to 3000 words in these or other areas. Contributions to this section should be sent to Pete Alcock, Dept. E.B.S. Sheffield City Polytechnic, Pond Street, Sheffield, S1 1WB.
