The disorders chosen for discussion are alcoholism, psychotropics in the elderly, biofeedback, anorexia nervosa, hypochondriasis, stuttering, phobias, and spinal injuries. A different selection of pertinent topics might well have come from a different perspective, for example, on what is often seen in emergency departments. Generally the book is organized, and presents the topics in an orderly and concise way. It suffers from being general rather than specific in many areas. It is not a treatment manual with sufficient information to treat cases of any complexity. In a day when all books are expensive, the suggested price may be somewhat greater than hoped for by students having to be discriminating in their library choices.
