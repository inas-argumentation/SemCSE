Open type charging barrel of cold chamber die casting machine for semisolid die casting
The invention belongs to the field of die casting of non ferrous metal and particularly relates to an open type charging barrel of a cold chamber die casting machine for semisolid die casting. The open type charging barrel of the cold chamber die casting machine for semisolid die casting is characterized in that a charging barrel body (1) is a hollow cylinder with the two ends open; the barrel wall of one end of the charging barrel body (1) is semi open, and the barrel wall of the other end of the charging barrel body (1) is closed; round holes (3) which are evenly distributed and penetrate into the charging barrel in the axial direction are formed in the cross section of the semi open barrel wall, and heating devices are arranged in the round holes; a flange boss (2) is arranged outside the end, provided with the closed wall, of the charging barrel body. By the adoption of the semi open design, the requirement for radial restraint of a punch of the die casting machine is met, and a semisolid metal slurry/blank die casting technique capable of achieving the solid fraction from 1% 80% is achieved; the heat loss of semisolid metal slurry/blanks in the charging barrel is reduced by means of the heating devices, temperature and structure differences of the semisolid metal slurry/blanks caused by local temperature fall are eliminated, and the quality of parts formed in the substrate stage is guaranteed.
