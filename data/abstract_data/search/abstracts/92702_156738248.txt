A Reflection on Reconstructing the Content of Track and Field Course for Major of Physical Education
Implementation of new course in physical education standard requires stronger teaching force as guarantee.Physical education major should cultivate PE teacher to adapt basic educational reform.This article takes the primary and junior high school physical education curriculum reform as the background,from the dimension of three great curriculum theories that has different emphases.5 proposals of reform are presented with an attempt to provide new ideas for track and field curriculum contents of physical education major.
