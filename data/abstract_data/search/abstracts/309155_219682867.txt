Financial Literacy and Investment Behaviour A Study on the Working Age Population of India
Financial literacy means the knowledge and the efficient skills required for a person to make effectual and well informed decisions regarding his personal finance. With the increasing number of new age financial products people sometimes it becomes difficult for a layman to assess and effectively understand the risks and returns associated with different types of instruments. Therefore, it is necessary for an individual to choose a well suited financial products by assessing its pros and cons. The paper seeks to assess and show the level of financial literacy and knowledge among the working age population of Delhi. This study also aims to find out whether there is a significant relationship between financial literacy and individual's behavior towards investment in various products.
