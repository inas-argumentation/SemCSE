REPORT OF THE MEDICAL RESEARCH COUNCIL OF GREAT BRITAIN
In the section on the treatment of cancer by radium mention is made of the work on radium beam therapy, which was the subject of a special report. This report was considered in TIn; MEDICAL JOURXAL OF AUSTRALIA of March 11, 1939, at page 396. It is also stated that there looms in the distance the need for the study of the therapeutic effects of neutrons and of temporarily radioactive substances, such as radio sodium, made possible by the discovery and development of the cyclotron by Lawrence in Caiifornia. Reports indicate that the biological effects of neutrons are of even greater interest than those of X rays and 'Y rays. It is suggested that in cancerous growths and allied conditions, such as the leuchremtas, neutrons may be of outstanding importance. There is even a possibility that both radium and X rays will be superseded by the cyclotron in the treatment of cancer by radiations and radio active substances. A member of the council's staff is at present working with Professor Lawrence in Caiifornia.
