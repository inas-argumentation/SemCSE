Asbjorn Wahl (2011) The Rise and Fall of the Welfare State London: Pluto Press. PS18.99, pp. 246, pbk.
A less generous reading of the book would be to say that it is completely incoherent, that it consists of a sample of stapled together papers with little joint focus of attention. Several of the chapters have little relation to the title or main framing of the book and there are no cross references between them. Volumes like these can sometimes be salvaged by a skilful editor, who might be able to contort an ex post facto common analytical frame for the book, or at least bring out some common themes or tensions from the chapters. Unfortunately, neither the introductory nor the concluding chapters make any attempt at this. They are self contained little reflections, without any systematic discussion of the contents of the rest of the volume. Perhaps the task to bring out the common core of the chapters was just impossible, but in my opinion it should at least have been tried. Since there is clearly a fair degree of tension between the chapters this could have been a theme for the framing of the volume. Not all authors seem to share the editors' gloomy outlook on the current prospects for the welfare state, or even their underlying scholarly or ideological commitments, and it would have been worthwhile to bring out these tensions in the framing chapters. Or we might have been offered some reflections on the past and current scholarship of the particular department which the volume is set up to celebrate, and how the current volume reflects these intellectual endeavours. I could even have settled with the faintest hint that all these scholars have something more in common than their affiliation to an academic institution located at the Humber estuary. But the readers get nothing of the sort. Publishers these days seem to view edited volumes with a fair degree of scepticism. Books like this one may be a reason why. stefan svallfors Umea University, and The Institute for Futures Studies, Stockholm stefan.svallfors@soc.umu.se
