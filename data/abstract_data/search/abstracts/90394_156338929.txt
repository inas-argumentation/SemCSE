Empirical Research of Platform Impact on Mobile Phone Price Based on Hedonic Price Model
This paper gets mobile phone trade data from Taobao net during January 2010,and selects 14 attributes including the attributes from mobile phone and from platform respectively to study platform impacts on product prices by using the hedonic price model.The results indicate that all the attributes from product(excluding ring) have positive impact on price,and the attributes from platform have positive or negative impact on price.The hedonic price of product amounts 90% of the total price,and the hedonic price of platform to 10%.It proves that the platform has definite impact on product price.
