Developpement des vaccins et strategies vaccinales contre la COVID 19. Des donnees pour une decision partagee
The public has many questions about vaccines against COVID 19. Informing general practitioners and other vaccinators provides health care users with clear and reliable information to support a shared decision. They are the bulwark against population's vaccine hesitancy but can be contaminated by doubt if they do not have a solid argument to answer the questions of health users.
