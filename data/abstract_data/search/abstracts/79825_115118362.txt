[The divine Libit Ishtar, the humble shepherd of Nippur when justice in Sumer and Akkad he had established, the temple of justice he built] Preservation Lab Treatment Report
A group of 6 cuneiform tablets owned by the Public Library were housed together in an Ethafoam and Volara filled enclosure. The Public Library's cuneiform tablets can be found on the Cuneiform Digital Library Initiative's site There are five tablets and one cone, found in 1903. The tablets are receipts for expenditures. The cone contains an inscription from the Libit Ishtar of Isin.
