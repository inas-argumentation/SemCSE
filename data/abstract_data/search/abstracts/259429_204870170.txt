LIOUVILLE THEOREMS AND A SCHWARZ LEMMA FOR HOLOMORPHIC MAPPINGS BETWEEN KAHLER MANIFOLDS
We derive some consequences of the Liouville theorem for plurisubharmonic functions of L. F. Tam and the author. The first result provides a nonlinear version of the complex splitting theorem (which splits off a factor of C isometrically from the simply connected Kahler manifold with nonnegative bisectional curvature and a linear growth holomorphic function) of L. F. Tam and the author. The second set of results concerns the so called k hyperbolicity and its connection with the negativity of the kscalar curvature (when k 1 they are the negativity of holomorphic sectional curvature and Kobayashi hyperbolicity) introduced recently in [30] by F. Zheng and the author. We lastly prove a new Schwarz Lemma type estimate in terms of only the holomorphic sectional curvatures of both domain and target manifolds.
