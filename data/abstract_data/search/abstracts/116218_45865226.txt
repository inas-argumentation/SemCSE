The teaching portfolio: a useful guide for pharmacists' teaching goals.
There are few opportunities in life that allow one to take more than they give. Despite the time needed for extensive preparation, teaching can really be this type of blessing in disguise. No matter the format, there is something to be said about the opportunity to impart some lasting mentorship and instruction on one's peers and students. Whether through subtle words of appreciation or through more tangible examples of appreciation, the gifts from teaching can really be priceless. These examples of gratitude do not go unnoticed by those teachers whose passion is to help others learn.
