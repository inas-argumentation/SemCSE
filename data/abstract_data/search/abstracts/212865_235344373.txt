Domain Adversarial Training For Robust Question Answering
In this project, we are building question answering systems that can be robustly extended to answer questions on domains outside the training question answering datasets. We used domain adversarial training methods to improve upon the baseline of a pretrained DistiIBERT system by creating a domain classifier that attempts to classify the domain of the input based on the last hidden layer of the DistilBERT model. This accomplished some improvement on the baseline model, with a score of EM 41.353 and F1 59.803 on the test set, and a score of EM 31.675 and F1 48.836 on the validation set.
