In the discussion that followed, controversially, he quoted from an American institution describing two kinds of children, one 'born to be criminals, who you cannot change' (p. 79) In contemporary times, such a view would be regarded as unethical. Lecture 4, on 'mind over matter' has Jung suggest that while individuals feel differently, as part of a group they share the same problems, that are symbolised in myth, legend and archetype, of which he gave extensive examples. That made this lecture the most interesting one for me. Jung claimed dreams to be the natural reaction to the self regulating psychic system. Lecture 5 describes transference as a form of projection, the carrying over from one form to another. Jung proposes that the emotional link between two people can be positive or negative and explains that countertransference can result from the fear of attraction. I recommend this book as an easy to read introduction to Jung's concepts, but with the caution that some of the ideas are of his time and now outdated.
