Relocating the Role of Counselor in Colleges under the "Learner centered" Educational Concept
"Learner centered" means that students are the principal part in the learning process while teachers are guiders and helpers only.There are many differences between the "learner centered" educational concept and the traditional one in terms of the guidance mode.The "learner centered" educational concept aims to develop and enhance the overall qualities and abilities of students.They are supposed to actively engage in the construction of meaning of "human",with the guidance and help from their teachers.Counselors must update their new features so that they can play a more diversified role in the process of education and management.
