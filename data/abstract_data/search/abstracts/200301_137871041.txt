Factors affeoting gravure print quality of coated paper. Part II: Influence of supercalendering conditions and pigment types
The effect of varying supercalendering conditions and different coating pigment types on gravure print quality were investigated. A series of successively increasing supercalendering pressures from 35 kN/m to 157.5 kN/m were used, with associated temperatures ranging from 20degC to 100degC. In addition, the influence on printing performance of incorporating various levels (10 50% of two sizes (0.4mm and 1.0 mm) of hollow plastic pigments in the coating layer, in conjunction with both conventional clay and GCC pigments, was evaluated. Two systems of assessing printing performance and quality were used to determine the influence of each variable/parameter under study, these were: 1. print density and print gloss, both measured by conventional instruments, and 2. ink lay down, assessed by low power (macro) optical photomicroscopy, using high angle incident illumination. Results obtained from assessing print quality showed that several clear trends were evident, including: print density increases with supercalendering pressure; print density increases with supercalendering temperature; incorporation of plastic pigments results in improved print density; presence of plastic pigment had different effects on print properties in coatings containing clay than those containing GCC. These effects are explained in terms of the measured physical properties of the sheets, and the reported effects of increasing supercalendering pressures and temperatures on coating structure. Results obtained from calendering of coatings containing plastic pigments are interpreted in terms of the assumed effects of the calendering conditions on the hollow thermoplastic spheres. It is proposed that the improvements in printing performance of coating containing plastic pigments are related to their influence on coating structure that these innovative pigments have, and also dependant on their unique response to calendering conditions.
