Finding your way in academic writing, Elizabeth Henning, Sarah Gravett and Wilhelm van Rensburg, eds. book review
The three authors are academics who have taught writing composition to Honours and Master's students at the University of Johannesburg for a decade. They have conducted workshops in this field for both students and academics at many South African institutions. Many books on this topic have been published or are presently available. The mere fact that it was necessary to publish a second edition of this book, however, suggests that there is still a real need for it.
