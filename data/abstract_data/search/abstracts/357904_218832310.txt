A Reconfigurable Time to Digital Converter based on Pulse Stretcher and Gated Delay Line
A Reconfigurable Time to Digital Converter (TDC) based on Pulse Stretcher and Gated Delay Line (GDL) with variable 12 to 15 coarse bit and 5 to 8 fine bits is proposed. The coarse bits measure the different range of input time and the fine bits provide different resolution. The TDC uses front (T_{F} and back (T_{B} fractional parts to achieve high resolution. The highest input time of 129.75 \mu s$ can be measured using a 15 bit coarse counter and a resolution of 39.37 ps is achieved by the proposed times stretching method.
