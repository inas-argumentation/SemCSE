Influence of Portevin Le Chatelier Effect on Shear Strain Path Reversal in an Al Mg Alloy at Room and High Temperatures
The main objective of this study is to characterize the mechanical behaviour of an Al Mg alloy in conditions close to those encountered during sheet forming processes, i.e. with strain path changes and at strain rates and temperatures in the range 1.2x10 3 1.2x10 1 s 1 and 25 200degC, respectively. The onset of jerky flow and the interaction of dynamic strain ageing with the work hardening are investigated during reversed loading in specific simple shear tests, which consist of loading up to various shear strain values followed by reloading in the opposite direction, combined with direct observations of the sample surface using a digital image correlation technique. Both strain path changes and temperature are clearly shown to influence the occurrence and onset of the Portevin Le Chatelier (PLC) effect. Moreover, the Bauschinger effect observed in the material response shows that the PLC effect has a major influence on the kinematic contribution to work hardening as well as its stagnation during the reloading stage, which could open up interesting lines of research to improve theoretical plasticity models for this family of aluminium alloys.
