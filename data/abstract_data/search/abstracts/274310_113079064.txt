RAILWAY VEHICLE MAINTENANCE AND INFORMATION SYSTEMS
To stop the continuous economic fall of railways in Slovakia, there is a strong need to cut down operational expenses. The rolling stock maintenance process is one of the candidates to economizing, but there is a high risk of endangering safety or lowering the traffic reliability resulting from a worsened technical state of vehicles. The paper analyzes the current maintenance management systems and attempts to monitor rolling stock technical conditions on Slovak Railways, as well as the approach of the Department of Machinery Maintenance Engineering, the University of Zilina, for improvements in the maintenance process on railways in the past. The paper also presents a proposal for implementation of a maintenance management system based on the Reliability Centered Maintenance method and a link of this system with project management methods for planning and scheduling maintenance activities.
