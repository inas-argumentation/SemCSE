Design of Supplemented Panel Surveys With Application to the National Resources Inventory
The National Resources Inventory (NRI) is a stratified, two stage area sample of the nonfederal lands in the United States conducted by the Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) of the U.S. Department of Agriculture. This survey is based on approximately 300,000 primary sampling units and about 800,000 points and is updated every 5 years. The NRI is used for studying the dynamics of natural resources, with an emphasis on agricultural land uses and soil erosion. There is currently interest in extending the NRI to an annualized inventory, with sample elements rotating across years. Using time series models fitted to the available historical data, we investigate the efficiency of estimates of level and change under the proposed rotation structure.
