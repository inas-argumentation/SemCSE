Optimization Research of Sectors Based on Weighted Voronoi Polygon
The controller workload is a major factor in constraining the airspace capacity.the purpose of sector optimization is to balance of controller workload accordance with certain rules.increase the capacity.In this paper,the planning sectors method based on weighted voronoi diagram is presented,the the controller workload statistical method is analyzed and gotten.Because of the low speed of the simulated annealing(SA) and deficiency of contraction earlier for genetic algorithm,the hybrid algorithm which based on growing algorithm and ant colony algorithm is used to solve.And then the paper optimize the Voronoi polygon.ultimatwly got the optimal borderlines of sector.Through the simulation of accrual airspace data,simulation result validates the rationality of the sector optimization method.
