Ecological variations in thermal infrared emissivity of vegetation
Thermal emissivity measurements in the 10.5 12.5 mm spectral region were taken for a variety of native common and dominant plants of the southern United States and Mexico. Results of these measurement are reported with a statistical analysis that suggests the present of a significant difference between the emissivity and hence the thermal properties of plants from desert, tropical, and temperate regions. A discussion of the significance and interpretation of these results is presented.
