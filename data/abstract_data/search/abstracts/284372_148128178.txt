Horror, materiality and synesthesia in video games
This study seeks to evoke moments of intensity through the description of affordances (Gibson, 1986) and Sensoriality (Regis, 2008) that may be observed in the game Rise of Nightmares (2011) It is believed that this "survival horror" game which is controlled by body movements read by the Xbox 360's Kinect accessory allows the feeling of fear and tension to be more intense than those experienced in other media products. Our hypothesis is that this occurs because of the immersion provided by the playability that the accessory engenders.
