Rancang Bangun Aplikasi Website Marketplace Pemasaran Perikanan Dan Kelautan Pada CV. Mina Nusantara Berjaya
Fisheries and marine marketing marketplace applications aim to accommodate fishermen, aquaculture and product processing industries to sell and promote fisheries and marine products in Pemalang district, Central Java. And, facilitating information to alumni or marine and fisheries student to conduct training and certification, and education. Marketplace as an online based media where the sellers and buyers meet. Buyers can search for as many products as possible with various desired criteria based on market prices, while sellers can find out the consumer needs of their products or services. In making this application, Waterfall method was applied in building the system starting from the identification of needs before doing the activity. For testing this system, the writer used black box and UAT User Acceptance Test. Fisheries and marine marketing marketplace website application system has been successful and has been accepted by the CV. Mina Nusantara Berjaya. This application can be used by 3 users, they are admin, seller, and buyer. Admin can monitor the number of users, manage data on certification, training, and certification as well as disbursing seller transactions. Sellers can sell products, provide product information, and prices for fisheries and marine products. Buyers can search for desired products, can make transactions to sellers.
