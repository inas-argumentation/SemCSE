From inFormation to knowledge. but seriously
The information flood on education coming from international assessments, big data and academic and professional research, now coupled with the enormous legacy of tacit knowledge characteristic of the teaching profession, is placing educators before the practical imperative to separate the wheat from the chaff and academics before the moral imperative to assign research findings their right value. This paper first offers an overview or informational overload and then concentrates in two aatempts to synthetize international research findings, Visible Learning and Actuaciones de exito en las escuelas europeas, whose contrast reveals the ultimate tension between science and ideology.
