Gyrotron multistage depressed collector based on E x B drift concept using azimuthal electric field. I. Basic design
Multistage Depressed Collectors (MDCs) are widely used in vacuum tubes to regain energy from the depleted electron beam. However, the design of an MDC for gyrotrons, especially for those deployed in fusion experiments and future power plants, is not trivial. Since gyrotrons require relatively high magnetic fields, their hollow annular electron beam is magnetically confined in the collector. In such a moderate magnetic field, the MDC concept based on E x B drift is very promising. Several concrete design approaches based on the E x B concept have been proposed. This paper presents a realizable design of a two stage depressed collector based on the E x B concept.
