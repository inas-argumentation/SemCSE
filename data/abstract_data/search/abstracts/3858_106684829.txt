General Zoology Laboratory Manual
This is an up to date zoology lab manual, which can be used with any general zoology text on the market. Has an evolutionary focus Photographs and illustrations added Old exercise 6 on animal taxonomy was replaced by a new exercise on the ecology evolutionary adaptations of stream invertebrates Learning objectives have been completely re written for clarity Lab Resource guide is available with this edition *Three part organisation allows for flexibility of use in most courses "Stop and Ask Yourself" questions offer strategic checkpoints for students to self test their understanding of the important concepts.
