Investigation the relationship between the three aspects of organizational structure and motivation of the organization's staffs in 2015
This study aimed to investigate the relationship between the components of organizational structure (formalization, centralization, and complexity) and motivationof the staffs of Bojnourd Payam Nour University in 2015. The method of this study was descriptive and correlational. The statistical population was all staffs of Bojnoord Payam nour University in 2015 (N= 50) and to do this the total numeration methodwas used. Data collection was done through, questionnaires, standardized organizational structure Robbins (1987) and employee motivation questionnaire Hackman and Oldham (1980) that were used in the study of Shahidi (1999) To answer the questions, Likert five degree scale, from too little to too much, was used that was validated previously by the professors and Experts, and its reliability by using Cronbach's alpha coefficient is 0/851and 0/827, respectively.For data collection, the Pearson correlation test was used to examine the relationships between hypotheses. To measure the components of the organizational structure, the variance measurement test (ANOVA) was used and to evaluate the effect of each component of organizational structure on staffs' motivation, the data collected by the Pearson correlation test were analyzed by spss software version 18. The results revealed that there is a significant relationship between the components of organizational structure that are formalization, centralization, and complexity. As the formalization and centralization have a direct relationship with the motivation and complexity and inverse relationship with the motivation. The highest relationship is between the complexity and motivation and the lowest relationship is between the centralization and motivation.
