The Use of Art and Culture in Modern Fabric and Clothing Design
With the continuous changes of the times, people's aesthetic is also constantly changing, but there are some arts and cultures that can always develop and continue under the promotion of the trend of the times. The main reason is that it takes geometric patterns as the basic arrangement basis, and finally forms a very special visual effect through the use of light and color. Based on this, this paper will focus on the analysis of the characteristics of the art style, and then study its important impact on modern fabric and clothing design, to ensure that it can be more perfect applied to the design process of modern clothing, so that the final pavement works can be more aesthetic.
