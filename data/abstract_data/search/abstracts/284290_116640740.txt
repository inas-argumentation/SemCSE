The umbrella pole structure of a light umbrella
The present invention belongs to the technical field lighting appliances umbrella, more particularly to a lighting rod umbrella structure in the umbrella. The present invention discloses a lighting rod umbrella structure in the umbrella, the umbrella pole structure comprises a support tube lighting umbrella, the sleeved tube is supported on the light pipe, the light pipe at both ends of the globe is provided the shade equipped with a light source, said light source and the shade is provided with the support tube through the LED light source by a plurality of evenly distributed welded on the circuit board, the light source was disposed on the radio, the circuit board is connected with a wire. The present invention is such that the entire light umbrella detachably more simple, and when needed also more convenient maintenance, so that the entire lighting umbrella more readily accepted by the public.
