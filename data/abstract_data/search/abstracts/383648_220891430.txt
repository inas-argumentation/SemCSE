Investigation of Regional Gap in the Yellow River Basin and Analysis of High quality Development Strategies
In order to further study the high quality development of the Yellow River Basin, based on the GDP and resident population data of 93 cities (or states, leagues) along the Yellow River Basin from 2000 to 2017, according to the analysis of the absolute differences in the Yellow River Basin, using the Theil index method, this paper measured the economic gaps in the three regions and empirically examined the impact of regional coordinated development strategies on the regional economic development gaps in different periods in the Yellow River Basin. The research shows that: (1) The per capita GDP gap among the provinces and regions in the Yellow River Basin has been increasing year by year. (2) From a spatial perspective, the overall gap in the Yellow River Basin is slowly declining; the development gaps in the three major zones have a greater impact on the overall basin, and after 2009, it has converged to a steady state. (3) Further research found that during the implementation of different regional coordinated development strategies, it has a significant impact on the regional economic gap in the Yellow River Basin. The implementation of the high quality development strategy of the Yellow River Basin needs to take full consideration of the province's own conditions, endowments, and competitive advantages, and take sustainability as its main feature to promote ecological protection and high quality development of the Yellow River Basin by classification.
