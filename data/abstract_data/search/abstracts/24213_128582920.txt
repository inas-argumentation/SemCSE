Late Cretaceous early Tertiary gastropods from southwestern Patagonia, Argentina
Morphometric analysis demonstrates that Strepsidura ficus (Gabb) of Harris (1895) from the lower middle Eocene Reklaw and Queen City Formations of Texas, is specifically distinct from Whitneya ficus Gabb (1864) of the Californian Eocene. Accordingly, the new name Strepsidura harrisi is proposed for the Texas species. Strepsidura s.s. is represented in North America by S. ficus (Gabb) S. harrisi n. sp. and an unnamed species closely related to S. harrisi in the upper Paleocene Sequin Formation of Texas.
