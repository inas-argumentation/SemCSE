The English Translating Strategies of Political Essays:A Case Study of Report on the Work of the Government in 2010
As one of the important practical texts,political essay is characterized with its unique stylistic features.This paper,guided by the German Skopos Theory,analyses the function of the political text and its translation ends.Taking as an example of both Chinese and English versions of Report on the Work of the Government in 2010,it further discusses its stylistic features and translating strategies from lexical,syntactic and rhetorical perspectives.
