Energy Efficiency Analysis of Cache Enable Small Cell Interference Networks under Different Caching Placement Strategies
Small cell caching is recognized as an essential technique for improving the ultimate limit of networks with the help of the embedded storage at both small cell base station (SBS) and mobile user (MU) In this paper, we consider a typical \pmb{3\times 3} cache enable small cell interference networks, and propose three different caching placement strategies, namely, the conventional caching placement strategy (CCPS) the cooperative caching placement strategy (CPCPS) and the coded caching placement strategy (CDCPS) In order to evaluate the system performance, we also mathematically characterize the energy efficiency (EE) of the downlink transmission w.r.t. the above three caching placement strategies. Numerical results demonstrate that CDCPS achieves a superior performance to both CPCPS and CCPS.
