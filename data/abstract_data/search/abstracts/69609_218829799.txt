The Impact of Social Media on Recruitment: Opportunities, Benefits, and Challenges
Authors investigate various types of activities of employers on social networks in the course of staff recruitment and provide an assessment of their effectiveness from the companies' point of view given in sociological surveys. The article also contains researches of the popularity of various social media within recruitment as well as general activities (for example, HR brand promotion) The article also discusses types of applicants' behavior in social networks during employment and their willingness to conduct business negotiations with employers through social media. Data for Russian labor market are compared to the results of similar studies among foreign countries. It justifies the conclusion that, in general, Russian companies rather highly evaluate the effectiveness of social networks as a tool for recruiting staff, but the lack of conventional practices for their use in processes such as communication with candidates of certain professions, as well as the lack of a professional social network, do not allow social networks to close a really large number of vacancies.
