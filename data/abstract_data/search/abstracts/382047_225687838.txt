The influence of Academic Procrastination and Test Anxiety on Dishonest Academic Behavior among College Students
Dishonest academic behavior is a ubiquitous global phenomenon that happens in all disciplines at the college or university level. Therefore, the understanding of the causes that affect students' decisions to involve in high frequency of academic dishonesty is important for academic institutions, in order to reduce its occurrence.So, the present investigation attempts to provide a practical based module to the educational society in order to endorse ethical culture and develop approaches for controlling the problem and producing an effective environment of academic integrity.The present study was conducted to explore the relationship between academic procrastination and test anxietywith dishonest academic behavioramongcollege students. The study is descriptive in nature and the participants of the study wereeighty eight college students selected via convenient sampling technique. The results of the study are: positive relationshipwas found between academic procrastination and dishonest behavior.On the other hand, insignificantpositive relationship was found between test anxiety and dishonesty behavior amongcollege students.Implications of the study for educational institutions are discussed.
