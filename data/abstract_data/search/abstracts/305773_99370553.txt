Handbook of Near Infrared Analysis, Second Edition. D. A. Burns and E. W. Ciurczak, Eds. Marcel Dekker, New York, 2001. Pp. 814. Price $225. ISBN: 0 8247 053 4 3. The application s for near infrared (NIR) spectroscop y continue to grow because it is fast, nondestruc tive, and it frequently requires little or no sample preparation. While not a trace technique, NIR spectroscop y is applicable to samples containing C H, N H, O H, and S H bonds where the analyte concentrati on is greater than about 0.1% In addition to compositional analyses, NIR spectroscopy has been used to determine various physical parameters such as aqueous solution temperature, seawater salinity, heats of formation, molecular weight, number of methyl groups per molecule, and octane number in petroleum analyses. Because of the dramatic progress made in NIR spectroscop y in the recent past, there is a demand for up to date information about the technique. The new edition of the Handbook of Near Infrared Analysis ful lls this need and can be recommended to anyone with an interest in NIR spectroscop y. This book is a revised, updated, and expanded edition of the previous work of the same title, edited by Burns and Ciurczak and published in 1992. The new edition, which is approxima tely 133 pages longer than the previous one, is divided into four major sections. Part I, which consists of three chapters, deals with the historical developm ent of near infrared (NIR) spectroscop y along with an introduction to the technique and a discussion of the theor y of diffuse re ectance. Part II, which consists of eight chapters, deals with instrum entation and calibration. Part III, which consists of four chapters, deals with methods developm ent. Part IV, which contains 14 chapters, deals with applications of NIR spectroscop y. The chapters are written by recognized experts in the eld of NIR spectroscop y. Compared with the previous edition, six new chapters have been added and two chapters in the previous edition have been dropped. The new chapters in the 2nd edition include: a chapter on Fourier transfo rm NIR spectrom eters, two chapters on multivariate calibration (one of which deals with calibration transfer) a chapter on biomedical applications a chapter on plastics analysis, and a chapter on detection of counterfeit currency and turquoise. The applications section of the book (Part IV) is quite extensive and has chapters on the applications of NIR spectroscop y to a wide variety of areas. Included are chapters on the use of NIR spectroscopy in biomedical application s, agricultural and forage analyses, and polymer chemistr y, along with chapters on the analysis of baked products, dair y products, petrochem icals, plastics, beverages, pharmaceuticals, and textiles. The chapter on the use of NIR spectroscopy in counterfeit detection illustrates the power of NIR spectroscopy for cluster analysis.
