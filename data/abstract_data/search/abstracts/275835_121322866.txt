Relaxation modulus and thermal expansion coefficient of polyimide films coated on substrates
A bending beam technique with the use of two substrates, silicon and gallium arsenide, has been successfully employed to simultaneously determine the biaxial relaxation modulus, Ef/(1 nf) and thermal expansion coefficient, af of a rigid rod like polyimide film, pyromellitic dianhydride benzidine (PMDA B) coated on a substrate. As measured, the two properties, especially af significantly increase with the increase of film thickness. At 150 degC, for example, the obtained Ef(1 nf) increases from 6.0 to 8.2x109 N/m2, and af from 0.33 to 2.42x10 6/degC for thicknesses ranging from 10 to 30 mm.
