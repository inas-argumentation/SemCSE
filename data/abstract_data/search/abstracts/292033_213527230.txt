PENGARUH IKLIM ORGANISASI, BUDAYA ORGANISASI, DAN MOTIVASI BERPRESTASI TERHADAP KOMITMEN ORGANISASI PEGAWAI DI INSTITUT TEKNOLOGI SUMATERA LAMPUNG
Tujuan penelitian ini adalah untuk menguji pengaruh iklim organisasi, budaya organisasi, dan motivasi berprestasi terhadap komitmen organisasi pegawai di Institut Teknologi Sumatera secara parsial, selain itu untuk mengetahui pengaruh iklim organisasi dan budaya organisasi terhadap komitmen organisasi pegawai apabila melalui variabel motivasi berprestasi. Penelitian ini merupakan penelitian pendekatan kuantitatif dengan menggunakan jenis ex post facto, dengan mengambil sampel 98 pegawai di Institut Teknologi Sumatera. Pengumpulan data dilakukan dengan kuisioner. Analisis data menggunakan analisis jalur. Hasil penelitian ini menunjukkan bahwa pengaruh langsung iklim organisasi, budaya organisasi, dan motivasi berprestasi berpengaruh positif dan signifikan terhadap komitmen organisasi pegawai secara parsial. Pengaruh langsung iklim organisasi terhadap motivasi berprestasi positif dan signifikan, tetapi pengaruh langsung budaya organisasi terhadap motivasi berprestasi positif namun tidak signifikan. Pengaruh tidak langsung iklim organisasi dan budaya organisasi terhadap komitmen organisasi pegawai melalui motivasi berprestasi tidak sebesar pengaruh langsung kedua variabel tersebut. Kata Kunci iklim organisasi, budaya organisasi, motivasi berprestasi, komitmen organisasi ABSTRACT THE INFLUENCES OF ORGANIZATIONAL CLIMATE, ORGANIZATIONAL CULTURE, AND ACHIEVEMENT MOTIVATION ON THE ORGANIZATIONAL COMMITMENT OF STAFF MEMBERS IN INSTITUT TEKNOLOGI SUMATERA LAMPUNG By GESCA SONARITA The purpose of this study was to determine the influences of organizational climate, organizational culture, and achievement motivation on the organizational commitment of staff members in Institut Teknologi Sumatera directly and parsially. This study was also intended to examine the effect of organizational climate and organizational culture on organizational commiment through achievement motivation (as intervining variable) This research is a quantitative approach using ex post facto type, by taking a sample of 98 staff members in Institut Teknologi Sumatera. Data were collected using quistionnaire. The collected data were then analyzed using path analysis. The results showed that positive and significant direct effects of organizational climate, organizational culture, and achievement motivation on organizational commitment parsially. In addition, the direct effect of organizational climate on achievement motivation was positive and significant, but the direct effect of organizational culture on achievement motivation was positive and not significant. The indirect effects of organizational climate and organizational culture on organizational commitment through achievement motivation were smaller than the direct effects, but were positive and significant. Keywords organizational cilmate, organizational culture, achievement motivation, organizational commitment
