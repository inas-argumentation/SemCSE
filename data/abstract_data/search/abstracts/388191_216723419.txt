Role play in education for sustainable development to overcome binary thinking: A case study in an elementary school
Role play is a form of debate in which the participants are provided with the arguments they must make. This article analyzes the linguistic exchanges that took place during a role play exercise with pupils aged eight to ten. We attempt to identify whether pupils are capable of mobilizing arguments and if the arguments put forward are coherent with the character they represent. The results show a high argumentation level, coherence in the arguments put forward, as well as an emergence of new proposals. While role play fulfills its argument appropriation function, it also enables students to develop eco citizenship skills.
