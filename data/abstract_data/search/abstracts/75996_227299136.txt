Autonomy, depression and affecting factors in the elderly people
Introduction The population aged 65 years and over is increasing all over the World (1) In Turkey, the elderly population was 5 million 891 thousand 694 people in 2013 and increased by 17% in the last five years and became 6 million 895 thousand 385 people in 2017. While the ratio of the elderly population in the total population was 7.7% in 2013, it increased to 8.5% in 2017 (2) High life expectancy and economic burdens may pose a problem for the health system that best serves elderly adults with chronic diseases and high risk of hospitalization (3) Prolongation of the life span and physical, sensory and mental regressions that occur together bring physiological, psychological, and social problems in the elderly (4,5) Physical and mental illnesses associated with the aging process may limit autonomic independence. Losses such as retirement or death of the spouse may also change the role performance.
