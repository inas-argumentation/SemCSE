Progress of research on the chitosan bearing cyclodextrin adsorbent
Cyclodextrin(CD) has the merit of a hydrophobic cavity,which is easy to assemble with other molecules.Chitosan and its derivatives has the merit of powerful absorbability.Therefore,the products obtained by CD grafting to chitosan using different methods have the properties of sorption of chitosan and inclusion ability of CD,and have a more powerful absorbability.They can absorb the cholesterin,phenol,dyes and controll release as adsorbent,are widely used in medicine,environmental protection and so on.In this review,various kinds of strategies involved in the grafting of CDs onto chitosan are discussed in detail.In addition,the inclusion ability and the properties of CD grafted chitosan derivatives and their applications are also discussed.
