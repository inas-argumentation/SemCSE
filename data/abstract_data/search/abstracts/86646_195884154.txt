Dynamic Threshold Voltage in $p$ GaN Gate HEMT
The <tex>$p$/tex> GaN gate HEMT with a Schottky gate contact is studied in this work. The threshold voltage <tex>\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{th}/tex> of the device is found to have a dynamic nature. When the device experiences a high drain voltage <tex>$V_{\text{DSQ}/tex> the gate to drain capacitance <tex>$C_{\text{GD}/tex> is charged to <tex>\boldsymbol{Q}_{\mathbf{GD}\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{DSQ}/tex> The negative part of <tex>\boldsymbol{Q}_{\mathbf{GD}\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{DSQ}/tex> is mainly located in the <tex>$p$/tex> GaN layer. When drain voltage drops to a lower value <tex>$V_{\text{DSM}/tex> the non equilibrium charges <tex>\Delta \boldsymbol{Q}_{\mathbf{GD}\boldsymbol{Q}_{\mathbf{GD}\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{GDQ} \boldsymbol{Q}_{\mathbf{GD}\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{GDM}/tex> cannot be effectively released, since the discharging current is blocked by the reversely biased metal/<tex>$p$/tex> GaN Schottky junction and <tex>\boldsymbol{p}/tex> GaN/2DEG PN junction. An extra <tex>$V_{\text{GS}/tex> is required to counteract the non equilibrium <tex>\Delta \boldsymbol{Q}_{\mathbf{GD}/tex> resulting in a change of <tex>\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{th}/tex> The change in <tex>\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{th}/tex> is well predictable by its linear relationship to <tex>\Delta \boldsymbol{Q}_{\mathbf{GD}/tex> During switching operation, <tex>\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{th}/tex> is adaptive along the load line. Without considering the dynamic <tex>\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{th}/tex> effect, there exist large discrepancies in switching transients between the modelled and the experimental results. Incorporation of the dynamic <tex>\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathbf{th}/tex> in device model result in greatly enhanced accuracy in simulated transient behavior.
