A study on Effects of Intrinsic Characteristics of Datasets on Classification Performance
The classification performance of standard machine learning classification algorithms is highly affected by some properties of the datasets like imbalance in the class distribution of non linearly separable datasets. The problem of class imbalance becomes more severe in the cases of datasets with class overlap and when there is lack of density in the training set. In this paper the effect of intrinsic characteristics of datasets on the classification performance of various standard algorithms has been analyzed.
