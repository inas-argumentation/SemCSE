The Construction of Innovative Platform of the High end Talent Assembling in Yangtze River Delta
In the background of industrial structure adjustment and the economic transformation,the issue of the integration of Yangtze River Delta has risen to national regional development strategy.High end talent assembling in Yangtze River Delta becomes the main thrust of.the high level integration.Therefore,this paper focuses on theory,problem and mechanism innovation of the Yangtze River Delta high end talent assembling.And the paper puts forward four major theoretical basis,and points out that the construction of four platforms of the Yangtze River Delta high end talent assembling.Finally,this paper puts forward some relevant policies.
