Enhanced Grey Wolf optimization for GMPP Tracking of PV Systems Under Partial Shading Condition
A maximum power point tracking algorithm (MPPT) for a photovoltaic (PV) system using an Enhanced Grey Wolf optimization (EGWO) technique is presented in this paper. The basic purpose is to study for the mismatches of power in PV modules for non uniform solar irradiance. MPPT varies the ratio between the voltage and current delivered to the load in order to deliver maximum power. Under partially shaded conditions (PSCs) MPPT algorithm must operate at the Global Maximum Power Point (GMPP) MATLAB/Simulink is used to model and simulate the proposed method. The simulation results reveals that EGWO algorithm outperforms some of the recent algorithms to detect the GMPP under different shading patterns applied to the PV system. EGWO is compared with the hybrid Artificial Neural Network assisted Perturb and Observe (ANN P O) and Multi Verse optimization (MVO) algorithms. Results show that EGWO outperforms both ANN P O and MVO in terms of efficiency and tracking speed.
