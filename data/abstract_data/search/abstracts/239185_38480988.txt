FAMILIES OF NON th CONGRUENT NUMBERS WITH ARBITRARILY MANY PRIME FACTORS
The concept of th congruent numbers was introduced by Fujiwara [Fu97] who showed that for primes p 5, 7, 19 (mod 24) p is not a p/3 congruent number. In this paper we show the existence of two infinite families of composite non p/3 congruent numbers and non 2p/3 congruent numbers, obtained from products of primes which are congruent to 5 modulo 24 and to 13 modulo 24 respectively. This is achieved by generalizing a result obtained by Serf [Se91] based on descent on certain elliptic curves, and by extending a method of Iskra [Is96] involving the classical (or p/2 congruent numbers.
