National Ownership and Local Procurement:A Study on the Development Trends of School Feeding Programmes in Sub Saharan Africa Cases from Botswana and Ghana
The history of school feeding in Sub Saharan Africa has experienced tortuous development for more than half a century.There are two development trends in School Feeding Programmes now:national ownership and local procurement.But These programs face such problems as the overall poorly political and economic environment,inadequate government capacity,and low local procurement ratio.However,China's Nutrition Programme can get some inspirations from Sub Saharan African experience on programme implementation,community participation and local procurement.Meanwhile,the Chinese government can add School Feeding Programme into the China Africa educational cooperation.
