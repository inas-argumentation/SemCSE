PENGGUNAAN CRYPTOCURRENCY BITCOIN DALAM TRANSAKSI MENURUT PERSPEKTIF HUKUM ISLAM
Perspective of Islamic Economic Law in Indonesia, bitcoin is not a payment transaction tool. This is because bitcoin has not fulfilled the criteria, transaction agreement elements and DSN MUI Fatwa provisions as a valid currency in Indonesia. The purpose of this study is to examine this problem on the basis of considerations for bringing in maslahah and avoiding harm from using bitcoin. Therefore, researchers direct this research to the problem of: whether or not the use of bitcoin transactions in Islamic Economic Law. This research uses library research or library research. The analysis of this study uses a descriptive analysis analysis method based on the views of the scholars. The results of this study stated that the existence of bitcoin cannot be said to be a currency, because bitcoin does not meet the criteria, contract elements and provisions of the fatwa DSN MUI and Ulama Contentporer, Al Ghazali and Ibn Khaldun something that can be said as money. The function of bitcoin is also not in accordance with the function of money in Islamic economics because bitcoin is more dominant as a commodity traded, not as a medium of exchange. Secondly, the law of buying and selling bitcoin is canceled because there are several contracts, criteria are violated, so it is not in accordance with Islamic law, namely selling transactions buying on bitcoin does not occur in cash and transactions are irreversible (irrevocably) despite pending orders.
