The Role of Job Rotation in Enhancing Administrative Reform Processes:An analytical Study of The Views of Senior Managers at Al Qadisiyah University:
Purpose: The current research aims at testing the relation and effect between job rotation and administrative reform process at Al Qadissiyah University. Design Methodology: Upon the research sample, 129 questionnaire forms were distributed to all faculties of Al Qadissiyah University (18 to the deans, 36 to the dean assistants, 75 to the departmental heads) A total of (16) forms were rejected for non validity and failure to meet the required conditions for answering the questionnaire. Thus, the number of valid forms for study and analysis was 113 with 88% retrieval rate, Which were analyzed using AMOS v.20 and SPSS v.24. Results: The results proved the validity of the hypotheses and reached a set of conclusions, The most important of which was the contribution of work rotation to the development of performance and to strengthen the capabilities of senior leaders and to resist the change resulting from the belief that the job belongs to the employee. Also, one of the main reasons for the failure of the administrative reform process is dealing with administrative problems independently of the environmental surroundings that interact with and influence the administrative system and are affected by it. Recommendations: In the light of the conclusions reached by the research, Some recommendations were formulated. The most important of these was the implementation of the law of periodic and permanent rotation to reduce the monotony and routine of work which may generate boredom and applying the principle of fairness and fair administration in implementing this law.
