Two domain optically compensated bend mode without bias filed using high pretilt angle configurations
Liquid crystal display (LCD) applications such as television, mobile phone, tablet computer require high performances such as wide viewing angle and fast response time characteristics. In order to obtain the better performance, various approaches have been explored including rubbing of polymer films which is most popular and conventional process. However, due to the fact that all LC molecules are aligned uni directionally over the whole panel area, a narrow and non uniform viewing angular characteristics have produced a limitation of the display performances. To improve the narrow viewing angle properties, many kinds of LC modes such as in plane switching (IPS) [1] multi domain vertical alignment (MVA) [2] and patterned vertical alignment (PVA) [3] have been developed using multi domain alignment of LC molecules. However, previous LC modes are difficult to obtain the fast response time characteristics due to the opposite flows of the switching LC molecules under an applied voltage. The uni directional flow of the LC molecules in the optically compensated bend mode (OCB) mode [4] gives rise to improvement of the response time. In addition, the OCB mode shows the two fold symmetric viewing angle due to self compensation structure of the bend deformation. In this study, we propose the two domain OCB mode to obtain the four fold symmetric viewing angle and fast response time. Also, to obtain the bend structure without bias voltage, we use stacked alignment system to control the pretilt angle of the alignment layer [5] Multi domain rubbing was performed by stamping assisted rubbing process to fabricated two orthogonal OCB configuration. Finally, we achieved the fast response and wide viewing angle LC devices
