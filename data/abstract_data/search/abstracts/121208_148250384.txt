Adolescent health risk behavior in Foshan: III addiction behavior such as video or computer games,internet addiction,gambling
Objective To understand the current status of addiction behavior such as video or computer games,internet addiction,gambling among urban adolescents in Foshan City,in order to provide the reference for making the intervention.Methods A questionnaire investigation on adolescent health related behavior was performed among 3 844 students selected from 12 schools(including middle schools,vocational middle schools and universities) in Foshan urban area.Results Among the urban adolescents,the incidences of addictive behavior were relatively high and increased with the grade,and 11.4% of students played video or computer games for more than 4 hours everyday,11.5% indulged in the internet for more than 4 hours everyday,4.27% were addicted to the internet,and 21.0% have ever gambled.On the incidences of psychological dependence,there were differences among different types of schools.The lowest incidence of video or computer games addiction existed in the key middle schools,and the highest one occurred in the vocational middle schools.Among the students who got poor learning achievement,the incidence of addictive behavior was relatively high.Conclusions It is necessary to ally the three sections such as school,family and community to reduce or prevent the addictive behavior,and the intervention should be drawn up in accordance with the different ages,genders and types of schools.
