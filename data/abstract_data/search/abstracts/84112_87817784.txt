Analysis of combining ability on ear traits of maize inbreeds from different groups
`Seventy five crosses were composed according to NCII design using 15 inbreeds as female and five as male parents from different heterosis groups,and trailed under split block design at three locations in Henan.And the effects of combining ability on ear traits were evaluated in this research.The results showed that: 1)highly significant differences had been found among crosses and their parents on ear characters as well as femalexmale parent interactions except for 1 000 kernel weight,respectively;2) significant differences in GCA effects existed among female parents,the inbreed lines M6 and M8 were the highest in ear length GCA effect,M15 in ear diameter as well as M1 and M4 in grain weight per ear,much better than other lines,respectively;3) the SCA effects were also significantly different among crosses in ear traits,and the crosses M3xN1 and M14xN1 had the highest SCA effects in grain weight per ear with positive value of 15.556 and 15.318,while the cross M12xN1 was the poorest with negative value of 21.587;4) the heritability was higher in ear length,ear diameter,row numbers per ear and kernels per row than in 1 000 kernel weight,ear weight,grain weight per ear and ratio of grain weight,especially the kernels per row with the highest hereditary capacity.
