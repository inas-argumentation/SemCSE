ANALISIS FUNDAMENTAL DAN TEKNIKAL DALAM PENENTUAN KEPUTUSAN INVESTASI PADA AKTIVITAS PERDAGANGAN SAHAM (Studi Pada Saham Perbankan BUMN yang Listing di Bursa Efek Indonesia)
The purpose of this research is to establish definite intrinsic value and price forecasts which has the intention to buy and sell BUMN bank stock. This researcher uses fundamental and technical analysis. Fundamental analysis intended to determine stock performance in undervalued or overvalue case. On the other hand, the aim of technical analysis is to establish the exact momentum to buy and sell the stock. Based on the fundamental analysis calculating, it is indicated to kinds of stock in the overvalued case; which are BBNI and BBTN. While there BMRI and BBRI in undervalued case. In the other words, a well worth stock that could be bought by the investor is BMRI and BBRI. While well worth stock to sell is BBNI and BBTN. Yet, based on technical analysis of the financial reports published it is not the right time to buy or sell stock. Since all of the stock are wait and see the case. So, sophisticated to hold for a while.
