DIFFERENCES AND SIMILARITIES OF NOUNS IN BOTH ENGLISH AND GERMAN LANGUAGE ACCORDING TO TIPOLOGY
This article's purpose is to teach how to distinguish using both English and German language. Because the fact that most learners find some difficulties with learning a new language. They make grammar and lexical errors. Such as overgeneralization, interference and transfer.This article is for people who learn German language through English. During learning they come across some words which are similar to English words. They think that sentence structure is also the same. But it different, German grammar is completely difficult. That is why we decided to write this article in order to improve learners' knowledge in German. We hope that after reading this article readers will learn how to use words, make sentences using nouns.
