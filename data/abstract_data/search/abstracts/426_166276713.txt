The Social Security Administration's Youth Transition Demonstration Projects: Interim Report on Transition WORKS
The Social Security Administration is funding a random assignment evaluation of six demonstration projects to improve employment and other outcomes for youth ages 14 to 25 who are either receiving disability benefits or are at high risk of receiving them in the future.
