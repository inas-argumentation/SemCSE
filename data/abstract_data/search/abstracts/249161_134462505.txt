Plasterboard and gypsum waste in a novel cementitious binder for road construction
Coventry University, in collaboration with Skanska and Lafarge Plasterboard, recently undertook research for the Waste and Resources Action Programme (WRAP) to develop a cost effective novel binder using recycled gypsum from waste plasterboard and a range of mineral wastes, for construction of road foundations. The lab testing and trials successfully proved that recycled gypsum can be used in a number of road construction applications.
