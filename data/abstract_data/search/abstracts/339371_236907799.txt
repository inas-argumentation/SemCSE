Social Distancing Monitoring Robot
Social distancing monitoring robot which aims to limit the spread of Covid by observing distance between disease spreading individuals. We present a method is essential for banks, Public Offices, Malls, Schools, Theatres etc usually see long queues for hours every day. In the absence of an effective treatment and vaccine the efforts to control this pandemic strictly to take effective measures to monitor the safety distance criteria to avoid more positive cases and to control the death toll. To ensure social distancing in queues we hereby design a social distancing monitoring robot. The purpose of this work is to provide an effective social distance monitoring solution .The robot consists of a 2 wheel design system used to drive the robotic vehicle. It makes use of a line following principle to constantly move along with the queue and monitor for social distancing violations. The robotic uses IR sensing to travel along with the queue to and fro in order to detect violations. The robot is now equipped with obstacle detecting ultrasonic sensor in order to detect obstacles in the vehicle path.
