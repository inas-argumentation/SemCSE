Stability of systems coupled through noisy mediums
This paper focuses on stability of interconnected systems whose connecting channels are exposed to stochastic noises. This class of systems includes interconnected systems whose coupling is brought in only by stochastic noises. Another example is coupling through noisy amplification. This paper shows how to construct Lyapunov functions to verify stability for such a class of systems, and illustrates that the behavior changes drastically when systems involve nonlinearities. Small gain type criteria are proposed for asymptotic stability, integral input to state stability and input to state stability. This paper also proposes a unique way to reduce possible conservativeness.
