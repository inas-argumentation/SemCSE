NGO accountability to beneficiaries: Examining participation in microenterprise development programs
Ultimately non government organisations (NGOs) involved in poverty alleviation programs are accountable to beneficiaries, the poor. However, the lack of meaningful participation by beneficiaries has been criticised. Drawing on notions of accountability, this paper reviews mechanisms for beneficiary participation in theory and practice. Interviews were conducted with senior executives within 10 international NGOs operating microenterprise development (MED) programs in Vietnam. Findings reveal a three level framework for effective beneficiary participation, involving consultation, partnership, and delegated control. This framework provides a graduated approach to empowering beneficiaries and a basis for enhanced accountability of MED NGOs at both a functional and strategic level.
