Antipodal linearly tapered slot antenna array for millimeter wave base station in massive MIMO systems
For massive MIMO systems, it is very challenging to have the large scale antennas for a compact base station. This paper presents a 36 sub sectors for a compact millimeter wave base station. The antenna array for each sub sector is comprised of 16 planar high gain dielectric loaded antipodal linearly tapered slot antenna (ALTSA) elements arranged in a 4x4 configuration. Down to 36 feed ports are required in a base station with 36x16 antennas. The realized gain of the antenna array is 25.6 dBi, and the half power beam widths (HPBW) are 10.7deg and 5.3deg in H plane and E plane, respectively.
