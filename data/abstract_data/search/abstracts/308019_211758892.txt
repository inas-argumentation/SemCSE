Technology development of online wallet (cashless payment) transactions in e commerce
The purpose of this research is to show the development of the technology of online wallet in transacting, and the benefits of using an Online Wallet. This research uses descriptive research method in which data is obtained based on the facts. The goal was to show how far the use of technology (cashless payment) as a substitute for real money in online transactions, and how excellence in technology implementation cashless if applied at the moment. The research results were obtained over human needs a versatile online and instant in the industrial age 4.0. Then some startup, as well as conventional banks, started developing technology cashless. The result is a discussion of the payment transaction system, charging the balance in doing transactions online wallet use technology.
