Information systems outsourcing myths, metaphors, and realities
From the Publisher: The outsourcing revolution is underway as increasing numbers of major corporations transfer their information systems (IS) assets, leases and staff to third party vendors. In depth interviews with individuals from 13 different companies who have gone through formal IS outsourcing reveal insights about the intentions, motivations and consequences of this rapidly growing area. Includes step by step instructions to negotiate a successful vendor contract.
