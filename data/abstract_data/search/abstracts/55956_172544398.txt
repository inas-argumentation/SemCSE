Evaluation de la rentabilite diagnostique de l'endoscopie digestive haute en mode optique a l'ere de la video endoscopie
ResumeButL'endoscopie est un examen indispensable pour le diagnostic et la prise en charge des lesions muqueuses oesogastroduodenales. Elle a subit des avancees technologiques importantes avec l'apparition de la video endoscopie (VE) Celle ci a offert de nombreux avantages par rapport a l'endoscopie optique (EO) qu'elle a rapidement supplantee en occident. Cependant, l'EO reste tres largement utilisee en Afrique. La question etait de savoir si la rentabilite diagnostique de l'EO etait raisonnablement elevee pour continuer a etre utilisee a l'ere de la VE. Le but de l'etude etait d'evaluer les performances diagnostiques de l'EO comparee a la VE.Malades et methodesEtude multicentrique comparative, a recueil retrospectif, menee au Centre hospitalo universitaire de Kamenge et au Centre des maladies digestives et du foie chez les patients consecutifs, examines en VE ou en EO entre mai 2012 et Juin 2014.ResultatsAu total, 3283 patients comparables au point de vue age et sexe ont ete retenus pour analyse. Ceux examines en VE etaient 1714 patients et ceux en optique etaient 1569 patients. L'EO a diagnostique moins de lesions millimetriques notamment la gastrite et les erosions (10,1% vs 14% p=0,01) Elle a egalement diagnostique moins de varices oesophagiennes et de gastrite d'hypertension portale (2,9% vs 5,4% p=0,001) moins de beance du cardia (1,1% vs 4,7% p=0,0001) et moins de cancer gastrique (0,4% vs 1,4% p=0,01).ConclusionL'EO a diagnostique moins de lesions de petite taille. Des efforts devraient etre deployes pour equiper tous les hopitaux en VE.AbstractAimEndoscopy is an indispensable exam for diagnosis and management of esophageal, gastric and duodenal lesions. There had been very important technological progress in endoscopy since the arrival of video endoscopy (VE) It offers great performances compared to the optic endoscopy (OE) This one had been quickly supplanted by the VE in Europe. However, the OE remains widely used in Africa. The issue was to know if the OE had an acceptable diagnostic efficiency so that it can be used at the era of VE. So, the aim of the study was to assess the diagnostic efficiency of the OE compared with the VE.MethodsIt was a multicenter retrospective and comparative study, carried out at Kamenge University Hospital and at Digestive and liver diseases Center "CEMADIF" among consecutive patients, examined in VE or in OE between May 2012 and June 2014.ResultsA total of 3283 patients, comparable according to age and gender, had been included in the study. Those examined in VE were 1714 patients and those in OE were 1569 patients. The OE diagnosed very few cases of lesions of small sizes like gastritis and erosions (10,1% vs 14% in VE, p=0,01) It also diagnosed very few cases of esophageal varices and gastritis of portal hypertension (2,9% vs 5,4% in VE, p=0,001) very few cases of open cardia (1,1% vs 4,7% in VE, p=0,0001) and very few cases of gastric cancer (0,4% vs 1,4% in VE, p=0,01).ConclusionThe OE diagnosed very few cases of lesions of small size. Efforts should be done to equip increasingly all hospitals in VE.
