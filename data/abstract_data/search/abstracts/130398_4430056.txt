A Highly Thermostable In2O3/ITO Thin Film Thermocouple Prepared via Screen Printing for High Temperature Measurements
An In2O3/ITO thin film thermocouple was prepared via screen printing. Glass additives were added to improve the sintering process and to increase the density of the In2O3/ITO films. The surface and cross sectional images indicate that both the grain size and densification of the ITO and In2O3 films increased with the increase in annealing time. The thermoelectric voltage of the In2O3/ITO thermocouple was 53.5 mV at 1270 degC at the hot junction. The average Seebeck coefficient of the thermocouple was calculated as 44.5 mV/degC. The drift rate of the In2O3/ITO thermocouple was 5.44 degC/h at a measuring time of 10 h at 1270 degC.
