The restricted nonlinear large eddy simulation approach to reduced order wind farm modeling
A new approach to the reduced order modeling of wind farms based on the recently formulated restricted nonlinear large eddy simulation (RNL LES) framework is introduced. The approach has been shown to accurately reproduce mean flow distributions and turbulence statistics in wall bounded flows at a fraction of the cost of traditional LES, making it a potentially attractive reduced order model for studying wind farm behavior over a range of conditions. An appropriately altered version of the actuator disk turbine model is used to evaluate the ability of the RNL LES framework to simulate very large wind farms in the fully developed regime through comparisons with the standard LES of wind farms under a variety of conditions.
