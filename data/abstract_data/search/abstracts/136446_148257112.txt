A Study of English Learning Strategies Used by Non English Major Freshmen
The paper investigates English learning strategies of freshmen majoring in P.E and Fine Arts through questionnaire and interview.The results show that the students of P.E and F.A.prefer cognitive strategies to metacognitive and social/affective as well as communicative ones;they do not differ significantly in using these strategies because of difference in majorities.Hence the author's suggestions: to familiarize students with learning strategies while teaching;to foster authentic communicative contexts and advocate extra curriculum activities;to improve examination content and evaluation system,and to enlarge the proportion of oral test in the final examinations.
