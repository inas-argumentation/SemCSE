Alloy wire drawing lubricant and preparation method thereof
The invention discloses an alloy wire drawing lubricant and a preparation method thereof. The lubricant and the preparation method are characterized in that the lubricant is prepared from following raw materials by weight: 15 20 parts of cottonseed oil, 0.3 0.5 part of aluminum borate nanoparticles, 3 4 parts of sodium lauryl sulfate, 1 2 parts of sodium tripolyphosphate, 2 3 parts of ricinoleic acid, 0.3 0.5 part of benzotriazole, 1 2 parts of polyvinylpyrrolidone, 2 3 parts of hydroxystearic acid, 1 2 parts of octadecanol, 0.3 0.5 part of melamine, 1 2 parts of sodium sulfate, 1 2 parts of citric acid, 3 4 parts of a poly(alkylene glycol) 10 14 parts of an antiwear agent and 1000 parts of water; nano aluminium nitride powder is added into the antiwear agent, thus promoting heat dissipation and enhancing film forming property; the lubricant contains a surfactant, thus effectively eliminating static produced during the wire drawing process and cleaning away dirt on the metal surfaces; and the lubricant is nontoxic, harmless, nonirritating to human skin, biodegradable and environmental friendly.
