Wild mushroom beef paste and preparation method thereof
The invention discloses a wild mushroom beef paste and a preparation method thereof. The wild mushroom beef paste comprises the following components by weight: 40 45 percent of wild mushroom, 8 25 percent of lean beef, 20 25 percent of corn oil, 2 5 percent of broad bean paste, 1.0 2.0 percent of spice, 2 3 percent of Capsicum frutescens L. 1 2 percent of garlic, 2 3 percent of white sugar, 1 2 percent of ginger and 1 2 percent of edible salt. The preparation method comprises the following steps: quickly freezing the cleaned wild mushroom at the temperature of minus 35 DEG C and refrigerating the cleaned wild mushroom at the temperature of minus 18 DEG C, defreezing and drying the wild mushroom during the use, the water content is 35 percent, and then adding the spice for stirring; precooking the beef for removing the unwanted smell, dicing the beef, and adding the spice for curing; heating the corn oil to medium well, then adding onion, the ginger, the garlic, the Capsicum frutescens L. and other auxiliary materials for stir frying, and then slowly stewing the wild mushroom and beef over a small fire for 60 minutes; and putting the wild mushroom beef paste in a container for sealing and sterilization. The wild mushroom beef paste is free of any additives, has strong beef flavor when ensuring the unique flavor of wild mushroom, and is a green food (rich in nutrition and convenient to eat) accompaniment to the meal, and the preparation method is simple, convenient, and suitable for large scale factory production.
