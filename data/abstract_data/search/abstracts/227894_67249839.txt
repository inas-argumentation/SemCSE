Intelligent table lamp control device
The utility model discloses an intelligent table lamp control device which includes a photosensitive sensor unit, a human body infrared induction module, a comparison unit, illuminating lamp driving links and a power supply. The illuminating lamp driving links include an optical coupling switch and a relay. The photosensitive sensor unit and the human body infrared induction module are connected with the comparison unit respectively. The comparison unit is connected with a coil of the relay via the optical coupling switch. A contact of the relay is connected to a power supplying circuit of the table lamp. The photosensitive sensor unit and the human body infrared induction module receive external light intensity signals and infrared signals and transmit the signals to the comparison unit. The comparison unit outputs a control instruction to the optical coupling switch according to the received signals. The optical coupling switch controls the contact of the relay to act. The intelligent table lamp control device introduces light intensity detection and human body infrared induction detection and uses the comparison unit to realize automatic control and decides the work state of the table lamp according to the intensity of light and according to whether any person is present so that the device is energy saving and environment friendly.
