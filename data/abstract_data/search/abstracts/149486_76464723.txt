Clinical study of treatment of elder COPD induced hypercapnic respiratory failure by bronchoscopy combined with sequential non invasive following short term invasive mechanical ventilation
Objective:To observe the efficacy of bronchoscope combined with sequential non invasive following short term invasive mechanical ventilation in COPD with hypercapnic respiratory failure.Methods:The cases were distributed into the study group and the control group at random.The patients were treated with bronchoscope combined with sequential non invasive following short term invasive mechanical ventilation as study group,the others control group. Compare the time of correting respiratory failure,initial time of PIC window (Pulmonary Infection Control window),the duration of invasive MV,the total duration of ventilatory support,the duration of hospitalization after ventilation,mortality rate and the incidence of VAP .Results:For study groups and control group,the groups had similar clinical mortality rate,10.53% vs 16.67%,P 0.05.The time of correcting respiratory failure was (9.27+ 4.67) vs (26.61+ 12.35) hours, P 0.01).The initial time of PIC window (4.93+ 1.77) vs (9.1+ 2.3) days, P 0.01).The duration of invasive MV was (5.2+ 2.1) vs(9.2+ 3.1) days, P 0.01).The total duration of ventilatory support was (11.48+ 2.37)vs (15.13+ 2.59)days, P 0.01).The incidence of VAP were 0 vs 3, P 0.05).The duration of hospitalization after ventilaton was (18.11+ 3.24)vs (24.56+ 4.62)days, P 0.01).Conclusions:The therapy of bronchoscope combined with sequenqtial non invasivc following short term invasive mechanical ventilation is effective in COPD with hypercapnic respiratory failure .It can control infection,decrease the durations of MV and hospitalization,reduce the incidence of the complication.For example,VAP and the difficulty of taking off mechanical ventilation.
