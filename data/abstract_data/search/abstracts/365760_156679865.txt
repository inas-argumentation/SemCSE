On higher education teacher ethics in the new era
The construction of higher education teacher ethics is indispensable for a teaching team with high professional level,the application of quality education,cultivation of standardized new century talents,and realization of socialism morality.This article points out such basic contents of higher education teacher ethics in the new era as the loyalty to the Party's and People's education,a personality of self example,selfishness and sacrifice,characteristics of creativity and constant pursuit.Meanwhile,the article points out such measurements to strengthen the construction of higher education teacher ethics as creating favorable teacher ethics environment,clarifying ethnic regulations,advocating moral education,and building complete system of education,evaluation and moderation.
