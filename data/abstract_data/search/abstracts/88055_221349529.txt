A search for traditional literature on knowledge organization and change yielded surprisingly few results, most of them recent, e.g. Lockenhoff (1994) and Schipper 1994) We are at the juncture where the new modes of communication and expression enabled by distributed hypermedia Internet, are being tested and experienced but where scholarly communication still (with some experimental exceptions) operates in the traditional mode writings intended for printing on paper. The writing of this paper has hence been persisted experiences of frustrations and impatience. Foremost among these have been the need to linearize the presentation of subjects that form networks, internally and between each other, and the inelasticity and passivity of pointers on papers compared to the immediacy and reach of links in the hypermedia environment of Internet.
