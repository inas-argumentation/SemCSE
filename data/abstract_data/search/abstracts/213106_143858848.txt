International Perspectives on Teaching English in a Globalised World
This is the book that emerged from the IFTE Conference of 2011 and, as such, has the strengths and limitations of articles based on conference presentations. 'A man's reach' writes Browning, 'should exceed his grasp' There is a real sense in this book of striving to reach outwards and forwards: outwards in going beyond the boundaries of individual countries; forwards in exploring the nature of subject English in the 21st century. Its grasp is, necessarily, limited by the nature of IFTE, which is a federation of the English subject associations currently, of Australia, Canada, New Zealand, UK, and USA. So no voices from South Africa, for instance, or the Caribbean, although there is a chapter by two contributors working in Singapore.
