A review report on the plating process of fused deposition modelling (FDM) built parts
Abstract This research paper describes implementation and applications of plating technology on Rapid Prototyping (RP) built parts. Among all RP processes Fused Deposition Modelling (FDM) is widely appreciated due to its advanced build technology. In order to improve the durability and adoptability of FDM build parts as end use parts, post processing is highly necessary. Coating is a highly appreciable technique to improve the wear strength and surface texture of the build parts. One of the most significant type of coating is plating. Both electroplating and electroforming have their own importance. Electroplating can be done by the conventional method of using Chromic acid. The effect of plating on tensile strength, flexural strength are also given. All the thermoplastics built parts are plated to improve their properties. Electroless and electrolytic deposition, Copper, chromium plating are also discussed in details. The formation of cracks, the preventive measures and causes of formation of cracks are also discussed. The applications of FDM built parts are also discussed in the report. Verification of the Plating Process by NACA0012 airfoil method is also given in brief. Present Review Report is a conclusive study of different types of Plating techniques with their advantages.
