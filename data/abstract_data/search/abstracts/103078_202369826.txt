The Tang Dynasty
The Han (206 BC AD 220) and the Tang (AD 618 907) are the two dynasties that can best represent China in history. We talked about the Han system in the last lecture and now we'll move on to the Tang, beginning with Tang government. As in the Han and since, government was separate from the imperial family. As regards the imperial family, succession to the throne remained the same, apart from the fact, of minor importance, that it changed hands from the Liu ruling family in the Han to the Li ruling family in the Tang. The Tang government, however, was very different from the Han government. The biggest difference lay in Prime Ministerial power, which was the most important in the government and which led to the change of the entire system.
