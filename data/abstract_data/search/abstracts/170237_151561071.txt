Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivation in Learning English as a Foreign Language
Motivation refers to reason that underline behavior that is characterized by volition. The aims of this research are to find out whether the students are motivated intrinsiclly in learning English as a foreign language, or they motivated extrinsiclly. This research used quantitative method. The result of the research are 52% of students are motivated extrinsically in learning English as a foreign language and 48% of students are motivated intrinsically in learning English as a foreign language. key word: English as a foreign language, extrinsic, intrinsic, learning, motivation
