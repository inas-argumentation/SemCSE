From Elite to Mass:Historical Changes of the Opportunity of Higher Education Entrance Taking the Development of Higher Education in the United States as a Case
The opportunity of higher education entrance is an important indicator to measure elite education and mass education.The major developed countries have experienced higher education from elite to mass,in this process,the opportunities of entrance have gradually expanded,and the higher education equity issues have been resolved from a certain extent.But it has also produced new ones.The paper takes the historical changes of the opportunity of higher education entrance in the U.S.as a case to probe the education equity issues,in order to provide reference and thinking for Chinese massification of higher education.
