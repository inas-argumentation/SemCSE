Cultural productivity and core competence of the corporation
Cultural productivity is effectiveness produced by influence and using of comprehensive cultural factors in enterprises' production. Core competence of the corporation is a unique and invisible market competitive power formed through management and combination. It can't be imitated, and it regards corporate culture as the origin, advantage technology as the core. Cultural productivity permeates through the key factor of core competence of the corporation, which determines the state of core competence of the corporation. We should improve the cultural productivity of enterprises through macroscopic or microscopic countermeasures, including developing the cultural industry, utilizing propaganda and policy guidance, improving entrepreneurs' quality, strengthening the culture of business administration, accelerating technological innovation, launching technical consultation and service, vocational training for the staff, etc. so as to promote core competence of the corporation.
