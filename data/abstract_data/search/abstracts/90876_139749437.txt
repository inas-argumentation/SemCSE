Integrated Electro optic Bragg Modulators in Lithium Niobate Nanowaveguides
We demonstrate a Bragg modulator integrated on a lithium niobate on insulator platform with a footprint of 10x500 mm2 that operates at 56 Gbit/s. This electro optic Bragg reflector has a DC tuning efficiency of 23.37+ 0.55 pm/V.
