Phosphorus chemistry nickel plating concentrated solution and plating technology in one
Phosphorus chemistry nickel plating concentrated solution and plating technology in one, concentrated solution is divided into A liquid, B liquid and C liquid three part: main salt, brightener, accelerator and deionized water room temperature are mixed into A liquid;First buffer agent, the first chelating agent, sodium hypophosphite, stabilizer, polyethylene glycol 6000, brightener and deionized water room temperature are mixed into B liquid;Second buffer agent, the second chelating agent, sodium hypophosphite, stabilizer, polyethylene glycol 6000, accelerator, brightener, ammonia and deionized water room temperature are mixed into C liquid;A liquid and B liquid are used for slotting, and A liquid and C liquid are used for adding.By volume percentage ratio, mixing A liquid, B liquid and dilution water;Become chemical nickel plating liquid;Plating, nickel plating liquid nickel content is less than 4.0g/L, adds A liquid and C liquid.During with this concentrated solution nickel plating, sedimentation rate is fast, and coating hardness and wearability are higher, it is adaptable to aluminium alloy, all kinds of ferroalloy, copper alloy, dilval, monel and the chemical nickel plating of some non conductive matrix.
