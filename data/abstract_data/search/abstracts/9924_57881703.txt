Modeling of product development process with graphical evaluation and review technique
To optimize process model in product R D under resource constraints, a modeling method of product development process by applying Graphical Evaluation and Review Technique (GERT) was proposed. Based on the work breakdown structure, a hierarchical GERT network was established to describe the product development process. The activity time parameters in the process were calculated, and the resource conflicts among parallel activities could be resolved by re adjusting the activities according to the weighted time resource priority rule. Finally, an illustrative example is given to show that it is an effective approach for modeling and analyzing the product development process.
