Multi Mode Rectifier Based LLC Resonant Converter for Wide Input Voltage Range Applications
A Photovoltaics system needs to be conditioned by a wide voltage gain converter to achieve high efficiency under a wide voltage range. The LLC resonant converter is a common solution in such applications, owing to its zero voltage switching and zero current switching capabilities. However, the frequency modulation in the LLC converter requires operating at a significantly different frequency from the resonant converter to cover the PV's wide voltage, sacrificing efficiency. In this paper, an LLC resonant converter using a novel rectifier structure is proposed to achieve high efficiency under wide PV voltage. The novel rectifier structure can operate in three modes of operation voltage tripler rectifier, voltage quadrupler rectifier, and voltage fifthfolder rectifier. In addition to the LLC converter's soft switching capability, this rectifier can narrow the switching frequency range and provide high voltage gain with a moderate transformer size. Finally, a 250 W experimental prototype with a high output voltage of 500 V was built and validated the proposed converter's effectiveness.
