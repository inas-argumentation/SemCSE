Research Paper Stability Indicating Spectrophotometric Methods For Determination Of Rosuvastatin In The Presence Of Its Acid Degradation Products By Derivative Spectrophotometric Tecniques.
The stability of rosuvastatin calcium (RC) was investigated and two stability indicating methods namely, first derivative and a derivative ratio spectrophotometric methods were used to determine rosuvastatin in the presence of its acid degradation products (RCD) using methanol as a solvent. A linear relationship was obtained in the range 5 35ug ml 1 for both methods. By applying the proposed methods, it was possible to determine rosuvastatin in its pure powdered from with accuracy 99.76+ 1.1199.35 1.410 (n 7) for the first derivative method and 99.99+ 0.994 (n=7) for the derivative ratio method. First derivative and derivative ratio methods were used for the analysis of laboratory prepared mixtures containing different ratios of rosuvastatin and its degradation product and they were valid in the presence of up to 80% degradation products. The proposed methods were validated and found to be suitable as stability indicating assay methods for rosuvastatin in pharmaceutical formulations.
