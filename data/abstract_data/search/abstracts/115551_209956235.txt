Constructions of MDS Euclidean Self dual Codes from smaller length
Systematic constructions of MDS Euclidean self dual codes is widely concerned. In this paper, we consider the construction of MDS Euclidean self dual codes from smaller length. We construct four new classes of $q$ ary MDS Euclidean self dual codes from MDS Euclidean self dual codes of known length via generalized Reed Solomon (GRS for short) codes and extended GRS codes.
