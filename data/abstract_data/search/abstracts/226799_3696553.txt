Triple Band Dual Polarized CPW Fed Planar Monopole Antenna
This article presents a novel, simple and planar design of a CPW fed tri band and dual polarized monopole antenna. The proposed structure includes inverted L shape monopole along with an L shaped parasitic resonator. Two radiating stubs have been connected to the ground to obtain triple band characteristics. Grounded flag shaped stub provides orthogonal components of electric fields with 90deg phase difference to implement 3 dB axial ratio bandwidth of 3.3% at center frequency 3.6 GHz. The proposed antenna is appropriate for different wireless application such as Bluetooth/WLAN (2.4 2.485 GHz) WiMAX (3.3 3.65, 5.25 5.85 GHz) and WLAN (5.15 5.35, 5.725 5.825 GHz) bands. The bandwidth of the 3 dB axial ratio for the RHCP state is about 120 MHz towards broadside direction for 3.6 GHz WiMAX.
