McGaughey states, 'the overarching purpose of the study is thus not to develop a new theory of internationalization from empirical data or to devise new methods, but rather to enrich existing and to foster new understandings of SME internationalization through ongoing conversations across the field' (p. xii) If one will explore this book with a mindset that will allow them to venture into the narrative and become a part of McGaughey's writing, an entirely new experience will be afforded to that reader as they become a party to the entrepreneurial activities being shared with them.
