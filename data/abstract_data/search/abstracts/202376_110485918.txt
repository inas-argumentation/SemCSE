Integrity of Optical Fiber Sensor for Measurement of Ground Thermal Conductivity
This paper investigates the applicability of optical fiber sensors for measurement of ground thermal conductivity since they exhibit good results in a monitoring system applied to geotechnical structures. An equipment for thermal response test (TRT) was developed, and it basically uses optical fiber sensors. New Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG) sensors as well as Resistance Temperature Detector (RTD) sensors were adopted to measure the ground thermal conductivity of soil filled in a steel box. TRTs using U type Ground heat exchangers (GHEs) were conducted. Ground thermal conductivity of soil obtained from TRTs was consistent with thermal conductivity evaluated by original line source model with time, and by heat flow meter method under similar properties of soil. It can be concluded that FBG sensors can be well integrated in the TRT equipment. Furthermore, they are more applicable to measure thermal properties of soils in spacious area continuously, especially in measuring ground thermal conductivity considering multi layers of soils.
