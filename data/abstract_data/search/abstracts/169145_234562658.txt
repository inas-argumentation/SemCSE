A vigilancia como pratica e valor jornalistico na cobertura da #VazaJato
When we discuss the moral values forming in a given society, we recognize that both journalism and surveillance structure themselves according to specific standards of virtue, duties, or social "goods." From the analysis of professional journalistic values present in the 98 news pieces of the #VazaJato, a coverage from eight media outlets between June 2019 and March 2020, we seek to verify whether those features are reflected in the online content circulation forms, shaping a wide surveillance system. Monitoring, follow up, and criticism actions on the spheres of power are seen throughout the coverage as the main definers of a fundamental journalistic duty. We observe, therefore, how the dynamics of discussing moral standards in the profession in this coverage has reinforced the presence of surveillance as a central aspect in the ways of justifying the structuring of journalism.
