Sustainable manufacturing strategy: an evidence based study from Indian metal industry
This research aims to provide an institutional theory perspective of organizational adoption of sustainable manufacturing practices. The institutional theory is based on the premise that not all organizational decisions have economic rationale behind them and many decisions are based on organizational desire to gain legitimacy and conform to social norms and standrads. The analysis presented explains that organizational adoption of environmentally sustainable manufacturing practices is more likely if institutional forces of coercive, normative and mimetic isomorphism are in play.
