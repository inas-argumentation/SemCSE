A Study on Chinese English Translation of Red Tourism Texts
With the increasing exchanges between China and the world, the tourism industry has been developing steadily and rapidly as an emerging industry with great potential. According to the statistics of WTO, China will become the largest tourism destination by 2020. More and more foreigners are interested in red tourism, but the C E translation quality of red tourism texts is far from satisfactory. The overall and systematic study is needed on translation of red tourism texts.
