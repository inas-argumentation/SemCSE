Interdisciplinary Teamwork in Special Education
JOHN HEYWOOD was a sixteenth century English writer who included in his famous book of "Proverbes" the saying: "Two heads are better than one." Herein lies the germ of an idea that has as one of its lineal descendents our belief that a pooling of talent helps us find better solutions to knotty problems. Two heads, or indeed several heads, are especially valuable for problem solving when they belong to individuals whose talents have been developed in diverse fields of human experience.
