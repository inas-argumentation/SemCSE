Analyzing Factors in Production Management Using Fuzzy Cognitive Mapping
The biggest challenge in the manufacturing industries is production in an optimum way. In this work we analyze about the planning of production in manufacturing plants by implementing a soft computing technique and it integrates the production and operation management requirements. To analyze this task we use a model 'Fuzzy Cognitive Mapping' of multi criteria decision making. AMS Subject Classification 60A86, 62A86.
