The theory of the hydrologic model, or: the struggle for the soul of hydrology
After an introduction to the basic problem of maintaining continuity of thinking through our current discontinuity in thought, the function of the sign within the process of theorising is considered. As understanding then proceeds through the ordering of signs, the two basic levels of orderings, of mathesis and taxinomia, are considered in relation to hydrologic theory. It is shown that hydrology cannot itself be a science, even though it draws upon many sciences, and it cannot itself be a technology, even though it serves many technologies. The nature of hydrology is identified, and with this the origin of its current theoretical despair. The means of overcoming this despair are then intimated.
