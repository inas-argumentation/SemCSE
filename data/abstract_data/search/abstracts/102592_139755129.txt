Flexible printed circuit board and manufacturing method
The invention relates to a flexible printed circuit board which comprises a conductive graphic area and an installation area surrounding the conductive graphic area. The conductive graphic area is provided with a plurality of welding disks exposed out of a solder mask layer. At least one electronic element is arranged in the conductive graphic area and is electrically connected with the welding disks through solder paste. A plurality of installation through holes are formed in the installation area. A plurality of steel rings are arranged on the installation area. The installation through holes are in one to one correspondence with the steel rings. Each steel ring is arranged in a mode of surrounding a corresponding installation through hole and is coaxial with the corresponding installation through hole. Each steel ring is fixed on the surface of the solder mask layer of the installation area through weld paste. The invention further relates to a manufacturing method of the flexible printed circuit board.
