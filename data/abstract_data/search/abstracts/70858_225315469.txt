How self assembled nanodomains can impact the organization of a phospholipid monolayer Flower like arrays.
We found that monolayers of dipalmitoylphosphatidylcholine (DPPC) and semi fluorinated tetrablock di( F 10 H 16) self assemble to form a new type of large, complex flower like patterns on the surface of water and on solid substrates. The hierarchical organization of these unusual self assemblies was investigated using compression and surface potential isotherms, in situ fluorescence and Brewster angle microscopies, and atomic force microscopy after transfer.
