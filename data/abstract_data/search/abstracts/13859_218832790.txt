Hybrid method for crosstalk analysis of multiconductor transmission lines
In this paper we give a global analysis of crosstalk in Multiconductor Transmission Lines (MTL) Knowing that both geometrics and physics parameters are strongly variables for such a problem. For this raison, we proceed by a probabilistic approach; voltage source magnitude and geometric parameters of MTL (length) are considered as random variables. The hybrid method that combines Equivalent Cable Bundle Method (ECBM) and the probabilistic approach like Polynomial Chaos (PC) is implemented for crosstalk analysis.
