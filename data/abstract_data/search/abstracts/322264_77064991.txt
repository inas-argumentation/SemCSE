MAGNETIC RESONANCE IMAGING AND ULTRASTRUCTURAL STUDIES OF BRAIN TISSUE IN THE CHRONIC RENAL HYPERTENSIVE RAT
Objective To study changes in brain magnetic resonance imaging(MRI) and ultrastructures of corresponding area in chronic renal hypertensive rats in order to further recognize the chronic brain damage in hypertension. Methods Rats were divided into the normal group and model group. The chronic renal hypertensive model was established by two renal arteries two clips method. Then two groups have been raised for twelve months. The change of rat brain was observed with MRI, and histopathological observation was performed by electron microscopy. Results 1.In normal group, there were no abnormalities in the blood pressures, MRI and ultrastructures. 2.In hypertensive group, the blood pressures of rats were high. On T2 weighted MRI, strip shaped prominent high signal intensity was present at caudate nucleus near lateral ventricle. The structures of myelin sheath were loose, and wide gaps appeared between axon and myelin sheath, and between layers of myelin sheath. Partial nerve fibers were lost, and exfoliation of myelin sheath was found near the caudate nucleus.Conclusion The prominent strip shaped high signal intensity on MRI in rats with chronic hypertension may suggeste a severe lesion in nerve fibers of the brain.
