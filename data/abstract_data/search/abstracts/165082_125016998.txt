On the Application of the Functional Equivalence Theory in the Movie Subtitle Translation Take "The Flowers of War" as an Ex ample
Movie subtitle translation,a new field of translation,as one of the mass media plays an extremely important role in the cultural exchange of movies between the countries.Wonderful and accurate subtitle translation can make the audience have a good understanding of the characters in the movie,and easier to grasp the theme of the movie.The functional equivalence translation theory of Nida fully considers the communicative environment and the target readers,which is widely adopted in the translation process of movie subtitle.This paper tries to study the application of the functional equivalence translation theory by virtue of the translation of the subtitle of "The Flowers of War"
