EmbedViz Graph Visualization of Learned Structured Embeddings of Knowledge Bases
In order to gather, organize, and make deliberate use of the massive amounts of information generated daily, special kinds of web based relational database specifically designed for knowledge management, collection, and retrieval called Knowledge Bases (KB)s have been built. The problem is, the data in these KBs is often locked up in a specific format making it difficult for them to be accessed. This poses a further problem since information held in these KBs could be of great use in training Artificial Intelligence algorithms and informing decision making. It would be useful if we could create accessible, lowdimensional embeddings of the items within these knowledge bases that retained their original semantic meaning to some extent, and are of sufficiently low dimension to be used as input for algorithms. This project presents a reproduction of the work by Bordes et al which accomplishes such an embedding task, and also reproduces their methods for learning new relations between entities in a KB. Novel contributions of this work include a design for visualizing the embedding and relational data produced by this algorithm, the implementation of this visualization tool, and implications for its use in validating relational learning algorithms.
