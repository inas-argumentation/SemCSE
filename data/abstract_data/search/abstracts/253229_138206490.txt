Design of a high efficiency boron loaded plastic scintillation detector for a wide spectrum of neutrons
For improving detection efficiency of 0.02 0.25 MeV neutrons by boron loaded plastic scintillation detector(BLPSD),surrounding the scintillator with polyethylene moderator is feasible way.In this paper,the detection efficiency of a BLPSD with front and side PE moderators of different thicknesses is simulated by MCNP code,and the thickness dependant detection efficiencies are given.The results show the side PE moderator of 80 mm thick increases the BLPSD detection efficiency of 0.02 0.25 MeV neutrons by a factor of about,but the front PE moderator decreases the detection efficiency.Finally,the design structure of BLPSD surrounded by side PE moderator can realize high detection efficiency for a wide spectrum is proved.
