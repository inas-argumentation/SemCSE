Time Series Prediction of Transformer Oil Chromatography Based on Hybrid LSTM Model
The safe and stable operation of power transformer plays an important role in the safety and stability of power system. Predicting the changing trend of transformer based on historical monitoring data has an important guiding role for power maintenance and operation. In this paper, a hybrid LSTM model is proposed to predict the time series of transformer oil chromatography. Encoder decoder is introduced and the parameters are optimized by particle swarm optimization. The hybrid LSTM is superior to the traditional LSTM and ARIMA methods in time series prediction of transformer oil chromatography.
