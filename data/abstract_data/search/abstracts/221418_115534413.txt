A High Precision Current Transformer for Loss Measurements of EHV Shunt Reactors
An overview of the development and performance of a special high precision multi ratio three stage CT and its application for loss measurements of EHV three phase shunt reactors with test voltages up to a phase voltage of 1100 kV at Royal SMIT Transformers are presented and discussed. It features current ratios of 500 A/1 A, 400 A/1 A, 300 A/1 A, 200 A/1 A, and 100 A/1 A with ratio errors within <inline formula> <tex math notation="LaTeX"$3 \times 10 6} /tex math>/inline formula> in both magnitude and phase with burdens up to <inline formula> <tex math notation="LaTeX"$1.0~\Omega /tex math>/inline formula> and at power frequencies of 50 60 Hz.
