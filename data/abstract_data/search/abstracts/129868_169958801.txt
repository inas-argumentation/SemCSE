Evaluating system for risk of pressure ulcer
PURPOSE: A system for measuring a risk factor of a bedsore is provided to sense a bedsore generation risk by measuring the pressure at each point in contact with a human body through a plurality of sensors. CONSTITUTION: A synchronization module(100) receives each data and synchronizes the data. An alternatively floating mattress includes a plurality of divided air pads(12) A floating channel(15 17) independently supplies air to the air pad or discharge the air from the air pad. A contact pressure measuring unit(30) includes a sensor which senses the pressure. A transcutaneous gas pressure measurer(50) measures the gas pressure of the skin. A determining unit(220) determines the blood flow condition of a skin contact point.
