Tree ring based average June July temperature reconstruction for Siguniang Mountains of West Sichuan Plateau, China
The high resolution proxy data are required, due to the limitation of climatic variability before instrumental records. Variations in radical growth of Picea purpurea Mast were used to develop tree ring width chronology in Siguniang Mountains of West Sichuan Plateau, China. Climate growth correlation analysis showed that tree ring growth was mainly influenced by average June July temperature variability. Based on tree ring width chronology, we developed the average June July temperature reconstruction for the past 358 years (A.D. 1653 2010) Our reconstruction showed a strong temperature signal and revealed eight warm periods (A.D. 1653 1684, 1689 1709, 1717 1735, 1743 1762, 1824 1868, 1875 1889, 1905 1964, 1981 1998) and eight cold periods (A.D. 1685 1688, 1710 1716, 1736 1742, 1763 1823, 1869 1874, 1890 1904, 1965 1980, 1999 2010) Sixty seven very warm years and 57 very cold years occurred during the past 358 years, accounting for 18.7% and 15.9% of total, respectively. Our reconstruction agreed in general with other tree ring and ice core based temperature reconstructions from nearby regions on a decadal timescale, and also was consistent with recorded glacier fluctuations and in the surrounding high mountains.
