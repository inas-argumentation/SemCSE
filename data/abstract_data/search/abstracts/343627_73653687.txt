Fast Algorithms for Enumerating Cliques in Huge Graphs
A vertex subset of a graph G (V, E) is called a clique if any two vertices of S are connected by an edge. A vertex subset of a bipartite graph G (V1 V2, E) is called a bipartite clique if any two vertices v1 V1 and v2 V2 are connected by an edge.
