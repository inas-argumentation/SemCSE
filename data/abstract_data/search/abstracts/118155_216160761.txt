Single l 500 Gbit/s PAM Signal Transmission for Data Center Interconnect Utilizing Mode Division Multiplexing
Single wavelength 502.5 Gbit/s MDM PAM 6 signal transmission over 20 m OM2 fiber with BER below HD FEC threshold (3.8 x10 3) is demonstrated for 400 G Data Center Interconnect without DSP for mode de multiplexing. This scheme shows good potential for future 800 G/1.6 T DCI.
