Gambaran pengetahuan perawat tentang standard operational procedures (sop) obat high alert di siloam hospitals makassar the image of nursing knowledge about standard operational procedures (sop) high alert medication in siloam hospitals makassar.
High alert medication is a drug that should look out for. Each hospital has each SOP are used to improve the safety of the drug. However, there are still many nurses who have not been following the SOP. Knowledge becomes a problem found in this research. This study aims to describe the level of knowledge of nurses will be high alert drugs SOP in Siloam Hospitals Makassar along with respondent characteristics. This research uses descriptive quantitative methods with cross sectional approach. Population of 83 respondents i.e. active nurse who works at Siloam Hospitals Makassar. Samples are taken using the method of purposive sampling with inclusion and exclusion criteria, with 69 respondents. The instruments used are the questionnaires amounted to 12 questions with scale Guttman. Data analysis using univariate analysis. Results of research of characteristics of respondents, i.e. most of the women and was about 20 29 years. Data obtained a large majority (68.1% of nurses have enough knowledge will SOP high alert. For Hospitals is expected to continue to renew the SOP at regular intervals and conducts dissemination as well as add posters SOP in Clean Utility. For nurses on its own, it is recommended to increase the knowledge and comply with applicable SOP.
