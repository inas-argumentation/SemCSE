A NEW TECHNIQUE OF CEMENTING QUALITY INSPECTION EVALUATION:AMK2000M
At present,cementing quality detecting instruments belong to acoustic wave series,and their evaluated results will be influenced by many factors.The Russian AMK2000M,a kind of cementing quality detecting instrument,is made up by acoustic wave device and gamma density radioactive device,which can keep records on data from the acoustic dynamics between casing tubes and formations,kinematic parameters and partisioning density in the cement sheath,and with the recorded data can help evaluate on the cementation results on the first and the second interface,volume density on each partition,and the cement sheath.This evaluation technique has broken through the bottlenecking problem of volume density in the cement sheath,from surface contact to the volume analysis,and its evaluation results will have higher accuracy and reliability because it can eliminate the influence by the factors such as fast formation and micro annulus.Through introduction on the working principle and the interpretation methods on this technique,this paper emphasize its actual practice in the Sichuan gas field with a success,providing references for its widely use in other fields.
