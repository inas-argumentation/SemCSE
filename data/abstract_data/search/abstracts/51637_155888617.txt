Background: Piriformis syndrome is the name for muscle piriformis who experience pain due to irritation of muscle and sciatic nerve In such cases can be overcome with physiotherapy modalities. Physiotherapy in this case can reduce pain, increase scope, and increase muscle strength using Transcutaneus Electrical Nerve Stimulation modalities, and exercise therapy. Objective: To know the implementation of Physiotherapy in reducing pain and increase muscle strength in the case of piriformis syndrome using Transcutaneus Electrical Nerve Stimulation modalities, and exercise therapy. Methods Giving TENS modality and exercise therapy in the form of MET and pas sive stretching exercise.
