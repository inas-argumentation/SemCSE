Monitoring on blood lactate acid of short track speed skating in winter training period
Blood lactate acid was one of the most effective indicators evaluating the intensity of training load and special training requirements,Observing lactate values changes in training,we can control exercise intensity and metabolism changes.Through monitoring of blood lactate changing of excellent short track athletes in winter training period,we found some short track athletes got low blood lactate after aerobic endurance training and did not meet the training effects in Heilongjiang Province.Some mixed oxygen training phase in anaerobic endurance training could Not effectively improve the athlete's anaerobic capacity.Therefore,monitoring of blood lactate acid should be rigorous,and the training load need adjusting at any time,in order to achieve effective training purposes.
