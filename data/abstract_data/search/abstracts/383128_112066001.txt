THE APLLICATION OF ABAQUS SOFTWARE IN THE PIPE/SOIL INTERACTION
The pipe soil contact pair has been established by the master slave method of the ABAQUS software,and also the finite element model has been established.The constitutive models of soil,such as nonlinear elastic,porous elastic and Ramberg Osgood models have been adopted relatively.The relationship between the pipe's subweights and pipe's penetration has been obtained,and also soil lateral mounding has been obtained in the computation.The results have shown that computation is in accordance with some test results,which indicates the pipe/soil interaction analysis is feasible by ABAQUS software.
