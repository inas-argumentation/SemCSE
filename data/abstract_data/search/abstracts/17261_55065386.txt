BENCHMARKING CONSTRUCTION REWORK IN AUSTRALIAN HOUSING
The direct cost of rework in the construction industry accounts for 12.4% of total contract value. Considering the annual turnover of Australian construction industry was $43.5 billion in 1996, the cost of the rework may be about $5.4 billion per annum. Although defects represent a large cost, few participants in the construction process have a clear understanding of why rework exists, or how to tackle the problem. Construction managers tend to 'bury' the errors on site without determining the causes and effects of those errors.
