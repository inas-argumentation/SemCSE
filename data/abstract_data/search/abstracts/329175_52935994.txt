Dynamic Filtering of Sparse Signals via \ell_{1} Minimization with Variant Parameters
With the widespread use of high dimensional data, algorithms dealing with sparsity signal models become very significant. There are some algorithms for sparsity signal models, but relatively few algorithms for dynamic filtering of time vary sparse signals. In this work, we present a algorithm based on Block Coordinate Decent (BCD) which is a variant of the Reweighted \ell_{1} Minimization (RWL1) to deal with dynamic filtering of time vary sparse signals. The proposed algorithm has a good convergence. It is more robust than the Basis Pursuit De Noising Dynamic Filtering (BPDN DF) which relies on the predicted accuracy of dynamic filtering. Moreover, the numerical results show the superior performance of our algorithm compared to existing methods.
