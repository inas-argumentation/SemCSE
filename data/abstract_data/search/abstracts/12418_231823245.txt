Integrated Positioning Error Bound Analysis and AP Motion State Detection for Indoor Wi Fi Localization
In indoor positioning environment, the existence of mobile Access Points (APs) has a potential risk in localization effectiveness. To address this problem, we first analogize the process of localization into the process of information propagation in a communication system to obtain the analytical result of localization error bound under the interference of both environment noise and mobile APs. Second, we propose a joint judgment criterion which consists of two judgment sub criteria to judge the state of each hearable AP. Finally, the extensive experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach can precisely detect mobile APs and preserve a satisfactory localization performance.
