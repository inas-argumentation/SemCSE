SPEECH EMOTION RECOGNITION WITHCONVOLUTIONAL NEURAL NETWORKS (CNN),RECURRENT NEURAL NETWORKS (RNN) ACOMPARATIVE STUDY
Speech Emotion Recognition is the section of Speech Recognition which has a great effect on the betterment of interaction between machines and humans. There are various applications related to speech emotion recognition that are often found in several areas like human computer interaction and call centers. This is capitalizing on the fact that voice often reflects underlying emotion through tone and pitch. In this paper, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) CNN based Long Short Term Memory(LSTM) Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) based Long Short Time Memory(LSTM) classifiers are used to classify eight emotions found in the RAVDESS database. The overall experimental results reveal that the feature combination of MFCC, Chroma, and Mel has the highest accuracy rate on the RAVDESS database using RNN based LSTM classifier 73.79% for 8 emotions, and 92.56% for 5 emotions with 4 datasets (RAVDESS, TESS, Emo DB, Custom) whereas the accuracy obtained for CNN is 57.59% and for CNN LSTM is 63.4%
