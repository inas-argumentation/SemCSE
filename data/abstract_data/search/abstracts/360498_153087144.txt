AN APPRAISAL OF POLICE COMMUNITY RELATION CRIME CONTROL IN NIGERIA; A STUDY OF IKOM LOCAL GOVERNMENT AREA
This paper is concerned with police community relations and crime control in Nigeria and a focus in Ikom, the paper appraises the relationship between the police and the houst community The data for the research were collected mainly through response from questionnaires administered and from personal interview session with selected police officers and the community. The method of data analysis is the simply percentages and chi square was used to test the hypothesis. The result of the hypothesis testing shows the police and the houst community do not have a cordial relationship, but their relationship can be improved. As a result of this discoveries, some solutions were proffered for the improvement of their relationship and for ameliorating crime in Nigeria at large, They include re orientation of the public and the police to see themselves as friends and not enemies in order to enhance their co operation in crime control, creating educational opportunities for the generality of Nigerian.
