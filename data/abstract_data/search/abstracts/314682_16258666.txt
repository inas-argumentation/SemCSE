Algebraic Bethe ansatz for the elliptic quantum group Et,e(sln) and its applications
We study the tensor product of the higher spin representations (see the definition in Section 2.2) of the elliptic quantum group E tau,E eta(sL(n) The transfer matrices associated with the E tau,E eta (sl(n) module are exactly diagonalized by the nested Bethe ansatz method. Some special cases of the construction dive the exact solution for the Z(n) Belavin model and for the ellipticA(n 1) Ruijsenaars Schneider model. (C) 2003 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.
