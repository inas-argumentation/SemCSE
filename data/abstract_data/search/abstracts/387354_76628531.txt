Therapeutic effect of integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine therapy in rats with xerophthalmia due to corneal and conjunctival exposure
Objective: To investigate the therapeutic effect of integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine therapy in rats with xerophthalmia due to corneal and conjunctival exposure and its mechanism. Methods: A total of 100 healthy Wistar rats( with 200 eyes)were randomly divided into normal control group( group A),model control group( group B),recombinant bovine basic fibroblast growth factor( rb bFGF) group( group C),and rb bFGF plus cool atomized traditional Chinese medicines group( group D),with 25 rats( 50eyes) in each group. The rats in groups B,C,and D were mechanically stopped from blinking to expose the cornea and conjunctiva and establish a model of xerophthalmia. After the model was successfully established,group A was given eye drops of normal saline,group B received no treatment,group C was given eye drops of rb bFGF,and group D was given eye drops of rb bFGF and cool atomized traditional Chinese medicines. Each group underwent Schirmer I test and fluorescein staining at 1,3,5,7,and 14 d after treatment. Then,all rats were sacrificed to collect the cornea for pathological examination. Results: The Schirmer I test showed that the lengths of wet filter paper in group C at 14 d and group D at 7 and 14 d were not significantly different from that in group A( P 0. 05) groups C and D had significantly increased lengths of wet filter paper compared with group B at 1,3,5,7,and 14 d( P 0. 01) The fluorescein staining of the cornea showed that groups C and D had significantly reduced stained dots compared with group B at 3,5,7,and 14 d( P 0. 01) The pathological examination showed that the corneal epithelium in group D became basically smooth with an approximately normal structure at5 d; group C had almost the same corneal structure as group A at 7 d; the cornea in group B was nearly repaired but not smooth at 14 d.Conclusion: Integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine therapy can increase tear secretion,reduce stained dots,and repair the injured corneal epithelium in rats with xerophthalmia due to corneal and conjunctival exposure.
