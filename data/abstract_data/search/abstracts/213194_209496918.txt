Smart Cage Bird Lovebird Based on Arduino Using Internet of Thing
The traditional design of the bird lovebird's cage has been implementing for many years. Typically, a healthy bird lovebird shows behaviors like actively whistle at any moment. Some factors are affecting the health of the bird lovebird, like temperature and humidity. In normal conditions, the temperature and humidity of the lovebird's cage around should continuously be in 250C 300C, with the humidity around is 77% 80% In irregular cases, the temperature and humidity are increased significantly higher or lower than should it be. In this inconsistency situation, it is causing the lovebird's health in some problems like increasing or decreasing its appetite, inactive movement, and unnormal whistling behavior. The new lovebird's cage designed has been implemented in order to improve the health's quality of the lovebird. It is complemented with microcontroller Arduino as a controller of the lamp and fan in order to keep the temperature and humidity around the cage in constant value using a fuzzy logic control method. It also integrated with the internet of things using things view in case of real time monitoring using a smartphone. The result, the new cage design shows the cage around is kept continuously in normal. So that, the breeding of the bird lovebird is significantly improved.
