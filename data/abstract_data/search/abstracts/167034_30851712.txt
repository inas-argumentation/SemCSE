Acoustic analysis of the vowel system of Yongding Hakka Chinese
The paper is an acoustic analysis of the formant frequencies and durations of the two sets of Yongding Hakka vowels, [i a i] in CV syllables and [i? a? u? e? c? in CVS syllables, from 10 male and 10 female speakers. The formant data show that the Yongding Hakka [i] has the F pattern of a mid central, rather than a high central, vowel, and the vowel [i? has the F pattern of [I? The durations of [i? a? u? e? c? are about half of those of [i a i] While shortened duration brings about vowel reduction and formant undershoot in [i? relative to [i] no apparent change in F pattern is observable in [a? compared to [a] The Yongding Hakka vowels [i i] and [i? u? e? c? excepting [a] and [a? have large between vowel category dispersion in the F1F2 plane, with no or minimum overlaps between any vowels. A larger vowel loop area for [i? a? u? e? c? than [i a i] supports the general expansion effect with respect to vowel inventory size as claimed by the vowel dispersion theory. A smaller Euclidean distance to the gravity center of the acoustical vowel space for [i? a? u? e? c? than [i a i] agrees with the target undershoot model which predicts reduction and formant undershoot in vowels of shortened duration.
