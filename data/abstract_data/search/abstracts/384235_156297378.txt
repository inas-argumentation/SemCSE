Three Realms of Green Operatio n in the Banking and the Sublimation of Applied Thinking on the Equator Principles
The sustainable development notion has experienced three large span introgression stages,which are the introgression from nation to manufacturing enterprises,from manufacturing enterprises to the international banking,and finally from the international banking to Chinese banking.This evolution process makes green operation of the banking possess three realms.In other words,green operation notion is taken as an adaptive strategy in the banking at the primary realm,as an operating strategy at the medium realm,and finally it is integrated into the enterprise culture of banks.At present,the majority of banks are between the first and the second realm or struggling in the gap between the second and the third.It will take enormously hard work for banks to reach the final realm,which requires them not only to sublimate the applied thinking of Equator Principles but also to achieve the integration of social responsibility,business responsibility,as well as personally professional responsibility.At the third realm,social responsibility will become the common value and driving force of whole members of a bank,which is a globally important research subject.
