Summary We present the case of a 19 year old nulligravida woman with severe dysmenorrhea since menarche; she was found to have a longitudinal vaginal septum, cervical duplication and two endometrial cavities, separated by a complete septum. Diagnosis and management of this unusual Mullerian anomaly are discussed in the context of a literature review.K ey woRdS congenital abnormalities. Uterine cervical diseases. Vaginal diseases.
