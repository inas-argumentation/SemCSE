Realist aesthetics in nineteenth century russian art writing
This article examines the presence of issues relating to representational painting in Russian nineteenth century art critical writings. These issues include imitation, copying, and the significance of detail, as they werediscussed in sources dating from the mid seventeenth century (with particular emphasis on the nineteenth century) Also considered are histonographic factors which may inform views of the parameters of'Realism' in Russian art. A consideration of primary textual sources dating before the mid century examines how such visual issues were discussed in aesthetic as well as ideological terms, before the advent of a more prominem, readily politicized 'Realism' from the 1870s onwards.
