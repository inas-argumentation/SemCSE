Effect of Post Harvest Treatments on Shelf Life and Quality of Guava (Psidium guajava L. cv. Allahabad Safeda
Guava (Psidium guajava L. known as "apple of tropics" belongs to family Myrtaceae. It is one of the dominant fruit crops of tropical and subtropical regions of India which bears delicious fruits in three different seasons' viz. summer, rainy and winter crops having lots of nutritional value and processing potential.
