Hoisting simulation method based on crane vehicle mounted control system
The invention belongs to the field of crane vehicle mounted control systems, and specifically relates to a hoisting simulation method based on a crane vehicle mounted control system. The method comprises the following steps: (1) modeling which comprises hoisting equipment modeling and working environment modeling; (2) working condition optimization and path planning; (3) hoisting simulation: a, process simulation and function selection; b, collision detection and minimum distance calculation; c, arm deformation display; d, recording according to the working condition setting, movement sequence and movement method of a crane in a simulation process, and automatic back playing; and the generation of a parameterized program segment which can drive the crane to operate according to the simulation process. The method mainly comprises the following advantages that the method improves the convenience of hoisting simulation and work efficiency through the hoisting simulation of a crane display; the method comprises arm collision detection through the precise calculation simulation, can achieve the precise deformation display of an arm system and improves the work safety.
