Handwritten Chinese Characters Recognition Based on QPSO BP
Handwritten Chinese character recognition has been applied to a number of applications. In the paper, a handwritten Chinese character recognition algorithm based on QPSO BP was proposed. In order to avoid the premature convergence and achieve higher precision, the proposed algorithm used QPSO algorithm to optimize BP neural network. In the experimentation, the texture features, morphology features and image matrix features were extracted as the features, then the handwritten Chinese characters were classified by the QPSO BP. The experimental results show that the QPSO BP algorithm has higher convergence speed. The proposed algorithm is effective method of handwritten Chinese character recognition.
