Traitement numerique d'images de fantomes mammographiques application au controle qualite des installations radiologiques et a l'aide au diagnostic du cancer du sein
Breast cancer is the most frequent cancer among women. At present, mammography is the most reliable method for early detection of abnormalities in a breast. The relevance of a diagnosis is mainly based on high quality mammograms. This is the reason why a quality control of mammographic facilities is necessary. This control is periodically performed using a phantom (artificial breast) It consists in giving a score from visual analysis, made by several observers independently, of mammographic phantom image. The aim of this study is to use image processing on digitized phantom films for a more objective evaluation of mammographic devices. For this purpose, we have studied two topics. The first one was to elaborate preprocessing algorithms for suppressing noise in high and low frequency range in order to improve image quality. Indeed initial images are poorly contrasted and highly noisy. The second topic was to elaborate object detection algorithms based on boundary model or on region model. This study clearly leads to automating quality control in mammographic facilities.
