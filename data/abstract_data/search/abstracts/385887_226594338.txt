Community Participation to Break the Nursing Profession Stigma in Pakistan of Preoperative Cessation Programs: A Mini Review
Nursing is a caring profession, facing numerous stereotypical issues in the community. Different factors are responsible for creating and building a negative image. Consequences of negative stereotyping because brain drain, dissatisfaction, violence, and shortening of the nursing profession. Rodger's and community participation framework focus to provide awareness and enhancing the nursing image in society.
