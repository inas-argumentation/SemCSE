NEW TECHNIQUES OF PAYING FOR PARKING ENFORCEMENT OF PARKING REGULATION TRAFFIC OPERATIONS AND MANAGEMENT. PROCEEDINGS OF SEMINAR N HELD AT THE PTRC SUMMER ANNUAL MEETING, SUSSEX UNIVERSITY, ENGLAND, JULY 14 17, 1986
The object of this paper is to present different French experiments in parking operation and supervision, highlighting the following points: (a) enforcement of parking regulation (1) nature of offences: the level of penalties, which is fixed by statutory law, has an influence on the user's behaviour; (2) parking control means parking surveillance: users must be made to feel that surveillance is ever present. It is generally accepted that a warden can supervise efficiently 150 to 200 parking places with parking limited to 2 hours (restricted parking zones (zones bleues) or metered) parking enforcement strategies: experience shows that cars overstaying the time limit belong very often to regulars: residents, office workers. Who have the means of guessing wardens' routines and who could warn one another of the warden's approach. Hence the need to resort to random enforcement tactics; (3) removal or clamping of vehicles: police pound, clamping devices: presentation of regulation and practices; (4) information for users erection of vertical or horizontal signposts, displaying clear information on parking regulation; stickers on windscreens together with parking tickets: in the case of illegal parking: a map, showing underused public parking facilities available locally; in the case of overstaying the time limit: an information sheet explaining the object of parking regulation. (b) techniques for pay parking metered or free parking is most of the time regulated by various devices with very diverse functions. Choices to be made in this field are made against a background of poor standardisation and rapid development of technologies. The most widely used device is the meter, a device for measuring a duration of stay determined by the amount of money inserted by the user. The new trends are as follows: proliferation of devices serving several parking spaces (indicating time of arrival and date (pay and display machine) research into machines, allowing differential tariffs (residential parking) or which allow a first free period; research into machines, allowing control at the machine and not at the vehicle (collective meters) research into the means of replacing coins by systems such as parking cheques or credit cards.(a) for the covering abstract of the seminar see IRRD 813008.
