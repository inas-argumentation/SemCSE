Max Wever: Sus Principales Aportes a la Ciencia Politicas
The topic object of study in this essay is linked to the contribution of a great German Scholar, Max Weber, to the modern Political Science. The main conclusion which was reached by the present article is that his contribution to the Political Science research and study in the twentieth century was absolutely relevant as shown by his academic works in Political Sociology and his definition of the state in relation with the monopoly of the use of force, the three kinds of leaderships and political domination, rationalization and bureaucracy.
