Research on Clothing Classification Based on Convolutional Neural Network
Convolutional neuron network is a deep learning technology in the field of image classification and image recognition. In this paper, two convolutional neuron network structures having different convolution layers and pooling layers are designed. Each model is train and test with Fashion MNIST dataset. The experiment results show that convolutional neuron network is an efficient method in clothing image classification. The complex structure model can achieve higher classification accuracy but it will increase the computation costs.
