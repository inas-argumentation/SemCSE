Surviving climate changes: high genetic diversity and transoceanic gene flow in two arctic alpine lichens, Flavocetraria cucullata and F. nivalis (Parmeliaceae, Ascomycota)
Aim We examined genetic structure and long distance gene flow in two lichenized ascomycetes, Flavocetraria cucullata and Flavocetraria nivalis, which are widespread in arctic and alpine tundra.
