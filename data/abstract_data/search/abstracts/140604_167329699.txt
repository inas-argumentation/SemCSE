Establishment of Pre Warning System Model for Crisis Management in Enterprises
An enterprise might be confronted with different kinds of crises during its development process. Based on a thorough analysis of three enterprise crisis cases, the paper develops the main topic crises and enterprise crisis management, pointing out the necessity of establishing a warning system for crisis management inside enterprises, the construction and establishment of the system. With the confirmation of enterprise warning resources, the establishment of warning analyzing index system, and the distinction of warning levels, the warning system can work properly to help enterprises to find the crisis, distinguish the level of crisis, take measures to keep away from potential crises and deal with them when crises occur.
