Depth dependent Clustering Analysis of Fractures in Crystalline Basement Rocks
Understanding the complex seismic, thermal, hydraulic and mechanical processes active during the hydraulic stimulation or continuous operation of Enhanced Geothermal Systems (EGS) requires an accurate description of the pre existing fractures and faults. However, the threedimensional characterization of the fracture network is challenging, as direct observation of the discontinuity network at great depth is limited. Fracture image logs and continuous core, which provide line samples through the fracture network, are most valuable in this regard as they provide the most precise option to place constraints on network attributes in stochastic realizations of the fractured rock mass. Among various geometrical attributes, the spatial clustering of fractures plays a critical role on the rock mass properties.
