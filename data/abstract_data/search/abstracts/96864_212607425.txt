A SURVEY ON NATURALNESS PRESERVATION OF IMAGES AND PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENTS
Image enhancement plays an important role in Image processing and analysis. The enhancement techniques are Retinex algorithm, Unsharp masking and Histogram equalization. Retinex based algorithm efficiently enhances detail and has being widely adopted. Here naturalness is essential for image enhancement to achieve pleasing perceptual quality. In order to preserve naturalness while enhancing details, an enhancement algorithm for naturalness preservation is proposed to assess enhanced images, can be introduced firstly measure for naturalness preservation. Secondly, decompose the image which insures the reflectance is restricted in the range. Thirdly, the process of illumination, that the illumination will not flood details, due to spatial variation while lightness order is preserved. Experimental result demonstrates that the proposed algorithm cannot only enhance the details but also preserve the naturalness for non uniform illumination images.
