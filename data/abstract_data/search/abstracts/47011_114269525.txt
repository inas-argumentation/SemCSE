Study on Earth Magnetic Method of Recording the Namber of Rotation Appling to Small Caliber Proximity Fuze
In this article, earth magnetic method of recording the numbers of rotation applying to small caliber proximity fuze is discussed, the distributing and characteristics of earth magnetic field are analyzed, some effective factors to sensor's output signal, such as variation of altitude, region are put forward, and it is pointed out that earth magnetic field's notable difference on different regionis the main effective factor. At last, method of measuring the earth magnetic signal is introduced and result curves are given.
