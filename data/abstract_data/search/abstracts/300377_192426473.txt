THE SPIRIT OF "MODERNITY" IN WESTERN MODERN AESTHETICS OF ARCHITECTURE: FORM AND MEANING OF WESTERN AESTHETICS OF ARCHITECTURE (2)
By reference to relevant research on Aesthetics, the paper studies the "modernity" in the development of Western Aesthetics Of Architecture. The spirit of "modernity" is summarized from the aspects of concepts evolution and differences between the thoughts to analyze the contents and characteristics of the "modernity"
