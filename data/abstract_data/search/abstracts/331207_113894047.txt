Optimization design of crankshaft die structure based on FEM
Aiming at the difficult filling problem of three cylinder crankshaft counterweight,the die structure was optimized from preform forging and pre forging die,the structure of pre forging die rib was redesigned based on the suggestion of the pre forging die and gradual parting concept. The flash spreading and final forging filling situation before and after optimization were obtained through the finite element simulation results. The simulation results show that through the improvement of parting and rib structure,the final forging filling effect is significantly improved. When the level parting distance of pre forging mold is in the range of 19 33 mm,and rib slope is 13deg,the best filling effect of forging is achieved,which verifies the feasibility of the die optimization.
