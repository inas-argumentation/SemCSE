Using Games for Learning to Improve Students Performance in Higher Education
This research design discusses different approaches to the concept of results in higher education, especially related to engineering and IT education. The main topics are the means to measure quality of both the learning process and the learning outcomes. The paper also discusses and outlines some techniques on how to implement real life trials using the presented concepts. It shows how it is possible to use a games based quality measurement model to obtain live quality feedback, to academic and administrative staff, during the learning process.
