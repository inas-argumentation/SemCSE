How to Improve Measurement Accuracy of Air Interface Type Ultrasonic Water Level Gauge
Various factor influencing the measurement accuracy of air interface type ultrasonic water level gauge were analyzed and countermeasures eliminating these influencing factors were proposed,in which,the error of measurement echo for ultrasonic water level gauge changed with the distance reflection angle and reflecting surface was analyzed.A method was put forward to analyze ultrasonic measurement echo front by using digital sampling technology for improving the measurement accuracy.Using the technology,the data repeatability is significantly improved when the echo signal intensity changes and the better measurement accuracy can be achieved for shorter time.
