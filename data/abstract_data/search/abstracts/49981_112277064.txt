Analysis on the Effect of Shape Coefficient and Window to Wall Ratio in the Glass Curtain Wall Construction
In this paper,taking Shanghai zone as an example,using the dynamic energy consumption simulating software ESP r to simulate and analyze the relation between shape coefficient,window to wall ratio of the glass curtain wall and cooling load.Research shows that when the shape coefficient increases,the cooling load increases.So does the window to wall ratio.In order to reduce the cooling load of the glass curtain wall,the window to wall ratio and the shape coefficient should be limited.
