Unequal arm Adaptive Rood Pattern Search with Early Terminations For Fast Block matching Motion Estimation on H.264
Based on a rood shaped pattern of the motion vector displacement distribution inherently resulted from block matching motion estimation, a series of adaptive rood pattern search (ARPS) algorithms have been introduced. Among them, the unequal arm ARPS has achieved superior performance on many accounts while maintaining a fairly close rate distortion performance compared with that of the full search. In this paper, an enhanced version of the unequal arm ARPS is introduced by incorporating early terminations at both the initial search and the refined local search stages. Its performance was compared with that of the UMHexagonS fast block matching motion estimation algorithm based on the JM 9.3 software encoder. The experimental results show that the unequal arm ARPS with early terminations has yielded significant search efficiency gain while consistently maintaining a fairly close rate distortion performance compared with that of the UMHexagonS. Furthermore, the proposed algorithm is fairly simple on implementation
