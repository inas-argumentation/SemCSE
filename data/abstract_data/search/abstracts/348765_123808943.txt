The orthogonal direct sum decomposition of cylindrical function space in L~2(E_(n+1),dxdy/y~(n+1)
In this paper,we give the orthogonal direct sum decomposition of cylindrical func tion space in L~2(E_(n+1),dxdy/y~(n+1) by using wavelet transform. Under this decomposition we de fine the Toeplitz Hankel type operators and obtain the similar Schatten Von Neumann prop erties for those operators.
