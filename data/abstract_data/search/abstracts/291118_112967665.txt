Study on Clutter Models for High Resolution Radar
Under system of high resolution radar,it is more suitable for the generalized gamma probability density function(GG pdf) that characterizes the speckle component and modulation component of the clutter.The expression of generalized compound(GC) pdf is derived based on this.Furthermore,its expression of incorporation with thermal noise is also discussed.Chosen special parameters,the generalized k(GK)pdf model is established.It analyzes the relation between the classical clutter models and GK pdf model possessed by variety values of parameters.The statistics characteristic of the clutter models above mentioned are researched,basic method of parameter estimation is derived.The simulation results show the conclusion in this paper.
