Theory and practice of the learner autonomy in ELT
Autonomous learning involves both a learning method and a teaching approach. It is more and more proved to be a cure to the tiresome and inefficient classroom teaching. Due to the fact that traditional teaching method still dominates the education, the paper tries to introduce a new concept in order to change the unsatisfactory condition in English teaching/learning by discussing two points: (1) the learning strategies; (2) how to promote the learner centered arrangement in classroom teaching.
