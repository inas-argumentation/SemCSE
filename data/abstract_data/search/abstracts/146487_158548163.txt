Corpus Linguistics, Critical Discourse Analysis, and Poverty
This chapter introduces readers unfamiliar with linguistics to the tools and methodologies that we have chosen to use. It includes an overview of corpus linguistics, what it is and what assumptions corpus analysis makes about language. We then introduce and define the term discourse and, leading on from this, we introduce the core principles of critical discourse analysis (CDA) We then consider the benefits of combining corpus linguistics and CDA for the analysis of social phenomena.
