Handedness Control of a Mid Infrared 3.5 mm Optical Vortex MgO:PPLN Parametric Oscillator
We generate the first millijoule level tunable mid infrared (3.362 3.677mm)optical vortex output with a topological charge \ell$ of 1 by a 1 mm vortex pumped quasi phase matching MgO doped periodically poled lithium niobate (MgO:PPLN)optical parametric oscillator.
