RESPONSE OF POTATO (Solanum tuberosum L. TO SOIL AND FOLIAR APPLICATION OF MULTI MICRONUTRIENTS MIXTURE IN ALFISOLS OF HASSAN DISTRICT
A field experiment was conducted during Kharif 2015 at College of Agriculture, Hassan to study the response of potato to soil and foliar application of multi micronutrients mixture. The experiment was laid out in CRBD with ten treatments combinations and three replications. Different micronutrient mixture grades viz. FMM1 (100g ZnSO4, 100g MnSO4, 50g FeSO4, 50g CuSO4 and 50g H3BO3 100L 1 FMM2 (double dose of FMM1) SMM1 (10kg ZnSO4, 6kg MnSO4, 8kg FeSO4, 4kg CuSO4 2kg H3BO3 ha 1 and SMM2 (deficient nutrients of Zn and B 10 2 kg ha 1 were tested with control (RDF+FYM) Foliar (FMM1 FMM2 sprayed at 30 and 45 DAP) and soil application (SMM1 SMM2 as basal) of micronutrients mixtures were tried either alone or in combinations. The results revealed that the highest tuber yield of potato (24.17 t ha 1 were recorded with soil application of SMM1 foliar application of FMM2 along with RDF+FYM and which was significantly higher than that of control (RDF FYM) The percent increase in tuber yield were 50.03 over control. The higher yield obtained in this treatment was attributed to significant improvement in growth and yield attributing parameters and higher uptake of both macro and micro nutrients of potato. This treatment found more profitable with higher gross and net returns with B:C ratio of 4.25 compared to other treatments.
