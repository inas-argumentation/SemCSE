Text image watermarking based on encryption permutation
A text image watermarking algorithm using encryption and hashing function is presented to choose Chinese characters sequence which need to be embedded watermarking.During the process of embedding watermarking,the algorithm imports redundancy to decrease the probability of error estimation and increase the resistibility of malicious attacks.On the one side,the algorithm can considerably im prove uncertain difficulty of wiping off watermarking and watermarking security.On the other side,the algorithm can dispence redundancy watermarking distributing,withstand attacker's clippling locally attack.The experiments demonstrate these effective results.
