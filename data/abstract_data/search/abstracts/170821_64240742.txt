A Numerical Algorithm for Ill conditioned Power Flow Problem
In allusion to calculation of power flow for ill conditioned power network,a numerical power flow calculation method composed of Runge Kutta method and multi starting extension method is presented.The core of the presented method is that: based on extension method the problm of solving abnormal power flow is turned into the initial value problem of differential equations and then is solved by Runge Kutta method.To make the presented algorithm meet the demand of engineering practive,during the solution,the multi starting extension method is adopted to expand the convergence range of the solution,and the numerical calculation scheme is turned into the way similar to conventional power flow calclation by Newton Raphson method to link up with the mature technique such as sparse technique.In short,the presented algorthm is a power flow algorithm for wide range convergence,and can be well adapt to the anlsysis on abnormal power flow and static voltage stability.Simulation results of modified IEEE 5 bus system and IEEE 118 bus system show that the presented algorithm is effective.
