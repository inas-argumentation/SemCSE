In general, parents who have children with special needs are not easy, because each child has different characteristics of needs. One part of children with special needs is Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a complex developmental disorder that has a lack of communication skills, lack of social abilities, and the presence of repetitive behavior. This study wants to see the relationship between parental acceptance and parental competence on the optimism of parents who have autistic children. In this study, 122 parents were taken by accidental sampling in various schools with special needs and therapy sites. Measuring instruments used in the study used Life Orientation Test Revised to measure optimism, Parental Acceptance RejectionQuestionaire to measure parental acceptance of children and Parenting Sense of Competence to measure perceptions of parenting competencies. The results of this study indicate that there is a relationship between acceptance with parental optimism with p. 0.001 <0.05 and there is a relationship between competence and parental optimism with p. 0.001 <0.05. The results of this study indicate that parents have high acceptance, perceptions of caring parenting competencies and moderate optimism. Hope that parents can increase the parents optimism by having a positive perspective on children.
