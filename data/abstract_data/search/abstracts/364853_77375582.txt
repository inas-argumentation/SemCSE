Scenario Oriented Calibration of the Radio Propagation Models in TD LTE Power Wireless Private Networks
Accurate radio propagation models in coverage analysis are highly critical for network planning and optimization of the TD LTE power wireless private networks. This paper proposes a method of calibration of the parameters in the ray tracing based propagation models to improve the model accuracy. This method divides the network coverage space in a dense urban environment into several geographical scenarios by spatial clustering, and then uses the information on terrain and the on ground objects such as buildings provided by the 3D digital map and the scenario oriented ray tracing propagation model to analyze the geographical distribution of the cell radio signals in indoor and outdoor environments. The analysis accuracy suffers from imprecision in distribution and geometrical dimensions of the oversimplified buildings, omitting of tree occlusion, and the inaccurate buildings' dielectric coefficients. Thus, we model calibration of the direct, reflection and diffraction parameters in the propagation models as a multi objective optimization problem, and take the driving test data and the multi objective evolutionary algorithm to adjust these parameters. We validate the effectiveness of this parameter calibration scheme by using the practical measurement data in the practical wireless private networks environment in Suzhou, and it is demonstrated that the proposed calibration scheme can guarantee the models' analysis errors below 5 db.
