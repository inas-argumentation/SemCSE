Integrated circuit interfaces for ECoG signal recording
This chapter presents an overview of the fundamentals and state of the art in physiological monitoring instrumentation with a focus on ECoG interfaces to the brain and micropower integrated circuit design for recording ECoG signals. Since the electrode to brain interface is a performance limiting factor in ECoG monitoring systems, practical interface configurations are offered for biopotential acquisition. A systematic approach to instrumentation amplifier (IA) design using CMOS transistors operating in weak inversion is shown to offer high energy and noise efficiency. Practical methodologies to obviate 1/f noise, counteract electrode offset drift, improve common mode rejection ratio (CMRR) and obtain sub Hz highpass cutoff are illustrated with a survey of the state of the art IAs.
