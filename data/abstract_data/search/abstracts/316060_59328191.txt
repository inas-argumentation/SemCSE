Isolation and identification of diazotrophic Bacillus subtilis strain SS2 from saline soil and its potential to be used as biofertilizer
Bio fertilizers are generally bio organic substances that have capability to improve the soil fertility significantly by fixing atmospheric nitrogen. These can solubilize insoluble phosphate and produce plant growth hormones. In the present investigation, genomic DNA was extracted from the diazotrophic isolate SS2 obtained from saline soil of HAU farm (Hisar) and subjected to the amplification of 16S rRNA gene for identification. The similarity searching of the sequence obtained after sequencing showed 100% similarity with Bacillus subtilis which was confirmed by morphological and biochemical tests. The Gram positive isolate showed positive results for citrate utilization, catalase production, nitrate reduction and hydrolysis of starch. It also utilizes the carbohydrates glucose, mannitol, xylose, mannose, maltose and trehalose which confirmed it as Bacillus subtilis. The exhibition of other PGP traits make SS2 a promising strain to be developed as biofertilizer to enhance soil fertility and promote the plant growth. Key words: Biofertilizer, diazotroph, 16S rRNA, Bacillus subtilis, Saline soil
