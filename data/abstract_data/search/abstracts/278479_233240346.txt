Muon and electron identification efficiencies and hadron lepton misidentification rates at Belle II for Moriond 2021
Lepton ID Group, The Belle II Collaboration Abstract We present a collection of selected results on the performance of Belle II in the identification of electrons and muons. This work is based on a combination of sub detector likelihoods and is carried out using data collected at the Belle II experiment from 2019 to mid 2020. These data correspond to a total integrated luminosity of L dt 62.8 fb 1 collected at the center of mass energy of the U (4S) with an additional sample corresponding to L dt 8.4 fb 1 of off resonance data collected over the same period.
