Depending on the harvestable component of interest, (i.e. fiber, grain, or cannabinoids) male plants and/or pollen might be vitally necessary or completely unwanted. Today, there is not much need for hemp rope or large hemp linens for sailing ships, etc. as there was in the nineteenth century; however, fibers from hemp possess several very positive attributes that may make them useful as modern natural fibers. Uses for hemp fibers today are different and much broader than when hemp was last grown in Kentucky.
