Environmental and social management framework for Lesotho
The Southern Africa Tuberculosis and Health Systems Support Project seeks to help ease the TB burden in the Southern Africa region by achieving the following overarching goals: (i) increase utilization of key TB control and occupational lung diseases services in targeted geographic areas of the four participating countries (Lesotho, Malawi, Mozambique and Zambia) and (ii) strengthen the sub region's capacity to address such conditions. Some of the negative impacts and mitigation measures include: maintain construction machinery regularly as recommended by dealers; workers must be provided with breathing masks to protect them from air pollution from paint smells, dust and particulates from refurbishment activities; construction and rehabilitation works must be carried out during the official government working hours; use machines that do not make a lot of noise; vehicles, plant and machinery must be regularly maintained as recommended by dealers; and provide appropriate containers across the works areas for waste disposal and easy collection to disposal site.
