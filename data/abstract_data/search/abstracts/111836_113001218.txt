Critical mass: My country, I still call Australia home, GoMA
In title, this exhibition reminds me of Diaspora, displacement, and the migr s. Salman Rushdie is sympathetic. Somewhere he wrote of the three disruptions of the immigrant: loss of language, loss of place, and being surrounded by beings 'whose social behavior and code is very unlike, and sometimes offensive to, his own'.1 However, Aborigina people, while facing the same disruptions, are unlike the immigrant: they did not have a choice; the disruptions were forced upon them.
