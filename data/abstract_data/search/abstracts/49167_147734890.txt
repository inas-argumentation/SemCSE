The Study of the Change of Clothing in the Five Etiquette System of the Song Dynasty
In the Song Dynasty,the participants to a ceremony who belonged to the classification and hierarchy system were required to wear appropriate clothes according to the Five Etiquette System. During a lucky ritual,celebration ritual,guest ritual,and military ritual,the monarch servant changed clothes so as to indicate their identity and status to adjust to the different occasions. During an ominous ritual,Xiaozong changed clothes in order to express his emotion. As a way to express power,etiquette was displayed from frequent change of clothing. Through these processes,power was recognized and functioned. In this way,the meaning of ritual system,which is the pursuit of order would be expressed and realized.
