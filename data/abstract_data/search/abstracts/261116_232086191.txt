COVID 19 and Dentistry in 72 Questions: An Overview of the Literature
The outbreak of Coronavirus Disease 2019 (COVID 19) caused by Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus 2 (SARS CoV 2) has significantly affected the dental care sector. Dental professionals are at high risk of being infected, and therefore transmitting SARS CoV 2, due to the nature of their profession, with close proximity to the patient's oropharyngeal and nasal regions and the use of aerosol generating procedures. The aim of this article is to provide an update on different issues regarding SARS CoV 2 and COVID 19 that may be relevant for dentists. Members of the French National College of Oral Biology Lecturers "College National des EnseignantS en Biologie Orale" CNESBO COVID19 Task Force) answered seventy two questions related to various topics, including epidemiology, virology, immunology, diagnosis and testing, SARS CoV 2 transmission and oral cavity, COVID 19 clinical presentation, current treatment options, vaccine strategies, as well as infection prevention and control in dental practice. The questions were selected based on their relevance for dental practitioners. Authors independently extracted and gathered scientific data related to COVID 19, SARS CoV 2 and the specific topics using scientific databases. With this review, the dental practitioners will have a general overview of the COVID 19 pandemic and its impact on their practice.
