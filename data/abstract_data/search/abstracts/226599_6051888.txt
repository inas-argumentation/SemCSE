Fully Key Homomorphic Encryption, Arithmetic Circuit ABE and Compact Garbled Circuits
We construct the first (key policy) attribute based encryption (ABE) system with short secret keys: the size of keys in our system depends only on the depth of the policy circuit, not its size. Our constructions extend naturally to arithmetic circuits with arbitrary fan in gates thereby further reducing the circuit depth. Building on this ABE system we obtain the first reusable circuit garbling scheme that produces garbled circuits whose size is the same as the original circuit plus an additive poly(l,d) bits, where l is the security parameter and d is the circuit depth. All previous constructions incurred a multiplicative poly(l) blowup.
