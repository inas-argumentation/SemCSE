Effect of muscular amino acids and peptides and nucleosides injection on renal ischemia/reperfusion injury in rats
Objective To investigate the effect of muscular amino acids and peptides and nucleosides injection (APN) and the change and role of plasma interleukin 4 (IL 4) and interleukin 6(IL 6) in acute renal ischemia/reperfusion(I/R) injury. Methods Rat renal I/R model was made by placing the traumatic vascular clamp in renal pedicles for 60 minutes. APN was used on the rat models. Plasma BUN, Cr, IL 4,IL 6, urine volume and renal weight were observed at various intervals. Results Plasma levels of BUN, Cr and IL 6 decreased, the urine volume increased and the change of renal weight was slight in rats treated with APN, when compared with the control. The IL 4 was undetectable during renal I/R. Conclusion APN might modulate the IL 6 response and possess a protective effect on renal I/R injury.IL 4 change was not obvious in the renal I/R injury.
