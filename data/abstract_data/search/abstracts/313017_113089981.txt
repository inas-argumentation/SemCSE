An Intelligent Theft and Fire Alarm System Used in the Dormitory
This paper designs an intelligent theft and fire alarm system based on single chip STC89C52 RC,which through the single chip controls the smoke sensor,pyro electric infrared sensors and photoelectric gate to realize fire detection,human body detection and personnel entry into and exit from the dormitory,and processes detected signal to realize door locking without prompt as well as sound and light alarm in alert situation. The alarm can be cancelled by giving a password in the matrix keyboard,and LCD1602 gives a realtime display of the detection and promptings. Data stored in the EEPROM of the single chip are used to avoid data loss in case of power break. Remote monitoring is realized through the wireless module.
