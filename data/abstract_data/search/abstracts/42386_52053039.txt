Locally repairable codes with strict availability from linear functions
Dear editor, In distributed storage systems, data is divided into small pieces and stored across a number of nodes. Since node failures are inevitable, redundancy is necessary. During the long run, the redundancy needs to be replenished. Regenerating codes were proposed to decrease repair bandwidth of distributed storage systems by accessing more nodes and downloading less data from each node [1, 2] Locally repairable codes [3] improve the repairability of distributed storage system by decreasing number of nodes connected in the repair process.
