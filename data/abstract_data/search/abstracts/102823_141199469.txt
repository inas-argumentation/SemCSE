Theorie financiere et strategie financiere;financial theory and financial strategy
(VF)L'objet de cet article est tout d'abord, de montrer les evolutions profondes des approches theoriques en finance, puis de faire l'inventaire des apports actuels et potentiels de la theorie financiere a la strategie financiere tant sur le plan academique que decisionnel. La reflexion proposee permet de presenter les orientations recentes de la recherche financiere, de montrer les applications de la theorie financiere a la strategie financiere et d'inister sur les dangers d'une recherche eloignee de toute preoccupation normative.(VA) The aim of this paper is first, to show the huge evolution of the different theoretical streams in finance and second, to make an inventory of present and potential contributions of financial theory to financial strategy from academi and decision making point of views. This discussion presents recent developemnts of financial research, shows uses of financial theory for financial strategy and insists on the drawbacks of a research disconnected of all normative preoccupation.
