Three Dimensions of the Upgrading and Transformation of Cultural Tourism
Promotion of cultural tourism and the appeal of cultural tourism sites requires the upgrading and transformation of the industry in three dimensions. Firstly,efforts should be made in terms of tourism ideology so as to promote the shift from form focused to content focused,from traditional service to multi layered service,from prescribed schemes to individual oriented schemes. Secondly,tourism products are to be optimized to enhance their cultural connotations,meet the customers' psychological needs,and further develop tourism facilities. Thirdly,tourism mechanism shall give full play to the market economy to transform the original production modes and establish a flexible running system.
