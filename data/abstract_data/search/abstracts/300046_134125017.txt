Radial artery puncturing arm support for catheter bed
A radial artery puncturing arm support for a catheter bed comprises an arm support main body; the arm support main body is an L shaped support surface formed by connecting a liner plate fixed on the catheter bed with a support plate for supporting an arm, wherein the upper surface of the support plate has an inclined angle of 10 20 degrees along the length direction; a chute is formed in the side wall of the support plate at the same side as the radial artery of a patient; a cup for receiving puncturing blood is glidingly arranged in the chute and is of a square structure; the cup is matched with the chute by a slider arranged at one side wall. The side wall is attached to the side wall of the support plate. The radial artery puncturing arm support for the catheter bed is simple in structure, the optimal puncturing point can be conveniently fast found, and the puncturing blood can be effectively collected and is prevented from polluting the arm support.
