Hydraulics Analysis of Pigging for Gas Pipeline after Water Pressure Test
Water pressure testing of long distance gas pipeline should be carried out to check its strength and leak after pipe laying.Then a pig would be pushed by air compressor to clean out water from the pipeline.We establish hydraulic model of pigging,based on the field data of one certain gas pipeline.To reduce the drain of local resistance coefficient or improve the pressure of air compressor we can increase pig operation speed.At the same time, pig operating speed is affected by outlet size.If the whole process does not cutout,instantaneous high pressure will be avoided in the process of cleaning line.
