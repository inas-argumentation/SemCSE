Functional Models of the Nickel Pincer Nucleotide Cofactor of Lactate Racemase.
A novel nickel pincer cofactor was recently discovered in lactate racemase. Here, we report three synthetic nickel pincer complexes that are both structural and functional models of the pincer cofactor in lactate racemase. DFT computations suggest the ipso carbon of the pyridinium pincer ligands act as a hydride acceptor for lactate isomerization, whereas an organometallic pathway involving Ni mediated b H elimination is less favored.
