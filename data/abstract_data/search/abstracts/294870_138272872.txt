Comparative Study of Joint and Joint Venture in the Domestic and Overseas EPC General Contract Project
The Consortium and Joint Venture always played important roles in the cooperating mode during domestic and overseas EPC projects. This paper analyzes the similarities and differences between Consortium and Joint Venture in Legal Features, the Presentation Forms, Interior Work Limit, the Division of Obligation, Project Management, Financial and Taxation Mode etc. It is concluded that Interior Work Limit and the Division of Obligation as well as the"Joint Win, Together Lose"are the main distinguish between both modes.
