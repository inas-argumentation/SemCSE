Mandarin Chinese children of sound pronunciation quality evaluation method and system
Mandarin Chinese children of sound pronunciation quality evaluation method and system relates to a method for evaluating voice and evaluation systems. In the present invention, the conventional method of pronunciation quality evaluation based on the one hand, extension of the standard pronunciation of Chinese sound vowel space, to include all of the children of the vowel, vowel sound establish an extended model comprising children of vowels and vowel of children comprising pronunciation space propagation in a wider range of pronunciation quality evaluation; on the other hand, for evaluating quality of pronunciation of vowels in children comprising pronunciation expansion space, the phonetic pronunciation of the children of the quality assessment is transformed into phonetic pronunciation quality classification of children problem, establish child of phonetic pronunciation quality classification model, by extracting a variety of acoustic features of syllables and their children in front of syllables, linguistic features, as well as their statistical characteristics and dynamic characteristics, access to pronounce the quality level of the children of sound, and then complete the children of phonetic pronunciation quality evaluation, can effectively improve the accuracy of Mandarin phonetic pronunciation of the child's quality evaluation.
