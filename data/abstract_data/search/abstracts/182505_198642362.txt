Innovative Tools for Teaching Marine Robotics, IoT and Control Strategies Since the Primary School
Digital skills are becoming increasingly central to the educational policy of every country and educational systems are facing new challenges in the era of the Fourth Industrial Revolution. All pupils should gain the knowledge, skills and competences they need, as they determine an individual's chance to succeed in the future labour market and to have an active role in the future society. In the last decade, a lot of projects showed how educational robotics (ER) can be a powerful tool for teaching basic skills and STEAM (science, technology, engineering, art and mathematics) subjects.
