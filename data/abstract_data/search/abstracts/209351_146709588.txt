Effect of the Use of Motivational Techniques on the Academic Achievement of the Teachers at the Higher Education Level in Pakistan
The study probed into a descriptive research to evaluate the effect of the use of motivational techniques on the academic achievement of the teacher at higher education level in Pakistan. Motivation is the heart of teaching and learning process. It is usually defined as an internal state that arouses, directs and maintains behavior a certain period of time. However, it is the process of arousing, sustaining and regulating activities, to be precise a concept limited to some aspects such as the energetic behavior or purposive regulation. Main objectives of the study were to investigate the motivational techniques used by the heads of institutions, examine the views of teachers about the effective and ineffective motivational techniques, and identify the students' opinions about the performance of the teachers and to find out the impact of motivational techniques.
