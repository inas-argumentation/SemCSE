Further scattering results for almost cubic nonlinear Schrodinger equation
We present three results on the scattering of solutions for the almost cubic nonlinear Schrodinger equations. When initial datum has a small X p norm, the solutions scatter in L 2 when the initial datum is in H s with a small L 2 norm with 0 s 1 then they scatter in H s Finally, whenever we have global existence of H 1 solutions, we also have scattering in the H 0 1 norm.
