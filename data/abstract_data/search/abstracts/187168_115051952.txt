Innovative Research on Salary Management System in Private Higher Learning Institutions
Private higher education is an important part of higher education in China.At present,there exist a lot of problems in the construction of talent team and talent support in private higher learning institutions.We should attach great importance and make efforts to solve this significant subject that how to establish a scientific and effective salary management system,attract and retain qualified personnel,train the talented personnel,strengthen the private higher education quality,as well as enhance the core competitiveness of schools.From the perspective of newly founded private colleges,the paper analyzes and discusses the current status of salary management system reform for reference.
