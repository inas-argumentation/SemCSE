Correlation between Emotional Intelligence and Creativity Factors
The purpose of this research was to study the relationship between emotional intelligence and creativity in Student University. The statistical population of the research included 1370 students. The sample size was determined by cluster sampling method (n=375) In this study two standard questionnaires were used, one relating to EQ and the other to creativity. The reliability and validity of these two instruments were reported to be significant in different studies. The findings indicated that there was significant and positive relationship between Creativity its subscales with EQ.
