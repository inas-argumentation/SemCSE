Research Progress of Treating Diabetic Nephropathy by Traditional Chinese Medicine
[Objective]We summarize research progress of treating diabetic nephropathy by traditional Chinese medicine in recent years in order to provide theoretical proof and clinical treating ways.[Methods]We summarize,analyze and conclude related scholars of traditional chinese medicine's reports on diabetic nephropathy literatures.We announce research progress of treating diabetic nephropathy by traditional chinese medicine respectively from different levels of pathogenesis,treatment according to syndrome differentiation,phases and types of disease,compound and single herb medicine.[Conclusion]Traditional chinese medicine and pharmacology have good treatment effect and study value.
