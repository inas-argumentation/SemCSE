The Hellenic Army Land Forces Learning Model and how it can be more effective under the shandow of fiscal crisis
The current financial crisis in Greece has affected its society resulting many negative implications at all levels and sectors. The fact that the crisis still is ongoing has as a result, the creation of an unfavorable external economic environment for all organizations, threatening directly their viability. For this reason, organizations must be pre emptive enough in order to be capable to avoid unexpected situations and be adapted continuously and rapidly for maintaining their competiveness and eventually to survive. Armed Forces as an organization, in no case could be remained unaffected. Given this fact, their adaptation is required in order to keep their operational capabilities at the highest possible level. Focusing on learning process in the Hellenic Army Land Forces, this study reached the conclusion that a structural transformation and the adoption and a modern concept are required in order the learning process to be facilitated and become more effective and efficient. However, the particularities that the Armed Forces present as organization, call for special studies for the development and implementation of a military learning model. In particular, further specific researches are required focusing on the Hellenic Armed Forces. The study provides the theoretical background and proposes appropriate methods and tools that can potentially be used, to the Greek reality and idiosyncrasies.
