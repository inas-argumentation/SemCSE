Functional Analysis Notes 7 Modes of convergence Strong and weak convergence
This section is about different types of convergence (of sequences) on normed spaces and their duals. Recall that for sequences of numbers we have different types of convergence: absolute, conditional, etc. and that they serve different purposes. Similarly, for a sequence of elements of a normed space, we will study two different types of convergence: strong and weak. This is a distinction that does not appear for sequences of numbers, because when the normed space is finite dimensional, the strong and weak convergence coincide.
