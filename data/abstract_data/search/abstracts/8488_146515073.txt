Les sources d'attachement a l'entreprise, du point de vue du salarie L'exemple des ingenieurs
The concept of attachment, primarily a psychological concept,enables you to understand the bond between person and organization. Within the concept of this relationship, attachment is defined as a psychological closeness that reflects the importance and considerable value given to the organization by the employee and wich can be compared to a stable and durable relational intensity globally perceptible. The puropse of this paper is, first, to specify the notion of attachment by presenting a typology of the different situations of attachment and consequently, to underline that attachment can be a synonym of loyalty, retention, compelled adhesion or inertia. Its second purpose is to give the outcomes of a survey, made with a sample of engineers, which endeavoured to identify the antecedants of such an attachment. In the last part of our paper, we discuss and comment upon all these results comparatively to those of previous research
