An epidemiological investigation on the sleep quality among retired elderly in Fuzhou military areas
Objective To investigate the prevalence of sleep quality and the correlative factors among retired elderly in Fuzhou Military areas.Methods Five hundred military retired elderly were evaluated with Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index(PSQI)and general situation questionnaire in sleep quality.Results The mean score of PSQI in 500 elderly was 5.72+ 4.01.Among them 129(25.80%)elderly had poor sleeping quality,192(38.40%)had common sleeping quality and 179(35.80%)had good sleeping quality.For elderly people,the influencing factors of sleep were:going to the bathroom in the evening,awaken from asleep or wake up early,hard to fall into sleep,cough at night,nightmare,pain and so on.There were no statistical differences in the age,sex,educational level and marital status in this investigation of sleep quality.Conclusions The incidence of sleep disorder in military retired elderly was higher in Fuzhou.Treatment of underlying diseases and adjusted bad sleep habits should be done in elderly to improve sleep quality.Sleep and health problems in elderly should be noticed by society.
