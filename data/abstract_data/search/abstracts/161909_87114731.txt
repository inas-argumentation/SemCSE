Molecular Identification of Pathogenic Microorganism in Aquaculture
The rapid development of shrimp, shellfish and fish culture has resulted in a concomitant appearance of pathological microorganisms. Therefore, quick and correct diagnosis becomes very important for aquaculture. Molecular biological techniques in identification of pathogenic microorganism including hybridization of nucleic acid,PCR,DNA fingerprint and 16S rRNA are applied and reviewed in aquaculture in detail.
