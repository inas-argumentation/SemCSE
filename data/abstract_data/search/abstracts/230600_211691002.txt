Thermal ageing effects on polypropylene properties
This work reports on the effects of the thermal ageing on the dielectric and physico chemical properties of polypropylene (PP) The dielectric properties such as AC electrical conductivity (sac) dielectric constant (e')and dielectric losses (e" are measured at temperatures between 0 degC and 80 degC in the frequency range of 1 Hz 107 Hz. Whereas, the changes in the PP's structure were highlighted by Fourier Transformer Infrared Spectroscopy (FT IR) Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) and the estimated Hydophobicity from measuring the contact angle.The experimental results revealed that thermal ageing leads to a degradation of the dielectric properties. Indeed, sac, e' and e" increased by ageing. These variations may be explained by the changes in the polymers structure
