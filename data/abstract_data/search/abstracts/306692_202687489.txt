Dual Band PGA Correction of Residual Range Cell Migration
In this article we present a dual band, PGA based, SAR autofocus algorithm, that can correct motion errors exceeding a range resolution cell. Most traditional autofocus algorithms operate by applying a 1D phase correction in the cross range dimension of the image spatial frequency domain [1] [3] When the motion errors exceed a range resolution cell, 1D phase only compensation is not sufficient. We will describe an algorithm that can deal with such errors by using a 2D phase correction. We compare the performance of this new algorithm with a similar minimum entropy based algorithm described by the first author in [4]
