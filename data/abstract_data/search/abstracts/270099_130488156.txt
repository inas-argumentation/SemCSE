Distribution characteristic and correlation of COD and TOC in Shenzhen Bay
The survey on chemical oxygen demand(COD) and total organic carbon(TOC) in Shenzhen Bay was separately carried out in Feb.(Winter),May(Spring),Aug.(Summer) and Nov.(Autumn) 2008.The plane and seasonal distribution characteristic of COD and TOC in Shenzhen Bay was acquired,combining the national standard methods.The correlation was analyzed between COD and TOC.The results showed that COD was decreased gradually form inside to outside of the Bay.The average contention of COD has a trend of summer spring winter autumn.TOC was also decreased gradually form inside to outside in the Bay.The average contention of TOC has a trend of autumn winter summer spring;There was a good linear correlation between COD and TOC,and the correlation coefficient was over 0.9.
