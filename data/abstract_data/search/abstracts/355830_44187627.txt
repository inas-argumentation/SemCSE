Technology mediated tutorial on RISC V CPU core implementation and sign off using revolutionary EDA management system (EMS) VSDFLOW
VSDFLOW (VLSI System Design Flow) is a 'plug and play (PnP) EDA management system, built for chip designers to implement their ideas and convert to GDSII. 'plug and play (PnP) refers to switching between any EDA tools, for e.g. user can plug Cadence Genus for synthesis, Synopsys ICC for PNR and Tempus for sign off STA. The output report will provide a QOR of entire design, which forms the starting point for design analysis. At this moment, it has been tested on medium sized designs like picorv32 a RISC V CPU core that implements RV32I instruction set, using 180nm PDK's from OSU and 45nm PDK's from Nangate. 'vsdflow' was also tested on designs like ARM Cortex M0, which is available as an evaluation IP on ARM website.
