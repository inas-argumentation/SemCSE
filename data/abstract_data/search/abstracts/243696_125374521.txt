Scalar and Vector Control of Induction Motor for Online Photovoltaic Pumping
The photovoltaic water pumping systems (PVPS) constitute a potential option to extract water in remote locations for agriculture. This technique requires an efficient control of the system components. The aim of this work is to compare the efficiency of the scalar and vector methods in controlling an asynchronous machine used for online water pumping. With a maximum power extracted from the photovoltaic panel, the two control methods are compared from the points of view of yield and the volume of the pumped water. For this, a typical situation is considered: A pumping system is composed of a 4500 W water pump driven by an asynchronous machine, which is powered by a 5000 W photovoltaic array. Using measured data of irradiation and temperature, a comparison has been carried out using a detailed simulation for a medium season under the outdoor conditions of Sfax site, Tunisia. Through the comparison of the obtained results of different parameters such as power, daily cumulative water, and the overall efficiency of each PVPS control methods, it has been proved that the vector control gives the maximum of pumped water volume.
