To be specific, we conducted an empirical study related to impulsive buying or unplanned purchases out of consumer purchase behaviors. We also empirically analyzed social interaction, positive affect, satisfaction of impulsive purchase, and intention of repurchase. The results of this study show that product stimulus and purchase promotion out of three marketing stimuli product stimulus, purchase promotion, and price stimulus have influences on buying impulse and impulsive buying through positive affect. Among the variables, social interaction has a positive influence on positive affect and buying impulse. Also, social interaction has significant effects on the relationship between impulsive buying and satisfaction; and the relationship between impulsive buying and repurchases intention. The result implies that impulsive buying using social commerce has a positive aspect and it will improve performance of companies and satisfaction of consumers by formulating marketing strategies customized in the purchase tendency of consumers.
