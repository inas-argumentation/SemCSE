Synbiotics and Infantile Acute Gastroenteritis
Acute gastroenteritis (AGE) is still a serious worldwide problem and continues to be a leading cause of morbidity, hospitalization, and mortality especially with children. The threat of mortality from diarrheal diseases is the greatest, particularly during infancy. While rehydration is important, nutrition and immune system protection are the other key factors and a number of studies have shown that use of synbiotics is a very effective method. Synbiotics, which are described as synergistic combinations of probiotics and prebiotics, are being used to treat and manage the effects of diarrhea. Synbiotics treatment not only treats diarrhea but also brings a few extra benefits since a well balanced gastrointestinal tract microbiota is crucial for immune system and metabolic functions; thus helping reduce the use of further medication together with reduced duration of diarrhea and hospitalization.
