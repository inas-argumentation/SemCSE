In this revised edition, a number of problems are set at the end of each section within a chapter, and exercises at the end of each chapter, all these being followed by relevant hints and solutions. The algebra is aimed at advanced undergraduates or graduates on Masters courses in linear algebra and linear models. This contrasts with most of the statistical concepts which may be encountered at school or undergraduate level. The statistical researchers of these days may not find it easy to link up with this model making; their reference works and needs may be very different, and may also relate to computers. Nevertheless, it is a source of standard results.
