Design and Implementation of Inventory Management System for Small and Medium sized Enterprises
The inventory management system is a typical management information system,including the development of the back end database establishment and maintenance of the front end application development aspects.The main function module of the system includes goods management,basic file,query statistics,system maintenance and help.It finishing the basic operation of goods,warehouse and supplier information,including adding and modifying and deleting.In addition,it also completed the warehousing goods monthly and annual statistics,data compression,backup and restore and usermanagement.
