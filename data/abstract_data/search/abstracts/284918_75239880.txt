Observation about the clinical effect of miconazole suppository on 100 patients with colpomycosis
Objective: To observe the therapeutic efficiency of miconazole suppository on colpomycosis. Methods: 200 patients were divided randomly into 2 groups. All of them took sodium bicarbonate solution (2% 4% to washed vulva and took the decoction Chinese medicine to fumigation vulva. The treatment group (100 cases) placed a miconazole suppository (200 mg) in vagina once daily. Contrast clinical efficacy after 7 days. Results: There were 89 cases clinical cure and 7 cases markedly in the treatment group, and the control group was 51, 12 cases, the effective rate of the treatment group was 96.0% and the control group was 63.0% the difference of the two groups was statistically significant (P0.05) The time consuming of the treatment group was (10.5+ 2.5) d and the control group was (17.0+ 2.0) d. The total therapeutic efficiency of the treatment group was much better than the control group (P0.05) Conclusion: Sodium bicarbonate solution (2% 4% to wash vulva, decoction Chinese medicine to fumigation vulva and placed a miconazole suppository (200 mg) in vagina is efficient in the treatment of colpomycosis.
