Synthesis and some transformations of 1, 2, 3, 4 tetrahydrophenothiazine derivatives
Abstract1,2,3,4 Tetrahydrophenothiazine derivatives were obtained from cyclic monoketones and 1,3 diketones and o nitroarenesulfenyl chlorides. The alkylation, aminomethylation, and oxidation of the compounds obtained were studied.
