Crisis Management Plan for the Jordanian tourism industry (CMP) Evaluation and Assessment
This study evaluates the Crisis Management Plan (CMP) for the Jordanian tourism industry, based upon opinions of managers and policy makers in the Jordanian tourism industry. The study focuses upon those outcomes of the evaluation process that provide additional insights into the details of tourism crisis management. These insights provide a foundation for elaborating on, and refining, the CMP for the Jordanian tourism industry. The study involves an overview of the methodological issues relating to the evaluation process together with its criteria. The Crisis Management Plan (CMP) for Jordanian tourism industry is also presented. Positive and negative views of managers and policy makers within the industry about this plan are also presented.
