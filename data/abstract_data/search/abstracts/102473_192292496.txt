Hermeneutic Analysis and Cultural Interpretation of Musical Works Case Study of Chopin's First Impromptu,Op.29
By an analysis of Chopin's First Impromptu acoustically and stylistically,the author interprets the music's conception,artistic value,historical meaning,and revelation for the present.This case study,with the composition research history as a starting point,is a critical review of the status quo of the research methods in this field both at home and abroad.
