The Disaster Survey of the Oxytropis glacialis Poisoning in Livestock in the Ngari of Tibet
The bionomics,growth habit,ecological distribution,disaster situation,characteristics of Oxytropis glacialis poisoning,and the control meansure in Ngari of Tibet were investigated by field survey and data collection.It was shown that the main poisoning weed in the Ngari Prefecture of Tibet was O.glacialis,and its distribution area over 2.092 5 million hm2,accounting for 10.97 of the total area.The most serious areas are Geji,Cuoqin and Gaize county,with a distribution area of 1.754 6 million hm2,accounting for 83.85% of the total distribution area of the O.glacialis.The herbs of the O.glacialis are toxin,with the highest toxin content in the flower and fruit stage.It mainly harms the equine,followed by goats and sheep,and the damage is more prominent on the lamb,but the yaks have a certain tolerance.A great damage and effect have been done to local animal husbandry.The suggestions about how to manage its damage are proposed,too.
