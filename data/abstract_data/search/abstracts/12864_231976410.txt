Bidding Strategy for FTR Auction Market Based on Deep Reinforcement Learning
This paper focuses on providing optimal bidding strategy for bidders in FTR auction market. To solve the examined problem, a novel deep reinforcement learning approach is developed through combining deep deterministic policy gradient (DDPG) algorithm with prioritized experience replay (PER) strategy. The proposed method is tested on the IEEE 5 bus system with 6 FTRs and 6 bidders. The numerical results demonstrate that DDPG PER outperforms other state of art reinforcement learning algorithms and enables to accurately perceive changes of the environment to adjust to optimal bidding strategy.
