Wheat Marketing During Pandemic COVID 19: Some Issues and Challenges
World Health Organisation announced a world health emergency named by Corona virus in January 2020. In March 2020, the virus started spreading in India. As a result of which many places in India too became hotspots of this pandemic. Precautions as a tool to fight with the corona virus, Government of India took some strict measures and declared a complete lock down in country. But the lock down came along with many problems for agriculture sector especially during the peak time of wheat harvesting. It affected harvesting as well as the marketing of the wheat. The present research paper focuses on the wheat marketing and highlights the farmers' preferences for selling their wheat under the fear of virus transmission and on other hand fetching higher price of their produce. The study is based on primary survey. Statistical tools like average, frequency and percentage have been used.
