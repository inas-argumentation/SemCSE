DYNAMIC ANALYSIS OF A MULTI LINK SUSPENSION MECHANISM WITH COMPLIANT JOINTS
The increasingly growing demand for more comfortable cars imposes a new way for dynamic analysis of the axle guiding linkages, with elaboration of models that are closer to the real mechanisms on the car. In this paper, the influence of the compliant joints on the dynamic behavior of the suspension system is approached, considering the axle guiding linkage used for an off road vehicle. A half car model is taken into consideration, which contains the suspension system of the rear axle. In the lack of the front suspension, modeling a fictive spherical joint between car body and ground ensures the equilibrium. The dynamic model has been tested in passing over bumps regime. The dynamic analysis simulation is performed by using the MBS environment ADAMS of MSC Software.
