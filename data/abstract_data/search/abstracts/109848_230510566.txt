A Low Power Multi Frequency Chopper Stabilized Readout with Time Domain Delta Sigma Modulator Suitable for Neural Recording
This paper presents a readout for multi channel neural recording. The proposed architecture is implemented with a system level frequency division multiplexing technique and a time domain delta sigma modulator. Subthreshold region operation and the time domain delta sigma modulator achieves a reduction of the current dissipation. The multiplexing technique in the frequency domain enhances the power efficiency by sharing an instrumentation amplifier and the delta sigma modulator in each channel. The proposed circuit employs a pulse width summation technique to achieve frequency division multiplexing in the time domain delta sigma modulator. The multiplexing technique of multiple frequency chopper stabilization can eliminate flicker noise and offsets. The proposed two channel readout occupies 0.067 mm2 per channel and is designed with 0.18 um CMOS technology. The spurious free dynamic range is up to 50 dB in a bandwidth of 625 Hz with a low power consumption of 520 nW per channel.
