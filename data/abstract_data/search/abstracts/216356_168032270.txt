On the Public Sector Human Resources Management in the Knowledge Economy Era
The knowledge economy is the economy that based on knowledge. Human resource is the first resource of organization in the era of knowledge economy. In the era of knowledge economy,human resources management in the public sector needs to adhere to the people oriented,knowledge management,dynamic innovation value orientation. Improving the level of human resource management of public sector in the knowledge economy era is an important topic in the field of public management. The psychological contract theory will be applied to the public sector human resources management in the knowledge economy era is conducive to improve the scientific and effective of management. Path choice of public sector human resources management innovation in the era of knowledge economy include to cultivate human resources management culture of public organization,careful recruitment and hiring,emphasize on staff training and development,perfect performance evaluation system,establish and improve the incentive system and mechanism,and design the reasonable compensation system.
