Fixed plate tooling fixture of car battery box baffle
The utility model discloses a fixed plate tooling fixture of a car battery box baffle, which comprises a fixture body (1) wherein a positioning element (2) is fixed at the upper end of the fixture body (1) and a fixed plate (3) comprises a cross beam (31) and fixed seats (32) which are respectively fixed at the two ends of the cross beam. The fixed plate tooling fixture is characterized in thatthe positioning element (2) is arranged at the outer side of the fixed plate, and clamping devices (4) which can clamp the cross beam (31) and the fixed seats (32) in the horizontal direction and limit the cross beam (31) in the vertical direction to facilitate welding are arranged at the outer side of the fixed plate; and the clamping devices (4) and the positioning element (2) are matched to limit the cross beam and the fixed seats of the fixed plate. The fixture achieves the mechanization and automation of the welding process, ensures the overall size of weldments and the manufacturing precision, improves productivity and reduces the welding technical requirements.
