Teacher Professional Development through ICT in Education:Interview with David Atchoarena,Director for Division of Teacher Development and Higher Education,UNESCO
The quality of teaching force and its continuous professional education and training play key roles in quality education.However,it is currently facing systematic challenges around the world,whether on its quantity or quality,teaching practice or teacher training,which must be changed from the view of UNESCO.To deal with these new era's challenges,UENSCO believes in that education systems and teacher education systems could be improved through a comprehensive and holistic way as well as harnessing ICT skills.Henceforth,UNESCO puts forward a lot of initiatives in recent years,such as enhancing the practicing bases for teacher education,strengthening the partnerships with stakeholders,fascinating capacity building for education policy makers,developing ICT framework for teacher's capacity,etc.To know more that how UNESCO integrates teacher professional development with ICT applying in Education,we interviewed with David Atchoarena,Director for Division of Teacher Development and Higher Education,UNESCO.This interview presents the great impact of ICT in education,which is changing the experience of educators and learners as well as the traditional school education's model,and calling for new role for new era's teachers.
