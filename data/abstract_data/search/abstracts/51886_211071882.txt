Bulk Chiral Halide Perovskite Single Crystals for Active Circular Dichroism and Circularly Polarized Luminescence.
Based on the chirality research interest of hybrid perovskite single crystals, we reported the controllable growth and chiral optical properties of bulk chiral (R) (S) and (R, S) methylbenzylaminonium (MBA) based lead (Pb) halide perovskite single crystals. And their crystal structure redeterminations and refinements, band gap calculations, nonlinear optical (NLO) properties, and photoluminescence (PL) and PL decay lifetime were systematically investigated, which clarified the previously ambiguous crystal structure problems. More importantly, (R) and (S) C6H5CH(CH3)NH3 (MBA) based hybrid perovskite single crystals with circular dichroism (CD) and circularly polarized luminescence (CPL) were studied in detail. It is concluded that (R) and (S) MBAPbBr3 single crystals not only show the notable CD signals, but also exhibit the outstanding CPL characteristics with short PL decay lifetime. It would be of great interest to conduct a comprehensive study on the chiral optical properties of these hybrid perovskite single crystals to pave the way for further studies of these chiral single crystalline materials in the optoelectronic applications.
