Electromechanical tuning of vertically coupled photonic crystal
We present the design, the fabrication and the characterization of a tunable one dimensional (1D) photonic crystal cavity (PCC) etched on two vertically coupled GaAs nanobeams. A novel fabrication method which prevents their adhesion under capillary forces is introduced. We discuss a design to increase the flexibility of the structure and we demonstrate a large reversible and controllable electromechanical wavelength tuning 15 nm) of the cavity modes.
