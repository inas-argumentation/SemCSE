Evolution of HRM to E HRM towards Organizational Effectiveness and Sustainability
This paper aims to study the evolution of HRM over the period of time and the challenging role of HRM in the 21 century. Paper will discuss about introduction of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) to HRM in terms of e HRM. In the last, paper will discuss about contribution of HRM to Organizational Effectiveness (OE) and organizational Sustainability and how e HRM can contribute towards them. Keywords e HRM, HRM, Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Organizational Effectiveness (OE) Sustainability,
