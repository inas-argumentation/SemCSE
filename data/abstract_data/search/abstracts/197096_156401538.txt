Environmental Problems and Countermeasures of Urban Drinking Water Sources in Chinese Cities
Massive urbanization and fast population growth have resulted in great pressure on urban drinking water sources.Environmental problems of current urban drinking water sources were identified in this paper.Research framework was as follows: analysis of urban drinking water sources property rights,economic causes and treatment,urban drinking water sources protection and comprehensive pollution control techniques.Based on the framework,key countermeasures were proposed,including investigation and assessment on urban drinking water sources pollution,development of a new socialist countryside in drinking water resources districts,perfection of the relevant policies,enhancement of pollution control and prevention techniques as well as innovation of environmental management mechanism.
