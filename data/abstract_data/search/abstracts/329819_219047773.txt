Fungi and paper based archives. A case report of fungal keratitis in a documentalist
Abstract A description of a case is presented on a relationship between paper based documents as a risk factor for fungal keratitis. A 32 year old woman, a long term contact lens user, presented with fungal keratitis in her right eye caused by Fusarium spp. while working with books and old documents as a librarian. Her visual acuity was hand motion in the right eye. She was satisfactorily treated with topical antifungal and antibiotic agents.
