Comprehensive Control of Spontaneous Combustion of Gas and Coal for High Gas containing Seam by Fully Mechanized Mining Technology
C 13 coal seam of Pansan mine of Huainan Mining Group Corp. has the property of low permeability and high gas containing and easy to spontaneous combustion. In order to realize safe mining by fully mechanized top coal caving technology, the comprehensive control measures, gas drainage with strike long horizontal holes in stress relief roof, gas drainage with bedding bores by using the mining stress relief characteristic, gas drainage and discharge by tail airway, pressure equalizing adjust of working face ventilation system timely, distributing air flow volume of working face reasonably, controlling air leakage in gob area and so on, had been implemented based on the affecting factors analyses of gas and coal spontaneous combustion control. By means of above measures, the gas problems in mining of fully mechanized top coal caving working face had been settled and coal spontaneous combustion in gob area had been effectively controlled. Therefore, the mining movement velocity of the working face had been evaluated obviously. The experimental results showed that gas drainage with strike long horizontal holes in stress relief roof was proved to be effective in settling the low gas drainage rate of coal seams with low permeability. The gas drainage rate was over 30% and gas drainage and discharge by tail airway was an effective assistant measure, which had some effect on coal spontaneous combustion in the gob area. The key factors were the mixed flow passing the gob area. The results might provide an important reference for mining safety to other analogical mechanized top coal caving coal seams which have high gas content and are prone to spontaneous combustion.
