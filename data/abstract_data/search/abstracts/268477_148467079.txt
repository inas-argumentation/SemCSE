Pedigree Used in the Conception of Original Eco Culture in China
The widespread use of the concept of original eco culture has constituted a fancy cultural landscape currently in China,which covers a wide variety of areas ranging from the cultural activities through the production and management of industries and businesses to the academic researches.As far as cultural objects are concerned,the original eco culture can involve the fields of music,dances,publishing,education,religion,food,clothing,beauty pageant,medical,traveling,sports,festivals,painting,photography,TV channels,mode of livelihood,civil law,food,folk cultural objects,process and so on.The widespread use of the concept,seen comprehensively from the form and content,has the very basic characteristics nature of being universal,diverse,traditional,ethnical,practical,reconstructionable,and ecological.
