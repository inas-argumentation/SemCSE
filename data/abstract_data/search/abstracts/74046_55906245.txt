THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN ANXIETY STATE SELF CONFIDENCE AND PERFORMANCE IN BASKETBALL
The main purpose of this study was to analyse the correlation between the intensity and direction of anxiety and self confidence and competitive performance in two basketball teams of different gender and competitive level (G1: 1st National Women's, G2: Provincial Men's) We administered the Competitive State Anxiety Inventory 2 (CSAI 2) with a directionality scale 30 minutes prior to every match of the season, and analysed the results of each of these, which gave us an individual performance index (IP) of each player.
