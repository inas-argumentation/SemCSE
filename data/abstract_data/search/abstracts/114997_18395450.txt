Phase Field Instabilities and Adaptive Mesh Reenement the Phase field Model
We show that the phase eld model, used for numerical simulation of solidiication, can possess unphysical stable intermediate states for certain values of the parameters. We demonstrate that these states can be seen in simple computations in one dimension; in two dimensions we use an adaptive mesh reenement algorithm to exhibit appearance of the intermediate states in realistic geometries of dendritic growth.
