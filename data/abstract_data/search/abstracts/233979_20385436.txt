Distributed parameter Luenberger observer for semi linear parabolic PDE systems with a mobile pointwise sensor
The paper addresses the problem of state estimation using the measurement output of a mobile pointwise sensor for a class of nonlinear distributed parameter systems, which are modeled by a scalar semi linear parabolic partial differential equation. A Lyapunov based co design method is proposed for simultaneous design of the guidance law of the mobile sensor and the distributed parameter Luenberger observer. Exponential stability is guaranteed for the observer error system under a linear matrix inequality (LMI) condition. Numerical simulation of a real world example shows the effectiveness of the method and the merit of the proposed design compared to a fixed sensor.
