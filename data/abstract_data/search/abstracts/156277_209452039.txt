THE EFFECTIVENESS OF YOUTUBE ENGLISH SONGS TOWARDS STUDENTS VOCABULARY COMPETENCY
Vocabulary has become a crucial aspect of learning English language. The aim of this study is to find out the effectiveness of using English songs from YouTube towards students' vocabulary competency. This study employed a quantitative research design. To fulfil this purpose, 50 Form One students were selected from a school in Negeri Sembilan. These participants were then divided into two equal groups of experimental and control. A vocabulary test was administered as a pre test for both groups prior to the treatment. There were three sessions of vocabulary lessons conducted with both groups in which the experimental group listened to English songs and the control group underwent the traditional teaching method. After the sessions, post test were given to both groups. The scores of both tests were collected and analyzed using descriptive statistics. Results revealed that the experimental group experienced improvement in their vocabulary mastery compared to the control group. Few recommendations are presented based on the research findings.
