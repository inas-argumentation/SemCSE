Enhanced thermal conductivity of epoxy composites by introducing graphene@boron nitride nanosheets hybrid nanoparticles
Polymer composites with high thermal conductivity have promising applications for the thermal management of modern electronic devices. Here, a novel strategy is proposed to enhance the thermal conductivity of epoxy composites by employing a hybrid graphene and boron nitride nanoparticle (GBN) In the GBN, boron nitride nanosheets (BNNSs) tightly adsorb onto the surface of graphene via p p interactions, and act as bridges for conveying phonons as well as barriers for electrons transporting between adjacent graphene particles. The thermal conductivity and dielectric properties of composites containing GBN are then systematically investigated.
