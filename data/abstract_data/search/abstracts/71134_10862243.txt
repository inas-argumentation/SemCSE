Synthesis and biological activity of adamantane derivatives. VIII. Adamantyl pyridinecarboxylates
Esters I VI were prepared by the reaction of nicotinyl or isonicotinyl chloride hydrochloride with the alcohol in anhydrous pyridine. The formation of esters I and IV is sterically hindered, and consequently prolonged heating was required to bring the reaction to completion. Oxidation of pyridine bases I III with hydrogen peroxide in glacial acetic acid gave the N oxides VII IX. Quaternary salts X XV resulted when esters I IV were heated with excess methyl iodide in anhydrous acetone.
