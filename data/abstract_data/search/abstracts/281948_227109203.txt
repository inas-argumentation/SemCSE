Relationship between Smad and transforming growth factor b signal transduction pathway
Members of transforming growth factor b(TGF b) superfamily play an important roles in regulating cell growth, differentiation and tissue homeostasis. Smad proteins possess special structure and property and play important roles in transforming growth factor b(TGF b) signal transduction pathway. TGF b activates the downstream Smad proteins,which initiate intracellular signaling pathways via its interaction with TGF b receptor(TGF bR) Research on the varieties,structures and function of Smad proteins,and the complicated regulating mechanism of Smad proteins in TGF b superfamily signaling transduction are very significative. Key words: Transforming growth factor b; Smad; Signal transduction
