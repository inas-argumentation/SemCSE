Time Delay of QSO 0957+561 and Cosmological Implications
We obtain the time delay between the arrival time of the A and B images of the QSO 0957+561. The results of applying two different methods (the discrete cross correlation function and the dispersion estimation technique) to the observed light curves of the A and B images are presented. The adopted value (time delay) is of DtBA 424 3 days (1 s) We have used this time delay as well as a recent measurement of the one dimensional velocity dispersion of the main lensing galaxy (sgal) to estimate H0. Two H0 H0(DtBA, sgal) relations based on different pictures of the lens galaxy, lead to H0 64+ 14 15 km s 1 Mpc 1 (2 s) (softened power law sphere) and H0 66+ 15 14 km s 1 Mpc 1 (2 s) (King profile plus a point mass at the center)
