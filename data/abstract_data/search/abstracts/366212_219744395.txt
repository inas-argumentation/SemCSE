A Study on the Effectiveness of Job Portal and Networking Sites Recruitment at Technoscience, Bangalore
Recruitment is the process of finding and attracting capable applicants of employment. The result is a pool of applicants from which new employees are selected. E recruiting, embracing the term web based recruiting can be described as any recruiting processes that a business organization conducts via web based tools, such as a firm's public Internet site or its corporate intranet. The following are the objectives of the study: To identify the job portal preferred by the employees, To analyse the recruitment strategy of Cerentral Consultants Techno Science, To analyse the candidates selected through recruitment of E mail employee referral, To analyse the remuneration package, To determine the convenience of portal usage.The resumes posted by candidates in the job portal might not be updated but mails are blasted through portals even to such candidates. In such cases the candidates do not meet the requirements of current opening.Incentives are released for employees who refer candidates for the requirement but if the selected candidate quits the company within 3 months, the incentive revoke from the employee might not refer a candidate in the future requirements. Descriptive research is used for this study. Non Probability sampling method is adopted. This study is conducted with sample size of 102 employees. Both primary andSecondary data are used.
