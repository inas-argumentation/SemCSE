Notice of RetractionShear response of a clamped beam subjected to intense dynamic loading with considering weakening effect of the shear sliding cross section
The present paper, adopting a rigid, perfectly plastic idealization material model and a square yield locus, with the consideration of two conditions of weakening and non weakening effects of transverse shear sliding section, analyses the shear response of a clamped beam in the initial plastic deformation stage subject to a uniformly distributed impulsive loading and the precondition of possible shear failure mode, writes the shear's movement equation, puts forward the expressions of shear velocity and shear sliding volume, finally the conclusion of the availability of different shear response model for weakening effect is drawn.
