Polar Codes and Their Quantum Domain Counterparts
Arikan's polar codes are capable of approaching Shannon's capacity at a low encoding and decoding complexity, while conveniently supporting rate adaptation. By virtue of these attractive features, polar codes have found their way into the 5G New Radio (NR) Hence, in this paper we provide a comprehensive survey of polar codes, highlighting the major milestones achieved in the last decade. Furthermore, we also provide tutorial insights into the operation of the polar encoder, decoders as well as their code construction methods. We also extend our discussions to quantum polar codes with an emphasis on the underlying quantum to classical isomorphism and the syndrome based quantum polar decoders.
