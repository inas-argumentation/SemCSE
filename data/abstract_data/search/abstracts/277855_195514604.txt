THE EFFECTS OF EXAMPLE PROBLEM BASED LEARNING ON KNOWLEDGE ACQUISITION, LEARNING TRANSFER, AND MENTAL EFFORT IN LEARNING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT THEORY
In the domain of Electrical Circuit Theory, the main goal of the experiment was to investigate a hypothesis that Example Problem Based Learning (EPBL) would lead to better efficiency than Teacher Centred Learning (TCL) strategy; which high test performance (specifically on knowledge acquisition test and learning transfer test) and less mental effort to complete the test activities. The participants were vocational diploma level students who were novices in completing the given tasks. As a means of data collection, 20 items multiple choice question test was applied as a knowledge acquisition test; 10 items open ended achievement test was applied as a learning transfer test, and the nine point mental effort rating scale was used to indicate how much mental effort they put in after completing the test activities. A sufficient reliability value for knowledge acquisition and learning transfer test was obtained: 0.54 and 0.74, respectively. Meanwhile, the reliability of mental effort rating scale showed a reliability coefficient (Cronbach's Alpha) of 0.95. It was hypothesised and confirmed that EPBL would lead to higher learning outcomes on the knowledge acquisition and learning transfer test than TCL approach. However, the results showed that there was not a significant main effect of instructional strategy on mean mental effort per problem invested during test phase.
