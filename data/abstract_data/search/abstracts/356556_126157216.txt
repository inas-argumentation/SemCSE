Based on the optical waveguide and the optical path superlattice quantum light source chip
A setting method of an optical waveguide superlattice of quantum based light source chip, the light path and the optical waveguide using a superlattice, an integrated electro optical modulator, the optical waveguide through the waveguide path into the beam splitter classic pumping laser beam, a beam after the laser light enters the optical superlattice region to the next frequency is obtained entangled photon pairs, and then moves entangled photon pairs interferometer quantum interference; phase of the interferometer is controlled by a built on chip electro optic modulator, the voltage regulator is obtained by several different quantum states.
