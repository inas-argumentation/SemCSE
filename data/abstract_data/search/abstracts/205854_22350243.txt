Topology of a ternary complex (proparacaine b cyclodextrin liposome) by STD NMR
The topologies of proparacaine (PPC) in b cyclodextrin (b CD) PPC in egg phosphatidylcholine (EPC) liposomes and PPC in b CD in EPC were investigated using NMR experiments (1D ROESY and saturation transfer difference (STD) This is the first description of the STD technique applied to PPC EPC b CD system, revealing that not only PPC was imbedded in EPC bilayer, but b CD was also interacting with liposome vesicles. These results are novel and were rationalized as the spontaneous formation of a ternary complex with some b CD molecules bound to external liposome vesicles surfaces.
