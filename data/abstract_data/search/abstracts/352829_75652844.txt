Progress in Research of Effective Catheter Management Preventing Unplanned Extubation of Endotracheal Intubation
Unplanned extubation of endotracheal intubation means that patients unplug endotracheal intubation themselves without extubation indication or that other factors cause intubation prolapse,which may lead to prolonging time of mechanical ventilation,hospital stay.This article reviews research of effective catheter management preventing unplanned extubation of endotracheal intubation,discuss placement of endotracheal tube,catheter fixing,airbag management,airway secretion management,timely extubation and so on,aiming at scientific catheter management to prevent unplanned extubation and provide basis for it.
