A Novel Strategy of In Situ Trimerization of Cyano Groups Between the Ti3C2Tx (MXene) Interlayers for High Energy and High Power Sodium Ion Capacitors
Highlights A novel N doped strategy of C2N3 in situ trimerization between the 2D MXene interlayers was first proposed. The ultra fast pseudocapacitive behavior of Ti3C2Tx/Na3TCM anode was managed and verified. The as fabricated sodium ion capacitor delivers excellent electrochemical performance by anode/cathode mass matching.
