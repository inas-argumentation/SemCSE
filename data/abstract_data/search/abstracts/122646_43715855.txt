Analysis and Design of Compact High Gain Microstrip Patch Antenna with Defected Ground Structure for Wireless Applications
Dual band Microstrip Line Fed low profile Microstrip Patch Antenna is proposed for wireless applications. A rotated rectangular shaped defect is embedded in the ground plane and fed with a 50 O open ended Microstrip Line. Dual band is achieved at 2.55 and 7.55 GHz. Antenna shows good radiation characteristics with peak gain of 8.1 dBi. Proposed antenna may be used for WiMAX applications with omnidirectional radiation pattern. Theoretical analysis is done for the proposed antenna by equivalent circuit model approach. Further, corners of the rectangular defect are truncated and a defected square ring is inserted inside the truncated corners rectangular defect on the ground plane. 40 of miniaturization is achieved and antenna starts to respond at 1.55 GHz with better radiation characteristics in both planes. Measured results of fabricated antennas are in good match with theoretical and simulated results.
