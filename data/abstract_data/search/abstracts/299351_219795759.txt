Breast Cancer Prediction Using Machine Learning Algorithm
Breast cancer is often the most lethal diseases with a large mortality rate especially among women. Despite the severe effect of the disease, it is possible to pinpoint the genre of breast cancer using different machine learning algorithms. However, many of these algorithms perform differently depending on their types and complexities. In our work, I have analyzed and compared the classification results of various machine learning models and find out the best model to classify between different types of breast cancers. I have used Logistic Regression, SVM, Random Forest, K neighbor classifier and Decision Tree classifiers for our comparative study.My task is to critically analysis different data and to classify them with respect to the efficancy of each algorithm in terms of accuracy, precision, recall and F1 Score.My best accuracy was 98.68% percent which was found using SVM. These techniques are coded in python and executed in Jupyter NoteBook, the Scientific Python Development Environment. Our experiments have shown that SVM is the best for predictive analysis with an accuracy of 99.48%.I infer from our study that SVM is the will suited algorithm for prediction and on the whole KNN presented Ill next to SVM.
