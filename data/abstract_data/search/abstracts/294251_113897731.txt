Research and Discussion of Walkthrough of Blasting Accident Emergency Rescue Plan
Based on the research and analysis of blasting emergency,an emergency plan and walkthrough was studied.In the study,a walkthrough of an emergency response plan through a simulated accident in blasting operation process was provided on the condition that the electric power circuit was cut by fly rock and a fire was induced to cause personal injury accident.The mode and contingency plan of blasting emergency response played an important role on blasting operation.
