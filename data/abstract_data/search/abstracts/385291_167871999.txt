Incorporating the Cost Management System in Halal Standard MS 1500:2009 Halal Food in Malaysia
Halal does not only relate to foods that are permissible for Muslims but it also hasother dimensions within Islam itself.With the rapid increase in demand, the halal industry is no longer merely an industry that complies with religious requirements but is also becoming an economic force in its own right,both domestically and globally. Currently, the MS 1500:2009 focuses on processing, but not on cost management. Therefore, themain purpose of this paper is to seek an opportunity of incorporating cost management system into halal processing. By reconciling the halal process and the cost management system, the current guideline on halalcan be enhanced. The directionis not only towards halal processing, but also towards cost effectiveness.These will encourage more food producers to engage with halal processes as a guideline to enhance their product as well as their company performance.
