Pressureless sintering of nanosilver paste at low temperature to join large area =100mm2) power chips for electronic packaging
Abstract We achieve robust bonding of large area power chip =100 mm 2 based on pressureless sintering of silver particles at low temperature, i.e. 250 degC. The particle size of silver ranges from 0.02 to 2 um and most organics can be evaporated rapidly from 70 degC to 180 degC. It is not necessary for metallic bond to be formed entirely by silver nanoparticles. Instead, silver microparticles also can rapidly grow up and aggregated together by atom diffusion from the silver nanoparticles. This point of view leads to a new way to bond the large area chips without additional pressure. The sintered silver joint shows high shear strength, low porosity, high thermal conductivity, and low void ratio.
