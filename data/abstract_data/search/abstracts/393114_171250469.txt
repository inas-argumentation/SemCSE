Liao Zhai Zhi Yi and Si Shu
As the traditional canons,Si Shu(Four Canons),including Da Xue,Zhong Yong,Lun Yu,Meng Zi,are the textbooks for the intellectuals during the feudal times. They are more important than Wu Jing(Five Classics),because from theme to content, the stereotyped writing prepared for the imperial examination is tightly connected with the Si Shu. Pu Songling spent more than half time of his whole life to prepare and attend the provincial examinations,hence he must know quite well the Four Canons. Liao Zhai Zhi Yi was influenced by Si Shu,on purpose or unconsciously,which is evident in words,phrases and stories,and we also find how deep and wide this influence was. Aware of this point,we could explore much better the writing skills in this collection of short novels,and collate better this book and correct the misunderstandings in today's comments.
