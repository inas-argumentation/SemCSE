Fast convergence hybrid functional link artificial neural network for active noise control with a mixture of tonal and chaotic noise
The noise generated by some rotating machines usually presents a mixture of tonal and chaotic signals. The hybrid functional link artificial neural network (HFLANN) consisting of sinusoidal noise canceller (SNC) narrowband ANC (NANC) and FLANN subsystems was a great success for this kind of noise. However, it has no ability to overcome nonlinear acoustic path and frequency mismatch, and its convergence rate is sub optimal. To address the problems of the nonlinear acoustic path and frequency mismatch, an improved HFLANN (IHFLANN) algorithm combining narrowband frequency acquisition and error separation techniques is proposed in this work. The NANC with error separation and adjoint structure (NANC E AS) is also introduced to simplify the computational complexity of IHFLANN without sacrificing its performance. Considering the independent NANC converges fast for tonal noise in previous work. We also propose a fast convergence IHFLANN (FIHLANN) algorithm based on the convex combination of NANC E AS and IHFLANN, which accelerates the convergence rate of IHFLANN without reducing noise attenuation result. A large number of simulations have proved the effectiveness of the proposed algorithms.
