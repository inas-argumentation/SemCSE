Impact of material and technology of powder liners on performance of shaped charges
In order to study the effect of the material ratio,wall thickness difference of liner in radial direction,and mass distribution of liner on penetration performance of shaped charges,the orthogonal experimental plan was designed with penetration depth as an indicator,and the optimum program was obtained for actual production. The results show the material ratio,wall thickness difference of liner in radial direction,and mass distribution of liner in axial direction play important roles in penetration,in which,the material ratio is the most primary factor,playing a decisive role in the penetration.Keywords shaped chargepowder linermaterial ratiowall thickness tolerancemass distribution
