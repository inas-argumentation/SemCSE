Sense measurement System Design for Mobile Robot Using Ultrasonic and Infrared Sensors
This paper puts forward a design of sense measurement for mobile robot with ultrasonic and infrared sensors.The non detection zone of ultrasonic sensor is compensated by infrared sensor,and the measurement range of mobile robot is enlarged.The software and hardware platforms of sense measurement system are designed.Theoretical and experimental studies of distance measurement are completed.The sense measurement system designed in this paper is of high efficiency,an explicit structure,high precision,better maintainability,repeatability,high reliability and wide application.
