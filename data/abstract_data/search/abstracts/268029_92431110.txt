Synthesis and properties of a Fe2O3 nanorods
We report synthesis of a Fe2O3 (hematite) nanorods by reverse micelles method using cetyltrimethyl ammonium bromide (CTAB) as surfactant and calcined at 300 degC. The calcined a Fe2O3 nanorods were characterized by X ray diffraction (XRD) high resolution scanning electron microscopy (HRSEM) transmission electron microscopy (TEM) energy dispersive spectrometer (EDS) fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) and vibrating sample magnetometer (VSM) The result showed that the a Fe2O3 nanorods were hexagonal structure. The nanorods have diameter of 30 50 nm and length of 120 150 nm. The weak ferromagnetic behavior was observed with saturation magnetization 0.6 emu/g, coercive force 25 Oe and remanant magnetization 0.03 emu/g. (c) 2010 WILEY VCH Verlag GmbH Co. KGaA, Weinheim)
