Mental health curriculum for Pakistan: integrating virtual, classroom and onsite training
'Pharmaceutical Scientific Research Training' for first year undergraduate students of pharmaceutical science was carefully designed by teachers from different pharmaceutical disciplines. Students' cooperative group learning combined with teachers' professional guidance (PBL LBL) was implemented, together with basic operational training in pharmaceutical experiments (inside school) and site visits to pharmaceutical practice institutions (outside school) Each group of four or five students was assigned investigation activities concerning drug discovery and research related issues. During the years 2014 2017, respectively, in the spring and summer semesters, 74, 79, 97 and 139 first year undergraduate students were enrolled in this course. Each group of students gave a keynote presentation and watched 15 31 speeches presented by other groups. The effectiveness of the course was evaluated by students in their course summaries at the end of each term, and any shortcomings of the course pointed out by students were improved in the next year. What lessons were learned? More than 82% of the students who took part in the course in 2014 strongly believed that the new practice curriculum provided meaningful and applicable learning, which was very helpful for their follow up study and extracurricular scientific research. The laboratory training and site visits inside and outside school provided opportunities to better understand pharmaceutical science, and group learning and presentations helped students develop the professional attributes and cultivate the team spirit they will need for the future. By 2017, the percentage of students reporting 'great satisfaction' was up to 96% and the module was considered by the majority of students to be the most distinctive and educational course they had ever taken. Of the first batch of students on this course, who graduated in 2017, 69% went on to postgraduate studies (24% of them went abroad) a trend in line with the talent cultivation objectives of Zhejiang University. The main weaknesses of this course concern the unequal participation of individual students and the lack of interaction between teachers and students in some LBL aspects. More attention to individuals and teaching ability is needed. As an early professional practice course for first year students, Pharmaceutical Scientific Research Training provides a positive educational environment in which students and teachers can engage together and which enhances their learning and teaching.
