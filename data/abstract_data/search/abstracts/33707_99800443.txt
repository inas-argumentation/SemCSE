Design Strategy for Ag(I) Based Thermally Activated Delayed Fluorescence Reaching an Efficiency Breakthrough
A design strategy for the development of Ag(I) based materials for thermally activated delayed fluorescence (TADF) is presented. Although Ag(I) complexes usually do not show TADF, the designed material, Ag(dbp)(P2 nCB) [dbp 2,9 di n butyl 1,10 phenanthroline, and P2 nCB nido carborane bis(diphenylphosphine) shows a TADF efficiency breakthrough exhibiting an emission decay time of t(TADF) 1.4 ms at a quantum yield of PhPL 100% This is a consequence of three optimized parameters.
