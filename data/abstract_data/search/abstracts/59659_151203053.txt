An Analysis of Students" Technological Creativity influenced by the Social Environment in a Technology Competition
According to Amabile's componential model of creativity, it points out four key elements that influence people's creativity: domain relevant skills, creativity relevant processes, task motivation, and social environment. This study was based on her theory to develop a teaching module and a technology competition. Through the processes of this teaching module, researchers were able to observe students' technological creativity that effected by the social environment component. The following are outcomes that accomplished in this study: 1. The technology competition teaching module has developed and implemented in a junior high school. 246 students involved in this 8 week teaching activity and then participated in the technology competition. 2. The technological creativity scale has developed via the modified Delphi technique. Nine items and their rating points were determined for evaluating students' technological creativity. 3. Two hundred and forty six students formed 82 teams and participated in the technology competition. The scores showed students in the competition were hard to think diversely and flexibly. 4. Seventy six valid questionnaires were returned from 82 teams. The results indicated some social environment factors, such as workgroup supports, pressure, and teacher and parents' encouragement, affected students' technological creativity.
