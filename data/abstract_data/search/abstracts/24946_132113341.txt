Wuhan International Horse Racing Festival to Raise the Overall Influence of Wuhan City
With the continuous development of Chinese economy, to promote the steps of city development, to expand the city's image and to enlarge urban influence force, holding an influential event has become an important force to promote Wuhan urban development. Based on sports such as economics, information economics and the horse racing industry economics as the theoretical basis, by the method of documentation, qualitative analysis and logical analysis, discussion and research on Wuhan racing festival to raise the overall strength of Wuhan city. Keywords International horse racing festival; Wuhan; Urban comprehensive influence
