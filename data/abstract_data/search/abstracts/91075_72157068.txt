Ross C. Brownson, Elizabeth A. Baker, Terry L. Leet, Kathleen N. Gillespie and W. True. Evidence based Public Health.
Ross C. Brownson, Elizabeth A. Baker, Terry L. Leet, Kathleen N. Gillespie, and W. True. Evidence based Public Health. 2nd edn. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2011. ISBN: 978 0195397895, 312pp, PS35.00 (hardback) The book is written for students and practitioners of public health. It is a valuable contribution to the literature in this field since it covers not only preparation, design and evaluations of interventions but also methods for collection and analysis of scientific evidence as well as chapters on community assessments. Thus, the book connects theory with practice. The book is written by US public health academics and is primary intended for a North American audience.
