A Speech Enhancement System Based on Real time Sound Source Localization and Super directional Fixed Beamforming
A speech enhancement system which combines real time sound source localization and super directional fixed beamforming is proposed in this paper. First, the source localization based on frequency division and tracking matching is used to summarize the localization results of different frequency bands to improve the robustness of localization. Second, because the designed filter for beamforming mainly considers the interference noise and spatial white noise, its output power of the interference and noise is minimized in condition of keeping the useful signal from distortion. Due to the fact that the suppression degree of white noise is opposite to that of interference, this paper proposes a balance factor to suppress the directional interference noise. Experiment shows that our method is better compared to the traditional delay and sum beamformer. Furthermore, it also can adjust width of the main lobe by controlling the angle range of directional noise which needs to be suppressed, and has the potential of real time application because of its fast computation speed.
