Placental abruption: Management UpToDate
INITIAL APPROACH FOR ALL PATIENTS Pregnant women with symptoms of abruption should be evaluated promptly on a labor and delivery unit to establish the diagnosis, assess maternal and fetal status, and initiate appropriate management. Patients who have an apparently small abruption and are initially stable may deteriorate rapidly if placental separation progresses. They may also deteriorate from sequelae of potential comorbidities, such as preeclampsia, cocaine use, or trauma.
