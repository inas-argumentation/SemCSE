Performance Analysis of Symmetric Uncertainty based Optimal Attribute Generation Algorithm for Multiclass ECG in Fuzzy Rough Domain
Optimal attribute selection is one of the crucial phases that every researcher working in the domain of machine learning and pattern recognition faces. Electrocardiogram, being an important indicator of heart condition, has been studied widely for decades, to achieve appropriate characteristic points for accurate classification. The data collected by the recording devices is massive and reduction of the collected data through optimal attribute selection becomes inevitable. Wavelet Packet denoising of the signal has been done which could provide accurate localization of fiducial points. This procedure has been used for development of a huge 10 class ECG database which has been employed for experimental purpose. We propose a symmetric uncertainty based optimal attribute reduction in fuzzy rough domain to have a speedy and accurate generation of reducts. The efficiency and the effectiveness of the proposed work is validated through the empirical results.
