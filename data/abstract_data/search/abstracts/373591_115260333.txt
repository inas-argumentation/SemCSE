Wireless communication method and apparatus with improved between vehicle and ground communication system
The present invention, aircraft aboard, other flight to vehicles, vessels, or while using a vehicle interior of a wireless network cell, such as trains, mobile using satellite communications between the vehicle interior of the cell and the ground communication system the present invention relates to communication systems. According to the present invention, the emulator functions are provided at both ends of the satellite communication link. The emulator, without involving the connection between the base station and the base station controller has the capability of providing a status signaling required, therefore, if not needed for the call or other information transfer, the satellite communication channels can be switched off. The present invention also provides the ability to use a different satellite connections according to the vehicle location.
