Cache Hierarchy Analysis and Performance A study of efficient cache configurations using STT RAM eDRAM 2011 2016
The objective of this paper is to discuss the fundamentals of caches and different types of cache structures. Caches are a fundamental part of the memory system as they help bridge the gap between the processor and Main Memory. Due to this reason a lot of research is being conducted around cache hierarchies and optimizing them for low energy consumption and low latencies. This paper explores different device technologies for caches, such as SRAM, STT RAM, and eDRAM. It explores and compares various metrics such as cache capacity, latency, energy and power consumption, and cache hierarchy. Trends explored in this paper show that on average STT RAM has higher energy consumption and lower read latencies compared to SRAM. In addition, this work shows that multi core processing is becoming more common, and for this reason STT RAM and eDRAM are coming to the forefront as substitutes for SRAM due to their high density and low leakage power. This work discusses some of the efficient cache configurations that have been proposed recently. For example, a design was proposed that consisted of a multi level STT RAM cache hierarchy, and this design resulted in a considerable amount of reduction in energy consumption[8] Another design was proposed in which eDRAM was used for the LLC cache. This design resulted in a 36% reduction in energy consumption over a SRAM LLC cache[9] Keywords SRAM,STT RAM, eDRAM, volatile, latency, energy,power, cache hierarchy, LLC, hit ratio, miss ratio, directmapped, fully associative, set associative
