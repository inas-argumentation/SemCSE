Human gait recognition using four directional variations of gradient gait energy image
In this paper, a robust feature level fusion of four directional vector's (i.e. horizontal, vertical, forward and backward diagonal) based approach is proposed. For each image sequence, GEI (gait energy image) is firstly constructed. Next, GGEI (gradient gait energy image) is obtained based on neighborhood gradient computation. Then, variations in all four directions are used as discriminative gait features. Finally, SVM (support vector machine) classifier is effectively incorporated into this work for the classification procedures. The proposed method is tested on considerably largest multi view dataset CASIA B (Chinese Academy of Sciences) and promising results were obtained. The prominent gait pragmatic conditions such as multi view and different walking speed conditions are effectively addressed in this work by considering whole, even, odd and multiple's of three numbering frames from each individual sequence.
