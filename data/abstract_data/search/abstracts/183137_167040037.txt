Decomposition of China's Export Growth in Trade with Korea
This paper analyses the factors affecting China's export penetration into Korean market. We use highly disaggregated Korean trade data between 1992 and 2008. Even if China exports a wide variety of products, the growth in China's exports is mainly driven by the intensive margin rather than the extensive margin. Moreover, the growth in the intensive margin is explained by an increase in exported quantities without a significant fall in the relative price of Chinese varieties. An increase in the relative quality of Chinese products is the driving force behind China's export performance. We present evidence regarding China's export prices and the relative quality of Chinese products, and conclude that improvement in the quality of Chinese product is an important factor of Chinese export growth. Keywords China's export growth, Korea, Intensive margin, Extensive margin, Variety, Price, Quality I.
