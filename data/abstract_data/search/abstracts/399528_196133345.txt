Slider tablet computer
The invention discloses a slider tablet computer. The slider tablet computer comprises a base and a movable upper cover. The base comprises a mother board, a first pogo pin, a second pogo pin, a radio frequency module and a plurality of metal traces. The radio frequency module is arranged on the mother board and used for transmitting or receiving a radio frequency signal. The metal traces are arranged on the mother board, and the first pogo pin and the second pogo pin are coupled to the radio frequency module respectively through the metal traces. The movable upper cover comprises at least one first antenna, and the first antenna is selectively coupled to the first pogo pin or the second pogo pin according to the operation status of the slider tablet computer. The production cost of the slider tablet computer can be substantially reduced, and a cable of the coupled antenna can not be damaged easily.
