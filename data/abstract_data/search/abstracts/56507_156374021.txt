Relationship between CSR Initiatives and Financial performance of Axis Bank in India Variance analysis
The present paper is to find the influence level of financial variables on the CSR initiatives (Business Responsibility Report) of the Axis Bank limited in India. The study is based on content analysis, collected from Annual report. Edward Altman's "Z" score be treated as modern financial performance and calculated for the same period from 2010 11 to 2014 15. CSR Cost variance, CSR Budget variance and CSR Volume variance were calculated through standard costing technique. The standard cost process is mostly used to control the operating task. All the parameters have been analyzed with one sample t test, Karl Pearson's correlation for its validity. The coefficient of determination has also been tested through linear regression analysis and the author found that the relationship between actual CSR initiative and the ancient financial variables (EPS, Book Value, Return on Asset and Operating Profit) were positively correlated and also influencing to the maximum (above 90% level extent, but the modern financial variable of Altman Z score not influencing the actual CSR contribution of Axis Bank limited in India during the study period.
