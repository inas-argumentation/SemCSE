MANTIKUT TAYR'IN MANZUM CEVIRILERI UZERINE BIR KARSILASTIRMA
This paper mainly focuses on the poetic translations of "Mantiku't Tayr" of Feriduddin Attar (1119 1193) the well known Persian mystic and poet, within the context of the language and narrative of the works. A stylistic analysis is aimed through a proper study of these works in terms of style. The following are dealt with within the scope of this comparison: the faithfulness to the original meaning and the extent to which the ideas in the original work are reflected in the translation; consistency of the discourse in the translations in contextual terms (siyak u sibak, context) use of the words, idioms and various patterns of words chosen to define certain concepts; the ways in which Persian syntax is manifested in the Turkish text; transfer of Persian idioms into Turkish via translation; the ways in which the translator poets, in terms of their artistic personalities, employ the means of language and its facilities of narration in a distinctive and subjective way in order to add a particular aspect to their discourse. Furthermore, in this paper, the understanding of translation of the old Turkish literature and Tezkireci Asik Celebi is examined.
