Design of Advanced Water Treatment Engineering of Meilin Waterworks
Objectives and process selection of the advanced water treatment engineering of Shenzhen Meilin Waterworks are introduced.The plan layout and features of process design are described.The operational results show that the combined advanced treatment process of ozone pretreatment,ozone oxidation post treatment and biological activated carbon filtration is reasonable and feasible for Shenzhen Reservoir which is slightly polluted and characterized by high algae and low turbidity.
