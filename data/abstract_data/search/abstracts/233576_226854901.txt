Mixed Integer Linear Programming Time Series Based Redispatch Optimization
The electric energy supply system is in a steady transformation process. During this process, operational concepts of transmission system operators are influenced as well. One of these concepts is redispatch, which is affected by the increasing installed power of renewable energy sources and the operation of backup power plants. Thus, the importance of considering the flexibility of conventional power plants when optimizing redispatch measures is permanently increasing. This paper introduces a time series based redispatch optimization that considers important dynamic parameters of conventional power plants. The influence of the dynamic parameters on the redispatch result is shown based on a test scenario. Moreover, the scalability of the proposed model is evaluated by a sensitivity analysis. The model can be solved for optimization problems of a scale that is typical for redispatch problems in Germany in around 25 min and thus can be applied for further analysis.
