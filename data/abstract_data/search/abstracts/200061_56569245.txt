Thoughts on Integrating Innovation and Entrepreneurship Education into the Whole Process of Talent Training
The employment prospects of graduates in China instill little optimism so far, and the employment pressure increases year by year, which undoubtedly brings a lot of pressure to the country, universities and individual students. In such a situation, in order to find a way out, it is necessary to fuse innovation and entrepreneurship education into the whole process of talent training. Only in this way can the purpose of innovation and entrepreneurship education of higher educational institutions to change students' employment ideas be achieved in a better way. At the same time, students' innovation and entrepreneurial ability can be cultivated and improved. Thus, not only students' comprehensive ability and quality are improved, but also the employment pressure is relieved to some extent by encouraging entrepreneurial actions, which is of great practical significance.
