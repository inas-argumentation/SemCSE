First principles study of hydrogen storage on Ti (Sc) decorated boron carbon nitride sheet
Abstract Ab initio first principles calculations are carried out to investigate Ti (Sc) decorated two dimensional boron carbon nitride (BC 2 N) sheets for their application as hydrogen storage materials. The results show that with four H 2 molecules attached to per metal atom the Ti (Sc) decorated BC 2 N can store up to 7.6 wt% (7.8 wt% of hydrogen in molecular form. The Kubas interaction and the polarization mechanism lead to the adsorption energy within the range of 0.4 0.56 eV/H 2 (0.13 0.27 eV/H 2 which is suitable for ambient temperature hydrogen storage.
