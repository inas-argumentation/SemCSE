Key population and health indicators of NFHS 3 2005 006.
IN the early nineties India initiated National Family Health Survey (NFHS) project. Since then NFHS has emerged as a nationally important source of data on population maternal and child health and nutrition. Due to the extraordinary efforts made to maintain and enhance the quality validity and transparency of the National Family Health Surveys they have been called the gold standard for surveys in India. The extensive use of NFHS 2 results as evidence base in drafting Indias Tenth Five Year Plan and a large number of research studies based on NFHS 1 and NFHS 2 data affirms the quality assurance of NFHS results. In 2005 2006 India implemented the third round of National Family Health Survey (NFHS 3) The present paper provides the preliminary findings of key indicators of population and health from NFHS 3.
