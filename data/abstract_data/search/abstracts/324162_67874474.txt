Cyclic Block Construction for SOQPSK TG With Frequency Domain Equalization
We consider the problem of cyclic block construction for single carrier frequency domain equalization (FDE) of SOQPSK TG. For FDE of SOQPSK TG, a cyclic prefix signal is required to counter ISI in each data block, and an additional intrafix segment is also needed to guarantee circular convolution and continuous phase. Based on the cyclic block construction methods for continuous phase modulation (CPM) with FDE, we propose the methods to compute the intrafix symbols for SOQPSK TG with a precoder. For SOQPSK TG with 8 symbols constraint of the frequency shaping pulse, only 2 intrafix symbols can make the data block satisfying circular convolution and maintain continuous phase in inter block and intra block. Then oversampling signal model and Laurent's PAM decomposition are applied to FDE of SOQPSK TG. Simulation results show that the performance is almost similar using three intrafix symbol construction methods based on oversampling signal model; and based on the PAM decomposition model, the performance loss of the method using only 2 infrafix symbols is within 0.2dB compared with the other two schemes.
