Design of Enclosed Clamp for Long Rod Suspension Ceramic Insulator in 500 kV DC Transmission Line
The long rod suspension ceramic insulator has been widely used in 500kV DC transmission line, this kind of insulator will become zero or low resistance under the outer circumstance after long time operation, so in order to guarantee the reliability of power supply, it is necessary to change porcelain insulator with live working. The author describes a kind of insulator clamp used in live working of 500 kV DC transmission line, this insulator clamp is to change a single of any long rod suspension ceramic insulator located in the middle of tension string, moreover, it is of light weight, high strength, as well as easy to carry, so it is very important to live overhaul of tension string which is mainly composed of rod porcelain insulators.
