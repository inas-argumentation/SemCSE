Investigation of risk factors for postpartum hemorrhage in different times of the amount of bleeding
Objective To investigate the risk factors of amount of bleeding for postpartum hemorrhage in different time. Methods The clinical data of the 687 cases of puerpera was analyzed retrospectively. The amount of bleeding in different time was counted and the risk factors were analyzed and compared. Results The amount of the bleeding from 1 day to 1 week was highest, which was significantly higher than amount of bleeding within 24 h and more than one week; Pregnancy with hypertension, uterine scar, anemia, great children and amniotic fluid volume abnormalities were the risk factors for postpartum hemorrhage. Conclusion It is important for the prevention and treatment of postpartum hemorrhage that investigating the risk factors of the amount of bleeding for postpartum hemorrhage in different time.
