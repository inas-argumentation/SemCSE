Globalization through FDI and Performance Evaluation of MSMEs in India: Finding Evidences from Policies, Linkages Performances of MSMEs
Across the world MSMEs are regarded as an engine of economic growth. MSME sector has emerged as a highly vibrant and dynamic sector of Indian economy. Not only in India, but also in other developing countries it helps in bringing economic and socio political development by industrializing rural and backward areas and hence reduces a regional imbalance which assures more equitable distribution of wealth and income. This sector is very much efficient in providing large employment at lower capital cost. It contributes around 69% of total employment, 45% to export, 45% to GDP (only manufacturing sector) which is very huge in figure. Therefore, it is very crucial to promote and enhance the activities of MSMEs in India and work positively on issues which challenge the growth and development of the same. Our present paper is focusing on such issues only. In current situation, FDI is coming in huge quantities which have both positive and negative impact on our economy. But our study is to trace to what extent FDI is affecting the working of MSMEs in India.
