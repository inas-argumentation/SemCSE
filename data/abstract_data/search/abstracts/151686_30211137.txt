Enhanced Sparse Representation Based Device Free Localization with Radio Tomography Networks
The sparse distribution of targets in monitored areas is an important prior for device free localization (DFL) with radio tomography networks. In this article, our goal is to develop an enhanced sparse representation based DFL method that takes the full potential of sparsity for location reconstruction. An expanded sensing matrix spanning the concatenation of a sampling matrix and a unit error correcting base is proposed for modelling the measurement process. The sampling matrix can either be composed of the ellipse model from calibrated networks or the received signal strength (RSS) fingerprint based model induced by training samples with one person at predefined locations. Thus, the sparsity of targets is enhanced under the expanded sensing matrix and the l 1 minimization based approximations are derived for the recovery of locations. Experimental studies in an open outdoor scenario, in a line of sight (LOS) indoor scenario, and in a non line of sight (NLOS) indoor scenario, are conducted to verify the efficacy of the proposed method.
