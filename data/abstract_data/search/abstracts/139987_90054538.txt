Konjak donkey meat sauce and preparation method thereof
The invention discloses a konjak donkey meat sauce and a preparation method thereof. The konjak donkey meat sauce is prepared from the following raw materials by weight part: 10 15 of konjak powder, 30 50 of donkey meat, 15 20 of sweet potatoes, 5 10 of red beans, 8 15 of chicken broth, 5 15 of rose paprica, 4 9 of dried Tremellodon gelatinosum, 2 3 of Vitex negundo leaf powder, 2 3 of rehmannia flowers, 2 3 of Polygonatum, 1 2 of astragalus, 2 3 of Gynostemma pentaphyllum, 1 2 of Thamnolia vermicularis, 1 2 of Agastache rugosa powder, 1 2 of apple flowers, 2 3 of orange peel, 3 8 of refined salt, 4 9 of soy sauce, 9 14 of yellow wine, 5 10 of peanut oil, and a proper amount of water. The konjak donkey meat sauce provided by the invention is salty, delicious and spicy, has rich and balanced nutrients, and is convenient to eat. The rehmannia flowers added in the formula have the efficacy of treating diabetes, kidney asthenia and lumbago, etc. The apple flowers and orange peel can tonify spleen and warm stomach, regulate qi flow and nourish blood.
