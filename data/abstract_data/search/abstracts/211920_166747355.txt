Observation and Reflection on an English Lesson The Great Famine in Ireland from 2013 Encyclopedia Britannica
This paper is an observation and reflection of an English lesson given by Professor Zou Weicheng from East China Normal University. In the first part, it presents the lesson plan according to the activities and performances in the 90 minutes' classroom observation. Then, it depicts detailed teaching and learning activities of the lesson and makes comments on them. Finally, a reflection and conclusion of the teacher's performance and the whole lesson are provided.
