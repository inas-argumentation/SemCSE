Prediction Potential Drug Target of RGD Tripeptide and its Binding Modes Simulation
Chemogenomic technology,which plays an important role in both drug target elucidation and illumination of the function of small molecular compounds on biological pathways,not only can be use to provide theoretical approaches for the new drug discovery and the new application of old drug,but also reduce the high cost in the drug discovery.Tripeptide Arg Gly Asp(RGD) was proved to be a character sequence specially connecting to extracellular matrix receptors,so it played a significant role in physiology.In this paper the potential drug target of RGD was searched with some of the methods of chemogenomics,such as reverse docking and data mining from the reverse matching of pharmacophore,and its binding modes were analyzed with the computer simulation technology.The result of reverse docking showed that the key drug targets and their relative diseases matched the experiment reports of the pharmacological activity of RGD,including anticoagulant,antitumor and relating to renal and cardiovascular functions.RGD was also found to be a potent inhibitor of neuraminidase.
