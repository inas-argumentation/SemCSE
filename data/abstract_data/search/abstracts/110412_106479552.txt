ISA 95 Setting the Stage for Integration of MES and ICD Systems
This presentation describes the reasons for, and history of, the emergence of ISA 95, including how it relates to other enterprise integration standards such as ISA 88, ISA 99, and various ISO, IEC, and CEN standards. It sets the stage for the wide application of ISA 95 and describes additional standards which have evolved around it. Finally, it discusses how ISA 95 may be integrated with traditional project engineering and systems development methodologies, to provide an overall master planning approach for ICD (industrial control domain) and MES (manufacturing execution systems)
