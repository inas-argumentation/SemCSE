A Low Profile Printed Quadrifilar Helical Antenna for Handheld UHF RFID Reader
This paper proposes a compact printed quadrifilar helix antenna (PQHA) that has quarter wavelength inverted F resonators at a low cost. The proposed PQHA is fed by a four port quadrature phase system which consists of a 180degpower splitter and two couplers implemented by lumped element circuit. The fabricated and miniaturized PQHA measures only.The measured impedance bandwidth is about 21% and the 3 dB axial ratio bandwidth range from 800 1000MHz can cover the whole UHF RFID bands. The measured maximum gain is around 3.6dBic and the 3 dB beam width is about 120degat 920 MHz, which small size and good performance is enough for practical handheld RFID applications.
