Assessing inter organizational innovation performance through relational governance and dynamic capabilities in supply chains
Purpose This paper aims to examine the factors influencing innovation performance and implementation in inter organizational relationships. Design/methodology/approach A novel research model comprises five research hypotheses with four constructs, including information technology infrastructure flexibility, institutional orientation, dynamic capabilities and innovation performance. The hypotheses are tested on data collected from 260 of the top 1,000 Taiwanese manufacturing firms in 2011 listed by Business Weekly in Taiwan, using structural equation modeling. Findings The study provides insight into how supply chain members should reinforce their dynamic capabilities and relational and institutional view of relational governance so as to improve their value based relationships and in turn enhance innovation performance.
