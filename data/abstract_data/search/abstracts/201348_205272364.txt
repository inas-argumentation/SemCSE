FDA guidelines issued in January permit sales reps to distribute medical literature for educational purposes. This information can describe new or off label uses of approved products, without the need for prior review by the agency. Nonetheless, even within that permissible context, companies continue to look for ways to push the marketing envelope. For example, on October 2, Allergan, in Irvine, California, filed a lawsuit against the FDA seeking to challenge off label regulations. The blockbuster agreement includes a $1.195 billion criminal fine the largest ever imposed in the United States for any matter for Pfizer's fraudulent promotion of Bextra.
