Study of leaching mechanism of heavy metals in cement mortar
The clinker prepared by raw meal mixed with chemical reagents of heavy metals was burnt in laboratary condition,and the flow way of heavy metal elements in cement during its application was studied to see the effect of hazard waste materials used in cement kiln.Under different pH conditions,the long term leaching behaviors of Pb,Cd,As and Cr were tested by simulation of the water bath test method of Germany.The effect of acidic condition on the heavy metal leaching was investigated.The results indicated that Pb,Cd and Cr3 could be solidified by mortar steady and the long term immobilized ratios were over 99.9%.The acidic condition could inhibit the extraction of Cr3+ and accelerate the extraction of Cr6+,but little effect on that of Pb,Cd and As.
