REVIEW ON QUALITY CONTROL OF HERBAL MEDICINES BY SOPHISTICATED ANALYTICAL TECHNIQUE: RECENT ADVANCEMENT
Herbal medicines have been explored exhaustively recently and it has been observed that utilization of analytical methods has become essential to ensure quality of herbal products. The standardization of herbal product is an important issue as per global acceptance concern. The herbal industries has expensed use amount for this purpose. There are various methods available for the standardization of herbal products including modern analytical techniques and recently many researchers put their efforts to ensure quality of herbal products using these methods; this article summarizes some of them.
