Ant Colony Clustering Algorithm for Personalized Recommendation of E Commerce
How to use the data mining and analysis algorithm to carry out e commerce recommendation has become one of the important contents of the current information technology development. Therefore, in this paper, research on ant colony clustering algorithm for personalized recommendation of e commerce was proposed. The domestic and foreign research on e commerce recommendation was simply summarized. Through the classical clustering algorithm in ant colony algorithm, an e commerce recommendation algorithm was designed. Then the experimental results of the proposed algorithm prove that the algorithm is a very effective e commerce recommendation algorithm, but also better than other e commerce recommendation algorithms.
