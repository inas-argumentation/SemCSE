Pneumatic Control System of Pole Piece Inspection Based on FluidSIM P
The pneumatic control system of automatic feeding mechanism for pole piece inspection was developed based on pneumatic control and simulation technology.According to the step type feeding principle of on line pole piece inspection,the pneumatic circuit of automatic feeding mechanism was developed and optimized using the FluidSIM P software and the PLC control system designed,finally the reliable control of automatic feeding mechanism was achieved.The equipment has been put into operation with sound effect achieved in the automatic inspection of pole piece.
