A Comparison of Embryo Culture Incubators for the IVF Laboratory
One of the most critical pieces of equipment within the IVF laboratory is the culture incubators. These lab workhorses are where gametes/embryos spend the majority of their time and are responsible for reducing environmental stress via provision of stable and appropriate environment. Incubators regulate temperature, carbon dioxide levels/pH, oxygen concentration, and humidity/evaporation/media osmolality; all which impact embryo development. Several models of IVF specific incubators now exist for use within the laboratory, and selection must consider various factors. Examination of key variables, such as temperature regulation method, type of gas sensors, form of gas/atmosphere filtration, as well as other adjuncts, is crucial. Other factors, logistical and technical, must be considered in the selection process. Perhaps most importantly, proper incubator management, giving consideration to number of patients and laboratory workflow, is paramount in optimizing function, regardless of the technology incorporated. This review highlights incubator function, reviewing key environmental variables controlled and the technology utilized in various available units. Additionally, existing comparative studies focused on embryo culture incubators are critically analyzed. Finally, strategies employed for incubator management, as well as future potential incubator improvements, are discussed.
