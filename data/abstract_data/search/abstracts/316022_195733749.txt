Research on Tax Planning Based on High tech Enterprises
The rapid development of China's market economy has brought certain opportunities to the development of high tech enterprises. The quality of enterprise tax planning work and the quality of management will directly affect the economic efficiency and competitiveness of enterprises. Enterprises need to increase their emphasis on tax planning. Therefore, in order to ensure the stable and rapid development of high tech enterprises, enterprises should pay attention to tax planning work. This paper analyzes the significance of tax planning for high tech enterprises, studies the tax planning strategies of high tech enterprises, and explores the risks and corresponding countermeasures of tax planning.
