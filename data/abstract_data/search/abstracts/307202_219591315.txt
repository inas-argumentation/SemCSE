Robotic Process Automation Through Advance Process Analysis Model
Robotics Process Automation is an advanced technology that builds an intelligent software robot that can emulate human interactions with a business process. RPA is an efficient automated method where software agents interact through a graphical user interface in a human like manner. RPA has various applications in most industries like banking and finance, human resources, healthcare, etc. In this paper, we have proposed our RPA process analysis model and have compared it with a traditional model using various comparison parameters like frequency of change, degree of complexity, time is taken, screen usage and volume of transactions and found out that proposed method gave efficient results over the traditional method.
