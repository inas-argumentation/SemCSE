Convergence From Power Law to Logarithm Law in Nonlinear Scalar Field Equations
In this note, we uncover a relation between power law nonlinear scalar field equations and logarithmic law scalar field equations.We show that the ground state solutions, as p$\downarrow} 2 for the power law scalar field equations, converge to the ground state solutions of the logarithmic law equations. As an application of this relation, we show that the associated Sobolev inequalities for imbedding from W1,2(\mathbb{R}{N}$RN) into Lp \mathbb{R}{N}$RN) converge to an associated logarithmic Sobolev inequality, giving a new proof of the latter inequality due to Lieb Loss (Analysis, 2nd edn, Graduate studies in mathematics, 14, American Mathematical Society, Providence, 2001) Using this relation, we also derive a Liouville type theorem for positive solutions of the nonlinear scalar field equation with power law nonlinearity, giving a sharp version of an earlier result in Felmer et al. (Ann Inst Henri Poincare Anal Non Lineaire 25(1) 105 119, 2008)
