Development of an intelligent system for drilling fluid hydraulics.
This paper introduces an intelligent system for designing drilling fluid hydraulics, with which drilling fluid rheology models can be automatically optimized. Cooperated with the input of the borehole structure, assembly of drilling tools, penetration rate and cuttings, the drilling fluid hydraulics parameters can be calculated and output graphically and dynamically. The output parameters can be adjusted according to the actual capacity of the mud pump, combined with the experience of the drilling engineer, optimized design of drilling fluid hydraulics is realized.
