Two new phenylethanoid glycosides from Ginkgo biloba leaves and their tyrosinase inhibitory activities.
Two undescribed phenylethanoid glycosides, Ginkgoside C (1) and D (2) together with ten known glycosides (3 12) were isolated from Ginkgo biloba leaves.
