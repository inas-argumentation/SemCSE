The article presents statistical data on reading and writing skills of primary school children in Russia and abroad. It gives insight into scientific and methodological issues related to psychological, neuropsychological, and speech language monitoring of students' reading and writing skills. The author points to the need for a large scale examination of primary school children to identify signs of reading and writing disorders, to consider preventive measures and to provide help at an early stage. Speech language screening is a universal method to examine a large group of children in secondary schools swiftly and properly and to obtain objective data.
