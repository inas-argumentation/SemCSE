WORKPLACE STRESS AND COPING STRATEGIES AMONG ARTS AND SCIENCE COLLEGE TEACHERS IN KANYAKUMARI DISTRICT
In organizational culture Stress is an independent variable influencing employee satisfaction and performance and it is incumbent on management to improve quality of life of organizational members. Work place stress is the result of the interaction between a person and the work environment or the organization. For the person it is the awareness of not being able to cope with the demands of their work environment, with an associated negative emotional response. The events or circumstances that lead to the feeling that physical or psychological demands are about to exceed his or her ability to cope are known as stressors. Stressors can be of several types. Stressors can be inherent in the job because of factors that make that occupation what it is and it can arise because of the way the job is organized. This may include physical factors as well as psychological factors that affect the body's balance. Stressors can also arise out of excessive work demands such as unrealistic deadlines, and can arise out of personal factors such as health status, relationships, ability to cope with difficult situations etc. The objectives of this paper is to identify and analyze the causes of stress, to highlight the relationship between the job profile and employer do things to reduce occupational stress, to find out the employers do things and self handling strategies to reduce occupational stress. The study is confined with both primary and secondary data. To study the workplace stress and coping strategies among arts and science college teachers in Kanyakumari district 971 teachers working in Arts and science colleges are selected as samples by adopting stratified random sampling method
