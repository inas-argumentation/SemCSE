Research on the Operation System of Online Community in Automobile Manufacturers
With the quickening pace of internationalization, the automobile industry is rising whose traditional marketing mode is innovational and transformational on and on from producer regional sales branch retailers the terminal customers, gradually across to the owner potential customers dealers automobile manufacturers. This is the marketing operation system of online community. This paper has tried to put the automobile manufacturers as the breakthrough point of the research, and write the research on operation system of online community.
