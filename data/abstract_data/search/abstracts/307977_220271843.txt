The transmembrane supercomplex mediating the biogenesis of OMPs in Gram negative bacteria assumes a circular conformational change upon activation
I present here the interaction network among the subunits of the b barrel assembly machinery (BAM) complex (BamA, BamB) and the periplasmic chaperone SurA, as well as the existence of a ternary BamA BamB SurA complex. Theoretical structures for the SurA BAM complex, which is part of the transmembrane supercomplex involved in the biogenesis of OMPs in Gram negative bacteria, were computed to interpret the mechanism.
