Process and apparatus for in mould labelling, a moulded article comprising slots, a moulded article comprising grooves, a laminate label, a method for forming a laminate pre form label
In mold labeling method and apparatus for injecting material into the mold void while clamping the label of the injection molding tool and the label is clamped in the mold. Clamping is associated with a plurality of clamping members biased elastically to extend partially into the mold void while clamping the label. The injection molded article will have a plurality of slots, the external surface of the molded article which extends through the injection molded article includes a groove that is received to the edge of the label. Laminated preform label for using the injection molding process comprises a substrate and a fabric layer. There is also a method for forming a label.
