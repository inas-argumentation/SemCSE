Fog Computing in Appliance for Electrical Monitoring System in Greenhouse
Cloud computing technology has made a big revolution by providing users with many utility applications located on Internet servers instead of personal devices. However, since the concept of Internet of Things (IoT) was released about two decades ago, the number of IoT devices has increased continuously. Therefore, it is difficult for cloud servers in the Cloud computing model to handle all the huge data from IoT devices. A new concept proposed to solve Cloud computing problems is Fog computing. In addition, energy consumption has become a major challenge in recent decades when emissions from uncontrolled burning of fossil fuels have led to climate change. Therefore, the control of electric power usage is very important. In this paper, the authors built a system to monitor the status of electrical appliances in a greenhouse using Zigbee communication network and the Fog computing model.
