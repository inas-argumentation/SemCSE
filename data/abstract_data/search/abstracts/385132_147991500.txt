A Strategy to Improve Teaching Effectiveness of the "Introduction to Basic Principle of Marxism" Course
The "Introduction to Basic Principle of Marxism" course is one of the required courses in universities and plays a very important role in cultivating the political beliefs of contemporary university students and establishing a correct outlook on the world,life and values.The teachers of ideological and political theory course should improve their own quality,design scientifically the teaching in classroom and actively explore the practical teaching mode so as to improve the teaching effectiveness of the ideological and political theory course.
