Preparation method of bamboo cotton toweling cloth containing environment friendly bonding agent
The invention relates to preparation of textiles and particularly relates to a preparation method of bamboo cotton toweling cloth containing an environment friendly bonding agent. The bamboo cotton toweling cloth is prepared by the following steps: preparation of bamboo fiber; preparation of bamboo cotton toweling cloth, namely, cleaning cotton, preparing yarns, weaving a blank, singeing on single surface, retreating, cooking, bleaching, mercerizing, carrying out pad dyeing and dyeing with same color, straightening wefts, impregnating and stentering; the impregnation comprises the following steps: impregnating the cloth after weft straightening into an environment friendly bonding agent for 18 25 minutes, and then draining gum for 5 8 minutes; the environment friendly bonding agent comprises the following materials in parts by weight: phthalic anhydride, furfuryl alcohol extracted from natural plants, triethylene glycol, glutaric acid, butantriol, zinc oxide, magnesium oxide, chloroprene rubber, 70 95 parts of elastic rubber, 2 3 parts of softening oil, dimethyl carbonate, benzoyl peroxide, acetic ether, emulsifying agent, 20 30 parts of flour, 5*10 9*10 parts of nano silver, flame retardant, 3 7 parts of 20 30g/L bamboo vinegar aqueous solution and 6 13 parts of bamboo charcoal powder with the particle diameter being 0.01 0.06mm; and the elastic rubber comprises first elastic rubber and second elastic rubber which are mixed according to the weight ratio of (1 3):1. The bamboo cotton toweling cloth prepared by the invention is good in softness and antibacterial property.
