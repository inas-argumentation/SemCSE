A Survey on Video based Facial Expression Recognition using Deep Learning
Facial expressions are the most effective communication medium in our daily lives. Most often communication takes place spontaneously between two end users. Hence, in this paper, efforts have been given in video based facial expressions i.e. spontaneous video image sequences. In video based facial expression recognition system, dynamic changes in human faces have been tracked. The paper exploits the existing methods on recent advances of video based facial expression recognition using deep learning. In recent years, deep learning approaches are widely used in facial expression recognition task.
