An Overview Of Optical Fiber Sensor Applications In Liquid Concentration Measurements
Article history: Received 21 July 2017 Received in revised form 25 August 2017 Accepted 31 August 2017 Available online 1 September 2017 This paper provides a qualitative overview of different Optical Fiber Sensors (OFS) which play important role in the field of sensors due to their excellent characteristics, spontaneous response and easy handling system. The current state of the art of optical fiber technology is reviewed, namely based on its main characteristics and sensing advantages. In addition, the working principle of OFS and their applications are discussed, particularly for sensor employment.
