Assessment of the Sustainable Livelihood Program Employment Facilitation Process
The Sustainable Livelihood Program Employment Facilitation Track (SLP EF Track) is a scheme that facilitates the employment of Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) beneficiaries. The SLP EF track has much potential to bring the 4Ps beneficiaries closer to reaching the poverty threshold because getting employed would provide additional income to the beneficiaries on top of the incentives provided to them by the 4Ps. For this to be realized, however, the SLP EF track would have to be effective in targeting beneficiaries and in identifying employment partners for 4Ps.
