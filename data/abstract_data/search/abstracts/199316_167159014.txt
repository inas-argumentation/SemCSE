Interactive Effects in Information Processing: A Conceptual Framework of the Relationship of Consumer Involvement and Brand Categorization
This paper addresses the relationship between consumer involvement and brand categorization. The Howard, Narayana and Markin, and Brisoux Laroche brand categorization paradigms are used as the theoretical frameworks. The major issues examined are the interactive effects of consumer involvement (i.e. high involvement versus low involvement on the brand categorization process. A number of implications for marketers are highlighted, specifically with respect to improving attitude intention consistency predictions.
