An Analysis on the Growth and Development Status of 5737 Children in Guiyang City,in 2003
Objective:To study the growth and development status and its tendency of children and adolescents in Guiyang city,and so as to provide scientific basis for the corresponding hygienic work in the region.Methods:Morph genesis,function,nutritional status and occurred common diseases of 5737 school children and adolescents aged 6 13 in the region were surveyed with cluster multistage sampling method.Results:The state of growth and development of children and adolescents in the region was good;the tendency of their growth and development corresponded to general regularity,and the indexes were increasing from year to year;The level of growth and development was higher than the provincial average,while the nutritional status was not as ideal,which showed a malnutrition rate of 7.74% and a obesity rate of 14.6%;The incidences of poor sight,dental caries and trachoma were relatively high among those of the common diseases in schools.Conclusion:The growth and development status of children and adolescents presents an increasing tendency,but an ascendant split blip tendency of unsymmetrically high malnutrition rate and obesity rate occurs.
