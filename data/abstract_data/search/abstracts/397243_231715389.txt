Development of Online Education Based on the Use of Digital Crowdsourcing Technology
The article examines the digital technology for improving the quality of online education based on the use of crowdsourcing. Crowdsourcing develops innovative online education based on electronic platforms and communication channels using large scale educational resources, modern practical activities, flexible and personalized support, more accurate and diverse feedback. The authors substantiate the profitability of crowdsourcing to improve the effectiveness of online education, form a classification of the main motivations that contribute to the use of crowdsourcing in educational activities, systematize the differences between crowdsourcing and outsourcing. Crowdsourcing is considered as a new opportunity for organizing an online educational process in order to choose their educational trajectory and reach the planned knowledge, skills, abilities, i.e. the competence of the student. The use of digital crowdsourcing technology in online education can increase the effectiveness of educational processes and affect the quality of modern education.
