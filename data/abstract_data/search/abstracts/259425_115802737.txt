Tower arm follow up control equipment, method, system and engineering machinery
The invention discloses tower arm follow up control equipment, a tower arm follow up control method, a tower arm follow up control system and an engineering machinery comprising the system, wherein the tower arm follow up control equipment comprises a receiving device and a controller, wherein the receiving device is used for receiving an inclined angle between a tower arm and a main arm; and the controller is used for computing a current applied onto a hoisting pump of a hoisting mechanism according to the angular deviation when the angular deviation between an inclined angle of the tower arm and the main arm and the initial inclined angle of the tower arm and the main arm is greater than a preset angle, and applying the current to the hoisting pump of the hoisting mechanism to control a rope of the hoisting mechanism to be reeled and unreeled, so that the angular deviation can not exceed the maximum follow up deviation angle. According to the technical scheme, the rope reeling or the rope unreeling of the hoisting mechanism can be adjusted according to the inclined angle change between the tower arm and the main arm timely, so that the deviation angle can not exceed the maximum follow up deviation angle, and therefore, the tipping or the backward smashing falling can be avoided.
