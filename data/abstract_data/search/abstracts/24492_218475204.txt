Secondary Impact of the COVID 19 Pandemic on Patients With Heart Failure
The novel coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID 19) pandemic has presented 2 sets of challenges to the heart failure (HF) community. First, we were forced to rapidly establish best practices for the care of patients with HF and COVID 19 and to manage its cardiovascular complications. Although health systems have been immediately and appropriately focused on mitigation, we must prepare for the second wave of challenges ahead the consequences of disruptions in HF care at the patient, provider, and systems levels.
