The book ends with a warning that simply allowing things to drift along is not a viable option for both aspirational and practical reasons. The sacrifice of each successive generation of people of color to continuing practices of structural discrimination increases the moral obligation to change as well as further reifies the concrete effects of discrimination. The public and contemporary makers of policy face a stark choice, and much is at stake in choosing correctly for the benefit of all Americans. Whitewashing Race, despite the foregoing criticisms, makes the case in elegant and urgent terms for restructuring the racialized distribution of resources. It thus belongs on any concerned and engaged citizen's bookshelf.
