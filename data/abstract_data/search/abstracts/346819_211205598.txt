A Compact Dual Band Circularly Polarized Antenna with Wide Axial Ratio Beamwidth
A compact circularly polarized patch antenna with wide axial ratio beamwidth (ARBW) operating at dual bands is proposed in this paper. The antenna consists of two stacked patches, which account for GPS L1 band $1575 \pm 2\ \text{MHz} and L2 band $1228 \pm 2\ \text{MHz} In the proposed antenna, four pillars are introduced to realize miniaturization. To adjust the beamwidth response of the far field components, the antenna is backed by a modified metallic cavity, which in turn widen the 3 dB axial ratio beamwidth (ARBW) at two operating bands effectively. Finally, the proposed design with excellent performance is realized in a size of $0.368\ \lambda}_{0} (diameter) and $0.105\ \lambda}_{0} (height) Simulated and measured results show that the ARBWs at L1 and L2 bands are as wide as 173deg and 133deg, respectively.
