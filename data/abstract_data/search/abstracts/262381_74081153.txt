Preliminary results of a randomized phase III trial of the TTD Group comparing capecitabine and oxaliplatin (CapeOx) vs. oxaliplatin and 5 fluorouracil in continuous infusion (5 FU CI) as first line treatment in advanced or metastatic colorectal cancer (CRC)
3524 Background: Ox plus CI 5 FU is one of the standard chemotherapy regimens for first line treatment in patients (p) with advanced CRC. Phase II trials have shown CapeOx as a convenient combination, with a high activity and a favourable safety profile. This study is a phase III trial comparing both schedules. Methods: The study is designed to include 348 p with histological or cytological confirmation of advanced/metastatic CRC adenocarcinoma, measurable disease, PS>=70% and adequate bone marrow, renal and hepatic function. Previous adjuvant chemotherapy was allowed. Treatment: P were randomly assigned to receive either Arm A: oral Cape 1000 mg/m2 twice daily from day 1 to day 15, plus Ox 130 mg/m2, iv, 2h, on day 1 (in 3 week treatment cycles) or Arm B: biweekly 85 mg/m2, Ox, iv, 2h, plus weekly CI 5 FU 2250 mg/m2, in 48h (TTD schedule) Treatment was continued, until progressive disease, unacceptable toxicity or consent withdrawal.
