A practical bandgap reference circuit applied to audio power amplifier
A practical bandgap reference circuit with simple structure based on the traditional bandgap reference circuit structure was designed to provide the reference voltage for audio power amplifier. Meanwhile,a start up circuit for quick establishment of the reference voltage was also designed. The 0.18 mm 1P4M BCDMOS technology is adopted in circuit design. The supply voltage is 3.3 V from LDO in audio power amplifier. The results of Cadence Spectre simulation show that the circuit has 1.26417 V output at room temperature in tt model,and the reference voltage has 5% deviation in the different process corner model at 40~150 degC.
