A Review: Therapeutic Effects of Hibiscus rosa sinensis Linn.
The natural plant products are widely used nowadays because of increasing the burden of diseases. Hibiscus rosa sinensis Linn. (Family Malvaceae) is a plant which is widely distributed throughout the world. Its leaves, barks, roots and flowers have been used in the Indian traditional system as medicine to treat various diseases. Various research studies proved that the different parts of Hibiscus rosa sinensis plants possesses Antioxidant, Antimicrobial, Antidiabetic, Antiulcer, Hepatoprotective, Antifertility, Antigenotoxic and Antiinflammatory properties, which helps in treatment of many diseases. Hibiscus rosa sinensis has been used in many herbal mix and drinks. Many research studies conducted in animal modal evaluate Hibiscus rosa sinensis flowers and leaves as antidiabetic and antioxidant compounds.
