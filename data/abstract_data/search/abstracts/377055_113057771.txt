Elastoplastic Contact Analysis of Metal O ring Based on ANSYS
Metal O ring as the sealing element of safety valve in nuclear power station,which demands very high reliability of sealing.Excellent spring back behavior and sufficient structural intensity are both essential to assure seal's credible.For analysis design method,because of O ring's unpredictable transfiguration and complicated integral of moment,the calculation error is inevitable.Considering these shortages,based on the elastoplastic contact analysis and Multilinear Isotropic Hardening(MISO) constitutive model of ANSYS.Did the nonlinear structure analysis of metal O ring.The results indicate that O ring's structure meets the requirement of intensity,the 0 position of O ring's inner wall is the most weakness place.
