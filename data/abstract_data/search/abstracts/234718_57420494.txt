An Investigative Research on Strategies of Learning Evaluation Reform of PE in Middle and Elementary School in China
In order to examine the conception of learning evaluation of PE teachers during the new basic curriculum reform in China,a comprehensive research which comprised documentation,questionnaire investigation,and mathematic statistics was conducted to a sample of 182 PE teachers from 13 provinces including Hunan,Jiangxi and Hubei and so on.The results showed that: After years of PE curriculum reform and corresponding learning evaluation reform,the PE teachers had changed the idea of PE teaching,and acknowledged the significance of the multiple evaluation system for PE learning;during the learning evaluation reform,PE teachers were confronted with some new problems and difficulties in how to respond to the evaluation system;reform of PE learning evaluation asked for further cooperative efforts from those that were concerned,especially for the practical examinations and improvement.
