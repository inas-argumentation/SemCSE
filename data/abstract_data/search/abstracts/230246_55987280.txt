The Effect of the Directly Reflective Approach to Teaching the Nature of Science in Science and Technology Education on Academic Achievement and Scientific Attitude
Information is constantly renewed and changed all over the world, which has reflections on all aspects of our lives. It is education that is especially influenced by the process. Various alternatives are put forward to how to teach students knowledge. One of these alternatives is teaching the nature of science. The present paper is a study on the effect of the directly reflective approach, a method used for teaching the nature of science, on students' academic achievements and attitudes towards science.
