Practice of environmental sanitation and hygiene in the light of "Swacha Bharat Abhiyan" a study among female residents of rural Punjab, India
Safe sanitation means promotion of safe disposal of human excreta, right use of toilet and avoiding open defecation as well as management of solid and liquid waste. WHO observed that one gram of faeces can contain 10,000,000 viruses, 1,000,000 bacteria, 1,000 parasite cysts and 100 parasite eggs. The U.S. Public Health Service identified 22 human diseases that are linked to improper solid and liquid waste management. India generates solid waste of about 1.2 lakh tonnes per day and unfortunately the waste management system in the country is very scratchy.
