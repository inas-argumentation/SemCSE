Diagnosis of the Subsequent Failure Mechanisms of Composite Laminates
The post failure analysis of symmetric E glass/Epoxy and Graphite/Epoxy (T300/934) laminates is presented in this paper. The main thrust of this research was to predict the progressive fracture mechanisms of composite laminates under load. Several composite laminates were tested under the three poi nt bending configuration to observe and record the subsequent failure events. On the other hand, an analytical computational procedure based on lamination theory, Tsai Hill failure theory, and an assumed stiffness reduction scheme was developed in an attempt to predict the failure mechanisms.
