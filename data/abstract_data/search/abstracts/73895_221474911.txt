Research on Tunnel Wireless Communication Technology
In view of the shortcomings of the existing tunnel communication technology, based on the principle of wireless sensor network (WSN) the important research achievements in the field of tunnel wireless communication in recent years are summarized in terms of topology structure, module design and tunnel application of Zigbee technology. On the basis of the above review, the future research direction of tunnel wireless communication technology is prospected, which provides reference for the development of tunnel wireless communication technology.
