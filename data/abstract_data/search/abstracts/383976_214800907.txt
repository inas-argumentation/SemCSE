On The Error Rate Performance Of Transmission Modes In Lte Wireless Systems
The number of users are increasing with more and more applications and more demand. At the same time, high transmission data rates and communication reliability have become a significant requirement. The LTE and LTE Advanced are developed by 3GPP. The LTE Advanced features improvements in spectral efficiency, peak data rates and user experience relative to the LTE. With a maximum peak data rate of 1Gbps, LTE Advanced has been approved by the ITU as an IMT Advanced technology. LTE achieve its various goals through its different transmission modes that help in enhancement of data rates. In this paper, I have studied the performance of various transmission modes used in LTE and their comparison with each other is done by the use of MATLAB.
