Short range order in cubic titanium nitride
The presence of a large number of structural vacancies in transition metals that react with hydrogen, carbon, and nitrogen permits one to determine the short range order in the arrangement of internal atoms. The character of the short range order and the structure of the ordered phases are interconnected. The authors trace the manner of the local order with regard to the internal atomic changes in those phase diagram regions where the long range order character is training. Neutron diffraction patterns were plotted of the polycrystalline samples of given compositions using a neutron diffractometer. The wavelength of the monochromatic neutron beam was lambda 0.1073 nm. The neutron diffraction patterns are shown. These investigations indicate that the character of the local distribution of nitrogen atoms actually depends on the composition of the samples and has, above 400 C, a varied nature.
