Uptake of green technology through sustainable procurement
VicRoads is in the process of standardising sustainable procurement requirements within its road construction projects. The assessment of sustainability aspects has evolved from practices established in other jurisdictions, and involves a relative evaluation of the sustainability components of tender submissions. All initiatives are assessed through a weighted criteria analysis, which rewards innovation whilst recognising whole of life impacts associated with the initiatives. This, in effect, provides a form of controlled innovation and allows contractors to commit to a level of sustainability without having to nominate a specific supplier or asphalt mix.
