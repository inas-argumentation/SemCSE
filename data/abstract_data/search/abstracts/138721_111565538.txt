Architectural space design for intellectualization education buildings
The impacts of today's technology development on education buildings are analyzed firstly. Based on that, the directions for developing intellectualization education buildings are pointed out. From the aspects of intellectualization education building system and assistant education system, this paper makes a deep exploration on the main characteristics and implications of intellectualization education buildings. From the angles of architectural function transformation and space design, this paper also researches the requirements of intellectualization on the space design of education buildings as well as the design tact and strategies on the corresponding aspects, such as architectural function design, environmental aesthetics of architectural space, architectural space design and so on. Finally, this paper looks forward the physical implementation of intellectualization education buildings in the future.
