Interactive comment on "Anthropogenic climate change and glacier lake outburst flood risk: local and global drivers and responsibilities for the case of Lake Palcacocha, Peru" by Christian Huggel et al
Reviewer comment: Based on the prominent case study of Lake Palcacocha in the Peruvian Andes the paper deals with the complexity of "socio economical, institutional and cultural processes" which become drivers of risk exposure and vulnerability and ultimately shape glacier lake outburst flood risk for the urban agglomeration of Huaraz. Questions of associated responsibilities, causality, and justice in the context of the adverse effects of climate change are also raised. This integration of a "normative responsibility framework" is rather unusual and innovative in scientific studies on GLOFs. C1
