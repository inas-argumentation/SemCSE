Students'Ability in Analyzing by Using a Flow Proof to Solve Problems in Real Analysis Lecture
Real analysis is one of subjects that help the students to experience critical thinking processes. Students must understand mathematical definitions and theorems in order to solve problems and perform a process of analytical thinking, so that their analysis will be developed to the given problems. The research that has been done showed that students' ability in analyzing can be improved by using Flow Proof in a preliminary analysis, but not their ability in narrating the proof yet. Keywords Real Analysis, critical thinking, Flow Proof, preliminary analysis
