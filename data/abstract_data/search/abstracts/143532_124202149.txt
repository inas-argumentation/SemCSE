Pragmatic Classification and Pragmatic Functions of English and Chinese Hedges
Hedges have important influence on the success of communication. From the pragmatic perspective,to explore classification of English and Chinese hedges,and in combination with conversational implicature,cooperative principle,politeness principle and face theory,under the concrete context,to study the pragmatic functions of English and Chinese hedges can help the speaker and the listener improve their hedge skills,maintain equal and cooperative relationship and communicate better.
