Application of 3x0.30 HT full rubber penetrated steel cord in radial car tire
The properties of 3x0.30 HT TRU full rubber penetrated steel cord were introduced and its application in radial car tire was investigated.3x0.30 HT TRU featured higher rupture strength and adhesion strength,and better rubber penetration when compared with 2+2x0 25HT.The high wear resistance,speed performance and endurance of finished tire were obtained when 3x0.30 HT TRU was used as reinforcing material in belt.
