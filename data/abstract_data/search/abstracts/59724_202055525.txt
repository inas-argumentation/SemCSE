Dynamic analysis of key parts of CNC milling machine based on ANSYS
According to the structure of two spindles CNC milling machine tool,the finite element (FE)model of two spindles housing was established by means of FE method. Analyzes the modal,the first ten natural frequencies and mode shapes of housing were given,and the response curves were obtained by analyzing the harmonic response of housing. In conclusion,the greatest response displacement of the housing suffering the external exciting force was found. Furthermore,the weakness of housing was found,and as a result,it offers suggestions for the optimization design of key pats of machine tool.
