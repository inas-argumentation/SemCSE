Study on Tripod with Anchor Plate Foundation Form of Wind Turbines in the Geological Environment of Calcareous Sand
Considering the high void ratio and compressibility of calcareous sand,the tripod with anchor plate foundation is used for the wind turbine in the geological environment of calcareous sand.In this paper,calculations of the various combinations of the foundation members are conducted according to the given load conditions,and the combination of the lightest weight under the given load conditions is found out.The study concludes that the tripod form with anchor plate is suitable for the wind turbine foundation in the geological environment.
