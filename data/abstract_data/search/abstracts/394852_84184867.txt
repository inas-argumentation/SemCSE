Robust Model Predictive Controller Design for Position DC Servomechanism System
In this article, a robust Model Predictive Control (MPC) scheme is designed for the basic position tracking problem of the DC servomechanism control system. The non linearity and parameters uncertainty are also considered in the design. The control law for MPC is modeled from the servo system dynamics and the stability is analyzed. The proposed design evaluation is performed under disturbance and without disturbance effect using an input reference signal. Moreover, for further validation, the results of MPC based model are compared with the Sliding Mode Controller (SMC) and Proportional Integral Derivative (PID) controller in order to estimate and evaluate the designed system performance. Simulation results show that the designed controller has much better tracking performance, efficiency, robustness and stability.
