ACCEPTABILITY JUDGMENTS OF VARIATIONS IN TONE REPRESENTATION IN CANTONESE SONGS
Representations of tones in Cantonese songs have been studied extensively from a compositional point of view. In contrast, the perception of lexical tones in sung Cantonese music has received significantly less attention. Existing research has looked at lexical comprehension, but perceptual acceptability has not been examined. This study compares Cantonese native speakers' acceptability judgments for short melodies that contain either matches or mismatches between the musical spoken and sung melodies. Results show that native Cantonese listeners prefer musical contours that match the tonal contour of the spoken tones of the language.
