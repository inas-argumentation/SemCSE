THE EFFECTIVENESS OF USING VOCABULARY SELF COLLECTION STRATEGY (VSS) TO IMPROVE STUDENTS VOCABULARY MASTERY ON THE EIGHTH GRADE AT SMP N 33 PURWOREJO IN THE ACADEMIC YEAR 2012/2013
This research is an experimental study. The population of this research is the eighth grade students of SMP N 33 Purworejo in the academic year of 2012/2013. Population of this research is 231 students. Sample of this research is two classes which are divided into control (VII F, 33 students) and experimental group (VII C, 33 students) To collect the data, the researcher used tests as the instrument. There were pre test and post test. Pre test is conducted before the researcher gives a treatment to the students. Post test is conducted after the researcher gives a treatment to the students. There are 20 items in the test and the type is a matching test. After analyzing the data, the researcher concluded that teaching vocabulary by using Vocabulary Self Collection Strategy (VSS) is effective in improving students' vocabulary mastery. As the fact, the result of t value is 3.67. Based on the 0.05 significant levels, the value of t table is 2.03. The result of computation shows that t value is higher than t table, that is 3.67>2.03. Another fact shows that the strategy used is effective. It shown by the mean of experimental group that is higher than the mean of control group (76.06 65.15) Based on the result finding, the researcher concluded that the use Vocabulary Self Collection Strategy (VSS) to improve students' vocabulary mastery at the Eighth grade of SMP N 33 Purworejo in the academic year 2012/2013 is effective. (Keywords: vocabulary self collection strategy, effectiveness, vocabulary mastery)
