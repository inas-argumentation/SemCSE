In Situ La, Ce, and Nd L Edge X Ray Absorption Fine Structure Study of an Intermetallic Metal Hydride Electrode in an Operating Alkaline Battery
The x ray absorption fine structure of a technologically important MmL 5 intermetallic compound (Mm=La 0.52 Ce 0.34 Nd 0.10 Pr 0.04 L 5 =Ni 3.5 Ca 0.8 Mn 0.3 Al 0.4 in the form of a hydrogen storage cathode was recorded in situ in the region 5440 6290 eV using an electrochemical cell which is essentially a fully functioning alkaline battery. Significant differences were observed in the x ray absorption near edge structure (XANES) of all of the absorption edges (La, Ce, and Nd) between the uncharged and fully charged states. In particular, a clear increase in the intensity of the La and Nd L III edge resonances (due to 2p 3/2 5d electronic transitions) was found upon charging the cell. This phenomenon was attributed to an increase in the density of empty d like states near the Fermi level following hydrogen injection into the lattice. Furthermore, the behavior of the Ce L II,III edges of this battery material upon charging was nearly the same as that observed for the gas phase hydrogenation of the Ce 2 Fe 17 and Ce 2 Fe 14 B intermetallics reported in the literature
