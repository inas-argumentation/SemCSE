American insecurity and radical Filipino community politics
In the immediate wake of the 9/11 terrorist attack, Congress held hearings to evaluate U.S. airport security. Kenneth Mead, inspector general of the Department of Transportation, testified that 80 percent of the airport security screeners were not U.S. citizens. In response, Republican Representative Harold Rogers queried, "What is wrong with this picture?" The Congressman's statement questions whether non citizens can be trusted with security work. In major metropolitan airports throughout the United States, low wage immigrant workers of color have largely performed airport security jobs. This went unquestioned until September 11. Ultimately, what became wrong with the picture of nonU.S. citizens working as airport screeners in America's airports was one thing: these workers were not white.
