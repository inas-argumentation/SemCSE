Multivariate contingent claims pricing model based on option theory
The option theory is adequate and efficient for pricing the contingent claims in flexibility investment and financing. The lattice approach is one of the most popular numerical methods to value complex options. Base on the option theory, this paper chooses reasonable jump sizes and jump probabilities relying on the risk neutral pricing rule. Then a multivariate lattice model for pricing of multivariate contingent claims is proposed. The valuation procedure is consistent and unconditionally stable. In the end, the numerical valuation shows that the multivariate contingent claims pricing model is computationally efficient.
