The Legal Regulation of the Identifiers Other than Trademarks as Domain Names
The disputes arising from domain names have been expanded from those between simple domain names and trademarks to those between domain names and a range of naming systems, including the names and acronyms of international intergovernmental organizations, personal names, geographical indications, trade names and standard nonproprietary names for pharmaceutical substances.In 2001, WIPO promulgated its "Final Report of the Second Internet Domain Name Process" (the "Second Report" which makes recommendations on how to deal with these disputes.Domain name legislation of our country has seldom concerned these problems.We may eliminate these conflicts by using "General Principles of Civil Law" "Anti Unfair Competition Law" and perfecting domain name legislation.
