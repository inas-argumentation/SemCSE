NILC at WebNLG+ Pretrained Sequence to Sequence Models on RDF to Text Generation
This paper describes the submission by the NILC Computational Linguistics research group of the University of Sao Paulo/Brazil to the RDF to Text task for English at the WebNLG+ challenge. The success of the current pretrained models like BERT or GPT 2 in text to text generation tasks is well known, however, its application/success on data totext generation has not been well studied and proven. This way, we explore how good a pretrained model, in particular BART, performs on the data to text generation task. The results obtained were worse than the baseline and other systems in almost all automatic measures. However, the human evaluation shows better results for our system. Besides, results suggest that BART may generate paraphrases of reference texts.
