The Chinese Communist Party's Solutions to the Problem of Gang Groups during The Early Labor Movements
The Communist Party of China started labor movements ever since its founding. The first barrier was gang groups active in places like plants, docks, mines and warehouses where poor workers gathered. Spiritual imprisonment and limitation of organization deprived workers of consciousness and freedom, impeding labor movements. Therefore, the Party made a series of appropriate policies to deal with gang groups, expelled their interference in labor movements and gave a big push to Chinese revolution.
