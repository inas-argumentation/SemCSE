Application of oblique impact theory of detonation waves at the explosive metal interface in design of shaped charge
The oblique impact theory of detonation waves at the explosive metal was applied in design of shaped charge.Relationship between the pressure peak P on the interface of copper and incident angle ph0was obtained by using LS DYNA.The numerical simulation results coincided well with the theoretical calculation ones and experimental data.An engineering method to calculate the proper liners height of tapered and spherical shaped lines was established under the condition of one point detonation,it was applied in the design of a grouped liner warhead.The proposed method provided a basis to determine liner height,and enriched the design methods of shaped charge warhead.
