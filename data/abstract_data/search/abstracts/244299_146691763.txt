Method for producing flower pot from waste stalk plate sanding powder
The present invention relates to a method of producing a flowerpot with the sanding powder of a waste straw board. In the technique, the sanding powder of the waste straw board is dried; the dried sanding powder of the waste straw board is uniformly mixed with auxiliary materials in a high speed mixer; meanwhile, adhesive is uniformly mixed with addition agent; composite hardener is added, so thatthe pH value of the adhesive solution is regulated to 4.5 to 6.0; the adhesive solution is sprayed into and then uniformly mixed with the mixed material; the material is dried; finally, compression molding is carried out. The present invention has the advantages that because raw materials, such as soil, plastic, timber or other plant fibres, are replaced by the sanding powder for the production of flowerpots, the flowerpots are cheap and high quality, the waste is turned into treasure, resources are saved, and the drainage and air permeability of the flowerpots is good. In the process of transplanting, the flowerpot does not need to be turned over, and the nutritious flowerpot in the soil can be naturally degraded into humic acid organic fertilizer. If freely falling down to the cement floor from an altitude of one meter, the flowerpot cannot be damaged. After being irradiated by an xenon lamp for total sixty six hours, the appearance and shape of the flowerpot are greatly changed, the mildewing phenomenon is severe, the surface goes rough and has a few of cracks, so the phenomena indicate that the flowerpot has the properties of controllable photodegradation and biological degradation, and therefore the flowerpot is degradable.
