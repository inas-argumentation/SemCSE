Effect of Bacillus coagulans on Growth Performance,Intestinal Function and Its Meat Quality of Yellow Feathered Chicken
The effect of different levels of Bacillus coagulans on growth and slaughter performance,intestinal flora,tissue structure of small intestine,and content of main nutrients in muscle of yellow feather chickens were studied in this article.Birds with the same age and health status were randomly segregated into four groups,which were control group(basal diet),group A(basal diet+1%0 B.coagulans),group B(basal diet+1.5%0 B.coagulans) and group C(basal diet+2%0 B.coagulans).They were slaughtered at the age of 65 days.The results showed that B.coagulans coagulans had a significant effect on the growth of yellow feathered chicken.Addition of 1.5%0 B.coagulans was the best, and did not affect slaughter rate,semi eviscerated rate, eviscerated rate,meat tenderness degree and water loss rate,crude ash and protein content of muscle(P0.05).The count of Salmonella enterica(7.905 0.026 Lg cfu/g vs. 6.433 0.092 Lg cfu/g vs. 5.705 0.021 Lg cfu/g,for group A,B and C,respectively) and Staphylococcus(7.218+ 0.043 Lg cfu/g vs. 6.885+ 0.031 Lg cfu/g vs. 6.245+ 0.076 Lg cfu/g,for group A,B and C,respectively) in experimental groups were obviously lower than the control group(P0.05).Otherwise, no significant differences were detected on the number of Escherichia coli in experiment groups(7.517+ 0.022 Lg cfu/g vs. 7.510+ 0.021 Ig cfu/g vs. 7.4800+ 0.032 Ig cfu/g for group A,B and C,respectively) compared with control group(P0.05).Three different levels of B.coagulans all significantly enhanced the villus height,ratio of villus height to crypt depth and fat content of the pectoral and thigh muscle(P0.05),while significantly reduced the crypt depth(P0.05).Higher leg and breast muscle rate(P0.05) were also observed in the treatment groups compared with the control,with the highest leg and breast rate found in group C.The results indicated that supplementation of B.coagulans can improve the productive level of the yellow feather chickens.The appropriate amount of B.coagulans added to basal diet is 1.5%0 under this experimental condition.
