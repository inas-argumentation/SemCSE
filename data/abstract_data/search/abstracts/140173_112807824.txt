A Fault Diagnosis System Based on Fault Tree Analysis Method for Telecom Equipment
The method of FTA reliability analyze for a fault diagnosis of telecom equipments is studied.The concept of FTA denotation for telecom equipment fault cause root and method of a fault diagnosis and analyze are discussed.Finally,the method is illustrated and validated by an example of RLOS alarm on an optical transport equipment.The method developed by practice scene is concise and convenient for engineering personnel to use.
