The Analysis of Boiler Fire Extinguishing Caused by High Drum Level in 600MW Unit
The paper analyzes the reasons and solutions of boiler abnormal fire extinguishing because of the high drum level during ignition process of cold start in the No.4 unit of Tuoketuo Power Plant in Inner Mongolia.The analysis is from three aspects,that is influncing factors of the drum level,the attention degree of the operators during the time between unit boiler ignition and coal mill starting,and the control capacity of operators.
