Problems and countermeasures of investor relation management of listed companies in China
In order to improve the quality of investor relation management of listed companies, to make them develop healthily and continuously, to ensure the effectiveness of the stock market as well as to make investors rationally invest so as to ensure the perfect operation of capital market, the current situation of investor relation management of the listed companies was discussed, and the existing problems were analyzed. The following countermeasures were put forward: the strengthening of investor relation management and the establishment of special information release departments, the improvement of shareholder meeting regulations and the enhancement of independent director system, the punishment of misleading activities of the listed companies by supervision departments.
