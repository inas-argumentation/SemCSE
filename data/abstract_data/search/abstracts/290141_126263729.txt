Well posedness and scattering for the mass energy NLS on R n x M k
Abstract We study the nonlinear Schrodinger equation posed on product spaces R n x M k \mathbb{R}{n}\times{\mathcal{M}{k} for n 1 {n\geq 1} and k 1 {k\geq 1} with M k \mathcal{M}{k} any k dimensional compact Riemannian manifold. The main results concern global well posedness and scattering for small data solutions in non isotropic Sobolev fractional spaces. In the particular case of k 2 {k=2} H 1 {H^{1} scattering is also obtained.
