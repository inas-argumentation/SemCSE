The present invention discloses a light emitting diode strip, characterized by: a substrate, extending along a longitudinal direction of the belt; a plurality of LED chips disposed on the substrate or in the substrate, wherein the light emitting diode chips arranged along the longitudinal direction at least one row; at least two contact strips, configured to supply electric energy to said light emitting diode chip, wherein a side surface of each of said contact strip at least partially along the substrate by the each said longitudinal direction extends, and wherein said light emitting diode chip in a transverse or oblique direction of the belt is turned on at least in part said contact strip. The present invention also provides a method of manufacturing a light strip and the above described configuration including the configuration of a light emitting diode strip.
