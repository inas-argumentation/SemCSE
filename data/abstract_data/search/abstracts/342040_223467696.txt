Sleep disorders in patients with ischemic stroke: pathophysiological mechanisms and treatment
Sleep disorder is a common complication after ischemic stroke. If it is not timely diagnosed and treated, it may affect the rehabilitation of stroke and recurrence. The manifestations of sleep disorders have various forms according to the different locations of infarction. For the treatment of sleep disorder after stroke, the method of the combination of drugs and physical therapy is used in clinical practice at present. In recent years, with the widely use of the polysomnography, poststroke sleep disorders can be diagnosed clearly and carefully. The treatment for sleep disorders after stroke may significantly improve the prognosis of patients. Key words: Stroke; Brain Ischemia; Sleep Wake Disorders; Continuous Positive Airway Pressure
