P44 H2S down regulates expression of hepcidin in response to inflammation via inhibition of IL 6/STAT3 and activin B/SMAD1/5/8 pathway
Hepcidin, which is produced primarily by liver, negatively regulates plasma iron. Its expression is induced in response to inflammation, which leads to anemia of inflammation. In this study, we used an in vitro co culture model to investigate the regulation effect of H 2 S. HuH7 hepatoma cells were exposed to the conditioned medium (CM) which was collected from LPS treated THP 1 macrophages. NaHS, or l cysteine, was applied at two different stages. One was "pre treatment" to THP 1 cells which generated different CM. The other was "post treatment" to HuH7 cells which were suffered same CM. Both treatments with H 2 S down regulated LPS induced hepcidin HAMP mRNA expression. H 2 S also inhibited the HAMP promoter activity induced by IL 6 in HuH7 cells. IL 6 concentration in CM was significantly reduced by H 2 S pre treatment, so did the phosphorylation of JAK2 and STAT3. Inhibition of IL 6/JAK2/STAT3 pathway by H 2 S counts for the down regulation of hepcidin expression in pre treatment model. In post treatment model, the transcription function of STAT3 was inhibited, which was proved by decreased SOCS3 mRNA expression and phosphorylation of STAT3. Increased SIRT1 activity decreased the acetylation of STAT3, which was related to decreased phosphorylation of STAT3. In vivo results also indicated H 2 S down regulated hepcidin expression through inhibition of IL 6/Jak2/Stat3 and activin B/Smad1/5/8 pathway. Our data suggest H 2 S inhibits inflammation hepcidin expression via multi pathways.
