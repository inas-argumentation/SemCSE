Introduction Oral submucous fibrosis (OSMF) is a chronic disease of oral mucosa characterized by inflammation and progressive fibrosis of lamina propria and deeper connective tissues, followed by stiffening of an otherwise yielding mucosa resulting in difficulty in opening the mouth. The diagnosis of OSMF is based on features such as burning sensation, difficulty in taking spicy foods, paleness, blanching of the mucosa, palpable fibrotic bands in the buccal and labial mucosa and progressive reduction in opening the mouth.
