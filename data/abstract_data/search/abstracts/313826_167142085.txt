Financing Industrial Development In Nigeria: A Case Study Of The Small And Medium Enterprises In Kwara State
The study was designed to address the financing of industrial development in Nigeria (especially on the Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) sub sector in Kwara State) Hence, the ownership structure, entrepreneuraEUR(tm)s capacity development, sub sector type of the SME, source of start up (seed) capital, and source of business/financial information and membership of business/trade organization formed the core indices that were examined. The analyses of the data collected including the testing of the hypotheses were carried out using simple descriptive statistical tools and the chi square. The major findings of the study concluded that the ownership structure, entrepreneuraEUR(tm)s capacity development level, Small and Medium Enterprises sub sector type, source of seed capital, source(s) of business/financial information and the membership of trade/business organization significantly affect the financing of Small and Medium Enterprises in Kwara State. The sole proprietorship type of ownership, low capacity development level of entrepreneurs, low size of annual revenue and the emphasis placed on Small and Medium Enterprise sub sector type constituted a clog in the financing of the SMEs cum industrialization process in Kwara State. Also, the recourse to owneraEUR(tm)s savings as source of seed capital, usage of unreliable information source(s) and the non membership of business/trade organizations by SMEs greatly impaired their financing.
