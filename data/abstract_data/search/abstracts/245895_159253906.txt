Artificial Intelligence as Global Commons and the "International Law Supremacy" Principle
The current paper analyzes the issue of Artificial Intelligence AI focusing on Artificial General Intelligence AGI and Artificial Super Intelligence ASI from the perspective of international law. It begins with the acknowledgment of the fact that AI poses historically unique challenges on the basis of its ontological characteristics and mainly of the emerging autonomy. Its autonomy bears the potential of leading to actual personhood or raising AI entities to the status of "being" thus necessitating the attribution of legal personhood too. Such a situation would be groundbreaking for all legal systems as well as for the way we comprehend various conducts, terms and legal relationships. In this framework, of the ecumenical challenges that AI poses, the article argues in favor of a common, international response, through the international law supremacy principle.
