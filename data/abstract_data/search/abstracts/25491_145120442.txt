Understanding the Market Mechanism before Adam Smith: Economic Thought in Medieval Islam
Economics, as we know it today, began to take shape in Europe during the eighteenth century. Yet, economic analysis has a much longer lineage. Such analysis may be found in the writings of the ancient Greek philosophers, Islamic scholars, medieval schoolmen, and the Mercantilists of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. The literature of ancient China, as well as that of India, also provides instances. 1992, preface)
