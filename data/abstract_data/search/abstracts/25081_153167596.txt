The state of population in Pakistan, 1987
This is the first in what is planned as an annual series presenting data on population in Pakistan as well as related socioeconomic data. Sections are included on population size and growth; spatial distribution; migration; age and sex distribution; fertility trends and differentials; mortality including infant and neonatal mortality causes of death life expectancy and differential mortality; nuptiality; socioeconomic data including educational status employment health and womens status; and the disabled. Consideration is also given to the relations between population and education health housing labor force food requirements natural resources and environment and economic development.
