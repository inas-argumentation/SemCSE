Many healthcare organizations struggle with the utilization of data collected through an organization's on line transaction processing (OLTP) system, which is not integrated for decision making and pattern analysis. Do you know what your top 10 paid procedures are? Do you know the DRGs that have the lowest reimbursement from Medicare/ Medicaid? Are you receiving timely and accurate reports for your analysis demands? Is your legacy system effective in identifying patterns to maximize your operational output? Are you drowning in data instead of sailing on the power of knowledge? This article focuses on data mining technology and its ability to uncover hidden and unexpected patterns in data for strategic decision making in healthcare.
