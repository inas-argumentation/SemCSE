The Comparison and Analysis of Recovery Strategy of Manufacture under Government Subsidy
In recent years,with the promotion of the concept of cyclic economy,increasingly importance has been attached to wastes recovery issues. Considering government subsidy,This paper creates a simple model of reverse logistics,and study manufacturer's two main take back models and build the manufacturer's profit function. By contrasting the manufacturer's best profit function of the two models,this paper analyze the choice of manufacturer's recovery strategies under the influence of government subsidy.
