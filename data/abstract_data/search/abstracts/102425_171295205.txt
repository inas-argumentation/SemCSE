Tragic Images of Intellectuals in Lu Xun's Novels
The article analyses the intellectual's tragic image of three generations in Luxun's novels, that is the old intellectuals who didn't stick to themselves in the final stage of the feudal society; the intellectuals who split themselves in Xinhai revolution; the disillusion of the intellectual's ideal in May 4th.Therefore we get deep insight into the root and the society value of the tragic image.
