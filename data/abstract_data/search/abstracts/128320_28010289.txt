Th e survey was distributed electronically via the American College of Physicians (ACP) Research Center's Internal Medicine Insider Panel, a national representative group of ACP members who have voluntarily agreed to participate in periodic physician surveys. Th e study was exempt from the University of Michigan's Institutional Review Board. Of 442 PCPs invited to participate, 210 responded (response rate=210/442, or 48% Twenty nine of these respondents were ineligible <25% clinical time or no referrals for colonoscopy) yielding 181 completed surveys. Respondent demographics are shown in Table 1. For a 60 year old with a normal high quality screening colonoscopy, 88% (159/181) correctly recommended repeat colonoscopy in 10 years.
