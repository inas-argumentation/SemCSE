L'intervention d'un banquier central dans l'industrie le role de la Banque d'Angleterre dans les annees 1920
Apres la fin de la Premiere Guerre mondiale, la Banque d'Angleterre (BoE) qui etait a la fois la banque centrale de la Grande Bretagne et une banque privee, participa au mouvement de rationalisation Elle intervint dans l'industrie, comme jamais auparavant, pour aider a la restructuration de firmes, son Gouverneur Montagu Norman etant convaincu que la rationalisation pouvait contribuer a faire renaitre les industries de base, durant cette periode de chomage de masse. La premiere partie presente le contexte economique des annees 1920. La seconde s'interesse aux interventions de la BoE. After the end of the First World War the Bank of England (BoE) the central bank of Great Britain and a private bank too was involved in the rationalisation movement and intervened in industry to rescue and reorganise firms on a scale never reached before. During a period of mass unemployment especially in the "old staple industries" her Governor Montagu Norman became convinced that the business principles of rationalisation could be helpful for the revival of staple industries. The first part deals with the economic environment of the 1920s. The second part concentrates on the industrial intervention of the BoE during that period.
