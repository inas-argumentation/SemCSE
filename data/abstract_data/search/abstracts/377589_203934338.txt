Comparison on the determination methods of leaf area of Cornaceae.
Leaf areas of six kinds of Cornaceae including Dendrobenthamia japonica var.chinensis plant species are measured by five different methods.It shows that leaf area is significantly different when the same species is determinated by different methods.The method of measuring the leaf area by the instrument SHY 150 is easy to be operated,but there are bigger margin for errors.The measuring results by digital image processing method are accurate with good repeatability,but the operation is relatively complex.The measuring results by grid notation and paper weighing method are higher in accuracy.but it will take spend a lot of time and labor.
