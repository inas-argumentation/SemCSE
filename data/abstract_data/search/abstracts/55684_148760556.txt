The ecology of perception and the aesthetics of landscape
The aim of this two day workshop was to facilitate a dialogue between different approaches to the aesthetics of landscape in social sciences, mainly between British and French traditions in anthropology and human geography. Rather than a synthesis between the symbolist approach more prevalent in France and the rather more phenomenological British approaches, the seminar pointed out the apparent incompatibility of these two theoretical poles. The potential misunderstanding between both traditions is probably increased by the lack of diffusion and awareness of French writings on the subject among Anglophone academics
