Nuove strategie per esplorare il campo emergente delle competenze digitali informali e per il mondo del lavoro 4.0
The contribution offers some reflections for the analysis of the current digital skills panorama, as well as how digital competences are interpreted at the theoretical and policy implementation level. The essay highlights how, nowadays, the tendency is to create frameworks and standard tools for coding and analyzing digital skills, but these tools may prove insufficient, in some contexts, to grasp and interpret a constantly changing environment. For this reason, especially in the fields of informal digital skills, and in those related to digital working practices in "industry 4.0" contexts, the essay proposes to integrate modeling and standardization attempts, built through top down frameworks and tests, with bottom up and micro ethnography tools. These approaches, in fact, are capable of intercepting changes from below, catching informal skills, micro innovations, local interpretations and counterculture trends, according to a non normative but receptive vision of the digital skills for the future.
