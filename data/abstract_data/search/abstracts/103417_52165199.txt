An Absorptive/Transmissive Frequency Selective Surface with a High selectivity Passband
An absorptive/transmissive frequency selective surface (FSS) with absorption bands at both sides of a passband is presented. A triple layer structure consisting of a lossy and two lossless FSS was employed to achieve the characteristics of transmitting the in band wave and absorbing the out of band wave. Simulation results show that a passband with a center frequency of 5 GHz and a minimum insertion loss of 1.2 dB was obtained. An ultrawide band from 2.3 to 7.9 GHz with S11 10 dB is achieved under normal incidence.
