Challenges and Prospect of Democracy and Development: An Assessment of Development Policies and Programmes in Nigeria from 2015 2019
Democracy promotes the principles of good governance and accountability by political leaders to the electorates. Political office holders in a democratic setting are duty bound to provide public goods such as infrastructure, education, health facilities, security and environmental sanitation services to the people. This paper intends to assess the progress made by the civilian administration in Nigeria from 2015 to 2019 in bringing about socio economic changes that impact on the lives of citizens. Development issues such as infrastructure renewal, security in the Northeast of Nigeria, N power, Trader Moni, anti corruption drive and Agriculture Promotion Policy are examined. The paper adopts qualitative method and content analysis as its methodology. The paper is anchored on Levy (1967) Modernization Theory which states that traditional societies need to imitate the political and economic ideas of the western developed countries in order to promote socioeconomic development. The findings of the paper showed that the government of Nigeria embarked on massive roads rehabilitation projects, created social investment programme, recovered looted funds from government officials and made giant strides in the agriculture sector. The paper recommends that the government should get to the root causes of social unrest in the country in order to create stability for meaningful development to take place.
