CONSEQUENCE OF HEALTH INFRASTRUCTURE OF NORTH EAST INDIA IN COMPARISON WITH INDIA
Good health is fundamental to everyone. Few dissimilarities, distinctions and diversity that exist among the health care systems adopted may further lead to difficulties in arriving at a universal definition of a health care system. Good Health confers on a person or group's freedom from illness and the ability to realize one's potential. Health is therefore best understood as the indispensable basis for defining a person's sense of well being. This paper is developed for understanding the healthcare system of the country in comparison with the healthcare system of the north eastern states .It shows how the health infrastructure of NorthEastern states of India influences on the economic development of the country. The three important health indicators used for measuring the influence of health infrastructure of NE India are Maternal Mortality rate (MMR) under five mortality rates, Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) which effects the economic development of the country in terms of per capita income, GDP etc. The analysis was based on the secondary data collected from different sources. The sources are mainly SAARC Development Goals (SDGs) Report, India Country Report, National Family and Health Survey Report, Census of India Report, Sample Registration system(SRS) Bulletin, World Bank Annual Report, Report from Ministry of Family and Child Welfare India healthcare. Comparison and interpretation were drawn from different source data and tables. It was found that the healthcare system of North eastern states is much weaker than that of the country as a whole in terms of healthcare infrastructure, North eastern states of India are least aware of ayurvedic practices.
