The answers to these questions are important, because motivation is considered by many to be one of the main determining factors in success in developing a second or foreign language (34) both referred to as L2. Motivation determines the extent of active, personal involvement in L2 learning. Conversely, unmotivated students are insufficiently involved and therefore unable to develop their potential L2 skills.
