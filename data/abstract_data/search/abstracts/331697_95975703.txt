Formation of Unsymmetrical 2 (Diacylmethylene) 2,3 dihydro 1H benzimidazoles During Acidolysis of 1 Benzoyl 2 (b benzoyloxy b phenylvinyl) 1H benzimidazole
The reaction of 1 benzoyl 2 (b benzoyloxy b phenylvinyl) 1H benzimidazole with carboxylic acids was investigated. A convenient method was developed for the synthesis of unsymmetrical 2 (diacylmethylene) 2,3 dihydro 1H benzimidazoles. 2 (4 Pyrazolyl) 1H benzimidazoles were obtained by the reaction of 2 (benzoylformylmethylene) 2,3 dihydro 1H benzimidazole with hydrazine.
