Electrically charging apparatus, electric charging station and method for controlling an electric charging device
The present invention relates to an electrical charging device (10) in particular for charging an electrical energy accumulator of a motor vehicle (12) comprising: an electrical connection (16) to connect the charging device (10) comprising a loading station (14) electrically and electrical energy with is to exchange the loading station (14) an electrical converter (18) connected to the electrical connection (16) to exchange and to convert the electrical energy, the electrical energy via the electrical connection (16) to the loading station (14) a control unit (20) which is connected to the electrical converter (18) to control the exchange of electric energy, wherein the control unit (20) is assigned to a communication interface (22) which is adapted to data (34) exchange of the charging station (14) and wherein the control unit (20) is adapted to control the exchange of electric energy on the basis of the data.
