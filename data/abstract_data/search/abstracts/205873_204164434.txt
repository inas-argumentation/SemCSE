Preliminary Analysis on Race of Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae in Yunnan and Screening of Differential Varieties
One hundred and fifteen strains of Xanthomonas oryzae pv.oryzae (Xoo)on rice were collected from 40 counties in Yunnan Province. Based on virulence analysis using a set of near isogenic lines,each harboring a single resistance gene,the uppermost fully open leaves were inoculated by clipping method. Nine rice near isogenic lines were selected as a set of differential varieties for identifing Xoo pathotype(race) including IRBB1,IRBB3,IRBB4,IRBB5, IRBB7,IRBB13,IRBB14,IRBB18 and IRBB21. The strains were grouped into 25 races, designated YN1 N25 tentatively.Among them YN1,YN13 and YN21 were predominant. The race YN13 strains distributed in 8 counties of Chixiong,Yuxi,Honghe,Lijiang cities; The race YN21 strains distributed in Dali and Honghe prefecture and so on. The diversity and distribution of Xoo race in Yunnan were proably associated with the geographical conditions
