The Common Fund Doctrine: Coming of Age in the Law of Insurance Subrogation
The "American Rule" the euphemistic title given to the attorney fee allocation doctrine associated with American jurisprudence has been the subject of much debate and criticism. Pursuant to the "American Rule," each party is obligated to pay its own attorney's fees, regardless of the outcome of the litigation.
