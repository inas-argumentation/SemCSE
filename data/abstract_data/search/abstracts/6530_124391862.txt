Verification of Integral Thermal Radiation Spectrum Model for Gas Thermal Spraying Jet Particles with Simulated Experiments
Rassmatrivaiutsia problemy, sviazannye s verifikatsiei matematicheskoi modeli integral'nogo spektra teplovogo izlucheniia chastits gazotermicheskoi strui, ispol'zuemoi dlia napyleniia zashchitnykh pokrytii na tekhnicheskie izdeliia. Dlia podtverzhdeniia adekvatnosti matematicheskoi modeli integral'nogo spektra teplovogo izlucheniia potoka chastits eksperimental'nomu spektru, registriruemomu lineinym fotopriemnikom na vykhode izmeritel'nogo trakta spektrofotometra, ispol'zovan i usovershenstvovan ranee razrabotannyi optoelektronnyi imitator teplovogo izlucheniia chastits gazotermicheskoi strui napyleniia. Imitatsiiu dannogo izlucheniia osushchestvliaet izluchatel' sostoiashchii iz gruppy blizko zakreplennykh drug k drugu miniatiurnykh lampochek nakalivaniia. V imitatsionnykh eksperimentakh gistogramma raspredeleniia temperatur izluchaiushchikh elementov (lampochek) realizuetsia razbieniem gruppy lampochek na podgruppy, v kazhdoi iz kotorykh pri ustanovlenii neobkhodimogo znacheniia protekaiushchego cherez nikh toka obespechivaetsia trebuemoe znachenie temperatury. Rezul'taty imitatsionnykh eksperimentov podtverdili adekvatnost' modeli integral'nogo spektra teplovogo izlucheniia potoka chastits. Kliuchevye slova: integral'nyi spektr teplovogo izlucheniia, potok chastits, gazotermicheskoe napylenie pokrytii, optoelektronnyi imitator, spektrofotometr. In this paper, we consider problems related to the verification of integral thermal radiation spectrum mathematical model for particles of gas thermal jet used for spraying of protective coatings on technical parts. Previously developed optoelectronic thermal radiation simulator for gas thermal spraying jet particles has been used and improved to confirm the adequacy of integral thermal radiation spectrum mathematical model to the experimental spectrum, recorded by a linear photodetector at the output of spectrophotometer measuring channel. Simulation of gas thermal jet particles thermal radiation is provided by a radiator constructed as a group of miniature electric bulbs closely fixed to each other. A temperature distribution histogram of emitting elements is formed by splitting the electric bulbs into subgroups and setting the desired value of electric current for each subgroup, thus providing the desired temperature. The results of simulated experiments confirm the adequacy of integral thermal radiation spectrum model for particle flow.
