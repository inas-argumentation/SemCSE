Structural Improvement for DTII Tripper Cars of Belt Conveyor
The annual yield of iron ore concentrate is 3. 1 million tons and the annual handling capacity of crushing system is 11 million tons in Barun Mining Co. Ltd. of Baotou Steel. With several years of production exploration,it is found out that the main failures of crushing system are at belt conveyor and the main faults of belt conveyor are about tripper cars. In the paper,the structural transformation for tripper cars aiming at the practical problems in operations are mainly introduced,with which the failure rate of tripper cars is reduced.
