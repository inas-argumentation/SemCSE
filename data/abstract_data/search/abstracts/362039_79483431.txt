Population education models and strategies.
Focus is on 31 models of population education for teacher training training evaluation and clarification of values. Some of the models were developed in the United States; others were designed by educators in the Philippines. The models were compiled in the effort to provide a guide for population education program development in the Philippines. There are 3 major sections to the report. 7 workshop models and strategies for teacher training in population education are presented in the 1st section. These models include self development and mobile approaches and deal with clarifying the roles and skills of administrators and teachers in implementing population education programs. 11 models for evaluating teacher training in population education are presented in the 2nd section. Some of the models are to be used in evaluating the training schemes described in the 1st section while others are more general strategies developed by sources including the United States Civil Service Commission and Daniel Stufflebeam of the Ohio State University Evaluation Center. 13 strategies for value clarification in population education are provided in the 3rd section. These strategies encompass human sexuality contraceptive use family size and delayed versus early marriage.
