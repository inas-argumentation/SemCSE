Reflection: the Key Factor for the Perfection of A Teacher's Professional Expertise
"Reflection is an integral part of becoming professional." Reflection on his teaching practice is the foundation for a teacher's becoming professional.A teacher's consciousness,action,ability,will and level of reflection reflect his level of professionalism.The connotation,interpretation,importance,function,methods,types and content of reflection,and the puzzles encountered in reflection,are explored and summerized.
