The Dual Influence of Non tariff Barrier on Chinese Foreign Trade
It is both blighted and positive that Non tariff barrier affects foreign trade of China.Non tariff barrier has the export of products of Chinese enterprises restricted by all kinds of barriers in international market,resulting in their massive losses,which is becoming worse with time passing by.Meanwhile,Non tariff barrier impels Chinese enterprises to advance the technical innovation,implement the Intellectual property,heighten manageable level,upgrade the domain and production framework,carry through combination and cooperation between enterprises,improve environment,wend connotative development way,and enhance investigation and research to international market,which are all conducive to increase the competition power of Chinese enterprises in international market,improve the future complexion of trade export and enlarge greater profiting space in both domestic and foreign markets.
