A Comparative Analysis of Venlafaxine and Paroxetine in the Treatment of Depression
Objective To study the venlafaxine and paroxetine clinical curative effect for the treatment of depression. Methods Our hospital from 2012 to 2013 treated in our hospital at 76 patients with depression, according to the different clinical treatment to 76 cases of patients, randomly divided into two groups, each group of 38 patients, for the treatment of patients of group A practical writing rafa sheen, paroxetine treatment in group B patients, two groups of treatment for 8 weeks. During treatment with Hamilton depression scale(HAMD) to assess the clinical efficacy of two groups of treatment. Results Group A efficient and cure rate were 80.50% and 34.57% respectively, and the effectiveness of group B and the cure rate was 81.20% and 33.33% Two groups in the efficient and cure rate were similar, both differences(P0.05) the clinical curative effect. Conclusion Venlafaxine and paroxetine treatment the clinical curative effect of depression were similar, are effective, safe inhibitor, can promote the use.
