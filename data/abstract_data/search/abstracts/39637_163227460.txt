Musical Diplomacy of Emperor Zhao Ji and Eastward Spreading of Chinese Music during Song Dynasty
The Korean culture has been under the deep influence of Chinese one since the ancient time. As the aspect of music, during the Tang and Song Dynasties, a number of musical instruments, music books, music dance were introduced into Korea with yanyue (banquet music) and yayue (ritual music) they made up a part of Korean traditional music. The music treatise by Zhu Xi and Cai Yuanding were looked as principle, and the music opinion of "mean concordant" as the aesthetic criterion. It is pity that there are few Chinese scholars to research the Chinese historical materials of music that existed in Korea music, even translation is rarely. On the other way, the Scholars from Korea, Japan, and some Britain and American has been to use the literature, archaeological music cultural relic, to do some comparative analysis of Chinese and Korean music. It is also introduced the research work on the musical intercourse between China and Korea in Song Dynasty by Professor Keith Pratt from East Asia Department, Durham University in Britain. The present writer wishes to arise the scholars' attention to Korea music historical research.
