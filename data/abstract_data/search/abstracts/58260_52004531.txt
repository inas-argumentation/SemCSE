Smart Attendance Monitoring System (SAMS) A Face Recognition Based Attendance System for Classroom Environment
In present academic system, regular class attendance of students' plays a significant role in performance assessment and quality monitoring. The conventional methods practised in most of the institutions are by calling names or signing on papers, which is highly time consuming and insecure. This article presents the automatic attendance management system for convenience or data reliability. The system is developed by the integration of ubiquitous components to make a portable device for managing the students' attendance using Face Recognition technology.
