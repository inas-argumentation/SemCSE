Notice of RetractionPaper empirical study on the impact of RMB exchange rate on Chinese textile import and export
Since July 21, 2005, with the government of China began to implement the managed floating exchange rate system, based on the supply and demand of market with reference to "A Basket of Currencies" RMB is under the pressure of appreciation against U.S. dollar, it has become a critical factor that constraints the growth in textile and apparel's imports and exports. Based on the monthly data of China textile imports and exports and exchange rates of RMB/USD from Jun 2006 to May 2008, this paper analyzes the relationship between the two variables by using econometrics methods such as ordinary least squares, error correction model and Granger causality Test.
