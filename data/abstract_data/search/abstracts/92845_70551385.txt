Driver Rehabilitation Service in Hong Kong: New Challenges and Opportunities
Rehabaid Centre has been a pioneer in Driver Rehabilitation (DR) for people with disabilities in Hong Kong since 1995. Being able to drive promotes well being and is a positive indicator of health and maintaining social network. With appropriate use of driving aids and specific training, people with physical disabilities can regain their independence and freedom through driving. Rehabaid Centre has set up a database to systemically review its Driver Rehabilitation Specialty Service. Descriptive statistics were used to compare patterns and distributions for discussion in clinical meetings. Peer reviews and client feedbacks were obtained at the end of each session of the programme. The results suggest that the service demand by drivers of commercial vehicles such as taxis and container trucks is increasing. The authors' clinical observations indicate that some disabled drivers have inadequate knowledge and bad habits in using the driving aids. To meet the service demand, the authors have devised checklists on the specific occupational tasks of different drivers. They have also prepared educational materials on topics such as methods of loading wheelchairs, commonly used driving aids etc. in the form of print or online pamphlets to enhance clients' driving competency and safety. Some of their DR clients have multiple needs other than driving for work or convenience. Referral to other Rehabaid community rehabilitation specialty services such as Cognitive Rehabilitation, Disability Coping Counselling, Domiciliary Occupational Therapy, Wheelchair Prescription and Training can offer a one stop solution to meet their complex needs for better community integration.
