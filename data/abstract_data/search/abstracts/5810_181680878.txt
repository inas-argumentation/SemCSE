Inibicao comportamental infantil: adaptacao portuguesa de uma medida situacional (Lab TAB) Behavioral Inhibition in Childhood: Adaptation of a Situational Measure (Lab TAB) to an European Portuguese Version
Child temperament assessment strategies typically focus on parent, teacher and self ratings, which may be a limitation to obtaining more ecologically valid measures. Several researchers propose the additional use of observational methods, because they provide a direct description of the child's functioning within different environmental contexts. The lack of a situational assessment of temperament in Portugal has led to the relevance of the adaptation of the Laboratory Temperament Assessment Battery (Lab TAB) as the focus of this investigation. method: In our study, we included 126 children between 3 and 9 years of age and their parents. The evaluation of the temperament of the child was made by parent report (the Behavioral Inhibition Questionnaire) and through laboratory observation, with Lab TAB episodes.
