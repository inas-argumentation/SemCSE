Learning Multivariate Polynomials from Substitution and Equivalence Queries
It has been shown in previous recent work that multiplicity au tomata are predictable from multiplicity and equivalence queries. In this paper we generalize related notions in a matrix representation and obtain a basis for the solution of a number of open problems in learn ability theory. Membership queries are generalized to \substitution" queries for learning non boolean functions and provide the value of the target function for a given input. In particular, using substitution and equivalence queries, we prove the learnability of the boolean XOR of terms, XOR decision trees, decision trees with integer variables and less than conditions, multivariate polynomials over a nite eld and rational functions over a xed nite eld. We also provide results for the case of innnite or large elds.
