Design and Fulfillment of Festival Activities for Kindergarten Kids in Top Class
Festival is a special kind of culture expression,festival activities are effective ways for young children to carry on the cultural adaptation successfully.In order to design and fulfillment of festival activities,first,we should take the traditional Chinese festival primarily,and the memorial day for auxiliary;secondly,the thematic education activity we design should center on festival connotation and demands of young children;thirdly,we should pay attention to the role of potential educational factors,and innovate the implementation form of theme activity;fourthly,we should create a good kindergarten cultural environments for young children to experience festive atmosphere.In doing so,we can facilitate young children' personalized development,innovate the contents of festival activities and improve the professional development level of teachers.
