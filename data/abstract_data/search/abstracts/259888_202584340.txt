THE EFFECTS OF CULTURAL INTELLIGENCE ON EMPLOYEES JOB PERFORMANCE THE MEDIATING ROLE OF AFFECTIVE COMMITMENT
In the era of globalization, multinational companies (MNCs) play a crucial role in providing opportunities to the invested countries in boosting its economy growth and also its citizen's welfare. However, one of the main concerns would be the diversified cultures among employees who come from various countries might lead to conflicts and misunderstandings that later would affect working relationship and job performance. With this, the study intends to find the impact of cultural intelligence that focuses on four dimensions (behavioral, cognitive, metacognitive, and motivational) on employees' job performance in MNCs. Furthermore, the mediating effect of affective commitment on the relationship between cultural intelligence and job performance will be examined too. A total sample of 210 usable data were collected from employees working in MNCs in Melaka and Kuala Lumpur using questionnaire approach and is analyzed with SmartPLS. The results showed that affective commitment partially mediates the effect of behavioral, motivation, and cognitive cultural intelligence on employees' job performance. Meanwhile, full mediation of affective commitment is observed over metacognitive cultural intelligence and employees' job performance. The findings will assist MNCs in formulating effective strategies in nurturing and developing employees' job performance. Field of Research: cultural intelligence, behavioral, cognitive, metacognitive, motivational, affective commitment, job performance.
