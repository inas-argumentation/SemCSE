Services and the New Economy: Data Needs and Challenges
For various reasons, outputs and intermediate inputs (in real terms) in service industries have not been measured well by statistical agencies. The U.S. Canada and Mexico will soon be providing value data for perhaps 500 service sector industries in the near future but adequate price deflators do not exist for most of these industries. The present paper discusses why it is important to measure the prices of service industry outputs accurately. In sections 4 9 of the paper, an attempt is made to classify some 506 NAICS service sector industries according to the difficulties involved in collecting constant quality prices for the outputs of these industries. Section 10 provides a summary of some of the biggest measurement difficulties.
