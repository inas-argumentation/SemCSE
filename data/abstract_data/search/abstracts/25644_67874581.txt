Smart Irrigation System Based Thingspeak and Arduino
Information and communication technology has contributed to the development of many sectors, particularly in the agricultural sector through the effective transfer and exchange of information across multiple media, such as the Internet. In this context and in this paper an IOT platform based on ThingSpeak and Arduino is developed and tested where the goal is the farmer will be able to control the irrigation by using a pc or smartphone from anywhere and anytime, to monitoring the water parameter and reduce his efforts and also to optimize the use of water.the results test show the success of the system idea.
