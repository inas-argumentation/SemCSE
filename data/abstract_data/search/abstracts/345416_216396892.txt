Design of Agricultural Products Information E commerce Platform
With the continuous development of Internet e commerce at home and abroad, people choose to shop their favorite goods through the e commerce platform. This paper for the sale of agricultural products to build an e commerce platform, merchants can be on line trading operations of agricultural products, a good solution to the user and business needs. Agricultural product information sales platform website front use ASP development technology, background using C language programming, database using ACCESS for compilation and storage of valid data. This design details the main methods and steps of the front page design of the agricultural product information e commerce platform and the back office design of the administrator, through the demand analysis and feasibility analysis, through the overall design of the system, to form a complete design plan for the agricultural product information sales platform.
