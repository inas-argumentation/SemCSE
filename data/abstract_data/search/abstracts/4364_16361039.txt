Insider Trading and Market Efficiency with Risk and Ambiguity Aversion
We extend Chau and Vayanos' (Chau and Vayanos, 2008) analysis of an asset market with a strategic insider to the case in which she is risk averse. As she finds it optimal to trade more aggressively and reveal her private information at a faster pace than her risk neutral counterpart, risk aversion is beneficial to the efficiency of the market. As her optimal strategy is identified by a min max choice mechanism, according to which in any round of trading she first identifies the worst liquidity shock and then selects the optimal market order against such shock, our analysis also represents an ambiguity averse informed trader who faces Knightian uncertain over market conditions and seeks to implement a robust trading strategy.
