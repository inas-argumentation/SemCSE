Morphologic Variations of Palmaris Longus Muscle in Thais
Palmaris longus (PL) muscle is a superficial forearm flexor with a slender shape. Absent PL muscle has been reported of normal hand function without defect. Academically, this study is to investigate the number of cases of agenesis of PL muscle including classified PL muscle variations in Thais. Eighty three Thai cadavers were formaldehyde fixed, dissected, identified, and classified. There were shown anatomical variations such as complete agenesis, aberrant attachment at insertion, and duplication tendon. Firstly, PL muscle agenesis was the most frequent variation: bilateral agenesis (2 cadavers) and unilateral agenesis (1 cadaver) Agenesis of PL muscle was found more in the bilateral side and in the female. The left side of PL muscle was absent more than the right side. Secondly, PL tendon was inserted at the same site of abductor pollicis brevis muscle. Eventually, a duplication of PL muscle was also exhibited. Accordingly, anatomical variants of PL are significant knowledge for reconstructive or plastic surgeries.
