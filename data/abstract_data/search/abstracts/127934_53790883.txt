Lead encephalopathy in children
Summary 1. Five cases of plumbism are described in children of the same family. In three of them cerebral symptoms were manifest. One of the children had retinal changes. 2. The diagnosis of lead encephalopathy was made by the encephalitic symptoms plus general signs of lead poisoning, such as stippling of red blood cells, presence of reticulocytes in the blood stream, a lead line in the long bones on x ray examination, and the detection of lead in urine, feces, and cerebrospinal fluid. 3. The lead sulphide scarification test has been found positive in four out of our five cases and is considered by us of diagnostic value. 4. Biopsy of the gums in one case showed lead deposits. 5. Encephalography showed marked cortical atrophy. 6. The source of lead in our cases was the burning of battery casings for heating purposes.
