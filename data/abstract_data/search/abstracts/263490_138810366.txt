Effect of B Content on the Morphology of TiB_2 in Ti 50Al xB Alloys
The microstructure and phase constitution of Ti 50Al xB(at%)alloys produced by in situ growing method were investigated by XRD and SEM respectively The results show that these alloys are composed of TiAl and TiB 2 phase, and boron additions to TiAl alloys introduce the refractory compound TiB 2 in variety of morphologies The morphologies of TiB 2 change from convoluted flakes to needles to plates and eventually to particles as the B content increases from 0 4 at% to 1 4 at% Growth mechanism and evolution of TiB 2 morphology were analyzed according to TiB 2 crystal structure and growth condition
