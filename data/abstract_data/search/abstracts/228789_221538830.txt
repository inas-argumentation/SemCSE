Correction
EVOLUTION Correction for "Resolving conflict in eutherian mammal phylogeny using phylogenomics and the multispecies coalescent model," by Sen Song, Liang Liu, Scott V. Edwards, and Shaoyuan Wu, which appeared in issue 37, September 11, 2012, of Proc Natl Acad Sci USA (109:14942 14947; first published August 28, 2012; 10.1073/ pnas.1211733109) The authors note the following error: The names of two species, Macaque (Macaca mulatta) and Wallaby (Macropus eugenii) were inadvertently transposed as taxon labels for 21 genes in our article's 447 gene dataset of Song et al. The genes in question are as follows: EP300, DSTYK, CYP7B1, GOLGA1, MOCOS, PRKG1, NR4A2, RBM6, TAOK1, SLC5A12, E2F7, SPG20, PIK3R1, RHOBTB1, C6orf105, SDCCAG8, SNX25, FCHSD2, ADNP, GAN, and ZNF507. We apologize for this mistake and thank S. Mirarab and T. Warnow for pointing it out. The errors have no effect on the results and conclusions of our article. We have deposited our article's 447 gene dataset with corrected taxon labels in the Dryad database: www.datadryad.org with the following identifier: 10.5061/dryad.3629v.
