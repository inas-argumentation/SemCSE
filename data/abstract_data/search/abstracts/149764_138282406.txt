Hydraulic Cement Based Binders for Mass Stabilization of Organic Soils
Mass stabilization is a new, environmentally friendly soil improvement method where stabilizer is mixed into peat, mud or soft clay. Mass stabilization is carried out by a mixing tool which is typically installed on an excavator machine. Mixing is done both in horizontal and vertical directions so that a homogeneous strengthened soil slab is formed due to effect of stabilizer. This method can even be applied to peat, which has not been possible to stabilize previously. Stabilization of organic soils is much more demanding than stabilization of clay. The traditionally used lime and lime cement mixtures are not necessarily the most suitable. Instead, mixtures of cement and granulated blast furnace slag are often used. These new binders have been extensively researched and tested both in the laboratory and in the field. The results have been encouraging showing that it is possible to stabilize organic soils cost effectively and to provide environmentally friendly solutions.
