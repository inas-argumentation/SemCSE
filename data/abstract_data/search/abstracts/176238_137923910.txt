Research on polyurethane elastomer to improve heat resistance
In order to improve the heat resistance of thermoplastic polyurethane elastomer(TPU),composite material MUF/TiO2 TPU was achieved by adding nano TiO2 powder(MUF/TiO2)which was coated by melamine modified urea formaldehyde resin to the TPU.The chemical structure,crystallinity,thermal properties and mechanical properties of composite materials were characterized and determined by FT IR,WXRD,SEM,DSC,TGA respectively.The results showed that the crystallization of TPU changed a little when MUF/TiO2 was added.The grain size was slightly reduced,while the heat resistance was improved greatly.The elongation and tensile strength of modified TPU first increased and then decreased.
