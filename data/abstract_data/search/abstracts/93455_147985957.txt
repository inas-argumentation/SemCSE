Subjects Archaeology Based on 30 Years Pragmatic Meaning Evolution of "Teacher Mobility"and Their Trust Games
Through archaeological study of pragmatic meaning Evolution of "Teacher Mobility"in China during last 30 years,the micro subjects appear in shift of decentralization and centralization of political power. With that,the struggling between political power and intellectuals on"social believes",and between political power and micro subjects on"efficiency of governance",are upgraded. Therefore suggestions are given to constructing legitimate"contract system"and flexible"bureaucracy"
