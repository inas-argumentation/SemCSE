Static versus interactive online resources about dementia: A comparison of readability scores
IntroductIon Online health information In the United States of America (USA) the vast majority of the adult population uses the Internet: a report by the Pew Internet Project shows that as of 2015, 84 per cent of adults in the USA are Internet users. Older adults are also spending time online: of adults aged 65 and older, 58 per cent use the Internet. The Internet is frequently used as a source of health information in both the USA, where 72 per cent of internet users say that they have looked online for health information, and in the United Kingdom, where nearly half of the individuals who searched the Internet within the previous three months used it to search for medical or health related information.
