Billionaires, Organizations, and Federalism: Developments in the Study of American Inequality
We are about two decades into a renewed focus in political science on the relationship between political and economic inequality. Galvanized by political and economic trends and facilitated by institutional initiatives, a bevy of scholarship has investigated whether the United States is a plutocracy as well as mechanisms that groups use to expand or mitigate plutocratic influence. Debates persist about the extent to which the American political system is captured by the rich (Gilens and Page 2014) but trends in socioeconomic outcomes tell a clear story of a winner take all economy that cannot be well explained by democratic processes.
