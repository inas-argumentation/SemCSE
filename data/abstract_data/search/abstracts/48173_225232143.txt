Overnight stock returns, intraday returns, and firm specific investor sentiment
In this study, we propose a new index for measuring firm specific investor sentiment using overnight and intraday stock returns. We use actual equity data to construct the firm level investor sentiment index and find that the new index has characteristics expected of a sentiment measure. In addition, we propose a novel sentiment weighted trading strategy and apply it to momentum and short term reversal strategies. We find that the sentiment weighted trading strategy generates better performance in momentum and short term reversal strategies. The sentiment weighted trading strategy's superior performance is evidence that our firm level investor sentiment index possesses predictive powers with regard to future returns.
