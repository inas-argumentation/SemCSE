Signatures of non Abelian statistics in nonlinear Coulomb blockade transport.
Signatures of the non Abelian statistics of quasiparticles in the n=5/2 quantum Hall state are predicted to be present in the current voltage characteristics of tunneling through one or two quantum Hall puddles of Landau filling n(a) embedded in a bulk of filling n(b) with (n(a),n(b)(2,5/2) and (n(a),n(b)(5/2,2)
