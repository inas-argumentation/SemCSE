Experiences of patient centred communication between nurse and patient: in the care of middle age patients with gastroscopy
Objective: In care of middle age patients with gastroscopy,to investigate the role of patient centered communication between nurse and patients.Methods: 640 cases undergoing gastroscopy were divided into control group(CG) and patient centered nurse patient communication group(PNCG).Traditional communication was applied in CG,while patient centered nurse patient communication was carried out for PNCG.The psychological status,psychological reaction and success rate of gastroscopy were recorded.Results: All cases in CG and PNCG succeeded at the first time.However,the settling time and endoscopy time in PNCG were significantly shorter than CG.Meanwhile,compared with CG,there was increased satisfaction in PNCG.During gastroscopy,the patients in PNCG showed more stable blood pressure and pulse,as well as better psychological states.Conclusion:Nurse patient communication based on patient center is an effective way in gastroscopy of middle age patients.
