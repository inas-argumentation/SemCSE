Meta Analysis for Efficacy of Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease Treated by Lansoprazole
OBJECTIVE To systematically evaluate the therapeutic efficacy of lansoprazole vs other proton pump inhibitors in the treatment of gastroesophageal refluxdisease( GERD),so as to provide reference for its clinical medication. METHODS The randomized controlled trials( RCT) that compare the efficacy of lansoprazole and other proton pump inhibitors in the treatment of GERD were collected from CNKI,Wanfang,VIP,Pub Med,Medline,Cochrane Library. The clinical symptom remission rate and endoscopic cure rate were used as the evaluation index,and meta analysis software Rev Man5. 0 in Cochrane Collaboration was used to do the data processing and analysis. RESULTS A total of 13 Chinese RCT and 6 English RCT were included,involving 9 143 patients. Results of Meta analysis showed that: there were no difference in the clinical symptom remission rate between lansoprazole,omeprazole [OR 0. 71,95% CI( 0.33,1. 54),P 0. 05],and rabeprazole [OR 0. 59,95% CI( 0. 26,1. 37),P 0. 05],but lansoprazole not as good as esomeprazole[OR =0. 38,95% CI( 0. 24,0. 62),P 0. 05],only one RCT compared with pantoprazole,no difference between the results. There were no difference in the endoscopic cure rate between lansoprazole,omeprazole [OR 1. 08,95% CI( 0. 86,1. 35),P 0. 05 ,pantoprazole [OR 0. 72,95% CI( 0. 44,1. 16),P 0. 05],but lansoprazole not as good as rabeprazole [OR 0. 44,95% CI( 0. 22,0.85),P 0. 05],esomeprazole [OR 0. 48,95% CI( 0. 33,0. 70),P 0. 05] CONCLUSION In the treatment of GERD,there were no difference in the therapeutic efficacy between lansoprazole,omeprazole and pantoprazole,but lansoprazole not as good as esomeprazole,rabeprazole. Large scale,multiple center studies are required to validate those conclusions.
