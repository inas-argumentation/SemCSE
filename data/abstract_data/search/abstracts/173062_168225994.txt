The Value Chain for Corporate Real Estate
This is an overview of selected developments and field experience in 1999 2000. Topics include: corporate real estate as a factor of production, not just a cost to be minimized; the role of portfolio management in linking corporate real estate to core business decisions; coping when the organization chart becomes out of date in today's rapidly changing environment; information management and data base needs; prioritization of projects for maintenance, repair and alterations; and other current trends.
