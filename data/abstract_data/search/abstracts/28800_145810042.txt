Motivational Interviewing: Preparing People for Change.
Why do people change? In time, most people with alcohol or drug problems adjust to a new style of life, give up their damaging habits and move on often without any professional interventions. This observation is the starting point of an enquiry that has had far reaching consequences for the way treatments for harmful behaviours are conducted throughout the world.
