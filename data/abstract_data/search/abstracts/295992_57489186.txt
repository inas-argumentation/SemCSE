A Review of the Factors of Lexical Inference of Second Language Reading
Lexical inference is a significant process in second language incidental vocabulary acquisition in L2 reading condition.This paper provides a general view of the researches on lexical inferencing in L2 reading condition both at home and aboard.And two main factors that influence lexical inferencing in L2 reading are summarized here: the textual factor and the learners own factor.The textual factor includes characteristics and density of the unknown words,topic familiarity and context.The learners' factor includes language proficiency,vocabulary knowledge levels and cross language transfer.The paper concludes by a discussion of the implications the vocabulary inferencing has for L2 reading instruction with an aim to provide help to the development and teaching of vocabulary and to improve language learners' reading comprehension and incidental vocabulary acquisition ability.
