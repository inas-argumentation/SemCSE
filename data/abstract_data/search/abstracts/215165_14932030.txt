An energy space finite element approach for elliptic Dirichlet boundary control problems
In this paper we present a finite element analysis for a Dirichlet boundary control problem where the Dirichlet control is considered in a convex closed subspace of the energy space $H^{1/2}\Gamma $H1/2(G) As an equivalent norm in $H^{1/2}\Gamma $H1/2(G) we use the energy norm induced by the so called Steklov Poincare operator which realizes the Dirichlet to Neumann map, and which can be implemented by using standard finite element methods. The presented stability and error analysis of the discretization of the resulting variational inequality is based on the mapping properties of the solution operators related to the primal and adjoint boundary value problems, and their finite element approximations.
