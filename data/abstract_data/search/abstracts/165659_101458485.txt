A Rapid Method for Identification of Untreated Cortex Cinnamomi, Rhizoma Gastrodiae and Their Confused Samples by FT Raman Spectroscopy
In this paper, cortex cinnamomi, rhizoma gastrodiae and their confused samples were directly identification by means of NIR FT Raman Spectroscopy respectively for the first time. The results indicated that the different raw Chhinese herbs had different characters in vibration frequency of Raman functional groups. Cortex cinnamomi had strong vibration bands at 1600 and 1330 cm 1 while its confused sample's strong bands at 1624 cm 1 and 1597 cm 1 and the weak band at 1330 cm 1. The C H vibration band at 2914 cm 1 of rhizoma gastrodiae shifted 20 cm 1 to 2934 cm 1 of false rhizoma gastrodiae which had strong Raman shift at 1605 cm 1, but there wasn't any Raman shift at the same site of Rhizoma gastrodiae. Based on the differences, the Chinese herbs can be identified. The method may be used in non destructive detection, and it is relatively more precise and quicker.
