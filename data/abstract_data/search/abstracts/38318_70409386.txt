Book Review: The Scientific Basis of Medicine Annual Reviews 1964
Psychosomatic Aspects ofNeoplastic Disease Proceedings ofthe Third International Conference ofthe International Psychosomatic Cancer Study Group, Cambridge, England, July 22 26 1963 edited byDM Kissen andLL LeShan pp 231 21s London: Pitman Medical 1964 This is a collection of papers of varying quality, some of which show interesting psychological associations with neoplastic disorders though I doubt whether the most enthusiastic would claim them to be causal. The demonstrations that handling young rats can affect the subsequent transplantation of tumours (confirmed by Professor Mitchell in a discussion at the symposium) and the finding that women with cancer of the breast were more extraverted than controls, suggest that adrenocortical factors may indeed play a part. If this is so it may provide a basis for psychological influence on neoplastic disorders.
