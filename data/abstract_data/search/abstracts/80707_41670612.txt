A Variable Order hp Adaptive Pseudospectral Method for Solving Optimal Control Problems
An hp adaptive pseudospectral method is presented for solving optimal control problems. This method adjusts both the mesh spacing and the degree of the polynomial on each mesh interval until a specified error tolerance is satisfied. In regions of relatively high curvature, convergence is achieved by refining the mesh, while in regions of relatively low curvature, convergence is achieved by increasing the degree of the polynomial. Using four examples, the hp adaptive pseudospectral scheme is shown to be more efficient than either a pure p or a pure h method.
