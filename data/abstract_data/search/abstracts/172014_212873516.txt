Gendhing Pepeling: Media Dakwah Melalui Budaya Lokal Masyarakat Jawa
Preaching is not only done through traveling lectures, but can also be done through songs or gendhing (in Javanese terms) Submission of dakwah through songs has been a local wisdom of the people since the time of Sunan Kalijaga. At present, Gendhing Papeling by Ki Anom Suroto is included in the Javanese song which has the influence to invite people to worship. This study aims to determine the dimensions of religious values in the Papeling song lyrics. In addition, this research also tries to convey the form of dakwah in Islam with nuances of local culture. This research is included in qualitative research with a type of phenomenology. The data source in this research is the song Pepeling by Ki Anom Suroto. Data collection in this research was carried out by interviewing Javanese culture activists, literature study to obtain information about research objects, and searching the internet. Data analysis in this study was carried out in 4 stages, namely (1) organizing all data or a comprehensive picture of the phenomena that had been collected, (2) reading and listening thoroughly to the recording of the Papeling song carefully and repeatedly, (3) translating the song in the form of Javanese into Indonesian, (3) analyzing the values contained in the gendhing papeling The results showed that Gendhing Papeling is a Javanese song that is rich in religious values. This song contains an invitation to carry out religious orders and become a 'mandatory' song when there are shadow puppet shows, campursari, ludruk, tayub, and other Javanese culture.
