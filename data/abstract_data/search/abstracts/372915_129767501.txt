A New velocity model for sand clay mixtures
A velocity model for clay sand mixtures has been developed in terms of the Kuster and Toksoz (1974) and Gassmann (1951) theories. In this model, the total gore space is assumed to be made of two parts: (1) pores associated with sand grains and (2) pores associated with clays (including bound water) The essential part of the model is the assumption that the geometry of the pores associated with sand grains is significantly different from that associated with clays. This new model can be used (1) to model the scattered porosity velocity behaviour, (2) to study the relationship between the effect of pore geometry and that of clays and, therefore, (3) to understand the physical mechanisme of the effect of clays on elastic wave propagation.
