Effect of storage temperature and moisture on the germination of papaya seeds
Seeds of papaya Carica papaya L. stored for 12 months at 15degC with 7.9 9.4% moisture content maintained their original germination. In contrast, many seeds stored cooler or drier lost viability, the losses occurring more rapidly at 20degC than at either 0degC or 15degC. The results are not compatible with the definitions of either orthodox or recalcitrant seed storage behaviour.
