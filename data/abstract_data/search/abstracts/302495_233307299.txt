Singular Perturbation based Reinforcement Learning of Two Point Boundary Optimal Control Systems
This work presents a technique for learning systems, where the learning process is guided by knowledge of the physics of the system. In particular, we solve the problem of the two point boundary optimal control problem of linear time varying systems with unknown model dynamics using reinforcement learning. Borrowing techniques from singular perturbation theory, we transform the time varying optimal control problem into a couple of time invariant subproblems. This allows the utilization of an off policy iteration method to learn the controller gains. We show that the performance of the learning based controller approximates that of the modelbased optimal controller and the accuracy of the approximation improves as the time horizon of the control problem increases. Finally, we provide a simulation example to verify the results of the paper.
