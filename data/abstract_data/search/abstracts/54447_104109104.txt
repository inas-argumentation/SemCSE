New phosphorus containing bis sulfone ligands
Derivatives of bis sulfone 1,3 bis{(p tolyl)SO2} 5 tertbutylbenzene 1, containing P(III) and P(V) were obtained and characterized in solution by multinuclear NMR spectroscopy. For two of the compounds [2,6 (p tolyl)SO2}24 t Bu C6H2]PCl2 2 and [2,6 (p tolyl)SO2}24 t BuC6H2]P(O)(H)OEt) 4 the crystal and molecular structures in solid state were also determined by single crystal X ray diffraction.
