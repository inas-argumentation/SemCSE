2. Introduction Optimization is of great importance in aeronautical design. Since small changes in geometry produce large changes in transonic flow characteristics and reductions in structural weight may lead to significant range increases, for example, it is critical to find a design that is very close to best a design that roughly resembles a good design may be a bad design. Therefore, optimization in some form is ubiquitous in aerospace: from aerodynamic shape optimization to structural design, optimal control, and trajectory optimization.
