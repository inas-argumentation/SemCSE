Abstract 18712: Fimasartan, an Angiotensin II Receptor Antagonist, Improves Heart Failure and Survival in Zebrafish Model
We developed zebrafish heart failure (HF) model and investigated the protecting role of an angiotensin II receptor antagonist, fimasartan (FMS, 200 mM) HF was induced by short term (12 hr) treatment of a known cardiotoxic drug, terfenadine (Ter, 20mM) in zebrafish larvae at 72 hour post fertilization. Markedly dilated ventricles, swelling of the chambers and reduced systolic function, all of which were the typical features of human HF, were observed in transgenic cmlc:GFP zebrafish, which express green fluorescent protein (GFP) in the cardiomyocytes.
