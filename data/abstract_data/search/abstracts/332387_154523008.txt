Issues in Accounting Information System and its Impact on the Information Quality
Information quality issues have become a critical concern for organizations. Inaccurate and incomplete information may affect the performance of a company. This research explores the issues in Accounting Information System and its impact on the information quality based on the information quality dimensions which are accuracy, timeliness, completeness, relevance and consistency. This study utilized the exploratory case study approach .The data were obtained through interview with the users of system in two private higher educational institution in Malaysia. The findings of this study show that the main issue in AIS in both college is mainly related to system error and this issue have considerable effect on information quality. In addition, it is found that training is important factor contributing on the information quality. This study contributes in theory and practice whereby it incorporates Model Delone and Mclean (1992) to see the issues in accounting information system and its impact on information quality. In terms of contribution towards policy, it provides discussions and findings for system users and organisation on the quality issues in Accounting Information System in terms of information quality and system quality. Future studies based on multiple case studies would enable generalizability of the findings.
