High Q silicon microsphere whispering gallery mode resonator fabricated by laser induced in fiber capillary instability
A method of fabricating silicon microsphere resonators feathered high quality factor whispering gallery modes of 0.8 x 10<sup>6</sup> is demonstrated. We fabricate the silicon microspheres from silica cladding/silicon core fiber based on the CO<inf>2</inf> laser induced in fiber capillary instability phenomenon.
