Summary: Conjugal Intimacy Layers
Despite the fact that clinical issues with intimacy are frequent in our practice, we lack technical references and tools for their appraisal, leaving room for our subjective positioning in most cases. The purpose of this work is to propose a means of investigating various forms of intimacy based on Shannon's information theory and its restatement according to Bateson, in such a manner that couples are required to assess each other in their way of life, which leads to a self reflexive process within the couple instead of evaluative interventions coming from the therapist.
