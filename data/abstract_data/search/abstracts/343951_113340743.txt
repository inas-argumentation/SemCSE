Trial Production of 30MnSiB Wire Rod for Prestressed Steel Rod
This paper describes the trial production course of 30MnSiB wire rod for prestressed steel rod from 150 mmx150 mm continuous casting billet.The results of test and the customers' usage are given.The results of practice production show that the development of 30MnSiB wire rod for prestressed steel wire is successful in the present equipments situation of our corporation.It can meet the customers' requirements.
