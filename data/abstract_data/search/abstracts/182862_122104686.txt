CONSTRUCTIVE WAVELET COEFFICIENTS MEASURING SMOOTHNESS THROUGH BOX SPLINES
We provide a constructive wavelet decomposition of L p (1 p 1) into box splines. To calculate wavelet coefficients, we apply the local polynomial L2 approximation and then quasi interpolation techniques. Furthermore, we characterize the Besov space, B q.Lq/ (qD.=dC 1= p/ 1 by the constructive wavelet coefficients with an explicit form. DeVore et al. have studied this characterization with a different proof; however, they have used a nonconstructive local approximation rather than the local L2 approximation when 0 q 1. Our explicit formula calculating wavelet coefficients can be directly employed for numerical implementations.
