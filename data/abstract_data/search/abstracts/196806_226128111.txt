Analysis of Shaanxi Free Trade Area on Xi' an Aviation Logistics
Shaanxi free trade zone is the third batch of approved Free Trade Zone in China, and also an important area covered by the belt and road strategy. Xi'an, as a hub connecting domestic and foreign countries in Northwest China, will inevitably undergo significant changes in the development of air logistics in the context of its free trade zone. This paper first analyzes the current situation the opportunities and challenges, the existing problems of Xi'an air logistics, and finally gives some development suggestions. Shaanxi free trade zone is the third batch of approved Free Trade Zone in China, and as a strategic Bridgehead City of the belt and road initiative, these two huge engines will have a huge impact on Xi'an logistics industry. Although the development trend of Xi'an air logistics is good in recent years, the construction of Shaanxi free trade zone and the strategic layout of "the belt and road" have higher requirements for Xi'an air logistics. Xi'an should make full use of its unique location advantages, transportation advantages and policy advantages to further improve the quality of air logistics, so as to promote the development of Shaanxi free trade zone. 1. At present, the service level of Xi'an air logistics is still low, and the satisfaction of some passenger goods transportation is not high. The collision of passenger boxes or bulk goods on the cargo turntable results in the damage of passenger goods and the decrease of customer satisfaction, which is a manifestation of low service level. This is not only in the aspect of aviation logistics, but also in all aspects of logistics. 2. Opportunities for Xi'an air logistics brought by Shaanxi Free Trade Zone 2.1 Brings considerable orders to logistics industry As a highly open special economic zone, the frontier of the bonded business field of the comprehensive bonded zone of the international port area integrates the functions of all the special customs supervision area policies, such as bonded zone and bonded logistics, bonded warehouse, bonded processing, commodity exhibition, inspection and testing, port logistics, and other functions. At present, the customs with the highest level, preferential policies, and complete functions are open Special regulatory areas. Stable economic development trend can play an important role in urban construction. In recent years, the economic situation of Shaanxi Province generally presents a stable upward trend, which is bound to bring a greater degree of logistics demand, while the free trade zone integrates domestic and foreign goods, while its bonded advantage can greatly improve the volume of goods, bringing huge resource increase and commercial orders for aviation logistics 2.2 Driving the development of airport economy Relying on the huge aviation hub to develop the airport economy is another opportunity brought by the construction of Shaanxi free trade zone. Xi'an Xianyang International airport is an important gateway for the development of export oriented economy in our province, and also an important window for countries to enter Xi'an and even the whole country. In the construction of free trade zone, the function of airport industry is becoming more and more perfect. Among them, the aircraft maintenance business is the most outstanding with an investment of 16.3 billion RMB in 2012, involving such projects as airport manufacturing, aviation logistics, aviation services and infrastructure.
