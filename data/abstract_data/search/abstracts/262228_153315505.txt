Using Graph Analytics for Predictive Modeling in Life Insurance
This paper discusses a specific example of utilizing graph analytics or social network analysis (SNA) in predictive modeling in the life insurance industry. The methods of social network analysis are applied to agents that share compensation, and the results are used to derive input variables for a model to predict the likelihood of certain behavior by insurance agents. Both SAS(r) code and SAS(r) Enterprise Miner TM are used to illustrate implementing different graph analytical methods. This paper assumes the reader is familiar with the basic process of creating predictive models using multiple (linear or logistic) regression, and, in some sections, familiarity with SAS Enterprise Miner.
