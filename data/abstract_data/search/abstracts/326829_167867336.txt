Mechanism of search and rescue in China Study on establishing China's social search and rescue force
China has been the biggest destination either in the seaborne transport cargoes and containers,and the SAR tasks in China search and rescue region have increased dramatically.In order to implement SAR obligations,China established its own marine SAR systems,managed by Inter Ministry Coordination Meeting Mechanism and China Maritime rescue co ordination Center(CMRCC) to co ordinate all the SAR agencies.Actually China have well accomplished the SAR tasks in recent years,but comparing with the USA,UK and Japan's SAR systems,China SAR mostly depend various governmental force,so I will review China's SAR system and compare to the experiences of the developed countries,giving some suggestions to develop the China's social SAR force to make China SAR system more efficiently and economical.
