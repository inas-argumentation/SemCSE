Strength and drying shrinkage of concrete containing high calcium fly ash
The strength and drying shrinkage of concrete containing high calcium fly ash (HCFA) and low calcium fly ash (LCFA) were tested and compared.The results show that HCFA can make more contributions to concrete strength than LCFA.When taken in concrete with proper quantity,HCFA can compensate the drying shrinkage of concrete,and accordingly reduce the cracking risk of concrete.Even in the dry circumstances and under the conditions of lower W/C ratio,the compensation is also in action.Based on the HCFA quality and concrete proportioning,the proper addition quantity of HCFA should be determined by experiments.Under the conditions given in this article,35% is the acceptable limit of addition quantity of HCFA.In General,the concretes with 25% HCFA show higher strength and better volume stability.
