Washout appearance in Gd EOB DTPA enhanced MR imaging: A differentiating feature between hepatocellular carcinoma with paradoxical uptake on the hepatobiliary phase and focal nodular hyperplasia like nodules
To identify the most reliable imaging features for differentiating hepatocellular carcinoma with paradoxical uptake on the hepatobiliary phase (HCCpara) from focal nodular hyperplasia (FNH) like nodules using Gd EOB DTPA enhanced MRI.
