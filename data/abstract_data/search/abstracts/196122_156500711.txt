Evaluation on Regional Economic Development Efficiency of Huangshi City Based on DEA
Based on the economic input and output situation of 7 districts and counties of Huangshi city in2011,2012,and 2013,the method of Data Envelopment Analysis is applied and the evaluating indicator system is established to evaluate the regional economic development efficiency of Huangshi city. The changes of economic development efficiency in every part of Huangshi city in the past two years are compared,their situations of input and output are analyzed,and the corresponding suggestions are brought forward.
