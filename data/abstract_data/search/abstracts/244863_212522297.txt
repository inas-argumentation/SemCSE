Food Hygiene and Handling Practices among Food Handlers in Cafes of Ambo University, Ehtiopia
Food hygiene remains a critical issue among professionals in the food service sector as well as consumers. This study was aimed to assess food hygiene awareness and handling practices among food handlers in Cafes of Ambo University, Ethiopia. Both primary and secondary types of data were used. Data were collected using open ended and close ended questionnaire methods. Multistage, purposive and random sampling methods were employed to determine 57 sample size. Data collected were analyzed using figure, frequency, percentage, tables, and explanation. The study revealed majority of food handlers have awareness of food hygiene. Regarding food hygiene practice, greater part of food handlers (77.2% were agreed they do not wash cooking utensils using hot water which significantly hinder food hygiene. Thus, the University should give food handlers a short term training of food hygiene practices especially on washing cooking utensils using hot water and working area after every procedure.
