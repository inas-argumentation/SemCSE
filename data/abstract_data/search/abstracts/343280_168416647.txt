The Study of Logistics Transportation Business Process Reengineering Based on Internet of Things
The paper based on the current situation of logistics transportation business process, development of internet of things technology theory and demand of logistics service optimization analyses the influence of technology application to logistics transportation business process on construction and function under internet of things environment. Combined with the improvement of logistics transportation business by internet of things technology, the article proposes a design scheme of logistics transportation business process reengineering and gives analysis to application of internet of things technology on the transportation business.
