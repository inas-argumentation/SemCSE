Erratum: Estimating mutual information [Phys. Rev. E 69, 066138 (2004)
The proof of Eq. (A5) in the Appendix of this paper [1] is incorrect. The correct statement is that the right hand side of Eq. (A5) is the only stationary solution of a Lagrangian variational problem that estimates the mutual information (MI) in terms of the covariance matrix. However, in general (except for Gaussian marginal distributions) it is not an extremal solution. In general, the infimum of MI is not reached in the space of functions admissible in the variational problem, whence it cannot show up as a solution. Lower bounds on the MI analogous to Eq. (A5) can be obtained only if one makes, in addition, assumptions on the marginal distributions. Details are given by Foster and Grassberger [2] This error has no effect on the other results of the paper.
