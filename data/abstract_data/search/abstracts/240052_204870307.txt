CONCEALER: Secure Time Varying Location Data Outsourcing
This paper proposes a model to outsource time varying spatial data (e.g. as produced by sensors) in encrypted form to an untrusted public cloud, where it can be accessed by third party services to provide location based services to users. Such services require aggregation queries over selected regions over time windows. While modern symmetric searchable encryption techniques (SSEs that exploit novel data structures over ciphertext) can be used, we argue that SSEs exhibit two shortcomings in our context. First, such techniques require specialized data structures that limit their usability/practicality in large scale deployments using database systems. Second, more importantly, existing SSEs leak data distributions, which in turn can leak the data records. We develop a system, entitled CONCEALER that overcomes the two shortcomings by exploiting carefully chosen encryption techniques to use indexes supported by database systems and by combining ways to add fake tuples in order to realize an efficient system that offers a higher level of security. We experimentally validate the efficiency of the proposed algorithms over a spatial temporal dataset (collected from a space space) and TPC H datasets, each of 136 Million rows, the size of which prior approaches have not scaled to.
