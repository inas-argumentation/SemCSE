Design and research of bank Internet financial product pricing model
With the rapid innovation of Internet information technology, the market competition environment of China's banking industry has undergone significant changes, and the traditional banking functions have gradually been weakened. Against this background, the survival and development of the traditional banking industry are facing great challenges. The reform of banking financial services must speed up the process. Various sectors of the national economy are beginning to invest in the Internet industry. Banking is no exception. With the help of Internet technology, we hope to expand the development path of the banking Internet finance industry.
