Covid 19 challenges and human resource management in organized retail operations
The study emphasizes on identifying and overcoming Covid 19 challenges, faced by human resource management (HRM) while managing personnel in organized retail stores Data for this study were drawn from a review of secondary sources on practical insights about Covid 19 challenges, suggesting tangible solutions to solve them, which should be addressed at the earliest to ensure successful retail operations avoiding unexpected chaos created by the Covid 19 pandemic (c) 2020, Universidad del Zulia All rights reserved
