Attempts to encompass multi methodological approaches usually amount to: "www criticism used to say xxx, but now yyy says zzz." And we are seldom confronted by a massive onslaught that demands reconceptualizing of the entire field. I suggested in an earlier review essay that Erhardt Giittgemanns' Offene Fragen zur Formgeschichte is such an onslaught, and that it demands opening biblical literary critical windows to some of the disciplines (semiotics, linguistics, social psychology) ignored in recent years. This essay, in many ways a sequel, also concerns primarily Giittgemanns, but also the work of the research team he heads, the "Generative Poetics" team at Bonn.
