Numerical simulation and analysis of soil nailed wall and pile anchor supporting structure for deep foundation excavation
The deformation mechanism of deep foundation excavation with two different supporting means(soil nailed wall and pile anchor) was numerically simulated and analyzed with finite element software.According to numerical simulation and analysis,the parameters such as displacement,stress,and bottom uplift of foundation pit with foregoing supportings were compared and analyzed.The result showed that in the case of soil nailed wall,the whole stress field,displacement field and horizontal displacement of foundation pit were very different from those with pile anchor supporting,and their force action characteristics were different;the characteristics of settlement after excavation and bottom uplift were similar without much alteration.
