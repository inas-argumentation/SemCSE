A Research on Modeling and Simulation of Eddy Current Separator
Based on the model of eddy current separator mainly used in solid waste reuse,tests according to the separation theory using mainly copper and aluminum has been carried out on the factors which effect the separation.First,a math as well as a physics model is given,then the Lorentz evolution equation is given which help solve the main magnetic repulsion.Particals with regular shapes such as balls and spheres are carefully selected with uniweight.The trajectory obtained both by numerical simulation and experimental method have been compared.At last,the error analysis is given.
