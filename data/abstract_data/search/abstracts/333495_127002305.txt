CHARLEVILLE MEZIERES TRAFFIC PLAN
THE AIM OF THE STUDY IS TO DEFINE A SYSTEM OF USING STREETS WHICH ENABLES ROAD USERS TO TRAVEL IN TOWN UNDER SATISFACTORY COMFORT AND SAFETY CONDITIONS. BASED ON ACCIDENT STATISTICS AND THE RESULTS OF A TRAFFIC FLOW SURVEY, TWO ONE WAY TRAFFIC PLANS WERE DRAWN UP. THEIR IMPLEMENTATION WOULD IMPROVE TRAFFIC FLOW CONDITIONS; THE MAIN OBJECT OF ONE OF THE PLANS IS A MODIFICATION IN THE LAYOUT OF JUNCTIONS, AN INCREASE IN THE NUMBER OF PARKING PLACES, FREE FLOW OF BUSES, AND CREATION OF A PEDESTRIANIZED SHOPPING ZONE.
