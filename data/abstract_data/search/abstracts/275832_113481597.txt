Research on the Influence of Piston Pin Offset on Piston Secondary Motion
Thermal profiles of piston skirt and piston stiffness matrix,which will be used for analysis,are obtained by analyzing thermal deformation and elastic deformation of internal combustion engine piston.Piston secondary motions under different piston pin offsets are analyzed by using AVL Glide,considering piston thermal deformation and elastic deformation.As results show,piston pin offset has a great influence on piston tilting angle.At last, 0.5mm is chosen as an appropriate piston pin offset from noise control perspective.
