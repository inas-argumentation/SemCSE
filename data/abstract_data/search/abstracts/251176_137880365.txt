Using Blast Furnace Iron Melt to Produce Gray Iron Ingot Mold with Big Height/Diameter Ratio
The configuration and technical requirements of a ingot mold casting with big height/diameter ratio was introduced. Casting method of the ingot was described in detail:adopting horizontal molding method and horizontal pouring process;using pressured gating system with section ratio as SFsprue:SFrunner:SFingates=1.4:1.2:(1.1~1.0);using venting riser with diameter of 50 mm;using furan resin sand for molding;the core iron made of nodular iron was fixed in the core box,the thickness of core sand lyer was of 40~50 mm;the ingot mold was poured by using iron melt from a blast furnace;the tapping temperature was controlled in the range of 1 420+ 20 ;0.3%~0.5% 75SiFe with grain size of 3~6 mm and 0.5%~1.0% 65MnFe with size of 3~6 mm was added into the tapping lauder during the tapping process;the final composition was as follows:w(C)4.1%~4.6%,w(Si)0.6% 1.0%,w(Mn)0.7%~1.0%,w(P)=0.10%,w(S)=0.03%;slag skimming was conducted 3~5 time;pouring temperature was controlled in the range of 1 260 ~1 300 ;pouring rate was controlled in the range of 2 ~3 t/min;the inner wall planeness and straightness tolerance of the finally produced castings was of 3~5 mm,the inner wall was smooth and the tensile strength also met the technical requirement.
