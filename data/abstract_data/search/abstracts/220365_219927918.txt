The Global Micro Impact of COVID 19 on Indian Economy
COVID 19, a name which proves to be a disaster to the whole world economy. It not effects a single country from where it origins but it grabs the whole world. It hammers all the sectors globally. The evolution of COVID 19 is very uncertain due to which it affects hundred, thousands of people around the world. The figures of CORONA VIRUS cases around the world outreach to 3,402,034 out of which total death cases around whole world is 239,622. There are speedy recoveries of COVID 19 patients but still 2,078,511 cases are active. Not a single corner of the world which is untouched with COVID 19. Every part of world is affected. If I talk specifically about India, Total cases were 37,336 out which 1223 are death cases and still everyday many new cases comes into limelight. COVID 19 not only affects human beings it brings the whole world to a hault as whole world is locked down and due to which there is no trading activities going on. COVID 19 slowdown the whole economy. How COVID 19 impacts the Indian economy is basically shared in this paper and also effect of COVID 19 on various sectors of Indian economy within coming year. This paper helps the policymakers and committees to uplift the Indian micro economy in the coming years.
