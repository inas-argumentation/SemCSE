Customer satisfaction on Online Purchasing Special Reference to "CLOTHES"(Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Club factory)
Now a day's online shopping is gaining importance day by day now in India also. It is the most popular shopping features in the world. People are tend to do online shopping by using social media apps. The objective of this research to asses the customer satisfaction level regarding shopping and their service which are provided by various E companies known consumer belief and the special reference was given to Clothes. This research use qualitative method. Design of the study based on descriptive research to test the customer satisfaction. Collected Data based on Primary data through Questionnaire and Face to face feedback and Secondary data through feedback from customer reviews. A sample of total 100 respondents was selected form Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh. Survey is based on following E shops Flipkart, Myntra, Amazon, Club factory.
