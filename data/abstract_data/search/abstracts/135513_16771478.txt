A new PFC converter using bridgeless single ended primary induction converter(SEPIC)
In this paper, a new bridgeless AC DC single ended primary induction (SEPIC) converter for power factor correction (PFC) is proposed. The proposed single phase rectifier has 2 bidirectional switch and 2 fast diodes. The output voltage depends on the switching frequency of the MOSFET. This converter produces low conduction loss, low total harmonic reduction and high power factor reaching near unity. The circuit of this converter is simulated with universal input voltage capacity for 20 25V DC output voltage connected to resistive load. All the simulation work is done using MATLAB Simulink.
