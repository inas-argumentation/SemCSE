Protein tyrosine phosphatase 1B and type 2 diabetes
Protein tyrosine phosphatase 1B(PTP 1B) is proved to contribute to the pathogenesis of type 2 diabetes through the following ways:(1)PTP 1B is associated with insulin receptor and down regulates insulin signaling which causes insulin resistance. (2) PTP 1B participates in the regulation of pancreas beta cells mass. (3) PTP 1B may play a role in leptin resistance and lipid metabolism disorder. All of this promote the pathogenesis and development of type 2 diabetes. Several kinds of PTP 1B inhibitors have been synthetized which can normalize hyperglycaemia, and have spacious prospect of clinical application.
