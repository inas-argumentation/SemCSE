A Study of the Legal Nature and Effectiveness of Valuation Adjustment Mechanism: from the Perspective of Haifu Investment Case
The effectiveness of the valuation adjustment mechanism was the focus of controversy in the Haifu Investment Case. The valuation adjustment mechanism is an aleatory contract; whether it is a conditional contract or not depends on the agreement of the parties. The interests of both parties in the valuation adjustment mechanism are protected by law and are consistent. The realization of the results of the valuation adjustment mechanism mainly depends on the efforts of the parties rather than luck, so the law should allow the existence of the valuation adjustment mechanism. In the Haifu Investment Case, the valuation adjustment mechanism between the company and the shareholders harmed the interests of other shareholders and violated the mandatory provisions of the law, but it did not violate the principle of risk sharing in the field of investment, so it is no means a loan contract. Existing among shareholders is not a valuation adjustment mechanism, but a contract of guarantee. When hearing such a new investment relationship as the valuation adjustment mechanism, the court should distinguish civil trial concept from commercial trial concept, so as to let judiciary adapt to the flexibility of commercial legal relations.
