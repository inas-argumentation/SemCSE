Spin Transport and Accumulation in a 2D Weyl Fermion System.
In this work, we demonstrate a new route to realizing an experimentally controllable spin Hall effect (SHE) on the surface of 3D topological insulators, which depends fundamentally on the spin orbit coupled (SOC) nature of the 2D Weyl fermions. Spin transport is solely due to the spin torque current in this system, and the SHE arises from chiral spin flip scattering off non SOC scalar impurities, of potential strength V and size a.
