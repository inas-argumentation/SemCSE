Toward a Satellite Based System of Sugarcane Yield Estimation and Forecasting in Smallholder Farming Conditions: A Case Study on Reunion Island
Estimating sugarcane biomass is difficult to achieve when working with highly variable spatial distributions of growing conditions, like on Reunion Island. We used a dataset of in farm fields with contrasted climatic conditions and farming practices to compare three methods of yield estimation based on remote sensing: (1) an empirical relationship method with a growing season integrated Normalized Difference Vegetation Index NDVI, (2) the Kumar Monteith efficiency model, and (3) a forced coupling method with a sugarcane crop model (MOSICAS) and satellite derived fraction of absorbed photosynthetically active radiation. These models were compared with the crop model alone and discussed to provide recommendations for a satellite based system for the estimation of yield at the field scale.
