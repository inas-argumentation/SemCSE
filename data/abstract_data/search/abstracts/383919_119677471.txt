Kirchhoff equations with Hardy Littlewood Sobolev critical nonlinearity
Abstract We consider the following Kirchhoff Choquard equation M u L 2 2 D u l f x u q 2 u O u y 2 m x y m d y u 2 m 2 u in O u 0 on O where O is a bounded domain in R N N 3 with C 2 boundary, 2 m 2 N m N 2 1 q 2 and f is a continuous real valued sign changing function. When 1 q 2 using the method of Nehari manifold and Concentration compactness Lemma, we prove the existence and multiplicity of positive solutions of the above problem. We also prove the existence of a positive solution when q 2 using the Mountain Pass Lemma.
