DIRECT NUMERICAL SIMULATION OF TURBULENT CHANNEL FLOWS UP TO RE=590
Numerical simulations of fully developed turbulent channel flow at three Reynolds numbers up to Ret=590 are reported. It is noted that the higher Reynolds number simulations exhibit fewer low Reynolds number effects than previous simulations at Ret=180. A comprehensive set of statistics gathered from the simulations is available on the web at http:/www.tam.uiuc.edu/Faculty/Moser/channel.
