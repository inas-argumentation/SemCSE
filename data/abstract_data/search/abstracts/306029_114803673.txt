On the Effective Way to the Seamless Connection of Secondary and Higher Vocational Education online Micro Courses
The seamless connection of secondary and higher vocational education is one of the development goal of the modern vocational education in China.This article analyzes the main problems hindering connection of secondary and higher vocational education.This article discusses the construction of online micro course system,and it is an effective way to realize the seamless connection of secondary and higher vocational education.It puts forward the concrete approach,and makes a bold exploration of the seamless connection of secondary and higher vocational education.
