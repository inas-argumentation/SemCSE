A new chaotic primitive and its application in customizing AES for lightweight multimedia encryption
Advent of mobile technology and internet in our daily lives has opened up a new dimension to our data exchange requirements. Multimedia like image, video, audio have emerged out be contributing in significant proportions to the data flowing over inherently insecure networks. The traditional encryption schemes which were designed to provide high security to textual data are unable to secure multimedia with similar security levels. The reason is manifolds. Firstly, multimedia is much bulkier as compared to text based data hence the computational cost incurred in traditional ciphers like AES, DES, IDEA etc. practically becomes unacceptable for multimedia encryption esp. in resource constrained environments like mobiles. Further, multimedia contains significant redundancies which get reflected back in the cipher obtained from traditional schemes. As textual data normally do not contain redundancies hence the traditional schemes never targeted this issue in their design. If redundancy is kept unaddressed, it may act as a point of compromise for security of multimedia. In this light, the paper proposes a lightweight cryptographic primitive based on chaos and its use in customizing the traditional scheme AES to make it applicable for multimedia security. The results show that the customized AES achieves better levels of security, show strong avalanche properties and completely diffuse redundancy in less than 15% time as compared to AES.
