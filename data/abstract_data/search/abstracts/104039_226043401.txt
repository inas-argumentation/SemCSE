Earlier Record of Accounting In 1494, Pacioli wrote Summa de Arithmetica, Geometria, Proportioni et Proportionalita, which included a twenty seven page treatise on bookkeeping titled, Particularis de Computis et Scripturis (Details of Calculation and Recording) on the subjects of record keeping and double entry accounting. Pacioli's book became the reference text and teaching tool on the subjects of bookkeeping and accounting for the next several hundred years. This was the first time that symbols for plus and minus appeared in a printed book. This book was the first known published work on the topic of double entry bookkeeping. Summa Arithmetica was also the first known book printed in Italy to contain algebra.
