The Annalysis of the Relationship Snoring and Type 2 Diabetes in the Elderly In Community,Tongling City,Anhui Province
Objective To investigate the relationship between snoring and type 2 diabetes in the elderly.Methods The questionnaire investigation by physical examinating were performed in 3286 elderly people (1824 male and 1462 female cases).The average age was 69+ 8(range,60~92 years old).Results In 3286 elderly people,959 cases(29.2% were present with snoring and 105 cases(3.2% were present with type 2 diabetes.There were 84 cases(8.8% of type 2 diabetes in snoring group and 21 cases(0.9% of type 2 diabetes in nonsnoring group.There was significant difference in incidence of type 2 diabetes between the two groups (P0.01).The logistic multivariate regression analysis showed that snoring was an independent high risk factor of type 2 diabetes.Conclusion According to the survey data,the morbidity of snoring in elderly people is 29.2%.The morbidity of type 2 diabetes in snoring group is much higher than that in nonsoring group,snoring shows positive correlation with type 2 diabetes.
