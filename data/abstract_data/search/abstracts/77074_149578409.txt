Attestation of host containing trusted execution environment
FIELD: calculating; counting. SUBSTANCE: invention relates to automation of management operations on virtual machines. In the method, the attestation service verifies the indication received from the host, and the indication relating to the host contains the trusted execution environment (TEE) After successful verification, the attestation service issues a certificate that can be used by the host to authenticate objects that have a trust relationship with the attestation service.
