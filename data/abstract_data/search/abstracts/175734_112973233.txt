Application of the H_ Control Theory in Autopilot Designfor the Missile Optimal Nominal Trajectory Tracking
In this paper the application of the H\ control theory in autopilot design is studied. The objective of the design is to guide the missile to stably and precisely track the optimal nominal trajectory. During the target interception, the missile is led onto the optimal nominal trajectory to hit the target. The H\ control theory and the design method are introduced. Taking a type of surface?to?air missle as an example, an autopilot is designed by using some characteristic points at the optimal nominal trajectory. The six?degree?of?freedom(6DOF) digital simulation results show that the design method is effective and feasible.
