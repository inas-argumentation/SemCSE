A Reflection on the Characteristic of Running a School for our University
The aduanced education of our country has entered popular stage,the society brings forward the more and more high requirements for the quality of colleges and universities to cultivate the talents,and the characteristic of running a school becomes the lifeline of survival and development.Through the development of 40 years,our university has established our own characteristic of running a school,that is,to persist in running a school on the basis of Daqing spirit,cultivating the students by means of Iron Person's spirit;to insist on serving the fundamental education and serving the oil field construction.We must insist on and develop our characteristic of running a school,and promote our university to develop healthily and continuously.
