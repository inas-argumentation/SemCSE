THE IMPACT OF VILLAGE SAVINGS AND LOANS ASSOCIATIONS (VSLAs) ON LOW INCOME HOUSEHOLDS, IN CHILIMANYAMA AGRICULTURAL CAMP OF PETAUKE DISTRICT
The purpose of the study was to establish how Village Savings and Loans Association had impacted positively on low income households especially in rural parts of the country where formal financial services were not available. The study examined the impact of VSLAs among rural families and it was ascertained that their Social Status of VSLA members in the community (i.e. Income Levels, access to loan funds, savings levels etc. had improved. On the other had, it was observed that savings pay outs among VSLA members were used for agricultural related activities. The researcher focused on 5 zones of Chilimanyama Agricultural camp in Petauke district of Eastern province, with each Zone having estimated households of between 1500 2500. Purposive technique method was used to select key informants from district community development office using an interview guide and a simple cluster random sampling technique was used to select VSLA respondents and questionnaires were administered for data collection. The quantitative data collected was analyzed using SPSS and later imported on a Microsoft Excel sheet while the qualitative data used the narrative form According to the research findings, it was observed that the majority of the total respondents were females which clearly showed that most women in these areas were actively involved in income generating activities at both community and household level. The survey also reviewed that 100% of the respondents accessed VSLAs loans and 57% of these women actively participated in decision making in terms of how their incomes should be spent at household level. At least 60% of the pay outs were used to purchase farming inputs such as Fertilizers, seed, ploughs as well as herbicides. It was further reviewed that 20% of the respondents used the pay outs to construct/improve their houses in order to have a decent housing unit in the villages.
