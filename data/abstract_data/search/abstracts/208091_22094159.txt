High flow Hemodialysis Arteriovenous Shunt with Concurrent Central Vein Stenosis Masquerading as Sigmoid Sinus Dural Arteriovenous Fistula
Dural sinus arteriovenous fistulas (DAVF) causing dural sinus hypertension may lead to severe venous congestion or bleeding in the brain [1] Magnetic resonance angiography (MRA) can easily demonstrate retrograde filling of the dural sinuses and which sinuses are affected. Central vein occlusion or severe stenosis is a relatively uncommon but potentially underdiagnosed entity, which may result from several conditions such as prior intimal trauma caused by jugular vein cannulation or elevated venous pressure caused by a hemodialysis arteriovenous shunt (AVS, [2, 3] This article reports the case of a 75 year old woman with central vein stenosis and a high flow peripheral AVS. This led to left temporal lobe edema with the vasculature in MRA mimicking a left sigmoid sinus DAVF.
