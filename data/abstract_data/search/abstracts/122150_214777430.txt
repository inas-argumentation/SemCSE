Perioperative management of suspected/ confirmed cases of COVID 19
Coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID 19) mainly transmits via droplets and contact Airborne precautions are required for aerosol generating procedures such as manual ventilation, intubation, extubation, non invasive ventilation (NIV) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) Modifications in airway management are required to minimise aerosol generation Regional anaesthesia should be considered where possible Disease transmission can be minimised when perioperative care is thoroughly planned
