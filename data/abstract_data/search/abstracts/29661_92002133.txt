SENSITIZATION OF POLLEN ALLERGENS FROM DELONIX REGIA, PELTOPHORUM PTEROCARPUM, COCOS NUCIFERA, AND PTYCHOSPERMA MACARTHURII
Type I Hypersensitivity, or allergy, is the immune response of the body due to exposure to an allergen, such as pollen. Though there is a global increase in allergic cases every year, there are only a limited study on pollen allergy, specifically from trees. Pollen allergy can cause diseases such as Allergic Asthma, Allergic Rhinitis, and Atopic Dermatitis. The study is aimed to determine the sensitization profile of Filipinos to pollen allergens from four common trees in Metro Manila, namely, Delonix regia Peltophorum pterocarpum Cocos nucifera and Ptychosperma macarthurii Pollen proteins from D. regia P. pterocarpum C. nucifera and P. macarthurii were extracted using phosphate buffered saline, and its concentrations were determined by Bradford Assay. The molecular weights of each protein component in the extracts were determined through Sodium Dodecyl Sulfate Polyacrylamide Gel Electrophoresis (SDS PAGE) In assessing tree pollen allergenicity, the clinical history of test subjects, ages 2 70 years old, were assessed using questionnaires, and their blood samples were collected for Enzyme Linked Immunosorbent Assay Test (ELISA) Test subjects were classified as allergic or non allergic based on the cutoff value of 100 IU/mL from total plasma IgE concentration. Cross reactivity of the two pollen species was determined through inhibition assay.
