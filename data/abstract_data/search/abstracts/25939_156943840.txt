The Factors of Influence and Constrain the Citizen Consumption in Sports Goods in our Country
Citizen as the main body of the consumption in sports goods market in our country, of which consumption structure, consumption level, consumption, consumption idea and consumption action influence and constrain the development of sports good market in large extent. At present, sports goods consumption structure is much simple and level is lower. The main cause of this phenomenon is citizens' much weaker economy base. Another is a large connection with theirs consumption idea, outlook in sports and socialized sports.
