Analysis on food hygienic quality survey of a western fast food chain store
Objective:To study the food hygienic quality of a western fast food chain store in Guangzhou city.Methods: Food were sampled from 8 western fast food chain stores governed by one famous multinational western fast food corporation.Some food and some using filtered water were sampled and hygienic of the food production and processing was investigated during 2005 2006.Results: 4 categories of food such as thin pancake,salad,fried food,cold drinks and filtered water in total 12 kinds of food were tested.As a result,the physical and chemical index of thin pancake,the qualification rate of peroxide value was 96.88%.The qualification rate of bacteriological index of salad was 47.9%,in that,the qualification rate of total numbers of colony and coliform group was 95.8% and 50%,respectively.In the physical and chemical index of fried food,the qualification rate of acid value of the vegetables fried food,the flour fried food with filling,the frying oil was 90.63%,93.75% and 90.6%,respectively.The qualification rate of bacteriological index of cold drinks was 75%,in that,the qualification rate of total numbers of colony and coliform group was 90.63% and 75%,respectively.The qualification rate of coliform group of ice cream in bulk was 81.25%.The qualification rate of bacteriological index of edible ice was 62.5%,in that,the qualification rate of total numbers of colony and coliform group was 75% and 78.13%,respectively.The qualification rate of bacteriological index of carbonated soft drinks adjusted in the spot by machine was 84.38%,in that,the qualification rate of total numbers of colony and coliform group was 90.63% and 87.5%,respectively.And,the qualification rate of bacteriological index of filtered water was 87.5%,in that,the qualification rate of total numbers of colony and coliform group was 90.63% and 96.88%,respectively.Conclusion: The current food hygiene quality status of western fast food chain store is not optimistic.There are still quite a few sanitation problems.Health administration should pay great attention to.
