Analysis of the Reliability of Anti resonance for Ball Screw Based on the Finite Element Method
In the condition of considering the bearing stiffenss,the critical speed of ball screw is calculated under rigid support and elastic support respectively.The effect of bearing stiffenss on dynamic behavior and fuzzy reliability of anti resonance for ball screw is analyzed applying finite element method respectively.The result shows that the first natural frequency for ball screw decreases dramatically in the condition of elastic support,the main factor that affects the flexural mode is radial stiffness,the main factor that affects the axial vibration is axial stiffness,and the fuzzy reliability of anti resonance for ball screw increases with the radial stiffness.
