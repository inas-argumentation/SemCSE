The Type II Topp Leone Power Lomax Distribution with Analysis in Lifetime Data
In this article, a new distribution, namely the type II Topp Leone power Lomax (TIITL PL) distribution, is proposed. Some statistical properties of the proposed distribution are derived including the survival function, hazard rate function, quantile function, moments, moment generating function, the density function of order statistics, Lorenz curve and Bonferroni curve. The proposed distribution has a special submodel, that is, the type II Topp Leone Lomax distribution. The maximum likelihood method is employed for model parameter estimation. For application study, the goodness of fit study is presented to illustrate the ability of the proposed model to fit various types of real data sets. The result shows that the TIITL PL model is a flexible alternative to fit and analysis of lifetime data.
