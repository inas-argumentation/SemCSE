Resistance to Root Knot Nematodes in Tomato
Host resistance to three major species of root knot nematodes, Meloidogyne incognita, M. javanica and M. arenaria, has been introgressed in the cultivated tomato (Lycopersicon esculentum Mill. from its wild relative L. peruvianum. The trait is dominant and has been located at a single locus, Mi 1, on chromosome 6. The Mi 1 conferred resistance is associated with a hypersensitive response in nematode infected tissues; it is not efficient at soil temperature higher than 28 degC, and can be overcome by some "virulent" pathotypes as well as by the species M. hapla. Novel resistances, that have broader specificity to root knot nematodes and are heat stable, are pursued and identified in the wild tomato germplasm.
