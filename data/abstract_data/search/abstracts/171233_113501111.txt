The Precision of Adjustment Technique for Deployable Truss Antenna Reflector Based on Orbit Thermal Environment
With the development of the space navigation,the size of the net shape deployable antenna is increasing,the expectation for the higher reflector precision(especially in high frequency) is needed,there is much difference in the thermal environment between on orbit and on ground,the mesh precision of adjustment technique based on thermal environment could be used to meet the requirement of the reflector precision.In this paper the theory and adjustment mean for the accuracy adjustment of a mesh antenna reflector in the thermal environment is discussed,the basic formula and adjust steps for shape adjustment of a mesh antenna reflector based on orbit thermal environment is given.Finally,given the calculation example,which is the validation of the mentioned mean of shape accuracy adjustment of a flexible mesh antenna reflector.
