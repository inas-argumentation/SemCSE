A Review of Frontier Research on Workplace Ostracism and Future Prospects
Workplace ostracism refers to the act of being excluded,rejected and ignored by others in the workplace perceived by employees.Based on a review of the literature,this paper analyzes the connotation of workplace ostracism and introduces corresponding measurement instruments.Then it studies the influencing factors in aspects of the excluded and environments of exclusion occurrence,and the effects of workplace ostracism including the psychological and behavioral consequences of employees,and discusses the moderators and mediators of workplace ostracism.Finally,it points out the shortcomings of previous research and the directions in future research in order to provide references for follow up research.
