Assembly, transportation and method for automatic adjustment of a seat dependent parameter a means of locomotion
There are proposed an arrangement, a means and a method for automatic adjustment of a seat dependent parameter a means of locomotion. The method comprises the steps of: detection of a first sensory event indicative of a proximity of a user to a sitting position of the means of locomotion, in response, increasing a probability indicator for the presence of the first seat occupancy configuration detection of a second sensory event indicative of a user further approach to the seating position of the means of locomotion, in response, again increasing the probability indicator of the presence of the first seat occupancy configuration.
