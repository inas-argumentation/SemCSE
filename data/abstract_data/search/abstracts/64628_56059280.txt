This research is has been compiled by Vonny Destriany (0215123003) with title "The Influence of Experiential Marketing's Factors Towards Customer Loyalty Starbucks Coffee Experience Bar Bandung" The aim of this research is to discover consumer's response about their experience in consuming coffee in Starbucks Coffee Experience Bar. The author do research on writer Starbucks Coffee Experience Bar's consumers at Buah Batu Street, Bandung. Descriptive research used as research methods where data analysis and exposure presented as is. From simultaneous test result, obtained F calculate value 11,761 F table 2,311 with significance value 0,000 where Ho rejected, and Ha accepted. This shows that experiential marketing simultaneously have influence towards customer loyalty. While the result of partial test shows that only one variable which is feel that have significant influence, this indicated by value of T calculate 2,612 T Table 2,277 and significance as much as 0,012 1,05 which means Ho rejected and Ha accepted. Based on conducted research, it can be concluded that the hypothesis author proposed can be accepted.
