Expression of the CLOCK, BMAL1, and PER1 circadian genes in human oral mucosa cells as dependent on CLOCK gene polymorphic variants
Cardiovascular disorders are related to polygenic pathologies whose etiology and pathogenesis being determined by both endogenous and exogenous (envi ronmental) factors. Impact of the environment on biochemical and physiological processes in the human body may be mediated by neuronal and humoral con trol, alteration of the activity of the immune system, and changes in physiological processes, including cir cadian rhythms. Most of these processes, especially circadian rhythms, are regulated at the genetic and transcriptional level. Disturbance of circadian gene network could lead to development of some pathologic states, such as cardiovascular diseases (CVD) diabetes mellitus, etc. There are several recent studies on the association of alleles of some circadian genes with essential hypertension [1] Earlier, we have shown that there was an association between the 3111TC and 862TC SNP markers of the CLOCK gene and develop ment of essential hypertension (EH) as well as coro nary heart disease (CHD) in the Russian population of Karelia. It was found that CC genotype significantly increased the risk of both EH and CHD in men [2, 3] However, the contribution of the SNPs of these circa dian genes to the pathogenesis of the above diseases is not clear. It is of common knowledge that the daily periodic ity of physiological processes and the activity of the cardiovascular system are determined by circadian fluctuations of the expression of many genes, includ ing those involved in the support of vascular integrity [4] regulation of vascular tone and fibrinolytic activity [5] which, in turn, depend on the expression of circa dian genes.
