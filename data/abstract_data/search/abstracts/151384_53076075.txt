Choice to Take Advanced Placement Course(s) in Preparing Students for STEM Majors: The role of Identification with Academics and School Motivation
Applying the expectancy value theory, the present study examined how motivational factors (including self efficacy, utility value, and interest in math and science respectively) and identification with academics (math and science identify respectively) predict whether students plan to take AP calculus and science courses while controlling for student's social economic status and achievement. The study utilized a nationally representative dataset from the High School Longitudinal Study of 2009 (HSLS:09) which surveyed more than 21,000 9th American students in 944 schools. The study expanded the extant research by showing possible pathways that motivate students to take advanced AP courses in preparing students for STEM majors.
