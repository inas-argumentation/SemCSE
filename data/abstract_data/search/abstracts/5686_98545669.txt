THE PREPARATION OF CERIUM, PRASEODYMIUM, AND NEODYMIUM HEXABORIDES
The hexaborides of the first three rare earth metals, cerium, praseodymium, and neodymium, have been prepared by the reaction of CeO2, Pr5O11 and Nd2O3 with elemental B, in vacuum at temperatures of 1200 1800degC according to the following general scheme: MemOn (6m n)B mMeB6 nBO It has been established that products of reaction of the composition close to stoichiometric are obtained at 1700 1800degC. By the borothermic reduction of the oxides, rare earth metals and B2O3 are obtained, the latter giving, with the excess of B, volatile boric suboxide. This reaction is an equilibrium process, displaced completely towards BO at high temperatures. The crystal patterns of CeB6, PrB6 and NdB6 were determined with a sharp focusing camera. The values obtained are in very good agreement with the values, given in the literature, for the same products obtained by other methods.
