MECKA: Methodologies for Constructing Knowledge Bases for NLP Systems
T his paper addresses the question of how to go about constructing knowledge bases for natural language processing systems from the methodological point of view, We report on research in progress in the MECKA project, where we work towards the definition of a linguistically anchored and sound strategy to drive the knowledge acquisition and elicitation process. We present the results obtained so far in the area of knowledge elicitation and discuss the directions we intend to pursue with respect to integration of statistical methods and reuse of existing linguistic resources in order to increase the cost efficiency and improve the quality of NLP knowledge base construction.
