The effects of combination chemotherapy of hydroxycampothecin with oxaliplatin on human colorectal cancer cell line
Objective To observe the effects of hydroxycampothecin(HCPT) ,oxalipiatin(L OHP) and two agents combined chemotherapy on human colorectal cancer line and to explore their anti cancer mechanism. Methods Nude mouse model for human colorectal cancer cell line was established,and then injected HCPTL OHP,L OHP+HCPT into peritoneal cavities of groups depicted above,respectively. Apoptosis of cancer cells were measured by flow cytometry. Electron microscope observations of cellular types and the apoptosis rate were made. Immunohistochemical staining was used to detect p53 and Fas ligand expressions. Results The results showed that the size of subcutaneous transplanted tumor on nude mice was significantly decreased in HCPT and HCPT+L OHP group,compared with pre treatment or control group. Both the HCPT and HCPT L OHP group showed apoptosis in tumor mass, while compared with the HCPT and control group,the apoptosis rate was obviously increased in HCPT L OHP group(P0. 05) The L OHP group mainly exhibited cell necrosis. The expression level of mutant p53 was significantly decreased, while Fas L was increased in HCPT+L OHP group as compared with the control group. Conclusion HCPT+ L OHP is an effective therapy to human colorectal cancer cell line. Its mechanism is L OHP enhances the role of HCPT in inducing the apoptosis of colorectal cancer, which is related to p53 and Fas L.
