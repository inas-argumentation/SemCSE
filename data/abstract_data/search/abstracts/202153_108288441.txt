ASSESSMENT METHOD FOR THE AMERICANS WITH DISABILITIES ACT COMPLIANCE USING 3 D MOBILE LIDAR
1 Sidewalk is an indispensable infrastructure for pedestrians, especially wheelchair users. Wheelchair 2 users rely on quality sidewalks to facilitate safe and uninterrupted trips in their everyday lives. 3 Transportation agencies are required to evaluate the regulatory compliance of the Americans with 4 Disabilities Act (ADA) and are responsible for timely maintenance of inadequate sidewalks. How5 ever, these timely evaluation and maintenance activities are usually lacking due to the labor6 intensive and cost prohibitive data collection process in the current practice. There is an urgent 7 need for an efficient and reliable sidewalk assessment method for the ADA compliance. This pa8 per aims at addressing such a need by proposing an automated sidewalk assessment method using 9 3 D mobile light detection and ranging (LiDAR) and image processing. The presences of side10 walks and curb ramps are extracted first using video log image and LiDAR point cloud. Then, the 11 corresponding key features regulated by the ADA, including sidewalk width, cross slope, grade, 12 and curb ramp slope, are automatically measured. Comparing with the manual ground truth from 13 field survey, the experimental tests conducted on Georgia Tech campus at Atlanta, Georgia show 14 accurate measurement results of the key features for sidewalk and curb ramps. A case study is 15 then conducted to demonstrate that the proposed method can support transportation agencies a 16 convenient and cost effective means for ADA compliance assessment by integrating the accurately 17 extracted sidewalk location and measurement information. 18 19
