Benefits and Costs of Soft Information for Credit Risk Management in Banks
University of Strasbourg, EM Strasbourg Business School, LaRGE, Po^le Europ'eende Gestion et d'Economie, 61 avenue de la For^et Noire, 67085 Strasbourg Cedex,FranceAbstractWe investigate the benefits and costs of soft information for credit risk man agement in banks in a principal agent framework with moral hazard with hiddeninformation. The results show the existence of an incentive of the loan officer to ma nipulate the soft information that he provides for risk management purposes. Wepropose an incentive compatible wage contract which prevents this manipulation.We then show that access to more accurate soft information allows the bank to holdless capital for VaR coverage and to expand credit limits. Overall, soft informationallows more efficient credit risk management although it implies the necessity toimplement proper incentives by the bank in order to avoid manipulation.JEL classification: D82; G21; G31.Keywords: Credit limits; Credit risk management; Hidden information; Manipu lation; Moral hazard; Soft information; Value at Risk.
