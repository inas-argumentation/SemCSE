Study on Activated Mechanism and Fertilizer Effect of Activated Calcium magnesium Phosphates
The four different kinds of activated agents(WZ,SMS,MSN and FM) were used to activated calcium magnesium phosphates.The activated mechanism and fertilizer effects of activated calcium magnesium phosphates(ACMPs) were studied by infrared absorption spectrum and the pot experiment of brassica parachinensis.The results of infrared absorption spectrum indicated that,the transmittance of characteristic absorption spectrum of HPO42 of four ACMPs were lower.This showed the contents of HPO42 of ACMPs increased.New characteristic absorption spectrum of HPO42 of two ACMPs activated by SMS and FM appeared.The result of pot experiment of brassica parachinensis indicated that the biomass of brassica parachinensis of four ACMPs were obviously higher than that of calcium magnesium phosphate(CMP) and the highest one increased by 20%.Compared to CMP,the contents of P and Mg in the ACMPs were higher and the P uptake increased by 4.8% to 24.3%.Thus,the losses of P reduced.The soil available P of ACMPs after harvested also increased,which indicated the residual effects of ACMPs were higher.
