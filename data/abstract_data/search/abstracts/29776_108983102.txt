Project planning and management
This training is presented through the Kansas Local Technical Assistance Program (LTAP) and the KU Transportation Center. Funding provided by the Kansas Department of Transportation and the Federal Highway Administration. About the workshop This class will provide information on project planning and management processes. It is anticipated that many of the participants are public employees that have not been involved in the selection and administrative oversight of a construction project. Participants will gain an understanding of the many steps and activities in a project that occur before, during, and after actual construction. The course also covers the activities and responsibilities of various public officials during the course of a project as well as the responsibilities of the contractor during construction. Participants also will learn essential components to project planning and management systems used by counties and cities in Kansas.
