Determination of polybrominated diphenyl ethers in sewage sludge by ultrasonic assisted extraction and GC/MS
A method for the determination of polybrominated diphenyl ethers(PBDEs) in sewage sludge samples was developed.Ultrasonic assisted extraction was employed,and multi layer silica column was used for cleaning up the extract of samples.The extract cleaned up was identified and quantified for the 7 PBDEs congeners by GC/MS with internal standard method.According to the validation of the quality control,the results proved that the analytical method satisfied the requirements of the determination of PBDEs in sewage sludge.The recoveries of the spiked standards ranged from 84.5% to 109.0%,of which the relative standard deviations were between 0.5% and 2.8%,and the method detection limits were in the range of 17 121 pg/g.Based on the determination method,sewage sludge samples from two sewage treatment plant in Hangzhou and Taizhou were detected.The results indicated that the contamination level of PBDEs in sample from Taizhou was higher than that from Hangzhou,and both of them were less than those from North America and Europe reported by previous literatures.
