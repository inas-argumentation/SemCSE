Techniques and Applications of Sentiment Analysis Review
People opinions and experiences are very valuable information in decision making process. The necessary content of reviews should be extracted and analyzed properly. This information is useful for manufacturer and consumers. Sentiment analysis is used to analyze each opinionated phrase or word and determine the sentiment orientation.
