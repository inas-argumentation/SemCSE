Zika virus (ZIKV) is a mosquito borne viral disease associated with central nervous system (CNS) symptomatology. ZIKV was first identified in Old World monkeys and is believed to be originated from a sylvatic cycle. Favoretto et al. aim to follow the natural susceptibility of neotropical primates to ZIKV and the possibility that these species could maintain an independent cycle in the Americas.
