Determination of cholesterol ester transport protein gene polymorphism in patients with coronal heart disease from Hainan Li nationality
Objective To investigate the roles of cholesterol ester transport protein(CETP)gene polymorphism in the incidence of coronal heart disease in Hainan Li nationality.Methods The six polymorphic loci of CETP gene was probed with an allele specific PCR(AS PCR) in 47 patients with coronal atherosclerosis heart disease and 330 healthy controls from Hainan Li nationality.The frequencies of genotype and allele in the two groups were compared.Results The TaqIB locus was detected in B1B1,B1B2 and B2B2 three genotypes in the two groups,the I405V locus was detected in II,IV,and VV three genotypes,the D442G locus was detected in DD and DG two genotypes;The appearing frequencies of alleles at TaqIB and the distributions of genotypes and appearing frequencies of alleles at D442G loci in the two groups(P0.05).Conclusions The polymorphism of TaqIB and D442G is significantly correlated with coronal heart disease in Hainan Li nationality.The B2 allele of TaqIB locus is the susceptibility gene,B1 allele has protective effects.The G allele of D442G is the susceptibility gene,and the DG genotype significantly increased the incidence of coronal heart disease in Hainan Li nationality.Sample size used in this research is not so big and the correlations between the polymorphism of CETP gene and coronary heart disease of Hainan Li nationality may be reveled by epidemiology research based on big quantity of samples.
