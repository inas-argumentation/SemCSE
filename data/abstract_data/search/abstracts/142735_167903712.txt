An Empirical Study on Employees Job Satisfaction towards a Private Limited Company at Salem
Human Resource Management is a term used to refer philosophy, policies, procedures and practices related to the management of people begin an organization. Today every organization has to face highly competition. Therefore organization tries to do right thing at the right time. In that situation HRM plays major role to achieve organizational goals. Satisfaction is the one of the major concept in HRM. Employee satisfaction is a measure of how happy workers are with their job and working environment. Keeping morale high among workers can be of tremendous benefit to any company, as happy workers will be more likely to produce more take fewer days off, and stay loyal to the company. There are many factors in improving satisfaction. This is a descriptive study. Data was collected from 50 respondents. Objectives of the study To evaluate the factors influencing job satisfaction of the employees in the study unit. To find out the employees job satisfaction toward monetary and non monetary benefits offered by the private company at Salem To examine the relation between the levels of satisfaction and employees' demographic factors. To identify the employees satisfaction with regard to welfare facilities provided by the concern.
