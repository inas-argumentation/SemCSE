Smart Solution for Efficient Precision Cleaning
With the newly developed UCMSmartLine series of multi chamber immersion cleaning systems, the Swiss based UCM, the division of the SBS Ecoclean Group specializing in ultra fine and precision cleaning, is responding to a trend that has been emerging for some time in a number of industries: Due to new and more demanding product requirements, changes in manufacturing, bonding and coating technologies, as well as stricter regulations, the demands on component cleanliness are constantly increasing. Companies from the medical sector, watch and jewellery industry, optics, precision and micro technology, automotive industry and suppliers, machine tool manufacturers and the coating industry are faced with the challenge of meeting increased particulate and film like cleanliness specifications reliably and at competitive costs. A similar situation can be observed in the MRO (maintenance, repair, overhaul) sector as well in the preparation of products from industries such as aerospace, electronics, medical engineering, among others.
