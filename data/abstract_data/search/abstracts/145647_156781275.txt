The Macroeconomic Policy Choice under China's Economic Imbalances
Inside and outside imbalances of China economy have come forth in the recent years.The main reason of China economic imbalances is export oriented growth strategy.China macroeconomic policies should be chosen renewedly in the face of economic imbalances.This paper indicates the fiscal expansion and RMB revaluation are the effective policy combination.The Central Bank of China should establish the monetary policy adjustment system based on the combination of national unification and regional difference.
