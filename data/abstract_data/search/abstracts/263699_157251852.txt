Skirting Merit Senior Leaders on Merit and Gender Equality (WITHDRAWN)
Merit is a seductive term which suggests that people are rewarded fairly and equally. Merit is also at the heart of how gender equality is conceptualised. The article uses interviews with 20 global chief executive officers (CEOs) to explore how senior leaders in organisations discuss merit in relation to gender equality. Drawing on a discourse analysis, three interpretative repertoires are analysed: first, gender tokenism is constructed as something that hinders rather than helps merit; second, gender essentialism, in the form of women's perceived special skills, is used to argue for more women meriting being employed at various levels in the workplace; third, it was suggested that bias hinders meritocracy and how the notion of merit is biased in itself. The article thereby makes a contribution towards understanding how senior leaders construct merit and gender equality with a view to highlighting which consequences those constructions have.
