Research on Land Use Spatial time Evolution Based on Geographical Conditions Monitoring in Qingyuan Development Zone
Based on the geographical conditions monitoring data and high resolution remote sensing data,through land use dynamic degree,spatial overlay analysis model by GIS technology,this paper takes Qingyuan high tech development zone as the study area to analyze the land use spatial time evolution from 2003 to 2012. The result shows that,(1) during 2005 to 2013,significant land use changes have taken place in the high tech zone,including the significant reduction in cultivated land,desert and bare surface,water,the remarkable increase in buildings,roads and other structures;(2) the high tech zone goes through a relative lag development before 2010,during 2010 to 2013,the construction is accelerated,especially the artificial digging land changes actively;(3) the infrastructure improves steadily in the zone and the land use structure becomes more complicated,at the same time,the ecological land is protected well. The conclusion of the study shows that land intensive utilization degree in Qingyuan high tech development zone remains to be strengthen,different function blocks and ecological space need overall planning and integration on the space.
