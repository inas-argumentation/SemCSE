THE LEXICAL AND GRAMMATICAL AMBIGUOUS MEANING FOUND IN THE CHICKEN SOUP FOR THE TEENAGE SOUL: THE REAL DEAL
Semantic is a kind of linguistic study that concerns about meaning. Meaning can be hard to interpret in the context because sometimes it has several meanings that called ambiguous meaning. This research discusses about ambiguous meaning which appears in the Chicken Soup for the Teenage Soul: The Real Deal. The problem of the research are finding words (lexical) and sentences (grammatical) that potentially ambiguous and the most appear between both of them. The purpose of this study is describing about the analysis of lexical and grammatical ambiguous meaning by observing lexical in words and grammatical in sentences. The method that the writer used is qualitative method that the data were collected by analyzing words and sentences from the book. The result of the analysis is finding 23 lexical ambiguous meanings in words, 25 grammatical ambiguous meanings in sentences, and the conclusion that grammatical ambiguos meaning is the most appear in the Chicken Soup for the Teenage Soul: The Real Deal.
