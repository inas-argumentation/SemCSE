GREEN ECONOMIC MODEL: PREREQUISITES FOR THE FORMATION AND DEVELOPMENT
Za poslednie dvadtsat' let v mire sformirovalas' kontseptsiia <zelenoi> ekonomiki, kotoraia soedinila v sebe kompleksnuiu uviazku dvukh kliuchevykh komponentov: ekonomicheskogo i ekologicheskogo. Takaia model' ekonomiki dolzhna sposobstvovat' bolee garmonichnomu soglasovaniiu mezhdu etimi komponentami, kotoroe bylo by umestno dlia vsekh gosudarstv. V stat'e izucheny predposylki stanovleniia i razvitiia <zelenoi> ekonomicheskoi modeli. Provedeno issledovanie podkhodov mezhdunarodnykh organizatsii k opredeleniiu poniatiia <zelenaia> ekonomika. Over the past twenty years, the concept of a "green" economy has emerged in the world, which combines a complex alignment of two key components: economic and environmental. Such an economic model should contribute to a more harmonious harmonization between these components, which would be appropriate for all groups of countries. The article studies the prerequisites for the formation and development of a "green" economic model. A study of the approaches of national and international organizations to the definition of the concept of "green" economy.
