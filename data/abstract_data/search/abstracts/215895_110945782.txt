JAPANESE STUDENTS' SATISFACTION WITH INTERNET BASED ENGLISH VIRTUAL CLASS
The development and advancement of technology enable businesses to adapt to the changing needs of customers. English, being the international language, is a necessity when it comes to communication. With the improvements in the internet infrastructure and the increasing demand in learning the English language, organizations offer internet based English virtual class to customers. Since customer satisfaction is considered as the fundamental key for a business to be successful, an investigation on the Japanese students' satisfaction with internet based English virtual classes might contribute greatly to the booming industry of online education in the Philippines. This study identified the experience of the Japanese students and aimed to determine the factors that affect the Japanese students' satisfaction with online learning, and analyzed the service performance of home based online English businesses. An unstructured interview using survey questionnaires has been conducted with selected Japanese students who are taking online English lessons. The statistical tools that were used to analyze the data were mean rating, stepwise regression and Levene Welch Games Howell One way ANOVA with post hoc test. Findings show that majority of the Japanese students' reason for studying English language is for them to enhance conversation skills (with mean rating 3.61) for their careers (3.55 mean rating) were obtained from 79% and 41% of the total population respectively. Some factors that affect the Japanese students' satisfaction level with online learning are clarity of internet connection and tutor's workplace at the 5% level of significance was derived using stepwise regression. The results showed that usefulness of established data and information can help improve the overall quality of service performance of an internet based English virtual class.
