An Integrated Approach to Acquiring L2 Listening Comprehension
For learners of a second language, listening comprehension is as challenging as it is complex. Generally, there are two approaches to acquiring listening skills in a second language: bottom up and top down. This paper provides a short, but comprehensive picture of what is involved in the process of listening comprehension from both a bottom up and a top down perspective. Furthermore, this paper provides a model for an integrated approach to acquiring listening comprehension skills that favors a mostly bottom up approach for lower proficiency learners that gradually moves toward integrating more top down approaches as learners become more proficient.
