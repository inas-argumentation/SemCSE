The Control of Ingestive Behaviour by Internal and External Stimuli A Theoretical Review
Ingestion of food and water is under the control of both internal and external factors. Various authors have proposed models of the interaction of those factors in determining ingestion (e.g. motivational isoclines, set points, settling points) and these are critically reviewed. It is argued that the evidence favours an incentive motivation model of the kind proposed by Bindra. This model is used as the basis of a review of self infused ingestion, experimenter controlled infusion, behavioural competition and sodium ingestion.
