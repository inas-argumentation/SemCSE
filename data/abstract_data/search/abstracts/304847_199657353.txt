Construction Process Management of Prefabricated Building Based on BIM and RFID Technology
With the continuous development of science and technology, the development and application of BIM technology and RFID technology in the production process of industrial manufacturing industry in China, the construction industry is developing more and more rapidly, and the building energy consumption of the whole construction industry is also relatively high. Therefore, in the actual construction and production, it is easy to produce certain damage and influence to the ecological environment. In view of this situation, it is necessary to apply BIM technology and RFID technology to improve the management quality and work efficiency in the practical construction of prefabricated buildings.
