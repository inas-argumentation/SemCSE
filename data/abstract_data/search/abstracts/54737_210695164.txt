Classification and Detection of Arrhythmia in ECG Signal Using Machine Learning Techniques
The electrocardiogram (ECG) records the cardiac beats in terms of electrical signals over voltage amplitude and time. It plays an important role in the medical sciences to discover cardiovascular disease. In this study, seven machine learning algorithms have been made use in classifying normal and abnormal ECG signals. The main objective of this study is to automatically separate regular and irregular heartbeats. The MIT BIH arrhythmia database is used as input to various classifiers. The efficiency and potential of different classifiers for arrhythmia detection is demonstrated and different comparisons with various other conventional machine learning algorithms are also made with the highest accuracy of 93.4% using Decision Tree classifier.
