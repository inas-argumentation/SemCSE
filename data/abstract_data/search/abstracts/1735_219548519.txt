Some Remarks on Digital Production and on Product Life Cycle Management All roads lead to PLM, (Omnes viae PLM ducunt)
The paper addresses some problems of Digital Production and of Lice Cycle Management. Some definitions and different ways to reach Digital Manufacturing will be discussed, mostly by means of lists of hardware and software components (and knowledge) which are necessary to build up digital factories. Some advantages and possibilities provided by digital manufacifturing are outlined. As one of the most interesting issues Product Life Cycle Management will be discussed in more details, as a Production philosophy.
