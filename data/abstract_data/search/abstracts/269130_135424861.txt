Cover locking device of automobile fuel tank
The utility model relates to a cover locking device of an automobile fuel tank, which is characterized in that a fuel tank cover is hinged to a fuel tank opening through hinges/hinge bases, a rubber sealing ring is assembled on the inner side of the fuel tank cover, a fastening device is assembled on the outer side of the fuel tank cover and comprises two claw shaped locking arms, two lock catches and connecting rods, the claw shaped locking arms are hinged with the fuel tank cover respectively, the connecting rods are respectively fixedly connected to bends of the two claw shaped locking arms, the lock catches are respectively mounted on the fuel tank opening, the rubber sealing ring seals the fuel tank opening when the fuel tank cover is capped on the fuel tank opening, and the claw shaped locking arms are respectively inserted into the two lock catches by means of rotating. The cover locking device solves the problem that a heavy duty automobile fuel tank cover is easy to be in oil leakage, hard to be sealed and difficult in opening, and effectively simplifies structure of the fuel tank cover, so that the fuel tank cover is simple in structure, convenient to open and reliable in sealing.
