Quality Measurement of Cooked Meats
Abstract The quality of cooked meats undergoes significant changes during processing, distribution, and storage. In fact, cooked meats are treated as a product of several different mixture systems and the overall quality, such as shape, internal pores, surface color, texture, tenderness, chemical composition, etc. depends on the quality of mixture ingredients (ie, properties of ingredients, amount, etc. as well as process parameters (ie, temperature used during processing) All these quality attributes are measured or evaluated traditionally by trained experts with naked eye observation or by sample destruction, which are mostly laborious, time consuming, and tedious. But due to the increased consumer's awareness and expectations, as well as the promising market demand, the meat processors desire nondestructive, competent, and faster technologies for meat quality judgment. Therefore, to replace the existing sample destructive methods, computer vision has recently been proven to have the potential to evaluate the various quality attributes without the personal bias.
