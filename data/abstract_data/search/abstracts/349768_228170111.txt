The Impact of Sharing Economy: Evidence from Airbnb and Taiwanese Hotel Industry
This paper investigates the impact of sharing economy focusing on the entry of Airbnb in Taiwan. Combining listing information scraped from Airbnb and a data set with detailed hotel performance metrics, we first quantify the impact of Airbnb's entry hotel revenues using difference in difference approach, and find approximate 3% decrease in revenue per available room. We also find that hotels delay major investments in response to Airbnb's entry. We further estimate a discrete choice logit demand model following Berry (1994) to analyze the impact of Airbnb on consumer demand.
