Hyperfine structure of S states in muonic ions of lithium, beryllium and boron
On the basis of quasipotential method in quantum electrodynamics we make precise calculation of hyperfine structure of S states in muonic ions of lithium, beryllium and boron. Nuclear structure and recoil corrections of orders a 1 and a 6, vacuum polarization corrections in first and second orders of perturbation theory are taken into account. Total values of hyperfine splittings are obtained which can be used for a comparison with future experimental data.
