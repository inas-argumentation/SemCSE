An Analysis on Types of Meaning Proposed by Geoffrey Leech to Improve College Vocabulary Teaching
This paper makes a brief analysis on the seven types of meaning proposed by Geoffrey Leech,and put an emphasis on the importance of associative meaning.Then three suggestions on how to improve college vocabulary teaching from the classification are discussed:1)In vocabulary teaching,cultural factors should be emphasized;2)discriminations of synonyms should be carefully explained;3)collocation should be paid much attention to.
