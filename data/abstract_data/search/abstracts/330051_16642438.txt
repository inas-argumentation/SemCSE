Nitric oxide as a secretory product of mammalian cells
0892 6638/92/0006 3051/$01.50.(c) FASEB 3051 ABSTRACT Evolution has resorted to nitric oxide (NO) a tiny, reactive radical gas, to mediate both servoregulatory and cytotoxic functions. This article reviews how different forms of nitric oxide synthase help confer specificity and diversity on the effects of this remarkable signaling molecule.Nathan, C.
