ACUTE TOXICITY STUDY OF GNIDIA GLAUCA IN FISH
Fresh leaves of plant Gnidia glauca were evaluated for its toxicity in fish. The LC50 for fresh crushed leaves of Gnidia glauca in juvenile Zebra fish was derived as per the bioassay method for evaluating acute toxicity of industrial effluents and waste water and it was 95.5 mg/L. Cyanide and nitrate were negative in the plant leaf extract where as the petroleum ether extract of Gnidia glauca showed the presence of steroids and terpenes. Thus fresh crushed leaves of Gnidia glauca were toxic to fish and it can be utilized for controlling predatory fish in shrimp and pisciculture.
