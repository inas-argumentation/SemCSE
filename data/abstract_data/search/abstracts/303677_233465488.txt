Watermarking E Government Document Images Using the Discrete Wavelets Transform and Schur Decomposition
This paper describes and efficient digital watermarking algorithm that will provide copyright protection to e government document images. The proposed algorithm is based on utilizing two frequency domain transforms: the discrete wavelet transformation (DWT) and the Schur transform. The unique characteristics of e government document images are taken into consideration in order to embed the watermark in a robust and imperceptible manner. The performance of the proposed DWT Schur based algorithm is evaluated with respect to robustness. The results show that the proposed algorithm produces robustness against Gaussian noise attacks.
