Determination of amino acids from Erythronium sibiricum's bulb by TLC and HPLC
AIM To establish the identification and quantitative method for analyzing amino acids from Erythronium sibiricum's bulb by TLC and HPLC. METHODS A TLC method was developed with n butanol acetic acid water( 4 1 1) as the developer,and 0. 2% ninhydrin ethanol reagent as chromogenic reagent. A pre column derivatization HPLC method was used for the measure of the contents of amino acids. The separation was carried out on a Hypersil BDS C18column( 5 mm,4. 6 mm x 250 mm) in a gradient elution mode. The mobile phase was consisted of A( acetonitrile sodium acetate solution,7 93,pH 6. 5),and B( methanol acetonitrile water,20 60 20) RESULTS TLC analysis indicated that the test samples contained arginine,valine,phenylalanine and threonine. The spots were clear and the separation was high. HPLC analysis revealed that the test samples had13 kinds of amino acids. CONCLUSION The contents of arginine,threonine and glutamic acid are higher in Erythronium sibiricum's bulb.
