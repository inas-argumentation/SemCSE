Comparison of b Ga2O3 thin films grown on r plane and c plane sapphire substrates
Abstract Solar blind UV detectors based on gallium oxide thin films have attracted extensive attention for potential civil and military applications. Substrate selection plays an important role for hetero epitaxial growth of gallium oxide thin films. Herein, b Ga2O3 thin films were deposited on r plane and c plane sapphire substrates by plasma assisted molecular beam epitaxy. The microstructure of the b Ga2O3 thin films were analyzed by X ray diffraction and transmission electron microscope. 2 01 oriented b Ga2O3 thin films were grown on c plane sapphire. While, b Ga2O3 thin films with both (100) and (001) oriented grains were prepared on r plane sapphire. Moreover, photodetectors were fabricated. Detectivity of the photodetectors made from b Ga2O3 thin films on r plane and c plane sapphire is about 1.3 x 1014 Jones and 7.2 x 1013 Jones, respectively. The b Ga2O3 thin films grown on r plane sapphire is benefit for solar blind UV detectors fabrication.
