A new class of hyper bent functions and Kloosterman sums
This paper is devoted to the characterization of hyper bent functions. Further, we reformulate this characterization in terms of the number of points on hyper elliptic curves. For some special cases, with the help of Kloosterman sums and cubic sums, we determine the characterization for some hyper bent functions including functions with four, six and ten traces terms. Evaluations of Kloosterman sums at three general points are used in the characterization.
