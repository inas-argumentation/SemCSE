The Countermeasures and Developmental Status of Electronic Scholastic Business for Universities
Electronic Scholastic Business that is continuously changing the traditional pattern of management, is a very kind of innovation among informationization for administrative management in universities and a kind of complex and systematic project as well. Based on the brief introduction of its background and essence of Electronic Scholastic Business, the paper analyzed the developmental statues and existing issues about the Electronic Scholastic Business in China's universities, meanwhile, the paper proposed developmental countermeasures on Electronic Scholastic Business for universities.
