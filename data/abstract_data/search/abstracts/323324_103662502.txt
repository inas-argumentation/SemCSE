Metal Organic Framework Derived CoWP@C Composite Nanowire Electrocatalyst for Efficient Water Splitting
Practical application of electrochemical water splitting demands durable, efficient, and non noble metal catalysts for both hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) and oxygen evolution reaction (OER) Herein, we report a new hydrogen evolution nanowire electrocatalyst, consisting of S doped CoWP nanoparticles embedded in S and N doped carbon matrix (S CoWP@(S,N) C) which is in situ transformed from Hofmann type (Co, W) based metal organic framework (MOF) nanowires. Because of S and N doping to the carbon matrix and the S doping to CoWP nanoparticles, the obtained S CoWP@(S,N) C catalyst reaches a current density of 10 mA cm 2 at 35 and 67 mV (vs RHE) in acidic and alkaline electrolytes, respectively. Powered by a lead halide perovskite solar cell, an unassisted two electrode solar water splitting device using MOF derived S CoWP@(S,N) C HER electrocatalysts and S CoW@(S,N) C OER electrocatalysts displays a solar to hydrogen conversion efficiency of 10.98% Our method is highly applicable for developing robu.
