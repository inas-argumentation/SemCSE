Adding Semantic Web Technology to the Object Oriented Method for Interoperability
Computer communications advances, functional similarities in related systems, and enhanced information description mechanisms have led to the widespread interconnection of existing systems to meet current and future system requirements in such diverse fields as healthcare, e commerce, and military applications. Full realization of the potential offered by such interconnections can only be achieved if systems are fully interoperable.
