Research on the Construction of Public Service System of Elderly Sports in Wuhan Community
By using the methods of literature review, interview, questionnaire survey and mathematical statistics, this paper investigates and analyzes the current situation and problems of the public service system for the elderly in Wuhan community, taking the development status and prospects of Wuhan elderly Sports Association and similar organizations as the research object. The research shows that there are several ways to construct the public service system of community sports for the aged in Wuhan. Gradually improve the laws and regulations of elderly organizations. Give full play to the role of instructors. We will further improve the social security system. Improve the management ability of the elderly organization.
