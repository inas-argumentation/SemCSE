Experiment on Human Thermal Comfort in Dynamic Thermal Environment
Indoor thermal environment is the key factor of influencing human thermal comfort.It's important significance for promoting building energy saving by researching on human thermal comfort temperature range.This paper takes several inhabitants of some residential district of Shenzhen as experiment object,tests and analyses human thermal comfort temperature of different condition and thermal comfort effect on natural ventilation,points out some proposal: residential building should use the natural ventilation as far as possible at satisfying comfortable condition.If it's not satisfy,some other mechanical ventilation ways such as fan can be used;human thermal comfort temperature can appropriately enhance 1~2 on national standards regulation 26 basis on air conditioning state.
