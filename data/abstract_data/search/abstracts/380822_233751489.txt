Direct Portfolio Selection Using Recurrent Reinforcement Learning
Portfolio selection has attracted increasing attention in machine learning and AI communities recently. Portfolio selection using on line learning method has been extensively investigated previously. Literature about portfolio selection using recurrent reinforcement learning (RRL) is relatively scarce and existing ones heavily rely on single asset trading system to heuristically obtain the portfolio weights. In this paper, we propose a novel method, the direct portfolio selection using recurrent reinforcement learning (DPS RRL) to trade the portfolio directly as a whole. Instead of trading single asset one by one to obtain portfolio weight, our method learns to quantify the wealth allocation weight directly via optimizing the Sharpe ratio of financial portfolios. In this way, we are able to allocate wealth to assets within a portfolio immediately and conveniently. We empirically demonstrate the effectiveness of our method, which is able to outperform some state of the art portfolio selection methods.
