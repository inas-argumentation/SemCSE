Finite Element Simulation of Crafts of Engine Press Fit Process
Aiming at the crafts of the engine press fit,a model of the engine bowl cypriot press fit is established and the pressure distribution of the interference contact is studied based on the nonlinear contact.The curve of fitting force displacement(F S) in the pressing process is obtained by using Ansys workbench finite element analysis software and the simulation results are compared with the theoretical calculations.According to the press fit process quality control,simulation fitting force displacement curve can provide a theoretical reference for the actual press fit quality control.
