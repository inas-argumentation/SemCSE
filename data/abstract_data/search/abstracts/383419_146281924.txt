method of removing an inverted emulsion mud crust after the drilling process using a single phase micro emulsion
Method of removing a crust emulsion reversed since the drilling sludge PROCESS USING A PHASE microemulsion SINGLE single phase microemulsions improve the removal of sludge crusts formed during drilling with invert emulsions. The single phase microemulsion of oil removes the deposited sludge crust. Optionally, an acid capable of solubilizing the sludge crust binding particles can also be used with the micro emulsion. In a non limiting embodiment, the carboxylic acid can be polyamino acid. It may be reduced to remove the coating from damage deposit of mud internal and external crust.
