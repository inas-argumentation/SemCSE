A Wideband Circularly Polarized GNSS Antenna for Satellite Platforms
This paper presents a wideband circularly polarized antenna for GNSS applications. The antenna consists of four triangular patches excited with 90deg phase differences in between to generate right hand circular polarization. The antenna operating frequency cover the whole GNSS band 1.164 1.610 GHz. The antenna is also modified to incorporate a cavity for LNA placement. The axial ratio and gain measurements indicate a good agreement with the simulations. The antenna presents a compact yet effective GNSS antenna solution for satellite platforms.
