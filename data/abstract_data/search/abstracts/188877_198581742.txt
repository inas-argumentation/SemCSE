Disclosable Restructuring Paper AR Flood Risk Management Support Project for the City of Buenos Aires P145686
The development objectives of Flood Risk Management Support Project for the City of Buenos Aires in Argentina is to strengthen the autonomous city of Buenos Aires to efficiently manage flood risk and improve the drainage systems in the Cildanez Basin, Maldonado Basin, and Vega Basin. This restructuring requires the following changes: (i) (i) revision of the institutional arrangements and change in implementing agency; (ii) modification of the activities under Sub component 1.3, as a result of the revised communications strategy; (iii) revision of the results framework to reflect changes in the activities under Component 1, correction of the targets under component 2, and addition of citizen engagement indicators; (iv) adjustment of disbursement arrangements; (v) revision of the procurement arrangements for the project, in order to reflect the new World Bank's procurement framework; and (vi) the inclusion of new requirements under the environmental and social incident response toolkit (ESIRT) including the need for the borrower to timely report to the Bank any incident.
