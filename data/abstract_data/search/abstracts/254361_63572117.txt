Control System Design of Bathroom Humidity Based on Single Chip Microcomputer with Fuzzy Control
Humidity is too large for the bathroom,when you take a shower easily felt chest tightness,easy damp,bathroom decoration materials and bathroom cabinets and other deformation or peeling paint,not only affects the service life of furniture,but also affects the appearance.The traditional detection device,on a certain part of detection,error prone,using multiple sensor detection,fuzzy processing,combination evaluation,and then by the single chip control,according to the different regulating humidity,open,close,dehumidification system in different rotational speed,achieves very good results.
