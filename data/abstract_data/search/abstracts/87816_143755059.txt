Dilemmas of Masculinity: A Study of College Youth
In a decade marked by increasing attacks on traditional masculinity, first by the women's liberation movement and more recently by men themselves, a volume based on data provided by 62 college men during 1969 70 seems almost obsolete on publication in the mid 70s. In addition to this problem of characterizing role conflict in a dynamic period, the relevance of interviews obtained from relatively inexperienced Ivy League seniors for the general problems of men in American society and the world may be seriously questioned. Finally, even if this work i s considered as an historical document limited to college populations, the data are self reported; and, as the author recognizes, even an extensive interview i s not always an accurate indicator of human attitudes and behaviors. But despite these problems, Komarovsky's approach provides a potentially valuable complement tcl recent critiques of the traditional male sex role (e.g. Fasteau, Farrell) Whereas most of these writers have critically assailed the traditional male armed with personal experiences, historical accounts, and persuasive rhetoric, the present volume depends on data systematically collected from men themselves to make its point. Results are presented in more than 25 tables and in numerous excerpts from individual protocols orgamized into an introduction, eight substantive chapters, and a theoretical summary. The first chapter reports on the men's ideals, perceptions, and belief! about masculinity and femininity. Placed in the context of similar previous studies, these data indicate a trend toward congruence of masculine and feminine ideals and an apparent reduction in sex stereotyping. But the chapter also reveals that the sample retains some extremely traditional views; for instance only one of the 62 men responded that he would be willing to postpone his education to enable his wife to complete hers. Generally these complex data are intelligently digested and discussed in the context of the relevant literature some of it post 1970.
