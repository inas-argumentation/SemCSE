Research On The Evaluation System Of Talent Supply Chain Management In The Five Cities Of South Jiangsu
After Shanghai Free Trade Area being found, the south of Jiangsu had faced to the fierce international talent competition, the traditional talent service couldn't meet the demands of economic development now.It is a new and popular idea about the talent management and service by the model of supply chain. The research according to Peter Cappelli(2008) whose model is talent supply chain management combines with the economic evaluation of five cities from South Jiangsu, and construction the evaluation system of talent supply chain management which be made up of talent pool, talent supply and talent management. By our calculating, the research gets results about the development of talent supply chain management in the five cities which ranks as "NanjinSuzhou Wuxi Changzhou Zhengjiang" At last, we analyze the advantages and weaknesses about their leaves of the talent management.
