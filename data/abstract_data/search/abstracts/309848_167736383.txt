A Study of Consumption of the "Customer" Viewed in the Combination of Production,Teaching and Research
To realize the combination of production,teaching and research,we must combine production,teaching,research and application together.The term "application" is actually referred to the consumption of "consumer",who is the realization of the target in the cooperation of production,teaching and research;the establishment of "technical basis";and the "final conditioner" of capital based power.In all,it plays the vital role for the healthy development of the cooperation.So we must try to upgrade the "consumer's" consumption ability.In reality the measurements taken to improve this ability should include these aspects: to support the creation of new "product",enhance "consumer's" consumptive payment,breed their consumptive makings and build up a well conditioned market environment.
