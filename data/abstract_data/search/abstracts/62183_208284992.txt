Comparison of DNA damage by sub ionized and ionized energy electron collisions and novel component separable nonthermal atmospheric plasma
Abstract: Low energy electrons (LEEs) under 15 eV can indirectly damage DNA subunits via dissociative electron attachment (DEA) We have compared DNA damage induced by LEE and high energy electron (HEE) collisions. We have also tried XPS analysis to clearly interpret the exact mechanism of DNA damage by LEE. Finally, we introduce a new design of nonthermal atmospheric plasma source to investigate plasma effects of plasma agriculture and food applications.
