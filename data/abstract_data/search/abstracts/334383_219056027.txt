Research on the Development and Transformation of Visual Communication Design in the New Media Era
in the Information Age, Traditional Media Has Been Gradually Replaced by New Media. New Media Has Promoted the Development of Visual Communication Design. Visual Communication Design Has Gradually Exceeded the Original Scope and Moved to a More and More Broad Field. Printing Design, Graphic Design, Book Design, Display Design, Image Design, Visual Environment Design and Other Branches Are Also Increasing. Starting from the Characteristics of New Media Era and Visual Communication Design, This Paper Studies the Development Trend of Visual Communication Design in the Future.
