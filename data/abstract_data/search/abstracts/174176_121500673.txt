Further investigation of the nonlinear theory for constant temperature hot wire anemometers
The nonlinear theory for constant temperature hot wire anemometers is outlined in more detail than before. It is shown that the domain of nonlinear effects is restricted to velocity fluctuations above at least 12% of the mean velocity in a frequency range larger than 10% of the cut off frequency of the anemometer. The nonlinear domain can be avoided in most cases by using an anemometer with good high frequency capabilities, by using thin hot wires and by proper tuning of the anemometer.
