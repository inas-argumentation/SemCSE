Kenneth L. Pike's analysis of American English intonation
Kenneth L. Pike's description of the intonation of American English is of great interest to structural Linguistics. The fundamental meaningful unit of intonation is according to him the "intonation contour" which contains one and only one heavily stressed syllable, and this contour is analysed into smaller meaningless units or "intonation phonemes" But Pike's own material offers the possibility of a somewhat different interpretation, where more attention is given to intonation groups of larger extent than his contours and within these to the relative pitch of stressed and unstressed syllables. And perhaps it would be better to distinguish between different categories of meaning in order to get a more coherent system. But even if part of this criticism might be right, Pike's book must be considered as a most valuable contribution to the discussion of these problems.
