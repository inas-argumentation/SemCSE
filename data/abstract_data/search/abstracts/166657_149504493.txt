Using Children's Literature to Practice Key Language Skills
This poster presentation will look at some key considerations for teachers who are thinking about using Children's Literature in the classroom, and provide a variety of suggestions for ways to engage students using a range of text types, including picture books, nursery rhymes, fairy tales, and novels. The presenter has used Children's Literature in a variety of classroom situations, and has devised and delivered an elective course in Children's Literature to third and fourth year Japanese undergraduates. This course combines theoretical considerations of the genre with practical activities, to explore a range of classic and contemporary Children's Literature texts. While this course is targeted for an adult academic EFL classroom, Children's Literature provides accessible materials for students of all ages to practice the key skills of speaking, listening, reading, and writing. It also provides opportunities to develop critical thinking, analysis, reflection and discussion skills. This poster presentation hopes to be of interest to anyone who wants to incorporate Children's Literature into their class, and offers practical examples of how to access and adapt materials depending on different contexts and requirements.
