Application of a near wall turbulence model to the flows over a step with inclined wall
A nonlinear low Reynolds number k model of Park and Sung (1995) was extended to predict the flows over a step with inclined wall, where a boundary layer flow without separation and a separated and reattaching flow coexist. For a better prediction of the flows, a slight modification was made on the function of wall damping mm and the model constant C1 in the equation. The model performance was validated by comparing the model predictions with the experiment. It was shown that the flows over a step with inclined wall are simulated successfully with the present model.
