Interactive Teaching,an Effective way in the Countryside Junior Middle School under the New Curriculum
Interactive teaching,as a sincere,harmonious communication between the teachers and students,as a unified dynamic process can adjust the relationship between teachers and students,produce a harmonious interactive circumstance,and improve the teaching effect. According to the present countryside junior middle school English teaching,with the reference to constructivism learning theory and other relevant educational idea,combined with the classroom teaching content,principle and model,the author will try to find a way to realize the teachers and students effective interaction.
