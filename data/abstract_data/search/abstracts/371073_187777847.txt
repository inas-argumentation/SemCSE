Pathways To Success Through Identity based Motivation
People can procrastinate, invest too little, and give up too quickly to attain important self goals. In this talk, Prof Oyserman focuses on identity based motivation to understand why that might be, focusing on what people infer about the relevance of their future self to their current choices and what people infer from task difficulty. Contextual cues influence interpretations of task difficulty. Oyserman presents evidence that these interpretations are highly malleable and are, in part, culture and social identity bound. Interpretation of difficulty affects students' possible selves and academic performance, as shown in experiments with college students and children in high poverty contexts.
