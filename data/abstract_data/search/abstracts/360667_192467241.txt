On the Daydream of Romance of the Three Kingdoms
There are three types of dreams in the daydream in the Romance of the Three Kingdoms such as dreams of the characters,the author's creation dream and the critics' dream.The production is closely related to the author and critics.The daydream's description has not only enriched greatly the text content,but also promoted the plot development and promulgated character's innermost feelings,and has the important text significance.
