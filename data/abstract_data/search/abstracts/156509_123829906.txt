Five Principles of Media Evolution Study
Media evolution study involves dynamic sequence in media system including gestation,generation,development,integration and vanish,as well as association structure like competition,interaction and harmonious state among different media.As an important part of media ecology study of North American,the theory of media evolution accords with the general media ecological rules,namely,observing the process of media evolution in view of media ecological circle.As vertical extension,media evolution has its own specific rules.In terms of the study mode of media,the construction of its theoretical structure is to follow five principles: progressive,successive,balanceable,symbiotic and integrative.
