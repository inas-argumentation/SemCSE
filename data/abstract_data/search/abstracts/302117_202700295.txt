The Multichannel Optical Spectrometer for Diagnostics of the Liquid Propellant Rocket Engine
The multichannel spectral device carries out the contactless spectrum analysis of an optical signals is considered in this paper. This device allows researching sources of optical radiation with which the direct contact is impossible or undesirable. In the first place the liquid propellant rocket engine plume radiation belongs to such sources. The developed device allows carrying out diagnostics of the rocket engine in real time for prevention of emergencies. The results of development of laboratory set up of the multichannel spectral device and results of its experimental researches are presented.
