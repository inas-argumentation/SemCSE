STEPANOV LIKE ALMOST AUTOMORPHIC MILD SOLUTIONS FOR SEMILINEAR FRACTIONAL DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS
This work is concerned with the existence and uniqueness of Stepanovlike almost automorphic mild solutions for a class of semilinear fractional differential equations Dt x(t) Ax(t) D a 1 t F (t, x(t) t R, where 1 a 2, A is a linear densely defined operator of sectorial type of o 0 on a complex Banach space X and F is an appropriate function defined on phase space.
