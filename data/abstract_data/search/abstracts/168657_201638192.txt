Research on Professional Ethics Construction of Chinese Civil Servants from the Perspective of Modernization of Government Governance Capacity
The Third Plenary Session of the Eighteen Central Committee points out that promoting the modernization of national governance system and governance capacity is the overall goal of deepening reform. The modernization of government governance ability is also a powerful tool to improve the professional ethics construction of civil servants in China. At the present stage, due to the invasion of bad ideas, the problems of short of self discipline consciousness exists in the professional ethics construction of Chinese civil servants, absent of service awareness and deficient of moral education. From the perspective of the modernization of government governance capacity, this paper analyzes the countermeasures of professional ethics construction of civil servants in China to provide some references for the relative researchers. 1. Concept of Modernization of Government Governance Capacity In the practice of more than thirty years of reform and opening up, the powerful governance capability of the Chinese government has played a leading role that cannot be ignored in promoting economic and social development. At present, China is facing new social contradictions and problems, has entered the crucial period of reform, improving the ability of national governance is of great significance for deepening the reform comprehensively. In the third Plenary Session of the 18th CPC Central Committee, the Communist Party of China put forward to promote the modernization of the national governance system and governance capacity" In order to improve the public interest, the government should strengthen its own governance and actively guide the private sector and citizen groups to participate in the process of public affairs governance embodied in the quality and level. It embodies the government's ability to regulate the economy and society effectively, helps to avoid the market failure, and promotes the overall welfare level of the members of the society. Based on this, we can understand the modernization of governance capability through three aspects, namely, governance body, power operation and organizational structure. The modernization of governance ability emphasizes the multi party cooperation governance of social public affairs. On the one hand, the government will guide the market, society and people into the level of national governance, and create a good multi governance environment for them. Because of the huge economic strength behind the market, the market has a strong governance capacity, and the profit of the capital leads to market participation in governance will be all efficiency first, ignoring the social appeal for fairness and justice. The modernization of governance capability needs to be based on the establishment of a scientific and rational government organizational structure. Problems of Professional Ethics Construction of Chinese Civil Servants 2.1 Short of self discipline consciousness. The sense of honesty and self discipline of civil servants is often measured by their own moral standards. If it cannot form a unified moral standard, its sense of probity and self discipline will be easily interfered by various factors. The lack of honesty and self discipline of civil servants is embodied in three aspects: greed, vanity and laziness. Greed is the enemy of honesty and self discipline. In the cases of civil servants' violation of law and discipline, the proportion caused by greed accounts for a large proportion. Some civil servants start from petty gains, and then dispose of public property as they wish. Some start with kickbacks, fees and other small things, seeking pleasure, money first, and finally embarked on the rotten road of no return. Vanity is the valve of human desire, but vanity is easy to relax, personal self discipline requirements, make people's desire to expand, hinder the cultivation of civil servants honest self discipline consciousness. For the performance of civil servants in vain in everyday life: at work, grandiose, resort to deceit. In relationships with others, jealous, and take revenge. As a civil servant, we should do our job conscientiously, consciously resist fraud, and prevent vanity. Laziness is a stumbling block to honesty and self discipline. Lazy people who follow in the position but not work, or idle, shirk responsibility. Some discipline phenomena often begin with laziness, and the habit of laziness will disappear. The idea of administration according to law not only requires the civil servants to manage and restrict the people according to the law, but also to restrain their administrative behavior according to the law. If the civil servants only regard the law as the tool and means in the hands of the ruling party, it distorts the understanding of the idea of administration according to law. 2.2 Absent of service awareness. Our country is the people's democratic dictatorship country, and the power of the state belongs to the people. Therefore, the power of the civil servant comes from the people and is endowed by the people. Due to the influence and restriction of various factors such as ideological pluralism during the social transition period, in real life, the power is granted, and it may be transformed from the profit for the people to satisfy their selfish desires. The weak sense of public servant is the fundamental reason for the lack of civil servants' Ideological and moral sense and sense of responsibility. It is because of civil servant consciousness, will produce power, ignoring the interests of the people. The civil servant group is the model of administrative morality, and the individual behavior should conform to the standard of justice and probity, which conforms to the interests of the public and the republic. However, at present, some civil servants ignore the purpose of serving the people in their work, and put themselves above the masses, and lack the initiative and enthusiasm of serving the masses. In dealing with the relationship with the masses, and there is no truly deep level, to understand the actual, do not listen to the people, do not observe public opinion, to the masses and between the concept of weak water. Although these problems occur in a small number of people, but directly affect the overall image of the civil service team, if not promptly curbed, will inevitably damage the relationship between civil servants and the masses. Some civil servants thought the lack of awareness of supervision is put forward to others in their own in the process of administrative law enforcement behavior objection cannot accept, cannot correctly understand people's objection to the decision made by the administrative organ of legal right, people put forward administrative reconsideration or administrative proceedings is not honest, disturbing public order and obstruction the behavior, reluctant to put themselves in the position of being supervised. They use their power to obstruct the legitimate exercise of the rights of the parties, or even take revenge. 2.3 Deficient of moral education.
