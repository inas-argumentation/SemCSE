Probe into Green Logistics of Principal Agricultural Products and the Construction of Supply Chain System in Henan Province
The author first puts forward that principal agricultural products are primary products which have not yet been intensively processed,such as grain food,cotton,fruit,vegetable,eggs,sugar,and so on.Principal agricultural products play an important role in food consumption and industrial production in Henan province,which is a major grain producing area.Integrating the concept of environmental sustainable development into the circulation of principal agricultural products and realizing green logistic operation and management can help us better coordinate the relationship of interest among suppliers of agricultural production material,farm households,agriculture product dealers and consumers,and realize the objectives of resources allocation optimization,the improvement of welfare,and the compatibility with the environment.The author also puts forward some countermeasures on how to promote the development of principle agricultural products in Henan province.
