An analysis on the effect of returning farmland to forest on the runoff and sediment on Zhidan hydrological station
From analyzing the runoff and sediment discharge on Zhidan hydrological station,it discusses the effect of returning farmland to forest on the runoff and sediment on Zhidan hydrological station.Proposaling to build the ecological compensation mechanism from the sustainable of returning farmland to forest on Zhidan.At the same time,getting the change law of sediment and runoff.
