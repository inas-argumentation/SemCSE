Multi study on the Concept of Information Quality
As soon as the concept of information. quality is put forward, there arises apparent differency about the definition of its concept in man's opinion. In American where this concept was early put forward, starting from the functional angle, the academic circles have held that the information quality mainly is a kind of function for obtaining and making use of information and such function is constantly raised with the development of information technology. Such understanding will surely turn to one sided place. In our country the academic circles mainly start from the view of structure and hold that the information quality is a kind of multi quality including the essential elements such as. information idea, information virtue and information knowledge. This is a kind of understanding view that closely unites with the future man's allover development.
