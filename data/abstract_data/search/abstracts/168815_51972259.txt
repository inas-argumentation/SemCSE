The Path Selection for Mixed Ownership Reform of SOE under the background of classified reform
In this paper, Mixed ownership reform is considered the breakthrough of State Owned Enterprise (SOE) reform. Under the background of classified reform, problems such as the depth and breadth control of SOE mixed ownership reform need to be solved urgently. With imbalanced profit structures, the focus of the reform lies in separating the ownership and management right. It is proposed that the elevation of operating efficiency of the state owned capital should be taken as the reform direction, reform barriers inside and outside SOEs broken, non state owned capital investment in SOEs of different fields promoted, the principles of classified reform obeyed, risks avoided reasonably, the self orientation of SOEs rectified and the collaborative innovation of SOEs and private enterprises promoted.
