Remaking Career and Technical Education for the 21st Century: What Role for High School Programs?
Remaking Career and Technical Education for the 21st Century, based on a paper prepared for the Aspen Institute Education and Society Program, is one of a series of Double the Numbers publications from Jobs for the Future. Double the Numbers is a JFF initiative to deepen support for policies that can dramatically increase the number of lowincome young people who enter and complete postsecondary education. The initiative is supported by the Bill Melinda Gates Foundation and the Ford Foundation.
