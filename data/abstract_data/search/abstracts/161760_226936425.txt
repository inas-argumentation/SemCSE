Qualitative research on experiences of caregivers in transitional care of military retired elderly patients from hospital to home
Objective To investigate the experiences of caregivers in transitional care of military retired elderly patients from hospital to home so as to provide evidence for establishment of nursing model for military retired elderly patients from hospital to home. Methods A total of 7 caregivers of military retired elderly patients in Beijing were interviewed using semi structured interviews via phenomenological research methods. The data were analyzed using Colaizzi 7 step method. Results Caregivers' experiences in transitional care of military retired elderly patients from hospital to home could be divided to 6 themes as following: physical fatigue in daily care, pressure of assuming multiple roles, anxiety affected by patients' emotions, sense of estrangement from lack of patients' trust, helpless in dealing with an emergency and craving of professional nursing guidance. Conclusions There are various experiences in the transitional care of military retired elderly patients from hospital to home. Exploration of completely effective transitional nursing model according to actual situation and needs is one of choices to improve quality of life and promote disease rehabilitation of elderly patients in future. Key words: Aged; Transitional care; Caregivers; Caregiving experience; Qualitative research
