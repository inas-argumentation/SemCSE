Effect of Fe and N co doping on structural, electrical and optical properties of tin oxide thin films
Abstract The physical and electrical properties of magnetron sputtering fabricated (Fe, N) co doping tin oxide thin films were investigated. The deposited films are in polycrystalline tetragonal structure. No Fe metal clusters or secondary phases are formed. All the films display n type conductivity and the carrier concentration of (Sn1 xFex)O1.982N0.018 films, x 0, 0.023, 0.042, 0.075 and Sn0.987Fe0.013(O2 yNy) films, y 0, 0.038, 0.091, 0.130 decrease monotonically with Fe and N co doping. Transport mechanism of the films can be attributed to the Mott variable range hopping (VRH) model. The SnO1.982N0.018 films showed maximum optical transmittance 90% in the visible region. The red shift in the band gap energy can be attributed to the sp d exchange interaction between the SnO2 band electrons and the substitutional N for O in SnO2 lattice.
