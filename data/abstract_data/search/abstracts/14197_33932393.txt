The Supplement Document of the Paper " A Grid Based Evolutionary Algorithm for Many Objective Optimization "
This supplement document includes four sections. Section I gives an example of the environmental selection process in the grid based evolutionary algorithm (GrEA) Section II shows the convergence and diversity comparison results of the six algorithms (e MOEA, POGA, HypE, MSOPS, MOEA/D, and GrEA) on the DTLZ and DTLZ5(I,M test problem suites. Section III analyzes the time complexity of the proposed algorithm and shows its computational cost. A comparison study between GrEA and its predecessor is presented in Section IV.
