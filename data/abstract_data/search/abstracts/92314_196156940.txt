Realization of Music Fountain Control system based on single Chip Microcomputer
With the development of society, people's living standards improve, on the side of entertainment have become increasingly demanding, musical fountain as a highly ornamental recreational facilities, increasing the public's favorite subject. According to the current development of musical fountain, this design uses STC89C52 microcontroller, AD capture chip, power amplifier chip, motors and other components made of musical fountain system.
