Soyfoods have been a part of Asian diets for centur ies. Today, there is a growing interest in these foods among westerners because of their proposed he alth benefits and also because their versatility makes them valuable for replacing meat and dairy foods in the diet. Soybeans provide excellent nutrition and contain a number of biologi cally active components that collectively may be responsible for a variety of health benefits. H owever, most of the interest in soy is due to their isoflavone content. Isoflavones have been r igorously studied for their protective effects against several chronic diseases including osteopor osis, coronary heart disease and certain forms of cancer.
