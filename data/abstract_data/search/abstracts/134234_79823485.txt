Chinese yam and lotus seed health porridge
The invention provides novel Chinese yam and lotus seed health porridge and belongs to the technical field of medical diets. According to the technical field, the Chinese yam and lotus seed health porridge is composed of the following food materials in parts by mass: 20 50 parts of gordon euryale seed, 20 50 parts of coix seed, 1300 1650 parts of Chinese yam, 45 70 parts of ginseng, 45 70 parts of poria cocos, and 220 300 parts of lotus seeds. The Chinese yam and lotus seed health porridge has efficacies of strengthening spleen and stomach and promoting digestion and absorption of five cereals, and has effect of invigorating qi and blood through nutritive substances in the porridge so as to achieve functions of tonifying spleen, supplementing and tonifying qi and improving eyesight.
