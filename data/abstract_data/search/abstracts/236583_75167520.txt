Cost minimization Analysis of 3 Kinds of Regimens in the Treatment of Gastric Ulcer
OBJECTIVE:To evaluate the therapeutic efficacy and pharmacoeconomic results of 3 kinds of proton pump inhibitors in the treatment of gastric ulcer.METHODS:134 patients with gastric ulcer were divided into 3 groups according to visiting time order.Group A were given omeprazole,amoxicillin and metronidazole and group B rabeprazole,amoxicillin and metronidazole,group C esomeprazole,amoxicillin metronidazole.Therapeutic efficacies of 3 groups were evaluated using cost minimization analysis.RESULTS:The cure rates of 3 groups were 85.42%,85.71% and 86.36 respectively(P0.05).The incidence of ADR were 6.25%,4.76% and 4.54% respectively(P0.05).The costs of drug were 936.32,2 011.52 and 1 076.32 yuan respectively.CONCLUSION:Regimen of group A is same to other 2 groups in respect of therapeutic efficacy but better than them in cost of drugs.
