Do homo sapiens ao homo convergente. E tempo de coisas e pessoas integradas.
The ubiquity of the digital world provides us with the possibility of a lifestyle transformation, extendable to the life of consumption. We understand that this process is already consolidated, although it is not implemented, because this lifestyle will be exercised by the generation that has already incorporated ubiquitous computing with an integral part of their lives. However, a technology in the development phase promises to integrate and digitize the planet and much of it, building smart cities, spaces and things that dialogue continuously for the exchange of information. Everything indicates that this will be the post digital era, dominated by the "Internet of Things" but always manipulated by the abilities and intelligence inherent in man.
