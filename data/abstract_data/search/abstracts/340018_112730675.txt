Study on Verification, Validation and Accreditation of Control and Guidance Simulation for Underwater Vehicle
In this paper the importance of the study on verification, validation and accreditation (VVA)techniques in the HIL simulation system for underwater vehicle is discussed. After the basic concepts and techniques of VVA are presented, a mathematical model of underwater vehicle is validated based on the characteristics of the HIL simulation system by using Theil's inequation, grey correlativity and spectrum analysis methods. The conclusion indicates the model is dependable in some degree. Then a general process including four stages and seven steps is discussed for the HIL simulation system.
