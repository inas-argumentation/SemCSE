Effect of electron beam sterilization on 3D printed PCL/b TCP scaffolds for bone tissue engineering
3D printing of composite materials such as polycaprolactone/beta tricalcium phosphate (PCL/b TCP) enables the design and manufacturing of scaffolds with advanced geometries, along with improved physical and biological properties for large bone defect repair. Terminal sterilization of the scaffolds is inevitable for clinical applications. Electron beam (E beam) is non toxic, and can be used for sterilizing heat sensitive scaffolds by the use of high radiation dose in a short period of time. In this paper, we assessed the influence of E beam sterilization on the properties of 3D printed PCL/b TCP scaffolds, focusing on the key physical and biological properties for bone tissue engineering. More specifically, we characterized the effect of a single dose E beam sterilization (25kGy, ISO 11137) on surface morphology, hydrophilicity, degradation and mechanical properties of the scaffolds as well as in vitro biological responses.
