Extrapolation and Splitting Extrapolation Algorithm for Multidimensional Weakly Singular Integral of Product Type
In this paper, we use mid rectangular quadrature rules to solve multidimensional weakly singular integrals of product type (MWSIP) and construct corresponding multi step and multi parameter asymptotic expansions of errors. The convergence order can turn from <inline formula> <tex math notation="LaTeX"$O(h_{i}\alpha _{i}+1} /tex math>/inline formula> to <inline formula> <tex math notation="LaTeX"$O(h_{0}{4} /tex math>/inline formula> <inline formula> <tex math notation="LaTeX" 1\leq \alpha _{i}\leq 0$ /tex math>/inline formula> <inline formula> <tex math notation="LaTeX"$h_{0}\max _{1\leq i\leq s}{h_{i} /tex math>/inline formula> by using extrapolation and splitting extrapolation algorithm. Moreover, we address <italic>a posterior</italic> error estimate and formulate self adaptive algorithm. Numerical results illustrate that the method is of high accuracy and efficiency for solving MWSIP.
