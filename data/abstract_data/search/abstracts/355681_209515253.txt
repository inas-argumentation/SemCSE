All or Nothing Phenomena: From Single Letter to High Dimensions
We consider the problem of estimating a <tex>$p$/tex> dimensional vector <tex>\beta$/tex> from <tex>$n$/tex> observations <tex>$Y=X\beta+W$/tex> where <tex>\beta_{j}\mathop{\sim}\mathrm{i.i.d}\pi$/tex> for a real valued distribution <tex>\pi$/tex> with zero mean and unit variance' <tex>$X_{ij}\mathop{\sim}\mathrm{i.i.d}\mathcal{N}(0,1)/tex> and <tex>$W_{i}\mathop{\sim}\mathrm{i.i.d}\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^{2}/tex> In the asymptotic regime where <tex>$n/p\rightarrow\delta$/tex> and <tex>$p/\sigma^{2}\rightarrow$/tex> snr for two fixed constants <tex>\delta, \mathsf{snr}\in(0, \infty)/tex> as <tex>$p\rightarrow\infty$/tex> the limiting (normalized) minimum mean squared error (MMSE) has been characterized by a single letter (additive Gaussian scalar) channel. In this paper, we show that if the MMSE function of the single letter channel converges to a step function, then the limiting MMSE of estimating <tex>\beta$/tex> converges to a step function which jumps from 1 to 0 at a critical threshold. Moreover, we establish that the limiting mean squared error of the (MSE optimal) approximate message passing algorithm also converges to a step function with a larger threshold, providing evidence for the presence of a computational statistical gap between the two thresholds.
