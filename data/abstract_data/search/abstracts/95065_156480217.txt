The Impact of Family Planning Policies On The Household Savings Rate
This paper uses China's inter provincial panel data to analyze the impact of the family planning policy on the household savings rate. The analysis results show that the implementation of the family planning policy will cause a decline in the household savings rate and that saving preferences of the one child families are weak. The analysis reveals that the one child families present the characteristics of "high risk and low savings",and they are vulnerable to risks. To defuse the risk of one child families,it is necessary to adjust the current family planning policy. The value of this study is to provide strong evidence for our country to develop a more reasonable long term population policy.
