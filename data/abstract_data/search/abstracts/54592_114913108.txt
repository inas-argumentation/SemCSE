NBA and CBA Mercerization Operation Comparison Research
From the perspective of market oriented operation,whether a professional sports league succeed or not is mainly decided by its operating mechanism,the profit ability,scientific and standardized management system,etc.By studying their development course of the NBA and CBA league respectively,the paper analyzes the difference under present situation of market operationand makes a summary of successful experience of the NBA in the management,operation,training mechanism and other aspects,which will provide references to the development of CBA league in the future.
