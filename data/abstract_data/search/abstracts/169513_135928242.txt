Line edge roughness (LER) reduction strategy for SOI waveguides fabrication
In this paper a strategy for the reduction of the line edge roughness (LER) of silicon on insulator (SOI) waveguides is presented. SOI technology is particularly attractive for next generation integrated optical components thanks to the very high index contrast, which gives a strong confinement for light propagation. On the other hand, such guiding structures pose a formidable challenge in the control of light to sidewall scattering process. Fabrication irregularities, as small as a few nanometers, may have a dramatic impact on propagation losses of such components.
