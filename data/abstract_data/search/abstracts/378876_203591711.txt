Study of e+e D+D p+p at center of mass energies from 4.36 to 4.60 GeV
We report a study of the $e^}e^ \to D^ D^ \pi^ \pi^ process using $e^}e^ collision data samples with an integrated luminosity of $2.5\\rm{fb} 1} at center of mass energies from 4.36 to $4.60 \rm{GeV} collected with the BESIII detector at the BEPCII storage ring. The $D_{1}(2420) is observed in the $D^ \pi^ \pi^ mass spectrum. The mass and width of the $D_{1}(2420) are measured to be (2427.2\pm 1.0_{\rm stat.\pm 1.2_{\rm syst. \rm{MeV}/c^2$ and (23.2\pm 2.3_{\rm stat. \pm2.3_{\rm syst. \rm{MeV} respectively. The first errors are statistical and the second ones are systematic. In addition, the Born cross sections of the $e^}e^ \to D_{1}(2420)+D^ c.c. \to D^ D^ \pi^ \pi^ and $e^}e^ \to \psi(3770) \pi^ \pi^ \to D^ D^ \pi^ \pi^ processes are measured as a function of the center of mass energy.
