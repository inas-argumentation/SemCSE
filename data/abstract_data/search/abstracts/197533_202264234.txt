The Woman Role in Qaisara Shahraz's "The Holy Woman" A Gender Perspective
Human has important role in the life. In the various aspect, human has idea until they could describe social life by novel. The holy woman is a one of many popular novel by Qaisra Shahraz. The object was as to be relevant for social problems describing about man act toward woman and the woman role with the main figure in Zarri Bano living in the middle rules that tradition built by man dominant. Where there was conflict in the works that was very interesting to consider. In analyze the data the writer used feminist approach in the study of gender perspective through exposure to descriptive qualitative. The result of this research showed about Zarri Bano as the main figure in facing some conflicts in her life, started from family until public. Zarri Bano acted as a woman had hopes and dreams the struggle in achieving success right equality to determine her life without pressure from other people. Through role was construction by tradition she able proved that woman have right and opportunity like man in various fields. The research used personality of Zarri Bano through man act toward woman, the woman role in private life and the woman role in public life based on gender perspective to explain human behavior in social life. Zarri Bano as woman from higher class lives under the men's oppression in her family.
