What is the issue? Neglecting product packaging in the design process and production creates waste that could have been avoided otherwise. When did this become unsustainable? Following the Industrial Revolution and mass production, concepts such as health, safety, marketing and consumerism have increased the need for packaging. Where does it go? Across the world, our oceans and landfills are flooded with waste and islands untouched by humans feel the aftermath of our inconsideration. Why should we care? Current packaging design facilitates unsustainable practices that cause devastating effects on our planet and its organisms.
