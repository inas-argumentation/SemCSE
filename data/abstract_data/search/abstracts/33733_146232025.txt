Recollection Is a Continuous Process for Dual Process Theories of Recognition
Dual process theory, which holds that recog nition decisions can be based on recollection or familiarity, has long seemed incompatible with signal detection theory, which holds that recognition decisions are based on a singular, continuous memory strength variable. Formal dual process models typically regard familiarity as a continuous process (i.e. familiarity comes in degrees) but they construe recollection as a categorical process (i.e. recollection either occurs or does not occur) A continuous process is characterized by a graded relationship between confidence and accuracy, whereas a categorical process is characterized by a binary relationship such that high confidence is associated with high accuracy but all lower degrees of confidence are associated with chance accuracy.
