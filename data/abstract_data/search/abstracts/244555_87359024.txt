Function of b Carotene and Tocopherol in Photosystem II
New and known structural and functional insights in the role of b carotene and of a tocopherol in photosytem II are reviewed. A concept is presented connecting the failure of P680 triplet quenching by b carotene with the formation of singlet oxygen and its scavenging in the turnover of the D1 protein and by tocopherol in the maintenance of PS II structure and function.
