Competency and strategy parameters selection based on fuzzy based modelling for Indian automobile industries
The automobile manufacturing units need to create their own core competencies in order to survive in the fiercely competitive market. A core competency is an internal capability that is critical to the success of business. It should provide organisations with significant performance enhancements and should fulfil three attributes: it should offer customer advantages, it should be difficult for competitors to replicate and it should find applications wide variety of products and markets. This paper presents fuzzy based modelling for competency and strategy parameters.
