Whole angle mode micromachined fused silica birdbath resonator gyroscope (WA BRG)
We present the fused silica micromachined birdbath resonator gyroscope (m BRG) operating in the whole angle (WA) mode. The key advantages of the whole angle mode operation is rotation angle measurement, large bandwidth, and full scale range which is needed in detecting the motion of fast moving objects. The m BRG is made with fused silica using a micro blow torching process and has n 2 wineglass modes at 10.46 kHz with a small frequency mismatch (Df 10 Hz) and a decay time (t) of 2.2s. The WA BRG achieves a stable angular gain (Ag) and a large full scale range (700deg/s)
