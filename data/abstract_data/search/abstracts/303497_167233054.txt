An Economic Analysis on the Mandated Disclosure System in American Securities Law
The mandated disclosure system in American securities l aw develops from agent information disclosure rule to value information disclosu re rule. From the perspective of information economics, the market failure theor y may underestimate the market's ability to get self adjusted and the possibil ity of government failure; while the external effect theory may undervalue the c omparative superiority of mandated disclosure system to provide public goods in producing information. Therefore, the proper solution lies between the two appro aches. The limited mandated disclosure system focusing on agent information and some historical facts related to securities value may be more efficient.
