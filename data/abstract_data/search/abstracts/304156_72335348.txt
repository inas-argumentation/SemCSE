C reactive protein, Epstein Barr virus, and cortisol trajectories in refugee and non refugee youth: Links with stress, mental health, and cognitive function during a randomized controlled trial
Highlights We observed multiple CRP, EBV, and HCC trajectories, indicating within population heterogeneity. Refugees and non refugee adolescents showed similar CRP and HCC trajectories. Rising CRP levels were related to perceived stress, high HCC to insecurity. Post intervention, HCC reduced by more than one third, while CRP and EBV did not.
