An Exploration of New Model of Quality Orientated Teaching Practice in Normal Schools
The new model of quality oriented teaching practice in this article is the product of practical teaching reform in the new century and it consists of experiential,reflective and research based teaching practice.It has many factors including cultivation of teachers' professional skills,research ability and quality preparation for being employed.Compared with the current traditional teaching practice model,it is featured by sufficient time,rich content,diverse forms,gradual progress and emphasis of student orientation.This model is quality based and up to date.Wide application of this model should be carried out in normal schools.
