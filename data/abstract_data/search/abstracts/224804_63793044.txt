Parallel machine scheduling problem with resource dependent processing times
We consider the parallel machine scheduling problem under the consumption that the processing time of each job is a linear decreasing function of the resource consumed.The objective is to minimize the total amount of the consumed resources with the constraint that the makespan does not exceed a given limit.We analyze the optimal algorithm for the corresponding single machine scheduling problem and the method to construct a lower bound.Two optimization strategies are proposed to design simulated annealing algorithm and tabu search algorithm.A large number of random instances with different characters are generated to examine the accuracy and efficiency of the algorithms.The computational results show that the presented simulated algorithm can yield high quality solutions for the parallel machine scheduling problems with resource dependent processing times in a reasonable time.
