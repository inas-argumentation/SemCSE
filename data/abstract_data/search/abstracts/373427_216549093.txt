International Human Resource Management: Paradox of Integrating Differentiating Global Local HR Practices
Liberalization, privatization and globalization of economies by most states and gradual penetration of information and communication technology (ICT) in all facets of business have given great impetus to growth of multinational corporations (MNCs) which has subsidiary/ branch offices in different parts of world. With prominence of MNCs, international human resource management (IHRM) is hallmark of HRM practices. IHRM is concerned with acquiring, deputing, effective and efficient utilization of manpower in a MNC. In multinational corporations HR professionals have to achieve two opposite objectives just like poles of the earth. Initially, Hr professionals must combine HR policies and practices across a number of workplaces in different nations so that core business goals can be achieved simultaneously flexible enough to encompass and inculcate significant differences in the type of HR policies and practices that are most relevant in different socio politico economic perspective. Efficiency and relevance of international HRM is a factor of perception and implementation to extent to which a global company should apply similar practices around the globe i.e. convergence or to make changes to suit local conditions i.e. divergence.
