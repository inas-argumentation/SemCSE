Corrosion behavior of functionally graded and self healing nanostructured TiO2 Al2O3 Benzotriazole coatings deposited on AA 2024 T3 by the sol gel method
Abstract The corrosion behavior of titania alumina Benzotriazole and functionally graded (FG) coatings deposited on AA 2024 T3 is investigated. The FG coatings are doped with different amounts of Benzotriazole (BTA) as an inhibitor and the Al2O3 TiO2 BTA coating has a similar amount of BTA in the layers. The coatings are deposited by the sol gel and dip coating techniques and the morphology, structure, and corrosion resistance are investigated systematically. The amorphous TiO2 Al2O3 BTA and FG coatings with thicknesses of 2.3 and 2.6 mm show corrosion resistance of 105,672 O Cm2 and 252,731O. Cm2, respectively, after immersion in 3.5% NaCl for 96 h. The difference is caused by controllable release of BTA from the FG coatings. The results show that the FG coatings are superior to conventional nanostructured TiO2 Al2O3 BTA coatings from the perspective of corrosion protection.
