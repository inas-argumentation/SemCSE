The Practice and Exploration of "De standardization"in Piano Education and Teaching in Higher Vocational Institutions
Combined with the author's her own education and teaching practice of piano basic course,this thesis elaborates that the cultivation of musical talents in higher vocational institutions can't only adhere to the "standard"strictly from the perspective of the educational ideal,the teaching practice and the assessment and evaluation,etc,simultaneously,pointing out that it is necessary to realize,respect and apply the students' differences when cultivating the musical talents in higher vocational institutions in the light of the principle of "de standardization"
