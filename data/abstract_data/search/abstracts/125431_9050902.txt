Genetic algorithms and parallel computing for a heterogeneous fleet vehicle routing problem with time windows and split deliveries
This paper proposes the use of genetic algorithms and parallel computing to solve a real heterogeneous fleet vehicle routing problem with time windows and split deliveries (HFVRPTWSD) The problem is based in a single warehouse, and most clients have got a demand larger than the vehicles capacity. The strategy adopted in order to solve the problem was an adaptation of the saving heuristic proposed by Clarke Wright (1964) as an initial solution. Afterwards the Meta RaPS procedure, genetic algorithms and parallel genetic algorithms are implemented, being the latter solved with the aid of a computer cluster. The algorithms were implemented in one of the biggest retail groups in Brazil. The results obtained show good solutions, reducing up to 13% the total operation cost of the company.
