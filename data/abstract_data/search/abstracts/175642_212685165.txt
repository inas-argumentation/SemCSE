Population food security nutrition and sustainable development Report of the Economic and Social Council Summary
The right to adequate living standards for the people was first addressed as a fundamental human right in the Universal Declaration on the Eradication of Hunger and Malnutrition and has always been considered as one of the most important rights throughout history. However, this right in many underdeveloped regions of the world has been violated due to extreme poverty, conflict and climate change. By 2015, a significant progress has been achieved, but it is still far from elimination as the UN proposed "Zero Hunger Challenge" in 2012. Today, over 800 million people are considered undernourished. It is also estimated that there will be over 2 billion people who will fall into starvation by the year 2050. As it is highly linked to the Sustainable Development Goals, urgent collaborations by different stakeholders, in particular, governments, foreign aid, food producers and consumers, have to be taken effectively to ensure the eradication of hunger and malnutrition.
