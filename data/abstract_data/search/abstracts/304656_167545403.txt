Modelling Receivables and Deferred Revenues to Detect Revenue Management
First, I propose a specific accruals model approach that utilizes the exact income statement and exact cash flow measures, which underlies the matching function of accruals. Second, using revenue manipulators identified by SEC enforcement actions and firms that manage to just meet or beat revenue benchmarks, this paper shows how researchers and regulators can detect aggressive revenue recognition not only by abnormal changes in accounts receivables, but also by abnormal changes in current and long term deferred revenues. The most robust application is to technology, internet, and software companies, whose elusive revenue recognition practices are of concern to the SEC, FASB, and IASB (e.g. ASU 2014 09 (Topic 606) and IFRS 1)
