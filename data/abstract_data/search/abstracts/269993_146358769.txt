Focus and Motivation: Two Contributing Factors That Lead a Student to Academic Success.
This study was conducted to determine whether or not a student's focus and motivation during a lesson is a contributor in their educational success rate. Six fifth grade students in an enrichment room from Cambria Elementary School, in Ebensburg, Pennsylvania, participated in the study that spanned through April to June of 2011. These students were the focus of the study because they lacked the focus and motivation that was needed when in the enrichment room. These students were seen three times per week for one hour each time.
