The Recruitment of Chinese Communist Party Members after the Failure of the 1911 Revolution
After the 1911 revolution, Chinese Communist Party began to "absorb" Party members due to the wrong estimation of the situation. In this way, CCP attempted to strengthen the revolutionary force and achieve rapid success. As a result, some devoted and outstanding members entered the Party. But in the meantime, the recruitment also allowed some people with ill motives entered the Party, which caused negative influence to the Party's career.
