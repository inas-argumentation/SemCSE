Survey on e Tourism: Intelligent Mobile Tourist Guide
Android platform is a new era of smart mobile phone platform and launched by Google. Android delivers the support of mobile maps and location services, which is probably an anxiety of vast numbers of developers. Till now, the development of mobile location and map applications is difficult and challenging, and is frequently required to pay high copyright fees to map creators. Android is free and open source it is providing a simple development kit having flexible map display, location support and control functions. Activity, Intent Receiver, Service and Content Provider are four building blocks to an Android application. Not every application requires to have all four, but a user's application will be written with some mixture of these. Once the user has decided which components are required for the application, they should be recorded in xml file called AndroidManifest.xml. A group of core applications are on the highest level in the framework, including an email client, a sms app, a maps application, web browser, contacts app, a calendar, and many more.The paper defines ontology for intelligent mobile tourist guide services interaction, service based application architecture, and evaluation. Developed application is available for download in Google Play market for Android users.
