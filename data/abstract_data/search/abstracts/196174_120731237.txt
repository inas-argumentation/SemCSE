Modeling Dynamic Ferroelectric Behavior with the Analytical Landau Theory
The Landau theory of phase transitions has been widely used to study the static and dynamic properties of ferroelectric materials but mainly through numerical procedures. However the exact solution of the Landau Khalatnikov equation produces several important consequences. Derivation of explicit expressions for the polarization switching time and the equilibrium polarization as functions of applied electric field and temperatures has enabled the unification of empirical phenomenological theories of dynamic ferroelectric behavior with the Landau theory of phase transitions for ferroelectrics under conditions of high electric fields and temperature. The role of the applied electric field in determining whether a ferroelectric material undergoes partial or complete polarization switching is established explicitly. Concurrently analytical tools for modeling dynamic properties of ferroelectrics have been developed as alternatives to empirical rules currently used in experimental work.
