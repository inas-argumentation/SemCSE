The U.S. public health community strongly believes that bioterrorism was not a factor in the genesis of the epidemic. Several counterterrorism experts have refused, however, to rule out the possibility that the outbreak was consequential to an act of bioterrorism or an on site accident at a covert Chinese bioweapons development program.
