One Pot Synthesis of Seven membered Heterocyclic Derivatives of Diazepines Involving Copper Catalyzed Rearrangement Cascade Allyl Amination.
In the presence of triphenylphosphine, using molybdenyl acetylacetonate and copper(II) trifluoromethanesulfonate as catalysts, a novel and efficient method has been established for the synthesis of 1,4 benzodiazepine 5 ones from o nitrobenzoic N allylamides. This transformation process involves nitrene formation, C H bond insertion, C=C bond rearrangement and C N bond formation cascade reactions via copper and molybdenum catalyzed mediation. The method features a wide substrate scope and a moderate to high yield (up to 90% thus exhibiting the possibility for practical applications.
