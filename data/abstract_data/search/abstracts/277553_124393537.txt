Application of Logistic regression to explore the form and effect size of the interaction in MDR
Objective:To explore how to use Logistic regression model to analyze the form and effect size of the gene gene environment interaction after MDR analysis.Methods:Data of a case control study which focused on the relationship of PAHs and lung cancer were used.After the gene gene interactions as well as the gene environment interactions models were built by the MDR software,logistic regression was carried out to further search the form and the effect size of the interaction as a supplement to MDR. Results:The combination of "rs4646782*rs2158041" gene polymorphisms had the multiplication effect, and "rs4646782+rs2158041+smoking year" had the additive effect;lung cancer risk were increased 0.097 or 0.199 times as values of the product or the additive of the interaction terms were increased one unit,respectively.Conclusions:Our findings suggested that the logistic regression could be used as a supplementary method after MDR to analyze the form and the effect size of the interaction.
