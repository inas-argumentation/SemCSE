Ovarian function after transplantation of frozen, banked autologous ovarian tissue.
To the Editor: Studies in animals and humans have shown that cryopreserved ovarian tissue can be transplanted successfully.1 4 We report the successful transplantation of frozen, banked autologous ovarian tissue according to a protocol approved by the institutional review board at our center. The patient was a 29 year old woman who had undergone a right salpingo oophorectomy and wedge resection of the left ovary at the age of 17 years in order to remove dermoid cysts.
