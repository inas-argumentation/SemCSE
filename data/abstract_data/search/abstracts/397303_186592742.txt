A Study of Educational Loans in India
This concise and compact book provides information about the Education Loan Environment in India. It offers valuable guidelines for improving the performance of banks in the disbursement of Education Loans and also gives key insights into modern research in Banking Industry. Using a simple and easy to understand approach, it discusses the issues like: the commitment of Commercial banks in the disbursement of education loan; the adequacy of education loans; the repayment pattern of beneficiaries; the impact of education loans on the socio economic status of beneficiaries; the Customer Bank Relationship .and many more.
