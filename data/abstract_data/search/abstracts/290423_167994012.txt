Defining Service Marketing from a Value Creation and Promise Management Perspective
The objective of the article is to develop a marketing definition for service providing organizations. The framework includes the foundations of a marketing definition, customer value, service as relational processes, marketing as one organizational function, how should marketing be implemented the intermediate variable. Drawing on the discussion of research into customer value, relationship marketing, service marketing and service logic as well as the promise concept in the previous sections, the article gave a marketing definition for services. The definition is based value creation as a foundational construct in marketing arid on the promise concept and can, therefore, be labelled a promises management definition (editorial notes)
