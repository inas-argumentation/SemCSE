The Study of Oily Sludge from Oilfield Sewage Treatment Plant
The target of sludge is analyzed.Under the optimum processing conditions,the oily sludge is put into static separation after mixing,the crude oil is recovered and the sludge is filtered after mechanical dewatering.The filtered sludge using calcium oxide separate thermo chemical oil sludge drying in the reactor,most cell water in the reactor reached evaporation,dehydration,sterilization etc..Using microwave heater heated part of low temperature steam to 250 degrees Celsius for further strengthening drying.Through test determination,by adding calcium after drying treatment sludge moisture is controlled within 35%
