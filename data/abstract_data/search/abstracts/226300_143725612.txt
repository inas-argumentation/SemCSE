Earth first! and the rhetoric of moral confrontation
Extreme elements within any major social movement create a tension that can threaten or energize the movement. In an examination of Earth First! a small but vocal environmental group, this paper analyzes the function of agitative rhetoric in the context of the contemporary environmental movement. Consisting of sit ins, sabotage, pranks, and other nondiscursive forms of persuasion, Earth First!'s agitative rhetoric illuminates the rhetorical dimensions of social movements in three areas. First, agitative rhetoric is both instrumental and consummatory as a form of communication and thus is symbolic at two levels. Second, such discourse forces the social movement to reexamine its goals as well as the strategies and tactics used to achieve such goals. Third, agitative rhetoric generates public attention for specific issues and gives the public a touchstone to differentiate extreme and moderate elements within the social movement.
