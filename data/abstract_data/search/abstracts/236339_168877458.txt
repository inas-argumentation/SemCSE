Entrepreneurial Learning, Novelty Centered Business Model Design, and New Venture Growth
How do entrepreneurs learn from prior entrepreneurial experience for business model design? Entrepreneurial learning literatures rooted in absorptive capacity theory and institutional theory offer conflicting predictions on this question. Business model literature also offers little insight. By unpacking different types of entrepreneurial experience, this study investigates how intra industry entrepreneurial experience and failure entrepreneurial experience affect novelty centered business model design and then new venture growth. We find that intra industry entrepreneurial experience has an inverted U shaped effect on novelty centered business model design while failure entrepreneurial experience has a positive effect on novelty centered business model design. Novelty centered business model design has a positive effect on new venture growth.
