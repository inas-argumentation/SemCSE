Research on shipping operating evaluation decision model based on data mining
In order to ensure shipping enterprises' operating profit, the period of shipping operating evaluation and decision are usually understood as a key. This article applies the financial evaluation indicators of shipping operating management process to analysis the relationship between income and expenditure of shipping operation, moreover, the article combines shipping operating data of shipping enterprises with the method of data mining to analysis the relationship of shipping income and cost in the period of shipping enterprises' operating process. Therefore, according to the relationship of shipping income and cost we can make a decision for shipping operating process demand.
