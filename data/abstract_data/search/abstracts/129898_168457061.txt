Strategic Planning and Business Performance: A Study of SMEs in Malaysia
There are many small and medium sized manufacturing enterprises operating in Malaysia. Some of these enterprises are successful and some face problems or failures within the first five years. Many strategists call for formal strategic planning in order to improve business performances of organizations. As strategic planning involves a process by which firms derive a strategy to enable them to anticipate and respond to the dynamic business environment, such efforts inevitably improve the competitiveness of business firms and eventually their performances. The purpose of this study is to examine whether by practicing strategic planning, the business performances of these SMEs can be resilient and sustainable over the long term.
