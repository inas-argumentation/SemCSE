Discussion on Improving the Deep hole Blasting Effect of Modified ANFO
Due to the large charge density of modified ANFO,the phenomenon of dead pressed is prone to in the deep hole blasting.The experimental research to reduce the charge density by adjusting the fineness and proportion of wood powder;adding a certain amount of perlite or expanded ammonium nitrate explosive,and so on,was carried out in this paper.Results show that adjusting the fineness of the wood and proportion(3% 5% has little influence on the charge density of ANFO,while adding 1 percent to 1.5 percent of perlite can effectively reduce the charge density with good safe reliability in the explosive production.And adding a certain amount(20%~30%)of the expanded ammonium nitrate explosive could significantly reduce the charge density,the greater the proportion of accession(50%,the more obvious effect,and at the same time it could effectively improve product initiation sensitivity.By improving the use of blasting methods it can also improving the deep hole blasting effect of modified ANFO.
