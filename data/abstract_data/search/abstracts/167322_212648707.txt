Single Side Band Direct Detection for Data Center Interconnect and Digital Radio Over Fiber
Distributed data centers have emerged as a key architecture for future optical networks to satisfy the ever growing demand for high speed connectivity and cloud services. This network architecture relies on high speed optical connections with interface rates beyond 100 Gb/s over 80 km. The conventional low cost IM/DD transmission scheme cannot meet this performance requirement while the full coherent detection solution is still too expensive. As a result, alternative self coherent detection (SCD) schemes such as single side band (SSB) transmission have attracted a lot of attention recently for high speed data center interconnect (DCI) applications. This invited paper provides a thorough overview about the progress of SSB transmission over the last five years. In addition, it also discusses an emerging application of SSB transmission scheme for high capacity digital radio over fiber (RoF) systems.
