Robust video denoising for mixed Poisson, Gaussian and impule noise
Most existing video denoising algorithms assume video noise to be additive Gaussian white noise, which is often impractical to deal with the mixed noises in reality. A robust video denoising algorithm is proposed in this paper to remove mixed Poisson, Gaussian and impulse noises from the video data. The denoising problem to remove mixed noises is formulated as a low rank and sparse video recovery minimization problem, which includes an effective prior to deal with mixed Poisson Gaussian noise. The numerical experiments indicate that the proposed video restoration method compares favorably against widely used video denoising algorithms RPCA, VBM3D and VBM4D.
