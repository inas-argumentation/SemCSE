Compressed Sensing and Implicit Cooperativity Based Data Gathering Algorithm in Mobile Crowdsensing Systems
Mobile crowdsensing technology is a new paradigm put forward for information perception in recent years. Through Mobile Crowdsensing Systems, we can obtain detailed peoplecentric information of our daily city life using mobile devices. This paper proposes a Compressed Sensing and Implicit Cooperativity based Data Gathering Algorithm (CIDG) in Mobile Crowdsensing Systems. This algorithm samples original data based on the compressed sensing sampling rule, and transmits sampled data over the network. Particularly, this paper discovers and defines implicit cooperativity between data and further exploit it to estimate un transmitted data, and finally recover the original data by exploiting the compressed sensing reconstruction algorithm. In the condition of sufficient mobile nodes in the network, the CIDG algorithm can recover all data in the whole sensing area using a certain part of data. The simulation results show that CIDG can remarkably reduce the amount of data transmitted over the network without losing data fidelity.
