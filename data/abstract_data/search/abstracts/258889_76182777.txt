The attitudes toward physician nurse collaboration among medical students and nursing students after clinical practice
Objective To compare the differences in the attitudes toward physician nurse collaboration among medical students and nursing students after clinical practice,and provide basis for education about physician nurse collaboration.Methods Totally 88 medical undergraduates,66 medical graduates,79 nursing undergraduates and 66 nursing graduates who had finished ten month clinical practice were recruited in the study.The participants were investigated with Jefferson Scale of Attitudes toward Physician Nurse Collaboration.Results In general,the participants' score of attitudes toward physician nurse collaboration was relatively high;the score of nursing students was significantly higher than medical students(P=0.036),especially on the attitude towards physicians rights(P=0.000).Medical students and nursing students showed high consistency on the needs of education about physician nurse collaboration.Conclusion It is suggested to strengthen the education about the physicians' and nurses' rights and duties and help medical students and nursing students to understand better and respect for each other,then lay the foundation of high quality medical service.
