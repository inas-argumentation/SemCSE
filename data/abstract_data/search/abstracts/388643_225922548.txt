A Study on The Impact of Covid 19 on Indian Economy
Today, the entire world is facing the great pandemic Covid 19. How to control such pandemic has gradually become a global issue. Social distancing and stay at home are the solutions put forward by the government to reduce the Covid cases. India's pandemic lockdown turns into a human tragedy. This has affected agriculture sector, manufacturing, transportation, import and export, health care sector, education sector and so on. The country is going through the great economic crisis which has to be made a conclusion. In order to protect our economy from all these issues a collective effort of society is very essential. Business firms and government should put hands together for making a better situation. The change in economic policies, increase in production and boosting up of foreign trade help in solving this situation. So in this paper we are trying to understand the actual economic situation of our country and some solutions to solve out this.
