Study on the Application of Excellent Performance Management in HACCP System of Rice and Brown Rice Milk Beverage Production Enterprises
[Objective]To investigate the application of excellent performance management in HACCP system of rice and brown rice milk beverage production enterprises.[Method]With brown rice and rice as raw material,the beverage was obtained by process of baking,gelatinization,enzymatic liquefaction.Through damage analysis on raw material,the key production processes,and anthropogenic factors affecting product safety,HACCP plan table was esablished,the excellent performance management was applied in HACCP system.[Result]The combination use of excellent performance management and HACCP could better play the prevention effect of HACCP management system.The product quality can be fully guaranteed,achieve the best effect of quality management.[Conclusion]The study can provide a reference basis for the combination use of excellent performance management and HACCP in the future.
