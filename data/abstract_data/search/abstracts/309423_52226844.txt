Private antitrust enforcement was much less significant than government enforcement in the 50 years of federal antitrust litigation that preceded World War II. The annual number of private antitrust suits grew markedly after the War, however, and is now over 10 times that of government actions. [1975] ANN. REP. OF DIRECrOR oF ADrIN.
