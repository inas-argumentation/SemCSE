Finite Element Analysis for Grilled Air Door of Mine Flameproof Fan
According to air door of flameproof fan work situation and its structure,a 3 D calculated model of air door is established with finite element analysis.With ANSYS software the air door structure including its stress,deformation under the load can be calculated.This offers theoretical evidence for optimizing the air door structure.The data is well analyzed,and the suggestion is given on improving the air door structure.The improved structure has yield good economic returns in direct and indirect aspect.
