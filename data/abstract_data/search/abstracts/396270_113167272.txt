Research on Function and Design of Walking Mechanism of Boom Type Roadheader
Boom type roadheader is mainly mechanical coal mine operation. Analysis of the roadheader walking mechanism function mainly as well as its main structure to set. The walking mechanism mainly include:track frame, drive unit, track, thrust wheel, tension pulley, take up device so on. According to the distribution of the different components used. Analysis of the roadheader walking mechanism design. This article analysis and design of according to the mechanism of boom type roadheader walking function and system. And put forward when the design should pay attention to those aspects of the problem.
