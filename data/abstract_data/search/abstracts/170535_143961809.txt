A Psychodynamic Approach: Some Thoughts on the Dynamics and Therapy of Self Mutilating Borderline Patients
My experience with borderline patients spans 23 years and five continents, and includes some 500 borderlines I have treated, supervised, followed up, known in an administrative capacity on an inpatient unit, or seen in consultation. The habitual self mutilators I have seen have been predominantly females between the ages of 15 and 40. In the majority, constitutional and environmental factors have interacted in such a way as not to lend support to an either/or interpretation regarding etiology. Where only one causative factor seems to be operative, it has more often been the environmental.
