Human Resource Management of Non human resource Manager
Human resource is one of the most important resources for the enterprise,improving HR manegement of non human resources manager is becoming one of vital methods to develop and better human resource in the organization.Generally,as the end user,non human resource manager act as HR management execute function,put the HR management in practice in the enterprise.On the contrary,HR manager is in charge of popularizing and establishing of HR policy.These two kinds of departments are both distiguish and cooperative,with different emphasis.
