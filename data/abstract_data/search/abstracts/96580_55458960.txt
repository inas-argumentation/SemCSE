Optical emission from single charge tunable quantum rings
We have succeeded in preparing excitons with a speci c charge in single semiconductor quantum rings. Buried InAs quantum rings are loaded with electrons from a reservoir through a tunneling barrier and an additional electron hole pair is generated by optical excitation. Single rings are addressed with nano optical techniques. We observe abrupt shifts in the emission energy as electrons are added one by one. Furthermore, the experiments provide unique insights into the interaction of electrons in semiconductor nano islands with their environment. 2001 Elsevier Science B.V. All rights reserved.
