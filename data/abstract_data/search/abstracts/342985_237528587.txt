Developing a Semi Automatic Pneumatic Bar Bending Machine
New products and development replacing manual labor to some extent is happening in every field and building structures area leading to automation. Most of the works carried by men involving great amount of time and effort in building construction is reduced by a machine. This project aims to do bending and cutting operations of steel bars using pneumatic technology. The main objective of this project is to build the pneumatic rod or bar bending machine, which can be used in the construction sites with less cost and man power compared to the existing bending machines, and increasing the productivity. The components of this machine are Pneumatic cylinder, Compressor, Cutting blades, Fixture, Electronic circuits, Valves, Pipes and Wiring. The rod is bent by the force developed by the Pneumatic cylinder piston arrangement, the fixture holds the rod in position and cutting or bending is effected. The main advantage of this machine is the square rectangular configuration of the rods, and is bent continuously without repositioning the rod in the machine.
