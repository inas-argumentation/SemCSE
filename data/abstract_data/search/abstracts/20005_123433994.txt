Approximation of Second Order Low Frequency Wave Loading in Multi Directional Waves
Further to the studies by Chen Rezende (OMAE2009) on the quadratic transfer function (QTF) of low frequency wave loading in which the QTF is developed by the series expansion associated with the difference frequency up to the order Do2 new formulations have been developed in order to take into account the effect of interactions between waves of different headings. It provides a novel method to evaluate the low frequency second order wave loads in a more accurate than usual order Do approximation (often called Newman approximation) and more efficient way comparing to the computation of complete QTF in multi directional waves. New developments including numerical results of different components of QTF are presented here. Furthermore, the time series reconstruction of excitation loads by quadruple sums in the motion simulation of mooring systems is analyzed and a new efficient and accurate scheme using only a triple sum is demonstrated.Copyright (c) 2010 by ASME
