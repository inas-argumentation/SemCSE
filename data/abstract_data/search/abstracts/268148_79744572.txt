Psychiatric morbidity in newly diagnosed carcinoma cervix and its comparison with benign condition
Cervical cancer is the second most common cancer in the world and ranked first in India. It is mainly caused by sexually transmitted infection (STI) of human papilloma virus (HPV) The patients of cervical cancer are associated with negative labels by general public because of their promiscuous behavior and unwillingness to have protected sex. They themselves have expressed shame, self blame, guilt and fear of social exclusion. The introduction of cancer cervix screening programme for selected population induces fear and anxiety, which may be detrimental for mental health. The diagnoses and treatment of cancer have significant impact on physical, psychological, emotional and social aspects of individual and culture.
