: Pregnant Targhee ewe lambs (n 32; BW 45.6 2.2 kg) were allotted 24 randomly to 1 of 4 treatments in a completely randomized design to examine effects of level and 25 source of dietary Se on maternal and fetal visceral organ mass, cellularity estimates, and 26 maternal jejunal crypt cell proliferation and vascularity. Diets contained (DM basis) either no 27 added Se (CON) or supranutritional Se included in diets from high Se wheat at 3.0 ppm Se (SW)28 or from sodium selenate at 3 (S3) or 15 (S15) ppm Se. Diets were similar in CP (15.5% and ME 29 (2.68 Mcal/kg of DM) and were fed to meet or exceed requirements. Treatments were initiated 30 at 50 5 d of gestation. The CON, SW, S3, and S15 treatment diets provided 2.5, 75, 75, and 31 375 ug Se/kg BW, respectively. On day 134 10 of gestation, ewes were necropsied and tissues 32 harvested. Contrasts, including CON vs. Se treatments (SW, S3, and S15) SW vs. S3, and S3 33 vs. S15, were used to evaluate differences among Se levels and sources. There were no 34 differences in ewe initial and final BW.
