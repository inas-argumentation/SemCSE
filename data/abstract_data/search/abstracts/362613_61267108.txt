A Study on the Techniques of Teaching English in the First Grade of the International Primary School Laboratory Of UM
Considering the importance of English in this globalization era, our government issued the decree no 20/2003 about the establishment of international standard school in every level of educations. It aims at increasing the quality of Indonesian human resources so that Indonesia can compete with other countries. As the realization of the decree, nowadays there are many international standard schools from primary until high school level. In international standard primary school, English is used as a medium of instruction. The students are expected to master English at functional and informational literacy stage. This is different from the objectives of the teaching English in non international primary school. Therefore, it is important to investigate the techniques of teaching English in international standard primary school. This study was conducted to describe the techniques of teaching English in the first grade of the International Primary School Laboratory of UM (SD Lab Internasional UM) The techniques investigated were the techniques of teaching language components which include vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar and teaching language skills namely listening, speaking, reading, and writing. This study employed a descriptive design. The subject of this study was the English teacher of the first grade of SD Lab Internasional UM. The instruments used to collect the data were the researcher, observation forms, field notes and interview guides. By employing all of the instruments, the relevant data were collected through observation and interview. The data taken from the observation and interview were analyzed and presented in the form of description. After observing and interviewing the English teacher, the researcher found that the teacher applied various techniques in teaching the language components. In teaching grammar, she taught simple patterns in simple sentences. She also applied question and answer, substitution, and drilling using flash cards. Then, to teach vocabulary she used definition, games and songs, flash cards and realia, demonstrations, and reading text on the work sheet. For teaching pronunciation, she used listen and repeat, song, and reading aloud. Besides, to teach listening she applied simple dictation, story telling, listen and color, listen and repeat, listen and identify, listen to the songs, question and answer and teacher talk. For speaking, she used simple dialog, classroom language, listen and repeat, role play, game and English camp activity. Next, to teach reading she applied reading aloud mostly. Finally, in teaching writing she used matching sentences, substitution writing and free composition. Based on the finding, the researcher concludes that most of the techniques used by the English teacher were appropriate for teaching English to the first graders. She applied the techniques by considering the students' characteristics. Besides, the techniques were also in line with teaching strategies in immersion program. However, when teaching using games, the teacher needs to simplify the instruction to avoid students' confusion. Then, to increase students' vocabulary she needs to provide class displays in English. Besides, it is also beneficial to provide a reading area in the classroom to increase students' interest in reading. In addition, she needs to use other more interesting techniques of teaching writing, reading and speaking.
