The purpose of further atmospheric electric measurements in the troposphere and stratosphere
Owing to the absence of the author from May to July while engaged in potential gradient and conductivity observations at a base station near "Strato Camp" and in preparations for recording of atmospheric conductivity during the proposed 1935 stratosphere flight of the National Geographic Society and United States Army Air Corps from Rapid City, South Dakota, the manuscript for this paper could not be completed for publication in the Transactions. It is expected it will appear later in the Journal of Terrestrial Magnetism and Atmospheric Electricity.
