CONTINUITY EDITING DALAM PENCAPAIAN CERITA PADA FILM MAMMON
Money is one of the most important things and also a source of problems for most people. A variety of quick ways to get money. However, there are also those who prefer shortcuts with unseen ways, namely to do pesugihan. Pesugihan is taken from the Javanese language, which means everything that makes it rich. The aim is to get success and wealth by making an agreement with a genie or demon. In this film there are 4 main characters namely Aura, David, Lia, and Rizky. The one doing the pesugihan here is the parent of Aura. Then when Aura and the 3 friends wanted to go on vacation, Aura's parents wanted all three of his friends to be sacrificial for his pesugihan. The author will use the theory of Continuity Editing that will be used during editing. Continuity Editing is a method of editing a film that contains the connection of two scenes that have continuity. Editing continuity is in the invisible cutting category. With invisible cutting, the audience does not "see" or feel a connection between the shots. By using Continuity in this film, making the audience feel a connection between shots so that the story becomes continuous. By using this theory the author seeks that the film can provide information to the audience.
