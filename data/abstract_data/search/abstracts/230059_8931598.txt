Cancer therapy using natural ligands that target estrogen receptor beta.
Estrogen receptor beta (ERb) is one of the two key receptors (ERa, ERb) that facilitate biological actions of 17b estradiol (E2) ERb is widely expressed in many tissues, and its expression is reduced or lost during progression of many tumors. ERb facilitates estrogen signaling by both genomic (classical and non classical) and extra nuclear signaling. Emerging evidence suggests that ERb functions as a tissue specific tumor suppressor with anti proliferative actions. Recent studies have identified a number of naturally available selective ERb agonists. Targeting ERb using its naturally available ligands is an attractive approach for treating and preventing cancers. This review presents the beneficial actions of ERb signaling and clinical utility of several natural ERb ligands as potential cancer therapy.
