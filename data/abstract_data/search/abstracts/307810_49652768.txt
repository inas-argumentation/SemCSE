Using adaptive multi populations GA and GANN to select signal features and classify signals
In order to optimize signal features selection and signals classification, the paper uses the F Ratio rule to calculate fitness function of GA(genetic algorithm) introduces the multi populations parallel and competition strategy into GA, and uses adaptive genetic operator to improve the GA. In addition, the paper uses the improved GA to optimize the neural network (NN) The results of the experiments for three typical radio signals show that the proposed methods of this paper can more effectively select the signal features subsets, and have higher classification accuracy.
