A soliton on a vortex filament
The intrinsic equation governing the curvature K and the torsion t of an isolated very thin vortex filament without stretching in an incompressible inviscid fluid is reduced to a non linear Schrodinger equation \frac{\rm l}{i}\frac{\partial \psi}\partial t} \frac{\partial^2\psi}\partial s^2}\textstyle\frac{1}{2}\psi|^2+A)\psi, where t is the time, s the length measured along the filament, ps is the complex variable \psi \kappa\exp\left(i\int_0^{s}\tau ,ds\right) and is a function oft. It is found that this equation yields a solution describing the propagation of a loop or a hump of helical motion along a line vortex, with a constant velocity 2t. The relation to the system of intrinsic equations derived by Betchov (1965) is discussed.
