Study on Drying Characteristics of Sewage Sludge Using Hot air and Microwave
The effect of thin layer thickness and other operating condition on the drying characteristics and energy consumption of sewage sludge were investigated respectively by experiments of thin layer drying sludge using hot air and microwave drying.The results showed that the water was migrated to surface of sludge from inner hardly and the drying rate reduced as "stepped",because the sludge surface is easy to "crust" with its "plastic" characteristic in hot air drying process.The microwave drying of sludge had short time,high drying rate and saved genergy.
