Effects of physical exercise in dual task performance and global cognition of elders with Alzheimer's disease
The increased prevalence of Alzheimer's Disease (AD) has been generating a pursuit for different strategies that may help its treatment1. Physical exercise has been getting increasing attention, considering the low coast and benefits for physical and mental health2. To evaluate the effect of 12 week multimodal training program in dual task performance, and on global cognitive function in elderly with AD.
