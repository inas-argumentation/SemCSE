A STUDY ON RISK ASSESSMENT IN CONSTRUCTION PROJECT OF AN EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTION
The risk involved in the construction works is relatively higher than the other works. The biggest challenge is to reduce the risks involved. Risk assessments include identifying risks, analyzing risk and controlling risk qualitatively by various methods. In this study, the Questionnaire checklist method is used to identifying the risk in the construction work, at an educational institution. Based on this, risk assessment is made to control the risks.
