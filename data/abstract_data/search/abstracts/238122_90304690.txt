THE "UNHOLY" CHIKUNGUNYA DENGUE ZIKA TRINITY: A THEORETICAL ANALYSIS
Aedes aegypti is the vector for numerous diseases in humans and other (reservoir) hosts, such as chikungunya, dengue fever and Zika virus. A new deterministic model is designed and used to assess the dynamics of the three diseases in a population where Aedes mosquitoes are abundant. The model to be designed incorporates the recently released imperfect vaccine against dengue virus (Dengvaxia(r) vaccine by Sanofi Pasteur) as well as allow for sexual transmission of Zika. Further, the model allows for the assessment of the population level impact of three biological hypotheses, namely a competitive dengue chikungunya Zika superinfection hierarchy, an antibody dependent enhancement of dengue over Zika and that the Dengvaxia vaccine can induce reduced susceptibility to Zika infection in vaccinated individuals. After carrying out detailed theoretical analyses to gain insight into its qualitative features, the model is then fitted to the data recorded during the 2015 2016 outbreaks of the three diseases in Mexico.
