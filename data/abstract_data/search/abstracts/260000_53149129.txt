In cerebellar development, we have shown that Hic1 expression coincides with the onset of differentiation of granule cell precursors. Loss of Hic1 function in mice cooperates with loss of Ptch1, a tumour suppressor in the Hedgehog pathway, to induce medulloblastoma. We have further shown that Hic1 is a sequence specific transcriptional repressor, and that deregulation of it target genes contributes to the pathogenesis of medulloblastoma. We now show that HIC1 is frequently hypermethylated in primary human glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) To search for target genes of HIC1 in this tumour type, we infected gliomasphere cells with a recombinant adenovirus expressing either HIC1, or a Gal control. Gene expression array analysis revealed a dramatic change in the transcriptional profile of these cells in response to HIC1 overexpression. Genes whose expression was repressed more than 4 fold include TGFa, ESR2, GFAP and IL6R. Unexpectedly, a large number of genes were induced by HIC1, including ID1, EPHA5, CCL26 and ID2, whose expression increased by over 7 fold.
