Detection of Phishing Websites Using SVM Technique
Internet has become an essential component of our everyday social and financial activities. Nevertheless, internet users may be vulnerable to different types of web threats, which may cause financial damages, identity theft, loss of private information, brand reputation damage and loss of customer's confidence in e commerce and online banking. Website phishing can be described as mimicking a trusted website to obtain sensitive information from online users such as usernames and passwords. Black lists, white lists and the utilization of search methods are examples of solutions to minimize the risk of this problem. Phishing is a website forgery with an intention to track and steal the sensitive information of online users. The attacker fools the user with social engineering techniques such as SMS, voice, email, website and malware. In this paper we describe various parameters to detect website as phishing or legitimate along with surveying the existed work done in phishing detection. As feature extraction is the main part in this work, we have given various feature parameters from which one can choose the desirable parameters for further classification of input website as legitimate or phishing. In further work, we will explore this topic on a collected dataset and will propose an algorithm which can classify the websites as legitimate or non legitimate
