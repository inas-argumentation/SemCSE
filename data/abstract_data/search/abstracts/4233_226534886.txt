MODERN DAY APPROACH FOR BUYING AND SELLING NEEDS WITH THE USE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY: A BARTER SYSTEM
BARTER System is an online platform for retail shops and customers to interact at a common place to fulfill their buying and selling needs. This platform makes it easier for a retail shop owner to add products to the database and sell their products with an insured and secure log of what is and what has been sold through online as well as in offline manner. This proposed system is first going to be deployed in a single locality for experimental purpose and to check the growth of the system. After that, as soon as positive response is gained then it can be deployed in other localities too. It will be easier for people nowadays, who are pretty much occupied 24/7 in the busy lives and feel stressed on getting even a little bit of time to get their daily necessary items. All their daily needs will be cared for at a single place.
