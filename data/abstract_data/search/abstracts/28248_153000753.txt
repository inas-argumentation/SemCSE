Supply Chain Management: Strategy, Planning and Operation
With the growth of new products and markets, the supply chain systems supporting both manufactured products and services have become intertwined directly or indirectly with the logistics operations for other competing products as well. While e business is simplifying the communications between suppliers and customers, many suppliers are still finding it challenging to provide timely deliveries of goods and services because of geographical distance and resource limitations. Thus, strategic planning and scheduling of logistics operations are important in supply chain systems. This book on supply chain management raises both theoretical and practical issues and discusses solution approaches for them. The book covers not only supply chain strategy and concepts, but it also gives students a clear understanding of the analytical tools necessary to solve some supply chain problems. It provides the foundations for understanding the strategic importance of good supply chain design, planning, and operation for different industries that generally encompass inventory, transportation, information, and facilities as the key drivers of supply chain performance. The first part of the book covers a strategic framework to analyze design, planning, and operational decisions within supply chains. Chapter I specifically provides fundamental concepts of a supply chain and establishes the impact that supply chain decisions have on the performance of a firm. Chapter 2 describes the supply chain strategy in the context of a firm's value. The relationship of competitive strategy and supply chain strategy of a firm is established and used to emphasize the importance of ensuring that strategic fit exists between the two strategies. Chapter 3 describes four supply chain performance drivers (inventory, transportation, facilities, and information) to identify and link to a company's ability to support its competitive strategy. The next three chapters explain the significance of planning in a supply chain, identify decisions that are part of the planning process, and discuss tools that supply chain managers can use for planning.
