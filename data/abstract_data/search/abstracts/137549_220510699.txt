ADVANCED SWAY SUPPRESSION CONTROL SYSTEM OF OVERHEAD CRANE
A gantry type of overhead crane plays a vital role in all the industries such as manufacturing and transportation industries to move the payloads from one machine to another machine. The position and controlling of cranes are affected by many factors such as weight of the component, height of the crane and distance between the rails etc. because of these factors, the cables for hoisting and support of payload can lead to natural swaying. This sway is detrimental to safe and efficient operation. Therefore the control of swaying is very important to place the payload in correct position.
