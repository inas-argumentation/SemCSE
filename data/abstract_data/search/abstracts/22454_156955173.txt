Study of Present Situation,Problems and Countermeasures of National Defense Intellectual Property Laws and Regulations System
The article summarizes the present situation of the national defense intellectual property laws and regulations System.On this basis,the article points out some problems of the system.The problems include the legislation lacks systematicness and there are many legislative blank,the legislation lacks scientificalness and there are many low rank regulations,the legislation lacks integrity and some core contents are not included.At last,the article puts forward some countermeasures such as strengthening the national defense intellectual property legal theory research and the top level design,constructing complete legal system,strengthening the regulations'cohesion,enriching the regulations contents.
