Design of electronic ballast for high pressure sodium lamp based on IR2155
Presents an electronic ballast for high pressure sodium (HPS) lamp, which uses a power factor correction chip L6560 and a half bridge driver IC IR2155, which features simple circuit topology, soft switching modes, unity power factor, constant lamp power, and short start time by adjusting the inverter operation frequency. Point out that the 3.5 kV ignition pulse generated by ignitor is ample for the ignition of the lamp in both cold and hot state and concludes from experimental results that the ballast has good performance with PF (power factor) 0.99 and Ts(start time)2.4 min.
