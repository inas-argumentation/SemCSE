Proceedings of the eighth international conference on offshore mechanics and Arctic Engineering. 1989
This book contains papers presented at a conference on materials technology. The topics covered are: European offshore steel projects; Thickness effects; Fracture control; Fatigue; Local brittle zone; Welding technology; Fabrication and quality control; Probabilistic FM and reliability; Material performance; High strength steels; Offshore steels and aluminum; Concrete and structures; and Composite materials.
