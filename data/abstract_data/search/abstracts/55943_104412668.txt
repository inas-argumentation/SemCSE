Effect of NH4H2PO4 content on properties of P Sn W B glass system with ultra low melting sealing temperature
The glass structure, thermal and electrical properties and water durability of the P Sn W B glasses, prepared by traditional melt quenching technique, were examined. The glasses exhibited relatively low transition temperatures (90 150 degC) ultra low sealing temperature (250 300 degC) high electrical resistivity (rv 1011 13 O*cm) high coefficient of thermal expansion(CTE 182 230*10 7/K) and excellent water durability, could be used as low melting sealing material. With the increasing NH4H2PO4/P2O5 ratio, the O/P ratio increased, from the EDX analysis. The glass structure changed with the composition, the infrared spectra showed that the P O double bonds, O H bending vibration decreased whereas new bonds like P O W formed, enhancing the glass structure, also, the water durability of glass was improved.
