Production line for elevator brake and safety tongs
The utility model discloses a production line for an elevator brake and safety tongs. The production line comprises a support, motors and a conveying belt. A conveying shaft is installed on the support, the conveying belt is arranged on the conveying shaft, output shafts of the motors are connected with the conveying shaft and provide power for rotation of the conveying shaft, and the conveying belt comprises crawler belt pieces, drawing conveying channels are formed in the crawler belt pieces, connecting rods are arranged in the drawing conveying channels, and the conveying belt further comprises installation platforms which are detachably installed on the connecting rods. When two production lines are needed, functions of the two production lines can be achieved, so that cost is reduced, and additional production lines do not need to be purchased. When only one production line is needed, the installation platforms on the connecting rods can be detached, the connecting rods are pushed into the drawing conveying channels, in this way, only one production line occupies the space, and therefore the space is saved.
