Contemporary impact of tobacco use on periodontal disease in the USA
Periodontal disease (PD) is a common chronic disease that can be expensive to treat, and when untreated, can inflict significant morbidity and tooth loss. Growing evidence also links PD to higher risks of cancer, cardiovascular and other chronic diseases. Cigarette smoking has been estimated to cause 8 million cases of PD in the USA or more than half of the 15 million cases of PD.1 However, these estimates are based on data from the third National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) conducted from 1988 to 1994.
