Institutionalization of Radical Movements and the Maintenance of Radical Identity in the Life Cycle
The rise of radical movements signifies a rejuvenation of the political arena. Social issues beyond the concern of the governing coalition are brought to the surface by new dissidents. In industrial and technological societies the development process is constantly creating new issues or forcing those long delayed to the surface. The moments of quiescence between periods of conflict are a hiatus between the resolution of historic issues and the emergence of unresolved social problems or developing social issues and forces.
