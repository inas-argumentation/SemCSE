Research on collaborative beamforming for a distributed satellite cluster based on Convex Optimization
Distributed satellite cluster (DSC) consisted of number of formation flying satellites is hot research topic in the space information network. In this paper, a collaborative beamforming algorithm is proposed to enhance transceiver capability of the DSC by forming a virtual large aperture array (VLAA) based on random antenna array theory. According to the location information characteristic of the formation flying satellites in a DSC, the array manifold vector of the VLAA can be divided into two parts: one is the random term associated with the satellite perturbation; the other is the fixed term related to the design of the formation. The random term is analyzed by the random antenna array theory. Furthermore, we also formulate an optimization problem to meet the requirement of a fixed electromagnetic wave transceiver task by a optimal formation of the DSC based on mixed integer programming (MIP) Simulation results showed that the proposed algorithm can obtain an optimal formation of a DSC to enhance electromagnetic wave transceiver capability and extend the collaborative beamforming technology to the research field of space information network.
