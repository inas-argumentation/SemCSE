Concentrated aqueous dispersions of low defect few layer thick graphene using surface active ionic liquid for enhanced enzyme activity
Low energy ultrasonic assisted aqueous phase exfoliation of rather defect free graphene (D/G 0.19) in high yield (3.0 mg/ml) is achieved using benzimidazolium based surface active ionic liquid (SAIL) appended with ester functionalized alkyl chain. The obtained dispersions are found to be stable against agglomeration or sedimentation for at least 15 days, owing to columbic repulsions offered by electron deficient p conjugated SAIL ions adsorbed onto graphene. The exfoliated few layer thick graphene sheets (2 layers 53% and 3 5 layers 20% offer high surface area in conjunction with high concentration, which is exploited for efficient loading of an enzyme, cellulase, via hydrophobic interactions. The graphene appended cellulase is found to exhibit remarkable increase in enzyme activity (13 fold) as compared to cellulase in buffer. It is expected that the present results would act as a platform for synthesis and utilization of new SAILs for graphene exfoliation, stabilization and functionalization required to widen the application arena of graphene.
