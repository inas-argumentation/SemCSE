Filter and Wrapper based Feature Selection Using Mutual Information for Rolling Elements Bearing Diagnosis
Corresponding author. Abstract Simultaneously to select most relevant and least redundant(MRLR) features is a crucial task in the complex engineering system such as rolling element bearing fault diagnosis. In practices, a common assumption is that features and class variables are linearly distributed, while features are evaluated in terms of relevancy and redundancy in the filter method. This study, therefore, proposes a hybrid feature selection scheme (HFS) is a combination of filter and wrapper algorithms for effectively identifying most discriminant feature subset that can be further utilized with a classier. This paper applies mutual information based most relevant and least redundant(MRLR) evaluation metric with genetic algorithm (GA) to yield a series of discriminatory subsets candidates. Outputs of GA are further refined in wrapper phase to select a most discriminate subset, which produces better classification accuracy. The efficacy of the proposed HFS MRLR is verified with a fault diagnosis application for low speed rolling element bearing under various conditions
