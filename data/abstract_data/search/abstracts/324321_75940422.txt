Prevention of ureteral injury in laparoscopic hysterectomy
Objective:To study the reasons and preventive measures of ureteral injury in laparoscopic hysterectomy.Methods:The clinical data of 6 patients with ureteral injury who underwent laparoscopic hysterectomy(LH,including total laparoscopic hysterectomy and laparoscopically assisted vaginal hysterectomy) from Jan.2009 to Dec.2010 were analyzed retrospectively.Results:Ureteral injury occurred in 6 cases of 337 LH in two years,with the rate of 1.78%.All 6 cases of ureteral injury had pelvic adhesion and occurred at right ureter.Ureteral break and ureteral fistula by heat injury were 2 and 4 cases respectively.The main reasons of ureteral injury in LH were using instruments incorrectly and coagulating mass tissue in handling with uterine blood vessels.Conclusions:LH must be carefully selected to the patients with severe pelvic adhesion.The key to avoid ureteral injury in LH required all instruments must be mastered and used correctly and do not coagulate massive tissue in dealing with uterine blood vessels.
