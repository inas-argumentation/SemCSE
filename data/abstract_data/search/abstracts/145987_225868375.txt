Von xa hoi, chia se tri thuc va hieu qua lam viec cua nhan vien trong khu vuc cong tai tinh Tien Giang
The study aims to propose and test a theoretical model that investigates the relationship between social capital, knowledge sharing and individual work performance in public sector. The survey was carried out to collect data using convenience sampling. This research collected 298 valid responses from officials who have been working at 18 departments, agencies in Tien Giang province. A structural equation modeling procedure was used to verify the proposed hypotheses. Our findings revealed that all three dimensions of social capital (structural, cognitive and relational dimensions) facilitate both tacit and explicit knowledge sharing. In addition, both explicit and tacit knowledge sharing practices influence individual work performance. We suggest some policies 132 Doan B. Son, Ha M. Tri. Von xa hoi va chia se tri thuc Von xa hoi bao gom ba chieu kich khac nhau do la chieu kich cau truc, quan he va tri nhan (Nahapiet Ghoshal, 1998) Cac chieu kich nay duoc su dung nhieu trong quan tri tri thuc (Ganguly et al. 2019) Chieu kich cau truc cua von xa hoi gom co cac moi quan he, mat do, cau hinh va su phu hop mang luoi (Nahapiet Ghosal, 1998) Moi quan he mang luoi cho phep truy cap vao cac nguon luc duoc nhung trong cac moi quan he xa hoi va tri thuc la nguon luc quy gia nhat duoc nhung trong moi quan he do (Ganguly et al. 2019) Cac nhan vien trong cac to chuc se thiet lap nhieu moi quan he truc tiep va mang luoi xa hoi lam tang them co hoi cho cac moi lien he do. Khi do, nhan vien san sang chia se nhung y tuong va nguon luc voi nhung nguoi ma ho co moi quan he gan gui (Chow Chan, 2008) Qua trinh tao va chia se tri thuc trong mot to chuc co quan he voi cac dac diem cua cau truc mang luoi xa hoi (Borgatti Cross, 2003) Cac moi quan he chat che va cau truc mang luoi gan ket cua chieu kich cau truc cua von xa hoi gop phan chuyen giao tri thuc hieu qua giua cac tac nhan trong mang luoi (Reagans McEvily, 2003) Dac biet, cac mang luoi xa hoi manh co cuong do cao voi su tuong tac gan gui va tan suat tuong tac cao se thuc day chuyen giao tri thuc an (Ganguly et al. 2019) Dua vao cac lap luan nay, nghien cuu de xuat cac gia thuyet nhu sau: H1: Von xa hoi cau truc co tac dong tich cuc den chia se tri thuc hien H2: Von xa hoi cau truc co tac dong tich cuc den chia se tri thuc an Chieu kich quan he cua von xa hoi duoc the hien bang niem tin va su dang tin cay la dieu can thiet cho cau truc mang luoi xa hoi day dac de no thuc day qua trinh tiep thu va dong hoa tri thuc (Ganguly et al. 2019) Niem tin giua cac thanh vien trong to chuc la nhan to lam cai thien cac moi quan he tuong tac va thuc day chia se tri thuc thanh cong (Chow Chan, 2008) Niem tin dong vai tro quan trong trong cac sang kien chia se tri thuc. Neu cac thanh vien cua to chuc co cac moi quan he dang tin cay thi ho san sang cho phep dong nghiep truy cap cac nguon luc cua ho. Chia se tri thuc va hieu qua lam viec cua nhan vien Chia se tri thuc giua cac nhan vien se tao ra nhung y tuong sang tao, doi moi, nho do ho co the giai quyet duoc nhung van de mot cach hieu qua (Lee, 2016; Nonaka, 1994) Chia se tri thuc hieu qua se giup giam chi phi san xuat, tang cuong kha nang doi moi va chat luong dich vu, vi vay se lam tang hieu qua cua to chuc (Wang Noe, 2010) Trong khu vuc cong, chia se tri thuc cung voi viec su dung cac he thong thong tin giup tiet kiem thoi gian va lam tang niem tin cua nhan vien trong qua trinh thuc hien cong viec. Do do, nhan vien se it bi loi va cai thien duoc hieu qua cong viec (Kang, Kim, Chang, 2008) Chia se tri thuc tich cuc tao dieu kien cho su ket hop va chuyen tu tri thuc hien co sang tri thuc moi va thuc day thuc hien cac y tuong sang tao, tac dong tich cuc den hieu qua cong viec (Yun Lee, 2017) Chia se tri thuc hien lam tang tri thuc va ky nang duoc ma hoa cua nguoi nhan va nguoi cho tri thuc cung co the hieu sau sac hon tri thuc cua chinh ho thong qua phan hoi va thao luan (Ipe, 2003) Su tuong tac va lien he ca nhan lien quan den chia se tri thuc hien se thuc day hieu qua hoc tap, lam thay doi cau truc tri thuc cua nhan vien va dan den cai thien hieu qua lam viec.
