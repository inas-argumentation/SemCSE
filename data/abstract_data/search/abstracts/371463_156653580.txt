A Brief Introduction of the Revision of Chinese Trade Barrier Investigation Mechanism And Reference of Section 301 of US Trade Law
The implementation of Section 301 of the Trade Act of symbolizes that the USA has become the first nation to establish the trade barrier investigation mechanism.For a long time,Section 301 of the US Trade Law is the most effective tool for U.S to open up overseas market and protect its own economic benefits.It is also one of the most important bases of domestic law to deal with trade barrier conflicts with foreign countries.Although China has preliminarily set up the trade barrier investigation mechanism,it still has a long way to go compared with the American trade barrier investigation mechanism stipulated by Section 301.Besides following current WTO regulations and domestic investigation rules,we shall also learn from other countries in order to provide new references to further improve China's trade barrier investigation mechanism and foreign trade laws in the future.
