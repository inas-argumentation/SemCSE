Remote Sensing Satellite Jitter Detection Based on Image Registration and Convolutional Neural Network Fusion
Attitude jitter of observation satellites is a problem that degenerates the imaging quality in high resolution (HR) remote sensing. In order to retrieve jitters with high accuracy, a new way to fuse image registration and convolutional neural network by a Kalman filter is described. In this method, a novel and complete framework based on image registration are proposed to estimate the on orbit attitude variations from multi spectrum remote sensing images. Then a convolutional neural network(CNN) is proposed to estimate the attitude jitter by automatically learning essential scene features from a single image. A Kalman filter is derived to fuse the jitter obtained from the two algorithms. The proposed methodology is trained and tested with PatternNet dataset. Simulation results show that the attitude variations are detected with high accuracy and the deformed images are compensated.
