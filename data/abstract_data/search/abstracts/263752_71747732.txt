Drug treatment of premature ejaculation: Pharmacodynamic and pharmacokinetic paradigms
Of all SSRIs, daily treatment with 20 mg paroxetine exerts the strongest ejaculation delay in men with lifelong premature ejaculation. Based on basic insights into serotonergic neurotransmission, animal and human studies, it is argued that on demand use of conventional SSRIs hardly delays ejaculation. Studies with the new type of SSRIs with short half life and short Tmax should elucidate whether an immediate high peak concentration will lead to a strong ejaculation delay within 1 2 h.
