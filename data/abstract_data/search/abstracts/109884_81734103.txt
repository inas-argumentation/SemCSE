Effects of neuromuscular electrical stimulation in medium and low frequency on muscle quadriceps: a comparative study.
Objective: The purpose of this study was to investigate the effects of neuromuscular electrical stimulation (NMES) in different frequencies on muscle quadriceps. A cross sectional, randomized controlled and blind study. Methods: 30 individuals (12 men e 18 women) average age of 24.67 years, weight 65.62Kg and height 1.69m were evaluated. Three random tests conditions were applied: maximum voluntary isometric contraction (MVIC) maximum voluntary isometric contraction with medium frequency current (MVIC MF) and maximum voluntary isometric contraction with low frequency current (MVIC LF) Four MVIC were applied in each situation. The time between different isometric contraction types was 90 seconds and between the same conditions of contraction was 10 seconds. Results: Two way ANOVA test show that MVIC MF had higher values on peak torque than MVIC LF (p=0.02) Significant statistical results was found when comparing MVIC MF and MVIC (p=0.03) but not in MVIC and MVIC LF (p=0.52) Conclusion: Maximum voluntary isometric contraction associated with medium electrical frequency stimulation was more effective than other conditions of NMES.
