Total level evaluation of the papers from Xi'an Polytechnic University collected by EI in 2006 2009
In order to quantitative analyze and evaluate the related result of stage of teaching and research,from the amount,author,organization,source,topic and citation frequence ect.six aspects,the situation of the papers collected from Xi'an Polytechnic University by "EI" during 2006 to 2009 were counted and analyzed.The results show that the papers'amount retrieved by EI present year by year up trend,and conference article is main document type;the authors above 50% concentrated in the school of electronics information,environment and the chemical engineering,science,the advantage of textile discipline is not obvious.Analysis of the contents show that the hot topic of research much concentrated in the textile and electronics,through interdiscipline study,assist with a mathematical model and the physical chemistry optimization methods and modern computer technology,and monitoring measures to improve the performance of fabric or material,to extend the application field in order to the innovation of performance,technologies and means.However,due to the level of cooperation between researchers inadequate,resulting in lack of depth and breadth.Compared with other similar universities,the research ability and academic level still have a bigger gap,the development of disciplines is still in unbalance.It is suggested that further improve research environment,support the research projects and innovation talent of development prospect,promote space of existence and development,gradually formate research advantage direction and academics special features,help the research papers to produce,exaltate the rate retrieved by EI.
