Nonlinear finite element analysis on composite reinforced concrete shear walls without opening
Based upon the experimental studies,nonlinear finite element analysis on composite reinforced concrete shear wall without opening by using finite element software ANSYS was comprehensively studied.Based on the reasonable mechanics models and methodologies,the seismic behavior was researched for the wall structure on the horizontal loading test.The crack expansion,failure mode were discussed.The prefabricated parts and the cast in situ concrete parts of the composite reinforced concrete shear walls could work together in coordination very well under loading.
