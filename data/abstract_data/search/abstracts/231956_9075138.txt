Mobile Heart rate Monitor and Arrhythmia Detection
Integration of mobile technologies and healthcare informatics sets the stage for the innovative emerging research discipline called Mobile Health. Mobile Health includes a wide variety of applications for preventive healthcare, fitness, disease diagnosis and monitoring. One application area is heart rate monitoring using mobile camera. This application area is based on signal processing. In this study, mobile heart rate monitor is examined. The Red based Contactless Heart rate Detection Algorithm is proposed and application results are presented. Arrhythmia detection capabilities are discussed.
