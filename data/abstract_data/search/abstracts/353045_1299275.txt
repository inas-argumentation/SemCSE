Independent Component Analysis
In the independent component (IC) model it is assumed that the p variate random vector x Oz m, where m is a location vector, O is a full rank px p mixing matrix, and z is a p variate vector with mutually independent components. In the independent component analysis (ICA) the aim is to find an estimate of an unmixing matrix G such that Gx has independent components. We talk about standardization of the IC model, and on the basis of n independent copies of x, we consider one sample testing and estimation procedures for O (or G) We also discuss comparison of different unmixing matrix estimates.
