Introduction to the Verb particle Experience: Semantics, Argument Structure and Morphology
<draft only; a condensed version, augmented with overviews by Nicole Dehe and Silke Urban of the syntactic and psycholinguistic aspects of particle verbs will appear in a volume published by Mouton de Gruyter, whose working title is 'Verb Particle Explorations' edited by N. Dehe, R. Jackendoff, A. McIntyre and S. Urban> This book is about those Germanic verbal constructions variously referred to as 'verb particle constructions' 'particle verbs' 'separable (complex) verbs' or 'phrasal verbs' This introduction tries to give an overview of the more important issues surrounding particle verbs, and of the existing theoretical approaches to these issues, devoting special attention to issues not treated elsewhere in the book.
