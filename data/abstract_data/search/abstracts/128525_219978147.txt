Graph Embedded Pose Clustering for Anomaly Detection Supplementary Material
The supplementary material provides additional ablation experiments, as well as details regarding experiment splits and results, and information regarding the proposed spatial attention graph convolution, and implementation of our method and of the baseline methods. Specifically, Section 2 presents further ablation experiments conducted to evaluate our model. Section 3 presents the base action words learned by our model in both settings. In Section 4 we go into further details regarding the proposed spatial attention graph convolution operator. Section 5 provides implementation details for our method, and Section 6 describes the implementations the of baseline methods used. For the Coarse grained experiments, per split results and class lists are available in Section 7 and in Section 8 respectively. Finally, the complete list of classes used for Kinetics 250 is provided in Appendix A.
