Disaster Resilience Assessment of Building and Transportation System
ABSTRACT The paper presents a new methodology to assist decision makers in the management of critical events such as earthquakes evaluating the recovery time, and the resilience index of a building system that is a component of the physical infrastructure dimension of the PEOPLES Resilience framework. The interdependencies between building system and transportation network in term of accessibility are modeled. Finally, the methodology has been implemented in a software and has been applied in two case studies: (a) the old medieval center of L'Aquila town and (b) the Treasure Island in the San Francisco Bay area.
