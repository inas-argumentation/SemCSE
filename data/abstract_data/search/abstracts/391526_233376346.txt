Manufacturing Cost Accounting Method under Accounting Informationization
Nowadays, most of the cost accounting personnel of enterprises are transformed from manual accounting, so they're familiar with manual costing processes and methods. In the accounting information environment, they are not familiar with the new cost accounting process and methods, which greatly reduces the efficiency of cost accounting. At the same time, the quality of cost accounting cannot be guaranteed. Therefore, this article describes the manufacturing cost accounting method under accounting informationization, and points out the problems when using software for cost accounting to improve the efficiency and quality.
