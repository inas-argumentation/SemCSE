Diversity: Managing the Open Corporation
There are two conflicting views on diversity management: DM as a management response to the political enforcement of societal goals in equality and justice, and DM as a tool to activate human potential and creativity. This article attempts to synthesize both views in a new ap proach based on Karl Popper's evolutionary epistemology and social philosophy. I transfer the categories of "open" and "closed" society to the analysis of the corporation. Challenging the prevalent hypothesis that corporate closure is concomitant with the open society, I argue that the open society and the open corporation are necessary correlates in the knowledge economy. I define DM as being the set of management practices, tools and visions that are specific to the requirements of managing an open corporation.
