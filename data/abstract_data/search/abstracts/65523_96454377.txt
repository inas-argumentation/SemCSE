Big Data "Kill Cooked" Management Policy Tool Selection
Since 2018, big data kill cooked has become the most important public topic in the Internet industry. The practice of big data kill cooked not only deviates from the most basic business ethics and violates the principle of good faith management, but also deprives consumers of the right to know and the right to choose. It seriously damages the legitimate rights and interests of consumers. The most important choice of policy tools for governance is that supervision should be in place, laws should be strengthened, and rigid laws and regulations should be used to severely punish it.
