Analysis on Causes of Glaze Variegated Defect on 252 kV Post Insulator of 4 th Class Pollution Severity
The technological features of 252 kV porcelain post insulator of pollution severity class 3 and 4 were compared.1It is considered that the causes of formation of glass variegated defect are: small shed spacing,large shed projection insulator body rotating at glazing,uncompleted exposure of shed roots with dim sight,on which the glaze slurry can hardly sprayed.With prerequisite to ensure the mechanical,electric and temperature cycling performance of the insulator,the root thickness of large shed was reduced from 22.67 mm to 17.61 mm and that of small shed from 20.07 mm to 15.66 mm,the distance between large and small sheds at body trunk was increased by 2.64 mm,and that between small and large sheds by 2.69 mm,the shed spacing was increased from 67.76 mm to 70.11mm.Thus the specific creepage distance at one sped pitch was increased by 5.61 mm with the same trunk diameter,shed spacing equal to 70.11 mm,the ratio of shed spacing and projection equal to 0.926 with projection different between large and small shed of 17.5 mm and the shed inclination of 17deg.Practice shows that the glazing work become favorite after optimization of shed profile which results in apparent enhancement porcelain body of satisfaction rate and elimination of glaze variegation defect.
