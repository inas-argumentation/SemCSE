Joint Modeling for Bidirectional Neural Machine Translation with Contrastive Learning
Standard neural machine translation (NMT) only captures unidirectional dependencies to model a translation process from source to target. Nevertheless, the inverse information is explicitly available to reinforce the confidence of the translation process. We therefore propose an end to end bidirectional NMT model to associate the standard source to target and target to source translation models through a novel soft connection approach, which opens up the information interaction between two directional models. We also adopt a contrastive learning approach to train the bidirectional NMT model so as to enhance the information interaction. Experiments on Chinese English and English German translation tasks demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms strong NMT and SMT baseline systems.
