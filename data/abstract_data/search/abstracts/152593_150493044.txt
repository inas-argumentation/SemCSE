Gambaran Asuhan Keperawatan Pemberian Terapi Mengontrol Emosi Secara Fisik Pada Pasien Risiko Perilaku Kekerasan Di RSJ Provinsi Bali Tahun 2018
NURSING DIRECTION OF NURSING APPLICATION THERAPY CONTROLS PHYSICAL EMOTIONS ON PATIENTS RISK OF VIOLENCE BEHAVIOR IN RSJ PROVINSI BALI YEAR 2018 ABSTRACT Mental disorders are maladaptive responses to stressors from internal and external environments, evidenced by unsuitable thoughts, feelings and behaviors that interfere with social, occupational and physical functions. Riskesdas Bali, 2013 prevalence of emotional mental disorder at age 15 years and over that is 4.4% Risk of violent behavior is at risk to physically, emotionally and or sexually harm to oneself or others. Efforts that can be done is to provide therapy to control the physical emotions of channeling the constructive energy in a physical way, in the form of relaxation and hitting the pillow. The purpose of this study to determine the Nursing Care Nursing Application of Physical Emotion Control Therapy on Risk Behavior Patients in RSJ Bali Province. The results of the study conducted through interviews, observations and documentation on the nurses and on the subjects of research one and two, obtained the results of subjective data of patients claimed threatening, cursing with harsh words and loud voice, objective data that the patient looked bulging or sharp eyes, hand mengapal and stiff body posture. Based on these assessments can be formulated nursing problems that is the risk of violent behavior As for the actions taken are Giving Therapy Physical Controlling Emotions.
