Why Electric Motors Should Be Assembled by Bonding
Tesla has been instrumental in establishing electric cars as an ideal solution for efficient and sustainable mobility of the future. However, electric motors are not only used for emission free driving, they are also found in window regulators and seat adjusters. In fact, they can be found everywhere in electric bikes, in tools, even in our kitchens. All manufacturers of electric motors have one common goal in mind: making them smaller and more powerful, while increasing their efficiency. In the effort to achieve this goal, engineers must consider many things: for example, the lamination design, an optimal embedding of the magnets into the lamination stack, and leaving the smallest gap possible between magnet and coil.
