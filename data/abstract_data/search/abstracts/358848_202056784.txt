The Framework Design of Weapon Effectiveness Evaluation Simulation System for Navy
In order to solve the problem that how to embody the combat effectiveness of complex weapon system through the whole battle,building a opening DIS framework based on HLA and analysis on the flow of navy combat simulation,designing function modules such as battlefield environment generation,weapon data management,simulation entity generation,scenario edit,simulation kernel,situation display and manual intervention.Through running simulation,this system can reflect the weapon effectiveness under the cooperation condition through the whole battle.
