Analyse Socio economique Du Secteur De Tabac Au Cameroun: Perceptions, Impacts Et Perspectives (Socio economic Analysis of the Tobacco Sector in Cameroon: Perceptions, Impact and Perspectives)
The English version of this article can be viewed at: <a href="https:/ssrn.com/abstract=3387027">https:/ssrn.com/abstract=3387027</a><br><br><b>French Abstract:/b> La vision 2035 du developpement national du Cameroun s'inscrit dans le ciblage des objectifs du developpement durable(ODD) Il s'agit essentiellement d'une vision orientee sur la transformation du Cameroun en un pays emergent d'ici 2035. Les objectifs en la matiere sont la reduction de la pauvrete, l'industrialisation, la democratie et l'unite nationale. La croissance socio economique etant au centre de son agenda, la premiere phase de 2010 2020 visait a porter la croissance economique a une nouvelle moyenne annuelle de 5,5% une reduction du chomage de 75,8% a 50% par la creation annuelle de milliers d'emplois, et la reduction du taux de pauvrete monetaire de 39,9% a 28,7%<br><br>Cette dimension economique de l'agenda revet une importance mondiale, d'autant plus qu'elle s'inscrit dans plusieurs ODD ODD 1, eliminer la pauvrete sous toutes ses formes partout dans le monde ODD 8, promotion d'une croissance economique soutenue, inclusive et durable, d'un emploi complet et productif et d'un travail decent pour tous et ODD 12, assurer des modes de consommation et de production durables. C'est a partir de la, et plus encore, que les reglementations et les politiques gouvernementales visent a favoriser et a renforcer la proliferation et la perennite des entreprises et d'autres sources d'emploi et de lutte contre la pauvrete. Malheureusement, ce progres economique pourrait avoir un cout social eleve et, en verite, l'analyse couts avantages de l'impact economique et social n'est generalement prise en consideration que superficiellement.<br><br>Le cas de l'industrie du tabac est un exemple unique a considerer. Comme toute autre activite du secteur economique camerounais, l'industrie du tabac contribue de maniere significative au developpement socio economique. Les operateurs apportent ici une contribution substantielle a l'assiette fiscale annuelle du Cameroun. Cette contribution est rendue encore plus significative par la politique de taxation dissuasive speciale, qui impose une taxe supplementaire. Les tentatives visant a decourager ou au moins a controler la production de tabac ne sont pas uniquement propres au Cameroun. C'est une preoccupation mondiale. En fait, l'editorial du Lancet Public Health d'avril 2017 preconisait que la lutte antitabac soit fermement ancree dans les objectifs de developpement durable (ODD) La question qui se pose est de savoir si de telles politiques et leurs supports sont correctement informes de toutes les ramifications economiques et sociales et les perspectives de l'industrie.<br><br><b>English Abstract:/b> Within the context of targeting the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and alongside the national development program of Cameroon is the 2035 Vision. This basically is a vision to transform Cameroon into an emerging country by 2035. The benchmarks for this are poverty reduction, industrialization, democracy and national unity. Given the centrality of socio economic growth to agenda, the first phase from 2010 2020 targeted taking economic growth to a new annual average of 5.5% a reduction of underemployment from 75.8% to 50% through the annual creation of thousands of jobs, and the reduction of the rate of income poverty from 39.9% to 28.7%<br><br>This economic dimension of the agenda is of global significance, especially as it is in line with several SDGs: SDG 1, ending poverty in all its forms everywhere; SDG 8, promoting sustained, inclusive and sustainable economic growth, full and productive employment, and decent work for all; and SDG 12, ensuring sustainable consumption and production patterns. It is in cognisance of this and more, that government regulations and policies target the nurturing and enhancement of the proliferation and sustainability of companies and other sources of employment and poverty alleviation. Unfortunately, this economic progress could come along with a high social cost; and verily, the cost benefit analysis of both the economic and social impact is usually given only a cursory consideration.<br><br>A unique example to consider this mixed blessing is the case of the tobacco industry. Like any other business in the Cameroonian economic sector, the tobacco industry significantly contributes to socio economic development. The operators here make a substantial contribution to the annual tax base of Cameroon. This contribution is made even more significant by the special deterrent taxation policy, which imposes an additional. This deterrent taxation policy springs from the health hazards associated with the consumption of the tobacco products. Suffice it to note that this is a major concern threatening the attainment of SDG 3: ensuring healthy lives and promoting wellbeing for all at all ages.<br><br>Attempts at deterring or at least controlling tobacco production is not peculiar to Cameroon. It is a global concern. In fact, the Lancet Public Health editorial in April, 2017, called for tobacco control to be "firmly anchored in the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)" The question that arises is whether or not such policies and their likes are adequately informed of all the economic and social ramifications and prospects of the industry.
