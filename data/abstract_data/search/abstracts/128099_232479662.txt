Strategic Human Resource Management and COVID 19: Emerging Challenges and Research Opportunities
At its core the COVID19 pandemic is a human crisis. Thus, human resource (HR) leaders have been central to the response in organisations globally. This contrasts with previous crises such as the global recession of 2008 09 or the Y2K crisis at the turn of the millennium that accentuated the roles of finance and IT leaders, respectively. By amplifying the role of HR leaders, COVID19 has become an inflection point with substantive implications for HR globally.
