The Relationship between Graphic Design and Economic Development
As a practical art form,graphic design is both artistic and economic.It is closely interrelated with the development of social economy that creates the prosperity of graphic design,in turn,graphic design promotes the development of the social economy.Graphic design has to be integrated into the socio economic development,then be developed himself in order to better serve the development of the social economy.Art design is a kind of purpose driven creativity,a means to coordinate the relationship between humans and environment,individuals and society and production and consumption,its economy is primary characteristic that distinguishes itself from other artistic and handicraft activities.The Technology,economy and culture of art desgin determine that only a highly developed social and economic prosperity will have the prosperity of art and culture.As a combination of art and technology,the art design is also a productive force and promotes the development of socio economy.
