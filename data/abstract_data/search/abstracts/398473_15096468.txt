Chapter 23 Approximate Max Cut 23.1 Problem Statement
Given an undirected graph G (V, E) and nonnegative weights o i j on the edge i j E, the maximum cut problem (MAX CUT) is that of finding the set of vertices S that maximizes the weight of the edges in the cut (S S) that is, the weight of the edges with one endpoint in S and the other in S. For simplicity, we usually set o i j O for i j E and denote the weight of a cut (S S) by w(S S) iS jS o i j. This problem is NP C, and hard to approximate within a certain constant. Given a graph with vertex set V {1, n} and nonnegative weights o i j the weight of the maximum cut w(S S) is given by the following integer quadratic program: (Q) max 1 2 i< j o i j (1 y i y j) 1 2 i< j o i j (1 y i y j) Solving quadratic integer programming is of course NP H.
