Power Loss Evaluation for SiC MOSFET Based Isolated Bidirectional Dual Active Bridge DC DC Converter
Efficiency is one of the most important parameters to evaluate the performance of power electronic converters. This paper proposes a power loss evaluation model for silicon carbide MOSFET which is based on a dual active bridge converter. Both power losses of silicon carbide MOSFET and its free wheeling silicon carbide Schottky diode are taken into account in this model, especially the switching time of the silicon carbide MOSFET is calculated accurately to estimate the switching loss. To validate the power loss evaluation model, a 10 kW dual active bridge prototype is designed and tested under different operation points to compare with the theoretical quantitative power loss evaluation results.
