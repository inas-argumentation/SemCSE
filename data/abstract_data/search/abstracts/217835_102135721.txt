Determination of inorganic anions in geological drilling slurry by ion chromatography
The slurry was dealt with by dilution,centrifugation(8000 r*min 1)and filtration( 0.1MPa vacuum and 0.45mm filter membrane).An suppressed conductivity ion chromatography method was used to determine main anions in the aqueous extract of the slurry.The concentrations of Cl and SO42 were 1656 and 804mg*L 1 with RSD (n=6)0.52% and 0.62%,and recoveries of 102.2% and 105.2% respectively.The method is easy to handle with characteristics accurate results and shorter analytical duration comparing to traditional chemical way.
