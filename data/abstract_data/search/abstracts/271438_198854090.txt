Study on the Construction of the Content System of College Students' Ideological and Political Education in the New Situation
Constructing the ideological and political education system of college students in the new era is conducive to the docking of ideological and political education of college students and ideological and moral education of middle school students, enhancing the pertinence of ideological and political education of college students and the realization of the goal of college students. The principles of subjectivity, systemicity, pertinence and humanity should be adhered to, and the ideological and political education system of college students should be constructed and optimized from the aspects of content system, method system, organization system, evaluation system, institutional system and innovation system.
