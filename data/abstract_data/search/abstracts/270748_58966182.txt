Watermarking embedding and extraction method based on the robust multi scale space
The present invention relates to a watermark embedding and extraction method based on multi scale robust space. The method comprising: constructing a multi scale space; multiscale Watermarking: Multiscale watermark generation and watermark embedding multiscale; multiscale watermark extraction: extracting feature points, multi scale spatial orientation, multi scale spatial watermark extraction; watermark contrast. Limitation of the current image watermarking method of the present invention is directed to large scale attack geometric transformation, multi resolution block by pyramid data structure divided into a large scale multi level attack small scale attacks, organic binding domain and the spatial domain transform together to ensure invisibility and robustness of the watermark information in the case of large scale geometric attacks. Compared to conventional digital watermarking method, on the premise of the same watermark capacity, the present invention has better imperceptibility and robustness advantages for large scale geometric transformation.
