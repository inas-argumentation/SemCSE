Traumatic adult cystic lymphangioma
It is aimed to call attention to to the role of trauma in the pathophysology of lymphangioma, and lymphangiomatosus which are rarely seen in the etiology in adults who presented with a mass on the neck. A 62 year old man presented with cystic mass on the left cervical region to our clinic. Ultrasonography and magnetic resonance imaging revealed a left cervical cystic mass which was totally excised and the pathologic examination supported the diagnosis of cystic lymphangioma. Lymphangiomas are congenital malformations of the lymphatic system especially in children younger than two years of age affecting mainly head and neck region. They are very rarely seen in adults. Cystic lymphangioma should be keep in mind in the differential diagnonis of the patients who had a neck mass.
