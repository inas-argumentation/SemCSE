Compact, side pumped 25 dBm Er/Yb co doped double cladding fibre amplifier
A compact 1.5 /spl mu/m Er/Yb doped double cladding fibre amplifier, diode side pumped through an embedded v groove, is demonstrated. A small signal gain of 47 dB, an output power of 312 mW and, separately, a noise figure of 4.5 dB are measured.
