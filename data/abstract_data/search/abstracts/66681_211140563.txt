Embrittlement in Weld Strain Affected Zone in Carbon Steel
Based on the work of an earlier report (Ref. 1) the authors have tried to find the cause of embrittlement in the strain affected zone of carbon steel weldments using postheating conditions as a parameter. First, various properties at the tip of the center notch of welded wideplate tensile specimens (Ref. 1) were investigated to find effective tests for revealing the material differences between as welded and postheated specimens.
