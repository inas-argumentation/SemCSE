People number counting method based on image grey level matching
The invention provides a people number counting method based on image grey level matching. The method comprises the following steps of: S1, obtaining the video streaming image of a monitoring area as an input image; S2, obtaining a difference image via an inter frame difference method according to the input image, and performing binaryzation processing on the difference image to obtain a moving target area; S3, scanning the moving target area via a rectangular window, and extracting a pedestrian area; S4, taking the grey level information of the pedestrian area as a template, and performing searching and matching in the central point neighbourhood of the pedestrian area in the next image; and S5, storing the central point position information of the pedestrian area to a target chain, so as to obtain the number of people. According to the invention, the grey level information of the pedestrian area is used as the template, and searching and matching are performed in the central point neighbourhood of the pedestrian area in the next image without a need to search in the whole image, thus reducing the operation processing capacity and the processing time, and realizing rapid tracking for pedestrians.
