Baseline sensitivity to benthiavalicarb, iprovalicarb and dimethomorph included into carboxylic acid amide (CAA) group was evaluated in 180 isolates of Phytophthora capsici over 4 years from 2005 to 2008. EC? (effective concentration inhibiting mycelial growth by 50% value of benthiavalicarb ranged from 0.015 ugUml?1 to 0.049 ugUml?1 with a mean of 0.033 ugUml?1. The mean values of EC? of iprovalicarb and dimethomorph were 0.411 (0.197 0.556) ugUml?1 and 0.271 (0.101 0.798) ugUml?1, respectively. Although there was no increasing tendency in EC? of benthiavalicarb and iprovalicarb during 4 years, EC? of dimethomorph was increased gradually by laps of time. There was no cross resistance between each fungicide used in this study and metalaxyl. Among fungicides included into CAA group, there was a positive correlation between benthiavalicarb and iprovalicarb, and between dimethomorp and mandipropamid.
