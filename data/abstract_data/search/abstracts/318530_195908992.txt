k pyeonggyun keulreoseuteoringeul iyonghan pilgi munseo yeongsangyi daneo bunribeob
bon nonmuneseoneun pilgi munseo yeongsangeul bunseoghayeo daneo danwiro yosodeuleul bunhalhaneun bangbeobeul jeanhanda. ilbanjeogeuro inswae munseoe bihayeo pilgi munseoeseoneun geulja gan gangyeogi iljeonghaji anheul bbunman anira pilgija ddoneun jagseongdoen eoneoe ddara teugseongi maeu dareuge natanagi ddaemune daneoreul bunrihaneun geoseun eoryeoun munjero ganjudoeeossgo manheun yeonguga jinhaengdoeeossda. jeanhaneun bangbeobeun i munjereul haegyeolhagi wihayeo geulja hoegyi duggereul goryeohayeo jeonggyuhwasikin gag yeongyeol yosogan gangyeoggwa gangyeog ane jonjaehaneun geulja pigselyi suro guseongdoen 2 caweonyi teugjinggabseul cuculhayeossda. i teugjinggabseul batangeuro, jeanhaneun bangbeobeun k pyeonggyun keulreoseuteoringeul iyonghayeo gag tegseuteuraineul guseonghaneun yeongyeol yosogan gangyeogeul daneo saiyi gangyeoggwa daneo naebu geuljaganyi gangyeogeuro bunryuhayeossda. ICDAR 2013 Handwriting Segmentation Contest deiteobeiseue daehan silheom gyeolgwa jeanhaneun bangbeobeun gajang usuhan seongneungeul natanaeeossda.
