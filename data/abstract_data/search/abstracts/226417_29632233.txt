Small Time Asymptotics for Basket Options the Bivariate SABR Model and the Hyperbolic Heat Kernel on H3
We compute a sharp small time estimate for the price of a basket call under a bivariate SABR model with both \beta$ parameters equal to 1 and three correlation parameters, which extends the work of Bayer, Friz, and Laurence [Large Deviations and Asymptotic Methods in Finance, Springer, Cham, Switzerland, 2015, pp. 449 472] for the multivariate Black Scholes flat vol model, and we show that their result has to be adjusted for strikes above a certain critical value $K^ where the convolution density admits a higher order saddle point. The result follows from the heat kernel on hyperbolic space for n=3 combined with the Bellaiche [Asterisque, 84 85 (1981) 151 187] heat kernel expansion and Laplace's method, and we give numerical results which corroborate our asymptotic formulas. Similar to the Black Scholes case, we find that there is a phase transition from one "most likely" path to two most likely paths beyond some critical $K^
