Experimental study on the influence of different designs of base plate in casting framework RPD to the levels of AST and ALP in gingival sulcus fluid
Objective To investigate the influence of different designs of base plate in casting framework RPD made by three materials to the levels of AST and ALP in gingival sulcus fluid, provide a evidence for the design of base plate in clinical removable partial dentures. Methods The defects of dentition were treated with three kinds of material cast frame removable partial denture: nickel chrome alloy, cobalt chrome alloy, titanium. Two designs: high base resistance and gingival exposure were separately used in the left and right homonymous teeth in the same dental prosthetic restoration. Determinations of the GCF, AST and ALP in gingival crevicular fluid were performed separately on repairing anterior, 1 month, 3 months and 6 months after the repair. The data were analyzed. Results Compared with before wearing dentures, the test data of the group of high base resistance at 1, 3 and 6 months after wearing dentures were significantly higher(P0.05) The differences of test data in the groups of nickel chromium alloys, cobalt chromium alloys and titanium at each period had no significant. Conclusion Compared with the design of gingival margin high base resistance, design of gingival margin exposed more reasonable, less influence on periodontal tissue.
