Low Power Wide Area Network Technologies for Smart Cities Applications
There are several existing Low Power Wide Area Network (LPWAN) technologies competing to provide M2M connectivity and secure niche in the Internet of Things (IoT) universe. Most of well known LPWAN approaches are quite recognizable thanks to sizable marketing efforts though most of it practically is not the right choice for the robust long range and large scale networks. We have compared the famous LPWAN technologies to provide a complete overview of the different perspective. From the comparison, the considering candidate to be used in the Smart Cities Application is then revealed based on its advantages.
