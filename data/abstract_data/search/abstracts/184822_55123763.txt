Evaluation of Oxidative Stress on Soybean (Glycine max L. Merrill) and Wheat (Triticum aestivum) Crops in Response to Chlorpyrifos
The use of synthetic pesticides as crop protection chemicals has become the most accepted ecological weapon for assured crop production. Merrill) is an important pulse as well as oilseed crop. It has become wonder crop of twentieth century and is often designated as "Golden bean."
