Computerized Data Mining for Adverse Drug Events in an Outpatient Setting
Introduction: Adverse Drug Events (ADE) are both common and costly. Most ADE's are identified by spontaneous monitoring or chart reviews. Computer monitors have been used sparingly to improve the detection and characterization of inpatient ADE's. Earlier but limited chart reviews indicate that ADE's occur in 1 15% of outpatient encounters. Computer data mining has never been used to demonstrate ADE detection in outpatient settings. While most data mining techniques require the employment of rule based methods, few have ever combined the power and strength of rules with lexical searches.
