SYNTHESIS AND CHARACTERIZATION OF HIGHLY STABLE PECTIN STABILIZED NANO ZERO VALENT IRON PARTICLES FOR EFFECTIVE ENVIRONMENTAL CLEAN UP
The demand for advanced water treatment system has been intensified all these days due to pollution and reduction in ground water table. A variety of methods have been accomplished for removal of various contaminants from groundwater such as chemical precipitation, coagulation, adsorption, ion exchange, membrane filtration and Biological process. In comparison to the other methods, zero valent iron are highly effective in removing most of the contaminants including heavy metals and chlorinated compounds without previous oxidative treatment and does not require any additional chemical product. NZVI is characterized by smaller particle size 100 nm) and higher surface area (22 54 m2g 1) as compared to micro zero valent iron (MNZI) which has a size in the mm range and surface area of 1 2 m2g 1 (Thompson et al,2010) NZVI is of particular interest for contaminated site remediation as the particles are theoretically smaller than pores in the soil structure. Given this size, it was originally believed that NZVI particles would be able to flow through the pore structure without significant mass loss.
