New Equivalent Time Sampling Method Based on Parametric Model Estimation
A new equivalent time sampling method based on parametric model estimation is invented to solve the problem of normal equivalent time sampling method,which needs accurate trigger circuit,precision timing circuit or time measuring circuit.Sometimes the normal equivalent time sampling method even cannot wholly reconstruct the signal waveform.In the equivalent time sampling method based on parametric model estimation,three kinds of sampling clock,frequency of which are coprime each other,are adopted to sample three times.And three groups of data are acquired.The basic frequency and other parameters of the periodic signal are estimated from three groups of data.And the periodic signal waveform is reconstructed.It has been tested by experiments that the equivalent time sampling method based on parametric model estimation can accurately reconstruct the periodic signal waveform and the reconstruction error is little.The equivalent time sampling method based on parametric model estimation has been applied in the high speed data acquisition system of the digital storage oscilloscope successfully.
