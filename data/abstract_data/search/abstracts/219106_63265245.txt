Research on the Dynamic Model Construction of Regional Generalized Virtual Innovation
The regional innovation system is divided into entity system and virtual system based on the viewof the generalized virtual economy, therefore, the regional innovation system has become a complete sense of time and space generalized virtual dynamic system. The mathematical description of the generalized virtual innovation system internal variables is established by researching the dynamic structure and operational mechanism of regional generalized virtual innovation system. Taking the innovation behavior of different innovation subjects as the main line, and the innovation resources as the basis, the dynamic structure model is set up in this thesis; What's more, the dynamics simulation is conducted though analyzing and reasoning the observation data based on the causal relationship between elements. Finally, The impulse, step delay and combination effect of the strategic countermeasures is further designed, thence, this simulation experiment will provide a scientific basis for the policy making.
