Design calculation and study on safe braking of heavy power inclined downward belt conveyors
According to the technical features and transmission principle of the heavy power inclined downward belt conveyor used in collieries, the design methods for relevant parameters are offered. After building a mechanical model of a brake, the relationship among braking torque, produced work and thermal capacity is deduced. Based on braking deceleration set by dynamic loading, the application of the on line monitoring system for hydraulic brakes is discussed. Moreover, existing brake research achievements are reviewed, and the brake differences are contrasted. The control technology must be applied to the brake of the heavy power inclined downward belt conveyor used in collieries, so as to make the braking deceleration adapt to the dynamic loading and meet the requirements of safe production in collieries.
