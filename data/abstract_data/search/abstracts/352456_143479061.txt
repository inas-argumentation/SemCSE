Paul Schachter and Fe T. Otanes. 1972. Tagalog Reference Grammar. Berkeley: University of California Press
Tagalog, the language on which the National Language of the Philippines is based, is perhaps one of the best studied among the various languages of the world. It was the favorite language of study and description among the early Spanish missionaries, who gave it more attention than any of the other hundred or so indigenous tongues of the archipelago (John Leddy Phelan 1955:159) and this interest in the language carried over to the American, Japanese, and Independence periods. Among the better known linguists who investigated it were Frank R. Blake (1925) Leonard Bloomfield (1917) Elmer Wolfenden (1961) Cecilio Lopez (1941) J. Donald Bowen (1965) and R. Stockwell (1957) Without doubt, this grammar by Fe T. Otanes and Paul Schachter is the most detailed and comprehensive of these studied; and, therefore, deserves the title &dquo;Tagalog Reference Grammar.&dquo; It took four years (1960 1964) to write, and had distinguished scholars among its editors and advisers. Moreover, it enjoyed the support of the U.S. Office of Education.
