Factors affecting the Academic Performance of university students residing in Student Housing Facility
In theory and literature, it is stated that self efficacy affects the individual in how they feel, think or act (Bandura, 1995) As far as "feeling" is concerned, low selfefficacy is related to anxiety and helplessness. Individuals possessing low selfefficcy have tendency to access pessimistic thoughts about themselves in terms of accomplishments and personal growth and development. For "thinking" sense of being competent facilitates performance in a variety of ways including acadcmic achievement (Schwarzer et al. 1997)
