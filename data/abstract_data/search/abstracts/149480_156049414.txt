CONSIDERATII POLITICO MILITARE PRIVIND INTRAREA ROMANIEI IN Al DOILEA RAZBOI MONDIAL
During the entire war, Germany's principal enemies, The Great Three, would clearly present their position regarding its political future and territories under the command of the third Reich, being preoccupied with giving them liberty and independence. Romania's participation in the second world war would be caused by political and military isolation, more and more pronounced, circumstances in which the possibility of recuperating the territories lost in the summer of 1940 were obliging the Romanian authorities to take into account the option of an alliance with Germany.
