Training drivers, competitive strategy and clients' needs: Case studies of three business process outsourcing organisations
Purpose The purpose of this paper is to examine the factors that drive a firm's decision to invest in training in India's IT enabled services/business process outsourcing (ITeS/BPO) sector. It aims to consider a much neglected area and an often cited need to consider external factors, especially the needs of an organisation's clients in a firm's decision to train.Design/methodology/approach The paper employs case study research strategy using semi structured interviews, organisational documents and non participant observation.Findings Employee turnover, competitive strategy, client specifications and quality management systems were important factors in shaping the nature and extent of training. The temporal dimension of the process was also significant in influencing volume of training.
