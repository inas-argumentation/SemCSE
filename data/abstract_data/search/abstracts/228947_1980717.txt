Impact of Fipronil on Crustacean Aquatic Organisms in a Paddy Field Fishpond Ecosystem
In the simulated ecological experiment, a paddy field and fishponds were built with cement, as shown in Figure 1. The paddy fie ld had an area of 10 m2 4m X 2.Sm) with a 25cm depth. Over the bottom was spread a 1 Scm thick layer of earth which were collected from suburban of Nanjing city where the fipronil never be used. Glass partitions were set at a certain interval so that water would flow circuitously between the glass partitions when drained from the field into the treated fishpond. On one side of the field were two fishponds, each had dimension of 2 m (length) X 1.5 m (width) X 1. 2 m (d epth) At the bottom of both ponds were placed a certain amount of mud where some water grass was grown. One of the ponds was used for experimental treatment, and the other as a control. Before the experiment, rice plants were growing in the field as usual. In both ponds were placed two net cages, where shrimp and crabs were separately bred so that the different sorts of organisms would not kill one another.
