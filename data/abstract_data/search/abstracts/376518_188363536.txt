Online Learning in Biology: An Investigation into Designing Online Learning Resources
Virtual Biology Lab (VBL) is a freely available online resource that contains interactive simulations on ecology and evolution. Since its inception (2010) VBL has become a popular learning tool in a variety of classes, but technology is beginning to render its current design obsolete. To remedy this, the coding for simulations are being updated to operate on a variety of mobile devices and computers. The need for new coding presented the opportunity to enhance the graphics and animations of the simulations, which also provided the opportunity to investigate whether an updated user interface affects student learning. Students in an introductory biology course at East Tennessee State University were randomly assigned to complete one original and one updated simulation on different topics. After completion of the assignment, students took a short quiz that included questions on content and their opinions about online learning activities.
