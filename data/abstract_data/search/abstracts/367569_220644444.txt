Transhumanism and Health: A Christian Perspective
This paper examines the compatibility of transhumanism with Christian teachings. It focuses upon health in the wide sense of freedom from disease, suffering and life enhancement. The term transhumanism, popularised by the biologist Julian Huxley in 1957, pertains to the idea that the capabilities of humans can be enhanced through technological means and are dependent upon reason, science and technology.
