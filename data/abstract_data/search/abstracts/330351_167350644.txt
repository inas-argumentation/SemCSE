A STUDY ON CUSTOMER SATISFACTION ON FIAT CAR AT CONCORDE MOTORS IN CHENNAI
The automobile sector is a key performer in the global and Indian economy. The automotive industry in India is one of the largest in the world and one of the fast growing globally. The study covers customer's expectation through Concorde motors and perceived value by the customers through Concorde motors and the level of satisfactions of the customers. Concorde Motors (India) Limited, a 100% subsidiary of the company engaged in sales and service of Tata and FIAT passenger cars recorded a turnover of Rs.580.90 crores and profit after Tax of Rs.3.71 crores. Concorde Motors (India) Limited has declared a dividend of Rs.2.50 per share for the FY 2010 11 and Rs.7 per share for the FY 2010 11 on the 7% cumulative redeemable preference shares. The research study is carried out to know the Customer Satisfaction on FIAT cars. The Indian automotive market is one of the most competitive markets with low costs, which make it an attractive assembly base for foreign automotive manufacturers.
