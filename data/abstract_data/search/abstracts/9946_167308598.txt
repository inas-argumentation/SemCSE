On the Administration of Police Network Information Security
At present,with the rapid development of information technology,new types of cyber crimes are followed,which serious threatened national security and social stability.In order to adapt to the development needs,how to enhance information security management,and how to prevent all kinds of leaks and theft of secret information,how to effectively combat all forms of cyber crime,which have become the important project of police network construction.The article focuses on the security system of public security information network under the new situation,puts forward corresponding prevention and administration countermeasures,to strengthen network security prevention work and make network control effectively.
