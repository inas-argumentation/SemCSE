Intelligent instrument circuit board intellectual property protection device and method
The invention provides an intelligent instrument circuit board intellectual property protection device and method. In the device, an industrial control computer board is used for controlling plugging of programmable logic device monitoring circuit boards from motherboard sockets and read write of a FLASH memory. Motherboards are used for realizing bus mode electrical connection and mechanical structure connection among the industrial control computer board, the sockets of the circuit boards of which the intellectual property needs to be protected in instruments and the motherboards. The circuit board sockets on the motherboards are used for fixedly plugging the sockets of the circuit boards of which the intellectual property needs to be protected on the motherboards via butting and realizing electrical connection of a power supply and an electric signal between the motherboards. With application of the aforementioned scheme, static protection of the intellectual property can be realized, and dynamic protection of execution time can also be realized so that acquisition or reverse engineering of FPGA or DSP core intellectual property codes on all the circuit boards of the instruments by illegal users can be prevented, and thus the intellectual property of the intelligent test instruments can be protected and the intelligent test instruments can be prevented from being imitated.
