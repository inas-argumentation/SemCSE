LUCC based Driving Force Analysis of Flood Risk in Rapidly Urbanizing Pearl River Delta the Case of Foshan
It is examined the linkage between rapid urbanization and flood risk in the hinterland of Pearl River Delta.The case study in Foshan city analyzed dramatic changes in land use and cover change(LUCC) during 1988 2003 and subsequent environmental impacts on flood risks by using GIS and remote sensing techniques.In addition,historical analysis on meteorological and hydrological data during 1957 2005 in Foshan was performed.The results showed that in addition to natural processes,human activities driven by socio economic factors should be responsible for the increasing level of flood risks.During the past decades dredging up the waterways,excavating sand and damming in the rivers significantly changed the hydrological condition and impaired the capacity of buffering the floods.Moreover,the substantial sprawl of impervious urban area and agricultural lands led to the increasing surface runoff and rising water levels during the flood reasons.As a result,the rapidly urbanizing area located in the Xijiang and Beijiang flood plains are facing the rising levels of flood risk than ever.Finally,countermeasures of flood risk management were discussed.
