Electrochemical behavior of ethyl acetate as electrolyte additive for overcharge protection of lithium ion batteries
The effects of ethyl acetate as electrolyte additive of lithium ion battery were investigated by the charge discharge cycle test,cyclic voltammetry,overcharge test,AC impedance analysis and others.The research shows that the oxidation reaction peak voltage of ethyl acetate is 4.25 V,the reduction peak voltage is 4.5 V,the charging discharging test shows that LiFePO4 battery recycling performance is good at 0.2 C /4.2 V,the overcharge tests shows that the battery doesn't happen leakage,fire or other bad conditions at 0.5 C /4.2 V,1 C /4.2 V,0.2 C 4.6 V;the AC impedance analysis reveals a greater impact of additives on the electrolyte resistance.
