Lessons from a Pandemic: The Educational System Evolving in the Time of COVID 19
This article continues a series initiated by members of the Bulletins editorial board The goal of the series is to provide insight on a topic or work related to the theme of the issue Here, editorial board member Palsdottir of Iceland offers a reflection and analysis of the impact of the COVID 19 pandemic on education Her comments were written in late April 2020 as various international governments were coming to grips with the impact of the pandemic and the proper steps for moving forward post pandemic
