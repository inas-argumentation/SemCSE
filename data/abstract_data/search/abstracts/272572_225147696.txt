Review of heat transfer enhancement on helical coil heat exchanger by additive passive method
Abstract A Helical coil Heat exchanger is a type of equipment built for reducing the space of heat exchanger without reducing its length of the tube. The heat carrier enrichment ways for swirl coil heat exchanger are widely applied in Test Cooling, Experimental Pre moderate, complete moderators, Condensers, Ultracool Vaporizers, Compressor, Other Moderators and Wide Uses. The heat carrier enrichment is splits into Active way and Passive way. Enriching the heat carrier by the utilization of vertically coiled swirl tubes have been considered and experimented by several researchers to increase its efficiency by several ways. Here we are investigating the enrichment of heat carrier, passive way by using the different fluids to the swirl coil heat exchanger.
