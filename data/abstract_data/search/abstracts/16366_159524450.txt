A Study of the Mechanism of Administrative Power:From the Perspetive of Transport Super Ministry Reform
The super transport ministry has been established in the super ministry reform of the State Council recently.Under such circumstance,the mechanism of administrative power should be innovated.Establishing the structure of power in which decision making,enforcement and oversight powers check each other and advancing the system of the comprehensive administrative enforcement are two approaches for the innovation of administrative power mechanism.Deepening the reform of the comprehensive administrative decision making system,perfecting the related legal systems and emending the laws and rules that conflict are the fundamental approaches to ensure the good functioning of the administrative power mechanism.
