Development of a Pharmaceutical Composition and Stablity of Liquid Dosage Forms Based on Monoclonal IgG1 Antibodies
A scheme for developing an excipient composition for liquid dosage forms based on monoclonal antibodies(mAbs) is proposed and used to develop a stable formulation of an active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) and finished dosage form (FDF) for s.c. injection of mAbs IgG1 against a tumor necrosis factor (TNF a) with API concentrations from 50 to 150 mg/mL.
