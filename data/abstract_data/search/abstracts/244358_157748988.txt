TECHNICAL EFFICIENCY AND ITS DECOMPOSITION: A STUDY OF SELECT BRANCHES OF UNITED BANK OF INDIA IN CACHAR REGION OF ASSAM
The wave of reforms in the banking sector all across the world has brought with it more transparency, accountability, efficiency, innovation, competition and dynamism. Efficient banking system contributes in an extensive way towards a higher economic growth. The market competition brought about by financial liberalization has oriented the banking system towards a more efficient organization. But mere presence of banks and expansion of their branches across length and breadth of a nation does not ensure overall development of an economy. What matters most is how efficiently banks and their branches work towards fulfilment of their set goal and planned development objectives of a nation. The present study is an attempt in this direction to assess the level of efficiency and productivity growth of bank branches of one of the leading banks The United Bank of India (UBI) in the context of Cachar region of Assam by adopting the Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA) With a sample of 21 branches, the study identifies a declining trend in efficiency both in respect of Technical Efficiency (TE) and Pure Technical Efficiency (PTE) Majority of the sample branches exhibit scope of potential cost saving. With respect to productivity, the Malmquist Total Factor Productivity Index (MTFI) is studied a productivity growth of 15.4% on an average is identified.
