On Fairy Tale Translation Strategies from the Perspective of Skopos Theory A Case Study of the Two Chinese Translated Versions of The Happy Prince
This paper extracts examples from Ba Jin's,as well as Su Fuzhong and Zhang Min's translations of Oscar Wilde's The Happy Prince,compares and analyzes them in light of the three major rules in Skopos theory. By this contrastive analysis,the author summarizes the effectiveness of Skopos theory in fairy tale translation and finds out several practical translation strategies in the hope of bringing new enlightenment to the study of Skopos theory and the translation of children's fairy tales.
