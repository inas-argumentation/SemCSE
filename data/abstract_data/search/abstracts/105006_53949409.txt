Mean Stress Correction in Plastic Strain Energy Fatigue Life Prediction Model for Turbine Disk of Aero Engine
A correction of plastic strain energy fatigue life prediction model accounting for mean stress effect is proposed, which is applicable to estimate fatigue life of turbine disk in the medium fatigue lifetime region. The proposed model not only includes multiple damage parameters such as stress, strain, stress ratio, but also reflects the change of mean stress effect with variation of material and fatigue life. The experimental fatigue data, including four sets of data from standard bars of materials commonly used in aero engine and two sets of data from simulated bolt hole specimen of turbine disk, are employed to investigate the prediction capability of the proposed model against some other fatigue life estimation models. It is found that Goodman model brings the most conservative results, while SWT model and Walker model show acceptable results but not as good as the proposed model for failing to consider the variation of mean stress effect with fatigue life. The proposed model is found to provide superior results to other candidate models for all cases examined, showing satisfactory fatigue life prediction capability for turbine disk.
