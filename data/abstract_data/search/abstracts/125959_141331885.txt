Double Orifices Pneumatic Servo Valve
Pneumatic servo valve is the core component of pneumatic servo system. Pneumatic servo valve can be divided into one side pneumatic servo valve, two side pneumatic servo valve, and four side pneumatic servo valve according to the number of throttling edges. There are three kinds of coordination states in axial size of even number of throttling orifices for two or four side pneumatic servo valves: symmetrical equality, symmetrical inequality, and asymmetrical unequal.
