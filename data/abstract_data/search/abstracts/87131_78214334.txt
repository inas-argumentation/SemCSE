Ficus Carica Latex; a Review with Focus on Cancer
AbstractFig (Ficus carica) tree latex first described in Iranian traditional medicine by Avicenna in his 10th century book Canon of Medicine. It has been reported that fig tree latex has various therapeutic effects including hypoglycemic induction, as a source of natural anti oxidant, anti helmintic and some other useful effects. The current review will provide some evidences about the effect of ficus carica latex on gastrointestinal cancer cell proliferation. We concluded that use of Ficus carica latex in traditional medicine and investigations which were performed to identify the biological features of its components. Ficus carica latex is a rich source of natural anti oxidant sustances with established features of anti carcinogenic properties that might be considered in biopharmaceutical industries.Keywords: Fig, Ficus carica, latex, cancer
