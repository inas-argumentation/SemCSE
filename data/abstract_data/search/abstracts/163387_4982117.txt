Personalizing Virtual Experiences: Metrics for Persuasive Prototypes
In these early days of educational Virtual Reality (VR) applications, it is critical to establish best practices for exploring quantitative and qualitative data revealing the subtle relationship between personal virtual experiences and learning. In contrast to prior research on virtual environments, the evaluation of a personalized VR experience offered by a prototype is unique with but requires methods for evaluating metrics. Our paper suggests the need for the exploration of metrics for persuasive prototypes for virtual experiences.
