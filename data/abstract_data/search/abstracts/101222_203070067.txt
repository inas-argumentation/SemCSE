Rapid and Continuously Tunable Narrow Linewidth Fiber Source Based on a SOA and a Linearly Chirped Fiber Bragg Grating
We demonstrate a tunable narrow linewidth laser based on a semiconductor optical amplifier and a linearly chirped FBG. High tuning resolution and small power variation over 40 nm tuning range were achieved by optimizing the drive current. (c) 2019 The Author(s)
