The Analysis on the Features of the Epedimic Hepatitis C from 2004 to 2012 in Xuchang City
Objective The thesis focuses on investigating the developing trending and features of Hepatitis C epidemic and researches on the reasons to this disease, which provides profound foundations for scientific measures made by the relative office to predict it, prevent and control it. Methods Through describing the epidemiology, it has a stastical analysis of the specific monitoring data of Hepatitis C from 2004 to 2012 in Xu Chang City. Results One of the result is that21.82 suffer Hepatits C every 100,000 persons and the rate of the outbreak is increasing one year after another, compatible with the trend of Henan Provine; this disease arises from time to time all the year and it doesn't rely on the obvious seasons changing; Female is more easy to get it than man and this disparity is full of stastical significance; Hepatitis C goes to the group of the age from 35 to 60 mainly and the rate of the outbreak is increasing with age obviously. Conclusion Finally, for the increasing Hepatitis C cases reports in Xuchang City, some general measures should be taken, such as earlier diagnosis and clinic therapy, streghtening the training of the clinicians about the standards of the clinic diagnosis, and health education to prevent the spreading of Hepatitis efficiently.
