HANS WOLFGANG HENN, DORTMUND THE CAS PROJECT MOBILE CLASSROOM
In the German federal state of Baden Wuerttemberg, extensive tests on the use of computer algebra systems have been going on since 1993. We started a pilot project at the beginning of the school year 1996/97 that is at present unique in Germany. All students of five 11 grade classes (age 16 17) from different towns in Baden Wurttemberg have been equipped with a modern notebook and will work with MAPLE for three years until their final exam. One of our major goals is to bring more applications and modelling into the everyday teaching experience.
