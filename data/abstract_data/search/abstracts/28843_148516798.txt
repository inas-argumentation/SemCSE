Comparison of English Teachers' Conception of Self regulated Learning between College and Secondary School in China
As the proposing of quality education and long life learning educational belief,people pay more and more attention to self regulated learning. When education is nationalizing today,it' s of vital significance of enhancing self regulated learning. This paper aims to compare and analyze the similarities and differences of English teachers' seeing of self regulated learning from college and secondary school from the dimensions of teaching belief, non intelligence factors,studying goal,learning strategies,teaching environment and teaching assessment on the basis of the understanding of self regulated learning in college and secondary school,thus providing implications to teachers' teaching,benefiting to promote the communication between teachers in two stages,and benefiting students to adapt learning from secondary school to college school.
