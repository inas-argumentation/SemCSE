Drought early warning definitions, challenges, and opportunities
Abstract This chapter provides an overview of the different definitions of drought. Droughts are slow onset disasters that always involve a shortage of water. While they are an extremely common disaster, droughts can be hard to identify and predict, in part because they can impact people, ecosystems, and economies in many different ways. Here, we briefly discuss how drought may be defined in terms of water supply and demand, magnitude (intensity, duration, and extent) and various types of sectoral impacts.
