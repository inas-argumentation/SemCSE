Translating legal texts in the EU
Abstract Starting off with a short description of the theoretical basis of the translator's approach in reformulating a text first understood, we proceed to an explanation of legal translation as specialist communication, with special reference to the concepts of European law. A specific difficulty in translating legal texts relates to variation in the levels of abstraction in legal terms that combine stereotypical and legal concepts in the same word. Adequate translation strategies concern equivalence of concepts and the functional style of texts. Constant revision of translation drafts is required.
