Orthogonally Polarized MIMO LTE/5G Terminal Antennas for Handsets and IoT Applications
A broadband resonant terminal antenna has been developed for handsets and IoT applications. It can cover the whole LTE band (0.7 3.8GHz) or the 5G Sub6GHz band (3.3 7GHz) without using any matching/tuning circuits. It is covered with a thin dielectric layer to reduce its length. The new antenna does not depend on the ground plane of the terminal. It is manufactured from a thin flexible material that can be folded around the narrow sides of the terminal. This allows putting two antennas on two perpendicular sides of the handset with a high isolation between them because of the polarization diversity.
