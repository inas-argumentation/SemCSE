The Effect of Pressure in Cruise Phase on the Thermal Runaway Behaviors and Smoke Components
More lithium battery fire or explosions incidents in civil aviation enhance the necessity of investigation about the characteristics of lithium batteries thermal runaway in an aircraft environment. In order to study the batteries thermal runaway hazard characteristics under the ambient pressure of cruise phase, the experiments were conducted under the pressure of 20 kPa and 95 kPa in the dynamic chamber (pressure range from 10 kPa to 101 kPa) of calorimeter platform. The parameters of heat release rate, smoke temperature, concentration percentage of flammable emission gases (CO, CO2 and CnHx) and smoke density were obtained. The results shown that the peak values of heat release rate, smoke temperature, concentration of CO2were lower, and the peak concentration values of CO and CnHx were higher, under 20 kPa environment compared with the tests undertaken in 95 kPa. What's more, the thermal runaway reactions were easier to be activated under the low pressure of 20 kPa.
