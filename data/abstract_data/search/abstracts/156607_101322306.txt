Spectrophotometric Determination of Fly Ash Zeolite Cation Exchange Capacity
On the basis of acetate magnesia method,titrimetric method was replaced by spectrophotometric method and be used to detect zeolite CEC.Through to research each kind of influencing factor of zeolite CEC in the measure process,it was discovered that the better zeolite CEC could be attained when the dosage of Ammonium acetate was 30mL,the oscillation time was 30min and the adsorption number of times was four.And the zeolite CEC was 169mmol/100g.
