Clinical analysis pulmonary tuberculosis with diabetes
Objective To investigate the effects of diabetes on the anti phthisic effectiveness of pulmonary tuberculosis(PTB).Methods To analyse the changes of focus,cavity and sputum of 36 cases of PTB patients by observing the curative effects of diabetes.Results 29 cases of diabetes were improved after treatments.Among these patients,the rates of focus absorption,cavity closing up or partclosing up and sputum coversion were 96.6%,93.8%,and 92.9% respectively;7 cases of diabetes deteriorated.In this group,the focus absorption rate was only 14.3%,none of cavities absolutely or partly closed up,the sputum remained positive and 2 cases died.The rates of focus absorption an sputum conversion in the two groups showed significant differences(P0.01).Conclusions The adverse influences exist between diabetes and PTB,and the results of treatment for diabetes may have marked effects on the prognosis of patients with PTB,thus,it is recommended that all the patients both with PTB and with diabetes should have routine blood sugar test and chest X ray examination.
