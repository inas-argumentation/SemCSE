IT companies Playgrounds or Serious Businesses?
In this paper we present and discuss two possible ways of interpreting the organization of IT consulting firms. These companies are described as organic in the literature, while they themselves describe their organizations as flat and organic. We base our two interpretations of this paradox on neo institutional theory, which argue that the formal structure of organizations is mostly only loosely coupled to the informal structure. Companies have two different organizations: one implemented organization that organize the work that is actually performed (instrumental function) and one that the company shows to its groups of interests in order to legitimize itself (symbolic organization) The aim of the paper is to investigate whether the hierarchy has an instrumental or symbolic function for the organization. A traditional neo institutional interpretation would probably imply that the hierarchy is the instrumental organization and the flatness is the symbolic what we call "serious business" An alternative and more likely interpretation is that the flatness is instrumental and the hierarchy is symbolic. Therefore, we argue that IT companies better can be understood as "playgrounds" Based on a study of six Swedish ITcompanies, we conclude that the organic structure as well as the hierarchy has an instrumental function for the organizations. Furthermore, we argue that both flat ness and hierarchy are used as symbolic arguments, depending of group of target.
