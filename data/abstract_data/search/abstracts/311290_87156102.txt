Effects of Light Intensity on Contents of Capsaicin and Its Metabolizing Competition Matters in Pepper Fruits
The paper investigated the effects of four different light intensity(40%,60%,80% and natural light) on the capsain content of hot pepper fruits,and its competition matters flavonoid,lignin and tannin during the growing stage.The results showed that capsaicin content decreased along with the weakening light intensity.There was an inverse relationship between capsain content and flavonoid.Lignin content increased with light intensity weakening.There was no relation between tannin content and light intensity.
