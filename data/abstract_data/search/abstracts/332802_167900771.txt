Technical Trade Barriers on Anhui's Export Trade Influence and Strategic Choice Research
In recent years,foreign trade in Anhui developed rapidly. As trade volume is growing,the international market competition is also increasingly intense. Technical trade barriers have become increasingly serious,it becomes the first non tariff trade barriers restricting Anhui exports to expand. Therefore,study of technical trade barriers on Anhui's export trade influence,and puts forward some positive measures,will have contribution to the development of the export trade of Anhui Province and foreign trade competitiveness. This paper will conduct a thorough analysis and put forward some suggestions,in order to accelerate foreign trade of Anhui Province.
