Application status of the fuzzy neural network in prediction of welding performance
By fuzzy inference system combined with artificial neural network technology,fuzzy neural network prediction model is established,the prediction of the welding performance can be achieved. The establishment of prediction system in the welding performance,shortens the development cycle of the electrode and improves the welding performance. In this paper,the application of artificial neural networks and fuzzy inference system in welding is described,simultaneously the situation of application and research of fuzzy neural network in welding performance prediction is introduced,and the problem in application and research and future trends are investigated.
