Comments on The Modernization of Idea of Higher Education Idea in China
Modernization of higher education was a problem all over the world,previously more attention had been paid to it.Chinese higher education also faced it from the day of engender. Firmly speaking,through Qing dynasty to the Republic of China, in view of the stratum of content and the stratum of system,the modernization of higher education had been basically accomplished in China,but the stratum of idea had been taken less notice of. Chinese universities will make greater progress when our ideas have been modernized, namely, university autonomy and academic freedom realized sufficiently, which should be an unavoidable phase in the process of development.
