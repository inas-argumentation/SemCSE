Kurt Russell pays tribute to dad's Portland Mavericks with 'The Battered Bastards of Baseball'
With spring training right around the corner, there's nothing like a heartwarming story about a man's pursuit of the American Dream through America's pastime: baseball. "The Battered Bastards of Baseball" debuted at Sundance this week, and follows Bing Russell's quest to create the only independent baseball team in our country at the time: the Portland Mavericks.
