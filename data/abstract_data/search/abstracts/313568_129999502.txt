EFFECT OF FYM AND CHEMICAL FERTILIZERS ON YIELD AND ECONOMICS OF POTATO BASED SEQUENCES IN LAHAUL VALLEY OF HIMACHAL PRADESH
Two clopping sequences, viz. Potato potato and potato pea were evaluated wdh the addition of farmyard manure and chemical fertihzers during 1999 and 2000 on a sandy loam, neutral soil under sprinkler urrigation at Kukumseri 10 Lahaul valley for yield, net gains and soil fertility build up Potato potato sequence resulted in higher yield and higher return than potato pea sequence but potato pea sequence proved superior in terms of soil fertility build up.
