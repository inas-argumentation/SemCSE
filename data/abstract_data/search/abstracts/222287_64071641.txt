Statements for Factual Question Generation
We address the problem of automatically generating concise factual questions from linguistically complex sentences in reading ma terials. We discuss semantic and pragmatic issues that appear in com plex sentences, and then we present an algorithm for extracting simpli ed sentences from appositives, subordinate clauses, and other construc tions. We conjecture that our method is useful as a preliminary step in a larger question generation process. Experimental results indicate that our method is more suitable for factual question generation applications than an alternative text compression algorithm.
