The Effect of Online Game Addiction on Adjustment Social in Adolescents
Technology is currently developing rapidly, one of which is online games. Excessive online game play can give teens a lot of trouble. The purpose of this study was to determine the effect of online game addiction on social adjustment. The populations in this study were adolescents in Kampung Dalam. The technique used is purposive so that the research sample is obtained as many as 23 adolescents in the inner village. The data collection method uses a questionnaire which will later be analyzed by SPSS. The results showed the value of r 0.586 with a significance of 0.03 (p <0.05) which means that there is a negative influence between online game addiction and adolescent social adjustment. Keywords: online game addiction, social adjustment, adolescents
