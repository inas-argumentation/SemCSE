A COMPREHENSIVE STUDY ON 3D PRNTING TECHNOLOGY
In the world of developing technology, 3d printing has replaced the entire manufacturing firm/system with its improved version of building parts layer by layer using additive approach and new trends. In the present article, a comprehensive study has been carried out for comparing it with traditional manufacturing method for production of components, complex objects for hundreds of different applications. 3D printing technology is extremely versatile and rapid process, accelerate innovation and reduce energy usage, minimise material and compress supply chains. This paper also describes the feed mechanism and process related information, supporting material, software used in 3D printer and some recent developments on 3D printing technology.
