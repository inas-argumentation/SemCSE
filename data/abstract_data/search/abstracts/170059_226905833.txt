Flipped Learning in ELT in Turkey: What Do the Studies Say?
The intensive use of technology in all areas of life has led to a dramatic change especially in the field of education. One of the outstanding change is Flipped Learning in which the instruction given by the teacher is converted. To increase the classroom activity time, the students watch asynchronous lecture videos, read online documents prepared by the teacher and carry out active, group based problem solving activities in the classroom. Both in the world and in Turkey there are studies which investigates the effectiveness of flipped learning. This research intents to provide survey of prior studies carried out in Turkey on ELT. A spreadsheet program with coding system of study features was used. These features are type of publication, year of publication, sample size, educational institution, study type, measurement instruments, in and out of class activities. It is thought that the findings of this research will shed light on the researchers working in this area.
