Vulnerability of juvenile clams to predation by shore crabs
Predation, especially by crabs, is a common source of mortality for natural and farmed populations of intertidal clams. Consumption of juvenile "seed clams" forces aquaculturists to try to exclude predators and/or raise juveniles in hatcheries until they can reach a size refuge.
