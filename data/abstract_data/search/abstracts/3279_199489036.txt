Self Attentive Networks for one shot Image Recognition
Despite recent breakthroughs in applications of deep neural networks, learning from a limited number of examples remains a key challenge in academia and industry. A prototypical example is one shot learning, in which we must make predictions when only given one sample of each unseen class. Most previous works either categorize instances by the pair similarities or use Long Short Term Memory (LSTM) structure based method, however, they usually suffer from inter and intra class variation problem or high computation cost. In this paper, we propose self attentive networks to address the above issues in one shot image recognition problem. Deep neural features and attention mechanism especially self attention are employed in the proposed self attentive networks. Experiments on Omniglot and MiniImagenet datasets show that our proposed self attentive networks achieve state of the art accuracy on one shot image recognition. In addition, owing to low time complexity and parallelizable architecture, self attentive networks require significantly less time to train and infer.
