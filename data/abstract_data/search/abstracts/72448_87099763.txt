Preparation of Polyclonal Antibody and Tissues Expression Analysis of Bombyx mori Serine Protease Inhibitor 5
In order to understand function of serine protease inhibitor 5(Serpin 5) in silkworm(Bombyx mori),we prepared its polyclonal antibody and analyzed its expression pattern in various silkworm tissues.We first cloned and obtained the Bombyx mori serpin 5 gene without signal peptide.This gene is 1 140 bp long and encodes 379 amino acid residues with predicted protein molecular weight around 42.6 kD and isoelectric point of 6.02.Then we constructed vector pET28a serpin 5 for prokaryotic expression and carried out induced expression of recombinant protein in E.coli BL21.SDS PAGE analysis showed that the recombinant protein Serpin 5 was expressed as inclusion body.The purified fusion protein had molecular weight consistent with the predicted value.This protein was used as antigen to immune Kunming mice.Western blot analysis displayed that the prepared polyclonal antibody had high specificity.ELISA assay indicated that the anti immune antigen titer of this polyclonal antibody reached 1 :24 000.Based on preparation of high titer antibody,Western blotting revealed that Serpin 5 protein had high expression levels in testis and ovary of day 3 silkworm larvae of the 5th instar,followed by midgut,silk gland,Malpighian tubule and fat body.The high expression of Serpin 5 in silkworm gonads showed that this protein is potentially involved in silkworm reproductive process.
