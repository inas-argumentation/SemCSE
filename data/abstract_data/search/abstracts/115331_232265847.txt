A Compact Network Using Attention Mechanism for Image Super Resolution
With the emergence of the 2K 4K Ultra High Definition (UHD) videos super resolution, single image super resolution based on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) is a critical method to transfer Standard Definition (SD) videos to High Definition (HD) videos. However, the state of the arts methods rely on a deeper or wider network, which poses a challenge in computational complexity and memory consumption. To address this problem, we proposed a compact super resolution framework using refined features. In addition, an attention channel sorted split (ACSS) module was proposed to significantly improve the performance of super resolution (SR) Especially, the ACSS module can make the network pay more attention to transfer features from coarse to fine. The experimental results show that our proposed method is efficient and effective.
