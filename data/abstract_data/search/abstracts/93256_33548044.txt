Factors Affecting Students' Achievement in English Language Learning
This article investigates the effect that some factors would have on the achievement of students learning English as a foreign language. Secondary school students' grades of the first semester 2013 2014 in English have been used as parameter of achievement. The factors (attitudinal, social, socioeconomic, and extracurricular) which are believed to influence students' achievement have been included in a 16 item yes no questionnaire. Findings show that there is significant relationship between the factors and students' achievement in English language learning. DOI: 10.5901/jesr.2016.v6n2p9
