Study on farmer laborer's rights and interests defense of Liaoning Province
The research group for subject"farmer laborer' s rights and interests defense of Liaoning Province"takes 200 farmer laborers as survey object which live and work in Shenyang,Dalian,Fuxin and Fushun,and makes the questionnaire investigation and chat.On the basis of questionnaire analysis,points out the actual situation and problems of farmer laborer's rights and interests defense work of Liaoning Province,puts forward the correlative countermeasures.
