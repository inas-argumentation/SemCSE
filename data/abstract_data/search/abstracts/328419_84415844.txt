Manisa, Turkey.A new subspecies Crocus antalyensis subsp. striatus subsp. nov. from western Turkey is described. It is characterized byrough papery, light brown corm tunics, leaves that exceed the flower at anthesis and are recurved, distinctly striped innerperianth segments and deeply branched pale yellow or white style. The karyotype of the new subspecies is presented.
