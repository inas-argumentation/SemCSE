Export Volatility with Trade Policy Uncertainty: Evidence from China
This paper studies the effect of trade policy uncertainty on firm level volatility. Using data on exporting firms from China around its WTO accession, we find that a reduction of trade policy uncertainty between China and the US decreases the volatility of firm level export to the US and increases the volatility of export to the rest of the world, suggesting a risk sharing effect among export destinations. In the aggregate level, the shock in trade policy uncertainty has no significant effect on the volatility of total firm export or the volatility of total firm sales.
