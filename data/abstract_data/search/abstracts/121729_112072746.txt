On the Construction of Legal Supervision of Product Placement
Product placement cannot be brought into the supervision of Advertising Law directly lacking the identity of traditional advertisement.From legislative progressions relative in America and the EU,it is necessary to clarify the connotation of Product Placement.Referring to the practical experience of the USA and the EU legislation,it asks for constructing fundamental regulations on the basis of improving the industrial chain and the acknowledgement of the legal status of Product Placement,so that the benefits between public interest and commercial interest could be balanced.
