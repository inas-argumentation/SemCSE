Book Review: Synchronicity: The Inner Path of Leadership Joseph Jaworski San Francisco, CA: Berrett Koehler, 1996, 211 pp. $24.95, cloth
The book is divided into four parts which are modeled on the heroic quest described in Joseph Campbell's book The Hero With a Thousand Faces (1949) In Part One, &dquo;Preparing to Journey,&dquo; Jaworski describes how his life as a highly successful Houston trial lawyer and entrepreneur came apart in the face of ethical issues raised by the Watergate scandal and his divorce. These events forced him to reevaluate his materialistic values and to recognize the importance of being over doing. During this period he began to develop his vision for the American leadership Forum, a national institute dedicated to servant leadership.
