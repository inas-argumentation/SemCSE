Diseno de un modelo estocastico usando cadenas de Markov para pronosticar la desercion academica de estudiantes de Ingenieria caso Escuela Colombiana de Ingenieria Julio Garavito
The design of a stochastic model was made using Markov chains to predit the academic defection of engineering studens, applied for the case of La Escuela Colombiana de Ingenieria Julio Garaqvito Ingenieria Julio Garavito, took the cohorts from 2003 to 2006.
