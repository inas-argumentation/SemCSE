Research on the Happiness of Researchers in A Third class Grade one Hospital
Objective To investigate the degree of happiness in hospital researchers and its influencing factors.Methods Happiness index questionnaires were used to survey 280 health care researchers in a class three grade one hospital in March 2013.And the results then were compared among different genders,professions,and titles.Results No obvious difference was found between different genders(t=0.55,P=0.083),but the difference was significant between different professions,including nurses and doctors.Their happiness(t=3.58,P=0.012) and life satisfaction(t=34.61,P0.01) were significantly different.All data were divided into primary,intermediate and advanced groups among whom there were also significant differences in happiness(F=7.48,P0.01) and life satisfaction(F=6.35,P=0.014).Conclusion Nurses and primary or intermediate health workers have more stresses in their work and life with a low degree of happiness,which should cause our great attention.
