Moi quan he giua von tam ly va su hai long cong viec cua nhan vien
The study aims to investigate the relationship between psychological capital and employees' job satisfaction. Psychological capital is a concept developed to measure an individual's psychological capital (Newman, Ucbasaran, Zhu, Hirst, 2014) which is rather new in Vietnam. Job satisfaction is one of many specific employees' attitudes towards the job. The results of the study are estimated by using structural equation modeling (SEM) and primary data collected from 848 office workers in Vietnam. The findings show that both psychological capital and its components (self efficacy, optimism, hope and resiliency) have a positive correlation with employees' job satisfaction. 4 Nguyen M. Gioi thieu Cung voi von con nguoi va von xa hoi, von tam ly cua nguoi nhan vien duoc xem nhu la nguon luc quan trong cua to chuc va co the tro thanh loi the canh tranh. Tuy nhien, vuot qua hai loai von noi tren, von tam ly xem xet den gia tri cua nguoi nhan vien trong thoi diem hien tai va trong ca chieu huong phat trien trong tuong lai. Von tam ly la ban chat cua con nguoi va la trang thai tam ly tich cuc trong su phat trien cua ca nhan (Avey, Luthans, Jensen, 2009) Noi cach khac, von tam ly la khai niem de cap den muc do tich cuc trong suy nghi, tam ly cua mot ca nhan va co lien quan den su phat trien ban than ca nhan do. Trong boi canh lam viec trong mot to chuc, chinh su phat trien ve trinh do chuyen mon, ki nang lam viec, nang luc lam viec cua tung nhan vien trong to chuc se gop phan vao su thanh cong va phat trien cua to chuc do. Cung lien quan den chu de ve tam ly nhan vien, thai do cong viec dai dien cho cac phan ung tam ly va cac danh gia cua nguoi nhan vien doi voi cong viec. Su hai long cong viec la mot trong nhung thai do cu the cua nguoi nhan vien doi voi cong viec cua minh va duoc xem nhu la mot thai do cong viec tich cuc (Avey, Reichard, Luthans, Mhatre, 2011; Larson Luthans, 2006; Lehoczky, 2013) Su hai long cong viec la trang thai cam xuc vui thich xuat phat tu danh gia cong viec cua mot ca nhan khi dat duoc hoac tao dieu kien dat duoc cac gia tri cong viec (Locke, 1969) Tai Viet Nam, sau khi da danh su quan tam dang ke cho von con nguoi va von xa hoi cua cac thanh vien trong to chuc, cac nha quan tri dan chuyen huong sang von tam ly cua con nguoi nhu la nguon luc moi de nang cao thai do va hanh vi cua nhan vien doi voi cong viec va hinh thanh nen loi the canh tranh cua to chuc. Nghien cuu thuc nghiem nay duoc thuc hien tai Viet Nam, nham tim hieu moi quan he giua von tam ly cua nguoi nhan vien va bon thanh phan co ban cua von tam ly voi mot thai do cong viec cu the mang tinh tich cuc cua nguoi nhan vien la su hai long cong viec. Tu do, dua ra cac de xuat giup nha quan tri nang cao su hai long voi cong viec cua nguoi nhan vien. 2. Co so ly thuyet 2.1. Von tam ly Tren co so ke thua ket qua nghien cuu cua cac nha khoa hoc khac trong linh vuc tam ly hoc lam sang va tam ly hoc tich cuc cung nhu ket qua tu cac nghien cuu thuc nghiem tu thuc hien, Luthans, Youssef, va Avolio (2015) cua minh da xay dung mot khai niem moi la "Von tam ly" Luthans va cong su (2015) dinh nghia von tam ly la trang thai phat trien tam ly tich cuc cua mot ca nhan va duoc mo ta bang cac thanh phan nhu: (i) su tu tin nang luc ban than de giai quyet cong viec; (ii) su lac quan ve ket qua cong viec; (iii) su hy vong va (iv) su kien cuong de vuot qua nhung kho khan nghich canh trong qua trinh lam viec. Cac khai niem thanh phan nay cua von tam ly duoc Luthans va cong su (2015) ke thua tu cac nghien cuu cua nhieu nha tam ly hoc, tieu bieu nhu Bandura (1997) ve su tu tin nang luc ban than, Snyder va cong su (1991) ve su lac quan, Seligman (1998) ve su hy vong, Masten (2001) ve su kien cuong. Su hai long cong viec Fisher (2003) cung cho rang su hai long cong viec la mot danh gia ve muc do phu hop cua cong viec voi nhu cau, mong muon va ky vong cua nhan vien. Theo do, trong dieu kien su ky vong va cac gia tri nhan duoc khac biet nhau mang tinh thuc nghiem, su hai long doi voi cong viec duoc xac dinh khi gia tri nhan duoc thuc te sau khi thuc hien cong viec lon hon su ky vong ban dau. Su hai long cong viec co the duoc giai thich hoac la phan ung mang tinh cam xuc hoac la ky vong ca nhan ve ket qua va cac khia canh lien quan den cong viec nhu tien luong, su thang tien, ban than cong viec, co so vat chat, ban chat cong viec, dong nghiep,(Luthans, 1998) 2.3. Moi quan he giua von tam ly va su hai long cong viec Tu tin vao nang luc ban than la niem tin cua mot ca nhan vao kha nang giai quyet tot bat cu van de gi bang su no luc cua chinh minh (Wood, Bandura, Bailey, 1990) Nho vay nguoi nhan vien co su tu tin cao vao nang luc ban than ua thich cac cong viec nhieu kho khan hon, dat ra cac muc tieu cong viec cao hon va no luc rat nhieu de hoan thanh chung. Khi duoc to chuc giao cong viec phu hop muc tieu ca nhan da dat ra va dat duoc thanh cong, nguoi nhan vien se hai long voi cong viec minh lam. H1a: Su tu tin vao nang luc ban than cua nhan vien co moi quan he duong voi su hai long cong viec cua nhan vien Ben canh do, nguoi nhan vien co von tam ly cao con co hy vong cao ve cong viec. Cac van de kho khan trong cong viec duoc nguoi co muc do hy vong cao xem nhu cac thu thach va la co hoi hoc tap de nang cao nang luc ban than va hoan toan co the giai quyet no (Youssef Luthans, 2007) nen ngay ca khi cong viec khong suon se, gap nhieu kho khan thi nguoi nhan vien co nhieu hy vong cung de hai long voi cong viec cua minh hon. H1b: Su hy vong cua nhan vien co moi quan he duong voi su hai long cong viec cua nhan vien Su lac quan va su kien cuong cao cua nguoi co von tam ly cao cung co lien quan den su hai long cong viec cua ho. Nguoi lac quan luon co cam xuc tich cuc voi cong viec va cuoc song cua minh nen ho cung co thai do hai long voi cong viec. Ho cho rang kho khan, that bai chi la mang tinh nhat thoi va khong ton tai lau dai nen ho co the thay doi tinh the. Trong khi do, su kien cuong lai giup nguoi nhan vien chong do tot hon va thich nghi de dang hon voi cac kho khan va that bai trong cong viec. Nho vay, ho nhanh chong hoi phuc tam ly va duy tri cam xuc tich cuc de hai long voi nhung gi dang co va san sang duong dau voi cac thu thach tiep theo. Phuong phap nghien cuu Trong qua trinh thuc hien, nghien cuu su dung ket hop phuong phap nghien cuu dinh tinh va phuong phap nghien cuu dinh luong. Cu the, hoat dong lay y kien chuyen gia duoc su dung trong xay dung thang do von tam ly va su hai long cong viec cua nhan vien. Phuong phap nghien cuu dinh luong duoc su dung de uoc luong ket qua nghien cuu thong qua cac ki thuat thong ke mo ta, kiem dinh do tin cay thang do, phan tich nhan to kham pha, phan tich nhan to khang dinh, phan tich mo hinh phuong trinh cau truc (SEM) 3.2. Thang do Tu su tham khao cac nghien cuu truoc va thuc hien nghien cuu dinh tinh, noi dung thang do cac khai niem su dung trong nghien cuu la phu hop voi boi canh nghien cuu tai Viet Nam. Trong hoat dong nghien cuu dinh tinh, hinh thuc lay y kien chuyen gia (bao gom cac nha nghien cuu ve quan tri nhan su, cac nha quan tri doanh nghiep, cac nhan vien co kinh nghiem lam viec tren 05 nam) duoc su dung de danh gia noi dung, bo sung cac bien quan sat moi va chinh sua noi dung cac bien quan sat da xay dung. Ben canh do, su phu hop cua viec su dung thang do 5 muc do de do luong phan hoi cua nguoi tra loi phong van cung duoc trao doi truc tiep voi cac chuyen gia. Co ba phien ban bang cau hoi thuong duoc su dung trong do luong von tam ly cua mot ca nhan la PCQ 24, PCQ 12 va I PCQ. Qua khao sat cac nghien cuu truoc, bang cau hoi PCQ24 duoc su dung pho bien nhat va trong nghien cuu nay, PCQ 24 duoc tham khao de xay dung thang do von tam ly. PCQ 24 la bang cau hoi bao gom 24 cau phat bieu, do luong bon nhan to thanh phan cua von tam ly.
