Using genetic and BP algorithms for image matching
The neural network theory is used for image matching; and a neural network image matching algorithm is proposed based on genetic al gorithm: first optimizing the BP networks learning process by genetic algorithm ,then accurately training it using the BP algorithm. Finally, according to the obtained weights and thresholds, the matching result are obtained. Experimental results show that this new image matching algorithm has merits in accuracy, sp eed, and robustness; and its convergence performance is also better than that of standard BP algorithm.
