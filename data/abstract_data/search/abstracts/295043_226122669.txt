Desarrollo de la competencia comunicativa en Lengua Castellana mediante el Aprendizaje Basado en Juegos de mesa (ABJ) en un contexto con metodologia de ambientes de aprendizaje
This Final Degree Project (TFG) is based in a small investigation on the Game Based Learning (GBL) and how it could be applied in a learning environment of a specific educational center that uses the methodology of the environments to be able to work the communicative competences in Spanish language. There will be a development of what materials, resources and procedures are necessary or advisable to carry it out.
