Explore the Control System of County Administration in Ancient China
In the feudal age in China, a county administration is the foundation of feudal rule. A good political authority in a county dependson a strict control system of county administration. The author holds that its features of strictness, directness, permanence and limitation are a valuble refference for us in the building of political authority in a county, and that we must guide against the demerits of the control system of county administration in ancient China and draw its lessons left.
