Research on Calibration Method of Camera Stabilizer Based on Binocular Vision
The calibration of the camera stabilizer is the basis of precision measurement. The traditional dual target needs to establish a complex mathematical model with large calculation, low real time performance, slow speed and low precision. The neural network can effectively deal with the nonlinear mapping problem, which can well describe the nonlinear relationship between the three dimensional spatial feature point coordinates and the corresponding points of two cameras in binocular vision. Therefore, this paper proposes an improved RBF binocular vision neural network calibration method, and introduces genetic algorithm and firefly algorithm to optimize the learning algorithm of radial basis function network. The improved RBF binocular visual neural network has less error in testing, uses fewer neurons, and has shorter training time. It can better meet the requirements of real time online calibration, and has strong self organization, self learning, self adaptation and strong The nonlinear mapping ability accurately establishes the relationship between the three dimensional object point coordinates and the two camera coordinates in binocular vision. Compared with the traditional method, the method has the advantages of fast reconstruction speed, high calculation precision, small calculation amount and good real time performance.
