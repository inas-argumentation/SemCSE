Wav2Letter+ A Fast Open source Speech Recognition System
This paper introduces wav2letter+ a fast open source deep learning speech recognition framework. wav2letter+ is written entirely in C+ and uses the ArrayFire tensor library for maximum efficiency. We explain the architecture and design of the wav2letter+ system and compare it to other major open source speech recognition systems. In some cases wav2letter+ is more than 2x faster than other optimized frameworks for training end to end neural networks for speech recognition. We also show that wav2letter+ training times scale linearly to 64 GPUs, the most we tested, for models with 100 million parameters. High performance frameworks enable fast iteration, which is often a crucial factor in successful research and model tuning on new datasets and tasks.
