Nuevas notas a proposito de la Galeria de Teatro Infantil de Antonio Vanegas Arroyo
It is an approach to dramatic literature written and printed for children by Antonio Vanegas Arroyo editor in the early twentieth century, both the structure and the themes of so called <juguetes comicos> Vanegas Arroyo noted for their originality salt the own canon of what is considered children's theater and its huge presence in Mexican children of the time.
