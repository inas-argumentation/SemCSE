Application and Energy Saving Analysis of Permanent Magnetic Speed Control in The Pump System
Abstract The article focuses on low efficiency with the method of valve adjustment in the operation of the domestic water pump, variable speed control by permanent magnet speed control technology,and achieves adjustive ways of replaceing the baffle with permanent magnet speed, hence saving much electricity and improving the efficiency of the pump, and realizing long period,safe, reliable operation of water pump system, safe and stable operation.
