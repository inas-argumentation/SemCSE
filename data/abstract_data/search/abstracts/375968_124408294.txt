The Life Table
Life tables traditionally have been used to estimate the probability of death for each age given the year of birth. More generally they are used to investigate discrete time to event data in homogeneous populations without taking explanatory variables into account. The basic life table estimator and smoothed versions are considered. The Kaplan Meier estimator for continuous time, which is closely related to the life table estimator, is briefly sketched. The use of life tables in demography is illustrated, and the Lee Carter model, which focusses on the dynamics of mortality over time, is presented.
