We answer an old question of Michael Hrušák by constructing a +Ramsey MAD family without the need of any additional axioms beyond ZFC. We also prove that every Miller-indestructible MAD family is +Ramsey, this improves a result of Michael Hrušák. Introduction A family A ⊆ [ω] is almost disjoint (AD) if the intersection of any two different elements ofA is finite, aMAD family is a maximal almost disjoint family. Almost disjoint families and MAD families have become very important in set theory, topology and functional analysis (see [7]). It is very easy to prove that the Axiom of Choice implies the existence of MAD families. However, constructing MAD families with special combinatorial or topological properties is a very difficult task without the an additional hypothesis beyond ZFC. Constructing models of set theory for which certain kinds of MAD families do not exist is very difficult. We would like to mention some important examples regarding the existence or non-existence of special MAD families: 1.
