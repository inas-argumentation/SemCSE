Resolving the low mass puzzle of Λc(2940)
For the long standing low mass puzzle of Λc(2940), we propose an unquenched picture. Our calculation explicitly shows that the mass of the Λ+c (2P, 3/2 −) state can be lowered down to be consistent with the experimental data of Λc(2940) by introducing the D∗N channel contribution. Additionally, we give a semi-quantitative analysis to illustrate why the Λc(2940) state has a narrow width. It means that the low mass puzzle of Λc(2940) can be solved. What is more important is that we predict a mass inversion relation for the 2P Λ+c states, i.e., the Λc(2P, 1/2−) state is lower than the Λc(2P, 3/2−), which is totally different from the result of conventional quenched quark model. It provides a criteria to test such an unquenched scenario for Λc(2940). We expect the future experimental progress from the LHCb and Belle II.
