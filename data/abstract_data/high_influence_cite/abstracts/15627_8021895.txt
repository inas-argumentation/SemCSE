THE EXTERNAL VERTICES CONJECTURE IN CASE N = 4 ∗
The determinantal conjecture of M. Marcus and G. N. de Oliveira is known in many special cases. The case of 3 × 3 matrices was settled byN. Bebiano, J. K. Merikoski and J. da Providencia. The case n = 4 remains open. In this article a technical conjecture is established implying a weakened form of the determinantal conjecture for n =4 .
