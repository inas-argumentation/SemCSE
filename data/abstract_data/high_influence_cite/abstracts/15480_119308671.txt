Determinantal processes and completeness of random exponentials: the critical case
For a locally finite point set $$\Lambda \subset \mathbb {R}$$Λ⊂R, consider the collection of exponential functions given by $$\mathcal {E}_{\Lambda }:=\{ e^{i \lambda x } : \lambda \in \Lambda \}$$EΛ:={eiλx:λ∈Λ}. We examine the question whether $$\mathcal {E}_{\Lambda }$$EΛ spans the Hilbert space $$L^2[-\pi ,\pi ]$$L2[-π,π], when $$\Lambda $$Λ is random. For several point processes of interest, this belongs to a certain critical case of the corresponding question for deterministic $$\Lambda $$Λ, about which little is known. For $$\Lambda $$Λ the continuum sine kernel process, obtained as the bulk limit of GUE eigenvalues, we establish that $$\mathcal {E}_{\Lambda }$$EΛ is indeed complete almost surely. We also answer an analogous question on $$\mathbb {C}$$C for the Ginibre ensemble, arising as weak limits of the spectra of certain non-Hermitian Gaussian random matrices. In fact we establish completeness for any “rigid” determinantal point process in a general setting. In addition, we partially answer two questions of Lyons and Steif about stationary determinantal processes on $$\mathbb {Z}^d$$Zd.
