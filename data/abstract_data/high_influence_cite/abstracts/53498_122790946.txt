Fabrication of irregular optical phased arrays on silicon-on-insulator wafers
A 16- and a 32-element irregular optical phased array based on silicon-on-insulator materials were fabri- cated. Such a device consists of an array of irregularly spaced single-mode waveguides, each of which has a spe- cific length to give an appropriate phase retardation control- lable by the wavelength. The optical beam can be steered continuously in the range of about 0 to 3 deg, corresponding to the wavelength varying from 1550.7 to 1551.9 nm. © 2008
