Some remarks on the Euler ring U(G)
Abstract.Let G be a compact Lie group and let U(G) denote the Euler ring of G constructed by tom Dieck [5, 6]. The main result of the paper (Theorem 4.1) describes the homomorphism 
$$\varphi ^{*}: U(SO(3)) \rightarrow U(SO(2))$$
 induced by the inclusion 
$${\varphi}: SO(2) \rightarrow SO(3)$$
.
