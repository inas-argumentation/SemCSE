Purity-bounded uncertainty relations in multidimensional space — generalized purity Mikalai Karelin
Uncertainty relations for mixed quantum states (purity-bounded position-momentum relations, developed by Bastiaans and then by Man’ko and Dodonov) are studied in general multi-dimensional case. An expression for family of mixed states at the lower bound of uncertainty relation is obtained. It is shown, that in case of entropybounded uncertainty relations, lower-bound state is thermal, and a transition from one-dimensional problem to multi-dimensional one is trivial. Results of numerical calculation of the relation lower bound for different types of generalized purity are presented. Analytical expressions for general purity-bounded relations in 2and 3dimensional cases are obtained. Well-known position-momentum uncertainty relation for standard deviations of x̂ and p̂ operators ∆x∆p > ~/2 (1) is valid for any state (described either by a wavefunction or by a density matrix [1, 2]) and plays an ultimate role in quantum physics. In particular, uncertainty relation sets the limits of measurement process for non-commuting observables [3]. Another important example is that generalized coherent states could be defined as a set of states which minimize an uncertainty relation [5]. The inequality (1) could be generalized to include extra dependence on degree of purity [6] of a quantum state μ = Tr(ρ̂) (2) (ρ̂ is a density matrix with Trρ̂ = 1), the parameter μ 6 1 and equality μ = 1 is achieved only for pure states. The other measures of overall purity are possible except the trace of squared density operator — see above cited papers for details, especially a recent comprehensive review on purity-bounded relations in Ref. 13. It also necessary to note that the inequality, mathematically practically the same as uncertainty relation, but with another physical meaning, is often used for classical wave fields, e. g. in optics [7–11,19]. Results of the present article, as well as of preceding papers [18,19] could be used, with adequate change of notations, for classical partially coherent fields and sources (in 2and 3-dimensional space). The uncertainty relation (5), (8) could be obtain by more general way, with dependence of inequality minimum on eigenvalues of density operator (preliminary report on this topic, with stress on partially coherent classical fields, was published in Ref. 20).
