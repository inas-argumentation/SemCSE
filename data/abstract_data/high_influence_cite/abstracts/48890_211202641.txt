The Dilemma of Raised Blood Levels of Folate and B12 in Autism
It has recently been reported that a raised level of folate and/or vitamin B12 in the blood of a newly parturient woman forecasts a marked increase in the possibility that the newborn infant will later develop autisma. The text that follows refers to the cases of two boys, each of which had suffered repeated episodes of acute febrile cervical lymphadenopathy. The medical history of one of the boys revealed that he had been previously admitted to a hospital during an episode of febrile lymphadenopathy. An enlarged cervical node had been removed for biopsy and it had been reported that its architecture, apart from its enlargement, was normal. In addition, it had been found that blood levels of folate and vitamin B12 were markedly increased. His mother had been accused of giving him too many supplements of vitamins, including folate and vitamin B12. She stated that she had not been giving the boy any vitamin supplements at all, a fact that was met with disbelief.
