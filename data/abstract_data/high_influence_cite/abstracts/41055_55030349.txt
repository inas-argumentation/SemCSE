ANALYSIS OF IODINE RELEASE FROM THE DEFECTIVE FUEL ELEMENTS OF THE RBMK-1500 REACTOR
The results of modelling and analysis of iodine release to the main circulation circuit of the RBMK-1500 reactor are presented. General approach proposed by Lewis and Husain for the CANDU reactor primary coolant activity prediction was applied. Specic features of the RBMK-1500 coolant activity measurement were assessed. Non-stationary activity measurement conditions were identied and quantied with corresponding correction factors. Parameter values of the adapted model are comparable with those characteristic of CANDU reactor defective fuel.
