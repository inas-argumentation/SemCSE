Classical solution to a multidimensional stochastic Burgers equation
In this work, we prove the existence and uniqueness of the global classical solution to the multidimensional stochastically forced viscous Burgers equation without potential-type assumptions on forcing or terminal conditions. The main tool in our analysis is a novel probabilistic a priori gradient estimate which holds uniformly over subintervals of a given bounded interval $[0,T]$. Moreover, as the viscosity $\nu$ goes to zero, we prove the convergence of the classical solutions to the local classical solution of an inviscid stochastic Burgers equation. Our results also cover a multidimensional deterministic Burgers equation in the non-gradient case.
