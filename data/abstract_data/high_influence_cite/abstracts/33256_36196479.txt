Endpoint bounds for quasiradial Fourier multipliers
We consider quasiradial Fourier multipliers, i.e., multipliers of the form $$m(a(\xi ))$$m(a(ξ)) for a class of distance functions a. We give a necessary and sufficient condition for the multiplier transformations to be bounded on $$L^p$$Lp for a certain range of p. In addition, when m is compactly supported in $$(0,\infty )$$(0,∞), we give a similar result for associated maximal operators.
