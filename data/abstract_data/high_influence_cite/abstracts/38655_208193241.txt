Percutaneous Coronary Intervention in a Cancer Patient with Dextrocardia and Situs Inversus
Although dextrocardia occurs rarely, the incidence of coronary artery disease is similar to the general population [1]. Few cases of coronary angiography and percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) in patients with dextrocardia have been reported [2]. Because of unfamiliarity with performing catheterization, transradial coronary angiography has seldom been performed in a patient with dextrocardia [1]. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first report of successful coronary angiography and ad hoc coronary stenting in a cancer patient with dextrocardia with situs inversus in Lebanon. We successfully performed right transfemoral coronary angiography in a patient with a right side heart using counter-directional torquing of the catheters and mirror-image angiographic angles.
