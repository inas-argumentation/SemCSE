Horizon thermodynamics in ${f(R,R^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu}})$ theory
We investigate whether the new horizons first law still holds in $f(R,R^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu})$ theory. We obtain the formula via the new horizons first law to compute the entropy of a static spherically symmetric black hole in $f(R,R^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu})$ theory. Using the degenerate Legendre transformation, we also obtain the formula to compute the energy of black hole. For application, we consider the quadratic-curvature gravity and firstly calculate the entropy and the energy for a static spherically symmetric black hole, especially for a Schwarzschild-(A)ds black hole.
