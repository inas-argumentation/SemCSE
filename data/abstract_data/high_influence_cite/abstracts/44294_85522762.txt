Small Scale Equidistribution of Random Eigenbases
We investigate small scale equidistribution of random orthonormal bases of eigenfunctions (i.e., eigenbases) on a compact manifold $${{\mathbb M}}$$M. Assume that the group of isometries acts transitively on $${{\mathbb M}}$$M and the multiplicity $${m_\lambda}$$mλ of eigenfrequency $${\lambda}$$λ tends to infinity at least logarithmically as $${\lambda \to \infty}$$λ→∞. We prove that, with respect to the natural probability measure on the space of eigenbases, almost surely a random eigenbasis is equidistributed at small scales; furthermore, the scales depend on the growth rate of $${m_\lambda}$$mλ. In particular, this implies that almost surely random eigenbases on the sphere $${{\mathbb S}^n}$$Sn ($${n \ge 2}$$n≥2) and the tori $${{\mathbb T}^n}$$Tn ($${n \ge 5}$$n≥5) are equidistributed at polynomial scales.
