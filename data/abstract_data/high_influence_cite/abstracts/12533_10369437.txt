Adolescent pruning and stabilization of dendritic spines on cortical layer 5 pyramidal neurons do not depend on gonadal hormones
Highlights • Spines in frontal cortex of female mice are pruned and stabilized during adolescence.• Pre-pubertal hormone exposure does not accelerate spine pruning and stabilization.• Pre-pubertal gonadectomy does not prevent spine pruning and stabilization.• Pre-pubertal hormone exposure and gonadectomy do impact spine morphology.
