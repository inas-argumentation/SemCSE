The Research on Chinese Corruption and the Strategy of Governance
In recent years, China’s economy has gained a great progress, but corruption is still more serious. Firstly, we discuss the strategy of the existing literature combined with the definition of corruption. Secondly, we make the review and analysis of the literature with China’s problems. At last, I pay attention to two strategies of the governance of corruption, and I also have the same idea that, to be consistent with that, China should strengthen system construction combining with its own culture tradition on the problem of governance of corruption.
