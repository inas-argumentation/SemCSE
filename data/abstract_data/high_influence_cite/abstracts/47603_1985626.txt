Duality of Channel Encoding and Decoding - Part I: Rate-1 Convolutional Codes
In this paper, we revisit the forward, backward and bidirect onal Bahl-Cocke-Jelinek-Raviv (BCJR) soft-input soft-output (SISO) maximum a posteriori probab ility (MAP) decoding process of rate-1 convolutional codes. From this we establish some interesti ng duality properties between encoding and decoding of rate-1 convolutional codes. We observe that the forward and backward BCJR SISO MAP decoders can be simply represented by their dual SISO cha nnel encoders using shift registers in the complex number field. Similarly, the bidirectional MAP d ecoding can be implemented by linearly combining the outputs of the dual SISO encoders of the respec tive forward and backward decoders. The dual encoder structures for various recursive and non-recu rsive rate-1 convolutional codes are derived.
