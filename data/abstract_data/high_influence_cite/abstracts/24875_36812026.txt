Cohomology of Deligne–Lusztig varieties for short-length regular elements in exceptional groups
We determine the cohomology of Deligne–Lusztig varieties associated with some short-length regular elements for split groups of type F 4 and E n . As a byproduct, we obtain conjectural Brauer trees for the principal Φ 14 -block of E 7 and the principal Φ 24 -block of E 8 .
