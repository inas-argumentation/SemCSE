As fast as sound lightweight formal scenario synthesis and verification
Synthesis and Model Checking are classical techniques for relating scenarios (here, Live Sequence Charts) and state machines, in the context of roundtrip Scenario-based Software Engineering. Unfortunately, current approaches do not scale up very well. To improve on this, we propose to lose some precision as a trade-off to gain efficiency. Usual algorithms are turned into sound ones, which act locally on the specification and deliver results in acceptable time.
