Phase III randomized, placebo‐controlled, double‐blind study of monosialotetrahexosylganglioside for the prevention of oxaliplatin‐induced peripheral neurotoxicity in stage II/III colorectal cancer
Monosialotetrahexosylganglioside (GM1) is a neuroprotective glycosphingolipid that repairs nerves. Oxaliplatin‐based chemotherapy is neurotoxic. This study assessed the efficacy of GM1 for preventing oxaliplatin‐induced peripheral neurotoxicity (OIPN) in colorectal cancer (CRC) patients receiving oxaliplatin‐based chemotherapy.
