Modification of the Armijo line search to satisfy the convergence properties of HS method
The Hestenes-Stiefel (HS) conjugate gradient algorithm is a useful tool of unconstrainednumerical optimization, which has good numerical performance but no global convergence result under traditional line searches. This paper proposes a line search technique that guarantee the globalconvergence of the Hestenes-Stiefel (HS) conjugate gradient method.
