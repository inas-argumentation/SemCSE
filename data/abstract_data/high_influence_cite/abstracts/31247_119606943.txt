Multisolitons for the Defocusing Energy Critical Wave Equation with Potentials
We construct multi-soliton solutions to the defocusing energy critical wave equation with potentials in $${\mathbb{R}^{3}}$$R3 and study their asymptotic stability in the energy space based on both regular and reversed Strichartz estimates developed in [GC3]. We also study related scattering problems in the stable case. Since each soliton decays slowly with rate $${\frac{1}{\left\langle x\right\rangle }}$$1x , some refined estimates for the charge transfer model are also established.
