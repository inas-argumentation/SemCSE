Artin group actions on derived categories of threefolds
Motivated by the enhanced gauge symmetry phenomenon of the physics literature and mirror symmetry, this paper constructs an action of an Artin group on the derived category of coherent sheaves of a smooth quasiprojective threefold containing a configuration of ruled surfaces described by a finite type Dynkin diagram. The action ex- tends over deformations of the threefold via a compatible action of the corresponding re- flection group on the base of its deformation space. All finite type Dynkin diagrams are realized.
