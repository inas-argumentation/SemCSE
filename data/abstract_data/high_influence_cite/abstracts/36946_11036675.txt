Eleven dimensional superstring: the brane-scan revisited
We suggest a D = 11 super Poincaré invariant action for the closed superstring which has free dynamics in the physical variables sector. The variables turn out to be x i , θ a , ¯ θ ˙ a , in close analogy to the D = 10 Green–Schwarz superstring. Instead of the standard approach based on the searching for an action with local κ-symmetry (or, equivalently, with corresponding first class constraints), we have found a theory with fermionic constraints of second class only. Then the κ-symmetry and the well known Γ-matrix identities are not necessary for the construction. Thus, at the classical level, the superstring action of the type described can exist in any spacetime dimensions and the known brane-scan can be revisited.
