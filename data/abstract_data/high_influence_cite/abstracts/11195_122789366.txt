FAR FIELD IMAGING RESEARCH BASED ON MULTILAYER POSITIVE- AND NEGATIVE-REFRACTIVE-INDEX MEDIA UNDER OFF-AXIS ILLUMINATION
In this work, a far fleld imaging model based on the array structure of positive- and negative-refractive-index media and modulation subwavelength-gratings is flrstly presented and is named as the multilayer far fleld superlens (MLFSL). This new lens is capable of producing optical images by enhancing evanescent waves to the far fleld. The principle of MLFSL is discussed in detail, and the necessary and su-cient condition for designing MLFSL is obtained. Simultaneously, ofi-axis illumination technology is introduced to MLFSL system to further improve super-resolution, and the transfer matrix which contains the incident angle is obtained. The results demonstrate that, compared with other far fleld superlens, the subwavelength resolution of MLFSL has been enhanced. Such remarkable imaging capability of MLFSL promises new potential for nanoscale imaging and lithography.
