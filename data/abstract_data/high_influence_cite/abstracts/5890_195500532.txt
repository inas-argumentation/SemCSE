Remarks on Multiplicative Atom-Bond Connectivity Index
The atom-bond connectivity (ABC) index is one of the most actively studied degree-based graph invariants that are found in a vast variety of chemical applications. This paper is devoted to establishing some extremal results regarding the variant of the ABC index, the so-called multiplicative ABC index (ABC<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Pi $ </tex-math></inline-formula>), which, for a graph <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$G$ </tex-math></inline-formula>, is defined as <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${{ ABC}}\Pi (G)=\prod _{uv\in E(G)}\sqrt {\frac {d(u)+d(v)-2}{d(u)d(v)}}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>. We have shown that the complete graph <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$K_{n}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> has a minimum ABC<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Pi $ </tex-math></inline-formula> index among connected simple graphs with <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$n$ </tex-math></inline-formula> vertices, while the star graph <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$S_{n-1}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> has the maximum ABC<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Pi $ </tex-math></inline-formula> index. As <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$S_{n-1}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> attains the maximum amongst bipartite graphs on <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$n$ </tex-math></inline-formula> vertices, we additionally show that the bipartite complete balanced graph <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$K_{\left \lfloor{ n/2 }\right \rfloor, \left \lceil{ n/2 }\right \rceil }$ </tex-math></inline-formula> attains the minimum in this class of graphs. As an interesting problem, we propose to characterize the trees with the minimum value of this index, and, here, we have some structural properties of these trees. We conclude this paper with few conjectures for possible further work.
