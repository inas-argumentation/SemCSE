M ay 2 01 9 Global existence and uniqueness of weak solutions to a generalized Camassa-Holm equation
In this paper, we prove the existence and uniqueness of global weak solutions, which satisfy the balance law in a weak sense, to a generalized Camassa-Holm equation. Due to the effects of forcing terms, the solution loses the conservation of H-energy. By introducing some new variables, the equation is transformed into two different semi-linear systems. Then the existence and uniqueness of global weak solutions to the original equation are obtained from that of the two semi-linear systems, respectively.
