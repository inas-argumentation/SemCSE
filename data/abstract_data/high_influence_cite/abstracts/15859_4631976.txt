The Variational Calculus on Time Scales
Abstract. The discrete, the quantum, and the continuous calculus of va riations, have been recently unified and extended by using the theory of time scales. Such unification and exten sion is, however, not unique, and two approaches are followed in the literature: one dealing with minimization o f delta integrals; the other dealing with minimization of na bla integrals. Here we review a more general approach to the calc ulus of variations on time scales that allows to obtain both delta and nabla results as particular cases.
