Polymorphous low-grade adenocarcinoma of submandibular gland origin
Received 14 July 2005; accepted 14 July 2005Summary Polymorphous low-grade adenocarcinoma (PLGA) occurs almostexclusively in minor salivary glands, and there are only a few reports of majorsalivary gland origin. We herein report a case of de novo PLGA arising in asubmandibular gland and discuss preoperative ﬁndings of magnetic resonanceimaging.
