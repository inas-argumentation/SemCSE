CSF YKL‐40 and GAP‐43 are related to suicidal ideation in older women
To investigate possible relationships between suicidal ideation and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) levels of glial markers YKL‐40 (also known as chitinase‐3‐like protein 1), growth‐associated protein‐43 (GAP‐43) and myelin basic protein (MBP).
