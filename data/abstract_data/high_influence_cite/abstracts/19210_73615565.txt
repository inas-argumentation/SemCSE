Orbital stability of standing waves for a system of nonlinear Schrödinger equations with three wave interaction
Abstract We study the existence and stability of standing waves solutions of a three-coupled nonlinear Schrodinger system related to the Raman amplification in a plasma. By means of the concentration-compactness method, we provide a characterization of the standing waves solutions as minimizers of an energy functional subject to three independent L 2 mass constraints. As a consequence, we establish existence and orbital stability of solitary waves.
