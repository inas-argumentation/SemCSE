A Renormalizable Landscape of Sixteen Supercharges: Electric-Magnetic Duality, Matrices, & Emergent Spacetime
We explain how certain well-known properties of perturbatively renormalizable superstring vacua, such as the 4d electric-magnetic duality symmetries and the disconnectedness of the 4d moduli spaces, can arise from the different multiple-scaled large N limits of a single matrix Lagrangian. We address the physical implications of the disconnectedness of the CHL moduli spaces, and the significance of the matrix re-unification. We assess the role of the electric-magnetic-dual global symmetry algebra within the framework of our proposal for non-perturbative String/M theory, raising the question of whether our goal should be a duality covariant, or merely a duality invariant, nonperturbative formulation.
