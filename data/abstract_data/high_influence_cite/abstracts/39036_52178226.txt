Neuromodulation of hippocampal long-term synaptic plasticity
Highlights • Acetylcholine, noradrenaline, dopamine and serotonin all facilitate long-term synaptic plasticity.• Neuromodulators facilitate long-term synaptic plasticity by common and divergent mechanisms.• Common mechanisms include NMDA receptor facilitation by potassium channel inhibition, gliotransmission and disinhibition.• Divergent mechanisms include diversity of disinhibition and temporal and spatial neuromodulator release.
