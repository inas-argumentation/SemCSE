Thermal quantum fields with spatially cutoff interactions in 1+1 space–time dimensions
Abstract We construct interacting quantum fields in 1+1 space–time dimensions, representing charged or neutral scalar bosons at positive temperature and zero chemical potential. Our work is based on prior work by Klein and Landau and Hoegh-Krohn. Generalized path space methods are used to add a spatially cutoff interaction to the free system, which is described in the Araki–Woods representation. It is shown that the interacting KMS state is normal w.r.t. the Araki–Woods representation. The observable algebra and the modular conjugation of the interacting system are shown to be identical to the ones of the free system and the interacting Liouvillean is described in terms of the free Liouvillean and the interaction.
