Local gravitational solutions dual to M2-branes intersecting and/or ending on M5-branes
A bstractWe construct local solutions to eleven-dimensional supergravity describing M2-branes intersecting and/or ending on M5-branes, in the near-horizon limit of the M2-branes. Global solutions describing these theories should have 16 supercharges and asymptotic AdS4 × S7 regions, as well as M5-branes wrapping AdS3 × S3 subspaces. We construct the local solution describing a stack of an arbitrary number of M5-branes with AdS3 × S3 worldvolume and arbitrary M2-brane charge. Our construction provides a mechanism to get around the no-go theorem that rules out the existence of global solutions preserving 16 supercharges interpolating between AdS7 × S4 and AdS4 × S7 regions.
