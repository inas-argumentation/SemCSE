Comparison of End Tidal Carbon Dioxide and Arterial Blood Bicarbonate Levels in Patients With Exacerbation Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease
Results: Fifty-seven patients were enrolled; 35 patients (62.5 %) were male. The mean of ETCO2 and PaCO 2 of patients were 41.49 ± 9.80 and 64.74 ± 9.76, respectively. The mean of PaCO 2 was significantly more than the means of ETCO2 in patients with COPD. The association between arterial bicarbonate and ETCO2 was weak and not significant (P = 0.136 and r = 0.04). Conclusions: Capnography can be used to assess the status of patients with respiratory acidosis in a noninvasive way.
