Injection Laryngoplasty: Techniques and Choices of Fillers
Since it was first introduced in 1911, injection laryngoplasty has evolved into a versatile technique for the treatment of dysphonia in patients with a glottic insufficiency. Currently, there are a number of materials available for injection augmentation with duration of efficacy that may last from weeks to years. Furthermore, the various approaches available for injection have provided for injection laryngoplasty to be performed accurately in both the office and operating room.
