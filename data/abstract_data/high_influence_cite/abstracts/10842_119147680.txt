Surgery formulae for the Seiberg–Witten invariant of plumbed 3-manifolds
Assume that $$M({{\mathcal {T}}})$$ M ( T ) is a rational homology sphere plumbed 3-manifold associated with a connected negative definite graph $$\mathcal {T}$$ T . We consider the combinatorial multivariable Poincaré series associated with $$\mathcal {T}$$ T and its counting functions, which encode rich topological information. Using the ‘periodic constant’ of the series (with reduced variables associated with an arbitrary subset $${{\mathcal {I}}}$$ I of the set of vertices) we prove surgery formulae for the normalized Seiberg–Witten invariants: the periodic constant associated with $${{\mathcal {I}}}$$ I appears as the difference of the Seiberg–Witten invariants of $$M({{\mathcal {T}}})$$ M ( T ) and $$M({{\mathcal {T}}}{\setminus }{{\mathcal {I}}})$$ M ( T \ I ) for any $${{\mathcal {I}}}$$ I .
