Simplified weak Galerkin and new finite difference schemes for the Stokes equation
This article presents a new finite difference scheme for the Stokes equation. This new finite difference method is shown to be equivalent to a simplified version of the weak Galerkin finite element scheme on rectangular partitions. Therefore, the convergence theory developed for the weak Galerkin methods is applicable to the finite difference scheme. The weak Galerkin formulation, in its simplified form, can be employed to provide a generalization of the finite difference scheme to irregular meshes. This new method preserves the important mass conservation property locally on each element. It is shown that the velocity approximation converges with the optimal order in the L2 norm. A superconvergence of order 2 is established for the velocity approximation in the usual H1 norm, so is true for the numerical pressure in the L2 norm. Some numerical results are reported to confirm the convergence theory.
