On multiparameter weighted ergodic theorem for noncommutative Lp-spaces
In the paper we consider T 1 , … , T d absolute contractions of von Neumann algebra M with normal, semifinite, faithful trace, and prove that for every bounded Besicovitch weight { a ( k ) } k ∈ N d and every x ∈ L p ( M ) ( p > 1 ) the averages A N ( x ) = 1 | N | ∑ k = 1 N a ( k ) T k ( x ) converge bilaterally almost uniformly in L p ( M ) .
