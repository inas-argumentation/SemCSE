Sequence of same‐day upper and lower gastrointestinal endoscopy does not affect total procedure' time or medication use: A randomized trial
Same‐day double upper and lower gastrointestinal endoscopy is frequently performed due to overlapping indications. However, it is unclear whether an upper–lower (U‐L) or lower–upper (L‐U) sequence is optimal. We analyzed the effect of sequence on total procedure time and sedation use.
