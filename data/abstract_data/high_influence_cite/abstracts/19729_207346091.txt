Psychoeducational interventions in adolescent depression: A systematic review
Highlights • Guidelines stress need for good information and psychosocial interventions.• Depression PIs include family/group, individual, school and online programmes.• PIs may affect: understanding, identification, family communication, mental health.• Limitations: only a few PIs evaluated, heterogeneity, inconsistent definition of PI.• Future work: develop/evaluate PIs in line with frameworks for complex interventions.
