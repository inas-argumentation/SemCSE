A class of differential inverse quasi-variational inequalities in finite dimensional spaces
In this paper, we introduce and study a class of differential inverse quasi-variational inequalities in finite dimensional Euclidean spaces, which are closely related to the differential variational inequalities. By using two important theorems on differential inclusions, we first prove some existence theorems for Carathéodory weak solutions of the differential inverse quasivariational inequality considered.
