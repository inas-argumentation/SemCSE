The homology groups of certain moduli spaces of plane sheaves
Using the Bialynicki-Birula method, we determine the additive structure of the integral homology groups of the moduli spaces of semi-stable sheaves on the projective plane having rank and Chern classes (5, 1, 4), (7, 2, 6), respectively, (0, 5, 19). We compute the Hodge numbers of these moduli spaces.
