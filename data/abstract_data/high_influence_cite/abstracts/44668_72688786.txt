Patency of the infarct-related artery and time-dependant infarct transmurality on cardiovascular magnetic resonance in patients with ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction treated by primary percutaneous intervention
Background 
Outcome in ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) is affected by patency of the infarct-related artery (IRA) on the initial angiogram. Therefore we decided to assess the relation between patent IRA and time-dependent infarct transmurality.
