Alternating bursts of low energy ions and electrons near the substorm onset
The substorm associated behavior of the low en- ergy particles (30 eV-28.5 keV) near the earthward edge of the plasma sheet is examined using data from CRRES during the late growth and early expansion phases of a substorm on 12 March 1991 and their significance for the substorm on- set mechanism is discussed. In this substorm, the CRRES was located on L 6.3 and 20 westward of the substorm onset and observed the sequence of the alternating bursts of the low energy ions and electrons. The bursts of the 0.633- 9.6 keV ions occurred 1-2 min before the (7.31-21.7 keV) electron bursts. The first ion burst happened 2 min before the substorm onset, at the moment of weak brightening of the most equatorial pre-breakup arc near the latitude 62 .
