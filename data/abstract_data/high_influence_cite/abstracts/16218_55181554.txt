Frobenius splitting in characteristic zero and the quantum Frobenius map
The aim of the article is to show that Lusztig’s Frobenius map (for quantum groups at roots of unity) can be, after dualizing, viewed as a characteristic zero lift of the geometric Frobenius splitting of G/B (in char p ? 0) introduced by Mehta and Ramanathan. c © 2000 Elsevier Science B.V.
