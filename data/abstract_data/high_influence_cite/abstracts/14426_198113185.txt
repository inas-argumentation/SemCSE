J ul 2 01 9 Clique immersions and independence number
The analogue of Hadwiger’s conjecture for the immersion order states that every graph G contains Kχ(G) as an immersion. If true, it would imply that every graph with n vertices and independence number α contains K⌈n α ⌉ as an immersion. The best currently known bound for this conjecture is due to Gauthier, Le and Wollan, who recently proved that every graph G contains an immersion of a clique on ⌈χ(G)−4 3.54 ⌉ vertices. Their result implies that every n-vertex graph with independence number α contains an immersion of a clique on ⌈ n 3.54α − 1.13 ⌉ vertices. We improve on this result for all α ≥ 3, by showing that every n-vertex graph with independence number α ≥ 3 contains an immersion of a clique on ⌊ 4n 9(α−1) ⌋ −⌊α2 ⌋ vertices.
