Assessment of musculoskeletal symptoms in academics in full‑time and part-time courses
Introduction: Musculoskeletal symptoms are affections, with or without tissue degeneration, that may present itself in many forms and be presented together with other multiple symptoms. Until the present moment its etiology is multifactorial and its pathological picture presents a great social relevance due to its coverage and magnitude. Objective: The objective of this study is to evaluate and compare the prevalence of musculoskeletal symptoms in academics of full time (daytime) and part-time (nighttime) courses of UNICENTRO. Methods: 120 students of both courses participate in this study and in order to select the individuals there was utilize the method of proportional stratified sampling. Standardized  Nordic questionnaires  for the  analysis  of  musculoskeletal symptoms and the Visual Analogic Scale (VAS) as a way of pain identification were used. Results: Through observation of the results obtained by the questionnaires it was noticed a high prevalence of the symptoms in the analyzed academics, being 49% from Biological Science course and 44.8% from Physiotherapy course, emphasizing the vertebral column, being lumbar (9.42%) in physiotherapy academics and thoracic region (9.14%) in Biological Science academics, it was also noticed a high involvement in multiple regions (53.9%). Besides that, it was observed trough VAS the presence of mild pain (3,78 ± 2,38). Conclusion: It has been concluded that the most involved region was vertebral column, specifically the lumbar and thoracic regions. In this way, assumed that a biomechanical overload is responsible for this finding. Thereby, preventive and ergonomic measures and health promotion must be stimulated between young students, however, for such a propose new studies are necessary.
