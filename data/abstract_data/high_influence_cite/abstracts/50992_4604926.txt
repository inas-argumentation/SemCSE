Aβ-oligomer uptake and the resulting inflammatory response in adult human astrocytes are precluded by an anti-Aβ single chain variable fragment in combination with an apoE mimetic peptide
An imbalance between production and clearance of soluble amyloid‐&bgr; (A&bgr;) initiates the pathological process in sporadic Alzheimer's disease (AD). A&bgr;‐specific antibodies seemed promising as therapeutic option in AD mouse models. In patients, however, vascular side‐effects and A&bgr;‐antibody complex‐induced microglial and/or perivascular macrophage inflammatory responses were encountered. To prevent inflammatory reactions, we designed a single chain variable fragment (scFv‐h3D6), based on monoclonal antibody bapineuzumab (mAb‐h3D6), but lacking the Fc region. ScFv‐h3D6 reduced A&bgr;‐oligomer burden and prevented AD‐associated behavioral and cellular changes in 3xTg‐AD mice. As scFv‐h3D6 lacks the Fc‐tail, it cannot enhance Fc‐receptor mediated A&bgr; clearance by microglia and probably exerts its beneficial effects in 3xTg‐AD mice through other mechanisms. ScFv‐h3D6 restored the increased apoE and apoJ levels in 3xTg‐AD brains back to normal. ApoE and apoJ influence cholesterol transport, A&bgr; aggregation and clearance, and their genetic variants are risk factors for sporadic AD. Astrocytes are constitutive scavengers of soluble A&bgr; from the CNS. We previously found apoE and apoJ to inhibit A&bgr; uptake by adult human astrocytes, in vitro, and thus to potentially protect astrocytes from A&bgr; cytotoxicity. In the present study, scFv‐h3D6 and mAb‐h3D6 inhibited A&bgr;‐oligomer uptake by adult human astrocytes. ApoE‐ and apoJ‐ mimetic peptides (MP) affected A&bgr; uptake as well as A&bgr;‐induced cytokine release similar to intact apoE and apoJ, without interfering with the strong inhibitory effects of scFv‐h3D6 on A&bgr;‐oligomer uptake. These results suggest that combining A&bgr;‐specific scFv and apoE‐MP, that inhibits A&bgr; oligomer‐induced cytokine release by astrocytes, could offer advantages over currently used therapeutics. Graphical abstract Proposed mechanism of action of apoE and apoJ mimetic peptides (MP) and a single chain variable fragment of a humanized monoclonal antibody that specifically detects Amyloid‐&bgr; (A&bgr;), scFv‐h3D6, on A&bgr; clearance and A&bgr;‐induced inflammation in adult human primary astrocytes. A&bgr; oligomers induce the release of proinflammatory cytokines IL‐6 and MCP‐1, which is reduced in the presence of apoE‐MP in vitro. At the same time, A&bgr; oligomer uptake is reduced by apoE‐MP, as well as apoJ‐MP and, more potently, scFv‐h3D6. Because apo‐MP do not interfere with the inhibitory effect of scFv‐h3D6 on uptake, a combination of the anti‐inflammatory apoE‐MP and scFv‐h3D6 merits further investigation for therapeutic use.
