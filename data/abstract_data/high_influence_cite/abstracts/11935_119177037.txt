Distributive lattices have the intersection property
Distributive lattices form an important, well-behaved class of lattices. They are instances of two larger classes of lattices: congruence-uniform and semidistributive lattices. Congruence-uniform lattices allow for a remarkable second, alternate order of their elements; semidistributive lattices naturally possess an associated flag simplicial complex, whose faces correspond to the canonical forms of this lattice. In this article we present a characterization of finite distributive lattices in terms of the alternate order and the canonical complex, and we show that the alternate order of a finite distributive lattice is always a meet-semilattice.
