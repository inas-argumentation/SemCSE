Existence and multiplicity for elliptic p-Laplacian problems with critical growth in the gradient
We consider the boundary value problem where $$\Omega \subset \mathbb {R}^{N}$$Ω⊂RN, $$N \ge 2$$N≥2, is a bounded domain with smooth boundary. We assume $$c,\, h \in L^q(\Omega )$$c,h∈Lq(Ω) for some $$q > \max \{N/p,1\}$$q>max{N/p,1} with $$c \gneqq 0$$c≩0 and $$\mu \in L^{\infty }(\Omega )$$μ∈L∞(Ω). We prove existence and uniqueness results in the coercive case $$ \lambda \le 0$$λ≤0 and existence and multiplicity results in the non-coercive case $$ \lambda >0$$λ>0. Also, considering stronger assumptions on the coefficients, we clarify the structure of the set of solutions in the non-coercive case.
