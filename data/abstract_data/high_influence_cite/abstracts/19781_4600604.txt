Growth, nodule morphology, and nitrogenase activity of Myrica gale with roots grown at various oxygen levels
Myrica gale L. plants were inoculated with Frankia strain HFP M+g15 and grown for 28 days with root systems exposed to 2, 5, 10, 21, and 40 kPa O2. Plant growth was similar under all treatments, except for a small decrease in final size of plants at 2 kPa O2. At the end of the experiment nitrogenase activity of representative plants was analyzed over a variety of Po2 levels using an open-flow cuvette. Optimum nitrogenase activity was found at Po2 levels close to the growth Po2 in all cases and plants showed no short-term adaptation to oxygen. Specific activity of maximum nitrogenase was similar for all treatments, being within a factor of two. Nitrogenase activity showed rapid transient responses to step shifts in Po2 during assay and irreversible decline in activity at Po2 levels above optimum.
