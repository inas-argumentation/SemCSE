Embedded desingularization for arithmetic surfaces - toward a parallel implementation
We present an approach for algorithmic embedded desingularization of arithmetic surfaces bearing in mind implementability. Our algorithm is based on work by Cossart-Jannsen-Saito, though we present a variant using a refinement of the order instead of the Hilbert-Samuel function as a measure for the complexity of the singularity. We particularly focus on aspects arising when working in mixed characteristic. The essential building block is the computation of the locus of maximal order in the arithmetic setting. Furthermore, we exploit the algorithm's natural parallel structure rephrasing it in terms of Petri nets for use in the parallelization environment GPI-space with SINGULAR as computational back-end.
