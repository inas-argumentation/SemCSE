Cameron–Liebler sets of k-spaces in $${{\mathrm{PG}}}(n,q)$$PG(n,q)
Cameron–Liebler sets of k-spaces were introduced recently in Filmus and Ihringer (J Combin Theory Ser A, 2019). We list several equivalent definitions for these Cameron–Liebler sets, by making a generalization of known results about Cameron–Liebler line sets in $${{\mathrm{PG}}}(n,q)$$PG(n,q) and Cameron–Liebler sets of k-spaces in $${{\mathrm{PG}}}(2k+1,q)$$PG(2k+1,q). We also present some classification results.
