On the Green Function and Poisson Integrals of the Dunkl Laplacian
We prove the existence and study properties of the Green function of the unit ball for the Dunkl Laplacian △k in ℝd$\mathbb {R}^{d}$. As applications we derive the Poisson-Jensen formula for △k-subharmonic functions and Hardy-Stein identities for the Poisson integrals of △k. We also obtain sharp estimates of the Newton potential kernel, Green function and Poisson kernel in the rank one case in ℝd$\mathbb {R}^{d}$. These estimates contrast sharply with the well-known results in the potential theory of the classical Laplacian.
