Topos quantum theory reduced by context-selection functors
In this paper we deal with quantum theories on presheaves and sheaves on context categories consisting of commutative von Neumann algebras of bounded operators on a Hilbert space. Our aim is first to reduce presheaf-based topos quantum theory via sheafification and then to import quantum probabilities to the reduced sheaf quantum theory. The first is done by means of a functor that selects some expedient contexts. Note that since the functor defines a Grothendieck topology on the category consisting of all contexts, it induces a sheaf topos on which we construct a downsized quantum theory. We also show that the sheaf quantum theory can be replaced by a more manageable presheaf quantum theory. Quantum probabilities are imported by means of a Grothendieck topology that is defined on a category consisting of probabilities and that enables to regard them as intuitionistic truth-values. From these topologies, we construct another Grothendieck topology that is defined on the product of the context category and the probability category. It reflects the selection of contexts and the identification of probabilities with truth-values.
