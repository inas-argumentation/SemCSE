On primitive elements of algebraic function fields and models of $$X_0(N)$$
For a Fuchsian group of first kind $\Gamma$ we look at non-zero linear subspace $W$ of $M_m(\Gamma)$, $m\ge 2$, which satisfies certain properties and prove the existence of plane models for $\mathfrak{R}_\Gamma$ of degree $\dim M_m(\Gamma)+g(\Gamma)-1$. We discuss finding primitive elements which are linear combinations of generators in algebraic function fields generated by quotients of forms in $W$. We apply this to the subgroup $\Gamma_0(N)$ and describe two procedures of finding models for $X_0(N)$ and $X_1(N)$ using the bases of spaces of cuspidal forms of different weight.
