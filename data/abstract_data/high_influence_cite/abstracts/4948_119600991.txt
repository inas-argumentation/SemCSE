Asymptotic Behavior of Weighted Power Variations of Fractional Brownian Motion in Brownian Time
We study the asymptotic behavior of weighted power variations of fractional Brownian motion in Brownian time $$Z_t:= X_{Y_t},t \geqslant 0$$Zt:=XYt,t⩾0, where X is a fractional Brownian motion and Y is an independent Brownian motion.
