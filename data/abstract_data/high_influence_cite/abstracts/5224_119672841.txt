The Extensions of $${L_{sl_{2}}(k, 0)}$$Lsl2(k,0) and Preunitary Vertex Operator Algebras with Central Charges c < 1
The extensions of the affine vertex operator algebras $${L_{sl_{2}}(k, 0)}$$Lsl2(k,0) and the preunitary vertex operator algebras with central charges c < 1 are classified. In particular, the unitary vertex operator algebras with central charges c < 1 are classified.
