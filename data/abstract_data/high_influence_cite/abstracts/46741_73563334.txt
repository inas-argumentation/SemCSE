Feasibility Studies on Compressive Strength of Ground Coal Ash Geopolymer Mortar
Coal ash obtained from the Thermal Power plants consists of a major portion of Flyash and less amount of Bottom ash. As a major portion of the coal ash, fly ash has been recognized widely as a source material for geopolymers while the utilization of bottom ash has received very less attention owing to its coarser size.Hence to promote greater utilisation of bottom ash in construction sector, this work proposes the use of ground bottom ash in geopolymer mortar. The effect of molar ratio of SiO2 /Na2 O, Na2 SiO3 /NaOH ratio and curing mode were studied on compressive strength of coal ash geopolymer mortar. Molarity of sodium silicate solution was kept as 8M. Ambient curing and steam curing at 60oC was attempted. Test results indicate that Na2 SiO3 /NaOH of ratio 2 with SiO2 /Na2 O molar ratio 1 mortar achieved higher compressive strength under both ambient curing and steam curing.
