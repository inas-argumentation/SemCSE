A P ] 6 D ec 2 00 7 The Fundamental k-form and Global Relations
In [5] AS Fokas introduced a novel method for solving a large class of boundary value problems associated with evolution equations. This approach relies on the construction of a so-called global relation: an integral expression that couples initial and boundary data. The global relation can be found by constructing a differential form dependent on some spectral parameter, that is closed on the condition that a given partial differential equation is satisfied. Such a differential form is said to be fundamental [7]. We give an algorithmic approach in constructing a fundamental k-form associated with a given boundary value problem, and address issues of uniqueness. Also, we extend a result of Fokas & Zyskin to give an integral representation to the solution of a class of boundary value problems, in an arbitrary number of dimensions.
