Oriented Shadowing Property and $$\Omega $$Ω-Stability for Vector Fields
We call that a vector field has the oriented shadowing property if for any $$\varepsilon >0$$ε>0 there is $$d>0$$d>0 such that each $$d$$d-pseudo orbit is $$\varepsilon $$ε-oriented shadowed by some real orbit.
