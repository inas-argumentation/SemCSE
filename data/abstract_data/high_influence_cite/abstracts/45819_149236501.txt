RELATIONSHIP AMONG LOWER EXTREMITY BIOMECHANICAL ALIGNMENT AND BALANCE ABILITIES IN COMMUNITY DWELLING OLDER ADULTS
Background: Balance deficit is one of the leading factors resulting in falls. Relative contribution of sensorimotor factors to balance abilities is well described in the literature. However, the relationship of lower extremity biomechanical alignment with balance abilities in older adults is still unexplored. The cross-sectional study was aimed to examine how changes in lower extremity biomechanical alignment were related to balance abilities in community dwelling older adults.
