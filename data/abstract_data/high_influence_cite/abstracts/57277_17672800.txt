2 3 M ar 2 00 5 Resolutions for Equivariant Sheaves over Toric Varieties
In this work we construct global resolutions for general coherent equivariant sheaves over toric varieties. For this, we use the framework of sheaves over posets. We develop a notion of gluing of posets and of sheaves over posets, which we apply to construct global resolutions for equivariant sheaves.
