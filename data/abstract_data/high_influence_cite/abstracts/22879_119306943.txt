Branching within branching II: Limit theorems
This continues work started in (4) on a general branching-within-branching model for host-parasite co-evolution. Here we focus on asymptotic results for rele- vant processes in the case when parasites survive. In particular, limit theorems for the processes of contaminated cells and of parasites are established by using martin- gale theory and the technique of size-biasing. The results for both processes are of Kesten-Stigum type by including equivalent integrability conditions for the martin- gale limits to be positive with positive probability. The case when these conditions fail is also studied. For the process of contaminated cells, we show that a proper Heyde-Seneta norming exists such that the limit is nondegenerate.
