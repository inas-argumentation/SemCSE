Fast implementation of the delta generalized multi-Bernoulli filter via Gibbs sampler
This paper proposes a new implementation scheme for the delta generalized multi-Bernoulli (δ-GLMB) filter via joint prediction and update. In contrast to the original approach which requires different cost matrices for prediction and update, the joint strategy involves only one single cost matrix and hence alleviates the need for inefficient intermediate truncation procedures. Furthermore, a randomized truncation procedure based on the celebrated Gibbs sampler has been developed to replace the computationally expensive ranked assignment algorithm. The superior performance of the proposed approach is demonstrated through extensive numerical studies.
