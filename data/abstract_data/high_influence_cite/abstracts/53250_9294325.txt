EVALUATION OF DRUG PRESCRIPTION PATTERN AMONG GENERAL DENTAL PRACTITIONERS IN RASHT, IRAN
Materials and Methods: This cross-sectional retrospective study; reviewing a convenience sample of 850 dental prescriptions selected randomly from social insurance prescriptions; assessed for two parameters: The first for WHO drug indicators such as the mean number of drugs per prescription, the percentage of durgs with generic name, the percentage of prescription with antibiotics and injectives, and the second for the prescription errors. Chi-sqaure test was used for analyzing the data.
