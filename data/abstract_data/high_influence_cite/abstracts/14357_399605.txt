L-visibility drawings of IC-planar graphs
An IC-plane graph is a topological graph where every edge is crossed at most once and no two crossed edges share a vertex. We show that every IC-plane graph has a visibility drawing where every vertex is of the form { ? , ? , ? , ? } , and every edge is either a horizontal or vertical segment. As a byproduct of our drawing technique, we prove that every IC-plane graph has a RAC drawing in quadratic area with at most two bends per edge. We study visibility representations of IC-planar graphs.We show that every IC-planar graph has an L-visibility drawing in quadratic area which can be computed in linear time.We prove that every IC-planar graph has a RAC drawing in quadratic area with at most two bends per edge.
