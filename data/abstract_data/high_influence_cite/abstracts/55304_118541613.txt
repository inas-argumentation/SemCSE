Electroweak and supersymmetry breaking from the Higgs boson discovery
We will explore the consequences on the electroweak breaking condition, the mass of supersymmetric partners and the scale at which supersymmetry breaking is transmit- ted, for arbitrary values of the supersymmetric parameters tanand the stop mixing Xt, which follow from the Higgs discovery with a mass mH ≃ 126 GeV at the LHC.
