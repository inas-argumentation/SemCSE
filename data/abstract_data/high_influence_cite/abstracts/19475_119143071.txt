Eigenvalues of the fractional Laplace operator in the interval
Two-term Weyl-type asymptotic law for the eigenvalues of the one-dimensional fractional Laplace operator ( − Δ ) α / 2 ( α ∈ ( 0 , 2 ) ) in the interval ( − 1 , 1 ) is given: the n -th eigenvalue is equal to ( n π / 2 − ( 2 − α ) π / 8 ) α + O ( 1 / n ) . Simplicity of eigenvalues is proved for α ∈ [ 1 , 2 ) . L 2 and L ∞ properties of eigenfunctions are studied. We also give precise numerical bounds for the first few eigenvalues.
