Regularity of weak solutions to the Monge–Ampère equation
We study the properties of generalized solutions to the Monge-Ampere equation det D 2 u = v, where the Borel measure v satisfies a condition, introduced by Jerison, that is weaker than the doubling property. When v = f dx, this condition, which we call D e , admits the possibility of f vanishing or becoming infinite. Our analysis extends the regularity theory (due to Caffarelli) available when 0 < A < f < A < ∞, which implies that v = f dx is doubling. The main difference between the D e case and the case when f is bounded between two positive constants is the need to use a variant of the Aleksandrov maximum principle (due to Jerison) and some tools from convex geometry, in particular the Hausdorff metric.
