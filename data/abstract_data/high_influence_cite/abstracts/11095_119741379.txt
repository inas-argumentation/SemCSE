On the continuity of solutions to doubly singular parabolic equations
This paper considers certain quasilinear parabolic equations with one singularity which occurs in the time derivative. A model equation is $$\begin{aligned} \partial _t\beta (u)-{\text {div}}|Du|^{p-2}Du\ni 0, \end{aligned}$$ ∂ t β ( u ) - div | D u | p - 2 D u ∋ 0 , where $$\beta :{\mathbb {R}}\rightarrow {\mathbb {R}}$$ β : R → R is monotonically increasing and discontinuous at the origin. We show that bounded weak solutions are locally continuous in the range $$\begin{aligned} 2-\varepsilon _0\le p<2, \end{aligned}$$ 2 - ε 0 ≤ p < 2 , provided that $$\varepsilon _0>0$$ ε 0 > 0 is sufficiently small, and the continuity is stable as $$p\rightarrow 2$$ p → 2 .
