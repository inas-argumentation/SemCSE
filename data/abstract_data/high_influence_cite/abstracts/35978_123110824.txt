Additive results for the generalized Drazin inverse in a Banach algebra
Abstract In this paper we investigate additive properties of the generalized Drazin inverse in a Banach algebra. We find some new conditions under which the generalized Drazin inverse of the sum a  +  b could be explicitly expressed in terms of a , a d , b , b d . Also, some recent results of Castro and Koliha [New additive results for the g -Drazin inverse, Proc. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh Sect. A 134 (2004) 1085–1097] are extended.
