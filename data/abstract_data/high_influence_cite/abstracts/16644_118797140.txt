Riesz Transforms Characterizations of Hardy Spaces $$H^1$$H1 for the Rational Dunkl Setting and Multidimensional Bessel Operators
We characterize the Hardy space $$H^1$$H1 in the rational Dunkl setting associated with the reflection group $$\mathbb {Z}_2^n$$Z2n by means of special Riesz transforms. As a corollary we obtain Riesz transforms characterization of $$H^1$$H1 for product of Bessel operators in $$(0,\infty )^n$$(0,∞)n.
