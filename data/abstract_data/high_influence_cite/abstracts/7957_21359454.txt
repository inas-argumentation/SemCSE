Risk of rupture of unruptured cerebral aneurysms in elderly patients
Hishikawa et al.1 compiled 3 follow-up studies to explore risk factors for rupture of unruptured cerebral aneurysm (UCA) in elderly patients. I have some concerns regarding the study.

First, the authors used the Cox model to conduct risk assessment for UCA rupture. As the number for subarachnoid hemorrhage was 68, unstable estimates could be partly observed in a wide range of 95% confidence intervals in hazard ratio (HR), such as larger aneurysm size.
