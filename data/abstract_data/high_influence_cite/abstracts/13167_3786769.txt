LDPC Codes Based on the Space of Symmetric Matrices Over Finite Fields
In this paper, we present a new method for explicitly constructing regular low-density parity-check (LDPC) codes based on <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$ {\mathbb {S}}_{n}( \mathbb {F}_{q})$ </tex-math></inline-formula>, the space of <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$n\times n$ </tex-math></inline-formula> symmetric matrices over <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$ \mathbb {F}_{q}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>. Using this method, we obtain two classes of binary LDPC codes, <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$ {\mathcal{ C}}(n,q)$ </tex-math></inline-formula> and <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$ {\mathcal{ C}}^{T}(n,q)$ </tex-math></inline-formula>, both of which have grith 8. Then, both the minimum distance and the stopping distance of each class are investigated. It is shown that the minimum distance and the stopping distance of <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$ {\mathcal{ C}}^{T}(n,q)$ </tex-math></inline-formula> are both <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$2q$ </tex-math></inline-formula>. As for <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$ {\mathcal{ C}}(n,q)$ </tex-math></inline-formula>, we determine the minimum distance and the stopping distance for some special cases and obtain some lower bounds for other cases.
