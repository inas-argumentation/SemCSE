Individual tax rates and regional tax revenues: a cross-state analysis
ABSTRACT Individual tax rates and regional tax revenues: a cross-state analysis. Regional Studies. This paper analyzes the effects of state-level personal tax rates on state tax revenue and individual welfare. The policy analysis based on a general equilibrium model suggests that tax revenues would benefit from higher wage–income, sales or property taxes, while any increase in dividend–income tax would result in a reduction of revenues. It is also shown that individuals would suffer from an increase in state-level wage–income tax, dividend tax or sales tax, while they would benefit from an increase in property taxes. The heterogeneity across states is determined by a TaxIndex, a weighted average of initial taxes at the state level.
