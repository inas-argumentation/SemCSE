On Critical Independent Sets of a Graph and Structure of Unicyclic Non-König-Egerváry Graphs
In this paper we solve a conjecture regarding the minimum number of inclusion minimal independent sets with positive difference and give a short proof of |Ker| + |Diadem| $\le$ 2{\alpha}. A characterization of unicyclic non-KE graphs is presented which gives a simple proof of a conjecture (by Vadim E. Levit) regarding critical difference of such graphs. We also make an observation about Ker using Edmonds-Gallai Structure Theorem.
