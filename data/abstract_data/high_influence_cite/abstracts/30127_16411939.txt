Expansion of random graphs: new proofs, new results
We present a new approach to showing that random graphs are nearly optimal expanders. This approach is based on recent deep results in combinatorial group theory. It applies to both regular and irregular random graphs. Let $$\Gamma $$Γ be a random $$d$$d-regular graph on $$n$$n vertices, and let $$\lambda $$λ be the largest absolute value of a non-trivial eigenvalue of its adjacency matrix. It was conjectured by Alon (Combinatorica 6(2), 83–96, 1986) that a random $$d$$d-regular graph is “almost Ramanujan”, in the following sense: for every $$\varepsilon >0$$ε>0, $$\lambda <2\sqrt{d-1}+\varepsilon $$λ<2d-1+ε asymptotically almost surely. Friedman famously presented a proof of this conjecture in Friedman (Memoirs of the AMS 910, 2008). Here we suggest a new, substantially simpler proof of a nearly-optimal result: we show that a random $$d$$d-regular graph satisfies $$\lambda <2\sqrt{d-1}+1$$λ<2d-1+1 a.a.s. A main advantage of our approach is that it is applicable to a generalized conjecture: For $$d$$d even, a $$d$$d-regular graph on $$n$$n vertices is an $$n$$n-covering space of a bouquet of $$d/2$$d/2 loops. More generally, fixing an arbitrary base graph $$\Omega $$Ω, we study the spectrum of $$\Gamma $$Γ, a random $$n$$n-covering of $$\Omega $$Ω. Let $$\lambda $$λ be the largest absolute value of a non-trivial eigenvalue of $$\Gamma $$Γ. Extending Alon’s conjecture to this more general model, Friedman (Duke Math J 118(1),19–35, 2003) conjectured that for every $$\varepsilon >0,$$ε>0, a.a.s. $$\lambda <\rho +\varepsilon $$λ<ρ+ε, where $$\rho $$ρ is the spectral radius of the universal cover of $$\Omega $$Ω. When $$\Omega $$Ω is regular we get a bound of $$\rho +0.84$$ρ+0.84, and for an arbitrary $$\Omega $$Ω, we prove a nearly optimal upper bound of $$\sqrt{3}\rho $$3ρ. This is a substantial improvement upon all known results (by Friedman, Linial-Puder, Lubetzky-Sudakov-Vu and Addario-Berry-Griffiths).
