The Penrose-Fife phase-field model with coupled dynamic boundary conditions
In this paper we derive, starting from the basic principles of ther- 
modynamics, an extended version of the nonconserved Penrose-Fife phase tran- 
sition model, in which dynamic boundary conditions are considered in order 
to take into account interactions with walls. Moreover, we study the well- 
posedness and the asymptotic behavior of the initial-boundary value problem 
for the PDE system associated to the model, allowing the phase con guration 
of the material to be described by a singular function.
