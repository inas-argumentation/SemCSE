Exponential Decay of Truncated Correlations for the Ising Model in any Dimension for all but the Critical Temperature
The truncated two-point function of the ferromagnetic Ising model on $${\mathbb {Z}}^d$$ Z d ( $$d\ge 3$$ d ≥ 3 ) in its pure phases is proven to decay exponentially fast throughout the ordered regime ( $$\beta >\beta _c$$ β > β c and $$h=0$$ h = 0 ). Together with the previously known results, this implies that the exponential clustering property holds throughout the model’s phase diagram except for the critical point: $$(\beta ,h) = (\beta _c,0)$$ ( β , h ) = ( β c , 0 ) .
