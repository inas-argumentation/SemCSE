Microbial Degradation of Organophosphorous Pesticide: Chlorpyrifos (Mini-Review)
Chlorpyrifos is one of the world's most widely used organophosphorus pesticides in agriculture. Exposure to chlorpyrifos and its metabolites have been related to a variety of nerve disorders in humans. Microbial degradation is considered to be an efficient and cost effective method for decontamination of toxic organophosphorus pesticides from the environment. Chlorpyrifos previously shown to be immune to enhanced biodegradation has now been proved to undergo enhanced microbe mediated decay into less harmful and non-toxic metabolites, under a set of favourable abiotic conditions. Recently, research activity in this area has shown that a diverse range of microorganisms is responsible for chlorpyrifos degradation. This article therefore aims at giving an overview of the present status of research in biodegradation of chlorpyrifos.
