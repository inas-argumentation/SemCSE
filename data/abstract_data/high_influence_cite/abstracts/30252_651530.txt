Cognitive control network connectivity in adolescent women with and without a parental history of depression
Highlights • Adolescent girls with a parental history of depression are at high risk for major depression.• This risk may be associated with deficits in cognitive control for emotional information.• Indeed, these girls show behavioral evidence of such deficits.• We report that they also show neural evidence of these deficits.• Specifically, connectivity deficits in a network associated with cognitive control of emotion.
