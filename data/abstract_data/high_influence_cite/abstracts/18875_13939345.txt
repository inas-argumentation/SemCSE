Analysis of simultaneous MEG and intracranial LFP recordings during Deep Brain Stimulation: a protocol and experimental validation
Highlights • Setup for MEG and intracranial recordings during Deep Brain Stimulation is described.• Phantom experiment showed correct recovery of oscillatory sources despite artefacts.• The method is applied to real data from a patient with Parkinson's Disease.• Cortico-subthalamic coherence profiles on and off stimulation were comparable.
