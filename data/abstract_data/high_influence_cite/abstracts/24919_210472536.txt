On the dimension of the Fomin-Kirillov algebra and related algebras
Let Em be the Fomin-Kirillov algebra, and let BSm be the Nichols-Woronowicz algebra model for Schubert calculus on the symmetric group Sm which is a quotient of Em, i.e. the Nichols algebra associated to a Yetter-Drinfeld Sm-module defined by the set of reflections of Sm and a specific one-dimensional representation of a subgroup of Sm. It is a famous open problem to prove that Em is infinite dimensional for all m ≥ 6. In this work, as a step towards a solution of this problem, we introduce a subalgebra of BSm , and prove, under the assumption of finite dimensionality of BSm , that this subalgebra admits unique integrals in a strong sense, and we relate these integrals to integrals in BSm . The techniques we use rely on braided differential calculus as developed in [8, 9, 28], and on the notion of integrals for Hopf algebras as introduced in [36].
