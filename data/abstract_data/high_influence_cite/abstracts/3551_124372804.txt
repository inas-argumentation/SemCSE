Distributionally chaotic families of operators on Fréchet spaces
The existence of distributional chaos and distributional irregular vectors has been recently considered in the study of linear dynamics of operators and $C_0$-semigroups. 
In this paper we extend some previous results on both notions to sequences of operators, $C_0$-semigroups, $C$-regularized semigroups, and $\alpha$-times integrated semigroups on Frechet spaces. We also add a study of rescaled distributionally chaotic $C_0$-semigroups. Some examples are provided to illustrate all these results.
