A comparison of outcomes for patients with intermediate and high risk prostate cancer treated with low dose rate and high dose rate brachytherapy in combination with external beam radiotherapy
Highlights • Superior biochemical progression free survival for LDR in combination with EBRT.• On multivariable analysis, HDR and EBRT and Gleason ≥8 predicted for progression.• Low cumulative incidence of ≥grade 3 GU and GI toxicities.
