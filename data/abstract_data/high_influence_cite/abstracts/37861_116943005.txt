Curvature Estimates for Four-Dimensional Gradient Steady Ricci Solitons
In this paper, we derive certain curvature estimates for 4-dimensional gradient steady Ricci solitons either with positive Ricci curvature or with scalar curvature decay $$\lim _{x\rightarrow \infty } R(x)=0$$ lim x → ∞ R ( x ) = 0 .
