The electrostatic properties of zeros of exceptional Laguerre and Jacobi polynomials and stable interpolation
We examine the electrostatic properties of exceptional and regular zeros of X m -Laguerre and X m -Jacobi polynomials. Since there is a close connection between the electrostatic properties of the zeros and the stability of interpolation on the system of zeros, we can deduce an Egervary-Turan type result as well. The limit of the energy on the regular zeros is also investigated.
