Some probabilistic properties of fractional point processes
Fractional Poisson processes of various forms have been introduced and studied in the last years by many researchers (for example, the time-fractional Poisson process in [7, 8, 2], the space-fractional Poisson process in [1, 12, 15]). The space-fractional Poisson process, introduced in [12] is a time-changed Poisson process N(H(t)) = N(t) where H(t) is a stable subordinator, independent from the homogeneous Poisson process N(t). In a paper appeared at the end of 2015 [13], a new class of time-changed Poisson processes N(H(t)) = N (t), including the space-fractional Poisson process as a special case, has been introduced.
