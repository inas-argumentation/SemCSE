Fermat-type configurations of lines in P3 and the containment problem
Abstract The purpose of this note is to show a new series of examples of homogeneous ideals I in K [ x , y , z , w ] for which the containment I ( 3 ) ⊂ I 2 fails. These ideals are supported on certain arrangements of lines in P 3 , which resemble Fermat configurations of points in P 2 , see [14] . All examples exhibiting the failure of the containment I ( 3 ) ⊆ I 2 constructed so far have been supported on points or cones over configurations of points. Apart from providing new counterexamples, these ideals seem quite interesting on their own.
