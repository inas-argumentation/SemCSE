The flatness of the $\Ok$-module of smooth functions and integral representation
We give a proof of the well-known fact that the $\Ok$-module $\E$ of smooth functions is flat by means of residue theory and integral formulas. A variant of the proof gives a related statement for classes of functions of lower regularity. We also prove a Briancon-Skoda type theorem for ideals of the form $\E a$, where $a$ is an ideal in $\Ok$.
