0 20 20 41 v 1 2 6 Fe b 20 02 Algebraic Quantization of the Closed Bosonic String ∗
The gauge invariant observables of the closed bosonic string are quantized without anomalies in four space-time dimensions by constructing their quantum algebra in a manifestly covariant approach. The quantum algebra is the kernel of a derivation on the universal envelopping algebra of an infinite-dimensional Lie algebra. The search for Hilbert space representations of this algebra is postponed.
