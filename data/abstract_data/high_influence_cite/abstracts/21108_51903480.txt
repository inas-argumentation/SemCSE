The Cauchy Harish-Chandra Integral and the Invariant Eigendistributions
In this paper we prove that the Cauchy Harish-Chandra integral maps invariant eigendistributions to invariant eigendistributions with the correct infinitesimal character and that it maps the orbital integrals on the larger group to the orbital integrals on the smaller one.
