Vacuum-type space-like string surfaces in AdS3 ? S3
We construct and classify all space-like minimal surfaces in AdS3 × S3 which globally admit coordinates with constant-induced metric on both factors. Up to O(2, 2) × O(4) transformations all these surfaces, except one class, are parameterized by four real parameters. The classes of surfaces correspond to different regions in this parameter space and show quite different boundary behavior. Our analysis uses a direct construction of the string coordinates via a group theoretical treatment based on the map of AdS3 × S3 to . This is complemented by a cross check via standard Pohlmeyer reduction. After embedding in AdS5 × S5 we calculate the regularized area for solutions with a boundary spanned by a 4-point scattering s-channel momenta configuration.
