Betti numbers of filtered modules over a regular local ring ∗
Let (R, n) be a regular local ring and let M be a finitely generated R-module. Given a n-stable filtration M of M, the Betti numbers of M are compared with those of the associated graded module gr M (M). The main goal of this paper is to give reasonable conditions for which they coincide. We apply the main result to the classical case of the comparison between the Betti numbers of the local ring and those of the corresponding tangent cone. In this case, it turns out that upper bounds on the Betti numbers are achieved. As a further application we present a result on the depth of the Symmetric algebra of the maximal ideal of a local ring which extends and recovers already known results.
