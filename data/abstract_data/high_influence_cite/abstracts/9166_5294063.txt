Ordinarity of Configuration Spaces and of Wonderful Compactifications
We prove the following: (1) if X is ordinary, the Fulton- MacPherson configuration space X(n) is ordinary for all n; (2) the moduli of stable n-pointed curves of genus zero is ordinary. (3) More generally we show that a wonderful compactification XG is ordinary if and only if (X,G) is an ordinary building set. This implies the ordi- narity of many other well-known configuration spaces (under suitable assumptions).
