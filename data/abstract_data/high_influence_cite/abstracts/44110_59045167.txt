SUPERLINEAR CONVERGENCE OF THE SHENG-ZOU-BROYDEN METHOD FOR NONLINEAR LEAST SQUARES PROBLEMS
We are concerned with nonlinear least squares problems. It is known that structured quasi- Newton methods perform well for solving these problems. In this strategy, two kinds of factorized structured quasi-Newton methods have been independently proposed by Yabe and Takahashi (1988), and Sheng and Zou (1988). Sheng and Zou introduced a BFGS-like update by considering how the normal equation based on an ane model may consist with the Newton equation, and dealt with a hybrid method that combines the Gauss-Newton method and their BFGS-like method. In this paper, we deal with the Sheng-Zou-Broyden family proposed by Yabe (1993), which is an extension of the update of Sheng and Zou to the Broyden- like family. Local and q-superlinear convergence of the method with this family is established for nonzero residual problems.
