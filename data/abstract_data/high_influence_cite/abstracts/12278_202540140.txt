Magnetically dependent plasmon drag in profile modulated permalloy films
We explore coupling of plasmonic, electric and magnetic effects in profile modulated permalloy structures, which have both plasmonic properties and uniaxial magnetic anisotropy with an in-plane easy axis. Strong photocurrents, observed under plasmon resonance conditions, show a clear dependence on the magnetic field with a characteristic hysteresis. The effects are tentatively attributed to the inverse spin Hall effect.
