Testing leptoquark models in $\bar B \to D^{(*)} \tau \bar\nu$
We study potential new physics effects in the B¯→D(*)τν¯ decays. As a particular example of new physics models, we consider the class of leptoquark models and put the constraints on the leptoquark couplings using the recently measured ratios R(D(*))=B(B¯→D(*)τν¯)/B(B¯→D(*)μν¯). For consistency, some of the constraints are compared with the ones coming from the current experimental bound on B(B→Xsνν¯). In order to discriminate various new physics scenarios, we examine the correlations between different observables that can be measured in the future.
