Asymptotically Schrödinger space-times: TsT transformations and thermodynamics
We study the complete class of 5-dimensional asymptotically Schrödinger space-times that can be obtained as the TsT transform of an asymptotically AdS5 space-time. Based on this we identify a conformal class of Schrödinger boundaries. We use a Fefferman-Graham type expansion to study the on-shell action for this class of asymptotically Schrödinger space-times and we show that its value is TsT invariant. In the second part we focus on black hole space-times and prove that black hole thermodynamics is also TsT invariant. We use this knowledge to argue that thermal global Schrödinger space-time at finite chemical potential undergoes a Hawking-Page type phase transition.
