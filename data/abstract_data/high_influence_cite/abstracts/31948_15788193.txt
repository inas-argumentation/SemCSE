PR ] 3 0 D ec 2 00 7 A Berry-Esseen type inequality for convex bodies with an unconditional basis
Suppose X = (X 1 ,. .. , X n) is a random vector, distributed uniformly in a convex body K ⊂ R n. We assume the normalization EX 2 i = 1 for i = 1,. .. , n. The body K is further required to be invariant under coordinate reflections, that is, we assume that (±X 1 ,. .. , ±X n) has the same distribution as (X 1 ,. .. , X n) for any choice of signs. Then, we show that E |X| − √ n 2 ≤ C 2 , where C ≤ 4 is a positive universal constant, and | · | is the standard Euclidean norm in R n. The estimate is tight, up to the value of the constant. It leads to a Berry-Esseen type bound in the central limit theorem for unconditional convex bodies.
