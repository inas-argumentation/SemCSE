Magnetic Raman continuum in single-crystalline 
H3LiIr2O6
Recently ${\mathrm{H}}_{3}{\mathrm{LiIr}}_{2}{\mathrm{O}}_{6}$ has been reported as a spin-orbital entangled quantum spin liquid (QSL) [K. Kitagawa et al., Nature (London) 554, 341 (2018)], albeit its connection to Kitaev QSL has not been yet identified. To unveil the related Kitaev physics, we perform Raman spectroscopy studies on single-crystalline ${\mathrm{H}}_{3}{\mathrm{LiIr}}_{2}{\mathrm{O}}_{6}$ samples. We implement a soft chemical replacement of ${\mathrm{Li}}^{+}$ with ${\mathrm{H}}^{+}$ from $\ensuremath{\alpha}\ensuremath{-}{\mathrm{Li}}_{2}{\mathrm{IrO}}_{3}$ single crystals to synthesize the single crystal samples of the iridate second-generation ${\mathrm{H}}_{3}{\mathrm{LiIr}}_{2}{\mathrm{O}}_{6}$. The unusual phonon broadening in ${\mathrm{H}}_{3}{\mathrm{LiIr}}_{2}{\mathrm{O}}_{6}$ implies the random positions of the substituted ${\mathrm{H}}^{+}$. As well as the lattice dynamics, Raman spectroscopy can also be used to diagnose the QSL state since the magnetic Raman continuum arises from a process involving pairs of fractionalized Majorana fermionic excitation in a pure Kitaev model. We observe a broad dome-shaped magnetic continuum in ${\mathrm{H}}_{3}{\mathrm{LiIr}}_{2}{\mathrm{O}}_{6}$, in line with theoretical expectations for the two-spin process in the Kitaev QSL. The possible scenario of the structural disordered magnet for the magnetic Raman continuum is also discussed.
