Association of household smoking status in childhood with young adults’ educational attainment and smoking status: Results from a series of population-based cross-sectional surveys in Japan
Highlights • Smoking in young adulthood is more common among those from households with smokers.• Being from smoking households is linked to low educational levels in young adulthood.• More childhood household smokers corresponds to worse outcomes for young adults.• Exposure to household smokers in childhood appears to perpetuate health inequality.
