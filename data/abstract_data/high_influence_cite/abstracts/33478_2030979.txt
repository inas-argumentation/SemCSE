Metric conformal structures and hyperbolic dimension
For any hyperbolic complex X and a ∈ X we construct a visual metric ď = ďa on ∂X that makes the Isom(X)-action on ∂X bi-Lipschitz, Mobius, symmetric and conformal. We define a stereographic projection of ďa and show that it is a metric conformally equivalent to ďa. We also introduce a notion of hyperbolic dimension for hyperbolic spaces with group actions. Problems related to hyperbolic dimension are discussed.
