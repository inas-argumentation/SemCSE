Genetic factors also seem to explain morphological differences, since these were to a large extent maintained even more than 1 yr after transplantation. Predation experiments with Asterias rubens showed that Baltic mussels grown for 12 mo in the North Sea were more readily attacked and more easily opened than equal-sized North Sea mussels. Relatively more of the available energy instead seems to be allocated to reproduction.
