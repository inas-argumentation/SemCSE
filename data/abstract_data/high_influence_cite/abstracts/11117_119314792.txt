Quenched asymptotics for Brownian motion of renormalized Poisson potential and for the related parabolic Anderson models
Let Bs be a d-dimensional Brownian motion and ω(dx) be an independent Poisson field on Rd. The almost sure asymptotics for the logarithmic moment generating function 
 
logE0exp{±θ∫t0V¯¯¯¯(Bs)ds}(t→∞)
