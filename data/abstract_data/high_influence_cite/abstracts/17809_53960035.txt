Total lightcone curvatures of spacelike submanifolds in Lorentz-Minkowski space
We introduce the totally absolute lightcone curvature for 
a spacelike submanifold with general codimension 
and investigate global properties of this curvature. 
One of the consequences is that the Chern-Lashof type inequality holds. 
Then the notion of lightlike tightness is naturally induced. 
Moreover, the lightcone Willmore conjecture is proposed.
