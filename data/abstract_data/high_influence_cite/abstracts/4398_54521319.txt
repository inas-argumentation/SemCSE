Full-Vector Gradient for Multi-Spectral or Multivariate Images
Gradient extraction is important for a lot of metrological applications, such as control quality by vision. In this paper, we propose a full-vector gradient for multi-spectral sensors. The full-vector gradient extends Di Zenzo expression to take into account the non-orthogonality of the acquisition channels thanks to a Gram matrix. This expression is generic and independent from channel count. Results are provided for a color and a multi-spectral snapshot sensor. Then, we show the accuracy improvement of the gradient calculation by creating a dedicated objective test and from real images.
