Fuzzy multivalued variational inclusions in Banach spaces
The purpose of this paper is to introduce the concept of general fuzzy multivalued variational inclusions and to study the existence problem and the iterative approximation problem for certain fuzzy multivalued variational inclusions in Banach spaces. Using the resolvent operator technique and a new analytic technique, some existence theorems and iterative approximation techniques are presented for these fuzzy multivalued variational inclusions.
