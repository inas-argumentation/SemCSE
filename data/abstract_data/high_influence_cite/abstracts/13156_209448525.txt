Hypervascular neck mass as the initial presentation of papillary thyroid cancer: A case report and review of current literature
Highlights • Papillary thyroid cancer can present as an unusual hypervascular neck mass.• Metastasis to regional lymph nodes is a common mechanism of thyroid cancer.• FNA can be inconclusive in majority of case reports.• CT, angio and MRI can be very helpful in narrowing the diagnosis of thyroid cancer.
