A Brief History of Space Climatology: From the Big Bang to the Present
Review of space climatology is presented with a view toward spacecraft electronics applications. The origins and abundances of space radiations are discussed and related to their potential effects. Significant historical developments are summarized leading to the inception of space climatology and into the space era. Energetic particle radiation properties and models of galactic cosmic rays, solar energetic and geomagnetic trapped particles are described. This includes current radiation effects issues that models face today.
