NR CFT3 duals in M-theory
We extend the search for supergravity solution duals of non-relativistic d = 3 CFTs with dynamical exponent z = 2 to d = 11 supergravity. We consider the internal space to be an S2 bundle over a product base: S2 × S2 and S2 × T2. For purely M-theoretic S2 × S2, we find only magnetic fluxes preserving two supersymmetries. S2 × T2 is far richer admitting, in addition to magnetic fluxes, various non-trivial electric fluxes which break all supersymmetry.
