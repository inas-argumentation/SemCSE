Convergence of cascade algorithms associated with nonhomogeneous refinement equations
This paper is devoted to a study of multivariate nonhomogeneous refinement equations of the form φ(x) = g(x) + ∑ α∈Z s a(α)φ(Mx− α), x ∈ IR, where φ = (φ1, . . . , φr) is the unknown, g = (g1, . . . , gr) is a given vector of functions on IR, M is an s× s dilation matrix, and a is a finitely supported refinement mask with each a(α) an r × r (complex) matrix. Let φ0 be an initial vector in (L2(IR)). The corresponding cascade algorithm is given by φk := g + ∑ α∈Z s a(α)φk−1(M · − α), k = 1, 2, . . . . In this paper we give a complete characterization for the L2-convergence of the cascade algorithm in terms of the refinement mask a, the nonhomogeneous term g, and the initial vector of functions φ0. This characterization complements the existing literature on homogeneous refinement equations. 1991 Mathematics Subject Classification. 41 A 58, 42 C 15, 41 A 17, 42 C 99.
