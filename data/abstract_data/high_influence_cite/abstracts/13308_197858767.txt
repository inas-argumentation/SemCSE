J ul 2 01 9 ERGODICITY OF UNIPOTENT FLOWS AND KLEINIAN GROUPS
Let M be a non-elementary convex cocompact hyperbolic 3-manifold and δ be the critical exponent of its fundamental group. We prove that a one-dimensional unipotent flow for the frame bundle of M is ergodic for the Burger-Roblin measure provided δ > 1.
