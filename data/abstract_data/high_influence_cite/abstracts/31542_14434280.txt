Categorical Duality for Yetter-Drinfeld Algebras
We study tensor structures on (RepG)-module cate- gories defined by actions of a compact quantum group G on unital C ∗ -algebras. We show that having a tensor product which defines the module structure is equivalent to enriching the action of G to the structure of a braided-commutative Yetter-Drinfeld algebra. This shows that the category of braided-commutative Yetter-Drinfeld G- C ∗ -algebras is equivalent to the category of generating unitary ten- sor functors from RepG into C ∗ -tensor categories. To illustrate this equivalence, we discuss coideals of quotient type in C(G), Hopf-Galois extensions and noncommutative Poisson boundaries.
