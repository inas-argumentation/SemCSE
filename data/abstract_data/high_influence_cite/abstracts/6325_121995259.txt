Integrability and algebraic limit cycles for polynomial differential systems with homogeneous nonlinearities
We consider the class of polynomial differential equations x ˙ = λ x - y + P n ( x , y ) , y ˙ = x + λ y + Q n ( x , y ) , where P n and Q n are homogeneous polynomials of degree n. These systems have a focus at the origin if λ ≠ 0 , and have either a center or a focus if λ = 0 . Inside this class we identify a new subclass of Darbouxian integrable systems having either a focus or a center at the origin. Additionally, under generic conditions such Darbouxian integrable systems can have at most one limit cycle, and when it exists is algebraic. For the case n = 2 and 3, we present new classes of Darbouxian integrable systems having a focus.
