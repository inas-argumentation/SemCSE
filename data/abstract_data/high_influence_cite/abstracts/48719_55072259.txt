THE EFFECT OF EMPLOYEE TRAINING AND PARTICIPATION ON CUSTOMER SATISFACTION
Purpose: Therefore, this study aims to test the effect of employee training and employee participation in TQM on customer satisfaction. Methodology/Approach: Data have been collected from 205 employees who have had training exposure as regards TQM, as well as actively engaging in TQM activities in their organizations. Findings: Results have indicated that training and participation have had a significant effect on customer satisfaction, mediated by employee satisfaction. These findings provide empirical evidence that supports such an assertion in the literature, regarding which employees are asserting themselves amongst key players in the implementation of TQM. Thus, the findings imply that managers of TQM-organizations should exert adequate attention, by taking action in the areas of training and participation amongst their employees.
