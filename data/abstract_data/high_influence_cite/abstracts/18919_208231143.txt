I know that I know nothing: Cortical thickness and functional connectivity underlying meta-ignorance ability in pre-schoolers
Highlights • At five years old, we start to be able to report that we do not know something.• Cortical thickness in mOFC linked to childrens' ability to report their ignorance.• This region was functionally connected to parts of the default-mode network.• The default-mode network might support the development of metacognitive ability.
