Perception of Maintenance Management Strategy on Healthcare Facilities
Abstract Main objective of this research is to look into the relationship between maintenance strategies implemented in healthcare facilities. The aim of this paper is to provide an overview of types of maintenance strategy applied to maintain facilities while satisfying the end user. Interviews and surveys were conducted to generate data from facility managers and end users in selected hospitals. The results show that there is a relationship between types of maintenance strategy implemented and end user satisfaction. Null Hypothesis was rejected due to the level of significant p>0.05 and p>0.01.
