Quantum corrections to broken N = 8 supergravity
A bstractWe show that the one-loop effective potential of spontaneously broken N = 8 supergravity is calculable and finite at all classical four-dimensional Minkowski vacua without tachyons in the spectrum. The reason is that the supertraces of the quadratic and quartic mass matrices vanish along the classically flat directions: Str $ {\mathcal{M}^2} $ = Str $ {\mathcal{M}^4} $ = 0. We also show that Str $ {\mathcal{M}^6} $ = 0 but Str $ {\mathcal{M}^8} $> 0 in a broad class of vacua with broken supersymmetry on a flat background, which includes all those explicitly identified so far. We find analytical and numerical evidence that the corresponding one-loop effective potential is negative definite.
