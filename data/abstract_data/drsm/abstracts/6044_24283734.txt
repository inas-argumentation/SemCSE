Lamotrigine use in twelve patients with the Lennox-Gastaut Syndrome
This brief report describes the effects of lamotrigine on 12 patients with the Lennox-Gastaut Syndrome in an open, uncontrolled study. Three patients are seizure free and five others substantially improved. Seven of these eight patients have shown a marked improvement in quality of life. Lamotrigine is an extremely useful adjunct in the management of the Lennox-Gastaut Syndrome.
