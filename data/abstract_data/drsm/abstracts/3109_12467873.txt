Epithelioid hemangioendothelioma of the liver
MR imaging findings. We report the MR appearance of epithelioid hemangioendothelioma (EHE) in a 51 year old man, who presented with a 14-month history of fatigue and anemia to his physician. The tumor showed massive replacement of the liver and had an unusual appearance of multiple high fluid content focal lesions with diminished vascularity.
