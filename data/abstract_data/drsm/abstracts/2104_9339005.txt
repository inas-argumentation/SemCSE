Multifocal epithelioid angiosarcoma of the small intestine
Apropos of a case. A case of angiosarcoma in the small bowel is reported. There were multifocal lesions in the duodeno-jejunum with peritoneal metastasis, revealed by an hemorrhagic syndrome. This tumor rare in the gastro-intestinal tract was epithelioïd. The authors present an histochemical and immunohistochemical study with a review of the literature.
