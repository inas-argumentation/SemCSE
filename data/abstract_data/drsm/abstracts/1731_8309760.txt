Gorham's disease involving the thoracic skeleton
Plain films and CT in two cases. Two cases of Gorham's disease (massive osteolysis, idiopathic osteolysis, vanishing bone disease) involving the thoracic skeleton are reported. Both patients were adolescent females with involvement of multiple ribs. Involvement of the thoracic spine and pleural effusions--unusual manifestations carrying a poor prognosis--were also present. The radiographic and computed tomographic appearances are included with discussion and review of this disease entity.
