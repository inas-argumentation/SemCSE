Radiative decays of the strange-bottomed $(0^+,1^+)$ mesons
In this article, we take the point of view that the strange-bottomed (0, 1) mesons Bs0 and Bs1 are the conventional bs̄ mesons, and calculate the electromagnetic coupling constants d, g1, g2 and g3 with the light-cone QCD sum rules. Then we study the radiative decays Bs0 → B sγ, Bs1 → Bsγ, Bs1 → B sγ and Bs1 → Bs0γ, and observe the decay widths are rather narrow. We can search the strange-bottomed (0, 1) mesons in the invariant Bsπ 0 and B sπ 0 mass distributions in the strong decays or in the invariant B sγ and Bsγ mass distributions in the radiative decays.
