ASSOCIATIVITY OF REPRESENTATIONS AND COCYCLES OF GAUGE GROUPS
It is shown that the consistency of certain representations of group requires a mod Z-cocycle condition which leads to some topological quantization constants in corresponding anomalous algebraic relations, such as anomalous commutators and anomalous Jacobi identities. It should be pointed out that the mod Z-cocycles for gauge groups could be constructed by means of the generalized secondary characteristic classes.
