Dark energy model with higher derivative of Hubble parameter
Abstract In this Letter we consider a dark energy model in which the energy density is a function of the Hubble parameter H and its derivative with respect to time ρ d e = 3 α H ¨ H − 1 + 3 β H ˙ + 3 γ H 2 . The behavior of the dark energy and the expansion history of the Universe depend heavily on the parameters of the model α , β and γ . It is very interesting that the age problem of the well-known three old objects can be alleviated in this models.
