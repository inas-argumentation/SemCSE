Development of a three dimensional four mirror optical cavity for laser-Compton scattering
This paper describes a design and performance of a three-dimensional 4-mirror optical cavity for development of intense photon sources by laser-Compton scattering. We achieved the finesse of 4000 and average power of 2.6 kW in the cavity with the vertical laser beam spot size of 13 ± 1 μ m in σ at the laser–electron interaction point. As a result, we observed 124±1 photons with average energy of 24 MeV per beam crossing, which corresponds to the generation of 2.7×10 8 photons per second.
