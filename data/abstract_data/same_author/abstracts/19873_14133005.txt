Probing small-x gluons by low-mass Drell-Yan pairs at colliders
The transverse-momentum (Q{sub T}) distribution of low-mass Drell-Yan pairs is calculated in QCD perturbation theory with all-order resummation of {alpha}{sub s}({alpha}{sub s} ln(Q{sub T}{sup 2}/Q{sup 2})){sup n} type terms. We demonstrate that the rapidity distribution of low-mass Drell-Yan pairs at large-enough transverse momentum is an advantageous source of constraints on the gluon distribution and its nuclear dependence. We argue that low-mass Drell-Yan pairs in the forward region provide a good and clean probe of small-x gluons at collider energies.
