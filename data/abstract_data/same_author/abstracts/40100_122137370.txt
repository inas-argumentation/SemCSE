The B parameter beyond the leading order of the 1/n expansion
We calculate the next-to-leading Image corrections to the B parameter which measures the size of the ΔS=2 matrix element Image . These corections turn out to be small which assures the validity of the 1/N expansion. For ΛQCD=0.3GeV we find B=0.66±0.10 to be compared with the leading order value B=0.75. We clarify the differences between these results and the results of the usual chiral perturbative calculations which give Image at the tree level but appear to signal the breakdown of chiral perturbation theory at the one-loop level.
