Gaussian decay estimates for the eigenfunctions of magnetic schrödinger operators
We consider the magnetic Schroedinger operator H = (p-A(x)){sup 2}+V(x) on L{sup 2}(R{sup 2}), where p = -i{partial_derivative}/{partial_derivative}x is the momentum operator, A(x) is the vector potential with the corresponding magnetic field: B(x)=rot A(x) or B=d{summation}A{sub j}dx{sub j} in general, and V(x) is the scalar potential. In this paper, we mainly study the case when the dimension n = 2 and the magnetic field B(x) is constant. In a recent paper, inspired by a question by T. Hoffman-Ostenhoff, L. Erdoes proved that the eigenfunction {psi}(x) of H has the Gaussian decay property, i.e., {vert_bar}{psi}(x){vert_bar}{le}Ce{sup -{alpha}}{vert_bar}x{vert_bar}{sup 2}, x {epsilon} R{sup 2}, with C, {alpha} > O, provided that the eigenvalue is below the essential spectrum and V(x) is analytic with respect to the angular variable. In the proof he uses the representation of H in the polar coordinates, and a generalized Feynman-Kac formula. The purpose of this paper is to give an alternative proof of his result using Agmon-type estimates [1] combined with an idea related to the phase space tunneling. Our proof is completely PDE theoretical (without using any probability theory), and relatively simple.
