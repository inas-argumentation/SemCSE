Diquark color excitation and the narrow resonances
The new narrow resonances are interpreted in the three-triplet model with integral baryon numbers as diquark color excitations of the qq +- q-barq-bar type. Hadronic decays, ..gamma.. transitions, and coupling to ..gamma.. occur through impurity qq-bar admixtures. Except for the coupling to ..gamma.., all decay rates are suppressed. The enhancements above 4 GeV in hadronic cross sections are attributed to qq-bar color octets or still higher diquark levels. (AIP)
