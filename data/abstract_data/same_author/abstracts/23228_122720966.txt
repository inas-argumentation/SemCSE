Jets with small missing energy at CERN and fermilab in supersymmetry
The O(1) SUSY contribution, associated with small missing energy ETmiss, to the three-to-two jet cross-section ratio confirms the bounds set on the s-parton masses by the large-ETmiss Collider events. For mq > mgmg≳60–70 GeV is obtained. This method, using good-statistics data, will be useful at Tevatron energies.
