Mining Large Itemsets for Association Rules
This paper provides a survey of the itemset method for associ ati n rule generation. The paper discusses past research on the topic and also studies the relevance and impo rtance of the itemset method in generating association rules. We discuss a number of variations of the associa ti n rule problem which have been proposed in the literature and their practical applications. Some inheren t weaknesses of the large itemset method for association rule generation have been explored. We also discuss som e other formulations of associations which can be viable alternatives to the traditional association rule ge neration method.
