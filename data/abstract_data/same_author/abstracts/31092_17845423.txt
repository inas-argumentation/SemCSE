Equivalence of geometric engineering and Hanany-Witten
We show the equivalence of three different realisations of gauge theory in string theory. These are the Hanany-Witten brane constructions, the use of branes as probes and geometric engineering. We illustrate the equivalence via T- and S-dualities with the simplest non-trivial examples in four dimensions: N=2 SYM with gauge groups Π SU(N i ).
