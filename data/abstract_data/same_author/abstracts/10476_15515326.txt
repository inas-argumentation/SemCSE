Galactic diffuse gamma-ray flux at the energy about 175 TeV
We present an upper limit of galactic diffuse gamma-ray flux, measured with the GAMMA experiment at energy about 175 TeV. The results were obtained using selection of muon poor extensive air showers at mountain level (700 g/cm 2 , Mt. Ara- gats, Armenia) for 5 GeV muon energy threshold.
