Multiple Meron Solutions of the Classical Yang-Mills Equation
Abstract We give a construction for multiple meron-antimeron solutions to the classical SU(2) Yang-Mills field equations. The corresponding charge density is Q(x) = 1 2 ∑ ± γ(x−x i ) , with the xi distinct points on a line. The problem is reduced to the solution of a scalar φ4 equation. We obtain a model with merons connected in pairs.
