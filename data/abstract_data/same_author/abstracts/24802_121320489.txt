What do we learn from proton-antiproton diffractive scattering at the CERN colliders
We point out that the recent proton-antiproton collision experiments at the ISR allow for the first time a comparison with proton-proton collisions and make it possible to test very general predictions based on axiomatic field theory and also to confirm certain aspects of conventional models and to eliminate certain “unorthodox possibilities”. We also discuss what will be the output of the measurement of total cross-sections and elastic scattering at the SPS
$$p\bar p$$
 collider, and describe what might happen in the ultra-high energy limit.
