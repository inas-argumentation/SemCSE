Final-state phase shifts in neutral-B decays to CP eigenstates
Abstract The effect of final-state-interaction phases on CP violation is analyzed for those nonleptonic neutral-B-meson decays to CP eigenstates which occur through the tree-level and the penguin processes. If the measurement of CP -violating asymmetries for these decays is realized both at the Z peak and at the ϒ(4S), we find that the final-state phase shifts can be determined without calculating the corresponding hadronic matrix elements explicitly.
