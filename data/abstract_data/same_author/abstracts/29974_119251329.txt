Pole Analysis of Unitarized One Loop $\chi$PT Amplitudes - A Triple Channel Study
In a previous paper [1], we proposed a method to distinguish poles of different dynamical origin, in a unitarized amplitude of ππ ,KK̄ system. That is based on the observation that ‘A Breit-Wigner resonance should exhibit two poles on different Riemann sheets which meet each other on the real axis when Nc = ∞’. In this paper, we extend our previous work [1] to the ππ-KK̄-ηη three channel system. We reconfirm most of the predictions of Ref. [1]. Especially the f0(980) is of KK̄ molecule nature. Other poles, including the σ, are of Breit–Wigner type.
