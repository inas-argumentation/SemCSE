An extremal chiral primary three-point function at two-loops in ABJ(M)
A bstractI compute the leading correction to the structure constant for the three-point function of two length-two and one length-four chiral primary operators in planar ABJ(M) theory at weak ’t Hooft coupling. The computation is reduced to four-loop propagator type Feynman integrals via a manifestly finite integration over the position of the length-four operator.
