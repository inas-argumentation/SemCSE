A nonet symmetry breaking model for (D, Ds+) decays into two pseudoscalar mesons
The authors have proposed a nonet symmetry breaking model to account for the observed branching ratios B(Ds+ to eta pi +) and B(Ds+ to eta ' pi +). Other consequences of this model are explored. They find that the branching ratios of (D, Ds+) decays into two pseudoscalar mesons, one of which is eta ', are enhanced. In particular, they predict B(D0 to K0 eta ') approximately=20%, B(D+ to eta ' pi +) approximately=3% and B(Ds+ to eta 'K+) approximately=1%, the last two hardly Cabibbo-angle suppressed.
