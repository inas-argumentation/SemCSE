Charm and fancy changing neutral currents with five quarks
We propose a five-quark weak interaction model based on the SU(2) × U(1) × U(1)′ gauge group. The fifth quark f with fancy flavor has electric charge 2 3 . The model induces the charm and fancy changing neutral currents in a natural fashion which are responsible for substantial D0 − D 0 mixing to explain the observed wrong-sign dimuon events. However, these flavor changing neutral currents do not couple to leptons, thus forbidding trimuons to appear as the experiments indicate thus far. We discuss in addition how our model can be extended to a vector-like theory.
