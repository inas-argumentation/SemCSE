Triple-vector-boson processes in gamma gamma colliders.
We study the production of three gauge bosons ([ital W][sup +][ital W][sup [minus]][ital Z][sup 0] and [ital W][sup +][ital W][sup [minus]][gamma]) at the next generation of linear [ital e][sup +][ital e][sup [minus]] colliders operating in the [gamma][gamma] mode. We analyze the total cross sections as well as several kinematical distributions of the final state particles. We find out that a linear [ital e][sup +][ital e][sup [minus]] machine operating in the [gamma][gamma] mode will produce 5--10 times more three-gauge-boson states compared to the standard [ital e][sup +][ital e][sup [minus]] mode at high energies.
