Infrared safety of impact factors for colorless particle interactions
We demonstrate, to next-to-leading order accuracy, the cancellation of the infrared singularities in the impact factors which arise in the QCD description of high energy processes $A+\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{B}{A}^{\ensuremath{'}}{+B}^{\ensuremath{'}}$ of colorless particles. We study the example where A is a virtual photon in detail, but show that the result is true in general.
