Domain-wall induced quark masses in topologically nontrivial backgrounds
In the domain-wall formulation of chiral fermions, the finite separation between domain walls (L{sub s}) induces an effective quark mass (m{sub eff}) which complicates the chiral limit. In this work, we study the size of the effective mass as a function of L{sub s} and the domain-wall height m{sub 0} by calculating the smallest eigenvalue of the Hermitian domain-wall Dirac operator in the topologically nontrivial background fields. We find that, just as in the free case, m{sub eff} decreases exponentially in L{sub s} with a rate depending on m{sub 0}. However, quantum fluctuations amplify the wall effects significantly. Our numerical result is consistent with a previous study of the effective mass from the Gell-Mann-Oakes-Renner relation. (c) 2000 The American Physical Society.
