QCD corrections to heavy flavour photoproduction and electroproduction
We investigate the QCD corrections to the total cross sections and single-particle inclusive differential distributions for γ + p → Q(Q) + X and e + p → e + Q(Q) + X. In the latter reaction we assume that the exchanged photon is almost real (Weizsacker-Williams approximation). This requires the calculation of the O(αs) corrections to the photon-parton reaction γ + g → Q + Q including the virtual gluon contributions and the soft and hard gluon radiation in the reaction γ + g → Q + Q + g. The contributions from γ + q(q) → Q + Q + q(q) are also calculated. We give exact results for the O(α2αS2) total cross sections and single particle inclusive differential distribution for b-quark production at HERA. Results for b- and t- production at future ep colliders (the LEP∗LHC option at CERN) are also presented.
