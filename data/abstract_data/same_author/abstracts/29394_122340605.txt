Accurate formulae for the calculation of high energy electron bremsstrahlung spectra
Abstract A compilation of formulae for the calculation of the bremsstrahlung spectrum for electron energies above 30 MeV is presented. The Bethe-Heitler extreme relativistic theory with intermediate screening and Coulomb correction is used with a correction for the finite Z-dependent intensity at the endpoint. The inclusion of this effect together with that of electron-electron bremsstrahlung allows these formulae to be applied over the entire photon spectrum and for any radiator atomic number Z. The effects of electron energy loss and straggling in the radiator and the spread in incident electron energy are also taken into account. The formulae have been reduced to a form convenient for computer calculations.
