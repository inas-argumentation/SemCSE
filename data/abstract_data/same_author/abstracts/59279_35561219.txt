An object oriented environment for modeling and synthesis of hardware circuits
Presents an object oriented design environment for modeling as well as synthesis of circuits. The modeling of circuits as classes and storage of presynthesized modules has been discussed. A design approach is presented which synthesizes both the datapath and control path of a circuit from its behavioral specification provided in object oriented style. The synthesis approach can synthesize circuits which reuse the presynthesized modules from the design library.<<ETX>>
