Greedy splitting algorithms for approximating multiway partition problems
.Given a system (V,T,f,k), where V is a finite set, is a submodular function and k≥2 is an integer, the general multiway partition problem (MPP) asks to find a k-partition ={V1,V2,...,Vk} of V that satisfiesfor all i and minimizes f(V1)+f(V2)+···+f(Vk), where is a k-partition of hold. MPP formulation captures a generalization in submodular systems of many NP-hard problems such as k-way cut, multiterminal cut, target split and their generalizations in hypergraphs. This paper presents a simple and unified framework for developing and analyzing approximation algorithms for various MPPs.
