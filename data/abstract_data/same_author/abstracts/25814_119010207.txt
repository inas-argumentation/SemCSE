Five-dimensional N=4, SU(2)×U(1) gauged supergravity from type IIB
We construct the complete and explicit non-linear Kaluza–Klein ansatz for deriving the bosonic sector of N =4 SU (2)× U (1) gauged five-dimensional supergravity from the reduction of type IIB supergravity on S 5 . This provides the first complete example of such an S 5 reduction that includes non-abelian gauge fields, and it allows any bosonic solution of the five-dimensional N =4 gauged theory to be embedded in D =10.
