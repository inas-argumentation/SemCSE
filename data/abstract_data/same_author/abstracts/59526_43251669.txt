Real Time Stokes Inversion Using Multiple Support Vector Regression
Solution of the Stokes inversion problem to estimate the vector magnetic field distribution on the sun from the profiles of the Stokes parameters of polarised light in magnetically sensitive spectral lines is a vital task in our understanding of solar activity. Recently machine learning techniques such as principal component analysis and neural networks have led to the development of real time inversion computer codes. This paper presents a novel alternative approach to real time inversion called Multiple Support Vector Regression which is applied here for the first time to synthetic Stokes profile data.
