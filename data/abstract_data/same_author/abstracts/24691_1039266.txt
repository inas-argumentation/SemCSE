Properties of a hyperon-quark mixed phase in compact stars
Properties of a hadron–quark mixed phase are studied taking account of the geometrical structures and chemical equilibrium of phases in a consistent way. We clarify the roles of the surface tension and the charge screening effect. We show that the screened Coulomb interaction tends to make the geometrical structure of the mixed phase less stable, and the resultant equation of state becomes similar to that given by the Maxwell construction. We demonstrate that hyperons are suppressed in the mixed phase, because the hadron phase is positively charged. This is a novel mechanism of hyperon suppression in compact stars.
