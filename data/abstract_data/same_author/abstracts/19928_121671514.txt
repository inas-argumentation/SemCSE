New experimental limit on the electron stability and non-paulian transitions in Iodine atoms
A new limit on the mean life of the electron in the "disappearance" approach has been established: tau(e) > 4.2(2.4).10(24) yr at 68% (90%) C.L., by using the similar or equal to 100 kg DAMA Nal(Tl) set-up at the Gran Sasso National Laboratory of INFN.
