Domain-wall fermions with U(1) dynamical gauge fields in (4 + 1)-dimensions☆
Abstract We carry out a numerical simulation of a domain-wall model in (4 + 1) dimensions, in the presence of a quenched U(1) dynamical gauge field only in an extra dimension, corresponding to the weak coupling limit of a (4-dimensional) physical gauge coupling. Our numerical data suggest that the zero mode seems absent in the symmetric phase, so that it is difficult to construct a lattice chiral gauge theory in the continuum limit.
