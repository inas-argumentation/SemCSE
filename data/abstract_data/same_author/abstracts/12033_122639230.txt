Quantitative limit on the structure of the neutrino
An upper limit of 7 x 10/sup -16/ cm on the structure of the muon-type neutrino is obtained by comparison of the Q/sup 2/ dependence of the nucleon structure function F/sub 2/(x,Q/sup 2/) determined from inelastic ..mu..-N and ..nu../sub ..mu../-N scattering.
