Supersymmetric configurations in Euclidean Freedman–Schwarz model
Abstract We study Euclidean D =4, N =4 gauged SU (2)× SU (1,1) supergravity theory which has been obtained from dimensional reduction of N =1, D =10 supergravity on S 3 × AdS 3 . We obtain supersymmetric configurations like domain wall, electro-vac type of solutions with geometries E 2 × S 2 , E 2 × AdS 2 and axio-vac type E 1 × S 3 solution in this Euclidean Freedman–Schwarz (EFS) model. We also show that the Euclidean gravitational instantons with nontrivial (anti)self-dual U (1) gauge fields are stable vacua preserving one fourth of the original supersymmetry.
