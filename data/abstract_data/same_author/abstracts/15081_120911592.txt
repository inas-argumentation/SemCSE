The singularity states of the system of a charged fermion and a Dirac dyon
For the system of the fermion-Dirac dyon it is shown that the LWP difficulty exists also for the angular momentum states j ⩾ |q| + 12 when the charge Zd of the dyon exceeds some critical value Zdc. In order to avoid the LWP difficulty, the Kazama-Yang term and the term iKZZde2 γ·r2Mr3 should be considered simultaneously. The result shows that for all angular momentum states the radial wave functions of the fermion possess physically reasonable asymptotic behavior at the origin. The necessary condition for the bound states is qualitatively discussed, which shows that it is quite different for the dyon and the monopole.
