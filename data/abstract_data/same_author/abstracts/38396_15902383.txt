Mass, confinement and CP invariance in the Seiberg-Witten model
Abstract Several physics aspects of the Seiberg-Witten solution of N = 2 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory with SU (2) gauge group, supplemented with a small mass term for the “matter” fields which leads to an N = 1 theory with confinement, are discussed. We find in particular that in the massive (confining) theory the low energy physics has an exact CP symmetry, while in a generic vacuum in the massless theory CP invariance is broken spontaneously.
