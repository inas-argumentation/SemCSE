Correlations between large transverse momentum π0 mesons and charged particles at the CERN ISR
A study of the charged particles emitted together with a large transverse momentum π O was made at the CERN ISR at a centre-of-mass energy s = 52.7 GeV . The π O mesons and the charged particles were observed in two detectors, each centred at θ cm = 90° and each covering a laboratory solid angle of 0.59 sr for π o 's and 1.1 sr for charged particles. The multiplicity of charged particles, observed in the same and in the opposite hemisphere as the π O , was found to be larger than the average multiplicity for inelastic interactions. Some rapidity and azimuthal correlations were observed.
