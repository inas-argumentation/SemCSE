Robustness of baryon-strangeness correlation and related ratios of susceptibilities
Using quenched lattice QCD simulations we investigate the continuum limit of baryon-strangeness correlation and other related conserved charge-flavor correlations for temperatures T{sub c}<T{<=}2T{sub c}. By working with lattices having large temporal extents (N{sub {tau}}=12,10,8,4) we find that these quantities are almost independent of the lattice spacing, i.e., robust. We also find that these quantities have very mild dependence on the sea quark mass and acquire values which are very close to their respective ideal gas limits. Our results also confirm robustness of the Wroblewski parameter.
