Recovering Individual Accessing Behaviour from Web Logs
In this paper, we present a new view on the data preparation in web usage mining. We concentrate on recovering individual usage behaviour from accessing records on web site. We defined five categories of individual behaviours such as granular accessing behaviour, linear sequential behaviour, tree structure behaviour, acyclic routing behaviour and cyclic routing behaviour.
