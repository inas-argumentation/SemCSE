A new proof of the log-concavity property of the wave function for a potential with laplacian of a given sign
A new proof (the third!) is given of the fact that log (u/rl+1) is concave (convex), where u is the reduced ground state wave function, for angular momentum l, solution of a Schrodinger equation with a potential such that its Laplacian is everywhere positive (negative). This property is a crucial step in the proof that if the Laplacian is positive (negative) the levels belonging to a given Coulomb multiplet have energies decreasing (increasing) when the angular momentum increases.
