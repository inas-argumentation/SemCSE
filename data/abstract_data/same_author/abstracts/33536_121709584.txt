Calculation of the hadron spectrum in lattice QCD
Abstract We present Monte Carlo results for the hadron mass-spectrum in lattice SU(3) using the Wilson action and the quenched approximation. We show that on a 6 3 · 10 lattice at β =6.0, there exist long-lived metastable states of the gauge-field, characterized by the eigenvalues of Z(3) and associated with the first-order deconfining phase transition. The hadron masses are very different in these state if the quark paths that wind around the lattice are not removed. We demonstrate two methods which eliminate a significant fraction of such quark paths. The final results for hadron masses do not agree with the experimental values. We find that the π and ϱ, as well as the proton and the Δ are degenerate.
