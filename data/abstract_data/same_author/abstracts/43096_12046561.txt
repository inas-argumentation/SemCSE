An upper bound for conforming Delaunay triangulations
A plane geometric graph <italic>C</italic> in <italic>R</italic><supscrpt>2</supscrpt> <italic>conforms</italic> to another such graph <italic>G</italic> if each edge of <italic>G</italic> is the union of some edges of <italic>C</italic>. It is proved that for every <italic>G</italic> with <italic>n</italic> vertices and <italic>m</italic> edges, there is a completion of a Delaunay triangulation of O(<italic>m</italic><supscrpt>2</supscrpt><italic>n</italic>) points that conforms to <italic>G</italic>. The algorithm that constructs the points is also described.
