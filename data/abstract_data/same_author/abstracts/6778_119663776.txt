Mass independence of critical exponents in perturbation theory
The suggestion of Dash and Harrington that the critical exponent $gamma$ at the fixed point may be found in perturbation theory by its independence of the mass is investigated in the Reggeon calculus and in phi$sup 4$ theory in three dimensions (phi$sup 4$)$sub 3$. We discuss an ambiguity due to the choice of energy scale and find an invariant criterion for determining the point of mass independence. Neither the Reggeon calculus nor (phi$sup 4$)$sub 3$ satisfies this criterion, and no natural choice of energy scale works for (phi$sup 4$)$sub 3$ in second order. (AIP)
