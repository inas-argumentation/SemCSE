Neutral hyperon production in K-p interactions at low momentum
New results are reported from a bubble chamber study of neutral hyperon production in K-p reactions with laboratory kaon momenta between 150 and 350 MeV/c. (For K- in flight below 150 MeV/c the bubble chamber technique is unsuitable in these channels). The statistics below 250 MeV/c are considerably better than those of previous experiments, which has made it possible for the first time to measure angular distributions over the whole momentum range. These measurements indicate the presence of a significant P-wave interaction in the Lambda pi 0 channel at momenta below 200 MeV/c where previously only S-wave interactions had been considered in most analyses of low energy K N data. This collaboration has now completed the analysis of all two-body channels in low momenta and stopped K-p interactions. The authors find that the K N scattering lengths determined from their new data sample are significantly different from those obtained using the K-p data previously available.
