High Precision Automatic Magnetic Field Mapping System for the Dipole Magnet
A real time automatic measurement system has been developed and used to measure and analyse the Triple Bending Achromat (TBA) magnet at SRRC. An IBM/PC/AT has been used as the system controller due to its cost effectiveness with simple and expandable configuration of this Hall probe automatic magnetic field mapping system.The development concept, hardware and software systems as well as the precision are introduced and discussed in this paper.
