ALX, an Action Logic for Agents with Bounded Rationality
Abstract We propose a modal action logic that combines ideas from H.A. Simon's bounded rationality , S. Kripke's possible world semantics , G.H. von Wright's preference logic , Pratt's dynamic logic , Stalnaker's minimal change and more recent approaches to update semantics . ALX (the x th action logic) is sound, complete and decidable, making it the first complete logic for two-place preference operators. ALX avoids important drawbacks of other action logics, especially the counterintuitive necessitation rule for goals (every theorem must be a goal) and the equally counterintuitive closure of goals under logical implication.
