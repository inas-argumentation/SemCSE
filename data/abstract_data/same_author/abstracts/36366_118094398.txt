Determining the Unitarity Triangle from Two‐Body Charmless Hadronic B Decays
We performed an updated global fits to charmless Bu,d → PP decays in the framework of flavor SU(3) symmetry, without referring to sin2β measured from the charmonium decay modes. Fit results directly constrain the (ρ, η) vertex of the unitarity triangle. Details regarding the analyses of different schemes of SU(3) breaking in decay amplitude sizes and predictions for the branching ratios and CP asymmetries of Bu,d,s → PP decays are given in Ref. [1].
