High-order strong-coupling calculation of the ground-state energy density in supersymmetric field theory.
This paper is a continuation of an earlier study of the ground-state energy density E/sub 0/ in two-dimensional supersymmetric field theories. Lattice strong-coupling techniques are used to calculate E/sub 0/ to eleventh order, extending the previous calculation by four additional orders. Our results continue to suggest that supersymmetry invariance is restored in the continuum limit. Moreover, new and unexpected large-order behavior in the lattice perturbation-expansion coefficients is revealed in this high order of perturbation theory.
