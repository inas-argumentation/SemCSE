On the calculation of the interquark potential generated by a string with massive ends
The variational equations, defining the stationary point of the effective action of the Nambu-Goto string with massive ends, are solved numerically by making use of an iterative procedure. The obtained solution enables one to evaluate the string functional integral. Proceeding from this, the interquark potential generated by a string with massive ends is derived. Calculations show that the higher iterative corrections turn out to be small, and the zero approximation fairly well reproduces the interquark potential generated by the Nambu-Goto string with massive ends. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
