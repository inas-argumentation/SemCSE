Phenomenological analysis of heavy hadron lifetimes
A phenomenological analysis of lifetimes of bottom and charmed hadrons within the framework of the heavy quark expansion is performed. The baryon matrix element is evaluated using the bag model and the nonrelativistic quark model. We find that bottom-baryon lifetimes follow the pattern {tau}({Omega}{sub b}){approx_equal}{tau}({Xi}{sub b}{sup {minus}}){gt}{tau}({Lambda}{sub b}){approx_equal}{tau}({Xi}{sub b}{sup 0}). However, neither the lifetime ratio {tau}({Lambda}{sub b})/{tau}(B{sub d}) nor the absolute decay rates of the {Lambda}{sub b} baryon and B mesons can be explained. One way of solving both difficulties is to allow the presence of linear 1/m{sub Q} corrections by scaling the inclusive nonleptonic width with the fifth power of the hadron mass m{sub H{sub Q}} rather than the heavy quark mass m{sub Q}. The hierarchy of bottom baryon lifetimes is dramatically modified to {tau}({Lambda}{sub b}){gt}{tau}({Xi}{sub b}{sup {minus}}){gt}{tau}({Xi}{sub b}{sup 0}){gt}{tau}({Omega}{sub b}): The longest-lived {Omega}{sub b} among bottom baryons in the OPE prescription now becomes the shortest lived. The replacement of m{sub Q} by m{sub H{sub Q}} in nonleptonic widths is natural and justified in the PQCD-based factorization approach formulated in terms of hadron-level kinematics. For inclusive charmed baryon decays, we argue that since the heavy quark expansion does not converge, local duality cannot be tested in this case.
