Axial properties of the nucleon and N* resonances
We report results from a theoretical study of axial charges and axial form factors of the nucleon N and its resonant states N*. By employing relativistic constituent-quark models we could produce for the first time covariant predictions for the axial-current matrix elements. The calculations are performed in the point form of Poincare-invariant quantum mechanics. The axial charge as well as the axial form factor of the nucleon are reproduced close to phenomenology. The axial charges of the N*(1535) and N*(1650) resonances – the only cases where such a comparison is possible – agree well with results from quantum chromodynamics on the lattice that have recently become available. Here, we present the axial charges of all N* excitations below ~2 GeV and two examples of N* axial form factors. These results should be interesting for comparison with other approaches, especially with regard to the issue of chiral-symmetry restoration in higher-lying hadron resonances.
