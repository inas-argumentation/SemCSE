Asymptotic equality of cross sections for line-reversed reactions
Abstract We show that if the ratio d σ d t ( AB → CD ) d σ d t ( C B→ A D ) has a generalized limit, for energies tending to infinity at fixed momentum transfer, this generalized limit is unity, if, in addition, the phases of the two amplitudes grow separately less fast that the logarithm of the energy. It is shown by explicit counter-examples that it is not sufficient to impose such a condition on the relative phase of the two amplitudes. Generalizations to non-fixed momentum transfer (case of a shrinking forward peak) are discussed.
