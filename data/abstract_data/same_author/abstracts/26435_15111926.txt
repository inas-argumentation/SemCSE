Enhanced antiproton production in Pb(160 A GeV)+Pb reactions: evidence for quark gluon matter?
Abstract The centrality dependence of the antiproton per participant ratio is studied in Pb(160 A GeV)+Pb reactions. Antiproton production in collisions of heavy nuclei at the CERN/SPS seems considerably enhanced as compared to conventional hadronic physics, given by the antiproton production rates in pp and antiproton annihilation in p p reactions. This enhancement is consistent with the observation of strong in-medium effects in other hadronic observables and may be an indication of partial restoration of chiral symmetry.
