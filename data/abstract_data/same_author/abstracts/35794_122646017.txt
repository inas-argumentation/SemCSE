In the present letter we assume also an existence of a p!n resonance, i.e., u-channel resonance (we call this the T' boson), lying on a Regge trajectory ar' (u), and introduce it into the previous model. Then, we can show that a new model reduces the energy dependence of quantities corresponding to the vector and axial-vector form factors to be much smaller than that in the previous model.
