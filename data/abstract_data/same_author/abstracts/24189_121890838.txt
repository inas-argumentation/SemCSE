Structure of Phase Transitions in Quantum Chromodynamics at Finite Temperature
Abstract We investigate the instanton paramagnetic medium at finite temperature. By analyzing the equation of state we show explicitly the presence of both first- and second- order phase transitions. Within the known framework of the mean field type of approximations we also include the effects of large-scale vacuum fluctuations. The numerical results are compared to other approaches to this problem in quantum chromodynamics at finite temperature.
