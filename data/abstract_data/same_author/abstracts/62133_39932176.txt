Increased DNA fragmentation in isolated rat islets following 24 hour co-culture with cytokine transgene-bearing adenovirus is dose dependent, but does not reduce glucose-stimulated insulin secretion.
Genetic manipulation of isolated rat islets before transplantation using adenoviral vectors is under investigation as a strategy to modulate the host immune response in the microenvironment of the graft. Viruses, including adenovirus, are known to be capable of modulating apoptosis by either including or suppressing specific processes, depending on the virus and cell system studied. Similar observations have been made using cytokines including the immunosuppressive cytokine TGF- β 2 . However, the effect of virus and inserted gene product on islet cell viability and function remain to be examined in detail. In this study, we investigated the effect of adenoviral infection and insertion of a novel gene (TGF-β) on inslet cell apoptosis and on glucose-stimulated insuling secretion (GSIS).
