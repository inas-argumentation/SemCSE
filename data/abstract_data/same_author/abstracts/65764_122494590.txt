1,145,135. Separating curds and whey. McKENZIE & RIDLEY Ltd. 11 July, 1967 [11 July, 1966], No. 31953/67. Heading A2B. [Also in Division B1] A drainage filter for separating curds and whey in cheese-making comprises an endless moving filter band 5, made up of perforate stainless steel slats 7, and two fixed side walls 8 making sliding seals with the inclined upper run of band 5 to form a trough into whose lower end mixture is delivered (Fig. 1, not shown). The two runs of the band enclose a tray 4 which receives filtrate from the upwardly moving upper run, and another tray 3 is located below the lower run. Mixture on the upper run is stirred by radial arms 22 of electric beaters (20). Each slat 7 has an imperforate margin, and its long edges are bent over and slats joined by split pins. Slat ends are screwed to the links of endless chains (6) (Figs. 4-7, not shown), whose pivots incorporate rollers 6b bearing on tracks 12. Metal shoes 19 on slats 7 bear on polytetrafluoroethylene runners 17 to support the upper run. Band 5 is driven by electric motor (14) through a variable speed gear, and emergency stop buttons are distributed over the machine. Tube system (25) delivers hot water for cleaning the machine. Legs 2 are adjustable to vary the inclination of the band, which could be horizontal.
