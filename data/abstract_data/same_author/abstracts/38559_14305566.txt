R-parity conserving supersymmetry, neutrino mass and neutrinoless double beta decay
We consider contributions of $R$-parity-conserving softly broken supersymmetry (SUSY) to neutrinoless double beta $(0\ensuremath{\nu}\ensuremath{\beta}\ensuremath{\beta})$ decay via the $(B\ensuremath{-}L)$-violating sneutrino mass term. The latter is a generic ingredient of any weak-scale SUSY model with a Majorana neutrino mass. The new $R$-parity-conserving SUSY contributions to $0\ensuremath{\nu}\ensuremath{\beta}\ensuremath{\beta}$ are realized at the level of box diagrams. We derive the effective Lagrangian describing the SUSY-box mechanism of $0\ensuremath{\nu}\ensuremath{\beta}\ensuremath{\beta}$ decay and the corresponding nuclear matrix elements. The one-loop sneutrino contribution to the Majorana neutrino mass is also derived. Given the data on the $0\ensuremath{\nu}\ensuremath{\beta}\ensuremath{\beta}$ decay half-life of ${}^{76}$Ge and the neutrino mass we obtain constraints on the $(B\ensuremath{-}L)$-violating sneutrino mass. These constraints leave room for accelerator searches for certain manifestations of the second and third generation $(B\ensuremath{-}L)$-violating sneutrino mass term, but are most probably too tight for first generation $(B\ensuremath{-}L)$-violating sneutrino masses to be searched for directly.
