Some TPC measurements in an oxygen beam at the AGS
The principal detector for AGS Experiment 810 is a Time Projection Chamber (TPC) in which it is intended to measure momenta and angles of a major fraction of the charged particles from each light ion collision. This report describes the results of a test of a prototype of the TPC in a beam of (14.6 × 16 = 233.6 GeV c ) oxygen ions run in June of this year.
