Use of an energy-dependent barrier for heavy-ion fusion.
An energy-dependent one-dimensional potential barrier for heavy-ion fusion reactions is shown to be consistent with the full dynamical coupled-channel calculation, provided the potential is written in terms of the kinetic energy {var epsilon} in the relative-separation coordinate at the fusion barrier. It is also shown that earlier conclusions about the inadequacy of the energy-dependent barrier-penetration model is a direct consequence of expressing the energy dependence as a function of the center-of-mass bombarding energy {ital E}{sub c.m.}.
