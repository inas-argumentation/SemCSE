Nucleon self-energy in relativistic nuclear matter with pion ring series.
Nucleon self-energies from the pion-ring series are studied in the relativistic mean-field theory of liner σ model with the ω meson and the △. Near the Fermi surface of nuclear matter, the pion rings generate attractive scalar and vector potentials of 10-15% of the nucleon mass. These strongly energy-dependent potentials cause the nucleons to have a significant probability to be in a collective N-hole or △-hole configuration
