Multiple scattering aspects of the. pi. -nucleus Low equation
The ..pi..-nucleus Low equation, under certain assumptions (implicit in conventional multiple-scattering theory), is equivalent to a linear equation. The propagator of this equation includes a factor of (z/H/sub 0/)/sup 2/ which vastly reduces off-shell scattering. As a result, the Kisslinger singularity is eliminated, and the size of the Lorentz--Lorentz effect and local-field corrections is reduced.
