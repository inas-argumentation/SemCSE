Remarks on the Qin–Ma parametrization of quark mixing matrix
Recently, Qin and Ma (QM) have advocated a new Wolfenstein-like parametrization of the quark mixing matrix based on the triminimal expansion of the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa (CKM) parametrization. The CP-odd phase in the QM parametrization is around 90 ◦ just as that in the CKM parametrization.
