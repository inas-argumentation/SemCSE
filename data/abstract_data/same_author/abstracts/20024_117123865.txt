Cosmic muon events coincident in two LEP detectors
A search for time- and direction-correlated cosmic muon events has been made using data collected during the period May to November 2000 by the CosmoALEPH and L3+C underground experiments being 6 km apart. Four threefold-coincidence events between L3+C, ALEPH’s hadron calorimeter, and one CosmoALEPH scintillator station have been found.
