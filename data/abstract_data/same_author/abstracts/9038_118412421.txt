X(3872) in Effective Field Theory
If the X (3872) resonance is a shallow boundstate of a the charm mesons D0D*0 and D*0D0, it can be described by an effective theory of nonrelativistic D mesons coupled to nonrelativistic pions (X‐EFT). In this talk, I give a brief overview of the X (3872), followed by a short review of X‐EFT. I end my talk with results from calculations of the the next‐to‐leading‐order correction to the partial decay width Γ[X→D0D0π0], and the decay of X (3872) to P‐wave quarkonia.
