Negative, anomaly-free, oblique radiative corrections.
Current electroweak precision measurements tend to prefer negative values of the oblique radiative parameters, {ital S} and {ital T}, relative to their standard-model expectations. This tendency is shown to be consistent with the existence of new colorless fermions in the anomaly-free combination of two doublets ({ital N}{sub 1},{ital E}){sub {ital L}} and ({ital E}{sup {ital c}},{ital N}{sub 2}){sub {ital L}} and one singlet {ital N}{sub 3{ital L}}, which are contained in some supersymmetric or {ital E}{sub 6} extensions of the standard model.
