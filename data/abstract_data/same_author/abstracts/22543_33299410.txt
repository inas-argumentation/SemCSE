Proton single-particle energies in 23F
We compare recent experimental results for the spectroscopic properties of {sup 23}F with theoretical results based on the new Hamiltonians USDA and USDB for the sd-shell model space. The implications for the proton single-particle energies in {sup 23}F are discussed. Conclusions are drawn about the level scheme of {sup 23}F and the accuracy of the new Hamiltonians from these comparisons.
