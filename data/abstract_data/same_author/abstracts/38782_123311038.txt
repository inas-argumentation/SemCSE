A fifth force and the mediation of supersymmetry breaking
In this talk I will discuss a new mechanism for the mediation of supersymmetry breaking [1]. It relies on the existence of a fifth force, or what is more often refer to as a Z′ gauge‐boson. The existence of supersymmetry and an extra gauge‐boson is well motivated by the type of high‐energy theories (GUT models and string constructions) considered in the literature. The fact that neither has yet been seen is a possible hint that the two are linked and the present work is an attempt at exploring this connection.
