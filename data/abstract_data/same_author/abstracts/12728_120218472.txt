Implications of recent neutrino oscillation data
Implications of the oscillation interpretations of solar, atmospheric, and LSND neutrino data are discussed. We list many open questions, and point out the promise of future data to answer these. Two issues discussed in some detail are (i) the difficult nature of measuring CP-violation in oscillations, and (ii) the promise of reconstructing the flavor-basis neutrino mass matrix from oscillation data.
