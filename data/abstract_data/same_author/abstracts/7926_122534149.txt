Chiral perturbation theory in the Extended Nambu-Jona-Lasinio model
The chiral perturbation theory is developed at the quark level within the Extended Nambu-Jona-Lasinio model which we used for the low-energy approximation of QCD in the leading order of the large N expansion. In terms of constituent-quark-loop diagrams we analyse all of the main low-energy effects caused by the first order corrections in the current quark mass expansions. For the correct description of theη →3π decays we confirm the important role of the final-state-interaction quoted by Gasser and Leutwyler.
