Dictionary of LHC Signatures
We report on a plan to establish a “Dictionary of LHC Signatur es”, an initiative that started at the WHEPPX workshop in January 2008. This study ai ms towards the strategy on distinguishing of 3 classes of dark matter motivated scenarios suc h as R-parity conserved supersymmetry, Little Higgs models with T-parity conservation and Univers al Extra Dimensions with KK-parity for generic cases of their realization in wide range of the model space.
