Atmospheric neutrino absorption cross-sections in Ar-40
We have calculated the inclusive quasielastic ${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{e}$ and ${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}$ cross sections in ${}^{40}\mathrm{Ar}$ relevant for the atmospheric neutrino experiments. The effects of the nuclear medium on the cross sections have been studied. These calculations are done in the local-density approximation and various nuclear effects, such as Pauli blocking, Fermi motion, and strong interaction renormalization of the weak vertex due to the presence of nucleons in the nucleus, are taken into account. Numerical results for the charged lepton yields are presented.
