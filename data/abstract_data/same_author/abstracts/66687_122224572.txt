Adams operations and integral Hermitian-Galois representations
<abstract abstract-type="TeX"><p>We give an algebraic interpretation of the action of the <i>j</i>-th Adams operator <i>j</i> on Hermitian Galois structure classes for tame covers of schemes of dimension <i>1</i>. This generalizes work of Erez and of Erez-Taylor concerning rings of algebraic integers when <i>j = 2</i>.
