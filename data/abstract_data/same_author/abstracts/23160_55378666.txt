Analysis of chiral symmetry breaking mechanism
— The renormalization group invariant quark condensate m× is determined both from the consistent equation for quark condensate in the chiral limit and from the Schwinger-Dyson (SD) equation improved by the intermediate range QCD force singular like d(q) which is associated with the gluon condensate. The solutions of m× in these two equations are consistent. We also obtain the critical strong coupling constant a c above which chiral symmetry breaks in these two approaches. The nonperturbative kernel of the SD equation makes a c smaller and m× bigger. An intuitive picture of the condensation above a c is discussed. In addition, with the help of the Slavnov-Taylor-Ward (STW) identity we derive the equations for the nonperturbative quark propagator from the SD equation in the presence of the intermediate range force and find that the intermediate-range force is also responsible for dynamical chiral symmetry breaking.
