A dynamic momentum compaction factor lattice in the Fermilab Debuncher ring
In the initial and present design of the Debuncher ring, the momentum compaction factor (/spl alpha/), or equivalently the slip factor /spl eta/=/spl alpha/-1//spl gamma//sup 2/, was chosen to have a value which is a compromise between the two competing functions of the ring - that of accepting and debunching a large number of p~s/pulse and subsequently employing stochastic cooling feedback systems to precool before injection into the Accumulator. The goal of this experiment is to reconcile this compromise by changing /spl eta/ between two desired values during each p~ production cycle.
