Soft supersymmetry breaking and radiative Higgs signatures
I review our recent formulation of the minimal FCNC schemes of the soft supersymmetry breaking via the squark mass-terms and scalar trilinear interactions, where the large O(1) mixings among scalar-tops and -charms are found to be consistent with all existing theoretical and experimental bounds. Such a feature is demonstrated in a class of attractive new models with horizontal U(1) symmetry which also explain the observed quark-mass pattern and solve the SUSY μ-problem. The soft SUSY breaking induced radiative H±bc and h0tc Higgs couplings are analyzed and are shown to provide exciting new discovery Higgs signatures at the Tevatron and LHC.
