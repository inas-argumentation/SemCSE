Spacings of Purely Angular Excitations in Potentials with a Laplacian of a Given Sign, and Applications
Purely angular excitations of two-body systems with a spherically symmetric interacting potential whose Laplacian has definite sign are considered. It is shown that the ratio of level spacings between different angular momenta is bounded below (above) by the ratio of the corresponding spacings for the Coulomb problem when the Laplacian of the potential is positive (negative). These estimates are applied to quarkonium models, muonic atoms, alcaline atoms, and exponentially screened Coulomb potentials.
