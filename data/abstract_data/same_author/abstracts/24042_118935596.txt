Hořava-Lifshitz gravity effects on Casimir energy in weak field approximation and infrared regime
We calculate the renormalized vacuum energy of a massless scalar field confined between two nearby parallel plates formed by ideal uncharged conductors, placed tangentially to the surface of a sphere with mass $M$ and radius $R$. This study will take into account the static and spherically symmetric solution of Ho\ifmmode \check{r}\else \v{r}\fi{}ava-Lifshitz gravity found by Kehagias-Sfetsos (KS), in both weak field and infrared limits. A slight amplification of the Casimir force between the conducting plates is found. Thermal corrections to the Casimir energy are analyzed. Based on current Casimir effect measurements, a constraint on the $\ensuremath{\omega}$ parameter of KS metric is also obtained.
