JINR tau-charm factory design considerations
Results of a JINR tau-charm conceptual study are given. The general considerations regarding the storage ring and accelerator complex in JINR, the tau-charm factory injector system and the main ring, the principles and the parameter estimation to achieve high luminosity are examined. The approximate cost of the tau-charm factory in JINR using the institute's infrastructure is 130 million rubles, 20.5 million being spent for civil engineering and installation works.<<ETX>>
