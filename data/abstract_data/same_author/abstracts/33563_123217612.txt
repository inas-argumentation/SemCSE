String tension, gluebal masses and finite size effects in lattice SU(3)
We estimate the string tension by looking at Wilson loops and measuring the correlation between Wilson lines at g 2 = 1.0 for lattice SU(3). The bound Λ L σ ⩾0.0087 ± 0.0011 is larger than the previous results. The mass of the 0 ++ glueball is also estimated. The finite size effects on these quantities are shown to be large by comparing results on a 6 3 × 10 lattice with those on 9 4 lattice.
