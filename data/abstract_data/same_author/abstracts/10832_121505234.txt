Interpolating lagrangians and U(1) gauge theory on the lattice
We apply the idea of a linear interpolating expansion to compact U(1) on the lattice, setting up an action S δ which at δ = 1 reproduces the standard gauge-invariant action but at δ = 0 has the form of its weak-coupling limit times an arbitrary parameter β′. We then evaluate the plaquette energy E p through O(δ 3 ), fixing β′ by the requirement that ∂E p / ∂β ′ = 0. Approaching the critical point β c from above, we are able to reproduce closely the Monte Carlo results down to a value of β at which the stationary point in β′ disappears. This value is progressively nearer to β c in higher orders of the δ expansion.
