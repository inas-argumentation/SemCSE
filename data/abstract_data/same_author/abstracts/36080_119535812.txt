Condition for primordial black hole formation in Randall–Sundrum cosmology
Abstract We consider spherical collapse in the Randall–Sundrum type II model and estimate the critical overdensity for black hole formation in the radiation dominated era. It is found that when (density) 2 -term is dominant in the modified Friedmann equation the critical density is smaller than in the standard cosmology, which implies that PBHs are more easily produced in the Randall–Sundrum model.
