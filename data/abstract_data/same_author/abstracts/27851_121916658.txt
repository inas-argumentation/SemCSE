Measurement of neutral to charged pion ratio in pp annihilations in the 2200 MeV mass region
The relative probability distribution of π±and π0 mesons in pp annihilation was measured in 10 MeV bins from 2.15 to 2.23 GeV. No significant variation in probabilities was found, and mean values are consistent with those reported at 2.28 GeV. Observed multiplicity distributions are consistent with two-body cascading on the statistical model under equal weighting of G = ±1 states.
