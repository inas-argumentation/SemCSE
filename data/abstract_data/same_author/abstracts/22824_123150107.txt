Low-temperature transport coefficients of nuclear matter with in-medium modifications
Transport coefficients of dense symmetrical nuclear matter in the low-temperature regime are calculated taking into account the modifications of the quasi-particle scattering amplitudes in the nuclear environment. The two-body scattering T -matrix, the in-medium nucleon-nucleon cross section, and the critical temperature for the superfluid phase transition for the different partial wave channels are evaluated using a separable form of the nucleon-nucleon interaction. As a precursor effect of the onset of superfluid phase, the transport coefficients show non-trivial rediction with the decreasing temperature. The in-medium transport coefficients are found to be considerably modified compared with the results obtained with either the free scattering cross section or the isotropic residual value σ ∼ 40 mb.
