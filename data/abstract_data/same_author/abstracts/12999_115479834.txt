Measurement of the absolute branching fractions for D0 decays into K- pi+, K- pi+ pi+ pi-, anti-K0 pi+ pi-
Abstract Using the ARGUS detector at the e + e − storage ring DORIS II we have measured the absolute branching ratios of the D 0 meson, Br( D 0 → K − π + )=(3.41±0.12±0.28)%, Br( D 0 → K − π + π + π − )=(6.80± 0.27±0.57)%, and Br (D 0 → K 0 π + π − )=(5.03±0.39±0.49)% .
