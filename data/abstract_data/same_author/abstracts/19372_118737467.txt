Inflation, scalar fields, and fourth-order gravity
SummaryWe review on new results for fourth-order gravity and for Einstein's theory plus a scalar field ϕ under the view point of cosmology. We apply theorems on a conformal rescaling between different types of gravitational Lagrangians under consideration and discuss its physical interpretation.
