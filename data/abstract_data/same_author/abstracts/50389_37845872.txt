The complexity of learning in PLN networks
Abstract In this paper, the complexity of learning in the feedforward PLN network is investigated by using Markov chain theory, when its training samples are incomplete (i.e., a network with hidden nodes). We present a learning algorithm. A formula for computing the average number of steps that the learning algorithm converges is obtained when the PLN network exists a solution. In the probabilistic sense, the completeness of the learning algorithm is proved. Some computer simulations are given to verify the analysis.
