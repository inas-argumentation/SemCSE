BAND SPECTRUM FOR SCHRODINGER OPERATORS WITH STRONG PERIODIC MAGNETIC FIELDS
In a recent paper [5], Hempel and Herbst considered the magnetic Schrodinger operator (without scalar potential): H(λA) = (p – λA(x))2, where p = - i ∂x and A(x) is a vector potential. They studied the behavior of H(λA), or its resolvent as λ→∞. They showed that if {x | A(x) = 0} = {x |dA(x) = 0} up to a null set, then H(λA) converges to the Dirichlet Laplacian on {x|A(x) = 0} as λ→ ∞ in the sense of strong resolvent convergence. With additional conditions, e.g., if A(x) is periodic, they also showed that they converges in the norm resolvent sense, and hence the spectrum converges. Thus, in such cases (with additional conditions), if λ is sufficiently large then H(λA) has spectrum bands, and the gaps are in fact open.
