PHENOMENOLOGY IN THE HIGGS TRIPLET MODEL WITH THE A4 SYMMETRY
We discuss the phenomenology of doubly and singly charged Higgs bosons [of $SU(2{)}_{L}$-triplet fields] in the simplest ${A}_{4}$-symmetric version of the Higgs triplet model. Mass eigenstates of these Higgs bosons are obtained explicitly from the Higgs potential. It is shown that their decays into a pair of leptons have unique flavor structures which can be tested at the LHC if some of their masses are below the TeV scale. Sizable decay rates for $\ensuremath{\tau}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\overline{\ensuremath{\mu}}ee$ and $\ensuremath{\tau}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\overline{e}\ensuremath{\mu}\ensuremath{\mu}$ can be obtained naturally, while other $\ensuremath{\tau}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\overline{\ensuremath{\ell}}{\ensuremath{\ell}}^{\ensuremath{'}}{\ensuremath{\ell}}^{\ensuremath{'}\ensuremath{'}}$, $\ensuremath{\mu}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\overline{e}ee$, and $\ensuremath{\ell}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\ell}}^{\ensuremath{'}}\ensuremath{\gamma}$, are almost forbidden in this model. Contributions of these Higgs bosons to the nonstandard interactions of neutrinos are also considered.
