First observation of the Sigma*+(c) charmed baryon, and new measurements of the Sigma0(c), Sigma+(c), Sigma++(c), and Omega0(c) charmed baryons
Using ~13.7 fb^{-1} of e^+e^- collision data recorded at near the Upsilon(4S) resonance by the CLEO II and CLEO II.V detector configurations on the Cornell Electron Storage Ring, we present the world's most precise measurements of the Sigma_c^0, Sigma_c^+, and Sigma_c^{++} charmed-baryon masses as well as the first measurements of the intrinsic widths of the Sigma_c^0 and Sigma_c^{++} baryons. We also report on the first observation and mass measurement of the Sigma_c^{*+} charmed baryon, M(Sigma_c^{*+}) - M(Lambda_c^+) = (231.0 +- 1.1[stat] +- 2.0[syst]) MeV/c^2, and the first CLEO observation of the Omega_c^0 baryon, for which we measure a mass M(Omega_c^0) = (2694.6 +- 2.6[stat] +- 1.9[syst]) MeV/c^2 from a sample of (40.4 +- 9.0[stat]) candidate events. All new results are preliminary.
