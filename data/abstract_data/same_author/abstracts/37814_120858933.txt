Generalized static electromagnetic fields in Brans--Dicke theory
A general class of static, axially symmetric solutions of the Brans–Dicke–Maxwell equations is obtained under the assumption −r2⋅grr=gtt⋅gφφ⋅gzz These solutions have been subjected to conformal transformation and they are found to be new solutions of the static, axially symmetric Einstein–Maxwell–scalar fields. We have also developed a more general set of solutions under the unit transformations given by Morganstern.
