Spacings, fine and hyperfine splittings of angular excitations, and applications to atomic and particle physics
After recalling previous results on angular excitations of a particle in a potential with a laplacian of a given sign, presenting new illustrations of the results in atomic physics, and deriving very successful inequalities on ionization energies of atomic systems, we obtain new inequalities on the fine splittings of angular excitations of a spin-12 particle submitted to a (vector) potential with a laplacian of a given sign, to leading order in 1/c2. Applications to muonic atoms and alkaline atoms are presented. We also obtain inequalities on the 1s wave function at the origin which are applied to quarkonium and to muonic atoms.
