U(1)gauge field localization on a Bloch brane with Chumbes-Holf da Silva-Hott mechanism
We follow the Chumbes\char21{}Holf da Silva\char21{}Hott mechanism to study the (quasi)localization of the $U(1)$ gauge field on the Bloch brane. The localization and resonances of the $U(1)$ gauge field are discussed for four kinds of Bloch brane solutions: the original and generalized Bloch brane solutions, as well as the degenerate Bloch brane solutions I and II. With the Chumbes\char21{}Holf da Silva\char21{}Hott mechanism, we find that the mass spectrum of the gauge field Kaluza-Klein modes is continuous and there is no tachyonic mode. The zero mode is localized on all the branes and there are resonant Kaluza-Klein modes on the degenerate Bloch branes.
