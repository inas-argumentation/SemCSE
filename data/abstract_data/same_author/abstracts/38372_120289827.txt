Spontaneous breaking of local supersymmetry by gravitational instantons
We study the gravitino condensate 〈(DμΨν − DμΨν)(DμΨν − DνΨμ)〉 in the one-loop approximation around a nontrivial background metric. It turns out that, among the known regular solutions of the euclidean Einstein equations, the Eguchi-Hanson metric is the unique relevant configuration. The standard functional integration gives a finite answer for the gravitino condensate. Due to the presence of an anomalous supersymmetry transformation law, this implies that local supersymmetry is broken spontaneously in all supergravity models with scalar multiplets.
