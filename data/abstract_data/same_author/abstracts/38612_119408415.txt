A general parametrization for the long-range part of neutrinoless double beta decay
Abstract Double beta decay has been proven to be a powerful tool to constrain B - L violating physics beyond the standard model. We present a representation for the long-range part of the general 0νββ decay rate allowed by Lorentz-invariance. Combined with the short range part this general parametrization in terms of effective B - L violating couplings will provide the 0νββ limits on arbitrary lepton number violating theories.
