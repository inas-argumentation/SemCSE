Ground state of high-density matter
Abstract It is shown that if an upper bound to the false vacuum energy of the electroweak Higgs potential is satisfied, the true ground state of high-density matter is not nuclear matter, or even strange-quark matter, but rather a non-topological soliton where the electroweak symmetry is exact and the fermions are massless. We examine this possibility in the standard SU(3) C ⊗ SU(2) L ⊗ U(1) Y model. The bound to the false vacuum energy is satisfied only for a narrow range of the Higgs boson masses in the minimal electroweak model (within a few eV of its minimum allowed value of 6.6 GeV), and a somewhat wider range for electroweak models with a non-minimal Higgs sector.
