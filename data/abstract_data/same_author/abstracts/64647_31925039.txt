Knowledge Mass and Automatic Reasoning System in Similarity Logic CQ
This paper introduces and investigate the ℚ−knowledge mass, ℚ−knowledge universe, ℚ−knowledge base and ℚ−automatic reasoning system bases on the similarity logic ℂℚ. We also present and investigate the ℚ−true level k knowledge circle, extended ℚ− knowledge base, extended ℚ−automatic reasoning system and the level (k,j) perfection of extended ℚ− knowledge base \(\mathbb{K}^V\) in this paper.
