$CP$-violating triple-product asymmetries in Charm decays
The use of triple-product correlations is described in relation to the search for $CP$ violation in 4-body charm meson decays. The latest results from the LHCb and BaBar Collaborations are reported. A novel interpretation of the asymmetries from triple-product correlations is used for the BaBar results, which enables the extraction of information on the properties of $D$ decays under parity and charge-conjugation transformations.
