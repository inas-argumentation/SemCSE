CP Tests and the Weak Dipole Moment in τ-Pair Production at LEP
Tests of CP in variance in the reaction e +e− →τ+τ− on the Z 0 peak are reviewed. Averaging the present LEP measurements, upper limits on the real and imaginary parts of the weak dipole moment of the τ lepton of ∣Re(d τ w (M Z 2 ))∣ < 3.3 • 10−18 ecm and ∣Im(d τ w (M Z 2 ))∣ < 9.8 • 10−18 ecm with 95% c.l. are obtained.
