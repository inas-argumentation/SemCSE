On projectively flat Hermitian manifolds
Let (M,g) be a n-dimensional compact hermitian manifold, with n > 2. (M, g) will be called projectively flat, if its curvature matrix is of the form 0 = a/n, where a is a (1, l)-form. Note that any metric conformal to g would also be projectively flat. In §1, we shall classify such manifolds, and in §2, we will give an application which may be considered as a generalization to higher dimensions of the Bogomolov's Theorem on VIIQ surfaces.
