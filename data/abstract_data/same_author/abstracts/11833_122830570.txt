EVEN INTEGER LATTICES FROM SIMPLY LACED GKO COSETS
We determine the branching functions (“coset characters”) for the decomposition of the characters, at genus one, of level-one Kac-Moody algebras based on simply laced simple Lie algebras (ĝ1) into the characters of their level-one subalgebras (ĥ1) to be (improved) theta functions on certain even integer lattices. These characters support unitary representations of the modular group and provide explicit examples of the characters of Gaussian RCFT associated with an arbitrary even integer lattice, as discussed in Refs. 1 and 9.
