Singularities of the space-time of plane domain wall when coupled with a scalar field
Abstract An exact solution of the Einstein-scalar field equations is given, which represents the Vilenkin planar domain wall coupled with a massless scalar field. It is found that, besides a big-bang singularity, the space-time has other curvature singularities, the proper distance from which to the wall is finite, and inversely proportional to the surface energy density of the wall.
