Lower bound to the pion polarizability from QCD sum rules
Making use of QCD sum rules a lower bound is found which relates the electromagnetic polarizability α E and mean-square radius 〈 r π 2 〉 of charged pions through the intrinsic polarizability α E = α E − α〈r π 2 〉 (3M π ) . We find that if present constraints on the QCD continuum (duality) threshold are accepted, this lower bound on the intrinsic polarizability α E is incompatible with some previous determinations of α E and 〈 r π 2 〉.
