Absorption and emission of radiation by a sourceless abelian gauge wall in a robertson-walker space-time
SummaryA model of a sourceless Abelian «gauge wall» consisting of a singular magnetic field occupying the (y, z)-plane is examined in the context of a flat Robertson-Walker space-time background. Exact solutions are found for the gauge field structure function. The solutions may be static or time dependent. Dynamic solutions exist which describe the absorption and emission of gauge field radiation by the gauge wall.
