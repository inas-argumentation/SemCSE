Nontopological soliton in three-dimensional supergravity
We construct numerical solutions for non-topological solitons in threedimensional U(1)-gauged N = 2 supergravity. We find the region of the solutions showing with the BTZ mass, the angular momentum and the magnetic flux and discuss the relation among the physical parameters for various values of a cosmological constant.
