A righthanded weak current and quantum chromodynamics as the origin of the dominant nonleptonic weak interaction
If a new charm and strangeness changing righthanded weak current is introduced, one obtains within quantum chromodynamics a new term in the nonleptonic weak Hamiltonian, corresponding to the decay s → d + glue. It is proposed that this interaction gives rise to the |ΔI| = 1 2 enhancement and other hitherto unexplained properties of the nonleptonic weak decays of strange particles.
