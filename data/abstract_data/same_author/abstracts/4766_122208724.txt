The calculation of quark number susceptibility at finite chemical potential and temperature
Abstract In this paper, we calculate the quark number susceptibility (QNS) at finite chemical potential μ and finite temperature T in the framework of the Dyson-Schwinger approach using the meromorphic quark propagator proposed in [Phys. Rev. D 70 , 014014 (2004)]. Analysis and discussions of the calculated result of the QNS is given.
