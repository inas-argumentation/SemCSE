The UV‐Cutoff Effects on the Chiral Critical Surface in Hot and Dense QCD
The search of the QCD critical point is one of the interesting issues both in theoretical and experimental particle physics. In this article, we use the Nambu Jona‐Lasinio model to study the UV‐cutoff effects on the critical surface which may play a crucial role to the possible existence/non‐existence of the critical point. Then we compare the indications between the results obtained through our effective approach and the lattice QCD simulations.
