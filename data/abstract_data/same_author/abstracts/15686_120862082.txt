Remarks on double poles and non-exponential decays
The model of an unstable particle with two internal states, which are able to change from one into the other, is analyzed with the help of the formalism outlined generally in the papers by J. Jersák: Jader fiz. (in press) and M. Lokajíček: Czech. J. Phys.B 19 (1969), 925. This formalism is developed further.
