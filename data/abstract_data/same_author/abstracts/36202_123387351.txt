Quarks and leptons as composite states of confined O(n) preons
We explore the possibility that quarks and leptons are composite states of preons, bound by an O( n ) gauge force. Solutions to the consistency conditions proposed by 't Hooft are found. The simplest solution consists in precisely n families of quarks and leptons, which can be interpreted as composite states of one preon and O( n ) glue. The breaking of the gauge symmetry associated to the observed interactions requires technicolor, besides O( n ) forces.
