Neutralino Dark Matter in Light Higgs Boson Scenario
The minimal supersymmetric model with the lightest Higgs boson whose mass is less than 114.4 GeV is called as the light Higgs boson scenario. We show that the scenario is consistent not only with many collider experiments but also with the observed relic abundance of dark matter. The allowed region may be probed by experiments of Bs→μ+μ− in near future and tested in present direct detection experiments of dark matter.
