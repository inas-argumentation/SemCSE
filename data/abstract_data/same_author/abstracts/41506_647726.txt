Learning unions of two rectangles in the plane with equivalence queries
This paper presents an (J(logz n) on-line learning alg~ rithm for unions of two rectangle in the discrete plane {1,..., n}z with equivalence queries. The hypothesis space of the learning algorithm is the same as the concept class. The learning algorithm consists of separate search procedures that search for the boundaries of the target concept. Compared to learning a single rectangle studied in [CM], learning uniona of two rectangles is substantially more complicated in two aspects: the obvious local search procedures have to tolerate both false positive and false negative counterexamples; it is nontrivial for the global algorithm to manage ita local search procedures. In order to overcome those difficulties, more powerful error-tolerant search strategies and management techniques have been developed in this paper.
