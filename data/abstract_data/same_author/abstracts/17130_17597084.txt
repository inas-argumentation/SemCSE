BOSONIC THERMAL MASSES IN SUPERSYMMETRY
Effective thermal masses of bosonic particles in a plasma play an important role in many different phenomena. We compute them in general supersymmetric models at leading order. The origin of the different corrections is explicitly shown for the formulas to be applicable when some particles decouple. The correct treatment of Boltzmann decoupling in the presence of trilinear couplings and mass mixing is also discussed. As a relevant example, we present results for the minimal supersymmetric standard model. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
