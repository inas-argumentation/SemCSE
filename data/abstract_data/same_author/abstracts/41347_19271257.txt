Extremal Cayley Digraphs of Finite Cyclic Groups
Let $\cay(m,A)$ denote the Cayley digraph on ${\bf Z}_m$ generated by $A$, where ${\bf Z}_m$ is the cyclic group of residues modulo $m$. Let $r(m,A)$ denote the average distance of $\cay(m,A)$. For any $r\ge1$ and $k\ge1$ define $m^*(r,k)$ as the largest positive integer $m$ such that the average distance of the Cayley digraph $\hbox{Cay}(m,A)$ is at most $r$ for some set $A$ with $k$ elements. In this paper, an asymptotic formula for $m^*(r,2)$ is proved and a lower bound for $m^*(r,k)$ is also obtained for $k\ge3$. Applications to the construction of optimal distributed loop networks is discussed in this paper. A lower bound of the average order of subsets for asymptotic bases in number theory is proved using the main theorem of this paper.
