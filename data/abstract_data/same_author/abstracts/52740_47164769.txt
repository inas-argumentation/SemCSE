On Levels in Arrangements of Lines, Segments, Planes, and Triangles%
. We consider the problem of bounding the complexity of the k th level in an arrangement of n  curves or surfaces, a problem dual to, and an extension of, the well-known k-set problem. Among other results, we prove a new bound, O(nk5/3) , on the complexity of the k th level in an arrangement of n  planes in R3 , or on the number of k -sets in a set of n  points in three dimensions, and we show that the complexity of the k th level in an arrangement of n  line segments in the plane is  
 $O(n\sqrt{k}\alpha(n/k))$  , and that the complexity of the k th level in an arrangement of n  triangles in 3-space is O(n2k5/6α(n/k)) .
