Resumming phase space logarithms in inclusive semileptonic B decays.
We study logarithms of the form ln({ital m}{sub {ital q}}/{ital m}{sub {ital b}}) which arise in the inclusive semileptonic decay of a bottom quark to a quark of mass {ital m}{sub {ital q}}. We use the renormalization group to resum the leading radiative corrections to these terms, of the form {ital m}{sup 2}{sub {ital q}}{alpha}{sup {ital n}}{sub {ital s}}ln{sup {ital n}}({ital m}{sub {ital q}}/{ital m}{sub {ital b}}), {ital m}{sup 3}{sub {ital q}}{alpha}{sub {ital s}}{sup {ital n}+1}ln{sup {ital n}}({ital m}{sub {ital q}}/{ital m}{sub {ital b}}), and {ital m}{sup 4}{sub {ital q}}{alpha}{sup {ital n}}{sub {ital s}}ln{sup {ital n}+1}({ital m}{sub {ital q}}/{ital m}{sub {ital b}}). The first two resummations are trivial, while the last involves a nontrivial mixing of four-Fermi operators in the 1/{ital m}{sub {ital b}} expansion. We illustrate this technique in a toy model in which the semileptonic decay is mediated by a vector interaction, before treating the more complicated case of left-handed decay. {copyright} {ital 1996 The American Physical Society.}
