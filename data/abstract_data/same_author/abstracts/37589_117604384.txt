Dark Matter Prospects with the ANTARES Neutrino Telescope
The ANTARES collaboration is building a large-area telescope at the bottom of the Mediterranean Sea, capable of detecting high-energy neutrinos from a number of sources. Of particular interest here is the possibility of detecting the presence of neutralino dark matter at the centres of galactic sources via the annihilation of neutralinos into products containing high-energy neutrinos. The principle of detection of high-energy neutrinos and the ANTARES detector are introduced and the sensitivity of the first stage, 0.1 km2 ANTARES detector to neutralinos is presented, along with comparisons to theoretical predictions and direct detection experiments.
