Constraints on the Tritium Beta Decay and the Neutrinoless Double Beta Decay in the Minimal Seesaw Model
We calculate the effective mass terms of the tritium beta decay (me) and the neutrinoless double beta decay (mm) in the minimal seesaw model with two heavy right-handed Majorana neutrinos. By using current neutrino oscillation data, we obtain the ranges of me and mee for two possible patterns of the neutrino mass spectrum: (1) 0.00424eV≤me≤0.0116eV and 0.00031eV≤mee≤0.0052eV for the normal neutrino mass hierarchy; (2) 0.0398eV≤me≤0.0571eV and 0.0090eV≤mee≤0.0571eV for the inverted neutrino mass hierarchy. The sensitivity of mee on the smallest neutrino mixing angle and the Majorana CP-violating phase is also discussed.
