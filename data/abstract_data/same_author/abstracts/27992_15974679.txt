Anomaly cancellation and the gauge group of the Standard Model in NCG
It is well known that anomaly cancellation almost determines the hypercharges in the Standard Model. A related (and somewhat more stronger) phenomenon takes place in Connes' NCG framework: unimodularity (a technical condition on elements of the algebra) is strictly equivalent to anomaly cancellation; and this in turn reduces the symmetry group of the theory to the standard SU (3) × SU (2) × U (1).
