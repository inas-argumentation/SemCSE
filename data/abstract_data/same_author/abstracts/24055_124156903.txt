Dynamical Symmetry Breaking for Weinberg–Salam Model and Restoration at Finite Temperature
In this paper, we utilize Nambu-Jona-Lasinio (NJL) mechanism to discuss the dynamical symmetry breaking for Weinberg-Salam model. In the NJL mechanism the symmetry breaking not only is determined by the potential of scalar field but also has important relation with condensate of the fermion pair . We also discuss the restoration problem of gauge symmetry breaking by the NJL mechanism at high temperatures.
