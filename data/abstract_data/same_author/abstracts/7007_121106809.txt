CHARACTER IDENTITIES IMPLIED BY SUPER-W3 INVARIANCE AND GENERALIZED COULOMB GAS DESCRIPTIONS
We study the super-W3, c=10/7 model and show that super-W3 invariance implies stringent constraints on the characters of the model. Taking into account these field identifications we can construct a Coulomb gas description of the model within the approach of Ref. 1. We suggest that Ref. 1 could be a general framework for constructing Coulomb gas descriptions for isolated solutions of W algebras not related to classical Lie algebras.
