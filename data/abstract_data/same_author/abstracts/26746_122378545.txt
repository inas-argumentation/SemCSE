De Sitter plane wave and plane wave-like solutions ofSO(4, 1) gauge field equations
A new classical solution to the sourcelessSO(4,1) gauge field equations is presented. The solution is found by identifying the internal symmetry indices with the space-time (conformai) symmetry indices in analogy with the cases of the instanton or the meron solutions. The form of the solution is analogous to the de Sitter plane waves found recently byDrechsler andSasaki.
