Quantum realizations of the superanomally in two-dimensional models
We show that the supersymmetric version of the Wess-Zumino action in two dimensions (SWZ) can be coupled in a supersymmetric and gauge-invariant way to external gauge superfields. 
 
We compute the generating functional for the Green functions of the current supermultiplet and show that it possesses a superchiral anomaly proportional to the superfield strength. We show that this functional is also the superdeterminant for a gauged fermionic theory, chiral SQCD2, equivalent to the gauged SWZ model at the quantum level.
