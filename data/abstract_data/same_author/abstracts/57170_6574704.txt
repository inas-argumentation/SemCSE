Internet/Web technology in higher education in China
With the dramatic development of the Internet and the WWW, Internet/Web technology has become an important educational technology and tool for developing higher education. This paper will examine Web technology in higher education in China. More specifically, it will overview the history of Web technology in China, and examine the application of Web technology in higher education in China. Furthermore, we will investigate the influence of Web technology on development in higher education in China from both a micro viewpoint and macro viewpoint.
