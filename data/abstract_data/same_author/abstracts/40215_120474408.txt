Fine structure of the P states in heavy quarkonium and the nature of the spin-dependent potential
We discuss the failure of existing theories to agree with the experimental measurements of the fine structure of the χ states of the b b and c c systems. We rule out various possible explanations, such as mixing of the 2P triplet with the 1F triplet. We use thefact that the spin-independent potential is well-determined in the relevant range to extract accurate determinations of expectation values of the spin-dependent potentials providing tests for future theories. We find that the usual ansatz that the q q interaction has effective vector and scalar contributions only is very unlikely to be sufficient to explain the fine structure of the 3 P levels and to accomodate the recent measurement of the mass of the 1 P state in charmounium.
