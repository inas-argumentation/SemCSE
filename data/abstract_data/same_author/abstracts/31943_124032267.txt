CP violation of $B_{d}-{\bar B}_{d}$ mixing in supersymmetry with gauged baryon and lepton numbers
We analyze the CP asymmetry in flavor-specific decays alpha(d)(fs) for the B-d - (B) over bar (d) mixing in the extension of the minimal supersymmetric standard model where baryon and lepton numbers are local gauge symmetries (BLMSSM). Considering the constraint of a 125 GeV Higgs, the numerical results indicate that the theoretical prediction of alpha(d)(fs) agrees with the current experimental result. For certain parameter sets, the BLMSSM contributions can be sizeable in B-d - (B) over bar (d) mixing and CP asymmetry in B decays, which may help understand questions in cosmology.
