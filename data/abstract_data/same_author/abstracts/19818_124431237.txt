Massive neutron stars with a hyperonic core: A case study with the IUFSU relativistic effective interaction
The recent discoveries of massive neutron stars, such as PSR J0348 + 0432 and PSR J1614–2230, have raised questions about the existence of exotic matter such as hyperons in the neutron star core. The validity of many established equations of states (EoSs) like GM1 and FSUGold are also questioned. We investigate the existence of hyperonic matter in the central regions of massive neutron stars by using relativistic mean field (RMF) theory with the recently proposed Indiana University Florida State University (IUFSU) model.
