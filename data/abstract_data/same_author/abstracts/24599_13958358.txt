Non-commutative Low-dimension Spaces and Superspaces Associated with Contracted Quantum Groups and Supergroups
Quantum planes, which correspond to all one-parameter solutions of Quantum Yang-Baxter Equation (QYBE) for the two-dimensional case of GL-groups, are summarized and their geometrical interpretations are given. It is shown that the quantum dual plane is associated with an exotic solution of QYBE and the well-known quantum h-plane may be regarded as the quantum analog of the flag (or fiber) plane. Contractions of the quantum supergroup GLq(1∣2) and corresponding quantum superspace Cq(1∣2) are considered in Cartesian basis. The contracted quantum superspace Ch(1∣2);ι) is interpreted as the non-commutative analog of the superspace with the fiber odd part.
