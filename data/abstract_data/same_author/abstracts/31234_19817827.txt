Strongly interacting vector bosons at the CERN LHC: Quartic anomalous couplings
We analyze the potential of the CERN Large Hadron Collider to study anomalous quartic vector-boson interactions through the production of vector-boson pairs accompanied by jets. In the framework of SU(2)L^U(1)Y chiral Lagrangians, we examine all effective operators of order p 4 that lead to new fourgauge-boson interactions but do not alter trilinear vertices. In our analyses, we perform the full tree-level calculation of the processes leading to two jets plus vector-boson pairs, W 1 W 2 ,W 6 W 6 ,W 6 Z ,o rZZ, taking properly into account the interference between the standard model and the anomalous contributions.
