Lattice performance of the PEP-II High Energy Ring
The High Energy Ring (HER) of PEP-II, an asymmetric B-factory, has been commissioned since June 1997. By the end of the last run, it has successfully stored the beam up to 300 mA with a beam-life time of a few hours. Its lattice was resilient and behaved remarkably well. Although the authors are still in an early stage of the commissioning, they will assess the status of the lattice quality and identify improvements necessary in the near future by simulating the lattice in a way that is closely related to the operation and comparing the results with the measured data.
