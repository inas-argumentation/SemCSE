Recent results on charm physics at BESIII
Abstract About 2.9 fb − 1 data set had been collected with the BESIII detector at the BEPCII collider. Taking advantages of data near open-charm threshold, we present preliminary results on leptonic and semileptonic charm-meson decays, as well as result on the FCNC process D 0 → γ γ from BESIII experiment. High precision charm data will enable us to validate Lattice QCD calculations at the few percent level. These can then be used to make precise measurements of CKM elements, V c d and V c s , which are useful to improve the accuracy of test of the CKM unitary.
