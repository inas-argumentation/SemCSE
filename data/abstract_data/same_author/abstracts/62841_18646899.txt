Cardinal invariants related to permutation groups
We consider the possible cardinalities of the following three cardinal invariants which are related to the permutation group on the set of natural numbers:

ag≔ the least cardinal number of maximal cofinitary permutation groups;

ap≔ the least cardinal number of maximal almost disjoint permutation families;

c(Sym(N))≔ the cofinality of the permutation group on the set of natural numbers.

We show that it is consistent with ZFC that ap=ag<c(Sym(N))=ℵ2; in fact we show that in the Miller model ap=ag=ℵ1<ℵ2=c(Sym(N)).
