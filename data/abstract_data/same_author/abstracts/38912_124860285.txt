Review of c, b and τ Lifetimes from e+e- Experiments
The current status of lifetime measurements of heavy flavored particles by experiments at e + e - colliders and their implications for the standard model are reviewed. This includes the most recent measurents of lifetimes of the tau lepton, the D mesons and B hadrons. The emphasis is on B hadron lifetimes, since there are several new results contributed to this conference. The first use of the B lifetime to tag and study bƃ events is also discussed.
