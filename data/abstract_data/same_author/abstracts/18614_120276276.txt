Surface versus volume emissions in photon–hadron correlations
High-pT photon–hadron correlations are studied within the next-to-leading order (NLO) perturbative QCD parton model with modified parton-jet fragmentation functions due to jet quenching in high-energy A+A collisions. In central A+A collisions, the away-side hadrons with large zT=pTh/pTγ are controlled mainly by the surface emission of the gamma-jet events, while a small zT region will be volume emission bias. In other words, gamma jets for a small-zT region probe the dense matter deeper than those gamma jets for a large-zT region, so the small-zT gamma jets are found to be slightly more sensitive to the properties of the dense matter than the large-zT gamma jets.
