SUPERSYMMETRIC QUANTUM FIELDS AND INFINITE DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS
Supersymmetric quantum fields are interesting from the point of view both of physics and of mathematics. Their interest for physics (see, e g. [35]) stems primarily from the fact that they may provide a natural framework for a unified theory of elementary interactions. In particular, recent developments in superstring theory [15] suggest that such a theory has the potential to unify gravitational forces with the strong, weak and electromagnetic forces.
