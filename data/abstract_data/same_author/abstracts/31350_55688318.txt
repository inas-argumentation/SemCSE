STUDIES ON THE DUST EFFECT IN THE BEPC STORAGE RING
In the routine dedicated synchrotron radiation operation, the normal beam lifetime is 8~10 hours at Ib ~ 80 mA for the single bunch case, and 20~25 hours for multi-bunch case with the total beam current of ~140 mA. A beam lifetime reduction was observed very often in operation for both single and multi-bunch cases.
