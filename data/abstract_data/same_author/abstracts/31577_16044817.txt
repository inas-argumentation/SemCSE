Hard-thermal-loop Resummation of the Free Energy of a Hot Quark-Gluon Plasma
The quark contribution to the free energy of a hot quark-gluon plasma is calculated to leading order in hard-thermal-loop (HTL) perturbation theory. This method selectively resums higher order corrections associated with plasma effects, such as screening, quasiparticles, and Landau damping. Comparing to the weak-coupling expansion of QCD, the error in the one-loop HTL free energy is of order αs, but the large α 3/2 s correction from QCD plasma effects is included exactly.
