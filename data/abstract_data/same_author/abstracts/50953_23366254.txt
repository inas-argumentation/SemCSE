Autonomous Information Services Integration for High-Assurance in Demand-Oriented Information Systems
With interdependent requests of multi-service increasing, the need for adaptability, timeliness and reliability to information service systems has become more and more important. In this paper, the relationship between the timeliness and the reliability of correlated services allocation and access is clarified. Based on these factors, the autonomous network-based information services integration technology to provide one-stop service for users’ multi-service requests is proposed. We proved the effectiveness of the proposed technology through the simulation and the results show that the integrated service can reduce the total users access time compared with the conventional systems.
