Transversity quark distribution function in the large Nc-limit
The chiral quark–soliton model, which is based on the large Nc-limit, reveals a transversity quark distribution δq(x) significantly different from the helicity distribution Δq(x) already at a low normalization point, in contrast to the prediction δq(x)≡Δq(x) of the non-relativistic quark model. An estimate for the double spin asymmetry in dilepton production from scattering of transversely polarized protons is given.
