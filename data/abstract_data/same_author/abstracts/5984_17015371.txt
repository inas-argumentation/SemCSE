Analysis of the X(1576) as a tetraquark state with the QCD sum rules
In this letter, we take the point of view that the X(1576) be tetraquark state which consists of a scalar-diquark and an anti-scalar-diquark in relative P -wave, and calculate its mass in the framework of the QCD sum rules approach. The numerical value of the mass mX = (1.66±0.14)GeV is consistent with the experimental data, there may be some tetraquark component in the vector meson X(1576). PACS numbers: 12.38.Lg; 12.39.Mk
