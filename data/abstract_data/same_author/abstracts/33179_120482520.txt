Nuclear shadowing and nuclear momentum conservation in the EMC effect
Abstract The spatial overlap of partons from neighboring nucleons of a nucleus is analyzed in the low- x region and the relationship between the spatially overlapping factor and the nuclear shadowing factor of sea quarks is given. The result shows that, with this nuclear shadowing factor, a good agreement with experimental data in the low- x region can be obtained. The problem of nuclear momentum conservation in the EMC effect is also discussed. In order to keep nuclear momentum conservation, different Q 2 - or x -rescaling parameters for the momentum distributions of valence quarks and sea quarks (gluons) in the nucleon structure function should be employed. With nuclear shadowing and nuclear momentum conservation considered together, the experimental data of the EMC effect can be well explained in the whole x -region.
