Hamiltonicity and circular distance two labellings
A k -circular distance two labelling (or k -c-labelling) of a graph G is a vertex-labelling such that the circular difference ( mod k ) of the labels is at least two for adjacent vertices, and at least one for vertices at distance two. Given G , denote σ(G) the minimum k for which there exists a k -c-labelling of G . Suppose G has n vertices, we prove σ(G)⩽n if G c is Hamiltonian; and σ(G)=n+p v (G c ) otherwise, where p v (G) is the path covering number of G . We give exact values of σ(G) for some families of graphs such that G c is Hamiltonian, and discuss injective k -c-labellings especially for joins and unions of graphs.
