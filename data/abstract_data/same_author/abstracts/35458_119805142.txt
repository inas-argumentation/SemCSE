Recent FOCUS results on charm mixing and CP violation
We summarize recent results on CP violation and mixing based on a high statistics photo-produced charm sample collected by the FOCUS experiment. We compare the lifetimes of two neutral D meson decays: D0 → K−π+ and D0 → K−K+. A mixing parameter for the neutral D system (yCP) is obtained under the assumption that K−π+ is an equal mixture of CP odd and even eigenstates. In addition, we searched for a CP width asymmetry in D+ → K−K+π+, D0 → K−K+ and D0 → π−π+. We found no evidence for CP violation by comparing the decay rates for the particle and antiparticle.
