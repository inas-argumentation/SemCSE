Diagrams with one and two loops in a chiral model and the problem of ordering of operators in the Hamiltonian
The role of the additional terms that arise in the Lagrangian when the ordering of the canonical operators in the Hamiltonian is taken into account is studied. It is shown that in a local field theory with derivatives in the interaction additional terms of two types appear. The terms of the first type cancel the leading divergences in two-loop diagrams of the ''theta'' type. The terms of the second type are proportional to the scalar curvature of the configuration space. The analysis rests on the quantization prescription involving the method of Hamiltonian path integrals - a prescription valid for curvilinear generalized coordinates and spaces with curvature. As an example a chiral model is considered.
