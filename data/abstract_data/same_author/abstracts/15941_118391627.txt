Measurement of hadron composition in charged jets from pp collisions with the ALICE experiment
We report the first measurement of charged pion, kaon and (anti-)proton production in jets from hadron colliders. The measurement was carried out with the ALICE detector using 2 × 10 8 minimum bias pp collisions at a centre-of-mass energy of s = 7 TeV at the LHC. We present the π, K and p transverse momentum ( p T ) spectra, as well as the spectra of the reduced momentum ( z ch ≡ p T track / p T,jet ch ), in charged jets of p T between 5–20 GeV/c. The measurement is compared to Monte Carlo calculations.
