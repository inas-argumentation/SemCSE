Chiral squaring
A bstractWe construct the states and symmetries of N$$ \mathcal{N} $$ = 4 super-Yang-Mills by tensoring two N$$ \mathcal{N} $$ = 1 chiral multiplets and introducing two extra SUSY generators. This allows us to write the maximal N$$ \mathcal{N} $$ = 8 supergravity as four copies of the chiral multiplet. We extend this to higher dimensions and discuss applications to scattering amplitudes.
