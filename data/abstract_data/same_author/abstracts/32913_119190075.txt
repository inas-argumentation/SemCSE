Some results on vector and tensor meson mixing in a generalized QCD-like theory
We present results for the mixing of quark flavor eigenstates to form low-lying vector and tensor meson mass eigenstates in a generalized QCD-like theory with l massless or light, degenerate quarks and one quark of substantial mass, m{sub Q}. We show that as m{sub Q}/{Lambda} gets large, where {Lambda} is the confinement scale, the angle governing this mixing approaches arctan(1/{radical}(l)). We give a generalization of the Gell-Mann Okubo mass relation for this theory. A comparison of the l=2 results with data on {omega}-{phi} and f{sub 2}(1275)-f{sub 2}{sup '}(1525) mixing is included.
