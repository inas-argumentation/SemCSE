Lepton pair decays of the K L meson in the light-front model
We analyze ${K}_{L}$ lepton pair decays of ${K}_{L}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{l}^{+}{l}^{\ensuremath{-}}\ensuremath{\gamma}$ and ${K}_{L}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{l}^{+}{l}^{\ensuremath{-}}{l}^{\ensuremath{'}+}{l}^{\ensuremath{'}\ensuremath{-}}(l,{l}^{\ensuremath{'}}=e,\ensuremath{\mu})$ within the framework of the light-front QCD approach (LFQA). With the ${K}_{L}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\gamma}}^{*}{\ensuremath{\gamma}}^{*}$ form factors evaluated in a model with the LFQA, we calculate the decay branching ratios and find that our results are all consistent with the experimental data. In addition, we study ${K}_{L}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{l}^{+}{l}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ decays. We point out that our prediction for ${K}_{L}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{e}^{+}{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ is about $20%$ smaller than that in chiral perturbation theory. We also discuss whether one could extract the short-distance physics from ${K}_{L}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\mu}}^{+}{\ensuremath{\mu}}^{\ensuremath{-}}.$
