Supersymmetric effects on heavy charged Higgs boson production in hadron colliders
Abstract The production of a heavy supersymmetric charged Higgs boson ( M H ± ⩾ 220 GeV ) at the Tevatron and at the LHC is studied. We include the leading one-loop quantum effects within the MSSM in the relevant high tanβ region. Whereas the chances for the Tevatron are limited, and critically depend on the size of the unknown NLO QCD effects, at the LHC the discovery range is more comfortable and may extend the reach above M H ± = 1 TeV .
