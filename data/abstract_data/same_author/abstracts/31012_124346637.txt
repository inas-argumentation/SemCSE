Some issues in polarimetry at RHIC
This talk was given in conjunction with a talk by F. Mariam on the toroidal magnet design for a pion inclusive polarimeter. Here we describe several issues including the 200 GeV pion inclusive asymmetry data from Fermilab E704, the possible uses of a calorimeter with the Toroidal Magnet polarimeter design, and some aspects of polarization Luminosity measurements.
