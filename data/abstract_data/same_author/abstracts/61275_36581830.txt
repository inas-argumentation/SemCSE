In particular, posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD) or symptoms of PTSD havebeenreportedinapproximatelyone-thirdofsuchpopulations 1 and it has been associated with significant impairmentinsocio-occupationalfunctioning.Providingevidencebased psychotherapy for large clusters of individuals with posttraumatic stress is an ongoing public health challenge. In the 1980s, Latin American researchers devised a PTSD treatment for survivors of state-sponsored violence. Testimony therapy, as it came to be known, requires autobiographical narrative as a key component 2 and also incorporates a social element into the therapeutic encounter. Based on theories that consider collective traumatization to be as significant as individual traumatization, testimony therapy aims to move the trauma narrative outside individual psychopathology and to reframe the experience in a social and historical context. 3,4
