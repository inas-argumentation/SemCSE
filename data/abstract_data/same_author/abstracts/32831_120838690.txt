How good is nonet symmetry for pseudoscalar mesons
It is pointed out that the assumption of nonet symmetry for ΔS =1 weak interactions is equivalent to excluding non-penguin contributions (i.e. the so-called “figure-eight” graphs). The radiative decays K L → γγ and K L → π + π − γ provide a most sensitive test on nonet symmetry. We find that this symmetry for ΔS =1 weak transitions of the pseudoscalar nonet holds at the level of (15–25)%. Implications on the low-energy penguin dominance of the ΔI= 1 2 K →ππ amplitude, the long-distance contribution to the K L −K S mass difference from single-particle intermediate states, and direct CP violation in K L →γγ are discussed.
