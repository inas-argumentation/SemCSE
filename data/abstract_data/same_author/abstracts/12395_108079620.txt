Overview and status of the University of Washington Nuclear Physics Lab booster project
We are in the process of building a superconducting booster LINAC using quarter wave resonators of lead plated copper. In this paper we give a brief description of the overall design of the accelerator and then present a status report on the construction. The overall layout of the Booster is shown. As is the case with many Booster accelerators, there are features of the design which are dictated by space constraints. In our case, the space in the tandem vault that the booster will occupy is part of the existing building.
