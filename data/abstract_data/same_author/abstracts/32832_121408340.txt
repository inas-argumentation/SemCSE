Photon-photon scattering close to the forward direction
Delbr\"uck scattering is the elastic scattering of a photon by a static Coulomb field via electron-positron pair creation. At high energies, there are two natural scales for the momentum transfer $\ensuremath{\Delta}$, namely, $m$ and $\frac{{m}^{2}}{\ensuremath{\omega}}$, where $m$ is the mass of the electron, and $\ensuremath{\omega}$ is the photon energy. When $\ensuremath{\Delta}$ is much larger than the smaller scale $\frac{{m}^{2}}{\ensuremath{\omega}}$, the impact factor representation holds at high energies. The impact picture is here extended to give also the high-energy behavior of the Delbr\"uck scattering amplitude when $\ensuremath{\Delta}$ is comparable to $\frac{{m}^{2}}{\ensuremath{\omega}}$. The result can be expressed in terms of generalized hypergeometric functions, which reproduces the known result in the forward direction when $\ensuremath{\Delta}$ is set equal to zero, and also joins smoothly to the impact factor representation when $\ensuremath{\Delta}$ is much larger than $\frac{{m}^{2}}{\ensuremath{\omega}}$. In the present analysis, the fine-structure constant $\ensuremath{\alpha}$ is assumed to be small, but not $Z\ensuremath{\alpha}$. In other words, all terms of the form $\ensuremath{\alpha}{(Z\ensuremath{\alpha})}^{n}$ in the amplitude are taken into account. It is also shown that the result for $\ensuremath{\Delta}\ensuremath{\ll}m$ is independent of the mass of the target, and hence is in particular applicable to Compton scattering by an electron.
