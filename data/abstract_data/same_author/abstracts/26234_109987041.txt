DEVELOPMENTS IN MAGNET POWER CONVERTERS AT THE SRS
A project to upgrade the magnet power converters of the SRS has commenced to ensure its efficient operation for its remaining operational lifetime. A recent risk analysis of the facilities equipment identified that the main areas for concern were the Storage Ring magnet power converters, kicker and septum pulse power supplies and the Booster Dipole “White Circuit” and associated power converters. This report details the development and replacement programs currently active at Daresbury Laboratory, including future work identified to support and improve SRS utilisation.
