Longitudinal polarization of positrons in Na-22 decay
Detailed descriptions are given of previously reported measurements of the longitudinal polarization (${P}_{L}$) of positrons in the allowed and unique second forbidden weak decays of $^{22}\mathrm{Na}$. The measurements were performed relative to the allowed decay of $^{68}\mathrm{Ga}$ by application of time-resolved spectroscopy of the n=1 hyperfine states of positronium formed in powder media. Assuming the theoretical value ${P}_{L}$${(\mathrm{}}^{68}$Ga)=\ensuremath{\beta}(0.999\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.001), the results are ${P}_{L}$${(\mathrm{}}^{22}$Na, ${3}^{+}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${2}^{+}$)=\ensuremath{\beta}(+1.003\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.011) and ${P}_{L}$${(\mathrm{}}^{22}$Na, ${3}^{+}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${0}^{+}$)=\ensuremath{\beta}(+1.00\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.05). The allowed decay result (combined with other $^{22}\mathrm{Na}$ experimental data on the decay rate, the capture ratio, the spectral shape, the \ensuremath{\beta}\ensuremath{\gamma} directional correlation, and the weak magnetic form factor) yields a determination of the full weak form factor structure of the transition through second order in recoil. The experimental value for the capture ratio is currently considered to be anomalously low compared to theoretical estimates. Our polarization result is not, however, sufficiently accurate to definitively favor one or the other value. The forbidden decay result, together with model-dependent nuclear structure calculations, restricts the contribution of previously neglected higher-order corrections to the allowed decay to be less than ${10}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}8}$.
