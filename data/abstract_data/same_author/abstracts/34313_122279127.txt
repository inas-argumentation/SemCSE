Difficulties in the determination of the gluon distribution function from electroproduction of charmed quarks
The QCD corrections to the charmed (anti) quark inclusive differential distributions in photoproduction and electroproduction are investigated. Here electroproduction is treated in the Weizsacker-Williams approximation. We show that at HERA energies the extraction of the gluon distribution function in the proton is hampered by the presence of the resolved component of the photon.
