Evidence for a new Sigma* resonance with J**P = 1/2- in the old data of K- p ---> Lambda pi+ pi- reaction
Distinctive patterns are predicted by quenched quark models and unquenched quark models for the lowest SU(3) baryon nonet with spin parity J P = 1/2 - . While the quenched quark models predict the lowest 1/2- ∑* resonance to be above 1600 MeV, the unquenched quark models predict it to be around ∑*(1385) energy. Here we reexamine some old data of the K - p → ∧π + π - reaction and find that besides the well-established ∑*(1385) with J p = 3/2 + , there is indeed some evidence for the possible existence of a new ∑* resonance with J P = 1/2- around the same mass but with broader decay width. Higher statistic data on relevant reactions are needed to clarify the situation.
