Coherent production on nuclei does not measure total cross sections for unstable particles
We calculate diffractive production in nucleon-nucleus collisions using a parton model. We fit the result by the standard optical-model formula, and obtain estimates of 12--30 mb for the cross section of the diffractively produced system on a nucleon. The actual value of that cross section in the model is 65 mb. Estimates for the total cross section of an unstable hadron which are based on the A dependence of coherent production should therefore not be trusted.
