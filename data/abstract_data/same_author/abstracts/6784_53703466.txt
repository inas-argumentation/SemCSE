Neutrinoless double beta decay and neutrino masses
Neutrinoless double beta decay (0νβ β ) is a promising test for lepton number violating physics beyond the standard model (SM) of particle physics. There is a deep connection between this decay and the phenomenon of neutrino masses. In particular, we will discuss the relation between 0νβ β and Majorana neutrino masses provided by the so-called Schechter- Valle theorem in a quantitative way. Furthermore, we will present an experimental cross check to discriminate 0νβ β from unknown nuclear background using only one isotope, i.e., within one experiment.
