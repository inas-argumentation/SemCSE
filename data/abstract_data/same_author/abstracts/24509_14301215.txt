N = 2 Topological Yang-Mills Theories and Donaldson's Polynomials
The N = 2 topological Yang-Mills and holomorphic Yang-Mills theories on simply connected compact Kahler surfaces with pg ≥ 1 are re-examined. The N = 2 symmetry is clarified in terms of a Dolbeault model of the equivariant cohomology. We realize the non-algebraic part of Donaldson's polynomial invariants as well as the algebraic part. We calculate Donaldson's polynomials on H2,0 (S, Z) ⊕ H0,2 (S, Z).
