The muon anomalous magnetic moment in broken supersymmetric theories
We consider the one-loop corrections to the muon anomalous magnetic moment in a class of spontaneously broken supersymmetric gauge theories. We add to the well known contributions from photon, W and Z exchange the effects of the corresponding fermionic superpartners, photino, w-ino and z-ino, and that of the massless goldstino. We reobtain the bound μ ≳ 15 GeV for the common mass of the scalar partners of the muon and the neutrino, as well as an excluded region in the μ − M ω plane, M ω being the w-ino mass. Consideration of goldstino exchange gives rise to the new bound d ≳1350GeV 2 for the order parameter d of supersymmetry breaking. The addition of a gaugino Majorana mass is also considered. In conjunction with chiral breaking scalar particle mixing, such a mass term leads to model dependent corrections linear in the muon mass.
