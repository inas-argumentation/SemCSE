A device to produce high center-of-mass energy e+e collisions - accelerator beam colliding with a stored beam☆
If 20-GeV electrons from the Stanford Linear Accelerator collide with 2-GeV electrons (or positrons) circulating in the storage ring now under construction at SLAC, then the reaction center-of-mass energy will be E c.m. = 12.6 GeV. The luminosity of this device is calculated to be about 2.4 × 10 29 cm −2 sec −1 , and the number of e+e→+e+e+χ reactions at this energy is estimated to be about 60 h −1 when χ = μ + + μ − , about 10 h −1 when χ = π + + π − , and about 1 h −1 when χ = π 0 or η .
