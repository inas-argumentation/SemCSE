Weak radiative B decays in the standard model and beyond
A discussion of the inclusive decays B → Xsγ and B → Xse+e− is given (Xs is a strange hadron), along with possible exclusive decay results from application of the Isgur‐Wise symmetry. The leading logarithmic strong interaction corrections are included. Decay rates for B → Xsγ include those predictions from the minimal standard model, the standard model with two Higgs doublets, and the minimal supersymmetric model. Decay rates for B → Xse+e− are reviewed in the standard model and the two Higgs doublet model.
