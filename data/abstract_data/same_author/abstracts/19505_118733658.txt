Generalized Ricci dark energy in Horava-Lifshitz gravity
In this work, we have considered generalized Ricci dark energy in the Horava-Lifshitz gravity. We have reconstructed Hubble’s parameter in terms of fractional densities. We have viewed the equation-of-state parameter in this situation. Also, we have examined the behavior of the deceleration parameter and investigated the nature of the statefinder diagnostics. The equation-of-state parameter has exhibited quintom-like behavior and from the plot of the deceleration parameter we have observed a transition from decelerating to accelerating phase of the universe.
