Entropy of quantum field in toroidal black hole without brick wall
The calculation of the brick-wall model gives the entropy as two terms: one is proportional to the horizon area and the other is a constant. Then they renormalized the entropy by introducing the Newton’s constant and the entropy correction arising from the higher curvature effective gravitational action. Finally, they got the renormalized entropy whose leading term is proportional to the horizon area of the black hole and the others is precisely the one-loop correction arising from the higher curvature terms of the gravitational action. Their work confirmed the suggestion by Susskind and Uglum [25] that these divergences have the correct form to be absorbed in the Bekenstein–Hawking formula as a renormalization of Newton’s constant.
