APPLICATION OF PCS IN NSRL CONTROL SYSTEM
The upgrade of the National Synchrotron Radiation Laboratory (NSRL) control system is currently being implemented using EPICS (Experimental Physics and Industrial Control System). For cost saving purposes, we use a large number of PCs. PCs play a dominant role as I/O controllers (IOCs) and device controllers in the new control system. In this paper, the application of PCs in the NSRL control system is described.
