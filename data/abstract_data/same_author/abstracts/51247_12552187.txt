Parallel algorithms for evaluating sequences of set-manipulation operations
Given an off-line sequence <italic>S</italic> of <italic>n</italic> set-manipulation operations, we investigate the parallel complexity of evaluating <italic>S</italic> (i.e., finding the response to every operation in <italic>S</italic> and returning the resulting set). We show that the problem of evaluating <italic>S</italic> is in <italic>NC</italic> for various combinations of common set-manipulation operations. Once we establish membership in <italic>NC</italic> (or, if membership in <italic>NC</italic> is obvious), we develop techniques for improving the time and/or processor complexity.
