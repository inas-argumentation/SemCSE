Lifetime measurements of excited states in neutron-rich Ar44,46 populated via a multinucleon transfer reaction
Lifetimes of low-lying excited states of the neutron-rich {sup 44,46}Ar nuclei, populated via multinucleon transfer reactions, are measured by means of the differential recoil distance Doppler shift method. The extracted electromagnetic transition probabilities are compared with previous intermediate-energy Coulomb-excitation measurements and with large-scale shell-model calculations. The increase in the deduced B(E2;2{sup +{yields}}0{sup +}) transition probability from {sup 44}Ar to the closed-shell nucleus {sup 46}Ar contradicts the earlier results of Coulomb-excitation experiments. Shell-model calculations using different effective interactions agree with the new measured values.
