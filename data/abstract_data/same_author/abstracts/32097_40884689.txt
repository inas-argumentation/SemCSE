On the Wilson coefficient of the penguin operator.
Recently an additional contribution to the real part of the Wilson coefficient function c/sub 5/ of the dominant penguin operator O/sub 5/ in K..-->..2..pi.. decay was found by Bardeen, Buras, and Gerard. We show that in the effective-Hamiltonian approach used by Gilman and Wise with the ''heavy''-charm-quark approximation, the additional contribution to c/sub 5/ originates from the penguin diagrams with u-quark loops which are left after the charm quark is integrated out. Penguin diagrams at the low-momentum scale are briefly discussed.
