Transverse-Mass Spectra in Heavy-Ion Collisions at energies E(lab) = 2-160-GeV/nucleon
Transverse-mass spectra of protons, pions, and kaons produced in collisions of heavy nuclei are analyzed within the model of three-fluid dynamics. It was demonstrated that this model consistently reproduces these spectra in wide ranges of incident energies, 4A GeV < or approx. E{sub lab} < or approx.160A GeV, rapidity bins, and centralities of the collisions. In particular, the model describes the 'step-like' dependence of kaon inverse slopes on the incident energy. The key point of this explanation is interplay of hydrodynamic expansion of the system with its dynamical freeze-out.
