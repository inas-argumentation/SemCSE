Quantum gravitational measure for three-geometries
The gravitational measure on an arbitrary topological three-manifold is constructed. The nontrivial dependence of the measure on the conformal factor is discussed. We show that only in the case of a compact manifold with boundary the measure acquires a nontrivial dependence on the conformal factor which is a surface term given by the Liouville action. A nontrivial jacobian (the divergent part of it) also generates the Einstein-Hilbert action. The Hartle-Hawking wave function of the universe is given in terms of the Liouville action.
