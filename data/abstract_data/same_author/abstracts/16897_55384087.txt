First Experience with the Superconducting Asymmetric Wiggler of the DELTA Facility
The superconducting asymmetric wiggler (SAW) is a unique insertion device operated at the DELTA facility. This multipole wiggler magnet with 21 poles provides the possibility of both symmetric operation up to 2.8 T and asymmetric operation up to 5.3 T, featuring the possibility to produce circular polarized photons of about 8 keV in a 1.5 GeV storage ring. This paper will give a detailed description of the device itself, and will further describe the first results of the operation of this wiggler in the storage ring.
