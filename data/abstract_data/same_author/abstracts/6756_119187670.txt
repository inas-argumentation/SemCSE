ANGULAR EIGENVALUES OF HIGHER-DIMENSIONAL KERR-(A)dS BLACK HOLES WITH TWO ROTATIONS
In this paper, following the work of Chen, Lu and Pope, we present the general metric for Kerr-(A)dS black holes with two rotations. The corresponding Klein-Gordon equation is separated explicitly, from which we develop perturbative expansions for the angular eigenvalues in powers of the rotation parameters with D ≥ 6.
