Shell-model calculation for two-neutrino double beta decay of Ca48
The 2{nu}{beta}{beta} decay matrix element of {sup 48}Ca has been studied in a large basis shell-model space. The theoretical and experimental {beta}{sup {minus}} and {beta}{sup +} spectra and their relation to 2{nu}{beta}{beta} are discussed. A new empirical effective interaction is found to give the best agreement to {beta}{sup {minus}} and {beta}{sup +} spectra with the effective Gamow-Teller operator {tilde {sigma}}{ital t}=0.77{sigma}{ital t}. The predicted {ital T}{sub 1/2}=1.9{times}10{sup 19} yr differs by a factor of 2 from the present experimental limit of {ital T}{sub 1/2}{gt}3.6{times}10{sup 19} yr. We also compare the matrix elements obtained at various levels of the truncation in the shell-model space.
