Absolute minima of the Higgs potential for the 75 of SU(5)
The problem of minimising the Higgs potential for the 75-dimensional representation of SU(5) is considered. The most general form of the potential is established. In the absence of a cubic term it is shown that the possible symmetry breaking patterns include SU(3)*SU(2)*U(1), and a new counterexample to Michel's conjecture (1979), is found.
