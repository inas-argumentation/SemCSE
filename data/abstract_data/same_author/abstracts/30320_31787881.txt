Pion absorption on the diproton.
A partial-wave analysis of recent low-energy pion-absorption data on a diproton (/sup 1/S/sub 0/) in /sup 3/He is supplemented by proton-neutron final-state--interaction phase shifts and mixing parameters, yielding a model-independent determination of the absorption amplitude. Several possible amplitudes describe the existing angular distribution, but these can essentially be distinguished by a polarization measurement.
