Planar domain walls emitting and absorbing electromagnetic radiation.
The emission and absorption of electromagnetic radiation of planar domain walls are studied. Exact solutions of the Einstein-Maxwell equations, which represent such models, are given. It is found that the rate of the emission or absorption is quite model dependent. A planar domain wall can lose nearly all of its energy stored only after a few times of emission. The same wall, on the other hand, can also absorb electromagnetic radiation, and has its surface energy density rapidly increase to a very high value.
