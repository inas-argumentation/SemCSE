Estimates for the probability of detecting neutral heavy leptons in hadron-initiated interactions
A consideration of two mechanisms for the production of neutral heavy leptons (${L}^{0}$'s) in hadron-initiated interactions lead to an estimate of ${10}^{\ensuremath{-}39}$ ${\mathrm{cm}}^{2}$ for the cross section per nucleon for observing the trimuons associated with the production of $\ensuremath{\mu}$-coupled ${L}^{0}$'s. This is well below the estimated background due to the production of a pair of ${\ensuremath{\rho}}^{0}$'s which decay into $\ensuremath{\mu}$ pairs.
