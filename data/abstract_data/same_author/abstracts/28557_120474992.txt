Correlations between two hadrons at large transverse momenta
We have studied the interactions of 400-GeV/c protons with beryllium nuclei and observed hadrons produced at large transverse momenta (${p}_{\ensuremath{\perp}}$) back to back near 90\ifmmode^\circ\else\textdegree\fi{} in the proton-nucleon center-of-momentum system. When both transverse momenta exceed about 2.6 GeV/c, we observe that the probability per single hadron of observing a second high-${p}_{\ensuremath{\perp}}$ hadron on the opposite side approximately scales in the ratio of the two transverse momenta (${x}_{e}$ scaling). Quantum-number correlations between the opposite-going high-${p}_{\ensuremath{\perp}}$ hadrons are weak.
