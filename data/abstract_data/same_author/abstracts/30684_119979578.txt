Universality in the mixed SU(2) lattice gauge theory: Nonperturbative approach to the ratio of Λ-parameters
The mixed SU(2) lattice gauge theory (LGT) is approximately represented as an effective SU(2) LGT with Wilson's action. This approach is applied to the nonperturbative calculation of the ratio of Λ -parameters in the mixed SU(2) LGT. It is shown that our formulas describe the Monte Carlo data fairly so that universality holds in the mixed SU(2) LGT.
