Forward Bremsstrahlung in Hard Hadron Collisions
In hard hadron collisions the jets provide a "tag" on the multiparton bremsstrahlung process. Exact evolution equations govern the distributions of the partons associated with the colliding partons. We study the amount and properties of the forward bremsstrahlung numerically. In events with transverse energy above 80 GeV the jet contribution dominates the bremsstrahlung.
