Implications of heavy quark symmetry and chiral dynamics
The low‐energy strong, electromagnetic and heavy‐flavor‐conserving nonleptonic weak interactions of heavy hadrons with light pseudoscalar mesons are studied in a formalism which incorporates both heavy quark symmetry and chiral symmetry. Some implications are discussed, including the strong decays of D* and Σc, the electromagnetic decays of D*, B*, Σc, Ξ’c, the semileptonic weak decays B→D(D*)πlν, and the nonleptonic weak decays Ξc→Λcπ, Ωc→Ξ’cπ.
