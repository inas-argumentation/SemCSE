Electroweak radiative corrections to single Higgs-boson production in e+e− annihilation
We have calculated the complete electroweak O (α) radiative corrections to the single Higgs-boson production processes e + e − →ν l ν l H ( l =e, μ , τ ) in the electroweak Standard Model. Initial-state radiation beyond O (α) is included in the structure-function approach. The calculation of the corrections is briefly described, and numerical results are presented for the total cross-section. In the G μ scheme, the bulk of the corrections is due to initial-state radiation, which affects the cross-section at the level of −7% at high energies and even more in the ZH threshold region. The remaining bosonic and fermionic corrections are at the level of a few percent. The confusing situation in the literature regarding differing results for the fermionic corrections to this process is clarified.
