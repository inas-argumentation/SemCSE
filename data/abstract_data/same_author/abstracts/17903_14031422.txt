Cosmological constant, gauge hierarchy and warped geometry
It is suggested that the mechanism responsible for the resolution of the gauge hierarchy problem within the warped geometry framework can be generalized to provide a new explanation of the extremely tiny vacuum energy density ${\ensuremath{\rho}}_{V}$ suggested by recent observations. We illustrate the mechanism with some 5D examples in which the true vacuum energy is assumed to vanish, and ${\ensuremath{\rho}}_{V}$ is associated with a false vacuum energy such that ${\ensuremath{\rho}}_{V}^{1/4}\ensuremath{\sim}{\mathrm{TeV}}^{2}{/M}_{\mathrm{Pl}}\ensuremath{\sim}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}3}\mathrm{eV},$ where ${M}_{\mathrm{Pl}}$ denotes the reduced Planck mass. We also consider a quintessence-like solution to the dark energy problem.
