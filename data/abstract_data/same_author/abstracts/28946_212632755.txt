The Geodesic Properties of the Hypercylindrical Spacetime
We study the geodesic properties of the static hypercylindrical spacetimes in (4+1) dimensions. The effective potential analysis and gravitational lensing effects are studied in the static spacetime. We give the marginal orbits by the effective potential in timelike case. This article is prepared for the proceedings of The Nineteenth Workshop on General Relativity and Gravitation in Japan (JGRG19), 2009.
