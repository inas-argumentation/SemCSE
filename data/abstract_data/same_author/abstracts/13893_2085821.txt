Applying Mellin-Barnes technique and Gröbner bases to the three-loop static potential
The Mellin‐Barnes technique to evaluate master integrals and the algorithm called FIRE to solve IBP relations with the help of Grobner bases are briefly revie wed. In FIRE, an extension of the classical Buchberger algorithm to construct Grobner ba ses is combined with the well-known Laporta algorithm. It is explained how both techniques are used when evaluating the three-loop correction to the static QCD quark potential. First results are presented: the coefficients of n 3 and n 2 , where nl is the number of light quarks.
