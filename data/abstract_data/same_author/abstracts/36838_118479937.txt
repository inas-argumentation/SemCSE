Comment on `A collider signature of the supersymmetric golden region'
We show that Z polarization measurement is useful for distinguishing the signal decay chain of the `golden region' [1] in the minimal supersymmetric standard model (MSSM) parameter space, which involves the stop decay of 2 → 1+Z, from other decay chains involving the neutralino decay of 20 → 10+Z in different MSSM scenarios. Within a given decay chain, Z polarization can also serve as an indicator of the mass difference of stops or neutralinos.
