One loop effects of supersymmetry on $Z$ decays into $b \bar{b}$
Virtual effects of supersymmetry on the partial width and on the branching ratio are calculated in the minimal supersymmetric standard model. We found that the virtual gluino contribution to the width is negligibly small if the squark and gluino masses take their experiment-allowed values. Also, we have calculated the contributions to Rb from the charged Higgs and charginos. As to the discrepancy between two previous works about the virtual chargino correction, our calculations provide a confirmation.
