Constraints on a new vector boson coupled to baryons
Abstract We consider several processes which are sensitive to the existence of a light gauge boson coupled to baryon number. In the mass region between 10 Mev and M π ≈140 MeV the best limits on the coupling come from rare π 0 decays, while a boson with mass between M π and 2 M π may be observed in K + decays, or η decays. Heavier gauge bosons will decay hadronically and are harder to find, also limits on their coupling are subject to strong interaction uncertainties.
