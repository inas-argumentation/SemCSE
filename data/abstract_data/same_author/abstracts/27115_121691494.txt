Study of the structure of hadronic events at LEP with L3
Global event shape variables and charged particle spectra are studied from hadronic collected by the L3 detector corresponding to hadronic center of mass energies between 30 and 189 GeV. Strong coupling constant (αs) and active number of flavours (Nf) are determined from the event shapes to be: αs(Mz) = 0.1220 ± 0.0015(exp) ± 0.0060(theo), and Nf = 4.4 ± 1.0(exp) ± 2.0(theo) respectively. The energy evolution of the moments of the event shapes have been fitted to a combination of O(αs2) calculations and power law parametrization of the non-perturbative part, and the universality of the power law behaviour is observed.
