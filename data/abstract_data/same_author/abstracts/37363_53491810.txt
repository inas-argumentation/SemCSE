Searches for the Higgs Boson at the Tevatron
We report on direct searches for Higgs bosons carried out by the CDF and DO Collaborations in proton‐antiproton collisions at a center‐of‐mass energy of 1.96 TeV. Limits on the production cross section times branching fraction will be presented and a brief discussion of searches for supersymmetric Higgs bosons will be given. We will focus mainly on recent results only (those released in 2006), based on data sets of 260 – 950 fb−1 of data collected between 2004 – 2006.
