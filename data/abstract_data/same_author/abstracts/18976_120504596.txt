Improved high-energy bound on the logarithmic derivative of scattering amplitudes
It is proved that the logarithmic derivative of the absorptive part of the scattering amplitude with respect to momentum transfer $t$ has an improved rigorous upper bound $\frac{(7\mathrm{ln}s)}{[2\sqrt{t}(2\ensuremath{-}\sqrt{t})]}$ for a sequence of $s\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\infty}$. It is also observed that the slope of the Regge trajectory is not only rigorously bounded from above by $\frac{7}{[2\sqrt{t}(2\ensuremath{-}\sqrt{t})]}$, but also by $\frac{1}{[2\sqrt{t}(2\ensuremath{-}\sqrt{t})]}$, if the total cross section increases in any fashion as $s\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\infty}$. Here $s$ is energy squared and $0ltl4$ (in units of pion mass).
