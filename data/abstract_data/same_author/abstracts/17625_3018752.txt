A generalization of Slavnov-extended non-commutative gauge theories
We consider a non-commutative U(1) gauge theory in 4 dimensions with a modified Slavnov term [1] which looks similar to the 3-dimensional BF model. In choosing a space-like axial gauge fixing we find a new vector supersymmetry which is used to show that the model is free of UV/IR problems, just as in the previously discussed model [2, 3]. Finally, we present generalizations of our proposed model to higher dimensions.
