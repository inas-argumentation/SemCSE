rho-meson properties in a nonpolynomial Lagrangian theory
Two possible explanations of the existence and the properties of the p meson are analyzed within the framework of the nonpolynomial Lagrangian. It is found that the p meson bootstrapping reciprocally with the B meson in the static Yrcu scattering provides a qualitatively satisfactory description of the p-meson properties. On the other hand, the forces in the 7t. 7). scattering are far too weak to generate the p meson. The calculations are performed within the framework of the N//D method, and are free of a cutoff parameter.
