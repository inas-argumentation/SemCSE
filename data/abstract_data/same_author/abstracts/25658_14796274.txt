From AdS black holes to supersymmetric flux-branes
We show that AdS black hole solutions admit an analytical continuation to become magnetic flux-branes. Although a static BPS AdS black hole generally has a naked singularity, the BPS flux-brane can be regular everywhere with an appropriate choice of U ( 1 ) -charges. This flux-brane interpolates from AdS D − 2 × H 2 at small distance to an asymptotic AdS D -type metric with an AdS D − 2 × S 1 boundary. We also obtain a smooth cosmological solution of de Sitter Einstein–Maxwell gravity which flows from dS 2 × S D − 2 in the infinite past to a dS D -type metric, with an S D − 2 × S 1 boundary, in the infinite future.
