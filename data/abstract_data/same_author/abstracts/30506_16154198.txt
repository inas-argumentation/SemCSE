THE HIGH-ENERGY LIMIT OF INCLUSIVE AND DIFFRACTIVE DEEP INELASTIC SCATTERING IN QCD
Following recent progresses in the understanding of high-energy scattering in QCD, we derive the first phenomenological consequences of Pomeron loops, in the context of both inclusive and diffractive deep inelastic scattering. In particular, we discuss diffusive scaling, a new scaling law that emerges for sufficiently high energies and up to very large values of $Q^2,$ well above the proton saturation momentum.
