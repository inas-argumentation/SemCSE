Vacuum oscillations of solar neutrinos: correlation between spectrum distortion and seasonal variations
We show that for vacuum oscillation solution of the solar neutrino problem there is a strict correlation between energy spectrum distortion and amplitude of seasonal variations of the solar neutrino flux. The slope parameter which describes distortion of the recoil electron energy spectrum and the seasonal asymmetry of fluxes has been calculated in relevant region of the oscillation parameters. The correlation of the slope and asymmetry gives crucial criteria for identification of this solution.
