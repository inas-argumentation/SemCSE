Oblique electroweak corrections and new physics.
Oblique electroweak parameters {ital {tilde S}}, {ital {tilde T}}, and {ital {tilde U}}, defined so as to be nonvanishing only for physics beyond the standard model, are determined by direct use of high-statistics data from the CERN {ital e}{sup +}{ital e{minus}} collider LEP at different energy points around the {ital Z} peak. Additional information from related electroweak measurements are used as constraints. The results are {ital {tilde S}}={minus}0.76{plus minus}0.71, {ital {tilde T}}={minus}0.70{plus minus}0.49, and {ital {tilde U}}={minus}0.11{plus minus}1.07. The consequent restrictions on extra fermion generations and an extra neutral gauge boson are discussed.
