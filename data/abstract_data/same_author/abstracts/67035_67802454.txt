Whitney-Cartan product formulae
The present invention is a device for setting a region of interest for a scintillation camera. It is employed for determining whether or not the coordinate signals X and Y which represent the positions of incident gamma rays are located within a specified region which is set by designating arbitrary positions and shape(s) on a rectangular coordinate system provided on the visual field of a camera. This device includes a region setting circuit for discriminating whether the coordinate signals X and Y are located within the specified region through comparison of the potential of the sum of the square of the values of the coordinate signals X and Y with a reference voltage.
