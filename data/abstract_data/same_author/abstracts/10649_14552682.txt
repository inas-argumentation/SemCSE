Probing large extra dimensions with spin configuration of top quark pair production at the JLC
We explore signatures of large extra dimensions with a polarized electron/positrn beam at the Joint Linear Collider (JLC). We point out that spin informations can be useful to study indirect signals for large extra dimensions due to spin-2 nature of Kaluza-Klein gravitons. The spin configurations of the top quark pair production at e + e and γγ collisions provide an unique testing ground to search for effects of the large extra dimensions.
