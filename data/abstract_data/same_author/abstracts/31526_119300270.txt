All-Order Corrections and Multi-Jet Rates
We discuss results from a recently proposed all-order description of hard, radiative corrections to certain multi-jet processes at hadron colliders. The descr iption is based on obtaining an all-order estimate of the t-channel singularities of scattering amplitudes. As a simp le example, we illustrate the similarities between qQ and qg-scattering. In particular, we discuss how at tree-level, a ll nonsuppressed helicity-amplitudes for these processes consi st of a pure t-channel pole. This structure is used in the construction of all-order approximations.
