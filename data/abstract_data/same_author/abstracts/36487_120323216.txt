Elastic and inelastic scattering of 12 C + 12 C at E/A≥10 MeV and the deformation parameters
Elastic and inelastic scattering of /sup 12/C+ /sup 12/C, especially excitations of the ground-rotational-band members in /sup 12/C, have been studied at 139.5 and 158.8 MeV. Although reasonable fits to the angular distributions for the band members were obtained with a shallow optical potential, rainbowlike gross bumps observed at large angles in the elastic scattering were not explained at all by coupled-channel calculations for the rotational band. The present results of Vertical Bar..beta../sub 2/RVertical Bar and Vertical Bar..beta../sub 4/RVertical Bar obtained by the coupled-channel calculations are in good agreement with previously reported results, except some from /sup 12/C+ /sup 12/C scattering. These analyses indicate a significant difference in the deformation lengths derived depending upon the signs of the deformations. The sign of the deformation parameters is also discussed.
