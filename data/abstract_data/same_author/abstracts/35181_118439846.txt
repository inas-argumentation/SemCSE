Disk and elliptical galaxies within renormalization group improved gravity
We review our contribution to infrared Renormalization Group (RG) effects to General Relativity in the context of disk and elliptical galaxies. We also present the new RGGR results for NGC 4374 with a different convention for the baryonic part and a new comparison between RGGR, MOND and NFW for the dwarf disk galaxy DDO 47. Considering the effective action approach to Quantum Field Theory in curved background, we argued that the proper RG energy scale, in the weak field limit, should be related to the Newtonian potential. In the galaxy context, even without dark matter, this led to a remarkably small gravitational coupling G variation, while also capable of generating galaxy rotation and dispersion curves of similar quality to the the best phenomenological dark matter profiles, and significantly better than the NFW profile or MOND.
