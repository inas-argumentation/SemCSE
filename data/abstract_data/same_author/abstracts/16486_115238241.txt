Hadronic fluctuations and correlations at nonzero chemical potential
Universitat Bielefeld, Fakultat fur Physik, Universitatsstrasse 25, D-33615 Bielefeld, GermanyE-mail:schmidt@physik.uni-bielefeld.deWe present a lattice study of ﬂuctuations and correlations a mong the conserved charges baryonnumber and strangeness in (2+1)-ﬂavor QCD. The lattice calculations are based on a Taylor ex-pansion of the pressure. Results are presented at zero and nonzero density on lattices with fourand six time slices, corresponding to a lattice spacing of a ≈ 0.25 fm and a ≈ 0.16 fm, respec-tively. The quark masses are almost physical, i.e. the light quark mass has been chosen to onetenth of the physical strange quark mass while the strange quark mass has been set to its physicalvalue.
