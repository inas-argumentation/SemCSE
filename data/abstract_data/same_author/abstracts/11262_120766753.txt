Stability of the electron in the SU(5) and GWS models
Within the framework of the SU(5) model and by using non-zero vacuum expectation values for both charged and neutral Higgs fields, the lifetime of the electron ( tau e) can be expressed in terms of the photon mass (mgamma ). Using the upper limit of 10-24 GeV for mgamma given by studies of Jupiter's magnetic field, a lower limit of 1027 years is obtained for tau e, while the upper limit of 10-36 GeV for mgamma given by studies of galactic magnetic fields yielded a lower limit of 1051 years for tau e. The values for tau e can be obtained without giving rise to light Higgs particles. In contrast, the standard GWS model with two Higgs doublets gave rise to comparable values for tau e, but yielded a light Higgs particle with masses similar to mgamma .
