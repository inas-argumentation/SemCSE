Management Assistance for Work First via a Dynamic Website
The purpose of the Work First project is to assist the state Dept. of Health and Human Services (DHHS) staff and each county Dept. of Social Services (DSS) across the state in the management of welfare reform initiatives. Work First is the cash assistance program in NC. One important objective is to encourage and support program managers to use data on outcomes to assess their performance. This is accomplished by providing them with easy to use and easy to understand comprehensive information on the experiences of families and individuals that have received assistance through their agency over time.
