Spin-12 particles in the field of monopoles
Abstract The equations of motion of a spin - 1 2 particle in the field of a point-like (Abelian and non-Abelian) monopole are solved; helicity states and scattering solutions are constructed. The motion in an extended monopole and dyon source is discussed: charge-exchange scattering amplitudes are computed in the distorted-wave Born approximation. By the introduction of suitable spinors, the separation of variables is accomplished for any value of isospin. The non-existence of fermion zero modes in the field of an SU(2) monopole when T > 1, J > 1 2 is shown.
