The signatures of a neutral vector meson in a realistic one-family technicolour model
We consider the possible experimental signatures of a light neutral vector meson rho 0 in a realistic one-family technicolour model. We construct the lowest-order vector meson operators. By using these operators, we estimate the decay widths of the vector meson rho 0 and discuss the possibility of detecting this particle in hadron colliders or at LEP II.
