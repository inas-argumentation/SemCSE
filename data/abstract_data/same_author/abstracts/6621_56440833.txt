The critical endpoint of the finite temperature phase transition for three flavor QCD with clover type fermions
We present preliminary results on the critical endpoint of three flavor QCD at zero chemical potential. We employ the renormalization-group improved Iwasaki gauge action and O( )-improved Wilson fermion action. The critical endpoint is determined by using the intersection points of kurtosis for the temporal size Nt=4 and 6. We find the critical endpoint is around mud =ms ∼m s both atNt = 4 and 6.
