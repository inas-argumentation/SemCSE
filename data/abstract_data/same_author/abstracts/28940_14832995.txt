Penguin diagrams for the HYP staggered fermions
Abstract We present results of the one-loop corrections originating from the penguin diagrams for the improved staggered fermion operators constructed using various fat links such as Fat7, Fat7+Lepage, gem, HYP (I) and HYP (II).16 The main results include the diagonal/off-diagonal mixing coefficients and the matching formula between the continuum and lattice operators.
