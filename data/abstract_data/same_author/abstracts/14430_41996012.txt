Stability of systems of three arbitrary charges: General properties.
The stability of systems consisting of a negative charge {minus}{ital q}{sub 1} and two positive charges {ital q}{sub 2} and {ital q}{sub 3} is discussed as a function of the values of the charges {ital q}{sub {ital i}} and of the constituent masses {ital m}{sub {ital i}}. We give general results and list some open questions of interest, which hopefully will stimulate further investigations.
