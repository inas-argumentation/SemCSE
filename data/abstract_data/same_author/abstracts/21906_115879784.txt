Functional Integral Methods in Quantum Field Theory
We give a special form of the Osterwalder-Schrader axioms in terms of conditions on a functional integral S{f} = ∫eiϕ(f)dμ. This yields a simple, self-contained construction of a Hamiltonian H, a relativistic, local boson quantum field Φ, and a Feynman-Kac formula to study perturbations H + Φ of H.
