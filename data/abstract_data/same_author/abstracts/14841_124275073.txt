Better Determination of αs by Measuring Cross Section Differences in Hadronic Collisions
Three methods are proposed for the determination of αs by measuring some special cross section differences in hadronic collisions. In these methods the calculated ratios αs/α depend on neither gluon and sea quark distribution functions nor any other parton fragmentation functions. So using experimental data one can determine αs more precisely in comparison with other methods of hadronic collisions.
