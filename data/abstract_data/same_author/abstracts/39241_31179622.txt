Elastic scattering of alpha particles from 209Bi at 24.8, 28.5, 34.7, 38.8, and 69.5 MeV.
Differential cross sections for the elastic scattering of alpha particles from {sup 209}Bi have been measured over a wide angular range at a number of energies between 24 and 70 MeV. The present data along with existing data in this energy range have been analyzed using the optical model with both phenomenological and double-folded potentials. The application of the dispersion relation of Mahaux, Ng{cflx o}, and Satchler allows a reproduction of the observed energy dependence of the real part of the potential. A nearside-farside decomposition of the data clearly reveals large-angle Fraunhoefer oscillations. The inelastic cross sections at 69.5 MeV have also been measured and the deformation lengths obtained from a distorted-wave Born analysis are consistent with values reported in the literature.
