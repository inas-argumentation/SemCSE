Refined reconstruction and calibration of the missing transverse energy in the ATLAS detector
The measurement of the missing transverse energy ( E T miss ) is fundamental for many analyses at LHC. Good E T miss resolution and calibration are essential for searches of new physics as well as precise measurements. We describe a refined reconstruction and calibration of E T miss developed by ATLAS and its performances on events containing Z and W bosons. The data sample was collected in proton-proton collisions at a center-of-mass energy of 7 TeV, and corresponds to an integrated luminosity of about 36 pb −1 . The determination of the absolute scale of the E T miss , fundamental for determining systematic uncertainties in all analysis involving E T miss measurements, is also presented.
