Tricriticality of the renormalization-group-generated BEG model
Abstract By virtue of the transfer matrix the classical spin-1 Ising model is transformed into a quantum spin-1 model in a transverse field. However, when the truncation renormalization group is applied to this quantum-mechanical model, new terms are generated. The resultant model that is closed under renormalization is the quantum version of the Blume-Emery-Griffiths (BEG) model. The tricritical exponents are calculated, and they are found to agree well with the values obtained from previous classical renormalization group studies.
