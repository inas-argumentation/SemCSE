Superstring corrections in simplest constraint set for D=10, N=1 superspace☆
Abstract We derive the complete general form of the pure-supergravity superstring corrections in what is called dual version of the β -function favored constraints, which is supposed to be the simplest constraint system among all possible constraint sets in the D =10, N =1 superspace. The possible non-Yang-Mills superstring corrections in superfield equations are given in terms of the A abc tensor generally used for superstring corrections. Remarkably, the supertranslation rules for the gravitino and gaugino fields receive no corrections to all orders in α ′. Since the Bianchi identities are exactly solved to all orders in our system, our results may be useful for understanding the non-perturbative aspects of (heterotic) superstring.
