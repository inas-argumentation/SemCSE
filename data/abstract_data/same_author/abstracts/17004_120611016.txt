Nuclear photoproduction of rho mesons
Abstract Targets of Be, C, Al, Cu, Ag and Au were exposed to a tagged photon beam in the 2.75 to 4.35 GeV energy range and cross sections are presented for the reaction γA → Aϱ0. Optical spark chambers were used to examine charged particles and γ rays from the targets, so that the data are believed to be free from inelastic contamination. The results are analysed both in terms of total cross sections σ(Aγ → ϱA), and in terms of forward differential cross sections ( d σ d t ) θ=0 (γ A → ϱ A ) . From the A dependence of these cross sections we determine the total rho-nucleon cross section, σϱN, to be (22.5 ± 2.7) mb, and the absolute values of the cross sections determine the forward photoproduction cross section from a nucleon ( d σ d t ) θ=0 (γ p → ϱ p ) = (72.7 ± 8) μ b/GeV 2 . These values correspond to an assumed value αϱN = −0.2 for the ratio of the real to imaginary part of the ϱ nucleon forward scattering amplitude. The corresponding value of the photon-rho coupling constant γ ϱ 2 4π =0.65±0.16 .
