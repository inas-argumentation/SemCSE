Studies on the chromaticity correction and dynamic aperture of the AGS booster
Sextupole configurations with periodicity six and 24 are considered; the higher periodicity is selected on the basis of reduced emittance coupling between the horizontal and vertical motion. Results are given for proton and heavy ion acceleration cycles. The dynamic aperture from particle tracking is compared with the chaotic dynamic aperture. 6 refs., 8 figs., 2 tabs.
