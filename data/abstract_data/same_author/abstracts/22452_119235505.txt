The impact of the Kaluza–Klein excited W boson on the single top at the LHC and comparison with other models
We study the s-channel single top quark production at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) in the context of extra dimension theories, including the Kaluza–Klein (KK) decomposition. It is shown that the presence of the first KK excitation of the W gauge boson can reduce the total cross section of the s-channel single top production considerably if (3.5 TeV) for 7 TeV (14 TeV) in proton–proton collisions. Then the results will be compared with the impacts of other beyond standard model (SM) theories on the cross section of a single top s-channel. The possibility of distinguishing different models via their effects on the production cross section of the s-channel is discussed.
