QCD corrections to the charged-Higgs-boson decay of a heavy top quark.
It is shown that up to an overall scale the lowest-order QCD corrections to [ital t][r arrow][ital H][sup +][ital b] and to [ital t][r arrow][ital W][sup +][ital b] are the same in the heavy-top-quark limit.
