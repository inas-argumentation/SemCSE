Perturbing Around A Warped Product Of AdS(4) and Seven-Ellipsoid
We compute the spin-2 Kaluza-Klein modes around a warped product of AdS4 and a seven-ellipsoid. This background with global G2 symmetry is related to a U(N) × U(N) = 1 superconformal Chern-Simons matter theory with sixth order superpotential. The mass-squared in AdS4 is quadratic in G2 quantum number and KK excitation number. We determine the dimensions of spin-2 operators using the AdS/CFT correspondence. The connection to = 2 theory preserving SU(3) × U(1)R is also discussed.
