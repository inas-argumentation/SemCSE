On some reconstruction problems
Much of Chapter 2 is concerned with a conjecture of F. Harary and B. Manvel, which is closely related to the Reconstruction Conjecture. Answering a problem raised by them, we show that the Reconstruction Conjecture implies their conjecture. We also prove a strong form of this conjecture for trees, as well as proving the equivalence of the problems of reconstructing graphs, with the problems of reconstructing certain classes of coloured graphs.
