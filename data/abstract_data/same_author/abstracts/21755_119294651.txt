Antihyperon polarization in high energy p p collisions with polarized beams
We study the longitudinal polarization of the ${\overline{\ensuremath{\Sigma}}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$, ${\overline{\ensuremath{\Sigma}}}^{+}$, ${\overline{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}^{0}$, and ${\overline{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}^{+}$ antihyperons in polarized high-energy $pp$ collisions at large transverse momenta, extending a recent study of the $\overline{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}$ antihyperon. We make predictions by using different parameterizations of the polarized parton densities and models for the polarized fragmentation functions. Similar to the $\overline{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}$ polarization, the ${\overline{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}^{0}$ and ${\overline{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}^{+}$ polarizations are found to be sensitive to the polarized antistrange sea $\ensuremath{\Delta}\overline{s}(x)$ in the nucleon. The ${\overline{\ensuremath{\Sigma}}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ and ${\overline{\ensuremath{\Sigma}}}^{+}$ polarizations show sensitivity to the light sea quark polarizations $\ensuremath{\Delta}\overline{u}(x)$ and $\ensuremath{\Delta}\overline{d}(x)$, and their asymmetry.
