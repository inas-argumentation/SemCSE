The study of lepton EDM in CP violating BLMSSM
A bstractIn the supersymmetric model with local gauged baryon and lepton numbers (BLMSSM), the CP violating effects are considered to study the lepton electric dipole moment (EDM). The CP violating phases in BLMSSM are more than those in the standard model (SM) and can give large contributions. The analysis of the EDMs for the leptons e, μ, τ is shown in this work. It is in favour of exploring the source of CP violation and probing the physics beyond SM.
