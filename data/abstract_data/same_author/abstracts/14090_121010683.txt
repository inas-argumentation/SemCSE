PARTICLE PRODUCTION BY 10-30 Bev PROTONS INCIDENT ON Al AND Be
The production of p-bar, mesons ( pi /sup s), and mesons (K/sup s) by the interaction of protons on Al and Be targets is studied at energies of 10, 20, and 30 Bev. The production ratios and momenta of these particles are measured at angles of 4.75, 9, 13, and 20 deg . (T.F.H.)
