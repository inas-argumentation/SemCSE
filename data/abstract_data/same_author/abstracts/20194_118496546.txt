Nucleon models of deep inelastic scattering
Nucleon models should describe both deep inelastic scattering and low energy phenomenology. We review some recent calculations of valence quark distributions. Models in which the valence quarks are the only dynamical variables fail to describe the data, since the valence quarks saturate the energy-momentum sum rule. This is in sharp disagreement with existing data which indicate that a substantial fraction of the nucleon`s momentum is carried by glue and sea even for Q{sup 2} as low as 0.5 GeV{sup 2}.
