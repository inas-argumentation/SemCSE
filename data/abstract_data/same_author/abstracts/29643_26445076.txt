Nonperturbative QED and loop bremsstrahlung of neutral lepton pairs in heavy-ion collisions.
A simplified model of an eikonal representation for the scattering of high-{ital Z} ions is formulated, involving an IR approximation to closed fermion loops which themselves carry full radiative corrections. Signals are found for the possible production of resonances'' from such a loop when the impact-parameter-dependent electric field between the ions reaches successive thresholds on the order of {ital m}{sup 2}c{sup 5/2}h{sup {minus}3/2}. Qualitative arguments are given leading to narrow widths ({similar to}30 keV) and a prediction of peak energies in reasonable agreement with the data. An explicit enhancement factor, arising from the computation of low-frequency radiative corrections across the loop in the presence of a sufficiently strong external field, compensates the smallness of the fine-structure constant.
