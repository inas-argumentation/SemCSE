Meson Masses and Widths in a Gauge Theory with Linear Binding Potential
Abstract A linear binding potential with one-gluon exchange corrections, within a colour gauge field theory including four types of quarks, is applied to the description of the meson masses and leptonic widths (both for light and heavy mesons). First order relativistic corrections are calculated by means of the Breit-Fermi Hamiltonian. Mixing effects and the validity of the non-relativistic approximation are also discussed. The results show a considerable agreement with the experimental data for mesons of masses ≳1 GeV, whereas the breakdown of the non-relativistic expansion puts limits on the analysis for the lighter particles.
