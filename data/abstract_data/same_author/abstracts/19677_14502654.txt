Beam test of novel n-in-p strip sensors for high radiation environment
Highly radiation tolerant n-in-p strip sensors have been developed for the high-luminosity LHC (HL-LHC). This paper reports the results of measurements with 392 MeV kinetic energy proton beam at RCNP in December 2011. The data was taken with a new DAQ system consisting of an universal read-out board ‘SEABAS’ and beam tracking telescopes whose spacial resolution is better than 5 μ m . The aim of this beam test is to evaluate the new 1 cm×1 cm n-in-p miniature sensors before and after 10 15  n eq  cm −2 irradiation. The median charge of un-irradiated sensor is 6.2 fC at full depletion voltage, while the median charge after 10 15  n eq  cm −2 irradiation of the sensor is 4.2 fC. The novel Punch-Through-Protection (PTP) has been implemented in these sensors. The length of active region of the sensor around PTP is observed to be decreased by 12 μ m in the irradiated sensors at 10 15  n eq  cm −2 .
