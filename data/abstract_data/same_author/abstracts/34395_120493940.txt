HEAVY STABLE CHARGED PARTICLE SEARCHES AT THE LHC
Several searches for heavy stable charged particles have recently been performed using data from $\sqrt{s} = 7 {\rm TeV}$pp collisions at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC). This review summarizes some of the theoretical and experimental motivations for such searches, and discusses searches for stable squarks, stable gluino, stable sleptons and multiply charged heavy stable particles with the CMS and ATLAS detectors.
