Production of very neutron-rich nuclei with a Ge 76 beam
Production cross sections for neutron-rich nuclei from the fragmentation of a $^{76}\mathrm{Ge}$ beam at 132 MeV/u were measured. The longitudinal momentum distributions of 34 neutron-rich isotopes of elements $13\ensuremath{\leqslant}Z\ensuremath{\leqslant}27$ were scanned using a novel experimental approach of varying the target thickness. Production cross sections with beryllium and tungsten targets were determined for a large number of nuclei, including 15 isotopes first observed in this work. These are the most neutron-rich nuclides of the elements $17\ensuremath{\leqslant}Z\ensuremath{\leqslant}25$ ($^{50}\mathrm{Cl}$, $^{53}\mathrm{Ar}$, $^{55,56}\mathrm{K}$, $^{57,58}\mathrm{Ca}$, $^{59,60,61}\mathrm{Sc}$, $^{62,63}\mathrm{Ti}$, $^{65,66}\mathrm{V}$, $^{68}\mathrm{Cr}$, and $^{70}\mathrm{Mn}$). A one-body ${Q}_{g}$ systematics is used to describe the production cross sections based on thermal evaporation from excited prefragments. Some of the fragments near $^{58}\mathrm{Ca}$ show anomalously large production cross sections.
