Reduction of the rational spin sl(2, C) WZNW conformal theory
Abstract We construct explicitly the correlation functions on the plane of the rational spin and level A 1 ( 1 ) WZNW conformal model. The corresponding Virasoro minimal correlations are recovered in the limit when isospin sl(2, C ) variables x i approach the coordinates z i . The Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equation reduces to a differential equation for the minimal conformal blocks, reflecting the decoupling of the Virasoro null vectors.
