Excited charmonium physics from lattice QCD
Properties of excited mesons are studied using a lattice QCD simulation of a system comparable to charmonium. We extract a spectrum of states, including those with manifestly exotic quantum numbers. Radiative transition form‐factors are also computed, in particular the transition from exotic ηc1 to J/ψγ which is found to be large on the usual scale of magnetic dipole transitions.Properties of excited mesons are studied using a lattice QCD simulation of a system comparable to charmonium.
