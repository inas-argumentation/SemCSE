Renormalizing chiral nuclear forces: A case study of 3P0
We discuss in this paper the subleading contact interactions, or counterterms, in the {sup 3}P{sub 0} channel of nucleon-nucleon scattering up to O(Q{sup 3}), where, already at leading order, Weinberg's original power counting scheme fails to fulfill cutoff independence due to the singular attraction of one-pion exchange in {sup 3}P{sub 0}. Treating the subleading interactions as perturbations and using cutoff independence as the criterion, we investigate whether Weinberg's power counting, although missing the leading order, could prescribe correct subleading counterterms. We find that the answer is negative. After establishing the correct subleading counterterms, we study the cutoffs where the subleading potential, instead of being treated as a perturbation, can be iterated together with the leading one.
