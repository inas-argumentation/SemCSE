Secondary characteristic classes and intermediate Jacobians.
A fundamental question in algebraic geometry is the classification of all subvarieties of a given algebraic variety X. As a part of the general scheme, there have been several attempts to construct abelian varieties, called "intermediate Jacobians", which ought to parametrize the group Σ(Χ) of all homologically trivial algebraic cycles of codimension p (formal sums of codim.p subvarieties) on X up to a suitable equivalence relation.
