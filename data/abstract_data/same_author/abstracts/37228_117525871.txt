Multiplicity and correlations of secondary protons in hadron-nucleus interactions at high energies
The results of analysis of the multiplicity of secondary protons produced in incoherent ..pi../sup -/C, ..pi../sup -/Ne, and pNe interactions in the range of primary energies from 4 to 300 GeV are presented. It is shown that the experimental data are described well by a model in which independent knockout of the nucleons of the nucleus in rescattering processes is assumed.
