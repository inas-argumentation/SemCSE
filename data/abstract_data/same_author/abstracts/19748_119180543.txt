Application of Time Projection Chambers with GEMs and Pixels to WIMP Searches and Fast Neutron Detection
Abstract We present work on the detection of neutral particles via nuclear recoils in gas-ﬁlled Time Projection Chambers (TPCs). We employ Gas Electron Multipliers (GEMs) to amplify the signal and silicon pixel electronics to detect the avalanche charge. These technologies allow ionization in the target gas to be detected with low noise, improved position and time resolution, and high efficiency. We review experimental results obtained in previous years, and report on ongoing simulation studies and construction of the ﬁrst prototype at the University of Hawaii. We also present prospects of using such detectors to perform direction-sensitive searches for WIMP dark matter and fast neutron from ﬁssionable material.
