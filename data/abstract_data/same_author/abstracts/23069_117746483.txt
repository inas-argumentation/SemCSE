$\eta$ Decay and the Quark Structure of the $\epsilon$
The decay rate for eta..-->..3..pi.. is calculated with use of the u/sub 3/ tadpole piece of the quark Hamiltonian, but without assumptions of chiral perturbation theory. The calculation is performed within the framework of the bag model; however, the results are independent of bag parameters and depend only on (1) the light quark mass difference Vertical Barm/sub u/-m/sub d/Vertical Bar and (2) the quark structure of the epsilon(700). Comparison of the present calculated eta decay rate with experiment shows that most theoretical estimates of m/sub u/-m/sub d/ imply a substantial four-quark component in the epsilon(700).
