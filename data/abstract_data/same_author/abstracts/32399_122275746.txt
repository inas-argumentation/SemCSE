Pion Electromagnetic Mass Difference for Physical Pions
We compute the on-mass-shell electromagnetic mass difference ${\ensuremath{\mu}}_{{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}}\ensuremath{-}{\ensuremath{\mu}}_{{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{0}}$, in a model consistent with chiral-algebra constraints and partial conservation of axial-vector currents, and find it to be logarithmically divergent. Some implications of this result are discussed.
