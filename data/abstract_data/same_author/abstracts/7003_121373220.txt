The phase structure of the SU(2) × SU(2) spin system in two dimensions
Strong coupling expansions up to g −16 of the correlation length for a mixed SU(2) × SU(2), SO(3) × SO(3) system in a rectangular lattice are considered. In addition to the asymptotic freedom fixed point, an unexpected line of second-order transitions is discovered. The common critical exponent of these transitions is ν = 1, like in the Ising model.
