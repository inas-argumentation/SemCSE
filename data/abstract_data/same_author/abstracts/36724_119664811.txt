QCD sum rule treatment of the lightest I=0 scalar resonance
A QCD Laplace sum rule framework is employed to investigate the relationship between the mass and the decay width of the σ[f0(400–1200], defined here to be the lowest lying isoscalar resonance in the scalar channel. Our analysis differs from prior analyses, not only by incorporating Breit-Wigner departures from the narrow resonance approximation, but also by including direct single-instanton contributions as well as three-loop-order perturbative contributions to the scalar current correlation function. The least upper bound we obtain for the mass of this state is shown to increase with its decay width. Our results indicate that a sigma with a mass of order 600–700 MeV should have a total width substantially smaller than its mass.
