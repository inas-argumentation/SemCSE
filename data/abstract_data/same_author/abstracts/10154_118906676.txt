Reply to “Comment on ‘Light-front Schwinger model at finite temperature’ ”
In the preceding comment, Blankleider and Kvinikhidze criticize the form of the thermal propagator used previously by us and propose an alternate thermal propagator for the fermions in the light-front Schwinger model. We show that, within the standard light-front quantization used by us, the thermal propagator for the fermions is unique as presented in that paper.
