Effect of anomalous couplings on the associated production of a single top quark and a Higgs boson at the LHC
A bstractWe consider the production of a single top quark in association with a Higgs boson at the LHC. In particular, we compute the cross sections for the processes pp → thj, thb, thW , thjj, thjb, thW j, thW b in the presence of the anomalous W tb, W W h and tth couplings. We find that the anomalous W tb and tth couplings can enhance the cross sections significantly. We also analyze a few signatures and show that, if these couplings are indeed anomalous, then with enough data, one should be able to observe the production of the Higgs boson in association with single top quark.
