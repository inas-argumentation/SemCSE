Search for charm production in neutron interactions near 250 GeV/c☆
Abstract We have searched for narrow two-body enhancements produced in the forward direction in n-Be interactions near 250 GeV/ c . We find no significant signal in any channel and report upper limits for charm production times branching rate to be 200–1000 nb/nucleon for masses in the range 1.7 ⩽ m ⩽ 3.5 GeV/ c 2 .
