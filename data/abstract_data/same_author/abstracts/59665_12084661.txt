On the intersection of edges of a geometric graph by straight lines
Abstract A geometric graph ( = gg) is a pair G = 〈 V, E 〉, where V is a finite set of points ( = vertices) in general position in the plane, and E is a set of open straight line segments ( = edges) whose endpoints are in V . G is a convex gg ( = cgg) if V is the set of vertices of a convex polygon. For n ⩾1, 0⩽ e ⩽( n 2 ) and m ⩾1 let I = I ( n , e , m ) ( I c = I c ( n , e , m )) be the maximal number such that for every gg (cgg) G with n vertices and e edges there exists a set of m lines whose union intersects at least I ( I c ) edges of G . In this paper we determine I c ( n,e,m ) precisely for all admissible n, e and m and show that I ( n , e , m ) = I c ( n , e , m ) if 2 me ⩾ n 2 and in many other cases.
