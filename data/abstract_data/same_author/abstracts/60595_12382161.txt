Knowledge Representation
A basic premise of current work in Artificial Intelligence is that the intelligence of systems is a function of the knowledge they contain, and that the utility of this knowledge is in part dependent on the form of its representation. In order to compare different forms of knowledge representation, I asked each of the panel members listed below to respond briefly to three questions. In the little space available, there was obviously no way of exploring these questions in any detail, or to explore more than a personal view of a particular approach. References are provided to more extended discussions in relevant papers. The reader should expect to use this paper as a pointer to what each panel member thinks is important, not as an introduction to the material discussed.
