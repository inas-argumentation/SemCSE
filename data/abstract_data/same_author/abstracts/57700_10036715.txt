Summability of orthogonal expansions of several variables
Summability of spherical h-harmonic expansions with respect to the weight function Πj=1d |xj|2kj (kj ≥ 0) on the unit sphere Sd-1 is studied. The main result characterizes the critical index of summability of the Cesaro (C, δ) means of the h-harmonic expansion; it is proved that the (C, δ;) means of any continuous function converge uniformly in the norm of C(Sd-1) if and only if δ > (d - 2)/2 + Σj=1d kj -- min1 ≤ j ≤ d kj. Moreover, it is shown that for each point not on the great circles defined by the intersection of the coordinate planes and Sd-1, the (C, δ) means of the h-harmonic expansion of a continuous function f converges pointwisely to f if δ > (d - 2)/2. Similar results are established for the orthogonal expansions with respect to the weight functions Πj=1d |xj|2kj (1 - |X|2)µ-1/2 on the unit ball Bd and Πj=1d xjkj-1/2 (1 - |X|1)µ-1/2 on the simplex Td. As a related result, the Cesaro summability of the generalized Gegenbauer expansions associated to the weight function |t|2micro;(1 - t2)λ-1/2 on [-1, 1] is studied, which is of interest in itself.
