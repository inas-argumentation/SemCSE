A mass-selecting apparatus to measure the energy loss of fission fragments in gases
Abstract We describe an apparatus combining solid-state and gas ionization chamber techniques to measure — for both fragments released in a fission event — the energy lost in a given gaseous range and the residual energy. This device allows to measure the energy loss in gases of fission fragments selecting their masses The performances of the apparatus are discussed referring to the data obtained by slowing down fission fragments from 252 Cf in gaseous argon.
