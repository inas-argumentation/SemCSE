The Higgs decay in the large N limit
The Equivalence Theorem is commonly used to calculate perturbatively amplitudes with gauge boson at higher energies than gauge boson masses. However, when the scalar sector is strongly interacting the theory is nonperturbative. We show that the Equivalence Theorem hold in the large N limit at next-to-leading order by calculating the width of the decays h → W+W− and h → π+π−. We also show, in the same scheme of calculations, that for the process h → π+π− unitarity is fulfilled.
