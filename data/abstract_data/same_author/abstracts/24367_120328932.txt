Magnetic monopoles in N= 4 supersymmetric low-energy superstring theory
Abstract A magnetic monopole solution to the field equations of N =4 super Yang-Mills coupled to N =4 supergravity in 3+1 dimensions is presented. The solution preserves half of the supersymmetries and satisfies a Bogomol'nyi bound. The corresponding metric has no event horizons or singularities for arbitrary values of the monopole mass or charge.
