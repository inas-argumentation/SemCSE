Hyperfine field of einsteinium in iron and nuclear magnetic moment of 254Es
The angular distributions of $\ensuremath{\gamma}$ rays and $\ensuremath{\alpha}$ particles from oriented $^{250}\mathrm{Bk}$, $^{253,254}\mathrm{Es}$, and $^{255}\mathrm{Fm}$ nuclei were investigated to extract hyperfine interaction information for these actinide impurities in an iron host lattice. The hyperfine field of einsteinium in iron was found to be $|{B}_{\mathit{hf}}(\mathrm{Es}\underline{\mathrm{Fe}}|)=396(32)$ T. With this value the magnetic moment of $^{254}\mathrm{Es}$ was then determined as $|\ensuremath{\mu}|=4.35(41) {\ensuremath{\mu}}_{N}$.
