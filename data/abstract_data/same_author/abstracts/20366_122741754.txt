Are There Significant Gravitational Corrections to the Unification Scale
Abstract Higher dimension operators may be induced in the lagrangian of a unified theory by gravity. We find that c tr( G μv G μv H )/ M Planck can lead to substantial corrections to the unification scale. For ordinary SU(5) a second solution unifying at 10 17 GeV can exist which has no observable proton decay but an unacceptable value of sin 2 θ w . Analogous effects in supersymmetry, however, can lead to enormous corrections. We find that supersymmetric SU(5) can unify anywhere between 5 × 10 16 M X 18 GeV with an acceptable sin 2 θ w for | c | ⩽ 1.0.
