Light fragment emission in Kr 86 - Sn 124 collisions at 25 MeV/nucleon
Light fragments emitted in $^{86}\mathrm{Kr}$-$^{124}\mathrm{Sn}$ collisions at intermediate energy (${E}_{\mathrm{beam}}=25$ MeV/nucleon) and larger impact parameters ($b=7\text{\ensuremath{-}}10$ fm) are studied by a multisource ideal gas model. The momentum component, transverse momentum, and azimuthal angle distributions of light fragments with mass numbers $A=1\text{\ensuremath{-}}4$ are given. Meanwhile, the correlation between the elliptic flow and transverse momentum, as well as the correlation between the fourth momentum anisotropy and transverse momentum for the mentioned light fragments are given, too. The calculated results are compared with the theoretical results of the Isospin-Dependent Quantum Molecular Dynamics model.
