The high-2 HERA anomaly and supersymmetric unification
Abstract We discuss an initial condition on the superpotential couplings of an SU(5) theory which allows the R -violating term Ld c Q but avoids the simultaneous presence of LLe c + d c d c u c . This same condition keeps under control the products of pairs of different coupling λ ijk L i d j c Q k , which are more strongly constrained by flavour-changing-neutral-current limits. In our view, this observation makes relatively more plausible the interpretation of the high- Q 2 HERA anomaly, if real, as caused by squark production.
