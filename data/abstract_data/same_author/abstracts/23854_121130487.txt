The Ising and anisotropy phase transitions of the periodic XY model in a transverse field
It is found that, for a periodic XY chain, the competition between periodicity and anisotropy gives rise to more than one phase-transition point at some parameter region. In this paper, the phase diagram of a period-two XY model in a transverse field is discussed. Two phase transitions, the Ising and anisotropy transitions, driven by the anisotropy parameter ?, are studied by singularities of the order parameters, the ground-state energy and the quantum entanglement. These two transitions are of second order and their traits are discussed.
