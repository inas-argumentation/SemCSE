On the consistency of supersymmetric instanton calculation in chiral gauge theories
Consistency of the algorithm of Amati and others for determining the properties of vacua in supersymmetric gauge theories, based on the instanton calculation at short distances, is further examined. When the summation over the degenerate vacua implicit in the instanton calculation is properly taken into account, the results for various condensates are found to be fully consistent in all cases studied. Our result strengthens the conclusion that supersymmetry is dynamically broken in some class of chiral gauge theories.
