Vortex model of extended hadrons composed of quarks and gluons
Starting from the chiral-invariant Lagrangian of quark and gluon fields, we derive the Higgs-type Lagrangian for field operators corresponding to quark-antiquark bound states. The Poincare invariance is preserved contrary to the work of Eguchi and Sugawara. Since the Lagrangian obtained here is just a relativistic generalization of the Ginzburg-Landau Lagrangian in the theory of type-II superconductors, we expect Abrikosov-type vortex solutions. This means that quark-antiquark pairs are confined inside the vortices which are regarded as hadrons. (AIP)
