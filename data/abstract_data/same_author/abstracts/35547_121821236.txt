The midpoint transformation in Witten's string field theory
Abstract We study the transformation to midpoint coordinates in Witten's string field theory using a ζ-function regularization. We find that the vertex is well defined and local (in that it makes no explicit reference to the midpoint coordinate) as the regulator is removed. This is in contrast to the heat-kernel regularization previously studied by others. We interpret the locality and unitarity anomalies of the heat-kernel regularization in terms of the associativity anomaly of the vertex. We also study the BRST charge in the same limit, verify that it is well defined, and propose a sense in which it is nilpotent. We then argue that the transformed vertex is BRST invariant. We discuss the structure of the space of states in the transformed theory; physical states are singular as the regulator is removed, suggesting that they be interpreted in a distributional sense.
