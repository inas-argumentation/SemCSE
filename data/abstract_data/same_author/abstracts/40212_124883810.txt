Strange B mesons
We have studied production of B* mesons at the ?(5S), detecting the quasimonochromatic photon from decay to the ground state. The observed signal is expected to be doppler broadened because of the motion of the B* d,u mesons from ?(5S) decays. The observed broadening is however considerably smaller than expected, and consistent with copious production of strange B mesons, B* s . Analysis of the data, for a large class of assumptions on the decay of ?(5S) to B*’s, leads to a lower limit of 30% for the fraction of strange mesons production.
