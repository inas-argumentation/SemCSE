Weak Interactions at Very High-Energies: The Role of the Higgs Boson Mass
We give an S -matrix-theoretic demonstration that if the Higgs-boson mass exceeds
 M c = ( 8 π 2 / 3 G F ) 1 / 2 , parital-wave unitarity is not respected by the tree diagrams for two-body scattering of gauge bosons, and the weak interactions must become strong at high energies. We exhibit the relation of this bound to the structure of the Higgs-Goldstone Lagrangian, and speculate on the consequences of strongly coupled Higgs-Goldstone systems. Prospects for the observation of massive Higgs scalars are noted.
