Guest Editorial: Secur(e/ity) Management: Two Sides of the Same Coin
Continuing political and military events, the accelerating frequency of cyber attacks on commercial and noncommercial enterprises, and revelations about management protocol and platform insecurities underscore the substantial risks associated with network, systems and application management. Through management systems, rogues can grab the keys to an enterprise’s IT kingdom. Security has become an essential feature—not an after-thought—for all IT aspects of an enterprise. Enterprises are increasingly at risk for cyber attack on mission critical resources as well as on the systems managing them. At stake are lost revenue, business continuity, competitive advantage, reputation with existing and prospective clients, and ultimately business survival. To complicate matters, enterprises are embracing business strategies to integrate their IT systems and resources with those of external partners. Assurance is needed that an enterprise will not be compromised by vulnerable management systems and components, internal or external. This special issue has been designed to capture the dual aspects of security and management, namely management of security and security for management.
