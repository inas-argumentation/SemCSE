Black hole binary inspiral and trajectory dominance
Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics, Waterloo, ON N2L 2Y5, CanadaGravitational waves emitted during the inspiral, plunge and merger of a black holebinary carry linear momentum. This results in an astrophysically important recoil tothe nal merged black hole, a \kick" that can eject it from the nucleus of a galaxy.In a previous paper we showed that the puzzling partial cancellation of an earlykick by a late antikick, and the dependence of the cancellation on black hole spin,can be understood from the phenomenology of the linear momentum waveforms.Here we connect that phenomenology to its underlying cause, the spin-dependenceof the inspiral trajectories.
