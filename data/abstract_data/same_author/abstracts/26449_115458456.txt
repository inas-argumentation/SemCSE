Nucleon and baryon densities in heavy ion collisions at 1 GeV/nucl
The baryon density calculated at the collision center in relativistic heavy ion collisions is not representative for the density really probed by baryons in the high density collision phase. A decomposition into nucleon- andΔ-resonance densities yields average values of 1.25ρN/ρo and 0.25ρΔ/ρ0 at the time of maximum compression.
