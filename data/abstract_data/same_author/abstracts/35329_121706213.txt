TeV LEPTOGENESIS IN THE MINIMAL SEESAW MODEL
We present a particularly economical and suggestive neutrino mass model, in which there are only two right-handed Majorana neutrinos of O(1) TeV and their masses are highly degenerate. Its novel Yukawa-coupling texture together with the seesaw mechanism allows us to achieve the normal neutrino mass hierarchy with ml = 0 and a nearly tri-bimaximal neutrino mixing pattern with the maximal CP-violating phase: eZ3 = n/4, 161 = ~ / 2 and sin2 = (1 -2 tan' eI3)/3. One may also obtain the inverted neutrino mass hierarchy with m3 = 0 and the corresponding neutrino mixing pattern with eZ3 = n/4 and eI3 = S = 0. In both cases, the observed matter-antimatter asymmetry of the Universe can naturally be interpreted through the resonant leptogenesis mechanism. An experimental test of this TeV-scale neutrino mass model might not be impossible at LHC and ILC in the near future.
