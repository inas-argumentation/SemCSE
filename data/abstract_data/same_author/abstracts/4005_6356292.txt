A Quark - meson coupling model for nuclear and neutron matter
Abstract An explicit quark model, based on a mean field description of non-overlapping nucleon bags bound by the self-consistent exchange of σ, ω and ϱ mesons, is used to investigate the properties of both nuclear and neutron matter. We establish a clear understanding of the relationship between this model, which incorporates the internal structure of the nucleon, and QHD. Finally, we use the model to study the density dependence of the quark condensate in-medium.
