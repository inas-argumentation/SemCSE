Using model-independent analyses, we ﬁnd that all ﬁve features are statistically signiﬁcant and we measure their location and amplitude. These are consistent with the adiabatic inﬂation- ary model. We also calculate the mean and variance of the peak and dip locations and amplitudes in a large 7-dimensional parameter space of such models, which gives good agreement with the model-independent estimates, and forecast where the next few peaks and dips should be found if the basic paradigm is correct. We test the robustness of our results by comparing Bayesian marginalization techniques on this space with likelihood maximization techniques applied to a sec- ond 7-dimensional cosmological parameter space, using an independent computational pipeline, and ﬁnd excellent agreement: Ω tot = 1.02 +0.06 vs. 1.04±0.05, Ω b h 2 = 0.022 −0.003 vs. 0.019 +0.005 , and n s = 0.96 −0.09 vs. 0.90±0.08.
