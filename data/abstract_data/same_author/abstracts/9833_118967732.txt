rho meson photoproduction at low-energies
The $\ensuremath{\sigma}$-exchange and ${f}_{2}$-exchange mechanisms for $\ensuremath{\rho}$ meson photoproduction are reexamined. Then the commonly employed $\ensuremath{\sigma}$-exchange amplitude is revised by using the recent information from the analyses on the $\ensuremath{\rho}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{0}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{0}\ensuremath{\gamma}$ decay and the $\ensuremath{\sigma}NN$ coupling constant from Bonn potential. Instead of relying on the Pomeron-$f$ proportionality assumption, the ${f}_{2}$ meson exchange amplitude is established from an effective Lagrangian which is constructed from the tensor structure of the ${f}_{2}$ meson. Phenomenological information together with tensor meson dominance and vector meson dominance assumptions are used to estimate the ${f}_{2}$ coupling constants. As a first step to improve the current theoretical models, we have also explored the effects due to the uncorrelated $2\ensuremath{\pi}$-exchange amplitude with $\ensuremath{\pi}N$ intermediate state. This leading-order $2\ensuremath{\pi}$-exchange amplitude can be calculated using the coupling constants determined from the study of pion photoproduction and the empirical width of $\ensuremath{\rho}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\pi}\ensuremath{\pi}$ decay. In comparing with the existing differential cross section data, we find that a model with the constructed $2\ensuremath{\pi}$, $\ensuremath{\sigma}$, and ${f}_{2}$ exchanges is comparable to the commonly used $\ensuremath{\sigma}$-exchange model in which the $\ensuremath{\sigma}$ coupling parameters are simply adjusted to fit the experimental data. We suggest that experimental verifications of the predicted single and double spin asymmetries in the small $\ensuremath{\mid}t\ensuremath{\mid}(l2\phantom{\rule{0.3em}{0ex}}{\text{GeV}}^{2})$ region will be useful for distinguishing the two models and improving our understanding of the nonresonant amplitude of $\ensuremath{\rho}$ photoproduction. Possible further improvements of the model are discussed.
