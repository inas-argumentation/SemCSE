Magnetic and thermal properties of the anyon superconductor
Abstract The properties of anyon superconductors are examined in the self-consistent field approximation. (1) There is a new critical magnetic field H′c such that if the external magnetic field Bext exceeds H′c, a uniform magnetic field Bin = Bext − H′c penetrates the material (the partial Meissner effect). At T = 0, H′ c ∼ ( n e 10 21 cm −3 )( m m e ) −1 · 30 G , where ne and m are the hole density and effective mass, respectively. (2) At finite temperature all physical quantities are smooth functions of T. There is no phase transition in the mathematical sense; nevertheless one can define a semi-critical temperature T′c above which H′c vanishes and superconductivity is effectively lost. (3) Numerically T′ c ∼ ( n e d = 2 10 14 cm −2 )( m m e ) −1 · 100 K , where ned = 2 is the two-dimensional hole density. (4) The penetration depth becomes very large near T′c, but never diverges. (5) At T ≠ 0 there exist a non-vanishing magnetic field B0 inside the superconductor even with vanishing external magnetic fields. However, its value is small (∼ 10−4 G at T ∼ 100 K). (6) The RPA result for the static response function for a neutral anyon gas is reproduced. (7) The effective theory for the electromagnetic and Chern-Simons gauge fields is derived, which replaces the Ginzburg-Landau theory of BCS superconductors.
