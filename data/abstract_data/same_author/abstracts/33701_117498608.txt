Full one-loop electroweak corrections to h0(H0, A0) H+- W-+ associated productions at e+ e- linear colliders
We study the complete one-loop electroweak (EW) corrections to the processes of single charged Higgs boson production associated with a neutral Higgs boson $({h}^{0},{H}^{0},{A}^{0})$ and a gauge boson ${W}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}$ in the framework of the minimal supersymmetric standard model (MSSM). Numerical results at the $\mathrm{SPS}1{\mathrm{a}}^{\ensuremath{'}}$ benchmark point as proposed in the SPA project, are presented for demonstration. We find that for the process ${e}^{+}{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{h}^{0}{H}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}{W}^{\ensuremath{\mp}}$ the EW relative correction can be either positive or negative and in the range of $\ensuremath{-}15%\ensuremath{\sim}20%$ in our chosen parameter space. While for the processes ${e}^{+}{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{H}^{0}({A}^{0}){H}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}{W}^{\ensuremath{\mp}}$ the corrections generally reduce the Born cross sections and the EW relative corrections are typically of order $\ensuremath{-}10%\ensuremath{\sim}\ensuremath{-}20%$.
