Exploring UDDI Registries Using Modified OFDAV Browser
This paper introduces a framework of interactive navigating through the web-based UDDI Business Registries. By creating a 2D interactive visual interface, service requesters are able to visually navigate through the large information hierarchies that are usually used to present the UDDI Business Registries. Instead of using the traditional keywords or click-through searching methodologies, which are often inefficient and less obvious, we use a visualization technique, namely Online Force-Directed Animated Visualization (OFDA VJ, to create our visual interface. A simplified prototype with visual interface has been developed for the purpose of demonstration.
