Prediction for top mass and Kobayashi-Maskawa parameters from a solution to the strong CP problem
I examine a simple model of spontaneous family SO(3) and CP violation in which the neutron naturally has a small but measurable electric dipole moment. In addition the model constraints several quark mixing angles and the top quark mass. At tree level the top quark mass satisfies mt⩽mbmcKub/mdKcb, where K is the Kobayashi-Maskawa (KM) matrix and mi is the mass of the i-type quark.
