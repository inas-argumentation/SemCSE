Could two NMSSM Higgs bosons be present near 125 GeV
We examine next-to-minimal supersymmetric model scenarios with partial parameter unification at the grand unification scale in which both ${h}_{1}$ and ${h}_{2}$ lie in the 123\char21{}128 GeV mass range. Very substantially enhanced $\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\gamma}$ and other rates are possible. Broadened mass peaks are natural.
