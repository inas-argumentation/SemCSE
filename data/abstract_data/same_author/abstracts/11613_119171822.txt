Hyperkähler metric and GMN ansatz on focus–focus fibrations
In this paper, we study the hyperkahler metric and practise GMN’s construction of hyperkahler metric on focus–focus fibrations. We explicitly compute the action–angle coordinates on the local model of focus–focus fibrations, and show its semi-global invariant should be harmonic to admit a compatible holomorphic 2-form. Then we study the canonical semi-flat metric on it. After the instanton correction, finally we are able to get a reconstruction of the generalized Ooguri–Vafa metric.
