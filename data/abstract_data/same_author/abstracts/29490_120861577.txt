Recursion relations in topological gravity with minimal matter
Abstract A complete set of recursion relations is obtained for correlation functions in two-dimensional topological gravity coupled with the ADE series of topological minimal models, by demanding the consistency of the contact algebra of physical operators. When formulated as linear constraints on the partition function, they coincide with the Virasoro constraints and the W-constraints that have been conjectured and partially verified in the multi-matrix models of two-dimensional quantum gravity. These recursion relations give a complete solution of the theory.
