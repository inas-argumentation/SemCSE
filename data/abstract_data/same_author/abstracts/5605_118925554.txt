Lambda Lambda interactions in finite-density QCD sum rules
The properties of Lambda hyperons in pure Lambda matter are studied with the finite-density quantum chromodynamics (QCD) sum rule approach. The first-order quark and gluon condensates in Lambda nuclear matter are deduced from the chiral perturbation theory. The sum-rule predictions are sensitive to the four-quark condensates, (2)(rho) and (rho) (rho), and the pi N sigma term. When (2)(rho) is nearly independent of density and (rho) (rho) depends strongly on density, we can obtain weakly attractive Lambda Lambda potentials (about several MeV) in the low Lambda density region, which agrees with the information from the latest double Lambda hypernucleus experiments. The nearly no-density dependence of (2)(rho) and strong density dependence of (rho) (rho) can be explained naturally if the properties of (2)(rho) and rho (rho) are assumed to be similar to those of pi pi and (K) over barK in nuclear medium, respectively.
