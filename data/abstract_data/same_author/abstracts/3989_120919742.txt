Chiral Quark/Parton Models: The Way QCD is Realized?
Quantum chromodynamics (QCD) is almost certainly the correct theory of the strong interaction. In the perturbative regime, involving high momentum transfer, it is well understood and phenomenologically successful. However, the matter of which we are made contains protons and neutrons. Their stucture is critically dependent on the long-distance behaviour of QCD, where the theory is not under control. It is believed that as a consequence of its non-Abelian structure QCD is singular at large distances, and that this singular behaviour means that only color singlet quark clusters have finite mass — the phenomenon of confinement.
