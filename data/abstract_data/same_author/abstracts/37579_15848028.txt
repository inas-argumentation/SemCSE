PRECISION TESTS OF THE STANDARD MODEL AT LEP AND RESULTS FROM THE 4-JET FINAL STATE AT LEP 1.5
First a short review is made of current limits on the Standard Model as derived from the electroweak sector concerning the Z boson at LEP. The second part of the paper presents an analysis of the four jet nal state at ALEPH, this analysis was performed on LEP data taken at centre of mass energies of 130 and 136 GeV. The results presented here are from approximate totals 1 of 12.3 millionhadronic Z decays and 1.3 million leptonic decays recorded by the four LEP experiments during the years 1990 to 1995 inclusive. These statistics permit a precise study of the Standard Model though the coupling of the Z boson to fermion pairs. This reaction is modiied by vacuum polarization corrections in the propogator such as t-quark and Higgs boson loops thus allowing a determination of m top and m Higgs to be made via indirect measurements. It should be noted that results for 1993 onwards are regarded as preliminary for all 4 LEP experiments. In order to combine electroweak results a common reference point is required.
