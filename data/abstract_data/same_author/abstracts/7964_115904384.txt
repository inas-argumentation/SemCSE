Review of Lepton Production in Hadron-Hadron Collisions
The production of leptons, especially at values of transverse momentum well above 1 fermi−1, is a sensitive probe of hadron structure at small distances. In these lectures I hope to summarize in an informal manner the present knowledge of hadronically-produced leptons, with emphasis on the general features of the data, rather than on details of individual experiments. The reader is referred to the many recent reviews1–3) of the subject (some still in press at this time) for other points of view. I have borrowed freely from them, and gladly acknowledge the authors. Also, some aspects of the subject were discussed by Professor Min Chen in his lecture at this School, and are not repeated here.
