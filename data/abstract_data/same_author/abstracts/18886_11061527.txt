{chi}{sub 1} and polarization asymmetries for quarkonia at high orders in nonrelativistic QCD
We study doubly polarized asymmetries of {bar c}c and {bar b}b mesons in hadroproduction and photoproduction at low transverse momentum in nonrelativistic QCD to high orders in the relative velocity of the pair, v. We give the complete set of expressions required for the asymmetries up to order v{sup 9}. The asymmetries in the production of {eta}{sub c,b} states are a stable measure of the polarized gluon densities. The asymmetries for {chi}{sub c,b}, J/{psi}, {psi}{sup {prime}}, and the various {Upsilon} states are stringent tests of the NRQCD scaling relations. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
