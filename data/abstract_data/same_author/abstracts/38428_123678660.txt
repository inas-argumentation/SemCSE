A Chiral SU6 Model and Consistency of Supersymmetric Instanton Calculus
Constancy of certain Green's functions, in asymptotically free gauge theories with global supersymmetry, allows us to relate the short-distance effects computed by the instanton method to the properties of the vacua. We study the properties of the vacua in a chiral SU6 model, and confirm the consistency of the approach by Amati, Rossi and Veneziano.
