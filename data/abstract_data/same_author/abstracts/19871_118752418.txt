High twist effects in hadronic collisions
We analyze the first nonleading power corrections to inclusive hadron‐hadron scattering cross sections. Such corrections decrease as 1/Q relative to leading power in the case of polarized hadronic collisions, or as 1/Q2 in the case of unpolarized hadronic collisions. We show why such corrections may be treated in perturbative QCD, in terms of generalized factorization theorems.
