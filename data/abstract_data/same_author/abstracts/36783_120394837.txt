Measuring QCD compton effects
Abstract We discuss QCD analogues of the Compton effect, where one of the photons in the Compton process is replaced by a gluon. “Gluon jets” are produced in photon-hadron scattering, “direct photons” in hadron-hadron scattering. The cross-sections for both processes are calculated in terms of the quark and gluon distribution functions of hadrons.
