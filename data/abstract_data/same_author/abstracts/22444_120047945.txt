Anomalous single top quark production at the LHC
The anomalous top quark interactions with gluon (tug, tcg) allow the production of single top quarks at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC). Using the angular distribution of the charged lepton from the W boson in the top decay, we show that with the data of 10 fb−1 of integrated luminosity and in the collisions with the center of mass energy of 14 TeV, the anomalous up and charm quarks coupling parameters κu, c/Λ can be measured down to 0.005 and 0.007 TeV−1, respectively.
