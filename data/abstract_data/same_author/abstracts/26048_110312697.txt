AN APPLICATION OF MULTI-STAGE ADJUSTABLE SHOCK ABSORBERS FOR THE GIRDERS OF STORAGE RING IN TAIWAN PHOTON SOURCE
Beam stability is a major concern for the operation of the Taiwan Photon Source. One of the many factors to instability of electron beam is mechanical vibration of the accelerator components. The TPS uses steel girders to support the magnets and vacuum chambers in the storage ring. Three pedestal and six mover assemblies support the girders. Multi-stage adjustable shock absorbers are designed for passive vibration damping, and presently installed between the girders and the pedestals. Through adjusting the amount of hydraulic fluid which bypasses the damping passage between two hydraulic chambers, the desired damping coefficient of the damping absorbers can be achieved. Experimental results of modal testing presented in this paper show that the multi-stage adjustable damping absorbers under the assembly of the girders reduced the level of girder vibration.
