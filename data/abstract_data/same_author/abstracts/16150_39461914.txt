Heavy quark mass determination from the quarkonium ground state energy: a pole mass approach
The heavy quark pole mass in perturbation theory suffers from a renormalon caused, inherent uncertainty of O(?QCD). This fundamental difficulty of determining the pole mass to an accuracy better than the inherent uncertainty can be overcome by direct resummation of the first infrared renormalon. We show how a properly defined pole mass as well as the mass for the top and bottom quarks can be determined accurately from the O(m?s5) quarkonium ground state energy.
