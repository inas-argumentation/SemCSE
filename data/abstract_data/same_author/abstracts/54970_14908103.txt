Mining Relationship between Triggering and Consequential Events in a Short Transaction Database
Since the earlier work in [2], a broad variety of data mining capabilities has been developed. These studies cover a broad spectrum of topics including: (1) association rule mining [1, 3, 11, 17]; (2) incremental updating [7, 14]; (3) mining of generalized [20], multi-level [8], multi-dimensional rules [22, 23]; (4) constraint-based rule mining [9, 18]; (5) temporal association rule [6, 13, 21]; (6) frequent episodes discovery [15, 16]; and (7) sequential patterns mining [4, 19]. Among others, mining association rules and sequential patterns received a significant amount of research attention. A popular area of applications is the market basket analysis, which studies the buying behaviors of customers by searching for sets of items that are frequently purchased either together or in sequence.
