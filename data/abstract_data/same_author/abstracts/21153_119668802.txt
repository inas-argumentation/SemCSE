A study of short-time periodic variation of the 8 B solar neutrino flux at Super-Kamiokande
Super-Kamiokande(SK) is a real-time detector capable of measuring the exact time of solar neutrino events. This, combined with a relatively high yield of these events of roughly 15 per day, allows a search for short-time variations in the observed flux. Using all 1496 days of SK-I’s solar data, we looked for periodic variations of the observed solar neutrino flux, and found no significant periodicity. The Lomb test was used to find possible periodicities in the measured fluxes of 10 day long samples.
