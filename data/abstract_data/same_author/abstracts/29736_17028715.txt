Calculation of QCD jet cross-sections at next-to-leading order
A general method for calculating next-to-leading order cross sections in perturbative QCD is presented. The algorithm is worked out for calculating N -jet cross sections in hadron-hadron collisions. The generalization of the scheme to performing calculations for other QCD processes, such as electron-positron annihilation or deep-inelastic scattering is also straightforward. As an illustration several three jet cross section distributions in electron-positron annihilation, calculated using the algorithm, are presented.
