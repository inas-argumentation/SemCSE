Axial Anomaly and Topological Charge in Lattice Gauge Theory with Overlap Dirac Operator
Abstract An explicit, detailed evaluation of the classical continuum limit of the axial anomaly and index density of the overlap Dirac operator is carried out in the infinite volume setting and in a certain finite volume setting where the continuum limit involves an infinite volume limit. Our approach is based on a novel power series expansion of the overlap Dirac operator. The correct continuum expression is reproduced when the parameter m 0 is in the physical region 0 m 0
