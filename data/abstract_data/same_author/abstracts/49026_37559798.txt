Measurement and continuous improvement of software reliability throughout software life-cycle
Software reliability is one of the most important aspects of software quality. However, most of the existing techniques focus on the system testing and other late stages of development life-cycle. Therefore, they are not effective for early problem identification and reliability improvement. This paper describes our recent work in establishing predictive linkage between software reliability and other entities that we can measure and control early in the development cycle, and using such predictive relations to drive continuous reliability improvement.
