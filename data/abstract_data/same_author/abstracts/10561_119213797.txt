Tensor to Scalar Ratio in Non-Minimal $\phi^4$ Inflation
We reconsider nonminimal λφ 4 chaotic inflation which includes the gravitational coupling term ξℜφ 2 , where φ denotes a gauge singlet inflaton field and ℜ is the Ricci scalar. For ξ ≫ 1, we require, following recent discussions, that the energy scale λ 1/4mP/√ξ for inflation should not exceed the effective UV cutoff scale m P /ξ, where m P denotes the reduced Planck scale. The predictions for the tensor-to-scalar ratio r and the scalar spectral index n s are found to lie within the Wilkinson Microwave Anisotropy Probe 1-σ bounds for 10 -12 ≲ λ ≲ 10 -4 and 10 -3 ≲ ξ ≲ 10 2 . In contrast, the corresponding predictions of minimal λφ 4 chaotic inflation lie outside the Wilkinson Microwave Anisotropy Probe 2-σ bounds. We also find that r ≳ 0.002, provided the scalar spectral index n s ≥ 0.96. In estimating the lower bound on r, we take into account possible modifications due to quantum corrections of the tree level inflationary potential.
