Reason for SU(6) grand unification
Abstract An SU(6) model can naturally guarantee strong CP invariance. This also includes Georgi and Glashow's SU(5) model. The axion in this model can be either invisible or visible, depending on the symmetry breaking scheme. The invisible axion is identical to a majoron. Also, there exists a relationship between 24 H and 45 H of SU(5).
