Systematics in the volume integrals of the imaginary part of the light ion optical potentials.
A systematic analysis of the imaginary part of the light projectile (A/sub p/ = 1--6) phenomenological optical potentials has been carried out. As a result of this analysis a global formula has been found which describes successfully the volume integrals of the imaginary potential in terms of the projectile energy in the range 0--50 MeV/nucleon and the target and the projectile mass numbers.
