A track fitting method for particles in non-uniform magnetic fields
Abstract A new method of fitting particle trajectories in non-uniform magnetic fields is described. It is assumed that measured points in three-dimensional space have already been obtained. The trajectory is broken into segments within which the magnetic field may be regarded as a linear function, for which an approximate analytic form of the trajectory is derived. With this form and its derivatives, a Newton-Rapheson iteration is carried out to minimize the sum of the squares of deviations of observed points from the trajectory. The method described has been used in a spark chamber experiment investigating K e4 and K πee decays.
