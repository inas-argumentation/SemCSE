Axial Form Factor of the Nucleon in a Finite Spherical Cavity
Within a chiral quark model, we examine the corrections to the axial form factor of the nucleon when it is embedded in a finite volume, in this case a spherical cavity. It is found that the contribution to the form factor from the pion cloud persists to quite large volumes. For sizes comparable to current lattice simulations of QCD, the axial radius tends to be considerably smaller than that given in the infinite volume limit of the model.
