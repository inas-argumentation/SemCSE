ENHANCED DUALITY SYMMETRIES FOR A PAIR OF DIRAC–BORN–INFELD ACTIONS
We consider a total action composed of two Dirac–Born–Infeld (DBI) actions: one for a vector field Aμ and another for an axial vector field Bμ. We impose a duality condition , where is the Hodge dual of Gμν, and g is a DBI interaction constant. Interestingly, there are two different global duality rotation symmetries in the presence of DBI interactions: (i) , , and (ii) δζAμ = - ζBμ, δζBμ = + ζAμ. Both of these symmetry are on-shell symmetries, including nonlinear higher-order terms. The remarkable aspect is that these symmetries are valid even in the presence of DBI interactions. The coupling of this system to N = 1 supergravity is also discussed.
