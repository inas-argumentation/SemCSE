Muonic atoms of radioactive nuclei
The latest progress of a feasibility study at the RIKEN-RAL muon facility using the cold hydrogen film method to extend muonic atom spectroscopy to the use of RI beams is being reported. This would allow studies of unstable nuclei by means of the muonic X-ray method at facilities where both intense μ − and RI beams would be available. New experimental results have just been obtained with stable argon ions implanted in solid deuterium films. Future perspective at the new Muon Experimental Facility in the J-PARC project is also discussed.
