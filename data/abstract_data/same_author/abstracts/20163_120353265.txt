Effects of odd-parity components of the deuteron on inelastic polarized-electron—deuteron scattering
We estimate the contributions to the asymmetry that arise from the parity-violating force between nucleons. For Q/sup 2/ = 1 GeV/sup 2/ the resulting asymmetry is about 10/sup 4/ times smaller than the one arising from the photon--Z-boson interference term.
