Ten-dimensional supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory with SO(8)-covariant light-cone superfields
The ten-dimensional supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory is formulated in terms of light-cone superfields that transform as vectors under the group of transverse Lorentz transformations (SO(8)). These superfields are constrained by a projection condition, which is solved explicitly. The superfield action is obtained by comparing its component expansion with the usual light-cone gauge component action.
