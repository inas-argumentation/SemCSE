Study of Thermodynamic Quantities in Horava-Lifshitz and f(R) gravity Theories
The present paper reports studies on thermodynamic quantities like temperature of the universe, heat capacity and squared speed of sound in f(R) and Horava-Lifshitz gravity theories. Considering the universe to be filled with dark matter and dark energy we have shown that in all cases the equation of state behaves like quintessence. The thermodynamic quantities have been studied graphically by plotting them against redshift z.
