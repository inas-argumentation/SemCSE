Unitary Sugawara constructions for affine superalgebras
Abstract The untwisted affine basic classical Lie superalgebras are considered. Unitary representations can exist only for certain compact type I superalgebras, and for those type I and II superalgebras yielding spinor gradings of rigid two-dimensional conformal transformations, provided that the central extension satisfies certain constraints. The Sugawara construction for these is presented and the value of the Virasoro central charge listed.
