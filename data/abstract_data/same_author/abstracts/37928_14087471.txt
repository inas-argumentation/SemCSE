Generalized lepton number and dark left-right gauge model
In a left-right gauge model of particle interactions, the left-handed fermion doublet $(\ensuremath{\nu},e{)}_{L}$ is connected to its right-handed counterpart $(n,e{)}_{R}$ through a scalar bidoublet so that ${e}_{L}$ pairs with ${e}_{R}$, and ${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{L}$ with ${n}_{R}$ to form mass terms. Suppose the latter link is severed without affecting the former, then ${n}_{R}$ is not the mass partner of ${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{L}$, and as we show in this paper, becomes a candidate for dark matter which is relevant for the recent PAMELA and ATIC observations. We accomplish this in a specific nonsupersymmetric model, where a generalized lepton number can be defined, so that ${n}_{R}$ and ${W}_{R}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}$ are odd under $R\ensuremath{\equiv}(\ensuremath{-}1{)}^{3B+L+2j}$. Fermionic leptoquarks are also predicted.
