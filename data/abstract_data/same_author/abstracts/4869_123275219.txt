Measurement of the Inclusive Z+Jet(s) Production Cross Section at CDF
We present preliminary results on inclusive Z(→ ee) boson plus jets production in p p ¯ collisions at s = 1.96 TeV , based on 1 fb −1 of data collected by the CDF experiment between February 2002 and March 2006. Inclusive jet measurements are performed as a function of p T jet and jet multiplicity for jets in the region p T jet > 30 GeV/c and | y jet | 2.1 . Results are compared to NLO pQCD predictions.
