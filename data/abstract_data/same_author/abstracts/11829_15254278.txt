D-instantons and Black Holes in N=2, D=4 Supergravity
We discuss T-duality between the solutions of type II A versus type II B superstrings compactiied on Calabi-Yau threefolds. Within the context of N = 2, D = 4 supergravity eeective Lagrangian, the T-duality transformation is equivalently described by the c-map, which transforms the IIA black hole solutions into the IIB D-instanton solutions. We construct via this mapping a broad class of D-instanton solutions in four dimensions.
