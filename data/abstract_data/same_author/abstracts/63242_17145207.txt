Silhouette Extraction in Hough Space
Object-space silhouette extraction is an important problem in fields ranging fr om non-photorealistic computer graphics to medical robotics. We present an efficient silhouette extracto r for triangle meshes under perspective projection and make three contributions. First, we describe a novel applic ation of 3D Hough transforms, which allows us to organize mesh data more effectively for silhouette computations tha n the traditional dual transform. Next, we introduce an incremental silhouette update algorithm which operate s on an octree augmented with neighbour information and optimized for efficient low-level traversal. Finally, wepresent a method for initial extraction of silhouette, using the same data structure, whose performance is linear in the size of the extracted silhouette. We demonstrate significant performance improvements given by our appro ach over the current state of the art.
