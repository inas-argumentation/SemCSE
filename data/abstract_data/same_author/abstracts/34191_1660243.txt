Observation of strong isospin mixing in proton emission from the astrophysically interesting isobaric analog state in 23Mg.
Utilizing the {sup 24}Mg({ital p},2{ital n}) reaction and unique particle telescopes, beta-delayed proton emission from {sup 23}Al proceeding via its isobaric analog state in {sup 23}Mg has been observed. The relevant proton group was detected at 223{plus_minus}20 keV with a proton decay branching ratio of 3.5{plus_minus}1.9 %. The proton width determined for this state is approximately an order of magnitude larger than that predicted by a full-basis 1{ital s}-0{ital d} shell model calculation which includes the expected isospin mixing. In addition, the resulting resonance strength (45{plus_minus}25 meV) has important astrophysical implications.
