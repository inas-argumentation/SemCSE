Measurement of the magnetic moment and of the decay parameters of the $Xi$$sup -$ hyperon
A measurement of the magnetic moment and of the decay parameters of the ${\ensuremath{\Xi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ hyperon is reported. The measurement gave ${\ensuremath{\mu}}_{\ensuremath{\Xi}}=(\ensuremath{-}2.1\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.8)$ nuclear magnetons, corresponding to a gyromagnetic ratio of ${g}_{\ensuremath{\Xi}}=4.2\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}1.6$. The measured ${\ensuremath{\Xi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ decay parameters are ${\ensuremath{\alpha}}_{\ensuremath{\Xi}}=\ensuremath{-}0.39\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.05$, ${\ensuremath{\beta}}_{\ensuremath{\Xi}}=0.08\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.26$, ${\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{\ensuremath{\Xi}}=0.92\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.03$ (${\ensuremath{\varphi}}_{\ensuremath{\Xi}}=5\ifmmode^\circ\else\textdegree\fi{}\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}16\ifmmode^\circ\else\textdegree\fi{}$); and the lifetime ${\ensuremath{\tau}}_{\ensuremath{\Xi}}=(1.637\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.050)\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}10}$ sec. In the accepted angular range for the production of the ${\ensuremath{\Xi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ (about $4.2\ifmmode^\circ\else\textdegree\fi{}l{\ensuremath{\theta}}_{\mathrm{lab}}l11.5\ifmmode^\circ\else\textdegree\fi{}$) in the reaction ${K}^{\ensuremath{-}}+p\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{K}^{+}+{\ensuremath{\Xi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$, the polarization of the ${\ensuremath{\Xi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ is 0.29 \ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{} 0.10, 0.44 \ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{} 0.09, 0.36 \ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{} 0.10, and 0.21 \ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{} 0.06 at laboratory momenta of 1.74, 1.80, 1.87, and 1.83 GeV/c, respectively.
