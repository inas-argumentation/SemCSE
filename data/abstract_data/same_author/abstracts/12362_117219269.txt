Correlation structures from soft and semi-hard components in p - p collisions at s**(1/2) = 200-GeV
Author(s): Porter, RJ; Trainor, TA | Abstract: We present preliminary two-particle correlations for unidentified hadrons in p-p collisions at √s =200 GeV. On two-particle transverse rapidity space yt⊗ yttwo distinct regions of correlated pairs are observed: a peaked structure at low yt(Pt≤ 0.4 GeV/c) and a broad structure at higher yt, where the correlation is distributed as a 2D Gaussian centered at yt1= yt2≃ 2.8 (pt1, pt2≃ 1.2 GeV/c). We select those regions separately, projecting correlations onto momentum- difference variables (ηΔ, φΔ), and observe structures interpretable in the context of string and parton fragmentations from soft and semi-hard components of p-p collisions.
