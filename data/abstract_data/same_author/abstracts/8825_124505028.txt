Negative particle production in the fragmentation region at the CERN ISR
Data are reported on the production of π−, K− and p in proton-proton collisions at the CERN ISR. Measurements have been made at fixed c.m. angles in the range (x, pT) = (0.12, 0.16 GeV/c) to (x, pT) = (0.82, 1.1 GeV/c), over a range of squared c.m. energy 558 < s < 3 950 GeV2. Within this range Feynman scaling is a good description of the data for all particles. The approach to scaling is discussed.
