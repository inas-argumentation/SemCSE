Strong coupling from the tau-lepton hadronic width
The strong coupling is determined using a novel renormalization-group-summed perturbation theory and a non power renormalization-group-summed perturbation theory with a tamed large-order behaviour. In this talk, the main features of these two schemes are presented and are applied to the extraction of αs(M 2 τ ) from the hadronic decay width of the τ lepton. While the renormalizationgroup-summed perturbation theory leads to a value of strong coupling close to that from contourimproved perturbation theory, the non power renormalization-group-summed perturbation theory yields a value closer to the standard fixed-order perturbation theory.
