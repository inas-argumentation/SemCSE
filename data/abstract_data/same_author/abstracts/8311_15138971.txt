The first calculation for the mass of the ground 4(++) glueball state on lattice
Under the quenched approximation, we perform a lattice calculation for the mass of the ground 4(++) glueball state in E++ channel on a D = 3+1 lattice. Our calculation shows that the mass of this state is M-G(4(++)) = 3.65(6)(18) GeV, which rules out the 4(++) or mainly 4(++) glueball state interpretation for xi(2230).
