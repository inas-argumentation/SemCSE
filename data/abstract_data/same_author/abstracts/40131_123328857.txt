Final state interaction phases in (B ---> K pi) decay amplitudes
A simple Regge pole model for $K\pi$ scattering explains the large $e^{i \delta}$ between isospin amplitudes which is observed at the D meson mass ($\delta \approx \pi/2$). It predicts $\delta \approx 14^{\circ}- 20^{\circ}$ at the B mass. Implications for ($B \to K \pi$) decays and extensions of the model to other two-body decay channels are briefly discussed.
