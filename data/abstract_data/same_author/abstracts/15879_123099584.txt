Cross sections and analyzing powers in the (p,d) reaction around 500 MeV
Cross sections and analyzing powers for the (p,d) reaction on 12 C, 13 C and 28 Si are measured around 500 MeV. DWBA calculations are found to reproduce the main features of the (p,d) data, when proton potentials are used which give strong oscillations in the (p,p) analyzing powers as required by the elastic data. Damping of the nuclear interior contribution is unimportant at this energy.
