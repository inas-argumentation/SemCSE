De Rham complexes of q‐analog of general linear group GLq(N)
In this paper, a set of De Rham complexes of quantum group GLq(N) determined by one parameter r is given, and it is proven that the differential calculi on the quantum group GLq(N) given in this paper are bicovariant. The noncommutative differential calculi on the quantum groups SLq(N) and SUq(N) are also discussed.
