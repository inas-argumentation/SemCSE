Applicability of Martin’s equations in high-energy elastic hadron scattering
The validity region of Martin`s equations enabling one to determine the t dependence of the real part of the elastic hadron amplitude from its imaginary part is critically reexamined. It can be concluded on the basis of a more precise analysis that quite unjustified and in principle incorrect physical results are obtained if the equations are used outside this region, i.e., for {vert_bar}t{vert_bar}{approx_gt}0.15 GeV{sup 2}. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
