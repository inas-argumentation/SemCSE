Proton compton scattering and neutral-pion photoproduction at large angles
The differential cross sections for ..gamma..p ..-->.. ..gamma..p and ..gamma..p ..-->.. ..pi../sup 0/p have been measured from abs. value (t) = 0.7 to 4.3 GeV/sup 2/ and for incident energies in the range 2 to 6 GeV. The energy dependence of Compton scattering is well described by parton model ideas. However, the energy dependences of ..pi../sup 0/ photoproduction and the ratio of Compton scattering to ..pi../sup 0/ photoproduction are not consistent with dimensional counting arguments.
