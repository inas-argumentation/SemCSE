Hadron spectrum, spontaneous breaking of Z(3) and fake loops in lattice SU(3)☆
We calculate the hadron mass-spectrum using the Wilson action and in the quenched approximation on a 6 3 × 10 lattice at β = 6.0. We show the existence of long-lived metastable states of the gauge field, characterized by the eigenvalue of Z(3). The hadron masses are very different in these states and do not agree with the experimental value.
