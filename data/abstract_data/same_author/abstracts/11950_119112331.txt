W' in new physics models at the LHC
We study the new heavy charged gauge boson W' in various models including Left-Right symmetric, Little Higgs, Randall-Sundrum and universal extra dimension model considering pp \rightarrow W' \rightarrow lepton + neutrino with the 7 TeV and 14 TeV CM energies at the LHC. Of particular, we show that the universal extra dimension model is highly constrained by existing and forthcoming data.
