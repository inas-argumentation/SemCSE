Comments on Covariant Formulations of String Theories
Abstract We clarify the role of the critical dimension in some covariant formulations of string theory. The action for the free string proposed by Banks and Peskin is shown to be equivalent to the light-cone dynamics of Goddard, Goldstone, Rebbi, and Thorn if and only if the dimension of spacetime is critical and α 0 =1. If these conditions are not satisfied, the action principle does not imply the physical state conditions in a class of completely fixed light-cone gauges.
