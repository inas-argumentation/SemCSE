Field equations, conservation laws in 2D quantum gravity and the Beltrami-KdV-Liouville system
We present the equations of motion and the conservation laws under various gauges of 2D induced gravity as a kind of the Beltrami-Liouville theory. We generalize the schwarzian derivative and derive its composition law. We also find the relation between the Beltrami-Liouville theory and the KdV system as well as the reccursion relations due to Gel'fand and Dikii based on the properties of the quasiconformal transformations.
