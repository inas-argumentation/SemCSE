Open problems in Monte Carlo Renormalization Group application to critical phenomenon
The Monte Carlo Renormalization Group (MCRG) methods and the theory behind them are reviewed. The Gupta-Cordery improved MCRG method is described and compared with the standard one. The emphasis is on the progress made in understanding the truncation errors in the Linearized Transformation Matrix and on open problems. Lastly, some of the existing methods for calculating the renormalized Hamiltonian are reviewed and evaluated. 35 refs.
