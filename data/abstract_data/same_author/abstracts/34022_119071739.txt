Gravitational leptogenesis and neutrino mass limit
Recently Davoudiasl et al. (hep-ph/0403019) have introduced a new type of interaction between the Ricci scalar R and the baryon current ${J}^{\ensuremath{\mu}},$ ${\ensuremath{\partial}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}{\mathrm{RJ}}^{\ensuremath{\mu}}$ and proposed a mechanism for baryogenesis, the gravitational baryogenesis. Generally, however, ${\ensuremath{\partial}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}R$ vanishes in the radiation-dominated era. In this paper we consider a generalized form of their interaction ${\ensuremath{\partial}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}{f(R)J}^{\ensuremath{\mu}}$ and study again the possibility of gravitational baryo(lepto)genesis. Taking $f(R)\ensuremath{\sim}\mathrm{ln}R,$ we will show that ${\ensuremath{\partial}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}f(R)\ensuremath{\sim}{\ensuremath{\partial}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}R/R$ does not vanish and the required baryon number asymmetry can be naturally generated in the early universe.
