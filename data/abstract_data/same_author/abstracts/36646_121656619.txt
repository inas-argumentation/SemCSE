Invisible axion at an intermediate symmetry breaking scale
We construct the simplest SO(10) model in which the scale characterizing the coupling of the “invisible axion” is not fixed by the renormalization group analysis to be greater than ≈10 15 GeV. An E 6 model is discussed which also has this property. We also point out that the Peccei-Quinn symmetry cannot be automatic for models based on SO( N ) with fermions in the lowest dimensional spinor representations.
