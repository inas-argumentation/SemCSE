The Parallel Complexity of Tree Embedding Problems
The question of the sequential complexity of various tree embedding problems arises in the recent work of Robertson and Seymour on graph miners; here we consider the parallel complexity of such problems. In particular, we present two CREW PRAM algorithms: an O (log 3 n ) time randomized algorithm for determining whether there is a topological embedding of one rooted tree in another and an O (log 2 n log log n ) time deterministic algorithm for determining whether or not a rooted tree with a degree constraint is a minor of a general rooted tree. These algorithms are two examples of a general technique that can be used for solving other problems on trees.
