Polynomial Interpolation on the Unit Sphere and on the Unit Ball
The problem of interpolation on the unit sphere Sd by spherical polynomials of degree at most n is shown to be related to the interpolation on the unit ball Bd by polynomials of degree n. As a consequence several explicit sets of points on Sd are given for which the interpolation by spherical polynomials has a unique solution. We also discuss interpolation on the unit disc of R2 for which points are located on the circles and each circle has an even number of points. The problem is shown to be related to interpolation on the triangle in a natural way.
