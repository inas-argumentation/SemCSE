Some particle physics aspects of neutrinoless double beta decay
Abstract In the analysis of double beta decay data it is usually assumed that 0νββ decay is triggered by the Majorana mass of the neutrino. However, 0νββ decay occurs in any extension of the standard model containing lepton number violation, a prominent example being supersymmetry. The amplitude of 0νββ decay in such models depends often not explicitly but only implicitly on the Majorana neutrino mass and 0νββ decay can be used to place stringent constraints on other model parameters. Here, supersymmetric contributions to 0νββ decay in both R-parity violating and R-parity conserving supersymmetry are discussed, and limits on supersymmetric parameters are derived. On the other hand, if the Majorana neutrino mass would be the dominant contribution to 0νββ decay, interesting information on the structure of the neutrino mass matrix could be obtained, once 〈 m ν 〉 is limited to be below 1 eV. A brief discussion on 〈 m ν 〉 is given in the second part of this paper.
