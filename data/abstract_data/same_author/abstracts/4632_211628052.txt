PLASMA-FILLED WAVEGUIDE FOCUSING LENS
Large transverse wakefields can be generated via the interaction between a relativistic electron or positron bunch and a plasma, and the bunch will therefore be self-pinched. Based on this effect, Chen suggested a plasma lens theory for future linear colliders; there are also many experimental studies on plasma focus devices.2In this paper, we study this problem further. Instead ofunbound plasma, we suppose that the plasma is bounded by cylindrical waveguide with ideally conducting wall. We find that the boundary can strengthen the focusing effect on the charged particle beam. Finally, a numerical example is given.
