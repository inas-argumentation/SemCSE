Extended CO(7-6) emission from warm gas in Orion
We mapped the quiescent emission from the 807 GHz J=7→6 transition of CO in Orion along a strip in R.A. extending from 0.7 pc west to 1.2 pc east of θ 1 C Orionis. The lines arise in warm gas with temperatures greater than 40 K. The line brightness temperature is greater than 160 K in the direction of θ 1 C, more than twice the dust temperature, and still exceeds 35 K more than a parsec east of θ 1 C. The lines are narrow, with a maximum velocity width of 7 km s -1 near θ 1 C and decreasing to 1.5-3 km s -1 at the map boundaries
