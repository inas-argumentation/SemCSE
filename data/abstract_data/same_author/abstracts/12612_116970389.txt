Charged Particle (Pseudo)Rapidity Distributions and an Expansive Three-Fireball Model in Nucleus-Nucleus Collisions at High Energies
An expansive three-fireball model is introduced in this paper to describe the rapidity (or pseudorapidity) distributions of charged particles produced in high-energy nucleus-nucleus collisions. A relatively strong transverse expansion is observed at lower energy (1 A GeV) and a relatively strong longitudinal expansion is observed at higher energy (above 14.6 A GeV). The calculated results of the expansive three-fireball model are in agreement with the experimental data over an energy range from 1 A GeV to 100 A TeV.
