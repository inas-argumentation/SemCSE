Relativistic Spinning Particle and a New Non-Commutative Spacetime
The relativistic spinning particle model, recently proposed by Staruszkiewicz, is analyzed in a Hamiltonian framework. The model is heavily constrained and constraint analysis, in the Dirac scheme, is both novel and instructive. Our major finding is an associated noncommutative spacetime the structure of which is completely new. We have shown that this non-commutative phase space algebra is essential in revealing the spin effects in the particle model through the angular momentum generator and Hamiltonian equations of motion.
