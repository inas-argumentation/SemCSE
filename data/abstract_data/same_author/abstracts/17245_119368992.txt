Evidence for the fourth P(11) resonance predicted by the constituent quark model
It is pointed out that the third of five low-lying P{sub 11} states predicted by a constituent quark model can be identified with the third of four states in a solution from a three-channel analysis by the Zagreb group. This is one of the so-called {open_quotes}missing{close_quotes} resonances, predicted at 1880 MeV. The fit of the Zagreb group to the {pi}N{r_arrow}{eta}N data is the crucial element in finding this fourth resonance in the P{sub 11} partial wave. {copyright} {ital 1999} {ital The American Physical Society}
