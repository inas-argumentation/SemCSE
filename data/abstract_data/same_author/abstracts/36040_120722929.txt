Constraint on the universal function in electroproduction
We show that analyticity and asymptotic behavior impose a severe contraint on the scale-invariant function widely suggested for the behavior of νW 2 (q 2 ν) as q 2 →∞. If the asymptotic behavior of the off-mass-shell Compton scattering amplitude is dominated by the usual (nonfixed) Regge trajectories, then our constraint is violated by the experimental candidates for the νW 2 curve. On the other hand, the introduction of a fixed pole corresponding to the one used by Damashek and Gilman alters the constraint, which is then consistant with the 18° data.
