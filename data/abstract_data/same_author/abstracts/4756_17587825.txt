Narrow width of a glueball decay into two mesons
The widths of a glueball decay to two pions or kaons are analyzed in the perturbative QCD (PQCD) framework. Our results show that the glueball ground state has a small branching ratio for the two-meson decay mode, which is around 10(-2). The predicted values are consistent with the data of xi-->pi pi, KK if the xi particle is a 2(++) glueball. The applicability of PQCD to the glueball decay and a comparison with X-cj decay are also discussed. [S0556-2821(98)04705-5].
