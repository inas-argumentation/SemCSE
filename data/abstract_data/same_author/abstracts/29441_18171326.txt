Direct Channel Effects in Nondiffractive Scattering
Direct channel or absorption effects are first considered for π exchange reactions, and in particular for πN ⇒ ρN. The discussion is then restricted to nondiffractive pseudoscalar‐meson baryon scattering reactions, where only two helicity amplitudes define the scattering process. The 6 GeV/c πp amplitude analyses are reviewed; energy dependences of various features of the data are then studied to obtain a qualitative understanding of the energy trends of the helicity amplitudes.
