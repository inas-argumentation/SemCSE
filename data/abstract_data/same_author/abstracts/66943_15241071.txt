Donaldson invariants of 4-manifolds with simple type
The Donaldson invariant of a smooth simply connected 4-manifold X with odd b+ ≥ 3 is a linear map DX : A(X) = Sym∗(H0(X)⊕H2(X))→ R defined on the graded algebra A(X), where elements of Hi(X) are defined to have degree 1 2(4 − i). Since its presentation by Simon Donaldson [8], this invariant has proven indispensible for distinguishing smooth 4-manifolds with the same homotopy type. Roughly, if x ∈ H0(X), α ∈ H2(X), and z = αx ∈ A(X) has degree d, one can define DX by the formula DX(z) = 〈μ(α) μ(x), [M ∈⌈ X ]〉 where [M ∈⌈ X ] is the fundamental class of the (compactified) 2d-dimensional moduli space of anti-self-dual connections on an SU(2) bundle over X and μ : H∗(X) → H4−∗(M ∈⌈ X ) is a canonical homomorphism. The instanton moduli spaces M ∈⌈ X have formal dimensions congruent to −3(1+bX) (mod 4), and DX is defined to be 0 in degrees other than 1 2(1+b + X) (mod 4).
