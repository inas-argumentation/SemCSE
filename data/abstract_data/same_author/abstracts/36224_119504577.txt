Calculation of the Annihilation Rate of P Wave Quark-Antiquark Bound States
In the framework of the gauge theory of strong interactions (in particular for the charm scheme) we calculate the annihilation rates of P wave quark-antiquark bound states of J PC = 0 ++ , 2 ++ . Applications can be made to the decays of the C = +1 states lying between ψ and ψ′, to their gluonic production, and to f′ decay. Annihilations into 2γ and the Primakoff productions are also discussed.
