The Design and Performance of a Halo Free Intense Extracted Proton Beam at Fermilab
A set of refocussing and collimation stages has been constructed in the Proton West beam line of the Proton Area at Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory which allows the operation of a halo-free intense proton beam. Measurements made at the first target point in the Proton West experimental hall demonstrate that this system is capable of targeting 10 12 pprotons in a 4 mm diameter spot with a halo density of about 150 particle/mm 2 at a distance of 5.6 cm from the focus. This system has been operated at 200, 300, and 400 GeV.
