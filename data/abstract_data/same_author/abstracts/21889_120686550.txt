Instantons in aU(1) lattice gauge theory: A Coulomb dipole gas
We study the decompositionA=AI+ASW of aU(1) lattice gauge field into instanton and spin wave parts. The action also decomposes,A=AI+ASW+R. HereAI is a Coulomb dipole gas,ASW is a zero mass free field, andR is a higher order remainder. We studyAI in detail, ford≧4, in the dilute gas case (which corresponds to the low temperature limit of the gauge field theory). We establish the leading behavior of the free energy:f ∼ ɛ−daζ. Here ɛ is the lattice spacing,a is a geometrical constant and ζ is an activity defined in terms of a small number of instanton configurations. Our methods suggest the absence of screening in the dilute dipole gas,d≧4, in contrast to Debye screening for the dilute monopole gas.
