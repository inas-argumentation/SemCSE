Tachyon condensation, boundary state, and noncommutative solitons
We discuss tachyon condensation in a single unstable D-brane in the framework of the boundary state formulation. The boundary state in the background of tachyon condensation and the Neveu-Schwarz B-field is explicitly constructed. We show in both commutative theory and noncommutative theory that the unstable D-branes behave like an extended object and eventually reduce to lower dimensional D-branes as the system approaches the infrared fixed point. We clarify the relationship between the commutative field theoretical description of tachyon condensation and the noncommutative one.
