Lattice results for magnetic moment and quark total angular momentum of the nucleon
We calculate magnetic moment and quark total angular momentum of the proton and neutron. All calculations are performed in quenched QCD formulation using Wilson fermion approach for 100 configurations. Lattice 16 3 × 32 was used for β = 6.0. Three values of quark mass were taken: 210, 124 and 80MeV. Value of the quark total angular momentum is consistent with the previous calculations. Contribution of the disconnected diagrams was studied for quark mass 210MeV using expansion technique.
