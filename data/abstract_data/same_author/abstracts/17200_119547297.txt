TEST ON APPLICATION RANGE OF QCD SUM-RULES FROM BARYON SPECTROSCOPY
By considering the nonperturbative QCD effect via quark and gluon condensates, the confinement terms in effective q-q potential can automatically be given. A potential in the form of (Yukawa-type terms) where positive and negative signs appear alternatively in 's is proposed. To testify the validity range of QCD sum rules, the experimental baryon spectrum is fitted and the values of 's as well as their signs are obtained. It is found that in the baryon spectrum calculation, the terms of and are important and higher terms beyond can be neglected. Comparing these values with the result calculated from QCD sum rules, some agreements and disagreements are found.
