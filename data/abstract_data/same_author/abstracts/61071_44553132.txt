The preventive and the penal: a comment on two models of intermediate treatment.
It is over 10 years since intermediate treatment received statutory recognition within the 1969 Children and Young Persons Act. During this decade there has been a change in social values and in the dominant political ideology, so that the control and containment of deprived young people is now stressed beyond and instead of their welfare. This has led to an attempt to redefine intermediate treatment, and the identification of two prevelent models of intermediate treatment. Following a review of some recent research on delinquency and deprivation, it is concluded that these models should not be seen as negating each other.
