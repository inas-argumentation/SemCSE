Two-dimensional SU(N) × SU(N) chiral models on the lattice
Lattice SU([ital N])[times]SU([ital N]) chiral models are analyzed by strong and weak coupling expansions and by numerical simulations. Twelfth-order strong coupling series for the free and internal energy are obtained for all [ital N][ge]6. Three loop contributions to the internal energy and to the lattice [beta] function are evaluated for all [ital N] and nonuniversal corrections to the asymptotic [Lambda] parameter are computed in the temperature'' and the energy'' schemes. Numerical simulations confirm a faster approach to asymptopia of the energy scheme. A phenomenological correlation between the peak in the specific heat and the dip of the [beta] function is observed. Tests of scaling are performed for various physical quantities, finding substantial scaling at [xi][approx gt]2. In particular, at [ital N]=6 three different mass ratios are determined numerically and found in agreement, within statistical errors of about 1%, with the theoretical predictions from the exact [ital S]-matrix theory.
