GEOMETRICAL DIAGNOSTICS FOR THE NEW AGEGRAPHIC DARK ENERGY MODEL WITH INTERACTION
In this paper, on the basis of Ref. 24 we will further study the new agegraphic dark energy (NADE) model with interaction term Γ = 3b2Hρc. Concretely, not only can the evolutions of some related cosmological quantities be described, but also the two geometrical diagnostic approaches are performed to this model, i.e. statefinder and Om diagnostics. By illustrating the evolutionary trajectories in r-s, r-q, w'-w and Om planes, we find that the constant n and coupling intensity b2 play very important roles in the NADE model, and the Om curves of the NADE model are more sensitive to the constants than ones of the OADE model. In addition, we also plot the LCDM horizonal lines in Om diagrams, and show the discrimination among the NADE, OADE and LCDM models.
