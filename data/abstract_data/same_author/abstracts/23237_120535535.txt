Jet signatures of scalar diquarks from superstrings at CERN and Fermilab
We calculate the contribution to the four-jet yield in pp collisions of two scalar, colour triplet, hypercharge ± 1 3 pure diquarks resulting from E 6 compactification in superstring theories. Measured cross sections at the CERN Collider are sensitive to this contribution for masses up to ∼40 GeV. Fermilab-Tevatron data with similar cuts will push this bound up to ∼70 GeV.
