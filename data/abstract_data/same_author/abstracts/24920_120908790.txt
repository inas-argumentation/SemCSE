A phenomenological investigation of the Pomeron in pp scattering
Abstract We have tried to fit the pp scattering data for 10 s 2 with a variety of models using a Pomeron pole (P) and associated cuts. We find surprisingly that α p(0) > 1, but no cuts, gives much the best fit, the dip at | t | ≃ 1.4 GeV 2 and the large | t | behaviour stemming instead from a non-shrinking “core” term.
