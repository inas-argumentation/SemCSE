A Monte Carlo model for diffractive dissociation in hadron-hadron collisions at high energies
We present a Monte Carlo generator for single diffractive dissociation in hadron-hadron collisions at high energies. The generator, which is based on some kinematic consideration and involves the minimum of theoretical hypothesis, reproduces the experimental data. Such a generator is useful to study the phenomenology of diffractive processes and to explore important roles of diffractive processes in cosmic ray interaction and propagation in the atmosphere and in interstellar space.
