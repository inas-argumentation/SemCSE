CP1 MODEL WITH HOPF TERM AND FRACTIONAL SPIN STATISTICS
We reconsider the CP1 model with the Hopf term by using the Batalin–Fradkin–Tyutin (BFT) scheme, which is an improved version of the Dirac quantization method. We also perform a semiclassical quantization of the topological charge Q sector by exploiting the collective coordinates to explicitly show the fractional spin statistics.
