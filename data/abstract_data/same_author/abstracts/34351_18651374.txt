NNLO Corrections to the Higgs Production Cross Section
We discuss the next-to-next-to-leading order (NNLO) corrections to the total cross section for (pseudo-) scalar Higgs boson production. The computation is carried out in the e.ective Lagrangian approach which emerges from the standard model by taking the limit m t → ∞ where m t denotes the mass of the top quark.
