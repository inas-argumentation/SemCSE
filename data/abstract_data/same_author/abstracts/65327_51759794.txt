Perturbation of periodic manifolds of hamiltonian systems
Introduction. I t is well known that a closed orbit of a hamiltonian system is preserved under small perturbations of the hamiltonian function, provided that the orbit is nondegenerate in a certain sense [5, §38], In this announcement, we describe some results about the behavior of manifolds of closed orbits of hamiltonian systems under perturbation of the hamiltonian function. This yields, in turn, new results concerning the existence of closed orbits near equilibria for which the linearized system exhibits degenerate behavior.
