On a class representations of the bilocal current algebra on a light-like ray
Abstract A class of representation of the algebra if bilocal currents on a plane perpendicular to a light-like ray is constructed. The saturation of these representations with states of definite momenta conjugate to the configuration space variables on the light-like plane reveals a Fock-space nature of the representation space, generated by quark and anti-quark fields obeying Fermi statistics, projected on the characteristics light-like ray. The mass spectrum, however, is not determined by the algebra. Representations on a subspace of the constructed Fock space are discussed for special momentum configurations.
