Gamma‐gamma topics, and a look at mesonic goo
Results are shown on four topics in γγ Physics: exclusive production of high‐mass hadron pairs, the photon structure function Fγ2, jet distributions and charm production; leaving time at the end for ‘‘new headlines’’ in 0++ meson spectroscopy, mention of two new results on γγ → resonances and a summary of conclusions. Originally listed as Low Energy Tests of QCD the talk is better represented by its present title. Most of the topics discussed do involve QCD, though the main goal is not so much to test it as to find out how it can be used to understand and describe what is going on. (Results from L3 and CLEO on γγ production of charmonium were passed to Jeff Appel for his talk on ‘‘Heavy Quark Production and Spectroscopy’’.)
