Results of an experiment to test for anI=2 term in the electromagnetic current
The ratio r between the differential cross sections of the reactions gamma + d yields pi /sup o/ + n + ps and + d yields pi /sup o/ + p + n/ sub s wa s measured at 270 to 370 MeV, and at an angle in the center of mass of the pion-nucleon system THETA /sub pi / ~ 115 deg . The results show that r is approximately 1, thus giving evidence against the presence of a large isotensor term. (LBS)
