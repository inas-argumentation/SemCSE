DWIA predictions of (p,p') data using electromagnetically constrained densities
DWIA predictions and comparison with experiment are made for 650 and 800 MeV proton scattering to low lying states in 24 Mg, 26 Mg, 28 Si, 30 Si, 34 S, 40 Ca and 42 Ca. Electromagnetic constraints on proton and neutron ground-state and transition densities are used to minimize nuclear structure uncertainties. Generally good agreement with the data is seen.
