NEUTRALINO DARK MATTER IN THE LEFT-RIGHT SUPERSYMMETRIC MODEL
We study the neutralino sector of the left-right supersymmetric model. In addition to the possibilities available in the minimal supersymmetric model, the neutralino states can be superpartners of the U(1)B−L gauge boson, the neutral SU(2)R neutral gauge boson, or of the Higgs triplets.
