The topological structure of Nieh-Yan form and the chiral anomaly in spaces with torsion
The topological structure of the Nieh-Yan form in a four-dimensional manifold is given by making use of the decomposition of spin connection. The case of the generalized Nieh-Yan form on a 2d-dimensional manifold is discussed with an example of an eight-dimensional case which is studied in detail. The chiral anomaly with nonvanishing torsion is also studied. The further contributions from the torsional part to the chiral anomaly are found to estimate from the zeros of some fields under the pure gauge condition.
