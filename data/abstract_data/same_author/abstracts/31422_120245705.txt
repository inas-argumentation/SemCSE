Sensitivity of a large volume LXe TPC to neutrinoless double beta decay
We examine the sensitivity of an unenriched 200 liter liquid xenon detector to the double beta decay of 106 Xe. Considerations of the backgrounds arising from the detector construction materials and subterranean operation suggest a sensitivity of order 10 25 √ t yr for the neutrinoless process, comparable with currently projected sensitivities of two enriched-germanium experiments now in progress. For the two-neutrino process, the background is however too severe to reach a similar limit without significant isotopic enrichment. In either case, the need for a track imaging detector with good spatial resolutions in addition to good energy resolution, is clearly evident.
