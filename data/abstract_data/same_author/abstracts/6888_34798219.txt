New test of nonsymmetric theories of gravity: Observational limits on gravity-induced depolarization of solar spectral lines.
We show that theories which couple the antisymmetric part of a nonsymmetric-tensor gravitational field to the electromagnetic field predict a depolarization of the Zeeman components of spectral lines emitted by extended, magnetically active regions near the limb of the Sun. This new effect is a consequence of the violation of the Einstein equivalence principle by these nonsymmetric alternatives to general relativity
