PROCESS AUTOMATION OF A 600 A HTS CURRENT LEAD CRYOGENIC TEST FACILITY USING A FIELD NETWORK WITH SMART INSTRUMENTATION
This paper reports on the control system of a cryogenic test facility for High Temperature Superconducting current leads (HTS600). The control system is based on a Programmable Logic Controller (PLC) implementing 22 Closed Control Loops (CCL), and a Profibus DP/PA network interconnecting 53 sensors and actuators from different manufacturers. These devices can be calibrated and parameterised remotely through the Profibus network. This is the first complex Profibus DP and PA network that has been implemented and commissioned at CERN.
