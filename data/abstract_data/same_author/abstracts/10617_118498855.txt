Energy Level Displacement of Excited np State of Kaonic Deuterium In Faddeev Equation Approach
We calculate the energy level displacement of the excited np state of kaonic deuterium in terms of the P–wave scattering length of K d scattering. We solve the Faddeev equations for the amplitude of K d scattering in the fixed centre approximation and derive the complex P–wave scattering length of K d scattering in terms of the S–wave and P–wave scattering lengths of ¯ KN scattering.
