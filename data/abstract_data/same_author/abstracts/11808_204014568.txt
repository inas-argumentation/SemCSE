Allowed Domains for Chiral-Symmetry- and Isospin-Breaking Parameters
In addition to the chiral-symmetry-breaking terms ${\ensuremath{\epsilon}}_{0}{\ensuremath{\sigma}}_{0}+{\ensuremath{\epsilon}}_{8}{\ensuremath{\sigma}}_{8}$ an isospin - breaking term ${\ensuremath{\epsilon}}_{3}{\ensuremath{\sigma}}_{3}$ is included in the study of the (3,\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}3)\ensuremath{\bigoplus}(\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}3,3) model. By assuming that the intermediate symmetry is either ${\mathrm{SU}}_{2}$\ensuremath{\bigotimes}${\mathrm{SU}}_{2}$ or ${\mathrm{SU}}_{3}$, allowed domains for the chiral-symmetry- and isospin-breaking parameters are derived. A $\ensuremath{\sigma}$-model Lagrangian is then constructed. The positivity condition for the masses reproduces the general features of the domain structure.
