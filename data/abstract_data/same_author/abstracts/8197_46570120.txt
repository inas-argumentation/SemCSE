Relaxation of gaseous spin-polarized 3He targets due to ionizing radiation.
The theory of spin-polarized /sup 3/He relaxation due to hyperfine coupling in /sup 3/He/sup +/ and due to spin-rotation coupling in /sup 3/He/sub 2//sup +/ is presented. Comparison is made between the theory and recent experiments which utilized spin-polarized /sup 3/He as a charged-particle beam target. The theory predicts that at low He pressures applying a magnetic field significantly reduces the /sup 3/He depolarization rate due to formation of /sup 3/He/sub 2//sup +/. At high He pressures the relaxation rate decreases with pressure. In addition, charge-transfer processes with foreign gases suppress /sup 3/He depolarization by reducing the /sup 3/He/sup +/ and /sup 3/He/sub 2//sup +/ concentrations.
