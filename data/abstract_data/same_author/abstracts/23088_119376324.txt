The fourth generation and N=1 supergravity
Abstract The effects of including a fourth generation of matter fields in the RG equations of broken N = 1 supergravity are considered. With small values ( h X ≅0.2) of the fourth generation Yukawa couplings at GUT energies M X , SU(2)×U(1) breaking can take place comfortably at the weak scale, with m t ≅40 GeV , m e R ⩾ 30 GeV . Larger values ( h > X ⩾ 0.5) force the breaking to occur via dimensional transmutation. When h X ⩾1, the dimensional transmutation cannot take place as low as the weak scale, yielding upper bounds on fourth generation masses: m t′ ⩽140 GeV, m b ′ ⩽135 GeV, and m τ ′ ⩽70 GeV. There are many solutions with m b ′ m b ′ , m τ ′ m τ ′ , and the “radiative” Higgs can have a mass as high as 35 GeV.
