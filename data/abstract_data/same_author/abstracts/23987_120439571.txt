New BRST quantization of the bosonic string and string field theory
Abstract A new BRST quantization for the open bosonic string is carried out. The new BRST charge and anti-BRST charge obtained are identical with the generators J − α proposed by Siegel and Zwiebach, justifying their postulate. A light-cone like action is written and casted into its manifest OSp (1, 1 2 ) invariant form in the physical sector. By integrating over the unphysical coordinates, we obtain the gauge invariant action of the string field.
