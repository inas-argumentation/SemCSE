pQCD Structure and Hadronization in Jets and Heavy-ion Collisions
Implications of hadronization as a rapid traversal of the QCD phase boundary are explored for correlation structures in jets, N-N collisions and heavy-ion collisions. Hadronization viewed as a partition of the prehadonic system restricts the scale interval over which power-law jet correlation structures survive. Rapid traversal of the QCD phase boundary may result in a lattice-like residual structure in N-N and A-A conifiguration space accessible to momentum-space correlation analysis via Hubble flow.
