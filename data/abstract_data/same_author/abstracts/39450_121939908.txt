Nuclear matter properties in the relativistic random phase approximation
Abstract Nuclear matter properties at zero temperature are studied in terms of the relativistic σ-ω model, including the random phase approximation (RPA) contribution. At normal density, the medium polarization adds about −35 MeV of binding energy to the mean field result. The scalar and vector effective interaction strengths are fitted to the nuclear matter saturation conditions under various approximations for the energy functional including the RPA term. The effective mass and bulk modulus are calculated with these parameters. The relative importance of different contributions to the binding energy is analyzed.
