Mesonic excitations of QGP: Study with an effective model
We study the correlations between quark-antiquark pairs in different quantum number channels in a deconfined plasma by using an effective model of QCD. Using the three flavor PNJL model, the finite temperature spectral functions for different mesonic states are studied at zero and nonzero quark chemical potentials. It is found that in the $\ensuremath{\eta}$ channel, resonance structures survive above the chiral transition temperature ${T}_{\ensuremath{\chi}}$, while the kaonic states seem to get washed off just above ${T}_{\ensuremath{\chi}}$. The sensitivity of the structures to the anomaly term are carefully investigated.
