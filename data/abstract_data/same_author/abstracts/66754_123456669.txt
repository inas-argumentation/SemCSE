State classification for a class of measure-valued branching diffusions in a Brownian medium
Summary. The spatial structure of a new class of measure-valued diffusions which arise as limits in distribution of a sequence of interacting branching particle systems is investigated. We obtain the following criterion of state classification for these superprocesses: their effective state space is contained in the set of purely atomic measures or the set of absolutely continuous measures according as ε=0 or ε≠0, when the coefficient of the motion generator is a smooth function.
