THE DELTA-I=1/2 RULE AND NONPERTURBATIVE EFFECTS IN QUANTUM CHROMODYNAMICS
Instanton-induced effects in QCD give rise to new, pure ΔI = 12 operators in the |ΔS| = 1 weak effective lagrangian. A new formalism is introduced such that the effects of instantons of up to a given size are explicitly taken into account, and such that a modified form of renormalization group equations hold. The results of our analysis suggest that instanton-induced effects might be important in the enhancement of the ΔI = 12 weak non-leptonic decay amplitudes.
