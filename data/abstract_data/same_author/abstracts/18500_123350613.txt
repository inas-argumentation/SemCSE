Formation of disoriented chiral condensates through quenching and annealing
We demonstrate that the chiral phase transition is automatically incorporated in the numerical simulations of the classical equations of motion in the linear σ-model. We find that domains of disoriented chiral condensate with 4–5 fm in size can form through a quench while an annealing leads to domains of smaller sizes. We also demonstrate that quenching cannot be achieved by relaxing a chirally symmetric system through expansion. Furthermore, we point out that the order of the phase transition has little significance for the domain formation.
