Tunnelling without barriers in curved space-time
Abstract The evolution in curved space-time of quantum fields in theories with relatively flat potentials is considered. It is known that bubble nucleation, a quantum mechanical tunneling process, may occur in flat space-time even if V ( φ ) has no barrier. It is shown that bubble nucleation can also occur in curved space-time even though there is no bounce solution in the standard formalism for the bubble nucleation rate in curved space-time. Some implications for inflationary scenarios are discussed.
