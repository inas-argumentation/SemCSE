Weak mixing and CP violation involving heavy quarks and possible measurements in e+e− experiments☆
We evaluate weak mass mixing among the neutral heavy mesons with a bottom (Q = −13) or top (Q = +23) quark and CP violation in the framework of six quark V − A models. It is argued that bottom and top mesons may distinguish the Higgs exchange mechanism of CP violation from a complex phase in the quark mass matrix, if bottom and top quark masses are sufficiently different. Estimates of weak mixing and CP violating effects for e+e− experiments at PETRA, PEP and CESR energies are presented.
