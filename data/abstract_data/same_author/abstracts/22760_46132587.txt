Physics results from RICH detectors
Abstract RICH detectors have become extraordinarily useful. Results include measurement of solar neutrino rates, evidence for neutrino oscillations, measurement of TeV γ -rays from gravitational sources, properties of QCD, charm production and decay, and measurement of the CKM matrix elements V cs , V cb and V ub . A new value |V ub /V cb |=0.087±0.012 is determined.
