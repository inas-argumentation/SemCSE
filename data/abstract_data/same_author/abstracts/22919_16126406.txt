Density cusps: restrictions on non-axisymmetric models
Galactic nuclei are now generally thought to have density cusps in their centres, and the evidence is mounting that as a consequence they are unlikely to be triaxial. Selfconsistent stellar dynamical models of non-axisymmetric cusps would be an interesting counter-argument to this conclusion.
