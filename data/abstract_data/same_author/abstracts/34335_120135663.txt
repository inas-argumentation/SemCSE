Studies of tau decay modes with K0 mesons from CLEO
We examine five final states involving K 0 mesons, presenting branching fractions for the decays τ → K 0 πν τ , τ → K 0 ππ 0 ν τ , τ → K 0 Kν τ , τ → K 0 Kπ 0 ν τ , and τ → K s K s hν τ . The mass spectra provide evidence for the dominant role of K * in Kπ decays and K 1 in Kππ modes. We find evidence for a significant K * K contribution to the KKπ decays.
