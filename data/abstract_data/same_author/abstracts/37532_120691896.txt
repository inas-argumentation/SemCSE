Precision heat inleak measurements on cryogenic components at 80 K, 4.2 K and 1.8 K
We have built and are operating a test bench for precision heat inleak measurements and thermal qualification of components for the superfluid helium cryostats of the CERN Large Hadron Collider (LHC) project. The bench features the three temperature levels used for heat interception in the LHC cryostats. The heat loads are measured using both calibrated thermal conductances (“heatmeters”) and standard boil-off methods. After validation of the measurement methods, we compare experimental results with thermal design calculations on a prototype magnet support post.
