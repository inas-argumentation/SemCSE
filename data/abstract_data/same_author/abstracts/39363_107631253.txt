AMPLITUDE DEPENDENT TUNE SPREAD AND FIELD ERRORS OF SUPERCONDUCTING LOW- QUADRUPOLES
The nonlinear effects in the lowinsertions are studied on the basis of the amplitude dependent tune spread. Several methods of estimating the tune spread in superconducting lowquadrupoles are compared. The main feature of these magnets is that the random errors dominate the multipole spectrum. The proposed methods allow an analysis of the final focus layout in the early stages of the insertion design, and point to the critical magnets and the dominant multipole errors.
