Constraints on both bilinear and trilinear R-parity violating couplings from neutrino laboratories and astrophysics data
Abstract We consider neutrino masses generated at tree level and at one loop, through fermion–sfermion loop diagrams, in the MSSM with R-parity violation. Using the (3×3) mass and mixing matrices for three generations of neutrinos and the present experimental results on neutrinos from laboratories and astrophysics simultaneously, we put bounds on both trilinear ( λ ijk , λ ′ ijk ) and bilinear ( μ e , μ μ , μ τ ) R-parity-violating couplings.
