Excluding light gluinos using four-jet LEP events: a next-to-leading order result
Based upon a next-to-leading order perturbative calculation of the four-jet production rate in electron-positron annihilation and assuming 8% for the theoretical error emerging from hadronization effects in the 0.002 ≤ ycut ≤ 0.004 range for the Durham clustering algorithm, we exclude the existence of the light gluinos at the 95% confidence level.
