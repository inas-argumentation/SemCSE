Higher Order Collins Modulations in Transversely Polarized Quark Fragmentation
Department of Physics, School of Science,Tokai University, Hiratsuka-shi, Kanagawa 259-1292, Japan(Dated: December 22, 2013)The Collins e ect describes the modulation of the hadron production by a transversely polarizedquark with the sine of the polar angle, ’, between the produced hadron’s transverse momentumand the quark spin. We employ a quark-jet model to describe multiple hadron emissions by sucha quark, taking the Collins e ect into account. The resulting hadron distributions exhibit modu-lation up to fourth order in sin(’) when only two hadron emissions are allowed, rising with anyfurther increase in the number of emitted hadrons. These new e ects are a direct consequence ofthe quark-jet mechanism for quark hadronization, which do not depend on the details of the modelused for elementary hadron emission. The size and the sign of the higher order terms are directlyconnected with the probabilities of quark spin ip in the elementary emission process, with opposingsign favored and unfavored Collins functions only being generated if quark spin ip is preferential.Experimental studies of these e ects should therefore provide a critical test of the quark hadroniza-tion mechanism, which in turn will lead to a deeper understanding of the transverse spin structureof hadrons.
