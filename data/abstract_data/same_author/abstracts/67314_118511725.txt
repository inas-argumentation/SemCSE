The trace class conjecture in the theory of automorphic forms. II
. Let G be the group of real points of a reductive algebraic 
$ \Bbb {Q} $-group satisfying the assumptions made in [H, Chapter I] and let 
$ \Gamma $ be an arithmetic subgroup of G. Let 
$ R_{\Gamma} $ be the right regular representation of G on 
$ L^2(\Gamma \backslash G) $ and denote by 
$ R^d_\Gamma $ the restriction of 
$ R_\Gamma $ to the discrete subspace. In this paper we prove that for every integrable rapidly decreasing function f on G, the operator 
$ R^d_\Gamma (f) $ is of the trace class.
