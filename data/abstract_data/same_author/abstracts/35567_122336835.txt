Validity of scaling relations between nucleon form factors
We prove, within the framework of analytic S-matrix theory and the physical mass spectrum, that the following scaling relations cannot be rigorously true for all t: (i) G M V (t)∝G E V (t), (ii) G M V (t)=R 1 (t)G E V (t)+R 2 (t), where R 1,2 (t) are rational functions of t, (iii) F Π (t)∝G E V (t), and (iv) F Π (t)∝G M V (t).
