Mass relations and neutrino oscillations in an SO(10) model
Abstract We present in detail a grand unified model based on SO(10) which naturally reproduces known fermion mass relations and mixing angles. It predicts m ν e ⪡ m ν μ , m ν τ , and predominant ν μ - ν τ oscillations. Several other features of the model are analyzed in detail: construction of the Higgs potential, predictions for τ p and sin 2 θ w in a temporarily free model and a relation between the mass of the t-quark and the lifetime of the B meson.
