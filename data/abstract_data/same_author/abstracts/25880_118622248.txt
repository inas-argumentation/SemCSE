Solution of the Graded Yang-Baxter Equation Associated with the Vector Representation of Uq(Osp(M/2N))
A general method of constructing spectral parameter-dependent solutions of the graded Yang-Baxter equation using quantum supergroups is briefly reviewed. Using this method, a quantum R -matrix associated with the vector representation of U q ( osp ( M 2n )) is obtained for all M and n .
