Simulation of magnetized beams
The angular momentums of particles in a bunch of beam are of importance in many applications. Usually the angular momentums are related to the end field of solenoids. In electron cooling facilities the solenoid fields are widely used as they are found very helpful not only in focusing and guiding the charge particle beams but also in enhancing the cooling rates. The RHIC electron cooler is a challenging project because it requires a high performance facility including a solenoid with high quality strong field (1 tesla). Issues caused by the end fields of the solenoid have been studied to minimize the extra temperature and coherent motions. In this paper we report recent results in simulating the angular momentums of the electron beam and its application on the RHIC electron cooler
