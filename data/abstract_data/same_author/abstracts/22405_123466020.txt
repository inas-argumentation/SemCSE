EROS and MACHO Combined Limits on Planetary Mass Dark Matter in the Galactic Halo
The EROS and MACHO collaborations have each published upper limits on the amount of planetary-mass dark matter in the Galactic halo obtained from gravitational microlensing searches. In this Letter, the two limits are combined to give a much stronger constraint on the abundance of low-mass MACHOs. Specifically, objects with masses 10(-7) M. less than or similar to m less than or similar to 10(-3) M. make up less than 25% of the halo dark matter for most models considered, and less than 10% of a standard spherical halo is made of MACHOs in the 3.5 x 10(-7) M.
