Doorway states in s -, p -, and d -wave entrance channels in Pb 207 + n reaction
The /sup 207/Pb + n reaction has been studied by means of high resolution neutron transmission and differential elastic scattering measurements. Information pertaining to resonance parameters (E/sub n/,l,J, and GAMMA/sub n/) for some 118 resonances in the energy interval from 3 to 485 keV has been obtained. Distributions of neutron strengths have been determined for each value of J that can be formed by s-, p-, and d-wave neutrons interacting with the /sup 207/Pb ground state (J/sup ..pi../ = 1/2/sup -/). The existence of a doorway state in the J/sup ..pi../ = 1/sup -/, s-wave channel at approx. 500 keV is confirmed. Evidence is also found for the presence of doorway states in the p-wave (J/sup ..pi../ = 1/sup +/) and d-wave (J/sup ..pi../ = 1/sup -/) channels. The doorway states in the p-wave channel are shown to be correlated with M1 ground-state radiative strength in /sup 208/Pb.
