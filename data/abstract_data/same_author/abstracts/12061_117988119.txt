Observation of weak neutral currents and bare charm in Fermilab experiment 1A
Fermilab experiment 1A, a collaboration of physicists from Harvard, the University of Pennsylvania, and the University of Wisconsin, began taking data early in 1973. It soon observed neutrino‐induced events indicating the existence of a weak neutral current. At the same time, a particle with a new hadronic quantum number was also observed. The story of those observations is told here through the medium of the E‐1A publications of the period.
