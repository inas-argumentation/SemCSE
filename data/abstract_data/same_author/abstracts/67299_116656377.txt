Sous-vari'et'es d''une vari'et'e ab'elienne et points de torsion
This disclosure relates to a self-closing dispenser comprising a dispenser body defining a generally elongated passage having exit and entrance end portions, the body being molded from synthetic polymeric material and including two diametrically opposite relatively rigid and immovable upstanding posts, and a pair of resilient concavely outwardly opening walls extending laterally between the posts and axially between the passage exit and entrance end portions. Due to the (1) concave configuration of the resilient walls, (2) the heavier and more rigid posts, and (3) the molding of the dispenser from heated polymeric material, the exit end portion of the dispensing passage is held intimately sealed and though openable during pressurized product flow, will because of items (1) through (3) return to its normally sealed condition.
