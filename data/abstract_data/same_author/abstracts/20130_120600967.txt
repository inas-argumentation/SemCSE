Low-energy absorption of pions on nuclei and the real rho/sup 2/(r) potential
A Wick-formalism treatment of the sigma+..omega.. model dynamics is used to obtain the real and imaginary parts of the ..pi..-nucleus optical resulting from the true absorption of ''s-wave'' pions. Good agreement is obtained with the empirical value of the imaginary potential at threshold. The corresponding (dispersive) real potential is attractive. All parameters are taken from other experiments.
