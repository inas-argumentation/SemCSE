A note on a delayed autoregressive process in continuous time
dX(t) + aX(t) dt = d W(t), (1 1) where {X(t)}, for t real, is the time series, a is a real positive constant, and { W(t)} denotes a Wiener process. Clearly, this model is Markovian and an extensive literature is available on its analysis. See, for example, Bartlett (1978). So far as we know, very little discussion is available in the time series literature on the situation where the rate of change may be affected by the past state, i.e. hysteresis is present. Notable exceptions are some works by P. M. Robinson; see, for example, Chapter 7 of his Australian National University Ph.D. Thesis and Robinson (1977). That such an effect abounds in nature needs no emphasis; see, for example, Bellman & Cooke (1963, introductory pages).
