Projectile fragmentation of Ca40, Ca48, Ni58, and Ni64 at 140 MeV/nucleon
We have measured fragment cross-sections of projectile fragmentation reactions using primary beams of $^{40}\mathrm{Ca}$, $^{48}\mathrm{Ca}$, $^{58}\mathrm{Ni}$, and $^{64}\mathrm{Ni}$ at 140 MeV/nucleon on $^{9}\mathrm{Be}$ and $^{181}\mathrm{Ta}$ targets. The cross-sections were obtained by integrating the momentum distributions of isotopes with $Z\ensuremath{\geqslant}5$ measured in the A1900 fragment separator. We compare the extracted cross-sections to the predictions of the empirical parametrization of fragmentation cross-sections (EPAX).
