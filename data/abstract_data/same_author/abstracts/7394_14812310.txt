Strings in twistor superspace and mirror symmetry
Abstract We obtain the super-Landau–Ginzburg mirror of the A-twisted topological sigma model on a twistor superspace—the quadric in CP 3 | 3 × CP 3 | 3 which is a Calabi–Yau supermanifold. We show that the B-model mirror has a geometric interpretation. In a particular limit for one of the Kahler parameters of the quadric, we show that the mirror can be interpreted as the twistor superspace CP 3 | 4 . This agrees with the recent conjecture of Neitzke and Vafa proposing a mirror equivalence between the two twistor superspaces.
