DESIGN AND FABRICATION OF A 5-CELL HIGH CURRENT SUPERCONDUCTING CAVITY
Superconducting Energy Recovery Linacs have various applications, such as hard X-ray, electron-ion colliders and electron cooler of ions. The key component for accelerating such high current beams is the superconducting radio frequency cavity. The design of a 1.3 GHz 5-cell high current superconducting cavity has been carried out under the cooperation between Peking University and Argonne National Laboratory. Radio frequency property and damping of the higher order modes of this cavity have been discussed and the final design is presented. Room temperature RF measurements are also described in the paper.
