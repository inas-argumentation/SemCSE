Circular chromatic numbers of Mycielski's graphs
Abstract In a search for triangle-free graphs with arbitrarily large chromatic numbers, Mycielski developed a graph transformation that transforms a graph G into a new graph μ ( G ), we now call the Mycielskian of G , which has the same clique number as G and whose chromatic number equals χ ( G )+1. Let μ n ( G )= μ ( μ n −1 ( G )) for n ⩾2. This paper investigates the circular chromatic numbers of Mycielski's graphs.
