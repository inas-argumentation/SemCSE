Inter-linker consistency in the manual construction of hypertext documents
The creation of hypertext links by manual means is di cult and costly, but is often preferred to automatic methods in attempts to optimize hypertext `quality'. Experiments show, however, that levels of consistency amongst human link-creators|in common with those historically observed amongst human indexers|are generally low and variable, and that the e ectiveness of retrieval frommanually-constructed hypertexts can be expected to su er as a result. This ndingmotivates further comparison both of manual and of automatic methods of link creation.
