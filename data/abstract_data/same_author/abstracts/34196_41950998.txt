Type II supernovae from prompt explosions.
Evidence is cited that supernova 1987A involved a large explosion energy, approx. =(2--3) x 10/sup 51/ ergs. Such a large explosion energy has not come from delayed shocks to date, nor is it likely to. Improved physics in the presupernova evolution, especially the inclusion of Coulomb interactions, has brought the iron-core mass down by approx. >0.1M/sub sun/ in the 13M/sub sun/ star which has recently been evolved. We find that supernova explosion energies up to 3 x 10/sup 51/ ergs can be obtained by the prompt-explosion mechanism, provided that a somewhat soft equation of state is used at supranuclear densities.
