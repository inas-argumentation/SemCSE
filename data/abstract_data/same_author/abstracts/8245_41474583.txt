The Spectrum of the Hybrid Mesons with Heavy Quarks from the B . S . Equation
We construct the B.S. equation for the hybrid mesons under instantaneous approximation. The kernel is chosen as the sum of an one-gluon exchange potential and a linear confining potential. The equations are solved by numerical method, and the spectrum of hybrid mesons bb̄g and cc̄g are obtained. PACS: 14.40.C, 11.10.St.
