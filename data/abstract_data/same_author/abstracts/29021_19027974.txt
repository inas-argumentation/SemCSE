Rigidity Theorems in the Braneworld Model
In the present paper, we give some theorems representing rigidity of a vacuum brane in static bulk spacetimes. As an application, we show that a static bulk spacetime with dimension d ≥ 4 and spatial symmetry I0(D - 2), O(D - 1) or 0 + (D - 2, 1) does not, allow a vacuum brane with a black hole on it. We also show that if a static bulk spacetime with dimension D > -1 satsifying the vacuum Einstein equations can be foliated by a continuous family of vacuum branes with asymptotically constant curvature, it is a black string solution.
