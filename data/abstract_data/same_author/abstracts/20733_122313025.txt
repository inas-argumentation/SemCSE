Electroweak radiative corrections in dynamical models with Majorana neutrinos
The electroweak radiative correction parameter T= δρ ∗ α is estimated in dynamical models such as technicolour and fourth family condensate models in the presence of Majorana neutrino masses. The parameter T is calculated in lowest order dynamical perturbation theory from a generalisation of the Pagel-Stokar formulae. Parameter ranges exist that give negative values of T which may help to alleviate problems with technicolour models with orthogonal technicolour groups, SO( N ) TC .
