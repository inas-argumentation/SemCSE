Development of a Superconducting Cavity for the High Intensity Proton Linac in JAERI
The R&D work of a superconducting (SC) cavity for the high intensity proton linac in JAERI has been started in collaboration with KEK. The RF field calculation and the structural analyses of the cavity have been made for the design in the proton energy range between 100 and 1500 MeV. The results indicate the feasibility of the SC proton linac, while more optimization is required. A vertical test stand with a cryostat, a clean room and a water rinsing system has been constructed. We present the preliminary cavity design and the overview of the test stand.
