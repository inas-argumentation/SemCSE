PROGRESS ON COUPLED RFQ-SFRFQ ACCELERATOR FOR MATERIALS IRRADIATION RESEARCH
There is always high interest to study material irradiation damage effects based on accelerators. The bombardment of solids with high energy particles causes some changes in many important engineering properties. A materials irradiation facility based on coupled RFQ-SFRFQ accelerator will be built in Peking University. The concept design and the structure optimization have been completed. The progress on the coupled RFQ-SFRFQ accelerator will be presented in this paper.
