Theoretical description of octupole correlations in the Xe 138 – 148 nuclei
The Gogny force is used to study, in the framework of the mean field theory and the collective Hamiltonian method, the octupole correlations present in the [sup 138--148]Xe nuclei. The energy splittings between the ground state and the lowest-lying negative parity state are computed as well as the [ital B]([ital E]1) and [ital B]([ital E]3) transition probabilities. A peculiar behavior of the [ital B]([ital E]1)'s as a function of neutron number is predicted.
