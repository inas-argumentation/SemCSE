Mass, width, and decay constant of the first pion-excitation: a QCD sum rule approach
A Laplace QCD sum rule treatment of the first pion excitation (Π) as a subcontinuum resonance in the pseudoscalar channel is presented. Parameters characterizing this resonance are obtained via a global sum rule fit to the hadronic (π+Π+continuum) content of the appropriate sum rule, incorporating 1) Breit-Wigner departures from the narrow resonance approximation, 2) explicit direct single-instanton contributions, and 3) purely perturbative field-theoretical contributions to two- and three-loop order. The results of this analysis [MΠ=1.15±0.28 GeV, ΓΠ<0.48 GeV, r≡FΠ2MΠ4/fπ2mπ4=4.7±2.8; uncertainties are 90% confidence levels] are consistent with the present empirical range of the Π(1300) mass, and predict a rather large decay constant for this resonance. A by-product of this analysis is the prediction (mu+md)/2=7.7±2.2 MeV for the light quark masses evaluated at an energy scale of 1 GeV.
