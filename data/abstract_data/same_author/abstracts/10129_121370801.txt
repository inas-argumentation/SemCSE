Gauge fixing ambiguities, flux strings, and the unconstrained Yang-Mills theory
We find the inconstrained Yang-Mills Lagrangian in the variables of Goldstone and Jackiw. It takes canonical form allowing immediate path integral quantization. There are no gauge fixing ambiguities except in the presence of instantons. We generalize the Lagrangian to include point sources, and show that it is only necessary to fix the gauge along a string connecting two sources. We discuss the significance of the singularities of the Hamiltonian and suggest an interpretation.
