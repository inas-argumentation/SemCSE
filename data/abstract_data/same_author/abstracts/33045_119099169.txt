Three-flavor analysis of long-baseline experiments
We compare the analysis of existing and future neutrino oscillation long-baseline experiments, where we point out that the analysis of future experiments actually implies a 12-dimensional parameter space. Within the three-flavor neutrino oscillation framework, six of these parameters are the fit parameters, and six are the simulated parameters. This high-dimensional parameter space requires the condensation of information and the definition of performance indicators for the purpose needed. As the most sophisticated example for such an indicator, we choose the precision of the leptonic CP phase, and discuss some of the complications of its computation and interpretation.
