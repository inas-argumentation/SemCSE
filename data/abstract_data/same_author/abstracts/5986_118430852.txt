Analysis of mass difference of the $\pi$ and $\rho$ with Bethe-Salpeter equation
In this article, we take into account the one-pion exchange force besides the onegluon exchange force to study the mass difference of the π and ρ mesons with the Bethe-Salpeter equation. After projecting the Bethe-Salpeter equation into an simple form, we can see explicitly that the bound energy |Eπ| ≫ |Eρ|. PACS number: 12.39.Ki, 12.39.Pn
