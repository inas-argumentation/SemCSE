Strong Interactions and Reactions of Hyperons and Heavy Mesons
Symmetry properties of strong interactions of K mesons and hyperons are reviewed, including: experimental attempts to determine the relative parities of the strange particles; analyses of parity conservation in strong interactions; and studies of various postulated symmetries for K-meson and hyperon interactions. Dynamical aspects of these interactlons are also reviewed, with particular attenti on to the phenomenological treatment of K- low-energy reactions. A bibliography containing 167 references is included. (T.F.H.)
