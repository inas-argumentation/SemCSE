Many flavor QCD as exploration of the walking behavior with the approximate IR fixed point
Computing Research Center, High Energy Accelerator Research Organization (KEK), Tsukuba,305-0801, JapanWe present the ﬁrst report of the LatKMI collaboration on the the lattice QCD simulation per-formed at the KMI computer, “ϕ”, for the cases of 4 ﬂavors and 8 ﬂavors, the latter being ex-pected to be a candidate for the walking technicolor having an approximate scale invariance nearthe infrared ﬁxed point. The simulation was carried out base d on the highly improved staggeredquark (HISQ) action. In this proceedings,we report preliminaryresults on the spectrum, analyzedthrough the chiral perturbation theory and the ﬁnite-size hyperscaling.
