A high-resolution cylindrical drift chamber for the KEK B-factory
The Central Drift Chamber of the BELLE detector at the KEK B-factory is a cylindrical wire chamber device that uses a helium-based gas and aluminum field wires and is situated in a 1.5 T magnetic field. The transverse momentum resolution for charged tracks with 1.0 GeV/c transverse momentum is 0.35%. This paper describes the chamber's configuration and performance.
