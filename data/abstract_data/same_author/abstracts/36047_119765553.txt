Q FACTOR MEASUREMENTS ON PROTOTYPE FUSED QUARTZ PENDULUM SUSPENSIONS FOR USE IN GRAVITATIONAL WAVE DETECTORS
Abstract This paper describes measurements of the pendulum mode Q factors of prototype fused quartz test mass suspensions for use in interferometric gravitational wave detectors. Pendulums were constructed using bobs of masses ranging from approximately 12 g to 1.9 kg suspended by cylindrical fibres of standard fused quartz and Q factors obtained greater than 10 7 .
