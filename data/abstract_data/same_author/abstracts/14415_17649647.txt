Variational approximation for two-time correlation functions in Phi 4 theory: Optimization of the dynamics.
We apply the time-dependent variational principle of Balian and Veneroni to {Phi}{sup 4} theory. An appropriate parametrization for the variational objects allows us to write coupled dynamical equations from which we derive approximations for the two-time correlation functions involving two, three, or four field operators. {copyright} 1995 The American Physical Society.
