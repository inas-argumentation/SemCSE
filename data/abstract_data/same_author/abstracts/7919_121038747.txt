EFFECTIVE CHIRAL LAGRANGIANS, THE QUARK MODEL OF SUPERCONDUCTIVITY TYPE AND THE PROBLEM OF P-A DIAGONALIZATION
The vertices appearing after the inclusion of P-A transitions and a subsequent diagonalization of the chiral Lagrangian are analyzed in the quark model of superconductivity type. These vertices can conditionally be divided into physical and nonphysical ones. It is shown that if the Pauli-Villars regularization is used, the contribution of the latter can be eliminated from the description of the physical processes. This is in full agreement with the experimental data.
