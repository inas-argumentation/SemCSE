Higher derivative corrections to Lifshitz backgrounds
A bstractWe explore the effect of curvature-square corrections on Lifshitz solutions to the Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton system. After exhibiting the renormalized Lifshitz scaling solution to the system with parameterized R2 corrections, we turn to a toy model with coupling $ g\left( \phi \right)C_{{\mu \nu \rho \sigma}}^2 $ and demonstrate that such a term can both stabilize the dilaton and resolve the Lifshitz horizon to AdS2 × $ {{\mathbb{R}}^2} $. As an example, we construct numerical flows from AdS4 in the UV to an intermediate Lifshitz region and then to AdS2 × $ {{\mathbb{R}}^2} $ in the deep IR.
