Rapidity distributions of strange particles in Pb-Pb at 158 AGeV
The production at central rapidity of KS 0 , Λ , Ξ and Ω particles in Pb-Pb collisions at 158 AGeV has been measured by the NA57 experiment over a centrality range corresponding to the most central 53% of the inelastic Pb-Pb cross section. We present the rapidity distribution of each particle in the central rapidity unit. The distributions are analysed based on hydrodynamical models of the collisions.
