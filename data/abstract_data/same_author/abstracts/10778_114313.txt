Baryon octet magnetic moments to all orders in flavor breaking: An application to the problem of the strangeness in the nucleon
Using the general QCD parametrization (GP) we display the magnetic moments of the octet baryons including all flavor breaking terms to any order. The hierarchy of the GP parameters allows to estimate a parameter ${g}_{0}$ related to the quark loops contribution of the proton magnetic moment; its magnitude is predicted to be inside a comparatively small interval including the value given recently by Leinweber et al. from a lattice QCD calculation.
