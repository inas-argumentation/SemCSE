The Correction Of Scattering Cross Section Led By Noncommutative Effects
Möller Scattering and Bhabha Scattering on noncommutative spacetime is restudied. A new result is reported. It is shown that the correction of scattering cross sections led by the noncommutative effects is not always enhancement with the total energy of colliding electrons, but there is an optimal collision energy to get the greatest noncommutative correction for noncommutative effect. Moreover there is a linear relation between the noncommutative threshold energy and optimal collision energy, so that the noncommutative threshold energy can be deterimed by experiment in the next generation collider.
