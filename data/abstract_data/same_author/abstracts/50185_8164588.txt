Reconstruction and Motion Estimation from Apparent Contours under Circular Motion
In thispaperweaddresstheproblemof recoveringstructureandmotionfrom thecontoursof a smooth-curvedsurface.A novel andsimplertechniquefor computingthestructureof anobjectfrom its profilesis introduced.Experimentswith real datashow encouragingresults,which are comparableto thoseobtainedfrom muchmore sophisticatedtechniques.Furthermore,a new methodfor motion estimationfrom sequencesof profilesis proposed. Preliminaryresultsdemonstratethefeasibilityof thealgorithm.
