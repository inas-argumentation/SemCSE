Analysis of parallel mixed-mode simulation algorithms
This paper presents a performance analysis of several parallel algorithms for the mixed-mode simulation of digital systems. The performance model is an architectural simulation of the parallel algorithms running on a hypercube multiprocessor. Workload data is collected from a uniprocessor-based mixed-mode simulator on several benchmark circuits, and two distinct circuit partitioning algorithms are considered. The results indicate that the performance of the parallel simulations is highly circuit dependent, in some cases performing worse than a uniprocessor and in others approaching optimal performance.<<ETX>>
