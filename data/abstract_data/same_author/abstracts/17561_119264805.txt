Supersymmetric vacua of mass-deformed M2-brane theory
We count the supersymmetric vacua of mass-deformed N = 6 U ( N ) × U ( N ) Chern–Simons-matter theory by calculating the Witten index. When the Chern–Simons level k is 1, our result perfectly agrees with that from the gravity dual, given by partitions of N . For general k , our index generalizes partitions of N , including additional degrees. We also comment on non-relativistic superconformal theories constructed from this model.
