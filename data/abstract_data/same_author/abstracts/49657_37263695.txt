A complete solution to a conjecture on chromatic uniqueness of complete tripartite graphs

Koh, Teo and Chia conjectured that for any integers n and k with n>=k+2>=4, the complete tripartite graph K(n-k,n,n) is chromatically unique. Let K(n,m,r)-S denote the graph obtained by deleting all edges in S from the complete tripartite K(n,m,r).
