What we have learned from 3-fluid hydrodynamics up to now
A possibility to interpret available data from AGS and SPS within purely hadronic scenario is analyzed. This is based on analysis of problems met in simulations of heavy-ion collisions within 3-fluid dynamics with purely hadronic equation of state. The only exception has been done for success of the model in reproduction of transverse-mass spectra of various hadrons, since the excitation functions of inverse slopes of kaon spectra are interpreted as an indication of a phase transition. In particular, we try to indicate those data which apparently require assumption of a phase transition into quark-gluon phase for their reproduction.
