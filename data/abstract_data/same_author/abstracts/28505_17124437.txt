Charm Physics with BES-III at BEPC-II
We report on the charm physics potential at BES-III at BEPC-II which will make a significant contribution to quark flavor physics this decade. The charm physics program will include studies of leptonic, semileptonic and hadronic charm decays, and tests for physics beyond the Standard Model. Event samples of the order of 30 million D D ¯ pairs, 2 million D S + D S − pairs at threshold and 10 × 10 9 J / ψ decays will be produced with one year design luminosity. High precision charm data will enable us to validate forthcoming Lattice QCD calculations at a few percent level. These can then be used to make precise measurements of CKM elements, V cd , V cs , V ub , V cb and V ts , which are useful to improve the accuracy of CKM unitarity tests.
