A heavy ion linac for the CERN accelerator complex
The injector linac required by CERN for heavy ions. e.g. Pb28+. is being made in collaboration with several institutions in CERN member states. Starting with Pb28+ ions at 2.5 keV/u from an ECR source, the beam will be analyzed and matched into a Radio-Frequency Quadrupole (RFQ) which operates at 101.3 MHz and accelerated to 250 keV/u. After a matching section the beam is accelerated to 4.2 MeV/u by a three cavity Interdigital-H (IH) structure. Then follows stripping, beam analysis and debunching to produce a Pb53+ beam for the booster synchrotron. This paper summarizes lthe design. the technical solutions proposed and the present status.
