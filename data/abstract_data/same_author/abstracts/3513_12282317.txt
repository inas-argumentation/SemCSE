A note on “a mapping algorithm for computer-assisted exploration in the design of embedded systems”
The scaling of autoclave and leaching-equipment surfaces during the high pressure leaching of nickeliferous oxide and silicate ores is controlled during leaching to favor the formation of scale containing substantial amounts of magnesium sulfate that is more easily removed by chemical dissolution using water or dilute sulfuric acid at temperatures ranging from about 50 DEG C. to 250 DEG C. than scale containing substantial amounts of alunite.
