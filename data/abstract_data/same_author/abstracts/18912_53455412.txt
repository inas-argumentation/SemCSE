Negative specific heat in a thermodynamic model of multifragmentation
We consider a soluble model of multifragmentation which is similar in spirit to many models which have been used to fit intermediate energy heavy ion collision data. In this model, ${c}_{v}$ is always positive but for finite nuclei ${c}_{p}$ can be negative for some temperatures and pressures. Furthermore, negative values of ${c}_{p}$ can be obtained in canonical treatment. One does not need to use the microcanonical ensemble. Negative values for ${c}_{p}$ can persist for systems as large as 200 particles but this depends upon parameters used in the model calculation. As expected, negative specific heats are absent in the thermodynamic limit.
