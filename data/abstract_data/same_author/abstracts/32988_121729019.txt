High statistics study of the gluon propagator in the Landau gauge at ß = 6.0
A non-perturbative, high-statistics study of the gluon propagator in the Landau gauge is presented. The dependence of the propagator on the momenta is well described by the expression G(p 2 ) ≈ [Z (M 2 + (p 2 ) (p 2 Λ 2 ) α )] −1 where α ≌ 0.4–0.6 and M 2 is compatible with zero. We also find that other functional forms, previously proposed in the literature, fail to give a good description of the observed behaviour.
