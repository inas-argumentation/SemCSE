Double Higgs production in the littlest Higgs model with T-parity at high energy $$e^{+}e^{-}$$e+e- colliders
In the framework of the littlest Higgs model with T-parity (LHT), we investigate the double Higgs production processes $$e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow ZHH$$e+e-→ZHH and $$e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow \nu \bar{\nu }HH$$e+e-→νν¯HH at high energy $$e^{+}e^{-}$$e+e- colliders. We calculate the production cross sections and find that the relative correction at the center-of-mass energy $$\sqrt{s}=500$$s=500 GeV can maximally reach $$-30\,\%$$-30% for the process $$e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow ZHH$$e+e-→ZHH and $$-16\,\%$$-16% for the process $$e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow \nu \bar{\nu }HH$$e+e-→νν¯HH in the allowed parameter space, respectively. These large relative corrections can reach the detection range of the future $$e^{+}e^{-}$$e+e- colliders so that they can be used to test the LHT effect. The two relevant decay modes $$e^{+}e^{-}$$e+e-$$\rightarrow ZHH \rightarrow l\bar{l} b\bar{b}b\bar{b}$$→ZHH→ll¯bb¯bb¯ and $$e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow \nu \bar{\nu }HH\rightarrow \nu \bar{\nu }b\bar{b}b\bar{b}$$e+e-→νν¯HH→νν¯bb¯bb¯ are studied and some distributions of the signal and background are displayed.
