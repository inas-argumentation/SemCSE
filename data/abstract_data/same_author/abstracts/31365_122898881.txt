Preliminary dark matter limits from a large-mass, low background SDD
Abstract SDDs (Superheated Droplet Detectors) consist of a superheated liquid dispersed in a gel matrix, which undergoes a detectable phase transition to the gas state by energy deposition of incident high-LET radiation. The development of large-mass, low-background SDDs dedicated to WIMP searches is briefly described and preliminary results on spin-dependent neutralino couplings from prototype devices are presented. We comment on the installation of the first modules in the “Laboratoire Souterrain a Bas Bruit (LSBB) de Rustrel-Pays d'Apt” (1,500 mwe).
