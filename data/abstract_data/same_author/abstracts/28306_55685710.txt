A NEW TUNER FOR TESLA
The TESLA cryomodule design, with very low clearance within cavities, and the performance required to the Tesla cavities, in term of Lorentz detuning, pointed out hard requirements on the tuner system that can not be completely satisfied by the actual TTF design. To achieve the required stiffening and tunability range within the clearance requirements we designed a tuning system based on a complete flexural system. The main movement is driven by a leverage system to amplify the forces and increase the resolution to the required one. We present the design, the conceptual and technological solution of the tuning system and of its components. Warm characterization of the main components and cold tests confirmed the expected performances. Four tuners of this new design have been installed on the superstructures helium vessel to be operated during the superstructure tests on TTF, scheduled for this summer.
