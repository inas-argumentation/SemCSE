CHARGE FRACTIONIZATION IN N = 2 SUPERSYMMETRIC QCD
It is shown that the physical {open_quotes}quark number{close_quotes} charges which appear in the central charge of the supersymmetry algebra of N=2 supersymmetric QCD can take irrational values and depend nontrivially on the Higgs expectation value. This gives a physical interpretation of the constant shifts which the {open_quotes}electric{close_quote}{close_quote} and {open_quotes}magnetic{close_quote}{close_quote} variables a{sub D} and a undergo when encircling a singularity, and show that duality in this model is truly an electric-magnetic-quark number duality. Also included is a computation of the monodromy matrices directly in the microscopic theory. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
