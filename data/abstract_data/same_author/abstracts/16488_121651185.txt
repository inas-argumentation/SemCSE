The phase diagram of the chiral transition from lattice QCD
We determine the location of the second-order end-point of the line of the first-order chiral phase transition in three-flavor QCD at vanishing baryon chemical potential ( μ B ). Using multiparameter reweighting we determine the μ B -dependence of the transition line. With an ansatz inspired by chiral perturbation theory, we extrapolate to the chiral critical point in (2+1)-flavor QCD. For both quantities we find a significant quark mass dependence.
