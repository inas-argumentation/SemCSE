Neutrino factories and the 'magic' baseline
We show that for a neutrino factory baseline of $L\ensuremath{\sim}7300\char21{}7600\mathrm{km}$ a ``clean'' measurement of ${\mathrm{sin}}^{2}2{\ensuremath{\theta}}_{13}$ becomes possible, which is almost unaffected by parameter degeneracies. We call this baseline the ``magic'' baseline, because its length depends only on the matter density profile. For a complete analysis, we demonstrate that the combination of the magic baseline with a baseline of 3 000 km is the ideal solution to perform equally well for ${\mathrm{sin}}^{2}2{\ensuremath{\theta}}_{13},$ the sign of $\ensuremath{\Delta}{m}_{31}^{2},$ and CP violation sensitivities. In particular, this combination can very successfully resolve parameter degeneracies even below ${\mathrm{sin}}^{2}2{\ensuremath{\theta}}_{13}l{10}^{\ensuremath{-}4}.$
