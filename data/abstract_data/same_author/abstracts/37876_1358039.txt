Vortex Solutions of Nonrelativistic Fermion and Scalar Maxwell-Chern-Simons Theories
We have constructed nonrelativistic fermion and scalar field theories coupled to a Maxwell-Chern-Simons gauge field which admit static multi-vortex solutions.
