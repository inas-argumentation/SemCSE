Probing the finite temperature phase transition with N(f) = 2 nonperturbatively improved Wilson fermions
The critical temperature and the nature of the QCD finite temperature phase transition are determined for ${N}_{f}=2$ dynamical flavors of nonperturbatively improved Wilson fermions. The calculations are performed on large lattices with temporal extents ${N}_{t}=12$, 10, and 8, and lattice spacings down to $a=0.075\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{fm}$. We find that the deconfinement and chiral phase transitions take place at the same temperature. Our results are in broad agreement with a second order phase transition in the chiral limit. The critical temperature at the physical quark mass is found to be ${T}_{c}=174(3)(6)\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{MeV}$.
