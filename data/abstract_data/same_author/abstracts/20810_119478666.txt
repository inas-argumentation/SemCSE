A CS J = 5 ->4 Mapping Survey Towards High Mass Star Forming Cores Associated with H2O Masers
Sixty-three high mass star forming cores selected from the study of Plume et al. (1997; T ∗ R > 1K) were mapped in CS J= 5 → 4 at the CSO between September 1996 and July 1999. Nearly all of the cores observed lie in the first and second galactic quadrants. The cores were mapped using the on-the-fly technique with a square grid in RA-DEC coordinates oversampled at 10 resolution. The maps were extended until the CS emission was negligible (±50 for the average map).
