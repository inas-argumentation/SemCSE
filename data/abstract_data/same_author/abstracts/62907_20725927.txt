The evaluation and application of manufacturing green product based on fuzzy comprehensive assessment method
As new strategic industry proposed by Premier Wen, environmental protection has once again to become the centre of attention. However, green product as the inevitable outcome of sustainable development of human beings, the evaluation study on it is of great significance. Based on green manufacturing and the conception of green product, this paper establishes product evaluation process of multilevel closed-loop hierarchical through embedded system process model, with the restrictive standard setting for environmental indicators, a one-off test and hierarchical evaluation mechanism, and it also makes summative evaluation and periodic evaluation, diagnostic evaluation and dynamic optimization process organically integrated. After that, with the application process of green washing machine product on fuzzy comprehensive assessment method satisfies the users, the business, the society and even the environment.
