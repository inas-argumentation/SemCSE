Timed Modal Specification - Theory and Tools
In this paper we present the theory of Timed Modal Specifications (TMS) together with its implementation, the tool Epsilon. TMS and Epsilon are timed extensions of respectively Modal Specifications [7, 9] and the Tav system [6, 4]. Also, the theory of TMS is an extension of real-timed process calculi with the specific aim of allowing loose or partial specifications. This allows us to define a notion of refinement, generalizing in a natural way the classical notion of bisimulation.
