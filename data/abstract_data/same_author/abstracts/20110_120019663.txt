The Kawarabayashi-Suzuki-Riazuddin-Fayyazudin relation and strong mesonic couplings in the cloudy bag model
The Cloudy Bag Model (CBM) provides a theory of ωϱπ, ϱππ, K∗Kπ and K∗K∗π couplings that yields excellent agreement with experiment in a parameter-free calculation. The Kawarabayashi-Suzuki-Riazuddin-Fayyazudin (KSRF) relation is derived from the CBM and ‖ϱ is computed.
