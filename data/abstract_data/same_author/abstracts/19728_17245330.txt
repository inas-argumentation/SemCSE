Conformal supergravity from the AdS / CFT correspondence
Construction of a five-dimensional conformal supergravity (D = 5 CSG) is attempted by applying the AdS/CFT correspondence to the F(4) AdS supergravity in six dimensions. As a first step, local transformation laws of D = 5 CSG have been established, from which the Weyl weights of the various fields in D = 5 can be predicted. (C) 2000 Elsevier Science B.V.
