Sum rules for the decay of the baryon decuplet into the baryon and meson octets in broken SU(3) symmetry
We have investigated the sum rules for the decuplet → octet+octet coupling constants, taking into account the SU(3)-symmetry-breaking interactions to first order. There are seven sum rules for the possible twelve coupling constants. One of these sum rules involves only the coupling constants for the decays N*→NΠ, Y 1 *→ΛΠ, Y 1 *→ΣΠ, and Ξ*→ΞΠ, all of which are experimentally known. We also give the corresponding sum rules for 10* → 8+8 decay.
