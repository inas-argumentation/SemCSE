Triviality Bound on Lightest Higgs Mass in NMSSM
We study the implication of triviality on Higgs sector in next to minimal su-persymmetric model (NMSSM) using variational field theory. It is shown that mass of the lightest Higgs boson in NMSSM has an upper bound ∼ 10 M W which is of the same order as that in standard model.
