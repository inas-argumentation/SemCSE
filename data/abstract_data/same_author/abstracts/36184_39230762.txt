Exact identities in the Hubbard model and t-J model.
Exact identities among the nth extended s-wave-pairing amplitudes in the Hubbard model are presented. They provide more constraints on the possible symmetries of the superconducting gap in the Hubbard model than that between the on-site and the first extended s-wave pairings pointed out by Zhang. For the t-J model, we also find an exact identity between the on-site and the first extended s-wave-pairing amplitudes, which is essentially consistent with that in the Hubbard model.
