CROSS SECTIONS FOR TWO-PAIR PRODUCTION AT INFINITE ENERGY.
We give here the total cross sections for the following three high-energy processes: (i) $\ensuremath{\gamma}+\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{e}^{+}+{e}^{+}+{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}+{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}$, (ii) $\ensuremath{\gamma}+\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}+{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}+{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}+{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$, and (iii) $\ensuremath{\gamma}+\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}+{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}+{e}^{+}+{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}$. Up to the eighth order, all of them approach constants in the high-energy limit. These constants are 6.5, 0.23\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{\ensuremath{-}5}$, and 0.26\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{\ensuremath{-}3}$ \ensuremath{\mu}b, respectively, for (i), (ii), and (iii). The corresponding photon-photon elastic scattering amplitudes in the forward direction are also given. The creation of $\ensuremath{\mu}$ pairs is also briefly discussed.
