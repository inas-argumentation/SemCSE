IMPROVEMENT ON THE SINGLE BUNCH OPERATION OF THE TLS INJECTOR
The improvement on single bunch operation of the TLS (Taiwan Light Source) injector is presented in this study. Limited by the existing design of the TLS injector, the single bunch operation was not optimized in terms of bunch purity for specific users of TLS. A high voltage pulser was implemented to improve the situation. This pulser has been integrated into the high-voltage-deck (HV-deck) electronics of electron gun for single bunch generation. Both high-voltage pulses and the associated electron bunches are monitored with a wideband digital oscilloscope. The result shows that the bunch purity can be greatly improved by using the newly installed pulser. It also greatly eliminates the beam losses while injected into the booster ring.
