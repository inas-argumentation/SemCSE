Quantum cosmological Friedman models with an initial singularity
We consider the Wheeler–DeWitt equation Hψ = 0 in a suitable Hilbert space. It turns out that this equation has countably many solutions ψi which can be considered as eigenfunctions of a Hamilton operator implicitly defined by H. We consider two models, a bounded one, 0 < r < r0, and an unbounded, 0 < r < ∞, which represent different eigenvalue problems. Note that r is the symbol for the scale factor, usually denoted by a, or a power of it. The ψi form a basis of the underlying Hilbert space. We prove furthermore that the implicitly defined Hamilton operator is selfadjoint and that the solutions of the corresponding Schrodinger equation satisfy the Wheeler–DeWitt equation, if the initial values are superpositions of eigenstates. All solutions have an initial singularity in r = 0. Under certain circumstances a smooth transition from big crunch to big bang is possible.
