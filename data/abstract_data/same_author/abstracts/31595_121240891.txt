Thermal effects in inflation power spectra
If inflation was preceded by a thermal era, then there will be a thermal distribution of gravitons at the time of inflation. Gravitons produced during inflation will be amplified by stimulated emission into the existing thermal gravitons. This will result in an enhancement of the B-mode signal at large angles and which could be observable in WMAP and Planck experiments. Non-observation of this signal will have important implications on standard inflation models and will rule out warm inflation.
