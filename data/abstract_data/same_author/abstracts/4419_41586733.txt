Combining universal and odd RR axions for aligned natural inflation
We successfully embed the Kim-Nilles-Peloso (KNP) alignment mechanism for enhancing the axion decay constant in the context of large volume type IIB orientifolds. The flat direction is generated in the plane of (C-0-C-2) axions corresponding to the involutively even universal axion C-0 and odd axion C-2, respectively. The moduli stabilization with large volume scheme has been established as well.
