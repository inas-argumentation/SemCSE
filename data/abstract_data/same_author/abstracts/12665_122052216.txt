Charm production by partons in neutrino scattering
Abstract We present quantitative quark-parton model calculations of charm production contributions in neutrino scattering, to compare with the experimental indications of possible charm production, namely dimuon events and ḡnN y -distributions. Within the uncertainties in the present data and in the quark density distributions, charm production in the Glashow-Iliopoulos-Maiani scheme appears to offer a viable explanation of both effects.
