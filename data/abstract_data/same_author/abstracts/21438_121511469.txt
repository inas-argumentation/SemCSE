The ϱ-peak in the dimuon spectrum as a clock for fireball lifetimes in relativistic nuclear collisions
We discuss the modification of the dipleton yield from vector mesons inside a nuclear fireball. We argue that the relative magnitude of the ϱ peak in the dilepton spectrum determines the lifetime of the hadron gas phase in nuclear collisions with an accuracy of about 1 fm/ c . We also find that the φ (ω + ϱ) ratio measured in the dimuon channel in 200 A GeV O + U and S + U collisions points towards a rather short-lived hadronic phase in these collisions.
