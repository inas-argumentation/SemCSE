A STUDY OF O-16+C-12 ELASTIC-SCATTERING AT E/A = 10-MEV IN THE ALPHA-PARTICLE MODEL
Using a folding model based on the alpha-particle model, the differential cross sections for O-16+C-12 elastic scattering at 168 MeV have been calculated. This folding model, having no need of the renormalization, provides a good description of the experimental data.
