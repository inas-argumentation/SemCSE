A Proof of a Conjecture of Mariño-Vafa on Hodge Integrals
Fluorinated thermoplastic elastomers having a block structure: -[-Rf-H1-H2-H1-]- where -Rf- is a perfluoropolyoxyalkylene sequence of formula: -CF2O-(CF2CF2O)m-(CF2O)n-CF2- with m/n=0.2 DIVIDED 5, H1 and H2, equal or different from each other, are bivalent radicals having arylene, alkylarylene, alkylene or cycloalkylene structure, optionally containing fluorine and/or other heteroatoms selected among oxygen, sulphur, nitrogen. Such products are obtainable by utilizing a perfluoropolyoxyalkylene diol having a functionality at least equal to 1.97, preferably at least equal to 1.99, and are characterized by a tensile strength at 23 DEG C. at least 50% higher than that of the corresponding polyesters obtained starting from perfluoropolyoxyalkylene diols having a functionality not higher than 1.96.
