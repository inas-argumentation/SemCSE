Quark mass effects in QCD evolution equations and distribution functions of heavy quarks
We study the evolution equations of QCD taking the quark masses into account. The initial conditions of these equations, i.e., the quark and gluon distribution functions, are chosen at a sufficiently low Q/sup 2/ to allow one to use only the distribution functions of the light (valence) quarks and of the gluons.
