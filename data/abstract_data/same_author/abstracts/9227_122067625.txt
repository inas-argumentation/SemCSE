Quasielastic charmed-baryon production and exclusive strange-particle production by high-energy neutrinos
Three charmed-baryon candidates produced in the quasielastic reaction ..nu..n..--> mu../sup -/..lambda../sub c//sup +/..--> mu../sup -/..lambda pi../sup +/ were found in an exposure of the Fermilab deuterium-filled 15-ft bubble chamber to a wide-band neutrino beam. We also found that the cross section of the associated-production reaction ..nu..n..--> mu../sup -/..lambda..K/sup +/ is (5.5 +- 1.3 +- 1.1) x 10/sup -40/ cm/sup 2/ for E/sub ..nu../>10 GeV, and is approximately independent of energy. In addition we studied the cross sections of some quasi-two-body associated strange-particle production reactions other than ..mu../sup -/..lambda..K/sup +/.
