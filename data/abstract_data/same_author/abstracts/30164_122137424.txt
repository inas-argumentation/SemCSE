VECTOR MESONS AND THE POMERANCHUK TRAJECTORY
Within the framework of dispersion theory, it was shown that an elementary pole describing a vector particle necessarily induces a Regge trajectory with alpha (0) = 1 and positive signature, provided the pole appears in the crossed channel of an elastic scattering amplitude. The possibility that the Regge pole with alpha (0) = 1 has a superimposed branch or that it is a double pole is not excluded. If a vector meson is described by a Regge pole, the argument for the existence of an induced Pomeranchuk trajectory does not apply. (C.E.S.)
