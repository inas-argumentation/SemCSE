Phenomenological approach to chiral - symmetry breaking
It is shown that all meson-baryon and meson-meson $\ensuremath{\sigma}$ terms which can be extracted from experiment can be reconciled with the (3, \ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}3) chiral-symmetry-breaking scheme, ${H}^{\ensuremath{'}}={u}_{0}+{\mathrm{cu}}_{8}$, provided that $c$ assumes the value $c\ensuremath{\sim}\ensuremath{-}1.0$.
