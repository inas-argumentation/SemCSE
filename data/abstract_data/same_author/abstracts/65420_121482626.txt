The problem of realizing constraints in dynamics
Abstract Problems associated with the limiting transition in the second-order Lagrange equations, when the coefficients of rigidity and viscosity and added masses tend to infinity are considered. Under certain conditions, the solutions of the initial equations approach those of the limiting problem with constraints. For integrable constraints, the limiting equations are identical with the usual equations with constraint multipliers. In the case of non-integrable constraints, the solutions depends closely on the way in which they are realized. The generalized models of the dynamics of systems with non-integrable constraints and the properties of the limiting equations of motion are discussed.
