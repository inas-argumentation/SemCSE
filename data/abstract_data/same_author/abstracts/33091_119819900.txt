Neutral vector boson production in high energyep collisions for minimal extensions of the standard model
We study the direct production of neutral vector bosons in the processep→eZX in the standard model and two of its minimal extensions with differentZ-fermion couplings. We find that a measurement of the integrated lepton asymmetry with longitudinally polarized electron beams seems promising to discriminate between the standard model and these minimal extensions, even at HERA energies.
