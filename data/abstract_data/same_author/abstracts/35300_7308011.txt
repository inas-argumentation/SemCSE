Calculable CP-violating phases in the minimal seesaw model of leptogenesis and neutrino mixing
We show that all nontrivial CP-violating phases can be determined in terms of three lepton flavor mixing angles and the ratio of Deltam(sun)(2) to Deltam(atm)(2) in the minimal seesaw model in which the Frampton-Glashow-Yanagida (FGY) ansatz is incorporated. This important point allows us to make very specific predictions for the cosmological baryon number asymmetry and CP violation in neutrino oscillations. A measurement of the smallest neutrino mixing angle will sensitively test the FGY ansatz, in particular, in the case that three light neutrinos have a normal mass hierarchy.
