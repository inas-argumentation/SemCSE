CHARGED TOP-PION CORRECTIONS TO THE TOP QUARK PRODUCTION AT LC IN TC2 THEORY
We discuss the contributions of PGBs (technipions, top-pions) to the top quark production at the high energy e+e- linear collider (LC) experiments in TC2 theory. We find that the corrections to the production cross-section mainly come from top-pions. The corrections of the charged top-pions are significantly large in most of the parameter space. With reasonable value of the parameters, the correction to can reach about 8% which may be detected at LC experiments.
