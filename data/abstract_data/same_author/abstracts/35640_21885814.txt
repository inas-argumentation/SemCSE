Measurable decay modes of barium isotopes via exotic cluster emissions.
Cluster decays of $^{112\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}120}\mathrm{Ba}$ nuclei are calculated within a preformed cluster model. The \ensuremath{\alpha}-nuclei $^{4}\mathrm{He}$, $^{8}\mathrm{Be}$, $^{12}\mathrm{C}$, $^{16}\mathrm{O}$, and $^{20}\mathrm{Ne}$ are predicted as the possible decay modes, lying within the limits of present experimental methods. Other than the \ensuremath{\alpha} particle, $^{12}\mathrm{C}$ decay of $^{112}\mathrm{Ba}$ is shown to be the most probable one with half-life time ${\mathit{T}}_{1/2}$\ensuremath{\sim}${10}^{4}$ s, stressing the role of doubly magic $_{50}^{100}\mathrm{Sn}$ daughter nucleus in cluster radioactivity. The use of different Q values for ${\mathit{T}}_{1/2}$ estimates and presence of nuclear structure effects in Geiger-Nuttall plots are also discussed.
