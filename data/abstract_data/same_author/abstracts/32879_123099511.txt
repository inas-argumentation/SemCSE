Photon-photon scattering at high energy and fixed angle
In this paper, we study the photon-photon elastic scattering amplitude in the limit of the incident energy $\ensuremath{\omega}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\infty}$ with the scattering angle $\ensuremath{\theta}$ fixed. In the lowest (4th) order, this amplitude is a function of $\ensuremath{\theta}$ only. We calculate explicitly these scaling functions of $\ensuremath{\theta}$ and find that they are surprisingly simple. The higher-order photon-photon scattering amplitudes have $\mathrm{ln}s$ factors. Thus scaling is violated. These $\mathrm{ln}s$ factors come from self-energy diagrams and vertex diagrams only and can therefore be absorbed into the running coupling constant. The photon-photon scattering amplitude of the $n\mathrm{th}$ perturbative order is hence of the form of ${e}^{4}{[e(t)]}^{n\ensuremath{-}4}$ times a function of $\ensuremath{\theta}$.
