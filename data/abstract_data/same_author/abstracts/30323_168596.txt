Upper limits on charm-changing neutral-current interactions.
We report on a search for charm-changing neutral-current decays and lepton-family-number--nonconserving decays of charmed hadrons using inclusive and exclusive decays of charmed hadrons into oppositely charged dileptons. We find no evidence for such interactions.
