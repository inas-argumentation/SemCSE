Nuclear dilepton production of Drell-Yan process in the constituent quark model.
The high-mass dilepton production on nuclear targets observed by the E772 Collaboration is analyzed by the constituent quark model. We show that the constituent quark model even without the nuclear shadowing improvement agrees better with the existing data on nuclear targets not only in the inelastic-scattering process, but also in the Drell-Yan process. Further, the QCD improvement for the partonic shadowing model is made. It is found that both the {ital Q}{sup 2} dependence of the shadowing strength and the momentum loss due to the annihilation of the shadowed sea quarks evidently depress the distributions of nuclear sea components in the antishadowing region.
