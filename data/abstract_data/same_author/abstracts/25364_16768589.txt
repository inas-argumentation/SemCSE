A complete parton level analysis of boson-boson scattering and electroweak symmetry breaking in ℓν + four jets production at the LHC
A complete parton level analysis of lν + four jets production at the LHC is presented, including all processes at order (αEM6), (αEM4αS2) and (αEM2αS4). The infinite Higgs mass scenario, which is considered as a benchmark for strong scattering theories and is the limiting case for composite Higgs models, is confronted with the Standard Model light Higgs predictions in order to determine whether a composite Higgs signal can be detected as an excess of events in boson-boson scattering.
