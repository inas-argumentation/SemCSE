The minimal supersymmetric standard model and electroweak precision data
Abstract Our general approach for a model independent analysis of precision electroweak data, based on the variables ϵ 1 , ϵ 2 , ϵ 3 and ϵ b , is applied to the particularly interesting case of the Minimal Supersymmetric Standard Model. The model is at least as good in agreement with the data as the standard model. Actually the fit to especially ϵ 3 and ϵ b becomes better than for the standard model if the chargino and the scalar top are light (that is, close to their experimental limits and within reach of LEP200).
