Spinfoam fermions
A serious shortcoming of spinfoam loop gravity is the absence of matter. See for instance [1, 2] and full references therein. Here we study a minimal coupling of the theory with a chiral fermion field and a Yang-Mills field. We find that the dynamical coupling of a fermion to quantum gravity is surprisingly simple in the spinfoam formalism. This result resonate with similar ones in early canonical loop theory [3, 4]: the naive fermion hamiltonian was found to be just the extension of the simple “shift” form [5] of the gravitational hamiltonian, to the open ends of the gravitational Faraday lines, which represent fermions. Here we find that in the spinfoams context the fermion dynamics is a natural extension of the gravitational amplitude to spin-networks with spin1 2 open-ends at the nodes. In the presence of fermions, it is then easy to add YangMills fields by promoting a global symmetry to a local symmetry. The dynamics can then be generated à la Zeld’hovic [6, 7] from fermion loops. For earlier studies on how to couple fermions to a discretized gravitational field, see [8–11]. For detailed studies of fermions in the loop quantum gravity canonical formalism, see [12–15]. On fermions in 3d, spinfoams and quantum cosmology and the effect of fermions on Holst gravity, see also [16–20].
