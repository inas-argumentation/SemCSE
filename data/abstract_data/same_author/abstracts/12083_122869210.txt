A possible new probe of the energy generating mechanisms of GRBs and AGN
Abstract The suggestion is made here to search for possible time and angular position correlations between energetic gamma-rays detected by the Compton Gamma-Ray Observatory and upward-going muons produced by energetic neutrino interactions in the rock surrounding the cavity in which the massive, underground neutrino detector Superkamiokande resides. The motivation and feasibility of the search are discussed.
