Properties of energy-independent nuclear optical potentials
For separable, coupled-channel model interactions we show how to construct energy-independent optical potentials, $\overline{U}$. The elastic phase shifts and nuclear wave functions obtained from $\overline{U}$ are identical at all energies with those of the conventional energy-dependent optical potential $U(E)$. Whenever there is strong absorption or a compound resonance $\overline{U}$ has an extremely rapid variation with momentum.
