Anyons from bosons
Abstract We consider systems containing two or more distinct species of particles in two spatial dimensions. In quantizations of these systems, the statistics of composites containing more than one type of particle are not completely determined by the statistics of the constituents. In particular there exist quantum theories in which two bosons can combine to form an anyon with any desired statistical angle. We demonstrate these results using the topological approach to quantum kinematics, which in this case leads to a generalization of ordinary braid theory in two dimensions. Comparisons are made to three-dimensional systems.
