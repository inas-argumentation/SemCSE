Current bounds on technicolor with scalars
Abstract Technicolor with scalars is the simplest dynamical symmetry breaking model and one in which the predicted values of many observables may be readily calculated. This Letter applies current LEP, Tevatron, CESR, and SLAC data from searches for neutral and charged scalars and from studies of b -physics to obtain bounds on technicolor with scalars. Expectations for how upcoming measurements will further probe the theory's parameter space are also discussed.
