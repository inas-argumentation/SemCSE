Does D=4, N=8 supergravity really know about heterotic strings?
A coincidence allows the sigma -model which describes a four-dimensional heterotic string propagating in a background of fields with spins Os2, to be coupled to all of the bosonic fields of a D=4, N=8 supergravity theory. This requires a special form of N=8 supergravity with fields forming representations of Spin(6). The usual SU(8) formulation cannot be coupled as a background to a four-dimensional type-II Green-Schwarz action or the heterotic sigma -model.
