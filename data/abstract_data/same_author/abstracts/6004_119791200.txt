Zero-Point Time of Quantum Fields
Based on the first principles and without resorting to any additional assumption, a new physical quantity describing the space-time position of particle-number center of quantum fields is introduced, which can be regarded as a generalized Noether charge related to the symmetry under a local gauge transformation. As an interesting result, one can show that quantum fields have a zero-point time, which is related to the zero-point energy of quantum fields via the Heisenberg uncertainty principle.
