Picture information measures for similarity retrieval
A family of picture information measures based upon the concept of the minimum number of gray level changes to convert a picture into one with a desired histogram is presented. This family of picture information measures is found to satisfy a number of axions and useful inequalities. A natural extension to a Lorenz information measure is discussed. Based upon these information measures, a structural information measure can also be defined for logical pictures. Application to similarity retrieval in a pictoral information system are then suggested.
