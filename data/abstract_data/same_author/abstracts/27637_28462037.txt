A preliminary study of beam instabilities in top-up operation at Taiwan Light Source
The storage ring of Taiwan Light Source (TLS) started to operate fully in top-up mode since October 2005. The beam current has been gradually increased to 300 mA in routine user operation. Phenomena of collective effects were observed at 300 mA in top-up operation mode. Active feedback systems were implemented to stabilize the beam in top-up mode. Results of beam observation and analysis will be presented.
