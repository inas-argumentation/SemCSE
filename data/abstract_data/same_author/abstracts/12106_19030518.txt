Hadron Spectroscopy from BES and CLEOc
Recent results from BES and CLEOc experiments on hadron spectroscopy and charmonium decays using J/ψ, ψ′ and ψ″ data samples collected in e+e− annihilation are reviewed, including the observation of X(1835) in J/ψ → γπ+π−η′, study of the scalar particles in J/ψ radiative and hadronic decays, as well as in χc0 hadronic decays, and the study of the “ρπ puzzle” in J/ψ, ψ′, and ψ″ decays.
