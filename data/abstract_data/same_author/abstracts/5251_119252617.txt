Holographic superconductor models with $RF^{2}$ corrections
We investigate the effect of the RF 2 correction on the holographic superconductor model in the background of AdS black hole. We find that, similar to the effect caused by the Weyl correction, the higher RF 2 correction term can make it easier for the scalar operator to condense and result in the larger deviations from the expected relation in the gap frequency. However, if a non-trivial hair for the black hole has been triggered, we observe that the RF 2 correction and the Weyl correction do play different roles in the behavior of the condensation, which can be used to support the existing findings.
