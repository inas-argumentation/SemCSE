Decays of scalar mesons in the nonlocal quark model
We consider the decays of the scalar (J/sup P/ = 0/sup +/) mesons and the pseudovector A/sub 1/ (J/sup P/ = 1/sup +/) meson in the nonlocal quark model. We investigate the hypotheses of these mesons being two- or four-quark states. The present experimental data favor two-quark states.
