The Problem of Mass: Mesonic Bound States Above Tc
By extending the Bielefeld LGS (Lattice Gauge Simulation) color singlet interaction, we find that the masses of π , σ , ρ and a 1 excitations, 32 degrees of freedom in all, go to zero (in the chiral limit) as T → T c . This result indicates a smooth phase transition at T c , at which from above the masses and couplings of mesons vanish a la Brown-Rho scaling. We discuss that our scenario successfully explains the STAR (STAR Collaboration) ρ 0 / π − ratio in Au-Au peripheral collisions at RHIC.
