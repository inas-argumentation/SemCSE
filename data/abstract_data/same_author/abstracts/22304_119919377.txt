On mass zero indecomposable representations of the Poincaré group
Considering the space of massless‐particle wavefunctions transforming locally according to the irreducible representation D(m,n) of the homogeneous Lorentz group, we determine explicitly, by a direct and elementary method, the matrices constituting the (reducible but indecomposable) representation of Lorentz transformations with respect to a helicity basis.
