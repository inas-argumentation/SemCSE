ASYMPTOTICAL BEHAVIOUR OF A TEST OF DISCORDANCY FOR AN INCREASING NUMBER OF OUTLIERS
In a previous paper the author introduced certain tests of discordancy in time series. The asymptotic distributions of the test statistics were calculated for increasing sample size, the number of outliers however was assumed to be fixed. The tests showed to be not consistent. In this paper we consider the asymptotic behaviour of a suitable transformation of a test statistic, if the number of outliers depends on the sample size. If the degree of contamination is not too big, the test of discordancy has an almost consistent behaviour. AMS 1980 Classifications: 62 M 10, 62 F 03
