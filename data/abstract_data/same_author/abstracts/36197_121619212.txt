Scale dependent quark mass in color gaue theory and e+e- annihilation
Withibn color gauge-theory we adopt renormalization prescription which introduces a scale-dependent quark mass to analyse τ(e + e - →hadrons). We obtain a satisfactory fit with four flavors (u, s, d, c) and one heavy lepton. Within such model one can conclude that a scale-dependent effective quark mass is more suitable to describe the experiments than a scale indpendent mass.
