Geometric approach to discrete series of unirreps for Vir.
Abstract We want to realize the discrete series of unirreps for the Virasoro-Bott group Vir (= the central extension of Diff+(S1)) in the space of holomorphic functions on the infinite dimensional Kahler manifold M = Diff+(S1)/S1. The explicit formulae are given for the action of Vir in the space of polynomial functions in the natural complex coordinates on M.
