Production mechanism of the X(1740) meson
Abstract New data from π - p charge-exchange at 38 GeV/ c confirm the existence of the narrow X (1740) meson, observed in its ηη decay mode. The upper limit on its width is 80 MeV. It is not observed decaying into either η'η or π 0 π 0 . This meson is produced in charge-exchange by a mechanism other than OPE with a nucleon isobar (probably Δ(1232)), and not with a neutron.
