Evidence for a strongly bound kaonic system K ppn in the 4 He(stopped K ,n) reaction
We measured a neutron energy spectrum by means of time-of-flight (TOF) from the 4 He(K − stopped ,n) reaction by stopping negative kaons in a super-fluid helium target. A distinct peak was observed in the neutron spectrum, indicating the formation of a strongly bound kaonic system, K − ppn, with the binding energy and width, BKppn = 173 ± 4 MeV and Kppn . 25 MeV, respectively.
