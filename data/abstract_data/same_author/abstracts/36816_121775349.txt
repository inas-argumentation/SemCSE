Comment on an Incorrect Interdependence of the {PCAC} - Quark Masses, the Potential {CP} Violating Phase in {QCD} and the Mass of the Alleged Axion
The potentially CP -violating phases revealed by the sectors with nontrivial topological charge within QCD are shown to be cancelled dynamically by the nontrivial structure of the ground state. The effects of arbitrary quark masses, not invalidating this result, are discussed.
