Transition from big crunch to big bang in brane cosmology
We consider branes N = I × S0, where S0 is an n–dimensional space form, not necessarily compact, in a Schwarzschild-AdS(n+2) bulk N . The branes have a big crunch singularity. If a brane is an ARW space, then, under certain conditions, there exists a smooth natural transition flow through the singularity to a reflected brane N , which has a big bang singularity and which can be viewed as a brane in a reflected Schwarzschild-AdS(n+2) bulk N . The joint branes N ∪ N can thus be naturally embedded in R × S0, hence there exists a second possibility of defining a smooth transition from big crunch to big bang by requiring that N ∪ N forms a C∞-hypersurface in R × S0. This last notion of a smooth transition also applies to branes that are not ARW spaces, allowing a wide range of possible equations of state.
