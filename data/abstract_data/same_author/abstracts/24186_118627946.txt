Recent Results on B Decays from CLEO
Using the CLEO detector[2] at the Cornell Electron Storage Ring (CESR), we have measured, or set limits on, a variety of hadronic decays of the B meson with branching ratios in the range of 10-3 to 10-5. The data sample consists of 3.1 fb-1 on the Υ(4S) resonance which contains 3.3 million B B events and 10 million continuum events.
