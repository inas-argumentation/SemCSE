The linear polarization of nearby bright stars measured at the parts per million level

The majority of stars in the sample show measurable polarization, but most polarizations are small with 75% of the stars having P < 2 ×10 5 . Correlations of the polarization with distance and position, indicate that most of the polarization is of interstellar origin.
