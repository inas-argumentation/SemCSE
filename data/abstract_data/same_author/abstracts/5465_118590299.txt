Trapped, Two-Armed, Nearly Vertical Oscillations in Disks with Toroidal Magnetic Fields II: Effects of Finite Thickness
We examine the radial trapping of two-armed (m = 2) vertical p-mode oscillations in geometrically thin relativistic disks that are vertically isothermal, but terminated at a certain height by the presence of hot and low-density corona. The disks are assumed to be subject to toroidalmagneticfields. The oscillationsare classified by n, anumber related to the node number of oscillations in the vertical direction, and starting from n = 1. This study is a preparation to examine whether these oscillations can describe kilo-hertz quasi-periodic oscillations (kHz QPOs), horizontal branch oscillations (HBOs), and their correlations.
