SCALAR AND TENSOR STATES IN PROCESSES WITH PSEUDOSCALAR MESONS
In approach, based on analyticity and unitarity, experimental data on the isoscalar S- and D-waves of processes ππ → ππ, , ηη, ηη′ have been analyzed for studying the status and QCD nature of the f0- and f2-mesons below 2.3 GeV. Assignment of scalar and tensor mesons to lower nonets is proposed.
