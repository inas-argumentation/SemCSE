Three-dimensional QED with four-fermion interactions at finite temperature.
Using the imaginary-time formalism we derive the temperature-dependent gap equation for the dynamically generated fermion mass of three-dimensional QED with four-fermion interactions. Solving the gap equation numerically, we obtain critical temperatures for several values of [ital N], which is the number of flavors of fermion, of the gauge coupling, and of the four-fermion coupling. We exhibit the diagrams of the relation between mass and temperature.
