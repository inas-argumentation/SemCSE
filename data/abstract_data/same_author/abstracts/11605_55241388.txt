Potential energy surfaces of actinide and transfermium nuclei from multi-dimensional constraint covariant density functional theories
Multi-dimensional constrained covariant density functional theories were developed recently. In these theories, all shape degrees of freedom β λμ deformations with even μ are allowed, e.g., β 20 , β 22 , β 30 , β 32 , β 40 , β 42 , β 44 , and so on and the CDFT functional can be one of the following four forms: the meson exchange or point-coupling nucleon interactions combined with the non-linear or density-dependent couplings. In this contribution, some applications of these theories are presented. The potential energy surfaces of actinide nuclei in the ( β 20 , β 22 , β 30 ) deformation space are investigated. It is found that besides the octupole deformation, the triaxiality also plays an important role upon the second fission barriers. The non-axial reflection-asymmetric β 32 shape in some transfermium nuclei with N = 150, namely 246 Cm, 248 Cf, 250 Fm, and 252 No are studied.
