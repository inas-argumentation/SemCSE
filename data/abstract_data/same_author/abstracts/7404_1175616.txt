Multiple dipole bands at prolate shapes in 136Nd and their description through the projected shell model.
Five bands dominated by strong {ital M}1 transitions have been observed in the {sup 136}Nd nucleus. Two of them consist of only dipole transitions, whereas the other three show also weak crossover transitions. The bands have been firmly connected to lower-spin states. Theoretical calculations using the projected shell model suggest quasiparticle configurations involving two protons and two neutrons for the four lowest-lying bands. {copyright} {ital 1996 The American Physical Society.}
