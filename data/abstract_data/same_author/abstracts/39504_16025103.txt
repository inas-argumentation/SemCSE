The Holomorphic Tension of Nonabelian Vortices and the Quark = Dual-Quark Condensate
We continue the work hep-th/0411075 considering here the case of degenerate masses. A nonabelian vortex arises in r-vacua upon the breaking by a superpotential for the adjoint field. The holomorphic tension, equal to the dual-quark condensate, is found to be exactly equal to the quark condensate. We explain this equality as a consequence of the invariance of the BPS tension along the Higgs branch.
