Braid group representations arising from the generalized chiral Potts models
A class of braid group representations are construced for each non-singular bilinear form B : (Z/NZ) l ×(Z/(NZ) l →Z/NZ with N odd. Associated link invariants are given as a Gauss sum involving the Seifert matrix and B. With a special choice of B these representations are Yang-Baxterized to the sl(n) generalizations of the chiral Potts model discovered recently
