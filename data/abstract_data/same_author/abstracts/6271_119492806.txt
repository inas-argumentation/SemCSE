The on-line satellite/central computer facility of the Multiparticle Argo Spectrometer System
An on-line satellite/central computer facility has been developed at Brookhaven National Laboratory as part of the Multiparticle Argo Spectrometer System (MASS). This facility, consisting of a PDP-9 and a CDC-6600, has been successfully used in a study of proton-proton interactions at 28.5 GeV/ c .
