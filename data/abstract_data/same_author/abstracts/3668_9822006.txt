Named Entity Recognition in Biomedical Literature Using Two-Layer Support Vector Machines
A beam receiver secured to an upper portion of a blade of an earth working equipment can rotate in a horizontal plane and move in a vertical direction and, accordingly, can catch a laser beam from a beam projector regardless of a direction of the equipment. When the beam has been deviated out of the beam receiver, the direction of the deviation is detected so that the beam can be received by the beam receiver again. The height of the beam receiver relative to the blade can be adjusted and the level of the blade can thereby be controlled very accurately.
