String Field Equations and the Dual S Matrix From the Exact Renormalization Group
We investigate explicitly the variation, with respect to the Liouville mode α, of the Polyakov path integral corresponding to the closed bosonic string, including sources for all massless and massive string modes. Global conformal invariance is seen to imply a set of equations of motion for these sources, which we derive in closed form. The consideration of non-trivial backgrounds requires one to carry out a renormalization procedure, which generates renormalization type equations valid away from the conformal point. In the trivial vacuum the equations of motion involve just a bilinear interaction term which is closely related to operator product coefficients. The iterative solution to these equations allows us to construct the dual S-matrix.
