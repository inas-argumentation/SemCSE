On levels in arrangements of lines, segments, planes, and triangles
We consider the problem of bounding the complexity of the k th level in an arrangement of n curves or surfaces, a problem dual to, and an extension of, the well-known k-set problem. Among other results, we prove a new bound, O(nk 5/3 ) , on the complexity of the k th level in an arrangement of n planes in R 3 , or on the number of k -sets in a set of n points in three dimensions, and we show that the complexity of the k th level in an arrangement of n line segments in the plane is \(O(n\sqrt{k}\alpha(n/k))\) , and that the complexity of the k th level in an arrangement of n triangles in 3-space is O(n 2 k 5/6 α(n/k)) . 26 June, 1998 Editors-in-Chief: la href=../edboard.html#chiefslJacob E.
