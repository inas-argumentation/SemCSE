The light-cone gauge, Chern-Simons theory and topologically massive Yang-Mills theory
In this paper, we complete our study of one-loop perturbative Chern-Simons theory in the light-cone gauge n · A a = 0, n 2 = 0. Using a gauge-invariant regularization procedure, which defines Chern-Simons theory as the large-mass limit of a topologically massive field theory, we evaluate the three-point function Λ μνω abc ( p 1 , p 2 , p 3 ) to one loop. The spurious poles of ( q · n ) −1 in the gauge propagator are treated with the n μ ∗ - prescription . It is shown that the vertex function, which contains both local and nonlocal terms, is UV finite, respects the Ward identity and agrees with the one-loop effective action proposed in the preceding paper.
