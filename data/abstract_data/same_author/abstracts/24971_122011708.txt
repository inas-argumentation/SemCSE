Amplitudes and production mechanisms for K∗(890) resonance production at 4 GeV/c☆
Amplitudes for the two line-reversed reactions, K−p → K∗(890)n and K+n → K∗(890)p, are calculated using 4 GeV/c ANL spectrometer data. A simple model consisting of exchange degenerate Regge poles together with non-evasive “cut” contributions provides a good description of all features of the data. The SU(3) predictions of the model for ϱ and ω production and for ϱω interference effects are found to be in good agreement with the existing data.
