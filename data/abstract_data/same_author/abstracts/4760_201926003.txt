THE BEPCII DCCT SYSTEM
A DC Current Transformer (DCCT) as a standard diagnostic system for beam current plays an important role in BEPCII, the upgrade project of the Beijing Electron Positron Collider. Two DCCTs are operating now, separately in the electron and positron rings, to monitor the beam current, the beam injection rate, the beam loss rate and the beam lifetime. In this paper, the mechanical structure design, readout system and data processing are presented. The progress of DCCTs on each step of BEPCII commissioning, such as improving the beam lifetime and reducing the background noise, are also included.
