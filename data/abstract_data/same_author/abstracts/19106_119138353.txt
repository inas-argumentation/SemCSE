Inner structure of Gauss-Bonnet-Chern Theorem and the Morse theory
We define a new one-form HA based on the second fundamental tensor , the Gauss–Bonnet–Chern form can be novelly expressed with this one-form. Using the ϕ-mapping theory we find that the Gauss–Bonnet–Chern density can be expressed in terms of the δ-function δ(ϕ) and the relationship between the Gauss–Bonnet–Chern theorem and Hopf–Poincare theorem is given straightforwardly. The topological current of the Gauss–Bonnet–Chern theorem and its topological structure are discussed in details. At last, the Morse theory formula of the Euler characteristic is generalized.
