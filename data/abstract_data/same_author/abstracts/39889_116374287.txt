Photon Bremsstrahlung in Collision of High-energy Electrons
Single bremsstrahlung occurring upon collision of high-energy electrons is considered. The angular distribution and the spectrum of the emitted photons is calculated in the c.m.s. and in the lab., and the radiation of the incoming particle and of the recoil particle is considered in the latter system. The method of the classical currents and the Weizsacker-Williams method are analyzed. It is shown that the latter is applicable to the calculation of the spectrum of the recoil photons emitted by the particle when w > m/2.
