On Dynamic and Concurrent Model of Web Service Components
The researches of Web service fasten on composition process, and are lack of the formalized description on dynamic attributes of service component itself. Aiming to these, a new dynamic and concurrent model of Web service component is presented. Firstly, this paper analyzes the process of state transition of service component under certain actions, and then depicts the dynamic transition process of service component with the state transition graph. At last, the physical representation method in memory and the algorithms to judge equivalence of state transition graph are given. Comparing with existing researches, the dynamic evolvement of service component under the actions is considered, and the algorithm to judge equivalence of service components provides an effective tool for model verification.
