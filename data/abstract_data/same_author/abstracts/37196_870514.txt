Two-Loop Electroweak Logarithms in Four-Fermion Processes at High Energy
We present the complete analytical result for the two-loop logarithmically enhanced contributions to the high energy asymptotic behavior of the vector form factor and the four-fermion cross section in a spontaneously broken SU(2) gauge model. On the basis of this result we derive the dominant two-loop electroweak corrections to the neutral current four-fermion processes at high energies. Previously neglected effects of the gauge boson mass difference are included through the next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic approximation. PACS numbers: 12.15.Lk ∗B. Feucht in previous publications.
