Weakly-induced strong CP-violation
Weak interaction contributions to the strong θ parameter are revisited in the frame of a large-Nc Chiral Perturbation Theory. Focusing on the hadronic η→ππ amplitudes, we express these second-order corrections in terms of the CP-violating parameter in K→ππ decays to obtain Δwθ≈10-17 at O(GF2e‧).
