Recent Tests of QCD at SLD
We present selected results on strong interaction physics from the SLD experiment at the SLAC Linear Collider. We report on several new studies of 3- and 4-jet hadronic Z{sup 0} decays, in which jets are identified as quark, antiquark or gluon. The gluon energy spectrum is measured over the full kinematic range, providing an improved test of QCD and limits on anomalous bbg and bbg couplings. The parity violation in Z{sup 0} {yields} b{bar b}g decays is consistent with electroweak theory plus QCD. New tests of T- and CP-conservation at the bbg vertex are performed. An improved measurement of the rate of gluon splitting into b{bar b} pairs yields g{sub b{bar b}} = 0.00244 {+-} 0.00059(stat.) {+-} 0.00034(syst.). We also present a number of new results on jet fragmentation into identified hadrons.
