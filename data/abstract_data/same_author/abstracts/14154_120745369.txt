Precise quark masses from R(s)
We discuss the extraction of the charm- and bottom-quark mass from the experimentally measured R -ratio in combination with higher order QCD corrections to moments of the vacuum polarization function. The results for the MS ¯ -masses are m c ( 3 GeV ) = 0.986 ( 13 ) GeV and m b ( 10 GeV ) = 3.609 ( 25 ) GeV .
