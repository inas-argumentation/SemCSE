IN-ACHROMATIC SUPERCONDUCTING WIGGLER IN TAIWAN LIGHT SOURCE: INSTALLATION AND TEST RESULTS
A 16-pole in-achromatic superconducting wiggler (IASW) has been installed and operated in the achromatic section of a 1.5 GeV storage ring to increase the generation of high flux X-ray photon beams for Taiwan Light Source (TLS), The 3.1 Tesla superconducting wiggler is operated in a 4.5-K liquid helium cryogenic system. This work describes the operation experience and test results of the in-achromatic superconducting wiggler.
