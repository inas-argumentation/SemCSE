Polarized proton and antiproton beams and experiments at the tevatron
A 200 GeV polarized proton (and antiproton) beam and experimental facility will be built as part of the Tevatron II construction program at Fermilab. The beam is being developed by a joint Argonne-Fermilab team. The detector facility for the first experiments using the polarized beam is the responsibility of an Annecy-Argonne-Berkeley-Fermilab-Kyoto-Northwestern-Rice-Sarclay-Serpukhov-Triests collaboration. 1 figure.
