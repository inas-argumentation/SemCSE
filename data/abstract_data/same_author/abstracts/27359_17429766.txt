Geometry, D-branes and N = 1 duality in four dimensions with product gauge groups
We study N = 1 dualities in four dimensional supersymmetric gauge theories by D 6-branes wrapping around 3-cycles of Calabi-Yau threefolds in type IIA string theory. We consider the models involving SU ( N c 1 ) × SU ( c 2 ) product gauge group without or with adjoint matter in terms of geometrical realization of the configuration of D 6-branes wrapped 3-cycles. We also find simple geometric descriptions for the triple product gauge group SU ( N c 1 ) × SU ( N c 2 ) × SU ( N c 3 ) interpreted recently by Brodie and Hanany in the context of D brane configurations together with NS 5-branes. Their introduction of semi infinite D 4-branes appears naturally by looking at the flavor group in the dual theory. We generalize to product of arbitrary number of gauge groups.
