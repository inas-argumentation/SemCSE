Higher twist and higher order contributions to the pion electromagnetic form factor
The
$$\left( {\frac{{m_\pi ^4 }}{{(m_u + m_d )^2 Q^2 }}} \right)$$
 andO (αS2) corrections to the leading term of the perturbative QCD calculation of the pion electromagnetic form factor are examined numerically. Both sets of terms provide significant corrections for values ofQ2 between 1 and 15 GeV2/c2.
