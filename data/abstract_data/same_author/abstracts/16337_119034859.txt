Cosmic inversion: II. An iterative method for reproducing the primordial spectrum from the CMB data
Pursuing the original idea proposed in our previous paper (I), we improve the method of determining the shape of the initial curvature perturbation spectrum P(k) from the CMB data. The thickness of the last scattering surface (LSS) and the integrated Sachs–Wolfe (ISW) effect, which we neglected in I, are taken into account and an iterative method is newly developed. The new method can reproduce the primordial power spectra well, given the correct values of the cosmological parameters. Conversely, there appear spurious peaks and dips in the reconstructed power spectrum if we use cosmological parameters slightly different from the true values, while there appear regions of negative P(k) in some cases if we use substantially different values. In other words, the tacit assumption that the cosmological parameters can be determined for an assumed initial spectrum is verified by our reconstruction method. This result leads to an interesting possibility that our method, if refined more and adapted to actual observational data, could give a new tool to constrain the cosmological parameters without recourse to models of the primordial power spectra.
