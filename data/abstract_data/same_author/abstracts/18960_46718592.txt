Nambu-Goldstone bosons and dynamical symmetry breaking in QCD at high temperatures.
We report on the renormalization-group study of the low-energy effective potential in QCD. We derive the dynamically induced linear {sigma} model (DSM), whose self-couplings are not free parameters but are determined in terms of the running coupling of the underlying QCD. In this DSM, the {sigma} meson is not a fundamental field, but a genuine quark-antiquark 0{sup +} bound state, while the {pi} is the quark-antiquark 0{sup {minus}} bound state. We show the masslessness of the {pi} at zero temperature, so that the {pi} is a true Nambu-Goldstone boson coupled to zero-temperature chirality. At high temperatures, the {pi} remains massless. Thus the zero-temperature chirality is not restored at high temperatures. The apparent chiral symmetry operative at high temperatures is thus to be distinguished from that at {ital T}=0. Comparison is made with the situation in the BCS and other theories where symmetry restoration does indeed take place.
