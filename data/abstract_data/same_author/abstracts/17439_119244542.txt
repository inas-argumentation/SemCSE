Applications of the leading-order Dokshitzer-Gribov-Lipatov-Altarelli-Parisi evolution equations to the combined HERA data on deep inelastic scattering
We recently derived explicit solutions of the leading-order Dokshitzer-Gribov-Lipatov-Altarelli-Parisi (DGLAP) equations for the Q{sup 2} evolution of the singlet structure function F{sub s}(x,Q{sup 2}) and the gluon distribution G(x,Q{sup 2}) using very efficient Laplace transform techniques. We apply our results here to a study of the HERA data on deep inelastic ep scattering as recently combined by the H1 and ZEUS groups. We use initial distributions F{sub 2}{sup {gamma}p}(x,Q{sub 0}{sup 2}) and G(x,Q{sub 0}{sup 2}) determined for x<0.1 by a global fit to the HERA data, and extended to x=1 using the shapes of those distributions determined in the CTEQ6L and MSTW2008LO analyses from fits to other data. Our final results are insensitive at small x to the details of the extension. We obtain the singlet quark distribution F{sub s}(x,Q{sub 0}{sup 2}) from F{sub 2}{sup {gamma}p}(x,Q{sub 0}{sup 2}) using small nonsinglet quark distributions taken from either the CTEQ6L or the MSTW2008LO analyses, evolve F{sub s} and G to arbitrary Q{sup 2}, and then convert the results to individual quark distributions.
