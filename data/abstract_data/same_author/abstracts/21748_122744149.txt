The colour-singlet technipion P0 and the recent experimental results at LEPII
We study the contributions of the technipion P0 predicted by the technicolour theory to the process e+e-→Zb. Our results show that the production cross section σ is very small. The value of the cross section σ is smaller than 3×10-3 fb for most technicolour models. It is impossible for the recent experimental results at LEPII to be explained within the framework of technicolour theory.
