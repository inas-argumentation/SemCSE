A sequential coloring algorithm for finite sets
Abstract Let X be a finite set and P a hereditary property associated with the subsets of X . A partition of X into n subsets each with property P is said to be a P-n -coloring of X . The minimum n such that a P-n -coloring of X exists is defined to be the P -chromatic number of X . In this paper we give a sequential coloring algorithm for P -coloring X . From the algorithm we then get a few upper bounds for the P -chromatic number. In particular, we generalize the Welsh-Powell upper bound for ordinary chromatic number to the case of P -chromatic number of any finite set X .
