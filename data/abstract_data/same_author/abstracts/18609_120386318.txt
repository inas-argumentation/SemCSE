Gamma-jet tomography of high-energy nuclear collisions in NLO pQCD
Within the NLO pQCD partem model study, suppression of away‐side hadron spectra associated with a high pT photon due to parton energy loss is shown to provide more complete tomographic picture of the dense matter formed in high‐energy heavy‐ion collisions. Hadron spectra at large ZT = PTh/pTλ≳ 1 are more susceptible to parton energy loss and therefore are dominated by surface emission of λ‐correlated jets, whereas small ZT hadrons mainly come from fragmentation of jets with reduced energy which is controlled by the volume emission. These lead to different centrality dependence of the hadron suppression in different region of ZT.
