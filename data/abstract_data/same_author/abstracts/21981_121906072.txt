AXION - FAMILON MODEL WITH A HARMLESS 17-KeV NEUTRINO
Abstract A model is discussed in which e + τ - μ emerges as an approximate, but very good, accidental symmetry of the lepton mass matrices. Consequently a heavy neutrino can be accommodated without conflicts with ν μ oscillation or neutrinoless double β decay data. The model has a Goldstone boson which may be interpreted as the axion, the majoron, or a familon. The decay of the heavy neutrino can proceed at a rate just compatible with cosmology if the K + → π + + Goldstone boson branching ratio is at the present experimental limit.
