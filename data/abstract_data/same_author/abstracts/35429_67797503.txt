First lattice QCD estimate of the g(D* D pi) coupling
We present the results of the first lattice QCD study of the strong coupling gDDπ. From our simulations in the quenched approximation, we obtain gDDπ = 18.8 ± 2.3 +1.1 −2.0 and bc = 0.67 ±0.08 +0.04 −0.06 . Whereas previous theoretical studies gave different predictions, our result favours a large value for bc.
