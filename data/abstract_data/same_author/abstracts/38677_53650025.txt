Heavy pseudoscalar leading-twist distribution amplitudes within QCD theory in background fields
In this paper, we study the leading-twist distribution amplitude (DA) of the heavy pseudoscalars (HPs), such as $$\eta _c$$ηc, $$\eta _b$$ηb, and $$B_c$$Bc, within the QCD theory in the background fields. New sum rules up to dimension-six condensates for both the HP decay constants and their leading-twist DA moments are presented. From the sum rules for the HP decay constants, we obtain $$f_{\eta _c} = 453 \pm 4\, \text {MeV}$$fηc=453±4MeV, $$f_{B_c} = 498 \pm 14 \,\text {MeV}$$fBc=498±14MeV, and $$f_{\eta _b} = 811 \pm 34 \,\text {MeV}$$fηb=811±34MeV. Based on the sum rules for the HPs’ leading-twist DA moments, we construct a new model for the $$\eta _c$$ηc, $$\eta _b$$ηb, and $$B_c$$Bc leading-twist DAs. Our present HP DA model is also adaptable for the light pseudoscalar DAs, such as the pion and kaon DAs. Thus, it shall be applicable for a wide range of QCD exclusive processes. As an application, we apply the $$\eta _c$$ηc leading-twist DA to calculate the $$B_c \rightarrow \eta _c$$Bc→ηc transition form factor $$f_+^{B_c \rightarrow \eta _c}(q^2)$$f+Bc→ηc(q2). At the maximum recoil region, we obtain $$f_+^{B_c \rightarrow \eta _c}(0) = 0.612^{+0.053}_{-0.052}$$f+Bc→ηc(0)=0.612-0.052+0.053. Furthermore we predict the branching ratio for the semi-leptonic decay $$B_c \rightarrow \eta _c l \nu $$Bc→ηclν and obtain $$\mathcal{B}(B_c \rightarrow \eta _c l \nu )=\left( 7.70^{+1.65}_{-1.48}\right) \times 10^{-3}$$B(Bc→ηclν)=7.70-1.48+1.65×10-3 for massless leptons, which is consistent with the light-cone sum rules estimation obtained in the literature.
