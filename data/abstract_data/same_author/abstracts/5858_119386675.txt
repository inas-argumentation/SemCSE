Regge phenomenology of inclusive spectra in the central region
Abstract In the light of new data from the ISR, initial steps are taken towards the Regge analysis of inclusive spectra in the central region. It is shown that some features in the data necessitate the introduction of a further singularity in addition to the Pomeron and the meson trajectories ϱ - f - ω - A 2 . The resulting model yields many predictions via duality and factorisation which can easily be tested at ISR and Batavia energies.
