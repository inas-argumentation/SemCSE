Universality and asymptotic limits of the scaling exponents in circle maps
Abstract In he quasiperiodic route to chaos as modeled by the circle map, the universal behavior is dictated by the degree of inflection z . Using a polynomial form of the circle map, we have studied the dependence of the scaling exponents α and δ on z . The asymptotic limits of α( z ) and δ( z ) as z →∞ have also been found.
