JET SHAPES AND DETERMINATIONS OF s FROM HADRONIC FINAL STATES IN DEEP INELASTIC SCATTERING AT HERA
Recent results from the study of hadronic nal states in deep inelastic scattering at HERA are reviewed. Jet shapes from neutral and charged current interactions at HERA are compared to those from e + e ? annihilation and p p collisions. The (2+1) jet event rate distributions R2+1(Q 2) and y2 are measured using the modiied JADE jet algorithm. The value of the strong coupling constant s is determined from a comparison with QCD predictions in next{ to{leading order. An analysis of event shape distributions in terms of next{to{leading order QCD calculations and power corrections is presented.
