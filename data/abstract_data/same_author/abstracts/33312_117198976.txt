On the neutrino luminosity from a type II supernova
The initial burst of neutrino luminosity from a supernova is expected to last approx.1 ms, in which approx.10/sup 51/ ergs are emitted from the shocked material in the outer core. A detectable fraction of this energy is expected to be in the form of electron antineutrinos, the exact value depending sensitively on the temperature behind the shock. During the deleptonization of the unshocked inner core, lasting approx.1 s, the neutrino energy approx.10/sup 52/ ergs is expected to be approximately blackbody radiation, with equal luminosities in neutrinos and antineutrinos.
