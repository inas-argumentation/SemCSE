D instanton partition functions
Duality arguments are used to determine {ital D}-instanton contributions to certain effective interaction terms of type II supergravity theories in various dimensions. This leads to exact expressions for the partition functions of the finite {ital N} {ital D}-instanton matrix model in d=4 and 6 dimensions that generalize our previous expression for the case d=10. These results are consistent with the fact that the Witten index of the {ital T}-dual {ital D}-particle process should only be nonvanishing for d=10. {copyright} {ital 1998} {ital The American Physical Society}
