Tests of HTS insert coils above 30 T
Recently HTS insert coils have been operated in background magnetic fields over 30 T, reaching record values for the central magnetic field. One YBCO double pancake coil generated 2.8 T in a 31 T background for a total of 33.8 T. A Bi-2212 layer wound coil generated 1.1 T in a 31 T background for a total of 32.1 T. Here we report on the test set-up and the obtained results.
