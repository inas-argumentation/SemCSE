Screening of multi-instanton corrections to anomalous processes in the standard model
We calculate the multi-instanton contribution to the logarithm of the cross-section for (B+L)-violating (anomalous) high-energy collisions in the next-to-leading order (in terms of ( E M sp ) 2 3 ) approximation. It is demonstrated that in this approximation there is no sign of “premature unitarization” of anomalous processes, i.e. there is no indication that multi-instanton contributions become of the same magnitude as the single-instanton contribution at energies where the single-instanton result is still exponentially suppressed.
