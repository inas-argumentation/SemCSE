Search for Leptoquarks and Lepton Flavor Violation at HERA
The data collected with the ep collider HERA at DESY in the years 1994–2000 at a centre-ofmass energy of 300− 320GeV has been searched for Leptoquarks and lepton-flavour violation. Leptoquarks are searched in the decay channels LQ− → `q(` = e,ν ,μ ,τ ). For masses below the centre-of-mass energy a leptoquark signals would show up as a resonance in the `q massspectra. At higher masses the signal may show up as a four-fermion interaction.
