Loop-induced radiative decays of neutral scalars and neutral vector bosons in left-right symmetric theories
Abstract We compute the decay widths of the radiative modes h 0 (H 0 , δ L 0r ) → γγ , γ Z and Z′→ γ h 0 (H 0 , δ L 0r ), where h 0 , H 0 and δ L 0r are the light neutral Higgs scalars of left-right symmetric (LR) models with a minimal Higgs potential. We use a nonlinear R -gauge to compute these one-loop induced decays. We find that only the decay modes which involve the scalar h 0 , the LR analogue of the standard model Higgs boson, are not suppressed by LR mixing angles.
