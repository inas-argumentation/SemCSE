NEGATIVE HYDROGEN-ION PROGRAM AT FERMILAB
Direct extraction from an ion source of the magnetron design is being studied for the production of 50 to 100 milliamperes of negative hydrogen ions that can be accelerated through the 750-kV column at Fermilab. After acceleration through the linac to 200 MeV, the ions will be injected into the booster and converted to protons by a foil stripper. The booster will be filled to high intensity by injecting many turns directly into a common orbit.
