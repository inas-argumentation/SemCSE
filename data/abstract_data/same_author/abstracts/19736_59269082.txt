Higher spin gravity with matter in AdS3 and its CFT dual
A bstractWe study Vasiliev’s system of higher spin gauge fields coupled to massive scalars in AdS3, and compute the tree level two and three point functions. These are compared to the large N limit of the WN minimal model, and nontrivial agreements are found. We propose a modified version of the conjecture of Gaberdiel and Gopakumar, under which the bulk theory is perturbatively dual to a subsector of the CFT that closes on the sphere.
