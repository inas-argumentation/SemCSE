Study of a tachyonic field and its statefinder diagnostics in various scenarios of the anisotropic universe
In the present work, we have considered N-dimensional Einstein field equations in which four-dimensional space- time is described by a Friedmann-Robertson-Walker metric and the extra d-dimensions by an Euclidean metric. By consid- ering the universe to be filled with a tachyonic field we have reconstructed the potential V(f) corresponding to the field re- constructions in an anisotropic universe under emergent - power law, emergent-intermediate, emergent-logamediate, and logamediate-intermediate scenarios. The statefinder parameters have been investigated in all of said scenarios.
