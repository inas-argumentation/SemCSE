Stringy restrictions on the Scherk-Schwarz mechanism
The authors consider the sigma model describing the propagation of a bosonic string in a manifold of the type M4(X)K obtained by compactification of D=26 spacetime using the Scherk-Schwarz mechanism. The requirement of 2D conformal invariance (at the 1-loop level) of this sigma model is shown to impose rather stringent restrictions on the mass parameters used in the Scherk-Schwarz mechanism. Implications for the background field mass spectrum in the model considered by Scherk and Schwarz (1979) are examined.
