H− Source Developments at CERN
Future CERN programmes for LHC and ISOLDE require increasing the beam intensity and brightness from the Proton Synchotron Booster (PSB). This could be achieved by injection from a higher energy H− linac. A new injector will require a high performance, high reliability, negative hydrogen ion source. This paper will present the requirements for such a source together with the first results for a prototype microwave driven source.
