Composition and stability of hybrid stars with hyperons and quark color-superconductivity
The recent measurement of a 1.97 ± 0.04 solar-mass pulsar places a stringent lower bound on the maximum mass of compact stars and therefore challenges the existence of any agents that soften the equation of state of ultra-dense matter.
