Low‐energy neutron‐atom scattering: Process at the intersection of particle and nuclear physics
The determination of the charge radius and the polarizability of the neutron from low‐energy neutron‐atom scattering data are discussed. We concentrate on the theoretical methods to separate these structure constants and discuss improvements of the analyses. Our analyses of the most recent total neutron‐atom cross section measurements are presented yielding the mean squared radius (〈r2〉chn=−0.119±0.004±0.003fm2) and a preliminary value of the electric polarizability of the neutron.
