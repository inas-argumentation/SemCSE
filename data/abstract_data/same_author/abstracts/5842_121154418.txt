Dual properties of strangeness annihilation processes
The dual properties of strangeness annihilation processes are studied. Experimental evidence is presented for such processes building up a low-lying Regge singularity. Quark-diagram arguments suggest that this singularity is the exchange degenerate f'- ϕ trajectory. The possibility of studying the factorization properties of strangeness annihilation processes using hyperon beams is pointed out.
