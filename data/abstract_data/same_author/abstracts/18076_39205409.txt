Impedance Calculation for Ferrite Inserts
Passive ferrite inserts were used to compensate the space charge impedance in high intensity space charge dominated accelerators. We study the narrowband longitudinal impedance of these ferrite inserts. We find that the shunt impedance and the quality factor for ferrite inserts are inversely proportional to the imaginary part of the permeability of ferrite materials. We also provide a recipe for attaining a truly passive space charge impedance compensation and avoiding narrowband microwave instabilities.
