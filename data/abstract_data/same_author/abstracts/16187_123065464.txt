One-loop supersymmetric corrections to the top width☆
Abstract The one-loop electroweak contributions of genuine supersymmetric particles (charginos, neutralinos and sfermions) to the SM top decay t → W + b are calculated in the minimal supersymmetric model. The analytic expression is given for the renormalized decay rate. The numerical example shows that the corrections can reduce the width by a few percent if the squarks take their lowest allowed masses. Also, we compare these corrections with the corrections of SUSY QCD and extra Higgs bosons.
