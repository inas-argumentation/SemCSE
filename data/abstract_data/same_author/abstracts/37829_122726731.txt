Bianchi type-I, V and VIo models in modified generalized scalar-tensor theory
In modified generalized scalar-tensor (GST) theory, the cosmological term Λ is a function of the scalar field ϕ and its derivatives $$
\dot \phi ^2 
$$. We obtain exact solutions of the field equations in Bianchi Type-I, V and VIo space-times. The evolution of the scale factor, the scalar field and the cosmological term has been discussed. The Bianchi Type-I model has been discussed in detail. Further, Bianchi Type-V and VIo models can be studied on the lines similar to Bianchi Type-I model.
