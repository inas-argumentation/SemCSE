A Measurement of K0(L) --> pi+ pi- pi0 Decay Parameters
The decay ${K}_{L}^{0}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{0}$ has been studied in an experiment using the Argonne 12-ft hydrogen bubble chamber exposed to a ${K}_{L}^{0}$ beam of momentum 550 \ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{} 35 MeV/c. After radiative corrections to the data, a fit using a matrix element of the form ${|M|}^{2}\ensuremath{\propto}1+\frac{g({s}_{3}\ensuremath{-}{s}_{0})}{{{m}_{{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}}}^{2}}+h{[\frac{({s}_{3} \ensuremath{-} {s}_{0})}{{{m}_{{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}}}^{2}}]}^{2} + k{[\frac{({s}_{1} \ensuremath{-} {s}_{2})}{{{m}_{{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}}}^{2}}]}^{2}$ gives $g = 0.681\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.24$, $h = 0.095\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.032$, and $k = 0.024\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.010$. The branching ratio $\frac{\ensuremath{\Gamma}({K}_{L}^{0}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{0})}{\ensuremath{\Gamma}({K}_{L}^{0}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\mathrm{charged})}$ was measured to be 0.163 \ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{} 0.003. These results, when compared to those obtained in other ${K}_{L}^{0}$ and ${K}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}$ decay experiments, indicate a substantial $\ensuremath{\Delta}g\frac{1}{2}$ contribution to the ${K}_{L}^{0}$ decay amplitude.
