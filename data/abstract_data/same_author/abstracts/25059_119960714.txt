An inequality on the slope of the diffraction peak in elastic collisions of particles of arbitrary spins
We obtain inequalities linking the slope of the diffraction peak, the total cross-section and the elastic cross-section of the form d d t log d σ A d ω t=0>R 1 18π (σ tot ) 2 σ el , for elastic collisions of particles of arbitrary spins ( dσ A /d μ is the “absorptive” differential cross-section). In the limit of infinite energy we get R > 0.9965, to be compared to R = 1 for the scalar case. Results for finite energies and finite slopes are also obtained, such as R > 0.989 as soon as the logarithmic derivative with respect to cos θ of the absorptive differential cross-section is greater than 33.
