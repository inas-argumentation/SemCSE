Weyl-Type Inequalities for Operators in Banach Spaces
Publisher Summary This chapter discusses the Weyl-type inequalities for operators in Banach spaces. The results on the asymptotic behavior of the eigenvalues of several classes of operators in Banach spaces have been derived. A Banach ideal of operators is a complete normed operator idea. A simple principle that yields asymptotic estimates for the eigenvalues of certain classes of operators is presented. It is found that the given asymptotic order is optimal, as the fractional integration operators shows. Some asymptotic estimates for the eigenvalues of nuclear operators are explained in the chapter. A sequence of independent Gaussian random variables defined on a probability space is presented. It is proved that any summing operator is strictly singular and no restriction of operator to an infinite dimensional subspace is an isomorphism. This implies that the spectrum of operator consists of a null sequence of eigenvalues of finite multiplicity. The results on the logarithmic behavior of the eigenvalues of summing operators are also discussed in the chapter.
