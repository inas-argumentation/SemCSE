A static axisymmetric exact solution of f(R)-gravity
We present an exact, axially symmetric, static, vacuum solution for f ( R ) -gravity in Weylʼs canonical coordinates. We obtain a general explicit expression for the dependence of d f ( R ) / d R upon the r and z coordinates and then the corresponding explicit form of f ( R ) , which must be consistent with the field equations. We analyze in detail the modified Schwarzschild solution in prolate spheroidal coordinates. Finally, we study the curvature invariants and show that, in the case of f ( R ) ≠ R , this solution corresponds to a naked singularity.
