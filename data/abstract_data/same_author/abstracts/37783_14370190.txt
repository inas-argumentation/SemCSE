Enhanced Diffusion and the Spontaneous Collapse Model of the Wave Function
After finding analogies between turbulence and the beable interpretation of the spontaneous collapse model of the wave function, we unravel the underlying Lévy dynamics in such model. Using an affine noise in the collapse process, we obtain the intermittency corrections to Richardson’s t law for the turbulent diffusion. PACS: 05.40.Fb, 03.65.Bz, 02.50.Ey Typeset using REVTEX
