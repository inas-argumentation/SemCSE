Design of the local trigger board for the Daya Bay reactor neutrino experiment
We have designed a local trigger board for the Daya Bay reactor neutrino experiment, which is aimed to measure the neutrino mixing angle sin(2) 2 theta(13) with a precision down to 1% level. The local trigger board processes both the total number of coincident photomultiplier tube (PMT) hits and the PMT energy sum to make trigger decisions. With this design, a high trigger probability is achieved to meet the system requirement. The design of the local trigger board is presented. (C) 2011 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.
