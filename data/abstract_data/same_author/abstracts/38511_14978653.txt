Generalized Chern–Simons form and descent equation
We present the general method to introduce the generalized Chern–Simons form and the descent equation which contain the scalar field in addition to the gauge fields. It is based on the technique in a noncommutative differential geometry (NCG) which extends the four-dimensional Minkowski space M4 to the discrete space such as M4×Z2 with two point space Z2. However, the resultant equations are not only dependent on NCG, but also are justified by the algebraic rules in the ordinary differential geometry.
