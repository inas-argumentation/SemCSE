Proposals for studying TeV W(L) W(L) ---> W(L) W(L) interactions experimentally
We discuss how to experimentally study the symmetry breaking sector by observing $W_L W_L \rightarrow W_L W_L$ interactions in the TeV region. We discuss some general features of the event structure in the signal and background events. Various tricks to enhance the signal--to--background ratio are also presented. We show how to detect longitudinal $W$--bosons either in the central rapidity region of the detector or in the beam pipe direction.
