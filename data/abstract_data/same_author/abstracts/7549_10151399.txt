Test of CPT symmetry in cascade decays
Abstract. Cascade mixing provides an place for elegant study of $B^0$–$\bar{B^0}$ mixing. We use this idea to study the CPT violation caused by $B^0$–$\bar{B^0}$ mixing. An approximation method is adopted to treat the two complex $B^0$–$\bar{B^0}$ mixing parameters $\theta$ and $\phi$. A procedure to extract the parameters $\theta$ and $\phi$ is suggested. The feasibility of exploring CPT violation and determining $\theta$ and $\phi$ in the future B-factories and LHC-B is discussed.
