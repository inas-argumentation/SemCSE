On generators and generative capacity of EOL forms
SummaryIn this paper we study the generative capacity of EOL forms from two different points of view. On the one hand, we consider the generative capacity of special EOL forms which one could call ‘linear like’ and ‘context free like’, establishing the existence of a rich variety of non-regular sub-EOL language families. On the other hand, we propose the notion of a ‘generator’ L of a language family ℒ We mean by this that any synchronized EOL system generating L generates — if understood as an EOL form — all languages of ℒ. We characterize the generators of the family of regular languages, and prove that other well known language families do not have generators.
