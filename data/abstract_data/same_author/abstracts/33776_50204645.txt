Supermembrane with U(1)-Gauging
We study the effect of a Killing vector for the isometry for the compactification of eleven-dimensional supermembrane into ten-dimensional superstring. We first see that all the effects by the Killing vector are cancelled by themselves, when we limit ourselves to target superspace, reflecting the uniqueness of eleven-dimensional super-gravity. However, a fundamental U(1) gauge field can be coupled to a super Killing vector in supermembrane action on eleven-dimensional superspace background with new physical effects on its world-volume, as usual in gauged sigma-models. Our total supermembrane action has fermionic κ-invariance, composite Λ-gauge, and local U(1) gauge invariance. As a technical but also important tool, we use teleparallel superspace with no manifest local Lorentz covariance, as the natural and consistent background for the compactification of supermembrane.
