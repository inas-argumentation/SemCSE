Halo and bulge/disk globular clusters in the S0 galaxy NGC 1380 ?
We investigated the globular cluster system of the S0 galaxy NGC 1380 in the Fornax cluster with deep BV R photometry. We identied two, presumably old, populations of globularclusters.Theblueglobularclustersseemtobecounter- parts to the halo globular clusters in our Milky Way. They have comparable colors and magnitudes, are spherically distributed around the elliptical galaxy but have a very flat surface density prole. The red population follows the stellar light in elliptic- ity and position angle, has a similar magnitude distribution to the blue clusters, and a surface density prole comparable to that of globular clusters in other Fornax ellipticals. From their colors the red clusters appear slightly more metal rich then the metal rich globular clusters in the Milky way. We associate this red population with the bulge and disk of NGC 1380. While these two populations are compatible with a merger formation, we unfortunately see no hint in favor nor against a past merger event in the galaxy. This would be the rst identication of halo and bulge glob- ular clusters in an early-type galaxy by their colors and spatial distribution, and could hint to the presence of halo and bulge globular clusters in all galaxies. The globular cluster luminosity functions in the three colors will be discussed in detail in a separate paper together with the implications of the absolute magnitude of SN 1992A.
