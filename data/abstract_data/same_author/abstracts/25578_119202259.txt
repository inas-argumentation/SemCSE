Open string Regge trajectory and its field theory limit
We study the properties of the leading Regge trajectory in open string theory including the open string planar one-loop corrections. With $SU(N)$ Chan-Paton factors, the sum over planar open string multiloop diagrams describes the 't Hooft limit $N\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\infty}$ with $N{g}_{s}^{2}$ fixed. Our motivation is to improve the understanding of open string theory at finite ${\ensuremath{\alpha}}^{\ensuremath{'}}$ as a model of gauge field theories. $SU(N)$ gauge theories in $D$ space-time dimensions are described by requiring open strings to end on a stack of $N$ $\mathrm{D}p$-branes of space-time dimension $D=p+1$. The large $N$ leading trajectory $\ensuremath{\alpha}(t)=1+{\ensuremath{\alpha}}^{\ensuremath{'}}t+\ensuremath{\Sigma}(t)$ can be extracted, through order ${g}^{2}$, from the $s\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{-}\ensuremath{\infty}$ limit, at fixed $t$, of the four open string tree and planar loop diagrams. We analyze the $t\ensuremath{\rightarrow}0$ behavior with the result that $\ensuremath{\Sigma}(t)\ensuremath{\sim}\ensuremath{-}C{g}^{2}(\ensuremath{-}{\ensuremath{\alpha}}^{\ensuremath{'}}t{)}^{(D\ensuremath{-}4)/2}/(D\ensuremath{-}4)$. This result precisely tracks the 1-loop Reggeized gluon of gauge theory in $Dg4$ space-time dimensions. In particular, for $D\ensuremath{\rightarrow}4$ it reproduces the known infrared divergences of gauge theory in 4 dimensions with a Regge trajectory behaving as $\ensuremath{-}\mathrm{ln}﻿(\ensuremath{-}{\ensuremath{\alpha}}^{\ensuremath{'}}t)$. We also study $\ensuremath{\Sigma}(t)$ in the limit $t\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{-}\ensuremath{\infty}$ and show that, when $Dl8$, it behaves as ${\ensuremath{\alpha}}^{\ensuremath{'}}t/(\mathrm{ln}﻿(\ensuremath{-}{\ensuremath{\alpha}}^{\ensuremath{'}}t){)}^{\ensuremath{\gamma}}$, where $\ensuremath{\gamma}g0$ depends on $D$ and the number of massless scalars. Thus, as long as $4lDl8$, the 1-loop correction stays small relative to the tree trajectory for $\ensuremath{-}{\ensuremath{\alpha}}^{\ensuremath{'}}t$ arbitrarily large. Finally we present the results of numerical calculations of $\ensuremath{\Sigma}(t)$ for all negative $t$.
