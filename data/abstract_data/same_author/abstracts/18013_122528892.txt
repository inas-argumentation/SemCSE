Effect of rf cavity temperature variation on electron beams with rf voltage modulations
In this paper, we will analyze this phenomenon and show that it is related to the combined effects of cavity frequency shift, the change of effective accelerating voltage resulting from the partially compensating low-level rf-feedback system, and the rf voltage modulation used to combat the CBI. This report explores possible mechanisms that can cause the horizontal beam size variation: including the effects of temperature variation on cavity resonance frequency and the loss factor in higher-order modes. Our paper is organized as follows. Section II reports the experimental data. Section III reviews the beam dynamics in the presence of the rf voltage modulation. Section IV calculates effects of possible mechanisms that can change the horizontal beam size. The calculation is compared with the experimental data. The conclusion is given in Sec. V.
