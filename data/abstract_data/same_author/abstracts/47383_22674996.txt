Prior research in CEMs h as focused either on the design of new protocols or on comparisons of complete sys tems. However, the inherent tradeoffs between different CEM designs, particu larly under a wide range of operating conditions, are not well understood. In this wo rk [1], we empirically and systematically evaluate CEM design choices.
