Non-Abelian tensors with consistent interactions
We present a systematic method for constructing consistent interactions for a tensor field of an arbitrary rank in the adjoint representation of an arbitrary gauge group in any space-time dimensions. This method is inspired by the dimensional reduction of Scherk-Schwarz, modifying field strengths with certain Chern-Simons forms, together with modified tensorial gauge transformations. In order to define a consistent field strength of a r-rank tensor B{sub {mu}{sub 1}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {mu}{sub r}}{sup I} in the adjoint representation, we need the multiplet (B{sub {mu}{sub 1}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {mu}{sub r}}{sup I}, B{sub {mu}{sub 1}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {center_dot}}{sub {mu}{sub r}{sub -}{sub 1}}{sup IJ}, {center_dot}{center_dot}{center_dot}, B{sub {mu}{sup I}1}{sup {center_dot}}{sup {center_dot}}{sup {center_dot}}{sup I}{sub r}, B{sup I{sub 1}}{sup {center_dot}}{sup {center_dot}}{sup {center_dot}}{sup I{sub r}{sub +}{sub 1}}). The usual problem of consistency of the tensor field equations is circumvented in this formulation.
