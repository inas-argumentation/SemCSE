{QCD} Prediction for the Structure Functions of the Photon
We compute the unpolarized deep inelastic structure functions of the photon in an asymptotically free gauge theory, including mass effects. We show that when the momentum squared of the struck photon is small relative to the contributing quark mass, m, the free quark model prediction is obtained to O(1/ln(Q2/m2)) and (g2ln(Q2/m2)). For the light quark contribution the perturbation expansion does not converge. For large momentum squared of the struck photon the free quark model result is renormalized by a finite amount computable in QCD. For forseeable experimental ranges ofQ2 this renormalization is small.
