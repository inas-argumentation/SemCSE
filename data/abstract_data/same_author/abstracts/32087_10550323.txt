Analysis of weak-interaction effects in high energy hadron-hadron collisions.
Parity-violating (PV) effects in inclusive hadron and jet production in high energy hadron-hadron collisions are analyzed. Such effects arise from the interference between strong and weak amplitudes. This interference gives rise to a nonzero value of the PV parameters [ital scrA][sub [ital L]] and [ital scrP][sub [ital L]], where [ital scrA][sub [ital L]] measures the difference in the inclusive cross sections of, for example, [ital p]+[ital p][r arrow]jet+[ital X] ([ital X]=anything), with one of the incident proton beams in a state of [plus minus] helicity, and [ital scrP][sub [ital L]] denotes the longitudinal polarization of a high-energy baryon (e.g., [Lambda]) produced in [ital p]+[ital p][r arrow][Lambda]+[ital X] with the initial proton beams unpolarized. In the present paper, the single helicity asymmetry [ital scrA][sub [ital L]] in one-jet, two-jet, and two-jet plus photon productions as well as in the Drell-Yan process [ital p]+[ital p][r arrow][ital l][sup +][ital l[minus]]+jet+[ital X] is probed, and the longitudinal polarization [ital scrP][sub [ital L]] of the [Lambda] produced in unpolarized [ital pp] collisions is studied. We conclude that the PV effects in high energy proton-proton collisions are in general only sensitive to the spin-dependent valence quark distributions.
