Nucleon scattering from 34S and the relative sign of neutron and proton transition matrix elements for the (0-->22+) transition.
Data obtained by scattering 29.8 MeV protons and 21.7 MeV neutrons from $^{34}\mathrm{S}$ are used in a consistent analysis to obtain values and relative signs for proton (${M}_{\mathrm{p}}$) and neutron (${M}_{\mathrm{n}}$) multiple matrix elements for E2 transitions to the first three ${2}^{+}$ states in $^{34}\mathrm{S}$. The data for the ${0}^{+}$(g.s.)\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${2}_{2}^{+}$(3.30 MeV) transition are consistent only with the assumption of the same relative sign of ${M}_{\mathrm{p}}$ and ${M}_{\mathrm{n}}$. .AE
