Heavy Majorana neutrinos in dilepton production in deep-inelastic lepton-proton scattering
AbstractThe cross section for the production of pairs of likely charged leptons in the deep-inelastic processes 
$$e^ + p \to \bar v_e \ell ^ + \ell '^ + X(\ell ,\ell ' = e,\mu ,\tau )$$
 induced by the exchange of heavy Majorana neutrinos is calculated. The effect of interference between different neutrino mass eigenstates is studied. The possibilities for observing this effect at future lepton-proton colliders are considered.
