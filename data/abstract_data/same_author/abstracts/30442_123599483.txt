Microscopic calculation of the form factors for nucleon transfer in deeply inelastic collisions
Previous calculations of form factors are extended to the case of inelastic nucleon transfer. The results generally agree well with those found earlier for inelastic scattering. The two main differences are (i) a systematic increase of the correlation length with distance, (ii) a significant contribution of angular momentum transfer of up to 7 or 8 units of h to the transfer process.
