Description of F2 at small x incorporating angular ordering.
We study the perturbative QCD description of the DESY HERA measurements of {ital F}{sub 2}({ital x},{ital Q}{sup 2}) using a gluon distribution that is obtained from an evolution incorporating angular ordering of the gluon emissions, and which embodies both GLAP and BFKL dynamics. We compare the predictions with recent HERA data for {ital F}{sub 2} and its charm component {ital F}{sup {ital c}}{sub 2}. We also present estimates of the longitudinal structure function {ital F}{sub {ital L}}. {copyright} {ital 1996 The American Physical Society.}
