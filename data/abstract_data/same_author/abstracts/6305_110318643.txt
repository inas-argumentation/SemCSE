PLANAR AND PLANAR HELICAL SUPERCONDUCTIVE UNDULATORS FOR STORAGE RINGS: STATE OF THE ART *
In the past planar superconductive undulators for single pass operation were built and successfully tested with beam [1]. As a next step devices suitable for storage rings are in preparation. Experiments will be conducted with the aim to clarify various open questions concerning the operation of a cold bore undulator in a storage ring, the heat load generated by the beam in the cold bore, phase error compensation etc. The beam tests will be performed in the ANKA storage ring in Karlsruhe. After completion of these tests the interest will focus on a next generation of superconductive undulators with electrically adjustable period length and variable polarization direction. The first steps in this direction are mentioned at the end this paper.
