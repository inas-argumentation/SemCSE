Ground State of a Trapped Bose-Einstein Condensate in Two Dimensions; Beyond the Mean-field Approximation
We consider the ground state of a trapped Bose-Einstein condensate in two dimensions. In the mean-field approximation the ground-state density profile satisfies the Gross-Pitaevskii equation. We compute the leading quantum corrections to the density profile to second order in an expansion around the Thomas-Fermi limit. By summing the ladder diagrams, we obtain a modified equation that includes gradient corrections.
