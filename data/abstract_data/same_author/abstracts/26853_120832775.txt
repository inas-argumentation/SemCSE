LEFT–RIGHT TWIN HIGGS MODEL AND THE SPIN CORRECTION OF TOP-QUARK PAIR PRODUCTION AT ILC
The left–right twin Higgs model predicts the existence of the new neutral gauge boson ZH. In the off-diagonal basis, we explore the effects of extra neutral gauge boson ZH on the spin configuration of the top-quark pair production in the high energy linear e+e- collider (ILC). We find that the new gauge boson ZH exchange can generate significant corrections to the differential cross-sections for the the and states. Furthermore, when the ZH mass MZH approaches the center-of-mass energy , the cross-section resonance emerges. We expect that the effects of the new gauge boson ZH on the spin configurations of the top-quark pairs production might be observed in future ILC experiments.
