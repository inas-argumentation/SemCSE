BPS states and supersymmetric index in N=2 type I string vacua
Abstract We study the moduli dependence of a class of couplings in K 3 × T 2 compactifications of type I string theory, for which one-loop amplitudes can be written in terms of an N = 2 supersymmetric index. This index is determined for generic models as a function of the BPS spectrum. As an application we compute the one-loop moduli dependence of the F g W 2 g couplings, where W is the N = 2 gravitational superfield, for type I compactifications based on the Gimon-Johnson K 3 orientifolds, showing explicitly their dependence on the aforementioned index.
