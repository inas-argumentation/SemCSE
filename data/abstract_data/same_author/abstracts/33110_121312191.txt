Neutrino and Z gauge boson physics
We present a short review of the physics of neutrino-photon interactions and the rare decays of the Z and Z′ gauge bosons. In particular, we emphasize on processes induced by the anomalous trilinear and quartic vertices VVV and VVVV, where V = Z,Z′ or a photon, within the Standard Model (SM), the 331 model and some extensions of the SM. We also include the phenomenological and experimental limits reported for these couplings.
