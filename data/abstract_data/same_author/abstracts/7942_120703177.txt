IS THE CRONIN’S REPRESENTATION CORRECT FOR THE PENGUIN OPERATOR?
It is shown that the Cronin’s operator, being used for a phenomenological description of non-loptonic K-meson decays within the effective chiral Lagrangian approach with non-linear realization of chiral SU(3)×SU(3) symmetry (so-called, chiral perturbation theory at the hadronic level), cannot be applied to the determination of the Penguin-operator (O5-operator) at the hadronic level. The affirmation is based on the proof of the violation of the low-energy theorem $\lim _{P + \to 0} \left\langle {\pi ^ + \left( {p_ + } \right)\pi ^ - \left( {p_ - } \right)\left| {O_5 } \right|K^0 \left( p \right)} \right\rangle = \frac{i}{{F_\pi }}\left\langle {\pi ^0 \left( p \right)\left| {O_5 } \right|K^0 \left( p \right)} \right\rangle $ which is valid within the framework of the Cronin’s representation.
