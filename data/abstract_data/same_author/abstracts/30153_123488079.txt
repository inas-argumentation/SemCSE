A segmented photon-hadron calorimeter using a two coloured wavelength shifter optical readout system
Abstract The construction and performance of two segmented scintillator photon and hadron calorimeters (a ring and a downstream calorimeter) are described. They were used in an experiment (NA5) at the CERN SPS to study deep inelastic hadron-hadron collisions. A novel light collection system was developed using acrylic rods doped with two different colour wavelength shifter materials (Yellow 323 and BBQ). This allowed signals from the photon and hadron calorimeter to be transmitted by a common readout rod and rendered a compact and economical construction of the calormeters.
