Multirefringence phenomena in nonlinear electrodynamics
Universite´ de Nice Sophia Antipolis, 06103 Nice Cedex 2, Franceand Dipartimento di Fisica and ICRA, Sapienza Universita` di Roma, I-00185 Rome, Italy(Received 19 April 2013; published 13 September 2013)Wave propagation in nonlinear theories of the electromagnetism described by Lagrangian densitiesdependent upon its two local invariants LðF;GÞ is revisited. On the light of the recent ﬁndings inmetamaterials, it is here shown that trirefringence is also a possible phenomenon to occur in the realm ofsuch nonlinear theories. A speciﬁc model exhibiting this effect is investigated in terms of both phase andgroup velocities. It is claimed that wave propagation in some well known nonlinear models for spin-oneﬁelds, like QED and QCD in certain regimes, may exhibit trirefringence.
