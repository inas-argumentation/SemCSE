Vertex partitions of chordal graphs
A k-tree is a chordal graph with no (k + 2)-clique. An 𝓁-tree-partition of a graph G is a vertex partition of G into 'bags,' such that contracting each bag to a single vertex gives an 𝓁-tree (after deleting loops and replacing parallel edges by a single edge). We prove that for all k ≥ 𝓁 ≥0, every k-tree has an 𝓁-tree-partition in which each bag induces a connected ${\lfloor k} /(\ell+1) \rfloor$-tree. An analogous result is proved for oriented k-trees. © 2006 Wiley Periodicals, Inc. J Graph Theory 53: 167172, 2006
