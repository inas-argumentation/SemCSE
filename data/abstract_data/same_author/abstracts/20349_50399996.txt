Large distance effects in CP violation and the K0-K0 mass matrix
Large distance contributions to the K0- K 0 mass matrix are often neglected but have important consequences for CP violation. If “penguins” dominate |ΔS| = 1 weak decays then these large distance contributions correspond to “(penguin)2” diagrams and receive potentially large CP-violating phases. We argue that this effect reduces the size of |ϵ′/ϵ| by ≈ 1 2.5 in the KM model. In the extreme penguin limit, ⨍ = 1 and ϵm = 0, we have |ϵ′/ϵ| ≈ 1 50 .
