Observation of modified hadronization in relativistic Au+Au collisions: a promising signature for deconfined quark–gluon matter
Measurements of identified particles from Au+Au collisions at s NN = 200 GeV are reviewed. Emphasis is placed on nuclear modification, baryon-to-meson ratios, and elliptic flow at intermediate transverse momentum (1.5 < p T < 5GeV/c). Possible connections between (1) these measurements, (2) the running coupling for static quark-anti-quark pairs at finite temperature, and (3) the creation of a deconfined quark-gluon phase are presented. Modifications to hadronization in Au+Au collisions are proposed as a likely signature for the creation of deconfined coloured matter.
