First experimental test of emittance measurement using the quadrupole-mode transfer function
We carried out the first experimental test of the emittance (thermal energy) measurement using the quadrupole-mode transfer function. We show that this method can be applied to measure the intrinsic thermal energy dynamical systems nondestructively. Since the QTF does not depend on the beam distribution, this method can be used to measure the rms emittance of different dynamical systems.
