Callan-Symanzik functions and asymptotic freedom in the Lee model
The Lee model was studied in a paper by Bender and Nash in the context of determining whether the model displays asymptotic freedom. This determination is based on the calculations of the Callan-Symanzik functions. Bender and Nash have renormalized the coupling constant off the mass shell. We have performed on-shell renormalization and evaluated the Callan-Symanzik ..beta.. and ..gamma.. functions. In agreement with Bender and Nash, we find that the Lee model is asymptotically free for space-time dimensions N 4 (odd N) the model is ultraviolet stable and not asymptotically free. Finally, we employ the Weinberg relation to demonstrate the mathematical equivalence between the ..beta.. function calculated in this paper and the one presented by Bender and Nash.
