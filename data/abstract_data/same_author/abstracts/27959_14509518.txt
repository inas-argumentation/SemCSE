Is the string coupling constant invariant under T duality
It is well known that under T-duality the sigma model dilaton (which is normally though to be related to the string coupling constant through the simple formula κ = exp ), undergoes an additional shift. On the other hand, Kugo and Zwiebach, using a simplified form of string field theory, claim that the string coupling constant does not change under the T-duality. Obviously, what seems to happen is that two different coupling constants, associated to different dilatons, are used. In this contribution we shall try to clarify this, and related issues.
