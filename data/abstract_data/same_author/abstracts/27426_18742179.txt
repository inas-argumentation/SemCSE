THE EXACT TACHYON BETA-FUNCTION FOR THE WESS-ZUMINO-WITTEN MODEL
We derive an exact expression for the tachyon β-function for the Wess-Zumino-Witten model. We check our result up to three loops by calculating the three-loop tachyon β-function for a general nonlinear σ-model with torsion, and then specializing to the case of WZW model.
