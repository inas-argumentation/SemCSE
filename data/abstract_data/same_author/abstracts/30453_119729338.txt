E 0 decays of. beta. vibrations in the O(6) limit of the neutron-proton interacting boson model
Expressions are derived for the {ital E}0 transition strengths for the symmetric and antisymmetric {beta}-vibrational bandhead states to the ground state for O(6)-like nuclei. These {ital E}0 transitions are the only ones allowed to the ground state, and we predict them to be of similar magnitude. The isomer shifts for these {beta}-vibrational states are also discussed, as well as the isotope shifts for the ground states. We propose the Ba isotopes around {ital A}{approx}132 as possible candidates for observing these {ital E}0 decays.
