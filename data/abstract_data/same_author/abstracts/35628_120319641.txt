Fractionally-charged atomic systems and the Stanford quark-search experiments
Abstract The binding energies for the one- and two-electron systems (fe−), (fe−e−), (ffe−) and (ffe−e−) containing fractionally-charged objects f are calculated. Results are given for the fractional-charge values 1 3 , 2 3 and 4 3 . A possible explanation of the experimental results of the Stanford group is given in terms of these. Our results seem to prefer the value 2 3 suggesting that one of the possibilities for f is that is might be a liberated u-quark.
