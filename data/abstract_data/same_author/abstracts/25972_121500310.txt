On the interplay between QCD coefficients and annihilation terms in charm hadronic decays
Two approaches most commonly used in fitting D to two-body hadronic data-one that neglects annihilation terms but treats the QCD factors as parameters and the other which uses the standard SU(3)C QCD factors and includes annihilation terms-are shown to be equivalent for the purposes of fitting data. The authors also present evidence that the annihilation term in Cabibbo-angle favoured D to VP decays is larger than that in D to PP decays. They interpret this to imply support for a dynamical mechanism in which K(1830) dominates the annihilation channel.
