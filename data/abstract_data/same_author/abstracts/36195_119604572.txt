An exotic top quark
If the top quark mixes with a charge 23 SU(2) singlet quark, the branching ratio for Z → tc + ct could be very large: several tenths of a percent. In supersymmetric theories, where the charge 23 vector is a gaugino, such mixing can be the dominant source of the top quark mass. The top quark is “eaten” by the gauginos; it is heavy because its mass, which is now calculable, is proportional to a gauge coupling rather than a Yukawa xoupling. The gauge group SU(4) × SU(2)L × SU(2)R × U(1) provides the most natural setting for this mechanism. Both SU(4) and supersymmetry are broken at the TeV scale; this requires an unusual interpretation for the electron, and results in an observable rare decay τ → Kμ with a branching ratio of order 10−4.
