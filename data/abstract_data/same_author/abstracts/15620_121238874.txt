Predictions for the low-x structure function in the modified GLR equation
The predictions for the proton structure function F 2 at small x are presented by using the modified Gribov-Levin-Ryskin evolution equation which restores the momentum sum rule in the evolution processes. It turns out that the shadowing effect is weakened by the antishadowing effect in the region x > 10 −3 and that the net shadowing effect will appear in the region x −3 and low Q 2 .
