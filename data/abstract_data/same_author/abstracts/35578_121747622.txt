Exact Solution of Schrödinger Equation in Aharonov-Bohm plus Dirac-Monopole Potential
An exact solution of the Schr\"odinger equation for a charge $e$ in the potential of a flux tube along the $z$ axis and a Dirac magnetic monopole of strength $g$ at the origin is obtained. Single-valued solutions exist even with $2eg\ensuremath{\ne}\mathrm{integer}$. When $2eg=\mathrm{integer}$ and single-valuedness holds, solutions with $|{J}_{z}|l|eg|$ have a centrifugal barrier (and hence behavior at the origin) independent of flux, but solutions with $|{J}_{z}|g|eg|$ have a flux-dependent centrifugal barrier.
