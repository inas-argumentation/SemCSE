Tau function and Hirota bilinear equations for the extended bigraded Toda hierarchy
In this paper, we generalize the Sato theory to the extended bigraded Toda hierarchy. We revise the definition of the Lax equations, give the Sato equations, wave operators, Hirota bilinear identities, and show the existence of tau function τ(t). Meanwhile, we prove the validity of its Fay-like identities and Hirota bilinear equations (HBEs) in terms of vertex operators whose coefficients take values in the algebra of differential operators. In contrast to HBEs of the usual integrable system, the current HBEs are equations of the product of operators involving e∂x and τ(t).
