The continuum limit of the static-light meson spectrum
We investigate the continuum limit of the low lying static-light meson spectrum using Wilson twisted mass lattice QCD with Nf = 2 dynamical quark flavours. We consider three values of the lattice spacing a ≈ 0.051 fm, 0.064 fm, 0.080 fm and various values of the pion mass in the range 280 MeV ≲ mPS 640 MeV. We present results in the continuum limit for light cloud angular momentum j = 1/2, 3/2, 5/2 and for parity $ \mathcal{P} = +, - $. We extrapolate our results to physical quark masses, make predictions regarding the spectrum of B and Bs mesons and compare with available experimental results.
