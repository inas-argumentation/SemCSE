Azimuthal correlations of forward dijets in d+Au collisions at RHIC
The color glass condensate (CGC) picture of the nuclear wavefunction at small-x successfully predicted the reduced production of high-pT particles at forward rapidities: RdA < 1 in deuteron–gold (d+Au) collisions at RHIC. Within the CGC framework, I present predictions for the correlation in azimuthal angle between forward dijets. Both multiple scatterings and nonlinear QCD evolution are taken into account. Measuring such correlations will further test the relevance of the CGC picture at RHIC.
