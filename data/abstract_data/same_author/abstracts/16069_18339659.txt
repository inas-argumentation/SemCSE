Resummations with renormalon effects for the leading hadronic contribution to the muon (g-2)
The leading hadronic contribution to the muon (g − 2) value is calculated by considering a known dispersion integral which involves the Re+e−(s) ratio. The theoretical part stemming from the region below 1.8 GeV is calculated by using a contour integral involving the associated Adler function D(Q2). In the resummations, we account for the renormalon structure of the usual and the modified Borel transform of D(Q2) via an explicit ansatz and by employing judiciously chosen conformal transformations. This pushes the value of the leading hadronic contribution to the muon (g − 2)/2 upwards as compared to the value given by Davier and Höcker [Phys. Lett. B435 (1998) 427], and therefore diminishes the difference between the recently measured and the SM/QCD-predicted value of (g − 2). Typeset using REVTEX
