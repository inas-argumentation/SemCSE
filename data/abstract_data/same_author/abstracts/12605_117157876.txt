Target Fragmentation in Oxygen-Emulsion Collisions at Dubna and SPS Energies
The experimental results of target fragmentation in oxygen-emulsion collisions at the Dubna energy (a few A GeV) and the Super Proton Synchrotron (SPS) energy (60-200A GeV) are reported. The multiplicity distributions of target fragments, the correlations between the multiplicity distributions and the projectile fragments, as well as the correlations between the black and grey fragments are given.
