Extended algebras at c=1, coset models and the fusion rules
Higher symmetry algebras, conjectured empirically in certain c =1 models in statistical mechanics, are identified with the extended conformal (and U(1) algebras in coset constructions of G/(H×U(1)), where G/H is SU( p + q )/SU( p )×SU( q ), SO(2 n )/SU( n ), SO(2 n )/SO(2 n −2), E 6 /SO(10) or E 7 /E 6 . The branching functions and their modular properties are derived, and associated algebras, compatible with the fusion rules, are examined.
