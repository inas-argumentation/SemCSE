Bounds on leptoquark parameters with nonvanishing leptoquark-Higgs couplings
Non-vanishing leptoquark-Higgs couplings result in mixing of different leptoquark (LQ) SU (2) L multiplets leading to new low energy four fermion vertices. The rare process K L → μ ± e ∓ is explored in order to derive bounds on the LQ parameter space when LQ mixing-matrices are taken into account.
