String model building in the free fermionic formulation
We consider the rules for string model building in the free fermionic formalism. Valid spin-structures on the string world-sheet correspond to automorphisms of the Lie algebra of the world-sheet supercharge. After reviewing and clarifying the model building rules we classify all such automorphisms. We then classify the mutually commuting sets of such automorphisms that are needed for model building. Finally we show how to construct all models with N = 4, N = 2, or N = 1 space-time supersymmetry. In four dimensional models, N = 1 space-time supersymmetry can only be achieved if the Lie algebra of the world-sheet supercharge is SU(2) 6 .
