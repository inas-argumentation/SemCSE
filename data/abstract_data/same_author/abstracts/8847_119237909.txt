Modified clock inequalities and modified black hole lifetime
Based on a generalized uncertainty principle, Salecker-Wigner inequalities are modified. When applied to black holes, they give a modified black hole lifetime: T-MB similar to M-3/m(p)(3)(1 - m(p)(2)/M-2)t(p), and the number of bits required to specify the information content of the black hole as the event horizon area in Planck units N similar to M-2/m(p)(2)(1 - m(p)(2)/M-2).
