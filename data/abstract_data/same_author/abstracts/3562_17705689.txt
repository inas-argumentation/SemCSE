The Harmonious Chromatic Number of Almost All Trees

A harmonious colouring of a simple graph G is a proper vertex colouring such that each pair of colours appears together on at most one edge. The harmonious chromatic number h(G) is the least number of colours in such a colouring. For any positive integer m, let Q(m) be the least positive integer k such that (*) S: m. We show that for almost all unlabelled, unrooted trees T, h(T) = Q(m), where m is the number of edges of T.
