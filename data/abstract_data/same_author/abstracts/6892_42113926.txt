QCD condensates and the hadronic contributions to the anomalous magnetic moment of the muon.
A theoretical sum-rule estimate of the hadronic contributions to the anomalous magnetic moment of the muon is obtained by including the effect of QCD-vacuum condensates in the low-energy region.
