Progress in Understanding RAMP Rate Sensitivity in High Energy Booster Dipole Magnets
Quench current on some string test magnets decreased significantly with increased ramp rate. As subcontractor for the design and development of the HDMs, Westinghouse is responsible for ensuring that HDMs reach full field during the bipolar operating cycle which ramps at 62 A/s, compared with only 4 A/s for CDMs. Our objective is to specify and control design and processes to consistently produce magnets that meet ramp rate requirements. Understanding observed ramp sensitivity is necessary, along with aggressive testing of candidate design and process improvements.
