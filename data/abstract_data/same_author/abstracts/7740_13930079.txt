The recurrent ultra-luminous X-ray transient NGC 253 ULX1
We present the results of ROSAT and XMM-Newton observations of the recurrent ultraluminous X-ray source (ULX) NGC 253 ULX I. This transient is one of the few ULXs that was detected during several outbursts. The luminosity reached 1.4 × 10 39 erg s -1 and 0.5 x 10 39 erg s -1 in the detections by ROSAT and XMM-Newton, respectively, indicating a black hole X-ray binary (BHXRB) with a mass of the compact object of ≥11 M ○. . In the ROSAT detection NGC 253 ULX1 showed significant variability, whereas the luminosity was constant in the detection from XMM-Newton. The XMM-Newton EPIC spectra are well-tit by a hremsstrahlung model (kT = 2.24 keV, N H = 1.74 x 10 20 cm -2 ), which can he used to describe a comptonized plasma. No counterpart was detected in the optical I, R, B, NUV and FUV bands to limits of 22.9, 24.2. 24.3, 22 and 23 mag, respectively, pointing at a XRB with a low mass companion.
