Lepton number conserving and non-conserving weak interactions☆
Abstract A gauge theory of weak and electromagnetic interactions based on a group containing the SU(4) group of unitary unimodular transformations on the lepton and quark quartets, is shown to be possible only if lepton and/or baryon number are not exactly conserved, or if there exist particles that cannot be built of quarks and leptons. The spontaneous breakdown of lepton number conservation in terms of tadpole-producing spinless leptons is considered.
