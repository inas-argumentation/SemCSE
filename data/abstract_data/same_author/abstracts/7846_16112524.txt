Implications of the Top Quark Mass Measurement for the CKM Parameters , x s and CP Asymmetries
Motivated by the recent determination of the top quark mass by the CDF collaboration, m t = 174 ± 10 +13 −12 GeV, we review and update constraints on the parameters of the quark flavour mixing matrix V CKM in the standard model. In performing these fits, we use inputs from the measurements of |ǫ|, the CP-violating parameter in K decays, x d = (∆M)/Γ, the mixing parameter in B 0 d-B 0 d mixing, the present measurements of the matrix elements |V cb | and |V ub |, and the B-hadron lifetimes. The CDF value for m t considerably reduces the CKM-parameter space previously allowed. An interesting result of our analysis is that the present data can be used to restrict the coupling constant product ratio f B d B B d to the range 110-270 MeV – in comfortable agreement with existing theoretical estimates of this quantity. We use the updated CKM matrix to predict the B 0 s-B 0 s mixing ratio x s , as well as the quantities sin 2α, sin 2β and sin 2 γ, which characterize CP-violating asymmetries in B-decays.
