Development and Implementation on a Fuzzy Multiple Objective Decision Support System
A fuzzy-goal optimization-based method has been developed for solving fuzzy multiple objective linear programming (FMOLP) problems where fuzzy parameters in both objective functions and constraints and fuzzy goals of objectives can be in any form of membership function. Based on the method, a fuzzy multiple objective decision support system (FMODSS) is developed. This paper focuses on the development and use of FMODSS in detail. An example is presented for demonstrating how to solve a FMOLP problem by using the FMODSS interactively.
