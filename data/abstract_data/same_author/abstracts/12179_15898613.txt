Diffraction patterns in deeply virtual compton scattering
We report on a calculation to show that the Fourier transform of the Deeply Virtual Compton Scattering (DVCS) amplitude with respect to the skewness variable ζ at fixed invariant momentum transfer squared t gives results that are analogous to the diffractive scattering of a wave in optics. As a specific example, we utilize the quantum fluctuations of a fermion state at one loop in QED to obtain the behavior of the DVCS amplitude for electron‐photon scattering. We then simulate the wavefunctions for a hadron by differentiating the above LFWFs with respect to M2 and study the corresponding DVCS amplitudes in light‐front longitudinal space.
