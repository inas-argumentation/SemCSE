Running of Gauge Couplings in AdS5 via Deconstruction
Running of gauge couplings on a slice of AdS5 is examined using the deconstruction setup. Logarithmic running instead of (linear) power law is justified when the cutoff is lower than the curvature scale. Most of interesting features including the localization of Kaluza-Klein modes and the widening of higher Kaluza-Klein spectrum spacing are well captured within the framework of deconstruction.
