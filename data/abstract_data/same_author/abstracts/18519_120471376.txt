QGP phase transition and multiplicity fluctuations
The scaled factorial moments in QGP phase transitions are studied analytically by the extended Ginzburg-Landau model. The dependence of lnFq on phase space interval is different for the first- and second-order QGP phase transitions. When lnFq, are fitted to polynomials ofX≡δ1/3, the relative sign between the fitted coefficients ofX andbq,1 calculated theoretically can be used to judge the order of phase transitions. Two sets of experimental data are reanalysed and the phase transitions are the first order for one set of data but the second order for another.
