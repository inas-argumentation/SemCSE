Regular meshes
This paper presents our preliminary results on regular meshes in which all faces have the same size and all vertices have the same valence. A regular mesh is denoted by (<i>n, m, g</i>) where <i>n</i> is the number of the sides of faces, <i>m</i> is the valence of vertices and <i>g</i> is the genus of the mesh. For <i>g</i> = 0, regular meshes include regular platonic solids, all two sided polygons. For <i>g</i> = 1 regular meshes include regular tilings of infinite plane. Our work shows that there exist infinitely many regular meshes for <i>g</i> > 1. Moreover, we have constructive proofs that describe how to create high genus regular meshes that consist of triangles and quadrilaterals (3, <i>m</i>, <i>g</i>) and (4, <i>m</i>, <i>g</i>).
