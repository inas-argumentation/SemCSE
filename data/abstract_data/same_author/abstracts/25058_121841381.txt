Study of the iterations of a mapping associated to a spin glass model
AbstractWe study the iterations of the mapping
$$\mathcal{N}[F(s)] = \frac{{(F(s))^2 - (F(0))^2 }}{s} + (F(0))^2 ,$$
 with the constraintsF(1)=1,F(s)=∑ansn,an≧0, and find that, except ifF(s)≡s,N[F(s)] approaches either 0 or 1 for |s|<1 ask→∞.
