Longitudinal spin transfer of Lambda and anti-Lambda in polarized pp collisions at \sqrt s=200 GeV at STAR
We report our measurement on longitudinal spin transfer, D_LL, from high energy polarized protons to $\Lambda$ and $\bar{\Lambda}$ hyperons in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s} = 200 GeV$ with the STAR detector at RHIC. The current measurements cover $\Lambda$, $\bar\Lambda$ pseudorapidity $|\eta| = 0.5$ and $ = 3.7 GeV/c$. The prospects with 2009 data and the future measurements are also given.
