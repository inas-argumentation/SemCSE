The Geometry of Consistency: Decohering Histories in Generalized Quantum Theory
The geometry of decoherence in generalized “consistent histories” quantum theory is explored, revealing properties of the theory that are independent of any particular application of it. Attention is focussed on the case of quantum mechanics in a finite dimensional Hilbert space H. It is shown how the domain of definition of a general decoherence functional naturally extends to the entire space of linear operators H = L(H) on H, on which the decoherence functional is an Hermitian form. This identification makes manifest a number of structural properties of decoherence functionals. For example, a bound on the maximum number of histories in a consistent set is determined. In the case of the “canonical” decoherence functional with boundary conditions at the initial and final times, it is shown that the maximum number of histories with non-zero probability in any decohering set is rαrω, where rα and rω are the ranks of the initial and final density operators. This is one reason that some coarse graining is generally necessary for decoherence.
