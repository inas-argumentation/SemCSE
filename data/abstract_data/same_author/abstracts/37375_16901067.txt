W Boson Physics at the Fermilab Tevatron
We present electroweak physics results from the D0 and CDF experiments using data from p \bar p collisions at \sqrt s = 1.8 TeV. Measurements of the cross sections times branching ratios for W and Z production, the inclusive width of the W boson and the W boson mass are presented. Direct tests of the WW\gamma and WWZ trilinear gauge boson couplings are also presented based on studies of diboson production.
