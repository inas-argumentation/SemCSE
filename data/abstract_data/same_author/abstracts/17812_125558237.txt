Hypernuclear spectrometer system (HNSS) at CEBAF
The hypernuclear spectrometer system at CEBAF and experimental setup are discussed. The SOS (short orbit spectrometer) built for Hall C at CEBAF and an existing high resolution Enge split‐pole spectrometer will form a hypernuclear spectrometer system with energy resolution on the order of 1 MeV and relative low cost. It will be used to perform the first phase experiments which will provide valuable and important information on rates and performance of the coincidence measurements. This information will guide us for future hypernuclear physics studies. This would enable us to start the first phase hypernuclear experiments as early as in 1995.
