Semileptonic decay of B and D ---> K*0 (1430) anti-l nu from QCD sum rule
We calculate B-(s) and D-(s) to K-0(*)(1430) transition form factors, and study semileptonic decays of B-(s) and D-(s)-> K-0(*)(1430)(l) over bar nu based on QCD sum rule. Measuring these semileptonic decays with high statistics will give valuable information on the nature of light scalar mesons.
