Light quark dependence of the Isgur-Wise function from QCD sum rules.
We study the light quark dependence of the Isgur-Wise function for ${\mathit{B}}_{\mathit{a}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathit{D}}_{\mathit{a}}$ and ${\mathit{B}}_{\mathit{a}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathit{D}}_{\mathit{a}}^{\mathrm{*}}$ in the framework of QCD sum rules. At zero recoil all the Isgur-Wise functions are equal, as required by heavy quark symmetry, and at nonzero recoil the Isgur-Wise function for ${\mathit{B}}_{\mathit{s}}$ decay falls faster than that for ${\mathit{B}}_{\mathit{u},}$d decay, which is just contrary to the recent prediction of the heavy meson chiral perturbation theory. As a by-product we also estimate SU(3)-breaking effects in the mass and the decay constant.
