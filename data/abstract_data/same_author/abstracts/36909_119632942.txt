SOLUTIONS OF THE BETHE--SALPETER EQUATION IN THE INFINITE-MOMENTUM LIMIT.
A method is presented for solving the O(3)-invariant integral equation which Kim and Zaoui obtained by taking the infinite-momentum limit of the Bethe-Salpeter equation. A partial-wave decomposition is made for this O(3)-invariant integral equation. A variational method is then developed to solve the resulting radial equation. The solutions of the present analysis give a mass-coupling-constant relation different from that of the solutions obtained by the conventional O(4) method.
