High accuracy measurements of drift time in the streamer mode
A nitrogen laser focused to a 50 μm spot diameter with a minimum ionising energy deposit was used to scan a streamer tube. Results were obtained for the drift velocity and the spatial resolution. The resolution averaged, σ = ±25 μ m over the total drift length of 8 mm at atmospheric pressure. The charge multiplication region was also observed and could be separated into two regions, a region of low charge multiplication with increased drift velocity and an avalanche region with constant drift time.
