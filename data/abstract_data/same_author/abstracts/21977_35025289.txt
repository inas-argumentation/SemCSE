Hadronic transition amplitudes under null-plane Bethe-Salpeter dynamics.
A two-tier form of the Bethe-Salpeter (BS) framework is employed for the evaluation of a few typical transition amplitudes involving strong and electromagnetic decays of qq-bar hadrons with Lgreater than or equal to0. The two-tier BS framework, whose rationale has been described in some recent publications, is characterized by (i) a three-dimensional reduction of the BS equation under the null-plane ansatz to make a first contact with mass spectral data and (ii) a reconstructed four-dimensional BS wave function to restore the virtual qq-bar effects (higher Fock states) for the evaluation of hadronic transition amplitudes through the lowest-order Feynman (quark-triangle) diagrams. The processes explicitly considered are the pion form factor F/sub ..pi../(k/sup 2/), (..omega..,a/sub 2/)..--> gamma pi.., (rho,f/sub 2/)->..pi pi.., (a/sub 1/,a/sub 2/)..-->..rho..pi.., and b/sub 1/..--> omega pi... These illustrate the different types of coupling structures that are generally encountered in such a composite-hadron model. The agreement with the data on almost all these amplitudes is excellent, using the same basic constants (..omega../sub 0/,C/sub 0/,m/sub u//sub d/) as employed recently for a successful description of the mass spectra of qq-bar and qqq hadrons, as well as of some pionic amplitudes.
