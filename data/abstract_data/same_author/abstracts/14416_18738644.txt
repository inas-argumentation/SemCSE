Observable jets from the BFKL chain.
We derive a modified form of the BFKL equation which enables the structure of the gluon emissions to be studied in small {ital x} deep-inelastic scattering. The equation incorporates the resummation of the virtual and unresolved real gluon emissions. We solve the equation to calculate the number of small {ital x} deep-inelastic events containing 0,1,2,... resolved gluon jets, that is, jets with transverse momenta {ital q}{sub {ital T}}{gt}{mu}. We study the jet decomposition for different choices of the jet resolution parameter {mu}. {copyright} {ital 1996 The American Physical Society.}
