A Beable interpretation of the GRW spontaneous collapse model
We extend the beable interpretation, due to Bell, to the Ghirardi, Rimini and Weber [GRW] model of spontaneous collapse of the wave function. In doing so we generalize results obtained by Vink, in the context of a unitary Schrödinger evolution, to the modified Schrödinger equation of Ghirardi, Pearle and Rim-ini [GPR]. We show that the GPR equation allows a beable interpretation for position and momentum, meaning that stochastic trajectories can be given to these dynamical variables.
