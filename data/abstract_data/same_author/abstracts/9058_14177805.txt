The Resummed Photon Spectrum in Radiative Upsilon Decays (And More)
In this talk I present the results of two calculations that make use of Non‐Relativistic QCD and the newly developed Soft‐Collinear Effective Theory. The first process considered is inclusive radiative Υ decay. The second process considered is the leading color‐octet contribution to e+e− → J/ψ + X.
