Gaugino condensation and the cosmological implications of the hidden sector
Abstract We investigate the cosmological implications of models in which the breakdown of supersymmetry proceeds via gaugino condensation in a hidden sector, only gravitationally coupled to the observable sector. Semistable particles in the hidden sector are forbidden in the range between 100 eV and a few times 10 10 GeV. These constraints derived from the restrictions on energy density and nucleosynthesis are shown to have consequences for model building. As a byproduct of these investigations we point out a particularly attractive solution of the μ-problem and the strong CP -problem with a composite hidden sector axion.
