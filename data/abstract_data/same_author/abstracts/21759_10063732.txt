Effective Lagrangian approach to weak radiative decays of heavy hadrons.
Motivated by the observation of the decay B\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}K${\mathrm{\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}}}^{\mathrm{*}}$\ensuremath{\gamma} by the CLEO Collaboration, we have systematically analyzed the two-body weak radiative decays of bottom and charmed hadrons. There exist two types of weak radiative decays: One proceeds through the short-distance b\ensuremath{\rightarrow}s\ensuremath{\gamma} transition and the other occurs through W exchange accompanied by a photon emission. Effective Lagrangians are derived for the W-exchange bremsstrahlung processes at the quark level and then applied to various weak electromagnetic decays of heavy hadrons. Predictions for the branching ratios of B${\mathrm{\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathit{D}}^{\mathrm{*}0}$\ensuremath{\gamma}, ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{\mathit{b}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Sigma}}}_{\mathit{c}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\gamma}, ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}_{\mathit{b}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}_{\mathit{c}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\gamma}, and ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}_{\mathit{b}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\Xi}${\mathcal{'}}_{\mathit{b}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\gamma} are given. In particular, we find scrB(B${\mathrm{\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathit{D}}^{\mathrm{*}0}$\ensuremath{\gamma})\ensuremath{\approxeq}0.9\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}6}$. Order of magnitude estimates for the weak radiative decays of charmed hadrons, ${\mathit{D}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}K${\mathrm{\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}}}^{\mathrm{*}0}$\ensuremath{\gamma}, ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{\mathit{c}}^{+}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Sigma}}}^{+}$\ensuremath{\gamma}, and ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}_{\mathit{c}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Xi}}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\gamma}, are also presented. Within this approach, the decay asymmetry for antitriplet to antitriplet heavy baryon weak radiative transitions is uniquely predicted by heavy quark symmetry. The electromagnetic penguin contribution to ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{\mathit{b}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\Lambda}\ensuremath{\gamma} is estimated by two different methods and its branching ratio is found to be of the order of 1\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}5}$. We conclude that weak radiative decays of bottom hadrons are dominated by the short-distance b\ensuremath{\rightarrow}s\ensuremath{\gamma} mechanism.
