Analysis of Backward Error Recovery for Concurrent Processes with Recovery Blocks
Although backward error recovery with recovery blocks (RPs) has received considerable attention from many researchers, no attempt has been made to structure its implementation alternatives and then to evaluate/analyze their effectiveness. The authors categorize three different methods of implementing RBs. These are the asynchronous, synchronous, and the pseudo recovery point (prp) implementations. The authors have developed probabilistic models for estimating (I) the interval between two successive recovery lines for asynchronous RBs, (II) mean loss in computation power for the synchronous method, and (III) additional overhead and rollback distance in the case when PRPs are used. 11 references.
