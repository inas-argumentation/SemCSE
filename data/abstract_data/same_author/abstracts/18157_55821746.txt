Trilepton final state from neutralino-chargino production in mSUGRA.
The direct production of neutralino-chargino pairs in the mSUGRA scenario with decays into pure trilepton final states has a significant cross section for low neutralino masses. The trilepton signature was studied with the full and fast CMS detector simulations. The 5 signal can be observed in the dilepton invariant mass distribution at the integrated luminosity of L 30 fb for GeV.
