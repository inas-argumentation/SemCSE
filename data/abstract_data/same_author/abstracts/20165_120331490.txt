Precocious dominance of point-like configurations in hadronic form factors
Abstract The transition between the perturbative and non-perturbative regimes of QCD is studied by using realistic models to examine the spatial extent of wave packets formed in high- Q 2 elastic electron-nucleon scattering. We find that such packets may be much smaller than hadronic ground states, for momentum transfers as low as about Q 2 = 1 GeV/ c 2 .
