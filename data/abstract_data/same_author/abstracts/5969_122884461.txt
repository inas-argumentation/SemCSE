On the composition laws of Beltrami-Liouville theories and 2D quantum gravity☆
Abstract We present the composition laws or cocycle conditions of the Liouville theory and Beltrami-Liouville theory as well as the quantum gravity in two dimensions. We show that for the 2D gravity, their special cases give rise to the composition laws of Polyakov's light-cone action and that of the geometric action of the Virasoro group. By means of the composition laws, we give the gravitational Ward identities and a relation between the actions of the Liouville theory and the topological gravity and find that the principal action of the 2D topological gravity is a coboundary which should obey a cocylce condition in the Liouville complex.
