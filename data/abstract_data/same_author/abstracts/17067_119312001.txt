Online Gamma-Ray Burst catalog for neutrino telescopes

Violent and high energetic explosions ofγ-ray emission known as Gamma Ray Bursts (GRBs) are perhaps on e of the main candidates of sources of hadron acceleration and therefore of neutrino emission. Neutrino telescopes search for signatures of cosmic neutri os such as an excess of neutrinos in time and space coincidence w ith a GRB. These searches use catalogues of GRBs by satellite experiments.
