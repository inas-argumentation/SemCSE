Instanton test of non-supersymmetric deformations of the AdS5 × S5
We consider instanton effects in a non-supersymmetric gauge theory obtained by marginal deformations of the = 4 SYM. This gauge theory is expected to be dual to type IIB string theory on the AdS5 times deformed-S5 background. From an instanton calculation in the deformed gauge theory we extract the prediction for the dilaton-axion field τ in dual string theory. In the limit of small deformations where the supergravity regime is valid, our instanton result reproduces the expression for τ of the supergravity solution found by Frolov.
