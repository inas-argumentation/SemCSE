Saturation and Wilson line distributions
We introduce a Wilson line distribution function ${W}_{\ensuremath{\tau}}(v)$ to study gluon saturation at small Feynman ${x}_{F},$ or large $\ensuremath{\tau}=\mathrm{ln}{(1/x}_{F}).$ This new distribution can be obtained from the distribution ${W}_{\ensuremath{\tau}}(\ensuremath{\alpha})$ of the color glass condensate model and the Jalilian-Marian\char21{}Iancu\char21{}McLerran\char21{}Weigert\char21{}Leonidov\char21{}Kovner (JIMWLK) renormalization group equation. ${W}_{\ensuremath{\tau}}(v)$ is physically more relevant, and mathematically simpler to deal with because of unitarity of the Wilson line $v.$ A JIMWLK equation is derived for ${W}_{\ensuremath{\tau}}(v);$ its properties are studied. These properties are used to complete Mueller's derivation of the JIMWLK equation, though for ${W}_{\ensuremath{\tau}}(v)$ and not ${W}_{\ensuremath{\tau}}(\ensuremath{\alpha}).$ They are used to derive a generalized Balitsky-Kovchegov equation for higher multipole amplitudes. They are also used to compute the unintegrated gluon distribution at ${x}_{F}=0,$ yielding a completely flat spectrum in transverse momentum squared ${\mathbf{k}}^{2},$ with a known height. This is similar but not identical to the mean field result at small ${\mathbf{k}}^{2}.$
