The metric and the central charge of U(N) four-fermion models in the 1/N expansion
The first nontrivial 1/ N correction to the metric of the generalized U( N ) Thirring models in two dimensions can be explicitly computed. The result is found to be consistent with the general renormalization group properties of the metric. As a consequence, by a simple application of Zamolodchikov's theorem the function c can be evaluated in the same approximation.
