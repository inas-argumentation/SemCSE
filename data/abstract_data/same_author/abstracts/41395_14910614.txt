An inequality between the diameter and the inverse dual degree of a tree
Let T be a nontrivial tree with diameter D(T) and radius R(T). Let I(T) be the inverse dual degree of T which is defined to be ΣueV(T) 1/d(u), where d(u) = (Συ∈ N(u)d(υ))/d(u) for u ∈ V(T). For any longest path P of T, denote by a(P) the number of vertices outside P with degree at least 2, b(P) the number of vertices on P with degree at least 3 and distance at least 2 to each of the end-vertices of P, and c(P) the number of vertices adjacent to one of the end-vertices of P and with degree at least 3. The latter inequality strengthens a conjecture made by the program Graffiti.
