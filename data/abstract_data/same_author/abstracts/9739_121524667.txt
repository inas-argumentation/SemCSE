Possible origin of isotopic spin from extended conformal symmetry
In conformally covariant lagrangian density for conformal spinors interacting with tensor fields, the terms with Lorentz scalar-pseudoscalar bosons have a maximal internal symmetry su(2) L ⊕ su(2) R ⊕ u(1) L ⊕ u(1) R and this internal algebra is Poincare invariant; pion-nucleon Yukawa interaction is a particular case.
