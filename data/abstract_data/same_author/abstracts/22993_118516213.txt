Sunyaev-Zel'dovich observations of LoCuSS clusters with the Arcminute Microkelvin Imager: high X-ray luminosity sample
We present observations from the Small Array of the Arcminut e Microkelvin Imager (AMI) of eight high X-ray luminosity galaxy cluster systems selec ted from the Local Cluster Substructure Survey (LoCuSS) sample. We detect the Sunyaev-Ze l’dovich (SZ) effect in seven of these clusters. With the assumptions that galaxy clusters a r i othermal, have a density profile described by a spherical β -model and obey the theoretical M-T relation, we are able to d erive cluster parameters at r200 from our SZ data. With the additional assumption of hydrosta tic equilibrium we are able to derive parameters at r500.
