Predictive cardiac motion modelling and correction
The quest for higher image quality and resolution for assessing cardiovascular anatomy and function requires an effective way of handing non-periodic, patient specific tissue deformation. This paper presents a new technique for predictive cardiac motion modelling and correction. It uses partial least squares regression to extract intrinsic relationships between 3D cardiac deformation due to respiration and 1D real time measurable intensity traces taken from tissue interfaces. This abstraction allows for the construction of a patient specific model for dense motion field prediction. The effectiveness of the proposed method is validated for a range of respiratory positions with data acquired from 10 asymptomatic subjects, using reconstructed image data sets.
