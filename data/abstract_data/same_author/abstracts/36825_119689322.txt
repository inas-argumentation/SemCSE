Internal Space-time Symmetries according to Einstein, Wigner, Dirac, and Feynman
When Einstein formulated his special relativity in 1905, he established the law of Lorentz transformations for point particles. It is now known that particles have internal space-time structures. Particles, such as photons and electrons, have spin variables. Protons and other hadrons are regarded as bound states of more fundamental particles called quarks which have their internal variables. It is still one of the most outstanding problems whether these internal spacetime variables are transformed according to Einstein’s law of Lorentz transformations. It is noted that Wigner, Dirac, and Feynman made important contributions to this problem.
