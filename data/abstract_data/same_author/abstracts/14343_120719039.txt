Duality constraints for mesons
Abstract A new method of imposing semi-local duality constraints is discussed, which satisfies the theoretical requirements of crossing symmetry and self-consistency. The duality relations involve only physical on-shell particles, and give many conditions on their masses and couplings. All constraints from PP→PP and PV→PP amplitudes with ordinary and strange particles give compatible results and are in good agreement with experiment. In particular, the near linearity of Regge trajectories is explained. The internal consistency of constraints with charmed particles is not as good, and implies stronger contributions from daughter resonances.
