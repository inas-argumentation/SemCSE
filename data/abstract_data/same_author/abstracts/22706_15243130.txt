An anti-turn clip is formed having an elongated body with a bolt hole adjacent one end accommodating the bolt clamping a bus bar and a line strap in electrical connection. Arms extending laterally from the clip body adjacent the bolt hole straddle the strap and a tab at the other end of the clip body is lodged in a hole in the bus bar, thereby to prevent angular movement of the strap should the bolted joint loosen.
