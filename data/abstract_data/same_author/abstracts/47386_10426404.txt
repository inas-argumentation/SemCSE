Load balanced tree embeddings
Abstract When an n -processor architecture T is embedded into an m -processor architecture H with n > m and every processor of H is assigned at least ⌞n/m⌟ and at most [ n / m ] processors of T , the embedding has a balanced processor load. We present efficient embeddings with a balanced load for the case when both architectures are complete binary trees. We show that T can be embedded into H with a dilation of 1 and congestion of at most min{[ n / m ], 2 log n }. We also consider embeddings that achieve a balanced l/i load; i.e. every processor of H simulates at most [( n + 1)/2 m ] leaves and at most [( n − 1)/2 m ] interior processors of T . We present an embedding that achieves a balanced l/i load, a dilation of 2[log log m ] + 1 and a congestion of O(log n ). We show that every embedding strategy achieving a balanced l/i load must have a dilation of at least 3. We also consider the embedding problem when every edge of T has a weight associated with it.
