Implications of the recent CERN LEP data on nonuniversal interactions with the precision electroweak tests
We explore the nonuniversal interaction effects in terms of the precision variables epsilons with the recent CERN LEP data reported by the Electroweak Working Group. The epsilon variables with the nonuniversal interactions are calculated and constrained by the experimental ellipses in the {epsilon}{sub 1}{endash}{epsilon}{sub b}, {epsilon}{sub 2}{endash}{epsilon}{sub b}, and {epsilon}{sub 3}{endash}{epsilon}{sub b} planes. We find that the new data enable us to make a stringent test on the correction to Z{r_arrow}b{bar b} vertex. The {epsilon}{sub b} variable is sensitive to the Zb{bar b} couplings and thus plays a major role to give constraints on the nonuniversal interaction effects. Upon imposing the new data on {epsilon}{sub b}, we have the allowed range of the model parameter {kappa}{sub L}=0.0063{plus_minus}0.0030 at 1-{sigma} level with m{sub t}=175 GeV. Along with the minimal contact term, we predict the new physics scale {Lambda}{approximately} 1.6 TeV.
