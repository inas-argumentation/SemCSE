Charged Kerr-de Sitter Black Holes in Five Dimensions
Abstract We construct a general class of non-extremal charged Kerr–de Sitter black holes in five dimensions, in which the two rotation parameters are set equal. There are four non-trivial parameters, including the mass, charge and angular momentum. All previously-known cases, supersymmetric and non-supersymmetric, that have equal angular momenta are encompassed as special cases.
