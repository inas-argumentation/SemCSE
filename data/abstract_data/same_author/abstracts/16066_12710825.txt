Coherent state quantization of SU(N) non-Abelian Chern-Simons particles
Abstract We present a new method of formulating the classical theory of SU( N + 1) non-Abelian Chern-Simons (NACS) particles for arbitrary N ⩾ 1 using the symplectic reduction of CP( N ) manifold from S 2 N +1 . Quantizing the theory using BRST formulation and coherent state path integral method, we obtain a quantum mechanical model for SU( N + 1) NACS particles.
