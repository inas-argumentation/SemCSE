Constraints on Cosmological Parameters from Future Cosmic Microwave Background Experiments
The Planck satellite experiment will soon let cosmologists to determine most of the cosmological parameters with unprecedented accuracy. In particular a strong improvement is expected in many parameters of interest, including neutrino mass, the amount of relativistic particles at recombination, the primordial Helium abundance and the injection of extra ionizing photon by dark matter self-annihilation. Here we review the constraints achievable by future experiments and discuss the implications for fundamental physics.
