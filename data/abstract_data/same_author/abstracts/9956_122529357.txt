Momentum boundary conditions and Euclidean field theory
The authors discuss the effect of the imposition of momentum boundary conditions on the semiclassical approximation to Euclidean path integrals. For free field theories they derive an explicit simple expression for the resulting Euclidean path integral. As an example they discuss the relationship between the formulation of a massless scalar field theory in terms of a 2-form potential which is used in the context of wormholes and its usual formulation.
