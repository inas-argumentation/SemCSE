Neutron knockout of 12(Be) populating neutron-unbound states in 11(Be)
Neutron-unbound resonant states of $^{11}\mathrm{Be}$ were populated in neutron knockout reactions from $^{12}\mathrm{Be}$ and identified by $^{10}\mathrm{Be}$\char21{}$n$ coincidence measurements. A resonance in the decay-energy spectrum at 80(2) keV was attributed to a highly excited unbound state in $^{11}\mathrm{Be}$ at 3.949(2) MeV decaying to the ${2}^{+}$ excited state in $^{10}\mathrm{Be}$. A knockout cross section of 15(3) mb was inferred for this 3.949(2) MeV state, suggesting a spectroscopic factor near unity for this $0p3/{2}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ level, consistent with the detailed shell model calculations.
