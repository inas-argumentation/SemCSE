Comments on SO / Sp gauge theories from brane configurations with an O(6) plane
We use the M theory approach in the presence of an orientifold O6 plane to understand some aspects of the moduli space of vacua for $N=1$ supersymmetric ${SO(N}_{c}{)/Sp(N}_{c})$ gauge theories in four dimensions. By exploiting some general properties of the O6 orientifold, we reproduce some results obtained previously with an orientifold O4 plane when the flavor group arises from the world volume dynamics of D6 branes. By using semi-infinite D4 branes instead of D6 branes, we derive the most general form of the rotated curve describing the moduli space of vacua for $N=1$ supersymmetric gauge theory.
