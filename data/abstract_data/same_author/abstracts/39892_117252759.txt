Predicted properties of a superheavy lepton
The basis properties of the decays of a superheavy, sequential lepton L/sup + -/ are examined. Special attention is focused on the multiplicity distribution in semihadronic decays of L/sup + -/. A comparison of the theoretical predictions with the experiment makes it possible to rule out the existence of such a lepton with m/sub L/< or approx. =13 GeV in the investigated energy region.
