Charged kaon spectra in SW interactions at 200 GeV/c per nucleon
New results on the production of charged kaons in Sulphur‐Tungsten interactions have been obtained by the WA85 Collaboration. We present mt spectra for K+ and K− particles and the ratio <K+≳/<K−≳ calculated in the kinematic region pt≥0.9 GeV/c and 2.3≤ylab≤3.0.
