Real-photon physics at the linear collider; an introduction
In principle any e + e − collider can generate real γγ and eγ collisions at a significant fraction of the luminosity and energy available in e + e − mode. More R and D is needed to prove that this will work, and to cost it. The outline of the physics case is known but it needs to be sharpened if the real photon option is to included in the technical proposals for future linear colliders. If a light Higgs boson is found then the importance of making a direct measurement of its two photon partial width would make the case very strong.
