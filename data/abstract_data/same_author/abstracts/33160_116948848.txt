Bounds on neutrino‐photon interactions from Z decays
It is shown that the LEP bounds on the rare Z decays Z → vvγ and Z → vvγγ are useful to put constraints on neutrino‐one‐photon and neutrino‐two‐photon interactions, i.e. v vγ, vvγγ. The respective constraints are then used to bound the τ neutrino magnetic moment μvτ and the τ neutrino decay vτ → viγγ. Some interesting consequences are discussed.
