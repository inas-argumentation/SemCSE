High momentum component in the deuteron
The high momentum component in the deuteron, which stems from the short range part of the nucleon-nucleon interaction, is studied in they-scaling function and the structure functionF2 of the deuteron. We use not only some non-relativistic wave functions but also relativistic ones. It is shown that the relativistic mechanism or a six-quark state in the nucleon-nucleon interaction yields a large high momentum component.
