GOVERNING DYNAMICS BY SQUEEZING IN A SYSTEM OF COLD TRAPPED IONS
Department of Physics, Technion–Israel Institute of Technology, Haifa 32000, Israel~Received 4 June 1998!We consider a system of laser-cooled ions in a linear harmonic trap and study the phenomenon of squeezingexchange between their internal and motional degrees of freedom. An interesting relation between the quantumnoise reduction ~squeezing! and the dynamical evolution is found when the internal and motional subsystemsare prepared in properly squeezed ~intelligent! states. Speciﬁcally, the evolution of the system is fully governedby the relative strengths of spectroscopic and motional squeezing, including the phenomenon of total cancel-lation of the interaction when the initial squeezing parameters are equal. @S1050-2947~99!01401-8#PACS number~s!: 03.65.Bz, 42.50.Dv, 42.50.Vk, 32.80.Qk
