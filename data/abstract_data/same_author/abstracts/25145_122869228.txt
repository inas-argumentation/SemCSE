Bargmann‐ and Calogero‐type bounds for the Dirac equation
The Dirac equation for a particle of mass m submitted to a spherically symmetric vector potential is considered. Similar to the Schrodinger case, a nodal characterization of solutions for energies in the spectral gap (−m,m) is shown. As a consequence some bounds on the number of bound states which reduce in the nonrelativistic limit to the classical bounds of Bargmann and Calogero are proven.
