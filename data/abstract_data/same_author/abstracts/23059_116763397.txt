Color Gluons, and the Decay of the psi-prime (3700) Into psi (3100)
The decay of psi (3700) into psi (3100) plus hadrons constitutes a violation of Zweig's rule. On the assumption that the de-excitation proceeds through the emission of Yang-Mills gluons in a color-singlet state, I predict a width of 190plus-or-minus40 keV for this process. All parameters entering the calculation are taken from previous fits to the decay widths of the psi (3100).
