Use of W-boson longitudinal-transverse interferencein top quark spin-correlation functions
.Most of this paper consists of the derivation of general beam-referenced stage-two spin-correlation functions for the analysis of top-antitop pair production at the Tevatron, at the Large Hadron Collider, and/or at an International Linear Collider. However, for the charged-lepton plus jets reaction $q \bar{q} \rightarrow t \bar{t} \rightarrow (W^ + b) (W^- \bar{b} ) \rightarrow (l^{ + } \nu b ) (W^- \bar{b} )$, there is a simple three-angle spin-correlation function for the determination of the relative sign of or for the measurement of a possible non-trivial phase between the two dominant λb=-1/2 helicity amplitudes for the t → W+b decay mode. For the CP-conjugate case, there is an analogous function and tests for $\bar{t} \rightarrow W^{-} \bar{b}$ decay. These results make use of W-boson longitudinal-transverse interference.
