A Study on Ricci Dark Energy in Bulk-Brane Interaction
We have investigated the effects of the interaction between a brane universe and the bulk in which it is embedded. Considering the effects of the interaction between a brane universe and the bulk, we have obtained the equation of state for the interacting holographic Ricci dark energy density in the flat universe.
