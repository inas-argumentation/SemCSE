Strong nuclear enhancement in intermediate mass Drell–Yan production
Abstract We calculate nuclear effect in Drell–Yan massive lepton-pair production in terms of parton multiple scattering in Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD). We present the nuclear modification to inclusive Drell–Yan cross section dσ / dQ 2 in terms of multiparton correlation functions. By extracting the size of the correlation functions from measured Drell–Yan transverse momentum broadening in nuclear medium, we determine the nuclear modification at O ( α s / Q 2 ).
