On the heavy-quark content of the nucleon☆
Abstract Recently QCD corrections to the light (massless)-quark and heavy (massive)-quark structure functions of the proton have been computed up to O ( α s 2 ). Using these results we present the O ( α s 2 ) contributions to the ratio of the heavy-quark content of the nucleon with respect to the sum of the heavy-quark plus light-quark content for charm and bottom quarks. We find that, at small x , the heavy-quark fraction is suprisingly large and remarkably insensitive to changes in the mass factorization scale and/or the choice of parton densities.
