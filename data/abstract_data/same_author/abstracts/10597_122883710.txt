Quantum fluctuations of the gluon string in lattice QCD
We study the origin of the Coulomb part of the quark-antiquark potential by lattice calculations. A cooling technique which reduces quantum noise in lattice configurations is applied. We demonstrate that the Coulomb part of the potential vanishes with cooling and is thus due to quantum fluctuations, while the string tension persists many cooling steps. Further, we give an estimate of the energy contained in quantum fluctuations.
