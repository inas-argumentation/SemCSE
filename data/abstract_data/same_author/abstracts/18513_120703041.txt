PAIR PRODUCTION OF CHARGED TOP-PIONS ASSOCIATED WITH Z GAUGE BOSON IN THE TOPCOLOR-ASSISTED TECHNICOLOR MODEL AT THE ILC
The top-pions are the three additional typical particles predicted by the topcolor-assisted technicolor (TC2) model and the observation of these particles can be regarded as the direct evidence of the TC2 model. We calculate the production rate of the process and present the distributions of the transverse momenta of Z gauge boson and charged top-pion . We find that the cross-section of the process can reach 1 fb with reasonable parameter space of the TC2 model. Considering the main decay mode of charged top-pion, we find that such a production may provide copious events.
