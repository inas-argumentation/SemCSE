Our ultimate goal is to follow the timeevolution of the phase space density distribution by solv-ing the 2D Vlasov-Maxwell (VM) system in the time do-main. In contrast to macroparticle methods, the VM ap-proach has no statistical noise and is better suited to thestudy of microbunching.
