GENERAL REVIEW OF BES PHYSICS, ACHIEVEMENT AND FUTURE
A general review of BES physics results are given. New results from BES on J/ψ, ψ(2S) and ψ(3770) decays are presented. Enhancement of the mass spectrum at the threshold is observed in the decay of J/ψ to . Analyzes of sigma and kappa signals are reported in J/ψ to ωπ+π- and J/ψ to respectively. Results of new ψ(2S) decay channels are reported and compared with 12% rule. Some results from ψ(3770) scanning and D decays are reported. The status of BEPCII/BESIII project is briefly reported.
