A LONG LOOP COIL SYSTEM FOR INSERTION DEVICE MAGNET MEASUREMENT
The “dynamic scan” and “static scan” measurement methods, using the long loop coil system, have been designed for magnetic field measurements of the elliptically polarizing undulator (EPU) as well as any other insertion device magnet. The basic design concept is to build a reliable and high speed measurement system for characterizing the two transverse magnetic field components. The main features of this system are its high speed of measurement, high precision and accuracy. This system can perform the multipole field measurement within ten minutes by the “ static scan” method, and in one minute by the “ dynamic scan” method.
