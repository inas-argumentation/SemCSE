Calculable nonminimal coupling of composite scalar bosons to gravity
The nonminimal coupling to gravity −ξRφ22 of a composite scalar field φ is calculated in the Nambu-Jona-Lasinio model. We find ξ = 16. The result is exact in a leading large-N approximation, or in a fully improved one-loop renormalization group approximation. We briefly discuss some related cosmological implications.
