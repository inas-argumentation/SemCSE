Medium modifications of vector mesons
We construct a relativistic and unitary approach to pionand photon-nucleon scattering taking into account the inelastic π∆, ρN , ωN , ηN,KΛ,KΣ channels. Our scheme dynamically generates the sand d-wave nucleon resonances N(1535), N(1650), N(1520) and N(1700) and isobar resonances ∆(1620) and ∆(1700) in terms of quasilocal two-body interaction terms. We obtain a fair description of the experimental data relevant for slow vector-meson propagation in nuclear matter. The s-wave ρand ω-meson nucleon scattering amplitudes, which define the leading density modification of the ρand ω-meson spectral functions in nuclear matter, are predicted.
