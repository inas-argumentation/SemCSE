Anti)proton and pion source sizes and phase space densities in heavy ion collisions
NA44 has measured midrapidity deuteron spectra from $\mathrm{AA}$ collisions at $\sqrt{{s}_{\mathrm{nn}}}\ensuremath{\approx}18$ GeV at the CERN SPS. Combining these spectra with published p, $\overline{p},$ and $\overline{d}$ data allows us to calculate, within a coalescence framework, p and $\overline{p}$ source sizes and phase space densities. These results are compared to ${\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}$ source sizes measured by Hanbury Brown\char21{}Twiss (HBT) interferometry and phase densities produced by combining pion spectra and HBT results. We also compare to $\mathrm{pA}$ results and to lower energy (AGS) data. The $\overline{p}$ source is larger than the proton source at $\sqrt{{s}_{\mathrm{nn}}}=17.3$ GeV. The phase space densities of ${\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}$ and p are not constant but grow with system size. Both ${\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}$ and proton radii decrease with ${m}_{T}$ and increase with $\sqrt{{s}_{\mathrm{nn}}}.$ Pions and protons do not freeze out independently. The nature of their interaction changes as $\sqrt{{s}_{\mathrm{nn}}}$ and the $\ensuremath{\pi}/p$ ratio increases.
