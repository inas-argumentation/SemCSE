Heavy leptons from W decay
We investigate the possibility of detecting a new heavy lepton at the p-barp collider through the decay mode W..-->..L..nu../sub L/. We consider both the leptonic (L..-->..e..nu../sub e/nu-bar/sub L/) and hadronic (L..-->..q-barQnu-bar/sub L/) sequential decays of the L. We present detailed calculations to show that the leptonic decay rate is exceeded, for all kinematic ranges, by background contributions from W..-->..e..nu../sub e/ and W..-->..tau..nu../sub tau/..-->..e..nu../sub e/nu-bar/sub tau/..nu../sub tau/ decays. On the other hand, the hadronic decay leads to a promising, distinctive signature: a large missing transverse momentum balanced by two recognizable jets. Selective cuts can be imposed to remove background contributions, and the remaining event rate is about 10% of the W..-->..e..nu.. rate if the mass of the lepton is below 50 GeV.
