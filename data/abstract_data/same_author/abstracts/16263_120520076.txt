Soliton mass and discrete coupling constants in the quantized sine-Gordon model
The soliton mass in the quantized sine-Gordon model over the whole range of the coupling constant (g2 from 0 to 8 pi ) is evaluated by means of the Gaussian effective potential method. The analytical expression with numerical results is presented. The authors find that the coupling constant g can only take discrete values. A new kind of soliton linking g Phi =0 to g Phi = pi emerges at a set of special coupling constants g.
