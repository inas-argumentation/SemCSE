Quantum creation of multidimensional universes
A non zero probability amplitude for the appearance of a multidimensional universe of (1+d) dimensions is found. This can happen either in a “symmetric phase”, in which all dimensions are in an exponential expansion, or else in a “broken phase”, withd1 dimensions inflating exponentially andd2 forming a sphere of constant radius. The value of these amplitude for different total number of dimensions is discussed, and so physical consequences for Kaluza-Klein cosmologies are drawn.
