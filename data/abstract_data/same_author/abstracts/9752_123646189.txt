On possible composite lepton states induced by neutrinos
SummaryStarting from the weak diagonal lepton Lagragian, a Bethe-Salpeter equation for the study of lepton bound states is set up. It is shown on general grounds that for aV−A current-current Lagrangian the free-lepton-bound-state vertex can only have the formΓ=γμ(1+γ5)Aμ. The kernel for a ladder approximation, corresponding to neutrino loop exchange, obeys a gauge condition and is only logarithmically divergent; it corresponds to ar−6 dependent force which is attractive for leptons-antileptons. A gauge-invariant technique of regularization of the kernel is given. The equations for the bound states have decoupled sectors. In particular, the equations for massless vector bound states having the quantum number of the photon are given. It is shown that in the Konopinski theory of leptons the muon would give a vertexΓ=γμ(1−γ5)Aμ and a model is presented by which a combination of electron-muon leptons could give rise to an effective electrodynamic Lagrangian for leptons. In such a model, lepton quantum electrodynamics would the finite; considering that in order to obtain massless vector composite states a regularization with masses of the order ofGF−1/2 is necessary, the standard local theory should keep its validity up to distances of the order ofGF1/2.RiassuntoPartendo dal Lagrangiano debole leptonico diagonale si costruisce un'equazione di Bethe-Salpeter per lo studio degli stati legati dei leptoni.
