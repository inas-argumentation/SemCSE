Review of D Semi-leptonic Decays
Semi-leptonic D decays continue to play an important role in the field of flavor physics. During this presentation, recent measurements from pseudo-scalar to pseudo-scalar modes, pseudo-scalar to vector modes, and rare modes will be discussed. These results are important for many purposes, such as validating the machinery of lattice QCD, extracting CKM matrix elements, and searching for new physics and new interactions.
