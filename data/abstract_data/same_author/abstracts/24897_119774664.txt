V. Glaser's results on the wave function at the origin and the semi-classical limit
Abstract We describe V. Glaser's rigorous bounds on the wave function at the origin and the derivatives of the wave function for non-zero angular momentum, for strictly lower bounded potentials. We show also that the method used allows us to obtain the semi-classical limit of the wave function at the origin for large radial excitations and indicate connections with the Thomas-Fermi approximation.
