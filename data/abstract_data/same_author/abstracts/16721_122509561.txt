Siegel gauge invariance and quantization
The quantization of chiral bosons, which is important in the context of the heterotic string, has been a controversial subject. On one hand, there is a straightforward Hamiltonian approach, which, however, is not manifestly Lorentz invariant. On the other hand, there is an inequivalent Lagrangian approach, with a special gauge invariance which is believed to become anomalous. In an attempt to throw some light on the problem, we analyze a simple quantum mechanical system with a similar gauge invariance and compare the two approaches. The Hamiltonian approach turns out to be the one giving the “correct” result.
