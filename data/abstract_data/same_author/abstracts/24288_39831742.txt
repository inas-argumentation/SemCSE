Exclusive pion radiative capture from nuclei in the Delta region.
Calculations are presented for the exclusive pion radiative capture reaction from complex nuclei [ital A]([pi],[gamma][ital N])[ital B] in the framework of the nonlocal distorted wave impulse approximation. Our results are found to be in reasonable agreement with a recent TRIUMF experiment [sup 16]O([pi][sup +],[gamma][ital p])[sup 15]O. Since pion radiative capture is related to pion photoproduction via time reversal invariance, the findings reported here are at variance with the recent BATES experiment [sup 16]O([gamma],[pi][sup [minus]][ital p])[sup 15]O.
