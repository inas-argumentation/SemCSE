Spinor field realizations of non-critical W2,s strings☆
In this paper, we construct the nilpotent Becchi-Rouet-Stora-Tyutin (BRST) charges of spinor non-critical W-2,W-s strings. The cases of s = 3, 4 are discussed in detail, and spinor realization for s = 4 is given explicitly. The BRST charges are graded. (C) 2004 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.
