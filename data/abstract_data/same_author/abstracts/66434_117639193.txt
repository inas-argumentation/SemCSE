Torsion preradicals and ascending Loewy series of modules.
In his paper [10] W. Krull pointed out that certain facts in the ideal theory of commutative Noetherian rings can be better understood in the light of the ascending Loewy series and the associated Loewy invariants. This fruitful idea was further developed in his subsequent paper [11]. Even in non-commutative ring theory, especially in the presence of minimum condition, the Loewy series turned out quite useful). The Loewy series of modules over rings with minimum condition modulo their radicals were discussed by Dauns [4]. As an example of his torsion theory, Dickson [5] also considered the ascending Loewy series of modules; his main interest lay in the direct decompositions (see also [6]). The primary aim of this paper is to get complete Information about the lengths of the ascending Loewy series of modules. Some of our results will be formulated in terms of Maranda's torsion preradicals of modules (see [13] and also Gabriel [8]), since they are valid in more general situations. [Actually, with not much extra effort we could have stated some of these results for certain abelian categories, but this would have led us too far away from our present goal.] In Part I we collect some basic facts on preradicals and in particular, on torsion preradicals F of modules which are relevant in our discussions. Part II is devoted to the study of the ascending Loewy series, i. e. when F(M) is the socle of M. Examples 3 and 4 exhibit remarkable modules (over non-commutative polynomial rings) whose submodule lattices are arbitrarily long well-ordered chains which are at the same time their ascending Loewy series.
