Quarkonium polarization in nonrelativistic QCD and the quark-gluon plasma
We examine angular distributions of leptons arising from the decay of $J/\ensuremath{\psi}$ in inclusive hadroproduction. Taking into account feed-down contributions from ${\ensuremath{\chi}}_{1,2},$ a flat distribution emerges without fine tuning parameters. Changes in the ratio of direct to total $J/\ensuremath{\psi}$ cross sections would change this distribution significantly. Such angular distributions are, therefore, confirmatory tests of the $J/\ensuremath{\psi}$ suppression signal for the production of a quark-gluon plasma. Related effects are predicted for the $\ensuremath{\Upsilon}.$
