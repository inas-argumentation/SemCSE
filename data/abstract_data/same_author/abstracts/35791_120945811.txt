Weak constraints in conformal field theories on higher-genus Riemann surfaces : calculation of the Schwarzian connection and modified vertex operators
We consider a quantization of conformal fields with weak constraints on higher-genus Riemann surfaces. This method is applied to the Coulomb gas model. We calculate the Schwarzian connection in order to obtain the KN algebra in this model, and then construct a new modified vertex operator. This vertex operator makes it possible to define the screening operators on higher-genus Riemann surfaces
