Test of heavy quarkonium production mechanism and NRQCD
We discuss some processes for studying heavy quarkonium production and polarization mechanisms, including photoproduction of η c and J /ψ polarization in DIS at HERA. The J /ψ hadroproduction at Tevatron in the k t factorization approach is also studied.
