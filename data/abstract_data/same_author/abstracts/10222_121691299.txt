Strong-coupling approximation in supersymmetric field theory
We show how to use the lattice strong-coupling approximation in a wide class of two-dimensional supersymmetric field theories. We demonstrate that this technique is highly accurate for supersymmetric theories by calculating the ground-state energy density E 0 through seventh order in perturbation theory and by showing that the result approaches the correct supersymmetric limit E 0 =0. We argue that, by their nature, strong-coupling methods are especially well-suited for practical calculation in supersymmetric theories.
