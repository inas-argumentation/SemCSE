Anomaly-induced charges in baryons
We study the Skyrme model of baryons with quantum chiral anomaly of QCD in magnetic backgrounds, and suggest a possible induction of a novel structure of electric charge inside the baryons. Due to the anomaly-induced gauged Wess-Zumino term $\ensuremath{\sim}({\ensuremath{\pi}}_{0}+\mathrm{multipion})\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{E}\ifmmode\cdot\else\textperiodcentered\fi{}\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{B}$, the Skyrmions giving a local pion condensation $⟨({\ensuremath{\pi}}_{0}+\mathrm{multipion})⟩\ensuremath{\ne}0$ would produce a local charge source, in the background magnetic field $\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{B}\ensuremath{\ne}0$. Since the appearance of the total additional electric charge on the baryon looks unrealistic and surprising, we discuss the validity of our detailed evaluation of the anomaly effects.
