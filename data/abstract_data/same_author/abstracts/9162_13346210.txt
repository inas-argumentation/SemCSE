Charge and Transition Form Factors of Light Mesons with Light-Front Quark Model
The charge and transition form factors of pion (Fπ, Fπγ , and Fπγ∗) are studied with the light-front quark model. We find that our results for Fπ and Fπγ agree very well with experimental data. Furthermore, the decay constants of η and η are evaluated. We also calculate Fηγ and Fη′γ and compare with the experimental data. PACS numbers: 12.39.Ki, 13.40.Gp Typeset using REVTEX
