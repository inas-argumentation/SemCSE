Limits on the presence of scalar and induced-scalar currents in superallowed beta decay.
Limits on the scalar and induced-scalar coupling constants are established from experimental {ital ft} values of superallowed Fermi transitions. The role of the uncertainties in the {ital ft} values is discussed, leading to minimal coupling strengths that can be extracted from {ital ft} values. The implications for the Kobayashi-Maskawa mixing angles are also discussed.
