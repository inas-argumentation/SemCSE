Chiral disorder in two-color QCD with Abelian external fluxes
Abstract We investigate the effects of several Abelian external fluxes /gf, on the Euclidean Dirac spectrum of light quarks in QCD with two colors. Our results provide for a novel way of assessing the pion weak decay constant directly from spectral fluctuations, and discriminating a flux-rich from a flux-poor vacuum, using QCD lattice simulations.
