Stop near threshold
Abstract The observation that perturbative QCD suffices to describe threshold production of heavy particles if their decay width is sufficiently large is extended to SUSY particles: the production of squarks — and there in particular of stops — and of gluinos is analyzed, both for e + e − annihilation and hadronic collisions. Some qualitatively new features — like intrinsic limitations to a non-relativistic treatment of P wave production — are pointed out and discussed.
