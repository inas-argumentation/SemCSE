Superconformal Ward Identities and the {WZW} Model
We discuss the supersymmetric Wess-Zumino-Witten model in the context of superconformal field theory. The values of the superconformal central charge and the anomalous dimensions of all fields are shown to follow from the defining relation of the model. The superconformal and super-Kac-Moody Ward identities are employed to find the exact super-four-point function for the elementary field of the model, both for SU(N) and(N) symmetry.
