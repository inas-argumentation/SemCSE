Study of Scalar Mesons at BES-II
Recent results from the BES-II experiment on hadron spectroscopy using J / ψ and ψ ′ data samples collected in e + e − annihilation are presented, including a study of the scalar particles in J/ ψ radiative and hadronic decays, the observation of X ( 1810 ) in J / ψ → γ ϕ ω , as well as pair production of scalars in χ c 0 hadronic decays.
