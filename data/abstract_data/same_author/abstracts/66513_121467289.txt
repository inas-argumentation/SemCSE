A note on fixed-point-free solvable operator groups
Let A be a finite group and let n be the number of primes, including multiplicities, which divide the order of A. Suppose further that G is a finite solvable group which admits A as a fixed-point-free operator group and such that (i A I, G|I ) = 1. Under these assumptions it has been conjectured that l(G), the nilpotent length of G, is at most n, while lp(G), the p-length of G, is at most [(n+1)/2]. These inequalities have been verified and shown to be best-possible in certain special cases ( [3 ][5], [1]).
