Performance issues of scientific programming in Ada 95
Motivate by the increasing size and complexity of scientific software, we cousider the benefits of Ada 95 versus Fortran and C++. Using a sample problem derived from a recent evaluation of C-t-+ [4], we test the performance of a series of Ada 95 implementations designed to supply an elegant interface for sCientific programming. We test two compilers, GNAT and PowerAda, on a siugle processor of the IBM SP2 at the Ohio Supercomputer Center, and we find that the Ada 95 implemeutations take between 1.5 and 4 times as long as haudcoded Fortrau and C implementations. We aualyze how all the implementations vary in performance, usability, and general applicability.
