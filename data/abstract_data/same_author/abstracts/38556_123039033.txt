The Heidelberg-Moscow double beta decay experiment with enriched 76Ge: First results
The status of the Heidelberg-Moscow ~3-experiment using isotopically enriched 76Ge is reported. The results of 14.8 tool yr (or 1.29 kg yr) of operation are presented. From these data a new half life time for the [~[30v-decay of 76Ge to the ground state of 76Se of TI/2 > 1.4 (2.5) × 1024 yr with 90% (68%) CL can be deduced. For a possible neutrinoless decay to the first excited state a half life of 4.3 (8.2) × 1023 yr can be excluded with 90% (68%) CL.
