Summary of Session 2 : Beam Dynamics and Intensity Challenges
We summarize the Session 2 of the HHH-2004 workshop, which was devoted to beam dynamics and intensity challenges. The topics discussed included the extrapolation of collimation system, machine protection, beam dump, and rf systems to various LHC upgrade scenarios, spacecharge studies for the GSI complex, electron-cloud effects at RHIC and SNS, and a review of vacuum physics, which addressed, in particular, the possible interplay of ions and electron-cloud effests.
