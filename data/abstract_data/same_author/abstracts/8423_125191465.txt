On the role of fixed angular-momentum-plane branch points, with regard to thet = 0 singularity structure of Reggeized scattering amplitudes
SummaryA generating function for the Feynman amplitude for the scattering of four massive spinless particles due to the exchange of a spin-J particle, described by a (J, 0) field of Weinberg, is obtained. The amplitude is then Reggeized by way of the Van Hove model, and the resulting Reggeized amplitude is found to receive contributions from a single Regge pole, and a sequence of fixedJ-plane branch points. The leading fixed branch point is shown to play an important role with regard to thet=0 singularity structure of the Reggeized amplitude, whilst the secondary fixed branch points are of no obvious significance.
