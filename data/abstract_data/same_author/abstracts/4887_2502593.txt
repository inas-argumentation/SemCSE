Dependence of the cross section for inclusive pion double charge exchange on nuclear mass and charge.
Total reaction cross sections for inclusive pion double charge exchange have been obtained at incident pion energies of 180 and 240 MeV for /sup 4/He, /sup 6.7/Li, /sup 9/Be, /sup 12/C, /sup 16/O, /sup 40/Ca, /sup 103/Rh, and /sup 208/Pb. An explanation of the dependence of these cross sections upon the mass and charge of the target nucleus and the charge of the incident pion is suggested in terms of a phenomenological model in which successive single-charge-exchange processes compete with more probable processes such as quasifree scattering.
