Next-to-Leading Order Shear Viscosity in lambda phi**4 Theory
We show that the shear viscosity of $\ensuremath{\lambda}{\ensuremath{\phi}}^{4}$ theory is sensitive at next-to-leading order to soft physics, which gives rise to subleading corrections suppressed by only a half power of the coupling, $\ensuremath{\eta}=\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}\frac{(3033.54+1548.3\sqrt{(N+2)\ensuremath{\lambda}/72})N{T}^{3}}{\frac{N+2}{3}{\ensuremath{\lambda}}^{2}}$. The series appears to converge about as well as the series for the pressure.
