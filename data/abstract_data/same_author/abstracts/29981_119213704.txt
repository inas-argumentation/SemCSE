Supersymmetric Custodial Triplets
We analyze the extension of the Minimal Supersymmetric Standard Model which includes extra Y = (0,±1) supersymmetric triplets with a global SU(2)L ⊗ SU(2)R symmetry spontaneousy broken to the custodial SU(2)V by the vacuum expectation value of the neutral scalar components of doublets and triplets.
