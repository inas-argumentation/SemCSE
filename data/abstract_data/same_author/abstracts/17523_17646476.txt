Running anti-de Sitter radius from QCD-like strings
We consider renormalization effects for a bosonic QCD-like string, whose partons have 1/p2 propagators instead of Gaussian. Classically this model resembles (the bosonic part of) the projective light-cone (zero-radius) limit of a string on an AdS5 background, where Schwinger parameters give rise to the fifth dimension. Quantum effects generate dynamics for this dimension, producing an AdS5 background with a running radius. The projective light-cone is the high-energy limit: Holography is enforced dynamically.
