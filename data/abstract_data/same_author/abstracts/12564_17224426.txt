New results for hadronic collisions in the framework of the Parton-Based Gribov-Regge Theory
We recently proposed a new approach to high energy nuclear scattering, which treats hadronic collisions in a sophisticated way. Demanding theoretical consistency as a minimal requirement for a realistic model, we provide a solution for the energy conservation, screening problems and identical elementary interactions, the so-called “Parton-Based Gribov-Regge Theory” including enhanced diagrams. We can now present some of our results for SPS and RHIC energies.
