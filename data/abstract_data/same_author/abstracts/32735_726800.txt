Penguin-induced radiative baryonic B decays
Abstract Weak radiative baryonic B decays B→ B 1 B 2 γ mediated by the electromagnetic penguin process b→sγ have appreciable rates larger than their two-body counterparts B→ B 1 B 2 . The branching ratios for B − →Λ p γ and B − →Ξ 0 Σ − γ are sizable, falling into the range of (1∼6)×10−6 with the value preferred to be on the large side, and not far from the bottom baryon radiative decays Λb→Λγ and Ξb→Ξγ due to the large short-distance enhancement for b→sγ penguin transition and the large strong coupling of the anti-triplet bottom baryons with the B meson and the light baryon. These penguin-induced radiative baryonic B decay modes should be accessible by B factories.
