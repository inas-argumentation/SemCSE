Universal Resistivity from Holographic Massive Gravity
Massive gravity provides a holographic model for theories exhibiting mo- mentum dissipation. We provide an analytic expression for the DC conductivity. The result is universal, depending only on properties of the infra-red horizon, and holds at nite temperature and charge density. In addition, we provide a derivation of black hole thermodynamics in holographic massive gravity and show that the resulting physics is sensible.
