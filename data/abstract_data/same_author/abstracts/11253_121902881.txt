Unified SL(8,C) model of spin-2 gluons and gravitons
A unified version of $f\ensuremath{-}g$ gravity models with Higgs field has been constructed using the SL(8, $C$) group. Leptons and quarks are represented in a four-color scheme using the SU(4) group. When the Higgs fields exhibit a spontaneous breakdown of symmetry in the tree approximation, the spin-2 gluons ($f$) and fermions acquire mass while the graviton ($g$) remains massless. The Higgs-fermion interaction is renormalizable in contrast to the $\mathrm{SL}(2,C)\ensuremath{\bigotimes}\mathrm{SL}(6,C)$ gravity models. The spin-2 gluons include the three-color gluon, an exotic gluon which mixes leptons with hadrons, and a weak gluon which mediates only between leptons.
