Relation of parton distribution functions in the Drell-Yan process to deeply inelastic scattering
We consider certain parton distribution functions P (x, k T ) that appear in a proposed form of factorization for the Drell-Yan process at measured Q T . We argue that these Drell-Yan parton distribution functions P q / A (x, k T ) are related by a light cone expansion to the distribution functions ƒ q / A (x) measured in deeply inelastic lepton scattering.
