Massless scalar and antisymmetric tensor fields in de Sitter space.
The theory of a massless, minimally coupled scalar field \ensuremath{\varphi} is closely related to that of a massless, antisymmetric tensor field ${A}_{\ensuremath{\mu}\ensuremath{\nu}}$. It is shown that in de Sitter space the field ${A}_{\ensuremath{\mu}\ensuremath{\nu}}$ has a de Sitter-invariant state with a well-defined two-point function, while it is known that no such states exist for the scalar field \ensuremath{\varphi}.
