Resolving minijets in the minimum-biased events of hadronic interactions.
We propose and demonstrate that the {ital p}{sub {ital T}} dependence of two-particle correlation functions can be utilized to study the minijet contribution to particle production in minimum-biased events of high-energy hadronic interactions, especially when the transverse momenta of these minijets are lower than allowed by the current cluster-finding method of identifying minijets. Two-particle correlations both in azimuthal angle {phi} and pseudorapidity {eta} are studied and the effects of a limited rapidity cut are investigated.
