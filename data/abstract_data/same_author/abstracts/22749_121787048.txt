K+p elastic scattering at 12.7 GeV/c
Abstract We have measured the elastic K + p scattering cross section at an incident K + beam momentum of 12.7 GeV/ c . The experimental data for four-momentum transfers − t ⩽3.5 rmGeV 2 are well described by a distribution of the form (ifd σ /d t ) o exp( At + Bt 2 ), with ifd σ /d t o =25 −4 +2 mb/GeV 2 , A =7.2±0.3 GeV −2 and B =1.2±0.2 GeV −4 . The cross section for scattering in the backward direction is 0.3 μb (one event).
