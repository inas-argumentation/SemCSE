Single photoproduction of neutral π-mesons on hydrogen in the extreme forward direction at 4 and 5.8 GeV
Abstract The differential cross section of the reaction γ p→p π o has been measured at the Deutsches Elektronen Synchroton (DESY) Hamburg, at mean photon energies of 4.0 and 5.8 GeV for pion c.m. angles between 0 o and 5 o in steps of 1 o . The production near 0 o is dominated by the Primakoff effect.
