A parameterization forSO(3) Yang-Mills-Higgs systems
SummaryA model consisting of anSO(3) gauge field interacting with an isovector Higgs field is investigated. The gauge fields and field tensors are parameterized by the Higgs field directionality and a gauge field structure function in terms of which the magnetic field and magnetic charge density of a 't Hooft-Polyakov magnetic monopole can be simply expressed. An isovector Debye potential for a 't Hooft-Polyakov gauge field is written in terms of the gauge field structure function.
