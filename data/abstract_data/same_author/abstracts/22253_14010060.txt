Initial Tests on First Full-size ECAL Endcap Crystals
At the end of last year the first full size ECAL endcap crystals were delivered to CERN.Thirty in number, they were produced to the final geometrical specifications; 220mm long with a rear square face of 30mm and a front square face of 28.6mm. All were delivered polished. The visual inspection , dimension, transmission, light yield and light yield uniformity tests carried out since are discussed , with particular emphasis on the light yield uniformity. The results are very encouraging. suggesting that the endcap crystals will not need uniformisation.
