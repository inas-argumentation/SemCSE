The orbit of a compact star is restricted on the equatorial plane. Then we estimate the post-Newtonian effect of scalar radiation to the orbital evolution of coalescing compact binaries. We find that the post· Newtonian effect of scalar radiation is small in the LIGO band if we take into account the experimental bound of the Brans·Dicke parameter (/)8D>500. On the other hand, we find that the post· Newtonian effect of the scalar radiation is not negligible for the LISA experiment.
