Temporal chiral spiral in QCD in the presence of strong magnetic fields
Vacuum properties of quantum chromodynamics in strong magnetic and nite electric elds are investigated. We show that when a uniform electric eld is instantaneously applied in the parallel direction to a strong magnetic eld, it induces temporal oscillation of the scalar and pseudoscalar condensates. This is a temporal analog to the chiral spiral. The oscillation originates with the propagation of the collective mode, which is protected by the axial anomaly and thus is nondissipative.
