Hidden Photons from the Sun
A brief account of the phenomenon of photon oscillations into sub-eV mass hidden photons is given and used to estimate the flux and properties of these hypothetical particles from the Sun. A new generation of dedicated helioscopes, the Solar Hidden Photon Search (SHIPS) in the Hamburg Observatory amongst them, will cover a vast region of parameter space. A Hidden Photon (HP) is the gauge boson of a local U(1) hidden symmetry. This kind of symmetries arise very frequently in many popular extensions of the Standard Model, especially in String Theory [1]. Known particles have no direct interaction with HPs (hence the name hidden) but couplings can still be generated through radiative corrections or gravity.
