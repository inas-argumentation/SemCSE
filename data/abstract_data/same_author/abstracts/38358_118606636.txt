Constructing non-Abelian vortices with arbitrary gauge groups.
We construct the general vortex solution in the fully‐Higgsed, color‐flavor locked vacuum of a non‐Abelian gauge theory, where the gauge group is taken to be the product of an arbitrary simple group and U(1), with a Fayet‐Iliopoulos term. The use of holomorphic invariants extends the scope of the powerful moduli matrix formalism in which the vortex moduli spaces have been determined.
