Higher order graviton scattering in M ( atrix ) theory
In matrix theory the effective action for graviton-graviton scattering is a double expansion in the relative velocity and inverse separation. We discuss the systematics of this expansion and subject matrix theory to a new test. Low-energy supergravity predicts the coefficient of the v{sup 6}/r{sup 14} term, a two-loop effect, in agreement with explicit matrix model calculations. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
