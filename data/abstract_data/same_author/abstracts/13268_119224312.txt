Quark Polarization in a Viscous Quark-Gluon Plasma
Nuclear Science Division, MS 70R0319, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Berkeley, CA 94720, USA(Dated: August 30, 2011)Quarks produced in the early stage of non-central heavy-ion collisions could develop a global spinpolarization along the opposite direction of the reaction plane due to the spin-orbital coupling viaparton interaction in a medium that has ﬁnite longitudinal ﬂow shear along the direction of theimpact parameter. We study how such polarization evolves via multiple scattering in a viscousquark-gluon plasma with an initial laminar ﬂow. The ﬁnal polarization is found to be sensitive tothe viscosity and the initial shear of local longitudinal ﬂow.
