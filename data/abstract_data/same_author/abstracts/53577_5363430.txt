Differential Methods for Finding Independent Sets in Hypergraphs
It is shown by using differential methods that if ${\cal H}$ is a double linear, $r$-uniform hypergraph with degree sequence $\{d_v\}$ such that any subhypergraph induced by a neighborhood has maximum degree less than $m$, then its independence number is at least $\sum_{v}f_{r,m}(d_v)$, where $f_{r,m}(x)$ is a convex function satisfying $f_{r,m}(x)\sim (\log x)/x$ if $r=2$ and $c/x^{1/(r-1)}$ if $r \ge 3$, as $x\to\infty$, and $c=c(r,m)>0$ is a constant. The proof yields a polynomial-time algorithm for finding such an independent set in ${\cal H}$.
