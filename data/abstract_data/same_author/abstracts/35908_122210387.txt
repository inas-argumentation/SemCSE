Multiple scattering analysis of hadron-nucleus collisions at 100 GeV/c☆
Inclusive cross sections for proton collisions on nuclei, pA→pX, are decomposed into a sum of contributions fx(x, p ⊥) for a proton collision with exactly n nucleons. We describe a method how to obtain the distributions fnx, p ⊥) from the data and apply it to pA experiments at 100 GeV/c. The function f1(x, p ⊥) drastically differs from those with n ⩾ 2.
