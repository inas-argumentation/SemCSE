Semi‐leptonic Octet Baryon Weak Axial‐Vector Form Factors in the Chiral Constituent Quark Model
The weak vector and axial‐vector form factors have been investigated for the semi‐leptonic octet baryon decays in the chiral constituent quark model with configuration mixing (ϰCQMconfig). The effects of SU(3) symmetry breaking and configuration mixing have also been investigated and the results are not only in good agreement with existing experimental data but also show improvement over other phenomenological models.
