Lorentz covariance and the physical states in dual-resonance models
SummaryThe properties of physical states in dual-resonance models under rotations in the little group of the resonance momentum are studied. It is shown how at a critical number of space-time dimensions this group can be represented just on the space of « transverse » states constructed by del Giudice, di Vecchia and Fubini (because these are then essentially the only states which couple).
