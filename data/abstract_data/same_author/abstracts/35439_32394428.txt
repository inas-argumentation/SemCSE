Thermodynamical fluctuations of meson fields for a quantum hadrodynamics model.
Using the real-time Green's-function method with a pair-cutoff approximation up to second order, the thermodynamical fluctuation effects of meson fields on the QHD-II model are studied. We find that the fluctuation effects of meson fields, in particular, the \ensuremath{\pi} meson field, on saturation energy, effective mass of nucleon, and pressure are remarkable in the low-density region and/or high-temperature region. The results given by the QHD-I and QHD-II models are compared. The value of the critical point of the liquid-gas phase transition for the QHD-II model is given.
