RADIATIVELY-INDUCED LORENTZ AND CPT VIOLATING CHERN-SIMONS TERM IN QED
Abstract We calculate the induced Lorentz- and CPT-violating Chern–Simons term arising from the Lorentz- and CPT-violating sector of quantum electrodynamics with a b μ ψ γ μ γ 5 ψ term. The result to all orders in b coincides with the previous linear-in- b calulation by Chung and Oh [hep-th/9812132] as well as Jackiw and Kostelecký [Phys. Rev. Lett. 82 (1999) 3572], since all higher order terms vanish.
