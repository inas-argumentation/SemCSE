Cancellation of soft interactions with spectators in the Drell-Yan process
We verify by explicit calculation, to 2-loop order, the cancellation of soft interactions with spectators in the Drell-Yan process. We employ a model in which a “meson” consists of a parton and a spectator parton, each moving along parallel eikonal lines. The process occurs in the collision of two such “mesons” at velocities near the speed of light. The calculation is sensitive to gluons with wavelengths of the order of or greater than the meson size.
