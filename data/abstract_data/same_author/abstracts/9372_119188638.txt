GeV Majorana neutrinos in top-quark decay at the LHC
We explore the $\ensuremath{\Delta}L=2$ same-sign dilepton signal from top-quark decay via a Majorana neutrino at the LHC in the top anti-top pair production samples. The signature is same-sign dilepton plus multijets with no significant missing energy. The most optimistic region lies where the Majorana neutrino mass is between $15\char21{}65\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{GeV}$. For $300\text{ }\text{ }{\mathrm{fb}}^{\ensuremath{-}1}$ integrated luminosity, it is possible to probe ${S}_{ij}$, the effective mixing parameter, to $\mathcal{O}({10}^{\ensuremath{-}5})$.
