Rapidity Distribution of Strange Particles in High-Energy Nucleus-Nucleus Collisions
The rapidity distributions of strange particles produced in high energy nucleus-nucleus collisions are studied by the thermalized cylinder model and the three-fireball model. The calculated results are compared with the experimental data of Au-Au collisions at 11.6A GeV, Pb-Pb collisions at 158A GeV, and S-S collisions at 200A GeV. The calculated results are shown to be in agreement with the available experimental data.
