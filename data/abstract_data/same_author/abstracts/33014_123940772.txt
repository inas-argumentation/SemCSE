Generating functionals, Ward identities and scalar mesons
SummaryWe shall present a general and concise treatment of Ward identities in current algebra with broken symmetry. An application is also presented to the mass spectrum and leptonic decay constants of the scalar and pseudoscalar mesons.RiassuntoSi presenta un trattamento generale e conciso delle identità di Ward dell'algebra delle correnti con rottura di simmetria.
