Is the Centauro-I event due to heavy primary nuclear break-up?
The possibility that the Centauro-I event reported by the Brazil-Japan group, is due to the break up of a primary cosmic ray iron nucleus is investigated. Monte Carlo simulations of propagation through the atmosphere of iron nuclei, injected at 400 g cm-2, show that all the features of the event can be reproduced. The expected number of events, when primaries of mass number >or=40 are also taken into account, is 2.4*10-5 against one event observed.
