Introduction to the special issue on IEEE Multimedia Software Engineering 2004
IEEE Sixth International Symposium on Multimedia Software Engineering (IEEE-MSE2004) is an international forum for researchers and practitioners in multimedia and software engineering to exchange information regarding advancements in the state-of-the-art and practice of multimedia software engineering, as well as to identify the emerging research topics and define the future of multimedia. From those papers included in MSE 2004 proceedings, two papers were selected for this special issue. The authors were asked to extend and revise their original MSE 2004 articles, and then each paper was reviewed by three referees. The first paper of this special issue is entitled “Bayesian Traffic Dynamics and Packet Loss Prediction for Video over IP Networks” by Andy Backhouse and Irene Y. H. Gu. A novel Bayesian-based approach is presented in this paper, which can dynamically predict the instantaneous packet loss probability and estimate the probability distribution of the cross-traffic rate by utilizing the observed losses and endto-end measurements of the packet delays in the network.
