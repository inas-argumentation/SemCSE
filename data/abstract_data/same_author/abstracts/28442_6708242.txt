Transition to perturbative QCD in two-photon collisions
We propose that the different angular distributions in two-photon collisions observed at low and high center-of-mass energies ${W}_{\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\gamma}}$ indicate the transition from nonperturbative to perturbative QCD. We calculate the differential cross sections of $\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\pi}\ensuremath{\pi},KK$ in the angle $\ensuremath{\theta}$ of one of the final-state mesons using QCD sum rules and the perturbative QCD approach based on ${k}_{T}$ factorization theorem. Our predictions from sum rules (perturbative QCD) decrease (increase) with $|\mathrm{cos}﻿\ensuremath{\theta}|$, consistent with the Belle data of $\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{K}^{+}{K}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ for ${W}_{\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\gamma}}\ensuremath{\approx}1.5\ensuremath{-}1.7(2.2\ensuremath{-}2.4)\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{G}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{V}$.
