On the Dynamics of Dark Energy with Higher Time Derivatives of Hubble Parameter in El-Nabulsi Fractional Action Cosmology
In this work, we discussed a new dark energy density model which contains one term proportional to the Hubble parameter H squared, one to the first and one to second time derivative of the Hubble parameter H based on El-Nabulsi fractional action cosmology (FAC). Some cosmological parameters, like the Hubble parameter, the Equation of State (EoS) parameter ωDE and the deceleration parameter q have been reconstructed and studied. Finally, through a test made using the squared speed of sound $v_{s}^{2}$, the proposed reconstruction model results to be classically unstable.
