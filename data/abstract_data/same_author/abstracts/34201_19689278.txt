Diproton decay of nuclei on the proton drip line.
The process of direct two-proton decay of nuclei with {ital Z}=22--28 on the proton drip line is considered. On the basis of new 0{ital d}{sub 3/2}-0{ital f}{sub {ital f}/2} shell-model mass extrapolations, {sup 39}Ti, {sup 42}Cr, {sup 45}Fe, {sup 48}Ni, and {sup 49}Ni are found to be bound to single-proton decay but unbound to two-proton decay. New estimates of the spectroscopic factors and lifetimes have been made. The decays of {sup 39}Ti, {sup 45}Fe, and {sup 48}Ni are found to be promising for further experimental investigation.
