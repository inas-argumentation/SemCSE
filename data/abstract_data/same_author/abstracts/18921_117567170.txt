The average mass number of primary cosmic rays around the knee region derived from Grapes III array at Ooty
We analyzed three months data on air showers (total 10 showers in 10<Ne<10) observed with the GRAPES III experiment during 2001. The shower array consists of 257 scintillation detectors (1m each) and 16 muon detectors (35 m each). Very large area (560m) of the muon detectors gives good statistics for estimating the number of muons even for small air showers. We present the correlation between muon multiplicity distribution and shower size. This result is compared with simulation (Corsika code with QGS Jet model) for primary energy between 10 eV and 10 eV. We find the average mass number in this energy region follows gradual increase and mixing of proton and heavy nuclei model fits well our observed data.
