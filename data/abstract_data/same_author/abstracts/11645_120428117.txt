Crossover phenomena in the multifractal behavior of invariant circles
Abstract The singularity spectrum f(α) and the generalized dimension Dq of the critical invariant circle in an area-preserving twist map with a variable degree of inflection z is studied. The scaling behavior is found to depend on z, and there is a crossover from the simple fixed point to the standard fixed point when z varies from 2 to 3. Thus z plays a role quite similar to that of dimensionality in phase transition, with z = 2 and 3 corresponding to the lower and upper critical dimensions respectively.
