Upper bound on baryogenesis scale from neutrino masses
We examine the constraints on baryogenesis if anomalous weak baryon violation is in thermal equilibrium at high temperatures. If neutrinos have Majorana masses, there is an upper bound on the scale of baryogenesis: T 0 ≲10 12 GeV(1 eV/ m ν ) 2 , where m ν is the mass of the lightest neutrino, and no baryon number is generated at temperatures below T 0 .
