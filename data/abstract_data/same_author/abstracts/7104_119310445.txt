Multicentered solution for maximally charged dilaton black holes in arbitrary dimensions
A family of static multicentered solutions to modified Einstein–Maxwell equations coupled with a dilaton is constructed in (1+N) dimensional space–time (N≥2). For N≥3, the solutions are generalizations of the Majumdar–Papapetrou solution. We also find the solution in (1+2) dimensions, where the scalar and vector forces cancel each other in the static case. The interaction between two extreme charged dilaton black holes in the low‐energy limit is investigated in (1+N) dimensions (N≥3). We find that there remains the residual velocity‐dependent force in general cases, except for the case with N=a2.
