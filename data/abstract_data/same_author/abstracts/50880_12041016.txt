Promoter Extraction from GenBank (PEG): automatic extraction of eukaryotic promoter sequences in large sets of genes
UNLABELLED
Promoter Extraction from GenBank (PEG) extracts promoter sequences for large sets of genes using information present in GenBank. For a gene whose promoter sequence is not found, PEG will attempt to extract promoter sequences of the orthologous genes instead.


AVAILABILITY
It is freely available to academic users at ftp://cshl.org/pub/science/mzhanglab/theresa/.
