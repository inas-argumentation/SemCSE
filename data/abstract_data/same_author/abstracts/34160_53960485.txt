TECHNIQUES OF SC CAVITY PREPARATION FOR HIGH GRADIENT
High gradient of sc cavities was much improved in the past one decade. Today the accelerating field gradient >30 MV/m is produced routinely in single cell niobium cavities by electropolishing (EP). Recently, such a gradient has been achieved in TTF 9-cavities. In this paper, essential preparation techniques for the high gradient are introduced. Especially, EP is a breakthrough technique and its superiority will be resolved from recent new phenomena. In the last, recent results of multi-cell cavities with both β=1 and medium β are reviewed.
