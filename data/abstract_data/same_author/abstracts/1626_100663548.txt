La 0.5 Sr 0.5 CoO 3 electrode technology for Pb(Zr,Ti)O 3 thin film nonvolatile memories
Oxide electrode technology is investigated for optimization of Pb(Zr,Ti)O 3 (PZT) thin film capacitor properties for high density nonvolatile memory applications. PZT thin film capacitors with RF sputter deposited La 0.5 Sr 0.5 CoO 3 (LSCO) electrodes have been characterized with respect to the following parameters: initial dielectric hysteresis loop characteristics, fatigue performance, microstructure and imprint behavior. Our studies have determined that the fatigue of PZT capacitors with LSCO electrodes is less sensitive to B site cation ratio and underlying electrode stack technology than with RuO 2 electrodes. Doping PZT thin films with Nb (PNZT) improves imprint behavior of LSCO//PZT//LSCO capacitors considerably. We have demonstrated that PNZT 4/30/70 // LSCO capacitors thermally processed at either 550°C or 675°C have almost identical initial hysteresis properties and exhibit essentially no fatigue out to approximately 10 10 cycles.
