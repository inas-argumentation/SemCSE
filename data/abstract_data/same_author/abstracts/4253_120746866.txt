Constraints on the colour dielectric model of the nucleus
We examine the consequences of an up-down quark mass splitting in the colour dielectric model of the nucleus. In particular it is seen that this introduces a binding energy difference for protons and neutrons in the nucleus. We show that if this binding energy difference is to be acceptably small then the colour dielectric model of the nucleus in its present form is insufficient to explain the EMC effect. However it does provide a possible explanation of the Nolen-Schiffer anomaly.
