The design and calibration of precision drift chambers for use in a gluonium search experiment at the CERN ISR
We describe the design, construction and calibration of a set of high precision drift chambers. The precise coordinate (σ ∼ 120 μm) is determined by drift time measurement, with the orthogonal coordinate given by the charge division method (σ∼1.2 mm). The chambers have been calibrated using a well-defined laser beam. Their use within a gluonium search experiment at the CERN ISR is also described.
