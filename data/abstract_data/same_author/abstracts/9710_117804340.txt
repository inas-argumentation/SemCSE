DESIGN OF PROTON LINEAR ACCELERATORS FOR ENERGIES UP TO 300 Mev
Extension of the conventional 2 pi structure for particle acceleration to 200 Mev or higher requires minimization of power losses by optimizing cavity and drift-tube parameters. Using a previously developed method, an optimization of the cavity drift-tube dimensions was made at 200 Mc and at energies of 50, 100, 150, and 200 Mev to give minimum power losses for cylindrical tubes. Drift tubes of other shapes may reduce observed high-energy power losses due to currents in the drift-tube surface. Ellipsoidal tubes appear more efficient than the cylindrical tubes. (D.C.W.)
