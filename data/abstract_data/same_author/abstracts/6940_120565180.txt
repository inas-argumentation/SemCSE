Lattice Gravity and Supergravity as Spontaneously Broken Gauge Theories of the (Super)poincare Group
Abstract We describe a lattice discretization of gravity which has a local Poincare (or de Sitter) invariance broken by a scalar field. The advantages of this formulation are a natural definition of the vierbein on a lattice and the possibility of a straightforward generalization of the method to include supergravity.
