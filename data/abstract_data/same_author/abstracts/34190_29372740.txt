Strength functions and spreading widths of simple shell model configurations.
The exact solution of the many-body problem in the framework of the nuclear shell model with a realistic residual Hamiltonian makes it possible to study the fragmentation of simple configurations as a function of excitation energy and interaction strength. The analysis is performed for 839 states with quantum numbers {ital J}{sup {pi}}{ital T}=0{sup +}0 in a system of 12 valence particles within the {ital sd} shell. Our statistics allow us to establish the generic shape of the strength function in the region of strong mixing. For the realistic interaction, the strength function is close to Gaussian in the central part and has exponential wings. The spreading width is larger than predicted by the standard golden rule. At the artificially suppressed interaction strength, we recover the Breit-Wigner shape and the golden rule for the spreading width. The transition between these regimes agrees with theoretical considerations based on the idea of chaotic dynamics. {copyright} {ital 1996 The American Physical Society.}
