Cosmological X-Ray Flashes in the Off-Axis Jet Model
The V/Vmax of the cosmological X-ray flashes detected by the Wide Field Cameras on BeppoSAX is calculated theoretically in a simple jet model. The total emission energy from the jet is assumed to be constant. We find that if the jet opening half-angle is smaller than 0.03 radian, the theoretical V/Vmax for fixed opening half-angle is less than ~0.4, which is consistent with the recently reported observational value of 0.27 ± 0.16 at the 1 σ level. This suggests that the off-axis gamma-ray burst jet with the small opening half-angle at the cosmological distance can be identified as the cosmological X-ray flash.
