Stability of the nonextremal enhançon solution: Perturbation equations
We consider the stability of the two branches of nonextremal enhan\ifmmode \mbox{\c{c}}\else \c{c}\fi{}on solutions. We argue that one would expect a transition between the two branches at some value of the nonextremality, which should manifest itself in some instability. We study small perturbations of these solutions, constructing a sufficiently general ansatz for linearized perturbations of the nonextremal solutions, and show that the linearized equations are consistent. We show that the simplest kind of perturbation does not lead to any instability. We reduce the problem of studying the more general spherically symmetric perturbation to solving a set of three coupled second-order differential equations.
