Combined potential of future long-baseline and reactor experiments ∗
We investigate the determination of neutrino oscillation parameters by experiments within the next ten years. The potential of conventional beam experiments (MINOS, ICARUS, OPERA), superbeam experiments (T2K, NO ν A), and reactor experiments (D-CHOOZ) to improve the precision on the “atmospheric” parameters Δ m 31 2 , θ 23 , as well as the sensitivity to θ 13 are discussed. Further, we comment on the possibility to determine the leptonic CP-phase and the neutrino mass hierarchy if θ 13 turns out to be large.
