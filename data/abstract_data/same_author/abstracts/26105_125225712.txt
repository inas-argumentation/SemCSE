J/Psi and phi electro-production in Pomeron exchange model
Based on Pomeron exchange model, J/psi and phi production in electro-proton interaction are investigated with both linear and non-linear Pomeron trajectory. The experimental differential cross sections measured as a function of the kinematic variable Q(2),W and t are reproduced successfully in the model. Our conclusions are that the Pomeron exchange model is a successful description of J/psi and phi electro-productions on the proton, and that the linear trajectory is a good approximation to non-linearity of the Pomeron trajectory.
