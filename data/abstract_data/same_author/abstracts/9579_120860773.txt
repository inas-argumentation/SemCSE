Semileptonic Bc decay within heavy quark spin symmetry
Abstract We calculate the semileptonic B c decay widths using heavy quark spin symmetry. The form factors are estimated from the wave function model of Isgur, Scora, Grinstein and Wise (ISGW). We also discuss the extraction of | V cs |/| V cb |, | V cs |/| V cd | and | V ub |/| V cs | from semileptonic B c decays. The theoretical uncertainties are minimized when using the ratios of B c differential decay rates to extract the ratios of CKM elements at zero recoil limit. The result is potentially important for extracting CKM elements from future measurements at LHC and SSC.
