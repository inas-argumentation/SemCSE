Strong Interaction Effects in Weak Radiative $\bar{B}$ Meson Decay
Strong-interaction corrections to the effective hamiltonian for weak radiative B-meson decay (i.e., B → Xsγ, where Xs denotes a strange hadronic final state) are calculated using the leading logarithmic approximation. The inclusive rate for decays B → Xsγ, where the energy of the photon is large, is computed in the standard model with minimal particle content and in extensions of the minimal standard model with two Higgs doublets.
