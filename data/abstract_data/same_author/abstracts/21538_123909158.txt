DESIGN AND OPERATION OF THE PRINCETON-PENNSYLVANIA PROTON SYNCHROTRON
The 16 sector magnet is weak focusing with n = 0.6 over a useful radial region of 16.5 cm and 6.1 cm vertically. A d-c biased, sine wave excitation at 19 cps is employed, the energy storage being in a resonant capacitor-inductor system. Injection at 3 Mev is provided by an electrostatic generator. Acceleration is achieved by a combination of drift tubes and resonant cavities which must provide 120 kv per turn, and must be precisely tuned over a 12: 1 range in frequency. To date, 2 x 10/sup 10/ protons per pulse have been injected, of which about 10% are captured by the r-f. A method for extracting the proton beam, which is similar to Piccionis' system, but freer of aberrations, is now under design. (auth)
