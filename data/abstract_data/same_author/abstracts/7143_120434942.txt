Solvable lattice models with broken ZN symmetry and Hecke's indefinite modular forms
A class of exactly solvable lattice models are studied, which at criticality reduce to the self-dual Z N model of Fateev-Zamolodchikov. The partition function and the local height probabilities are obtained. The latter are expressible in terms of Hecke's indefinite modular forms which appear as level- N string functions for the Kac-Moody Lie algebra A 1 (1) . Critical exponents are found to be in agreement with those of the Z N invariant conformal field theory as proposed by Fateev-Zamolodchikov.
