Double beta decay with the NEMO experiment: status of the NEMO 3 detector
Abstract The NEMO 3 detector, now mounting in the Frejus underground laboratory, is devoted to search for neutrinoless double beta decay and will be able to reach the sensitivity to the effective neutrino mass ( m ν >) in the order of 0.1 eV. The expected performance of the detector for signal detection and both internal and external background rejections are presented. A specific study of the neutron induced background is given.
