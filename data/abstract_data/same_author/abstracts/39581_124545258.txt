The Borexino experiment: Test of the purification systems and data analysis in the counting test facility
The work I carried out in the last three years was focused on the development and test of purification systems and data analysis techniques for the Borexino experiment, a detector specifically designed for low energy neutrino spectroscopy. I participated to the study of the adsorption technique based on activated carbons for removing krypton from nitrogen and to the investigation of the purity of nitrogen available on the market. I analyzed the data from the third campaign of the Borexino prototype detector, the Counting Test Facility (CTF), to identify each contamination source in the scintillator and to evaluate the efficiency of the purification system. 11C represents the main background source in the CNO and pep neutrino energy range. I investigated on the possibility to apply the three-fold coincidence technique with the parent muon track and the subsequent neutron capture on protons in Borexino in order to tag and remove 11C nuclides on a one-by-one basis. Furthermore, I implemented and tested such technique on the CTF data.
