Zeros of QCD partition function from finite density lattices
Email: xjin@riken.jpPartition function zeros steer the critical behavior of a system. Studying four-ﬂavor lattice QCDat ﬁnite temperature and density with the Wilson-clover fermion action and the Iwasaki gauge ac-tion using a phase-quenched fermion determinant, we combine statistics from multiple chemicalpotentials to improve sampling of the conﬁguration space, and aim at unraveling the movementof zeros in ﬁnite systems. Preparing for further investigations, we discuss methods and criteriaused to sieve through complex parameter space spanned by (Rem;Imm) and (Rem;Imb), andpresent statistically robust zeros of the partition function.31st International Symposium on Lattice Field Theory LATTICE 2013July 29 – August 3, 2013Mainz, Germany
