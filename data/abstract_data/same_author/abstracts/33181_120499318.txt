Role of nuclear binding in the EMC effect
Abstract We reexamine the role of nuclear binding in the EMC effect. With the correct average single-particle energy and the proper light-cone normalization of the vertex functions, it is shown that the nuclear binding and the Fermi motion in the convolution model of the nucleons account for about 20% of the EMC effect in the mid- x region. Q 2 - and x -rescaling are used to make up the difference.
