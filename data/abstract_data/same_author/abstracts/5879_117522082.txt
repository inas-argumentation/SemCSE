First results on dilepton production at the Bevalac
We report on preliminary results of direct electron pair measurements in p+Be at 4.9 GeV and 2.1 GeV and Ca+Ca at 1.95 GeVA collisions at the Bevalac. The results are compared to existing data in p+Be at 12.1 GeV and ..pi../sup -/p at 15.9 and 16.9 GeV.
