What does the CMS measurement of W-polarization tell us about the underlying theory of the coupling of W-bosons to matter?
A bstractWe discuss results of the CMS collaboration on the sensitivity of the LHC to W boson polarisation in the process pp → W± + jet → e±jet+ using the LP variable directly connected to θ∗ angle of the outgoing lepton in the rest frame of the decaying W. We have shown that for a given LP, interference between different polarizations of the W- boson is not negligible, and needs to be taken into account when considering the differential cross-section with respect to LP. The LP variable suggested by CMS collaboration is highly suitable variable to study LHC sensitivity to gV, gA couplings of W -boson to fermions. We note that the experimental sensitivity to W-boson polarization which is much higher than that to (gV, gA) parameter space can be turned around and used to identify deviations from the Standard Model as a signal for new physics at the LHC.
