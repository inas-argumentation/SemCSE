A Constraint on EHNS Parameters from Solar Neutrino Problem
We suppose that the solar neutrino problem is only due to the mechanism introduced by Ellis, Hagelin, Nanopoulos and Srednicki (EHNS), then a lower limit constraint on EHNS parameters is obtained. We find that γ ≥ 7.4 × 10−22 GeV if α < 2 γ and α ≥ 1.5 × 10−21 GeV if α > 2 γ, this limit is consistent with the upper limits extract from the K0 − K0 system and can be detected in the future experiments.
