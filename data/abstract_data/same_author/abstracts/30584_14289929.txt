Effect of charged scalar loops on photonic decays of a fermiophobic Higgs boson
Higgs bosons with very suppressed couplings to fermions ('fermiophobic Higgs bosons', h{sub f}) can decay to two photons ({gamma}{gamma}) with a branching ratio significantly larger than that expected for the standard model Higgs boson for m{sub h{sub f}}<150 GeV. Such a particle would give a clear signal at the LHC and can arise in the two Higgs doublet model (type I) in which h{sub f}{yields}{gamma}{gamma} is mediated by W{sup {+-}} and charged Higgs boson (H{sup {+-}}) loops. We show that the H{sup {+-}} loops can cause both constructive and destructive contributions with a magnitude considerably larger than the anticipated precision in the measurement of the photonic decay channel at future hadron and lepton colliders.
