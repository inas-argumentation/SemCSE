On the behavior of the adjoint Wilson line and susceptibility around deconfinement in SU (2) lattice gauge theory
Abstract The Wilson line L a and the susceptibility χ a in the adjoint representation are investigated in SU (2) lattice gauge theory around deconfinement. Our simulations are performed on N 3 σ ×6 lattices with N σ =8,12,18,24. In the region close to the deconfinement temperature for T → T c + we find that the behavior of the adjoint Wilson line is consistent with the predictions of (o 4 ) 3 field theory. The adjoint susceptibility is shown to be a smoothly increasing function around the deconfinement temperature. Thus χ a does not show the critical fluctuations.
