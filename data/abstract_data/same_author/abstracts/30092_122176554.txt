S-matrices for perturbations of certain conformal field theories
We present a family of factorizable S -matrix theories in 1+1 dimensions with an arbitrary number N of particles of distinct masses, and find the conservation laws of these theories. An analysis of the conservation laws of the family of nonunitary CFTs with central charge c = c 2,2 N +3 = −2 N (6 N +5)/(2 N +3) perturbed by the φ (1,3) operator, leads us to conjecture the identification of these perturbed CFTs with the S -matrix theories we found. The case N =1 was treated by Cardy and Mussardo. We also present the S -matrix of an E 7 -related unitary model.
