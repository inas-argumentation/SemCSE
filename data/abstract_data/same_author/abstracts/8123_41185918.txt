Anomalous electromagnetic moments of the W boson in left-right symmetric models.
We calculate the contribution from left-right symmetric models to the anomalous magnetic dipole ({Delta}{kappa}) and quadrupole ({Delta}{ital Q}) moments of the light {ital W} gauge boson. We obtain that both {Delta}{kappa} and {Delta}{ital Q} are suppressed by the small mixing angle sin{zeta} with respect to the standard model result. {copyright} {ital 1996 The American Physical Society.}
