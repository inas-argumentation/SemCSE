Non-perturbative corrections to one-gluon exchange quark potentials
Abstract The leading non-perturbative QCD corrections to the one-gluon exchange quark-quark, quark-antiquark and q q pair-excitation potentials are derived by using a covariant form of non-local two-quark and two-gluon vacuum expectation values. Our numerical calculation indicates that the correction of quark and gluon condensates to the quark-antiquark potential improves the heavy quarkonium spectra to some degree.
