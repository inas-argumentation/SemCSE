An experimental system for detection of high energy cosmic gamma ray bursts
Abstract While bursts of cosmic gamma rays of energies 0.1–10 MeV can be detected and studied only with satellite-borne detectors, bursts of gamma rays at GeV energies can be detected using sensitive ground-based detectors. An experimental system to detect these high energy gamma ray bursts is being operated at the mountain altitude laboratory at Ootacamund, India. The design features and the experimental details related to this system are described.
