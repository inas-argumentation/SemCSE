Lepton-number-violating decays of B+, D+, and D+s mesons induced by the doubly charged Higgs boson
The lepton-number-violating decays of B+, D+, and D-s(+) mesons induced by the doubly charged Higgs boson have been studied. It is found that although the yielded results of the branch ratio are much smaller than the present limits from the data, they are consistent with the previous conclusions calculated in the framework of the relativistic quark model where the processes happened via light Majorana neutrinos.
