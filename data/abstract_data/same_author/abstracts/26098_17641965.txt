Search for Missing Charmonium States in B-meson Decays
The recent progress in experiments at B-factories suggests an opportunity to search for the missing charmonium states η c(2 S0) and hc(1 P1). The feasibility of such a search in B-meson decays are discussed. PACS: 14.40.Gx, 13.25.Hw, 13.25.Gv, 13.40.Hq ∗Email: guyf@mail.ihep.ac.cn
