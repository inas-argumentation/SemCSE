OUT OF EQUILIBRIUM PHASE TRANSITIONS AND A TOY MODEL FOR DISORIENTED CHIRAL CONDENSATES
We study the dynamics of a second order phase transition in a situation that mimics a sudden quench to a temperature below the critical temperature in a model with dynamical symmetry breaking. In particular we show that the domains of correlated values of the condensate grow as $\sqrt{t}$ and that this result seems to be largely model-independent.
