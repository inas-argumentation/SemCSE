High pT Spectra of Identified Particles Produced in Pb+Pb Collisions at 158 GeV/nucleon Beam Energy
Transverse momentum spectra of π ± , p , p ¯ , K ± , K s 0 and Λ at midrapidity were measured at high p T in Pb+Pb collisions at 158 GeV/nucleon beam energy by the NA49 experiment. Particle yield ratios ( p / π , K / π and Λ / K s 0 ) show an enhancement of the baryon/meson ratio for p T > 2 GeV / c . The nuclear modification factor R CP is extracted and compared to RHIC measurements and pQCD calculations.
