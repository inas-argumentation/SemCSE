A Molecular Model for High-Spin Heavy-Ion Resonances
A new molecular model is presented in detail, in which collective motions of the di-nuclear system are described in the rotating molecular frame. The model is applied to high-spin resonances observed in 24 Mg + 24 Mg scattering. A stable configuration of the system is found to be a pole-pole one, due to the prolate shape of the 24 Mg nuclei. Normal modes at the equilibrium are solved to give rise to various molecular levels, which appear to be in good correspondence with the experiment
