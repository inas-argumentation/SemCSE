POLARIZED CROSS-SECTIONS FOR e+ e- ---> W- lepton-sneutrino slepton+
We investigate the single W production associated with sleptons in longitudinally polarized e+e- beams. The polarized cross-sections and the angular distributions of W- are calculated for charged sleptons in left-handed and right-handed multiplets. It is shown that this process will be major background one for the standard one in the single W production at √s = 1 TeV.
