Some Rigourous Results on Potential Models of Hadrons
I shall present here two completely distinct contributions to the spectroscopy of hadrons in the framework of potential models. Both are rigorous results, which, in principle, can be applied to other fields of physics. The first one concerns the order of levels in two-body systems bound by a potential. The second one concerns “Regge” trajectories, i.e., angular excitations of two-or-three-body systems.
