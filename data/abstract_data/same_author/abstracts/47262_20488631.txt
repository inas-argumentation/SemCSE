Improved time and space bounds for Boolean matrix multiplication
SummaryUsing modular arithmetic we obtain the following improved bounds on the time and space complexities for n × n Boolean matrix multiplication: O(nlog27 lognlogloglognloglogloglogn) bit operations and O(n2loglog n) bits of storage on a logarithmic cost RAM having no multiply or divide instruction; O(nlog27(logn)2−1/2log27(loglog n)1/2log27−1) bit operations and O(n2log n) bits of storage on a RAM which can use indirect addressing for table lookups. The first algorithm can be realized as a Boolean circuit with O(nlog27lognlogloglognloglogloglogn) gates. Whenever n×n arithmetic matrix multiplication can be performed in less than O(nlog27) arithmetic operations, our results have corresponding improvements.
