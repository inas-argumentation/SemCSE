An SU(2) ⊗ U(1) model with massive neutrinos and CP violation in the leptonic sector
Abstract We examine an SU (2) × U (1) model incorporating massive neutrinos and CP violation for the leptons. The model has Dirac and Majorana mass terms for the neutrinos and two doublets and one triplet of Higgs bosons. We consider the implications of our model for the neutrino oscillations and for the electric dipole moment of the leptons.
