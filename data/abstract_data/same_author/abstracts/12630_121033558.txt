Dependence of shower particle pseudorapidity and pseudorapidity gap distributions on multiplicity in high-energy neutrino-emulsion collisions
The pseudorapidity and pseudorapidity gap distributions of shower particles in different event groups selected by multiplicity in 3\char21{}210 GeV neutrino-emulsion collisions are reported and analyzed by the thermalized cylinder model. It is shown that the pseudorapidity distribution has a slight dependence on the multiplicity and the pseudorapidity gap distribution does not depend on the multiplicity. The calculated results by the thermalized cylinder model are compared and found to be in agreement with the experimental data.
