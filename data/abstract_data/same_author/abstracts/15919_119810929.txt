Plane walls interacting with gravitational waves and matter fields
Plane walls with reflection symmetry are studied. A method for generating such solutions to the Einstein equations is developed. It is found that plane wall solutions require very strong restrictions on the metric coefficients and on their derivatives. Using the generalized Bianchi identities, the interaction of plane walls with gravitational waves and matter fields is investigated. In particular, it is shown that a plane wall, in general, interacts with both gravitational plane waves and matter fields. But, a plane domain wall interacts only with matter fields. No matter whether the space‐time out of the wall is curved or not, a plane domain wall is always repulsive.
