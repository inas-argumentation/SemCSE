Bounds on Heavy-to-Heavy Mesonic Form Factors
We provide upper and lower bounds on the form factors for B → D,D by utilizing inclusive heavy quark effective theory sum rules. These bounds are calculated to leading order in ΛQCD/mQ and αs. The O(α 2 sβ0) corrections to the bounds at zero recoil are also presented. We compare our bounds with some of the form factor models used in the literature.
