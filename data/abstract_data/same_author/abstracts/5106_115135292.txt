Resummation of jet-veto logarithms in hadronic processes containing jets
We derive a factorization theorem for production of an arbitrary number of colorsinglet particles accompanied by a xed number of jets at the LHC. The jets are dened with the standard antikT algorithm, and the xed number of jets is obtained by imposing a veto on additional radiation in the nal state. The formalism presented here is useful for current Higgs boson analyses using exclusive jet bins, and for other studies using a similar strategy. The derivation uses the soft-collinear eective theory and assumes that the transverse momenta of the hard jets are larger than the veto scale. We resum the large Sudakov logarithms n log 2n m p J =p veto up to the next-toleading-logarithmic accuracy, and present numerical results for Higgs boson production in association with a jet at the LHC. We comment on the experimentally-interesting parameter region in which we expect our factorization formula to hold.
