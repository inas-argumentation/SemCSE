LOOP QUANTUM GRAVITY AND ULTRA HIGH ENERGY COSMIC RAYS
A device for introducing particles into an analyzer of dustiness of a gaseous medium comprises a cell having a transparent cover with a microscope arranged thereabove. A through guide duct is made in a wall of the cell, while provided in the bottom of the latter are a vertical feed duct communicating with the dustiness analyzer and a horizontal channel which is an extension of the through guide duct and intersects the vertical feed duct. The device also comprises a source of a pure gaseous medium which is fed under pressure into the through guide duct wherewith the source communicates.
