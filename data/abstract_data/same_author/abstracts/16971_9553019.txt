IRAC Imaging of Lockman Hole
IRAC imaging of a 4.7 × 4.7 area in the Lockman Hole detected over 400 galaxies in the IRAC 3.6μm and 4.5μm bands, 120 in the 5.8μm band, and 80 in the 8.0μm band in 30 minutes of observing time. Color-color diagrams suggest that about half of these galaxies are at redshifts 0.6 < z < 1.3 with about a quarter at higher redshifts (z >1.3). We also detect IRAC counterparts for 6 of the 7 SCUBA sources and all 9 XMM sources in this area. The detection of the counterparts of the SCUBA sources and galaxies at z > 1.3 demonstrates the ability of IRAC to probe the universe at very high redshifts.
