Elliptic Flow and Shear Viscosity in a Parton Cascade Approach
Within a parton cascade approach we investigate the scaling of the differential elliptic flow v2(pT ) with eccentricity ex and system size and its sensitivity to finite shear viscosity. A central issue we address here is the relation between v2(pT )/ex and v2(pT )/〈v2〉 scalings, as raised by recent experimental observations. We show that, with the inclusion of a freeze out condition, a cascade approach for a plasma at finite η/s can supply a reliable tool for the description of several observables on elliptic flow such as the breaking of v2(pT )/ex scaling and the persistence of v2(pT )/〈v2〉 scaling. Results are moreover compatible with a coalescence plus fragmentation hadronization mechanism acting at intermediate pT .
