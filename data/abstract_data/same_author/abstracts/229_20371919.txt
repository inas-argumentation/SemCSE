An Insight Into User Privacy and Accountable Anonymity for Mobile E-Commerce Transactions
: In this paper the need to develop an accountable anonymity scheme specifically for mobile e-commerce is established. The requirements of such a scheme are identified and shortfalls in existing anonymity services are identified. A novel approach based on the concept of unlinking processes to prevent any meaningful collation of information on a particular on-line identity being performed is discussed. We then propose (in outline) an anonymity scheme based on this concept that will then be extended to provide accountability through on-line revocation.
