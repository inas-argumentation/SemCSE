Charmless B decays to a scalar meson and a vector meson
The hadronic charmless B decays into a scalar meson and a vector meson are studied within the framework of QCD factorization. The main results are: (i) The decay rates for the f{sub 0}(980)K*{sup -} and f{sub 0}(980)K*{sup 0} modes depend on the f{sub 0}-{sigma} mixing angle {theta}. (ii) If the a{sub 0}(980) is a qq bound state, the predicted branching ratios for the color-allowed channels a{sub 0}{sup +}{rho}{sup -} and a{sub 0}{sup 0}{rho}{sup -} will be very large, of order 28x10{sup -6} and 21x10{sup -6}, respectively. (iii) For the a{sub 0}(1450) channels, the color-allowed modes a{sub 0}{sup +}(1450){rho}{sup -} and a{sub 0}{sup 0}(1450){rho}{sup -} are found to have branching ratios of order 20x10{sup -6} and 30x10{sup -6}, respectively. A measurement of them at the predicted level will favor the qq structure for the a{sub 0}(1450). (iv) Contrary to the naive expectation that {gamma}(B{sup -}{yields}a{sub 0}{sup 0}{rho}{sup -}){approx}(1/2){gamma}(B{sup 0}{yields}a{sub 0}{sup +}{rho}{sup -}), we found that this naive relation is violated especially for a{sub 0}=a{sub 0}(1450) due to additional contributions to the a{sub 0}{sup 0}{rho}{sup -} mode from the a{sub 0}{sup 0} emission.
