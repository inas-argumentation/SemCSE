Automorphism groups of tetravalent Cayley graphs on regular p-groups
Let Cay(G,S) be a connected tetravalent Cayley graph on a regular p-group G and let Aut(G) be the automorphism group of G. This implies that if p 2,5 then the Cayley graph Cay(G,S) is normal, namely, the automorphism group of Cay(G,S) contains R(G) as a normal subgroup.
