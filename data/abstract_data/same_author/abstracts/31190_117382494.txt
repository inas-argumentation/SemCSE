The Higgs sector of the 4DCHM after the XLVIII Rencontres de Moriond
In this proceeding, we present the current status of a $\chi^2$ fit extracted from the profiling of the Higgs couplings performed at the LHC in the context of the 4-Dimensional Composite Higgs Model. Especially, we consider the data presented by the ATLAS and CMS collaborations during the XLVIII Rencontres de Moriond.
