Breakdown of perturbation theory at tree level in theories with scalars
It is shown that the cross section for q q →n Higgs in the standard model becomes non-perturbative in tree approximation at CM energies and multiplicities approximately given by √ s 100–300 TeV 2 /m H , nm H /√ s 2 3 . A similar result ho lds in the standard SUSY model with two doublets, and hence is not related to triviality. The large tree amplitudes are shown to imply that the overlap of a high energy incoming q q state with a classical scalar field configuration Hc(x) will be unsuppressed as long as |Hc (0)|≳ mH/ √λ, where λ is the quartic coupling in the Weinberg-Salam model. It is possible that this overlap can be become large for parton CM energies available at SSC.
