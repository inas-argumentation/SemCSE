Dynamic tool integration in heterogeneous computer networks
Tool installation and automation of administrative tasks in heterogeneous computer networks becomes of increasing importance with the availability of complex heterogeneous computer networks. This paper introduces a new approach for dynamic network tool management, i.e., TRMS. A variant of TRMS using SNMP-a well established standard for network administration-is outlined and illustrated by the application of the integration and management of design tools for Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs).
