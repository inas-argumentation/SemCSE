Lepton flavor violation in SUSY SO(10) with predictive Yukawa texture.
We analyze the scalar lepton mass matrices in a supersymmetric SO(10) grand unified model with soft SUSY-breaking terms generated at the Planck scale and a Georgi-Jarlskog Yukawa texture at the GUT scale induced by higher dimensional operators. This model predicts lepton flavor violation. The predictive features of the Georgi-Jarlskog texture are used to estimate branching ratios for the radiative decays {ital e}{sub {ital a}}{r_arrow}{ital e}{sub {ital b}}+{gamma}, and we find rates that could provide an experimental test for this kind of model. {copyright} {ital 1996 The American Physical Society.}
