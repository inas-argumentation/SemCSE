Mini review on saturation and recent developments
I discuss the saturation regime of QCD: the weak-coupling regime that describes large parton densities inside hadrons and the resulting high-energy behavior of scattering amplitudes. I briefly review past successes in the context of deep inelastic scattering at HERA and forward particle production at RHIC, and I present some recent progresses that significantly improved our understanding of high-energy scattering in QCD.
