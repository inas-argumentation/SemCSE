Young diagrams, Brauer algebras, and bubbling geometries
A bstractWe study the 1/4 BPS geometries corresponding to the 1/4 BPS operators of the dual gauge theory side, in $ \mathcal{N} = {4} $ SYM. By analyzing asymptotic structure and flux integration of the geometries, we present a mapping between droplet configurations arising from the geometries and Young diagrams of the Brauer algebra. In particular, the integer k classifying the operators in the Brauer basis is mapped to the mixing between the two angular directions.
