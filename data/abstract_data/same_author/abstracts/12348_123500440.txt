Solution of the Schwinger-Dyson Equation Involving the Gluon condensate Kernel*
The form of the quantum chromodynamic Schwinger-Dyson (SD) equation modified by the nonperturbative kernel associated with the gluon condensate is obtained. It is shown that the solution for the renormalization group invariant quark condensate from the SD equation is in agreement with that obtained from the consistent equation for quark condensate when the leading behavior of the dynamical quark mass is concerned. It is also found that the gluon condensate kernel makes the critical coupling constant above which the chiral symmetry breaks smaller and increases the value of the quark condensate.
