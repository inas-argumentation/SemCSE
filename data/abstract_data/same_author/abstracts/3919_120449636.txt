The new Bonn frozen spin target for experiments with real photons
At the new Bonn Electron Stretcher Accelerator ELSA scattering experiments with real photons in the single pion photoproduction γ N → π N and in the photodisintegration of the deuteron γ N pn are prepared. To determine these processes completely, it is nessesary to measure single and double polarisation observables. For these experiments a new’bonn frozen spin target’ BOFROST was developed. The target with a maximum volume of 30 cm3 is cooled in a vertical 3He/4He — dilution refrigerator. In the’frozen spin mode’ a lowest temperature of 64 mK was reached.
