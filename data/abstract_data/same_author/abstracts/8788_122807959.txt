The distribution in transverse momentum of 5 GeV/c secondaries produced at 53 GeV in the centre of mass
Data are reported on the distribution in transverse momentum of 5 GeV/c π±, K±, p and p, produced in pro ton proton collisions at 53 GeV centre of mass energy at the CERN ISR. At this energy the magnitude and pT dependence of the invariant cross-section appears to be approximately equal to that at 19 GeV accelerator energy (at the same value of the Feynman variable x), for π± and K+ in the range 0.15 < pT < 0.95 GeV/c.
