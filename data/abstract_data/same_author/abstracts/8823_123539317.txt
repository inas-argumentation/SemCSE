High precision spectrometers for very forward protons in CMS
We plan to add proton tracking and timing detectors at z = 240-250 m on both sides of CMS to study central exclusive production, with one or both protons measured, and single diffraction. They provide measurements of p + p → p + X + p, where X = Z,H,W+W− and multiparticle states (with or without jets), as well as single high mass diffraction in low pile-up runs.
