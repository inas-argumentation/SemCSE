Extracting Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa elements in Bc semileptonic decays and heavy quark spin symmetry.
We discuss the extraction of \ensuremath{\Vert}${\mathit{V}}_{\mathit{c}\mathit{s}}$/${\mathit{V}}_{\mathit{c}\mathit{d}}$\ensuremath{\Vert},\ensuremath{\Vert}${\mathit{V}}_{\mathit{c}\mathit{s}}$/${\mathit{V}}_{\mathit{u}\mathit{b}}$\ensuremath{\Vert}, and \ensuremath{\Vert}${\mathit{V}}_{\mathit{c}\mathit{d}}$/${\mathit{V}}_{\mathit{u}\mathit{b}}$\ensuremath{\Vert} from semileptonic ${\mathit{B}}_{\mathit{c}}$ decay in the heavy quark limit. The first corrections to the corresponding ratios of the differential rates of ${\mathit{B}}_{\mathit{c}}$ semileptonic decays are calculated in the zero recoil point within HQET combined with the results of QCD sum rules and chiral perturbation theory, and within the quark model. Special attention is paid to minimize the theoretical uncertainties in the procedure of extracting the ratios. We find many uncertainties can be canceled naturally in the leading level. The ratios also are insensitive to the QCD energy scale \ensuremath{\mu} in the next-to-leading level. The result is potentially important for extracting CKM elements from future measurements at the CERN LHC.
