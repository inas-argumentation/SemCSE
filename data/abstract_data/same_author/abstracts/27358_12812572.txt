Towards Holographic Gravity Dual of N=1 Superconformal Chern-Simons Gauge Theory
As we re-examine the known holographic = 1 supersymmetric renormalization group flow in four dimensions, we describe the mass-deformed Bagger-Lambert theory or equivalently the mass-deformed U(2) × U(2) Chern-Simons gauge theory with level k = 1 or 2, that has G2 symmetry, by adding a mass term for one of the eight adjoint superfields. We obtain a detailed correspondence between the fields of AdS4 supergravity and composite operators of the infrared field theory in three dimensions. The geometric superpotential from an eleven dimensional viewpoint is obtained for M2-brane probe analysis.
