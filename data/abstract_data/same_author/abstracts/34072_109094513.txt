Development of a superconducting cavity for high gradient accelerators
A collaboration between KEK and CEBAF is pursuing the development of a superconducting high gradient accelerating structure for application in future accelerator which optimally requires the high field gradient above 25 MV/m. In the first phase of this collaboration three single cell 1300 MHz niobium cavities have been built and tested. In the second phase a full scale 9-cell niobium cavities was manufactured. This paper describes the fabrication and measurement result of the joint 9-cell Nb cavity.
