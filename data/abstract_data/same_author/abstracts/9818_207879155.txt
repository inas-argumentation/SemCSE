Isospin purity of T = 1 states in the A = 38 nuclei studied via lifetime measurements in 38
The Doppler Shift Attenuation Method has been used to measure lifetimes for levels in K at excitation energies of 1698, 2404, 2830, 2996 and 3671 keV, populated using the Ca(d,α)K reaction at a beam energy of 4.5 MeV. Values of 109(29), 95(22), 457(63), 130(40) and 160(50) fs respectively were measured and are compared with previous values obtained using different stopping powers. The matrix element for the transition between the Jπ = 2+T=1 and 0 + T=1 states in this Tz=0 nucleus is compared with the analogous transition in the other nuclei in the T=1 triplet, Ca (Tz=-1) and Ar (Tz=+1), and with the results of shell-model calculations.
