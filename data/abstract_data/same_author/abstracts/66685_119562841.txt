On integral Stark conjectures (and multiplicative Galois module structures).
In [4] Fröhlich investigates the interplay between the values of Artin L-functions at zero and the Galois module structure of multiplicative unit groups of algebraic number fields. By means of a new integral regulator he demonstrates that results on the behaviour of L-values at zero can, via the Hom-description of locally-free class groups, translate into explicit results on the module structure of S-unit groups, an area in which previously there had been no general results, or indeed any systematic technique of investigation. The results obtained in this way are strikingly parallel, both in Statement and in proof, to results concerning Galois module structures of integers in wildly ramified Galois extensions. However, whilst the additive results of [4] are valid in complete generality the results on the behaviour of L-values at zero are restricted to L-functions associated to complex representations of real absolutely abelian number fields, or to rationally defined representations of arbitrary finite abelian Galois extensions. Our aim in this paper is to extend these results to include the case of L-functions attached to complex representations of finite abelian extensions of imaginary quadratic fields. Our proof for this case combines the known validity of the Strong Stark Conjecture for such L-functions with the recent beautiful method of Kolyvagin (äs presented by Rubin in for example [8] and [9]) which gives bounds for the Orders of eigenspaces of class groups of abelian extensions of imaginary quadratic fields in terms of so called 'special units' of the extension fields.
