Mean, median and mode of hadron spectrum in QCD jets☆
We present predictions of the three common statistical measures of the “typical” value of the quantity ξ=ln(1/x) where x is the momentum fraction of a hadron in a QCD jet. Although the distribution of ξ is asymptotically gaussian, its mean, median and mode (or peak) should differ by constants plus corrections of order αs. An experimental study of these quantities would provide a detailed test of the underlying QCD dynamics of jet fragmentation.
