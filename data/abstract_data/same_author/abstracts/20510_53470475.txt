Implications of dynamical generation of standard-model fermion masses
We point out that if quark and lepton masses arise dynamically, then in a wide class of theories the corresponding running masses m{sub f{sub j}}(p) exhibit the power-law decay m{sub f{sub j}}(p){proportional_to}{lambda}{sub j}{sup 2}/p{sup 2} for Euclidean momenta p>>{lambda}{sub j}, where f{sub j} is a fermion of generation j, and {lambda}{sub j} is the maximal scale relevant for the origin of m{sub f{sub j}}. We estimate resultant changes in precision electroweak quantities and compare with current data. It is found that these data allow the presence of such corrections. We also note that this power-law decay renders primitively divergent fermion mass corrections finite.
