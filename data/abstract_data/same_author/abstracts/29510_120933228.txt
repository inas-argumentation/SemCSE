Free-field formulation of topological supergravity
Abstract Two-dimensional topological supergravity is taken to be defined by topological OSP(2, 1) Yang-Mills gauge theory. A free-field formulation is achieved by choosing a suitable contraction of the gauge group. This formulation enables us to represent explicitly the complete det of BRST invariant local operators. Because of an inadequate understanding of supermoduli space, the physical content of the theory remains to be fully explored.
