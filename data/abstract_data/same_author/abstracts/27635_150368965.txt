LONGITUDINAL BEAM INSTABILITY OBSERVATION WITH STREAK CAMERA AT SRRC
The longitudinal beam instability of the 1.3 GeV TLS storage ring at SRRC was investigated by using a HAMAMATSU C5680 streak camera. The single bunch length as a function of beam current was measured and a longitudinal broad band impedance was deduced. The longitudinal beam motions in the case of single bunch, two bunches and a few bunches were studied. A HAMAMATSU PLP-02 pico second pulse laser had been used to calibrate the streak camera and the results are presented.
