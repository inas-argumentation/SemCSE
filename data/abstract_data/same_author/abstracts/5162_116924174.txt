Search for new strange resonances in inclusive charged kaon spectra from p̄p annihilation at rest
Charged spectra from pp annihilations at rest have been measured using the PS183 spectrometer at LEAR. From these data, charged kaon spectra are produced using time‐of‐flight separation. The spectra show structures corresponding to annihilation into the K±K−+ and K*±K−+ channels. A search for structures indicating other resonances is discussed.
