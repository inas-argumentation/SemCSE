COMMISSIONING OF 360 mA TOP-UP OPERATION AT TLS
Taiwan light source (TLS) started the 200 mA top-up operation in October [1], 2005, and subsequently, the stored top-up beam current was raised gradually to 300 mA in November, 2005. Several machine issues were encountered and have been solved in the past three years. Presently, the TLS is performing routinely as user mode operation. However, due to the increasing need of higher photon intensity from the users, it is proposed to explore the possibility of raising the top-up operation to 360mA. This beam-test study has been performed recently. The experimental results show that the TLS is capable of carrying out 360mA top-up operation.
