Light front treatment of the nucleus: Implications for deep inelastic scattering
A light front treatment of the nuclear wave function is developed and applied, using the mean field approximation, to infinite nuclear matter. The nuclear mesons are shown to carry about a third of the nuclear plus momentum p{sup +}; but their momentum distribution has support only at p{sup +}=0, and the mesons do not contribute to nuclear deep inelastic scattering. This zero mode effect occurs because the meson fields are independent of space-time position. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
