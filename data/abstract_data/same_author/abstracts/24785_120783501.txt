Constraints on the decay rates of quarkonium
Abstract We derive inequalities between the leptonic decay rates of 1S and 2S states of quarkonium, when the binding potential is an increasing concave (convex) function of the inter-quark distance in a framework where some relativistic corrections have been made to the Van Royen-Weisskopf formula for these rates. Experimental decay rates of the γ and γ ′ rule out the convex increasing potential.
