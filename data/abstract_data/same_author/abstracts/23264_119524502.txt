Jet signatures of scalar diquarks from superstrings at e−e+ colliders
We determine the 4-jet contribution in e − e + collisions of heavy scalar diquarks from E 6 compactification in superstring theories. For CM energy √ s >150 GeV the signal exceeds the QCD background (for √ s >200 Gev by an order of magnitude or more). At the Z 0 peak the background somewhat exceeds the signal, but we show that measured cross sections are sensitive to this contribution for diquark masses up to ∼ 45 GeV.
