Fast electric dipole transitions in nuclei near N=82.
Doppler-shift attenuation measurements following the inelastic neutron scattering reaction have been employed to determine the lifetimes of a large number of states in {sup 146}Nd, and many fast {ital E}1 transitions have been identified. From an examination of this new information and similar data for two {ital N}=82 nuclei, ambiguities in determining the degree of nuclear collectivity from reduced {ital E}1 transition rates are illustrated.
