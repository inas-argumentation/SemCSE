Heavy-element abundances and massive star formation
We investigate the observational consequences that arise for the heavy-element abundances in normal spiral galaxies from variations in the initial mass function (IMF). In particular, we calculate the oxygen yield for a given IMF, using the most recent calculations of supernova nucleosynthesis. Assuming instantaneous recycling of oxygen, we calculate the resultant oxygen abundance. The predicted and observed oxygen abundances in our Galaxy are compared, and we find that the overall abundance is well explained by the IMF determined in the solar neighborhood
