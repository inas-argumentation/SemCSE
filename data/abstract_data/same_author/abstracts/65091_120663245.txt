Bounds, Inequalities, and Monotonicity Properties for Some Specialized Renewal Processes
Renewal processes with increasing mean residual life and decreasing failure rate interarrival time distributions are investigated. Various two-sided bounds are obtained for M(t), the expected number of renewals in [0, t].
