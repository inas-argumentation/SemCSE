The Top Forward Backward Asymmetry with general Zcouplings
The measurement of the top forward-backward asymmetry in tproduction measured at the Tevatron shows deviation from the Standard Model prediction. A u → t transition via a flavor changing Zcan explain the data. We show that left handed tLuLZcouplings can be constrained from Bd,s mixing while the constrains on the right handed couplings tRuRZvanish in the limit of mu → 0. We then consider the most general form of the tuZinteraction which includes vector-axial vector as well as tensor type couplings and study how these couplings affect the top forward-backward asymmetry.
