Isobars, transition potentials and short-range repulsion in the nucleon-nucleon interaction
Abstract A chiral dynamic model of πN scattering, including N and Δ(1230) intermediate states, is constructed and shown to be in agreement with empirical amplitudes for NN → ππ in the pseudophysical region. With this model we calculate the Δ(1230) contribution to the NN interaction and indicate the importance of explicitly including pion “crossed-box” processes which simplify the isospin structure of the resulting NN interaction. We show that inconsistent nonrelativitic approximations in the coupled-channels approach lead to a serious overestimate of pion box diagrams. We show that ρ-meson exchange together with intermediate isobars leads to a short-range repulsion which may be described as an effective ω-meson exchange with G ω 2 4π = 5–8 .
