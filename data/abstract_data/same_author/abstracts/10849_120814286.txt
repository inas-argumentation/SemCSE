The Transverse Momentum Distribution of {Drell-Yan} Pairs in Perturbation Theory
We calculate, in lowest-order perturbation theory, the cumulative transverse momentum cross section for the Drell-Yan process in the regime m 2 ⪡ p T 2 ⪡ q 2 , keeping both single and double logarithmic terms. We verify that this distribution takes the proposed form of a product of structure functions evaluated at p T 2 and the square of the Sudakov form factor S ( p T 2 / q 2 ), evaluated to subleading order.
