Minimal models of U(1) conformal current algebra
A detailed study of the degenerate representations of the U(1)×U(1) conformal current algebra and field‐theoretical models based on it is presented. The main peculiarity of these models is that they contain an infinite set of fields with unfixed anomalous dimensions. Simple fusion rules for these fields are found, and their four‐point functions are calculated explicitly. The case of Thirring‐like two‐dimensional models is discussed in detail.
