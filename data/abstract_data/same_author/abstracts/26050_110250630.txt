Results of the Cryogenic Testing of the SNS Prototype Cryomodule
Jefferson Lab has developed a prototype of the medium beta SNS cryomodule. Tests were recently performed on the module, which includes three 805 MHz cavities of β=0.61, with coaxial power couplers and frequency tuners (mechanical and piezoelectric). The cavities exceeded accelerating gradients of 16 MV/m (design value 10.5 MV/m) with Qo’s of about 10 10 at the design field. One of the power couplers has been tested up to peak powers of over 700 kW. Results of the tests are reported in this paper. 1E+09 1E+10 1E+11
