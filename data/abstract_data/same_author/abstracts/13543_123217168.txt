Contribution of Space-like Intervals to the S-Matrix
It is proved that the usual asymptotic condition in the theory of quantized fields can be written down in a form which is equivalent to the statement that the space-like regions outside the cones of family (l) do not contribute to the S-Matrix. The asymptotic condition thus formulated makes it possible to set up a relativistically invariant scattering formalism for nonlocal fields which is analogous to the local field theory formalism.
