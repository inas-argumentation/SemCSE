Evolution in opening angle combining DGLAP and BFKL logarithms
We present an evolution equation which simultaneously sums the leading BFKL and DGLAP logarithms for the integrated gluon distribution in terms of a single variable, namely the emission angle of the gluon. This form of evolution is appropriate for Monte Carlo simulations of events of high energy $$pp$$pp (and $$p\bar{p}$$pp¯) interactions, particularly where small $$x$$x events are sampled.
