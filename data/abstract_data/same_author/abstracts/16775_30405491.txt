Neutral pion decay into nu anti-nu in dense skyrmion matter
We study the weak decay of the neutral pion to a neutrino-antineutrino pair, $\pi^0\to \nu \bar{\nu}$, in the Skyrme model. In baryon free-space the process is forbidden by helicity while in a dense baryonic medium, the process becomes possible already to leading order in $G_F$ due to the break-down of Lorentz symmetry in the background medium.
