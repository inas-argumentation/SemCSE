Observation of Upsilon, Upsilon', and Upsilon'' at the Cornell electron Storage Ring
The UPSILON, UPSILON', and UPSILON'' states have been observed at the Cornell Electron Storage Ring as narrow peaks in sigma(e/sup +/e/sup -/ ..-->.. hadrons) versus beam energy. Data were collected during a run of 1000 nb/sup -1/, using the Columbia University--Stony Brook segmented NaI detector. The measured mass differences are M(UPSILON')-M(UPSILON)=559 +- 1( +- 3) MeV and M(UPSILON'')-M(UPSILON)=889 +- 1( +- 5) MeV, where the errors in parentheses represent systematic uncertainties. Preliminary values for the leptonic width ratios were also obtained.
