The algebra of flat currents for the string on AdS5 × S5 in the light-cone gauge
We continue the program initiated in [41] and calculate the algebra of the flat currents for the string on AdS5 × S5 background in the light-cone gauge with κ symmetry fixed. We find that the algebra has a closed form and that the non-ultralocal terms come with a weight factor e(σ) that depends on the radial AdS5 coordinate. Based on results in two-dimensional sigma models coupled to gravity via the dilaton field, this suggests that the algebra of transition matrices in the present case is likely to be unambigous.
