Regge trajectories of baryons in the MIT bag model
Abstract We investigate Regge trajectories of baryons in the MIT bag model. It is shown that the energy spectra shown by the trajectories can be described fairly well by the stringlike solutions of the bag model. In particular, three strings with one junction and three ends can explain the delta trajectory better than one string with two ends. The three ends occupied by each quark are assumed to be an equilateral triangle configuration in our calculations.
