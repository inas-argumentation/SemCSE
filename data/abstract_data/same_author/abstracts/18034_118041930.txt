Total and elastic pp cross sections at RHIC
We have examined the feasibility of studying elastic proton-proton (pp) scattering {radical}s = 500 GeV, in the small angle scattering hall at RHIC. The requirements for the intersection region and the detector are described. The lattice configuration and the angular coverage of the detector will allow the simultaneous study of all three regions that characterize elastic scattering, namely the Coulomb dominated region, the Coulomb-hadronic interference region and the hadronic dominated region. 11 refs., 2 figs.
