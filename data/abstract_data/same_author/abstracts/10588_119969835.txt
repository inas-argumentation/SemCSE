Chiral condensate in the vicinity of color sources
Abstract Virtual quark-antiquark fluctuations around static charges are studied by calculating the correlations r 1 … L † ( r N ψ ψ( r ))> between static quarks and the fermion condensate. We find a decrease of the quark-polarization cloud in the vicinity of static quarks and try to give an explanation within the hopping parameter expansion. A comparison with a quenched computation yields practically no effect. Further, we study the behavior of the local chiral condensate in the deconfinement regime.
