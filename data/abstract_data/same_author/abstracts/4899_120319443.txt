Associated multiplicities in proton-proton collisions with a charged hadron atX=0 at the CERN ISR
SummaryCharged-particle multiplicities and angular distributions associated with a π±, K±, p and 

$$
\bar p
$$

 produced at 90°, have been measured at the CERN ISR. The triggering particle was identified by a magnetic spectrometer, within a range of transverse laboratory momentumpT which was different for different particles: 0.075<pT<3.0 GeV/c for pions, 0.075<pT<1.5 GeV/c for kaons, 0.075<pT<3.0 GeV/c for protons and antiprotons. Associated multiplicities have been obtained as a function ofpT at the different ISR beam energies. The distributions of the associated particles have been studied as functions of the azimuthal angleϕ and of the pseudorapidityη. In the high-pT region associated multiplicities increase for all particle types approximately linearly withpT. The momentum of a high-pT particle is seen to be balanced over a wide cone ofϕ andη in the opposite hemisphere. An increase in the multiplicities of secondaries in the same hemisphere near the triggering particle is also observed for the high-pT baryons.
