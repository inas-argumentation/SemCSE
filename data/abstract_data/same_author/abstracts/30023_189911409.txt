Signals of left-right supersymmetry ine+e− collisions
We study chargino and neutralino pair production at the Next Linear e+ e− Collider in a supersymmetric left-right model. We investigateon- and off-resonance signals via the processes:
$$e^ + e^ - \to \tilde \chi _1^ + \tilde \chi _1^ - \to e^ + e^ - + p_{mis} $$
 and
$$e^ + e^ - \to \tilde \chi _2^0 \tilde \chi _1^0 \to e^ + e^ - + p_{mis} $$
. These reactions could lead to observable signals as they compare with the background signal from W± -pair production.
