Self-dual formulation of the Ising model in a random lattice
Abstract A self-dual formulation of the Ising model on a random lattice is presented. In the language of the large- N matrix models, this property is non-universal; i.e., it is not independent of the detailed form of the potential. The fact that it is possible to preserve duality in the presence of two-dimensional gravity, when defining the model non-perturbatively, is very important in the context of superstring theory.
