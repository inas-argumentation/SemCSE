Partial waves and large NC resonance sum rules
Using 1/NC expansion and dispersion theory techniques, without relying on any explicit resonance lagrangian, we generalize the KSRF relation by including the scalar meson effects, at leading order of chiral expansion. Two sum rules for the low energy constants L2, L3 and a new relation between resonance couplings are also derived. A rather detailed examination to the new relation is also given. We also discussed the Nc properties of partial wave amplitudes and the broad σ resonance.
