Holder Inequalities and QCD Sum-Rule Bounds on the Masses of Light Quarks
QCD Laplace Sum-Rules must satisfy a fundamental Holder inequality if they are to consistently represent an integrated hadronic spectral function. The Laplace sum-rules of pion currents is shown to violate this inequality unless the u and d quark masses are sufficiently large, placing a lower bound on mu+md, the SU(2)-invariant combination of the light-quark masses.
