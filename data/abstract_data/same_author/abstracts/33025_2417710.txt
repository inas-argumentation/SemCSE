Does the W mass reconstruction survive QCD effects?
In the hadronic decay mode of a pair of W bosons, e + e → W + W → q1q 2 q3q 4 , QCD interference effects can mix up the two colour singlets q1q 2 and q3q 4 , i.e. produce hadrons that cannot be uniquely assigned to either of W + and W . We show that interference is negligible for energetic perturbative gluon emission, and develop models to help us to estimate the non-perturbative effects. The total contribution to the systematic error on the W mass reconstruction may be as large as 40 MeV.
