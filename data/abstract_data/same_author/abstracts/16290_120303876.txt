Generation of Growing Modes in Cosmological Density Fluctuations by Black Hole Evaporation
We investigate the possibility that growing density fluctuations are generated in the early universe by the evaporation of primordial black holes whose distribution has small in­ homogeneities on large scales. We show that the growing modes are actually generated and their amplitudes are independent of the temporal behavior of evaporation. It is generally believed that the observed strong inhomogeneity in the matter distribu­ tion on scales of galaxies and clusters of galaxies results from small fluctuations of matter distribution in the early universe.') Much work has been done on the growth of fluctuations,2) but most of it has attributed the origin of fluctuations, especially the growing modes of them, to the very initial condition of universe at the Big-Bang. Recently some authors have analysed the possibility that the growing modes are generated in the course of cosmic evolution. 3 ) In this paper we exam­ ine one such possibility, i.e., the generation of growing modes from inhomogeneously dis­ tributed black holes through their evapora­ tion.
