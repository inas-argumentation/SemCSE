Are ghost condensates necessary for the Slavnov-Taylor identities?
Abstract The dimension-four gluonic and ghost condensate contributions to the gluon propagator are reassessed. It is demonstrated that the longitudinal part of the gluon propagator is proportional to condensates composed of the non-abelian gluon and ghost equations of motion. This will identically satisfy the Slavnov-Taylor identity without imposing additional constraints on the condensates. The modified version of the plane-wave method introduced for this purpose should prove useful for higher-loop calculations in QCD sum rules.
