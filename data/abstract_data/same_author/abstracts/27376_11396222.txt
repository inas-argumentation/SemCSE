Holographic supergravity dual to three dimensional = 2 gauge theory
By examining the previously known holographic N = 2 supersymmetric renormalization group flow solution in four dimensions, we describe the mass-deformed Bagger-Lambert theory, that has SU(3)I × U(1)R symmetry, by the addition of mass term for one of the four adjoint chiral superfields as its dual theory. A further detailed correspondence between fields of AdS4 supergravity and composite operators of the infrared field theory is obtained.
