Explanation of the More Effective Effective Field Theory
We show that the so-called more effective effective field theory (MEEFT) is essentially equivalent to the proper nuclear effective field theory (NEFT) in describing low-energy electroweak processes in nuclei. A key to understanding their equivalence is the relation between aN EFT-based NN -potential and a phenomenological potential through the Wilsonian renormalization group equation. We also discuss an important advantage of MEEFT.
