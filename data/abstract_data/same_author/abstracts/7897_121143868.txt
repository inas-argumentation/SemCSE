Chiral anomalies in non-leptonic decays within chiral perturbation theory at the quark level
By the example of the K + → π + π 0 γ decay the contribution of chiral anomalies to non-leptonic weak decays of K -mesons has been investigated at the quark level. The theoretical prediction for the probability of the K + → π + π 0 γ decay with the direct emission of a photon is B ( K + → π + π 0 γ (DE)) anom ⩽(1.1±0.2)×10 −5 . It is stressed that the mechanism of the ΔI= 1 2 transition in the K + → π + π 0 γ decay differs from that governing the enhancement of the ΔI= 1 2 transitions in the K 0 → π + π − decay.
