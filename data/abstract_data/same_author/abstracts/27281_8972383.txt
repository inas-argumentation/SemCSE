Top Condensation: the Idea and Its Phenomenological Viability
We discuss Dynamical Symmetry Breaking via top condensation models and argue that phenomenologically viable scenarios are possible. First model independent symmetry and order of magnitude arguments in favor of such ideas are given. Then models which realize these features are listed with comments on their advantages and problems. Finally it is argued that scenarios with a scale of new physics = O(TeV) may produce a low enough top mass and may be consistent with available electro{ weak precision data.
