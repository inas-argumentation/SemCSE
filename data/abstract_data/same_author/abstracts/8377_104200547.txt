Chiral effective field theory on the lattice: Ab initio calculations of nuclei
This proceedings article reviews recent results by the Nucl ear Lattice EFT Collaboration on an excited state of the12C nucleus known as the Hoyle state. The Hoyle state plays a key rol in the production of carbon via fusion of three alpha particles in r ed giant stars. Our collaboration has completed lattice calculations which show the structure of the Hoyle state, along with evidence for a low-lying spin-2 rotational excitation. For the 12C ground state and the first excited spin-2 state, we find a compact triangular configuration of alpha clusters. For the Hoyle state and the second excited spin-2 state, we find a bent-arm or obtuse triangular configuration of alpha clusters.
