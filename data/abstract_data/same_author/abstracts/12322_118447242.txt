Heavy and light meson wavefunctions
We present a short review on the properties of heavy and light mesons’ light-cone wavefunctions (LCWFs), and their distribution amplitudes (DAs). The $$B$$B meson LCWFs can be treated by taking the heavy quark limit ($$m_b\rightarrow \infty $$mb→∞) and by using the heavy quark effective theory. Furthermore, we propose a simple model for the $$B$$B meson wavefunctions with 3-particle Fock states’ contributions, whose behaviors are controlled by two parameters $$\bar{\varLambda }$$Λ¯ and $$\delta $$δ. Using such model, the form factors $$F^{B\rightarrow \pi }_{+,0,{\text{T}}}$$F+,0,TB→π and $$F^{B\rightarrow K}_{+,0,{\text{T}}}$$F+,0,TB→K in large recoil region are studied up to $$\mathcal{O}(1/m_b^2)$$O(1/mb2) within the $$k_{{\mathrm{T}}}$$kT factorization approach. On the other hand, we adopt Brodsky-Huang-Lepage (BHL) prescription for constructing the WFs of the lighter pseudoscalars as $$\eta _c, D$$ηc,D-meson, pion, kaon, $$\eta ^{(\prime )}$$η(′) and etc. Such BHL-like model can be conveniently extended to construct the LCWFs for light scalar or vector mesons. Within such model the longitudinal distributions of those WFs are basically determined by a parameter $$B$$B, whose value can be determined via a global fit of experimental data.
