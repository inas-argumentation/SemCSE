CAN THE NEUTRINOS FROM Z0-DECAY OSCILLATE?
A common prejudice is that neutrinos produced by the decays of real or virtual Z0-bosons do not oscillate. We will show that this is incorrect even in case of the universality of interactions and of the absence of sterile neutrinos. In Z0-decay the coherent state of neutrino pairs is produced. The oscillation picture can be observed in principle by measuring the flavors of both neutrinos from the same Z0-decay.
