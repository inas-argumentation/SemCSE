3ν oscillations in matter and solar neutrino data
Abstract A method of searching for 3ν solutions of the solar neutrino problem in terms of 2ν solutions is proposed. It gives “iso-SNU strips” on the plot of Δm 2 versus sin 2 2θ. A combined analusis of ClAr experiment data and results of the 8 B neutrino flux measurement by νe scattering (Kamiokande II) is presented.
