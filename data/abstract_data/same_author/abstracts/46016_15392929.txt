Optimality of Wait-Free Atomic Multiwriter Variables
Abstract Known implementations of concurrent wait-free atomic shared multiwriter variables use Θ( n ) control bits per subvariable. It has been shown that implementations of sequential time-stamp systems require Ω( n ) control bits per subvariable. We exhibit a sequential atomic shared multiwriter variable construction using log n control bits per subvariable. There arises the question of the optimality of concurrent implementations of the same, and of weak time-stamp systems. We also show that our solutions are self-stabilizing.
