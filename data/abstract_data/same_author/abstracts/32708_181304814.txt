The Behavior of cross-sections at very high-energies
What motivated us to do this torturous calculation to begin with? Consider the scattering of a photon of a few GEV (which was high energy then) from a proton, the kind of events seen in the CEA those days. The lowest-order process is of the second order: the proton absorbs a photon and then emits another one (or the other way around), just like what an electron does in Compton scattering. This amplitude is of the dimension of the inverse of mass and at high energies is approximately energy independent.
