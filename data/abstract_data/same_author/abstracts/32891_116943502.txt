Possibly New Charmed Baryon States from $\bar B^0\to p\bar p D^{(*)0}$ Decays
We examine the invariant-mass spectrum of ${D}^{0}p$ in ${\overline{B}}^{0}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}p\overline{p}{D}^{0}$ decay measured by BABAR and find that, through the two-step processes of ${\overline{B}}^{0}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\mathbf{B}}_{c}^{+}(\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{D}^{0}p)\overline{p}$, where ${\mathbf{B}}_{\mathbf{c}}$ denotes a charmed baryon state, some of the peaks can be identified with the established ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Sigma}}}_{c}(2800{)}^{+}$, ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{c}(2880{)}^{+}$, and ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{c}(2940{)}^{+}$. Moreover, to account for the measured spectrum, it is necessary to introduce a new charmed baryon resonance with $(m,\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Gamma}})=(3212\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}20,167\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}34)\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{MeV}$.
