Photoproduction—DIS Transition
Recent measurements of the proton structure function F2 at low Q2 in e+p deep inelastic scattering (DIS) by the H1 Collaboration are summarized. This measurements are performed in the region of low squared four‐momentum transfers, 0.2 GeV2⩽Q2⩽12 GeV2, and low Bjorken x, 5⋅10−6⩽x⩽0.02.
