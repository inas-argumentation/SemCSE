The saturation temperature is also found to be dependent on partonic interaction. At a given collision energy, it is found that both Tch and µB decrease towards more peripheral collisions in the grand canonical approach. The energy and centrality dependence of other chemical freeze-out parameters, strangeness chemical potential µS , strangeness undersaturation factor S , and the volume of the fireball V are also presented in this paper. The chemical potential ratio µs/µB is also compared with lattice QCD calculation. The AMPT default model gives better descriptions on both the particle yields and the chemical freeze-out parameters than those from the AMPT string-melting model.
