Parity nonconservation in proton-water scattering at 1.5 GeV/c.
A search has been made for parity nonconservation in the scattering of 1.5-GeV/c (800-MeV) polarized protons from an unpolarized water target. The result for the longitudinal asymmetry is A/sub L/ = (1.7 +- 3.3 +- 1.4) x 10/sup -7/, where the second uncertainty is an estimate of systematic effects. This result is significantly smaller than the value of A/sub L/ for H/sub 2/O reported at 6 GeV/c.
