Improved Weizsaecker--Williams method and its application to lepton and W- boson pair production
A Weishcker-Williams method is derived which handles the elastic and inelastic target form factors properly. The method is applied to calculate energy-angle distributions of photoproduced lepton pairs: electrons, muons, and heavy leptons, using target form factors appropriate to each case. The agreement with the exact result is found to be excellent. Simple formulas for pair production of spin-0 and spin-1 particles are also given. (auth)
