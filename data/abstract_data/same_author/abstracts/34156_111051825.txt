Cryomodule development for superconducting RF test facility (STF) at KEK
The Current status of cryomodule development for the superconducting RF test facility (STF) at KEK is described. An objective of this effort is to experience fabrication of 5-m long cryomodule and to perform high power testing of superconducting RF cavities towards ILC (International Linear Collider). The STF features two 5-m long cryomodules, each holding up to four 9-cell cavities. The cavities, about 1-m long each, will be cooled by saturated superfluid helium at 2.0 K
