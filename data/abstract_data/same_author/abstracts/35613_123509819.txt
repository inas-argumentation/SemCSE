Generalized coherent states and the uncertainty principle
We derive from a dynamical symmetry property that the linear and nonlinear Schrodinger equations with harmonic potential possess an infinite string of shape-preserving coherent wave-packet states with classical motion. Unlike the Schroedinger state with ..delta..x..delta..p = h/2, the uncertainty product can be arbitrarily large for these states showing that classical motion is not necessarily linked with minimum uncertainty. We obtain a generalization of Sudarshan's diagonal coherent-state representation in terms of these states.
