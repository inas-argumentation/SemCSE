Smirnov's integrals and the quantum Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equation of level 0
We study the quantum Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equation (1984) of level 0 associated with the spin 1/2 representation of Uq(SL2). We find an integral formula for solutions in the case of an arbitrary total spin and mod q mod <1. In the formula, different solutions can be obtained by taking different integral kernels with the cycle of integration being fixed.
