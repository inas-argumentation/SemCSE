Future of Heavy Flavour Physics: Experimental Perspective
I discuss what measurements need to be done to search for physics beyond the Standard CKM model, rather than just what studies can be done in the near future. It is also important to accurately measure the CKM matrix elements. Current best estimates for two important elements are: jVcbj=0 :03810:0021 andjVub=Vcbj=0 :0850:019. Finally, future experiments are discussed.
