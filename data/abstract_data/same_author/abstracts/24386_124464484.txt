Test of Fermi gas model predictions of level density in Xe-137
We have studied the unbound levels of /sup 137/Xe via neutron resonance reactions and ..beta.. decay of /sup 137/I. High-resolution neutron transmission data revealed a total of 35 resonances below a neutron energy of 500 keV. At least 27 of the resonances are p-wave resonances. The near absence of s-wave resonances in this energy region resulted in an extremely low value for the s-wave neutron strength function. The current ..gamma..-ray data complement earlier studies of ..gamma.. rays and delayed neutrons following the ..beta.. decay of /sup 137/I. By combining all data, we have obtained a detailed picture of the level density in /sup 137/Xe for a wide range of angular momenta. With the exception of (1/2)/sup +/ and (1/2)/sup -/ levels, the overall agreement is good between the current data and predictions of the Fermi gas model.
