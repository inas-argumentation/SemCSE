Holonomy observables in Ponzano-Regge-type state sum models
Observables on group elements in the Ponzano–Regge model are studied. These observables are shown to have a natural interpretation in terms of Feynman diagrams on a sphere and are contrasted to the well-studied observables on the spin labels. The physical interpretation is elucidated by showing how they arise from the no-gravity limit of the Turaev–Viro model and Chern–Simons theory.
