RADIAL EXCITED STATES OF THE NUCLEON IN QUARK MODELS WITH DYNAMICAL CONFINEMENT
The Roper state is described in the framework of the chiral chromodielectric model as a single quark excitation in self-consistent meson potentials. The interplay between quark and meson excitations is discussed and we emphasise the role of non-quark degrees of freedom in the electroproduction of nucleon excitations.
