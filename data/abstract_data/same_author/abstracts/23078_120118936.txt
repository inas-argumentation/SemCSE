Strong lower bound on squark masses in minimal N=1 supergravity
With flavor-changing gluino couplings as recently calculated in minimal N=1 supergravity, it is shown that virtual gluino-gluino 0−+ bound states make potentially very large contributions of the wrong sign to the K0K0 mass matrix.
