A generalization of Gauss sums and its applications to Siegel modular forms and L-functions associated with the vector space of quadratic forms.
The main purpose of this paper is the study of Gauss sums associated with Symmetrie matrices over finite fields and its applications to twisting operators on Siegel modular forms and L-functions associated with the vector space of Symmetrie matrices.
