Wave functions for weakly coupled bound states
It has been observed' that a short-range attractive potential always produces a bound state in one or two dimensions. This property has been investigated in the weak-coupling limit, with the intention of obtaining perturbative expressions for the bound-state wave functions and energies. In particular, Hausmann has analyzed the bound states in one-dimensional, short-range potentials by using the homogeneous Lippmann-Schwinger equation for the bound-state wave functions.
