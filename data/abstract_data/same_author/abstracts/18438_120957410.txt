Gluon shadowing and jet quenching in relativistic heavy ion collisions
Abstract The effects of parton shadowing and jet quenching are considered in relativistic heavy ion collisions. We find that particle production is sensitive to these nuclear effects on jet production. We illustrate that the energy loss of a large p T parton in dense matter and nuclear shadowing of gluon structure function can be measured by systematic study of moderate and high p T single particle spectra in pp , pA , and AA collisions.
