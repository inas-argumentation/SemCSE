No massless scalar adjoints for N=1, D=4 heterotic strings in the free fermionic formulation
Using the free fermionic formulation of four-dimensional heterotic superstrings with N =1 spacetime supersymmetry, we prove that the massless spectrum does not contain that transform as the adjoint representation of the gauge group. Some implications for model building are also discussed. We also show that no higher dimensional massless representations occur except possibly when the gauge group has an SU(9) or SO(16) factor. We discuss these two cases and their connection to the possible appearance of an E 8 factor.
