General form of string corrections to supersymmetry transformation in D=10, N=1 supergravity
Abstract We present the O( α ′ 3 ) heterotic string corrections to the supersymmetry transformation rules of D =10, N =1 supergravity multiplet, which satisfy the on-shell closure of supersymmetry. We also generalize this result to higher orders in α′, assuming a natural form of the leading O( α ' n ) heterotic string corrections to the effective action of the supergravity multiplet. We find no modification to the Killing spinor equations for the gravitino and dilatino at O(α′ 3 ) or higher, whenever fermionic condensates are absent.
