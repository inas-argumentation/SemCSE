Quark mass matrix and discrete symmetries in the SU(2) ⊗ U(1) model
Abstract We show that in the SU(2) × U(1) model with an arbitrary number of quarks there exists no set of discrete symmetries which may determine the Cabibbo angle in terms of quark masses. An enlargement of the “minimal” group is therefore crucial for the success of the discrete symmetry approach to this problem.
