Nucleus-nucleus potential from fusion and elastic scattering.
An analytical expression for the calculation of the nucleus-nucleus potential has been derived from an empirical formula which describes the fusion excitation function. The nucleus-nucleus potentials calculated for the projectiles /sup 6/Li, /sup 9/Be, and /sup 12/C in the interaction region --3--6.5 fm by the above procedure agree remarkably well with the real part of the respective optical potentials determined from the analysis of elastic scattering data which exhibit the ''rainbow'' phenomenon.
