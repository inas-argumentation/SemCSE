On Limit Multiplicities for Spaces of Automorphic Forms
Let be arank-onearithmetic subgroupofasemisimple Liegroup G. Forfixed K-Type, thespectral side of the Selberg trace formula defines a distribution on the space of infinitesimal characters of G, whose discrete part encodes the dimensions of the spaces of square-integrable -automorphic forms. It is shown that this distribution convergesto the Plancherel measure of G when shrinks to the trivial group in a certain restricted way. The analogous assertion for cocompact lattices follows from results of DeGeorge-Wallach and Delorme.
