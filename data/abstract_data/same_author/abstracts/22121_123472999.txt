Finite Symmetry Transformation Groups and Exact Solutions of Three Generalized Breaking Soliton Equations
We investigate Bogoyavlenskii's generalized breaking soliton equations and their extension form with the modified direct Clarkson-Kruskal (CK) method. Then, we get the Lie point groups and the full symmetry group, and a relationship is constructed between the new solutions and the old one. Based on the relationship, the new solutions can be obtained by using a given solution of the equations.
