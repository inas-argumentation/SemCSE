Transverse properties of jets at Collider energies from ISR data
SummaryThe universality features observed in comparing ISR data with (e+e−) and deep-inelastic scattering (DIS), prompt a comparison, at Collider energies, of high-pT and low-pT jets with constant

$$\sqrt {\left( {q_{tot}^{bad} } \right)^2 } $$

. The expectations of the transverse properties of the particles in the jets are discussed.
