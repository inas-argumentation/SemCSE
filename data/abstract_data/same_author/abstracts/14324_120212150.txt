Measuring the triple-gluon coupling in hadron collisions
The fourth moment of the pT distribution (pT4) of jets or hadrons can be reliably predicted in QCD at the pp and pp collider energies. It depends on the gluon distribution function only via its first moment, i.e., the energy fraction carried by gluons. The dominant contribution to pT4 is from gluon-gluon and gluon-quark collisions. Hence, a measurement of pT4 can be used to determine the gluon self-interaction.
