Sub-eV limit for the neutrino mass from 76Ge double beta decay by the HEIDELBERG-MOSCOW experiment
The HEIDELBERG-MOSCOW double beta decay experiment using HP germanium semiconductor detectors enriched in 76Ge is the first ββ experiment to penetrate into the sub-eV range. After a measuring time of 10.2 kg·a (117.0 mol·a) the half-life limit for 76Ge 0νββ decay is T 1 2 ≥ 5.6×10 24 a (90% C. L.) or T 1 2 ≥9.4×10 24 a (68% C.L.) which corresponds to an upper limit for the effective Majorana neutrino mass 〈mν〉 ≤ 0.65 eV (90% C.L.) or 〈mν〉 ≤ 0.50 eV (68% C. L.). For a superheavy neutrino, as it occurs, e. g., in seesaw models, we extract a limit of 〈mH〉 ≥ 5.1×107 GeV. These numbers are of importance in the context of presently discussed indications of physics beyond the Standard Model.
