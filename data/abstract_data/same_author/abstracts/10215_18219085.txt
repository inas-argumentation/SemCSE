Background field quantization and non-commutative QED
We quantize non-commutative QED canonically in the background field gauge for weak and slowly varying background fields. We determine the complete basis for expansion under such an approximation. As an application, we derive the Wigner function which determines the leading order high temperature behavior of the perturbative amplitudes of non-commutative QED. To leading order, we also give a closed form expression for the distribution function for non-commutative QED at high temperature.
