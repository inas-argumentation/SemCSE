A naturally heavy fourth generation neutrino
Abstract We propose that there exists a fourth generation, all neutrinos have Dirac masses of order the corresponding charge lepton partner, and right-handed neutrinos have Majorana masses of order the weak scale. The see-saw mechanism then implies the light neutrino masses are O( mM lepton 2 / M ), while a fourth generation neutrino is heavy with mass O( M )∼O( M w ), and would not have been seen in the Z 0 width a LEP. Such a solution appears to be compatile with all known phenomenological and cosmological constraints, and predicts masses for neutrinos near their current upper bounds.
