MeV tau neutrino in a gauge-mediated supersymmetry breaking model
A supersymmetric model which naturally accommodates MeV tau neutrinos within the framework of gauge-mediated supersymmetry breaking is described. The lepton number violation is originally introduced in the messenger sector of the theory. A large slepton-Higgs mixing mass and a small lepton-higgsino mixing mass are generated at one loop. The scalar tau neutrino has a non-vanishing vacuum expectation value. This results in a non-zero vτ mass which is in the range of 1–10 MeV.
