Solution of the Hamiltonian problem for self-complementary graphs
A graph G is said to be highly constricted if there exists a nonempty subset S of vertices such that (i) G − S has more than | S | components, (ii) S induces the complete graph, and (iii) for every u ∈ S and v ∉ S , we have d G ( u ) > d G ( v ), where d G ( u ) denotes the degree of u in G . In this paper it is shown that a non-hamiltonian self-complementary graph G of order p is highly constricted, unless p = 4 N and G is a particular graph G ∗ (4N) . It is also proved that if G is a self-complementary graph of order p (≥8) and π its degree sequence, then G is pancyclic if π has a realization with a hamiltonian cycle, and G has a 2-factor if π has a realization with a 2-factor, unless p = 4 N and G = G ∗ (4N) .
