On The Computation of Certain Roots by the use of The Binomial Series
When the author was a student in Professor Ford's calculus class at the Rice Institute, the subject under discussion on one occasion was that of finding roots -in this instance, cube roots-of numbers by using the binomial expansion. The class was invited to propose numbers of which to compute the cube root, and the number 37 was suggested.
