Design and Construction of the Magnets and Magnet Support Structure for the MURA 50‐MeV Electron Accelerator. III
Design and construction of the magnets and magnet support structure of the MURA 50‐MeV electron accelerator are discussed. The choice of the basic magnet structure as determined by the accelator parameters and methods of matching the magnets and winding the coils to produce magnets with uniform properties are described.
