Higher twist effects in the Drell‐Yan process
We calculate the angular distribution of the lepton produced in the Drell‐Yan reaction taking into account pion bound state effects. We work in the kinematic region where one of the pion constituents goes far off shell, which allows us to treat the bound state problem pertubatively. We show that the angular distribution is very sensitive to the shape of the pion distribution amplitude. The model we discuss fits the data if we choose a two‐humped pion distribution amplitude suggested by QCD sum rules.
