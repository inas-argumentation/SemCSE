Status and Future Perspectives on the Measurement of the Unitarity Triangle Angle α
A summary on the status of the measurements of the Unitarity Triangle angle α using the decays B → ππ, B → ρπ and B → ρρ and on the future perspectives at the Large Hadron Collider experiment LHCb is presented. The UTfit collaboration, combining the BABAR measurements from B → ρπ and B → ρρ, give as Standard Model solution the value α = (106 ± 8)°. The LHCb Collaboration has estimated the sensitivity on α from the B0 → ρπ decay obtaining a relative error σαα ⩽ 0.1, in one nominal year of data taking.
