Exchange by the hard and soft quanta in the high energy scattering of particles
In the framework of the functional integration method in quantum field theory the method is proposed for constructing scattering amplitude of the particles which interact by means of the exchange of the infinite number of soft quanta and the finite number of hard virtual particles. In this method the form factors of the interacting particles arise in calculations of the scattering amplitude at large angles. It is in agreement with the Wu--Yang conjecture about the elastic form factor role in high energy scattering. (auth)
