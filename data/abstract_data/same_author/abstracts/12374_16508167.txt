Low-q 2 Partons in P-p and Au-au Collisions
We describe correlations of lowQ2 parton fragments on transverse rapidity yt and angles(η ,φ) from p-p and Au-Au collisions at √ s= 130 and 200 GeV. Evolution of correlations on yt from p-p to more-central Au-Au collisions shows evidence for parton dissipation. Cuts onyt isolate angular correlations on(η ,φ) for low-Q2 partons which reveal a large asymmetry about the jet thrust a xi in p-p collisions favoring the azimuth direction.
