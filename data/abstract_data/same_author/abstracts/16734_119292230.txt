Scalar susceptibility from the instanton vacuum with meson-loop corrections
The scalar susceptibility (${\ensuremath{\chi}}_{s}$) of QCD, which represents the response of the chiral condensate to a small perturbation of explicit chiral-symmetry breaking ($m\ensuremath{\ne}0$), is investigated within the nonlocal chiral quark model ($\mathrm{NL}\ensuremath{\chi}\mathrm{QM}$) based on the instanton vacuum configuration for ${N}_{f}=2$. We also take into account $1/{N}_{c}$ meson-loop (ML) corrections including scalar and pseudoscalar mesons. It turns out that the chiral condensate is modified to a large extend by the ML corrections in the vicinity of $m=0$, whereas its effect becomes weak beyond $m\ensuremath{\approx}100\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{MeV}$. As numerical results, we find that ${\ensuremath{\chi}}_{s}=\ensuremath{-}0.34\text{ }{\mathrm{GeV}}^{2}$ with the ML corrections and $0.18\text{ }\text{ }{\mathrm{GeV}}^{2}$ without it. From these observations, we conclude that the ML corrections play an important role in the presence of finite current-quark mass.
