Spectrum Property Analysis of a Wiggler-like Undulator
A wiggler with the property of low total radiation power and that maintains a large photon flux in the hard x-ray region, 5-20 keV, which is necessary for the special demand of users, was investigated to decrease the difficulty of the design of optical components in the beam line and to decrease the load of RF cavity power. Such an insertion devise is called a wiggler-like undulator. The spectrum of this wiggler-like undulater was investigated with a code of which the algorithm is based on a compromising between useful photon flux and total radiation power of insertion devices for spectrum optimization. The properties of the spectrum of the wiggler-like undulator are discussed herein. Furthermore, the power distribution is somehow also discussed.
