Packed integer wavelet transform constructed by lifting scheme
A new method for speeding up the integer wavelet transforms constructed by the lifting scheme is proposed. The proposed method packs multiple pixels (wavelet coefficients) in a single word; therefore, it can make use of the 32-bit or 64-bit computational capability of modern computers to accomplish multiple addition/subtraction operations in one instruction cycle. As a result, our method can save the decomposition/reconstruction time by up to 37% on 32-bit machines and require much less working memory in comparison with the original wavelet transform algorithms.
