Measurement of large-angle cross-sections for coherent pion photoproduction on deuteron
SummaryCoherent pion photoproduction from deuteron is studied by measuring the differential cross-sections at c.m. angles between 90° and 130° in a photon energy range from 500 to 1000 MeV. The experimental data are compared with a model calculation which takes into account two kinds of contributions, the nonresonant contribution consisting of pion photoproduction from single nucleons and pion rescattering and that from the dibaryon resonances3F3(2.26) and1G4(2.51).
