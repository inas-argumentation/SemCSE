Threshold corrections in K3 × T2 heterotic string compactifications
Abstract We consider compactifications of the heterotic string on K 3 × T 2 so that the resulting theory in d = 4 space-time dimensions has N = 2 supersymmetry. The gravitational and gauge coupling constants of the low-energy effective theory receive threshold corrections from loops of superheavy string states. We calculate these corrections for the case when the K 3 surface is a Z n orbifold of a four-torus T 4. The results are used to determine the one-loop prepotential F 0 (1) for the vector multiplets and the gravitational coupling F 1 (1) .
