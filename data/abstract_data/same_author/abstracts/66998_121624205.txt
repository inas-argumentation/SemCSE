A product theorem for ideals over orders
Frohlich(1) has obtained certain invariants for modules over commutative separable orders over dedekind domains, whose values are ideals in the dedekind domain. In two particular applications he has shown that these invariants supply a criterion for a module over a given order to be projective ((1), Theorem 4), and another one for a fractional ideal to be invertible ((1), Theorem 5).
