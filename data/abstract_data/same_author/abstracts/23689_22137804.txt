Inclusive forward-backward asymmetry for light quarks.
The energy-differential forward-backward asymmetry in the reaction {ital e}{sup +}{ital e}{sup {minus}}{r arrow}M+ anything, {ital M} being a leading, light-quark-constituted meson in a jet, is considered. The measurement of this quantity yields rank-1 and higher-rank fragmentation functions for light quarks hadronizing into light mesons, separately. It is also possible, in the case of a large event sample and good particle identification capabilities at high energies, to obtain information on {ital Z} couplings to light quarks.
