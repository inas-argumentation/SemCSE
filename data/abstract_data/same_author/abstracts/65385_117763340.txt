Chapter VIII The Equivariant Loop Theorem for Three-Dimensional Manifolds and a Review of the Existence Theorems for Minimal Surfaces
Publisher Summary The chapter discusses the equivariant loop theorem for three-dimensional manifolds that is needed in settling the Smith conjecture and reviews the existence theorems for minimal surfaces. The equivariant version of the loop theorem says that there are a finite number of properly embedded disks in M that satisfy the required properties. The loop theorem respects the action of the group G in a suitable manner. The chapter puts a metric on M so that the group G acts isometrically and so that ∂M is convex with respect to the outward normal. Then with respect to this metric, the existence of an immersed disk D 1 is demonstrated, in M whose boundary ∂ D 1 , represents a nontrivial element in π 1 ( S ) and whose area is minimal among all such disks. The chapter describes Morrey's solution for the plateau problem in a general Riemannian manifold. The existence theorem for manifolds with boundary proves that M is homogeneously regular if M is a subdomain of another homogeneously regular manifold N that has no boundary.
