On the quantum corrections to two-dimensional (1,0)-supersymmetric Abelian gauge models
The machinery of (1, 0)-supergravity is employed to evaluate 1-loop corrections to the effective action and chiral symmetry supercurrent in the framework of an (1, 0)-supersymmetric Abelian gauge model. As a by-product, a dynamically-generated mass is found out for the gauge (1, 0)-superconnections.
