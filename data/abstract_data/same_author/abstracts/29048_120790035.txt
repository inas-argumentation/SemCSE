No evidence for medium effects in the12C(e, e'p)11Bg.s. reaction
The12C(e, e'p)11Bg.s reaction has been measured in parallel and anti-parallel kinematics over apm range of −120<pm<120 MeV/c at a centralTp of 90 MeV. In contrast to previous measurements at NIKHEF, both kinematics can be described without enhancement of the transverse form factor of the bound proton (η=0.98(5)). The spectroscopic factor for this transition as determined with the new Mainz data (Sα=1.54(5)) is 22% smaller than the NIKHEF value.
