The energy of the high-temperature quark-gluon plasma
Abstract For the quark-gluon plasma, we study the energy-momentum tensor corresponding to the high-temperature Braaten-Pisarski effective action. The tensor is defined by considering the interaction of the plasma with a weak gravitational field. It is first calculated to second order in g (the QCD coupling constant). Then, by exploiing local invariance, we write down the complete effective action in curved space. From this we infer the energy tensor to all orders in g . For definiteness, we write this in retarded form. Finally, we discuss positivity of T 00 to second order in g .
