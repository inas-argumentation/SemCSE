On the Rate of Convergence of Cubature Formulas
In the current chapter we address the problem of the rate with which the norm of the error decreases as the number of nodes of a cubature formula increases. For the formulas with a given lattice of nodes hHγ we establish estimates exact in order for strong convergence on the Banach space of periodic functions meeting several natural conditions. We prove analogous results for periodic Banach spaces without weight in Bessel scales. We also derive estimates for the norms of errors with equidistributed nodes which act in the L 2 (m) space of aperiodic functions.
