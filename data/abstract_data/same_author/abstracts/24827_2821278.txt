The SM prediction of g-2 of the muon
We calculate (g − 2)/2 of the muon, paying particular attention to the hadronic vacuum polarisation contribution and its uncertainties. The different data sets for each e + e − exclusive channel (as well as for the inclusive e + e − → hadrons channel) have been combined in order to produce the optimum estimate of the cross sections and their uncertainties. QCD sum rules are evaluated in order to resolve an apparent discrepancy between the inclusive data and the sum of the exclusive channels.
