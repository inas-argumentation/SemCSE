String-breaking description of the baryon-meson interaction
We use the intuitive string-breaking picture of the baryon-meson ( B BM) interaction in quantum chromodynamics to describe this interaction. We find that the B BM vertex falls off at a characteristic momentum of the order of the inverse size of the baryon. We hope that our picture can be used as an alternative to the bag model. In our model, in contrast to the bag model, exotic single-centered hadrons lie high in mass compared to the corresponding several hadron states.
