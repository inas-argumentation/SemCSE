Two theorems on the level order in potential models
We study the potentials of the form U ( r )=− r −1 + λV ( r ), ( d d r )( r 2 d V d r )≷0 , and show that the energy levels satisfy the inequalities E(N c , l)≷E(N c , l+1) to first order in λ, where N c denotes the coulombic principal quantum number and l the angular momentum. Similarly for potentials U(r)=r 2 +λV(r), ( d d r 2 ) 2 V(r)≷0 , we prove to first order in λ that E (N H , l )≷ E (N H , l +2) , where N H denotes the harmonic oscillator quantum number. In the latter case, we give also quantitative restrictions on the relative positions at the l th and ( l +1)th states.
