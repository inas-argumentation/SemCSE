Mapping High x Structure of the Nucleon: which data is missing?
The analysis of numerous experiments on lepton‐nucleon and lepton‐nucleus deep inelastic scattering reveals several non‐trivial features of the nucleon structure, which are particulary interesting in the range of large Bjorken x (x > 0.8). It is shown that new data form the lightest nuclei can provide desisive information needed for the understanding of the nucleon structure.
