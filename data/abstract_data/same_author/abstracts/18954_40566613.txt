Spectra of symmetric-top molecules and the quantum-deformed-symmetric-top model.
By means of geometric quantization, we discuss the quantum-deformed-symmetric-top model. We show that the quantum-deformed symmetric top and the quantum-standard symmetric top obey the same Heisenberg equations of motion. The quantum-deformed-symmetric-top model possess quantum group symmetry; thus the quantum group can be dynamical symmetries of the symmetric-top system
