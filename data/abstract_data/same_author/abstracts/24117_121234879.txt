GAMOW-TELLER STRENGTH FUNCTION IN GE-71 VIA THE (P, N) REACTION AT MEDIUM-ENERGIES
The Gamow-Teller strength function in /sup 71/Ge has been measured by use of the /sup 71/Ga(p,n) reaction at E/sub p/ = 120 and 200 MeV. While a significant fraction of the total strength is associated with excited states located below particle-emission threshold, the first excited state exhibits little strength. Excited-state contributions to the total solar-neutrino capture rate for a /sup 71/Ga detector are about 14 solar neutrino units for the neutrino spectrum of the standard solar model and about 3--4 solar neutrino units for representative nonstandard solar models.
