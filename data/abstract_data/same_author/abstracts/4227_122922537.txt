NN scattering above the pion threshold
We studied the NNπ system in a on‐relativistic field theory with given NN and πN interactions (VHBE, i.e. heavy boson exchanges) which cannot be generated by the also given NNπ vertex1. Using graph summations or reduction techniques, we obtain regorous solutions in the one‐pion approximation2.
