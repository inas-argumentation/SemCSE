ARES Upgrade for Super-KEKB
ARES is a normal-conducting RF accelerating cavity system for KEKB, which has been successfully operated to store the high-current beams. Aiming at luminosity frontiers beyond 10cms, an upgrade project toward Super-KEKB is being proposed, where about four times more beam currents are to be stored and supported by the RF system. In this report, upgrade items relevant to the ARES system are reviewed and discussed.
