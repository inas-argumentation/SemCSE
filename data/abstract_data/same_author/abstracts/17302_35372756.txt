Inclusive B-meson decays into charm.
We have measured the B-meson decay fractions into D/sup 0/, D/sup +/, and D/sup *//sup +/ mesons to be 0.39 +- 0.05 +- 0.04, 0.17 +- 0.04 +- 0.04, and 0.22 +- 0.04/sub -0.04//sup +0.07/, respectively, if we use the D/sup 0/..-->..K/sup -/..pi../sup +/ and D/sup +/..-->..K/sup -/..pi../sup +/..pi../sup +/ branching ratios determined by the Mark III Collaboration. These results suggest that B decay to noncharm final states is substantial. We also find that B decay to spin-1 D/sup */ mesons dominates over direct decay to spin-0 D mesons and that the ratio (D/sup 0/+D/sup *//sup 0/)/(D/sup +/+D/sup *//sup +/) is consistent with unity.
