Modelling power corrections to the Bjorken sum rule for the neutrino structure function F1
Direct measurements of the structure functions Fνp1 and Fνn1 at a neutrino factory would allow for an accurate extraction of αs from the Q2-dependence of the Bjorken sum rule, complementing that based on the Gross–Llewellyn-Smith sum rule for F3. We estimate the power (1/Q2-) corrections to the Bjorken sum rule in the instanton vacuum model. For the reduced matrix element of the flavour-nonsinglet twist-4 operator ūgμνγνγ5u − (u → d) we obtain a value of 0.18 GeV2, in good agreement with the QCD sum rule calculations of Braun and Kolesnichenko. Our result allows one to reduce the theoretical error in the determination of αs.
