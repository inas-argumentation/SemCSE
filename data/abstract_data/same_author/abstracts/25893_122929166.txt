Affine superalgebras, unitary representations and the sugawara construction
Unitary highest weight representations of the untwisted, affine, basic classical Lie superalgebras are considered. Unitary constraints (for both even and odd generators) relate the value of the central extension to the vacuum quantum numbers. The only consistent possibilities are for certain compact type I superalgebras, or for those type I and II superalgebras yielding spinor gradings of rigid two-dimensional conformal transformations. The Sugawara construction for these is presented and the value of the Virasoro central charge listed.
