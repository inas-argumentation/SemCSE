SIMULATION STUDY OF LASER-WIRES AS A POST-LINAC DIAGNOSTIC FOR CLIC AND ILC∗
Realistic ILC bunch trains are simulated in the linac, including intra-train collective effects, and then analysed via a realistic simulation of a laser-wire system, including effects of laser-wire signal extraction and detection. Implications are drawn for the use of laser-wires as a post-linac machine diagnostic.
