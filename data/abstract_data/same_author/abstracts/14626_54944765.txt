Preliminary results on the flux of TeV g-rays from crab system obtained with the PACT at Pachmarhi
We have observed the Crab system (Nebula and Pulsar) and the corresponding background regions during many clear nights in the years 1999-2002 using the atmospheric Čerenkov array at Pachmarhi, PACT. Preliminary results indicate a steady emission of TeV γ-rays from the Crab Nebula.
