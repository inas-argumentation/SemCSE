Intermediate scale dependence of non-universal gaugino masses in supersymmetric SO(10)
We calculate the dependence on the intermediate scale of the gaugino mass ratios upon breaking of SO(10) into the standard model via an intermediate group H. We see that the ratios change significantly when the intermediate scale is low (say, 10(8) GeV or 1 TeV) compared to the case when the two breakings occur at the same scale.
