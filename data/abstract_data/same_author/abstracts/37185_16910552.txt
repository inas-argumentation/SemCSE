Threshold behavior of Feynman diagrams: the master two-loop propagator
Abstract An asymptotic expansion of the two-loop two-point “master” diagram with two masses m and M , on the mass shell Q 2 = M 2 , is presented. The treatment of the non-analytical terms arising in the expansion around the branching point is discussed. Some details of the calculation of a new class of two-loop integrals are given.
