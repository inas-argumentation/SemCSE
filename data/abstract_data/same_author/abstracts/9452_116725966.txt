Vanishing Vacuum Energies for Nonsupersymmetric Strings
We review a mechanism which has been proposed for the vanishing of the cosmological constant in nonsupersymmetric superstring theories. We discuss in particular how the idea might work at two and three loops, although no concrete model is proposed. One of the main difficulties with the approach is finding explicit models exhibiting the mechanism: only two examples in two uncompactified dimensions are known.
