APPROXIMATE GRAVITATIONAL WAVES VIA DEFORMATIONS OF EMBEDDINGS
Many Einstein spaces can be embedded globally in pseudo-Euclidean spaces of dimension N > 4. The geometrical quantities characterizing the embedded manifold can be expressed by means of derivatives of the embedding functions zA (xµ), A, B, = 1, 2, …N, µ, ν, …= 0, 1, 2, 3. An infinitesimal deformation of embedding can be expanded into a series ${\tilde{z}}^A = z^A (x^{\mu}) + \varepsilon v^A \, (x^{\mu}) + \varepsilon^2 w^A \, (x^{\mu}) +.$, giving rise to a similar expansion of geometrical quantities of the embedded Einstein space, and the Einstein equations in vacuo, too. We show how gravitational wave solutions appear naturally in this context.
