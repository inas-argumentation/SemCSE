A novel method of skeletal fixation in an above-elbow replantation: the dowel pin technique.
Major reimplantation of the upper limb, or macroreimplantation, is a tremendous challenge for both the patient and the surgeon. Although the technical aspects of the microsurgery associated with this procedure are often less challenging than replantation of more distal segments because of the size and caliber of the neurovascular structures, there are many other difficulties. As multiple studies have documented, the degree of injury to both the stump and the amputated segment dictates the success of the surgical outcome.1–5 The more traumatized segments tend to produce less predictable surgical outcomes. A recent study documented that the serum potassium concentration of the amputated segment was the best objective predictor of surgical outcome.2 The surgical procedure can be long and of significant medical morbidity for the patient. Postoperatively, the patient must be actively involved with a rigorous rehabilitation regimen. Although skeletal fixation often plays a secondary role in replantation of more distal segments, attention to bony fixation is critical with macroreimplantation. The method of fixation must provide rigid stability for soft-tissue healing and yet still meet the functional demands of the local environment. These goals must be met in an expedient manner to decrease the morbidity associated with prolonged operative time. Options for skeletal fixation include rigid internal fixation with plates and screws, percutaneous Kirschner wire fixation, and external fixation. If the macroreimplantation is near the elbow, the demands on the skeletal fixation are even greater, as attention to immediate rigid stability must be balanced with the longterm need for motion at the elbow. Although initially reported for use with a metacarpal, a modification of the technique described by Littler for thumb agenesis reconstruction may be used with a macroreimplantation near the elbow.6 This article reports the successful replantation of an above-elbow amputation with the use of a novel method of skeletal fixation: the dowel pin technique.
