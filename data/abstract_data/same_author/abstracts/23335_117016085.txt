Inclusive production of lambda and neutral kaon in K−p interactions at 3.93 GeV/c and 14.3 GeV/c
SummaryLambda and neutral-kaon production is studied in K−p interactions at 3.93 and 14.3 GeV/c. Topological inclusive cross-sections are given at both energies. Invariant cross-sections for lambdas and neutral kaons are presented as a function of the Feynmanx-variable and are found to decrease in magnitude and vary their shapes with energy. The

 production, which represents (9±2)% and (29±3)% of the lambda inclusive cross-section at 3.93 GeV/c and 14.3 GeV/c respectively, yields mainly backward lambdas. The shapes of the transverse-momentum invariant distributions vary withx, but are roughly independent of energy.
