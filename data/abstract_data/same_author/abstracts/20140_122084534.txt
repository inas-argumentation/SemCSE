Nuclear polarizability corrections to pion elastic scattering
We study the corrections to pion elastic scattering given by the process in which the pion excites a collective mode of the target nucleus and then de-excites it leaving the target in the ground state. A perturbative scheme enables us to calculate these corrections easily and without solving coupled-channel equations. Results are presented for pion scattering on 12 C, 58 Ni and 176 Yb. The corrections that we calculate are small except at the diffraction minima where they cause a deepening, and at back angles.
