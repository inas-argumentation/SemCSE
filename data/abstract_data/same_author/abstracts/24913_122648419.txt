Some comparison theorems on moments in the ground state and in excited states of a two-body system
Abstract We prove and extend some conjectures by Dib, Franzini and Gilman on the comparison of expectation values of r v in the ground state and the first radial excited state of a two-body system for a given, non-zero angular momentum.
