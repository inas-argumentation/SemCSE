Transformation Properties of Nonleptonic Weak Interactions
The DELTA T = 1/2 rule for nonleptonic decays of strange particles requiring the part of the Hamiltonian H/sub W/( DELTA S = + 1), responsible for these decays with DELTA S = 1, to transform like the K deg meson with respect to the hypercharge gauge transformation and isospin rotations is discussed. It is proposed that H/sub W/( DELTA S = + 1) transforms like the K deg meson with respect to all SU(3) transformations, and H/sub W/STAH/sub W/ = H/sub W/( DELTA S = + 1) + H/sub W/( DELTA S = -- 1)! is approximately invariant under the product of parity conjugation and R conjugation. The consequences of these proposals are discussed. (R.E.U.)
