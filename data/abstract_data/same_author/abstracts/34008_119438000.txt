On stability of the crystal universe models
We generalize the Goldberger-Wise mechanism and study the stability of the crystal universe models. We show that the model can be stabilized, however for configurations of crystal universe in the absence of fine-tuning, brane crystals are not equidistant, i.e., a "-+" pair is far away from adjacent "-+" pair, except for the fixed points of the orbifold, which differs from the assumptions taken in the literature.
