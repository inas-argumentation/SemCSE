THE EVOLUTION OF GENERALIZED CHAPLYGIN GAS
We consider the generalized Chaplygin gas (GCG) proposal for the unification of dark matter and dark energy with p = pde and ρ = ρdm+ρde. The unified equation of state for GCG has been obtained: . On the basis of the function χ(z), some cosmological quantities such as the fractional contributions of different components of the universe Ωi (i respectively denotes baryons, dark matter and dark energy) to the critical density, the equation of state for dark energy ωde, the deceleration parameter q are all obtained, which are consistent with observations. In addition, the transition from deceleration to acceleration is described in our model. We find that the behavior of GCG will be like ΛCDM in the future. So, it has been ruled out in our model that our universe will end up with Big Rip in the future.
