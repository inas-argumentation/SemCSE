Non-Abelian Chern-Simons quantum mechanics and non-Abelian Aharonov-Bohm effect
We construct a classical action for a system of N point-like sources which carry SU(2) non-Abelian charges coupled to non-Abelian Chern-Simons gauge fields, and we develop a quantum mechanics for them. Adopting the coherent state quantization and solving the Gauss` constraint in an appropriately chosen gauge, we obtain a quantum mechanical Hamiltonian given in terms of the Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov connection. Then we study the non-Abelian Aharonov-Bohm effect, employing the obtained Hamiltonian for a two-particle sector. An explicit evaluation of the differential cross section for the non-Abelian Aharonov-Bohm scattering is given. 48 refs., 2 figs.
