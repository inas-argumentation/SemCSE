Bubbling 1/2 BPS geometries and Penrose limits
We discuss how to take a Penrose limit in bubbling 1/2 Bogomol'nyi-Prasad-Sommerfield geometries at the stage of a single function z(x{sub 1},x{sub 2},y). By starting from the z of the AdS{sub 5}xS{sup 5}, we can directly derive that of the pp wave via the Penrose limit. In the course of the calculation, the function z for the pp wave with 1/R{sup 2} corrections is obtained. We see that it surely reproduces the pp wave with 1/R{sup 2} terms. We also investigate the pp wave with higher-order 1/R{sup 2} corrections. In addition, the Penrose limit in the configuration of the concentric rings is considered.
