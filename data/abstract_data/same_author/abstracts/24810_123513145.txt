Systematic Procedures for Identifying T Quarks in $\bar{p} p$ Collider Events With a Muon and Jets
Substantial rates are predicted for events containing a muon and jets resulting from W..-->..tb-bar, Z..-->..tt-bar, and tt-bar hadroproduction, with t..--> mu nu..b decay. We investigate selective cuts to reject backgrounds from bb-bar, cc-bar, and cs-bar production and to identify the t-quark events. For example, from a data sample containing 50 W/sup + -/..-->..e/sup + -/..nu.. events, we expect that clean samples of approximately 4 tb-bar-(or t-barb-) and conservatively 4 tt-bar-initiated ..mu../sup + -/+jets events can be selected for a t-quark mass below 45 GeV. We suggest methods for distinguishing the contributions and for determining m/sub t/.
