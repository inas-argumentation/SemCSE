Separation of isoscalar, isovector, orbital, and spin contributions in M1 transitions in mirror nuclei
Experimental data for $M1$ matrix elements in the $T=1/2$ mirror nuclei ${}^{27}\mathrm{Al}$ and ${}^{27}\mathrm{Si}$ are used to determine the isoscalar and isovector $M1$ components. The obtained isovector $M1$ matrix elements are compared to the Gamow-Teller matrix elements deduced from $\ensuremath{\beta}$ decay and the ${}^{27}\mathrm{Al}{(}^{3}\mathrm{He}{,t)}^{27}\mathrm{Si}$ charge exchange reaction to determine the contributions of the isovector spin and orbital components. The exchange current effects are taken into account. The results are compared with shell model calculations.
