A new consistency constraint on the validity of grand unified theories
It has been demonstrated recently by the author that the electric charge quantization is an intrinsic property of the Standard Model. It is shown here that this imposes a new consistency constraint on Grand Unified Theories. This could be used as a guiding principle to help pinpoint valid extensions of the Standard Model.
