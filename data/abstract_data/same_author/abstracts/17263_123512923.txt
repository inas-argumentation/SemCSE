Sequential fragmentation and rate oscillations in exclusive decays
We consider the decay of heavy quarkonia into two light mesons, and assume that quark-antiquark pairs are created sequentially when the final state is formed. We find quantum oscillations in the decay rate as a function of total energy. These oscillations can explain the energy dependence observed in the πϱ and K ∗ K channels at the J ψ and ψ ′ and predict the behaviour at the heavier Ψ family. In the mechanism we discuss, a given final state is produced only in a narrow range of the possible distances between the initial light quark and light antiquark.
