Why have Higgs particles not been found yet
It is suggested that within the effective potential approach, convex-ity and spontaneous symmetry breaking(SSB) make the Higgs bosons bizarre and experimentally nonexistent, a possible resolution of the Higgs puzzle. This observation might have quite some nontrivial consequences for the Standard Model (SM) and particle physics. By now, the experimentally nonexistence of Higgs particles predicted by the SM has become a puzzle in spite that the SM has been deemed as well established by the community of high energy physics (HEP), especially given that the estimated mass range of Higgs bosons has already become accessible [1]. There can be found a number of attempts to explain this puzzling situation. There is even modeling work trying to provide masses for intermediate gauge bosons without introducing Higgs model [2].
