Chiral symmetry restoration at finite temperature in the SU(3) × SU(3) sigma model
Abstract The temperature dependence of the effective potential of the SU (3) & SU (3) σ model is studied in the one-loop approximation. In the chiral limit (mquarkcurrent = 0), and with masses mη′0 = 850 (from the anomal), mδ0 = 950 MeV, and mϵ0 = 800 MeV in this limit, it is found that the chiral symmetry restoration transition in first order and occurs at a temperature of ≅180 MeV.
