Weak vector and axial-vector form factors in the chiral constituent quark model with configuration mixing
The effects of SU(3) symmetry breaking and configuration mixing have been investigated for the weak vector and axial-vector form factors in the chiral constituent quark model ({chi}CQM) for the strangeness changing as well as strangeness conserving semileptonic octet baryon decays in the nonperturbative regime. The results are in good agreement with existing experimental data and also show improvement over other phenomenological models.
