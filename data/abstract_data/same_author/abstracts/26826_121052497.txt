The phase diagram of three degenerate flavour QCD for small density from imaginary chemical potential
We present results for the phase structure of three degenerate flavour QCD at finite temperature T and small baryon chemical potential μB in the Wilson fermion formulation. By using the imaginary chemical potential μI combined with an analytic continuation to real quark chemical potential μ, we obtain the transition temperature as a function of small μB. We attempt to determine the nature of transition with the imaginary chemical potential by monitoring finite size scaling which reveals that the transition at aμI = 0.0 is a rapid crossover. Our simulations are performed at points where the ratios of pseudo-scalar meson mass to the vector meson mass mπ/mρ = 0.934(2).
