KM3NeT: designing a cubic-kilometre scale neutrino telescope for the mediterranean
The KM3NeT consortium is currently designing and planning an interdisciplinary deep-sea observatory which will incorporate a cubic-kilometre scale neutrino telescope to be deployed in the Mediterranean Sea. This poster reviews the challenges facing such a venture and outlines some of the technological solutions under review. The scientific programme that can be addressed by a detector of this scale is presented and the potential performance of KM3NeT device is summarised.
