Neural network generated parametrizations of deeply virtual Compton form factors
We have generated a parametrization of the Compton form factor (CFF) $ \mathcal{H} $ based on data from deeply virtual Compton scattering (DVCS) using neural networks. This approach offers an essentially model-independent fitting procedure, which provides realistic uncertainties. Furthermore, it facilitates propagation of uncertainties from experimental data to CFFs. We assumed dominance of the CFF $ \mathcal{H} $ and used HERMES data on DVCS off unpolarized protons. We predict the beam charge-spin asymmetry for a proton at the kinematics of the COMPASS II experiment.
