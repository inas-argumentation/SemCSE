Single charged Higgs boson production in polarized photon collisions and the probe of new physics
We study single charged Higgs boson production in photon-photon collisions as a probe of the new dynamics of Higgs interactions. This is particularly important when the mass ${(M}_{{H}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}})$ of charged Higgs bosons ${(H}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}})$ is relatively heavy and above the kinematic limit of the pair production ${(M}_{{H}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}}g\sqrt{s}/2).$ We analyze the cross sections of single charged Higgs boson production from the photon-photon fusion processes $\ensuremath{\gamma}\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{\ensuremath{\gamma}}{\ensuremath{\tau}}^{\ensuremath{-}}\overline{\ensuremath{\nu}}{H}^{+}$ and $\ensuremath{\gamma}\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{\ensuremath{\gamma}}b\overline{c}{H}^{+},$ as motivated by the minimal supersymmetric standard model and the dynamical top-color model. We find that the cross sections at such a $\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{\gamma}$ collider can be sufficiently large even for ${M}_{{H}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}}g\sqrt{s}/2,$ and is typically one to two orders of magnitude higher than that at its parent ${e}^{\ensuremath{-}}{e}^{+}$ collider. We further demonstrate that the polarized photon beams can provide an important means to determine the chirality structure of Higgs Yukawa interactions with the fermions.
