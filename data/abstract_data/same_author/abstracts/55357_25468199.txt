Edge vulnerability parameters of split graphs
Abstract A graph is called a split graph if its vertex set can be partitioned into a clique and an independent set. In this work, we investigate three vulnerability parameters of split graphs when edges are removed, i.e., edge-connectivity, edge-toughness and edge-integrity. It is proved that, for a noncomplete connected split graph G , its edge-connectivity is δ ( G ) , and its edge-toughness is min { δ ( G ) , | E ( G ) | | V ( G ) | − 1 } , where δ ( G ) , V ( G ) and E ( G ) , are the minimum degree, the vertex set and the edge set of G , respectively. Furthermore, we show that the edge-integrity of a noncomplete connected split graph equals its order when its minimum degree is greater than half of the size of its largest clique.
