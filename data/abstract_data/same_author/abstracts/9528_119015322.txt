Anomalies, Gauss laws, and Page charges in M-theory
We review the E 8 model of the M-theory 3-form, and its applications to anomaly cancellation, Gauss laws, quantization of Page charge, and the 5-brane partition function. We discuss the potentially problematic behavior of the model under parity. To cite this article: G.W. Moore, C. R. Physique 6 (2005).
