Nonhamiltonian approach to conformal quantum field theory
A requirement of completeness of the operator set involved in the theory at small distances is formulated which replaces the unitarity condition of the S- matrix in the usual theory. Explicit expressions are obtained for the contribution of the intermediate state with a definite symmetry in the Wightman function. Together with the localization'' condition the completeness condition leads to a set of algebraic equations for the anomalous dimensionalities and interaction constants which may be regarded as the sum rule for these quantities. The approximate solutions of the equations found in 4- epsilon dimension space yield results which are equivalent to the Hamiltonian approach. (auth)
