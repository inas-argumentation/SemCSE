Spinor field realizations of the half-integer W2,s strings
The grading Becchi–Rouet–Stora–Tyutin (BRST) method gives a way to construct the integer W 2 , s strings, where the BRST charge is written as Q B = Q 0 + Q 1 . Using this method, we reconstruct the nilpotent BRST charges Q 0 for the integer W 2 , s strings and the half-integer W 2 , s strings. Then we construct the exact grading BRST charge with spinor fields and give the new realizations of the half-integer W 2 , s strings for the cases of s = 3 / 2 , 5/2, and 7/2.
