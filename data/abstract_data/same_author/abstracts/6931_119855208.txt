RG flows in generalized supersymmetric and fractional supersymmetric sine-Gordon theories
We study RG flows of generalized supersymmetric and fractional supersymmetric sine-Gordon models. It is shown that in the large p limit the difference of central changes at UV and IR fixed points is twice as much as the minimal amount in the generalized supersymmetric case, and K times is the generalized fractional supersymmetric case, respectively. It agrees with the known CFT calculations.
