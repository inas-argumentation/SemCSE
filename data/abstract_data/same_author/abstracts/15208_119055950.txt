Testing factorization in B ---> D(*)X decays
In QCD the amplitude for ${B}^{0}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{D}^{(*)+}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ factorizes in the large ${N}_{c}$ limit or in the large energy limit $Q\ensuremath{\gg}{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ where $Q={{m}_{b}{,m}_{c}{,m}_{b}\ensuremath{-}{m}_{c}}.$ Data also suggest factorization in the exclusive processes $\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{B}{D}^{*}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{0}$ and $\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{B}{D}^{*}\ensuremath{\omega}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}};$ however, by themselves neither a large ${N}_{c}$ nor a large Q can account for this. We give exact large ${N}_{c}$ relations among isospin amplitudes for $\overline{B}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{D}^{(*)}X$ and $\overline{B}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{D}^{(*)}{D}^{(*)}X,$ which can be used to test factorization through exclusive or inclusive measurements. Noting that the condition for large energy release in ${B}^{0}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{D}^{+}{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ is enforced by the small velocity limit, ${m}_{b}\ensuremath{\gg}{m}_{b}\ensuremath{-}{m}_{c}\ensuremath{\gg}\ensuremath{\Lambda},$ we propose that the combined large ${N}_{c}$ and SV limits justify factorization in $\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{B}{D}^{(*)}X.$ This combined limit is tested with the $\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{B}{D}^{*}X$ inclusive decay spectrum measured by CLEO.
