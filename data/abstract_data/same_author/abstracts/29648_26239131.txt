Renormalization of the delta expansion in curved space-time.
Renormalization of a recently proposed {delta} expansion for a self-interacting scalar field theory in curved space-time is examined. The explicit calculation is carried out up to order {delta}{sup 2}, which indicates that the expansion is renormalizable, but reduces to essentially the {lambda}{phi}{sup 4} theory when the cutoff is removed. A similar conclusion has been reached in a previous paper where the case of flat space-time is considered.
