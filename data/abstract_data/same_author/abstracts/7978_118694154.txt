Production of Dimuons by Pions and Protons at Fermilab
In this lecture I shall describe an experiment in progress1 at the Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory (FNAL) to study the production of μ+μ− pairs in hadron-hadron collisions. So far, we have used beams of 225 — GeV π+, π− and protons incident on carbon and tin targets for most of the data. In an earlier run, a 150 GeV positive beam was used with a beryllium target. The results I report of the 150 GeV run are final; those at 225 GeV are preliminary.
