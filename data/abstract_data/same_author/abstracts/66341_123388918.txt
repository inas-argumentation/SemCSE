Magnetohydrodynamic Stability of Boundary Layers along a Flat Plate in the Presence of a Transverse Magnetic Field
The purpose of the paper is to consider theoretically first the behavior of two-dimensional steady laminar boundary layer flow of an incompressible, electrically conducting fluid past a semi-infinite flat plate in the presence of a transverse magnetic field by using the difference-differential method. The neutral stability curves for wavelike disturbances of Tollmien-Schlichting type are then presented for the velocity profiles obtained above in the wide range of magnetic parameter (mx). The results show that the skin friction and the critical Reynolds number decrease with increasing mx, so that the MHD boundary layer becomes less stable.
