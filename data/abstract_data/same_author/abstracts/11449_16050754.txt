Cosmological constant and soft terms in supergravity.
Some of the soft supersymmetry breaking parameters in the hidden sector supergravity model depend on the expectation value of the hidden sector scalar potential, [l angle][ital V][sub [ital h]][r angle], whose tree level value is equal to the tree level cosmological constant. The current practice of calculating soft parameters assumes that [l angle][ital V][sub [ital h]][r angle]=0. Quantum correction to the cosmological constant can differ from the correction to [l angle][ital V][sub [ital h]][r angle] by an amount of order [ital m][sub 3/2][sup 2][ital M][sub Pl][sup 2]/8[pi]. This implies that, for the vanishing cosmological constant, the [l angle][ital V][sub [ital h]][r angle]-dependent parts of soft terms can be sizable, and hence the supergravity phenomenology should be accordingly modified.
