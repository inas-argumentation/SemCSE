Lepton mixing in W+ W− pair production with polarized e+ e− collisions
Abstract We investigate the effects of neutrino and electron mixing with exotic heavy leptons on the reaction e + e − → W + W − , in the framework of electroweak models based on E 6 gauge symmetry. We also discuss possibilities of distinguishing exotic-lepton mixing from Z  Z ′ mixing, by considering polarized cross sections, and derive the typical limits obtainable at future e + e − colliders, which in general are considerably more severe than the present ones.
