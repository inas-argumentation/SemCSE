Search for resonance structures in inclusive charged pion spectra from p̄p annihilation at rest
The charged pion momentum spectra from pp annihilation at rest have been measured with high statistics. A search for structures finds four narrow lines which are identified with the absorption and decay processes of kaons stopping in the target. Limits of 1‐6×10−4/p (90% C.L.) are placed on the yield of a narrow state in the mass range 1000–1660 MeV.
