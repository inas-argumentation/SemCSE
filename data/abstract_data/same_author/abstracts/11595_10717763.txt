Quark mass dependence of the hadron spectrum in holographic QCD
We compute a shift of baryon mass spectra due to quark masses in perturbation, in the Sakai-Sugimoto model of holographic QCD. We find the shift for the ground state nucleons to be {delta}M=4.1m{sub {pi}}{sup 2} GeV{sup -1}, which is consistent with the current lattice QCD result. We predict the same value of the shift for N(1535), while a larger value 7.7m{sub {pi}}{sup 2} GeV{sup -1} for Roper N(1440). We also present some evidences that the shifts of the vector meson masses are suppressed in the large 't Hooft coupling limit.
