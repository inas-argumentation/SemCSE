Measurements of Parity-Violation Parameters at SLD
We present direct measurements of the parity-violation parameters A{sub b}, A{sub c}, and A{sub s} at the Z{sup 0} resonance with the SLD detector. The measurements are based on approximately 530 k hadronic Z{sup 0} events collected in 1993-98. Obtained results are A{sub b} = 0.914 {+-} 0.024 (SLD combined: preliminary), A{sub c}, = 0.635 {+-} 0.027 (SLD combined: preliminary), and A{sub s} = 0.895 {+-} 0.066(stat.) {+-} 0.062(sys.).
