Interpolation of SUSY quantum mechanics
Interpolation of two adjacent Hamiltonians in SUSY quantum mechanics Hs def = (1 − s)A † A + sAA † , 0 ≤ s ≤ 1 is discussed together with related operators. For a wide variety of shape-invariant degree one quantum mechanics and their ‘discrete’ counterparts, the interpolation Hamiltonian is also shape-invariant, that is it takes the same form as the original Hamiltonian with shifted coupling constant(s).
