Theory of the pair meter for high energy muon measurements
Abstract A systematic outline of the theory of the pair meter — a method of measuring the muon energy via multiple production of electron-positron pairs in thick layers of matter — is given. Possible variants of the technique of derivation of the muon energy in the pair meter are considered in unified terms on the basis of the maximum likelihood method. Analytical expressions for the energy measurement error have been obtained. An asymptotic limit of the measurement error at high energies has been found. The important role of the background process — the inelastic interaction of muons with nuclei — is elucidated. The problem of optimization of the pair meter for measuring the spectrum of cosmic ray muons is formulated and solved. The results of numerical simulation for the real pair meter are compared with the experimental data of the pair meter of the MUTRON arrangement. The pair meter technique can be used for the spectrometry of muons in the energy region ≳10 12 eV in new experimental arrangements.
