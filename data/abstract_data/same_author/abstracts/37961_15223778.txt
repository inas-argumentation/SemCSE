THE PROGENITOR OF SUPERNOVA 2004 DJ IN A STAR CLUSTER
The progenitor of type II-plateau supernova (SN) 2004dj is identified with a supergiant in a compact star cluster known as ”Sandage Star 96” (S96) in the nearby spiral galaxy NGC 2403, which was fortuitously imaged as part of the Beijing-Arizona-Taiwan-Connecticut (BATC) Multicolor Sky Survey from Feb 1995 to Dec 2003 prior to SN 2004dj. The superior photometry of BATC images for S96, taken with 14 intermediate-band filters covering 3000 − 10000Å, unambiguously establishes the star cluster nature of S96 with an age of ∼ 20Myr, a reddening of E(B−V ) ∼ 0.35 mag and a total mass of ∼ 96, 000M⊙. The compact star cluster nature of S96 is also consistent with the lack of light variations in the past decade. The SN progenitor is estimated to have a main-sequence mass of ∼12M⊙. The comparison of our intermediate-band data of S96 with the post-outburst photometry obtained as the SN has significantly dimmed, may hopefully conclusively establish the nature of the progenitor.
