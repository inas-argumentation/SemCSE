Accelerated polynomial approximation of finite order entire functions by growth reduction
Let f be an entire function of positive order and finite type. The subject of this note is the convergence acceleration of polynomial approximants of f by incorporating information about the growth of f(z) for z → ∞. We consider near polynomial approximation on a compact plane set K, which should be thought of as a circle or a real interval. Our aim is to find sequences (f n ) n of functions which are the product of a polynomial of degree < n and an easy computable second factor and such that (f n ) n converges essentially faster to f on K than the sequence (P n * ) n of best approximating polynomials of degree ≤ n. The resulting method, which we call Reduced Growth method (RG-method) is introduced in Section 2. In Section 5, numerical examples of the RG-method applied to the complex error function and to Bessel functions are given.
