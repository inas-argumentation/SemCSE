The Gauss-Knörrer map for the Rosochatius dynamical system
We found a nonlinear integrable system dual to the Rosochatius dynamic system in arbitrary dimensions by means of the Gauss-Knorrer map. The relationship between the Rosochatius system and its dual system is elucidated from the point of view of constrained Hamiltonian systems. Dirac brackets for dynamical variables and conserved quantities for the dual system are derived explicitly.
