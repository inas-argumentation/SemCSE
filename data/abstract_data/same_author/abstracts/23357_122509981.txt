Search for direct electron production in p-p and p-Be collisions at 12 GeV/c
We have made a systematic search for direct e/sup +/ and e/sup -/ production in p-p and p-Be collisions at 12 GeV/c incident proton momentum. Data were collected at c.m. angles of 20/sup 0/, 35/sup 0/, 63/sup 0/, and 84/sup 0/. We find no evidence for the previously reported rise in the ratio e/..pi.. at low momentum transfer. The data are consistent with zero direct electron production from other than previously known sources.
