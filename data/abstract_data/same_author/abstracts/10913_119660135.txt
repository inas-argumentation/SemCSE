ρ exchange and density matrices in baryon resonance production
SummaryWe propose and explore two alternative schemes for the ρ Regge coupling to higher-spin baryons. The first is based on the ρ-γ analogy, the second on a principle of minimal transversity flip. The schemes coincide in their predictions for the density matrices in

$$\pi \mathcal{N} \to \pi \Delta $$

, where they correspond to the Stodolsky-Sakurai model, but in general yield significantly different predictions. These are spelled out in detail for the particular case of

$$\mathcal{N}^ * _{\frac{5}{2} + } $$
