Fermion Bound States by the Method of Stationary Phase
The stationary phase method is applied to gauge theory path integrals, taking into account the fermion determinant. This approximation is shown to lead to a unique bound state equation in QED. In the nonrelativistic limit, the equation reduces to the usual Schrodinger form. In 1 + 1 dimensions it leads to "bag"-type wave functions and an energy spectrum which agrees with known exact results. An analytic expression is given for the wave function of states with two massless fermions connected by an unbroken string.
