Baer Involutions in Translation Planes Admitting Large Elation Groups
This article is concerned with compatibility of planar and non-planar involutions in translation planes π of even order q. It is shown that if β is a Baer 2-group and ℰ is an elation group of order q which normalizes β then ¦β¦ ≤ 2. This extends the analogous theorem of Ganley for semifield planes.
