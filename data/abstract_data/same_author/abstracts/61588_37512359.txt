Calculation of creatinine clearance based on unadjusted body weight leads to errors in renal and heart failure patients.
To the Editor:

Renal insufficiency characterizes a high-risk heart failure patient subset. McAlister et al,1 in a prospective study of a cohort of 754 heart failure patients, found a high prevalence of renal failure (56% with creatinine clearance [CCr] <60 mL/min).
