Most general spherically symmetric M2-branes and type-IIB strings
We obtain the most general spherically symmetric M2-branes and type-IIB strings, with ${\mathbb{R}}^{1,2}\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}SO(8)$ and ${\mathbb{R}}^{1,1}\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}SO(8)$ isometries, respectively. We find that there are 12 different classes of M2-branes, and we study their curvature properties. In particular, we obtain new smooth M2-brane wormholes that connect two asymptotic regions: one is flat and the other can be either flat or ${\mathrm{AdS}}_{4}\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{S}^{7}$. We find that these wormholes are traversable with certain timelike trajectories. We also obtain the most general Ricci-flat solutions in five dimensions with ${\mathbb{R}}^{1,1}\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}SO(3)$ isometries.
