New formulation for the lattice-fermion derivative. Locality and chirality without spectrum doubling.
We present a formulation for the lattice fermion derivative which is both -local and explicitly chiral. Provided that the continuum and infinite volume limits are taken properly the formulation avoids spectrum doubling, and gives a satisfactory weak coupling perturbation theory for the case of Yukawa-coupled scalars. We find that one can avoid the impli. cations of the Nielsen-Ninomiya theorem’ because, as with most no-go theorems, its proof requires an implicit assumption; namely that one should take the limit of infinite volume before taking the continuum limit. We present a method which explicitly demonstrates these properties. Our formulation involves an additional parameter E which vanishes in the continuum limit.
