Gluonic corrections to spin dependent weak structure functions
We compute gluonic corrections to the spin dependent weak structure functions g1, g3 and g4. g1WP is found o be directly proportional to the singlet part of g1μP and is ∑i(Δqi + Δqi−αsΔg/2π). In the gluon sector, g3 and g4 are both free from infrared and collinear divergences since they do not receive any gluonic corrections. Hence the Callan-Gross relation in the weak sector is nor corrected by the g→q, q subprocess. g3WP measures Δuv + Δdv. The distribution functions are identified with corresponding OPE matrix elements.
