Low momentum scattering of the Dirac particlewith an asymmetric cusp potential
.We study the exact solutions of the bound and scattering states of the one-dimensional Dirac equation with an asymmetric cusp potential and derive the condition of the supercriticality for this quantum system. We find that the scattering properties are invariant under reflection of the potential's shape, and the supercritical value for the potential amplitude V0 varies with the degree of the potential asymmetry.
