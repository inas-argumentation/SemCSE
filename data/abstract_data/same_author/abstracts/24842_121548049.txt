Detection of small angle elastic scattering events at high energy based on a multiscintillator target
We describe the prototype of a target sensitive to low energy protons suitable for the study of high energy elastic scattering in the small momentum transfer region. It consists of a set of thin plastic scintillator slices, separated in space and optically coupled to individual photomultipliers. We report the performance of this device in measuring πp elastic scattering in the momentum transfer region between −0.012 and −0.004 GeV2 at 4 GeV/c in a π− beam at the CERN PS.
