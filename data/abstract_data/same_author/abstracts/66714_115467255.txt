Infinite Dimensional Modules for Finite Groups
In the first lecture, I give an introduction to some of the questions currently under investigation in the theory of finite dimensional modular representations of finite groups. In the second lecture, I give some idea of how the theory of infinite dimensional modules has recently had an impact on our understanding of the category of finite dimensional modules. In the third lecture, I present recent progress on understanding phantom maps. These may be viewed as the aspects of infinite dimensional module theory which are invisible from the point of view of the finite dimensional modules.
