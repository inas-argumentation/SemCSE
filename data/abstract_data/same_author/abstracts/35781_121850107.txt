Affine Connections and Two-Dimensional Topological Gravity on a Torus
The topological conformal algebra for topological gravity is considered on higher-genus Riemann surfaces. Associated with the U(1) anomaly the affine connection is automatically introduced into the algebra. The affine connection guarantees coordinate-independence of the algebra. Since the affine connection is admitted only on a torus, the algebra with the affine connection can be defined only on the same surface. The U(1) anomaly d is given by d= −2−2y, where y is a parameter contained in the model. However, the value of y is fixed to be y = 0 from the condition that the algebra is coordinate-independent. Some types of topological gravity are excluded on a torus
