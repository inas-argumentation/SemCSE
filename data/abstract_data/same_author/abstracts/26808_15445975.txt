Exactly Solvable 'Discrete' Quantum Mechanics; Shape Invariance, Heisenberg Solutions, Annihilation-Creation Operators and Coherent States
Various examples of exactly solvable ‘discrete’ quantum mechanics are explored explicitly with emphasis on shape invariance, Heisenberg operator solutions, annihilation-creation operators, the dynamical symmetry algebras and coherent states.
