Lepton-nucleon scattering in the relativistic oscillator model
The scattering of charged leptons by a system of three quarks locked in by a relativistic oscillator potential is considered. The calculations are performed in the rest frame of the target, which allows the derivation of the explicit x and Q/sup 2/ dependences of the nucleon structure functions F/sub 1/(x,Q/sup 2/) and F/sub 2/(x,Q/sup 2/) and the quantity R = sigma/sub L//sigma/sub T/ in the region of moderate Q/sup 2/ values.
