Heavy quarkonium production in a strong magnetic field
It is well known that in noncentral heavy-ion collisions a transient strong magnetic field is generated in the direction perpendicular to the reaction plane. The maximal strength of this field is estimated to be eB m 2 0:02 GeV 2 at the RHIC and eB 15m 2 0:3 GeV 2 at the LHC. We investigate the eects of a strong magnetic field on B and D mesons, focusing on the changes of the energy levels and the masses of the bound states.
