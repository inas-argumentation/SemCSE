Empirically Successful Automated Reasoning: Applications Issue
With improving technology, theorem proving and related methods are being successfully applied to larger problems and used in more domains. This special issue of the Journal of Automated Reasoning contains papers about empirically successful automated reasoning applications, and the previous companion issue contains papers about empirically successful automated reasoning systems. The papers in these special issues focus on “really working” systems and applications, rather than on theoretical ideas that have not yet been translated into working software. The special issues arose out of the CADE-20 Workshop on Empirically Successful Classical Automated Reasoning (ESCAR). Participants of the workshop and other authors were invited to submit contributions for the special issues. Thirty-one papers were submitted, 13 about systems and 18 about applications. All papers were reviewed by at least three expert reviewers. Ten papers were accepted, 4 about systems and 6 about applications. Thanks go to the original workshop program committee and to the additional reviewers for the special issues.
