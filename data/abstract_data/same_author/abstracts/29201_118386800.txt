Proper identification of the gluon spin
Properties of the recently proposed gauge-invariant gluon spin S(g) are studied and compared to the usually defined "gluon polarization" Delta g. By explicit 1-loop calculations in a quark state. it is found that S(g) = 5/9 Delta g. Furthermore, 4/5 of S(g) can actually be identified as a "static-field" contribution and shown to cancel exactly an analogous static term in the gluon orbital angular momentum L(g), leaving S(g) + L(g) unaltered. These observations suggest that by an alternative definition, the gluon contribution to the nucleon spin may be drastically smaller than in the conventional wisdom. (C) 2011 Elsevier B.V.
