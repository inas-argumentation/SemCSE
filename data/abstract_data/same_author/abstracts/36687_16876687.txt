TRAILING EDGE UNIFICATION VIA AN INTERMEDIATE PATI–SALAM GROUP
We demonstrate to two-loop order that an intermediate symmetrically embedded Pati–Salam SU(2)L×SU(2)R×SU(4) level of symmetry is all that is necessary to accommodate empirical values of α(Mz), αs(Mz) and sin2θw(Mz) within a grand unification context but with a high (1014 GeV) intermediate mass scale and with a concomitant higher GUT scale.
