CP nonconservation and rare processes in an S4 model of permutation symmetry
Further implications of a model based on permutation symmetry, that predicts 25 GeV ⩽ m t ⩽ 35 GeV and mixing angles consistent with τ B , are worked out in detail. The model is shown to be of the superweak type as far as CP violation is concerned, with δ = O and CP violation arising through flavor-changing Higgs exchange. We calculate CP and mixing parameters for K, D, B and B s systems; the electric dipole moments of different quarks and leptons; and rates for rare decays.
