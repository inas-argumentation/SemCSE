The Gauge Dual of Gauged N=8 Supergravity Theory
The most general SU(3)-singlet space of gauged supergravity in four-dimensions is studied recently. The SU(3)-invariant six scalar fields are realized by six real four-forms. A family of holographic supersymmetric RG flows on M2-branes in three-dimensions is described. This family of flows is driven by three independent mass parameters from the theory and is controlled by two IR fixed points, -invariant one and -invariant one. The generic flow with arbitrary mass parameters is supersymmetric and reaches to the fixed point where the three masses become identical. A particular supersymmetric SU(3)-preserving RG flow from the -invariant fixed point to the -invariant fixed point is also discussed.
