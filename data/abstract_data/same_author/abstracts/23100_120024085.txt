Relations among the radiative decay widths of charmonium states from superconvergent sum rules
Relations among the decay widths for ${\ensuremath{\psi}}^{\ensuremath{'}}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\chi}}_{J}\ensuremath{\gamma}$,${\ensuremath{\chi}}_{J}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\gamma}$ (where ${\ensuremath{\chi}}_{J}$ is one of the $^{3}P$ charmonium states) are derived from two Drell-Hearn-type sum rules. The relations, which make no use of the nonrelativistic $c\overline{c}$ picture of the ${\ensuremath{\psi}}^{\ensuremath{'}}$, $\ensuremath{\chi}$, and $\ensuremath{\psi}$ states, predict${\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{2}^{\ensuremath{'}}:{\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{1}^{\ensuremath{'}}:{\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{0}^{\ensuremath{'}}=1.2:1.3:1.0$, ${\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{2}:{\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{1}:{\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{0}=0.9:0.8:1.0$, where ${\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{J}^{\ensuremath{'}}\ensuremath{\equiv}\frac{\ensuremath{\Gamma}({\ensuremath{\psi}}^{\ensuremath{'}}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{\ensuremath{\chi}}_{J}\ensuremath{\gamma})}{(2J+1){k}_{J}^{\ensuremath{'}3}}$,${\ensuremath{\gamma}}_{J}\ensuremath{\equiv}\frac{\ensuremath{\Gamma}({\ensuremath{\chi}}_{J}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\gamma})}{k_{J}^{}{}_{}{}^{3}}$.
