Proton-proton correlations, the fireball model, and inclusive pion spectrum
Two proton correlations seen in the reaction /sup 12/C on /sup 12/C are calculated in the framework of the two fireball model. Substantial correlation is obtained. It is necessary to do the calculation in phase space, i.e., to use the microcanonical ensemble rather than the grand canonical ensemble. A model calculation shows that the latter will also overestimate pion production in the high energy tail compared to an energy- and momentum conserving phase space calculation.
