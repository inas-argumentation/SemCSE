Gauge Models with Spontaneously Broken Local Supersymmetry
An effective low-energy lagrangian for gauge theories based on local supersymmetry spontaneously broken at an intermediate energy between the weak interaction and the Planck scale is obtained. The derivation uses the general coupling of the Yang-Mills matter system to n = 1 supergravity. As illustrative examples of this framework we exhibit realistic models of supersymmetric QED and of the electroweak theory with supersymmetry breaking induced by purely gravitational effects.
