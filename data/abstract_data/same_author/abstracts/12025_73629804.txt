Status of the search for muonless events in the broad band neutrino beam at NAL
The current status and results of the search for muonless events in the broad band neutrino beam at NAL are presented. An excess of events unaccompanied by muon is observed which cannot be explained by instrumental effects. The ratio of the unaccompanied events to the customary charged current events is 0.20+-0.05 for the mixture of $nu$ and anti $nu$ in this beam. (FR)
