Baryons and Skyrmions in QCD with Quarks in Higher Representations
We study the baryonic sector of QCD with quarks in the two-index symmetric or antisymmetric representation. The minimal gauge invariant state that carries baryon number cannot be identified with the Skyrmion of the low-energy chiral effective Lagrangian. Mass, statistics, and baryon number do not match. We carefully investigate the properties of the minimal baryon in the large N limit and we find that it is unstable under formation of bound states with higher baryonic number.
