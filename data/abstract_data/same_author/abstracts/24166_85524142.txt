Constrained exceptional supersymmetric standard model with a Higgs signal near 125 GeV
We study the parameter space of the constrained exceptional supersymmetric standard model (cE6SSM) consistent with a Higgs signal near 125 GeV and the LHC searches for squarks, gluinos and Z 0 .
