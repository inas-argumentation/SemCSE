Nonleptonic Interactions of Kaons in the Quark Confinement Model
Nonleptonic, electromagnetic decays of K-mesons and K°–K° transition are treated with the effective Hamiltonians with ΔS = 1, 2. Matrix elements of four-quark operators are calculated in the framework of Quark Confinement Model. The account of the intermediate scalar mesons turns out to allow to explain ΔI = 1/2 rule. The account of intermediate scalar and pseudo-scalar states leads to experimental value of mass difference of KL°–KS° mesons. The obtained values for nonleptonic and electromagnetic decay widths as well as mass difference ΔmLS are in good agreement with experimental data.
