FAD, DCCs and Minimax
SummaryThere is a need to more fully exploit the intrinsic diversity of hadron-hadron collisions. The Full-Acceptance Detector (FAD) initiative was a proposal to do so at the SSC. With the demise of the SSC, it is useful to review the motivation for FAD, and the prospects for pursuing Full-Acceptance Physics at present and future hadron colliders. Particular attention is paid to T864 (MiniMax) at Fermilab, a chiral condensate search which has developed out the FAD enterprise.
