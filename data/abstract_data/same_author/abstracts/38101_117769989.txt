Black Halos and Dwarf Galaxies
Dissipation of infalling gas into a preexisting dark halo of nondissipative matter provides an attractive scenario for the formation of disk galaxies. Tidal torquing of gas against the gravitationally dominant dark halo can account for the observed angular momentum (Fall and Efstathiou, 1980), if halos form by hierarchical clustering in the expanding universe (cf. White and Rees, 1978). Moreover recent observations demonstrate that galactic bulges and the less luminous ellipticals are rotationally supported (Davies et al. 1983), suggesting that this model may have more widespread application.
