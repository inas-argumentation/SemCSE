Editorial on the special issue for the second Workshop on e-Business
This focused issue is dedicated to research papers addressing the theme of the Second Workshop on e-Business (WeB 2003): ‘‘Web Services: A New Paradigm of e-Business.’’ As stated in the call for paper of WeB 2003, Web services—an emerging computing paradigm—promise to drastically reduce application integration cost, shorten development time of new applications, and improve business connectivity. Web services, defined as ‘‘loosely coupled, reusable software components that semantically encapsulate functionality and are programmatically accessible over the Internet,’’ have quickly become the focal point of interests for both the industry and the academia. WeB 2003, a pre-ICIS workshop sponsored by the Special Interest Group on e-Business (SIGeBiz) of Association for Information Systems, provided a forum for researchers and practitioners in e-businesses to share research findings, foster new ideas, and develop future directions of Web services. We would like to thank all those who have contributed to WeB 2003 and this focused issue. This focused issue contains five well-written papers on various aspects of Web services. Zhangxi Lin, Sathya Ramanathan, and Huimin Zhao propose a Web services-based resource pricing system of interests to service providers in proposing new business models with optimized quality of services (QoS).
