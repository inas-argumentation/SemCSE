Discovering Statistically Significant Clusters by Using Iterative Genetic Algorithms in Gene Expression Data
This study presents an iterative genetic algorithm (GA) approach to find significant clusters in gene expression data. Many heuristic algorithms have been developed to cluster genes on microarray data. However, their results may be dominated by some predefined criteria, such as the number of clusters and initial mean points of clusters. Moreover, most current algorithms assign each one of genes in the dataset into some cluster while some of them are irrelevant to the interested conditions. To automatically cluster informative genes together and discard the irrelevant genes, we consider the clustering problem as maximizing correlativity among genes in a cluster. An iterative GA approach is proposed to find the tightest clusters (no irrelevant genes) and the most appropriate number of clusters. The proposed approach is applied on Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) dataset, and 21 significant clusters derived while 336 genes are discarded. The derived clusters have the deserving characteristics of intracluster tightness and intercluster distinctness. Furthermore, our method compares with K-Means heuristics under several clustering validation criteria and performs better than K-Means.
