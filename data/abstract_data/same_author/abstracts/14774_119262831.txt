Properties of X(3872) as a hadronic molecule with a negative parity
We discuss the possible interpretation of X(3872) as a DD ∗ hadronic molecule with J PC = 2 −+ . Using the phenomenological Lagrangian approach, we studied its radiative and strong decay properties. We find that our model with about 97.6% isospin zero component explains the existing data nicely.
