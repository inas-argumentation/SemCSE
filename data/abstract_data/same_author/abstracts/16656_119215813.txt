Symmetry violations in few-body reactions: old and new approaches
As an attempt to find a way to resolve the discrepancy between calculated by traditional methods PV e_ects and the available set of experimental data, we analyze the methods of calculations of symmetry violating e_ects in fewbody nuclear systems. The overview of methods of calculations of PV and TRIV e_ects in few-body neutron induced reactions is presented with the analysis of values of calculated parameters and their accuracy.
