A New Load Balancing Scheme for Distributed Multi-Agent Simulations
Disclosed is a heat sink clip assembly for releaseably attaching a heat sink to a DIP or similar electronic device package. The clip assembly includes a non-conductive attachment clip which extends between the leads on the underside of the DIP and means for latching the attachment clip to a heat sink disposed adjacent the opposite side of the electronic device package with the heat sink in intimate thermal contact with the electronic device package.
