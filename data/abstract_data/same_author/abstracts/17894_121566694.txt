Neutrino oscillations, rare decays and warped geometry
We discuss how the solar and atmospheric neutrino anomalies can be satisfactorily resolved via neutrino oscillations in a five-dimensional framework with warped geometry. In one particularly significant example, no new fields such as singlet (right-handed) neutrinos have to be introduced. We also consider rare processes including proton and neutrinoless double beta decays. The Kaluza–Klein excitations of the standard model particles are predicted to possess masses in the 10 TeV range.
