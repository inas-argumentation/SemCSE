SU(8) hyperunification: Composite quarks and leptons derivable from SO(8) extended supergravity
The electroweak, color, and hypercolor interactions are unified in an SU(8) gauge model which can be derived from an SO(8) extended supergravity. Some known quarks and leptons (${u}_{L}$,${u}_{R}$,${d}_{L}$,${e}_{R}$) are fundamental in the usual sense and the others (${d}_{R}$,${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{e}$,${e}_{L}$) are composite fermions composed of three hypercolored fermions. The total fermion spectrum is $({10}_{L}+{5}_{R})+({10}_{L}+{5}_{R})+({5}_{L}+{5}_{R}+1)$ under the SU(5) of the Georgi-Glashow model. The minimal model does not contain the disputed $t$ quark. Because of the hybrid nature of the quarks and leptons, there does not exist a conflict of ($g\ensuremath{-}2$) for leptons with observations. Nucleon decay is predicted to produce always a left-handed charged antilepton (${e}_{L}^{+}$ or ${\ensuremath{\mu}}_{L}^{+}$).
