Derivation of anomalous commutator and Jacobian from very general conditions.
Using only the equation of motion and the canonical commutator of anomalous gauge theories, we show clearly the relation between the Schwinger-Jackiw-Johnson (SJJ) term, the anomalous Jacobian, and the current-divergence anomaly by obtaining both the SJJ term and the anomalous Jacobian from the current-divergence anomaly.
