Constraints on neutrino mixing parameters by neutrinoless double beta decay experiments
Abstract Constraints on neutrino mixing parameters are extracted from results of neutrinoless double beta decay experiments and displayed as allowed regions on the m l -cos2 Θ 12 plane. For example, 0.05 eV(0eV) ≲ m l ≲ 1.85 eV is obtained for the normal (inverted) hierarchy by using the LMA best fit parameters and the 0νββ ersult announced late 2001.
