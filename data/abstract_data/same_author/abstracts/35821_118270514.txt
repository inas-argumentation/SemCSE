Covariant lagrangian formulation of interacting heterotic strings
A manifestly Lorentz-covarariant lagrangian formulation of the Neveu-Schwarz-Ramond heterotic string interacting with external fields is presented. The action is derived from the action for fully 26-dimensional semi-superstring by a simple ansatz. The internal space compactification occurs automatically from the action. The twist factors associated with the Kac-Moody algebra are involved in the theory without introducing them by hands.
