Progress in light hadron spectroscopy at BESIII
Based on the 225 million J / ψ events and 106 million ψ ′ events taken at the BESIII detector, the recent results on light hadron spectroscopy are presented, including the partial wave analysis of J / ψ → γ p p ¯ and ψ ′ → p p ¯ η , confirmation of X(1835) and observation of two new resonances in J / ψ → γ π + π − η ′ , observation of a new resonance X(1870) in J / ψ → ω π + π − η and the first observation of isospin-violating decay mode of η ( 1405 ) → f 0 ( 980 ) π 0 .
