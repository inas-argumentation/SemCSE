Chiral-symmetry and strange four-quark matrix elements☆
We consider the matrix elements of the left-handed flavor-conserving four-quark operators in the nucleon and pion states. Using chiral symmetry, we derive relationships among these matrix elements. We argue that the δI = 1 2 rule of hyperon and kaon non-leptonic weak decay implies a possible large strange-quark content in the nucleon and pion.
