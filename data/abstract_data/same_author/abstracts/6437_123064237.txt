The charged trigger of the experiment E835 at Fermilab
Fermilab experiment E835 studies the charmonium states primarily through their electromagnetic decays. A kinematical charged trigger has been designed to select the electromagnetic final states e + e − and e + e − X and the hadronic decay c c → φφ →4 K ± . A detailed description of the charged trigger is presented here. The design, its implementation and the performances are given.
