Solutions of the Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equation with rational isospins and the reduction to the minimal models
Abstract In the spirit of the quantum hamiltonian reduction we establish a relation between the chiral n -point functions, as well as the equations governing them, of the A 1 (1) WZNW conformal theory and the corresponding Virasoro minimal models. The WZNW correlators are described as solutions of the Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equations with rational levels and isospins. The technical tool exploited are certain relations in twisted cohomology. The results extend to arbitrary level k + 2 ≠ 0 and isospin values of the type J = j − j′ (k + 2), 2j, 2j′ ϵ Z + .
