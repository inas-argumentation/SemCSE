Symplectic Treatment of a Finite Crossing Angle in the Beam-Beam Collision
We introduce a symplectic method to handle a large and finite crossing angle in the beam-beam interaction. This method has been implemented in a parallel computer program to simulate three-dimensional effects in the beam-beam interaction. Our simulation results are compared with the known analytical solutions, the simulations using the Lorentz boost and experimental observations.
