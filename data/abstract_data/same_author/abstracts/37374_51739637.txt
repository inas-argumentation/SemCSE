EXISTENCE AND PROPERTIES OF AN EQUILIBRIUM STATE WITH BEAM-BEAM COLLISIONS
The equilbrium phase distribution of stored colliding electron beams is studied from the viewpoint of Vlasov-Fokker-Planck (VFP) theory. Numerical integration of the VFP system in one degree of freedom revealed a nearly Gaussian equilibrium with non-diagonal covariance matrix. This result is reproduced approximately in an analytic theory based on linearization of the beam-beam force. Analysis of an integral equation for the equilbrium distribution, without linearization, establishes the existence of a unique equilbrium at su ciently small current. The role of damping and quantum noise is clari ed through a new representation of the propagator of the linear Fokker-Planck equation with harmonic force.
