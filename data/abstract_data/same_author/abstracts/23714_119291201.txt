Asymmetric left-right model and the top pair forward-backward asymmetry
The forward-backward asymmetry measurement in top-pair production at the Tevatron is about $2\ensuremath{\sigma}$ from the standard model prediction. We propose an asymmetric left-right model, which includes a ${W}^{\ensuremath{'}}$ boson with a right-handed coupling of down to top quark, and a ${Z}^{\ensuremath{'}}$ boson with diagonal couplings to the up, top, and down quarks with ${M}_{{W}^{\ensuremath{'}}}\ensuremath{\approx}175\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{GeV}$ and ${M}_{{Z}^{\ensuremath{'}}}\ensuremath{\approx}900\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{GeV}$. The model accounts for the asymmetry while remaining consistent with the top-pair total cross section and invariant mass distribution.
