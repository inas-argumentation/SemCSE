On the relevance of the Dirac equation to the scattering of medium-energy nucleons from nuclei☆
The differences between relativistic and nonrelativistic descriptions of medium-energy nucleon—nucleus scattering are discussed. Special attention is paid to a recently developed Dirac optical model in which we point out an ambiguity resulting from the use of an incomplete representation for relativistic nucleon—nucleon scattering amplitude. This ambiguity is reflected in contributions from negative-energy states and in the implementation of the impulse approximation. We also review how a nonrelativistic optical potential can be modified to include relativistic projectile kinematics.
