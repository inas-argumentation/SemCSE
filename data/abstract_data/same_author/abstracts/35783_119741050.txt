Geometric and BRST Formulations of Interacting Strings
The total Ricci curvature of Bowick-Rajeev vector bundles over Diff SI/SI is shown to corre­ spond exactly to the element of BRST charge squared. Some properties of the "interacting" BRST charge are also pointed out. 6 ) In this case it has been found that the requirement of vanishing of the total curvature above leads not only to the critical dimension, but also to constraint equations for vertex operators. The constraint equations are equivalent to the closure of "interacting" Virasoro algebra. 7 ) The purpose of this paper is to consider a relationship between the geometric and the BRST formulations for interacting bosonic strings. We first recalculate the total curvature G above by using the vector bundle with the ghost Fock space as the fiber instead of the original canonical line bundle of BR. However, we do not exploit the relationship between the canonical line bundle and the ghost vaccum, but we calculate the ghost curvature in the same spirit as the calculation of the bosonic curvature. Since the bosonic Fock ~pace contains negative-norm states and we are concerned with the Faddeev-Popov ghosts, we are forced to the BRST formulation. We intro­ duce the "interacting" BRST charge Q. Then we point out that the total curvature G corresponds to {( em, Q2), en}, where the c in'S are the anti-ghost modes. Other important properties of Q are also derived.
