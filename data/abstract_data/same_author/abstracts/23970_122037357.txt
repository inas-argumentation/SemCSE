A Z2-gauge symmetry in CPT and the Weinberg-Salam model
By means of an approach to the generalized gauge theory on discrete groups, we present a model of GL*GR*Z2 gauge invariance where the Higgs field is a gauge field with respect to the Z2-gauge symmetry, a subgroup of the CPT symmetry. As an example, we reformulate the Weinberg-Salam model.
