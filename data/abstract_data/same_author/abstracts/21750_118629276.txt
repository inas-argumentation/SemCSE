Longitudinal polarization of hyperons in the forward region in polarized pp collisions
We study the longitudinal polarization of hyperons and antihyperons at forward pseudorapidity, $2.5l\ensuremath{\eta}l4$, in singly polarized $pp$ collisions at Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider energies by using different parametrizations of the polarized parton densities and different models for the polarized fragmentation functions. The results show that the ${\ensuremath{\Sigma}}^{+}$ polarization is able to distinguish different pictures on spin transfer in high energy fragmentation processes; and the polarization of $\ensuremath{\Lambda}$ and $\overline{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}$ hyperons can provide sensitivity to the helicity distribution of strange sea quarks. The influence from beam remnant to hyperon polarization in the forward region is also discussed.
