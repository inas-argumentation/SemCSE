Non-equilibrium charge transport at a first-order phase transition
Abstract We have studied penetration of a charge density disturbance cause by a moving bubble wall in relativistic plasma, using the self-consistent field approximation. We have shown that the charge density as a function of distance from the wall has a power-law tail 1 z 2 . We find that penetration of charge in plasma is most effective when the wall velocity is close to unity. However, for wall velocities u⪷0.7 , the effective, in the integral sense, penetration length is found to be essentially equal to the static Debye radius.
