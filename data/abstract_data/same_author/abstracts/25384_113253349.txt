The SuperB Project Site Layout
The SuperB collider project aims at the construction of an asymmetric high luminosity B-Factory in the Tor Vergata University campus in Rome (Italy). The engineering aspects of the SuperB design and construction, aiming at reusing the PEP II components is be presented here. Synergies with the Italian FEL project SPARX, which will start civil construction this year, will be discussed. The two projects can share the LINAC tunnel and other facilities.
