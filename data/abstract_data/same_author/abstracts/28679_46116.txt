Initial results of RF gun experiment
A photocathode RF gun was built and it has been operated for beam measurement at SPring-8. The cavity was tested by using high power RF up to 18 MW. The electric field gradient on the cathode reached 127 MW/m. After RF conditioning, a laser pulse was irradiated on a copper cathode and the photo-emitted beam was accelerated up to 2.9 MeV. An effective quantum efficiency of the cathode was obtained by changing laser power and field gradient.
