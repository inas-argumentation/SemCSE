Search for new physics in CP-violating B decays
We consider three possible scenarios of new physics in B{sub d}{sup 0}-{bar B}{sub d}{sup 0} mixing and propose a simple framework for analyzing their effects. This framework allows us to study the CP asymmetry in semileptonic B{sub d} decays (A{sub SL}) and those in nonleptonic transitions such as B{sub d}{r_arrow}J/{psi}K{sub S} and B{sub d}{r_arrow}{pi}{sup +}{pi}{sup {minus}}. Numerically we find that new physics may enhance the magnitude of A{sub SL} up to the percent level within the appropriate parameter space. So measurements of A{sub SL} and its correlation with other CP asymmetries will serve as a sensitive probe for new physics in B{sub d}{sup 0}-{bar B}{sub d}{sup 0} mixing. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
