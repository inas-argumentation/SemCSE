Improved Parton Distributions and W, Z Production at p anti-p Colliders
The authors obtain improved parton distributions from a structure function analysis which incudes new data on deep inelastic scattering and on dilepton production. These lead to improved predictions for a {sigma}(W)/{sigma}(Z), which when combined with the forthcoming data from pp-bar colliders, will give tighter constraints on the values of m/sub t/ and N/sub {nu}/. The authors emphasize how new measurements of the W /sup +-/ charge asymmetry may resolve the EMC = BCDMS F/sub 2/ discrepancy and hence sharpen the prediction for {sigma}(W)/{sigma}(Z) even further.
