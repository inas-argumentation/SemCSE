The search for the H dibaryon with the BNL 2.0 GeV/c kaon beam
The status is given for two experiments being carried out to search for evidence of theH dibaryon. BNL experiments E813 and E836 will use the new 2 GeV/c kaon beam line. The former has recently begun data taking. They cover complementary regions of mass‐sensitivity and promise to provide sensitive tests of the existence of the H.
