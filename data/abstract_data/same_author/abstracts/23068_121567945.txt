An optical model consistency condition between the regeneration phase and the real parts of the KON forward scattering amplitudes
Using just the magnitude of the regeneration amplitude we show that both the regeneration phase, ϕ r , and the sum of the ratios of the real to imaginary parts of the K O N forward scattering amplitudes, γ pn , can be determined for nuclei with zero or moderate neutron excess. We find: ϕ r = (43.5 ± 2.5) o ; γ pn = −0.73 ± 0.1 + 1.7 γ pn 〈 2.7 GeV /c 〉 = −0.65 ± 0.07 + 1.6 γ pn 〈 4.5 GeV /c 〉 , where γ pn is corresponding quantity for K O N scattering. Applications to regeneration in hydrogen and the ϕ-meson are discussed.
