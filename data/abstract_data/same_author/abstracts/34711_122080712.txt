Natural mass scale and neutrino oscillation
Two principles, the standard SU(2)/sub L/ x U(1) electroweak theory below 100 GeV and the natural mass scale, are suggested for studying possible connections between neutrino oscillation and a mass hierarchy above 100 GeV. Neutrino-oscillation parameters of order or approx. = 10/sup 8/--10/sup 9/ GeV. An SU(7) model in this scenario is described in some detail.
