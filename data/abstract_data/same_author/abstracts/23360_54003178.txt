A source of highly polarized electrons at the stanford linear accelerator center
A polarized electron source based upon photoionization of a state-selected 6 Li atomic beam has been developed as an injection gun for the Standford two-mile electron linear accelerator. The source (PEGGY) produces a pulsed beam of electrons with a maximum intensity of 2.6×10 9 electrons per pulse, a polarization of 0.85, a pulse length of 1.6 μs, and a repitition rate of 180 pulses/s. Since its installation at SLAC in July 1974, PEGGY has been used in several energy electron-nucleon scattering experiments.
