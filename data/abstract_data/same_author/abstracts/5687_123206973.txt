The interior of the charge transition density and the structure of low-lying states in deformed nuclei
The RPA calculations of charge transition densities (CTD) rho lambda mu nu (r) for the E2 transitions from the ground state to the low-lying Ipi Knu =2+2nu and 2+0nu states in 164Dy predict rather strong peaks of single-particle nature in the interior of the CTD. A simple explanation of the results is given. The reliability of the results obtained, their possible application to the study of the structure of low-lying states and some predictions for nuclei of the rare-earth region are discussed.
