The Bäcklund transform for the Liouville field in a curved background
Abstract We discuss the Backlund transformation which maps the Liouville field on a curved 2D manifold to a noninteracting pseudoscalar field on the same curved manifold. For the classical theory, we show that the transformations imply the respective field equations as well as the equality of energy-momentum tensors. In addition we extend the treatment to canonical hamiltonian dynamics, with a view to the quantum theory. In particular, we show how the canonical phase space action transforms under the Backlund transformation. This will be useful in understanding how the Backlund transformation works in path integral quantization. Finally, we consider how these results can be used to describe subcritical string theory.
