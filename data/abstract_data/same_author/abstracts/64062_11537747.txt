A Service-Oriented Architecture for Semantic Recommendation and Integration of Products/Application Services (SOA-RIPAS) in Globalization
Globalization requires enterprises to suit different customers' demands and integrate different countries' resources. Current research on SOA could not provide an appropriate way for support. This paper proposes a SOA (SOA-RIPAS) in globalization. It describes customers semantically and recommends worldwide products/services to customers. It develops products and new application services by semantically conversing and compositing resources from worldwide Application Service Providers (ASP) and Manufacturers.
