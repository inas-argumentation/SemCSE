QCD condensates and the Slavnov-Taylor identities
Abstract The dimension-four gluonic and ghost condensate contributions to the Slavnov-Taylor identities (STI) for the two- and three-point gluon functions are evaluated to lowest order in α s . In order to satisfy both identities, a single constraint between the ghost and gluon condensates is required. It is also shown that the plane-wave and coordinate space methods for evaluating OPE coefficients lead to identical results.
