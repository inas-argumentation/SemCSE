N-body nuclear forces at short distances in holographic QCD
We provide a calculation of $N$-body ($N\ensuremath{\ge}3$) nucleon interactions at short distances in holographic QCD. In the Sakai-Sugimoto model of large-${N}_{c}$ massless QCD, $N$ baryons are described by $N$ Yang-Mills instantons in 5 spacetime dimensions. We compute a classical short-distance interaction Hamiltonian for $N$ 't Hooft instantons. This corresponds to $N$ baryons sharing identical classical spins and isospins. We find that genuine $N$-body nuclear forces turn out to vanish for $N\ensuremath{\ge}3$, at leading order. This suggests that classical $N$-body forces are always suppressed compared with 2-body forces.
