THE MAPPING CLASS GROUP FOR GENUS 2
This article represents joint work with Tullio Regge and essentially concerns the symmetries of the hexagon. When space {Sigma} is a genus 2 Riemann surface the observable of the theory are the gauge-invariant normalized traces of the SL(2,R) matrices which form the spinor representation of {pi}{sub 1}({Sigma}). It was extremely difficult to find a suitable notation for the 15 possible traces, in fact we shall simultaneously use two equivalent (and hopefully not too ambiguous) notations which are now described. First, define B{sub n} = c(U{sub 1}{sup a}V{sub 1}{sup b}U{sub 2}{sup c}V{sub 2}{sup d}) where c(x) = 1/2 Tr(x), x element of {pi}{sub 1}, and n = a + 2b +4c + 8d = 1 . . . 15. There are two exception to , namely B{sub 3} = c (U{sub 1}V{sub 1}{sup {minus}1}) and B{sub 12} = c(U{sub 2}V{sub 2}{sup {minus}1}). SL(2,R) identities will reduce all other traces. These can be arranged to fit on a hexagon where each line, internal or on the border, represents one of the B{sub n}.
