Finite-temperature nuclear matter properties within a relativistic σ − ω coupling model
We use the relativistic mean field theory to study the finite temperature nuclear matter properties. The effects of isoscalar $\ensuremath{\sigma}\text{\ensuremath{-}}\ensuremath{\omega}$ coupling on the equation of state and finite temperature properties of symmetric and asymmetric nuclear matter are investigated carefully. For comparison, we also discuss the properties of two nonlinear $\ensuremath{\sigma}$ self-coupling models, namely NL-SH and NL3. It is found that the $\ensuremath{\sigma}\text{\ensuremath{-}}\ensuremath{\omega}$ coupling term can soften the equation of state, but has very limited influence to the critical temperature of the nuclear matter liquid-gas phase transition.
