W and Z hadroproduction as a probe of very small χ
Abstract The cross sections for W and Z production at hadron colliders are calculated as a function of the collider energy using various sets of parton distributions characterised by gluons with differing small χ behaviour. A new feature emerges at LHC and SSC energies: the cross sections are found to contain a sizeable and distinctive component at large W, Z rapidity which arises from a valence quark colliding with a parton with extremely small χ ( χ −4 .
