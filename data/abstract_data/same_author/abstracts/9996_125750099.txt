STUDY FOR A RACETRACK FFAG BASED MUON RING COOLER
A design study for the muon cooling in scaling FFAG lattices with racetrack-shape in underway. The ring has straight sections with scaling FFAG magnets, which make enough space to install kicker magnets to inject and extract the muon beam. A muon cooling ring based on the PRISMFFAG has been studied. This paper reports progress of the study.
