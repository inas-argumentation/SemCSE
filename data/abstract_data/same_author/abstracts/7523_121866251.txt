Rescaling for Kaon Structure Function
We use the valon model to analyse the dynamical rescaling of x in the inelastic structure functions and point out that the rescaling is just the delta approximation of the valon distribution functions. We apply the contribution of valence quarks to the mass of a hadron bound state to determine a few parameters for the x-rescaling and thus obtain the Kaon structure functions. The result is in good agreement with experimental data.
