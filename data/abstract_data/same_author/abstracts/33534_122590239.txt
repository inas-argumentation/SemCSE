Monte-Carlo Renormalization Group for SU(3) lattice gauge theory
We present the first results for the nonperturbative ..beta.. function along the Wilson axis for the SU(3) pure gauge theory using the Monte Carlo renormalization-group method. The results show that there is no asymptotic scaling for the coupling in the fundamental representation K/sub F/<6.1. The renormalized action generated by the ..sqrt..3 block transformation is also estimated for use in future calculations.
