Shear viscosity of a multi-component hadronic system
The shear viscosity and entropy density s of a hadron gas with zero baryon number density are calculated using the Chapman-Enskog and virial expansion approaches, respectively. Interactions are included via the K-matrix parametrization of cross sections preserving the unitarity of the S-matrix. In the four component mixture ( K N ), a total of 57 resonances up to 2 GeV mass are included. Interactions forming resonances reduce the magnitude of and increase s, both eects serving to progressively reduce =s as the temperature nears the QCD phase transition temperature.
