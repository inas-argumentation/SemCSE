Cryogenic systems for the low field RLHC study cases
Cryogenic system requirements are discussed for three different choices of superconductor and consequent operating temperature for the high-field RLHC. All three cases appear to be feasible. Cryogenic system parameters are determined, including power requirements. We are able to study the case using a magnet based on NbTi superconductor with particular confidence because it is a straightforward scaling of LHC technology.
