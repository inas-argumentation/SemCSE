Minimal four-color model with quark-lepton symmetry and constraints on the Z{prime}-boson mass
A minimal model with quark-lepton symmetry is considered. This model, which describes unified electroweak and strong interactions, is a minimal extension of the standard model that involves four-color symmetry of quarks and leptons. Predicted relations between the masses of leptoquarks and W{sup +-}, Z, and Z{prime} bosons are studied, and the contributions of an additional Z{prime} boson to the cross sections for e{sup +}e{sup -} annihilation and the asymmetry A{sub FB} at TRISTAN and LEP200 energies are analyzed. Experimental data are shown to be consistent with the existence of a relatively light Z{prime} boson associated with four-color quark-lepton symmetry. 12 refs., 3 figs., 1 tab.
