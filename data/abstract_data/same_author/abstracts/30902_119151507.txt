Non-Gaussianity from two right-handed sneutrino curvaton decays
In this paper, we consider the effect of two right-handed sneutrino curvaton decays and investigate the parameter space. We compare the difference of the result between single- and two-curvaton cases. We find one Yukawa coupling of the right-handed snetrinos can be as large as λ ~ 0.1 while the other one is much smaller. When the curvatons decay, we assume both of them subdominate the energy density of the universe. We find that, unlike a single curvaton case, here a small or negative, as well as a large fNL can be generated.
