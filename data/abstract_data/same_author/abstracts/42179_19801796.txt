Multimedia Over IP For Thin Clients : Building A Collaborative Resource-Sharing Prototype
This paper describes the issues and the techniques of enabling multimedia applications with thin-client/server computing. A prototype of a video communication system based on H.323 family of ITU-T recommendations has been developed to demonstrate the efficient support to multimedia applications with a terminal server and thin clients in an IP-based thin-client computing environment. With the proposed architecture, a collaborative multi-user audio/video conference system is presented as an illustrative application. The collaborative multiuser audio/video conference system leverages the benefit of sharing common components among multiple conference terminals as well as increases their extensibility and ease of use. As a result, we can attain a balanced architecture that supports both centralized management of application softwares and distributed multimedia processing for I/O devices.
