Excited leptons at the CERN large hadron collider
We analyze the potential of the CERN Large Hadron Collider (LHC) to search for excited spin- 1 electrons and neutrinos. Assuming a SU(2)LU(1)Y invariant model, we study in detail the single production of excited electrons and neutrinos and respective backgrounds through the reactions pp ! e + e V and eV with V = , W ,o rZ. We show that the LHC will be able to tighten considerably the direct constraints on these possible new states, probing excited lepton masses up to 1{2 TeV depending on their couplings to fermions and gauge bosons.
