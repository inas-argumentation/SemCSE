Precision determination of |V(ub)| from inclusive decays
We propose determining $|{V}_{\mathrm{ub}}|$ from inclusive semileptonic B decay using combined cuts on the leptonic and hadronic invariant masses to eliminate the $\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{b}c$ background. Compared to a pure dilepton invariant mass cut, the uncertainty from unknown order ${\ensuremath{\Lambda}}_{\mathrm{QCD}}^{3}{/m}_{b}^{3}$ terms in the operator product expansion is significantly reduced and the fraction of $\stackrel{\ensuremath{\rightarrow}}{b}u$ events is roughly doubled. Compared to a pure hadronic invariant mass cut, the uncertainty from the unknown light-cone distribution function of the b quark is significantly reduced. We find that $|{V}_{\mathrm{ub}}|$ can be determined with theoretical uncertainty at the 5\char21{}10% level.
