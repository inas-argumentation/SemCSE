Experimental observation of a copious yield of electrons with small transverse momenta in pp collisions at high energies
Inclusive electron and positron emission have been observed for θ cm = 30° and s = 2800 GeV 2 at the CERN Intersecting Storage Rings (ISR). Over the transverse momentum interval 0.2 GeV/ c p T c , electrons and positrons, which are equal in number within the experimental accuracies, appear to grow with respect to other particles (pions) approximately like 1/ p T . We are unable to explain their number and p T -dependence in terms of “conventional” mechanisms.
