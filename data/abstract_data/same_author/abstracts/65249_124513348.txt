Cesàro-type operators on Hardy, BMOA and bloch spaces
Let Hp, p ∈ (0, ∞], BMOA and Ba, a ∈ (0, ∞) be the classical p-Hardy, analytic BMO(∂) (bounded mean oscillation on the unit circle) and a-Bloch space on the unit disk. In this paper, we prove that the Cesàro-type operator: Cα, α ∈ (−1, ∞) is bounded on Hp, p ∈ (0, ∞) and on Ba, a ∈ (1, ∞), but, unbounded on H∞, BMOA and Ba, a ∈ (0, 1]. In particular, we give an answer to the Stempak’s open problem.
