CP-violation effects in some two-body baryonic decays of Bd0
The CP -violation effects in some two-body baryonic decays of B d 0 − B d 0 are studied. The partial-decay-rate asymmetries and corresponding numbers of b b pairs needed for testing the CP -violation effects are estimated. It turns out that the best decays for testing CP violation are B d 0 → p p , n n , λ c + λ c + and λ λ , where ∼ 10 7 to ∼ 10 8 b b pairs are needed. In addition, it is very difficult to detect the CP -violation effects in B d 0 → Σ + Σ + and ξ 0 ξ 0 , where only W-exchange diagrams contribute and ∼ 10 10 b b pairs are needed. It is impossible to detect CP -violation effects in those decays where the penguin diagram dominates.
