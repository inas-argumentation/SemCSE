Operator Formalism of the Dual Resonance Model with Different Trajectories
In the dual resonance model the generalized harmonic oscillator formalism is constructed when relevant particles lie on different trajectories. Requiring the factorization in the sense of the operator method, one can see that the Regge intercepts are expressed in terms of quark quantum numbers. Propagator, general vertex, gauge operator and so on, appearing in the usual dual resonance model with a single trajectory, are redefined. The duality of the four­ point tree diagram between general non-spurious virtual states can also be shown.
