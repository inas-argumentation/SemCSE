A tentative program for the development of quantum field theory as an extension of the equivalence principle of general relativity theory
Abstract The paper contains an attempt towards a generalized quantum field theory based on a formal extension of Einstein's principle of equivalence to electromagnetism and more general interactions between Fermi-fields and Bose-fields. This paper is what I hope to be an ameliorated summary of my work on a generalization of the essence of the Einstein relativity theory, so as to embrace non-space-time symmetries based on the equivalence argument, beginning with electromagnetism, themmain stages of this work being stateed below. Regarding these I shall make the following remark: although the periodicity as an interpretation of the elementary quantum of electricity occured to me almost at the beginning (in 1925) and later led me to the consideration of the corresponding group, from early times until recently I oscillated between this way and a formalism related to isospin, obtained by cutting down the higher harmonics of the Fourier expansion. This, however led to two difficulties: the linearity in the momenta in the Lagrangian density could not be maintained unless the group was limited in analogy to the transformation group (10); and, that the equivalence claim could not be satisfied. With the full periodicity these difficulties are absent.
