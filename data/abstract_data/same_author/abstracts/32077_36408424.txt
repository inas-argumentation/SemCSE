Vector dominance effects in weak radiative decays of B mesons.
The long-distance vector-meson-dominance (VMD) effects on the weak radiative decays B\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\rho}\ensuremath{\gamma} and B\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{} $^{0}\mathit{D}^{\mathrm{*}0}$\ensuremath{\gamma} are studied. For B\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\rho}\ensuremath{\gamma} decays, the VMD contribution is (10--20)% of the short-distance penguin amplitude. The pole effect is as important as the VMD one in the decay ${\mathit{B}}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\rho}}}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}}$\ensuremath{\gamma}, but it is suppressed in B\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{} $^{0}\mathrm{\ensuremath{\rho}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\gamma}. The branching ratio of B\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\rho}\ensuremath{\gamma}, estimated to be of order ${10}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}6}$, strongly depends on the sign of the Wolfenstein parameter \ensuremath{\rho}. A measurement of any deviation of the ratio R=\ensuremath{\Gamma}(${\mathit{B}}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\rho}}}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}}$\ensuremath{\gamma})/\ensuremath{\Gamma}(B\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{} $^{0}\mathrm{\ensuremath{\rho}}^{0}$\ensuremath{\gamma}) away from the isospin value 2 will provide a probe on the long-range contribution and possibly indicate the sign of \ensuremath{\rho}: Rg2 for \ensuremath{\rho}0 and R\ensuremath{\sim}2 for \ensuremath{\rho}g0. The decay B\ifmmode\bar\else\textasciimacron\fi{} $^{0}\mathit{D}^{\mathrm{*}0}$\ensuremath{\gamma} does not receive short-distance contributions, and its branching ratio, predicted to be 0.9\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}6}$, is dominated by W exchange accompanied by a photon emission.
