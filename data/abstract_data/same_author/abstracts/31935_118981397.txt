125 GeV Higgs boson decays in the μ from ν supersymmetric standard model
Recently the ATLAS and CMS Collaborations have reported significant events that are attributed to the neutral Higgs boson with mass around 125 GeV. In this work, we investigate the signals of the Higgs boson decay channels $h\rightarrow\gamma\gamma$, $h\rightarrow VV^*$ ($V=Z,W$), and $h\rightarrow f\bar{f}$ ($f=b,\tau$) in the $\mu$ from $\nu$ supersymmetric standard model ($\mu\nu$SSM). In the numerical results, we show the light stop and stau effects on the signal strengths for the 125 GeV Higgs boson decay channels in the $\mu\nu$SSM, which can account for the updated experimental data on Higgs.
