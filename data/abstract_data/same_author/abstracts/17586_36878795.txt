The superconformal index of the (2,0) theory with defects
A bstractWe compute the supersymmetric partition function of the six-dimensional (2, 0) theory of type AN −1 on S1 × S5 in the presence of both codimension two and codimension four defects. We concentrate on a limit of the partition function depending on a single parameter. From the allowed supersymmetric configurations of defects we find a precise match with the characters of irreducible modules of WN algebras and affine Lie algebras of type AN −1.
