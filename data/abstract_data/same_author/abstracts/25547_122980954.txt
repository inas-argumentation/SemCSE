Subcritical dual models as subcritical strings
Abstract We reconsider the nonplanar loop of the bosonic dual resonance model with space-time dimension D in the range 1 D ϱ(μ)=2| sinh πμ[ (25−D) 48 + i (D−1) 48 ]| 2 . We point out many parallels of this result with the picture of subcritical strings advanced by Polyakov. These include the continuous mass spectrum in the closed string sector, the restriction to the odd-parity zero-mode sector, and the appearance of the Liouville coupling constant β= (25−D) 48 + (1−D) 48 where the Liouville lagrangian is − [(∂φ) 2 +M 2 exp (2φ/β)] 2π .
