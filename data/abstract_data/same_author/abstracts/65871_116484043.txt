Scattering theory for the shape resonance model. II. Resonance scattering
Continuing the analysis of the part I, we consider the asymptotic behavior of the scattering matrix for the shape resonance model. We give an asymptotic formula for the scattering matrix near the (complex) resonance eigenvalues under the assumptions similar to those in Combes-Duclos-Klein-Seiler [2] (exterior scaling analyticity, non-trapping condition, etc.) On considere le comportement asymptotique de la matrice de diffusion pour le modele de resonance de forme.
