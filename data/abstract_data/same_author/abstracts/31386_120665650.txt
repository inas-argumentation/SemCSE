Limits on nonstandard weak currents from the positron decays of O-14 and C-10
The relative polarization of positrons emitted in pure Fermi ${(}^{14}$O) and Gamow-Teller ${(}^{10}$C) decays has been measured using a new technique based on time-resolved positronium decay. The result ${\mathit{P}}_{\mathrm{GT}}$/${\mathit{P}}_{\mathrm{F}}$=0.9996\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.0037 provides constraints on possible right-handed and scalar-tensor current admixtures to the weak interaction of -4\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{4}$7 and \ensuremath{\Vert}(${\mathit{C}}_{\mathit{S}}$/${\mathit{C}}_{\mathit{V}}$)-(${\mathit{C}}_{\mathit{T}}$/${\mathit{C}}_{\mathit{A}}$)\ensuremath{\Vert}=(1\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}9)\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}3}$ (90% C.L.), respectively.
