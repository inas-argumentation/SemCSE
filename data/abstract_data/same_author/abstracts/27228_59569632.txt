THE ROLE OF THE PION CLOUD IN THE NUCLEON FORM FACTORS
Many properties of the nucleon can be rather successfully explained by assuming a cloud of pions surrounding the quark core. Here we are interested in those quantities which are particularly sensitive to the pionic content of the nucleon and can not be explained in a model with only quark degrees of freedom (without making additional ad hoc assumptions). Examples are the electric and magnetic polarizabilities and different form factors as measured in pion electroproduction experiments. We calculate these properties in the linear sigma model (LSM) [1,2] as a generic example of models predicting a rather strong pion cloud in (nonstrange) baryons as well as in the chiral version of the chromodielectric model (CDM) [2] which has a weaker pion cloud. Coherent states are used to describe the meson clouds around the quark core and we perform an exact projection of isospin and angular momentum [3].
