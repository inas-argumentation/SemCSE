Nuclear charge-exchange excitations in localized covariant density functional theory
The recent progress in the studies of nuclear charge-exchange excitations with localized covariant density functional theory is briefly presented, by taking the fine struc- ture of spin-dipole excitations in 16 O as an example. It is shown that the constraints in- troduced by the Fock terms of the relativistic Hartree-Fock scheme into the particle-hole residual interactions are straightforward and robust.
