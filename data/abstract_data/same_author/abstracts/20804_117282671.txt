Extremely high velocity outflows.
Extremely high velocity (EHV) wings, with full widths of 72 to 140 km s −1 , are seen on the CO J=3→2 lines toward W3 IRS 5, GL 490, NGC 2071, W28 A2 (G05.89-0.39), GL 2591, S140, and Cepheus A. Observations of 12 CO and 13 CO J=3→2 and J=2→1 lines indicate that optical depth generally decreases with increasing velocity separation from the ambient cloud velocity. Maps of the extremely high velocity (|V−V 0 |≥20 km s −1 ) and the high-velocity (5≤|V−V 0 |≤20 km s −1 ) CO emission components show that the morphology of the two components is similar in W3 IRS 5 and W28 A2 but may be different in GL 2591, S 140, and Cepheus A
