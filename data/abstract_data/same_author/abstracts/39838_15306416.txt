Hadronic returns to the Z in electron positron annihilation at high-energy
The production of four hadronic jets in e+e− collisions above the Z pole is dominated by the QCD e+e−→qq,qgg processes and, for sufficiently high energy, the electroweak e+e−→W+W−→qq process. However, there is another mechanism for producing four jets, e+e−→Zgg→qgg, which can be regarded as a ``hadronic return'' to the Z pole. We investigate this process in detail.
