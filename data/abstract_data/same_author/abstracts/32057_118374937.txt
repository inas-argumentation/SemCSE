Search for the Exotic States at Belle
We review recent results on charmonium-like exotic states from the Belle experiment. The two-photon process γγ → φJ/ψ is measured to search for Y (4140). No signal for the Y (4140) → φJ/ψ is observed. But a narrow peak with a significance of 3.2σ deviations including systematic uncertainty is observed at 4350.6 MeV/c that we named X(4350). We also search for charmonium-like states, including X(3872), Y (4140), X(3915) and X(4350), in Υ(1S) and Υ(2S) radiative decays. No significant signal of any charmoniumlike state is observed. The processes γγ → V V (V = ω or φ) are also measured to search for the possible exotic states in low mass region. There are clear resonant structures in all the decay modes.
