QCD analysis of deep inelastic lepton-scattering data
We have carried out a QCD analysis of data on deep inelastic lepton-scattering (using data of the collaborations BCDMS, EMC, and CDHS) in the leading and next-to-leading orders in the coupling constant ..cap alpha../sub s/. We have considered the effect of the sea quarks and gluons. We discuss the dependence of our results for ..lambda.. on effects of twist corrections, nuclear target effects, and heavy-quark production thresholds.
