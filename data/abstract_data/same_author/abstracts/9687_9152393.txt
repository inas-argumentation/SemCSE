Caustics in tachyon matter and other Born-Infeld scalars
We consider scalar Born-Infeld type theories with arbitrary potentials V (T) of a scalar fleld T. We flnd that for models with runaway potentials V (T) the generic in- homogeneous solutions after a short transient stage can be very well approximated by the solutions of a Hamilton-Jacobi equation that describes free streaming wave front propaga- tion. The analytic solution for this wave propagation shows the formation of caustics with multi-valued regions beyond them. We verifled that these caustics appear in numerical solutions of the original scalar BI non-linear equations. Our results include the scalar BI model with an exponential potential, which was recently proposed as an efiective action for the string theory tachyon in the approximation where high-order spacetime derivatives of T are truncated. Since the actual string tachyon dynamics contain derivatives of all orders, the tachyon BI model with an exponential potential becomes inadequate when the caustics develop because high order spatial derivatives of T become divergent. BI type tachyon theory with a potential decreasing at large T could have interesting cosmological applications because the tachyon fleld rolling towards its ground state at inflnity acts as pressureless dark matter. We flnd that inhomogeneous cosmological tachyon ∞uctuations rapidly grow and develop multiple caustics. Any considerations of the role of the tachyon fleld in cosmology will have to involve flnding a way to predict the behavior of the fleld at and beyond these caustics.
