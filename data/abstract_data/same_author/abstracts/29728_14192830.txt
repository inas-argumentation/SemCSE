Next-to-Leading Order Calculation of Four-Jet Shape Variables
We present the next-to-leading order calculation of two four-jet event shape variables, the D parameter, and acoplanarity differential distributions. We find large, more than 100{percent} radiative corrections. The theoretical prediction for the D parameter is compared to L3 data obtained at the Z{sup 0} peak and corrected to hadron level. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
