A calculation of the electromagnetic vacuum polarization in lattice QCD
Abstract We present a calculation of the electromagnetic current vacuum polarization in the framework of lattice QCD. A 16th order hopping-parameter expansion of 〈 J em μ J em μ 〉 0 is reported on an 8 4 lattice. It gives a reasonable description of the mass m J ψ and the electromagnetic current-vector meson coupling constant f J ψ , in the vector meson dominance approximation.
