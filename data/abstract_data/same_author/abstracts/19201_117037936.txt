HIGH-FIELD AND FLUX MEASUREMENTS ON THE HYBRID MARK II MAGNET
The results of comparsive magnet field measurements on identical electromagnets made of four different steels (U. S. Steel Transformer 72 and Transformer 52 and Allegheny Ludlum Transformer Grace C and Silectron are presented. Particular attention is directed to the high-field (~9.5 kiloguass) characteristics. Also measurements of the flux distributions of the C.E.A. model magnets powered by a-c are described and extrapolations are made the accelerator magnets. (auth)
