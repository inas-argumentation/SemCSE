Extending the frontiers: Reconciling accelerator and cosmic ray p-p cross sections
We simultaneously fit a QCD-inspired parametrization of all accelerator data on forward proton-proton and antiproton-proton scattering amplitudes, together with cosmic ray data (using Glauber theory), to predict proton-air and proton-proton cross sections at energies near {radical}(s){approx_equal}30 TeV. The p-air cosmic ray measurements provide a strong constraint on the inclusive particle production cross section, as well as greatly reducing the errors on the fit parameters--in turn, greatly reducing the errors in the high-energy proton-proton and proton-air cross section predictions.
