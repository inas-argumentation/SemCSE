Universality of low-energy scattering in 2+1 dimensions: The nonsymmetric case
For a very large class of potentials, V(x),x∊R2, we prove the universality of the low-energy scattering amplitude, f(k′,k). The result is f=π∕2(1∕logk)+o(1)∕[log(1∕k)]. The only exceptions occur if V happens to have a zero-energy bound state. Our new result includes as a special subclass the case of rotationally symmetric potentials, V(∣x∣).
