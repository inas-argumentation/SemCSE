Thermodynamic Properties of Color Superconducting Quark Matter
Thermodynamics for color superconducting quark matter is investigated on the basis of the one-gluon-exchange model. Thermodynamic quantities are calculated by using a mean field (BCS) approximation. It is shown that there exists a phase transition of second order between the superconducting phase and normal phase. The pressure and density become larger in the superconducting phase. The specific heat and compressibility have a discontinuity at the critical temperature.
