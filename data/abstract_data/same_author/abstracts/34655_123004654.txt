Light Pseudoscalars, Particle Physics and Cosmology
Abstract We review physics of light pseudoscalars (axions, majorons and familons) and their effects in cosmology. The axions which solve the strong CP problem play important roles in astrophysics and cosmology. The allowed band of the axion scale 10 8 GeV ≲ F a ≲ 10 12 GeV may be the next high energy scale. We discuss the ideas, techniques and phenomenologies leading to this interesting mass scale. Majorons and familons are also discussed.
