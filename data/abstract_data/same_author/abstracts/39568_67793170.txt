GAMMA-RAYS GENERATION WITH 3D 4-MIRROR CAVITY FOR ILC POLARIZED POSITRON SOURCE
We are developing a ILC polarized positron source based on the laser-Compton scattering. We have already performed a photon generation experiment at the KEK-ATF using a Fabry-Perot type 2-mirror laser pulse stacking cavity [1]. The laser pulses are accumulated and their power was enhanced in the Fabry-Perot cavity. In order further improve performance of the laser power enhancement, a new three dimensional 4-mirror cavity is designed. In this article, we report status of the photon generation experiment.
