Analysis of the $X(1835)$ as a baryonium state with Bethe-Salpeter equation
In this article, we take the X(1835) as a pseudoscalar baryonium state, and calculate the mass spectrum of the baryon-antibaryon bound states pp̄, ΣΣ̄, ΞΞ̄, and ΛΛ̄ in the framework of the Bethe-Salpeter equation with a phenomenological potential. The numerical results indicate the pp̄, ΣΣ̄ and ΞΞ̄ bound states maybe exist, and the X(1835) can be tentatively identified as the pp̄ bound state. PACS number: 12.39.Ki, 12.39.Pn
