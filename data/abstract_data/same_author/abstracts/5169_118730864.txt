Quasinormal modes of a black hole in the deformed Hořava-Lifshitz gravity
We study the quasinormal modes of the massless scalar perturbation in the background of a deformed black hole in the Hořava–Lifshitz gravity with coupling constant λ = 1 . Our results show that the quasinormal frequencies depend on the parameter in the Hořava–Lifshitz gravity and the behavior of the quasinormal modes is different from those in the Reissner–Norstrom and Einstein–Born–Infeld black hole spacetimes. The absolute value of imaginary parts is smaller and the scalar perturbations decay more slowly in the deformed Hořava–Lifshitz black hole spacetime. This information can help us understand more about the Hořava–Lifshitz gravity.
