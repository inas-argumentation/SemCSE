Towards a bosonization of quantum electrodynamics.
We study the strong phase of QED with more than one flavor, N f >1, and spontaneously broken chiral symmetry, using an effective Lagrangian. The effective Lagrangian contains a massless e + e − bound state (the «electropion») and the photon. This theory is shown to predict the existence of a fermionic soliton. We argue that this soliton must be the electron. The mass of the electron is calculated to be in the range 2.2eF < m < 3.3eF, where e is the electron charge and F is the electropion decay constant.
