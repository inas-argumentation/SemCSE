N*(1920)(1/2+) STATE IN THE $NK\bar{K}$ SYSTEM
We study the three body system by using the fixed center approximation to the Faddeev equations, taking the interaction between N and , N and K, and and K from the chiral unitary approach. Our results suggest that a hadron state, with spin-parity JP = 1/2+, and mass around 1920 MeV, can be formed.
