Three-loop {phi}-derivable approximation in QED
In this paper we examine {phi}-derivable approximations in QED. General theorems tell us that the gauge dependence of the n-loop {phi}-derivable approximation shows up at order g{sup 2n} where g is the coupling constant. We consider the gauge dependence of the two-loop {phi}-derivable approximation to the Debye mass and show that it is of order e{sup 4} as expected. We solve the three-loop {phi}-derivable approximation in QED by expanding sum-integrals in powers of e{sup 2} and m/T, where m is the Debye mass which satisfies a variational gap equation. The results for the pressure and the Debye mass are accurate to order e{sup 5}.
