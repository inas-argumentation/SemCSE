Transport coefficients of gluonic fluid
The shear ($\ensuremath{\eta}$) and bulk ($\ensuremath{\zeta}$) viscous coefficients have been evaluated for a gluonic fluid. The elastic, $gg\ensuremath{\rightarrow}gg$ and the inelastic, number nonconserving, $gg\ensuremath{\rightarrow}ggg$ processes have been considered as the dominant perturbative processes in evaluating the viscous coefficients to entropy density ($s$) ratios. Recently the processes: $gg\ensuremath{\rightarrow}ggg$ has been revisited and a correction to the widely used Gunion-Bertsch (GB) formula has been obtained. The $\ensuremath{\eta}$ and $\ensuremath{\zeta}$ have been evaluated for gluonic fluid with the formula recently derived. At large ${\ensuremath{\alpha}}_{s}$ the value of $\ensuremath{\eta}/s$ approaches its lower bound, $\ensuremath{\sim}1/4\ensuremath{\pi}$.
