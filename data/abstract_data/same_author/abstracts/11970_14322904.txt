QED vacuum between an unusual pair of plates
We consider the photon field between an unusual configuration of infinite parallel plates: a perfectly conducting plate (ǫ → ∞) and an infinitely permeable one μ → ∞). After quantizing the vector potential in the Coulomb gauge, we obtain explicit expressions for the vacuum expectation values of field operators of the form < ÊiÊj >0 and < B̂iB̂j >0. These field correlators allow us to reobtain the Casimir effect for this set up and to discuss the light velocity shift caused by the presence of plates (Scharnhorst effect [1, 2, 3]) for both scalar and spinor QED.
