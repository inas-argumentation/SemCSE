Rapid Notification of TeV Transients with the Milagro Telescope
The Milagro telescope is a wide field of view TeV observatory that is ideally suited for the observation of transients in the 300 GeV–10 TeV band. Milagro has a very large field of view (about 2 sr), nearly continuous operation, and a fluence sensitivity that is comparable to satellite experiments. Milagro is currently performing real time searches for TeV transients, with the goal of prompt notification and multi‐wavelength observation of any detected TeV transients.
