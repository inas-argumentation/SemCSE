Computing the Extreme Distances Between Two Convex Polygons
Abstract A polygon in the plane is convex if it contains all line segments connecting any two of its points. Let P and Q denote two convex polygons. The computational complexity of finding the minimum and maximum distance possible between two points p in P and q in Q is studied. An algorithm is described that determines the minimum distance (together with points p and q that realize it) in O (log m + log n ) time, where m and n denote the number of vertices of P and Q , respectively. This is optimal in the worst case. For computing the maximum distance, a lower bound Ω(m + n) is proved. This bound is also shown to be best possible by establishing an upper bound of O ( m + n ).
