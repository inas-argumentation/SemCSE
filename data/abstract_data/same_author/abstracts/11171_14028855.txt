Kappa-symmetric derivative corrections to D-brane dynamics
We show how the superembedding formalism can be applied to construct manifestly kappa-symmetric higher derivative corrections for the D9-brane. We also show that all correction terms appear at even powers of the fundamental length scale l. We explicitly construct the first potential correction, which corresponds to the kappa-symmetric version of the ∂4F4, which one finds from the four-point amplitude of the open superstring.
