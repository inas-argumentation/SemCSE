Status Report and Recent Results from the UK Dark Matter Collaboration
The UK Dark Matter Collaboration (UKDMC) has an ongoing programme of detector development and operation with the eventual aim of reaching dark matter rates below 0.1/day/kg. Limits for the interaction rate have been set by this group and others using pulse shape discrimination in sodium iodide scintillation detectors. Methods for improving these limits through increased light output will be discussed, including avalanche photodiode R&D. To reach the lowest interaction rates we are collaborating with members of ICARUS on liquid xenon systems and this work is briefly reported.
