D-wave charmonium production in B decays
The calculation of D-wave charmonium production rates in B meson decays under the NRQCD factorization formalism is presented. We find that the inclusion of the color-octet contributions permits us to detect the D-wave charmonium states in B decays at present experimental facilities. The same amount of signals of the 2{sup {minus}{minus}} D-wave state as that of {psi}{sup {prime}} could be observed at CERN LEP and CESR. We also predict that the relative production rates for four D-wave states are {delta}{sup c}:{delta}{sub 1}{sup c}:{delta}{sub 2}{sup c}:{delta}{sub 3}{sup c}=2.5:3:5:7, where {delta}{sup c}, {delta}{sub 1}{sup c}, {delta}{sub 2}{sup c}, {delta}{sub 3}{sup c} represent, respectively, the 2{sup {minus}+}, 1{sup {minus}{minus}}, 2{sup {minus}{minus}}, 3{sup {minus}{minus}} states. {copyright} {ital 1997} {ital The American Physical Society}
