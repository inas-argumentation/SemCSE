Lepton flavor violation in the $\mu\nu$SSM with slepton flavor mixing
The μνSSM, one of supersymmetric extensions of the Standard Model, introduces three right-handed neutrino superfields to solve the μ problem and violates lepton number. Within framework of the μνSSM, we investigate the lepton flavor violating (LFV) processes with slepton flavor mixing. Simultaneously, we consider the LFV processes and muon conversion to electron in nuclei.
