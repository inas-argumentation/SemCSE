Quark mass ratios and mixing angles from SU(3) family gauge symmetry
Abstract We explore a framework for the computation of quark mass ratios and CKM mixing angles based on an SU ( 3 ) family gauge symmetry. The four ratios m d / m b , m s / m b , m u / m t , and m c / m t can be fit at one loop in the family gauge interaction. The same is true of the quark mixing angles θ 12 and θ 23 , although the result for θ 13 is too small. The CP-violating phase is naturally O ( 1 ) .
