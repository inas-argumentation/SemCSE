Exceptional coset spaces and unification in six dimensions
The coset spaces E 8 /SO(10)×H F allow complex structures which can account for three quark–lepton generations including right-handed neutrinos. We show that in the context of supersymmetric SO(10) gauge theories in 6 dimensions they also provide the Higgs fields which are needed to break the electroweak and B − L gauge symmetries, and to generate small neutrino masses via the seesaw mechanism.
