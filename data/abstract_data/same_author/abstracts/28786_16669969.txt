Millimetre and submillimetre continuum observations of the core and hotspots of Cygnus-A
We present millimetre photometry and submillimetre imaging of the central core and two hotspots in the radio lobes of the galaxy Cygnus-A. For both hotspots and the central core the synchrotron spectrum continues smoothly from the radio to a frequency of 677GHz.
