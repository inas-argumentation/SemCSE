Effectiveness of a Shielding Cabinet on the Storage-Ring Septum Magnet of Taiwan Light Source
Abstract To avoid a parasitic magnetic field in the stored beam, the pulsed-magnet system of Taiwan Photon Source (TPS) requires the stray field to be minute: the stray field from the storage-ring (SR) injection septum is required to be less than 0.1 % of the main field in the gap. The most common method to protect against a parasitic magnetic field is to use a highly permeable and conductive material, such as mu-metal. A 300-ms half-sine-wave pulse up to a current maximum 9922 A is supplied to a septum and results in an eddy-current loss in a magnet and a conductor during rapid charging of a magnet. Competition between an eddy-current effect and the magnetic permeability would produce a complicated phenomenon inside the magnetic-shielding screen and affect the shielding performance. We examined the performance of magnetic shielding of a shielding screen with OPERA 2D for varied geometry and thickness of the screen.
