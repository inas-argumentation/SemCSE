The search for top quarks at hadron colliders: a realistic appraisal
AbstractWe study means of identifying top quarks of mass in the range 70–120 GeV at the CERN and FNAL
$$\bar pp$$
 colliders. We show that “W+dijet” production presents a serious background to the conventional “isolated electron+dijet” signature of top. We study an improved signature, calculate the expected event rates, and discuss the determination of the mass of the top quark.
