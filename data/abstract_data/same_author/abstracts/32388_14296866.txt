Noncommutative field theory description of quantum Hall Skyrmions
We examine the quantum Hall system with no Zeeman splitting energy using the noncommutative field theory. We analyze the Bogomol'nyi-Prasad-Sommerfield (BPS) condition for the delta-function interaction near the filling factor $\ensuremath{\nu}=1.$ Multi-Skyrmions are shown to saturate the BPS bounds. The dimension of the moduli space of k Skyrmions is $4k+2.$ The advantage of the noncommutative field description is demonstrated through the derivation of the effective nonlinear $\ensuremath{\sigma}$ model Lagrangian.
