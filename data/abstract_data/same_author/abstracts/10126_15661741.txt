Propagators with the Mandelstam-Leibbrandt prescription in the light-cone gauge
We show that the Feynman propagator in the light-cone gauge with the Mandelstam-Leibbrandt prescription has a logarithmic growth for large n-tilde{center_dot}x which is related to the presence of a residual gauge invariance. Furthermore, we show that the retarded propagator for the n-tilde{center_dot}A component of the gauge field develops a coordinate dependent mass. We argue that this feature is unphysical and may be eliminated by fixing the residual gauge degrees of freedom.
