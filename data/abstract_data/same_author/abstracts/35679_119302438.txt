The rise and fall of the ridge in heavy ion collisions
Recent data from heavy ion collisions at RHIC show unexpectedly large near-angle correlations that broaden longitudinally with increasing centrality. The amplitude of this ridge-like correlation rises rapidly, reaches a maximum, and then falls in the most central collisions. In this Letter we explain how this behavior can be uniquely explained by initial-state coordinate-space anisotropies converted into final-state momentum-space correlations. We propose v n 2 / e n , part 2 as a useful way to study length scales and provide a prediction for the ridge in Pb + Pb collisions at s NN = 2.76 TeV .
