Shear Viscosity in Weakly Coupled N=4 Super Yang-Mills Theory Compared to QCD
We compute the shear viscosity of weakly coupled N=4 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory. Our result for {eta}/s, the viscosity to entropy-density ratio, is many times smaller than the corresponding weak-coupling result in QCD. This suggests that {eta}/s of QCD near the transition point is several times larger than the viscosity bound, {eta}/s{>=}1/4{pi}.
