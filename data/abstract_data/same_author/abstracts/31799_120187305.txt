Geometric phase for the relativistic Klein-Gordon equation
Abstract The conditions under which the geometric phase may be defined for the relativistic Klein-Gordon equation are studied. In the absence of particle creation in the external electromagnetic field, the geometric phases introduced by Berry, Wilczek-Zee, and Aharonov-Anandan are shown to exist. The problem of a charged Klein-Gordon particle in an external homogenous magnetic field (relativistic Landau levels) is solved. Berry's phase is then evaluated for a slowly changing magnetic field.
