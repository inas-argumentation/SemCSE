The scattering of e- by the μ-p ground state
The scattering amplitude for the scattering of e- by the mu -p ground state is analysed by carrying out an expansion in powers of me/mmu . The analysis includes corrections due to the finite mass of the proton. The first two terms in the series provide the bounds -1.718*10-13 cm<or=a<or=-1.677*10-13 cm for the scattering length, with corrections of order me/mmu . The dependence of the differential cross section on the scattering angle, though small, is not insignificant.
