Isospin mixing at finite temperature in 80Zr
Isospin mixing induced by the Coulomb interaction has been studied in the compound nucleus 80 Zr at T~2 MeV produced in the fusion-evaporation reaction 40 Ca+ 40 Ca at E beam = 136 MeV. The isospin impurity was measured using the first step of the Giant Dipole Resonance γ decay. The preliminary value of the Coulomb spreading width has been extracted via statistical model analysis of the measured γ spectrum.
