EXCLUSIVE ELECTRODISINTEGRATION OF THE DEUTERON IN THE BETHEÄSALPETER APPROACH
An exclusive process of the deuteron electrodisintegration is analyzed in the framework of BetheA Salpeter formalism with a phenomenological Graz II rank-three separable interaction. The approxi- mations made are the neglect ofnal-state interaction, two-body exchange currents, negative-energy components of the bound-state vertex function and the scattering T matrix.
