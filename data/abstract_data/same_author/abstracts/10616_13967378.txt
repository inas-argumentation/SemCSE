On the S-wave πD-scattering length in the relativistic field theory model of the deuteron
The S-wave scattering length of the strong piondeuteron (πD) scattering is calculated in the relativistic field theory model of the deuteron suggested in [1,2]. The theoretical result agrees well with the experimental data. The important role of the Δ-resonance contribution to the elastic πD-scattering is confirmed.
