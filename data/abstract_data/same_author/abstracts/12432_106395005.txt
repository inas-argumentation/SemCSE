Muon Pair Production by High Energy Muons and Muon Bundles Underground
Cross section for electromagnetic production of muon pairs by high energy muons taking into account atomic and nuclear formfactors has been considered. A convenient approximate formula for a differential cross section is given. The ratio of the flux of double and triple muon events produced in this process to flux of single muons has been calculated for various observation depths. It is shown that earlier performed calculations seriously overestimated the contribution of muon pair production to the intensity of narrow muon bundles underground.
