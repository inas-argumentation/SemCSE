Hadronic correlation functions in the QCD plasma phase.
We present results for static hadronic correlation functions in the high-temperature phase of QCD with four flavors of dynamical quarks. We confirm chiral-symmetry restoration through the degeneracies of the spectrum of screening lengths. We also find that the screening lengths for the {rho} and {ital N} channels at {ital T}{approx gt}1.2{ital T}{sub {ital c}} are close to that in a free quark gas.
