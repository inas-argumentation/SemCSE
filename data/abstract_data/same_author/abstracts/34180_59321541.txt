Discovery of the Needless of Outgas Annealing after Horizontally Continuously Rotated Electropolishing with Niobium Superconducting RF Cavities
Eletropolishing has a superiority for high gradient: Eacc > 30 MV/m with superconducting niobium bulk cavities, however a hydrogen out gas procedure is inevitable. This requirement makes the surface treatment procedure complicated and more expensive. The requirement was fixed in the R&D of TRISTAN sc project. That time the R&D schedule was so tight and that investigation was not always enough. There is a room to be reconsidered it on the horizontally continuously rotated electroplishing (HRC-EP). We have studied the relationship between the successive material removals by a step of 40μm with HRC-EP and hydrogen Q-disease. Any Q-diseases were not observed in every material removal up to 240μm and the needless of the annealing even with a heavy electropolishing was discovered. This contribution describes the results.
