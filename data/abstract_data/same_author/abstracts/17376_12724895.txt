Reexamination of the perturbative pion form factor with Sudakov suppression.
The perturbative pion form factor with Sudakov suppression is reexamined. To guarantee the reliability of the perturbative calculations we suggest that the running coupling constant should be frozen at {alpha}{sub {ital s}}({ital t}={l_angle}{ital k}{sub {ital T}}{r_angle}) and {l_angle}{ital k}{sub {ital T}}{r_angle} is the average transverse momentum which can be determined by the pionic wave function. In addition we correct the previous calculations about the Sudakov suppression factor which plays an important role in the perturbative calculations for the pion form factor.
