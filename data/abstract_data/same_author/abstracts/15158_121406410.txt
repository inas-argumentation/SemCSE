Search for an isotensor electromagnetic interaction
In order to establish limits on an isotensor component of the electromagnetic interaction, an inelastic-electron-scattering experiment on 12C, 16O and 28Si has been performed. Due to the significant contribution of two-body processes (i.e. meson exchange currents) at large-momentum transfers, ΔT = 2 transitions are in principle possible. No excitation of T = 2 levels has been observed at the level of 10-1 of the expected meson exchange current contribution to T = 0, 1 levels.
