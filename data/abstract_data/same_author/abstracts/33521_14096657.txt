Geometric measurement of topological susceptibility on large lattices
Abstract The topological susceptibility of the quenched QCD vacuum is measured on large lattices for β = 6.0, 6.2 and 6.4. Charges possibly induced by O ( a ) dislocations are identified and shown to have little effect on the measured susceptibility. As β increases fewer, such questionable charges are found. Scaling is checked by examining the ratios of the susceptibility to three physical observables computed in Refs. [1–4] and Λ latt .
