Particle Production and Inelastic Proton Spectra at the CERN ISR
Results are presented on the production of charged partiles at small c.m. angles in proton‐proton collisions at the CERN ISR. Scaling features are emphasized for produced particles π, K, p. The inelastic proton spectra show features associated with diffractive excitation. This interpretation is supported by two‐particle correlation measurements.
