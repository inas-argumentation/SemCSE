Small‐x effects in forward‐jet production at HERA
We investigate small‐x effects in forward‐jet production at HERA in the two‐hard‐scale region kT ∼ Q ≫ ΛQCD. We show that, despite describing different energy regimes, both a BFKL parametrization and saturation parametrizations describe well the H1 and ZEUS data for dσ/dx published a few years ago. This is confirmed when comparing the predictions to the latest data.
