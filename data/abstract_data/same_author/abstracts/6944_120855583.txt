Vortex condensation and the spectrum of the lowest mass states in two-dimensional σ models
We discuss the spectrum of the lowest mass states as a function of the vortex condensation in two-dimensional σ models with a non-trivial topology and argue that the phase where the vortices condense cannot attain the weak coupling limit. Finite size scaling analysis with Monte Carlo methods indicates a second order Ising-like transition toward a vortex free vacuum in the O(3) × O(3) chiral model.
