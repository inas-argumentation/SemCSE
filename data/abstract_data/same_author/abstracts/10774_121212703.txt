Comment on a paper by Buckingham and Herring on the magneto-electric force in quark search experiments
Abstract Buckingham and Herring have given an explanation of the magneto-electric force that we did identify in our quark search experiment. We agree with the explanation, but show that the relation between the quantity β and the experimental data used by Buckingham and Herring in their analysis needs a 13% modification; the statements by BH implying an inconsistency in the values of some of our residual charges are shown to be incorrect. The unexpected check of internal consistency of our data implied by the BH explanation of the magneto-electric force is in fact satisfied.
