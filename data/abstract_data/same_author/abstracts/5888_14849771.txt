Is D_s(2700) a charmed tetraquark state ?
In this article, we take the point of view that the Ds(2700) be a tetraquark state, which consists of a scalar diquark and a vector antidiquark, and calculate its mass with the QCD sum rules. The numerical result indicates that the mass of the vector charmed tetraquark state is about MV = (3.75±0.18)GeV or MV = (3.71 ± 0.15)GeV from different sum rules, which is about 1GeV larger than the experimental data. Such tetraquark component should be very small in the Ds(2700). PACS number: 12.38.Aw, 14.40.Lb
