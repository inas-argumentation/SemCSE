Supersymmetric dynamical generation of the grand unification scale
The grand unification scale M GUT ∼ 10 16 GeV may arise from dynamical effects. With the advances in understanding of sypersymmetric dynamics, we show how the grand unified group may be broken dynamically by a strong gauge group with a dynamical scale ∼ 10 16 GeV. We also show how this mechanism can be combined with solutions to the doublet-triplet splitting problem. The same method could also be used for other symmetry breakings at intermediate scales as well.
