What We can Learn from High-Energy, Soft (pp) Interactions
The purpose of this review is to report on a series of similarities between multiparticle hadronic systems produced in (pp) interactions and in (e+e−) annihilation1–12. The new feature of this work is the fact that, in order to establish these similarities, the basic principle is to evaluate, for each (pp) interaction, the correct energy available for particle production. The old trend was to select high-p T processes.
