Search for the exotic nucleus He10
A search has been conducted for the exotic neutron rich nucleus /sup 10/He among projectile fragments produced by an E/A = 30 MeV /sup 18/O beam. No /sup 10/He/sup 2+/ ions were observed. The upper limit on the production rate of /sup 10/He is determined to be less than 3 x 10/sup -5/ that of /sup 8/He.
