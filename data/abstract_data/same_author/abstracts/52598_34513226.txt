Incorporating priority scheme in IPv6 over ATM networks
An effective method to support multiple QoS classes in IPv6 over ATM networks is implemented by mapping of the priority field in IPv6 to the ATM cell. Such a scheme might not be necessary for simple congestion control in a network of equal capacity links; however, it might be important for multicasting to destinations reachable through a very diverse capacity network. When network congestion occurs, the cell loss requirements for different services can be satisfied by selectively discarding low priority cells. The results obtained show how the QoS can be affected by this field and how priority can be given for more important traffic in times of network congestion.
