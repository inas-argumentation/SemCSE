Very light axion
Here, the question arises, “Why is |θ̄| so small?”, which is the parameter problem in the standard model. It is commonly called the strong CP problem. Most small parameters in physics have led to some ideas, mostly those related to symmetries. For example, the small ratio MW /MP ≪ 1 (the so-called gauge hierarchy problem) led to supersymmetric solution [3], and mu,md ≪ 1 GeV led to the chiral symmetry [4]. The nicest solution of the strong CP problem is the existence of axion (very light, or invisible(?)
