Gluino zero-modes for non-trivial holonomy calorons
We couple fermion fields in the adjoint representation (gluinos) to the SU(2) gauge field of unit charge calorons defined on R 3 × S1. We compute corresponding zero- modes of the Dirac equation. These are relevant in semiclassical studies of N = 1 Super- symmetric Yang-Mills theory. Our formulas, show that, up to a term proportional to the vector potential, the modes can be constructed by different linear combinations of two contributions adding up to the total caloron field strength.
