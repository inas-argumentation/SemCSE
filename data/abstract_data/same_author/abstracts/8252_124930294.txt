A Rigorous Calculation for the Radiator in e~+ e~-Process
Using the precise expression of electron, positron distribution function we obtained earlier, we derive the rigorous analytical expression of radiator in e~+e~- collision process. This series expression converges rapidly to the precise result required. The comparison with existing approximate expression is also given. Our result benefits the precice calculation for the radiative corrections in e~+e~- collision processess.
