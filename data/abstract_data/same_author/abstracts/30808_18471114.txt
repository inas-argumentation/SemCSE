The littlest Higgs model with T-parity and single top production at $ep$ collision
Based on calculating the contributions of the littlest Higgs model with Tparity (called LHT model) to the anomalous top coupling tqγ (q = u or c), we consider single top production via the t-channel partonic process eq → et at ep collision. Our numerical results show that the production cross section in the LHT model can be significantly enhanced relative to that in the standard model. ∗E-mail:cxyue@lnnu.edu.cn
