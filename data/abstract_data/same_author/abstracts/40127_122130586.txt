Phenomenological Constraints On Special Forms Of Quark Mass Matrices
Using simple analytical methods based on the Wolfenstein phenomenological parametrization of the Kobayashi-Maskawa mixing matrix, we consider the predictions arising from the quark mass matrices proposed by Fritzsch, Stech and Gronau-Johnson-Schechter, respectively. The latter ansatz turns out to be too much constrained while the semi leptonic decays of the B-meson discriminate between the first and the second models. Predictions for the Image system are also considered in the framework of the standard electroweak model and its left-right symmetric extension.
