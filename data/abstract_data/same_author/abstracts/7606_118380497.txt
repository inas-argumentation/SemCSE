Gravitino Lightest Supersymmetric Particle as Dark Matter in the Constrained Minimal Supersymmetric Standard Model
In this talk, I present the possibility of gravitino lightest supersymmetric particle (LSP) as the dark matter in the framework of the Constrained MSSM. We find the prospects of the gravitino LSP for the implication on the vacuum structure of our universe and the solutions to the cosmic lithium problems.
