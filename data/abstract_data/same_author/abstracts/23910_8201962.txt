The algebraic structure of the gl(n|m) color Calogero–Sutherland models
We extend the study on the algebraic structure of the su(n) color Calogero–Sutherland (CS) models to the case of gl(n|m) color CS model and show that the generators of the super-Yangian Y(gl(n|m)) can be obtained from two gl(n|m) loop algebras. Also, a super W∞ algebra for the SUSY CS model is constructed.
