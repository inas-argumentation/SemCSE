Anomalous moments in N=2 and 4 supersymmetries.
We calculate anomalous magnetic moments of charged particles with spin 1/2 and anomalous electric quadrupole moments of spin-1particles in /ital N/=2 and /ital N/=4 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theorieswith a spontaneously broken gauge group. All the anomalous moments vanish for/ital N/=4 while they do not vanish for /ital N/=2.
