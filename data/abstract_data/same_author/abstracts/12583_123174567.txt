Conditional moments in nuclear fragmentation and liquid-gas phase transition in hot nuclei
The conditional moments of the charge distributions of the projectile fragments in sulphur fragmentation at 200 GeV/N are obtained using the simple Monte Carlo partition method. Thus, the logarithmic correlations between the conditional moments can be observed. The distributions of conditional moments are investigated. The results of the nuclear diffractive excitation, electromagnetic dissociation, and Monte Carlo methods are compared.
