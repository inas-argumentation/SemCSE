Hadron Dynamics with Vector Mesons: Matching Theory and Experiment to Identify New Resonances
In this paper, we discuss the γp → KΣ and γn → KΣ reactions, with a peculiar behavior around the K∗0Λ threshold, due to a 1/2− resonance around 2035 MeV. Similarly, we discuss a BES experiment, J/ψ → ηK∗0K̄∗0 decay, which supports the existence of a new h1 resonance around 1830 MeV that was predicted from the vector–vector interaction. A short discussion is then made about recent advances in the charm and beauty sectors.
