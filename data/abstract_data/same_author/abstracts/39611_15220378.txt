Decay Widths of Excited Heavy Mesons From QCD Sum Rules at the Leading Order of HQET
The widths for pionic decays of lowest two excited doublets (0+, 1+) and (1+, 2+) of heavy mesons are studied with QCD sum rules in the leading order of heavy quark effective theory. The ambiguity due to presence of two distinct 1+ states are solved. The soft pion approximation is not used. BIHEP-TH-97-005 AS-ITP-97-12 SNUTP 97-049 April 1997
