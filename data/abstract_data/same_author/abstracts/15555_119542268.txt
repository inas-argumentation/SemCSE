The J = 3/2 Theta* partner to the Theta(1540) baryon
If the exotic baryon $\Theta(1540)$ is $udud\bar{s}$ with $J^P = 
{1/2}^+$, we predict that there is a \10bar with $J^P = {3/2}^+$ containing a $\Theta^*(1540-1680)$. The width $\Gamma(\Theta^* \to KN)$ is at least a factor of three larger than $\Gamma(\Theta)$. The possibilities of $\Theta^* \to KN\pi$ or $\Theta \gamma$ via $M1$ and $E2$ multipoles are discussed.
