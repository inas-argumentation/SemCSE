Results from the ARGO-YBJ test experiment
Abstract An RPC carpet covering ∼ 104 m2 (ARGO-YBJ experiment) will be installed in the YangBaJing Laboratory (Tibet, P.R. China) at an altitude of 4300 m a.s.l.. A test-module of ∼ 50 m2 has been put in operation in this laboratory and about 106 air shower events have been collected. The carpet capability of reconstructing the shower features is presented.
