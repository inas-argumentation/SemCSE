Difference equations for the correlation functions of the eight-vertex model
The authors propose that the correlation functions of the inhomogeneous eight-vertex model in the anti-ferroelectric regime satisfy a system of difference equations with respect to the spectral parameters. Solving the simplest difference equation they obtain the expression for the spontaneous staggered polarization conjectured by Baxter and Kelland (1974). They also discuss a related construction of vertex operators on the lattice.
