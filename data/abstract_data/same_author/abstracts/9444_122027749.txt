The Ising Model, the Yang-Lee Edge Singularity, and 2D Quantum Gravity
We consider one-and two-matrix models recently used to define nonperturbative 2D quantum gravity. We prove the equivalence of the m = 3 multicritical one-matrix model and the Yang-Lee edge singularity coupled to 2D quantum gravity. We also derive formulae for the correlation functions for the Yang-Lee edge singularity and for the Ising model in their summed over topologies formulations.
