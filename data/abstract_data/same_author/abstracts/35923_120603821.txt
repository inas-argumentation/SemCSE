Strange particle enhancement in ultrarelativistic nucleus-nucleus scattering
We demonstrate that a proper treatment of double-colour exchange in the strin model VENUS leads to double strings. Such strings correspond to a (say) forward moving quark being linked via two ordinary strings to two backward moving quarks. Leading baryons now require the breaking of two strings. Having twice the chance of producing a strange q q pair, these strings produce twice as many strange baryons as compared to ordinary strings, therefore leading to an enhancement of strange baryons in AA relative to pp collisions.
