Finite-size effects at the hadron-quark transition and heavy hybrid stars
We study the role of finite-size effects at the hadron-quark phase transition in a new hybrid equation of state constructed from an ab initio Br¨ uckner-Hartree-Fock equation of state with the realistic Bonn-B potential for the hadronic phase and a covariant nonlocal Nambu–Jona-Lasinio model for the quark phase. We construct static hybrid star sequences and find that our model can support stable hybrid stars with an onset of quark matter below
