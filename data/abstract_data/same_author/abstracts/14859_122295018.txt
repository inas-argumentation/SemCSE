Gepner Models With Arbitrary Affine Invariants and the Associated Calabi-yau Spaces
Abstract The spectra of (2,2) superconformal Gepner type models with arbitrary (in particular D-type) affine invariants are analyzed. We present a complete survey of the algebraic manifolds corresponding to these models. We report on a calculation and a comparison of Euler numbers of most of them with those resulting from the Gepner construction.
