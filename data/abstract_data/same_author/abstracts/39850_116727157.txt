Production of a pair of heavy quarks in e+ e- annihilation in the threshold region
The process of e/sup +/e/sup -/ annihilation to a pair of quarks for which the semiweak decay channels Q..-->..Wq are open is discussed. The total cross section of e/sup +/e/sup -/ annihilation to a pair of such quarks near threshold is obtained. This cross section is a calculable function of the quark mass and width and the coupling constant ..cap alpha../sub s/.
