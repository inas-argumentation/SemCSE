Determination of Pseudo-Goldstone-Boson-Photon Coupling by the Differential Time Delay of Pulsar Signals
Pseudo Goldstone bosons couple with photons through a [ital P] and [ital T] violating interaction of the form L=[ital g][sub [alpha][gamma][gamma]][alpha][ital F[tilde F]]. Strong magnetic fields in rotating compact stars induce a nonzero [bold E][center dot][bold B] outside the stellar surface which acts as a source for the pseudoscalar field. Pulsar signals propagating through this pseudoscalar hair'' suffer a differential time lag between the left and the right circularly polarized modes because of the [ital P] and [ital T] violating pseudoscalar-boson--photon interaction. Determination of this time lag up to a microsecond accuracy can lead to a measurement of (or rule out) pseudo-Goldstone-boson--photon coupling up to [ital g][sub [ital a][gamma][gamma]][le]2.0[times]10[sup [minus]11] GeV[sup [minus]1].
