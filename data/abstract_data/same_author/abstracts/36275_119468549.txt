Remarks on hadronic annihilations of heavy quark-antiquark bound states in QCD
Abstract We point out that QCD contains the prediction that, to a good approximation, no interference should occur, between direct and virtual photon channels, in the inclusive hadronic annihilation of a heavy quark-antiquark 1-- bound state (but not necessarily so for an exclusive channel). Quantitative predictions are made for υ-decays. We also comment on the decay of J ψ into even-G states.
