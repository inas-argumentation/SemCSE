Measurement of direct electron-positron pair production from hadronic bremsstrahlung
We have made a measurement of very-low-energy ${e}^{+}{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ pair production from ${\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}}p$ collisions at 18 GeV/c. Using the SLAC 1-m bubble chamber to study a sample of 178\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{3}$ interactions, we find a signal of centrally produced direct ${e}^{+}{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ pairs which accounts for (48\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}13)% of all ${e}^{+}{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ pairs detected with $|x|l0.02$ and ${P}_{T}l10$ MeV/c. This measurement agrees with a calculation of ${e}^{+}{e}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ pair production from the internal conversion of hadronic bremsstrahlung.
