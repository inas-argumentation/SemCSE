Heavy to light baryon transition form factors.
Recently, Stech found form factor relations for heavy to light transitions based on two simple dynamical assumptions for a spectator particle. In this paper we generalize his approach to the case of baryons and find that for ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{\mathit{Q}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\Lambda} (Q=b or c) only one independent form factor remains in the limit ${\mathit{m}}_{\mathit{Q}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\infty}. Furthermore, combining with the model of Guo and Kroll we determine both of the two form factors for ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{\mathit{Q}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\Lambda} in the heavy quark limit. The results are applied to ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{\mathit{b}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\Lambda}+J/\ensuremath{\psi} which is not clarified both theoretically and experimentally. It is found that the branching ratio of ${\mathrm{\ensuremath{\Lambda}}}_{\mathit{b}}$\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\Lambda}+J/\ensuremath{\psi} is of order ${10}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}5}$. \textcopyright{} 1996 The American Physical Society.
