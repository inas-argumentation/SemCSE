Dimensional reduction, spontaneous symmetry breaking and the absence of chiral fermions
Abstract It is shown that, within a special but wide class of coset space schemes, chiral, fermions arising from the dimensional reduction of a gauge theory do not survive the subsequent spontaneous symmetry breaking. This symmetry breaking, due to the existence of Higgs particles arising from the dimensional reduction, is such that there is always a final left-right symmetry in the fermion sector.
