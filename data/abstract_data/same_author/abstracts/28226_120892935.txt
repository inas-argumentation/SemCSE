Representation of supercharges over the scattering data for super-sine-Gordon system
We determine the form of the superchargesQ1 andQ2 in terms of the scattering data for the super-sine-Gordon equation. The technique is to ascertain the effect of supertranslation on the scattering data used in the inverse scattering transform. The same result was obtained by Kulish and Tsypleyv from the Riccatti equation by expanding in A.
