Analysis of Bose-Einstein condensation for an ideal relativistic Bose gas in d dimensions
We investigate the conditions for Bose-Einstein condensation of an ideal relativistic Bose gas in various spatial dimensions. From the direct numerical evaluations of the equations for the density and the specific-heat anomaly, we are able to show the qualitative difference between the massive and massless relativistic systems. As a consequence we are able to discuss the dependence of the critical exponents upon the spatial dimensionality for this system.
