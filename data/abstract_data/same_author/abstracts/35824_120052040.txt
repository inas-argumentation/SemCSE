Axially Symmetric Solutions for Field Strengths in Non-Abelian Gauge Theories
We find axially symmetric cla"ical solutions to static equations for SU (2) gauge field strengths. These equations can also be derived from the hydrodynamic Lagrangian of Seo and Okawa, so it is possible to interpret our solution hydrodynamically. In these solutions there appears a circulation flow of fluid around the z-axis owing to the vorticity source.
