2D FRACTIONAL SUPERSYMMETRY FOR RATIONAL CONFORMAL FIELD THEORY : APPLICATION FOR THIRD-INTEGER SPIN STATES
A 2D fractional supersymmetry theory is algebraically constructed. The Lagrangian is derived using an adapted superspace including, in addition to a scalar field, two fields with spins 13, 23 This theory turns out to be a rational conformal field theory. The symmetry of this model goes beyond the super-Virasoro algebra and connects these third-integer spin states. Besides the stress-momentum tensor, we obtain a supercurrent of spin 43. Cubic relations are involved in order to close the algebra; the basic algebra is no longer a Lie or a super-Lie algebra. The central charge of this model is found to be 53 Finally, we analyze the form that a local invariant action should take.
