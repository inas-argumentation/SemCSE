Modular thermostatic vapour-cooled current leads for cryogenic service
Delivering high currents efficiently and reliably from a source at ambient temperature to a cryogenic environment can still create problems, despite numerous developments in the field. Vapour-cooled current leads usually suffer from shortcomings inherent to design principles. Their efficiency depends critically on optimized conductor cross-section and good heat exchange with cooling vapour, a challenge to simple construction in a limited space. Moreover, optimized leads are susceptible both to thermal runaway should the cooling be insufficient, and to build-up of ice on terminations at reduced current. Finally, a sound construction of the terminations is essential to provide adequate mechanical support and ensure electrical insulation to ground combined with leaktightness of the helium circuit in all service conditions.
