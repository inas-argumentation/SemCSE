The Generalised scaling function: A Note
Abstract A method for determining the generalised scaling functions f n ( g ) arising in the high spin behaviour of long operator anomalous dimensions in the planar sl ( 2 ) sector of N = 4 SYM is proposed. The all-order strong coupling expansion is detailed for the prototypical third and fourth scaling functions ( f 3 ( g ) , f 4 ( g ) , respectively) and — together with high-precision numerical computations — reveals itself as crucial for disentangling the emergence of the O ( 6 ) Non-Linear Sigma Model mass-gap from different SYM ‘mass’ functions. Remarkably, only the fourth one gains contribution from the non-BES reducible densities and also shows up, as first, NLSM interaction and specific model dependence. Finally, the computation of the n -th generalised function is sketched and might be easily finalised for checks versus the computations in the sigma model or the complete string theory.
