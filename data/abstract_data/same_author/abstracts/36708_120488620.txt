Mass of the scalar glueball. Higher-loop effects in the QCD sum rules
One-loop gluon condensate (〈 α s G 2 〉) corrections to the 0 ++ glueball correlation function will be used to obtain the leading 〈 α s G 2 〉 behaviour in the unsubtracted QCD sum rules (SR). The influence of these and other higher-loop effects on the scalar glueball will be investigated. Predictions of the 0 ++ mass and coupling are given based on stability criteria of the SR analysis.
