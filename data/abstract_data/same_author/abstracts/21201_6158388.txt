D4 brane probes in gauge/gravity duality
We propose a Dirac-Born-Infeld vertex brane+N(c) fundamental strings configuration for a probe baryon in the finite-temperature thermal gauge field via AdS/CFT correspondence. In particular, we investigate properties of this configuration in QCD(4) and warped AdS(6)xS(4). We find that, in the D4-D8 system, a holographic probe baryon can be described as N(c) fundamental strings connecting through a vertex D4 brane wrapped on S(4). In QCD(4) background, a closed vertex can exist in a confined phase but cannot exist in a deconfined phase. In the low temperature region, the screening effect still exists in the confined phase like a meson, and the vertex D4 brane dominates the baryon mass. The lower energy state corresponds to the vertex brane closer to the radial cutoff position (r=r(c)), and the higher energy state corresponds to the vertex brane a little farther away from the cutoff position. The high energy limit of this configuration is just like the unclosed vertex brane configuration in a higher temperature deconfined phase. In warped AdS(6)xS(4) background, a closed vertex can exist in a deconfined phase and the vertex contains a spike, while fundamental strings are relatively short. The screening length should be defined through the distance between the top position of the vertex spike and the boundary.
