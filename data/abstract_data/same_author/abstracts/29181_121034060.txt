On radial oscillations in viscous accretion discs surrounding neutron stars
Radial oscillations resulting from axisymmetric perturbations in viscous accretion discs surrounding neutron stars in X-ray binary systems have been investigated. Within the framework of the α-viscosity model a series of hydrodynamic calculations demonstrates that the oscillations are global for α∼1. On the other hand, for α≤0.4 the oscillations are local and are confined to the disc boundaries.
