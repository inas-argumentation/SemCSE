Antiproton--proton annihilation into charged pion and kaon pairs
Events of the type anti pp $Yields$ K$sup -$K$sup +$, $pi$$sup - $$pi$$sup +$ were studied at 0.79 to 2.43 GeV/c. Differential cross sections and Legendre coefficient ratios are shown. Resonances found as a result of model fitting showing spin--parity assignment versus mass squared are also shown. (JFP)
