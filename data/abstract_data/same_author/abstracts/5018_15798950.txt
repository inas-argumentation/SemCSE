Heavy quark limit in the model with confined light quarks and infrared heavy quark propagators
We calculate the weak coupling constants of B and D mesons, and the Isgur‐Wise form factor within the relativistic quark model with confined light quarks and with taking into account the infrared behavior of the heavy quark propagator. We investigate their dependence on the mass difference E=mH−MQ, and the parameter, characterizing the infrared behavior of the heavy quark propagator in the heavy quark limit. It is found that the weak decay constants depend rather strongly on the mass difference E with the infrared parameter ν being fixed. As for the Isgur‐Wise function its E dependence is found weak whereas it depends more strongly on the infrared parameter, leading to the suppression of this function at maximum recoil.
