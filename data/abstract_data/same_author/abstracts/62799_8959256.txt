Adjoining Almost Disjoint Permutations
We show that it is consistent with ZFC + ¬CH that there is a maximal almost disjoint permutation family A ⊆ Sym(N) such that A is a proper subset of an eventually different family E ⊆ N and |A| < |E|. We also ask several questions in this area. Mathematics Subject Classification: 03E35, 20A15, 20B07, 20B35.
