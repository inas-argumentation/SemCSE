The general QCD parametrization and the large Nc description: Some remarks
Stimulated by a recent paper of Buchmann and Lebed,a comparison is presented of the two methods mentioned in the title for treating hadron properties in QCD.Doubts arise on the equivalence of the large Nc description to real QCD. (PACS: 12.38.Aw; 11.15.Pg; 13.40.Dk)
