Low-pressure drift chamber with a built-in scintillator
Abstract A low-pressure drift chamber with a built-in scintillator has been developed. Pulse height characteristics and the velocity of electrons, drifting in various electric fields in isobutane, have been measured. A standard deviation of the coordinate determined by measuring the drift time of the electrons has been found to be around 100 μm for a 20 mm drift length at 50 Torr of isobutane. A standard deviation of the second coordinate measured by a delay line is better than 100 μm. The work was performed at the High Energy Laboratory, JINR.
