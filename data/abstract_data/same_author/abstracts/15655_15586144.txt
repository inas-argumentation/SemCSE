A geometrizing higher twist effect on nuclear target
The higher twist effect in deep inelastic scattering on the nuclear target are studied by using time ordered perturbation theory. We shown that the collinearly rescattering of the outgoing quark on the extra nucleon via the contacting gluon-pair dominates a nuclear size-dependent effect. The Qiu-Vitev resummation is justify by using the geometric properties of the high twist amplitudes. The leading contributions of nuclear enhanced effect to the standard QCD evolution equation are resumed in the same framework.
