Diffractive production of jets and weak bosons and tests of hard-scattering factorization
We extract diractive parton densities from diractive, deep inelastic (DIS) ep data from the ZEUS experiment. Then we use these ts to predict the diractive production of jets and of W ’s and Z’s in pp collisions at the Tevatron. Although the DIS data require a hard quark density in the pomeron, we nd fairly low rates for the Tevatron processes (a few percent of the inclusive cross section). This results from the combined eects of Q 2 evolution and of a normalization of the parton densities to the data. The calculated rates for W production are generally consistent with the preliminary data from the Tevatron. However, the jet data from CDF with a \Roman pot" trigger are substantially lower than the results of our calculations; if conrmed, this would signal a breakdown of hard-scattering factorization.
