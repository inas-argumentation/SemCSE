FORMULATION OF NEGATIVELY CHARGED PARTICLE RAPIDITY DISTRIBUTION IN NUCLEUS–NUCLEUS COLLISIONS AT HIGH ENERGY
The negatively charged particle rapidity distribution in nucleus–nucleus collisions at high energy has been described by the thermalized cylinder picture. The calculated results are compared and found to be in agreement with the experimental data of the reactions 16O + Au at 60A and 200A GeV, 32S + Ag and S at 200A GeV, and 208Pb + Pb at 158A GeV bombarding energies.
