Single and double π − / π + ratios in heavy-ion reactions as probes of the high-density behavior of the nuclear symmetry energy
Based on the isospin- and momentum-dependent hadronic transport model IBUU04, effects of the nuclear symmetry energy on the single and double ${\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}/{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}$ ratios in central reactions of $^{132}\mathrm{Sn}+^{124}\mathrm{Sn}$ and $^{112}\mathrm{Sn}+^{112}\mathrm{Sn}$ at a beam energy of 400 MeV/nucleon are studied. It is found that around the Coulomb peak of the single ${\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}/{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}$ ratio the double ${\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}/{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}$ ratio taken from the two isotopic reactions retains about the same sensitivity to the density dependence of nuclear symmetry energy. Because the double ${\ensuremath{\pi}}^{\ensuremath{-}}/{\ensuremath{\pi}}^{+}$ ratio can significantly reduce the systematic errors, it is thus a more effective probe for the high-density behavior of the nuclear symmetry energy.
