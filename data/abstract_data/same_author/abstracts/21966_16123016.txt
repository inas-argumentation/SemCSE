A Unified Model of Short and Long Gamma-Ray Bursts, X-Ray Rich Gamma-Ray Bursts, and X-Ray Flashes
Taking into account the recent suggestion that a short gamma-ray burst (GRB) looks like the first 1 s of a long GRB, we propose that the jet of a GRB consists of multiple subjets or subshells (i.e., an inhomogeneous jet model). The multiplicity of the subjets along a line of sight is an important parameter. If is large ( k1) nn ss the event looks like a long GRB, while if is small (∼1) the event looks like a short GRB. If our line of sight ns is off-axis to any subjets, the event looks like an X-ray flash or an X-ray–rich GRB. The lognormal distribution of durations of short and long GRBs are also suggested in the same model.
