Maximum matchings in a class of random graphs
Let Vn = {1,2,…,n} and D(n, m) be the set of digraphs with vertex set Vn in which each v ϵ Vn has outdegree m. D(n, m) is chosen uniformly at random from D(n, m) and then D(n, m) is obtained by ignoring the orientation of the edges of D(n, m). We show that 
Limn → ∞n evenPr(D(n, 1) has a perfect matching) = 0 
, 
Limn → ∞n evenPr(D(n, 2) has a perfect matching) = 1 
.
