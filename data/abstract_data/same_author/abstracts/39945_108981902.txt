The BEPCII project
Publisher Summary This chapter discusses Beijing Electron–Positron Collider spectrometer and its high statistical data samples for the Tau-Charm physics. BEPC runs at the threshold of the Charmonium family, the production cross sections are high, and the backgrounds are low. The high power klystrons of 45 MW will replace the existing 30MW klystrons to provide the full energy injection up to 1.89GeV. This is essential for the top-up injection to optimize the average luminosity of BEPCII. The design goals of the Beijing Spectrometer upgrade (BES III) are (1) to improve the detector resolution, specially the measurement of photon, and improve the particle identification to reduce the systematic errors, matching the high statistics of BEPCII, (2) to increase the acceptance of the detector; and (3) to adapt to the high event rate (3 KHz at the J/ψ peak) and the short bunch spacing (8 ns). Assuming BEPCII runs 5 months per year for physics, the average luminosity is 30% of peak luminosity, and the acceptance of 60–70%, each year, one could expect to obtain 6000 M–10000 M J/ψ events, or 2000 M ψ' events, or 10 M Tau pairs at 3.67GeV.
