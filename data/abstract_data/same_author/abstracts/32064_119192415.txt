Recent results from BESIII
Using about 226× 106 J/ψ events and 106 × 106 ψ ′ events collected with the BESIII detector at the BEPCIIe+e− collider, the Dalitz plot ofη ′ → ηπ+π− decay is studied, the direct measurement ofa0(980)− f0(980) mixing is performed,X(2120) andX(2370) are observed in the π+π−η ′ invariant mass spectrum, ψ ′ → γπ0 andψ ′ → γη are observed for the first time, and the decaysχcJ → γV (V = φ , ρ0, ω) are studied.
