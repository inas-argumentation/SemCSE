The Demo: A Real-Time Score Detection and Recognition Approach in Broadcast Basketball Sports Video
For broadcast sports video, score information is an effective mid-level representation to facilitate high-level video content analysis. In this paper, we propose a real-time approach to detect score region and recognize scores in broadcast basketball sports video. The flow chart of our proposed approach has two major modules: score detection and score recognition. Using our approach, we can locate score regions automatically and reliably without detecting all the texts in the frame, and recognize the scores without training process. The running speed of our algorithm is fast.
