Protein synthesis and morphological changes in the secretory epithelium of the venom gland of Crotalus durissus terrificus at different times after manual extraction of venom.
Protein synthesis and morphological changes in the secretory epithelium of the venom glands of the South American rattlesnake Crotalus durissus terrificus were studied at 0, 2, 4, 8, 10, 16 and 32 days after manual extraction of the venom. The incorporation of 3 H-tyrosine was measured in gland homogenates and followed by radioautography. Milking stimulates protein synthesis and causes an increase in the size of the cells of the secretory epithelium. There was a good correlation between the height of the cells and the incorporation of labeled amino acids. The most active stage of secretion was at the 4 day interval after milking.
