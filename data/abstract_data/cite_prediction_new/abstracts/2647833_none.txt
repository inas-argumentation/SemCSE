Characterizations and Decompositions of Almost Strictly Positive Matrices
A nonsingular matrix is called almost strictly totally positive when all its minors are nonnegative, and furthermore these minors are positive if and only if their diagonal entries are positive. In this paper we give a characterization of these matrices in terms of the positivity of a very reduced number of their minors (which are called boundary minors), improving previous characterizations that have appeared in the literature. We show the role of boundary minors in accurate computations with almost strictly totally positive matrices. Moreover, we analyze the QR factorization of these matrices, showing the differences and analogies with that of totally positive matrices.
