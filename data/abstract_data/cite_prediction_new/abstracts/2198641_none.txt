Two new irregular acyclic sesquiterpenes aldehydes from Santolina corsica essential oil
Two isomeric irregular sesquiterpene aldehydes, namely 3,9‐dimethyl‐6‐isopropyl‐2(E),7(E),9‐decatrienal and 3,9‐dimethyl‐6‐isopropyl‐2(Z),7(E),9‐decatrienal, were isolated from the essential oil of Santolina corsica and their structures were elucidated by 1D and 2D NMR spectroscopy.
