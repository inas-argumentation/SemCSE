Application of n-type microcrystalline hydrogenated silicon oxide film to hetero-junction microcrystalline silicon solar cells
In this study, the effect of wide-gap n-type μc-Si<inf>1−X</inf>O<inf>X</inf>:H film on the performance of hetero-junction μc-Si:H solar cells has been investigated. We have developed n-μc-Si<inf>1−X</inf>O<inf>X</inf>:H films by RF-PECVD (13.56 MHz) using a gas mixture of silane (SiH<inf>4</inf>), hydrogen (H<inf>2</inf>), 1%-hydrogen-diluted phosphine (PH<inf>3</inf>) and carbon dioxide (CO<inf>2</inf>) at a low substrate temperature of about 200 °C. It was found that optical band gap (E<inf>04</inf>) of n-type μc-Si<inf>1−X</inf>O<inf>X</inf>:H films were increased to larger than 2.4eV with increasing CO<inf>2</inf>/SiH<inf>4</inf> ratio and power density. Under the optimized condition, a film showed the E<inf>04</inf> of 2.38 eV, dark conductivity (σ<inf>d</inf>) of 0.086 S/cm, activation energy of 0.076 eV and crystal volume fraction (X<inf>C</inf>) of 37%. The effect of n-μc-Si<inf>1−X</inf>O<inf>X</inf>:H was confirmed by J-V characteristics and spectral response of the hetero-junction μc-Si:H solar cells. The short-circuit current (J<inf>SC</inf>) was increased from 23.1 mA/cm<sup>2</sup> to 23.7 mA/cm<sup>2</sup> and the spectral response in the long wavelength region was increased. Even if this observation needs further confirmation, our study demonstrated that wide-gap n-type μc-Si<inf>1−X</inf>O<inf>X</inf>:H films is beneficial for improving the performance of hetero-junction μc-Si:H solar cells.
