Electromagnetic characteristics of composite coating based on Z-type hexaferrite at microwave
The paper presents the results of an experimental study of the reflection coefficient of the layer composite coatings based on Z-type hexaferrite on metal. The influence of the binder on the measured characteristics is investigated. The values of the imaginary part of the complex permeability from the frequency are calculated. It is shown that the natural ferromagnetic resonance in composites is considered at frequencies above 4 GHz.
