Top quark production at threshold with O(αs2) accuracy
We calculate the next-to-next-to-leading order correction to the cross section for top quark pair production in e + e − annihilation in the threshold region, resumming all O [( α s / β ) n × ( α s 2 , β 2 , α s β )] terms of perturbation series. We find that the magnitude of the NNLO correction is comparable to the size of the NLO corrections.
