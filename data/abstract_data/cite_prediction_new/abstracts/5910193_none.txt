M / M / 2 / K Interdependent Queueing Model with Controllable Arrival Rates and Feedback
In this paper the M/M/2/K queueing Model with controllable arrival rates, different service rates and feedback is considered. The steady state solution and system characteristics are derived for this model. The analytical results are numerically illustrated and the effect of the nodal parameters on the system characteristics are studied and relevant conclusions are presented.
