The transferability of library research skills from high school to college
M. Elspeth Goodin has been the director of the library media center at Kittatinny Regional High School in Newton, New Jersey, for the past sixteen years. She also teaches graduate courses as an adjunct professor at Rutgers University’s School of Communication, Information’ and Library Studies. A member If AASL’s Affiliate Assembly and past president of the Education Media Association of New Jersey, she received her Ph.D. from Rutgers in 1987. Under her direction, the library media center has been cited as one of New Jersey’s model schools for professional visits and observation.
