The Cognitive Goal Achievement of 3 Physics & Laboratory Courses in Secondary School
This paper presents a report on the level of achievement in the third grade of high school courses of physics and laboratory. This research is assessment-oriented and evaluative and in collecting of data follows the grounded theory method. Curricular goal achievement was assessed with evaluating mathematics and science students (N=369) from five districts in Tehran via academic achievement test. The analysis of data showed that: 1The goals of physics curriculum in cognitive domain had been achieved. 2There were no significant differences between male and female mathematics and science students in goal achievement in cognitive domain. 3There were significant differences in cognitive domain in between students who studied in different districts of Tehran.
