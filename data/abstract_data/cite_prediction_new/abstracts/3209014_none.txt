Evolving the experimental design for self-assembling amphiphilic systems
Amphiphilic molecules in particular environments may self-assemble and originate chemical entities, such as vesicles or micelles, which are relevant in technological applications. Experimentation in this field is difficult because of the high dimensionality of the search space and the high cost of each experiment. In this research we address the problem of a design of the experiments, which selects just a small number both of variables and experiments. We adopt a Nature-inspired approach based on the paradigm of the evolution and through a genetic algorithm and a neural network model we discover the very small set of variables that are relevant in affecting the optimization process.
