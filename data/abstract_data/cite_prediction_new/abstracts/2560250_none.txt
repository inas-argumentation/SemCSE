ANNEALING TREATMENTS AND CHARACTERISATION OF NICKEL-DOPED ANTIMONY SULPHIDE THIN FILMS
The influence of post-deposition annealing on the structural and optical properties of thin films of nickel-doped antimony sulphide grown using the solution growth technique is reported. The films were grown at room temperature and annealed at annealing temperatures ≤ 200 o C. The films were then characterised using X-ray diffractometry to investigate the structural properties and UV-VIS optical spectroscopy to determine the transmittance versus wavelength measurements, thus enabling to evaluate the optical constants. The results show that the transmittances of the films were significantly modified by the heat treatments. In particular the transmittance were higher for films annealed at annealing temperatures ≥ 50 o C. The optical absorption coefficient were all > 10 4 cm -1 , the energy bandgap was direct with values in the range of 2.3 eV to 2.46 eV. The dielectric constant was between 5.2 to 7.9. The resistivity was observed to decrease with an increase in the annealing temperature, with values in the range suitable for application in various optoelectronic applications.
