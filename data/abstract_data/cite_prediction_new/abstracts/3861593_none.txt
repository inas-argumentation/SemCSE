Monopole instantons and rotating four dimensional solutions in string theory from abelian dimensional reduction
We compute the one loop string vacuum backgrounds corresponding to the non-compact coset theory SU(2, 1)/SU(2). The five dimensional singular solution factorizes in an asymptotic region as the linear dilaton solution and a S3 model, the quantization of the level k resulting from the axionic term. It presents two abelian compact isometries that allow to reinterpreting it from a four dimensional point of view as a static and magnetically charged instanton in one case, and as a stationary and magnetically charged rotating space-time resembling in some aspects the Kerr-Newman solution of general relativity in the other one. ∗This work was partially supported by CONICET, Argentina †Electronic address: lugo@dartagnan.fisica.unlp.edu.ar
