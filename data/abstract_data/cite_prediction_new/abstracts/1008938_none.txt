THE PURPOSE OF THIS PAPER is to highlight important wood machining research published during 1972 and 1973. No attempt is made to elaborate on the impact or significance of individual contributions. Rather, the style and format of previous reviews (Koch 1968, McMillin 1970) were followed in an attempt to give the reader a concise summary of activity in various categories of endeavor. --
