Conditions for Existence of Uniformly Consistent Classifiers
We consider the statistical problem of binary classification, which means attaching a random observation <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$X$ </tex-math></inline-formula> from a separable metric space <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$E$ </tex-math></inline-formula> to one of the two classes, 0 or 1. We prove that the consistent estimation of conditional probability <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$p(X)= \mathsf {P}(Y=1 \mid X)$ </tex-math></inline-formula>, where <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$Y$ </tex-math></inline-formula> is the true class of <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$X$ </tex-math></inline-formula>, is equivalent to the consistency of a class of empirical classifiers. We then investigate for what classes <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$P$ </tex-math></inline-formula> there exist an estimate <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\hat p$ </tex-math></inline-formula> that is consistent uniformly in <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$p\in P$ </tex-math></inline-formula>. We show that this holds if and only if <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$P$ </tex-math></inline-formula> is a totally bounded subset of <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$L^{1}(E,\mu )$ </tex-math></inline-formula>, where <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\mu $ </tex-math></inline-formula> is the distribution of <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$X$ </tex-math></inline-formula>. In the case, where <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$E$ </tex-math></inline-formula> is countable, we give a complete characterization of classes <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Pi $ </tex-math></inline-formula>, allowing consistent estimation of <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$p$ </tex-math></inline-formula>, uniform in <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$(\mu ,p)\in \Pi $ </tex-math></inline-formula>.
