DIAGNOSIS OF TOOTH DECAY USING POLARIZED MICRO-RAMAN CONFOCAL SPECTROSCOPY *
The tooth enamel presents a Raman spectrum with strong polarization anisotropy. Carious lesions of the enamel will produce an alteration of local symmetry. This will reduce the anisotropy of the Raman spectra. Thus micro-Raman polarized spectroscopy could be a used in early detection of teeth caries.
