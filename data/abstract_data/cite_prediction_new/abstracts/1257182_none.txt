A national perspective on the conflict in Gambella
The western Gambella region is one the most conflict-ridden regions in Ethiopia. The paper identified five types of conflict: Inter-ethnic; intra-ethnic; indigenes and migrants; the state against ethnic groups, and cross-border conflicts. All these conflicts have distinctive local and regional roots but a comprehensive understanding of the conflict situation in the region entails linking them up with the national level dynamics. The paper explored this interface from five angles.
