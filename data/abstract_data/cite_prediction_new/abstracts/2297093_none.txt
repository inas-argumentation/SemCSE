Comparison of quantitative ELISA and semiquantitative Dustscreen™ for determination of Der p 1, Der f 1, and Fel d 1 in domestic dust samples
Background: Quantitative ELISA kits used for the measurement of indoor all_ergens are time‐consuming. Therefore, an immunodot assay (Dustscreen™) has been introduced, so that results can be obtained within 4 h. In order to assess the potential applications of the new method, we compared quantitative ELISA methods and Dustscreen.
