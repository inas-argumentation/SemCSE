Multi carrier PWM switching technique for multilevel inverter for various loads
In this paper the multilevel inverters are used to reduce the harmonic distortion (THD) by cascading a full bridge with half bridge cells. The proposed technique for the reduction of the THD is multicarrier PWM technique to generate the voltages for both nine and eleven level inverters. The designed technique allows the capacitors in the half bridge cell to have Better voltage in spite of the load changes. Further, the work is extended to the eleven level and the simulation results of the inverters are compared and the best suited is selected.
