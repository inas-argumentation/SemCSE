AWG misalignment tolerance of 16 /spl times/ 155 Mb/s WDM-PON based on ASE-injected FP-LDs
Crosstalk penalties from adjacent and nonadjacent channels are experimentally analysed as a function of wavelength misalignment between AWGs at remote node and central office in a 16/spl times/155 Mb/s WDM-PON based on ASE-injected FP-LDs.
