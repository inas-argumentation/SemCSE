Gastrin Induces Expression and Promoter Activity of the Vesicular Monoamine Transporter Subtype 2.
Gastric enterochromaffin-like cells produce histamine in response to the antral hormone gastrin and accumulate the biogenic amine in secretory organelles via vesicular monoamine transporter subtype 2. The putative effects of gastrin on vesicular monoamine transporter subtype 2 expression and promoter activity are poorly understood. In the present study we used highly enriched rat enterochromaffin-like cells (purity, >90%) and rat pheochromocytoma cells stably transfected with a gastrin/cholecystokinin B receptor to investigate the expression and transcriptional regulation of vesicular monoamine transporter subtype 2. Stimulation of vesicular monoamine transporter subtype 2 mRNA and protein expression was observed in isolated enterochromaffin-like cells after 3- to 7-h incubation with gastrin (10−7 m), forskolin (10−5 m), or ionomycin (10−5 m).
