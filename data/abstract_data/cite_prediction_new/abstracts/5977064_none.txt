Probing under pressure: a look inside the compartmental haemodynamics of skeletal muscle during rest and contraction
The cardiovascular system supplies oxygen (O2 ) and nutrients to the muscle to meet increasing physiological demands during exercise. Small O2 stores in living species require a formulated and specific balance between O2 delivery (Q˙O2 ) and utilization (V˙O2 ).
