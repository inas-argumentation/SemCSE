The effect of Russian encephalitis and other viruses on mouse leukemia.
R.S.S.E. virus has been demonstrated to inhibit leukocytosis and organ infiltration in Ak4 mouse leukemia. Survival time was not affected. The Massachusetts strain of N.D.V., Uganda S virus, Anopheles B virus, Bunyamwera virus, and Theiler's G.D. VII virus had slight antileukemic effect. Nine other strains of viruses had no detected effect upon this leukemia.
