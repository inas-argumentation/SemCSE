The Fiscal Space of Thailand: An Historical Analysis
textabstractThe concept of ‘fiscal space’ has emerged in the context of the Millennium 
Development Goals (MDGs). It is clear that enormous efforts are required from 
international donors and government and society in developing countries to achieve 
the goals. Whether a poor household can escape poverty and malnutrition, send its 
children to school, get access to safe water and good housing, live a healthy live, etc. 
depends on three factors: whether its income increases sufficiently, whether the 
government provides the required services and support and whether the international 
community assists where necessary.
