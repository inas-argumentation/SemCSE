INTRODUCTIONRough and Fuzzy set theories have been the dominantapproaches to vagueness and approximate reasoning from amathematical perspective. In rough set theory (RST), vagueand imprecise information are dealt with through binary rela-tions (for some form of indiscernibility) on a set or covers ofa set or through more abstract operators. In classical RST [2],starting from an approximation space consisting of a pair ofa set and an equivalence over it, approximations of subsets ofthe set are constructed out of equivalence partitions (these arecrisp or deﬁnite) that are also regarded as granules in manysenses. Most of the developments in RST have been within theZFC or ZF set-theoretic framework of mathematics.
