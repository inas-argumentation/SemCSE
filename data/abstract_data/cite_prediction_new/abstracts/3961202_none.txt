Using multiple metrics for analysis of improvement
To make global decisions about a project or group of projects, it is necessary to analyse several metrics in concert, as well as changes in individual metrics. This paper discusses several approaches to collective metrics analysis. First, classification tree analysis is described as a technique for evaluating both process and metrics characteristics. Next, the notion of a multiple metrics graph is introduced. Developed initially as a way to evaluate software switch quality, a multiple metrics graph allows collections of metrics to be viewed in terms of overall product or process improvement.
