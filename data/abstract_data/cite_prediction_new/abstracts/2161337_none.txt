Draft Genome Sequence of “Paramesorhizobium deserti” A-3-ET, a Strain Highly Resistant to Diverse β-Lactam Antibiotics
Here, we report the draft genome sequence of “Paramesorhizobium deserti” A-3-ET, a strain isolated from the Taklimakan Desert of Xinjiang, China, which is resistant to multiple β-lactam antibiotics and other antibiotics (kanamycin, erythromycin, streptomycin, etc.) as well.
