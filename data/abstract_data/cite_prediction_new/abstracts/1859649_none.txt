Effects of hypoxia on test performance of cebus monkeys.
Cebus monkeys were subjected to controlled periods of hypoxia by using special oxygen-nitrogen mixtures. Periods of hypoxia ranged from 4. to 60 min with oxygen concentrations of inspired gas from 4% to 8%. Animals were pretrained on a battery of six psychological tests and retested after recovery from the experimental procedure. Results of these tests indicated brain damage in three of five experimental animals. No gross neurological signs were seen in any of the subjects, and it was evident that they varied in their susceptibility to hypoxia.
