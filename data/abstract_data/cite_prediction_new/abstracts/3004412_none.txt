Modeling and efficient decoding of large vocabulary conversational speech
Capturing the large variability of conversational speech in the framework of purely phone based speech recognizers is virtually impossible. It has been shown earlier that suprasegmental features such as speaking rate, duration and syllabic, syntactic and semantic structure are important predictors of pronunciation variation. In order to allow for a tighter coupling of these predictors of pronunciation, duration and acoustic modeling a new recognition toolkit has been developed. The phonetic transcription of speech has been generalized to an attribute based representation, thus enabling the integra-tion of suprasegmental, non-phonetic features.
