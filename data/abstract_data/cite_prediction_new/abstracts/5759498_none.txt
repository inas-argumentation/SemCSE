CP asymmetry in the Higgs boson decay into a top quark pair due to top squark mixing
We investigate a potentially large CP violating asymmetry in the decay of a neutral scalar or pseudoscalar Higgs boson into a t{bar t} pair. The source of the CP nonconservation is the complex mixing in the top squark {tilde t}{sub L,R} sector. One of the interesting consequences is the different rates of the Higgs boson decays into CP conjugate polarized states.
