DEVELOPMENT AND VALIDATION OF AN ULTRA PERFORMANCE LIQUID CHROMATOGRAPHIC METHOD FOR ASSAY OF ALFUZOSIN HYDROCHLORIDE IN BLEND SAMPLE
Objective: The main objective of current study is to develop and validate UPLC method, simple, precise, accurate and specific chromatographic method for the determination of assay of alfuzosin hydrochloride in blend sample. Methods: An ultra performance liquid chromatography instrument and Inertsil ODS-3, 3.0 x 50 mm, 2µ were used for determination of alfuzosin hydrochloride. The flow rate of 1.0 mL/min was set with isocratic, the temperature of column compartment maintained at 25°C and Ultra violet detection done at 245nm wavelength. The injection volume was 5 µL. The alfuzosin hydrochloride peaks eluted at 1.303 minute and run time was set as about 2 minutes. Results: The correlation coefficient (≥ 0.999) shows the linearity of response against concentration over the range of 10 to 300%. The observed result shows that the method was rapid, precise, accurate and simple. The method was validated as per ICH guidelines. Conclusion: The developed and validated ultra performance liquid chromatographic method was suitable for determination of assay of alfuzosin hydrochloride in pharmaceutical formulations which is more useful with respect to regular laboratory analysis. The novelty of present work is first UPLC method developed and validated. This method can be conveniently used in a quality control laboratory for routine analysis of assay of alfuzosin hydrochloride.
