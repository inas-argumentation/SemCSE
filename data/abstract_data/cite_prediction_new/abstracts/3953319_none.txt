Alcaligenes xylosoxidans is a rare cause of peritonitis and is associated with high mortality despite catheter removal and antibiotic treatment. Peritoneal dialysis patients may be predisposed to infection with this organism due to their compromised immunity and the fact that the PDF provides an aqueous environment that favors the growth of this organism. Alcaligenes xylosoxidans is especially virulent due to its resistance to most antibiotic therapies, and the majority of case reports required catheter removal. We present the first case of APD-associated Alcaligenes xylosoxidans peritonitis that was successfully treated with imipenem and ciprofloxacin.
