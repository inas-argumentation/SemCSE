The effects of withholding water on the body temperature of poults.
IT might be stated that water is the most critical of the essential nutrients. Yet relatively little has been published as to its requirements by the turkey poult or of the effects of its withdrawal for extended periods of time. The fact that poults do require water has been taken for granted by turkey raisers for years yet stated in the literature by only a few researchers. Morehouse (1949) has reported weekly water consumption data for Broad Breasted Bronze poults for their first 26 weeks. That care must be exercised when attempting to change from one type of poult waterer to an unfamiliar one is suggested and demonstrated by Marsden et al. (1965).
