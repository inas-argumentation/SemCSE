Topic-Based Selective Delivery of Event Messages in Peer-to-Peer Model of Publish/Subscribe Systems in Heterogeneous Networks
In this paper, we discuss a peer-to-peer (P2P) model of a topic-based publish/subscribe (PS) system (P2PPS model) where each peer process (peer) can both subscribe and publish event messages with topics and there is no centralized coordinator. Each peer has to causally deliver event messages in which the peer is interested. In our previous studies, the TBC (topic-based causally delivery) protocol is proposed for a homogeneous synchronous network where maximum delay time between every pair of peers is the same and the maximum clock offset of each peer is the same. In this paper, we propose a heterogeneous TBC (HTBC) protocol where peers are interconnected in heterogeneous networks where maximum delay time between every pair of peers and maximum clock offset of each peer may not be the same. In the evaluation, we show the number of pairs of event messages unnecessarily ordered is reduced in the HTBC protocol.
