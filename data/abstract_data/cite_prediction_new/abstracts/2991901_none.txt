Direct dry deposition of SWCNTs for flexible thin film electronics applications
We discuss the thin film electronics applications of single walled carbon nanotube (SWCNT) networks, especially in the field of flexible electronics. We present novel method - direct dry printing developed by Canatu - for SWCNT direct, dry deposition from the reactor at ambient temperature and pressure to manufacture transparent, flexible thin film conductors and field effect transistors (TFT). Flexible SWCNT-PET conducting films show transparency-sheet resistance properties surpassing those of ITO-PET films [1]. SWCNT network TFTs on both silicon as well as polymeric substrates show mobilities of 35 cm2/Vs and on/off ratio of 5 * 106. We also fabricated logic circuits on flexible substrates [3]. Also, we developed free standing SWCNT films for various applications, including nanoparticle filter, gas flow meter and heater, laser pulsing, loadspeaker and electrochemical sensing [2].
