SELECT: An expert system shell for selecting amongst decision or management alternatives
The selection of suitable candidates from amongst a range of alternatives is a problem which is common in agricultural decision making. This paper describes the development of an expert system shell (SELECT). This shell is different from conventional rule-based expert systems because knowledge is not entered as a series of IF-THEN rules but rather as a series of questions with related conclusions. This structure means that SELECT has a knowledge representation closer to the original natural language form (i.e. English). The paper describes the development of an application, using this shell, designed to answer queries by farmers and field advisers relating to the selection of suitable winter wheat varieties in Scotland. The shell was found to be an effective medium for the development of selection problems.
