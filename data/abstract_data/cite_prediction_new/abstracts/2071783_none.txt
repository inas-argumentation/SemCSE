Khellin. Part I. Ultraviolet, infrared, and polarographic studies on three principles isolated from Ammi visnaga Lam.
The ultraviolet and infrared absorption, as well as the polarographic half-wave potentials, are reported for khellin, visnagin, and khellol-glucoside; three principles found in the seeds of the plant Ammi visnaga Lam. These properties have been found useful in the identification and assay for the principles in plant extracts and pharmaceutical preparations. Ultraviolet absorption in 95 per cent ethanol and cyclohexane have been reported for each of the principles in the region from 210 to 380 mμ. Khellin, visnagin, and khellol-glucoside may be reduced at the dropping mercury electrode and the polarograms obtained have been found useful in assay procedures. Infrared absorption curves of khellin, visnagin in chloroform, and khellol-glucoside as a Nujol mull, have been obtained. Comparison of the infrared spectra of synthetic and natural khellin and visnagin provides further confirmation to the structures assigned to these compounds.
