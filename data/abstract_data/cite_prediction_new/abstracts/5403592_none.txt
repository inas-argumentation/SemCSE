Analysis of proper configuration of wireless power transfer system for electric vehicle charging
This article deals with the description of topology configuration of wireless energy transfer system. The analysis of system is focused on the preparations of the recommendation for wireless battery charging in electric vehicles, what is target application area of proposed system. In the article the advantages and limits of the several compensation configurations of the main circuit in terms of control and transmission capabilities are discussed. This procedure shall also be used during the development of master control system of wireless energy transfer system. The main quality indicators are high transmitted power, high efficiency and the transmission distance, which might be the in range of the ground clearance of standard vehicles.
