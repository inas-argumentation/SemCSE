PFC / JA-96-43 Transport Studies in the Scrape-off Layer and Divertor of Alcator C-Mod
Parallel and cross-field heat and particle transport in the divertor and scrape-off layer (SOL) of Alcator C-Mod have been systematically studied using an extensive array of divertor/SOL diagnostics. Some key results include: (a) Classical parallel heat transport is obeyed with ion-neutral momentum coupling effects. (b) Cross-field heat transport is proportional to local gradients. (c) Cross field heat diffusivity is found to scale with local density, n, electron temperature, Te, and magnetic connection length, L, approximately as Xi ~ Te-0 .6 n-0 .6 L-0.7 in ohmic L-mode discharges, insensitive to toroidal field strength. (d) X± depends on divertor neutral retention. (e) H-mode transport barrier effects partially extend into the SOL. (f) Thermoelectric currents in the SOL may play a role in forming inside/outside divertor asymmetries. (g) Reversed parallel flows in the SOL depend on inside/outside divertor asymmetries and may be caused by ionization source imbalances in the divertor legs. 1.
