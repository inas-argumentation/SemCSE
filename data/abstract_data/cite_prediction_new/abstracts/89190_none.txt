Investigation of simulating methods for self-similar traffic flows: The QoS-characteristics depend on the type of distribution in self-similar traffic
To evaluate the quality of service parameters of self-similarity traffic you can use simulation methods. However, the result of this assessment will depend on the chosen modeling method. Since self-similar traffic (time interval between requests) best describes the Weibull or Pareto distribution, it is for him gives investigation. The influence of simulations methods to parameters QoS in conditions of self-similarity traffic flow is probed.
