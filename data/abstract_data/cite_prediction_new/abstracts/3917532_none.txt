We agree that increasing the dose of PPI may not provide the expected effect. In a recent study from our lab omeprazole 20 mg b.i.d. and hs only reduced percent time gastric pH ,4 from 48% to 31% compared to 20 mg omeprazole b.i.d. A better effect on control of nocturnal pH was seen by adding ranitidine 150 mg or 300 mg hs to 20 mg of omeprazoleb.i.d. This regimen reduced overnight intragastric pH,4 from 48% to 5 and 6% of the time (6). This regimen may be preferable to increasing the dose of PPI in patients with refractory nocturnal reflux on PPI b.i.d.
