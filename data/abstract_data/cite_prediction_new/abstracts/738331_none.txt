Translation Invariant Extensions of Finite Volume Measures
We investigate the following questions: Given a measure $$\mu _\Lambda $$μΛ on configurations on a subset $$\Lambda $$Λ of a lattice $$\mathbb {L}$$L, where a configuration is an element of $$\Omega ^\Lambda $$ΩΛ for some fixed set $$\Omega $$Ω, does there exist a measure $$\mu $$μ on configurations on all of $$\mathbb {L}$$L, invariant under some specified symmetry group of $$\mathbb {L}$$L, such that $$\mu _\Lambda $$μΛ is its marginal on configurations on $$\Lambda $$Λ? When the answer is yes, what are the properties, e.g., the entropies, of such measures? Our primary focus is the case in which $$\mathbb {L}=\mathbb {Z}^d$$L=Zd and the symmetries are the translations. For the case in which $$\Lambda $$Λ is an interval in $$\mathbb {Z}$$Z we give a simple necessary and sufficient condition, local translation invariance (LTI), for extendibility. For LTI measures we construct extensions having maximal entropy, which we show are Gibbs measures; this construction extends to the case in which $$\mathbb {L}$$L is the Bethe lattice. On $$\mathbb {Z}$$Z we also consider extensions supported on periodic configurations, which are analyzed using de Bruijn graphs and which include the extensions with minimal entropy. When $$\Lambda \subset \mathbb {Z}$$Λ⊂Z is not an interval, or when $$\Lambda \subset \mathbb {Z}^d$$Λ⊂Zd with $$d>1$$d>1, the LTI condition is necessary but not sufficient for extendibility. For $$\mathbb {Z}^d$$Zd with $$d>1$$d>1, extendibility is in some sense undecidable.
