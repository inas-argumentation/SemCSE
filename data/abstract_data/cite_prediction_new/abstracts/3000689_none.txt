Amino acid sequences of the amino and the carboxyl terminal cyanogen bromide peptides of bovine plasma albumin.
The amino acid sequences of two of the five cyanogen bromide peptides of bovine plasma albumin have been determined. These two peptides, containing 88 and 36 amino acid residues, represent, respectively, the amino and the carboxyl terminal portions of the albumin molecule. The single cysteine residue of ablumin was shown to be at position 34 of the molecule.
