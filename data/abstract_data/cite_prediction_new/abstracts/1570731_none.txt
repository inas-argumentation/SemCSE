Comparative analysis between PI and fuzzy controllers applied to a workbench with an induction motor
This paper presents experimental and simulation results for an induction motor operating with PI (proportional and integral) and Fuzzy speed controllers. This results aim to stablish a comparative analysis between both controllers regarding steady state and transient speed behaviour, just like perfomance under disturbances. To collect the results tests were made in a workbench integrated by several elements, including an induction motor. The workbench development is presented as well.
