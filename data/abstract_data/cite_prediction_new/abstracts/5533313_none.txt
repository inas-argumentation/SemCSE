Maxillomandibular-Labial Reconstruction: An Autogenous Transplant as an Alternative to Allogenic Face Transplantation
There are limited reconstructive options that adequately address large-volume maxillomandibular-labial defects, particularly if the labial defect includes over 75 percent of the oral stoma. Allogenic face transplantation has emerged as a possible solution for patients with total oral stoma loss.1,2 The accumulated risk of long-term immunosuppression for these patients has not been quantified and is not currently known with certainty.3 Although there is great value in a face that does not attract negative attention, face transplantation has not yet addressed the issue of function. We present an autogenous reconstruction that provides a competent, functioning oral sphincter, allowing for eating and speaking in public.
