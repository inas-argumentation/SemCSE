NARIDAS - Navigational Risk Detection and Assessment System for the Ship&#146;s Bridge
The navigational risk detection and assessment system (NARIDAS) is a novel approach for technological assistance on the ship's bridge, providing a task-oriented integration of nautical information. Navigational risks are assessed online on eight dimensions, calculated from about 100 input variables of the navigation process. The paper presents an evaluation study of a NARIDAS prototype, that was conducted with nine nautical experts. Results show that the navigational risk values calculated by the system are consistent with the experts' judgements. It is concluded that NARIDAS is a promising approach to a new quality of technological assistance on the ship's bridge
