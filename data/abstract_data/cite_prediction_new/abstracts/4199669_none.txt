Effect of cyclic AMP and theophylline on phagocytotic activity ofTetrahymena pyriformis
Dibutyril cAMP and the cPDE-inhibitor theophylline both enhance the phagocytotic activity ofTetrahymena. Theophylline and cAMP-activating histamine are synergistic. It follows that the cAMP-adenylcyclase system functions in the unicellular animalTetrahymena.
