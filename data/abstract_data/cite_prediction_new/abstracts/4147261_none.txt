Herein, we report an extremely rare and very interesting case of AML located on the thumb of a patient. Although an AML on the finger is very rare, understanding the clinical and molecular features is imperative since the differential diagnosis involving other soft tissue tumours is relatively extensive. AML should therefore be included in the initial differential diagnosis for tumours originating on the finger(s).
