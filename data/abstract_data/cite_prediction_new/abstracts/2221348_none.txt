Power factor improvement of agriculture feeder by using capacitor banks
In rural areas the Transmission and Distribution losses are high due to various reasons like inductive and isolated loads, outdated infrastructure, transformer overloading and low power factor. This study aims to quantify the T & D losses in rural areas through power flow simulation. The study also analyses the possibility of losses reduction in these areas. The village under study is ‘Malshi’ and Power Factor in this region is identified to be particularly low. The reduction in losses possible though fixing of capacitor banks and improving power factor is quantified in this study. Cost analysis for such an intervention is also done in succeeding sections. The second major part of the study involves actual implementation of capacitor banks in the field (under a single transformer) and quantifying the losses reduced. The ultimate aim of this study is analyzing the feasibility of a project where MSEDCL could invest and install capacitors at lowest distribution level possible. Actual implementation helped identifying the various technical and policy limitations involved in implementing such a project. The project is also compared with more centralized form of solutions like single large capacitor installation (rather than at each load) and reactive power supply through decentralized generation.
