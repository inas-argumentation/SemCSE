Radionuclide imaging with SPECT/CT in suspected cerebrospinal fluid leakage.
Accurate anatomic localization of the leakage site can often be troublesome with planar radionuclide cisternography. The findings of a pilot study demonstrating the usefulness of SPECT/CT fusion imaging in cerebrospinal fluid leakage are presented in this report. Eight patients were imaged with SPECT/CT following conventional planar radionuclide cisternography. In all but one of the patients, radiologic imaging (CT and/or MRI) was performed. SPECT/CT could identify the leakage site in greater number of patients. It is concluded that SPECT/CT performs a more privileged role compared with conventional planar imaging in the detection and anatomic localization of cerebrospinal fluid leakage.
