Many educational institutions face a variety of pressures to adopt emerging technologies in their every-day endeavors. If emerging technologies are adopted without adequately considering the systems in whichthey are to be used, the results can be less than desired. This paper describes one College's efforts toeffectively adopt emerging technologies on a general basis. The various systems whichcome into play in this context are discussed, along with a theoretical model examining faculty needs when adopting emerging technologies in education.
