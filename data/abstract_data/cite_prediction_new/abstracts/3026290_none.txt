A13C2H2frequency-stabilized, polarization-maintained erbium fiber ring laser with no frequency modulation
We have constructed a new<sup>13</sup>C<inf>2</inf>H<inf>2</inf>frequency-stabilized fiber laser whose output beam has no frequency modulation. The frequency stability reached as high as 1.8 × 10<sup>-10</sup>for τ of 1 s, and 1.5 × 10<sup>-11</sup>for τ of 100 s.
