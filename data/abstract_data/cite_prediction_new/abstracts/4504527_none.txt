Transport and urban regeneration in London Docklands: A victim of success or a failure to plan?☆
The market-led regeneration policies of the London Docklands Development Corporation, combined with broader economic processes, st imulated a property development boom in London Docklands in the 1980s. The lack of a transport strategy has led to inadequate provision holding up the regeneration process, extra costs from poor integration and coordination between transport modes and the neglect of social needs in favour of the wishes of developers. Future init iatives may alleviate some difficulties but an opportunity has been missed to tackle comp rehens ive ly some long-s tand ing transport problems.
