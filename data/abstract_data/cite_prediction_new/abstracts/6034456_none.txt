A 3-D Eulerian two-phase model is proposed to simulate frazil ice evolution in the diversion channel of hydropower station in this paper. The heat transfer among ice, water, and air are considered in the temperature equation. The study on frazil ice evolution has a great significance to the construction and operation of hydropower station.
