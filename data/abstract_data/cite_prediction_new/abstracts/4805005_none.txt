FORMATION OF SUPERSATURATED ( TI , AL ) N SOLID SOLUTION BY HIGH ENERGY BALL MILLING OF TIN-ALN POWDERS
In the present study (Ti,Al)N nano-size powders with various fraction of AlN were synthesized from the mixtures of TiN and AlN powders by mechanical alloying using a planetary high energy ball milling (HEBM). X-ray diffraction (XRD), energy-filtered transmission electron microscopy spectrum imaging (EFTEM) and energy dispersive x-ray spectroscopy (EDS) confirm the formation of the (Ti,Al)N supersaturated solid solution (up to 50 mole%AlN). The crystalline size of the mechanically alloyed powders after 100 hours of milling is about 13nm.
