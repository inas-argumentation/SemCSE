Factor VIII-related protein in the plasma and platelets of cirrhotic patients.
SummaryThe factor VIII related protein has been studied by crossed Immunoelectrophoresis in both the plasma and platelets of patients suffering from livercirrhosis.The results show that the anodal migration of factor VIII related protein is increased in their plasma but not in their platelets.It is concluded that the qualitative abnormality of the factor VIII related protein which is observed in the plasma of cirrhotic patients may be due to a plasmatic degradation.
