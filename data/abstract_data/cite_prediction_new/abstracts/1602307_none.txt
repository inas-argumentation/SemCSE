The effect of in-situ ozone annealing per cycle on Al2O3 gate dielectric deposited by atomic layer deposition using TMA and H2O for InGaAs MOS capacitor
The effect of in-situ ozone annealing per cycle on Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> gate dielectric deposited by atomic layer deposition for InGaAs MOS capacitors was demonstrated and studied. An in-situ ozone annealing was performed after each atomic layer deposition cycle of Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> deposited using TMA/H<sub>2</sub>O as precursors. The electrical characterization of the deposited Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> layers has shown that after the in-situ ozone annealing, Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> gate dielectric exhibited superior leakage current and density of border trap characteristics, as compared to those grown using TMA/H<sub>2</sub>O or TMA/O<sub>3</sub> as precursors.
