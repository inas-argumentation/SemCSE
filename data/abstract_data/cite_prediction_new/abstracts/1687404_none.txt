THREE-STAGE CMOS OPERATIONAL TRANSCONDUCTANCE AMPLIFIER
The bulk driven operational trans conductance amplifiers (OTAs) is presented which is suitable for operation under sub 1-v single supply, is made up of three gain stages and as an additional features, provides inherent class-AB behaviour with accurate and robust standby current control. The OTA is fabricated in a 180-nm standard CMOS technology and is powered from 0.7 V with a standby current consumption of around 36μA. Overall good large-signal and smallsignal performances are achieved, making the solution extremely competitive in comparison to the state of the art.
