As educators, we teach knowledge to students; yet, students often fail to learn the skills to communicate this knowledge to others. Stapleton (1988) expressed his concern about schools moving away from the business of teaching skills in favor of just teaching subjects. As educators, our challenge is to develop innovative teaching techniques that stimulate intellectual thought while developing oral communication skills within the student.
