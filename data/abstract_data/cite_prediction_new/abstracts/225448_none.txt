Design and fabrication of microstrip equal Wilkinson RF power divider at 650MHz using MWO
This paper presents the theoretical investigation and experimental verification for the design and fabrication of two-way equal Wilkinson RF power divider at 650MHz using Microwave Office tool of AWR-DE software for dividing the power equally amongst the 32 amplifiers used in the SSPA unit used at Deptt. Of Atomic Energy, RRCAT, Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. The fabricated and assembled versions of the power divider are tested both at low power and high power. The circuit simulation and the practically tested results of the divider obtained by low power testing are matched. The high power testing results reveals that it is a low power design with 100W power rating. Therefore, it is a power divider/splitter with lower power handling capability.
