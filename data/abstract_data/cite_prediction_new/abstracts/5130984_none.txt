The relationships between quantitative characteristics of stomata and epidermal cells of leaf epidermis
Negative linear correlations were established inMagnolia soulangeanaSoul. —Bod. andLigustrum vulgare L. leaves between the frequency and the size (length) of the stomata and between the frequency of the stomata and the area of epidermal cells. Most correlations were statistically significant at theP = 0.01 level. Positive linear correlations were established between the area and the thickness of epidermal cells. These correlations were near to theP = 0.05 level of statistical significance. A high variation coefficient “v%” of the total number of stomata per leaf was found in both plant species.AbstractU dvou dřevin, Magnolia soulangeana SOUL.-BOD. a Ligustrum vulgäre L., byly zjištěny negativní lineární korelace mezi hustotou a délkou prßduchů a mezi hustotou prßduchß a plochou epidermálních buněk, převážně statisticky vysoce průkazné, a positivní lineární korelaoe mezi plochou a tlouštkou epidermélních buněk, pohybující se na pokraji statistické prßkaznosti. U obou druhů byl zjišětén vysoký variační koeficient „v %“ celkového počtu průduchß na listu.
