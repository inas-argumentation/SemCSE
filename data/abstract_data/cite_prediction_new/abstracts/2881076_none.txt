PRODUCTION OF PHENOLIC RESIN / LAYERED SILICATE NANOCOMPOSITES
Polymer/layered silicate nanocomposites having phenolic matrix were produced. The method of production was melt-intercalation. This method consists of mixing the modified montmorillonite clay with phenol formaldehyde resin mechanically, and curing the polymer to form a nanocomposite material, via intercalation of polymer in between the clay galleries and the exfoliation of these layers homogenously throughout the matrix. The effect of several parameters such as different resin structure, cure treatments, clay type, clay modifier and amount are examined by a full characterization of the produced specimens using x-ray diffraction, mechanical tests and electron microscopy. Further trials are still being carried out to examine the effects of any change in processing parameters.
