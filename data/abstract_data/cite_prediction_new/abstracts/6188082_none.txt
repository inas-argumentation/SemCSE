A novel approach to time-varying formation control
This paper addresses the dynamic formation control problem for directed multi-agent networks, in which the desired spatial shape is time-varying instead of fixed one as usually assumed in the literature. The desired geometric shape is usually specified by agents' absolute positions, relative positions, inter-agent bearings, and inter-agent distances in the existing works, which however cannot intrinsically describe a spatial shape since one geometric shape will not be deformed by rotations, translations, and scalings. In this regard, a new description on formation shapes is subsequently proposed building on several formation parameters, and then the dynamic formation control problem is handled with the aid of a new matrix-valued Laplacian. It is proved that any desired dynamic geometric shape can be reached using this approach and in the meantime, the entire network can accurately follow a desired trajectory asymptotically. Finally, one numerical example is provided to support the theoretical results.
