Fuel Cell Systems for Electrical Vehicles : Solutions , Modelling and Test benches design
This paper presents the modelling of fuel cell (FC) power generator oriented toward the integration into an electrical vehicle simulation. First a brief description of the FC principles as well as the benefits of the Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cell (PEMFC) technology for automotive applications are given. Next, the model of the whole fuel cell system that has been implemented in the MATLAB/Simulink environment is described. Finally, after a brief description of a test bench dedicated to PEMFC, experimental results are compared with those obtained using the proposed simulation model.
