Multivariate image analysis methods applied to XPS imaging data sets
Recent improvements in imaging photoelectron spectroscopy enhance lateral and vertical characterization of heterogeneous samples at the cost of increasing complexity in the XPS data sets acquired. These imaging data sets require more sophisticated analysis methods than visual inspection if the data are to be interpreted effectively. Multivariate analysis (MVA) methods are increasingly utilized in surface spectroscopies to aid the analyst in interpreting the vast amount of information resulting from these multidimensional data set acquisitions.
