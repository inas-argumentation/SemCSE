Ferroelectric Properties of Chemically Synthesized Perovskite BiFeO3-PbTiO3 Thin Films
In this study, we investigated the solid solution of BiFeO<sub>3</sub>-PbTiO<sub>3</sub> (BF-PT) system to improve the structural stability of perovskite phase and to enhance of several properties by using a morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) composition. Perovskite BF-PT single phase thin films were successfully fabricated though the formation of solid solution with PbTiO<sub>3</sub>. Although the electrical resistivity of BF-PT thin films was not sufficiently high at around room temperature, the large ferroelectricity was observed at low temperatures. The 0.7BiFeO<sub>3</sub>-0.3PbTiO<sub>3</sub> thin films exhibited the P<sub>r</sub> value of 60 muC/cm<sup>2</sup> at -190degC.
