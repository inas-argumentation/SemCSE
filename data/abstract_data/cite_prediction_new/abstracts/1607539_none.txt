Instability of the electromagnetic surface waves supported by the bianisotropic column in dielectric medium
A study is made of the linear stage of the parametric instability of electromagnetic waves guided by bianisotropic composite cylinder surrounded by uniform dielectric space. A three wave interaction between the external field and the guided surface waves is investigated. The expressions of instability increment of the surface waves propagating in opposite directions are obtained. For the some practically interesting cases numerical analyses have been performed.
