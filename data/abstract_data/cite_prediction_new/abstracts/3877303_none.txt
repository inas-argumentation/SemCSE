28 NEW THIN FILM STRUCTURES OBSERVED IN THE Sr-BiTa-0 SYSTEM
Investigation of the Sr-Bi-Ta-0 system uncovered several phases which were not yet identified within the powder diffraction database. Attempts to isolate these phases as powders failed. However, they were successfUlly isolated via thin film preparation. Samples were prepared as thin films by a chemical preparation route using Sr-acetate, Bi-acetate, and Ta(V)-ethoxide. Thin film characterization was performed using GIXRD. The observed phases included a Sr-BiTa-0 fluorite structure, a SrTa03 cubic perovskite phase, and a series of Sr-Bi-0 fluorites.
