Single-equation, multiple-regression methodology: Is it an appropriate methodology for the estimation of the structure-performance relationship in banking?
Tests of the structure-performance paradigm of the industrial organization literature have been carried out almost exclusively using a single-equation, multiple-regression methodology. The purpose of this paper is to suggest that where the firms being considered are multiple product in nature and may pursue objectives in addition to maximizing the value of the firm, such a methodology may be inappropriate. The results presented in this paper suggest that the absence of a consistently strong, positive, and statistically significant relationship between market concentration and bank profitability may be traced in part to such an inappropriate methodology.
