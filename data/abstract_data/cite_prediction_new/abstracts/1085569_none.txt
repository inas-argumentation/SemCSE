A lead synthesizer for the Frank system to simulate the standard 12-lead electrocardiogram.
Summary The X, Y, Z signals obtained from the Frank lead system may be used to simulate a standard 12-lead ECG. An outline of a method for designing a lead simulator based on the Frank image surface data has been presented in a manner intended to provide some elucidation of the image surface concept. In contrast to various lead resolvers in the literature, the two simulators described in this communication provide near optimal simulation of the standard 12-lead ECG from tape recorded orthogonal lead signals.
