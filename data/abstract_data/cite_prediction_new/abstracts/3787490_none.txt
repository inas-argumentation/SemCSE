Scintillation camera nonuniformity: effects on cold lesion detectability.
Based on these data it is concluded that false negative and true negative readings decrease with increasing nonuniformity causing a severe decrease in physicians' ability to distinguish normals. False positive readings increase with increasing nonuniformity showing physicians' reading accuracy to be seriously affected by field nonuniformity. On this basis, the maintenance of scintillation camera performance is critical to the quality of clinical nuclear medicine practice. An expansion on this study including the parameters of spatial distortion and resolution, is currently underway at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory under auspices of the Food and Drug Administration's Bureau of Radiological Health.
