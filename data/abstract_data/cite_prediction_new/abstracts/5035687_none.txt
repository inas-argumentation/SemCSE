Ribozyme‐mediated inhibition of PKCα sensitizes androgen‐independent human prostate cancer cells to cisplatin‐induced apoptosis
Therapeutic strategies to target the molecular basis of hormone and drug resistance of prostate cancer cells are needed. Since protein kinase Cα (PKCα) is thought to have a role in the development of the androgen‐independent phenotype of prostate cancer cells and in apoptosis suppression, the objective of the present study was to test whether specific inhibition of PKCα by a hammerhead ribozyme was able to sensitize androgen‐independent prostate cancer cells the effects of apoptosis‐inducing anticancer drugs.
