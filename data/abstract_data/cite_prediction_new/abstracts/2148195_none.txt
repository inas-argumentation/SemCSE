Travellers from the tropics-a practical approach to common problems.
Increased immigration and travel abroad by Canadians have increased the need for family physicians to know more about tropical and parasitic diseases. This paper provides the practitioner with a practical approach to the following problems seen in travellers from the tropics: eosinophilia, fever, diarrhea, and the passage of worms in the stool.In addition, a discussion of the principles governing the treatment of parasitic infections is included.
