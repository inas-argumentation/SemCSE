Tuning Range Optimization of a Planar Inverted F Antenna for the LTE Low Frequency Bands
This paper presents a Planar Inverted F Antenna (PIFA) tuned with a fixed capacitor to the low frequency bands supported by the Long Term Evolution (LTE) technology. The tuning range is investigated and optimized with respect to the bandwidth and the efficiency of the resulting antenna. Simulations and mock-ups are presented.
