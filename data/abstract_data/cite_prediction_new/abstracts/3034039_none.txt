Multi-disciplinary Investigation of Energy Use in New Zealand Households
The Household Energy End Use Project was established in late 1995 as a long-term research activity to create and make available a scientifically and technically rigorous, up-to-date public knowledge base of energy use and end-uses, energy services provision and key occupant, building and appliance determinants. This paper describes the research approach, the data and information collection and analysis undertaken to date, application of data to other research activities and the expected contribution of this investigation to the development of an efficient energy market in the household sector.
