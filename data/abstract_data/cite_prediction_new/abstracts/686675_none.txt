A technique for simultaneous radiography and electromyography.
Radiographic observations have been combined with recordings of other parameters in studies of a variety of body systems. Simultaneous cineradiographic and electromyographic recordings have been made of movements of the hyoid apparatus during chewing (Crompton et al, 1975) and combined radiographic and intraluminal pressure studies have been made of oesophageal motility (Vantrappen et al, 1958; Cohen & Wolf, 1968), gastro-intestinal motility (Smith et al, 1957; Ritchie et al, 1962; Oigaard & Dorph, 1974) and during voiding cystourethrography (Blaivas, 1979). A difficulty in making other recordings during radiographic examinations lies in establishing precise relationships between X-ray images and events recorded on other equipment, such as chart recorders or cathode-ray oscilloscopes.
