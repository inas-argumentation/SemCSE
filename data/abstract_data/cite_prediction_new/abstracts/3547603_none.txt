Correlations between green luminescence efficiency, intrinsic defects and co-doping Cl and S in ZnO phosphors
First-principles pseudo-potential approach on complete zinc oxide (ZnO), vacant O (ZnO∶V<inf>o</inf>), vacant Zn (ZnO∶V<inf>Zn</inf>), interstitial Zn (ZnO∶Zn<inf>i</inf>), chloride (Cl) and sulfur (S) co-doped defective ZnO (ZnO∶ClSV<inf>Zn</inf>, ZnO∶ClSZn<inf>i</inf>) reveal that V<inf>o</inf> is a shallow donor dopant which has nothing to do with green emission. V<inf>Zn</inf> and Zn<inf>i</inf> both induce green emission recombination levels in the gap. When Cl and S co-dope in defective ZnO, they will enhance the green emission luminescence intensity, because they can promote the formations of V<inf>Zn</inf> and Zn<inf>i</inf> defects.
