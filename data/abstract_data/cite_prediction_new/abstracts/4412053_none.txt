AIDIA - Adaptive Interface for Display InterAction
This paper presents a vision-based system for interaction with a display via hand pointing. An attention mechanism based on face and hand detection allows users in the camera’s field of view to take control of th e interface. Face recognition is used for identification and customisation. T he system allows the user to control the screen pointer by tracking their fist. On-screen items can be selected using one of four activation mechanisms. Current sample applications include browsing image and video collections as well as viewing a gallery of 3D objects. In experiments we demonstrate the performance of the vision components in challenging conditions and compare it to that of other systems.
