Cohomology and intersection cohomology of algebraic varieties
Let X be an equidimensional variety, and X ′ a compactification of X . We show that the middle weight part of the cohomology with compact supports of X is a subspace of the intersection cohomology of X , or equivalently, the middle weight part of the (so-called) Borel-Moore homology of X is a quotient of the intersection cohomology of X . This is a refinement of a theorem of G. Barthel, J.-P. Brasselet, K.-H. Fieseler, O. Gabber and L. Kaup that any algebraic cycle classes can be lifted to intersection cohomology. The proof uses Gabber’s purity theorem for intersection complexes.
