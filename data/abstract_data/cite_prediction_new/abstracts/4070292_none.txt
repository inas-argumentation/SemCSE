Reaction of Cu++ with some polyamines of biochemical significance, a 13C-NMR study.
The initial complex formation of low concentrations of Cu++ and some aliphatic diamines of biochemical significance starting from C2 to C9 was examined with high resolution 13C-NMR spectroscopy. It is demonstrated that all carbon atoms were affected by the copper chelates. The C-α and the C-β carbons were preferentially involved.
