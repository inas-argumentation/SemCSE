Effect on reproductive performance of turkey breeders of nutrient restriction during the development period.
In two experiments with Large White turkey breeder hens, weight gains, in a “holding” period before the egg laying session, were restricted by limited-feeding or by diluted diets. Weights at sexual maturity were reduced 750 g. in one experiment and 1400 g. in the second experiment. In the first experiment, production rate was increased and mortality was decreased significantly by limited feeding. These criteria were not significantly affected in Experiment 2, and other reproductive criteria were not significantly affected by growing-period diets in either experiment. Protein concentration of laying diets (12, 14, 16%) did not cause significant differences in any reproductive criterion.
