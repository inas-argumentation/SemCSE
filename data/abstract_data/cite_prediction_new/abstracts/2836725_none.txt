Synthesis and analgesic activity of some 2-azabicycloheptane derivatives
The subject of this investigation was inspired by comparative analysis of the following objective data. On the one hand, the results of experimental and clinical investigations indicate the good prospects of a search for new analgesic drugs (free of addiction effects) among agonists and antagonists of GABA [1 3], On the other hand, it is known that GABA-receptor effector activity is inherent in compounds belonging to various structural types: in particular, pronounced GABA-lytic properties were reported for halogencontaining compounds with a bicyclic structure [4]. Unfortunately, many of these GABA antagonists are characterized by comparatively high (lethal) toxicity. An example is 5,6-dichloro-2,2-dicyano-3,3-bis(trifluoromethyl)bicyclo[2.2.1 ]he ptane having LD50 -0.2 mg/kg (rats, p.o.) [5]. Experiments show that this compound produces effective blocking of chloride-ion conductivity in membranes of GABA-dependent CNS neurons [ 6 10]. Apparently, the search for new analgesic agents among the bicyclic GABA antimetabolites is expedient only within a group of low-toxicity compounds. There are data suggesting that analgesic agents may be found among the halogen-containing heterobicyclanes [11].
