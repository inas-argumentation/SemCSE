The American Geriatrics Society/National Institute on Aging Bedside‐to‐Bench Conference: Research Agenda on Delirium in Older Adults
The American Geriatrics Society, with support from the National Institute on Aging and the John A. Hartford Foundation, held its seventh Bedside-to-Bench research conference, entitled ‘‘Delirium in Older Adults: Finding Order in the Disorder’’ on February 9–11, 2014, to provide participants with opportunities to learn about cuttingedge research developments, draft recommendations for future research involving translational efforts, and opportunities to network with colleagues and leaders in the field. This meeting was the first of three conferences that will address delirium, sleep disorders, and voiding difficulties and urinary incontinence, emphasizing, whenever possible, the relationships and potentially shared clinical and pathophysiological features between these common geriatric syndromes (Figure 1).
