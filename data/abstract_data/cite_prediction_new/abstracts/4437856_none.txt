Effect of mechanical treatment on the particle size distribution and rheological properties of tomato concentrate
In this study effect of mechanical treatment on the particle size distribution and rheological properties of tomato concentrate has been investigated. Tomato paste samples after dilution to 17% dry matter content were treated in blender for the time of 1, 3, 6, 10 and 15 minutes. The Boswick consistency, particle size distribution, steady-shear viscosity and dynamic rheological measurements were performed. As expected, the mechanical treatment reduced the mean particle diameter, but the shape of distribution curve did not change substantially, it was only shifted towards small particles. The disruption in a blender improved tomato concentrate consistency and also an increase of apparent viscosity, values of G’ and G” moduli and the Bostwick value were observed.
