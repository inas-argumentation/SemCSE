Genome Sequence of Lactococcus lactis subsp . lactis bv . diacetylactis LD 61 FALENTIN
Lactococcus lactis is widely used in the dairy industry. We report the draft genome sequence of L. lactis subsp. lactis bv. diacetylactis LD61, an industrial and extensively studied strain.
