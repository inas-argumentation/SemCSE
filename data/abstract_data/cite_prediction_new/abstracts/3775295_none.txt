Segmentation of merged characters by neural networks and shortest-path
One major problem with neural network-based ap preach to printed character recognition is the segmentation of merged characters. This paper proposes a hybrid method which combines a neural network-based de ferred segmentation scheme with conventional immediate segmentation techniques. In the deferred segmentation, a neural network is employed to distinguish single characters from composites. To find a proper vertical cut that separates a composite, a shortest-path alg~ rithm seeking minimal-penalty curved cuts is used. Integrating those components with a multiresolution neural network OCR and an efficient spelling checker, the resulting system significantly improves its ability to read omnifont document text.
