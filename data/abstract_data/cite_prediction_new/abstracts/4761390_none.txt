Accretion flows in the magnetospheres of Vela X-1, AO535 + 26 and Her X-1
We suggest a model for the complex pulse shapes of the long period, pulsating X-ray sources, Vela X-1 and AO535+26. We discuss the flow of accreting matter on to these slow rotators and contrast it with that expected in systems such as Her X-1 which contain a rapidly rotating neutron star.
