Application of I DDQ test in failure analysis of microcontroller devices
This paper presents a real case study on the testing of 8-bit mixed-signal CMOS micro-controller devices by applying the IDDQ testing methodology. The aim of the study is to evaluate the feasibility of using the IDDQ test to enhance the overall fault coverage.
