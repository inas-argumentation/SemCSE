Rimonabant—a new hope in the treatment of obesity?
DESIGN AND INTERVENTION This trial is an update from the Rimonabant in Obesity–Europe (RIO–Europe) study group. Between September 2001 and November 2003, patients were recruited into this 12-month multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled phase III trial of rimonabant. Inclusion criteria included age 18–70 years, BMI 27–40 kg/m2, total-cholesterol:HDL-cholesterol ratio >4.5, fasting plasma tri glyceride levels 1.7–7.9 mmol/l, and <5 kg variation in weight within the 3 months before enrollment. Patients previously given drugs for obesity or dyslipidemia, or those diagnosed with diabetes mellitus or cardiovascular disease were excluded. After a 4-week run-in period with placebo, patients were stratified and randomly assigned 5 mg rimonabant, 20 mg rimonabant, or placebo.
