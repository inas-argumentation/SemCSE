- 1-Rainfall Amount and Intensity measured by the Thies Laser Precipitation Monitor
The Laser Precipitation Monitor (LPM) manufactured by Thies is a Present Weather sensor based on an optical disdrometer. Thus rainfall amounts can be derived from the disdrometric information. The factory calibration process for the volume measurement is described and the resulting measurement uncertainty is given. In this paper rainfall amount measured by the LPM is compared with measurements of reference rainfall gauges such as a pit gauge. Rainfall intensities are evaluated in comparison with postprocessed high resolution data of a Pluvio (OTT) weighing rain gauge.
