Addendum to Technical Proposal A Facility to Search for Hidden Particles ( SHiP ) at the CERN SPS The SHiP Collaboration
With the Technical Proposal (TP) submitted to the SPSC committee in April 2015 [1], the SHiP collaboration declared its interest in proceeding towards a comprehensive design study phase with the aim of preparing for the Technical Design Reports pending an approval by the CERN committees. Following the recommendation by the SPSC, it has been decided to complement the TP with this addendum that provides an update of the key aspects for the review of the SHiP project.
