Protocol merging: a VHDL-based method for clock cycle minimizing and protocol preserving scheduling of IO-operations
This paper presents a new algorithm for clock cycle minimizing and protocol preserving scheduling of input and output operations. The algorithm relies on the possibility to overlap protocols at di erent ports in time without changing the behavior. It merges the operations required for complete protocols and allows thus for a compact schedule of a set of correlated protocols. Both, input and scheduled output for subsequent High-Leveland RT-Synthesis use VHDL for description.
