Effect of the sintering temperature on the properties of 0.96(K0.5Na0.5)(Nb0.96Sb0.04)-0.04LiTaO3 ceramics
Lead-free piezoelectric ceramics 0.96(K<inf>0.5</inf>Na<inf>0.5</inf>)(Nb<inf>0.96</inf>Sb<inf>0.04</inf>)-0.04LiTaO<inf>3</inf> (96KNNS-4LT) have been prepared by using the conventional technique. The effect of the sintering temperature on the piezoelectric properties of 96KNNS-4LT ceramics was studied. The X-ray diffraction (XRD) and scanning electron microscope (SEM) show that all the ceramics possess a pure perovskite phase and dense microstructure. The ceramics sintered at 1090 °C show better piezoelectric properties: d<inf>33</inf>=315.5 pC/N, k<inf>p</inf>=0.455, and T<inf>C</inf>=348 °C, the optimized sintering temperature range is very narrow for KNN-based ceramics, and the optimized sintering temperature range in which the d<inf>33</inf> and k<inf>p</inf> are in the range of 0.9 times the maximum one is less than 20 °C. From the results obtained it was pointed out that the sintering temperature is a very critical factor for the K<inf>0.5</inf>Na<inf>0.5</inf>NbO<inf>3</inf>-based lead-free piezoelectric ceramics.
