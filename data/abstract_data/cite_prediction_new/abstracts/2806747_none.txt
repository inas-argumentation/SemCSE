An extension of the spectral Tau method for numerical solution of multi-order fractional differential equations with convergence analysis
The main purpose of this paper is to provide an efficient numerical approach for the fractional differential equations (FDEs) based on a spectral Tau method. An extension of the operational approach of the Tau method with the orthogonal polynomial bases is proposed to convert FDEs to its matrix-vector multiplication representation. The fractional derivatives are described in the Caputo sense. The spectral rate of convergence for the proposed method is established in the L^2 norm. We tested our procedure on several examples and observed that the obtained numerical results confirm the theoretical prediction of the exponential rate of convergence.
