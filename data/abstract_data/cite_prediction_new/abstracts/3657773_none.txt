Dispersive estimates for the three-dimensional Schrödinge equation with rough potentials
<abstract abstract-type="TeX"><p>The three-dimensional Schrödinger propogator <i>e<sup>itH</sup>, H = -Δ + V</i>, is a bounded map from <i>L</i><sup>1</sup> to <i>L</i><sup>∞</sup> with norm controlled by |<i>t</i>|<sup>-3/2</sup> provided the potential satisfies two conditions: An integrability condition limiting the singularities and decay of <i>V</i>, and a zero-energy spectral condition on <i>H</i>. This is shown by expressing the spectral measure of <i>H</i> in terms of its resolvents and proving a family of <i>L<sup>p</sup></i> mapping estimates for the resolvents. Previous results in this direction had required <i>V</i> to satisfy explicit pointwise bounds.
