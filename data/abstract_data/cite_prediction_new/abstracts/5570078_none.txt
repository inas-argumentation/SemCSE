Hidden starbursts and active galactic nuclei at 0< z <4 from the Herschel-VVDS-CFHTLS-D1 field: Inferences on coevolution and feedback
We investigate of the properties of ~2000 Herschel/SPIRE far-infrared-selected galaxies from 0 0.5), the highest LTIR galaxies in a given redshift bin are unobserved by SPIRE at subsequently lower redshifts, a trend linked to downsizing. In conjunction with other results, this evidence is used to argue for prevalent AGN-driven quenching in starburst galaxies across cosmic time.
