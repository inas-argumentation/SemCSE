Calibration of indicator dilution curves in man by the dynamic method.
The original method for the measurement of cardiac output by the indicator dilution technique re- quired the timed collection of multiple samples of arterial blood and the subsequent estimation of the concentration of indicator in each sample et al., 1928). The introduction of continuously recorded indicator dilution curves Heim-becker, and Bing, 1950) greatly simplified the method but introduced problems of calibration.
