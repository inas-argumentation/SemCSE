On the impact of the "business" of pain medicine on patient care: an introduction.
Continuing the special ethics series in Pain Medicine on the transformation of the profession of pain medicine into the business of pain medicine, this article “The Impact of the ‘Business' of Pain Medicine on Patient Care,” adds a significant chapter by focusing on the clinical and ethical implications of the impact of increasing economic forces of pain medicine on patient care. Despite rising costs of chronic pain care in the United States today as well as an unprecedented availability of increasingly expensive and invasive diagnostic tests and treatment choices, it is unclear if pain care has improved. Dr. Mary Lou Taylor's contribution to this special ethics series offers a sobering account of our current crisis of pain care—the extent to which the profession of pain is now largely mediated by economic forces …
