Deadly Engagement: FcγRIIa-Antibody Interaction is Key in Sepsis.
Staphylococcal sepsis presents a significant clinical challenge due to the rapid progression of disease as well as associated hemostatic dysregulation. Systemic infection with Staphylococcus aureus causes thrombocytopenia, which leads to bleeding into the tissues and thrombotic complications. This causes the hallmark petechiae and purpura and associated disseminated intravascular coagulation. Aggressive treatment with antibiotics is required; however, a drop in blood pressure and organ damage can lead to death or significant morbidity before antibiotics can clear bacteria.
