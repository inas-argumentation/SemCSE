Standard conjectures for the arithmetic Grassmannian G(2,N) and Racah polynomials
We prove the arithmetic Hodge index and hard Lefschetz conjectures for the Grassmannian G = G(2,N) parametrizing lines in projective space, for the natural arithmetic Lefschetz operator defined via the Plucker embedding of G in projective space. The analysis of the Hodge index inequality involves estimates on values of certain Racah polynomials.
