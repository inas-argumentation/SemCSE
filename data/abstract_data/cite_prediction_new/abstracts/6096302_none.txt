Effect of Sun‐Dried Raisins (Vitis vinifera L.) on the In Vitro Composition of Gut Microbiota
Our aim was to investigate the effect of sun‐dried raisins on the colonic microbiota using an in vitro batch culture seeded with representative human gut bacteria. A model of gastric and small intestinal digestion was used to simulate the upper gastrointestinal digestion. Samples collected during a time course at 0, 4, 8 and 24h were analyzed for microbial composition and organic acid concentrations by 16S V4 rDNA analysis and high‐performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), respectively.
