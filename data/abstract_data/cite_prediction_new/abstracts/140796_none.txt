Growth and livelihoods in conflict-affected situations: what do we know? SLRC Briefing Paper No. 3
Key messages The evidence base on growth and livelihoods in conflict-affected ■ situations is comprised of four distinct ‘categories’ of evidence We know strikingly little about the impacts of livelihood and ■ economic interventions in conflict-affected environments, and programming choices often appear to be driven by assumptions of effectiveness and unsubstantiated narratives of success Conflict-affected economies demonstrate surprisingly strong ■ GDP growth rates, but methodological weaknesses suggest the data need to be treated with great caution
