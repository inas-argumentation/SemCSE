Over the same period, Korea and Taiwan have also increased their technological and scientific activities to the point where in the listings of US patents granted to foreign nationals, Korea appeared 7 th in 1995 (with 1161 patents), and Taiwan ranked 6 th (with 1620 patents). This should be compared with their positions in 1985, when Korea was 24 th with just 41 patents and Taiwan 16 th with 174 patents (USPTO 1996). In scientific activities, represented by the SCI (Science Citation Index) index of scientific papers, Korea ranked 23 in 1995 (with 5277 papers) compared with its position of 40 th (with 1178 papers) in 1987. In the same year Taiwan appeared 19 th (with 6550 papers) compared to 37 th (with 1510 papers) in 1987 (NSCa).
