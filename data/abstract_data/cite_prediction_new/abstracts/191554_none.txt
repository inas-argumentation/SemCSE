method of treating insects comprising the separation of the cuticles of the soft part of the insects and the separation of the soft part in three fractions
An insect processing method comprising separating the soft cuticle of insects, and then separating the soft part insects into an oil fraction, a solids fraction and an aqueous fraction. The invention further relates to powders, in particular a powder obtainable by the insect processing method according to the invention and use of these powders in the diet.
