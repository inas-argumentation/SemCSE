A Banach-Stone theorem for Riesz isomorphisms of Banach lattices
Let X and Y be compact Hausdorff spaces, and E, F be Banach lattices. Let C(X, E) denote the Banach lattice of all continuous E-valued functions on X equipped with the pointwise ordering and the sup norm. We prove that if there exists a Riesz isomorphism$: C(X, E) -» C(Y, F) such that Of is non-vanishing on Y if and only if f is non-vanishing on X, then X is homeomorphic to Y, and E is Riesz isomorphic to F. In this case,O can be written as a weighted composition operator:Of(y) = II(y)(f(φ(y))), where φ is a homeomorphism from Y onto X, and π(y) is a Riesz isomorphism from E onto F for every y in Y. This generalizes some known results obtained recently.
