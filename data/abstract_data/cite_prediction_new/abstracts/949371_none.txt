Complex hand operation recognition method using salient features of EEG
The purpose of the research is to discriminate different human hand operations which is a helpful tool for real time control of robotic hand. In this paper, the salient features (amplitude, mean, median, maximum frequency, delta value) of EEG signal are extracted utilizing spectral analysis methods such as Statistical, Fourier and Wavelet analysis. And on the basis of SVM algorithm, the overall recognition rate found for statistical and DFT analysis is 85.1% and 77% respectively while the figure is 98.6% for DWT analysis. The results show that our proposed method provides highly improved performance than the traditional ones.
