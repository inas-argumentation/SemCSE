A computerised management information system to monitor annual milk production
A computerised system for targeting expected monthly milk production at farm level is described. The system is run by the Economics Department of the Milk Marketing Board for Northern Ireland and utilises a database of records from their Dairy Information Service. These records are combined with a set of forecast data on calving, culling and replacement and a model for the lactation curve of the dairy cow to produce, on a cumulative basis, a comparison of target and actual milk production throughout the financial year. The system enables farmers to make a quantitative assessment of the likelihood of their herd being above or below quota at the end of any current financial year, thus allowing decisions on culling and feeding policy to be made at key points in the year.
