Segmental eruptive neurofibromatosis infliximab‐induced
therapy psoriasis has significantly improved without worsening of MS. Neurological adverse effects of TNFa inhibitors are well described and administration of such biologics in patients with demyelinating diseases is contraindicated. By contrast, ustekinumab was promising novel treatment for relapsing-remitting MS that, however, failed to prove its efficacy in controlled trial, but did not show any exacerbation of MS. This is the reason we decided to restart the therapy of ustekinumab. Two reports of four cases in total have showed significant improvement of psoriasis or psoriatic arthritis after high-dose intravenous immunoglobulin treatment. There are no reports in literature of worsening of psoriasis after IVIG. Presented patient is an exemplary case of very complex management of severe psoriasis developing MS on biologic therapy and probably the first reported case of psoriasis worsened by IVIG therapy.
