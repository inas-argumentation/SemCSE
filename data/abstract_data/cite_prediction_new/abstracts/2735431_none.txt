The effect of coils axial misalignments on yhe efficiency of an inductive powering unit with class E power amplifier
Advancement in inductive powering technology appears to be a promising direction for development of implantable medical devices. A class E power amplifier is often used together with an inductive link to achieve high efficiency of inductive powering unit (IPU). The class E amplifier is sensitive to the changes in its loading network. Therefore, coils misalignment in the link leads to the decreased system efficiency. This paper presents simulation results of the IPU with the class E amplifier for different coil pairs. The results of the simulation show a decrease in efficiency for both cases: when the coils get closer and farther away. The coils with lower inductance (3.4 μH) lead to smaller losses than the coils with higher inductance (9–12 μH) in the case of axial distance reduction. In the case of axial distance increase the coils with lower inductance lead to higher losses than the coils with higher inductance.
