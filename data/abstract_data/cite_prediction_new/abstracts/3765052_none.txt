Targets for phosphate control in chronic kidney disease
Patients on dialysis have an approximate 10–20-fold increased risk of cardiovascular mortality compared with the general population when adjusted for age, sex and presence of diabetes. Elevated levels of phosphate and the calcium ¥ phosphate product have been identified as major risk factors for cardiovascular mortality, and are quantitatively linked to the burden of coronary artery calcification in young patients undergoing dialysis. Effective phosphate management is often difficult, but the need to avoid vascular calcification and its complications now coincides with new insights into the mechanism of this process and options for management.
