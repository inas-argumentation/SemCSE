For doing so, we use the ordered weighted averaging – weighted average (OWAWA) operator. It is an aggregation operator that unifies the weighted average and the OWA in the same formulation. As a result, we form the belief structure – OWAWA (BS-OWAWA) aggregation. We study some of its main properties and particular cases. We also present an application of the new approach in a decision making problem concerning political management.
