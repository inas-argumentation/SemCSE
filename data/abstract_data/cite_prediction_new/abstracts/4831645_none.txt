Applications of Calculus of variations to Fluid mechanics
Fluid mechanics involves analysing and modelling fluid behaviours under different physical conditions, and variational principle is an essential part to solving such problems. The motion of fluids can be modelled using Lagrangian or Eulerian description, based on the need to focus on individual particles or fixed points respectively. In this report, Lagrangian variational principle is used to derive equations of motion for ideal fluids, which is later extended to a more general result, yielding the Navier-Stokes equation. Lastly, optimisation of heat transfer is addressed to demonstrate application of calculus of variations in optimal control problems.
