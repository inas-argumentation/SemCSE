ONTHE PERFORMANCE OFHEURISTICS FOR BROADCAST SCHEDULING
In the Broadcast Scheduling Problem (BSP), a finite set of stations are to be scheduled in a time division multiple access (TDMA) frame. In a TDMA frame, time is divided into equal length transmission slots. Unconstrained message transmission can result in a collision of messages, rendering them useless. Therefore, the objective of the BSP is to provide a collision free broadcast schedule which minimizes the total frame length and maximizes the slot utilization within the frame.
