An Improved Community Detection Algorithm Based on the Edge-Graph of Network under the Spark Environment
This paper designs a community detection algorithm based on edge-graph of network and it can be called PENG, in which the network graph is transformed into edge-graph and the similarity of nodes in edge-graph substitutes for the edge betweenness of GN algorithm. It solves the problems that GN algorithm can not detect the overlapping communities and has high time complexity. The experiment demonstrates that the PENG algorithm, comparing with the traditional community detection algorithms, can effectively detect the structures of overlapping communities when the structures of communities are unidentified and the scale of social network is large.
