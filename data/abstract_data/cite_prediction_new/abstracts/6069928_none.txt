Neuropathic pruritus as the p]resenting symptom of lupus transverse myelitis
Pruritus is an unpleasant sensation that causes an urge to scratch [1]. It can be caused by dermatological, systemic, neurological or psychiatric diseases [2]. However, the last two etiologies are rare. It can be classified as neurogenic, when induced by mediators that stimulate neurons involved in itching sensations, or neuropathic, when generated by damaged neurons [1]. The latter is thought to be responsible for 8% of all cases of chronic itching [3], and it is reported in up to 15% of CNS disorders [4], including spinal cord lesions of various etiologies. We report a case of systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) transverse myelitis presenting as neuropathic pruritus.
