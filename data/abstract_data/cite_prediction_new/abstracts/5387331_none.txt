New Zealand's Past and Tomorrow's Schools: Reasons, reforms and results
Educational reform is a phenomenon that appears to be sweeping the world. In 1987 New Zealand took initial steps to reform its educational system. This article is a study of the evolution of New Zealand's educational system including its recent reform, the driving forces behind such radical change, and some of the results and issues that have arisen from the changes. Because this educational reform did not happen in isolation but as part of the government's overall restructuring process, the context for the study involves the examination of the country's foundations with respect to the prevailing values and the evolution of the country's social and economic structure.
