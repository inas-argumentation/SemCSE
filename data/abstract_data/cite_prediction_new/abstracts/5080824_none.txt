Alterations of L-cell cultures infected by influenza viruses
Earlier it was shown that the infection of L-cell cultures by influenza viruses did not induce the reproduction of infectious viral particles (1, 2). Only very high concentrations of the human influenza A or B viruses produced a cytopathic effect (CPE), but without the fromation of infectious virions (3, 4). Fowl plague virus (FPV) induced in L-cells an abortive cycle of infection with the formation of noninfectious hemagglutinins and S-antigen, but infectious virus was not synthesized (5). We conducted investigations of L-cell cultures infected with avian and human influenza viruses and found in such cultures some changes which persisted on subcultures.
