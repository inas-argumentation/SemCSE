The Synthesis of Nano-Crystalline Metal Oxides by Solution Method
The performance of materials in many of their uses in industries and scientific researches is directly dependent on their crystal structure, which correlates to the chemical and physical properties of the materials. And also, as the size of crystal particles decrease to nanometer scale, nanocrystals exhibit some unexpected properties that are evidently different in physics and chemistry from their bulk crystals and their cluster compounds as well.
