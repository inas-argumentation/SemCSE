A preliminary study of the influence of experiential backgrounds of respondents to a reference survey for verification of FSV
The Feature Selective Validation method was originally developed as a heuristic technique to compare data sets, broadly in the manner of a group of experts. However, little research has been undertaken on how less experienced engineers compare data and whether they are more-or-less similar to those experienced engineers. This paper considers a group of engineers and separates them into three groups based on their experience levels. It indicates that there is a difference in the groups' responses. While the numbers of individuals involved is not sufficient to claim statistical significance, there is an indication of a trend. This paper concludes by posing the question “Could or should FSV be used as a training tool to reduce the time from `novice' to `experienced' when considering data comparison?”
