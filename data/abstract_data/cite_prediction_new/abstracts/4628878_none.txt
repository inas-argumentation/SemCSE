Second Order Nonlinearities of Some Classes of Cubic Boolean Functions Based on Secondary Constructions
The higher order nonlinearity of a Boolean function is a cryptographic criterion, which play a role against attacks on stream and block ciphers. Also it play a role in coding theory, since it is related to the covering radii of Reed-Muller codes.
