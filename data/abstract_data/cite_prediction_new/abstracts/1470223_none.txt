Exceptional directions for Sierpinski's nonmeasurable sets
In (2) the question was considered in how many directions can a nonmea- surable plane set behave even "better" than the classical one constructed by Sierpinski in (6), in the sense that any line in a given direction intersects the set in at most one point. We considerably improve these results and give a much sharper estimate for the size of the sets of those "better" directions. I. Let us establish some terminology to be used later.
