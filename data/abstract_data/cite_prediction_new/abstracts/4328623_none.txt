Simulation and analysis of an ultra-wideband TEM horn antenna with ridge
An ultra-wideband TEM horn antenna with ridge is simulated and designed. The feed mode of the antenna adopts the form that the coaxial line is parallel to the radiation direction. By modeling and simulating in CST Microwave Studio® software, it can realize that the impedance bandwidth of |S 11 |< −10dB is 1‱30 GHz or more. Within this operating bandwidth, the antenna has directional radiation pattern with the gain 6.70–19.40dBi. The simulation results of the antenna are analyzed, and it shows the principle of ultra-wideband operation and directional radiation. The antenna's bandwidth covers L/S/C/X/Ku/K band and it can be widely used in communication and radar system of the band mentioned above.
