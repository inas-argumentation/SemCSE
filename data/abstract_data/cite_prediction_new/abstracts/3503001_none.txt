Tension cysts of the lung in infancy and childhood.
Summary 1. Eight cases demonstrating various forms of intra-pulmonary positive pressure phenomena, occurring in infancy and childhood, are presented. 2. Tension developmental cysts and obstructive emphysema due to congenital atresia of a bronchus or bronchioles are not uncommon causes of acute asphyxia during the first few weeks of life. 3. The radiological differentiation between tension cysts, obstructive emphysema and tension pneumothorax is difficult and unreliable. 4. Needling of the chest in these conditions is dangerous. 5. Exploratory thoracotomy and excision of the ballooned segment or lobe may prove a life-saving emergency surgical procedure.
