Kinetic study of the photopolymerization of a bisphenol‐A–bis(glycidylmethacrylate)/triethyleneglycol dimethacrylate system in hydroxyapatite‐filled composites
Photocurable composites based on hydroxyapatite and quartz as fillers and a monomeric mixture of bisphenol-A-bis(glycidylmethacrylate)/triethyleneglycol dimethacrylate (40/60 wt%) as a matrix were prepared. Both fillers were also treated with 3-trimethoxysilylpropyl methacrylate, and the effectiveness of the silane treatment was verified by FTIR spectroscopy. The kinetic study of the photopolymerization of the different composites was carried out at maxi- mum filler content, and at variable and constant filler contents. The differences found in the kinetic behaviour of the composites are explained using the three-layer model for particulate composites (TLMPC) and the optical phenomenon that takes place by direct interaction of the irradiation light with the filler particles. # 1999 Society of Chemical Industry
