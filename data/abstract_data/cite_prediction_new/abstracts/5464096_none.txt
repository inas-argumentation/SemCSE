ABO BLOOD GROUPS AND THE ÆTIOLOGY OF SCHIZOPHRENIA
In previous studies, ABO blood groups have been found to be associated with several disease states, such as peptic ulcer, carcinoma of the stomach, etc. A study of blood groups was undertaken in 97 hospital in‐patients suffering from chronic schizophrenia. The proportions of ABO blood groups were compared with those reported previously for the local population. It was found that there was a significantly lower proportion of blood group O in the schizophrenic sample. This finding is discussed in relation to genetic theory.
