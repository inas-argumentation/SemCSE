A new principle and its experimental verification for autonomous measuring free form surfaces on a CNC machine tool
This paper proposes a simple autonomous measuring principle on CNC types of machine tools for free form surfaces. The fundamental ideas includle combining two displacement sensors with a servo motor, keeping the position of the first sensor to directly detect surface at the central value of the sensor measuring range by controlling the motor rotation, measuring the movement distance of the first sensor by the second sensor, and performing scanning motion with NC functions of the machine tool used in measurement. Because the form defining the measured surface is not necessary prior to the measurement, the principle is appropriate for the inspection of a contour profile in unknown form. A non-contact prototype measuring device applicable to a machining center has been produced to verify this principle. The results of the measuring experiments sufficiently demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed principle. Moreover, some basic experiments have been1 performed to verify the inspection accuracy of the prototype measuring device.
