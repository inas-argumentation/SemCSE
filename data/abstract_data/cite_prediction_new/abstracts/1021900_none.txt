Applying Coloured Petri Nets and Design/CPN to an Air-to-Air Missile Simulator
In this paper the communication mechanisms of a missile engagement simulator are modelled and analysed. The simulator is developed as a testing platform for missile guidance and control algorithms. The simulation uses concurrency and remote execution concepts to enhance performance. Coloured Petri nets are a well suited formal approach for modelling and analysis of these concepts. Design/CPN is used to create and analyse the model of the simulation. A new requirement of the graphical user interface is identi ed for the simulation to operate successfully. The communication mechanisms are without deadlocks and are suitable for the simulator.
