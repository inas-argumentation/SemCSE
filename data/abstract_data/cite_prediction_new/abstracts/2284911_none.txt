Identification of an antigenic protein recognized by anti-Sm autoantibody.
Serum from some patients with systemic lupus erythematosus contains Sm autoantibodies which recognize small nuclear ribonucleoprotein (snRNPs) particles. These snRNP particles contain U1, U2, U4, U5 or U6 RNA and several proteins ranging in molecular weight from 8000 to 94000 daltons. The Sm antibodies recognize one protein of 28000 daltons by “Western” transfer with an apparent isoelectric pH of 9.5 as estimated by non-equilibrium IEF.
