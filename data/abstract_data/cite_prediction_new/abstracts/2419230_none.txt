Why “Community Good” Initiatives of the Past Struggle to Appeal to the Millennial Volunteers of the Present
When it comes to community service, the Millennial Generation only participate on their terms. In order to achieve high young adult participation, the process of getting them involved must be short and concise, be meaningful in a big way, involve family, friends, and like-minded peers, allow them to utilize their professional skills, and take on leadership roles. Currently, community exchange and service groups are doing very little to shift outdated marketing strategies in their direction. In order to succeed in attracting Millennial and other generations, organizations need to adopt “A Millennial Style of Communication”.
