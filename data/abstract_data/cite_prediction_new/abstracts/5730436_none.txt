Networks Formed by Chaotic Units NCASO / NLP Project , Spring 2010
I propose to study a network of coupled logistic maps that follow a simple rule to change links similar to the models in [1, 2]. What distinguishes the model I will study is the rule for rewiring links, which is more realistic and less stochastic than that in [1, 2]. I will study the network that results from these interacting logistic maps—and compare the results to those in [1, 2]—and, time permitting, introduce different “maps” (other than logistic) into the network to see whether the different maps cluster into communities.
