Advanced communication strategies for relationship-centered care.
The pediatric encounter requires skillful communication between patient and physician, expert communication with parents and other family members, and an understanding of family dynamics and the child’s cognitive and developmental stage. There is a direct association between the physician’s interpersonal and communication skills and ability to refl ect, and health care quality and outcomes.
