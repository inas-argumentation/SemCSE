Parametric versus non-parametric complex image analysis
In this paper we compare parametric and non-parametric method for the analysis of complex valued high-resolution SAR data. Gauss-Markov Random Field (GMRF) model with a quadratic energy function as a parametric analysis parameterizes the spectogram of the signal, whereas nonlinear short time Fourier transform (STFT) method, the method based on time frequency analysis (TFA) as a non-parametric approach exploits the signal's non-stationarity in the time-frequency domain for information extraction. This comparative analysis helps to understand, characterize and analyze complex valued SAR data.
