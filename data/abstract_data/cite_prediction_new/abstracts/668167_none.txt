Ultraviolet inactivation of murine leukemia and sarcoma viruses
The ultraviolet (UV) sensitivity of Friend leukemia virus and a murine sarcoma virus (MSV) was determined. The inactivation dose D10 was 2,000 to 3,000 ergs/mm2 for both. This value is close to the one obtained with Rous sarcoma virus, 3,000 ergs/mm2 (Goldé et al., 1961). MSV produced by the cells incubated in 5‐fluorouracil (5 × 10−3 M) was significantly more UV sensitive than control MSV, suggesting the sensitization of the viral nucleic acid by the base analogue.
