A protocol for the efficient screening of putatively transformed plants forbar, the selectable marker gene, using the polymerase chain reaction
Amplification of thebar gene usingTaq DNA polymerase in PCR is often not successful, possibly due tobar's high GC content. We describe a PCR protocol in which reliable amplification at a sensitivity of one gene copy per genome (in this study, barley) present in the reaction was achieved using a novel pair of primers and Expandtm High Fidelity DNA polymerase mix (Boehringer Mannheim). This method should allow for rapid screening of plants putatively transformed withbar.
