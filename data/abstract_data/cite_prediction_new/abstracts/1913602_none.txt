The company they keep: Background similarity influences transfer of aftereffects from second- to first-order stimuli
Highlights • Task irrelevant backgrounds of adapting and test stimuli influence tilt aftereffect.• Matched backgrounds enable transfer from second to first-order orientations.• Mismatched backgrounds reduce normally large, within-order aftereffects.• This background similarity effect is robust for weak foreground orientations.• Null aftereffect in many studies may be partially caused by a background mismatch.
