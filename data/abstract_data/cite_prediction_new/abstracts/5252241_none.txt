Conductometric Studies of Charge Transfer Complexes Interaction of Amino Pyridine with π- Acceptors
Charge Transfer Complexes of Amino Pyridine derivatives as donor with the organic π- Acceptors, maleic anhydride,-nitroso--naphthol ,p-chloroaniline ,picric acid ,anthraquinone and p- bromoaniline in polar solvent have been determined . The study was performed in three different polar solvents namely N,N-dimethylformamide (DMF), acetonitrile (ACN) and dimethylsulfoxide (DMSO). The stoichiometric ratios of these complexes were found to vary from (1: 1) to (1: 1.5) (acceptor - donor) depending on the system solvent used and the (ασM) values has been examined . The distribution profiles of the various pyridine derivatives species and organic -acceptors formed, are obtained.
