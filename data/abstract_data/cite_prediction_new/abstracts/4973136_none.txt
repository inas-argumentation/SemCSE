Paper chromatographic studies of urinary organic acids.
1. A new method based on a liquid ion exchange resin has been devised, providing better results than solid resins for the extraction of urinary acids. 2. A new solvent system was used for urine chromatograms, eliminating completely the streaking in the acidic phase, presenting only a few streaks in the basic phase, and separating oxalic acid from other acids well. 3. The characteristics of many as yet unidentified organic acids likely to be found in urine have been described for our system.
