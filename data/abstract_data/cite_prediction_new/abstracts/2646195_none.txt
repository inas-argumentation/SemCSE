Inhibition of pituitary response to gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) by a melatonin-free pineal extract.
Publisher Summary Melatonin-free pineal extract administered to rats was proved to decrease significantly testicular and serum testosterone, and luteinizing hormone (LH), follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) in basal condition and after castration. The same pineal extract inhibits the stimulating effect of human chorionic gonadotropin (HCG) and of HCG + FSH combination on mice uterus, and decreases the weight of the ventral prostate in rats. This chapter studies the effect of this pineal extract on the pituitary response under conditions of exogenous stimulation with gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH). The results obtained show that pretreatment with pineal extract decreased the pituitary sensitivity to GnRH administration. The data show that the pineal extract acts directly on the pituitary, although the hypothalamic way, cannot be excluded. Pineal extracts may, in fact, act both on the hypothalamus to inhibit GnRH secretion and on the pituitary to suppress the stimulatory effect of the hypothalamic hormone on LH and FSH release. The mechanism of action seems to be rather complex. This chapter provides evidence that this suppressive action is due, at least in part, to a direct and inhibitory effect on pituitary responsiveness to GnRH.
