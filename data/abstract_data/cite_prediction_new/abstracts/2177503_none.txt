Ratio between serum IL‐8 and pepsinogen A/C: a marker for atrophic body gastritis
Background and aims Elevated serum gastrin and a low pepsinogen A/C ratio are well‐recognized markers for atrophic body gastritis (ABG). We have shown that the presence of body atrophy is also associated with elevated serum pro‐inflammatory cytokines. This study tested the hypothesis that serum cytokines provide additional information to gastrin and pepsinogens in screening for ABG.
