The Relevance of Mediterranean Neutrino Telescope Sites on Earth-skimming ντ detection
A study of the UHE ν τ detection performances of a km 3 Neutrino Telescope sitting at the three proposed sites for ANTARES , NEMO and NESTOR in the Mediterranean sea is here performed. In particular, it is analyzed the effect of the underwater surface profile on the total amount of yearly expected τ crossing the fiducial volume in the limit of full detection efficiency and energy resolution.
