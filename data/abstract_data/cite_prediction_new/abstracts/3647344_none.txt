Isolation of DNA satellites by thermal chromatography in the presence of sodium perchlorate.
A modification of thermal chromatography permits the isolation of or enrichment for (G + C)-rich satellite DNA's in native form. The addition of 7.2 M NaClO4 to a low concentration of phosphate buffer allows the elution of DNA at greatly reduced temperatures. The potential of thermal chromatography for the fractionation of DNA is increased by extending the useful temperature range of fractionation.
