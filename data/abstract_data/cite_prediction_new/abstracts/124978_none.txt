Comments on ‘Sleep Disordered Breathing and Periodic Limb Movements in Patients with Narcolepsy with Cataplexy'
Dr. Nitin K. Sethi serves as Associate Editor of the Eastern Journal of Medicine . Dear Sir, I read with interest the study by Pizza et al. [1] on the occurrence of sleep disordered breathing (SDB) and periodic limb movements (PLMs) in patients with narcolepsy with cataplexy (NC). They found a high prevalence and mutual association of SDB and PLMs in patients with NC though there was no clinical correlation with severity of daytime symptoms of excessive daytime sleepiness.
