Case series : Sacrococcygeal sarcoma – a case series
Introduction Chordomas are rare tumours. They are slow growing but locally aggressive neoplasms derived from primitive notochordal elements. Approximately 50 % originate in the sacral region, 35%in the clivus (Base of skull) and 15% elsewhere along the axial skeleton. These tumours rarely metastasize but local recurrence is common due to largely intralesional excision of the tumour. Therefore local irradiation becomes essential. Here we present 3 patients with sacrococcygeal teratoma for whom wide local excision was done and are now on regular follow up.
