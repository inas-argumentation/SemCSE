Fault tolerant control for power side current sensor in wind energy conversion system with cascaded brushless DFIG
In order to improve the reliability of a cascaded brushless doubly fed induction generator-based wind energy conversion system (CBDFIG-WECS), a current observer is established for the estimation of actual power side current based on the adaptive observer for grid-connected operation in the power winding flux frame. By constructing the reference model and the adaptive model, the observed value of power winding current can track its actual value with excellent dynamic performance. Then the influence of parameter variations on the current estimation is investigated over the whole operation region and is constructed as design guideline of optimal threshold for fault detection. In the static αβ coordinate system, residual values can be obtained by calculating the difference between the measured and observed values for the purpose of fault diagnosis and isolation. The proposed method can identify the specific source of sensor failure when the faults occur and realize fault tolerant control. The effectiveness of the proposed method is validated by experimental results.
