Microcatheter CSA in a young woman with Fontan repair undergoing elective sterilization
We present the case of a 28–yr–old woman with Fontan anastomosis undergoing elective sterilization. Because of concerns about laparoscopic surgery with capnoperitoneum and positivepressure ventilation, a mini–laparotomy was performed in microcatheter spinal anaesthesia. Tiration of small doses of plain bupivacaine 0.5% supplemented with 5 μg sufentanil provided high cardiocirculatory and respiratory perioperative stability in this patient.
