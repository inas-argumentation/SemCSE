The Derivation of Uniform Recurrence Equations for the Knapsack Problem
A mapping procedure for synthesizing uniform recurrence equations from the dynamic programming formulation of the knapsack problem is proposed. Two new systolic arrays are synthesized from the systems of recurrence equations produced. One of these arrays is optimal with respect to both speedup and efficiency and requires O( nb/q + n − n/q) time and O( q[wmax /2]) processors, where b is the knapsack capacity, n is the number of items, wmax is the maximum weight taken over all items, and q > 0 is an arbitrary chosen design parameter.
