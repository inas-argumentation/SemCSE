Optical Efficiency and R(T,I) Measurements of ACTPol TESes Using Time Domain Multiplexing Electronics
We present new data on feedhorn-coupled transition-edge sensor devices fabricated for the second-generation receiver (ACTPol) for the Atacama cosmology telescope (ACT). First, we describe optical efficiency measurements of the latest ACTPol detector wafer, which has a $$86\,\pm 15\,\%$$86±15% average optical efficiency. Next, we discuss measurements of the TES resistance as a function of temperature and bias current ($$R(T,I)$$R(T,I)) using the ACTPol time-domain multiplexing electronics. Qualitative agreement between $$R(T,I)$$R(T,I) data at low bias current and the two-fluid model prediction is shown. Using the two-fluid model and low bias current $$R(T,I)$$R(T,I) data, $$\alpha $$α and $$\beta $$β at our operating bias current are calculated.
