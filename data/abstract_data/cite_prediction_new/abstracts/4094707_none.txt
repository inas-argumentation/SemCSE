Lelong numbers with respect to regular plurisubharmonic weights
Generalized Lelong numbers v(T, φ) due to Demailly are specified for the case of positive closed currents T = ddcu and plurisubharmonic weights φ with multicircled asymptotics. Explicit formulas for these values are obtained in terms of the directional Lelong numbers of the functions u and the Newton diagrams of φ. An extension of Demailly’s approximation theorem is proved as well.
