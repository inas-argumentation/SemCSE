Symmetry for solutions of two-phase semilinear elliptic equations on hyperbolic space
Assume that f (s) = F' (s) where F is a double-well potential. Under certain conditions on the Lipschitz constant of f on [-1, 1], we prove that arbitrary bounded global solutions of the semilinear equation Δu = f (u) on hyperbolic space ℍ n must reduce to functions of one variable provided they admit asymptotic boundary values on s n-1 = ∂ ∞ ℍ n which are invariant under a cohomogeneity one subgroup of the group of isometries of ℍ n . We also prove existence of these one-dimensional solutions.
