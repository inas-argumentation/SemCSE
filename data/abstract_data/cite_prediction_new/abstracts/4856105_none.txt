Abdrücke aus hochmolekularen Polyamiden von Kristallgitterflächen als Matrizen
The macromolecules of high molecular polyamide films obtained in contact with (100) of KCl, KBr, and sucrose as a matrix are oriented by overgrowth in the form of a characteristic network of fibrils. This network formation of polyamides in contact with the atomic arrangement in crystal lattice planes as a matrix is proposed as a model for antibody formation.
