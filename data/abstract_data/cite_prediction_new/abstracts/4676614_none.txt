A finite element model for the diastolic anine left vcntricle based on large deformation theory has been developed. The myecardial stress-strain relation determined by the diastoric pressure-yolume relation was used in this study, The ventricle was assumed as a homogeneous, isotropic, thick-walled solid of revolution with variable thickness. The predicted shapes based on the model were in close agreement with the shapes recorded by two-dimensional echocardiegraphy.
