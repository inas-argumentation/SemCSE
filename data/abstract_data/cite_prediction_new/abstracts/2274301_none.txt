CONSIDERATIONS IN THE PATIENT WITH DIMINISHED RENAL FUNCTION.
Knowledge of the renal functional defect and the application of corrective fluid and electrolyte therapy make it possible to operate on patients with minimal functional impairment without increasing operative morbidity. Extensive surgical procedures are possible in patients with severe uremia if careful attention is paid to anesthesia and fluid replacement. The use of extrarenal methods of treating uremia makes possible surgery in patients previously deemed inoperable.
