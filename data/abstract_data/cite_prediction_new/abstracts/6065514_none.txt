DC distribution protection — Analysis, solutions, and example implementations
DC protection is designed based on the DC system and its fault features. DCCB based or converter based protection solutions are different in protective devices and protection coordination methods. Equivalent inductances can be used to estimate DC fault features and the mutual impacts between the protection and system design. Fast fault interruption is essential and critical to DC distribution protection. Specific speed requirements are analyzed for different DC protection solutions. The selection and design of proper DC protection solutions are demonstrated by example implementations.
