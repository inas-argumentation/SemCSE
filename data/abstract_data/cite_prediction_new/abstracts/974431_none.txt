50 / 60 Hz Grid Code Test and Verification Experience
The wind industry’s supply chain, R&D initiatives and project development already involve a substantial amount of international coordinated activities and efforts. Nonetheless, grid Code requirements continue to vary greatly from country to country along with the testing or compliance procedures of those countries. An issue arise as turbines are manufactured in countries with a grid frequency of 50 Hz and as many of the turbines are then exported to countries operating with 60 Hz grid frequencies : These countries have various grid requirements which are often not in agreement with the standards applicable in the countries where the turbines are manufactured. In this paper, various Grid Codes will be analyzed and relevant requirements will be highlighted with a focus on test and verification practices currently applicable in various 50 and 60 Hz countries. Model simulation and validation will also be proposed as the preferred way to validate compliance of a generation facility or equipment. Previous hands-on experiences in that field will be presented.
