Corrosion characterization of Sn37Pb solders and with Cu substrate soldering reaction in 3.5wt.% NaCl solution
The electrochemical corrosion behavior of Sn37Pb solder, Cu<inf>6</inf>Sn<inf>5</inf>, Cu<inf>3</inf>Sn and Cu in 3.5% NaCl solution was investigated by using Galvanic corrosion testing, potentiodynamic polarization methods. The galvanic current densities for Sn37Pb solder in 3.5wt.% NaCl solution are 38, 16 and 5μA/cm<sup>2</sup> for Cu<inf>3</inf>Sn, Cu, and Cu<inf>6</inf>Sn<inf>5</inf>, respectively. Polarization studies revealed that an increase in Cu content and formation of IMCs shifted the corrosion potential (E<inf>corr.</inf>) towards more noble values and increased the corrosion current density (I<inf>corr.</inf>).
