Numerical Modelling of Direct Contact Condensation of Steam in BWR Pressure Suppression Pool System
This paper presents the results of computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations of chugging direct contact condensation (DCC) mode observed in the suppression pool (POOLEX) experiments of Lappeenranta University of Technology. The CFD simulations were done by using Eulerian-Eulerian twofluid approach of the compressible flow solver of the OpenFOAM CFD code. The interfacial heat transfer between steam and water was modelled by using the surface renewal model of Hughes and Duffey (1991) [1]. Flow turbulence was solved by employing the k-e turbulence model. Both 2D-axisymmetric and 3D computational domains were modelled and results were compared to the corresponding NEPTUNE_CFD simulations of Tanskanen [2] in order to distinguish possible differences in the solver.
