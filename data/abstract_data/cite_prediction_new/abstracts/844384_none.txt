Extraction of SOI thickness deviation based on resonant wavelength analysis for silicon photonics devices
In the fabrication of photonics integrated circuits using silicon photonics technology, dimension control for silicon waveguide is a critical issue to ensure the reproducibility of circuit performance. In this paper, a method to extract fabrication deviations in the waveguide from refractive index change in silicon microring resonators (MRRs) is examined for optical devices after full integration process. By using C-band MRRs formed on 300-mm SOI as an optical monitor device, it is successfully demonstrated that a wafer-level SOI thickness variation can be extracted with sub-nm accuracy, showing the effectiveness of the method for process control monitoring.
