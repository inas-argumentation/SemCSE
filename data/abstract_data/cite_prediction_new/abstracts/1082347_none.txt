How to “Integrate” Minorities Without Giving Them a Political Voice? The Sénart Project: A Local Roma-Targeted Affirmative Action
Roma migrants in Europe and their relationships with the Nation-State has for long been a sensitive political issue. Roma are widely perceived as a population of which the presence is straightaway thought as temporary. Moreover, their “freedom” is conceived as the proof of their lack of will of integration and of respect of the rules of conviviality. Far from being the result of the enactment of an imagined nomadism which would be inherent to a so-called “Roma culture”, the migration trajectories of Roma are related to the opportunities taken after the fall of the communist regimes in Eastern Europe and the European Union construction.
