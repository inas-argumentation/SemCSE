Preparation and Dielectric Characterization of Arrowroot-Chitosan Film for Microwave Phantom Applications
Arrowroot (Maranta arundinacea) and Chitosan are two well-known materials used in medical and scientific applications. Both materials possess medicinal properties and have the ability to form thick gels. Chitosan-reinforced Arrowroot film was developed and its dielectric characterization was performed at microwave frequencies. Cavity perturbation method was used for the measurement. The study proposes the use of Arrowroot-Chitosan film as phantom material representing human body counterparts in microwave imaging applications.
