Primjena Markowitzeve teorije na tržište dionica Zagrebačke burze
In the context of the development of croatian capital market observing movement of return and risk of seurities through time using various methods of empirical analysis in becoming more and more important. The growth of number of stock that quote on the capital market, and what is more important, the growth in their trading volume, enables investors to diversify risk. The term portfolio analysis, which was introduced in economic theory by Harry Markowitz, is not new in financial literature available in Croatia. However, there are few examples of the application of the portfolio analysis tools on the Croatian capital market.
