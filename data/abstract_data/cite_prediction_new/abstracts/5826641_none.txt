A construction of Hadamard matrices from BIBD (2k2-2k+1, k, 1)
It is shown that the existence of a BIB design with parameters v = 2k 2 − 2k +1 ,b =2 v, r =2 k, k, λ = 1 implies the existence of Hadamard matrices of orders 4v and 8vt ,w heret is an integer for which an orthogonal design of order 4t and type (t, t, t, t) exists.
