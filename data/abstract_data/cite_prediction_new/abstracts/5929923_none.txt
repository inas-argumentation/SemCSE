Analysis and Comparison of Software Product Line Frameworks
Software product line architectures have been much attention in the software research community in past years. Although many methods and frameworks have been used to create software product lines, but there are still many issues and challenges in this field. This paper describes the issues and challenges surrounding software product lines, then introduced four software product line frameworks. Finally, these frameworks have been compared based on the issues and challenges such that, each framework is improved which of the issues and challenges.
