Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm Surgery: Something Old, Something New
'Aneurysms which are permanent dilatations of arteries have been recognized for centuries. Galen knew of their existence and the writings of surgeons throughout the last 1000 years provide an extensive literature on this subject. Ambroise Pare was aware of their association with syphilis and his description of the clinical features of a peripheral arterial aneurysm published in the 17th century is worth quoting today'l.
