KNOWLEDGE ACQUISITION BY OBSERVATION : APPLICATION TO INTELLIGENT TUTORING SYSTEMS
. This paper presents an architecture for an Intelligent Tutoring System (ITS) based on a pedagogic model. This model takes into account the student's behavior in a "short term supervisory control loop" and improves the initial pedagogic knowledge base in a "long term evaluation loop". Observation is the key factor in this approach. It allows incremental knowledge acquisition from an initial pedagogic knowledge base. A learning technique by Specialisation-Structuring has been developed and is presented here. This approach is being applied in instruction of aircraft pilots.
