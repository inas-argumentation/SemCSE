Review of optimization models in the pollution prevention and control
The objective of the present work is to investigate the extent and effectiveness of the implementation of optimization methods for the solution of environmental problems. In order to fulfill its objective, the present work reviews a significant number of scientific / research papers dealing with the application of optimization approaches for the solution of environmental problems, in the areas of air pollution, solid, liquid and industrial waste management, and production integrated pollution control. The review focuses its attention to the identification of the basic problem parameters, the type of the optimization model for each particular problem category and the results obtained. Amongst its various other conclusions, the present work exhibits the contribution of the optimization modeling to the identification of all the characteristics of the environmental problems and their integrated approach.
