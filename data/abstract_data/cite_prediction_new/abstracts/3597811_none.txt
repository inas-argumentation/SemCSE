DIAMOND OPTICAL DIAGNOSTICS : FIRST STREAK CAMERA MEASUREMENTS
We present in this paper the first measurements of the electron bunch profile with a streak camera during phase I of the commissioning of Diamond. We recorded the bunch profile during injection. Phase and energy offset were observed. Moreover, estimates of the bunch length at injection and of the stored beam are measured. Finally, we derived the minimum charge in a single bunch to be measurable with the streak camera based on a comparison between bench measurements and synchrotron radiation measurements.
