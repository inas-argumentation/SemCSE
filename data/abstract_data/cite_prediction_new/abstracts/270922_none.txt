Proteomic analysis (GeLC-MS/MS) of endoscopically (ePFT) collected pancreatic fluid
Mass spectrometry-based proteomics offers a means for enhancing our understanding of disease pathogenesis and progression of the exocrine pancreas. For example, the diagnosis of chronic pancreatitis can often remain elusive in mild disease due to a scarcity of predictable and reliable radiological and endoscopic abnormalities of the pancreas. Improved strategies for the diagnosis and treatment of chronic pancreatitis are necessary to reduce healthcare and patient burdens.
