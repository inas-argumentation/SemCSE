Strategies For The Environmental Work In The Iraqi Provinces
This paper illustrates the importance and the need for environmental work in the provinces of Iraq and the pillars which, according to researcher opinion, depend on are: i) environmental awareness, ii) environmental legislations and determinants, iii) environmental assessment and mitigation and iv) funding and financial support. The researcher has developed an administrative and applicable plan to activate the environmental work in the provinces which can be summarized in the formation of environmental council in each province and the construction and management of this council will be performed by experts from the province and nearby universities. This council will prepare and supervising the implementation of researches and studies in all areas related to the environment, which are helping to prepare the development plans in accordance with local and global environmental standards. The paper also included onsite study through a questionnaire prepared by the researcher to determine the level of environmental awareness among the Iraqi society, where the sample included different segments of the citizens and decision makers. The most important indicator that arrived by the researcher is the low level of environmental sense and awareness, not only the citizens but also for decision-makers, which is not commensurate with their responsibilities.
