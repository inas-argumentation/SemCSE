Reduction of harmonic distortion in micro grids and distribution systems with photovoltaic generators
The problem of power quality is a major issue these days and hence there is an area related to the research in power sector. The improvement of technology is making it possible to keep power sector free from power quality problems. This paper is about the harmonics related to power systems. Distortion of the voltage waveforms i.e. harmonics may cause overheating of natural conductors and electrical distribution transformers, the malfunction of electronic equipment and also the distortion in communication systems. In this paper, the reduction of harmonic distortion using photovoltaic generators has been discussed. The improvement of power quality in micro grids connected with the distribution system using photovoltaic generators by reducing the harmonics is shown in this paper.
