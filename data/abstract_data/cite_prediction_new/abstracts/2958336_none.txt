A Search for the Standard Model Higgs Boson in the Channel ZH → ννbb at √ s = 1 . 96 TeV The DØ Collaboration
This note describes a search at DØ for the Standard Model Higgs boson produced in association with a Z boson, where the Higgs decays to a bb pair and the Z boson decays to neutrinos. An integrated luminosity of 0.930 fb −1 was used. The pp → ZH → ννbb channel is one of the most sensitive ways to search for a light mass Higgs, but is problematic at hadron colliders due to the absence of visible leptons and the presence of only two jets in the final state. We thus require a large missing transverse energy and that the jets be b-tagged, with any Higgs signal producing a peak in their invariant mass. This analysis updates the previously published DØ analysis in this channel [1]. In the absence of a significant excess we set limits on σ(pp → ZH) × (H → bb) at the 95% confidence level of 2.7 pb – 1.6 pb for Higgs boson masses from 105 – 135 GeV.
