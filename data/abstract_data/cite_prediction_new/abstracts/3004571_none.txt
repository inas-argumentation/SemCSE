THE EFFECT OF SOME OPERATIONAL FACTOR ' S ON COLOUR TRANSFER DURING PAN BOILING
The SMRI pilot batch pan was used to investigate the effects of a number of operational factors on the transfer of colour from mother-liquor to crystal during A-massecuite boiling. The factors investigated include stirring, supersa-turation, crystalsizeand crystallisation rate. Colourtransfer was found to increase with supersaturation, crystallisation rate and crystal size but to decrease with stirring. Although far from complete, this work indicates that the effects of seasonal factors and canequalityon colourtransferare much larger than those of the operational factors investigated.
