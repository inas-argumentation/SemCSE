Sterilization of Mail by Means of an Electron Beam Accelerator
Industrial electron beam accelerators are widely used for sterilization of medical equipment, drugs, food, and packing materials. In some countries, fairly wide production of such accelerators, especially lowpower ones (no more than 15 kW), has already been organized. It should be noted that Budker Institute of Nuclear Physics was one of the founders of the manufacture and supply of industrial accelerators [2]. In the United States, one of the largest companies that produce accelerators is the Titan Sure Beam (http://www.titan.com).
