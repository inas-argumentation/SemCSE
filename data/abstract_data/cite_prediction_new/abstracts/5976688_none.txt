Association Rule Mining of Household Electrical Energy Usage
In a Smart Grid network, household energy consumption is monitored by the use of residential smart meters. However, these do not provide data for individual appliance consumption. In this paper we consider publicly available disaggregated electrical energy data obtained from six homes. Using the Apriori algorithm we determine association rules between appliances so as to understand which appliances are used in conjunction with others. This information can then be used to determine better ways to control the on/off, activity of these devices. Our objective was to determine if such associations can in fact be determined. We conclude that this is indeed the case as demonstrated by our results. In general, houses with a large number of appliances generated more association rules while the accuracy of classifying rules reduced with an increase in the number of appliances.
