SHARP ERROR BOUNDS FOR THE TRAPEZOIDAL RULE AND SIMPSON'S RULE
We give error bounds for the trapezoidal rule and Simpson's rule for "rough" con- tinuous functions—for instance, functions which are Holder continuous, of bounded variation, or which are absolutely continuous and whose derivative is in L p . These differ considerably from the classical results, which require the functions to have continuous higher derivatives. Further, we show that our results are sharp, and in many cases precisely characterize the functions for which equality holds. One consequence of these results is that for rough functions, the error esti- mates for the trapezoidal rule are better (that is, have smaller constants) than those for Simpson's rule.
