Mid-infrared broadband absorber of full semiconductor
We demonstrate a mid-infrared broadband absorber of full semiconductor epi-layers theoretically. The structure is of MIM-like with grating InAs(n-type, 220 nm)/InAs(I,220 nm)/InAs(n-type, 1000 nm). The absorptivity is over 80% from 8 μm to 12 μm.
