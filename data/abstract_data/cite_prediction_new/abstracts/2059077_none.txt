A Pedestrian Detection Method Using Background Modeling and Object Proposal
As the technique of pedestrian detection has been used in a wide application, the methods of background modeling and object proposal become one of the popular research projects in the field of computer vision. In this article, we review some common methods of background modeling and object proposal, we also analyze basic targets in object proposal and clarify the strength and weakness of these methods. To avoid some disadvantages of common methods particularly low computational efficiency or poor localization quality, we put forward a method combined with Gaussian Mixture Model for background modeling and BING++ (Binarized Normed Gradients for Objectness Estimation) for object proposal for adaptive efficiency in more complex conditions. Finally, we evaluate our method by precision and recall for the localization, and make comparisons of results processed by BING and BING++. It is observed that the detection recall and mean average best overlap of the proposals processed by BING++ is higher than BING, and just with little more time as cost. In sum, BING++ can supple higher localization quality. Respectively, GMM can make detection more adaptive as well as flexible and BING++ can achieve comparable performance in the simulation of pedestrian detection.
