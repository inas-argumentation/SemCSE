Irregular edge-colorings of sums of cycles of even lengths
P. Wittmann showed that for the irregular coloring number c(G) of a simple 2-regular graph of order n the inequality c(G) 6 √ 2n + O(1) holds. We determine the exact value of this number in the case when the 2-regular graph consists of cycles of even lengths. For this purpose we consider decompositions of several classes of graphs.
