Effect of Curing Method on the Color of Vinyl Addition Cured Silicon (Effect of processing temperature on the color parameters of maxillofacial silicon)
The study investigated the effect of curing methods on the color parameters of the vinyl addition silicon maxillofacial materials. The study also aims to find the curing method that provide best matching with the uncured material with minimum color changes. Materials and methods: 45samples were made from maxillofacial silicon rubber. The samples (20*10*3)mm length, width, and thickness, were divide into three groups group1 white, group2 yellow and group3 pink(5 samples as control subgroup, and 5 samples as self cure subgroup and 5 samples as heat cure subgroup in each group) and then tested to record the color parameters. Results: means of L*value of the three groups were significantly different between the three subgroups where the control samples had the least translucency and the self cured samples had the highest L*values. Conclusion: The control subgroups in the three groups demonstrated changes in the color parameters after polymerization. Translucency of the self cured samples was higher than that of the heat cured samples, due to the effect of processing heat. Forty five samples were made from maxillofacial vinyl addition silicon rubber material (polymerkit, wales). The samples (20*10*3)mm length, width, and thickness, were divide into three groups according to their color: group1 white, group2 yellow and group3 pink(5 samples as control subgroup, and 5 samples as self cure subgroup and 5 samples as heat cure subgroup in each group). Samples were prepared by mixing the material manually according the to the manufacturer's instruction 10:1 part A to part B, an intrinsic liquid pigment (white, yellow, and pink) colored added 0.2% by weight(14), for all samples of the control subgroups the mixture were put in cellophane 0.25mm thickness container (20*10*3)mm length, width, and depth, and then they were measured by the colorimeter (Easy shade, vita company, Germany) before polymerization, the other samples were prepared by pouring the material in stone molds, clamped in their flasks and pressed by hydraulic pressing machine up to 150 psi to avoid air entrapment. The self cured samples left for self polymerization for 24 hours according the manufacturer's instruction. The heat cured samples polymerized for 1 hour at 100°C according the manufacturer's instruction. After the polymerization of the self and heat cured samples they were tested by the colorimeter to record the color parameters. Color observations were performed using one illumination light, and the same white background below the samples. The color values h, b, and c that were collected from the colorimeter and transformed into their corresponding L*a*and b* by Photoshop computer program to facilitate the statistical analysis, the statistical examination were carried out by using SPSS program using ANOVA and Duncan's multiple range tests at (P<0.05).
