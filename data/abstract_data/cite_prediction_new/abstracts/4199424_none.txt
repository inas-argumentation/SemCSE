Predicting Mn concentration in water reservoir using Artificial neural network ( Chahnimeh 1 reservoir , Iran )
Water samples were collected from Chahnimeh1 reservoir was the most important source of drinking water in Sistan-balochistan and analyzed for physical quality parameters including: EC(Electrical Conductivity), TDS(Total Dissolved Solids), T(Temperature), pH and Mn concentration using standard methods. In this study, a three-layer artificial neural network (ANN) model was investigated to predict the Mn concentration in water of Chahnimeh1 reservoir. The input variables are EC, TDS, T and pH while the Mn concentration in water is the output. The Levenberg–Marquardt (LM) algorithm was used to train ANN. The results showed that a network with 10 hidden neurons was highly accurate in predicting the Mn concentration. The mean squared error (MSE) and coefficient of correlation (R) between experimental data and model outputs were calculated as 0.0011 and 0.9515 for training, 0.0034 and 0.8934 for validation and 0.0033 and 0.8847 for testing date sets. It has been seen a good agreement between actual data and the ANN outputs for training, validation and testing data sets. The results indicated that the ANN model explained in this study is an applied tool to predict the Mn concentration.
