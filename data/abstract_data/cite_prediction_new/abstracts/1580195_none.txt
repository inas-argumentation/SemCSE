In vitro synthesis, transport, and assembly of ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase subunits.
It is well established that the chloroplast synthesizes only a limited number of its own proteins. Many stromal enzymes and thyla-koid membrane polypeptides are made in the cytosol and must be transferred subsequently from their sites of synthesis to their final locations inside the chloroplast (1). The mechanism of intracellular transport of proteins across the two chloroplast envelope membranes and the events associated with it are unknown.
