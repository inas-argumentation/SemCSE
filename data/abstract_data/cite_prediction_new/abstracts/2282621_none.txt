Building social capital through corporate social investment
Purpose – Whereas previously the objectives of the corporation are seen by many as antithetical to the aims of the not‐for‐profit sector, we are now in fact seeing far greater interaction between the two. The purpose of this paper is to examine this trend and the motivation of both to form partnerships.Design/methodology/approach – Reference is made to a range of literature as well as interviews with those immersed in this field and case study material involving large corporations.Findings – What emerges is that typically if the not‐for‐profit sector has approached business for support it has been an appeal to altruism.
