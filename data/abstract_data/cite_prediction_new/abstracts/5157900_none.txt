Electrophysiological investigation of the antiarrhythmic action of the antioxidant ionol
Antioxidants have been shown to prevent lowering of the fibrillation threshold and an increase in ectopic activity of the heart during stress, and also in experimental myocardial infarction [6, 8]. This paper describes a study of the action of the antioxidant ionol on postdepolarization oscillations of membrane potential, and also on the duration of the excitation wave spreading over the myocardium, on isolated preparations of the heart.
