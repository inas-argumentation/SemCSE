Correction to: Southernmost record of the Giant Manta Ray Mobula birostris (Walbaum, 1792) in the Eastern Pacific
Correction to: Marine Biodiversity Records (2017) 10:27 DOI: 10.1186/s41200-017-0130-1In the original version of this article (Moreno & Gonzalez-Pestana, 2017), published on 9 November 2017, the first sentence of the ‘Conclusions’ contains an error: the species “M. alfredi” has been wrongfully mentioned. The original publication of this article has been corrected.
