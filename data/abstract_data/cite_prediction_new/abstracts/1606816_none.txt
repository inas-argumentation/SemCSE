Phonon-Polariton Resonances in Hexagonal Boron Nitride Transdimensional Lattices
We analyze optical antennas made of hexagonal boron nitride which is a natural material with hyperbolic dispersion in mid-infrared range. The antennas in the periodic array support phonon-polariton resonances including electric and magnetic dipoles and higher multipoles. The resonances are controlled by the size and shape of the antenna as well as period of the array. We regard these arrays as transdimensional lattices because of their 2D/3D translational nature. The antenna resonances allow for efficient control of light scattering, reflection, and propagation and can be utilized in metasurfaces and flat optical components.
