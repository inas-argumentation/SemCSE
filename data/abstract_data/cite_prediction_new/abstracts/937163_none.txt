A comparison on blood lipids and fatty acid composition of lipoproteins between normal adults and patients with coronary heart disease
SummaryThe blood lipids and the fatty acid composition of lipoproteins were analyzed in normal adults and patients with coronary heart disease (CHD), all aged over forty-five.1.There was a significant difference in the level of blood cholesterol between normals and CHD-patients (P<0.0l). The HDL-chol was higher and the LDL-chol lower than that reported at home or abroad. The difference of LDL-chol between normals and patients was significant (P<0.00l), but that of HDL-chol was not.2.The fatty acid composition of total lipids, HDL and LDL + VLDL were analyzed by gas chromatography in normals and CHD-patients. There was no difference in the composition of fatty acid of all lipoproteins except that linoleic acid in HDL was lower in male CHD-patients (P<0.01).
 Owing to the decrease of concentration of linoleate in HDL, it is possible that the efflux of cholesterol from arterial wall is less effective, thereby facilitating the development of CHD.
