As mentioned above, resistin is highly expressed in human monocytes and macrophages, and many authors have shown that inflammatory markers such as CRP, interleukin 6, tumor necrosis factor, and plasminogen activator inhibitor 1 have been well documented to be associated with MetS. All these data suggest that resistin could be related to MetS and inflammation and that its relationship with other metabolic parameters like blood lipids is a secondary effect. In conclusion, serum resistin levels are high in elderly Chinese men with MetS. The role of resistin in MetS warrants further investigation.
