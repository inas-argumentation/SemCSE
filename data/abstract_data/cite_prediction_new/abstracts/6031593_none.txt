Microstructures and shear properties of mixed assembly BGA structure SnAgCu/SnBi(Ag)/Cu joints in board-level packaging
To reduce the cost of soldering materials and alleviate the thermal shock of temperature-sensitive electronic components during board-level packaging process, SnBi(Ag) solder pastes were explored to replace SnAgCu solder pastes due to the feature of low melting temperature of SnBi(Ag) solders. In this study, Sn3.0Ag0.5Cu (SAC305) solder balls as IC pins were assembled with SnBi(Ag) solder pastes in the substrate board, and the microstructures and shear properties of mixed assembly BGA structure SnAgCu/SnBi(Ag)/Cu joints were studied comprehensively. Results show that the thickness of the interfacial Cu6Sn5 layer of the SAC305/SnBi(Ag)/Cu joint is thinner than that of the SAC305/SAC305/Cu joint, and the SAC305/SnBiAg/Cu joint has the lowest interfacial Cu6Sn5 layer. The shear strength of SAC305/SnBi(Ag)/Cu joints is higher than that of SAC305/SAC305/Cu joints. Moreover, for BGA structure SAC305/SnBi/Cu and SAC305/SnBiAg/Cu joints, the shear strength of all the joints increases firstly and then decreases with increasing the loading rate in shear test, and the SAC305/SnBiAg/Cu joint has higher shear strength than SAC305/SnBi/Cu joint under a low loading rate, while being reverse at a high loading rate. Both SAC305/SnBi/Cu and SAC305/SnBiAg/Cu joints exhibit a mixed ductile and brittle fracture mode, and the fracture occurs mainly in the solder matrix and partially at the solder/IMC interface.
