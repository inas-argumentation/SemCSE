Cervical Cancer : Issues of Sexuality and Fertility
Carter et al provide a nice summary of current knowledge of sexual dysfunction in and rehabilitation of women with invasive cervical cancer. The prevailing perspective of their review, however, seems to be that most women treated for cervical cancer are white, middleclass patients at major cancer centers. In order to make a difference in the quality of life of the majority of cervical cancer survivors, we have to understand who they are and recognize the impact of social and gender inequality on their lives and relationships.
