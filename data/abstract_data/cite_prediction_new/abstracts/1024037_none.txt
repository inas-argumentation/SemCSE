COST-EFFECTIVE MANAGEMENT OF PSYCHIATRIC AND MENTAL HEALTH DISORDERS IN THE COMMUNITY RELATIVE TO INSTITUTION ALIS ATION
Management of psychiatric and mental health disorders in the community is a new healthcare approach introduced in the UK and the USA in the last ten years for strategic and socio-economic reasons. The evidence has shown that the cost of caring for patients with psychiatric and mental health disorders in institutions on regular basis is very high and the approach alienates patients from their home environment. There is apparently no patient satisfaction. Hence, it became necessary to adopt a community-based approach that would minimise cost as well as provide adequate quality care for patients within a home environment. This paper explores the cost-effective management of community-based care for people with psychiatric and mental health disorders, and evaluates the quality of care being received by patients. Areas of particular interest that might affect cost-effectiveness are discussed and evaluated. These include: prevention, mental health screening, ethnic consideration, disability, treatment with psychotropic drugs, close monitoring, socio-economic costs of stress among caregivers, and accommodation costs.
