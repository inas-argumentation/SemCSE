Effects of Purinergic Stimulation on Ciliary Beat Frequency and Chloride Secretion in Sinusitis
Objectives: We aimed to identify the functional abnormality of sinusitis‐affected mucosa by observing the responsiveness of the mucosa to purinergic stimulation after the onset of sinusitis and during the recovery period. We also aimed to identify possible beneficial effects of purinergic agonists on sinusitis.
