Virtual Machine Optimizations Using Markov Chain Data Analytics in Heterogeneous Cloud Computing
Capacity planning is a crucial issue in web server deployments, which has a strong impact on the performance of the network connections and service quality. Prior researches have explored this field from various perspectives in order to obtain a deployable networking connection for successful service deliveries. However, the rapid development of heterogeneous cloud computing has brought a variety of new elements that impact on the networking capacity controls, improvements, and maintenances. Accurately addressing the network capacity in the heterogeneous cloud system has become a critical problem in guaranteeing the stability of the networks. This paper concentrates on this issue and presents a research work on data analytics by using Markov chain method for network capacity management. The proposed model is called Smart Predictive Capacity Management (SPCM). The model is proposed to analyze and understand the network conditions, which is designed to reduce or avoid the rate of networking disconnections or other interferences.
