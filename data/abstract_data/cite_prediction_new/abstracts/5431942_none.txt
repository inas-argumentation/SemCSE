Skills Portfolio Management for Information Systems Professionals: Managerial Issues and Research Topics
Recent surveys and reports have highlighted “information technology (IT) skill shortages.” However, it is not entirely a new phenomenon. The diffusion of new technologies always seems to alert us to a potential skill crisis. For firms, how should they manage the portfolio of IT skill sets? For researchers, what issues are high priorities on research agendas? For educators, do changes that occurred to the IT portfolio skill set affect what students need to learn and integrate. Towards these ends, the paper summarizes the recent trends, managerial issues and research issues related to the management of IT skills. It also gives some research directions for future agendas and endeavors.
