On characterizing nonlocality and anisotropy in magnetorotational instability
The extent to which angular momentum transport in accretion discs is primarily local or nonlocal and what determines this is an important avenue of study for understanding accretion engines. This helps to quantify the intuitive importance of global simulations for addressing the question of locality of transport, for which similar analyses can be performed.
