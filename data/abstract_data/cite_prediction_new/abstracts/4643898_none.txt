To confirm this supposition, we studied the bactericidal properties of complex XVII having the highest activity of the synthesized compounds (the MIC and MBC vary from 1.25 to 75 ~g/ml). The experiment showed that the microorganisms used as test cultures have varied sensitivity towards XVII. The most pronounced activity was shown by this complex with respect to B. anthracis: even after I h, in all concentrations XVII showed an absolute bactericidal effect. With respect to Escherichia coli, Staphylococcus~ and Proteus, compound XVII has a bactericidal effect after 1 h in a concentration of 500 ~g and above.
