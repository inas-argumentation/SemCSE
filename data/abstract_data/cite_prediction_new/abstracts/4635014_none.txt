Effect of Mold Oscillation on Powder Consumption and Hook Formation in Ultra Low Carbon Steel Slabs
Mold oscillation in continuous casting affects several important phenomena, including mold powder lubrication and cast slab surface quality. Recently, attention has focused on improving mold powders and optimizing mold oscillation and other casting conditions for high speed casting, in order to minimize break-out occurrence , and to minimize surface quality problems. One important quality problem is the formation of deep oscillation marks and subsurface hooks, especially in ultra-low carbon steels. These can cause defects such as slivers and pencil-pipe blisters in the final rolled sheet product , even after expensive surface grinding to remove them.
