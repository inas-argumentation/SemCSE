Gel permeation chromatography of maltooligosaccharides at different temperatures.
The separation of maltodextrins (glucose to maltoheptaose) on polyacrylamide gel has been studied at various temperatures. A negative temperature dependence of elution volume was established so that the distribution coefficients decreased with increasing temperature. This peculiarity gives rise to a negative enthalpy for the interacting effects between solute and the gel matrix.
