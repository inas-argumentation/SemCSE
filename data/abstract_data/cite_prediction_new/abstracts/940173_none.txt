Exponentiating the hypergeometric series 0FL(1, 1, . . . , 1; z), L = 0, 1, 2, . . . , furnishes a recursion relation for the members of certain integer sequences bL(n), n = 0, 1, 2, . . .. For L > 0, the bL(n)’s are generalizations of the conventional Bell numbers, b0(n). The corresponding associated Stirling numbers of the second kind are also investigated. For L = 1 one can give a combinatorial interpretation of the numbers b1(n) and of some Stirling numbers associated with them. We also consider the L ≥ 1 analogues of Bell numbers for restricted partitions. The conventional Bell numbers [1] b0(n), n = 0, 1, 2, . . ., have a well-known exponential generating function B0(z) ≡ e(e z − 1) = ∞ ∑ n=0 b0(n) z n! , (1) which can be derived by interpreting b0(n) as the number of partitions of a set of n distinct elements.
