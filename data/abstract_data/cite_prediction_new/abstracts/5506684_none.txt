Rapid Reversal of Human Intestinal Ischemia-Reperfusion Induced Damage by Shedding of Injured Enterocytes and Reepithelialisation
Intestinal ischemia reperfusion (I‐IR) has primarily been studied in animal models whereas human data on I‐IR are scarce. We developed a harmless human experimental model aiming to unravel the sequelae of human small I‐IR.
