Design and Analysis of DNG Metamaterial Based Microstrip Patch Antenna with Tetra-Band Resonance
In this paper, design of a rectangular microstrip patch antenna loaded with DNG metamaterial has been proposed. This antenna provides tetra-band resonance all falling between 4 GHz and 9 GHz. The DNG metamaterial property has been achieved using rectangular SRRs (Split Ring Resonators) and a wire strip. The performance of the proposed antenna was analyzed by simulation results. In order to demonstrate the impact of presence of DNG metamaterial in the proposed antenna, another simple rectangular patch antenna was designed by removing the DNG (Double negative) metamaterial from the proposed antenna structure but keeping the dimension and materials of other elements same. Simulations were run to analyze the performance of this new antenna as well. And this new antenna provides dual-band resonance instead of tetra-band. Comparison between the new rectangular microstrip patch antenna and the proposed antenna demonstrates the impact of loading metamaterial in antenna structure. It has been successfully illustrated here that the use of metamaterial makes antenna a better resonator.
