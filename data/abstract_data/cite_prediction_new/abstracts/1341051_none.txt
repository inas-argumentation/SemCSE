Investigation of O3 Adsorption on Ultra-Thin ALD SnO2 by QCM
This paper investigates the properties of room temperature adsorption of O<sub>3</sub> on ultra-thin ALD SnO<sub>2</sub>. Adsorption is characterized by gravimetric measurements on QCM and frequency shift is converted to mass calculations. Both Langmuir and Freundlich isotherm parameters are calculated for O<sub>3</sub> on Au, as-deposited SnO<sub>2</sub>, and annealed SnO<sub>2</sub>. Of the samples, annealed SnO<sub>2</sub> shows the greatest mass shift and calculated number of adsorption sites. The surface resistivity is estimated to transduce the adsorbed quantity into conductometric respsonse; <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\mathrm {\Delta }{R}_{s}=4.037e5\,\,\mathrm {\Omega /sq}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> for an O<sub>3</sub> saturated as-deposited ALD SnO<sub>2</sub> surface and <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\mathrm {\Delta }{R}_{s}=4.859e3\,\,\mathrm {\Omega /sq}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> for the annealed ALD SnO<sub>2</sub>.
