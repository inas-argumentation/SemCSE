Conventional Nuclear Theory of Low-energy Nuclear Reactions in Metals: Alternative Approach to Clean Fusion Energy Generation
Low-energy nuclear reactions (LENRs) in metals are described using conventional nuclear theory based on the optical theorem formulation. It can be applied to both deuteron and proton induced LENRs. Cryogenic ignition of deuteron fusion in metal particles is proposed as an alternative approach to clean fusion energy generation. © 2014 ISCMNS. All rights reserved. ISSN 2227-3123
