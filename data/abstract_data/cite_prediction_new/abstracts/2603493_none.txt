Qualitative characteristics of alkaloids of species of the genus Vinca
SummaryThe qualitative characterization of 38 indole alkaloids has been performed by chromatography on kieselguhr G and Silufol with the reagents cerium ammonium sulfate and ferric chloride.The possibility of a preliminary determination of the chromophoric group (α-methyleneindole, indoline, indole, and hydroxyindole) of alkaloids by means of the reagents cerium ammonium sulfate and ferric chloride has been shown.
