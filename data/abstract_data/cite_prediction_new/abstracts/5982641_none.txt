Efficient Interface Prediction- Correction Method For The Solution of Multi Dimensional Parabolic Problem Over Non Overlapping Subdomains
In this article we present an e-cient time lagging interface prediction method with implicit cor- rection (TLIC) for the solution of multi dimensional parabolic problem deflned over non overlapping sub- domains. The time lagging method have been imple- mented with difierent integral multiples spatial spac- ing displacements from the interface line(s). We con- sidered the additive splitting up method with respect to the spatial variables to solve the multi dimensional parabolic problem over each subdomains. The pro- posed method have been implemented to solve two dimensional model problem over multi non overlap- ping subdomains with respected to each spatial vari- able.
