A Preliminary Discussion on the Application of Big Data in Urban Residents Travel Guidance
This paper discusses the application of big data in the residents travel guidance system. The system is based on traffic service information platform and tries to make the information collection and analysis system suitable for more types of data through applying the big data with characteristics of volume, variety, velocity. The application of big data processing technology in this system will form a complete transportation guidance system with the big data, geographic information system and the cloud storage. The big data processing technology will be used to simplify the program of residents travel guidance and raise the quality of urban traffic information.
