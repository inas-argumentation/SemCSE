Running on rough terrains : Energetics and stability
How do animals run stably on uneven terrain? Multiple strategies to improve stability, such as increasing rate of swing leg retraction and increasing step width have been proposed as well as shown to correlate with experimental data [1– 3]. However, these incur a substantial additional energetic cost [4–6]. Recent experiments [7, 8] have measured energy consumption for uneven terrain walking and running and found that the increase in energy expenditure (compared to flat ground) for walking is 28% (0.73 W/kg), while for running it is 5% (0.68 W/kg).
