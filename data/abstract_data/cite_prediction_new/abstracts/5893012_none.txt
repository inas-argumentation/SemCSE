Paediatric amendment to adult BSH Guidelines for aplastic anaemia
The guideline group was selected to be representative of UK-based paediatric aplastic anaemia (AA) medical experts. Recommendations are based on review of the literature using MEDLINE and PUBMED up to June 2017. The Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation (GRADE) nomenclature (http:// www.gradeworkinggroup.org) was used to evaluate levels of evidence and to assess the strength of recommendations. The objective of this guideline is to provide healthcare professionals with clear guidance on the management of children with AA. The guidance may not be appropriate to every patient and in all cases individual patient circumstances may dictate an alternative approach.
