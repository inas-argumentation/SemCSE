One potential answer is the availability of more substitutes, meaning, with few exceptions (some portion of the film industry, for example), what work performed in Los Angeles could be performed elsewhere—not necessarily in San Francisco but some place other than Los Angeles. And in this sense, perhaps there is a simple explanation after all. To paraphrase Massey, Los Angeles’s regional problem is not a problem produced by the region.
