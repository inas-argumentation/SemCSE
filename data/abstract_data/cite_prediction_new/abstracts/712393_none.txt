Resident CAPS on dense-core vesicles docks and primes vesicles for fusion
The exocytosis of dense-core vesicles requires a priming step during which SNARE protein complexes assemble. CAPS, which assembles SNARE complexes and is required for vesicle priming, was imaged during events of single-vesicle fusion by TIRF microscopy. CAPS was found to be a resident on vesicles, with residence required for docking and fusion.
