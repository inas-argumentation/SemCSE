Participative Personal Projects - a Chance for Meaningful and Fulfilling Occupational Life? Work Related Personal Projects Analysis
Although research on employee participation is of interest to many managers, psychologists, sociologists and organizational scientists, studies on psychological aspects of participation are not satisfactory. This paper presents an alternative approach to examining organizational participation by using personal projects perspective which allows to examine psychological aspects of the individual person acting together with others in the organizational context.
