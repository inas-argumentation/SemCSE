Method to control the state of IGBT in voltage source inverter
One of the main tasks in the design of frequency converters is the protection of semiconductor elements. There are various types of passive and active methods of voltage and short-circuit control at the collector-emitter and gate-emitter of IGBT-transistors. Such systems as these provide safe disconnection of power module — this function is implemented on the driver of each IGBT-transistor. But the main problem is precise control of IGBT-transistors opening and closing and lack of protection against incorrect IGBT inclusions in operating modes and control system debugging. The proposed device should implement active protection methods, formulate recommendations for installing snubber capacitors and also correct the switching signals of transistor modules. The system monitors the opening and closing of each IGBT transistor and jumps of voltage during the switching.
