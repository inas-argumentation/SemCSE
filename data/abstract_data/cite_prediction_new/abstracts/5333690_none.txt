Optimization of Al/a-SiC: H optical sensor device by means of thermal annealing
The optimization of optoelectronic properties of Al/a-SiC:H Schottky diodes grown as Al/a-SiC:H/c-Si(n) structures is studied by means of thermal annealing of a-SiC:H thin films. According to the spectral response of the Schottky diodes the measured quantum efficiency, @h"m"e"a"s"u"r"e"d, increases with increasing annealing temperature (400-600^oC), whereas @h"m"e"a"s"u"r"e"d decreases for T"a>600^oC. For T"a=600^oC, optimum material quality of a-SiC:H films is achieved and the spectral response of the Al/a-SiC:H/c-S(n) structures present very high and almost constant values (@h"m"e"a"s"u"r"e"d>=80%) for the whole range of wavelengths from 500 up to 850nm. These results show that our Al/a-SiC:H/c-S(n) structures can be very attractive as optical sensors. Diffusion length calculations as well as the mobility by lifetime product (@[email protected])"p of the minority carriers (holes) of a-SiC:H films present a dependence on T"a similar to that of the measured quantum efficiency. Finally, the quantum efficiency of films processed with T"a=675^oC is found to increase when the Al/a-SiC:H/c-S(n) structures are exposed to hydrogen, a result that could be promising for the construction of a hydrogen detection sensor.
