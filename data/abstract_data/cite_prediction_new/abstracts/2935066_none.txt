Weight Averaging for Neural Networksand Local Resampling
Recently methods for combining estimators have been popular and enjoyed considerable success. Typically, one obtains several models by considering diierent model architectures (e.g. linear or nonlinear) or uses diierent partitions of the data (overlapping or non-overlapping) for estimating the model. In a nal step the outputs of each model are combined, either by assigning xed weights a priori (e.g. equal weight-ing) or be estimating these weights from data as well. Here we propose a strategy similar to Breiman's (1994) \bagging": we use a resampling scheme (nonlinear Cross-Validation in this case but the idea is applicable to other local resampling schemes (bootstrap, jack-knife)) to estimate the expected out-of-sample error as a model selection criterion in the context of neural network models. We want to make further use of the models estimated via resampling by combining them and propose that under certain conditions one can average model parameters directly instead of retaining all networks and combine their outputs. We demonstrate that for the examples considered the network performance is comparable to performance obtained by averaging outputs while ooering improved recall speed and savings in storage requirements.
