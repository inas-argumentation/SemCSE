Colorimetric Determination of Procaine hydrochloride in Pharmaceutical Preparations using Diazotization Coupling Reactions
A simple and direct spectrophotometric method for quantitative estimation of procaine hydrochloride in pure forms and injections was adopted. The established method is based on coupling reaction between diazotized procaine hydrochloride with 7-iodo-8-hydroxyquinoline 5- sulphonic acid in alkaline medium to form an intense orange, water-soluble dye that is very stable and has a maximum absorption at 500 nm. Regression analysis of Beer's law plot showed a good correlation in the concentration range of 1-14 μg ml -1 with a molar absorbtivity of1.429 ×10 4 L mol -1 cm -1 , Sandell's sensitivity of 19.085 ×10 -3 µg ml -1 . The optimum conditions for full color development are described and the proposed method was probably applied satisfactorily to pharmaceutical injections.
