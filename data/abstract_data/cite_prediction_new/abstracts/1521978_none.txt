Daidalos-A scenario based approach from scenarios towards integration
The Daidalos results – architectural framework, conceptual solutions, software components and subsystems , and the overall demonstrators based on two scenarios – will contribute to the next generation of networks. Having this goal, an appropriate methodology on how to guide the overall development process is required, which relies on formal methods and tools which closely follows a scenario based overall design defining a " red line " along the overall work of any activity from the formal definition of a scenario down to the final integration.
