Subclasses of Goldie-Steutel-Bondesson class of innitely divisible distributions on R d by -mapping
Bondesson (1981) studied the class of generalized convolutions of mix- tures of exponential distributions on R+ = (0;1), which is the smallest class that contains all mixtures of exponential distributions and that is closed under convo- lution and weak convergence on R+. Barndor-Nielsen, Maejima and Sato (2006) extended this class to R d , which they call Goldie-Steutel-Bondesson class B(R d ) for a historical reason. This class is characterized by the so-called -mapping of in- nitely divisible distributions in terms of stochastic integrals of L evy processes. In this paper, we introduce nested subclasses of B(R d ) by the iteration of -mapping, and characterize them in terms of stochastic integrals of L evy processes as well as L evy measures.
