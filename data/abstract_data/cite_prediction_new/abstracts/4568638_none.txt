Mucocele of the sphenoid sinus presenting as an intrasellar mass. Case report.
I T IS well recognized that diverse pathological processes in the vicinity of the sphenoid bone can produce radiographic evidence of sellar enlargement. The best known lesion is the chromophobe adenoma2 Other entities are parasellar meningiomas, 4 carotid artery aneurysms, TM the "empty sella, ''6 craniopharyngiomas, 1 gliomas of the optic chiasm and nervesf enlargement of the anterior portion of the third ventricle secondary to hydrocephalusf and other uncommon lesions. 3 Less well recognized as a cause of enlargement of the sella turcica is a mucocele of the sphenoid sinus. This is a report of a mucocele which produced a ballooning and erosion of the sella similar to that observed in patients with chromophobe adenoma. The rarity of this lesion prompted this communication.
