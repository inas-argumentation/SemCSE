Ultrasound, Computed Tomography and Gallium 67 Scintigraphy
Non-invasive diagnostic imaging has been greatly enhanced by the recent advances in the technology of ultrasound (vs), computed tomography (CT), and Gallium 67 scintigraphy (GA67).Because of the rapid development and acceptance of these new procedures, it is imperative for the diagnostician to understand their capabilities. Concurrently, appropriate concern should be focused toward their efficacy and cost-effectiveness. In this way, a rational clinical approach for ultrasound, CT, and gallium scanning will be developed. The views presented in the following discussion will be based partly on literature review and partly on my own clinical experience. The initial portion of this review will briefly discuss the technical considerations of each of the diagnostic modalities. Following a brief description of their clinical applications, the advantages and disadvantages of each of the modalities will be evaluated.
