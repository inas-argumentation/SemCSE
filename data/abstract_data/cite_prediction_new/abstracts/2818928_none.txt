BVDSS (drain to source breakdown voltage) instability in shielded gate trench power MOSFETs
In this paper, we develop a constant current avalanche injection stress method for evaluating BV DSS instability in shielded gate trench MOSFETs, a low/medium voltage power transistor technology used in a broad range of applications. We show this test is very sensitive to the BVDSS instability in charge balance devices whereas traditional HTRB (high temperature reverse bias) reliability test cannot capture the BVDSS instability issue. The new stress methodology allows us to study the root cause for BVDSS instability, suggesting ways for stabilizing the breakdown voltage for this important class of power transistors.
