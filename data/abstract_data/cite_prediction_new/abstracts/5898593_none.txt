Automorphisms of Normalizers of Maximal Tori and First Cohomology of Weyl Groups
Let T be a maximal torus in a connected compact Lie group G, and let W be the corresponding Weyl group with its natural action on T as a reflection group. The cohomology group H 1 (W;T) is computed for all simple Lie groups, and the general case is studied. The method is based on a suitable interpretation of H 1 (W;T) as a group of (outer) automorphisms of the normalizer of T.
