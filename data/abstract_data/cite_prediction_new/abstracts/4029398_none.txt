Secular Evolution of Barred Galaxies with Massive Central Black Holes
The influence of central black holes on the dynamical evolution of bars in disk galaxies was examined. Once a bar formed by a dynamical instability in an infinitesimally thin stellar disk was fully developed, a black hole (BH) was adiabatically added at the center of the disk.
