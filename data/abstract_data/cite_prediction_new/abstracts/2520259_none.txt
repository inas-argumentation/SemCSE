Removal of fine-grained and thin filter cakes by a counter-current backwash treatment
In industrial solid/liquid separation, the removal of fine particles (≤ 10 μm) in a suspension with a low solids content (0.01-1 v/v %) is a challenging and demanding task. This application takes place in a wide array of industrial production such as metal processing and treatment industries. In order to achieve the required purity, different types of filters like candle and leaf filter can be used.
