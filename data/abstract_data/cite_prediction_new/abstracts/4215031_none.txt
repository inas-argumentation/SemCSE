Applying a Nested Petri Net Modeling Paradigm to Coordination of Sensor Networks with Mobile Agents
In this paper, we provide a formal definition of a framework of two layered nested predicate transition nets. We address the various technical issues in such a definition. We show the applicability of our approach in addressing different cooperation behaviors of mobile agents in modeling a two-tiered network consisting of a top-level mobile-device network and a lower level network of sensor nodes, and demonstrate the usefulness of formal modeling in revealing hidden and missing requirements.
