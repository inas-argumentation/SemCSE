Doing Time: the impact of prison work experience on learning and teaching
This paper discusses an innovative approach to teaching and learning, under which students on the BSc (Hons) Criminology programme (and related programmes), are offered work experience positions in prisons within the North East of England. It reviews the background to setting up the work experience scheme in one of the prisons, considers the initial impact on the first cohort of students taking part, and discusses the staff and student work experiences to date. It is co-authored by three students who worked within HMP Durham and the member of staff responsible for setting up the work experience initiative.
