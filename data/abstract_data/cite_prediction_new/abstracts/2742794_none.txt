Deposition of Radioactivity in North-West England from the Accident at Windscale
Measurement were made of the deposition of radioactivity in north-west England following the accident at Windscale No. 1 Atomic Pile on October 10, 1957. The influence of meteorological conditions is discussed. Data are tabulated on the concentration of various nuclides relative to iodine-131 found in filrwe paper and grass samples. Data on radiation dosage are included. (C.H.)
