GeoProfile 1 Running head: GEOPROFILE: A NEW GEOGRAPHIC PROFILING SYSTEM GEOPROFILE: DEVELOPING AND ESTABLISHING THE RELIABILITY OF A NEW GEOGRAPHIC PROFILING SOFTWARE SYSTEM
The current study documents the development of GeoProfile, a new geographic profiling software system, and tests its reliability against CrimeStat and Dragnet. A set of 55 serial offenders from Baltimore County, MD, were analyzed with GeoProfile, CrimeStat, and Dragnet using search cost and error distance as measures of accuracy. GeoProfile was found to be noninferior to CrimeStat and Dragnet when measured by search cost and non-inferior to Dragnet when measured by error distance. The hypothesis that GeoProfile is as accurate as CrimeStat and Dragnet was supported by the finding that GeoProfile was non-inferior to at least one program in each category of accuracy measures.
