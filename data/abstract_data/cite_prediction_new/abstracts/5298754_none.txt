SETH: A VLSI Chip for the Real-Time Information Dispersal and Retrieval for Security and Fault-Tolerance
In this paper, SETH,(a) a hardwired implementation of the recently proposed \Information Dispersal Algorithm" (IDA) [7], is presented. SETH allows the real-time dispersal of information into pieces as well as the retrieval of the original information from the minimal intact subset of these pieces. We begin the paper by introducing IDA and overviewing SETH operation. Next, the di erent algorithms used are described and schematics of varying levels of details are presented. Next, we present an implementation of SETH [1]-[2] using Scalable CMOS technology that has been fabricated using a MOSIS 3-micon process. We conclude the paper with potential applications and extensions.
