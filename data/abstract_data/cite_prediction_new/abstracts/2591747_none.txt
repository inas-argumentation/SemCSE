The Socio-pragmatics of Nigerian English in Nollywood
The introduction of English language into Nigeria has precipitated a variation in the forms of English spoken within the country in relation with contact with the various indigenous languages (Adegbija, 2004). Also, new forms of English in the country have developed in accordance with influences of different socio-cultural contexts on the use of the forms. However, previous works on the subject of Nigerian English have not given much attention to its use in the Nollywood linguistic context, which is another interesting domain of language use. It is believed that an analysis of the use of Nigerian English, particularly in the Nollywood domain, can provide further insights into the linguistic peculiarities of Nigerian English in other contexts. This study investigated the use of Nigerian English in the Nollywood context with a view to discussing the socio-pragmatics of the variety. First, this study identified some peculiar linguistic features and uncovered the underlying socio-pragmatic variables that would inform their use. This study argues that the socio-pragmatic contexts of the Nigerian multilingual setting are significant predictors of the nature of English usage in Nollywood.
