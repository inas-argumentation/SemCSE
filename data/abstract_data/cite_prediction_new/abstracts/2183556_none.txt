Most early cases of the disease are fatal, with the diagnosis being made after postmortem examinations.1–3 In recent years, ultrasonography and computed tomography (CT), which can detect easily an abscess in the liver, have improved the prognosis,4 however, it remains a lifethreatening disease in newborn infants. Methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) is one of the pathogens causing severe nosocomial infection in neonatal intensive care units. To our knowledge, however, there has been no report on a solitary MRSA liver abscess in a low birth weight infant. In the present study we describe a very low birth weight infant treated successfully for a solitary liver abscess caused by MRSA.
