Improving outcomes in totally anomalous systemic venous connection
Sir, We congratulate the authors for their elegant description and case-based illustration of totally anomalous systemic venous connection (TASVC).[1] We would like to present a few points that the authors could clarify. The concept of connection and the physiology of drainage are well illustrated. We would like to extend the concept of venoatrial concordance here, in that the systemic venous drainage in left isomerism is always discordant venoatrial connection, but can be physiologically concordant by its drainage into pulmonary arteries from the right side. Then, the systemic veins draining into left-sided atrium have both physiological and anatomical discordance and major surgical implications.
