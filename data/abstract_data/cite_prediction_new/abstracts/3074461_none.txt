Mathematical Modelling and Description of the Technological Process of Aluminum Anodic Oxidation by Using the Neural Networks
—In this paper the usage of neural networks and Design of Experiments methodology in order to control and optimize the technological process of aluminium anodic oxidation is presented. The influence of the input factors on the resulting AAO (anodic aluminum oxide) film thickness was monitored at defined current density of 4.00 A·dm-2 , 5.00 A·dm-2 and 6.00 A·dm-2 .The thickness of the formed AAO layer has been investigated as the relationship of physical and chemical factors acting during the anodic oxide process. A higher-order neural unit based on the iterative Levenber-Maquardt algorithm was used to evaluate experimentally obtained data in order to predict the thickness of the resulting AAO layer and determine the optimum selection of process conditions.
