Gas phase study of the reactivity of optical coating materials with hydrocarbons using a compact EUV laser
We have conducted single photon ionization mass spectroscopy studies of the chemical reactivity of Si<sub>m</sub>/Ti<sub>m</sub>/Hf<sub>m</sub>/Zr<sub>m</sub>/Ru<sub>m</sub>O<sub>n</sub>, metal oxide nanoclusters. The results are relevant to the carbon contamination of capping layers in extreme ultraviolet (EUV) reflective coatings.
