Active Source Estimation for Improved Source Separation
The goal in blind source separation (BSS) is to determine the original source signals, given mixtures of these sources. Recent work in blind source separation (the DUET and DASSS methods) allows source reconstruction from mixtures when the time-frequency components of the sources rarely overlap in the mixtures. While speech mixtures often approximate this constraint, music mixtures rarely do, requiring new approaches. We introduce a method to assign energy to source estimates from music mixtures that builds on partial signal estimates provided by the existing DUET and DASSS algorithms. We estimate harmonic masks from the initial source estimates and assign energy from the mixed signal to sources by estimating the number of active sources in each timefrequency frame and allowing source estimates to share mixture energy from a single frame. This allows dealing with mixtures that contain time-frequency frames in which multiple harmonic sources are active without requiring knowledge of source characteristics.
