Pathological changes produced by coronary embolization with metallic mercury in the dog.
Pathological studies of myocardium at intervals up to one year after embolization of the left circumflex coronary artery in dogs have shown that the changes are those of a granulomatous myocarditis and arteritis rather than infarction, and suggest that this technique does not provide a suitable model for the study of acute infarction or chronic ischaemic heart disease.
