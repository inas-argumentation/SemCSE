Digital Watermarking Applications and Techniques: A Brief Review
The frequent availability of digital data such as audio, images and videos became possible to the public through the expansion of the internet. Digital watermarking technology is being adopted to ensure and facilitate data authentication, security and copyright protection of digital media. It is considered as the most important technology in today's world, to prevent illegal copying of data. Digital watermarking can be applied to audio, video, text or images. This paper includes the detail study of watermarking definition and various watermarking applications and techniques used to enhance data security.
