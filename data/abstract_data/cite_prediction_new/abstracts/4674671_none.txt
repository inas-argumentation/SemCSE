A REMARK ON SETS IN INFINITE DIMENSIONAL SPACES WITH FULL OR ZERO CAPACITY
We give a simple proof that for classical Dirichlet forms on infinite dimensional linear state spaces the intrinsic closure of a set of full measure has full capacity. Furthermore, we show that the C1,q-capacity of a set, enlarged by adding the linear span of a basis in the generalized CameronMartin space remains zero if it was zero for slightly bigger capacities a priori.
