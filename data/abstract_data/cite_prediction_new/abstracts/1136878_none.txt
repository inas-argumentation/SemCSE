Induction of immune tolerance in hemophiliacs with inhibitors.
To the Editor. —We read with interest the article by Ewing et al 1 about induction of immune tolerance to factor VIII in hemophiliacs with inhibitors by giving daily doses of factor VIII concentrates (50 U/kg) lower than those originally used in the "Bonn protocol" (200 U/mL). 2 Considering their results, we feel even more reluctant than before to use this type of therapeutic approach. In fact, we agree with Ewing et al that the seroconversion of one patient who developed antibody to human immunodeficiency virus when infused only with "safe" heat-treated concentrates is alarming. Moreover, eight of the 12 patients studied had marked decreases in circulating T4 cell counts during the treatment, to which the overload of allogenic proteins might have contributed.
