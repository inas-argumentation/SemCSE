Coexistence of External and Internal Ca‐Binding Sites of the Sarcoplasmic Reticulum Ca‐Pump
Kinetic models, as first formalized by de Meis and Vianna,’ describe the mechanism of Ca2+ transport by the sarcoplasmic reticulum (SR) Ca-pump in terms of two major states of the enzyme (El and Ez). According to these schemes, the Ca-binding sites are either exposed to the cytoplasm and have high affinity in the El state, or they face the lumen of SR and have low affinity in Ez. Ca2+ transport against the gradient is achieved by an EI-E2 conversion, which is fueled by a parallel decrease in the chemical potential of the phospho-enzyme (El P vs E2-P). We recently showed2 that during the rapid phase of Ca2+ uptake, Ca2+ is simultaneously bound to and occluded3 within the enzyme, resulting in a supernumerary Ca/EP stoichiometry. Here we report findings that indicate that the binding of Ca2+ to cytoplasmic and luminal sites can also occur simultaneously, suggesting that the interconversion of Ca sites, as proposed by the EI-E2 model, might not occur during Ca2+ transport.
