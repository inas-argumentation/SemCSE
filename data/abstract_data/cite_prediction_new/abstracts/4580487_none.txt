Antemortem diagnosis of metastatic sarcoma to the heart: a case report.
The case of a 57-year-old patient with suspected primary sarcoma of the pelvis and antemortem diagnosis of cardiac metastasis is presented. Postmortem examination revealed a fibrosarcoma of the pelvis with cardiac involvement. One of the metastatic nodules was found encompassing and constricting the descending branch of the left coronary artery, producing electrocardiographic evidence of myocardial infarction. A review of the clinical manifestations and pathology of metastases to the heart is also presented.
