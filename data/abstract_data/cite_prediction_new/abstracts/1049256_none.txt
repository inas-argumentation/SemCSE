The authors noticed an error in Appendix Fig S1 as follows: In the original publication, we show the same panel for SG-Leu medium at both 30°C and 37°C, although this panel represents only 30°C, and another panel should have been shown for 37°C. In the corrected Appendix Fig S1, we fixed this error and present the correct panel for 37°C. This mistake does not alter the scientific conclusion from this figure, namely that the over-expression of Mcp3 does not influence the growth of yeast cells under these conditions.
