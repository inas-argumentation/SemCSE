Losing Neverland: creating a better world to age in.
The long-term care (LTC) system is broken. Even those with extensive training and knowledge cannot get it to do what it should. Policy makers concentrate on the cost of care without stopping to ask whether what we are already spending is buying anything like care we want. It is time to focus on the infrastructure of LTC and to ask how we can create a system of care that comes closer to what we would want for ourselves and our parents. Almost everyone agrees that the LTC system we have today is not what anyone would have designed if they were starting from scratch. Instead it has grown in fits and starts shaped by extreme reactions to bad events and a need to make hasty decisions. Although we will never get a clean slate, perhaps it is still worthwhile to consider what we might wish for, at least to provide some basic building blocks.
