Rapid Drop in Serum Iron Concentration Associated with Stress
In order to further identify the stressful conditions associated with a sudden, pronounced drop in serum iron concentration, a concomitant rise in white blood cell count was utilized to select the charts of 47 patients for review. These patients were grouped into the four categories of surgical, infectious, neurologic, and obstetric events. The rapid drop in serum iron concentration appeared to be a non-specific response to a variety of stressful situations, and possible mechanisms are discussed.
