The development of a prototype computerised management information system for a mixed enterprise farm
The design and implementation of a prototype computerised management information system for a large mixed enterprise farm is described. The prototype was developed for the 600-ha farm run by University College Dublin comprising dairy, beef, sheep, pigs and arable crop production. Each enterprise performs both as a commercial unit and a research and educational facility, and is managed by academic staff. A farm manager has operational control of the whole farm, and each enterprise is serviced centrally from a farm office. This paper outlines the major management information requirements on the farm and the information system developed to satisfy them. Specific methods for unifying common information processes and structures for recording and analysing research-oriented data are detailed. The system was coded in COBOL and implemented on a minicomputer on an experimental basis. An alternative approach using a network of microcomputers was explored by taking the information subsystem of one enterprise and using a microcomputer-based database software package, dBaseIII+, to implement the module design. It was concluded that for a mixed enterprise farm the best approach is to use microcomputer-based information systems for each enterprise, integrated via a network into an overall management system. The requirements of such a system and the potential for upgrading with other hardware and software are discussed.
