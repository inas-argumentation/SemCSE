Consider a sample space X and a σ-field B of subsets of X . The Dirichlet process (Ferguson, 1973, 1974) is a random probability measure Q on (X ,B), which therefore, equivalently, defines a random distribution function G on X . It is characterized by two parameters, Q0 a specified probability measure on (X ,B) (equivalently, G0 a specified distribution function on X ) and α a positive scalar parameter. The stochastic process Q = {Q(ω,B) : ω ∈ Ω, B ∈ B}, with sample paths Qω ≡ Q(ω,B), B ∈ B, that are probability measures on (X ,B), is a Dirichlet process if, for any finite measurable partition B1,...,Bk of X , the distribution of the random vector (Q(B1),...,Q(Bk)) is Dirichlet(αQ0(B1),..., αQ0(Bk)), where Q(Bi) (a random variable) and Q0(Bi) (a constant) denote the probability of set Bi under Q and Q0, respectively.
