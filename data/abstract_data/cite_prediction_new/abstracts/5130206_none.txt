Currently, archaeologists visually analyze ancient Chinese bronze mirrors for their shape di ffer nce. The practice requires a huge amount of time and e ffort. In this paper, we propose the automatic method for detecting the shape difference between a pair of ancient mirrors. The 3D data of the mirrors are obtained by the laser range scanner. Our proposed algorithm provides fast analysis for shape di fference, as well as the further analysis without damaging the actual mirror.
