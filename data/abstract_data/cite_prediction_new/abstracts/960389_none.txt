Tonoplast inorganic pyrophosphatase in Vicia faba guard cells
. Lysed guard-cell protoplasts of Vicia faba L. exhibited hydrolytic activity characteristic of tonoplast inorganic pyrophosphatase (V-PPase; EC 3.6.1.1). Activity was inhibited by the specific V-PPase inhibitor aminomethylenediphosphonate, stimulated by K+ (Km= 51 mM) and inhibited by Ca2+ (80 nM free Ca2+ was required for 50% inhibition at 0.27 mM free Mg2+). Patch-clamp measurements of electrogenic activity confirmed enzyme localisation at the tonoplast. This is the first report of V-PPase activity in guard cells; its possible involvement in stomatal opening is discussed.
