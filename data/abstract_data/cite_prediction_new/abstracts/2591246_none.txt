Discussion on: "The Reliable Stabilization Problem for Discrete Time-Varying Linear Systems"
The paper by Tianqiu Yu is an interesting contribution to the important area of control design for the stabilization of infinite-dimensional discrete-time linear timevarying system using a multi-controller configuration. There are several very interesting aspects in this paper and a number of these aspects can be considered for further investigations. The potential impact of the achievements in this paper is significant. The objective of this discussion is to help the readers know the framework of the paper better and provide some issues for further study.
