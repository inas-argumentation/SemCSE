Case 6 of hadwiger's conjecture. III. The problem of 7-vertices
According to a well-known conjecture of Hadwiger, every k-chromatic graph can be contracted to a complete graph of k vertices. In order to prove it, G.A. Dirac defined and studied k-chromatic contraction-critical graphs (that we designate shortly as k-c.c. graphs). In the present article, concerning 6-c.c. graphs, we investigate the neighbourhoodsubgraph of the 7- vertices, which is the subgraph induced by the neighbour vertices of a vertex of degree 7; they present 124 different types. In a further study, the 82 types of one of the lists will be reduced, leaving 42 possible types. (We call a subgraph of ? reducible when its presence in ? prevents the graph to be 6-c.c.; either ? is contractible to K6, or it is 5-colourable when a partial 5-colouring of its vertices is given.)
