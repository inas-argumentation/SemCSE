COMPOSITION OF THE URINE OF THE FASTING HUMPBACK WHALE (MEGAPTERA NODOSA).
1. 1. The urine concentration in the humpback whales fasting for at least 3 months indicates that seawater is being swallowed but no net gain of water results. 2. 2. The most concentrated urine gave a freezing point depression of 2·79°C which is below the maximum reached by many land animals 3. 3. Metabolic water derived from tissue catabolism would appear to be sufficient for the whale's needs and this is derived from a far greater catabolism of fat relative to protein (29 : 1) than seen in man (2·5 : 1).
