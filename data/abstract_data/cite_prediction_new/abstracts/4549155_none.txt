Distribution Utility survey on electricity prepaid metering
This paper reports the result of a survey participated in by 43 electric Distribution Utilities (DU) in the Philippines regarding their perceptions on Prepaid Retail Electricity Service (PRES). Participants indicated that perceived major advantages of this technology (to their organization) include: simplified meter reading, billing, and collection and improved cash flow. On the other hand, challenges to offering PRES include: integration of this service to existing postpaid system in terms of infrastructure, service and tariff-setting. To address some of the issues, an effective regulation balancing the concerns of the DU with the rights of the consumers will invigorate more DU to offer this service. Among the participants, three DUs are pursuing wide-scale PRES to their customers; others are in various stages of preparation.
