Exact boundary controllability of a Schrödinger equation with an internal point mass
We consider a linear system composed of two Schrödinger equations of equal length connected by a point mass interface condition. We show that the system is exactly controllable with either a Dirichlet or Neumann boundary control at one end. The novelty of this paper concerns the nature of the space of exact controllability. We find that in the case of Dirichlet control, the space of exact controllability is symmetric in terms of regularity with respect to the point mass. This is in contrast to the case of Neumann control where the exact controllability space is asymmetric with respect to the point mass in the sense that the regularity is one degree higher on the side of the point mass opposite the control.
