The immunopathology of pemphigus and bullous pemphigoid.
This review article was prepared for the Journal of Investigative Dermatology by invitation. It is the first of a series of such articles which will appear in this journal in the months to come. Doctor Richard Winkelmann has taken on the responsibilities of obtaining and organizing these reviews. The Society for Investigative Dermatology is deeply grateful for his efforts and is particularly indebted to Doctors Beutner, Jordon and Chorzelski for their excellent opening contribution to the series.
