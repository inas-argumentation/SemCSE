Optimization of photovoltaic farm under partial shading effects using artificial intelligent based matrix switch controller
Partial shading on photovoltaic array due to weather conditions reduces the energy generated and worsening the entire photovoltaic farm efficiency. The purpose of this research was to electric physically reconflgurate PV string through switch matrix by control of smart controller. It is great to find current and voltage at TCT point approaching work of range from MPPT, despite being awarded shading effect.
