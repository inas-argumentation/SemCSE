Maternal and lateral transmission of scrapie in sheep.
Scrapie is common in the Suffolk breed of sheep, but has not been reported in Scottish Blackface flocks. Reciprocal crosses (F 1 , F 2 and Bx) between these breeds show that maternal transmission of the infectious agent plays a dominant role and the results also indicate that there is an inherited component in the level of incidence. Scrapie occurred in 28 per cent. of purebred Scottish Blackface sheep in lifetime-contact with the crossbreds, due to lateral transmission under normal field conditions.
