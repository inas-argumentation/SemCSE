A New Subclass of Harmonic Univalent Functions defined by Fractional Calculus Operator 1
In this paper, authors introduce a new fractional calculus operator and study a subclass of harmonic univalent functions. We obtain coefficient conditions, extreme points, distortion bounds, convolution and convex combination for the above class of harmonic univalent functions. Relevant connections of the results presented here with various known results are briefly indicated.
