Idempotents in quotients and restrictions of Banach algebras of functions
We say that a commutative Banach algebra B is generated by its idempotents if the algebra of all linear combinations of idempotents in B is dense in B. For a compact Hausdorff space X, it is easily proved, using the Stone-Weierstrass theorem, that C(X) is generated by its idempotents if and only if X is totally disconnected. In this paper we discuss conditions under which quotient and restriction algebras of certain Banach algebras of functions on the unit circle T are generated by their idempotents.
