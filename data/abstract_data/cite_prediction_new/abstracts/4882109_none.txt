The study of setting up the middle school seismic emergency evacuation time model based on the regression algorithm
According to analyzes the close relationship between the school students earthquake casualties and the evacuation time. This paper explains the establishment of an emergency evacuation time model for the middle schools the need for and details of the emergency evacuation time regression model method and the factors associated with the time model The selection and setting, using SPSS software, the analysis of regression algorithms, the final application the method of object-oriented programming with Visual Studio 2005 C # Software realized the earthquake emergency evacuation time model system and we tested it in a case.
