A Highly Accurate Blind Frequency-Offset Synchronization for OFDM Communications
We address the problem of carrier frequency offset (CFO) synchronization in Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing (OFDM) communications systems in the context of frequency-selective fading channels. We consider the case where the transmitted symbols have constant modulus, i.e., Phase-Shift-Keying (PSK) constellations. A novel blind CFO synchronization algorithm is developed. The new algorithm is shown to greatly outperform a recently published blind technique that exploits the fact that practical OFDM systems are not fully loaded, i.e., the number of modulated sub-carriers is smaller than the size of the FFT block. Further, the proposed algorithm is consistent even when the system is fully loaded. Finally, the proposed CFO estimator is obtained via a one-dimensional search, the same as with the existing VSC-based estimator, but achieves a substantial gains in performance (10 dB SNR or one order of magnitude in CFO MSE).
