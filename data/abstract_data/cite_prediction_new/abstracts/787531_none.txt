Maternal mortality in Panamá city (CHMCSS), 1992–1996
Data for this study were obtained from the C.H.M.C.S.S. hospital, the main hospital of the Republic of Panama where there are approxí mately 5000 annual deliveries. Retrospective data were collected on deaths for the period between 1992 and 1996. Maternal mortality, presented as a ratio, measures the obstetrics risk with a denominator of 100 000 live births and was defined as all deaths of women during pregnancy or within 42 days after the end of pregnancy. Maternal mortality, one of the major causes of death worldwide among women in their reproductive years, is mainly a problem in developing countries; it has declined markedly in the develw x oped world during this century 1 .
