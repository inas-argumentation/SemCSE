Complete Posterior Dislocation of the Talus: A Case Report
Complete posterior dislocation of the talus is a very disabling ankle injury.' ,2,6,8,9,11 ,15 Extreme supination or pronation force accompanied by dorsiflexion of the foot, provide the mechanism which causes complete posterior talar dislocation.1,2,6,8,9,11,15 This injury leads to talar avascular necrosis.1,3,5,7, 10-12,14,16 Only one case report of complete posterior dislocation of the talus without concomitant fracture has been reported." A below-knee amputation 1 year after injury was necessary. The case report detailed herein is a complete talar posterior dislocation treated by immediate reduction, with 1O-year follow-up.
