Growth of mouse plasmacytoma cells in serum-free, hormone-supplemented medium: procedure for the determination of hormone and growth factor requirements for cell growth.
The procedure of analyzing hormone and growth factor requirements for the growth of MPC-11 cells and of developing a serum-free medium for this cell line has been described. In this medium, MPC-11 cells grow as fast as in serum-supplemented medium, up to 50 generation. MPC-11 cells grown in serum-supplemented medium secrete IgG2b and K light chain into the medium as they do in serum-containing medium.
