An Evaluation of Facilitating the Future 2008 Professional Development Workshop for Educators
The purpose of this field study was to administer an evaluation of Facilitating the Future (FTF) 2008 – a professional development workshop for educators. During its 20-year history of existence, no formal evaluation of the workshop had ever been conducted. However, at its 20year mark, in the age of accountability, FTF‘s Leadership Team decided to learn what its participants—K-12 and technical college educators—actually thought of the workshop as a whole, and its learning communities, post-workshop, in addition to determining if FTF meets the needs of its stakeholders through the collection of qualitative (focus groups) and quantitative (surveys) data.
