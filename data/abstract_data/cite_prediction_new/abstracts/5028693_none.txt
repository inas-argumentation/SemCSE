Signaling by a wide variety of cytokines, including interferons, interleukins, and growth factors, involves activation of JAK kinases and Stat (Signal transducers and activators of transcription) proteins. At present, not much is known about the molecular mechanisms by which interleukin-5 (IL-5) exerts its diverse biologic effects. Human eosinophils are one of the most important target cells for IL-5 and were used here to study IL-5 signaling in a primary human cell. IL-5 induced rapid and transient tyrosine phosphorylation of JAKZ.
