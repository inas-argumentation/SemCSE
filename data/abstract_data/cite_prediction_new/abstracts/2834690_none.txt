A study of the conformational states of arginine-containing polypeptides of regular structure
SummaryOn the basis of the results obtained it may be concluded that the arginine-containing polypeptides with a relatively low degree of polymerization that have been synthesized exist in aqueous solution predominantly in the form of a coil, and on the addition of organic solvents they may exist as mixtures of two conformations — coil and helix with a predominance of the helical conformation.
