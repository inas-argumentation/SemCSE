Chemical constituents and yield of Simmondsia chinensis plants as affected by foliar application of gibberellic acid and zinc sulphate
This experiment was conducted to study the response of jojoba (Simmondsia chinensis) to foliar application of gibberellic acid and zinc sulphate on growth, flowering, seed yield and chemical consistent at privet farm in EL-Behira governorate, Egypt during two successive seasons of 2015/2016 and 2016/2017. Jojoba plants were sprayed with all combination treatments of zinc sulphate (0, 25, 50 and 75 ppm) and gibberellic acid (0, 50, 100 and 150 ppm). Results revealed that all treatments enhanced vegetative growth characteristics, along with flowering characteristics (flowering date, flowering percentage), fruiting (initial fruit set and final fruit set), seed yield, oil content of seeds and their consistent and chemical composition of leaves (chlorophyll A, chlorophyll B and total chlorophyll contents as well as N, P and K%) compared to the control in the first and second seasons, respectively. There was a steady increment in all the parameters investigated with the increase in ZnSO4 and/or GA3 levels. Therefore, combination between 75 ppm ZnSO4 plus 150 ppm GA3 is recommended as it achieved the maximum improvement in the vegetative and flowering growth which leaded to increase seed yield and its oil content and so, raise the economic value of the jojoba (Simmondsia chinensis) yield. In addition this treatment was able to improve the fixed oil composition of jojoba seeds.
