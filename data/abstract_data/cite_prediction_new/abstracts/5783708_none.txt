Performance comparison between buck and boost CRM PFC converter
This paper presents a detailed performance comparison (including efficiency, EMC performance and component electrical stress) between boost and buck type PFC under critical conduction mode (CRM). In universal input (90–265Vac) applications, the CRM buck PFC has around 1% higher efficiency compared to its counterpart at low-line (90Vac) condition. Due to the low voltage swing of switch, buck PFC has a better CM EMI performance than boost PFC. It seems that the buck PFC is more attractive in low power applications which only need to meet the IEC61000-3-2 Class D standard based on the comparison. The experimental results from two 100-W prototypes are also presented for side by side comparison.
