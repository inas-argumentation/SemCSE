The dependence of tumor formation on the composition of the calorie-restricted diet as well as on the degree of restriction. 1945.
Progress in medical research is slow and recognition is even slower. The magnitude of the contribution made may take years or decades to validate and, if appropriate, confirm in man. Albert Tannenbaum was a gifted investigator who made significant contributions to our current understanding of the interrelationship between tumor formation and the influences of dietary restriction and dietary fat content. During a 24-y period, from 1940 to 1964, as first author, he wrote 33 articles. As an indicator of “worth,” we examined the number of citations in the current literature. During the 10-y period from 1985 to 1994, a full 45 years after the first article was published, he was cited 364 times! The “classic” article, “The Dependence of Tumor Formation on the Composition of the Calorie-Restricted Diet as Well as on the Degree of Restriction” was published in Cancer Research in 1945. This article alone has been cited 53 times from 1985 to 1994. Certainly, this deserves the title classic and meets the criteria for a significant contribution to current thinking.
