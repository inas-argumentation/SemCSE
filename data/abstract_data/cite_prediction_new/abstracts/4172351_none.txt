Assessments of infant play: applications to research and practice.
From early in the first year of life, an infant's play with objects and with people is a mirror of what the child knows. In addition, play behavior is correlated with language development and is related to the quality of mother-infant attachment. Because play is related to other aspects of development and because it is so readily visible, play assessment provides data nurses can use in practice and in research. This article (a) integrates research describing infant and toddler play, its correlates, and assessment techniques and (b) proposes applications of play assessment for practice and research.
