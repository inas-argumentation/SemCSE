AReal-time congestion Pricing SystemBasedon GPSTechnique
Thispaper mainly concerns ourpreliminary research ontheReal-time congestion pricing system. Theproposed system will enjoy theadvantage ofcombining real-time roadcharging andon-route traffic information service, bywhichto alleviate theincreasingly traffic congestion inlarge cities moreefficiently. Inthefirst part ofthis paper, wepresent the concept oftheGPSbased real-time congestion system, including theframework ofthis system, its basic functions, and workflow. Inthefollowing part, afurther investigation ontheidea ofcharge scheme update andthetoll ratio optimal is described. We employed thebi-level optimal programtomodeling therelationship between transportation administration andthemotorists inroadpricing. Thenamulti-class multi-criterion elasticity demandbi-level road pricing optimal program forcharge schemeupdate isstated. Fortheoptimal modelinvolves bothdiscrete and continuous variables, isaNP-hard problem, weprovide asolution method using genetic algorithm. Finally, themodel andalgorithm aredemonstrated withanumerical example.
