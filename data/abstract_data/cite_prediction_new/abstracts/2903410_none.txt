Third and Fourth Pharyngeal Pouch Syndrome, Associated Vascular Anomalies and Hypocalcemic Seizures
CytoxanO therapy was given for 12 weeks, 25 mg. orally daily. The pulmonary infiltrate cleared completely and the lymph nodes decreased in size. But when another osteolytic lesion, I cm. in diameter, appeared in the occipital region after three months we decided to discontinue this therapy. Vinblastine sulfate intravenously was then begun. The initial dose given was 0.05 mg. per Kg. body weight. Subsequent doses were increased by 0.05 mg. per Kg. body weight at weekly intervals. The patient received a total dose of 2.35 mg. per Kg. body weight over three months. During that time, the lymph nodes completely regressed to normal size and all evidence of the disease disappeared, including the radiologic lesions in the skull.
