Pion and thermal photon spectra as a possible signal for a phase transition.
We calculate thermal photon and neutral pion spectra in ultrarelativistic heavy-ion collisions in the framework of three-fluid hydrodynamics. Both spectra are quite sensitive to the equation of state used. In particular, within our model, recent data for S+Au at 200[ital A] GeV can only be understood if a scenario with a phase transition (possibly to a quark-gluon plasma) is assumed. Results for Au+Au at 11[ital A] GeV and Pb+Pb at 160[ital A] GeV are also presented.
