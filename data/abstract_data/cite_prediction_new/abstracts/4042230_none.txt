Here, we report the psychometric properties of the FEHC that was administered to Spanish-speaking family members in Florida and Texas. Spanish and then back-translated the instrument to English. Focus groups were utilized to examine whether the survey was clear and understandable to participants. Three hundred and one death certificates from and Texas were pulled in which the ethnicity was listed as Hispanic. Five problem scores were used; these corresponded to the scores used in the validation of the English-language FEHC survey. Inter-item and item-to-total correlation coefficients were calculated for each score. The frequency of missing data was also examined to identify any problems with questions. The Cronbach’s alpha, a test of internal reliability, was examined for each problem score. The construct validity score was based on the degree to which each problem score correlated with the respondents’ overall satisfaction with the quality of care.
