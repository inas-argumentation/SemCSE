Anaerobic Bottles are Still Important in Blood Culture Sets
Endogenous endophthalmitis as a presentation of endocarditis is such a rare finding that its diagnosis and optimal treatment has been infrequently discussed [6]; only 20% of cases are bilateral. The organisms most frequently isolated in endogenous endophthalmitis cases are gram-positive bacteria (70%) such as Staphylococcus aureus and Streptococcus spp. Fungi are observed in 2.5% of cases, most often in the course of systemic fungal infections [7] due to Candida spp. [8]. Only one case of endophthalmitis as the only manifestation of infection by Scedosporium prolificans has been reported; it occurred in an intravenous drug user [8].
