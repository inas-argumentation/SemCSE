Phospholipid synthesis during flight muscle development in the American silkmoth Hyalophora cecropia.
1. 1. The major phospholipid fractions of the flight muscle and sarcosomes are phosphatidylcholine and phosphatidylethanolamine. 2. 2. The cardiolipin fraction increases from 1·3 to 11·8 per cent of the lipid phosphorus of the flight muscle during muscle maturation. 3. 3. Unsaturated fatty acids predominate in the various flight muscle phospholipids with linolenic acid comprising about 70 per cent of the total fatty acids in the cardiolipin fraction. 4. 4. The phospholipid composition of the moth sarcosomes more closely approximates that of beef heart mitochondria than that of housefly flight muscle sarcosomes. 5. 5. Radioisotope and chemical studies indicate the synthesis of phospholipids during adult development of Hyalophora cecropia .
