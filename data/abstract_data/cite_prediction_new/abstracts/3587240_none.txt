Magnetic interactionbetween neighboring grains in insulating granular films of Co-Al-O
The insulating granular film of CO-AI-0 consists of nano-scaled magnetic granules of cobalt embedded in amorphous alumina, which is attracting more and more interests owing to its large tunnel-type giant magnetoresistance effect with an enormous resistivity simultaneously[l]. The magnetic grains may correlate each other through dipole interaction. However, this kind of interaction can not he detected by the usual magnetization measurement due to the short-range character of it, which does not contribute to the macroscopic global magnetization. In this paper, we measured the magnetoresistanc(MR) at low external field(<2000e) to study the interaction influence on the MR effect hearing in mind that the tunnel-type MR is directly related to the angle between the two neighboring grains’ moments[Z].
