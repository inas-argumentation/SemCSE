Change of AMPA Receptors in Spinal Nociceptive Synapses during Inflammatory Hyperalgesia
Nociceptive primary afferent fibers, which convey noxious information from the periphery to the spinal dorsal horn, are commonly divided into those that express neuropeptides such as substance P and calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP), and those that show little or no expression of neuropeptides
