AN APPLICATION OF A GLOBAL BIFURCATION THEOREM TO THE EXISTENCE OF SOLUTIONS FOR INTEGRAL INCLUSIONS
An optical waveguide having a core/sheath structure whose sheath comprises a polysiloxane or a terpolymer based on vinylidene fluoride, tetrafluoroethylene and hexafluoropropylene is suitable for transmission paths of ca. 10 to 100 m and can also be used at a temperature above 100 DEG C. without significant reduction of the transmission path.
