Study of carbon-14 labeled sterculic acid in chickens.
Recently the synthesis of methyl-(9, 10 methylene)-octadec-9-enoate (methyl sterculate) has been reported by Gensler et al. (1970) and Altenburger et al. (1970). In the present study (methylene-14 C)-sterculic acid was synthesized and administered to laying hens in order to determine whether the acid was metabolized or accumulated in the animal. (Methylene-14 C)-methyl sterculate was prepared from methyl stearolate and 14 C-diazomethane (Altenburger et al., 1970) and hydrolyzed to 14 C-sterculic acid for intravenous injection into twelve-month old S.C. White Leghorn hens. Two of the three birds were fasted for 24 hours after injection and then sacrificed. A third bird was fed a standard laying diet and sacrificed after five days. The experimental procedure and the preparation of the samples was described by Berry et al. (1969).
