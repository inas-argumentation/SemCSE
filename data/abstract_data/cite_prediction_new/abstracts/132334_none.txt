Multimodality treatment of migraine headache: a case study utilizing biofeedback, relaxation, autogenic and hypnotic treatments.
The longitudinal treatment of a well-documented case of migraine headache using an additive treatment paradigm is presented. The relative effects of various pharmacological treatments, biofeedback, relaxation, autogenic and hypnotic approaches was assessed. Only autohypnotic anesthesia treatment produced significant symptom remission maintained over one-month and eight-month follow-up periods.
