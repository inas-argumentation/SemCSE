A convenient synthetic route to 2-deoxy-L-ribose and 2-deoxy-D-xylose.
2-Deoxy- l -ribose (2-deoxy- l -erythro-pentose) and 2-deoxy- d -xylose (2-deoxy- d -threo-pentose) were synthesized from 2-deoxy- d -galactose (2-deoxy- d -lyxo-hexose) and 2-deoxy- d -glucose (2-deoxy- d -arabino-hexose), respectively. Conversion of the 2-deoxy- d -hexoses to the methyl 2-deoxy- d -hexofuranosides, followed by periodate oxidation, reduction with borohydride, and mild acid hydrolysis, gave 2-deoxy- l -ribose and 2-deoxy- d -xylose in 55 and 44% yield, respectively. 2-Deoxy- l -ribose was identified by paper chromatography, GLC, and as its “anilide.” 2-Deoxy- d -xylose was identified by paper chromatography. This procedure provides a rapid and convenient method for obtaining these unusual sugars, and may be extended to the synthesis of other 2-deoxy-pentoses.
