Purification of membrane proteins: chloroplast cytochromes f and b559.
Publisher Summary This chapter illustrates a beginning of a rational experimental approach to the purification, proof of purity, and study of properties of membrane proteins. It was developed while purifying cytochrome f and further extended and modified while purifying cytochrome b 559 . A key innovation in the approach is the combination of nonionic detergent with disc gel electrophoresis that provides for the first time: (1) easy reliable diagnosis whether a desired protein in optically clear extracts exists as molecules, which can be easily purified, or a0073 mixed aggregates, where severe difficulty may be expected; (2) an analytical tool in evaluating the purification procedure; (3) a preparative chromatographic tool; (4) a method for proof of purity. Because the overall approach shows promise for the purification and study of properties of membrane proteins in general, the main purpose of this article is to examine those features of the work with cytochromes f and b which may be of general value and to identify problems still to be solved.
