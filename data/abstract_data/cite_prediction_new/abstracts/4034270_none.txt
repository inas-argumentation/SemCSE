Existence, Stability and Dynamics of Nonlinear Modes in a 2D PartiallyPT Symmetric Potential
It is known that multidimensional complex potentials obeying parity-time(PT)symmetry may possess all real spectra and continuous families of solitons. Recently, it was shown that for multi-dimensional systems, these features can persist when the parity symmetry condition is relaxed so that the potential is invariant under reﬂection in only a single spatial direction. We examine the existence, stability and dynamical properties of localized modes within the cubic nonlinear Schrodinger equation in such a scenario of partiallyPT-symmetric potential.
