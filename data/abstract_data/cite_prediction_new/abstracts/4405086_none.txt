Extension of Bessel sequences to oblique dual frame sequences and the minimal projection
An extension of two Bessel sequences to oblique dual frame sequences and its applications to shift-invariant spaces are considered. The best-known situation where this kind of extension is necessary is the construction of a pair of biorthogonal multiresolution analyses, where two generating sets whose shifts are only assumed to be Bessel sequences are given.
