Study on the influential factors of radar target RCS in resonance region
Frequency variation could cause resonance region's Radar Cross Section (RCS) changes in oscillation, which always has a couple of resonant peaks, and the resonant frequency is a useful index that can help target detection and tracking. This paper, which is based on calculated data, analyzes the relationship between the resonant frequency and a series factors, including the attitude angle, size, volume, etc.
