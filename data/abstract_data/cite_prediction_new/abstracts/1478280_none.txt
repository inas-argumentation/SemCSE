Finite Element Analysis of Dynamic Charateristic for the XK717 CNC Milling Machine
In this paper, dynamic characteristics of XK717 CNC milling machine are analyzed by using the finite element method. Vibration resistance is the very important to evaluate the dynamic characteristic as well as the natural vibration frequency and mode shape. The defective parts of the machine are found by means of analyzing three indexes. The improving suggestion for this milling machine is put forward accordingly.
