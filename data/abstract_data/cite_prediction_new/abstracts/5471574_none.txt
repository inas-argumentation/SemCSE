The ARPO-2: an expert system for building contract
The theme of expert legal systems is considered in a general form and a general model for such systems is suggested. A description is given of the ARPO-2 prototype which has been developed to deal with problems relating to building contracts. The system acts as an aid to the jurist in discovering whether it is possible to make a legal claim relative to a building contract as well as documenting the posture to be taken with respect to the case. Some results of the use of IDEAL methodology for expert system development are shown, as obtained for ARPO-2. Future lines of work are discussed, with the aim of improving this prototype.
