Modelling of Polymer Crystallizationin Temperature
This paper is devoted to the mathematical modelling of polymer crystallization processes in a bounded region under heat transfer conditions, i.e. in time-dependent temperature elds. A stochastic model in a general setup is developed based on the theory of marked point processes, and a hybrid model on a macroscopic scale is derived using expected values. The stochastic modelling part is supplemented by a detailed discussion of the relevant parameters and their dependence upon temperature. In the special case of one-dimensional crystallization a system of partial diierential equations describing the evolution of temperature and of the degree of crystallinity is derived.
