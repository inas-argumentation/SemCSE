Squamous Cell Carcinoma Arising from a Keratocystic Odontogenic Tumor
Keratocystic odontogenic tumors (KCOTs) were reclassified by the World Health Organization in 2005 as neoplastic tumors, characterized by aggressive growth and frequent recurrence, and genetic studies show that they harbor inactivation of tumor suppressor genes demonstrated in head and neck carcinomas. Malignant transformation of KCOTs into primary intraosseous squamous cell carcinomas (PIOSCCs) is well-documented, albeit uncommon.
