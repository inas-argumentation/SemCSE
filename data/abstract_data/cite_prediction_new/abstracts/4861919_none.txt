The robustness of the proposed observers is tested through a physical example. The obtained results show that for non linear systems the performances of sliding mode observer observer is better than using a classic kind of observer. The synthesis of nonlinear observers will be used for actuator fault detection and isolation using residual generation. Finally, a comparison of observers" performances will be interesting for judging the effectiveness of this approach.
