Survey of multiagents systems application in Microgrids
Microgrid will constitute the heart of the next generation of power systems. They are expected to incorporate more distributed generation (DG) mainly coming from renewable energy (RE). They are characterized by their ability to manage and control electricity by using the advance of information technology. In parallel, development of multi agent systems (MAS) and their tested proprieties increase researchers interest in applying them in Microgrid (MG) context. This paper presents a survey of MAS application in MG. It revisits the concept of MG and MAS. It introduces the agency domain and its characteristics. MAS design methodologies and implementation platform are also reported.
