Distributed energy dispatch of electrical energy storage systems
For multiple electrical energy storage systems (EESSs), the state-of-charge (SoC) balance ensures the maximal power capacity of the group of EESSs, and the supply-demand balance maintains the load operation and reduces energy lost. In this paper, a distributed energy dispatch algorithm is proposed for achieving both the balances of EESSs over an undirected connected communication network. Each EESS shares the information with communication neighbors through the network. The algorithm design contains three parts. Firstly, the sign function is utilized to integrate the charging and discharging models of EESSs into a unified switching form. Secondly, for each EESS, the desired power in system steady state is calculated and containing global coefficients of EESSs, and a distributed observer based algorithm is derived to estimate the desired power, which provides the local power reference for each EESS. Thirdly, for each EESS, based on the local power tracking error and the SoC error between itself and its communication neighbors, the energy dispatch algorithm is constructed by employing the distributed receding horizon control (DRHC) approach. The algorithm realizes the two balances and simultaneously satisfies the power and SoC constraints. Effectiveness of the proposed dispatch algorithm is demonstrated by simulation results.
