Testing for abuse liability of drugs in humans. Proceedings of a conference. November 5-6, 1988, Princeton, NJ.
In summary, there are a number of factors to be considered in abuse liability testing such as the purpose of the testing and its potential applications that determine which subject population we should employ. Furthermore, we should not expect to see a perfect correlation between abuse liability testing and the actual abuse of these drugs because there are many factors that will modulate or modify whether or not abuse liability becomes activated and the abuse of any specific drug becomes a social problem.
