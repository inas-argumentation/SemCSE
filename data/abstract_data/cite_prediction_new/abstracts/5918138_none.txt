Design guidelines for audio presentation of graphs and tables
General Line Graph Sonification Guidelines Audio can be used to make visualisations accessible to blind and visually impaired people. The MultiVis Project has carried out research into suitable methods for presenting graphs and tables to blind people through the use of both speech and non- speech audio. This paper presents guidelines extracted from this research. These guidelines will enable designers to implement visualisation systems for blind and visually impaired users, and will provide a framework for researchers wishing to investigate the audio presentation of more complex visualisations. The first set of guidelines are general guidelines for the sonification of line graphs, and are applicable regardless of the number of data series present in the graph.
