The strategic discount— Protecting new business projects against DCF
There is a bias against long-term strategic investment strongly evident in today's business culture and formalized in orthodox financial appraisal techniques. This bias results in a disinvestment spiral and puts otherwise valid businesses in jeopardy. The Strategic Discount is both a specific means of offsetting the bias in financial appraisal and a general means of adapting business culture to more positive and creative entrepreneurial strategies.
