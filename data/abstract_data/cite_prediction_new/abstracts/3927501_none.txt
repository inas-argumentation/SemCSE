Urinary steroid excretion pattern in diabetes mellitus.
The 24-hour urinary excretion of a number of neutral steroids was determined in diabetics with and without complications and in non-diabetic controls. In male and female diabetics the excretion-rates of, notably, androstenetriol, pregnanetriol, and pregnenetriol were significantly lower than those found in controls: in females excretion-rates of aetiocholanolone, androsterone, and 11-oxoaetiocholanolone were significantly lowered also. These findings suggest an analogy with breast cancer (in which an abnormality in steroid excretion has been found), but it is impossible to say, at this stage, whether the special steroid pattern found is the effect or the cause of the diabetic disorder.
