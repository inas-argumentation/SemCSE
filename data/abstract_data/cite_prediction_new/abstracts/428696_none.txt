The ultrastructure of neointima of canine infrarenal vena caval Teflon prostheses.
Long-term patency of infrarenal vena caval Teflon replacement prostheses was maintained in four of nine dogs by creating femoral arteriovenous fistulas and placing supporting metallic rings around the prostheses. The neointima of the long-term successful cases was studied by light and electron microscopy. The caval wall adjacent to the graft was used as a control for morphologic comparison. Despite the well-formed appearance of the neointima by gross and light microscopic examination, there was electron microscopic evidence of minimal progressive alterations in the neointima. Only minor ultrastructural differences between the endothelium of the graft and that of the control were noted.
