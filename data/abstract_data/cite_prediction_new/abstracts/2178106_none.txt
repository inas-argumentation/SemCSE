A Study on the Disparity between the Job Satisfaction of Private and Public School Teachers in Bangladesh
A teacher actively contributes to eliminate the darkness of ignorance from a society. Although teaching is recognized as a highly respectable and decent profession in the society, the payment and benefits are found unsatisfactory among the teacher community of Bangladesh. The condition of primary teachers are assumed to be more vulnerable which is regarded less attractive and hardly chosen by many a lot qualified people. This paper is a little effort from the authors’ side on measuring and compares the level of job satisfaction of school teachers focusing the disparity who are working in both private and public sector in Bangladesh. This study identified significant disparity between the job satisfaction of private and public school teachers. Job satisfaction is found to be an important factor of quality teaching. The teachers work more passionately with dedication only if they are satisfied, and project a positive image of the institutes they belong to. 5
