Plate-shaped nuclear pole bodies in the monothalamous foraminiferKibisidytes sp.
SummaryIn the monothalamous foraminiferKibisidytes sp. a pair of flat small electron dense bodies of 0.6 μm in length can be demonstrated at the nuclear envelope of interphase nuclei. During karyokinesis these extranuclear bodies enlarge to plate-shaped nuclear pole bodies with a diameter of about 2 μm. They are located at the poles within a depression of the persisting nuclear envelope and in cross section they show an asymmetrical structure.
