The Marketing Research Practices of Nigerian Companies
A survey research was designed to investigate the marketing research practices of Nigerian companies, the influences on such practices, and the relevant empirical relations. Using executive responses and descriptive and correlation analysis methods, it was found that analysis of company records, test marketing, random sampling, projective psychological technique and reliability test were, respectively, the most used data collection, research design, sampling, measurement, and data analysis methods employed by Nigerian companies. Secrecy in giving out relevant data by respondents was found to be the most influencing factor constraining marketing research practice in Nigeria. Nigerian marketing research managers were advised to acquaint themselves with the purposes and mechanics of multi-variate marketing research tools because of the many variables encountered in practical marketing management decisions. Additionally, Nigerian marketing research managers were advised to devise other data-collection approaches and strategies in order to deal with the problem of secrecy encountered among Nigerian executive respondents.
