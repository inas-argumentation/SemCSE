The Study of Combination Forecasting Model for Urban Distribution Demand Based on Sharpley Value Method
A network of urban distribution, which is improved and designed based on the analysis of urban distribution demand, can not only satisfy the demands for logistics, but also avoid redundant construction. Due to the limitations of the individual forecasting model, this paper puts forward the combination-forecasting model and chooses indirect index for analyzing urban distribution demands based on applying the Shapley value method, in which the weight of every member is determined according to the distributed error, and verifies the model with concrete examples. The result of analysis shows that a combination-forecasting model considerably improves precision and validates its usage in forecasting urban distribution demands.
