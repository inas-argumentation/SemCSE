Nutrition Awareness and Branding in College/University Foodservices
Both nutrition awareness and branding are trends that have changed and are continuing to change the nature of college/university foodservices. These two trends have evolved from opposing customer motivations: a desire to improve health and Wellness and a need to have expectations of food products met. Customer nutrition awareness has developed as a result of research into dietary practices recommended for improved health and Wellness. Branding has emerged from customer desire to have familiar products that consistently meet their expectations. The purpose of this study is (1) to examine the factors motivating these trends, (2) to determine the criteria various segments of college customers use for food selection, and (3) to assess the impact of these trends on future strategic planning in college/university segment.
