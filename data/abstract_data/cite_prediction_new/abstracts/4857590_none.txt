On the lowest excited singlet state of osmium tetroxide
Two‐photon spectra of OsO4 in the region below the first strong one‐photon transition are observed and analyzed. The data fit a linear Jahn–Teller (T×t) calculation. An assignment of the two‐photon features as arising from a T1 electronic state, origin at 27 295 cm−1, with a dominant linear Jahn–Teller active t2 vibration ν3, ν′3 = 588 cm−1 (ν′′3 = 960 cm−1), and with Jahn–Teller parameter D = 0.5 is proposed. Based on these findings the two observed one‐photon states are discussed and qualitatively analyzed in a parallel fashion.
