He quotes from a survey that “the top three reasons why Americans oppose development are, in order: protecting community character; protecting the environment; and too much traffic” (p. 106). He advocates designing more of nature into higher density areas, by providing parks, small green spaces, and green infrastructure. He especially advocates for multitasking or making strategic combinations within these small green spaces, such as making a park for children with a restful space for adults, providing an extensive tree canopy to reduce the heat island effect, and creating a space that is important to the community. Doing all of these things is a challenge, but multitasking may be one way to utilize expensive urban spaces, while addressing environmental and cultural values.
