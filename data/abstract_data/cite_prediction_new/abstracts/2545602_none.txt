Corporate crisis planning: tensions, issues, and contradictions
Purpose – Crisis planning, which symbolizes an organization's crisis preparedness and often conceptualized at the corporate headquarters, is increasingly decentralized to regional centers of global companies. These centers, in turn, synchronize their crisis master plans with its national units for expeditious management of localized crises. The purpose of this paper is to capture the decision‐making processes that practitioners at a regional center faced as they nurtured their master plan from conception to implementation.Design/methodology/approach – The qualitative method is used. This is a case study of a Fortune 500 company with plants in every continent.
