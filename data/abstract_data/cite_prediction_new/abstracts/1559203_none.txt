The review and development of the brake system for civil aircrafts
The brake system is meeting with an important opportunity and challenge with the research of the civil aircraft deeply. This paper describes the main techniques of the brake system for the famous civil aircraft A380 and B787. And points out the developing trend of civil aircraft brake system, including the electric brake technology, the auto-brake technology, the advanced control law or control policy, the system integration technology, the redundant control, the fault detection, the system reconfiguration technology, the green taxiing control and the brake system auxiliary measures. Comparing the difference of here and abroad advanced technology, some viewpoints about the development of aircraft brake system are proposed.
