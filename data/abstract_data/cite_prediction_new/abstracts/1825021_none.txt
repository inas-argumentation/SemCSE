Reflections from a College President: When Access is Not Enough, or The Significance of Academic Advising
Editor’s Note: As members of the NACADA Board of Directors, the AAT Editorial Board, and the Executive Office staff have talked with our membership around the globe, it has become clear that we share a common concern about the pressures that we all face in the current economic climate. We open this edition with the positive, constructive measures that have been taken at Georgia Perimeter College to ensure the success of the academic advising program at that institution. Then, in the Advising Issues section, we’ll hear a variety of perspectives on this topic from our membership.
