Emerging Applications for Polymers in Ophthalmology
The past 30 years in ophthalmology have been marked with tremendous advances in instrumentation and deeper understanding into the pathophysiological basis for ocular disease. It has also been a generation of equally impressive strides in the polymeric sciences. These advances in chemistry have enabled an ever-growing number of new devices to emerge that have transformed how we manage ocular disease. This review focuses on emerging technologies and applications of novel polymers in 3 distinct areas: advanced intraocular lenses (IOLs), vitreous substitutes for retinal tamponade, and implants for orbital and oculoplastic surgery.
