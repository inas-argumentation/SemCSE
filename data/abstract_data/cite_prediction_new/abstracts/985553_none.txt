Expanding Algorithmic Randomness to the Algebraic Approach to Quantum Physics: Kolmogorov Complexity and Quantum Logics
Physicists usually assume that events with a very small probability cannot occur. Kolmogorov complexity formalizes this idea for non-quantum events. We show how this formalization can be extended to quantum events as well. c ⃝2011 World Academic Press, UK. All rights reserved.
