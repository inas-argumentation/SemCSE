Экономическая социология: исследования налоговой культуры и этики
The article analyzes the features of the theoretical study of taxation in economic sociol ogy. In recent years there has been much research dealing with the studies of values, social norms and attitudes to the economy, as well as their impact on economic behavior. One of the areas in which such research is particularly relevant is tax compliance law and tax system. The authors of this trend believe that the tax compliance depends on a number of factors beyond the standard economic theory. The paper deals with the study of national tax culture, tax moral and compliance. The article also presents the results of modern research, analyzing the differences between countries depending on their tax compliance and tax moral.
