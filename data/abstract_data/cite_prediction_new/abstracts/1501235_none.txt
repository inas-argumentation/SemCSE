Degradation of sevin (1-naphthyl-N-methyl carbamate by Achrombacter sp.
SummaryA strain of Achromobacter utilized 1-Naphthyl-N-methyl carbamate (Sevin, Carbaryl) as the sole source of carbon in salt medium. Four degradation products of sevin were identified to be 1-naphthol, hydroquinone, catechol and pyruvate. The organism grew on 1-naphthol, hydroquinone and catechol.
