Effect of thyroid hormones on the intestinal transport of valine in vitro
SummaryInjection of 4 mg. thyroxine on the first day and 8 mg. on the second, third, and fourth days decreased (p<0.05) by the fifth day all the parameters of valine transport in everted intestinal sacs from the rat. Thyroidectomy-parathyroidectomy, 50 days prior to sac studies, profoundly accelerated all the parameters of valine translocation. The injection of 4 µg. of thyroxine daily into the thyroidectomized group reduced the valine transport parameters to control levels. An isolated deficiency in the parathyroid hormones had no significant effect on valine transport. Serum glutamic oxalacetic transaminase levels were found to vary inversely with the thyroid status of the rats.
