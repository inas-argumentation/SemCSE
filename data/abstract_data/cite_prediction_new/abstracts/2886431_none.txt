Asymptotic Behavior of Singular and Entropy Numbers for Some Riemann–Liouville Type Operators
The asymptotic behavior of the singular and entropy numbers is established for the Erdelyi–Köber and Hadamard integral operators (see, e.g., [Samko, Kilbas and Marichev, Integrals and derivatives. Theoryand Applications, Gordon and Breach Science Publishers, 1993]) acting in weighted L 2 spaces. In some cases singular value decompositions are obtained as well for these integral transforms.
