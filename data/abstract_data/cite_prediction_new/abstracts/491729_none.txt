Association between polymorphism in IgG Fc receptor IIIa coding gene and biological response to infliximab in Crohn's disease
Aim : To test the hypothesis of an association between polymorphism in FCGR3A (the gene coding for FcγRIIIa, which is expressed on macrophages and natural killer cells, is involved in antibody‐dependent cell‐mediated cytotoxicity and has recently been associated with a positive response to rituximab, a recombinant immunoglobulin G1 antibody used in non‐Hodgkin's lymphomas) and response to infliximab in Crohn's disease.
