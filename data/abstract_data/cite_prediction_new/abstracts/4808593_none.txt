High-efficiency and wideband bridge linking waveguide waves and spoof surface plasmon polaritons
Spoof Surface Plasmon Polaritons (SSPPs) have shown many significant practical applications for new waveguide and devices in microwave frequency range. For these practical applications, it requires efficient conversion between spoof surface plasmon polaritons and guided waves when feeding energies into and extracting signals from functional plasmonic waveguides or devices. In this work, a smooth bridge between the plasmonic waveguide structure and the traditional waveguide port is proposed. Two oblique wedge waveguide connectors are respectively used to compress the field energy and provide impedance conversion. A grade varied patches array is etched on the copper grounded substrate, that helps mitigating the momenta difference. Simulation results show good transmission performance under rather broad bandwidth. The proposed design scheme of the matching transition is also valid for other types of plane plasmonic waveguide.
