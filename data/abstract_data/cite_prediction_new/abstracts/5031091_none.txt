Epidemiology of Pulmonary Tuberculosis in Western U.P.
Pulmonary tuberculosis is endemic globally and responsible for considerable morbidity and mortality. Present study was carried out from 2007-2009 to assess the prevalence and risk factor of pulmonary tuberculosis in Western Uttar Pradesh. The prevalence of pulmonary tuberculosis was significantly higher (χ2=6.2, d.f.=2, P=0.045) in low income group as compared to high income group. Odds ratio (OR 0.15, 95% CI 0.03-.0.79) also reveals that the population of high income group has low prevalence than low income group. Poor socio-economic status with its poor education is associated with poor knowledge of tuberculosis, risk of infection and dissemination, inadequate and delayed availability of health care. Poverty also results in poor nutrition, which is likely to render the immune system more vulnerable to the invading organism.
