Global Generalized Bianchi Identities for Invariant Variational Problems on Gauge-natural Bundles
We derive both local and global generalized Bianchi identities for classical Lagrangian field theories on gauge-natural bundles. We show that globally defined generalized Bianchi identities can be found without the a pri- ori introduction of a connection. The proof is based on a global decomposition of the variational Lie derivative of the generalized Euler-Lagrange morphism and the representation of the corresponding generalized Jacobi morphism on gauge-natural bundles. In particular, we show that within a gauge-natural invariant Lagrangian variational principle, the gauge-natural lift of infinites- imal principal automorphism is not intrinsically arbitrary. As a consequence the existence of canonical global superpotentials for gauge-natural Noether conserved currents is proved without resorting to additional structures.
