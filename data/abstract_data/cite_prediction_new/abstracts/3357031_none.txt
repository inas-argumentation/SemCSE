A model for drug-receptor remote recognition applied to certain hydrogen bonding systems.
The energies associated with the interaction and conformational change of model compounds simulating drug and receptor features have been predicted using CNDO/2 calculations on a united molecule. In this second study of the hypothesis of remote recognition of preferred conformation we have examined systems involved in hydrogen bonding. We have found that a system containing interacting electron lone pairs behaves at variance with our hypothesis. Other systems in this study support the hypothesis.
