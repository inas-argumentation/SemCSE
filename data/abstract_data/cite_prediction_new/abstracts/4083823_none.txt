Whirling instability of a rotating elastic filament based on a bacterial flagellar structure
When an elastic filament spins in a viscous incompressible fluid it may undergo a whirling instability, as studied asymptotically by Wolgemuth, Powers, Goldstein [1]. We use the Immersed Boundary (IB) method to study the interaction between the elastic filament and the surrounding viscous fluid as governed by the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations and to determine the nature of the bifurcation, which turns out to be subcritical. This allows the study of the whirling motion when the shape of the filament is very different from the unperturbed straight state.
