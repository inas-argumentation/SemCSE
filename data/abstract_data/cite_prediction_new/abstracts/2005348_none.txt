Recurrence of the Twisted Random Walk in the Plane
Suppose that (Xk)k≥1 is a stationary process taking values in the complex plane. For any choice of β from the interval [0, 2π) we consider the random walk recursively defined by the equations S (β) 0 = 0 and S (β) n = e S (β) n−1 + Xn−1 for n ≥ 1, and prove recurrence under diverse additional assumptions on the increment process (Xk)k∈Z. For example if the increment process is α-mixing and E(|Xk| 2) is finite, then S (β) n is recurrent for every fixed choice of the angle β out of a set of full Lebesgue measure, no matter how slowly the mixing coefficients decay. 1.
