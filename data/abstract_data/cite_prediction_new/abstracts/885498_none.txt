NUMERICAL ANALYSIS OF SEALING CONDITIONS IN ELASTOMERIC RINGS
This paper describes the latest developments in a research, carried on during several years, aimed at investigating sealing conditions in elastomeric ring. Seal’s lip displacement near the dynamic clearance is measured with a strain gauge transducer in actual working conditions. A detailed study of transducer characteristics and limits is included. Numerical models of clearance forming has been developed taking in account the effect of temperature, defining the material constituting properties according to empiric WLF function. Both 2D and 3D models give results very similar and compatible with experimental measurements. The tridimensional model makes use of the experimental data for investigating the influence of tangential strain on clearance frequency.
