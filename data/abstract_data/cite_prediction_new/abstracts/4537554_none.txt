Reports of the Scientific, Technical and Economic Committee for Fisheries - Special request for evaluating Baltic cod additional measures (STECF-16-23)
Commission Decision of 25 February 2016 setting up a Scientific, Technical and Economic Committee for Fisheries, C(2016) 1084, OJ C 74, 26.2.2016, p. 4–10. The Commission may consult the group on any matter relating to marine and fisheries biology, fishing gear technology, fisheries economics, fisheries governance, ecosystem effects of fisheries, aquaculture or similar disciplines. This report deals with a special request for evaluating Baltic cod additional measures.
