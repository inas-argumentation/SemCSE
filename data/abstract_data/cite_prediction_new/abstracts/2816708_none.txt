Distribution of intestinal stem cell markers in colorectal precancerous lesions
Intestinal stem cell (ISC) markers such as LGR5, ASCL2, EPHB2 and OLFM4, and their clinical implications have been studied extensively in colorectal cancers (CRCs). However, little is known about their expression in precancerous lesions of CRCs. Here, we investigated the expression and distribution of ISC markers in serrated polyps and conventional adenomas.
