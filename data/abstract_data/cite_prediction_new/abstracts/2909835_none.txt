How does the transformation of an avatar face giving a favorable impression affect human recognition of the face?
We investigated how different appearances in the favorable impressions of 3D avatar faces affect face-recognition performances by humans. We conducted an encoding and testing experiment using synthesized facial images and artificially manipulated the strength of the perceived impressions in three different dimensions. We also subjectively assessed the favorability of the synthesized faces that were used as visual stimuli in face-recognition tests and found that facial transformation, which decreased the favorability impressions, generally deteriorates human face-recognition performance.
