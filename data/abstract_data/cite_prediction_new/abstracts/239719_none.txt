Ontology-based Reflective Iot Middleware-Enabled Agriculture Decision Support System
Modern agricultural management relies strongly on many different sensing methodologies to provide accurate information on crop, climate, and environmental conditions. Strong challenges in Decision Support Systems still regard the way a smart IoT-based architecture is designed in order to make it robust with respect to the contextual changes it continually undergoes. In this paper we propose an extensible self-adaptive Decision Support System for managing heterogeneous data sources.
