High-voltage electron microscopy of whole, critical-point dried plant cells—fine cytoskeletal elements in the mossBryum tenuisetum
SummaryWhole, critical-point dried cells of protonemata of the mossBryum tenuisetum have been examined in the high-voltage electron microscope at 1 MV. Fine cytoskeletal elements form a three-dimensional meshwork in the cytoplasm. This resembles, but has some differences from, the microtrabecular lattice seen in spread animal cells examined by the same technique.
