MacAndrew scale scores of smoking and nonsmoking alcoholics.
The MacAndrew Scale (MAC) (1) of the Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory (MMP•) has been validated as an instrument for identifying alcoholics and drug addicts. It is an objective personality measure that is fairly stable and can be used to identify college students who later became alcoholics (2). Therefore, it is believed to measure characteristics common to users of a wide variety of chemicals (3). There seems to be an association between alcoholism and cigarette smoking (4, 5).
