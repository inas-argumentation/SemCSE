Effect of temporary hypovolemia on early postresuscitation centralization of the circulation and survival of animals recovering after clinical death
The writers showed previously that centralization of the circulation takes place in the initial stage of the postresuscitation period, and that the intensity and duration of this response correlates postively with the severity of the state before recovery [I]. The aim of this investigation was to study the effect of temporary exclusion of part of the blood volume from the circulation on cardiac output, the distribution of its principal fractions, and the survival rate of animals recovering from clinical death.
