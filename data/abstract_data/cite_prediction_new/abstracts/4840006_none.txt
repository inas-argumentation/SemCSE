Quality of Life Assessment in Gynaecological Cancer Patients
EDITORIAL COMMENT: Quality of life assessment is becoming more and more important in gynaecological oncology. At present, quality of life issues are not commonly considered when treatment is assessed. With new treatments costing more and more it is likely that quality of life assessment will be required in the future for all trials, and for the justification of various ongoing treatments in cancer patients Although the numbers in this paper are small and provide only limited information in the way of results, it is a step in the right direction and will serve to inform, remind or encourage readers to consider adopting or developing similar assessment tools relevant to Australia.
