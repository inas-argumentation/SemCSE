Morphology of growth: The effects of country size, structural characteristics and linkages
This paper investigates the extent to which tests concerning the influence of various kinds of structural imbalance indexes on the overall rate of economic growth may be sensitive to (1) differences in the extent to which various country characteristics are reflected in the normal growth path and hence in the imbalance indexes, (2) differences in the level of disaggregation or part of the economy at which imbalance is measured, and (3) the type of countries to which the analysis is applied. The results based on imbalance indexes and growth rates for a sample of 42 countries 1960–1970 and 1970–1978 are shown to be sensitive to all three of these considerations.
