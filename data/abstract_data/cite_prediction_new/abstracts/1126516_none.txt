Induction of immunological tolerance by a hapten (DNP) bound to a non-immunogenic protein carrier.
IT is not known whether immunological tolerance and immunity are two aspects of the same process or fundamentally distinct. If substances inducing tolerance were not always immunogenic, tolerance and immunity could be dissociated from each other and their cellular mechanisms analysed independently. In the experiments described here we used two versions of the dinitrophenol (DNP) determinant, one which is not immunogenic and one which is. We found that the non-immunogenic substance induced tolerance of its normally immunogenic counterpart.
