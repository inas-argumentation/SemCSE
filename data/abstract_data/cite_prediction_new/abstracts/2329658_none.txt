Climate change: water rights and electric utilities
Continued accumulation of CO 2 and other greenhouse gases in the atmosphere may result in significant global warming and in altered patterns of precipitation and runoff within the next century. The impacts on electric utilities of possible reductions in runoff or change in its timing and reliability will depend on the pre-existing nature and distribution of water rights and on the adaptability of water institutions to changing conditions. The major features of the two systems of water rights in use in the USA, and a potential model for institutional adjustment to climate change are described.
