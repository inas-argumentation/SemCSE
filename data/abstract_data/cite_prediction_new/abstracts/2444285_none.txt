Sympathetic blocks for reflex sympathetic dystrophy
&NA; Seventy‐one patients with reflex sympathetic dystrophy of the lower extremities were studied during a 3‐year period. Of the 27 patients managed by conservative means, 11 (41%) showed signs of improvement 3 years after the onset of syndrome. Of the 43 patients treated by sympathetic nerve blocks, 28 (65%) experienced progress at the 3‐year evaluation. Data suggest that early treatment with repeated sympathetic nerve blocks appears to improve the long‐term outcome.
