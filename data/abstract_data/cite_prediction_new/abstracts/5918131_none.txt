Investigation on DFIG based wind turbine for short term frequency regulation techniques
Power sector are moving towards to renewable energy sources for meeting the ever increasing power requirement. Therefore, wind power installed capacity has reached a substantial level which make essential to provide frequency support to the grid by using wind turbines. The inertial control and droop control techniques are utilized for participation of wind turbines in grid frequency regulation. Present paper has investigated the frequency response of DFIG based wind turbine for constant wind speed. This paper aims to investigate the affect of increasing wind power potential on frequency response when the wind power plants operated without control as well as with both inertial and droop controllers. Also, the impact of droop parameter has simulated for various installed wind power capacity. Novelty of this paper lies in the impact of increasing wind power level without and with frequency regulation techniques along with varying droop parameter in totality. The two area interconnected test system has been used for simulation by using MATLAB/SIMULINK. The simulation results support the beneficial impact of wind turbine equipped with inertial control and droop control.
