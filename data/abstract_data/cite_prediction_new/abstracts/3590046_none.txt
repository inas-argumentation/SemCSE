Studies on polycarboxylates and related cements. 4. Properties of cements.
The mechanical properties, setting times, apparent pH of setting cements, solubility, film thickness and adhesion to enamel and dentine have been determined for a range of zinc polycarboxylate cements. The properties are discussed in relation to the suitability of the cements as linings and luting agents. The effect of composition on properties is also discussed.
