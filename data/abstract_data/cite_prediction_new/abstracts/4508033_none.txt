Reputations vs Citations: Who Are the Top Scholars in Political Science?
When Albert Somit and Joseph Tanenhaus sought to determine which political scientists have made the most substantial contributions to the discipline, they used a reputational technique. Somit and Tanenhaus asked a systematically-drawn sample of political scientists, " In your judgment, which political scientists have made the most significant contributions to the discipline from 1900-1945? from 1945 to the present [1963]?" Walter Roettger replicated and updated that study in 1978 and found remarkable stability over time in rankings by reputation.
