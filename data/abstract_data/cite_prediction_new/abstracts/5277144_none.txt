3rd International Syposium on Aerothermodynamics of Internal Flows A Contravariant Velocity Based Staggered Implicit Finite Volume Scheme for Solving the Compressible/Incompressible Navier-Stokes Equations
In this paper, an implicit nite volume method is developed to solve the 3-D compressible / incompress-ible Navier-Stokes equations in general curvilinear coordinates. A distinctive feature of this method is that the contravariant velocities are employed as the dependent variables. The momentum equations of contravariant velocities are discretized in staggered control volumes while the energy conservation equations are integrated basically by using a cell-centered nite volume scheme. This method was rst tested by appling to three dimensional ow calculations for quite complicated circular-to-rectangular ducts integrated with a convergent-divergent transonic nozzle. The computational results were found in good agreement with the experimental data.
