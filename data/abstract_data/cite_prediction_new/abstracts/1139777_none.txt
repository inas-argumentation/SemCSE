Optical , Wear and Corrosive Properties of TiSiN Nanocomposite coatings
In this paper Ti-Si-N nanocomposite coatings were prepared by reactive magnetron sputtering on to different substrates at different power 50, 100, 150 and 200 watt at substrate temperature 400 o . The photoluminescence (PL) spectra of the films show good optical properties.
