Object Oriented and Logic Based Knowledge Representation
This paper is a survey of a number of languages systems based on both Object Oriented and Logic Programming and designed expressly for Knowledge Representation tasks My goal in the paper is to argue that the integration of these two paradigms particularly the synergism that emerges from such an integration forms a stable basis for Knowledge Representation at the symbolic level I try to support this claim both by examples from the papers surveyed and by considerations in a more general context Some more advanced topics concerning special purpose non classic logics are also discussed
