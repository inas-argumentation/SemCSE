The preoperative predictors of optimal cytoreductive surgery in women with advanced epithelial ovarian cancer
To evaluate the ability of different preoperative clinical, laboratory and radiographic parameters to predict feasibility of optimum cytoreduction, 44 patients were enrolled in this study as they had malignant epithelial ovarian tumor. Pre-operative staging was assessed by CT scan based on FIGO classification and compared to the standard laparotomy surgical staging. Maximal surgical effort for Optimum cytoreduction was achieved and the risk factors for sub-optimal cytoreduction were studied. Optimum cytoreduction was achieved in 62% of cases. There was a statistically significant difference between women with optimum and non-optimum cytoreduction regarding liver infiltration (p=0.006), pelvic side wall encasement (p=0.006), hydroureter (p=0.018) and a highly significant difference regarding pleural effusion (p=0.001) and pre-operative staging (p=0.001) detected at CT. The preoperative CT staging, peritoneal involvement, pelvic wall or pelvic peritoneum involvement and hydroureter must be used as preoperative predictors for achieving optimum cytoreduction.
