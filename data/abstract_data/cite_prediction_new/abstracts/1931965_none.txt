Targeted therapies: Congratulations, you are still in the running towards becoming ovarian-next-top treatment
For many cancer types, the response rates to antiangiogenic agents have not been as successful as anticipated according to the results in preclinical models, and most patients develop resistance to this type of treatment. Ovarian cancer, however, seems to respond well to antiangiogenic therapy and several studies have reported improved progression-free survival (PFS) when administered alone and in combination with platinum-based therapies, making angiogenesis inhibition an encouraging approach for this often fatal cancer.
