UNIVERSITY STUDENTS, EMPLOYERS AND THE LABOR MARKET: THE IMPORTANCE OF 'VALUE EDUCATION'
Values, knowledge, skills and attitudes are important in educating students for professional careers. Despite this, values are practically ignored in University courses. This paper identifies personal values of employers and of students at a Center of the Spanish University of Huelva, Department of Work Science. The methodology was based on the values scale proposed by Rokeach [1973]. Gaps between the values of both groups were found. Methods are suggested for students to increase their employability by adopting employer values.
