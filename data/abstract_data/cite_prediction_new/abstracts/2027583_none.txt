Effects of pH on expression and stabilization of β-galactosidase by recombinantE. coli with a thermally-inducible expression system
SummaryEffects of pH on β-galactosidase expression and stabilization were investigated using recombinantE. coli harboring an expression vector with a thermally-inducible PL promoter. Expression of β-galactosidase was strongly promoted by lowering culture pH when culture temperature was raised to the induction temperature. Optimal pH for induction ranged from 5.4–5.8. The degradation of expressed β-galactosidase could be reduced by lowering the culture temperature while at the same time slightly increasing the culture pH in the β-gal degradation stage.
