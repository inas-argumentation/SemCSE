Time‐Dependent Bias in Instantaneous Ceptometry Caused by Row Orientation
Time of day can strongly bias inferences based on instantaneous ceptometry. This bias is caused by interaction of solar zenith angle and row orientation. Continuously recording ceptometers could be used to avoid time‐dependent bias. We detail how to build high‐accuracy low‐cost continuously recording ceptometers. These could be used to develop correction factors for instantaneous measurements.
