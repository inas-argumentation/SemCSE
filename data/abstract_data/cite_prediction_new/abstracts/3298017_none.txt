The resistance signal detection of lubricant debris in micro-channel based on LC resonance
There are of great importance to monitor the working condition of hydraulic equipment in industry. Some abnormal wear can be discovered from content and character of metal debris in lubricant. Detecting the debris can prognosticate failures in time and reduce losses. In this paper, the authors design a micro detecting system of lubricant debris based on LC resonance to improve the measuring accuracy and sensitivity of a micro-channel detecting chip in detecting ferrous metallic debris in lubricant. The working principle of the micro-channel detecting chip is analyzed firstly. When debris pass through the detecting coil, the magnetic field of the debris is changed. This change can be captured by coil and leads to the change of the coil inductance and the resistance of the LC resonance circuit which can be easily detected. In the experiments, the LC resonance coil detecting chip and an original coil detecting chip both are excited by a high frequency AC voltage source. The experiments are carried out in a 300μm diametric micro-channel and iron debris with a diameter 80μm is tested. The results show that signal-to-noise ratio is proportional to the excitation voltage, which is improved by approximately 400% comparing to the original coil detecting chip.
