Workforce Transformation and Equal Opportunities in the New European Steel Industry: A Case Study Analysis of the Effects of Recent Structural Changes
Dean Stroud is a research associate in the School of Social Sciences. His research interests include skills and training, local labour markets and life-long learning. Peter Fairbrother is a professorial fellow in the School of Social Sciences. His research interests include state restructuring, the intersection between locality and global organisation, and questions relating to trade unionism at local, national and international levels. Amanda Coffey is senior lecturer in the School of Social Sciences. Her research interests include young people and the transitions to adulthood, gender and transforming labour markets, and qualitative research methodologies.
