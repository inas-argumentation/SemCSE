Effect of Addition of Antioxidants on the Oxidative Stability of Refined Bleached and Deodorized Palm Olein
The addition of 50, 100, 150 and 200 ppm of butylated hydroxytoluene (BHT), butylated hydroxyanisole (BHA), tertiary butylhydroquinone (TBHQ), propyl gallate (PG) and α-tocopherol to refined, bleached and deodorized palm olein resulted in the retardation of the oxidation of the oil when stored at 65°C for 13 days.
