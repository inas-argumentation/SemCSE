Technical UpdateJune 2013 Effects of DRAXXIN ® at weaning for control of swine respiratory disease in pigs experiencing a natural outbreak
• Comparative weight distribution results showed that more DRAXXIN group pigs reached market weights sooner than control group pigs as a derived benefit resulting from the control of SRD, which suggests that even greater return on investment might be realized as more pigs would hit packer premium price targets, depending on when they leave the finisher.
