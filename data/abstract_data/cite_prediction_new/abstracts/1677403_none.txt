The role of inventory investment as a leading indicator of the US business cycle
This article compares several time series of inventory investment as they relate to overall economic fluctuations. Inventory investment is shown to provide a consistent lead, particularly ahead of downturns in overall business activity. A case is made for retaining changes in inventories-on-hand as part of the U.S. composite index of leading indicators. The predictive power of inventory investment in the context of a recursive downturn probability model is measured, and the computational program used for the related calculations is described.
