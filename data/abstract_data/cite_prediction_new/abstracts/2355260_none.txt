A VERSATILE AUTOMATED SYSTEM FOR THE SPECTROPHOTOMETRIC ANALYSIS OF SINGLE TABLETS
The content uniformity of tableted drugs is recognized as an important aspect of quality control in their manufacture. In order to obtain useful information about the uniformity of drug content from tablet to tablet within a batch, repetitive analyses of single units of the dosage form are necessary. Statistically valid data may require that a relatively large number of analytical results be obtained. It has been recognized that automation of the analytical procedure is a means of facilitating these analyses. Automated colorimetric methods applicable to the determination of a number of drugs in tablets have been developed. A completely automated system has been developed as the result of studies a t CIBA Pharmaceutical Company, Summit, N.J., and the American Instrument Company, Inc., Silver Spring, Md., for the analysis of single tablets (and other solid dosage forms) by UV absorption analysis. Automation has been achieved by the development of suitable hardware for sample handling and the design of an electronic system to program automatically the three basic variables mentioned previously.
