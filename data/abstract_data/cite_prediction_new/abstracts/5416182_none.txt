Fluoride Levels of Drinking Waters of Farm Animal in Iğdir Province, Turkey (1)
Summary The aim of this study was to determine fluoride (F) levels of water consumed by animals in Igdir province located on a volcanic area. As control, water samples were collected from Dogubeyazit for fluorosis and Cappadocia (Kapadokya) for fluorosis free area. Fluoride level measurements were made by ion selective electrode (ISE) system. In general, F levels of Igdir, Dogubeyazit and Kapadokya water samples were 0.292±0.026 mgF/L, 9.460±0.701 mgF/L and 0.303±0.056 mgF/L, respectively. There was a statistically significant difference between Igdir and Dogubeyazit (P 0.05). In conclusion, it was observed that the water F levels consumed by animals were not at danger limits according to fluorosis in Igdir, Turkey. It was also supported by bone fluoride levels and carcass findings..
