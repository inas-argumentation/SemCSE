Tobacco mosaic virus in dividing mesophyll cells of Nicotiana.
Cells in differentiating mesophyll of Nicotiana tabacum L. leaves infected with tobacco mosaic virus divide in the presence of large accumulations of the virus. After the nuclear envelope breaks down during mitosis, scattered virus particles and small aggregates of particles occur among the chromosomes and continue to be associated with them until the late stages of mitosis. When the new daughter nuclei become enclosed in envelopes during late anaphase and telophase, virus particles are usually left outside the nuclear envelope.
