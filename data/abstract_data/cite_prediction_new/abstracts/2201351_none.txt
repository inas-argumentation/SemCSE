If this was in fact the case, infusion of bicarbonate may have compounded the problem by raising blood PCo2. This case presentation demonstrates that measurement of blood Pco2, pH, and standard bicarbonate is mandatory to determine whether the patient has a metabolic or respiratory acidosis, as appropriate treatment will differ according to which type of acidosis is present.
