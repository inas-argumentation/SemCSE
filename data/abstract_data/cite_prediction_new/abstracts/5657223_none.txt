Patient-controlled epidural analgesia for labour in a patient after Ross procedure for congenital bicuspid aortic valve
The Ross procedure involves the replacement of the aortic valve or aortic root with a pulmonary autograft in patients with aortic valve disease, particularly growing children and young adults with active lifestyles (Frigiola et al. 2010). However, concerns regarding progressive dilatation of the neoaortic root (pulmonary autograft) with or without concomitant neoaortic valve regurgitation have been raised in patients who undergo this procedure (Pasquali et al. 2007). To our knowledge, no case reports regarding the anaesthetic management of labour in women with neoaortic root after the Ross procedure have been published. Here, we present the first such case.
