Cogenerated energy recovery from modular refuse incinerators
Burning of municipal refuse in incinerators has become an important alternative to conventional landfill owing to the dual benefits of energy recovery and solid waste disposal. This paper presents a method for thermal-economic appraisal of plants employing modular incinerators for cogeneration of process steam and electricity. A case study of a proposed facility in Chicago is used to illustrate incorporation of the refuse disposal credit and income from sales of steam and electricity as well as incentives dealing with taxes, cogeneration and financing.
