Water-Soluble Methacrylates as Embedding Media for Preparation of Ultra-Thin Sections
AT present, the embedding of preparations for cutting of ultra-thin sections is most frequently carried out by the procedure originally described by Newman et al.1 and later perfected by a number of other authors2–4. This method, however, suffers from the drawback that the embedding media used are, in their monomeric form, immiscible with water and usually good solvents of fats. The preparation must be dehydrated—usually with alcohol or other organic solvent—before embedding and this, together with the solving of fats, may lead to the alteration of biological structures and formation of artefacts.
