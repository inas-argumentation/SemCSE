Effect of thiazolidine-4-carboxylic acid (Thioproline) on sister chromatid exchanges in CHO cells and human lymphocytes, and on the kinetics of human lymphocytes in vitro.
Thioproline is the first organic inducer of reverse transformation of malignant cells in vitro that has been used as a human therapeutic with contradictory results. We report that this compound does not increase SCE, but does depress PHA-induced lymphocytes mitogenesis at approximatively the same concentration reported to induce restoration of contact inhibition of Hela cells. This gives further argument to the definition of thioproline as a reverse inducer.
