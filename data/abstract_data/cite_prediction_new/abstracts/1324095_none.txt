Study of the Statistical Significance of Large ( r ) Network Motifs
Motifs are significantly reoccurring patterns that are used to study networks. Large(r) motifs have not found to be of great significance. This paper compares the significance of large(r) motifs to smaller ones, and determines which is more significant. This was done utilizing the motif detection tool FanMod to find motifs of size three to six, in eleven different networks. The data resulting from the search showed that current methods define significance for motifs were lacking. A visual study of certain significant motifs, found by a weighting system, showed that large(r) motifs were super position of smaller key motifs such as the bifan, along with extraneous nodes. This shows the lack of support for significance for large(r) motifs. There however is more analysis to be done on data that we obtained.
