Incorporation of Time Delayed Measurements in aDiscrete-time Kalman
In many practical systems there is a delay in some of the sensor devices, for instance vision measurements that may have a long processing time. How to fuse these measurements in a Kalman lter is not a trivial problem if the computational delay is critical. Depending on how much time there is at hand, the designer has to make trade oos between optimality and computational burden of the lter. In this paper various methods in the literature along with a new method proposed by the authors will be presented and compared. The new method is based on \extrapolating" the measurement to present time using past and present estimates of the Kalman lter and calculating an optimal gain for this extrapolated measurement.
