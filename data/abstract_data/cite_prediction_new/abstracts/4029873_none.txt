Adipofascial Turn-Over Flap for the Reconstruction of Complicated Soft Tissue Defects of Distal Forearm and Hand
Soft tissue defects of distal forearm and hand present as a challenging problem often requiring immediate coverage because of exposure of bone, joint, blood vessels, nerves, and tendons. Several different local, distant, or free flaps were defined for this purpose, each having its own advantage and disadvantage. Adipofascial turnover flaps provide adequate thin, pliable soft tissue coverage with reliable blood supply. This one-stage operative procedure also allows early ambulation and physical therapy. We present our experience with adipofascial turnover flaps for the reconstruction of complicated distal forearm and hand soft tissue defects. Use of this procedure for hand amputations is stressed as an extended clinical application area of this well-established method.
