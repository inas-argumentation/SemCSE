Higher order flux-limiting methods for steady-state, multidimensional, convection-dominated flow
Numerical modeling of convection suitable for nite volume methods for steady-state, multidimensional, incompressible, turbulent ows is considered. Two methods used to achieve higher order accurate and oscillation-free solutions are discussed, namely ux limiting and variable normalization. A uniied formulation, which brings together most existing higher order monotonicity preserving schemes, is discussed. The uniied formulation allows the inclusion of higher order non-monotone schemes, and a new class of ux limiters is presented. Some numerical results are shown for two-dimensional ows, and the superiority of the ux-limiting schemes is connrmed by comparison with results of the rst order upwind scheme.
