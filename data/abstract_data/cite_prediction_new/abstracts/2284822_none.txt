Cultural approaches of improving public exercise among Iranian women
Women’s participation in sport has great importance due to its useful effects on a society’s health. For such tasks as housekeeping, childcare, and many other tasks, women are facing more hurdles than men to take part in public sport. Part of these obstacles concern cultural and social issues that affect women’s presence and activity in every society.In the current study, after determining inner validity and reliability, an adapted questionnaire resulted from former literature, interview with relevant specialists and managers was distributed among 38 experienced professors and staff managers of public sport. Priorities concerning sociocultural approaches of public sport were considered among the country’s women. Results of the analyzing Friedman test showed that further involvement and attempts toward women’s sport in media, activating and utilizing civil and social organizations to persuade women to exercise, changing perspectives regarding women’s sport in religious leaders and chief officials of the country, improving social security and reducing women’s concern in activitieswithin society, and removing popular false beliefs regarding women’s involvement in sport have allotted to themselves the highest priorities.
