Cultural Heterogeneity of African Immigrant Families in the Delaware Valley
This paper examines the major challenges facing African immigrant families in the Delaware Valley. It focuses on the reciprocal interaction within families and among their interacting communities. The implications of these challenges for social work practice in a framework that recognizes the cultural heterogeneity of African families are discussed. This framework highlights the complexity of cultural diversity competencies required by social workers for providing appropriate services to African families. It may provide a foundation for diversity training for human service professionals.
