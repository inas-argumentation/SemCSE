Contextual communication patterns for vehicular cloud computing: Towards realizing Internet of Things
Through Vehicular Cloud Computing we can push more contextual information nearer to the victim during service entity identification. This paper proposes the use of these hybrid vehicular networks to assist Automated Parking Lot management, as an example, through identification of contextual communication patterns. Taking the example of an Automated Parking Lot Management this work explores the importance of Vehicular Cloud Computing to unearth the latent information available in the context itself which also compensates for the time-invariant, costly and inaccurate information available through distant sources like far-off Information Servers. We finally simulate and compare the results for the Contextual Communication Patterns between a Vehicle and a Road Side Unit to offer Automated Parking Assistance.
