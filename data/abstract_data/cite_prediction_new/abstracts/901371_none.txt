Efficiency of five selective media for recovering coagulase-positive staphylococci from mixed cultures.
DURING the examination of turkey rolls for staphylococci it became apparent that additional information was needed regarding the ability of the plating media to aid in the detection of these microorganisms. Numerous plating media have been developed for isolating and enumerating coagulase-positive staphylococci from various foods. On some media, it is difficult to differentiate staphylococcus colonies from those of other species, especially when the population of these other species is much larger than the population of staphylococci. As a selective agent, some media contain a high percentage of sodium chloride, while others contain tellurite.
