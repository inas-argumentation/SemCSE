Influence of sex, developmental time and food onβ-N-acetylglucosaminidase activity in the rice weevilSitophilus oryzae L.
The effects of some biological parameters on β-N-acetylglucosaminidase activity have been investigated inS. oryzae. There is no significant influence of sex and developmental time on the enzyme activity level, which appears in contrast to be greatly influenced by food (wheat or sorghum). Sorghum contains competitive inhibitors which are almost completely removed after dialysis. Fasting relieves this inhibition very quickly, suggesting that inhibitors act directly at the gut level.
