Linear Transverse Flux Reluctance Machine With Permanent Magnets
A new structure of linear transverse flux machine (TFM) is presented in this paper. It is in fact a variable reluctance linear machine having permanent magnets on the mover. Its structure and its working principle will be discussed. After a preliminary design of the motor a 3D FEM analysis was carried out before optimizing the designed linear motor. The flux densities in the machine and the static characteristics (tangential and normal forces vs. displacement) were computed.
