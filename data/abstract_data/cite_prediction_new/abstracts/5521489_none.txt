Research on Data Processing Algorithm of Data Fusion Simulator
A data processing algorithm of data fusion simulator is proposed in this paper to solve the problem that it is difficult to process the information of target tracks from detectors of mother ship to data fusion simulator. The proposed algorithm is utilized to execute fusion process, form main track list and send comprehensive situation of mother ship imitatively, thereby the value of global correlation decision can be acquired, furthermore, the correlativity between tracks can also be acquired. Finally the difficulty in data processing of data fusion simulator is solved.
