The microwave distributed informative-control system for high-speed Maglev transportation
In this work it's considered the conception of microwave distributed informational-control system for Maglev-monitoring, the principles of construction of a marker-connection waveguide line, as its basic component. The system includes marker-connective main waveguide, arranged along active way structure, with the slots for the communication with microwave-generator, arranged on platform, superfast contactless autogenerative meters of the distance between platform, active way structure and lateral guides walls. These meters (measuring transducers) solves the problem of the determination of the spatial position of the vehicle
