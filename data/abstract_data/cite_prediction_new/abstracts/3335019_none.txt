The Entomophthorales of Israel and their arthropod hosts: Seasonal occurrence
The seasonal occurrence of entomophthoralean host-pathogen combinations and of conidialvs. resting spore production in Israel is presented and discussed. The data recorded during 1968–1979 concern the following species and their recorded hosts:Conidiobolus apiculatus, C. coronatus, C. osmodes, C. thromboides, andConidiobolus spp.;Entomophthora planchoniana;Neozygites fresenii; Erynia erinacea, E. neoaphidis, E. nouryi, E. occidentalis, E. phalloides, E. phytonomi andE. radicans.
