ON GEODESIC FLOWS MODELED BY EXPANSIVE FLOWS UP TO TIME-PRESERVING SEMI-CONJUGACY
Given a smooth compact surface without focal points and of higher genus, it is shown that its geodesic flow is semi-conjugate to a continuous expansive flow with a local product structure. Using the fact that the semi-conjugation preserves time-parametrization, it is concluded that the geodesic flow has a unique measure of maximal entropy.
