DREAMS OF SUBJECTS WITH BILATERAL HIPPOCAMPAL LESIONS
INTRODUCTION Experimental evidence has been recently accumulated suggesting that dreams represent a partial perception of the visual concomitants of a potentially continuously ongoing process of encoding longterm memories (Torda ( 1967 a, b, 1968 a, b, c, 1969 a, b) ). Dreams appear to be memory traces of old and new events woven into ever changing stories under emotional impact. The manifest content of dreams is heavily loaded with percepts originating in recent daily experiences (Greenberg & Leidcrman ( 1966) ). In order to gain a better understanding of the nature of dreams, it occurred, therefore, to study the dreams of subjects with amnesia for recent events.
