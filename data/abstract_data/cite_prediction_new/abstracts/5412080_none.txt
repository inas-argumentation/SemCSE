FARM CREDIT AND THE FACTORS AFFECTING THE UPTAKE OF LOANS BY FARMERS IN IRAN / CRÉDIT AGRICOLE ET FACTEURS INFLUENÇANT L'INCIDENCE DE PRÊTS PAR LES FERMIERS IRANIENS
A notable feature of Iranian agriculture is the predominance of small and poor farmers. Therefore improving small farmers' productivity is an important objective of economic policies. Provision of agricultural credit has been a focus of attention in this regard. The purpose of this paper is to examine the impact of credit and the contribution of different characteristics of farmers towards their borrowing behaviour. The principal finding from this analysis suggests that the use of credit is associated with higher levels of farm output and income. A Multivariate Discriminant Analysis (MDA) methodology is used to identify the factors that are likely to influence farmers' borrowing behaviour significantly. The application of discriminant analysis in this study has made an important contribution over simple univariate analysis in that it identified the impact of different variables simultane ously. The analysis suggests that credit from Agricultural Bank in Iran is mainly directed towards large size, high income and more educated farmers. Therefore, increasing an uptake of loans by small size and low income farmers would help improve not only their productivity but also income equity.
