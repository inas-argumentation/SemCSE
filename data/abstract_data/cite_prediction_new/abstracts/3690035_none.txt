Purification and properties of rod outer segment kinases.
Publisher Summary This chapter discusses the purification and properties of rod outer segment kinases. Bovine rod outer segments (ROS) contain two general classes of protein kinases that differ in substrate specificity and mechanism of activation. One is rhodopsin kinase (RK), which catalyzes the phosphorylation of the membrane protein rhodopsin in a light-dependent manner. The other is the cyclic nucleotide-dependent protein kinase (CNPK) in ROS. The activity of CNPK in ROS is measured by the transfer of phosphate from adenosine triphosphate (ATP) to a protein substrate, usually histone or a “protein kinase substrate” prepared from rat small intestine. Phosphorylation of endogenous rhodopsin was measured with a ROS homogenate or a membrane suspension that possesses endogenous RK.
