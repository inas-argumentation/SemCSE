Size-miniaturized bandpass filter made of folded stepped-impedance resonators with adjacent and nonadjacent coupling for selectivity enhancement
In this paper, a size-miniaturized microstrip bandpass filter with quasi-elliptic function responses is presented. The proposed filter consists of four A/2 folded stepped-impedance resonators (FSIRs), which can save nearly 50% circuit size compared with its corresponding conventional SIR counterpart. By utilizing the adjacent and nonadjacent coupling topology, three different cross-coupling path-pairs are built up, so that three different transmission zeros (TZs) can be generated, which will contribute to high sideband selectivity and wide stopband range for the proposed filter. In order to verify the aforementioned concept, a prototype of the proposed filter is implemented, with its measured results are in good agreement with the simulated ones. Meanwhile, the sideband roll-off rejection of the proposed filter is better than 50 dB within 500 MHz away from the edge of passband, indicating a high selectivity. Moreover, with a stopband rejection better than 34 dB, its upper stopband can be as high as 12 GHz, i.e., over the third harmonic of the passband. As compared to some reported similar works, the proposed filter exhibits a size-reduction over 23%, which illustrates its suitability for the microwave system integration and packaging application.
