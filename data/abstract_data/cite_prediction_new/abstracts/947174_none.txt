In-plane Hole Transport in Polypyrrole-diamond In terface
Sur face of intrinsic monocrystalline diamond is selectively terminated by hydrogen and oxygen atoms to create electrically conductive microscopic square with contacts in corners. Polypyrrole is electrochemically deposited onto the H- terminated square. The contacts are used to characterize in-plane resistivity (van der Pauw configuration) and Hall mobility of charge carriers in the PPy-diamond system. These experimental results are used to discuss the transport path of the charge carriers.
