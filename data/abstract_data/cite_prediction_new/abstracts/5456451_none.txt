Construction et exploitation d'un corpus français pour l'analyse de sentiment
Building and exploiting a French corpus for sentiment analysis This work introduces a French corpus for sentiment analysis. We describe the construction and organization of the corpus. We then apply machine learning techniques to automatically predict whether a text is positive or negative (the opinion classification task). Two techniques are used : logistic regression and classification based onSupport Vector Machines(SVM).
