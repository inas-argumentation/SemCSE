Phase Transitions and Mineralogy of the Lower Mantle
Recent technological progress in both high-pressure experiments and ab initio calculations has provided important constraints on the composition and constitution of the deep mantle. Here, we briefly review the advancement of these techniques over the last two decades and summarize the results on the phase transitions of minerals under lower mantle P , T conditions obtained by these methods. The phase transitions and associated density changes in mantle rocks and subducted slabs under these conditions are also summarized and are used to discuss the origin of seismologically observed discontinuities, dynamics, and fate of subducted slabs, and the nature of the D″ layer in the lower mantle.
