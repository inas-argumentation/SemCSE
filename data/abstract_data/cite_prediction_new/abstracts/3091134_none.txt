A NEW PROOF OF A THEOREM CONCERNING DECOMPOSABLE GROUPS
We give an elementary proof of the following result: If G is a compact non-zero Abelian group with dual isomorphic to a subgroup of Q, such that U u (- U) = G \ G(2) and U n (- U) = 0 for some open subset U c G, where G(2) = {a E G: 2a = O}, then G is topologically isomorphic with T.
