Microstructure and Interface Phase Formation in ${\rm YBa}_{2}{\rm Cu}_{3}{\rm O}_{7-\delta}$  Prepared by an MOD Process
Understanding the evolution of microstructure in YBCO (YBa<sub>2</sub>Cu<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7-zeta</sub>) is critical to optimizing the process for production of high quality superconducting wire for commercial applications. Therefore, microstructure and interfaces have been investigated in fully reacted YBa<sub>2</sub>Cu<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7-zeta</sub>, prepared by the metalorganic deposition (MOD) process, and deposited on CeO<sub>2</sub>/YSZ/Y<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>/NiW. YBCO nucleates on CeO<sub>2</sub> with a specific orientation relationship where [011] CeO<sub>2</sub> || [100] YBCO and (200) CeO<sub>2</sub> || (002) YBCO, and with the [001] YBCO axis parallel to the film growth direction. A 25-50 nm thick reaction layer of BaCeO<sub>3</sub> was observed at the YBCO : CeO<sub>2</sub> interface. Formation of this layer releases a considerable amount of Cu and Y into the YBCO matrix. Details of the interfacial phase formation in the MOD-processed YBCO films are presented in this paper.
