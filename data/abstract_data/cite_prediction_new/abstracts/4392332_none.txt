Nonparametric multiple comparison procedures for ordered parameters in balanced incomplete blocks
In the randomized complete block designs, all treatments appear in each block. When blocks are not large enough to accommodate all of the treatments or when restrictions imposed by practical considerations prevent the use of all of the treatments in each block, then a balanced incomplete block design provides an effective alternative. These designs provide an effective method of reducing heterogeneity of the experimental units as well as provide the ability to compare any pair of treatments with the same precision. In this article, a distribution-free Scheffe-type monotone contrast procedure and a Tukey-type pairwise comparison procedure under the simple order are developed for the balanced incomplete block designs. The distribution-free χ rank 2 statistic for detecting simple order alternatives in complete blocks is extended to the balanced incomplete blocks. The critical values of this statistic are required for the Scheffe-type monotone contrast procedure. Exact tables of the critical values, as well as approximations based on large scale simulations are generated for a total of 41 balanced incomplete block designs for the χ rank 2 statistic as well as the Tukey-type range statistic based on ranks. It is demonstrated that the limiting critical values for the χ 2 -type statistic provide poor approximations of the small sample critical values, and hence result in conservative tests in majority of cases. These procedures are applied to a data set resulting from a balanced incomplete block design.
