Entrepreneurs: Invent a new brand name or revive an old one?
The decision that entrepreneurs must make as to whether their firm should acquire a dormant name brand or create a new one is investigated.Brand recognition is often a key factor in the buying decisions of consumers.To aid in the analysis, perspectives gathered from interviews with ten executives who successfully acquired dormant brands and relaunched them into the market are utilized. In order for an entrepreneurial venture to be successful, there must be a well thought out market strategy and the capital necessary to promote the brand.In acquiring a dormant name brand, consideration must be given to whether the brand is still recognized and the values previously associated with it.There are two ways in which to market the brand: nostalgic remembrance or repositioning the brand. The executives suggest using a marketing scheme that combines both approaches and designate one method as primary.Acquiring a brand with the right characteristics, for the right price, and knowing how to successfully relaunch the brand are three of the keys for an entrepreneur.Brand revival can prove to be a very cost-effective method for entrepreneurs who choose brands with a positive history and continued relevance.
