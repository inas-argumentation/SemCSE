Behavior Study of Entropy in a Digital Image through an Iterative Algorithm of the Mean Shift Filtering
Image segmentation is a critical step in computer vision tasks constituting an essential issue for pattern recognition and visual interpretation. In this paper, we study the behavior of entropy in digital images through an iterative algorithm of mean shift filtering. The order of a digital image in gray levels is defined. The behavior of Shannon entropy is analyzed and then compared, taking into account the number of iterations of our algorithm, with the maximum entropy that could be achieved under the same order. The use of equivalence classes it induced, which allow us to interpret entropy as a hyper-surface in real mdimensional space. The difference of the maximum entropy of order n and the entropy of the image is used to group the the iterations, in order to caractrizes the performance of the algorithm.
