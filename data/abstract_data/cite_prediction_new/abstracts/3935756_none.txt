A game-theoretic approach for communication-free voltage-VAR optimization
Increasing penetration of distributed energy resources (DERs) has given rise to both opportunities and challenges for voltage regulation in distribution systems. Limited communication infrastructure in current distribution systems complicates this voltage regulation task with stringent budget on sensing and communication. In this paper, we develop a communication-free algorithm for DERs to optimize system voltage profiles by managing their reactive power (VAR) outputs. Under the payoff-based learning framework from game theory, the proposed approach can achieve the Pareto optimum using only instantaneous local measurements at no information exchange among DERs. The efficacy of the approach is corroborated through numerical simulations.
