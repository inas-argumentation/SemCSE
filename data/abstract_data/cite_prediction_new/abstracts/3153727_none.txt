A Compact Size, Multi Octave Bandwidth Power Amplifier, Using LDMOS Transistors
A broadband push-pull power amplifier is presented that operates from 30MHz to 1000MHz. Coaxial cable transmission line baluns are used to split and combine the signal differentially. The balun acts also as a broadband 2:1 impedance transformer. The matching circuit consists of a piece of coaxial cable and some lumped elements. Using a packaged laterally diffused metal oxide semiconductor (LDMOS) transistor, a one dB compression output power of 39 dBm and a gain of 10 dB have been measured at 900 MHz. The input return loss is less than -10 dB across the entire band. The transistor is not internally matched and broadband matching techniques are used to match the transistor over multi octave bandwidth.
