Changing human resources to make flexible manufacturing systems (FMS) successful
As manufacturers face a more competitive environment, they need to meet productivity, quality, and flexibility requirements simultaneously. To meet these requirements and remain competitive, many manufacturers have upgraded their manufacturing technology by acquiring flexible manufacturing systems (FMS). The evidence, however, suggests that because FMS are limited in what they can do, payoffs from investing in FMS do not materialize unless human resources lever FMS. This paper identifies human resources requirements to lever FMS, human resources management (HRM) practices that facilitate having these human resources, and organizational values and beliefs congruent with these human resources and HRM practices. Moreover, because to have these human resources, HRM practices, values, and beliefs requires changes that are both major and time consuming, this paper suggests activities useful to manage these changes successfully.
