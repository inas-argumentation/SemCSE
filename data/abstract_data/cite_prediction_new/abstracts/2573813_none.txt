Rapid fluorometric or spectrophotometric determination of isoniazid.
Aromatic acid hydrazides and β-diketones condense in aqueous solution and the products form yellow anions in the presence of strong bases. This reaction can be used for the rapid determination of isoniazid in biological materials. A colorimetric procedure can determine less than 1 μg in 1 ml serum, and a fluorometric procedure as little as 0.005 μg in 0.5 ml serum.
