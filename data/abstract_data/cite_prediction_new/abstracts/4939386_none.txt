Agriculture-Environment Series : Sorghum / Millet and Environment in South Asia & Sub-Saharan Africa
DISEASES: Breeding for early maturation exposes flowers to moisture, allowing mildews to thrive. Downy mildew is a continuing threat to pearl millet due to changing races of the disease; this disease evolution may hasten with widespread fungicide use. IRRIGATION: Growing sorghum or millet is often an adaptation to water constraints where other crops might fail. Sorghum grows best with early water access through irrigation. Millet performs best with water access throughout the season. Pre-Production Production Post-Production
