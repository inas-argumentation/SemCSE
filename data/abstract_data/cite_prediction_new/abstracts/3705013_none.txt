TRANSACT 2008 D READLOCKS : Efficient Deadlock Detection for STM
We present DREADLOCKS, an efficient new shared-memory spin lock that actively detects deadlocks. Instead of spinning on a Boolean value, each thread spins on the lock owner’s per-thread digest, a compact representation of a portion of the locks’ waits-for graph. Digests can be implemented either as bit vectors (for small numbers of threads) or as Bloom filters (for larger numbers of threads). Updates to digests are propagated dynamically as locks are acquired and released. DREADLOCKS can be applied to any spin lock algorithm that allows threads to time out. Preliminary experimental results show that DREADLOCKS outperform timeouts under many circumstances, and almost never do worse.
