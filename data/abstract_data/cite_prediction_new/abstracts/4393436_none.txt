CHỦ QUAN TÍNH VÀ KHÁCH QUAN TÍNH TRONG HỆ THỐNG XƯNG HÔ VIỆT VÀ ANH
In this article, working definitions of subjectivity and objectivity are offered, and T-V paradigm of addressing terms/dyads in languages discussed. Expressions of subjectivityobjectivity in Vietnamese and English addressing systems are critically analysed on the basis of primary and secondary sources.
