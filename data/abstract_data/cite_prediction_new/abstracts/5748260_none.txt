A Study of Legible Braille Patterns on Capsule Paper : Diameters of Braille Dots and Their Interspaces on Original Ink-printed Paper
A legibility experiment was conducted using eleven braille readers in order to determine the suitable ranges of braille dot diameters and their interdot spaces on the original image for capsule paper braille. The obtained result shows that the reading of capsule paper braille with few mistakes in the dot diameter range of 1.17 to 1.43 mm and interdot space range of 1.05 to 1.15 times the standard Japanese interdot space took a short time and was rated high. On the other hand, the reading of braille with many mistakes at a large dot diameter and a narrow interdot space took a long time and was rated low. The three-dimensional measurement of braille dot shapes clarified that difficulties in reading braille under these conditions stem from the fusion of dots due to the expansion characteristic of capsule paper.
