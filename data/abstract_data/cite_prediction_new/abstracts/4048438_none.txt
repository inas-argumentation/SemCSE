State-of-the-art in the design and manufacture of low acceleration sensitivity resonators
This paper reviews the design and manufacture of an improved quad relief mount (QRM) resonator. It discusses the mechanical resonance and how the lead-frame structure was modified to push the mechanical resonance well above 2 kHz. The results of a design of experiments (DOE) are discussed and show that the azimuthal mounting angle (/spl Psi/) has a dramatic effect on the g-sensitivity performance of the QRM resonator. Finally, preliminary results on a QRM design that uses a larger diameter blank (0.390") are compared to the current 0.320" QRM design.
