Design and impact of an ERP and automation model in the administration sectors
Business source outlining is the most essential systems which is used across many organizations and across various functional areas like finance, organisations performance, risk, decision making, and automation. The ERP systems have changed the traditional methods of providing information in the organizations gradually. The organizations with the ERP systems installed can easily reduce the organization risk and increase the performance, production and profit. The ERP systems with functional and operational modules are found to be having more impact on the organizational risk reduction. Since The ERP system automates the decision making system with well organised information it also has greater impact on the organizations business. In this paper the design and the impact of ERP and its automation on organization finance, production, performance, risk administration, decision making are discussed.
