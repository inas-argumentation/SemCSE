Vibration-Assisted Machining Research at Fraunhofer IPT – Diamond Turning and Precision Grinding
INTRODUCTION First investigations on ultrasonic assisted grinding were published in 1956 by Colwell [1] and on ultrasonic assisted turning in 1964 by Dohmen [2]. Since then a lot of approaches were made regarding oscillation assisted manufacturing processes. Either the tool or the workpiece were superimposed with an ultrasonic oscillation. All these approaches showed significant advantages regarding reduction of tool wear for ultrasonic assisted turning and achievable surface qualities [3, 4, 8] or reduction of process forces regarding ultrasonic assisted grinding [5, 6, 7].
