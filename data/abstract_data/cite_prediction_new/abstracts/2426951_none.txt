Production of two non-conjugate leptoquarks in e−e− collisions
We study the production of scalar and vector leptoquarks in e − e − scattering. We use the most general couplings to the known fermions which are dimensionless, baryon and lepton number conserving, and SU (3) c ⊗ SU (2) L ⊗ U (1) Y invariant. Expressions are presented for production cross sections and predictions are given for future linear colliders.
