Combining Local Search with the Multilevel Paradigm for the Traveling Salesman Problem
Multilevel refinement schemes refer to the process of dividing large and difficult problems into smaller ones, which are hopefully much easier to solve, and then work backward towards the solution of the original problem, using a solution from a previous level as a starting solution at the next level. In this paper, we present a multilevel genetic algorithm and a multilevel construction algorithm for the traveling salesman problem.The resulting two multilevel algorithms start by coarsening the original problem into a sequence of smaller problems using a coarsening scheme. Thereafter a tour is determined at the smallest problem and is projected back to the original problem by going through a refinement phase at each intermediate level. We present experimental results to demonstrate the effectiveness of these two multilevel algorithms.
