A rapid method for the preparation of the neutrophil fraction of granulocytes from human blood by centrifugation on isotonic nycodenz gradients
SummaryA rapid method for the preparation of neutrophils of the granulocyte fraction of human blood is described. A leucocyte-rich fraction is loaded onto preformed isotonic Nycodenz gradients, formed by mixing a stock isotonic Nycodenz solution with a NaCl diluent solution. After low speed centrifugation a neutrophil fraction of high viability, yield and purity can be isolated from the gradients.
