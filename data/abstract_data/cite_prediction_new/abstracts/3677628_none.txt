The Analysis of Ultracapacitor Charging Efficiency
From the ultracapacitor theoretical charging model, the influence of charging efficiency on constant current and constant voltage charging mode is studied in the paper. According to the actual environment, the different charging modes of the ultracapacitor are tested with different current. Through the analysis of the test data, the validity of the model is verified by the comparison of the test result and theoretical analysis. Finally, the best charging mode for ultracapacitor is given.
