Pulmonary amyloidosis: treatment with laser therapy and systemic steroids.
A 47-year-old housewife presented with a 3-month history of right-sided pleuritic chest pain, repeated colds and chest infections, 2 stone weight loss, poor appetite and night sweats. She was a smoker of 3 cigarettes day ’ but had smoked 20-40 cigarettes dayi. Six years previously, she had been investigated for haemoptysis and had a bronchoscopy which showed bronchial mucosal inflammation but no endobronchial abnormality. In her past medical history, she had whooping cough as a child, a hysterectomy and recurrent urinary tract infections. In her family history, her mother had Crohn’s disease and her father had renal disease and died in his thirties. Physical examination was unremarkable. Chest radiograph showed right upper lobe shadowing with loss of volume and a prominent right hilum (Plate 1). A computed tomographic (CT) scan showed a soft tissue mass around the right hilum extending into the precarinal space (Plate 2). There was some calcification within the mass.
