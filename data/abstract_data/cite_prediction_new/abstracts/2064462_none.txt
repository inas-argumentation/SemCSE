Analysis of In-Line Process Parameters of the Unity Gain Frequency of HKMG Bulk FinFET Devices
This research explores the electrical characteristics of the unity gain frequency (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${F}_{t}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>) in relation to the experimental in-line process parameters of 16-nm high-<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\kappa $ </tex-math></inline-formula> metal gate bulk fin-type field effect transistor devices. Because <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${F}_{t}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> is dependent on the transconductance (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${G}_{m}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>) and effective gate capacitance (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${C}_{\textsf {gg}}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>), a sensitivity analysis of these two factors is applied to determine key in-line process parameters. The engineering results of this letter indicate that <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${G}_{m}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> and <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${C}_{\textsf {gg}}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> are significantly fluctuated by six in-line process parameters, including the gate length, fin height, high-<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\kappa $ </tex-math></inline-formula>/interfacial layer thickness, source/drain (S/D) proximity, S/D depth, and gate height. These six parameters fluctuate <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${F}_{t}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> at the same time, with <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${G}_{m}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> as the dominant factor.
