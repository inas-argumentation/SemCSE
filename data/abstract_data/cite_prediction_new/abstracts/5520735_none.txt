INSTANTANEOUS POWER FACTOR CORRECTION IN THREE-PHASE USING POWER FACTOR PREREGULATOR IN CONVERTERS
In this paper a novel three phase power -factor correction scheme is proposed using two single phase PFC modules. A Power Factor Preregulator (PFP) is used to provide soft switching . In this approach the three phase input is first transformed to two phase by means of bifilar winding transformer . Two standard single phase PFC modules are then employed to process the two phase power to dc output. The proposed approach has simplified control and high performance features. KeyWords: Power factor correction , PFP, Soft switching .
