OPTIMAL HEATING CONTROL TO PREVENT SOLID DEPOSITS IN PIPELINES
Heat transfer analysis in oil and gas pipelines is of major importance for the prediction and prevention of paraffinic deposits an d hydrate formations, which can interrupt the oil and gas flow and result in large financial losses. This is specially the case for deep sea pipelines used in offshore produc tion. In such pipeline, hydrates can be formed, even at relatively high temperatures wit hin the oil-gas-water mixture pumped from the production wells, due to the high p ressures involved. Traditional methods to prevent paraffinic deposits and hydrate formations are generally based on thermal insulation, depressurization of the line, iof hot dead oil, pigging and injection of chemical inhibitors. Recently, active heating systems have been under study to maintain the fluid temperature above a critical value in order to avoid the formation of solid deposits. The main objective of this paper is to examine an optimal control approach for a typical heating system during shutdo wn conditions. A quadratic cost functional is minimized through the solution Riccat is equation.
