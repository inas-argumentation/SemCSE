Spontaneous portal‐right renal vein shunt in portal hypertension.
In portal hypertension, spontaneous portosystemic collaterals most commonly via esophageal varices, left lienorenal vein shunts, and from a recanalized umbilical vein. Other relatively common shunts include the inferior mesenteric vein to the inferior hemorrhoidal vein and gastric varices. We report the color Doppler sonographic and CT findings in a case of a main portal vein to a right renal vein collateral occurring spontaneously in portal hypertension.
