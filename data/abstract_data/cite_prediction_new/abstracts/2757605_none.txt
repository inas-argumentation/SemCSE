Vibration Analysis of a Sensor-Integrated Ball Bearing
This paper presents an analysis of the vibrational behavior of a deep groove ball bearingwith a structurally integrated force sensor. The miniaturized force sensor, accommodatedwithin a slot on the bearing’s outer ring, provides on-line condition monitoring capabilityto the bearing. Analytical and ﬁnite element models were developed to predict the sensoroutput due to bearing dynamic load and rotational speed variations. Experimental studieswere conducted on a ball bearing to validate the analytical and numerical solutions.Good agreement was found between the model-predicted sensor outputs and the experi-mental results.
