Response to Comment on Vassy et al. Polygenic Type 2 Diabetes Prediction at the Limit of Common Variant Detection. Diabetes 2014;63:2172–2182

First, our clinical and polygenic prediction models do not include time-varying assessments of known risk factors such as BMI and fasting glucose (2).
