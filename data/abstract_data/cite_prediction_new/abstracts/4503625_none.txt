DESIGN OF COMPACT COUPLED MICROSTRIP LINE BAND PASS FILTER WITH IMPROVED STOPBAND CHARACTERISTICS
A compact symmetrical band-pass fllter design using coupled microstrip line is presented in this paper. The microstrip line sections connected to the two input and output ports of the fllter structure are printed over the Defected Ground Structure (DGS). The proposed symmetrical structure ofiers a simple and compact design and exhibits improved stop-band characteristics in comparison to the conventional coupled microstrip line fllter structure. The prototype model of the proposed fllter structure is developed and tested. The measured results are found to be in good agreement with the simulation results.
