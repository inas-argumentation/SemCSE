Chitozyme : First Peroxidase-like Activity of Chitosan 2 for Multiplexed Visual Detection of H 2 O 2 , Glucose 3 and Lactate on Paper-based Device 4
Visual read-out diagnostics tools are promising candidates for field applicable medical 10 devices. Current colorimetric biosensors require introduction of natural enzymes or nanozymes, 11 which has some serious drawbacks for practical applications. Chitosan, a natural polymer, 12 provides safe and efficient compound in medical and pharmaceutical technology. Herein, we 13 report on a simple, cost-efficient, field-portable, environmental friendly and ultra-sensitive 14 multiplex detection platform based on peroxidase-like activity of chitosan in the presence of 15 3,3',5,5'-Tetramethylbenzidine (TMBZ) and H2O2. This straight forward signal amplification 16 strategy was successfully applied to detect H2O2, glucose and lactate with the limit of detection 17 (LOD) of 2.64 pM, 0.104 μM and 2.8 nM respectively, represents the lowest LOD of H2O2, glucose 18 and lactate with visual read-out. The chitosan-based assay performance was also retained in 19 complex biological media for glucose and lactate detection. Furthermore, the proposed assay was 20 successfully demonstrated as a paper-based colorimetric biosensor. Most importantly, the 21 simplicity, biocompatibility and sensitivity of the proposed assay will open new doors for 22 instrument free naked eye visual detection of H2O2, glucose and lactate detection. 23
