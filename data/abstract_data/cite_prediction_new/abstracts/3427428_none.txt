Schema Reasoning and Semantic Representation for Citation Semantic Link Network
Citation network lacks the semantics and can not reason. At present, research on citation network has mostly used scientometrics method combined with statistical physics and social network analysis approach to research and analyze citation network structure. The limited citation information is acquired, however, and efficiency is not high. In order to sufficiently and efficiently acquire citation information, we propose the schema of citation semantic link network (CSLN) by combining semantic link network with citation network. Schema of CSLN not only makes citation networks to have semantics and reasoning ability, but also can gain the potential citation semantic link in network and the new semantic relationship between references according to citation semantic link reasoning rules. Schema of CSLN provides a more efficient method than the traditional schema for the searching and retrieval of citation network and the research of complex networks.
