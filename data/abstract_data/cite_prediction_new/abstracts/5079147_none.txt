A p p N o te 1 1 / 20 12 Fully Automated Analysis of Doping Substances in Equine Urine by Disposable Pipette Extraction ( DPX ) coupled to GC / QqQ-MS
This application note describes a fully automated analysis method for selected equine doping compounds in equine urine. A GERSTEL MultiPurpose Sampler (MPS) with Disposable Pipette Extraction (DPX) option is employed for extraction and cleanup. After gas chromatographic separation the analytes are detected by a triple-quadrupole mass spectrometer (QqQ-MS). The method is rugged, provides an adequate cleanup of the complex sample matrix and shows good limits of detection, from below 0.1 to just under 10 ng/mL for the various analytes that are determined.
