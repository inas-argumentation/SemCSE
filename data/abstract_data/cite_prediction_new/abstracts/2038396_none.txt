The action of crude bacterial alpha-amylase on tropocollagen. A case of strictly limited proteolysis.
Crude bacterial α-amylase had no detectable proteolytic activity on the N-terminal telopeptide region of tropocollagen under our standard conditions (pH 5.3, 4°, 96 h, enzyme; substrate ratio 1:300). However at higher temperatures, 18°, higher pH values, e.g. 7.0, and at higher E : S ratios, e.g. 1:10, a limited proteolysis took place which resulted in the partial conversion of β- to α-chains and this could be inhibited by phenylmethylsulphonylfluoride. Less than one new N-terminal residue per molecule of tropocollagen resulted from this proteolytic digestion carried out at 4° pH 5.3 for 96h with E : S 1:1. No change could be detected in the fingerprint pattern of pepsin released peptides from tropocollagen and crude bacterial α-amylase treated tropocollagen ( E : S 1:10). The reconstituted fibrils obtained from tropocollagen treated with the crude enzymes ( E : S 1:1) appeared to be normal type fibrils as distinct from the modified forms of fibrils obtained from tropocollagen preparations in which either the N- or C-terminal telopeptides had been removed.
