A Structural Investigation of Salesforce and Price Competition in the Prescription Drug Industry
This paper proposes and estimates a structural model of salesforce compensation and pricing in a market characterized by oligopolistic competition. In particular the theoretical model describes the interaction between the physician, patient, salesperson and firm in one cohesive framework. The paper then proceeds to propose some interesting research and policy questions that are relevant to managers and academics alike. We use comprehensive real data on market shares, detailing effort and prices and implement sophisticated econometric methods to estimate the structural model and obtain results. These results provide strong evidence of risk and effort aversion on the part of the salesperson, forward looking dynamic behavior on the part of all parties involved and a strong influence of marketing activity of prescription drug choice. The estimates also allow us to compute profit margins, projected salary and commission rate estimates and understand how the key constructs move over time. The paper contributes to the understanding of the dynamics of the pharmaceutical industry, salesforce compensation, prescription behavior and pricing.
