User Perception of Quality of Distributed Multimedia
Quality of Perception (QoP) is a measure of perceptual quality of multimedia from a user point of view, whereas, Quality of Service (QoS) is a measure of the low-level parameters of a multimedia system infrastructure. QoP of multimedia depends on several system level (or QoS) parameters such as frame rate, network delay, jitter, data-loss, distortion, region of interest, display type, audio-video synchronization (lip sync), etc. Perceptual quality models are very useful as are the QoS models in determining the effectiveness and success of a distributed multimedia system, especially when the resources are constrained. Developing QoP models is a challenging effort; it involves not only in-depth study of system level technicalities but also an understanding of the user perception of the multimedia presentation. In our attempt, we hope to review and critique some of current approaches to the study of perceptual quality of multimedia. It is convincing that with a better understanding of user perspective, system level parameters can be tuned to achieve best performance.
