Children in the Mental Deficiency Hospitals of Glasgow and Argyll
A review is made of the children under the age of 15 years occupying mental deficiency beds for the population of Glasgow and Argyll, Scotland, at January 1 1967. Of 269 children (1: 1,000 population under age 15 years) 253 are severely subnormal and of these 60 per cent are idiots. 33 per cent had epilepsy. The children mostly come from average homes and in 70 per cent of cases their relatives continue to show an active interest in them. Thirty‐two of the children were illegitimate and 58 had their admission to hospital accelerated for social reasons.
