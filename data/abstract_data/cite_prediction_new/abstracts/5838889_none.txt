Numerical simulations of combustion in a lean stratified propane-air mixture
Direct numerical simulation (DNS) of combustion in globally lean non-homogeneous propane-air mixtures has been performed for several initial distributions of non-homogeneities. Results have shown a strong influence of the scale and asymmetry of this distribution on both the combustion efficiency and thermal NO production, in accordance with experimental results. The simulations also have made possible a quantitative assessment of flamelet modeling for the primary flame, which yielded remarkably good results.
