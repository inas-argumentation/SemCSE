The investigation on soft sensor technique of sinter mixture moisture
Measurement about sinter mixture moisture is an important and indispensable part. In recent years, appeared in a variety of new moisture measuring devices, but each measurement method has to adapt to situations and limitations. In this paper, the analysis and research based on sintering technology and sintering process parameters, determine the sinter mixture moisture soft sensor auxiliary variable and soft sensor model modeling method. The results show that sinter mixture moisture soft sensor technique is effective, also the technique with high measuring accuracy.
