Is Antibiotic Therapy of Mice and Humans Useful in Escherichia coli O157 : H7 Enteritis?
E. Seidman Division of Gastroenterology, Mucosal Immunology Laboratory, Sainte-Justine Hospital, University of Montreal, Montreal, Canada Hemolytic uremic syndrome was first reported in 1955 by Gasser et al. [1]. Its association with verotoxinproducing Escherichia coli enteritis was subsequently established 30 years later [2]. Epidemic and sporadic forms of hemolytic uremic syndrome continue to constitute a public health problem of significant concern in many countries. Nevertheless, to date no therapy has been shown to be beneficial for the prevention of Escherichia coli O157 :H7-associated hemolytic uremic syndrome. Indeed, very few clinical trials have been performed [3, 4].
