PSYCHOGENIC NONEPILEPTIC “SEIZURES” OR “ATTACKS”? IT’S NOT JUST SEMANTICS: SEIZURES To the Editor: I agree with Dr. Benbadis.1 There is no advantage to keeping the word “seizures” in the phrase “psychogenic nonepileptic seizures.” But I also disagree with using the word “attack.” Who wants to have an attack? In addition, telling patients that they have “psychogenic” seizures can be deleterious to the patient– physician relationship. I think that the best term to use is “nonepileptic spells.” It is short, simple, and does not have a negative connotation.
