The method of mutual influence object getting along with two parties based on repetitive game control
To meet the goal of promoting the effectiveness of game analysis towards the governmental regulations and the rules and regulations for enterprise safety, a game analysis method of governmental regulations and the rules and regulations for enterprise safety is proposed on the basis of distributed power and controlled by repeated game. First and foremost, based on the Nash negotiation and the limited repeated game, the distributed repeated game control scheme is established, and the game strategy is effectively promoted. Secondly, on the basis of proposed game analysis, the game analysis model for governmental regulations and the rules and regulations for enterprise safety is established, and the analysis of the relations between governmental regulations and the rules and regulations for enterprise safety is carried out. Lastly, the through the analytical investigation carried out towards the practical cases, it is indicated that the proposed method can be more precise, and this method can be applied for guiding the perfection of enterprises’ regulation and promoting the enterprise benefit.
