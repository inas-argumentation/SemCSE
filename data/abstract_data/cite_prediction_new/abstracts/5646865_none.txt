A study of application of rough sets theory in vegetation classification of remote sensing image
During the recent years, the topic of image classification has become more complex but inspiring with the development of subjects such as nerve network, and genetic algorithm. Under such a condition, many scholars are working toward a more intelligent method of image classification. This study provides a review of latest development and achievement of RS, combined with artificial nerve network, application to vegetation classification of image. A case study is also conducted in our analysis. Keywords-rough sets;remote sensing; vegetation classification
