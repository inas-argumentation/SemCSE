Where quants go wrong: a dozen basic lessons in commonsense for quants and risk managers and the traders who rely on them
Quants and risk managers keep making the same mistakes over and over again. Each time this happens the losses increase in value. A small amount of commonsense and some basic mathematics can stop this happening.
