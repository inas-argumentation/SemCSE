Doses in excess of 10-6 mol and up to 10-4 mol elicited increases in all parameters. The maximum increase in each parameter was consistently less than that produced by histamine. For the increases, the potency of 2-pyridylethylamine relative to histamine (100%) on all parameters, was about 0.2%. This could indicate histamine H2-receptor stimulation, as 2pyridylethylamine has been shown to have about 0.2% the activity of histamine on a histamine H2-receptor system (Durant et al., 1975). The results indicate the importance of histamine H2receptors in the mediation of the changes in cardiac function produced by histamine in this preparation. The involvement of histamine Hl-receptors is less clear from this study although the data suggests that they may mediate a selective cardiac depression.
