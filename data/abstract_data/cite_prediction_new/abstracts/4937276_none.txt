Design of Automatic Controller for Laser Manufacture Based on Intelligent Vision
In order to improve the stability and robustness of the laser manufacturing automation controller, this paper proposes a design method of intelligent visual laser manufacturing automation controller based on laser tracking system. Manufacturing automation controller static measurement and dynamic tracking using intelligent visual dynamic, kinematic calibration of controller adopts the circle point method, according to the geometric parameters of the visual controller the information calculation and parameter estimation of automatic control system, laser manufacturing automation controller D-H parameter model, according to the parameters of D-H controller parameters feedback compensation, improve control stability, optimize the control law design. In the hardware design of the controller, the signal laser manufacturing automation controller anti-interference design using FIR bandpass filter using ADG3301 design of laser level manufacturing automation controller circuit, ADG3301 Is a single channel double level production of ADI conversion chip PLC design logic control circuit based on automatic controller optimization design of laser intelligent manufacturing based on vision. The simulation test results show that the system designed in this paper can control the laser manufacturing automation with higher output gain, it has better robustness, and higher precision of laser manufacturing.
