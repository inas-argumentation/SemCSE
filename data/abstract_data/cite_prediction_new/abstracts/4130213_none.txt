Operating Systems for Internet of Things: A Comparative Study
The intense growth of Internet of Things (IoTs), its multidisciplinary nature and broadcasting communication pattern made it very challenging for research community/domain. Operating systems for IoTs plays vital role in this regard. Through this research contribution, the objective is to present an analytical study on the recent developments on operating systems specifically designed or fulfilled the needs of IoTs. Starting from study and advances in the field of IoTs with focus on existing operating systems specifically for IoTs. Finally the existing operating systems for IoTs are evaluated and compared on some set criteria and facts and findings are presented.
