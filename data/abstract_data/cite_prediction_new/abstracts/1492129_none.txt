Cytochrome content of kidney mitochondria in experimental lead poisoning.
Cytochromes aa 3 , b , c 1 , and c were quantitated in isolated renal mitochondria of control and lead poisoned rats. Lead poisoning was induced by ingesting a diet containing 1% lead as lead acetate. Concentration of cytochrome aa 3 was decreased in the lead-poisoned animals when related to mitochondrial protein. On the basis of kidney DNA, cytochrome aa 3 , and b were also significantly reduced. The protein content of mitochondria from the experimental animal was decreased 18%. Kidney homogenate protein was decreased by about 11%. The lead effect is attributed to inhibition of either porphyrin synthesis or protein synthesis. It is suggested that the observed reduction in cytochrome content may be at least in part responsible for the respiratory abnormality that occurs in renal mitochondria of lead-poisoned rats.
