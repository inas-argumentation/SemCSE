Energy consumption: Manufacture of building materials and building construction
When transportation changed from a technical problem to a cost problem, the availability of local materials was no longer a major factor determining the use of materials in industrial countries. The main emphasis was on construction costs guiding the choice of materials. The total costs, however, covering the service life of a building have not been of prime concern. The time aspect was dealt with in estimating the performance over time, but not commonly related to costs. The performance concept has in the last couple of decades been met with increased interest as a more valid base for selecting materials. The performance requirements on the materials are based on user requirements relevant to the in use situation. Increased running costs have caused greater concern for materials’ performance over time.
