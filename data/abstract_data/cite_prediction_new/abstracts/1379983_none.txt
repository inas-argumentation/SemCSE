Renal and cardiovascular events: do they deserve the same consideration in clinical trials?
In recent years, a substantial amount of evidence has documented the tight relationship between renal function and cardiovascular risk. Even in the earliest stages of chronic kidney disease, a simultaneous rise in cardiovascular risk is observed, and conversely, an elevated global cardiovascular risk contributes to progressive renal damage in a vicious circle of great pathophysiological relevance [1]. Hence, there is a need to estimate renal function in patients presenting with or at risk of developing cardiovascular disease.
