Relation between low 50‐g glucose challenge test results and small‐for‐gestational‐age infants
There is no consensus opinion regarding a possible relation between low 50‐g glucose challenge test (GCT) results and small‐for‐gestational‐age infants (SGA). This study aimed to clarify the relation between low 50‐g GCT results and SGA, after adjusting for potential confounding factors, using a large clinical dataset.
