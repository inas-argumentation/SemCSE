Nanoscale potential fluctuation in non-stoichiometric HfOx and low resistive transport in RRAM

We study the structure of non-stoichiometric HfOx films with variable composition using the methods of X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy and spectroscopic ellipsometry. HfOx, to a first approximation, is a mixture of HfO2 and Hf metal with a small amount (~10-15%) of hafnium sub-oxides HfOy ( y < 2 ). Spatial potential fluctuations, due to chemical compound fluctuations, lead to the percolation charge transport in such electronic systems. An application of these phenomena in resistive memory physics is discussed.
