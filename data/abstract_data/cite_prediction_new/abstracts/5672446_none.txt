Generation of stable sub-10 fs pulses at 400 nm in a hollow fiber used for UV pump-probe experiment
Stable sub-10fs pulses at 400nm were obtained using a beam pointing stabilizer before the hollow fiber compressor system. By using the obtained pulse, we observed the C-H vibration mode of C<inf>6</inf>H<inf>12</inf> at 2955cm<sup>−1</sup> in real-time.
