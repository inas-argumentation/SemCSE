Metal Magnetic Memory Testing Technique for Stress Measurement
Stress concentration is the main reason to cause fati gue failure and damage. However the early damage, especially the hidden that with no-continua nce in it is very difficult to be taken effective evaluation. Metal magnetic memory (MMM) testing technique is a new technique in the field of non-destructive testing, which is effective for the diagnosis before ferromagnetic parts failure. MMM testing principle was presented, and MMM diagnostics instrument (EMS-2003) was used to test the specimens. The relationship between torsion and the vertical component of surface magnetic intensity-Hp(y) was also analyzed. Hp(y) gradient as well as dots where Hp(y) changes its polarity was used to diagnose the s tress concentration zones. Conclusions were derived based on the experiment result s, and its trend was highlighted.
