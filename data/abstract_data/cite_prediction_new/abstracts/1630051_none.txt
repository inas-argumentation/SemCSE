Comparison of the morphology of vital and dead human spermatozoa.
The distribution of different morphological types of spermatozoa have been compared in the live and dead populations of an eosin-stained dry film. There were significant differences between the distribution of sperm types within each of the 80 individuals studied (P < 0\m=.\05)and between the two groups taken as a whole (P < 0\m=.\001).Normal oval, and small oval forms appeared preferentially in the vital population while spermatozoa with defects of the tail were preferentially found in the dead population. It is concluded that assessment of the total number of functional spermatozoa in an ejaculate should be made by reference to the percentage of normal oval forms in the live population.
