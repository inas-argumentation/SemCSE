Proposal : A Study of Campus Safety based on GPS Location-based Metrics and a Pattern Induction of Rules based on Deontic Logic
With the advent of the technological tools and efficient search engines and algorithms, it is not hard to find several datasets on the number of the crimes committed in the cities with universities located in them and the surrounding areas. Moreover, the number of the fire outbursts as an integral part of campus safety is easily accessible. However, these datasets do not analyze the data based on a location point of view and the fact that this stand-point can result in a practical application. Above all, analyzing the backbone of the rules is of great importance. Answering questions such as: Which rules are the most obeyed rules on and off campus?, Which rules are the most broken ones?, What is the efficient pattern for inducing rule sets that result in rule sets with high-obedience outcomes?, What are the best statistical analysis diagrams and charts that can guide us to the optimum rule sets?, etc. is of great importance. The answers can be found out with the help of Deontic Logic, a logic for analyzing and inducing rules. Obviously, this application is of great importance to the students as well as their Families. In addition, several international applicants apply for dierent universities based on some factors, such as the safety of the campuses. Another point is that, it can give the universities administrators and also the cities authorities a criterion for improving the campuses and the cities safety standards. The application can work based on the given dataset with a User Interface to inform the user about the number of crimes on-campus, local areas, and the number of res. In addition, it should provide the structure of the rules mostly broken based on Deontic Logic analysis to the user.
