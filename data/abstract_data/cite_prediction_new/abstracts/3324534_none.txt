International Conference on Competitive Manufacturing Cooperation of Industrial Robots with Indoor-GPS
In recent years, the interest in cooperating robots has grown substantially as possible applications for these manipulators are explored. Research in this field tends to focus on the development of specialised controllers. In this paper, however, the problem of achieving absolute accuracy in the positioning and movement of cooperating robots is addressed. The proposed solution to achieve absolute positioning is to use the Indoor-GPS technology as an external position measurement system allowing external control of the robot motions. With the project in its preliminary stages, this paper introduces the technologies involved and presents the current system. The introductory work that has been done and future outlook are given.
