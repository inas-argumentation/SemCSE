LINEAR STABILITY OF SUPERSONIC BOUNDARY LAYER ON POROUS SURFACE
Stability of compressible flat plate boundary layer on porous perforated surface is investigated in the framework of linear stability theory. Our computations confirm conclusions of earlier theoretical and experimental papers about stabilizing influence of permeable coating on the second instability mode in hypersonic Mach 6 boundary layer, while first instability mode in supersonic Mach 2 boundary layer becomes more unstable in the wide range of decisive parameters. However principal possibility of the first mode stabilization in Mach 2 boundary layer is found. It is shown that stabilizing or destabilizing influence of porous coating on boundary layer stability is determined by phase shift between pressure and normal velocity disturbances at the wall. Existence of a certain optimal phase shift is revealed which leads to a reduction of the boundary layer eigen unstable oscillations growth rates. If it would be possible to fabricate a permeable surface able to support such a phase shift, it would lead to supersonic boundary layer stabilization. Possibilities to realize that situation in practice are discussed. Key-Words: compressible boundary layers, laminar-turbulent transition, hydrodynamic stability.
