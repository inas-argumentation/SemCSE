Improved detection of EPO in blood and urine based on novel Velum SAR precast horizontal gels optimized for routine analysis
To enhance the endurance performance the misuse of erythropoietin (EPO) is currently detected by isoelectric focusing (IEF) based on the profile of altered isoforms of EPO. Changes within the new WADA technical document allows for the detection of the different erythropoietins by using the SarcosylPAGE, a modified SDS-PAGE. The Sarcosyl-PAGE which separates by difference in the molecular mass of EPO isoforms introduces a number of advantages: clear evidence between endogenous and the new modified exogenous EPO even when applied in smaller quantities; extended detection time of exogenous EPO, and a more simplified analysis. For EPO routine analysis a protocol based on a new type of Sarcosyl precast gels was established. For the evaluation of this improved detection method blood and urine samples of a micro-dose study were investigated.
