Fine structure of retinal vessels in man and the macaque monkey.
The fine structure of the capillaries, arterioles, and venules of the human and monkey retina are described. The capillaries consist of endothelial tubes loithout fenestratiom and a basement membrane in which pericytes are embedded. The structure of endothelial cells indicates that they are very active. Some difference in composition of the basement membrane material on the outer and inner surface of the pericytes may exist. The outer layers toere vacuolated in the adult but not in young human or monkey material. This may represent a change due to age. The cytoplasmic organelles of the pericytes are concentrated on the outer (glial) side. Fibrillar material was found in their cytoplasm somewhat similar to that in smooth muscle. These are related to other cytoplasmic membrane structures.
