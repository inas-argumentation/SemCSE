Synthesis of 2′-deoxythymidine by an enzymatic transdeoyrinosylation
Summary2′-Deoxythymidine was synthesized by an enzymatic transdeoxyribosylation of thymine using either (i) dGuo, dCyd or dAdo, or (ii) the mixture of the same 2′-deoxynucleosides resulting from enzymatic hydrolysis of DNA as donors of 2-deoxyribofuranose moiety.
