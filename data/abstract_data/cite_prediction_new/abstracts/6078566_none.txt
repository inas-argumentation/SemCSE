Noise prediction for channels with side information at the transmitter
The computation of channel capacity with side information at the transmitter side (but not at the receiver side) requires, in general, extension of the input alphabet to a space of "strategies", and is often hard. This capacity is realized by a state-independent code, followed by a shift by the "noise prediction" t/sub min/(S) that minimizes the entropy of Z/sub s/-t(S). Furthermore, for this case we also derive a simple formula for the capacity when the state process has memory.
