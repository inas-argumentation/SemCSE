Photographic Documentation for Veterinary Dentistry and Oral Surgery
Oral disease is the most prevalent pathology at all ages in our dog and cat patients. As examples, 27 % of dogs have fractured teeth, about one-third with pulp exposure, and 85 % of domestic cats over 6-years of age have periodontal disease. The increasing evidence of systemic ramifications from untreated periodontal inflammation will eventually gain the attention of those pet owners and veterinarians who are still circumspect about animal oral health, further increasing demand for dental care and veterinary accountability. Standard diagnostic procedures and techniques in human general dentistry are only slowly being adopted by veterinarians in general practice. Estimates of the availability of veterinary dental radiography range from 10-15 % of all practices to 50 % of veterinarians attending veterinary dental education lectures.
