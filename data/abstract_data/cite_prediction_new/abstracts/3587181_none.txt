Quality of Agricultural Products and Protection of the Environment: Training, Knowledge Dissemination and Certification. Synthesis Report of a Study in Five European Countries. CEDEFOP Reference Series.
This publication reports on a study which examined existing environmental education and agricultural practices that were environmentally friendly with the aim of defining new knowledge, skills and qualifications related to environmental issues in organic farming, and how these qualifications can best be acquired. The report is structured as follows: the first chapter outlines the background, objectives and design of the study; chapter two contains the European policy on agriculture and employment; chapter three describes the European agricultural sector, including the European organic farming sector, and the organic farming and certification systems in the five countries (Germany, Greece, Portugal, Spain, and the Netherlands); chapter four discusses education, training and innovation transfer in these five countries; chapter five describes the processing and distribution of organic products and presents the types of information dissemination among the consumers of organically produced products; chapter six provides national case studies; chapter seven contains conclusions and suggestions; chapter eight provides a summary of the main results (that regardless of education level, education, training, innovation transfer, and information on the production of certified organic products as well as on their distribution in the market, are important for all bodies involved in the chain); and the final chapter contains a bibliography.
