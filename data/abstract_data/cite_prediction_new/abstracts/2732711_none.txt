PLEXIFORM NEUROFIBROMA ASSOCIATED WITH CUTANEOUS PIGMENTATION AND BONE MANIFESTATIONS (REPORT OF TWO CASES)
We have recently encountered two cases of plexiform neurofibroma, in a Japanese girl aged six years and a boy aged two years. Neurofibromatosis was classically described by von Reckhghausen in 1882") and is characterized by diffuse proliferation of the peripheral nerve elements associated with Caf&aii-lait pigmentation of the skin. This is thought to be one variety of misdevelopment of the entire nervous system or any of its component parts, with a strong hereditary tendency. This overgrowth may take place along the course of the nerve, producing encapsulated tumors called neuroJibromata or may form a general thickening, elongation and tortuousity of nerves called a plexiform neurojibroma. It may occur about the peripheral terminations of the nerves producing non-encapsulated nodules in the skin called cutaneous neurojbromata. The purpose of this paper is to report a very rarely encountered disease entity and the presence of vacuolated cells within the tumor as well as never described bone manifestations associated with plexiform neurofibroma in one case.
