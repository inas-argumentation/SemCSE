Endoscopic Mucosal Resection Vs Endoscopic Submucosal Dissection for Colon Polyps.
PD EMR is performed with a snare to capture the target tissue. An electrosurgical current is then used to transect the tissue that has been grasped, although recently a cold snare EMR has been described. Injection is usually given in the submucosal space to elevate the lesion but is not always necessary; some techniques, such as underwater EMR, do not require injection into the submucosa. If the lesion is larger than 15 to 20 mm, it typically has to be removed in piecemeal fashion. The main use of EMR is to remove dysplastic polyps that are larger than 10 mm.
