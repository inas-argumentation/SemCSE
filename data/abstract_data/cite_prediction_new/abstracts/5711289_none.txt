WIDE-FIELD INTERFERENCE MICROSCOPY FOR AREAL TOPOGRAPHY OF PRECISION ENGINEERED SURFACE
Interference microscopy is a well-established optical method for areal surface topography and characterization. Of the available techniques, the dominant method for general applications in precision engineering is coherence scanning interferometry or CSI, because of its ability to measure a wide variety of surface textures. CSI microscopy relies on interference fringe contrast, optionally combined with interference phase, to determine surface heights [1-3].
