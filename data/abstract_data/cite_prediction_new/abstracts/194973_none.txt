Predicting persistence of wheezing: one algorithm does not fit all
From the authors: 

Scott et al. 1 have commented, in the light of outcomes of their “Isle of Wight Birth Cohort study” (IoWBC) 2, 3, on our findings on the risk factors for asthma in the Multicentre Allergy Study (MAS) birth cohort study 4. We agree that the two studies lead to similar conclusions in regard to early origins of persistent wheezing, heterogeneity of childhood wheezing and the relevance of an atopic background. In addition, we followed their suggestion to build a prognostic score in accordance with the IoWBC 3 and to test its performance in the MAS database. Here we report the results of our analysis.
