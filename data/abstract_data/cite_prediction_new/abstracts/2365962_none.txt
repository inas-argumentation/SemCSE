Ultrafast time resolved spectroscopy of protons in water : an experimental study
We present here the first measurement of the femtosecond dynamics of protons in liquid water [Eigen-type H<sub>3</sub>O<sup>+</sup>(H<sub>2</sub>O)<sub>3</sub>] by vibrational time resolved spectroscopy. The proton relaxation occurs in less than 200 fs
