Correlative light-, fluorescence- and electron-microscopic studies of catecholamine-storing cells in the adrenal medulla of the domestic fowl
SummaryCorrelative light-, fluorescence- and electron-microscopic examinations of catecholamine-storing cells in the adrenal medulla of the domestic fowl present unequivocal criteria to identify two types of avian adrenomedullary cells. A modified Karnovsky's fixative containing s-collidine was found to be a reliable prefixative to obtain a characteristic feature of the secretory granules in adrenaline- and noradrenaline-storing cells consistently.
