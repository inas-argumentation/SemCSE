MONOPTEROS directly activates the auxin-inducible promoter of the Dof5.8 transcription factor gene in Arabidopsis thaliana leaf provascular cells
Highlight An Arabidopsis gene, Dof5.8, is a target of the auxin-responsive MONOPTEROS transcription factor. Mutations in Dof5.8 interfere with embryo development and vein patterning when combined with a monopteros allele.
