Motor food conditioning before and after destruction of the red nucleus in cats
Conclusions1.Destruction of RN does not prevent the performance of established motor-food reflexes to a natural conditioned stimulus in cats. It leads only to a temporary disturbance of the accuracy and skill of acquired movements.2.In animals trained to approach the left-hand feeding bowl and press the pedal in response to one signal and to approach the right-hand bowl in response to another signal, destruction of RN leads to the appearance of mistakes in choice of the side of reinforcement (in 4–18% of cases) and to doubling of the latent periods of the responses. These disturbances are of short-term character (15–20 days).
