Estimating cell concentration in the presence of suspended solids: A light scatter technique
A novel sensor was developed, based on light scatter, to estimate the cell concentration in the presence of suspended solids. The light scatter properties of cells in the presence of suspended solids were investigated. Two crucial observations were made: first, that the light scatter from cells is essentially a linear function of cell concentration and, second, that invariant regions are present in the light scatter spectrum of cell/solid substrate mixtures. Invariant regions are wavelength intervals of the light scatter spectrum in which the light scatter reading is independent of solid substrate concentration and only a function of cell concentration. The occurrence of invariant regions is the key behavior which allowed the quantification of cell concentration in the presence of suspended solids.
