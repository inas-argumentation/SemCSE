A Perspective for the Comparative Analysis of Political Parties
The investigation of political parties within compatible theoretical frameworks and across cultural lines has not progressed far. This article addresses basic problems that require evaluation before such research is feasible. These questions include the formulation of an acceptably precise, yet necessarily flexible, definition of the agency, a setting of the reasonable parameters for research ventures, an illustrative discussion of the types of data relevant to illuminating varying dimensions of party behavior and aspects of the institution’s interrelationship with its environment, and the presentation of a focus that could prove useful in coordinating a series of related explorations.
