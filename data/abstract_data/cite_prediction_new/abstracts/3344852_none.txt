Learning organizations and quadruple loops of feedback part I. Theoretical models
The learning organization should not be limited to training and development; it should include the higher levels of re-organizing of all aspects of corporations by changing to more distributive leadership and more teamwork, which increases participation to knowledge development by all the people in the organization. Four types of feedback loops are described to emphasize the need for multi-leveled learning. New roles are emphasized, including ethical leaders, transformational leaders, distributed leadership, practitioner faculty, and Self-Managed Teams.
