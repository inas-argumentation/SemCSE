First, how will SSIGN compare with current nomograms? We have initial data that nomograms are more accurate [4] and more appealing than other predictive tools [8], but formal head-to-head comparisons are eagerly awaited. Second, can current predictive tools be further ameliorated? The use of models integrating clinicopathologic features with biomolecular characteristics may be the most reliable way to individualise prognosis [9,10]. They will likely enhance our ability to predict tumour behaviour on an individual basis, ultimately achieving the goal of delivering targeted therapies. Third, to what extent will clinicians be familiar with these potentially powerful decision aids? This study clearly represents a step towards desacralising them and should encourage the average urooncologist to begin to routinely rely on them in real-life practice. In conclusion, we believe that the premises have been set for the new risk-oriented approach in uro-oncology to rapidly grow.
