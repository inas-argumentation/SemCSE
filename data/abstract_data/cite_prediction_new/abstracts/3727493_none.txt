Relation between suicide and homicide in Portugal from 1970 to 1982
To evaluate to what extent a suicide can be considered a hetero‐aggression converted into self‐aggression, this study analyses whether the increase in suicides corresponds to a decrease in homicides and vice‐versa. Rates of suicide and homicide from 1970 to 1982 have been analysed in Portugal, Azores and Maderia, and the district and city of Lisbon; age and sex groups have also been compared. There is no inverse variation between suicides and homicides either globally or for males or females. There is also a difference in age distribution for suicides and homicides ‐ suicides being mainly over 50 years, homicides mainly under. This suggests that it is not very plausible that self‐ and hetero‐aggressive behaviours alternate in the same individual, although it is possible that, with increasing age, he may move from hetero‐ or potential hetero‐aggression to self‐aggression.
