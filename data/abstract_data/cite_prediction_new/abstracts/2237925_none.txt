Influence of Behavioral Finance on the Share Market
This article concerns the behavioral finance influence on the share market. The basic reasons for investors’ irrational behavior are the following: informational and emotional problems. Some behavioral aspects such as the framing effect, herding effect, illusion of control and thought contagion are indicated for analyses. Behavioral dependence in the Russian share market will be explained in this article. In conclusion, besides fundamental and technical analyses, investors should take into account psychological factors.
