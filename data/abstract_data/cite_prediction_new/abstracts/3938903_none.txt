An Overview DWT Compression Technique Used For Cheque Truncation System of Bank
A Cheque Truncation System (CTS-2012) has been applied by the banks throughout the country.CTS means that instead of sending the cheques in physical form by collecting the bank to the paying bank, an electronic image of cheque is transmitted to the drawee branch for payment through the clearing house, thereby eliminating cumbersome physical presentation of the cheque to the paying bank. Thus, saving time and cost involved in the traditional clearing system. The transmission of data i.e. cheques images requires more bandwidth for transmission. To transmit the cheques images using minimum bandwidth, it is going to discuss the use of the “Discrete Wavelet Transforms Compression Techniques for cheques images transmission”. Keywords— Transforms, DWT, Compression,Quantization.
