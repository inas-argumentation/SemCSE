Efficient Emulation of MIMD Behavior on SIMD Machines
SIMD computers have proved to be a useful and cost eeective approach to massively parallel computation. On the other hand, there are algorithms which are very ineecient when directly translated into a data-parallel program. This paper presents a number of simple transformations which are able to reduce this SIMD overhead to a moderate constant factor. It also introduces techniques for reducing the remaining overhead using Markov chain models of control ow. The optimization problems involved are NP-hard in general but there are many useful heuristics, and closed form optimizations for a probabilistic variant.
