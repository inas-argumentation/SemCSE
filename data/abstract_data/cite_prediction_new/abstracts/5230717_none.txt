Economies of Scale in Western River Steamboating: A Comment
In a recent article, Haites and Mak have provided significant and apparently conclusive evidence concerning the absence of scale economies in antebellum western river steamboating. By demonstrating that constant returns to scale prevailed they have substantiated their earlier claims that steamboating in America was characterized by perfect competition—a condition essential to their work. However, the Haites and Mak results contradict contemporary evidence and the results of returns to scale studies in present-day transportation systems; it therefore seems appropriate to reappraise their findings
