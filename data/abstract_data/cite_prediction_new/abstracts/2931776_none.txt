Design of Classification of Black Plastics Based on Fuzzy Radial Basis Fnction Neural Networks Using Raman Spectroscopy
Since many plastics are widely used in the variety of industrial filed, the amount of plastic waste produced by humans every year is massive. Therefore, plastic recycling is getting more important by the day and has been seriously studied. Some of recyclable plastics such as Polyethylene terephthalate (PET), Poly propylene (PP), Polystyrene (PS), etc. are hand-sorted or automatically sorted by the type of materials in recycling centers. Existing automatic sorting system is consisted of near infrared ray (NIR) sensors, which are commonly used to classify colored plastics. But classification of black plastics still remains a challenge, because of usage carbon black. Black plastic samples do not reflect a sufficient signal of NIR spectrometer. In this research, we introduce the way how to identify black plastics. Raman spectroscopy is used to get qualitative as well as quantitative analysis of black plastics. Besides, in order to improve the identification's performance, intelligent computing algorithm like Fuzzy Radial Basis Function Neural Networks (FRBFNN) classifier and Principal Component Analysis(PCA) are applied to analyze and classify the obtained data.
