PROFESSOR V. LAKSHMIKANTHAM : A BRIEF REVIEW OF CONTRIBUTIONS AND LEGACY
A portion of this work is based on a spacial and a featured invited lecture given in the Third International Conference on the, “Dynamic Systems and Applications” that was held at Morehouse College, Atalanta, Georgia, USA during, May 26-29, 1999. This featured presentation was to honour Professor V. Lakshmikantham’s 75th birthday and his half centuary contributions to the mathematics and the teaching profession. I am honored by the Marathwada Mathematical Society for inviting me to write an article on the occasion of honoring the 80th birthday of Professor V. Lakshmikantham. This work is to honour Professor V. Lakshmikantham’s 80th birthday and his more than half century contributions in mathematical sciences together with his professional legacies. This invited work is an updated version of my above cited presentation. The work sheds light on Professor Lakshmikantham as: (1) a mathematical scientist (2) a teacher (3) an administrator, and (4) outlines his major legacies.
