Conflict Management and Animated Film in Elementary Schools
For the last 50 years, peer mediation has been used as a strategy for teaching students how to handle conflicts in the US and Canada (Johnson & Johnson 2004; 2002) and in Trinidad and Tobago since 2003 (Ministry of Education, 2004). Most US, Canadian and UK studies have used experiments, surveys, ethnographic studies and content analyses to assess pedagogical approaches to conflict management education.
