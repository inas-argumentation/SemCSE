T-laminoplasty — a surgical approach for cervical spondylotic myelopathy
SummaryThe T-laminoplasty allows for a generous decompression of the cervical canal. At the same time, it avoids the risk of late instability. The T-laminoplasty is an improvement over various other methods of laminoplasty for several reasons:1.A bone graft from donor site is not needed.2.The implanted bone graft is firmly secured, avoiding dislocation and compression of the spinal cord.3.A fusion of two segments becomes unnecessary.4.Implants (met al or bone) are not needed.
