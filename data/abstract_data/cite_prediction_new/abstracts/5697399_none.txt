Preoperative diagnosis of unilateral tubal twin ectopic pregnancy with one line twin
Introduction Although the overall incidence of ectopic pregnancy is increasing, unilateral tubal multiple gestation is still exceedingly rare. There are probably less than 100 cases reported to date and the fetal cardiac activity was demonstrable in only six cases (Storch and Petrie, 1976; Neuman et al., 1990; Sherer et al., 1990; Hanchate et al., 2002). The incidence of higher-order tubal gestation is even more rare (Singhal and Chin, 1992). All the reported cases were diagnosed retrospectively either intraoperatively or histologically. This is a case of unilateral tubal twin pregnancy with one live twin, diagnosed prospectively by preoperative transvaginal ultrasonography. A second unique feature of this case was that the woman had two consecutive multiple gestations.
