Abo’s ‘application-adaptation (hybrid) model’: A commentary
Professor Abo presents in this journal a historical overview on the international transfer of the Japanese management and production system. Our commentary argues that the model would benefit from integrating recent theoretical developments on MNEs and from refining the cultural analyses in individual sub-regional contexts. We also present some future research questions raised from the JMNESG’s work, and conclude that although a Japanese way of conducting business internationally exists, more research is needed on how the Japanese management system evolves in the context of a more global and interconnected world where subsidiaries are developing as knowledge creators.
