Identification of O-methyloximes of ketosteroids by gas chromatography, thin-layer chromatography, mass spectra, and kinetic studies.
The O-methyloximes (MO) of 25 steroids having an oxo group at carbon 3, 11, 16, 17, or 20 have been studied. The actual number of derivatives (MO) for each steroid has been established and for most the rate of formation has been determined. A few derivatives have been identified by mass analysis, m.p., and infrared spectrum. Only the O-methyloximes of the tested 3-ketosteroids (3-keto-Δ4, 3-keto-5α-, and 3-keto-5β-) gave rise to two isomers: the two isomers of testosterone 3-O-methyloxime give the same mass spectrum, but differ by their m.p. and IR spectrum. The two isomers (MO) obtained from 3-keto-Δ4-steroids are interconvertible by thin-layer chromatography on alumina or silica gel. Steroids with a ketonic group at 3 and 16 or 3 and 20 give five MO derivatives (three mono-MO intermediates and two di-MO). An 11-ketone can form an O-methyloxime in the absence of a C-18 methyl group. The rate of formation of the MO differs, depending on the location of the reacting ketone. The C-20 ketone is the most reactive ( T 1 2 min ) the 17-ketone is the least reactive ( T 1 2 > 1 hr ), while the 3-ketone takes an intermediary position ( T 1 2 = 15–20 min ). The influence of steric hindrance has been investigated with different steroids and the utility of the MO for microassay of steroids for a doubleisotope method has been established.
