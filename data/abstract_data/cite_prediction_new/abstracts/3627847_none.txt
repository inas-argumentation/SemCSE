The Differential Quadrature Solution of Reaction-Diffusion Equation Using Explicit and Implicit Numerical Schemes
In this paper, two 
different numerical schemes, namely the Runge-Kutta fourth order method and the 
implicit Euler method with perturbation method of the second degree, are applied to solve the 
nonlinear thermal wave in one and two dimensions using the differential 
quadrature method. The aim of this paper is to make comparison between previous 
numerical schemes and detect which is more efficient and more accurate by 
comparing the obtained results with the available analytical ones and computing 
the computational time.
