IGP Routing Protocol Extensions for Discovery of Traffic Engineering Node Capabilities
It is highly desired, in several cases, to take into account Traffic
Engineering (TE) node capabilities during Multi Protocol Label
Switching (MPLS) and Generalized MPLS (GMPLS) Traffic Engineered Label
Switched Path (TE-LSP) selection, such as, for instance, the
capability to act as a branch Label Switching Router (LSR) of a Point-
To-MultiPoint (P2MP) LSP. This requires advertising these capabilities
within the Interior Gateway Protocol (IGP). For that purpose, this
document specifies Open Shortest Path First (OSPF) and Intermediate
System-Intermediate System (IS-IS) traffic engineering extensions for
the advertisement of control plane and data plane traffic engineering
node capabilities. [STANDARDS-TRACK]
