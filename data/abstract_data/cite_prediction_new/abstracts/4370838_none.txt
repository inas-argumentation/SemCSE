Configuration and coordination of global advertising
The purpose of this study was to investigate the organization and management of the advertising activities of multinational corporations. The specific foci were a) the configuration or location of responsibilities for advertising activities, and b) the extent of their coordination by multinational corporations' headquarters across markets. The results of a survey of advertising managers in large, U.S. multinationals indicate that individual advertising functions are managed as three broad sets (strategic, executional, and evaluative) and that each is either centralized or dispersed to subsidiaries, with only a few cases of coordination by both headquarters and subsidiaries.
