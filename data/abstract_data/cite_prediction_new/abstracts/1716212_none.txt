Is pancreatic duct obstruction or stricture a major cause of pain in calcific pancreatitis?
Forty‐seven patients with calcific pancreatitis were submitted to ERCP, 24 of whom were designated ‘painless’ and 23 ‘painful’, according to whether or not pain had occurred during the previous year. The pancreatic duct was cannulated in 39 of these patients. The incidence of pancreatic duct obstruction or stricture was of the same order in the painless (13/20, 65 per cent) and painful (15/19. 79 per cent) groups.
