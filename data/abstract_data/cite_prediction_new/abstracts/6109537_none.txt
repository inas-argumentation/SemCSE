CRESCIMENTO DE INDIVÍDUOS DE ANDIROBA (Carapa guianensis Aubl.) EM DIFERENTES PERÍODOS SAZONAIS EM SANTARÉM-PA
The knowledge about physiological responses regarding the growth of native species in different seasonal periods is the basis for the creation of adequate silvilocultural treatments. The objective of this study was to evaluate the ecophysiological aspects of Andiroba plants (Carapa guianensis Aubl.) In different seasonal periods. The study was carried out in the experimental farm of the company pematec, in a plantation of Carapa guianensis (Abul.), Where 30 species were chosen in a random way that constituted the sample space of the research. Dendometric bands were used to measure the increase of biomass, which were measured with a digital caliper (mm). In addition, the volumetry of the shaft was measured with a diametric tape. From these values were determined the patterns of growth dynamics as: monthly increase, mass gain of carbon following the methodology of Potter (1983). The difference between the collection months that were related to the dry and rainy periods of the region was analyzed. The meteorological data were obtained from the Meteorological Data Bank for Teaching and Research (BDMEP) of the conventional station of the municipality of Belterra, where data of precipitation (mm) and insolation (W / m2) monthly average. The graphs and calculations of the analyzed variables were carried out with the aid of the computer program Microsoft Excel 2013. The month of February was characterized by a larger increment with 0.06 cm, where the monthly precipitation reached 229.6 mm and the level of was the lowest recorded during the entire observation period at 109.9 W / m2. In the months of August to December were recorded the lowest gains in mass of carbon. During this period, the low precipitation and the highest sunshine rates were recorded among the analyzed months. The values referring to the average mass gain (g / day) show that the months of August to December have the lowest g / day. In this way, the highest growth of the plants of Carapa guianensis Aubl. occurred in the rainy season, since it is a favorable factor to the development. Rainfall is one of the factors limiting the growth of Carapa guianensis Aubl., Since water availability and sunshine are partial and incandescent during the months.
