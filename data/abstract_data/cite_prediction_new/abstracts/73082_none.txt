Interleukin (IL)-1 Gene Cluster in Inflammatory Bowel Disease: Is IL-1RA Implicated in the Disease Onset and Outcome?
IL-1 represents two structurally distinct forms: IL-1a and IL-1b. The naturally occurring antagonist of IL-1, endogenous IL-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1RA), regulates normal immune homeostasis in the gut. The IL-1/IL-1RA parallels colitis activity, remaining constant in the nonaffected part of the colon and in non-IBD inflammatory controls. Furthermore, IL-1b promotes Th17 responses from CD4? T lymphocytes and innate lymphoid cells in the intestine, suggesting synergistic interactions between IL-1b and IL-23 signals that sustain innate and adaptive inflammatory responses in the gut. IL-1b also mediates the regulation of Th17 response in the intestine at steady state by a MyD88-dependent microbial signal.
