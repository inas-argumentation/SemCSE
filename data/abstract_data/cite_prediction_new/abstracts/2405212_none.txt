The Effects of Halothane and Methoxyflurane Metabolites on Lipolysis In Vitro
The effects of halothane and methoxyflurane on lipid metabolism in rats were further analyzed by studying the effects of their metabolites on lipolysis in vitro. Trifluoroacetic acid sodium and trifluoroacetaldehyde hydrate caused no alterations in lipolysis in vitro. Trifluoroethanol (TFE) and sodium fluoride stimulated lipolysis in vitro seen as increased accumulation of free fatty acids (FFA) in the adipose tissue and increased release of glycerol into the incubation medium.
