Psychotropic activity of some aminoketones belonging to the adamantane group
Aminoketones are known to exhibit a broad spectrum of pharmacological activity, including anticonvulsant, psychotropic, and antitumor properties [1]. For example, the drug mydocalm, producing a central n-cholinolytic effect, is used for the therapy of patients with spinal and cerebral paralysis; the drug phepranon is an anorexigenic agent [2]. It was reported that the adamantyl radical can modify the pharmacological action of other substances. In particular, the organogermanium complexes of adamantane possess neurotropic properties [3], S-8-(adamantyl-l-carbonyloxy)-7-chloro-3-methyl1 -phenyl-2,3,4,5-tetrahydro1 H-ben zazepine produces a neuroleptic and antidepressant action [4], while the amides of 1-adamantanecarboxylic and 3-bromo-l-adamantanecarboxylic acids exhibit anticonvulsant effects [5]. In connection with this, we have synthesized a series of aminoketones of the adamantane group and studied their psychotropic activity. In the first stage, we performed the reactions of bromomethyl(1-adamantyl)ketone (II) with piperidine and morpholine in ethanol in the presence of Na2CO 3, which led to the corresponding 2-amino-l-(1-adamantyl)ethanones. Subsequent treatment of the ether solutions of these bases with dry gaseous HC1 resulted in the formation of hydrochlorides la and lb.
