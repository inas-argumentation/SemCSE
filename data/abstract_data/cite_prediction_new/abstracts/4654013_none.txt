Use of Direct Thrombin Inhibitors for Treating Non-ST-Segment Elevation Acute Coronary Syndromes in Special Patient Groups : Women , Diabetics , the Elderly , and Chronic Renal Insufficiency
Antithrombin and antiplatelet therapies remain the foundation for the treatment of patients with NSTE ACS. Special groups including women, elderly, diabetics and those with chronic renal insufficiency represent a particular challenge for the clinician evaluating and treating these patients. These individuals are at high risk for ischemic complications including death, myocardial infarction, and unplanned intervention. Typically such patients are under-treated with antithrombins and antiplatelet agents because of an appropriate fear of bleeding complications, resulting in the significant proportion of these patients having ischemic adverse events.
