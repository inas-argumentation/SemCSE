Influence of nitrilotriacetate (NTA) on the toxicity, excretion and distribution of cadmium in female rats.
In nonpregnant rats, po administration of NTA with cadmium chloride reduces the mortality of cadmium administered po as 64 and 80 mg/kg cadmium chloride alone. NTA with cadmium chloride also prevents the marked weight loss observed with administration of cadmium chloride alone. NTA enhances urinary and fecal excretion of cadmium when administered po with cadmium chloride as compared with the urinary and fecal excretion of cadmium chloride alone. Finally, cadmium analyses of liver, kidney and muscle reveal NTA inhibits the deposition of cadmium in these tissues when administered po with cadmium chloride. It is concluded that NTA exerts a protective effect against the toxicity of cadmium chloride and enhances excretion of the metal which inhibits organ and tissue deposition of cadmium.
