Equations solvable by radicals in a uniquely divisible group
We study equations in groups G with unique mth roots for each positive integer m. A word equation in two letters is an expression of the form w(X, A)=B, where w is a finite word in the alphabet {X, A}. We think of A, B∈G as fixed coefficients, and X∈G as the unknown. Certain word equations, such as XAXAX=B, have solutions in terms of radicals: X = A−1/2(A1/2BA1/2)1/3 A−1/2, while others such as X2 A X=B do not. We obtain the first known infinite families of word equations not solvable by radicals, and conjecture a complete classification.
