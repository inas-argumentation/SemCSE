A 280-/300-GHz Three-Stage Amplifiers in 65-nm CMOS With 12-/9-dB Gain and 1.6/1.4% PAE While Dissipating 17.9 mW
This letter reports the design of terahertz amplifiers using the concept of maximum achievable gain (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$G_{\mathrm {max}})$ </tex-math></inline-formula> of a transistor embedded in a linear, lossless, reciprocal network. Implemented in a 65-nm CMOS, by adopting the optimized <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$G_{\mathrm {max}}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>-core, 280- and 300-GHz amplifiers achieve peak gain of 12 and 9 dB, peak power-added efficiency (PAE) of 1.6% and 1.4%, and gain per stage of 4 and 3 dB, respectively, while dissipating 17.9 mW, which is the best performance up to date in terms of operating frequency, gain per stage, and PAE in CMOS process.
