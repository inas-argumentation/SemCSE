A Gastric Perivascular Epithelioid Cell Tumor : A Case Report
Perivascular epithelioid cell tumors (PEComas) of gastro intestinal tract are very rare, with only few cases reported in the literature. The PEComas are a family of rare mesenchymal tumors characterized by perivascular epithelioid cell differentiation. We herein report our experience with a patient who had a primary gastric PEComa that was diagnosed by an expert pathologist review of the excised tissues and which showed neopastic epithelioid cells arranged as sheets with abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm, nuclei with a prominent nucleolus and expressed melanocytic and muscle markers.
