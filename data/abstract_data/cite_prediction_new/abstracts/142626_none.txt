Karyotype, immunophenotype, and clinical outcome: correlations in childhood acute lymphoblastic leukemia.
The correlation between classical subgroups of childhood acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) (clinical presentation, morphologic and cytochemical characteristics) and immunologic and cytogenetic subgroups has yet to be defined. The present retrospective study was done to establish the prognostic significance of and correlations between clinical, immunologic, and cytogenetic anomalies and outcome.
