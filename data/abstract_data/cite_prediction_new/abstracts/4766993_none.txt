A Double-Beam System for Infra-Red Spectrometers employing Rotating Twin Cells
THE desirability of using direct-ratio instruments for the recording of infra-red spectra is immediately obvious when one considers the labour involved in measuring energy curves with the customary ‘cell in—cell out’ technique, and then carrying out the corresponding point-by-point division necessary to obtain a spectrum of percentage transmission versus wave-length. There are also the attendant difficulties arising from possible variations in source intensity or in atmospheric absorption between two consecutive runs. An instrument which performs these functions automatically must be capable of viewing the radiant energy passing through both the sample and standard cells simultaneously.
