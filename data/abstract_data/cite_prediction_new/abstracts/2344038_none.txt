Evaluation for Ecological Compensation Based on AHP and Fuzzy Theory
The evaluation for current situation of ecological compensation is significant to strengthen the system of ecological compensation. In the study, the method based on the combination of analytic hierarchy process(AHP) and fuzzy theory is proposed to evaluate ecological compensation. Firstly, evaluation indexes for ecological compensation are described, and the evaluation model of ecological compensation is constructed based on analytic hierarchy process(AHP) and fuzzy theory. Finally, ecological compensation for China is evaluated to testify the effectiveness of the proposed method.
