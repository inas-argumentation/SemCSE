Impact of Clostridium botulinum genomic diversity on food safety
Highlights • C. botulinum Groups I and II form botulinum neurotoxin and cause foodborne botulism.• Increased knowledge of C. botulinum Group I and II genomes and neurotoxin diversity.• Impact on food safety via improved surveillance and tracing/tracking during outbreaks.• New insights into C. botulinum biology, food chain transmission, evolution.
