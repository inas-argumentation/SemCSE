Análise jurídica do sistema de solução de controvérsias da organização mundial do comércio.
Research about The World Trade Organization’s (W.T.O.) dispute settlement system from the perspective of international law. After general considerations about international law and international disputes, this article analyses the structures and procedures of the W.T.O.’s dispute settlement system, and concludes that: a) the Dispute Settlement Body (D.S.B) is not a tribunal; b) the legal nature of the D.S.B. resolution is of an individual legal norm comprised in a decision of an organ of an international organization; c) these decions are legally binding and non-compliance can be sanctioned; d) they are condemnatory decisions; e) they have legitimacy because they were decided by consensus; f) they are not self-executing; the W.T.O. system is a jurisdictional dispute mechanism.
