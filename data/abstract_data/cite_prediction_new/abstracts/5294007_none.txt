A Method for Bounding Transmissions Errors For the Stewart Platform based Manipulator
Parallel link mechanisms are increasingly being studied for possible use in multi-axis machine tools. An important consideration in the design of such mechanisms is their accuracy. In this paper, a model is developed to determine the bounds for transmission errors of the Stewart Platform based manipulator. Transmission errors are caused by sources such a the spindle error motion, vibration of the machine structure (forced and self-induced), controller errors, thermal growth, feed rates, and machining velocities. These errors change rapidly by their nature and predominantly affect the local characteristics of the machined parts such as surface roughness and under-cutting of corners. Bounding of such errors become an important accuracy criterion during the design of such machines.
