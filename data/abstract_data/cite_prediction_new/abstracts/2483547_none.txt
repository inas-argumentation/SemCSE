Selective fungitoxicity of cyclohexylamine and related aromatic amines
Cyclohexylamine, 10−1 mole/l, selectively inhibited the growth of various fungi in culture.Pythium ultimum was highly sensitive;Fusarium oxysporum was more tolerant. Phenylethylamine was toxic to these 2 fungi at lower concentrations than cyclohexylamine and hydroxiphenylethylamine.
