Design and Control of a Ball on Ball System With a Programmable Logic Controller and Vision Feedback
In this publication a Ball on Ball setup is modeled, implemented and analyzed. The goal of this setup is to stabilize a ball on top of a second ball in the unstable upper rest position. The position measurement of the upper ball is done optionally by two triangulation sensors or an industrial camera. With the designed control and image processing, implemented on a standard industrial programmable logic controller, it is possible to stabilize the upper ball with both measurement types.
