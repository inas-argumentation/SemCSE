The Physiological Basis for Altered Na+ and Cl− Movements across the Gills of Rainbow Trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss) in Alkaline (pH = 9.5) Water
To test the hypothesis that internal ion imbalances at high pH are caused by altered branchial ion transporting capacity and permeability, radiotracers (24Na+ and 36Cl−) were used to measure ion movements across the gills of intact rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss) during 3 d exposure to pH 9.5.
