Increased hypothalamic sensitivity to estrogen inhibition induced by L-dopa, phenytoin, epithalamin, and phenformin in old rats
Daily administration of 0.57 μg diethylstilbestrol propionate (DESP) to hemicastrated rats inhibited by 48% compensatory hypertrophy of the ovary (CHO) in 3-month-old animals but by only 3% in 18-month-old rats. After injection of phenytoin, L-dopa, epithalamin, or phenformin concurrently with this same dose of DESP into old rats, inhibition of CHO amounted to 65–98%. L-Dopa and epithalamin produced the same effect when injected into the third ventricle. The results of these experiments indicate the functional nature of the age changes in hypothalamic sensitivity to estrogens.
