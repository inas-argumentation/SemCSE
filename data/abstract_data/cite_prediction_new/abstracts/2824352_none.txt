Frequency of Nest Visits by Non-Resident Hatch-Year Tree Swallows
Introduction Stiles and Taylor report on a number of instances of recently fledged tree swallows (Tachycineta bicolor) being detected visiting nest boxes from which they did not originate.1 Such encounters with “non-resident” hatch-year (HY) swallows appear to be rare, but it is unclear how often such visits actually occur. The account by Stiles and Taylor1 prompted me to examine my own records of the nesting biology of tree swallows, as well as review some of the literature, to gain further insight into the frequency of occurrence and some of the potential explanations for this phenomenon.
