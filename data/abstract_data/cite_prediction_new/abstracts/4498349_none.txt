A High Performance Direct Torque Control of PMBLDC Motor using AI
This paper deals with the direct torque control (DTC) of PMBLDC motor using Artificial Intelligence (AI) to improve the performance of the DTC scheme. Though the conventional controllers are commonly used in practice, they have failed to perform satisfactorily under nonlinear conditions and parameter variations.
