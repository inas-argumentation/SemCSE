A study of electrodeless fluorescent lamp's cores of ferrite N87 and IP12E
This paper presents a study of the induction electrodeless fluorescent lamp cores' material. Experiments were performed in order to obtain the material features such as, magnetization curve and power losses. From the experimental data results, it was possible to calibrate the simulation of the core and, from the simulation, more data were acquired, for instance the flux density variation. Results show extreme similarity between simulation and experimental results. Therefore the study of the comparison among materials is possible.
