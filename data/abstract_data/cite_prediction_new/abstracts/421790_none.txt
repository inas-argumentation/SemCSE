Is there enough evidence for the use of intravesical instillations of glycosaminoglycan analogues in interstitial cystitis?
Introduction Interstitial cystitis/painful bladder syndrome (IC/PBS) is a clinical chronic urogenital pain syndrome with non-specific symptoms, e.g. pain in the bladder and or pelvis and urinary symptoms, such as frequency and urgency. Widely accepted definitions are those proposed by the ICS and the European Society for the Study of IC/PBS. Its epidemiology and patho-physiology is still poorly understood. Its heterogeneous nature makes diagnostic criteria and appropriate treatments very difficult to achieve.
