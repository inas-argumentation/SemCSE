RELATIVE SOLIDITY FOR SPACES OF HOLOMORPHIC FUNCTIONS
We de fine and investigate relative solidity for sequence spaces, and use it to 
study spaces of holomorphic functions on the unit disk and related coefficient 
multiplier spaces. In particular, we find the multipliers (l(u; v);H(p; q; t)) and 
(H(p; q; t); l(u; v)) for many values of the parameters u; v; p; q; t.
