Factors Affecting the Assessment of the Quality of a Company's Auditors: The Case of the Czech Republic
The requirement to have a corporate audit in the Czech Republic was implemented in 1992. This article summarizes the results of a questionnaire about auditing quality that was circulated to Czech enterprises in early 1996. There were 451 responses to the questionnaire from a broad selection of enterprises. Over 65% of respondents were happy with the audit service received. Statistical analysis of the questionnaire results indicated that personal contact with advisors from the audit firm and how long the respondent had known the auditors had the most positive influence on overall perceptions of audit quality among preparers. The only audit firm where there seemed to be a significant association between the name of the audit firm and perceptions of audit quality was KPMG. The analysis indicated that those respondents to the questionnaire who were part of overseas enterprises had a more critical view of audit quality in the Czech Republic.
