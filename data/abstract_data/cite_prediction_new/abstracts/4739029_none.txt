Evaluation of the Community Placement of the Tapestry Tourism Futures Project
The Tapestry Tourism Futures Project was an STCRC funded project from 2000- 2003, aimed at developing a regional sustainable tourism model. This paper reports on an evaluation of the community placement of the project. Key objectives of the TTFP were regarded as being for marketing, planning. and promotion (74%). The most difficult aspects of the placement of the project were obtaining participation by stakeholders, operational issues relating to the ability of respondents to become involved and communication issues between stakeholders. Promotion of the project, ongoing feedback and community skill development were seen as key factors for further implementation.
