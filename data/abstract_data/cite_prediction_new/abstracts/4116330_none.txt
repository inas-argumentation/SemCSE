The Regional particularities of Information Society development: digital divide
The article examines a problem of a digital divide among regions of Russian Federation. There were three main causes distinguished. Data and official statistics, information from official web-sites, composite indices on Information Society development were analyzed. A theme of research was suggested, as well as practical solutions on development of the Information Society and elimination of the digital divide among regions. The analysis presented in the paper is a preliminary study intended to further the implementation of a research project. Special attention was paid to certain groups of citizens, such as women, middle-aged, and elderly. The importance of such research was being justified.
