Burst firing of cerebellar Purkinje neurones induced by benzodiazepines
The responses of cerebellar Purkinje cells to benzodiazepines have been studied in urethane-anaesthetised rats. lontophoretic applications of chlordiazepoxide and nitrazepam, and iontophoretic or systemic applications of flurazepam caused, or increased the number of, high-frequency bursts of spikes and silent periods of these neurones.
