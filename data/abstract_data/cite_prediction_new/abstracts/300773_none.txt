Dynamics of shape memory alloys patches with mechanically induced transformations
A mathematical model is constructed for the modelling of two dimensional thermo-mechanical 
behavior of shape memory alloy patches. The model is constructed on the basis of a modified 
Landau-Ginzburg theory and includes the coupling effect between thermal and mechanical 
fields. The free energy functional for the model is exemplified for the square to rectangular 
transformations. The model, based on nonlinear coupled partial differential equations, is 
reduced to a system of differential-algebraic equations and the backward differentiation 
methodology is used for its numerical analysis. Computational experiments with 
representative distributed mechanical loadings are carried out for patches of different sizes 
to analyze thermo-mechanical waves, coupling effects, and 2D phase transformations.
