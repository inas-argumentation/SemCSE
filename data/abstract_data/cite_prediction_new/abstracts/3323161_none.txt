Metabolizable energy values of wheat and some by-product feedstuffs for growing chicks.
ALTHOUGH metabolizable energy values have been published for many feed ingredients, values for many products used in feeds are unavailable and the published values for others vary considerably. The purpose of this paper is to present metabolizable energy values of some of the principal wheats grown in the Pacific Northwest and of some by-product feeds available in this area. PROCEDURE Determinations of metabolizable energy of the wheat varieties and samples of wheat mixed feed, barley bran, barley flour and Alaska peas were made by the method of Hill and Anderson (1958), with the exception that chromium oxide was determined by the method of Czarnocki et al. (1961). Metabolizable energy values were corrected for nitrogen retention by the equation of Hill et al. (1960).
