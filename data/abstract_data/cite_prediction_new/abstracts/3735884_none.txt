Research of singularity constraint in motion platform's design
Stewart Platform is the first choice of full flight simulator motion system. However, it has an inherent singularity in the structure, within which the circumvention of singularity is a common problem of the platform's motion control and trajectory planning. This article starts with studying the Jacobian Matrix singularity criterion of Stewart Platform, analyzes the relationship of singularity and the particular geometric position of Stewart Platform, and then proves the equivalence of those two factors with application of the matrix theory. Based on the analysis, a group of inequalities is given as a geometric criterion for platform design.
