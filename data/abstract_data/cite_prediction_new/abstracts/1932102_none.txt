Modeling and Simulation of Mobility Based Adaptive Reservation Bandwidth in Wireless Cellular Networks
Modeling and performance analysis of cellular networks are an evolving and challenging issue. Cellular networks have been analyzed via analytical modeling and simulation. The extensive and large nature of cellular networks imposes a demand to run simulation experiments on multiprocessor platforms. Thus, imposing performance analysis to be run on high-powered machines and the possession of analytical skills. This paper presentssimulator for a detail description and analysis of resource reservation schemes. The simulator encompasses the analysis of a cellular network and bandwidth reservation schemes. The performance of the adaptive scheme is evaluated in terms of call blocking and handoff dropping probability and achieves high bandwidth utilization. The simulated network consists of a high number of cells, mobile users with various movement patterns are assumed, and a variety of multimedia applications (e.g., audio phone, video conference, video on demand, file transfer, etc.) is considered. In order to evaluate the performance of the developed simulator, a simulation model was built for an extensive mobile environment. Simulation results indicate that, for QoS sensitive multimedia services, our simulator is able to recapture the results of both the numerical result and simulation results of previous work.
