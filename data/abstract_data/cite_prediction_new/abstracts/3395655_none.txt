Discrimination in Financial Services: What Do We Not Know?
Chicago, the site of this conference on discrimination in the financial industry, is also the site of concern about mortgage redlining. It is the home of the greenlining coalition, whose existence is based on the belief that banks discriminate against neighborhoods by refusing to make conventional mortgage loans on properties located there. At the outset, I want to point out that the race of potential mortgagors was not considered in the initial redlining studies and assertions nor was it the direct concern of the groups or politicians who worked to enact the Community Reinvestment Act (CRA).
