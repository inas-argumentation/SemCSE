Title Arithmetic linear series with base conditions
In this note, we study the volume of arithmetic linear series with base conditions. As an application, we consider the problem of Zariski decompositions on arithmetic varieties. Introduction Let X be a projective and flat integral scheme overZ. We assume that X is normal and the generic fiber of X→ Spec(Z) is a d-dimensional smooth variety overQ.
