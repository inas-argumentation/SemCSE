A study of the structure of lactosomatotropic hormone
Summary1. Peptides from tryptic hydrolysis of the cyanogen bromide fragments B-1 and B-2 of LSTH have been isolated and characterized.2. The differences found in the amino-acid sequence of the individual peptides of fragment B of LSTH as compared with the corresponding sections of the O-LTH molecule are not due to species differences of the hormones.
