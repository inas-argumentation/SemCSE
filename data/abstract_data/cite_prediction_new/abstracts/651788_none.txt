Cichoń’s diagram, regularity properties and $${\varvec{\Delta}^1_3}$$Δ31 sets of reals
We study regularity properties related to Cohen, random, Laver, Miller and Sacks forcing, for sets of real numbers on the $${\varvec{\Delta}^1_3}$$Δ31 level of the projective hieararchy. For $${\varvec{\Delta}^1_2}$$Δ21 and $${\varvec{\Sigma}^1_2}$$Σ21 sets, the relationships between these properties follows the pattern of the well-known Cichoń diagram for cardinal characteristics of the continuum. It is known that assuming suitable large cardinals, the same relationships lift to higher projective levels, but the questions become more challenging without such assumptions. Consequently, all our results are proved on the basis of ZFC alone or ZFC with an inaccessible cardinal. We also prove partial results concerning $${\varvec{\Sigma}^1_3}$$Σ31 and $${\varvec{\Delta}^1_4}$$Δ41 sets.
