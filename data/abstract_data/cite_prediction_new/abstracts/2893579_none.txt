The Decision-Making Process in Nutritional Surveillance in Europe
Surveillance in general has been defined as the systematic collection and analysis of data pursued with well-defined objectives. More specifically, a Nutritional Surveillance System (NSS) scrutinizes the occurrence in a population of dietary-mediated nutritional conditions, with the objective of describing the current situation, detecting trends, forecasting changes, highlighting priorities and targeting corrective and preventive measures (World Health Organization, 1976; Eylenbosch & Noah, 1988; Kelly, 1988; Mason ef al . 1987).
