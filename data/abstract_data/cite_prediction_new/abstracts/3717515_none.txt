Lattice Constants and Magnesium Contents of some Naturally Occurring Whitlockites
CALCIUM phosphates occurring in Nature are in general of the apatite type, more or less crystalline. So far, in only one domain have calcium phosphates of the whitlockite type been found to be common—in the calcified deposits found on human teeth1 and in the adjoining salivary ducts and glands2. We have recently examined a large number of samples of dental calculus and some other natural and synthetic whitlockites3.
