Robust output prediction of differential — Algebraic systems — Application to drinking water distribution system
The paper presents the recursive robust output variable prediction algorithm, applicable for systems described in the form of nonlinear algebraic-differential equations. The algorithm bases on the uncertainty interval description, the system model, and the measurements. To improve the algorithm efficiency, nonlinear system models are linearised along the nominal trajectory. The effectiveness of the algorithm is demonstrated on the model of the Chojnice drinking water supply system (DWDS).
