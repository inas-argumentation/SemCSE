Simulation on fused biconical tapering of the fiber based on creep experiments
Fused biconical tapering is the main method to fiber coupler manufacture. The fiber rheological shape has an important influence on the optical performance of couplers. In order to obtain the influence of process parameters such as the drawing force and melting temperature in the fiber rheological forming process, the fused biconical tapering technology is simulated based on the viscoelastic model and creep experiment in this paper. Firstly, viscoelastic mechanics performance of fiber is described by the Maxwell model and time temperature equivalence principle. Then the creep experiment is performed to obtain viscoelastic parameters. Finally, simulation analysis on the fused biconical tapering is carried out using finite element method. The simulation results show that the drawing force is inversely proportional to the diameter of neck area. When the drawing force increased to 500mgf, the shape of neck region is unstable. And the fiber deformation is more stable, when the melting temperature is above 1170 □.
