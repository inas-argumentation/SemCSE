MOLECULAR ANALYSIS OF SOLIDASTER CV. LEMORE, A HYBRID GOLDENROD (ASTERACEAE)
analysis of nuclear ribosomal its and ets sequence data was used to assess the relationships of the garden ornamental solidaster cv. lemore, a cultivar that was at one time believed to be an intergeneric hybrid ( solidago × a ster ). part of the analysis involved assessment of the generic placement of one of its putative parents, the Upland white aster, solidago ptarmicoides. Because of its superficial external appearance this species is still often treated as an aster , although there is evidence from several sources that suggests it should be clas sified within solidago. phylogenetic analysis of the combined its and ets sequence data showed that there was strong support for the inclusion of s. ptarmicoides within solidago, based on its placement in a well supported clade composed of all other sampled species of the genus. the its sequence data of solidaster showed evidence of a hybrid origin based on the presence of several intraindividual base pair polymorphisms. cloning experiments recovered two different individual its sequences, one identical to that obtained from
