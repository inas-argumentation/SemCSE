A multiply-and-accumulate selection algorithm for dynamic entropy coding
Entropy coding is a well-known tool for reducing the redundancy present in communication signals. As a signal's redundancy is reduced, the bandwidth required for its transmission over a low-noise channel also decreases. Memory requirements may likewise be reduced for coded information stored in low-error media. A method is presented for determining entropy codes by means of a sequence of multiply-and-accumulate and look-up operations. Structuring the process in such a fashion allows the effective use of a digital signal processor for dynamically generating adaptive codes in response to changing source statistics.
