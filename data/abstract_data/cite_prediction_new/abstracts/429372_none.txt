Toward Categoricity for Classes with no Maximal Models
We provide here the first steps toward a Classification Theory of Abstract Elementary Classes with no maximal models, plus some mild set theoretical assumptions, when the class is categorical in some λ greater than its Lowenheim-Skolem number. We study the degree to which amalgamation may be recovered, the behaviour of non μ-splitting types. Most importantly, the existence of saturated models in a strong enough sense is proved, as a first step toward a complete solution to the Łoś Conjecture for these classes. Further results are in preparation.
