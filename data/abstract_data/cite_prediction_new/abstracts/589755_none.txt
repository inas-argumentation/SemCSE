Management Essentials for Urgent Repair of Highway after Disaster -- Taking a Tunnel of a Highway as an Example
In recent years, various disasters have taken place frequently, and then highway blocking has happened from time to time. In order to be open to traffic as soon as possible, the project management should take the corresponding measures not consistent with the normal construction. This paper mainly studied the project management of urgent repair of highway after disaster(This paper uses "this kind of project"), analyzed the characteristics of urgent repair of highway after disaster, main tasks of project management, and proposed the measures to improve the ability and level of project management of this kind of project based on the actual situations and project management experience, so as to perfect the this kind of project and create greater benefits for people.
