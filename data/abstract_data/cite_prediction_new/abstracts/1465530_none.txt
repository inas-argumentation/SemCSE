Stereochemistry of the active site of -chymotrypsin. The effect of some tricyclic bromomethyl ketones on -chymotrypsin.
4:5-Benzindanyl-1-bromomethyl ketone, 4:5-benzindanyl-2-bromomethyl ketone (II), 4:5-benzindanyl-3-bromomethyl ketone, 5:6-benzindanyl-2-bromomethyl ketone, 1,2-dihydronaphtho[2,1-b]-furanyl-2-bromomethyl ketone (V), and 2,3-dihydronaphtho[1,2-b]-furanyl-2-bromomethyl ketone were tested as inhibitors for α-chymotrypsin (EC 3.4.4.5). Compounds II and V inactivated the enzyme rapidly and their actions were stereospecific, confirming that the binding site of α-chymotrypsin is planar, elongated, and curved. While Compound V alkylated a histidine residue Compound II did not.
