Antimicrobial Effect of Rana ridibunda Skin Gland Peptides against Multidrug Resistant Pathogens
Given the continuous emergence of virulent multidrug resistant (MDR) strains of microorganisms, the scientific world has turned attention into identifying new sources for antimicrobial compounds. These include bacteria, fungi, plants and animals (Zasloff, 2002; Sang and Blecha, 2008; Wang et al., 2009). It is well established in previous studies that amphibian skin represents an excellent source for antimicrobial peptides used by the animals as an innate pharmacological defensive mechanism to ensure their survival in a habitat full of a variety of pathogens.
