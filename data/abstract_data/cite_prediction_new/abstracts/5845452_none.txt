Challenges in the Design of Diffraction-limited Storage Rings
We review the present developments in the design of ultra-low emittance storage ring light sources those having emittances approaching the diffraction limit for multi-keV X-ray production and the associated design challenges. An overview is presented of the design approaches for these 4 generation storage ring (4GSR) light sources now in construction or being studied for near-term implementation, together with concepts for future rings that would operate with emittances at or below the diffraction limit for 1-Å (12-keV).
