Effect of diffusion of elements on network topology and self-organization
Centre for Interdisciplinary Research in Basic Sciences, Jamia Millia Islamia,New Delhi 110025, India.(Dated: September 2, 2011)ABSTRACTWe study the inﬂuence of elements diﬀusing in and out of a network to the topological changes ofthe network and characterize it by investigating the behavior of probability of degree distribution(Γ(k)) with degree k. The local memory of the incoming element and its interaction with theelements already present in the network during the growing process signiﬁcantly aﬀect the networkstability which in turn reorganize the network properties. We found that the properties of Γ(k)of this network are deviated from scale free type, where the power law behavior contains aexponentially decay factor supporting earlier reported results of Amaral et.al. [11] and Newman[12] and recent statistical analysis results on degree distribution data of some scale free network[13]. Our numerical results also support the behavior of this Γ(k). However, we found numer-ically the contribution from exponential factor to the Γ(k) to be very weak as compared to thescale free factor showing that the network as a whole carries the scale free properties approximately.KEYWORDS : Scale free network, Probability of degree distribution, Master equation, Preferentialattachment, Growing network.
