Treatment of cancer of the pancreas with high energy photons and electrons.
A technique for using high energy photons and electrons in the treatment of unresectable pancreatic cancer is described. This technique consists of opposed lateral 45 Mv photon beams and an anterior mixed beam (45 Mv photons and 20 Mev electrons). This technique achieves greater sparing of adjacent normal structures than traditional 60 Co techniques. The case of a patient so treated is reported. Other applications of high energy photon and electron beams to pancreatic neoplasms are briefly discussed. The authors conclude that with high energy photon and electron beams it should now be possible to provide improved palliation if not definitive radiotherapeutic management of localized but unresectable cancers of the pancreas.
