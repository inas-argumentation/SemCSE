Magnetotransport in a pseudomorphic GaAs/Ga0.8In0.2As/Ga0.75Al0.25As heterostructure with a Si delta -doping layer.
Magnetotransport properties of a pseudomorphic GaAs/${\mathrm{Ga}}_{0.8}$${\mathrm{In}}_{0.2}$As/${\mathrm{Ga}}_{0.75}$${\mathrm{Al}}_{0.25}$As heterostructure are investigated in pulsed magnetic fields up to 50 T and at temperatures of T=1.4 and 4.2 K. The structure studied consists of a Si \ensuremath{\delta} layer parallel to a ${\mathrm{Ga}}_{0.8}$${\mathrm{In}}_{0.2}$As quantum well (QW). The dark electron density of the structure is ${\mathit{n}}_{\mathit{e}}$=1.67\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{16}$ ${\mathrm{m}}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}2}$. By illumination the density can be increased up to a factor of 4; this way the second subband in the ${\mathrm{Ga}}_{0.8}$${\mathrm{In}}_{0.2}$As QW can become populated as well as the Si \ensuremath{\delta} layer. The presence of electrons in the \ensuremath{\delta} layer results in drastic changes in the transport data, especially at magnetic fields beyond 30 T. The phenomena observed are interpreted as (i) magnetic freeze-out of carriers in the \ensuremath{\delta} layer when a low density of electrons is present in the \ensuremath{\delta} layer and (ii) quantization of the electron motion in the two-dimensional electron gases in both the ${\mathrm{Ga}}_{0.8}$${\mathrm{In}}_{0.2}$As QW and the Si \ensuremath{\delta} layer in the case of high densities. These conclusions are corroborated by the numerical results of our theoretical model. We obtain satisfactory agreement between model and experiment.
