FACTORS INFLUENCING VOTING RESULTS OF LOCAL TRANSPORTATION FUNDING INITIATIVES WITH A SUBSTANTIAL RAIL TRANSIT COMPONENT : CASE STUDIES OF BALLOT MEASURES IN ELEVEN COMMUNITIES
This study is a follow-up study to a previous publication titled Why Campaigns for Local Transportation Funding Initiatives Succeed or Fail: An Analysis of Four Communities and National Data. This study uses the same case study methodology and expands its coverage to eleven communities from the past four years. The study concludes that voting behavior on ballot measures that involve funding for a transportation package with a substantial rail component are influenced by three elements: 1) the nature of the transportation package, 2) the process used to determine it, and 3) communications campaigns used by both proponents and opponents.
