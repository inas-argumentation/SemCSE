Estimating overspray exposure potential from aerosol sprayed products onto surfaces.
T he portion of the original mass sprayed by an aerosol product that does not impact and stay on the treated surface has been termed overspray, which is not only inefficient but, to the extent that it remains airborne, it can represent a significant exposure to the applicator and other persons proximate to the spraying operation. In this column, some approximating theoretical considerations are applied to estimate the potential amount of overspray coming from aerosol products with known spray particle size distributions and spray velocities. A simple spreadsheet tool is provided as an online supplement to assist the user in these estimations. Brief and summary comparisons of the model with experimental work are provided along with a discussion on the effect of surface target characteristics on the overspray potential.
