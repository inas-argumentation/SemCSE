DYNAMIC DISTRIBUTION OF BALANCED SALT AND MANNITOL INFUSION AFTER BLOOD LOSS IN MAN
1. During post‐hemorrhagic transcapillary refilling, the distribution of extracellular fluid is biased toward the plasma. The ratio of plasma volume to interstitial fluid, normally about 0.23, remains elevated after blood volume restoration is complete at 24 to 36 hr following non‐shocking hemorrhage.
