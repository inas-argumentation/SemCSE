There was a much greater and faster accumulation of m-tyramine than of the other amines which may be of importance for the dopamine receptor stimulation. All the P-hydroxylated amines reached about the same peak concentrations but stimulation of the noradrenaline receptors was only seen after injection of cr-methyldopa. In conclusion, treatment with m-tyrosine and a-methyldopa caused a stimulation of central dopamine and noradrenaline receptors, respectively, whereas no effect on these receptors was observed after treatment with a-methyl-m-tyrosine.
