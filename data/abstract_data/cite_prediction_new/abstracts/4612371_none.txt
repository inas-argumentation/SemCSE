Kidney cancer in utility workers exposed to polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs)
We wish to report a cluster of kidney malignancies (adenocarcinoma) that has occurred among three young male utility workers who were responsible for maintaining electrical transmission equipment including power transformers. Each was employed by a single public utility company during the same period and worked at some time repairing and maintaining transformers. The most common coolants used in these transformers until relatively recently were PCBs.
