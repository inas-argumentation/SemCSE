Time-domain impulse injection method for crest factor reduction of OFDM signals
This paper treats the problem of crest factor reduction (CFR) for orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) signals. The proposed impulse injection method reduces the crest factor of the time-domain signal by injecting judiciously designed complex-valued impulse in selected discrete-time locations while keeping both the in-band error vector magnitude (EVM) and out-of-band spectral emission under control. Numerical examples are provided to demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
