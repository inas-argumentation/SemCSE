Professional Autonomy in a Comparative Perspective
Two contradictory tendencies can be found in advanced capitalist societies regarding professionals and professional work. On the one hand, transformations in these societies are said to impose logics that result in the weakening of the traditional power and status of professions and professionalism in society; but, on the other hand, the number and the importance of professional groups, as well as those aspiring to see their occupational groups socially, politically and culturally acknowledged and rewarded with the profession ‘label’, have been increasing.
