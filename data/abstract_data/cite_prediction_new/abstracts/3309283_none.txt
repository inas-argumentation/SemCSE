Choledochal cysts demonstrated by oral cholecystography.
A choledochal cyst is a congenital anomaly of the common bile duct in which all or part of the duct is dilated. Occasionally the cyst occurs as a pouch-like outcropping of the duct wall, but more commonly it represents a fusiform dilatation of the duct. A typical clinical triad of abdominal pain, jaundice, and palpable abominal mass is associated with the choledochal cyst in approximately 60 per cent of the cases. The demonstration of a choledochal cyst by oral cholecystography is considered rare. Only 6 documented cases of such demonstration have been described in the world literature among a total of about 500 reported cases of choledochal cysts. This report presents 2 additional cases of choledochal cysts diagnosed by oral cholecystography. In both, the diagnosis was unsuspected prior to oral cholecystography.
