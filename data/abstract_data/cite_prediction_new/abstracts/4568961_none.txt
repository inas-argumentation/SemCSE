Supporting Efficient Recursive Query Processing in DHTs
Although various approaches have been proposed to process complex queries, such as range queries, join queries, aggregate queries and etc., in Peer-to-Peer data management research, we find currently there are no efficient techniques to support linear recursive queries, whic h is also one indispensable types of queries and exhibits crucial importance in many scenarios. In this paper, we propose a novel algorithm to process recursive query in DHT networks. The contribution of this paper mainly lies in: (1) presenting an innovative subgraph-based fragmentatio n method to organize the computation in a scalable execution tree, (2)theoretically proving the completeness and perfo rmance of the algorithm, (3)extensively evaluating the performance of the algorithm in popular graphs. In addition, our approaches can also be applied in grid systems and large-scale distributed databases with little modificatio n.
