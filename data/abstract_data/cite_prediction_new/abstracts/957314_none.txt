A comparison of the survival time and gross pathology in producing and nonproducing White Leghorn hens deprived of water.
BIERER et al. (1965) reported the average survival time for adult White Leghorn hens to be 6 days when deprived of water and receiving feed. Ambient chamber temperature for these hens was 29°C. Gee and Huston (1965), working with White Leghorn hens receiving feed but no water, reported an average survival time of 22 days when at 9°C. and 11 days at 30°C. White Plymouth Rock hens survived for longer periods of time. The work reported by Bierer et al. (1965) was with hens in a state of egg production. It is the purpose of this study to compare the effect of water deprivation on laying and nonlaying White Leghorn hens. MATERIALS AND METHODS In a group of 38 one-year-old caged White Leghorn hens, it was determined by daily egg production records that 15 of the hens were not producing eggs.
