Predicting the Topics to Review in Preparation of Your Next Meeting
Memory augmentation is the process of providing human memory with information that facilitates and complements the recall of an event in a person’s past. In this paper, we propose a novel time-series method for predicting the topics that one should review in preparation of one’s next meeting. This can be seen as a way of augmenting human memory. Since the number of topics that are discussed in different meetings of a typical person might be very large, there is a need for detecting topics that are more likely to continue in subsequent meetings, in order to focus one’s attention just on them. Our experimental results on real-world data demonstrate that our proposed method significantly outperforms the state-of-the-art Hidden Markov Model (HMM) baseline.
