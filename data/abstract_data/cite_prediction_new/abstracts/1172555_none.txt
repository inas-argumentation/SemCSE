Stator Current Harmonic Assessment of Induction Motor for Fault Diagnosis
This paper presents an assessment of motor current signature analysis for fault diagnosis of an induction motor. Single phasing fault has been considered and stator current harmonics are analyzed by assessing peaks and symmetry in harmonic spectrums both at normal condition and at fault condition. ‘db4’ is used for current signature analysis wavelet decomposition. A comparative observation is made based on the result obtained by computer simulation. Some distinct and significant changes are found at fault condition which may be very useful for diagnosis of such faults.
