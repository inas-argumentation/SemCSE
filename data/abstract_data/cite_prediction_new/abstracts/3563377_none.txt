New results on robust exponential stability for uncertain system with interval delay
This paper is concerned with the robust exponential stability analysis of the continuous system with interval time-varying delay and norm-bounds uncertainties. By using Lyapunov-Krasovskii functional method and employing some novel techniques to overcome the disadvantages of the method in existing references, several delay-range-dependent conditions are derived in term of the linear matrix inequalities. The merits of the proposed criteria lie in the decay rate being a free value and the less conservativeness. Finally, some numerical examples are illustrated to show the improvement of this paper.
