Current treatment of head and neck squamous cell cancer
Head and neck cancer is a heterogenous group of cancers involving the upper aerodigestive tract. In this review article, the most common sites will be discussed, including the oral cavity, oropharynx, hypopharynx, and larynx. Etiology and epidemiology will be discussed followed by staging, treatment, and survival. J. Surg. Oncol. 2014 110:551–574. © 2014 Wiley Periodicals, Inc.
