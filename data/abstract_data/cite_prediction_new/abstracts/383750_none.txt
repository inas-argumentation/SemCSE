Cable shield bonding effectiveness A tested case with lightning current surge
This paper shows the result of tests with standard 8/20 μs current impulse to evaluate the efficiency on the diversion of lightning surges from cables entering a shielded enclosure, for a few typical cable bonding configurations. A discussion is made about the test results and on the use of a parameter like transfer impedance to characterize cable transits. Keywords— Earthing, bonding; pigtail, 360 bond; cable shield, cable bonding, cable earthing; lightning protection.
