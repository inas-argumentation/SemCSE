The set of eigenvalues of a square matrix M is denoted by A(M). We also write A(G) = A(A(G)) for the set of eigenvalues of a graph G. A pair (T, G) where G is a connected graph and T a subgroup of aut(G), is distancebitransitive if G is a nonregular, not complete bipartite graph with bipartition A u B = VG satisfying that for any four vertices u, v, u', v ' with u and u' both in the same part and d(u, v) = d(u', v'), there exists an automorphism g in Tsuch that (u)g = u' and (v)g = v'. A graph G is also called distance-hitransitive if the pair (aut(G), G) is distance-bitransitive. A distance-bitransitive pair (T, G) is imprimitive if either the permutation group (T, A) or the permutation group (T, B) is imprimitive.
