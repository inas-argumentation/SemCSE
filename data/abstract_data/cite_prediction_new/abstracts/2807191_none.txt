Morphology, kinetics and service reliability of Cu6Sn5 texture formed on Sn/Cu interface
We proposed a hexagonal-rod growth behavior to describe the growth mechanism of the intermetallic Cu<inf>6</inf>Sn<inf>5</inf> phase inside the liquid Sn-base solder. We found that the interfacial Cu<inf>6</inf>Sn<inf>5</inf> phase formed on Sn/Cu joint interface also has the similar characteristics in terms of the shapes and surface textures. Based on this anisotropic growth behavior, a supply-controlled kinetic model of Cu<inf>6</inf>Sn<inf>5</inf> phase was established. In addition, this study could deepen our understanding of the solid-state phase transformation in Cu<inf>6</inf>Sn<inf>5</inf>, and also be of great significance in the interfacial reliability of solder interconnections.
