Planar Graphs on the World Sheet: The Hamiltonian Approach
April 4, 2008 LBNL-xxxxx Planar Graphs On The World Sheet: The Hamiltonian Approach 1 Korkut Bardakci Department of Physics University of California at Berkeley and Theoretical Physics Group Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory University of California Berkeley, California 94720 Abstract The present work continues the program, initiated by the author and C.B.Thorn, of summing planar Feynman graphs on the world sheet. Although it is based on the same classical action introduced in the earlier work, there are two important new features: Instead of the formalism based on path integral used in the earlier work, the model is quantized using the canonical algebra and the Hamiltonian picture. The new approach has an important advantage over the old one: The ultraviolet divergence that plagued the earlier work is absent. Also, using a family of projections operators, we are able to give an exact represention on the world sheet of both the φ 3 theory, on which most of the previous work was based, and also of the φ 4 theory.
