ρ0 Production in Cu+Cu Collisions at sNN=200GeV and 62.4 GeV in STAR
The results on ρ ( 770 ) 0 production in Cu+Cu collisions at s N N = 200 and 62.4 GeV in STAR are presented. The ρ 0 is measured via its hadronic decay channel and used as a sensitive tool to examine the collision dynamics in the hadronic medium.
