Determination of nucleoside composition of ribonucleic acid by gas-liquid chromatography.
A procedure for analyzing microgram quantities of RNA for nucleoside composition, including pseudouridine, is described. It involves enzymically digesting RNA to the constituent nucleosides, reducing the reaction mixture to dryness, and forming trimethylsilyl derivatives by treating with N,O -bis(trimethylsilyl)acetamide without first removing salt and protein. Gas-liquid chromatography of an aliquot of nucleoside derivatives containing 10 μg or less of total nucleosides is performed with linear temperature programming. Chromatography time is less than 40 min.
