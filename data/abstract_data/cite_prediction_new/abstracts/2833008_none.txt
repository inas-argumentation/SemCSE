A calculus on Fock space and its probabilistic interpretations
We introduce on the Fock space Φ ( L 2 ( R + )) two operators ∇ ⊖ and ∇ ⊗ expressing the infinitesimal perturbations of random variables by time changes in the Wiener and Poisson probabilistic interpretations of Φ ( L 2 ( R + )). These operators have close connections with stochastic integration, regularity of laws, chaotic expansions and complement the annihilation and creation operators ∇ - and ∇ + that are related to perturbations by shifts of trajectories.
