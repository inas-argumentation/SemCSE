The Mutative Impact on Family Life of Serious Mental and Physical Illness in a Parent *
• Academic Lecture delivered at the 19th Annual Meeting of the Canadian Psychiatric Association, Toronto, 13 June 1969. Supported by U.S. P.R.S. MH12043-01 and R01-MH-14052-o1. 'Ittleson Professor of Child Psychiatry, Washington University School of Medicine, St. Louis, Missouri 63108. Canad. Psychiat. Ass. J. Vol. 14 (1969) authors to differentiate between the effects of mental and physical illness, both being treated as if they exercised a similar influence. Several different perspectives on illness, starting with illness viewed in a global and popular sense as 'trouble' for the family, is presented here.
