The fair value of FX options. Do you get what you pay for?
The latest BIS (2010) report tells us that average daily turnover in the FX options market is 207 billion US dollars. We can see from figure 1 that this is much larger than it used to be; nevertheless, it is much smaller than the daily spot turnover which in 2010 was 1.5 USD trillion. However, a substantial part of the spot turnover is actually due to option activity; this is estimated to be 30% of the total spot flow due to delta and gamma hedging (James et al. 2012). So the FX option market is responsible for a quite enormous amount of global daily FX flow. Who buys them, and who sells them?
