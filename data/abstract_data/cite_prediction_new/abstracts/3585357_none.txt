I have selected as my research object ’the period of night frost’ in SovietFinnish relations in 1958-59 and it has been possible to collect quite rich empirical material on this case. In the main, the studies of Johan Galtung, Fredrik Hoffmann, Peter Wallensteen and Hikan Wiberg have given me useful insights in constructing the framework of my analysis. I have partly constructed my study around some of the sanction theories as introduced by Wallensteen.2 In spite of this my article is largely ’untheoretical’ and descriptive because of the confining effects of this kind of case study.
