Fractal dimension predicts arrhythmia recurrence in patients being treated for life-threatening ventricular arrhythmias. ESVEM Investigators.
Ambulatory electrocardiographic (Holter) recordings are widely used for arrhythmia detection, risk stratification, and to guide therapy of various arrhythmias. The most common information obtained is the frequency of single ventricular premature complexes (VPCs), couplets, and runs of ventricular tachycardia (VT). Recently, additional useful information has been acquired from Holter recordings: parameters of heart rate variability and fractal dimension (fractal D). ',2 The latter is derived from the application of signal-processing methods that use nonlinear dynamics to assess the time distribution of VPC occurrence.
