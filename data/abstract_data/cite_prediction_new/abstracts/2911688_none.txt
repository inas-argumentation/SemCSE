Primordial abundances of 6 Li, 9 Be, and 11 B with neutrino degeneracy
Recent measurements of deuterium abundances from QSO absorption spectra show two conflicting numbers, which differ by an order of magnitude. Allowing the neutrino degeneracy together with gravitational constant variation at the epoch of primordial nucleosynthesis, the implication of these observations on the ${}^{6}\mathrm{Li},{ }^{9}\mathrm{Be},$ and ${}^{11}\mathrm{B}$ abundances is discussed. Within the permitted ranges consistent with D, ${}^{4}\mathrm{He},$ and ${}^{7}\mathrm{Li},$ we observe the strong $\ensuremath{\eta}$ dependence of ${}^{11}\mathrm{B}$ as in standard big-bang nucleosynthesis but no significant dependence is observed for ${}^{6}\mathrm{Li}$ and ${}^{9}\mathrm{Be}.$ The predictions of ${}^{6}\mathrm{Li}$ and ${}^{9}\mathrm{Be}$ for low and high deuterium differ by an order of magnitude and predictions for ${}^{11}\mathrm{B}$ differ by several orders of magnitude at most.
