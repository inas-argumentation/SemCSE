INFLUENCE OF STRAIN − TEMPERATURE TRAINING PARAMETERS ON SHAPE MEMORY EFFECTS IN Ti − Ni − BASED ALLOYS
The paper attempts to analyze and summarize the results of world-wide works devoted to the problem of the shape memory effect (SME) and the two-way SME (TWSME) inducing in shape memory alloys and analyzes the reasons of their variant results. The prospective ways of enhancing functional properties and their precise regulation are discussed.
