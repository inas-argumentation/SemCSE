Link travel time prediction for decentralized route guidance architectures
This paper presents a prediction technique for decentralized route guidance architecture for identifying time-dependent link travel times. When the travel times are communicated to drivers, it leads to time-dependent fastest paths consistent with the forecast. An iterative process of traffic simulations, followed by dynamic route determinations, assure the fixed-point property of the forecast. An accurate and user-optimal forecast of congestion is achieved. A back-dating process is used to insure the stable routing after a finite number of iterations. The paper includes results from an experimental case study based on a road network in Troy, Michigan.
