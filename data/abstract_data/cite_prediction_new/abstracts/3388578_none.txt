Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology of Growth Hormones
In its modern conception, biotechnology is the use of genetic engineering techniques to manipulate microorganisms, plants, and animals in order to produce commercial products and processes that benefit man. These techniques, which are the backbone of the biotechnological revolution that began in the mid 1970s, have permitted the isolation and manipulation of specific genes and the development of transgenic microorganisms that produce mainly eukaryotic proteins of therapeutic use, such as vaccines, enzymes, and hormones.
