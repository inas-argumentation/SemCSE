What Niagara University College Teachers Think: Findings from the 2014 Survey
Last year a small, inter-disciplinary group of faculty members met regularly as part of a faculty learning community (FLC) to read and discuss Ken Bain’s book What the Best College Teachers Do (2004). He studied 60 to 70 college teachers at two dozen institutions whom he identified as “great college teachers”, exemplars recognized by peers who had achieved lasting learning impact with their students. He studied them to identify the special ways they think about their teaching and understand how students learn effectively. Bain wanted to discern the lessons he took from the great college teachers that can also help others teach well.
