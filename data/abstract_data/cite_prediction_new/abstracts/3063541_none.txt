Fast dynamic simulation of flexible and rigid bodies with kinematic constraints
Realistic real-time computer animation of a large number of objects requires the development of special methods and algorithms. In this paper we present a new method that allows the fast simulation of the dynamics of both-flexible and rigid bodies. The algorithm uses various kinematic constraints and can handle arbitrary arrangements of objects with linear time complexity. Fast simulations of rigid and flexible bodies is possible due to the combination of a reduced-coordinate formulation and a simple-but visually correct deformation model.
