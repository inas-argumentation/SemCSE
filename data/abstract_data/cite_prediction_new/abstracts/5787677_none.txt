Tau lepton polarization in quasielastic neutrino-nucleon scattering
We derive structure functions for the quasielastic production of octet baryons in ν τ n and ν ¯ τ p interactions and study the polarization of τ leptons produced in the Δ Y = 0 reactions. Possible impact of the charged second-class currents is investigated by adopting a simple phenomenological parametrization for the nonstandard scalar and tensor nucleon form factors. Our choice of the unknown parameters is made to satisfy the limits obtained in the (anti)neutrino scattering experiments and rigid restrictions derived from the nuclear structure studies.
