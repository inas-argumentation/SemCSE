REIN ALGORITHM AND THE INFLUENCE OF POINT CLOUD DENSITY ON NDSM AND DEM PRECISION IN A SUBMEDITERRANEAN FOREST
The REIN algorithm makes use of the redundancy in lidar point cloud to generate bare ground DEM and vegetation canopy nDSM. The influence of the input lidar point density on the DEM precision and consequently also on the nDSM precision in the context of REIN have been analyzed in a rough-relief submediterranean karstic forested site. Different lidar point densities were simulated by thinning the density of the basic lidar dataset by factors of 2, 4, and 8. The DEMs and nDSMs were calculated separately from entire dataset and from the thinned lidar data. Strong smoothing effect of lidar data thinning was found in the study area both for DEM and nDSM. Based on the preset minimum precision criteria, the three highest point densities (i.e., 2.71, 5.43 and 10.85 last and only returns per m 2 ) may be used for DEM generation in the study area, and the two highest point densities (i.e., 8.29 and 16.56 of all returns per m 2 ) may be used to generate the nDSM. A coarser DEM raster resolution than 1 m is advised for all the lidar point densities except the highest one.
