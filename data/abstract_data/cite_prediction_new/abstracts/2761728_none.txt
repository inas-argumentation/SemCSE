Kcy-words: Retina, Retinal detachment, Retinal Separation, Mechanics of cystoid bodies Retinal Separation is caused by a time spectrum of histologic alterations beginning with edema. This cdema forms in the inner layers due to fluid accumulation in the extracellular spaces. As the edcma incrcases the cystoid spaces thicken due entirely to mechanical forces, although the tissue behavior is purely biological. This paper attempts an analytical study in determining the various discontinuities within the yield surface. One important findings is that the forces in themselves do not distort the retina.
