1-[4-(1H-imidazol-1-yl)phenyl]ethanone monohydrate
In the crystal structure of the title compound, C11H10N2O·H2O, the solvent water molecule links the organic molecules through O—H⋯O and O—H⋯N hydrogen bonds, forming chains that run diagonally across the bc face. These chains are connected to adjacent chains through weak C—H⋯O interactions, resulting in hydrogen-bonded sheets extending along the b and c axes.
