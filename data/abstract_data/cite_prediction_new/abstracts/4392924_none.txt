Research on Household Load Scheduling Based on Time-Sharing Electricity Price
In the paper, the lowest electricity cost scheduling plan and scheduling plan considering satisfaction were proposed in new system of household energy management involved by PV solar power generation system. The scheduling plan was optimized with PSO particle swarm optimization algorithm, wherein electricity cost can be effectively lowered through the lowest electricity cost scheduling plan, but user satisfaction was low. Electricity cost consumption and user satisfaction were emphasized synchronously in scheduling plan considering satisfaction. Satisfaction of users can be maximized during reduction of electricity cost consumption with wide practical significance.
