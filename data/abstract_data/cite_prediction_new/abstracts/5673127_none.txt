Investigating several alternatives for estimating the lead time demand distribution in a continuous review inventory model
Using Monte Carlo experiments this paper analyzes the cost differences between several alternative approximations for the Lead Time Demand Distribution (LTDD) in a continuous review (s,Q) inventory model. The information on LTDD is assumed to be composed of two components: demand per time unit and lead time.
