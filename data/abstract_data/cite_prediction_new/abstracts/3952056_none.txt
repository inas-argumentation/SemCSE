X-ray diffraction studies of phase transitions in the membrane of Mycoplasma laidlawii.
X-ray diffraction patterns from dispersions of isolated membranes show that the membrane fatty acyl chains experience a thermal phase transition. Below the transition the chains are in an hexagonal array like the hexagonal phase of the long-chain paraffins and give a sharp diffraction maximum near 4.2 A. Above the transition they are in a more fluid state and a broad maximum centered at 4.6 A is observed. The transition is gradual, and its temperature varies with the fatty acyl composition of the membrane. The data demonstrate the presence of a lipid monolayer or bilayer in the membrane below the transition, and the low angle diffraction indicates that the same general configuration is retained at higher temperatures.
