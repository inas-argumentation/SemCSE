Treatment of petroleum industry oil sludge by Rhodotorula sp.
SummaryA Rhodotorula sp., isolated from soil, which showed a versttile capacity to degrade various aromatic and aliphatic hydrocarbons, was used to treat oil sludge. As a result of treatment, there was significant cecrease in BOD, COD and contents of various petroleum fractions. The susceptibility to degradation was in the following order: saturate fraction>aromatic fraction>asphaltic fraction.
