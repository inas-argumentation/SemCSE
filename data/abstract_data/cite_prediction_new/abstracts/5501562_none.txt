Characterization of nanostructures STM images with the wavelet and ridgelet transforms
This study is first intended to show by means of the Ridgelet and Wavelet Transforms, that image processing performed on magnetic nanostructures can put into evidence some strong anisotropic characteristics of the structure and, moreover, could corroborate electric and magnetic measurements performed on magnetic nanostructures. We show here that specific characteristics of these nanostructures can be detected by means of image processing applied to pictures taken with a Scanning Tunneling Microscope. We also demonstrate the capacities of Wavelet Multiresolution in the filtering of very specific high and low frequency noises.
