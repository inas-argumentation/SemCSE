A written test as an alternative to performance testing
Summary. Performance tests are logistically complex and time consuming. To reach adequate reliability long tests are imperative. Additionally, they are very difficult to adapt to the individual learning paths of students, which is necessary in problem‐based learning. This study investigates a written alternative to performance‐based tests. A Knowledge Test of Skills (KTS) was developed and administered to 380 subjects of various educational levels, including both first‐year students and recently graduated doctors.
