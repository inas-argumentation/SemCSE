Mainstream sustainable development: The challenge of putting theory into practice
This paper outlines the main features of the mainstream of sustainable development thinking and the documents from which it is drawn. It then explores these in the context of a wetland conservation project in northern Nigeria. Issues of environment and development have been tackled in different ways, through protected area designation, development microprojects and concern for river basin management. It is concluded that the practical implementation of ‘sustainable development’ projects is far more complex and problematic than might at first appear.
