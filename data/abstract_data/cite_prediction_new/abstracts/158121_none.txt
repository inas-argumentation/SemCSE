Measuring Service Prices of U.S. Manufacturing Capital Input, Inventories, and Financial Working Capital, 1947-1981
In this paper, we construct and compare various service price indexes of capital input, in­ ventories, and financial working capital, using com­ plete sets of variants of the Jorgensonian user cost formula. We demonstrate that variations in the con­ struction of the series yield substantially different results. We also find that proper measures of service prices should incorporate a complete set of vari­ ables suggested by economic theory. In addition, Divisia indexes of financial working capital and its service price are superior to the direct aggregates.
