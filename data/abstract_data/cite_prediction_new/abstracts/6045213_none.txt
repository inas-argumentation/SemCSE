The evolutionary status of the bright high-latitude supergiant HD 190390

Newobservational material has been obtained with four diﬀerent instruments and is presented here. An extensive abundance analysisbased on high resolution, high signal-to-noise NTT+EMMI spectra conﬁrms the metal deﬁciency of this object ([Fe /H]=−1.6),together with a high lithium content (logǫ(Li)=1.9). A variability analysis based on Geneva photometry over seven yearsreveals beating with a period of ∼3000 days. It is, however, not clear whether this beating is caused by a stable triplet, or itis the consequence of small changes in the main frequency. More recent data obtained with the HIPPARCOS satellite and theMercator telescope not only conﬁrm the main period, but also support the presence of a second periodicity of 11 days, whichwas also found in the Geneva photometry. A conclusive evolutionary status of this object is not given, but alternative to theUU Her (i.e. post-AGB) status, a W Vir classiﬁcation is discussed.Key words.
