ESMO Minimum Clinical Recommendations for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of osteosarcoma.
. Patients with radiological findings suggesting a bone sarcoma should be referred without prior biopsy to a centre with particular bone sarcoma experience. Histological diagnosis is made by open surgical biopsy. Osteosarcoma is divided into osteoblastic, chondroblastic, fibroblastic, telangiectatic and mixed subtypes. Paraosteal osteosarcoma is a low grade tumor arising on the bone surface.
