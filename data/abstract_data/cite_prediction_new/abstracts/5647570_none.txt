Seismicity in Iceland 1991 – 2000 monitored by the SIL seismic system
The SIL seismic system was designed and installed in the South Iceland Lowland in 1989–1990 and was fully operational in automatic mode in June 1991. In 1994 the system was expanded to the northeastern part of Iceland and has been gradually covering larger parts of Iceland since 1996. During the first decade of operation some 160,000 events were recorded and processed. In that period 4 volcanic eruptions and 2 large earthquakes ( =6.6) have occurred that have all been monitored by the system.
