Semiconductor electrical properties from the frequency dependence of the dielectric constant: application to n-type ZnSe heteroepitaxial thin films
We discuss the derivation of the carrier concentration n and mobility of thin films from transmission and reflection in the far-infrared, through the expression of the dielectric constant when plasmon-phonon coupling is incorporated. We measure transmission of n-type-doped ZnSe on undoped ZnSe on GaAs, and derive approximate values of the absorption coefficient α. We find α ∝ n 1 2 at 100 cm-1 and constant versus wavenumber W at low W and high n. These variation laws are also found from approximate expressions of α in the same conditions.
