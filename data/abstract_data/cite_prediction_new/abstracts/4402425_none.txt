Searching approximate global optimal Heilbronn configurations of nine points in the unit square via GPGPU computing
In this paper we present a method of applying the GPGPU technology to compute the approximate optimal solution to the Heilbronn problem for nine points in the unit square, namely, points $$P_1,P_2,\ldots ,P_9$$P1,P2,…,P9 in $$[0,1]\times [0,1]$$[0,1]×[0,1] so that the minimal area of triangles $$P_iP_jP_k\,(1\le i<j<k\le 9)$$PiPjPk(1≤i<j<k≤9) gets the maximal value $$h_9(\Box )$$h9(□). We construct nine rectangles with edge length 1 / 80 in the unit square which contain all optimal Heilbronn configurations up to possible rotation and reflection, and prove that $$\frac{9\sqrt{65}-55}{320}=0.054875999\cdots<h_9(\Box )<0.054878314$$965-55320=0.054875999⋯<h9(□)<0.054878314, the lower bound here comes from Comellas and Yebra’s paper.
