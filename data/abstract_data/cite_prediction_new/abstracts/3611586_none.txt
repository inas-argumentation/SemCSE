The effects of Cu nanoparticles addition in Sn-3.0Ag-0.5Cu solder paste on the microstructure and shear strength of the solder joints
This paper investigated effects of Cu nanoparticles addition in Sn-3.0Ag-0.5Cu solder paste on the microstructure and shear strength of the solder joints. Experimental results indicated that the microstructure of the SAC-nano Cu solder joints was composed of β-Sn dendrites and Cu6Sn5 intermetallic compounds (IMC). As the increase of the Cu nanoparticle content, the grain size of the β-Sn and Cu6Sn5 decreased significantly. Meanwhile, the amount of the Cu6Sn5 IMC grains increased in the solder bulk of the SAC-nano Cu solder joint. With 0.05 to 0.3 wt% Cu nanoparticles addition, the average shear force of the SAC305 solder joints increased from approximately 90 N to 100 N. However, as the concentration of the Cu nanoparticles reached 2 and 3 wt%, the shear force of the solder joints dropped to 70 and 50 N. The results of the X-ray observations revealed that the void percentage in the SAC-nano Cu solder joints increased greatly when the concentration of Cu nanoparticles was higher than 0.5 wt% in the solder paste. The microstructure degradation due to the increase of void percentage was suggested as the dominant factor for the decrease of the shear performance of the solder joints.
