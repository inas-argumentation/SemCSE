Shouted speech detection in noisy environments based on rahmonic and mel-frequency cepstrum coefficients
Crime prevention systems have been developed to detect the hazardous situation. In general, the systems by utilizing a camera have been used to monitor situations with the image information. They have however a problem that it is di cult to detect in dark or blind area. To address this problem, it is possible to detect such situations by utilizing not only image informations but also acoustic ones. This paper describes a detection method of a shouted speech which is one of acoustic informations and is uttered for helping in the hazardous situation by using mel-frequency cepstrum coe cients (MFCCs) and the rahmonic. MFCCs collectively make up mel-frequency cepstrum, and the 2nd order MFCC especially tends to arise in a shouted speech. On the other hand, the rahmonic shows a subharmonic of fundamental frequency in the cepstrum domain, and it also tends to arise in a shouted speech. In this paper, we attempt to detect shouted speeches by integrating the features which are able to distinguish modal and shouted speeches. To con rm e ectiveness of the method, we conducted the experiments of shouted speech detection in clean and noisy environments. Furthermore, we tried to reduce the dimensions of the feature vector for the shouted speech detection and evaluate the detection performance in noisy environments. As a result, we could achieve more than 95 % of the detection performance for shouted speeches of both male and female speakers. Moreover, we could also reduce about 73 % of the dimensions for male speakers and about 12 % of ones for female speakers.
