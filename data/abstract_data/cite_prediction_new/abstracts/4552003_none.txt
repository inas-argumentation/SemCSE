X-band composite microwave absorber using doped strontium ferrite
In this work, cobalt and aluminium doped strontium ferrite nanoparticles (SrCo<inf>x</inf>Al<inf>y</inf>Fe<inf>12−x−y</inf>O<inf>19</inf>, x=0.8, y=3) are used as inclusions in Linear Low Density Polyethylene (LLDPE) matrix to develop a composite. SrCo<inf>x</inf>Al<inf>y</inf>Fe<inf>12−x−y</inf>O<inf>19</inf> is prepared at an annealing temperature of 950°C. The microstructural studies of the powder as well as the composite are conducted on SrCo<inf>x</inf>Al<inf>y</inf>Fe<inf>12−x−y</inf>O<inf>19</inf>. The complex permittivity and complex permeability is measured at X-band. Reflection loss of SrCo<inf>x</inf>Al<inf>y</inf>Fe<inf>12−x−y</inf>O<inf>19</inf>/LLDPE composite is determined using transmission line modelling (TLM) from the measured permittivity and permeability values. Thickness optimization carried out for the same sample from i mm to 4 mm, showed maximum reflection loss for 3 mm thickness and −10 dB bandwidth (i.e. 90% absorption) over the whole band.
