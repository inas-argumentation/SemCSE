Planar waveguides in ZBLAN fabricated by He ion implantation
Single and double planar waveguides were fabricated by 2 MeV and 4 MeV He + implantation in ZBLAN. Calculation of ion implantation parameters, cut-off wavelength measurement and near-field detection indicate that Δn is negative, typically -1x10 -3 . Bulk ZBLAN glass (53%ZrF 4 -20%BaF 2 -4%LaF 3 -3%AlF 3 -20%NaF) samples were prepared using the standard melt and cast technique and cut to dimensions of 0.5 x 1.5 x 1.5 cm.
