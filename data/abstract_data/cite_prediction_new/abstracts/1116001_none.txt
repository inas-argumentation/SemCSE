The Cluetrain Manifesto: Customers and employees as people
The Cluetrain Manifesto is a document, published in 1999, on the World Wide Web which, on the basis of the opportunities offered by the Net, introduces some rather innovative ideas on company communication whose effects involve company management as well as, in a broader sense, the way the company is conceived. Being considered a contribution deserving some interest, in this paper a critical analysis of the Cluetrain Manifesto's contents is carried out in order to highlight the positive aspects as well as those that seem to be less convincing. In the final evaluation, a thesis is exposed according to which the Cluetrain Manifesto belongs to that heterogeneous group of studies which, from different points of view, are presently challenging the role of marketing as a company function, the legitimacy--or at least the reputation--of the management as a professional activity and, last but not least, the position of the company as an institution of our society.
