Exploring One-and Two-Touch Interaction for 3 D Scientific Visualization Spaces
CONTRIBUTION For exploring large 3D datasets, scientists often have to interactively navigate through and explore large single 3D spaces such as medical volume data or astronomical simulations. We explore the use of large and touch-sensitive displays for this task. In particular, we provide means to control 7 degrees of freedom (DOF: location, orientation, scale) for exploring these spaces with both dual-touch and singletouch interaction. Our single-touch interaction makes use of the visualization’s frame and provides means for precise control for all 7 degrees of freedom.
