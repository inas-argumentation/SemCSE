Why physicians should not have any contact with pharmaceutical companies?
The relationship between pharmaceutical industry and physicians is a controversial one. This is because whenever money and ethics clash, there are conflicting interests. Pharmaceutical companies operate in a world of high stakes with potentially high returns of their investments. Recouping the money they have invested in drug research and development means using marketing techniques that will ensure increased sales. There are about 20 multinational and more than 400 local companies.1 The pharmaceutical industry in Pakistan is worth more than US$ 1.5 billion. With such money at stake companies do everything to get as big a share of the market as possible.
