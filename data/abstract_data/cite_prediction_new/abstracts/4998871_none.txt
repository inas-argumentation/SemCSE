We study the isoscalar and isovector J = 0,1 mesons with the overlap operator within two flavour lattice QCD. After subtraction of the lowest-lying Dirac eigenmodes from the valence quark propagator all disconnected contributions vanish and all possible point-to-point J = 0 correlators become identical, signaling a simultaneous restoration of both SU(2)L× SU(2)R and U(1)A symmetries. The ground states of the π,σ ,a0,η mesons do not survive this truncation. All possible J = 1 states have a very clean exponential decay and become degenerate, demonstrating a SU(4) symmetry of a dynamical QCD-like string.
