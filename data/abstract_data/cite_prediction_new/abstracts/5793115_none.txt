Factors Contributing to the Success of Hospitality Graduate Students
Introduction Graduate hospitality programs are attracting more students (Zabel, 1992; Rappole, Bott, & Klein, 1997). The growth of enrollment in twoand four-year hospitality programs worldwide may have influenced the number of students applying for graduate programs (Zabel, 1992; Rappole, Bott, & Klein, 1997). The increased opportunities in both higher education and the hospitality industry also may be influencing more individuals to pursue graduate education (Partlow & Gregoire, 1994; Morse, 1999). Regardless of the reasons why individuals pursue an advanced degree, graduate students invest a significant amount of resources to increase their competitiveness in the job market.
