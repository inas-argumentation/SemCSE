The role of vitamin B12 in increasing the nutritive effectiveness of parenterally administered protein hydrolysate in surgical trauma
SummaryIncreased breadkown of tissue proteins was observed to follow an operation on the gastrointestinal tract. It was not eliminated by parenteral feeding with protein hydrolysates. A daily intramuscular injection of 20 μ g of vitamin B12 into dogs, under these conditions, restored the nitrogen blance, and on some days there was a net gain of nitrogen assimilated. The increased effectiveness of parenteral protein hydrolysate caused by vitamin B12 was connected with the increased assimilation of the protein ingredients administered, and not with any reduction of protein breakdown.
