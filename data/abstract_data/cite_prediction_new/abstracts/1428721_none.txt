Lichen aureus induced by interferon-alpha plus ribavirin.
A 36-year-old man with a medical history of chronic active hepatitis C started treatment with subcutaneous peginterferon-alfa (120μg/week) plus ribavirin orally (1000 mg/day). Two months later he was referred for evaluation of a purpuric eruption on both hands. The patient had no personal history of dermatological diseases. Physical examination showed circumscribed yellowbrown macules on the palms and interdigital spaces (Fig. 1). These lesions had a well-defined border and were non-scaling. A biopsy was performed, revealing a band-like infiltrate of lymphocytes in the upper dermis beneath a Grenz zone of normal tissue with extravasated erythrocytes and perivascular lymphocytic infiltrate (Fig. 2). The lesions disappeared spontaneously when hepatitis C treatment was stopped, thus the diagnosis of lichen aureus induced by antiviral therapy was established. DISCUSSION
