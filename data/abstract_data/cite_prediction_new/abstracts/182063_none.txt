INTRODUCTION oam concrete is either a cement paste or mortar, classified as lightweight concrete, in which air-voids are entrapped in mortar by suitable foaming agent. It possesses high flowability, low selfweight, minimal consumption of aggregate, controlled low strength and excellent thermal insulation properties [1]. By proper control in a dosage of foam, a wide range of densities (400–1600 kg/m3) of foamed concrete can be obtained for application to structural, partition, insulation and filling grades.
