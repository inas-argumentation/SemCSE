Characterization of anti‐inflammatory effect and possible mechanism of action of Tibouchina granulosa
Tibouchina granulosa, popularly known as ‘quaresmeira’, belong to a genus widely used in the traditional medicine as infusions from their leaves. Other species of Tibouchina are used as antibacterial, antioxidant or antileishmanial. In this work, our objectives were to investigate the biological effects of T. granulosa in models of acute inflammation.
