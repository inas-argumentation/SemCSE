Temperature regulation in the small Indian mongoose (Herpestes auropunctatus).
o 1. Small Indian Mongooses were exposed to air temperatures between 5°C and 45°C. 2. Within the thermoneutral zone of ambient temperature (28–38°C), the mean rectal temperature of the animals was 39.76°C ± 0.50 (S.E.) and the oxygen consumption was 0.66 ml. O2/g. hr. ± 0.18 (S.E.). 3. The mongooses responded to cold (5°C) with a threefold increase in oxygen consumption, but their thermal conductance and rectal temperature did not decrease. 4. In a hot environment, the respiratory frequency increased and total evaporative heat loss exceeded metabolic heat production. 5. It was concluded that the mongoose has effective physiological mechanisms for dealing with hot and moderately cold environments, which may help to explain its establishment in a wide range of habitats.
