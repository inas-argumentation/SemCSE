A Catalan Broadcast Conversational Speech Database
Data driven methods in speech and linguistic research, and system develoment require appropriate speech databases. A new Catalan speech database has been developed with a particular emphasis on broadcast conversational speech. The article describes origin and nature of the broadcasts and its acoust ic environment. Annotation and transcription provide statistics on specific phenomena of exhibited speech, speaker characteri stics and acoustic events. It concludes with perspective uses and limitations. Index Terms: Catalan, audio video speech database, broadcast conversation, literal transcription, spontaneous speech
