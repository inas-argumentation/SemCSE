Mg-doping and n+-p junction formation in MOVPE-grown InxGa1-xN (x∼0.4)
Mg-doping and n<sup>+</sup>-p junction formation of MOVPE-grown In<inf>x</inf>Ga<inf>1-x</inf>N (x∼0.4) have been studied. InGaN films with an In content up to 0.37 are successfully grown without both phase separation and metallic In segregation. By doing with Mg using CP<inf>2</inf>Mg, InGaN films with an In content up to 0.23 show p-type conduction with a net acceptor concentration around 5 x10<sup>16</sup> cm<sup>−3</sup>. The sample with an In content 0.37 shows phase separation when CP<inf>2</inf>Mg flow rate is increased. By using In<inf>0.23</inf>Ga<inf>0.77</inf>N films, an n<sup>+</sup>-p homo-junction structure is fabricated on a GaN template. For such a device, the response to the AM1.5 illumination is observed and Voc =1.5 V and Jsc=0.5 mA/cm<sup>2</sup> are obtained.
