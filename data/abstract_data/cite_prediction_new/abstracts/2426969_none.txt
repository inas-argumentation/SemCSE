Nonlocal protons and deuterons opposed to disorder: a single-crystal neutron diffraction study of KH0.76D0.24CO3 and a theoretical framework
We show with neutron diffraction that a single crystal of potassium hydrogen deuterium carbonate at room temperature, namely KH0.76D0.24CO3, is isomorphous with the KHCO3 and KDCO3 derivatives. Protons and deuterons are not disordered particles located at definite sites and Bragg peaks are best fitted with separate H and D sublattices. We propose a theoretical framework for nonlocal observables and macroscopic states compatible with the crystal field.
