Methods for determining minority carrier lifetime in HgCdTe photovoltaic detectors
Using three methods, i.e., modified photo-induced open-circuit voltage decay method (OCVD), small parallel resistance method and pulse recovery method, the minority carrier lifetime in the base region of Hg<inf>1−x</inf>Cd<inf>x</inf>T e pn junction devices has been measured at liquid nitrogen temperature. The lifetime is in the range of 18≈407 ns for x=0.231≈0.4186, 1≈100 ns for x=0.2234≈0.305, and 4≈20 ns for x=0.231≈0.4186 for the modified photo-induced OCVD method, the small parallel resistance method and the pulse recovery method, respectively. The results indicate that the lifetime become longer with the increase of Cd composition.
