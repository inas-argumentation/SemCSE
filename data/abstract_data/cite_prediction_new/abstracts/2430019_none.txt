Weaver ' s Model of Communication and its Implications
The most common usages of the terms "information" and "communication" are not applicable with objective validity when the object of discourse is the mind-brain system. Neither can these terms be validly employed in the description of biological objects except in a very specific and limited context. Over the past several years some writers have adopted some very careless habits of misusing these terms in ways that promote ontological fallacies and misguided metaphors. Softening the meaning of technical terms used in any science damages the science, and persistent misusages over time tend to render the words we use to explain Nature meaningless.
