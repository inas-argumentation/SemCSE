Surgical management of laryngotracheal stenosis resulting from posttraumatic scarring
SummaryThree cases of traumatic cicatricial subglottic and tracheal stenoses were presented. They were repaired and reconstructed with local mucosal flaps, autogenous nasal septal composite graft with intact mucosa and free costal cartilage with perichondrium attached on one side. A good airway and improved phonation were achieved.
