Taxusumatrin, a New Taxoid from the Stem Bark of Taxus sumatrana
A new taxoid, taxusumatrin (1), has been isolated from the stem bark of Taxus sumatrana, together with six known compounds, taxinine J (2), baccatin VI (3), 2α,5α,7β,10β-tetraacetoxy-taxa-4(20),11-diene (4), β-sitosterol (5), β-sitostenone (6), and 6β-hydroxystigmast-4-en-3-one (7). The structure of new compound 1 was determined through spectroscopic and MS analyses.
