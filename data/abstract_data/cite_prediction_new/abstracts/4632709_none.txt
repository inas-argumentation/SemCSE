Ten years of sale‐and‐leaseback transactions in The Netherlands
Purpose – The purpose of this paper is to give an overview of the sale‐and‐leaseback transactions in The Netherlands over the past ten years, and to compare the rents and yields in those transactions with what is common on the market at that moment.Design/methodology/approach – The method chosen is a straight mathematical calculation, the only possible way at this initial stage.
