The influence of a magnetic field on dielectric relaxation processes
The influence of a magnetic field on the dielectric losses in weak-polar dielectric compounds is investigated. By the solution of the Fokker-Planck equation describing the rotational diffusion of the polar group over a potential barrier it is shown that with the increasing of a magnetic field the maximum of the dielectric loss curve tg δ is shifted to the higher temperatures and diminished, in accordance with the experimental data
