Monetary Policy Transmission: Cointegration and Vector Error Correction Analysis
In the backdrop of the financial crisis of 2008, this chapter investigates the role played by the bank lending channel in monetary policy transmission . The study utilizes cointegration and vector error correction models for the time period from September 2008 to March 2011. It focuses on three policy instruments viz. Cash Reserve Ratio , Repo rate and Reverse Repo rate . In order to investigate the inter-linkages between the real and monetary policy variables, the model includes endogenous variables, namely Index of Industrial Production, Wholesale Price Index, Money Supply, Nominal Effective Exchange Rate and the three policy instruments along with controlling for exogenous influences. Results of the econometric exercise indicate that all the three policy variables significantly impact the real variables in the economy. It seems that the three policy instruments have worked as complements during the crisis to enable monetary policy management by the Reserve Bank of India.
