Integrable systems associated with the Bruhat Poisson structures
The purpose of this note is to give a simple description of a (complete) family of functions in involution on certain hermitian symmetric spaces. This family, obtained via bi-hamiltonian approach using the Bruhat Poisson structure, is especially simple for the projective spaces, where the formulas in terms of the moment map coordinates are presented. We show how these functions are related to the Gelfand-Tsetlin coordinates. We also show how the Lenard scheme can be applied.
