Comparison principle and stability of impulsive stochastic functional differential systems in terms of two measures
This paper investigates the stability properties in terms of two measures for impulsive stochastic functional differential systems. A comparison principle for impulsive stochastic functional differential systems is established for the first time. Employing the comparison principle and theory of differential inequalities, sufficient conditions ensuring stability and asymptotical stability in terms of two measures are obtained. An example is given to illustrate our results.
