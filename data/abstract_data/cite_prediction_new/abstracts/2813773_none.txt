In vivo whole‐blood T2 versus HbO2 calibration by modulating blood oxygenation level in the femoral vein through intermittent cuff occlusion
To investigate the feasibility of estimating calibration constants (K and T2o) in vivo for converting whole‐blood T2 to blood hemoglobin oxygen saturation (HbO2) according to the Luz‐Meiboom model, 1/T2=1/T2o+K(1−HbO2)2 , where K and T2o are relaxivity and transverse relaxation time of fully saturated blood, respectively.
