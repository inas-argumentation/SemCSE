A voltage‐controlled varactorless LC‐tank oscillator with a transformer‐feedback technique
A differential‐voltage‐controlled LC oscillator employs a gm‐tuned technique instead of a varactor is presented, verified, and measured. The design was demonstrated in a standard 0.18‐μm CMOS process. The oscillator can operate at the range of 4.73–5.05 GHz under a 1.8‐V supply voltage. The measured phase noise is −100.76 dBc/Hz at 100‐kHz frequency offset and −121.53 dBc/Hz at 1‐MHz frequency offset away from the 4.73‐GHz carrier with power consumption of 6.48 mW. © 2007 Wiley Periodicals, Inc.
