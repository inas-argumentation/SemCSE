Low female educational attainment in rural Pakistan: causes and remedies.
Women's social development in terms of their education is a fundamental force, which influences all health, socio-economic, cultural and political fabrics at micro- and macro-levels in any society. Higher level of female education causes reduction in fertility and infant mortality rate. Increasing the educational levels of females, especially in under developed societies, is a viable strategy to enhance the health status of individuals. The present study attempts to identify the causes of low levels of education among rural females in Pakistan. The analysis of this study is based on 700 cases, selected through multistage random sampling technique. The ordinal regression model revealed that higher levels of parental education, female education ratio of the village and income of the household are supportive for raising the educational level of female children. Long distances of schools, female child-labour and low social status of family in community are major hurdles in females' access to higher-level education. Harmony in the marital relation has also positive effect on the schooling levels of female children. This study suggests focussing on the establishment of more secondary level schools for girls, controlling child-labour and educating the females belonging to low social status castes.
