No One Way to Jam: Game Jams for Creativity, Learning, Entertainment, and Research
Game jams have formed a creative backbone to the MIT Game Lab, as a means to connect students, researchers, developers, and the general public together to explore the potentials of games in various ways. The author has been in a position to see how game jams have evolved to be not just learning opportunities for participants, but sources for innovation and inspiration for sponsors. How did these game jams shift in this direction and what impact does this have on the designers? The author noticed a trend towards professionalization of game jam participants and commercialization of game jams which can both expand opportunities for game jams beyond being purely about game development, but can also limit participation in unwanted ways if not thought through in game jam design.
