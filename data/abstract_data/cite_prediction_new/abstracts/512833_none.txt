kQ-product analysis of inductive power transfer system with two transmitters and two receivers
This paper formulates the maximum RF-to-RF efficiency of inductive power transfer system with two transmitters and two receivers under the assumption that cross-coupling exists between transmitters and between receivers. Maximum efficiency is derived by jointly optimizing the input and output currents. We express the maximum efficiency by a system kQ-product, which is determined by system Z-parameters. Simulation results verify the derived formula and also show that, the square of system kQ-product can be approximated as the sum of square of kQ-products of individual transmitter-receiver links.
