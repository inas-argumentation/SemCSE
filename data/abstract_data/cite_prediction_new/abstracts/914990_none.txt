Relative Navigation for the TanDEM-X Mission and Evaluation with DEM Calibration Results
The TanDEM-X mission is designed to acquire a global digital elevation model (DEM) with an unprecedented accuracy of 2 m at a grid size of 12 m x 12 m. This is done by bi-static synthetic aperture radar (SAR) data takes from the TSX and TDX satellites. In order to achieve the required accuracy, the baseline vector between the two spacecraft has to be determined with an accuracy of 1mm (1D-RMS). The baseline is computed in a relative navigation using the measurements of the on-board GPS receivers combined with dynamic models. The accuracy of the baseline will be evaluated with dedicated calibration data takes over well known areas. Systematic errors can be detected and corrected for.
