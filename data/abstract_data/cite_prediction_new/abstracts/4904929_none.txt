An equitable partition for a distance-regular graph of negative type
Let Γ denote a distance-regular graph with diameter d ≥ 3. Assume Γ has classical parameters (d, b, α, β) with b < - 1.We investigate the extent to which Γ is 1-homogeneous in the sense of Nomura. We show that either Γ is 1-homogeneous, or else Γ has a certain equitable partition of its vertex set which involves 4d-1 cells.
