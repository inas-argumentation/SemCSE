Physical Activity Recommendations for the Aging Brain: A Clinician-Patient Guide.
The positive benefits of regular physical activity on cognitive function are well-known today. However, older adults experience challenges beginning or sustaining regular exercise throughout their lives. At a time when >40 million people in the United States are 65 years old, there is a need for health care providers to advise patients and their families on safe physical activity practice aimed to maintain or improve cognitive health.
