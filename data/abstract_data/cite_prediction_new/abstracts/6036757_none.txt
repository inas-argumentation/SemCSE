A new antagonist of heparin: partially N-oxidized poly-allyldiethylamine.
The antiheparin action of poly-allyldiethylamine with different degrees of N-oxidation was studied and compared with that of protamine. Tests were carried out by the test-tube method; the degree of activity of certain doses of the substances under examination was controlled by the thromboelastographic method. 
 
As shown by the results obtained, poly-allyldiethylamine-N-oxide has in vitro a marked antiheparin activity, analogous to that of protamine; however, it offers the advantage over the latter of a far lower direct anticoagulant action. Such an advantage is more evident in the polymer with higher degree of N-oxidation, and is probably connected with the lower amount of free amino groups present in the molecule.
