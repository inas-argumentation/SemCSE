The research of typical control strategy of electronic power transformer in parallel operation
The electronic power transformer has been used widely in power system, electronic power transformer in parallel operation can help to improve power system reliability and capacities. In this paper, the typical control strategy of parallel operation is further discussed. Based on adjusting characteristics of active power and inactive power, the model is built using MATLAB/Simulink. typical operating processes of two power electronic transformers in parallel operation are simulated. The results show that reasonable distribution of active load and inactive load between power electronic transformers in parallel operation is realized on condition of maintaining rated power frequency, and have good dynamic characteristics.
