Tuberous sclerosis and angiomyolipomas of the kidneys: a case report.
SUMMARY Tuberous sclerosis is a disease with a wellknown association with renal masses, both cysts and angiomyolipomas. Reported here is a case of a 26 year old woman who had, in addition to angiomyolipomas of the kidneys, adenoma sebaceum, paraungualfibromas, shagreen patches and cerebral tubers.
