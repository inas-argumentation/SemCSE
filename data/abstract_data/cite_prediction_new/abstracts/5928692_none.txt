'Graduates with low academic achievements; attributes and employment prospects'
Academic performance based on the cumulative grade point average (CGPA) has been one of the determinants of graduate employability. For graduates that underwent academic programme using the semester system, a score of 2.5 and below is defined as the underachievers. Using the respondents from the graduate tracer study of Ministry of Higher Education in 2007, the underachievers comprised of about 7.6% of the respondent at public universities in Malaysia; of which 53.9% are males. Underpinning the graduate employability are the employability skills, level of competency or some other relevant attributes of graduates. This study examines graduates perception of the acquired skills, identified further skills and training which they perceived as useful in meeting the job challenges. The study found that despite their shortcoming i.e. a low academic achievement and average self-assessment on acquired job skills, about 11.6% are employed with gross monthly income of RM1500 and less.
