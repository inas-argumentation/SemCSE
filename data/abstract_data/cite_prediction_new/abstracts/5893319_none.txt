Almost optimality of orthogonal super greedy algorithms for incoherent dictionaries
We investigate the efficiency of weak orthogonal super greedy algorithm (WOSGA) for n-term approximation with respect to dictionaries which are (n,D)-unconditional in arbitrary Hilbert space ℋ. For an element f ∈ℋ, let fAn be the output of WOSGA after An steps for some constant A. We show that the residual ∥f − fAn∥ can be bounded by a constant multiplying the error of best n-term approximation to f. Moreover, we get an element fn∗, through a simple postprocessing of fAn by retaining its n largest components in absolute value, which realizes near best n-term approximation for f. Our results are obtained for dictionaries in ℋ which satisfies the weaker assumption than the RIP condition.
