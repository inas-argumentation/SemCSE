19 Purpose The herbicide diuron has the unfortunate property of being strongly adsorbed onto 20 soil organic matter particles, and hence, is slowly degraded in the environment because of its 21 reduced bioavailability. The aim of this work was to gain insight into the fate and behaviour 22 of diuron in the soil-water system, and develop and test an environmentally friendly soil 23 decontamination technique that could give rise to an enhancement of diuron mineralisation by 24 sensitive soil endogenous microorganisms, by means of increasing the bioavailability of the 25 pollutant employing cyclodextrin (CD) solutions what would represent an improvement 26 from both economic and environmental standpoints. 27 Materials and methods Selected soil colloidal components: montmorillonite, a synthetic 28 humic acid and a synthetic acicular goethite, and two different soils were employed in this 29 study to perform batch adsorption-desorption experiments.
