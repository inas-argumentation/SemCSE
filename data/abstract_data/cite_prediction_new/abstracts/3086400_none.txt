Acute pancreatitis associated with systemic lupus erythematosus: Successful treatment with plasmapheresis followed by aggressive immunosuppressive therapy
Plasmapheresis has been recommended in extremely ill patients with autoimmune disorders who have not responded to conventional therapies. 3 It is expected that plasmapheresis diminish circulating immune complexes, antibodies and active complement compon ents as well as pro-infl ammatory cytokines. Herein we present the case of a girl with SLE who developed acute pancreatitis shortly after presentation. Plasmapheresis, followed by i.v. pulse therapy with methyl prednisolone and cyclophosphamide, produced complete recovery from this fatal complication.
