Modeling and Simulation of Receiving System for Simultaneous Wireless Information and Power Transfer
Simultaneous wireless information and power transfer (SWIPT) formed by the mixture of communication technology and power transmission is one of the research focuses in the field of wireless communication. This paper introduces the architecture of the SWIPT system, analyzes the key technologies, presents a 2.45GHz microwave rectifier circuit at low input power for energy harvesting. Finally, with the use of Ultra-Narrow Band (UNB) as the modulation scheme, the practical SWIPT receiver system is modeled and simulated. The results provide that using UNB signal as the modulation scheme is a new feasible way for SWIPT.
