Modular multilevel converters: A review on topologies, modulation, modeling and control schemes
This article is devoted to the Multi-level inverters review and in particular to the form and function of modular multilevel converters (MMC), with their different topologies, modulation, modeling and control schemes. The MMC is built up by identical, but individually controllable submodules. Therefore the converter can act as a controllable voltage source, with a large number of available discrete voltage steps. Detailed analysis with their functions of MMC has been made in comprehensive manner with existing literature available till date. All existing methods are compared in detail with proposal for the best methods available. The article has made strategic conclusions on MMC to make the system more robust in operation with less complexity in design and control.
