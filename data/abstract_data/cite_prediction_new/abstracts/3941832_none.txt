A Vibroacoustic Application of Identification and Control for Linear Time-Varying Systems
This paper presents an application of H2 and H∞ gain-scheduled and robust state feedback control for a time-varying vibroacoustic setup whose dynamics is highly sensitive to variation in the ambient temperature. An LPV model is derived for this system using the State-space Model Interpolation of Local Estimates (SMILE) technique. This approach interpolates linear time-invariant models estimated at distinct operating conditions of the system (in this case, different temperatures). The obtained LPV model is used in recently developed gain-scheduled and robust H2 and H∞ synthesis procedures that can consider a priori known bounds on the rate of parameter variation.
