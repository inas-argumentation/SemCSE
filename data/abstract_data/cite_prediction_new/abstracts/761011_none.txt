On the model of microteaching skill training for cloud computing
Traditional microteaching skill training of normal university in China cannot meet the requirement of teacher's teaching skills and professional ability because the student in normal university have no enough time to practice it in the classroom teaching. With the development of network technology and communication technology, the learning space of student has been expanded and extended by internet. So, we construct the platform of microteaching skill training for cloud computing so as that students can train the teaching skill autonomously and be directed by teacher after class.
