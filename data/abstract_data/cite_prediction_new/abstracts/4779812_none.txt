Integrated optimization model for industrial self-generation and load scheduling with tradable carbon emission permits
Energy intensive enterprises (EIEs) need to coordinate the production scheduling with carbon emission in consideration of the carbon market. In this paper, a mixed integer linear programming (MILP) based self-generation (SG) and load scheduling model with consideration of carbon emission permits and time-of-use (TOU) electricity tariffs is proposed to obtain optimal economical and ecological benefits, in which both the electricity cost and the carbon trading cost (CTC) are included in the optimization objective of the model. CPLEX is used to solve the model. Experimental results and analyses show that the proposed integrated model is effective in cutting down total system costs (TSC) and reducing carbon emission, besides that, with less carbon emission in future power grid, the proposed model appears to be better for making economical and ecological benefits.
