Neural Network Approach to Thai Part Of Speech Tagging
Thai part of speech (POS) tagging is a challenged problem in natural language processing. Many techniques including artificial neural network techniques are suggested for POS tagging. Research works in Thai POS tagging so far only focused on assigning word types, but not word features. This paper proposed a technique using multilayer perception for tagging word features in Thai sentences. The features indicate roles of the word in the sentence. This POS tagging is called Role-POS tagging. The experimental results show that the neural network technique is an appropriated tool for word feature tagging in Thai language.
