CONCEPT OF INVERSE DYNAMICS PROBLEMS FOR С ASCADE SPEED CONTROL OF DIRECT CURRENT MOTORS WITH FIELD WEAKENING
Kiselychnyk O., Ph.D., Asc. Prof., Pushkar M., BSEM, Ostroverkhov M., Ph.D., Asc. Prof. National Technical University of Ukraine “Kiev Polytechnic Institute” 03056, Кiev, Peremohy Avenue, 37, Ukraine E-mail: koi@gala.net, pushkar@gala.net, ostroverkhov@list.ru This paper deals with the design of cascade two-zone speed control system of DC motor with controllers based on the concept of inverse dynamics problems with minimization of local functionals of the instantaneous motion energy values. This method provides robustness to changes of the electromechanical time constant and the rigidity of mechanical characteristics.
