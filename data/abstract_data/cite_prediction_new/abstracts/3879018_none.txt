Modeling of HCD Kinetics for Full ${V}_{{G}}$ / ${V}_{{D}}$ Span in the Presence of NBTI, Electron Trapping, and Self Heating in RMG SiGe p-FinFETs
A SPICE compatible compact modeling framework is proposed for the time kinetics of threshold voltage shift (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Delta {V}_{T}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>) in FinFETs subjected to hot carrier degradation (HCD) stress. The model is valid for the entire drain (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${V}_{D}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>) and gate (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${V}_{G}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>) voltage space and calculates the generation of interface traps for pure HCD (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Delta {V}_{\text {IT-HC}}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>) and for negative bias temperature instability (NBTI) (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Delta {V}_{\text {IT-BT}}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>), hole trapping (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Delta {V}_{\text {HT}}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>), and electron trapping (<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\Delta {V}_{\text {ET}}$ </tex-math></inline-formula>) as necessary, and considers the self-heating effect (SHE). The model is validated by using ultrafast (10-<inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\mu \text{s}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> delay) measured data from replacement metal gate (RMG) silicon germanium (SiGe) p-FinFETs having a different fin length (FL) and different nitrogen (N%) in the gate insulator stack. A calibrated TCAD framework is used to verify the impact of pure NBTI for varying <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${V}_{G}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> and <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">${V}_{D}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> stress in the presence of SHE but no HCD.
