AC/DC Power System Modeling and Analysis for Shipboard Applications
This presentation will discuss Mississippi State University's (MSU) efforts over the last five years to develop models and simulations for different applications of shipboard AC/DC power systems. The MSU research team is part of the Electric Ship Research Development and Consortium (ESRDC) and has been involved with research work related to modeling and simulation, protection, reconfiguration, stability, and power electronics of AC/DC shipboard power systems (SPS).
