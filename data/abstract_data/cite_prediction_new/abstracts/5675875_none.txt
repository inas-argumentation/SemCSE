A large proportion of our patients were in remission and none had detectable anti-drug antibodies. Indeed, this translated to very few patients with secondary loss of response, a situation where reactive therapeutic drug monitoring has been shown to help guide management decisions. 5 Further studies are needed that compare within-patient variability across cycles with a longer period of follow-up using more appropriate analytic methodology such as non-linear mixed-effects modelling. A comprehensive population pharmacokinetic analysis is required to address the hypothesis generating associations between factors such as delivery device and smoking status and drug levels before their potential role can be confirmed. Until such studies are conducted we recommend that clinicians perform therapeutic drug monitoring for adalimumab at trough.
