Looking at levels of medicalization: a comment on Strong's critique of the thesis of medical imperialism.
-In a recent issue of this journal, P. M. Strong presented a critique of the thesis of medical imperialism. We find his conceptualization of medical “imperialism” problematic and his view of medicalization unnecessarily narrow. Medicalization occurs not only on the level of doctor-patient interactions, but on conceptual and institutional levels as well. Strong's critique is marred by an irrelevant and unconvincing argument positing a thesis of sociological imperialism. While we concur with some of Strong's points, we challenge others in an attempt to clarify some of the misunderstood aspects of the medicalization thesis.
