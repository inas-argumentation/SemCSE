AIRP best cases in radiologic-pathologic correlation: mediastinal mature cystic teratoma.
History A 26-year-old white woman came to the emergency department after being involved in a motor vehicle accident. With regard to her medical history, the patient reported that she had experienced atypical chest pain and palpitations 3 years earlier; however, the current findings at physical examination and electrocardiography were unremarkable, and she had normal troponin levels. She denied any other cardiovascular symptoms, including light-headedness, dizziness, syncope, or even presyncope. She was otherwise in good health, without any significant findings in the medical or surgical history.
