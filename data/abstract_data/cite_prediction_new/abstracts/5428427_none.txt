Systematic characterization of potential cellulolytic marine actinobacteria Actinoalloteichus sp. MHA15
Highlights • Isolation and detailed taxonomical investigation of potential cellulolytic actinobacteria.• 19 morphologically distinct actinobacterial strains were subjected to cellulose degradation assay.• Proved actinobacteria (MHA15) is an ideal organism for the bioconversion of cellulose.• If detailed polyphasic taxonomic analysis of the strain MHA15 is made, it could be a new species.
