A Lattice-Based Data Structure for Information Retrieval and Machine Learning
A lattice-based data structure, called a compressed lattice, is proposed as a generic data structure. It is closely related to a concept lattice and can be used in applications such as machine learning, information retrieval and document browsing. The data structure, essentially a bipartite graph with an embeddedlattice, combines desirable features of concept lattices in a structure that allows for a flexible mechanism of scaling the size of the embedded-lattice, by means of defined operations that compress and expand the embedded lattice. A compression strategy or criterion is required to guide this process.
