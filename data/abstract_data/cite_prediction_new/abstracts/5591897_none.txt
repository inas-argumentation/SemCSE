Blood gas partial pressures and the onset of lung ventilation in the chick embryo.
1. 1. Experiments are described in which the importance of carbon dioxide in the initiation of pulmonary respiration in the bird was assessed. 2. 2. The partial pressure of carbon dioxide in arterial blood (PaCO2) falls after day 18 but rises slightly before breathing begins. 3. 3. No evidence was obtained to indicate that either a high PaCO2 alone or a change in the ratio of PaCO2 to PaO2 was the pulmonary stimulus.
