Defining benchmark suites with reusability in mind
The Semantic Web technology needs to be thoroughly evaluated for providing objective results and attaining a massive technology improvement, being current evaluation and benchmarking activities over Semantic Web technology insufficient and a barrier to the maturity of this technology. This paper is intended to provide some guidelines to develop benchmark suites with usability in mind. To this end, it presents what a benchmark suite is and what its desirable properties are and provides a questionnaire for evaluating the usability of a benchmark suite.
