A method to optimize cross-polarization of full polarimetric conformal antenna array
A method to suppress cross-polarization and optimize polarization purity of scanning beam for the full polarization conformal antenna array is proposed. The method is to utilize the co-polarization fields excited in the elevation-plane to compensate the cross-polarization in the azimuth-plane. In respect of obtaining the global optimum solution of array's excitation, a method based on convex optimization is addressed for the dual-polarized element array. The convex problem of theta polarization purity is solved with an acceptable sidelobe level. The method is applied to a simulated spherical full polarization conformal antenna array; the low cross-polarization level patterns are presented and the proposed method can be demonstrated.
