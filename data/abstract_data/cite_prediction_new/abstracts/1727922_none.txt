Serum prolactin and progesterone concentrations in ovulatory infertility
The luteal phase serum prolactin and progesterone concentrations were studied by radioimmunoassay in 31 ovulatory but infertile women and 58 women wearing intrauterine device (IUD). In the infertile patients the prolactin concentrations were significantly higher (mean ±SE 24.0 ±1.0μg/l vs. 18.7 ± 1.1 μg/l) than in IUD users (p<0.001), and the progesterone concentrations were lower (22.9 ± 1.7 vs. 37A ± 2.3 nmol/l) (p<0.001). However, infertile patients with a high serum prolactin did not have a mid-luteal phase progesterone level different from those with normal prolactin. There was no correlation between prolactin and progesterone levels.
