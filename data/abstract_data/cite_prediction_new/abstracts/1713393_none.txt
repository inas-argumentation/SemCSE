A wideband circularly polarized antenna for low mutual coupling Ka-band phased arrays
in this paper, we propose a wideband circularly polarized antenna with low mutual coupling for Ka-band phased array. The antenna element is composed of a square-ring integrated square patch radiator and a parasitic square patch with a coaxial feeding probe. The antenna element is with size of 7.0mm × 7.0mm × 1.0mm and achieves a impedance bandwidth (10-dB return-loss) of 6.85GHz (27.0GHz–33.9GHz) and circular polarization bandwidth (3-dB axial ratio) of 3.2GHz (27.7GHz– 30.9GHz), and boresight gain of greater than 7.2dBic. By partially removing the substrate of a stacked antenna element, the surface waves are able to be suppressed so that the interelement coupling in an antenna array can be reduced. A 2×2 array antenna exhibits a mutual coupling of less than −20 dB with element spacing (distance between the centers of the elements) of 0.48λ 0 at 29GHz.
