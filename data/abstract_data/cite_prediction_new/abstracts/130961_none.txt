False-positive results of an iodine-131 whole-body scan caused by an ectopic kidney.
Iodine-131 is used to detect and treat functioning metastases of differentiated thyroid carcinoma. False-positive images of I-131 whole-body scans are rare but possible. In these cases, thyroglobulin measurements indicate constantly low levels. The authors describe a patient with lung metastases from carcinoma of the thyroid (and therefore with a high level of thyroglobulin) and false-positive uptake in the pelvis caused by an ectopic kidney. This finding can be added to the list of misleading conditions that may cause false-positive results on whole-body scans and supports the use of complementary diagnostic procedures to establish the exact origin of I-131 uptake and to avoid diagnostic mistakes and unnecessary treatment.
