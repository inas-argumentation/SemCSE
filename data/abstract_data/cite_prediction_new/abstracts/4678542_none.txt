Association and path co-efficient analysis among yield and its components in bitter gourd (Momordica charantia L.)
HIND AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH AND TRAINING INSTITUTE ABSTRACT : Correlation and path analysis were carried out in order to quantify the contribution of explanatory characters towards yield for bitter gourd cultivation.The characters like number of fruits per plant and fruit length had significant and positive correlation with yield. Number of fruits per plant and average fruit weight had the highest positive direct effect on fruit yield per plant.
