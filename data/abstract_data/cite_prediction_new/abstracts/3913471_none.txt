Human chorionic gonadothropin levels in maternal blood in late pregnancy: relation to birth weight, sex and condition of the infant at birth
Summary. A total of 527 blood samples was obtained from an unselected population of women between 36 and 40 weeks gestation. Serum human chorionic gonadotrophin (hCG) levels were measured using a specific radioimmunoassay for the (β1‐subunit of hCG. Serum hCG levels were higher in primigravidae and in women carrying female fetuses. They were also related to the birthweight of the child and to the occurrence of fetal distress.
