Intentional Attunement. The Mirror Neuron system and its role in interpersonal relations
Consider the general biological problem of making sense of presence of another like oneself. One approach is to have a signal that indicates the category of the other. An example of this is a chick using an "imprint" to identify its mother. Another approach is to use a history of observations to characterize the other. But as the relevant state space and decision capabilities of the organism expands, the optimal strategy shifts to attunement.
