Comparative study of self-organizing map and deep self-organizing map using MATLAB
We are interested in testing the efficiency of different algorithm which are traditional SOM (self-organizing map) and new DSOM (deep self-organizing map) for image cataloging. DSOM consist of SOM and the sampling operator are arranged in the way to get the better result than the SOM which also include generating another 2D map, then it is fed into the next self-organizing layer this manner confined in order is gained, is made better in the higher layers. The making of DSOM is unique and we will test the accuracy and other parameters which are shown in this document. Experiment were carried out to see how DSOM influence the presentation.
