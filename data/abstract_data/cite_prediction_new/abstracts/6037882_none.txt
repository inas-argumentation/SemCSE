Electron microscopy of type 4 adenovirus strain R1-67.
Investigations of the size and shape of purified adenovirus type 4, strain RI-67, revealed a spherical or polyhedral form depending on the method of preparation for electron microscopy and a calculated spherical particle diameter of about 70 mμ. The size of the virus-like particles in sections of infected cells agreed with this figure and supported the viral identity of these particles. The virus particle appears to consist of a limiting membrane about 4 mμ thick enclosing an inner mass with a dense central body of about 30 mμ in width.
