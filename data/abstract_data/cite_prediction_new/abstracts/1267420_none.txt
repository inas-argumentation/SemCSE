Economic versus Accounting Rates of Return for Farm Land
A well-known characteristic of agriculture is the traditionally low accounting (current) rates of return on farm land. Ratios of cash rent to land values as well as reported rates earned on total assets of crop farms typically have fallen in the three to six percent range over time despite swings in general inflation and changes in the levels of real returns to farm assets (Melichar 1979; Barry 1980).
