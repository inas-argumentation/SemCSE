Modeling of piezoelectric sensors for fetal heart rate signal
The monitoring1 of the fetal and maternal vital signals is very important in diagnosis for evaluation of well-being of the fetus during pregnancy and labor. In this paper, we propose a comparative study of the structures of sensors based on piezoelectric films like Polyvinylidene Fluoride polymer-based sensor and Lead Zirconium Titanate ceramic-based sensor. We point out the advantages of the proprieties of piezoelectric materials choosing to record the fetal heart rate. Then we describe each of these structures. After that, we simulate the structure of curved PVDF polymer film based-sensor, the structure of flat PVDF film polymer film based-sensor, and the structure of curved PZT film polymer film based-sensor. In this simulation work, we use the finite element method.
