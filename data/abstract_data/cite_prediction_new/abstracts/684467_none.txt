Preparation, properties and structure of dihalogenobis(N,N-DI-n-butyldithiocarbamato) complexes of palladium and platinum
The preparation and properties of M(Bu2dtc)2X2 (M = Pd, Pt and X = Br, I) are reported. From the platinum complexes two isomers were isolated. The crystal structure of the cis-isomer of Pt(Bu2dtc)2I2 is determined by a threedimensional X-ray analysis. The orthorhombic cell, space group Fddd, with a = 33.37(3), b = 34.16(3), c = 20.19(2) A and V = 23010(40) A3 contains 32 formula units. Atomic parameters were refined to a conventional R value of 0.073 for 1417 independent non-zero reflections. The platinum atom is distorted octahedrally coordinated by two iodine and four sulfur atoms with average distances of Pt - I = 2.66 and Pt - S = 2.35 A. The intra- and one of the intermolecular I-I distances (resp. 3.79 and 3.37 A) are short compared with an I-I Van der Waals contact. 
 
 
 
ESCA spectra show the other isomer to be a Pt(IV) complex too. NMR data suggest a trans-structure for this isomer. 
 
 
 
With Bu4tds these complexes yield M(Bu2dtc)3X (M = Pt, Pd and X = Br, I) of which some properties are given.
