In vitro study of antifungal activity of pentamidine and stilbamidine
ELSON (1945) made the observation that propamidine had antifungal activity. The discovery by ELsoN of the fungistatic activity of propamidine (p.p. Trimethylendibenzamidine) against a number of pathogenic fungi led to further confirmatory investigations of other aromatic diamidines by BOCOBO et al (1953) by CHRISTISON & CO,ANT (1953) and by other investigators. The other aromatic Diamidines which were tried were 16entamidine (p.p. pentamethylenedibenzamidine) and stilbamidine (p.p. stitbene diacarboxamidine). Singular success has been obtained with the use of stilbamidine in the therapy of North American Blastomycosis. As such it was considered worthwhile to s tudy the antifungal activity of pentamidine and stilbamidine against the organisms isolated from cases of Maduromycosis (Black granule type) in India.
