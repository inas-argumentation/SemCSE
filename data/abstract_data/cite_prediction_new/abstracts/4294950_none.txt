Perianal cellulitis associated with group A streptococci.
OCCASIONALLY in the practice of pediatrics, a mother will bring in a child with a complaint of painful defecation. This symptom is often accompanied by soiling and anal soreness with secondary constipation. In time anal abrasions or small fissures occur which cause more pain, and a vicious cycle of events has become established. Anal erythema or soreness not associated with diaper rash is seldom mentioned in the pediatric literature. Treatment of these patients by general practitioners, pediatricians, or proctologists has been empirical and symptomatic, usually consisting of local ointments and stool-softening adjuvants.
