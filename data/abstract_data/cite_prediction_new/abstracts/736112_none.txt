THE ROLE OF OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY IN TOTAL QUALITY MANAGEMENT
Nowadays, occupational health and safety studies are becoming more important then ever before. The organizations should make every effort to increase the safety of working conditions for their employees. This in turn, increases the moral, motivation, working ethics etc. which directly influence the quality of the work carried out. This paper investigates the role of occupational health and safety in total quality management. The success of safety efforts may increase the success of total quality effort. The investigation is carried out in terms of employee participation, adaptive hazard management, innovative safety management, continuous improvements as well as integrated safety and quality management.
