A Wavelet-based Random-effect Model for Multiple Sets of Complicated Functional Data
Multiple sets of complicated functional data curves appeared in many engineering studies such as improving stamping quality of an automobile manufacturing process, detecting process faults in a semiconductor fabrication experiment or characterizing process behavior in a nano-machining system. To handle potentially large size of these data, this article proposes a wavelet-based randomeffect model and develops a vertical-energy based thresholding procedure for data reduction and denoising purposes. The selected wavelet coefficients are treated as the “reduced-size” data in subsequent data analyses for decision-making in engineering studies. This enhances the ability of the existing statistical procedures in handling large-size complicated functional data. Analytical properties of the proposed methods are provided. Simulation studies show that our methods perform better than several ad hoc techniques extended from single-curve based data modeling and denoising procedure. A few real-life examples motivate the importance of the current research.
