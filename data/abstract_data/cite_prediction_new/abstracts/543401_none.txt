On bosonic limits of two recent supersymmetric extensions of the Harry Dym hierarchy
Two generalized Harry Dym equations, recently found by Brunelli, Das, and Popowicz in the bosonic limit of new supersymmetric extensions of the Harry Dym hierarchy [J. Math. Phys. 44, 4756 (2003)], are transformed into previously known integrable systems: one, into a pair of decoupled KdV equations, the other one, into a pair of coupled mKdV equations from a bi-Hamiltonian hierarchy of Kupershmidt.
