Glioblastoma Multiforme of Optic Nerve in Adults with Hyper intense Diffuse Weighted Imaging
We present a case of glioblastomamultiforme of the optic nerve with Hyper intense Diffusion Weighted Imaging(DWI) in a 56 year old man without Neurofibromatosis1 who presented with generalized tonic clonic seizures and loss of vision in right eye. Presence of hyperintenseDWI imaging of optic nerve makes this case unique.Further details about diagnostic methods available with major emphasis on Magnetic Resonance Imaging in adults is been done.
