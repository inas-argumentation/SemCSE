GEOPHYSICAL TECHNIQUES FOR EXPLORATION AND PRODUCTION OF GAS HYDRATE
The ever-increasing demand of energy for sustainable growth and development of human society has forced all of us to search for the renewable and alternative energy resources for the future. To meet the gigantic demand of energy, the geoscientists have taken initiatives towards the exploration and exploitation of unconventional energy resources available as Coal Bed Methane (CBM), Shale Gas (gas extracted from shale), and Gas Hydrate. The natural gas hydrates do have the potential of becoming an alternate energy resources due to their huge deposits envisaged worldwide. The present paper is a brief review of the role of geophysical techniques for the exploration and exploitation of gas hydrate with emphasis on possibilities and challenges associated with the commercial production of gas from the gas hydrate reservoirs.
