Elder abuse, as a topic of research, has had an extremely short history?barely a decade. Cronin and Allen (1982) trace trace the beginnings of the 1978 congressional hearing on domestic violence which included information on elder abuse as part of the testimony on the "Overlooked Aspects of Domestic Violence" provided by Steinmetz. They note that, although the congressional hearing was not the first reference to elder abuse, it was the ' 'first to gain such national prominence /' and was the "sole portion of the Hearings to earn coverage in the New York Times, as well as a mention from Walter Cronkite."
