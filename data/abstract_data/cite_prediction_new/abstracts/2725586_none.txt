The aim of this paper is to construct p-adic twisted two-variable Eulerh,q -L-functions, which interpolate generalized twisted h,q -Euler polynomials at negative integers. In this paper, we treat twisted h,q -Euler numbers and polynomials associated with p-adic invariant integral on Zp. We will construct two-variable twisted h,q -Euler-zeta function and two-variable h,q -L-function in Complex s-plane.
