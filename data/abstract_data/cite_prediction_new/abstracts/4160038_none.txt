Discrimination Between Active and Latent Tuberculosis – Immunological Tools
Increasing morbidities of nontuberculous mycobacterial (NTM) lung disease, a never ending number of newly described mycobacteria species, and multidrug-resistant tuberculosis (TB) that continues to be a public health threat have lead National Jewish Health to develop the NTM-TB INSIGHTS newsletter. It provides the latest information for patients, advocates, educators, health care providers, laboratory scientists, researchers, public health officials and all who want to learn more about NTM disease and TB. NTM-TB INSIGHTS will be published 4 to 6 times a year. We hope you enjoy the publication.
