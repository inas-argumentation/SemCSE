Resolution Enhancement of Holographic Imaging of Concealed Object using Burg Method
This paper describes a simple and inexpensive technique for the imaging (detection) of concealed object. Burg method is used as a high resolution technique to model the holographic imaging (detecting) problem. Ultrasonic waves are used for imaging a concealed object. The in-line holography is employed. The results demonstrate the enhanced performance capability of the combination of Burg and holographic methods. Also the method showed a much better performance when compared with Fourier transform.
