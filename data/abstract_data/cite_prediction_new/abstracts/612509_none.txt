What use are ethical codes? An analysis of three possible rationales for the use of ethical codes in medical schools and a review of the evidence relating to them
Medical education is experiencing a strong resurgence of interest in the codi®cation of ethics. However, popular as they are, the use of ethical codes as educational interventions ought to be defensible in two ways. Firstly, it should be based on a clear rationale. Secondly, there should be evidence that supports this rationale in practice. Here I analyse whether their use is defensible in both of these ways.
