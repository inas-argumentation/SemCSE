An Indexing Method for Similarity Search in High-Dimensional Image Databases
In this paper, we propose an efficient indexing method for high-dimensional multimedia databases using the filtering approach (known also as vector approximation approach). It is based on partitioning the data space in compact and disjoined regions. Then, each region will be approximated by two bit-strings according to the RA-Blocks technique. Our method improves the division strategy of regions compared to this one. To evaluate our technique, we conducted several experiments for searching the nearest K neighbours. The results obtained show the interest of our method on both synthetic and real data.
