Calculations for steady propagation of a generic ram accelerator configuration using the Roe method
This study describes a methodology and gives analysis to determine the steady propagation speed of a projectile fired into a gaseous mixture of fuel and oxidizer. So that numerical results can be analytically verified in limiting cases, a simple model and geometry are used: the steady supersonic flow of an inviscid, calorically perfect, ideal, reacting gas with high activation energy over an unconfined symmetric double wedge is considered. Typical predicted flow fields consist of a lead inert oblique shock followed by a thick induction zone, thin reaction zone, rarefaction, and trailing oblique shock.
