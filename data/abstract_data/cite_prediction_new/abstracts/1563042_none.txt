A modeling method of radar seeker in the presence of electronic warfare
Due to the requirements of evaluating the performance of the radar seeker in the combat conditions or in the presence of electronic warfare, this paper gives a design of A Sensitive and Generalization Modeling of The Radar Seeker simulation system, and describes its Modularization, Parameterization, Interface support, Sensitivity Analysis Support design method, a pulse Doppler radar seeker based simulation system which is designed according to the method is given in details, the working flow of the simulation system and conclusions are given at last*.
