Distribution of acetyl cholinesterase in the hippocampal region of the guinea pig
Summary1.The distribution of acetyl cholinesterase (AChE) has been studied in two areas of the hippocampal region of adult guinea pig: The subiculum and the hippocampus.2.Formaldehyde-fixed serial frozen sections and unfixed cryostat sections were treated according to a modification of the Koelle thiocholine method with suppression of non-specific cholinesterase by ethopropazine. A single series was subjected to the copper ferricyanide method of Karnovsky and Roots.3.A high content of AChE was found in each of the examined regions and with a highly differentiated distribution pattern extensively corresponding to fields and layers defined on the basis of cyto- and fibroarchitectonics.4.Comparison has been made between the distribution observed in the guinea pig and that in the rat, reported earlier by Storm-Mathisen and Blackstad (1964). The subiculum appeared almost identical in the two species, while in the hippocampus essential differences were observed in stratum lacunosum-moleculare and oriens.5.A possible correlation of the AChE content to fibre systems known from normal material is considered in detail.
