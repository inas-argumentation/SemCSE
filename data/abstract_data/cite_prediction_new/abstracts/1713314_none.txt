[Clinical features of Goldmann-Favre vitreoretinal degeneration].
CASE REPORT
We present the case of a 39-year-old woman with Goldmann-Favre vitreoretinal degeneration. The patient showed a decrease in visual acuity with nyctalopia, and foveal and peripheral retinoschisis, vitreal veils, retinal degeneration with clumped pigment, and an unusual electroretinogram.


DISCUSSION
Our patient had most of the reported clinical features, with dendritic vessels of peripheral retina.
