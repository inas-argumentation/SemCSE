TRUNCATED MULTIVARIABLE MOMENT PROBLEMS WITH FINITE VARIETY
Let β ≡ {βi}i∈Zd+,|i|≤2n denote a real d-dimensional multisequence of degree 2n, with moment matrix M(n), and let V ≡ V (M(n)) denote the associated algebraic variety. For the case v ≡ card V < +∞, we prove that β has a representing measure if and only if r ≡ rank M(n) ≤ v and there exists a positive moment matrix extension M ≡ M(n + v − r + 1) satisfying rank M ≤ card V (M). For the class of recursively determinate moment matrices M(n), we present a computational algorithm for establishing the existence (or nonexistence) of an extension M as above and, in the positive case, for computing a minimal representing measure for β. We also show that for the case r < v < +∞, it is possible for β to admit a representing measure μ with card supp μ < v; equivalently, in this case supp μ may be a proper subset of V (M(n)).
