More Accurate Iris Verification in the Encrypted Domain Using Bit Masking
In the eld of biometrics, verication of identities can be performed by comparing an iris scan probe to an iris scan template from a database. However, since iris scans contain personal information, they need to remain encrypted while doing the comparison. Several techniques exist to compute a similarity score of two iris scans in the encrypted domain using a so called ’homomorphic encryption scheme’, which makes computations in the encrypted domain possible. Currently, these techniques do not take into account erroneous bits caused by for example eyelashes, eyelids or reection that appear on the iris scan. In this research we develop, implement and evaluate two approaches that compute the Hamming distance as similarity score in the encrypted domain disregarding erroneous bits by bit masking. For both implementations, we use the BV-scheme which is a ’somewhat homomorphic encryption’ scheme. We found that for the current set of parameters, our implementation is very poor, as it takes about 1.5s in the most optimal case to do the comparison for iris code with a length of 4 bits. With our research, a higher accuracy in the verication of iris scans in the encrypted domain can be obtained.
