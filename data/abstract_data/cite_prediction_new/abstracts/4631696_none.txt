Respiration of muscle homogenates from selected lines of chickens with inherited muscular dystrophy.
A FORM of muscular dystrophy is inherited as an autosomal recessive trait in the domestic fowl. Several strains homozygous for the disorder have been established and are the object of genetic, anatomical, physiological and biochemical study (Julian and Asmundson, 1963; and Asmundson et al., 1966). The myopathy is a progressive one leading to the eventual atrophy of the muscles involved. The pectorals are the first muscles affected. The progressive nature of the disorder (Julian and Asmundson, 1963), investigations into the growth (Herrmann et al., 1964) and the composition of the free amino acids (Peterson et al., 1963) and enzymes (Dawson et al., 1964) of the muscles have focused attention on their development and energy metabolism. This report concerns a study of the respiratory metabolism of muscle slices and homogenates from embryos and chicks with inherited muscular dystrophy.
