Promoting Learner Autonomy: Student Perceptions of Responsibilities in a Language Classroom in East Asia.
Literature Review With the advent of communicative language teaching in East Asia, the idea of learner autonomy has become a topic of discussion and a goal among language teachers. The idea of autonomy raises important questions that need to be further explored, particularly in terms of students’ taking responsibilities for learning.
