A CMOS K-Band 6-bit Attenuator With Low Phase Imbalance for Phased Array Applications
A 6-bit digital-controlled attenuator with low phase imbalance for a K-band phased array system is presented in this paper. To decrease the insertion phase difference, the proposed design adopts a phase correction capacitor in the shunt branch of the conventional switched T/Pi structure. The capacitor and the parallel resistor compose a phase compensation network to correct the insertion phase error. The attenuator is designed and fabricated in <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$0.18~\mu \text{m}$ </tex-math></inline-formula> CMOS process. From 19 to 21 GHz, the insertion loss is 7.2–8 dB. The rms phase imbalance is less than 3.8° over 19–21 GHz. The attenuator has a maximum attenuation range of 32 dB with 0.5-dB step (64 states). The core cell chip size is 1.32 mm <inline-formula> <tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\times0.34$ </tex-math></inline-formula> mm excluding pads.
