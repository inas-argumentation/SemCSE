Phenylalanine-hydroxylating system in the human fetus at different developmental ages.
1. 1. Human fetal tissue extracts of gestational age, 11–20 weeks were investigated for the phenylalanine-hydroxylating system. 2. 2. The studies demonstrate that both phenylalanine hydroxylase and dihydropteridine reductase as well as the natural cofactor are present in the liver at an early stage of fetal development. 3. 3. Hydroxylation of phenylalanine with liver supernatant in the presence of synthetic cofactor 2-amino-4-hydroxy-6,7-dimethyltetrahydropteridine, is stimulated by added catalase and/or dithiothreitol; there was no effect with the endogenous cofactor, when assayed under present conditions. 4. 4. In similar experiments no tyrosine formation occurs with human fetal brain extracts.
