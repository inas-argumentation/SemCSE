SINGULAR PERTURBATIONS AND TIME SCALES IN CONTROL THEORIES AND APPLICATIONS: AN OVERVIEW 2002-2012
This paper presents an overview of singular perturbations and time scales (SPaTS) in control theory and applications during the period 2002-2012. The previous overviews/surveys were provided for the period up to 1976 [241], 1976-1983 [377], and 1984-2001 [312]. Due to the limitations on the scope and space, this is in no way intended to be an exhaustive survey on the topic.
