Russian August 1998 Financial Crisis: Is Asia to Blame?
The paper shows that the crisis in Russia was not likely to result from the traditional contagion spread from East Asian economies, instead it was the result of Russian domestic problems. Two channels of contagion are analyzed: trade and speculaative. Neither seems to be present in any signicant way between Asia and Russia. The paper then argues that the crisis in Russia was the result of deep scal problems and poor debt management.
