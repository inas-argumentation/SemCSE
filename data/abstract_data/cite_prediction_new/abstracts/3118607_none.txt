Microvascular changes in Down syndrome with Alzheimer's-type pathology: Insights into a potential vascular mechanism for Down syndrome and Alzheimer's disease
The mechanism triggering degeneration in Alzheimer's disease (AD) remains uncertain. Therapeutic failure following amyloid β (Aβ) removal casts doubt on amyloid neurotoxicity per se as the primary cause of AD. Impaired microvascular function has been suggested as an alternative etiology. People with Down syndrome (DS) develop Alzheimer's pathology, but whether microvascular impairment also occurs in DS (as in AD) is unknown.
