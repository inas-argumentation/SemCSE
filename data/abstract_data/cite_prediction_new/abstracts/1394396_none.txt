Resource Allocation in WDM OMS-SPRing Architectures with Arbitrary Demand Patterns
In this paper we concentrate on wavelength routing and wavelength allocation issues in single OMS-SPRing architectures. we examine the effectiveness of wavelength translation. Where wavelength translation is not possible we propose novel algorithms for wavelength allocation. Simulation work using arbitrary traffic demand patterns is carried out and a number of traffic and architectural issues that affect the performance of the architecture and the algorithms are examined. The results obtained can produce an insight into the applicability of wavelength translation and can demonstrate the potential of the proposed algorithms.
