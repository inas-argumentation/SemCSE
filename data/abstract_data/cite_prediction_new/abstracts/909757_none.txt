Dependence of the ATLAS Muon Chambers Drift Time on the Gas Mixture
Adetailed analysis and comparison with the expected from the GARFIELD simulation studies of the obtained muon time spectra from the cosmic ray setup, which has been assembled for the certification of the endcap chambers produced in Michigan, is presented. The measured drift times were found to be longer that the expected ones. GARFIELD simulation is focused on the effect of the contamination elements detected during the gas analysis.
