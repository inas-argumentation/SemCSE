Announcement of European Network for Mental Service Evaluation (ENMESH)
In collaboration with the European Office of the World Health Organization, a European Network for Mental Service Evaluation (ENMESH) is now being established. Active researchers in the field are invited to request a registration form to join the network from Dr Aart Schene, (ENMESH Secretary), Associate Professor of Psychiatry, Academisch Medisch Centrum, Polikliniek Psychiatrie, Meibergdreef 9, 1105 AZ, Amsterdam (ZO), The Netherlands.
