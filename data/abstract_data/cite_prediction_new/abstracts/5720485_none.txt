In one private school, founded and maintained by outsiders, correlations were made between chronological age and I. Q., and between chronological age and Educational Quotient on 257 pupils. A correlation of -0.23 0.041 was discovered to exist between the former, and between chronological age and Educational Quotient the correlation was -0.1 A 0.04.
