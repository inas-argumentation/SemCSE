SCIENTIFUR SCIENTIFIC INFORMATION IN FUR ANIMAL PRODUCTION
The activities of the protective enzymes, total superoxide dismutase (SOD, EC 1.15.1.1) and catalase (CAT, EC 1.11.1.6) were measured in organ homogenates of liver, kidney, heart, spleen and lung from three mammalian species of Canidae family (raccoon dog, silver and polar fox). Significant distinctions between these enzymes in different species were found in most of the organs. Raccoon dog had higher specific enzyme activity in all organs while in the silver foxes it was the lowest. A difference in the distribution or ratio of these enzymes in various tissues may result in ecological specificity of species.
