European Society of Cardiology guidelines: preoperative cardiac risk assessment and perioperative cardiac management in noncardiac surgery.
The European Society of Cardiology (ESC) Committee for Practice Guidelines was founded in 1994 and has, throughout the years, published guidelines for cardiologists world-wide on behalf of the ESC, mainly in the areas of the most frequent cardiovascular diseases. The aim of the ESC Guidelines is to guide clinicians in order to help us to provide the best management for our patients. Apart from producing high-quality guidelines written by the best experts in the field, the aim of the ESC is to support implementation of the guidelines in the ESC countries. Another goal of the ESC is to monitor adherence to the ESC Guidelines via the EuroObservational Programme and other surveys.
