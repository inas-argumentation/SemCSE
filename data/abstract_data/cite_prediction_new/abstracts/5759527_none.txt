Study on existence of solutions for some nonlinear functional-integral equations with applications
In this paper, using the technique of measure of noncompactness in Banach algebra, we prove an existence theorem for a nonlinear integral equation which contains as particular cases a lot of integral and functional-integral equations considered in nonlinear analysis and its applications. Our claim is also illustrated with the applications to some nonlinear functional-integral equation for proving the existence results. AMS subject classications : 47H10
