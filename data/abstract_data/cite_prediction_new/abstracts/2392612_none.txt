Descriptor-topological model of a molecule in the analysis of structure-activity relationship of substituted phenylethylamines
Topological methods are of special interest in the analysis o f structure-activity relationships (SAR); these methods make it possible to reveal some general regularities o f a primary pharmacological effect on the basis of just the information from the molecular structure of the studied compounds exhibiting a specific activity [ 1, 2]. Topological methods are most effective in studying sets of molecules with a regularly changing structure, i.e., with the main fragment (nucleus) and varying substituents.
