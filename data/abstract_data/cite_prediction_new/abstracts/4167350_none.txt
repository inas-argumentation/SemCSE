Involvement of Sarco/endoplasmic Reticulum Ca2+ ATPases in Cell Function and the Cellular Consequences of Their Inhibition
Calcium serves as an important signaling molecule that modulates diverse cellular processes. Elevation of cytosolic Ca (Cai) follows the activation of many cell surface receptors as well as depolarization of neuronal and muscle tissues [4]. Such Ca 2+ transients can regulate gene transcription in a variety of cells including, among others, those of neuronal [24, 25, 90], cardiac [65] and endocrine [105] origins. Cytosolic Ca 2+ can also modify the function of certain classes of enzymes like the kinases, phosphatases and phospholipases [16, 71].
