Current medical and surgical therapy for cerebrovascular disease.
THE past several years have witnessed a flurry of prospective clinical trials of new treatments for transient ischemic attack, cerebral infarction, and cerebral hemorrhage, and the results have markedly altered our management of these conditions (Table 1). Furthermore, another generation of Phase II and controlled prospective studies (based on pilot studies) are planned or under way (Table 2).
