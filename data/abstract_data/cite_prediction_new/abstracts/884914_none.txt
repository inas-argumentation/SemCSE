IMMUNOHISTOCHEMICAL STUDIES ON α‐FETOPROTEIN‐ AND ALBUMIN‐CONTAINING CELLS IN THE LIVER DURING ONTOGENESIS AND EARLY STAGE OF 3′‐ME‐DAB HEPATOCARCINOGENESIS a
Since Watabe' first reported the transient appearance of a-fetoprotein (AFP) in rat serum during the early stages of azo-dye feeding, when no overt hepatoma was detected in the liver, this finding has been confirmed by many investigators?" We have been studying the pathologic changes in the liver of rats fed 3'-methyl-4-dimethylaminoazobenzene (3I-Me-DAB) and found occurrence of marked fluctuation among various cell types in the liver during the early stages of 3'-Me-DAB feeding, marked oval cell proliferation around the third week, followed by transition and differentiation from the oval cells to renewed hepatocyte~.~~', We identified AFPproducing cells in some of the transitional cells and the small hepatocytes using immunofluorescent technique? From these findings, it is evident that early appearance of AFP occurs as a result of this fluctuation of cell types in the liver." But the fate of the AFP-producing cells in this stage still remains obscure. From a histologic viewpoint, it was suggested that the AFP-producing small hepatocytes in the later period had more abundant cytoplasm when compared with those in the early stage. We suppose that they may mature in the later stage of 3'-Me-DAB feeding. By means of the immunoperoxidase technique, changes in the AFPand albumin (ALB)producing cell populations in rat liver during the early stages of 3'-Me-DAB feeding were examined. The results were compared with the fate of AFP-producing cells in the process of ontogenetic differentiation of the liver.
