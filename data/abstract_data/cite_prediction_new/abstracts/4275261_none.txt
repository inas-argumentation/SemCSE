Cerebrospinal fluid findings in central neurocytoma
Central neurocytoma is an uncommon primary tumour of the central nervous system that is characterized by uniform round cells with evidence of neuronal differentiation. Accurate diagnosis is important for these tumours, as they usually carry a good prognosis if treated by surgical excision only. Therefore, preoperative and intraoperative diagnosis is important, as a complete surgical excision will often be attempted if such a diagnosis is considered. Descriptions of the cytological features in the cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) have not been widely reported. There is a single report of neurocytoma tumour cells in the CSF of a patient who, unusually for this tumour, had widespread craniospinal dissemination. We describe the cytological features of the CSF in a patient with a more typical presentation.
