A behavior based robust fault detection approach for LTI systems
In this paper, a robust fault detection scheme based on the behavioral system theory and concept of dissipativity is proposed for linear time-invariant systems. Process model uncertainty is described using a dissipativity condition on the system from process input/output to the residue. Faults are detected in real time when such dissipativity condition (dissipation inequality) is violated. The proposed fault detection approach does not require models of faults. It can also be integrated with a robust control system. The connection and difference between the proposed approach and some existing fault detection methods are also discussed.
