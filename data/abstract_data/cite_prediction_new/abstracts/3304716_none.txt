Biogenesis of vaccinia: isolation and characterization of a surface component that elicits antibody suppressing infectivity and cell-cell fusion.
Tubular structures that cover the surface of vaccinia could be dissociated from pure virus and isolated in a polymeric state. These surface tubules (ST) appeared on polyacrylamide gels as a single polypeptide with a molecular weight of 58,000. Rabbit antiserum prepared against surface tubules neutralized virus infectivity and suppressed cell fusion. Pure ST blocked the neutralizing capacity of both rabbit antiserum against whole virus and the ST. On the basis of these findings, the biological functions of ST in cell-virus and cell-cell interactions are discussed.
