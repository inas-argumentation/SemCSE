Initiation of stem cell differentiation involves cell cycle-dependent regulation of developmental genes by Cyclin D
Pauklin et al. show that cell cycle regulators Cyclin D1–3 control cell fate decisions in human pluripotent stem cells by recruiting transcriptional corepressors and coactivator complexes onto neuroectoderm, mesoderm, and endoderm genes. This activity results in blocking the core transcriptional network necessary for endoderm specification while promoting neuroectoderm factors.
