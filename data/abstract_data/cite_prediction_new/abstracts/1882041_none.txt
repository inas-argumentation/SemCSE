Computers in Russian schools: current conditions, main problems, and prospects for the future
This paper illustrates the current conditions of Russian schools and describes the major problems of Russian education. It also examines the past and present technological resources used in Russian classrooms, the variety and availability of hardware and software, and the prospects for Russia's future computerization. The information provided has been compiled from research, media, personal observation, surveys, and interacting with teachers, school administrators, students and other individuals.
