Bentley Was Right: The Significance of LGBT Politics for Political Science
In his seminal 1908 book, The Process of Government, Arthur Bentley outlined a framework for understanding politics and policy that has directly or indirectly inspired a considerable amount of research for generations of social scientists. Bentley argued that in order to understand politics and what government does (and does not do) we should focus our attention on interests, rather than individuals. In short, Bentley argued that that politics is about interactions between groups and that government policy is the result of these interactions.
