P1 plasmid maintenance: a paradigm of precise control.
Certain bacterial plasmids, P1 prophage among them, are stably inherited despite their very low copy number (59). In each lysogenic Cell, the mechanisms that guarantee a P1 replication prior to Cell division and an equipartition of the daughter plasmids at Cell division operate with error frequencies comparable to rates of mutation (7,61). In reviewing what we know about how such fidelity is achieved, this article will concentrate on plasmid replication and its control; the accompanying one by Austin and Abeles will consider partitioning.
