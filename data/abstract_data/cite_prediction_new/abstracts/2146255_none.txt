Innovative Ideas Seminars: A Method for Eliciting Innovations and Their Effect on Quality and Cost
In successful, established, not-for-profit healthcare centers, it is easy to believe that the model for quality and cost is the result of repeated testing and refinement over time. It would follow that the organizations hold a valued place in the market, safely operate at the highest quality levels, and continually reinvent processes. Unfortunately, cost effectiveness, quality, and safety do not always point to the greatness that organizations transmit. How would the healthcare organization continually rediscover itself and remain open to new concepts and processes? What influences the need for healthcare organizations to proactively search for renewal? First, it would be beneficial to identify several drivers that affect the healthcare organization as they pertain to cost, quality, and safety. Healthcare providers are under increased scrutiny by the public to provide the safest care, in a most confidential manner, and customer services like those delivered in a fourstar hotel atmosphere. Every patient wants to be cared for as if he or she is the healthcare provider’s only concern. The patient also wants to receive the care in a respectful, dignified way for a charge that the patient considers reasonable. Charges are dictated by healthcare costs that continue to escalate as providers attempt to meet and exceed expectations, standards, and imposed regulations.
