Tracheal reconstruction with silastic reinforced dacron.
THE USE of synthetic materials for reconstruction of the trachea presents some special problems in that the material should be airtight and also rigid enough to remain patent during inspiration. Various materials have been used in the past, including Marlex, skin grafts, stainless steel tubes, etc.1-4These have all had certain disadvantages, and the availability of silastic reinforced with Dacron suggested the possibility of using it for a tracheal graft. This is a translucent silicone rubber reinforced with dacron and is 0.040 inches thick. The material is very pliable and yet is rigid enough so that when it is rolled in tubular form it does not collapse. The sheet material can be rolled into any size and then glued (with RTV 732) in cylinder form either at the operating table or previously, and the material can then be autoclaved.
