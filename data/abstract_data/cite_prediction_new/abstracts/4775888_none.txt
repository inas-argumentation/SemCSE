Safety Versus Defense: Comments on “CAPPS II: The Foundation of Aviation Security?”
Physicians often face a decision problem— whether to refer a patient to a more or to a less severe diagnostic procedure. The former may have higher probability (H) of detecting certain existing disease, the latter may be less sensitive, with lower probability (L) of detection; yet, the latter may be cheaper and less burdensome to the patient. The physician would consider prior probability of the disease in order to decide which inspection to choose.
