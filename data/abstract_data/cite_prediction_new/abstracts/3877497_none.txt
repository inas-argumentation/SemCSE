Hemodilution, Low Flow Rates and Moderate Internal Hypothermia for Extracorporeal Circulation: Concepts Obviating the Use of Blood for Priming
Spurred by the experimental studies of Gollan, Blos and Schuman/ (1952-1955), by our initial attempts (1953),2 and by the clinical success of Sealy and co-workers (1957),3 the problem of internal hypothermia was experimentally evaluated in our laboratories (1959).4 Somewhat later, we formulated and experimentally proved the feasibility of complete primes of the heart-lung machine with a blood substitute (1959).
