A first mass production of gas electron multipliers
We report on the manufacture of a first batch of approximately 2000 Gas Electron Multipliers (GEMs) using 3M’s fully automated roll-to-roll flexible circuit production line. Production techniques and preliminary measurements of GEM performance are described.
