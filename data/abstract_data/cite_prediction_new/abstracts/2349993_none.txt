Geology of the East Harrison Lake Belt, Southwestern British Columbia
The East Harrison Lake belt (EHLB) refers to a northwest-trending belt of rocks occurring to the east of Harrison Lake in southwest British Columbia (Figure 1) which is currently the focus of exploration for ultramafic-related Cu-Ni (PGE). The geology of the EHLB will also be summarized as a 1: 50 000-scale compilation map (Ash et al., in preparation) incorporating both published and unpublished data sets that include recent industry mapping.
