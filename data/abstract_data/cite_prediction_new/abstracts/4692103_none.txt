A highly sensitive fluorometric assay of bradykinin and its fragments released by a kininase.
The activity of kininase was studied using both techniques of fluorometry and thin-layer chromatography. The enzyme purified from rat brain, cleaved the bradykinin (BK) molecule at the peptide bond Phe5-Ser6 releasing two peptide fragments Arg-Pro-Pro-Gly-Phe and Ser-Pro-Phe-Arg. Conversion of these peptides and BK to fluorescent compounds using 1-dimethylaminonaphthalene-5-sulfonyl (DNS)-chloride, made it possible to detect the peptides on a silica gel plate at 0.25 – 2.0 nmol. These dansylated peptides were clearly separated using a mixture of toluene:monochloroethanol:25% NH3 (6:10:0.5, v v% ) as developing solvent. With the aid of this solvent and dansylation, it was possible to determine the rate of the enzymatic reaction by the appearance of products and/or the disappearance of the substrate on the chromatogram. The kinetics of the enzyme were examined using this method.
