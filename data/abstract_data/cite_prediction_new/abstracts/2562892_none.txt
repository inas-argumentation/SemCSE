CHEMICAL CONSTITUENTS AND BIOLOGICAL ACTIVITY FROM CHLOROFORM EXTRACT OF ZILLA SPINOSA
Six known compounds, bergaptane, psoralene, umbleferone, s-amyrin, friedelene and sterol-β-D-glucopyranoside in addition to Campesterol, Spinasterol, s -Sitosterol and Stigmasterol were isolated and identified from chloroform extract of the aerial parts of Zilla spinosa. Air-dried powder of the aerial parts of Zilla spinosa was extracted with petroleum ether (40-60oC) and the solvent was stripped off under reduced pressure. The above lipoidal matter was subjected to saponification for subsequent investigation. GLC analysis of the unsaponifiable matter from petroleum ether extract revealed series of hydrocarbons ranging from C14- C28 in addition to Campesterol, Spinasterol, s-Sitosterol, α-Amyrine, s–Amyrine, Squalene, and Stigmasterol. Fatty acids analysis by using GLC technique revealed the presence of nine fatty acids. The anti-inflammatory activity of CHCl3 extract from the aerial parts of Zilla spinosa was assayed in male rate using carrageenan–induced rat paw edema in comparison with Indomethacin. Analgesic activity of CHCl3 extract of Zilla spinosa was assayed in mice using acetic acid. The crude drugs were maximally active at dose (400mg/kg b.wt). Biological screening of the chloroform extract showed that the plant is highly safe and has analgesic, anti-inflammatory, and anti-microbial effects. The chloroform extract was tested against two human tumor cell lines namely; colon (HCT116) and liver (HepG2). The extract showed the more active against colon (HCT116) cell line IC50 14.5 ug/ml.
