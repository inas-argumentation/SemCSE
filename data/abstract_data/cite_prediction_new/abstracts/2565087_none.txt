Dexmedetomidine: Exploring Its Potential Role and Dosing Guideline for Its Use in Intractable Pain in the Palliative Care Setting
Intractable pain continues to pose problems for patients with life-limiting disease. The authors review the potential role of dexmedetomidine (Precedex), an α2-adrenergic agonist, as a bridge to obtaining effective analgesia. The authors offer criteria to consider in utilizing this medication within the context of palliative care.
