The M-Commerce Architecture Study Based on SaaS Model
As the M-Commerce(mobile commerce) has become a hotspot of the e-commerce development, enterprises focus their eyes on how to make greater profits by M-Commerce. The emergence of SaaS has provided an opportunity for the mobile commerce development of the enterprises, especially SMEs(small and medium-sized enterprises). Aiming at the existing problems of M-Commerce (such as the limited resource of mobile terminals, network security, physical security, the high cost of building a mobile commerce system and so on), this paper puts forward a mobile commerce architecture based on the SaaS model, then introduces the business operations under this architecture by mobile terminals, and pointes out the advantages of the architecture. KeywordsM-Commerce; mobile terminal ;mobile network;SaaS
