THE EFFECT OF HOMOGENISATION COOLING RATE ON DEFORMATION STRESS AND HARDENEBILITY OF Al-6063 ALLOY
In this study, specimens taken from Etial-60 alloy billets (in the as-cast condition) were cooled at six different cooling rates following the soaking period in the homogenisation furnace. Specimens were then subjected to hot pressing test at 450, 500, 550 o C and water quenched. These samples were finally subjected to artificial ageing to establish homogenisation cooling rate-flow stress and cooling rate-hardening capacity relationship. Experimental results show that homogenisation cooling rate has a significant effect on both the flow stress and ageing behavior. A decrease in cooling rate results in the reduction in both values for flow stress and hardness. It is believed that the optimum cooling rate should thus be determined by taking into consideration of hardness value set by the standards.
