Coupling 40S ribosome recruitment to modification of a cap-binding initiation factor by eIF3 subunit e
Recruitment of ribosomes to the mRNA 5′ terminus involves the activity of a large number of functionally discrete eukaryotic translation initiation factors (eIFs). However, how multiple eIFs coordinate during translation initiation remains poorly understood. Walsh and Mohr now identify an eIF3 subunit that regulates eIF4F modification and show that eIF3e is required for inducible eIF4E phosphorylation. This study establishes a mechanism by which 40S ribosome loading imparts a phosphorylation mark on the cap-binding eIF4F complex that regulates selective mRNA translation.
