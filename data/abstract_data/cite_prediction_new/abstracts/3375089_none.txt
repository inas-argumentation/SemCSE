Human cell culture: an important tool for the diagnosis and understanding of disease.
Human cells in culture can behave in many ways but the most common culture type is the diploid or chromosomally normal cell line established from biopsy of human tissue. A specialized variety of short-term culture is applied to peripheral blood and is especially useful for the demonstration of human chromosomes. Metabolic defects of the whole individual are reflected in cell culture which then becomes useful for definitive diagnosis and detailed study of the defect. Important processes such as aging, neoplasia, differentiation, and hybridization are appropriately investigated in cultures of human cells. Furthermore, the cell as a unit of biological activity, continues to be an important concept in the study of human disease.
