Conformational study of poly‐ΔAbu peptides and construction of amphipathic nanostructure
Generally, it is believed that α,β‐dehydroamino acid residues are most stable in the Z‐ form rather than in the E‐ form. In this study, it has been shown that poly‐ΔAbu exists in alternate ΔEAbu and ΔZAbu form, with ϕ, ψ values of ∼0°, 80° and 35°, 27° for the E‐ and Z‐ forms, respectively, rather than the all Z‐ form. The conformational results for the peptides Ac‐ΔAbu2‐NHMe and Ac‐L/D‐Ile‐ΔZAbu/ΔEAbu‐NHMe suggest the incorporation of ΔAbu in both the Z‐ and E‐ forms, which is consistent with the available observations in natural systems. Because of adoption of ϕ, ψ values corresponding to the collagen type structure (∼−30°, 120°) by Ile residue and with ϕ, ψ values in the left‐handed helical region for ΔZAbu residue, the peptide Ac‐(L‐Ile‐ΔZAbu)3‐NHMe adopts an amphipathic left‐handed helical β‐sheet type structure. This structure is stabilized by network of hydrogen bonding, carbonyl–carbonyl, and CH—O interactions and can be exploited for the construction of antimicrobial peptides and nanomaterials. © 2008 Wiley Periodicals, Inc.
