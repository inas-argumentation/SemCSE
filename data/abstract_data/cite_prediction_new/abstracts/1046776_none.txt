Variational and norm criteria in high-order perturbative-variational wavefunctions
The behaviour of high-order perturbative-variational wavefunctions, with one or more free parameters, is studied. A variational criterion, and a criterion based upon variation of the norm of the perturbative approximation function with order, are used for fixing free parameters. For the quartic anharmonic oscillator, both criteria seem to be equivalent. The obtained energies, not only for the ground state but also for excited states, are in excellent agreement with previous results, although when the authors go to higher excited states they lose precision because of the worsening of the perturbative-variational wavefunctions.
