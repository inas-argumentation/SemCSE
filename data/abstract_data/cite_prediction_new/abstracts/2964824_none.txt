Role of diffusion‐weighted magnetic resonance imaging in the evaluation of scalp dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans
Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP) is a rare tumor that grows with tentacle‐like projections. In particular, on the scalp this could explain the frequent incomplete resections and the high rate of multiple local recurrences. Adequate pre‐ and postoperative imaging methods to visualize these prolongations are required to determine the extent of the tumor and to intercept recurrences in a short time base.
