Data, Phenomena, and Theory: How Clarifying the Concepts Can Illuminate the Nature of Science
Learning about the Nature of Science (NOS) has been an item in most science curricula reforms for the past few decades. It has been given special prominence in recent U.S. science reform projects such as the National Science Teacher Association’s (NSTA) Scope Sequence and Coordination; American Association for the Advancement of Science’s (AAAS) The Liberal Art of Science, Project 2061 and Benchmarks for Science Literacy; the U.S. National Academy of Science’s National Science Education Standards, and their subsequent publication Inquiry and the National Science Education Standards: A Guide for Teaching and Learning.
