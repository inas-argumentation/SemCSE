Surfaces of Low Degree Containing a Canonical Curve Izzet Coskun
In this paper, we generalize a classical theorem of del Pezzo [D] and Fujita [F1] and a recent theorem of Casnati [Ca] about low degree surfaces containing a canonical curve Ccan. Every canonical curve of genus g is contained in a surface of degree less than or equal to 2g− 3. We study canonical curves that are contained in a surface of degree smaller than 2g−3. Fix an integer κ ≥ −1. Our main theorem is that if g > 3κ+12 and (g, κ) 6= (10,−1), then the minimal degree surface S containing Ccan has degree d = g+κ if and only if κ is odd and C is a double cover f : C → B of a curve B of genus κ+3 2 .
