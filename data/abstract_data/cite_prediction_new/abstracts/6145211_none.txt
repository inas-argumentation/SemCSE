Effective stimulation of neutropoiesis with rh G-CSF in dyskeratosis congenita: a case report
SummaryDyskeratosis congenita (DC) is a rare congenital X-linked disorder. The major clinical manifestations are abnormal skin pigmentation, nail dystrophy, and leukoplakia of mucosal membranes. About 50% of the patients develop bone marrow failure, which is partly responsible for the poor prognosis. Recombinant human granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (rhGM-CSF) has been administered to some neutropenic patients with DC, but only a moderate stimulation of neutropoiesis has been observed. We report on a patient with DC treated with recombinant human granulocyte-colony-stimulating factor (rhG-CSF). This treatment resulted in a substantial dose-dependent increase in the neutrophil count.
