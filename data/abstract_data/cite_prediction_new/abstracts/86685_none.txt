Electrophysiological evidence for voltage-gated calcium channel 2 (Cav2) modulation of mechano- and thermosensitive spinal neuronal responses in a rat model of osteoarthritis
Highlights • MIA-dependent antinociceptive effect of TROX-1 on neuronal activity.• Alterations in Cav2.2 channel function contribute to osteoarthritic (OA) pain.• Blocking Cav2.2 channels has therapeutic potential for treating OA pain.
