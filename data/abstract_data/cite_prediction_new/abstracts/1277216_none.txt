Erratum: Bulk flow and shear moments of the SFI++ survey
The paper ‘Bulk flow and shear moments of the SFI++ survey’ was published in Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 387, 825–829 (2008). There is a typographical error in the penultimate paragraph that reads ‘The composite SFI++ catalogue gives an estimate of the mean bulk flow to have a magnitude of 22 km s−1 ± 71 km s−1 toward l = 285◦ ± 14◦ and b = 11◦ ± 10◦ where l and b are the galactic longitude and latitude, respectively.’ The correct bulk flow magnitude should be 288 km s−1 ± 71 km s−1. Nothing else in the article should be affected.
