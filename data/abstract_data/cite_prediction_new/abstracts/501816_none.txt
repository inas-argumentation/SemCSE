An assessment of forest biomass maps in Europe using harmonized national statistics and inventory plots
Highlights • We assessed four biomass maps for Europe using harmonized biomass reference data.• The harmonized statistics were derived from ∼430,000 plots from 26 countries.• All maps overestimated at low biomass and underestimated at high biomass.• All maps had an overall negative bias (23–43 Mg ha−1 at national level).• The maps relative errors was 29–40% at national level and 63–72% at cell level.
