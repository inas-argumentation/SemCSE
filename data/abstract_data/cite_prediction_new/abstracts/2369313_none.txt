Fertility of bull semen with added beta-glucuronidase.
Motility and fertility of bull sperm were determined following storage in semen extender containing β -glucuronidase. β -glucuronidase retarded loss of sperm motility during 7 days storage at 5C.A preliminary trial revealed no harmful influence of β -glucuronidase on fertility of 279 cows inseminated with frozen semen. Consequently, 120 ejaculates from 14 bulls were used to determine fertility of frozen semen extended in yolk-citrate-glycerol with 0, 10, 30, or 150 units of added β -glucuronidase, or with 10 or 20 μ g of added α-amylase per milliliter. Bach enzyme treatment was replicated three times. The 60- to 90-day nonreturn percentages and numbers of first services (in parentheses) for 0, 10, 30 and 150 units of β -glucuronidase were 72.0 (3,401), 72.3 (1,961), 73.2 (1,912) and 77.9 (1,734). Fertility for 150 units was greater than that for 10 and 30 units of β -glucuronidase (P≃0.02). Comparable values for 10 and 20 μ g of α-amylase per milliliter of semen extender were 74.3 (1,504) and 74.2 (2,057).
