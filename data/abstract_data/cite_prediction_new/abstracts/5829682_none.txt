Startup sequence for a grid connected single phase voltage source inverter
Startup is known as the most critical phase of any power electronic converter. Uncharged capacitors and resonance circuits cause high current spikes during startup that reduce the lifespan of semiconductors or in the worst case, immediate break down. Although the startup of power electronic converters is a very practical and necessary topic, only few papers cover different startup algorithms. No papers analyze in detail the startup of a grid connected voltage source inverter (VSI). Nevertheless, it is the most widely used topology in many power electronic applications. In this paper, general principles of the startup are formulated. Different startup strategies of a grid connected VSI are analyzed and simulated. Based on the simulation results, the best method is chosen.
