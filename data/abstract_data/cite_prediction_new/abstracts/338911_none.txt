The in(ter)visible subjects of security and the (im)possibility of a ‘public’
This short commentary argues that while Barnett’s critique of critical security and resilience studies needs to be made, the route suggested through the emphasis on ‘the public’ as the milieu of security lacks a necessary engagement with concepts of visibility and transparency. These concepts are used in Barnett’s piece very much in the same gestural way as he observes the critical security studies that he critiques.
