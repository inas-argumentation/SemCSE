Rapid Infusion of Rituximab in a Heterogenous Pediatric Population: Safety and Feasibility
To the Editor: Many oncologic and immunologic disorders are treated with rituximab, a monoclonal anti-CD20 antibody. This treatment carries a risk for infusion-related reaction (IRR),1 therefore, a slow rate of infusion is recommended, resulting in almost 6 hours of infusion for first dose and 4 hours for subsequent doses. Nevertheless, rapid infusions of rituximab (RIR) seem safe in adults,2 but data are missing on RIR in children. To address this issue, we assessed a 90-minute infusion (RIR) protocol in a pediatric population at the SainteJustine University Hospital Center, a 500-bed tertiary hospital in Montréal, Canada.
