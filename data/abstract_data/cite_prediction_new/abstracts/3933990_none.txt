Graphs Admitting k-NU Operations. Part 2: The Irreflexive Case
We describe a generating set for the variety of reflexive graphs that admit a compatible k-ary near-unanimity operation; we further delineate a very simple subset that generates the variety of j-absolute retracts; in particular we show that the class of reflexive graphs with a 4-NU operation coincides with the class of 3-absolute retracts. Our results generalise and encompass several results on NU-graphs and absolute retracts from the past 30 years.
