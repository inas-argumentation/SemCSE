“Particle-sieve” electrophoresis of viruses in polyacrylamide gels, exemplified by purification of turnip yellow mosaic virus
Turnip yellow mosaic virus has been purified by electrophoretic “particle-sieving” in a polyacrylamide gel. This technique can be generally applied to fractionation of viruses, provided their shape and size allow them to penetrate the pores of the gel.
