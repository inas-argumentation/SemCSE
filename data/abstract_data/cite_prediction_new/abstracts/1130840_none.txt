Nutrient conditions in relation to bacterial flocculation, extracellular polymer production and sludge settlement
SummaryThree bacterial species (one flocculent, one non-flocculent and one filamentous) were grown in pure culture under varying conditions of dissolved oxygen and nutritional balance. 60 hour growth samples were harvested and the extracellular polymer extracted. The analytical data from the fermentation and the extracted polymer were used to explain how the presence of the bacteria in activated sludge might affect the settlement of that sludge.
