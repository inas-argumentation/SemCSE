Navigating the Local Modes of Big Data: The Case of Topic Models.
We consider the problem of multi-modality as it arises in the use of latent variable models to analyze big data. We focus in particular on unsupervised topic models which provide a powerful method for discovering latent structure in large corpora of unstructured texts. Although these approaches have proven useful throughout the social sciences, these models often have extremely multi-modal likelihood surfaces and different starting values will produce different parameter estimates. In this chapter, we develop tools for assessing sensitivity to initialization, selecting a model to analyze and improving the quality of starting values. We offer the perspective that even if the user is unable to identify the global maximum, local modes can provide useful and sometimes even better solutions for the data under study. ∗Prepared for “Data Science for Politics, Policy and Government”, R.
