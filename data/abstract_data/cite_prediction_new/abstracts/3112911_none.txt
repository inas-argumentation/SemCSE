Contemporary Public School Curriculum: Costs and Consequences
Matthew Weinstein is an Associate Professor at University of Washington–Tacoma, where he coordinates the secondary science education program. His research focuses on science education and the cultural studies of science, the history and culture of human guinea pigs, and critical approaches to science literacy. He is author of Robot World: Education, Popular Culture, and Science, and many articles and chapters on the politics of human subjects and its implications for science education.
